Jack climbed out of the sled as Annie stepped off the runners. A thin layer of ice covered their faces and clothes, but they were both laughing as they stamped their feet and swung their arms. 杰克从雪橇上爬下来，安妮则从雪橇的滑板上跳了下来。他们脸上和衣服上覆盖着一层薄冰，但他们都笑着，踩着脚，挥舞着胳膊。

“You made it back!” Oki shouted. “你们终于回来了！”奥基喊道。

“What time is it?” asked Jack. “现在几点了？”杰克问。

“Almost six o’clock!” said Oki. “差不多六点啦！”奥基说。

Jack smiled at Annie. “With time to spare!” he said. 杰克对安妮笑了笑。“还有时间！”他说。

“Thank God you are safe!” said Uncle Joe. “感谢上帝你平安无事！”乔叔叔说。

“Did you find Gunnar Kaasen?” Oki asked. “你们找到冈纳·卡森了吗？”奥基问。

“We did!” said Annie. “On the trail below Port Safety! His sled had overturned. The medicine was lost, but Balto helped us find it!” “我们找到了！”安妮说。“在波特安全港下面的小路上！他的雪橇翻倒了。药品丢了，但巴尔托帮我们找到了！”

“Gunnar and his team arrived in Nome just ahead of us,” said Jack. “They’re delivering the medicine to the hospital!” “冈纳和他的队伍比我们早到诺姆，”杰克说。“他们正在把药品送到医院！”

Uncle Joe closed his eyes and sighed with relief. Oki couldn’t stop grinning. He pumped Jack’s mittened hand, and Annie’s, too. “Thank you! Thank you! You are champions!” 乔叔叔闭上眼睛，松了口气。奥基忍不住一直笑。他握了握杰克和安妮戴着手套的手。“谢谢你们！谢谢你们！你们是冠军！”

“Don’t thank us!” said Jack. “Thank your dogs—they’re the real champions!” “别谢我们！”杰克说。“谢谢你们的狗——它们才是真正的冠军！”

Oki opened the gate to the dog pen. The team of huskies walked in, panting wearily. Jack and Annie helped Oki and Uncle Joe unhitch the dogs from their neck lines and tug lines and pull off their harnesses. Then they massaged the dogs’ backs and legs. 奥基打开狗舍的门。这群哈士奇走进来，喘着气。杰克和安妮帮奥基和乔叔叔解开狗的颈绳和牵引绳，取下它们的挽具。然后他们给狗按摩背部和腿部。

Oki gave the dogs water, and Jack and Annie fed them dried fish. The huskies wolfed down their meal and then howled with gratitude. As the sun rose over Nome, the eight brave dogs curled their bodies into the bright snow, covered their noses with their tails, and closed their eyes. 奥基给狗倒了水，杰克和安妮用干鱼喂它们。哈士奇们狼吞虎咽地吃完饭，然后高兴地嚎叫起来。当太阳升起在诺姆上空时，这八只勇敢的狗蜷缩在雪地里，用尾巴盖住鼻子，闭上了眼睛。

“Take a long rest, team,” said Jack. “大家好好休息一下，”杰克说。

“You deserve it,” said Annie. “你们值得休息，”安妮说。

“Let’s go to the hospital,” said Uncle Joe. “我们去医院吧，”乔叔叔说。

“Yes!” said Oki. “好！”奥基说。

“We’ll walk with you,” said Annie. “我们会陪你们去的，”安妮说。

Uncle Joe hobbled on his crutch as the four of them headed to town. Reflecting the brilliant blue of the early-morning sky, the snow itself looked blue. 乔叔叔拄着拐杖一瘸一拐地走着，他们四个人正往镇上走。雪地反射着清晨天空明亮的蓝色，看起来也变成了蓝色。

By the time they arrived at the hospital, a small crowd had gathered. Jack saw Mayor Maynard and Gunnar Kaasen surrounded by a group of reporters with cameras and notebooks. 他们到达医院时，已经聚集了一小群人。杰克看到梅纳德市长和冈纳·卡森被一群拿着相机和笔记本的记者围住了。

Gunnar’s huskies were off to the side. Children were petting them, and photographers were taking pictures. As the dogs smiled and panted, Jack could see their clouds of white breath. 冈纳的哈士奇们在旁边。孩子们正在抚摸它们，摄影师们也在拍照。当这些狗开心地喘着气时，杰克看到它们呼出的白色气息像云朵一样。

“The newspapers are here!” said Oki. “The whole world will soon hear this story! We will tell everyone what you have done, Jack and Annie. They will honor you, too!” “报纸来了！”奥基说，“很快全世界都会听到这个故事！我们会告诉每个人杰克和安妮做了什么，他们也会表扬你们的！”，

Oki and his uncle walked faster. 奥基和他的叔叔走快了一些。

“Whoa, wait,” Jack said to Annie. “Stardust.” “等等，”杰克对安妮说，“星尘。”

“Right.” Annie reached into her pocket and pulled out the dark blue box. “对。”安妮把手伸进口袋，拿出那个深蓝色的盒子。

“Are you coming?” Oki called back to them. “你们要来吗？”奥基朝他们喊道。

“Yes! We’ll be right there!” shouted Jack. “来啦！我们马上就到！”杰克喊道。

As Oki and his uncle headed toward the crowd, Annie lifted the lid of the tiny box. Silvery powder shimmered inside. “We wish to be forgotten by everyone who saw us on our mission!” Annie said. Then she tossed the contents of the box into the air. 当奥基和他的叔叔朝人群走去时，安妮掀开了那个小盒子的盖子。盒子里闪着银色的粉末。“我们希望所有在我们执行任务时看到我们的人，都把我们忘了！”安妮说。然后她把盒子里的东西撒向空中。

Like tiny grains of windswept snow, the stardust flashed brilliantly over the street. Before anyone could even look up, the silver dust evaporated, and the sharp, crisp air was clear again. 就像被风吹过的雪粒一样，星尘在街上闪着耀眼的光芒。还没等任何人抬头看，银色的尘埃就消失了，空气又变得清澈起来。

Annie put the tiny box back in her pocket. “Done,” she said. 安妮把那个小盒子重新放回口袋里。“完成了，”她说。

Then she and Jack headed toward the gathering in front of the hospital. When they drew close to the crowd, Oki caught sight of them—but then he looked away, as if he’d never seen them before. His uncle did the same. Gunnar Kaasen also glanced in their direction, but his eyes passed over them without interest. 然后她和杰克朝医院前面聚集的人群走去。当他们走近人群时，奥基看到了他们——但接着他转过头去，好像从未见过他们一样。他的叔叔也是一样。加纳尔·卡森也朝他们的方向看了一眼，但他的目光从他们身上掠过，毫无兴趣。

“I guess it worked,” said Jack. “我想它起作用了，”杰克说。

“Yeah, no one remembers us at all,” Annie said. She sounded sad. Jack felt a little sad, too. “是啊，没人记得我们了，”安妮说。她听起来有点难过。杰克也感到有点难过。

“Mayor Maynard! Tell us about the heroes of the great serum race!” a reporter said. “梅纳德市长！告诉我们关于伟大血清赛的英雄们吧！”一位记者说道。

Mayor Maynard proudly stepped forward. “For five and a half days, twenty mushers and more than a hundred and sixty dogs hauled the lifesaving medicine to Nome!” he proclaimed. 梅纳德市长自豪地走上前。‘在五天半的时间里，二十位雪橇手和一百六十多只狗把救命的药品运到了诺姆！’他宣布道。

“How many miles was that?” a reporter asked as he scribbled on a pad. ‘那有多远呢？’一位记者一边在便签本上写着，一边问道。

“Nearly seven hundred miles!” said the mayor. “And two of our citizens from Nome heroically took part in the relay—Leonhard Seppala and Gunnar Kaasen. Both mushers had strong lead dogs. Leonhard and his dog Togo ran across frozen Norton Sound to Golovin, and Gunnar and his dog Balto brought the medicine from Bluff to Nome!” ‘将近七百英里！’市长说道。‘而且，诺姆的两位市民英勇地参与了接力——莱昂哈德·塞帕拉和冈纳·卡森。两位雪橇手都有强壮的领头狗。莱昂哈德和他的狗托戈穿越了结冰的诺顿湾，到达了戈洛文，而冈纳和他的狗巴尔托则从布勒夫把药品带到了诺姆！’

Another reporter yelled to Gunnar Kaasen, “Congratulations, Mr. Kaasen! To what do you owe the success of your part of the relay?” 另一位记者朝冈纳·卡森喊道：‘恭喜你，卡森先生！你认为自己接力部分成功的原因是什么？’

“I owe everything to my lead dog, Balto!” said Gunnar. The famous musher pointed at the husky sitting patiently nearby. “He’s a working dog, like all the others. But he’s the best!” ‘我一切都归功于我的领头狗巴尔托！’冈纳说道。这位著名的雪橇手指着旁边耐心坐着的哈士奇。‘他是一只工作犬，和其他狗一样。但他是最棒的！’

In the daylight, Jack got a good look at Balto. The husky had shaggy black fur, except for white paws and a white chest and a bit of white around his mouth. He had gentle brown eyes. 在白天，杰克好好地看了看巴尔托。这只哈士奇有着蓬松的黑毛，除了白色的爪子、白色的胸部和嘴周围的一点白色。他有一双温柔的棕色眼睛。

“You told the mayor that you lost the medicine in an accident at one point,” the reporter said. ‘你告诉市长，你在某个时候因为一次事故失去了药品，’记者说道。

Gunnar nodded. “Yes, that’s right. Yes, I did.” 冈纳点了点头。‘是的，没错。是的，我确实这样做了。’

“And how did you find it?” the reporter asked. ‘那你是怎么找到它的？’记者问道。

“I…well, I…” The musher shook his head. “I’m not sure. After the accident, I discovered it missing. Somehow the package had come untied from my sled. I panicked and…well, I dug around in the snow. And…I don’t know…I guess I just found it.” “我……嗯……”赶狗人摇了摇头。“我不太确定。事故发生后，我发现自己少了这个东西。不知怎么的，包裹从我的雪橇上松开了。我慌了，然后……嗯……我在雪里翻找了一番。然后……我不知道……我想我只是找到了它。”

“It seems like a miracle!” the reporter said. He turned back to the mayor. “What a disaster it would have been if the medicine were lost on the trail!” “这简直是个奇迹！”记者说道。他转身面对市长。“如果药品在途中丢失了，那将会是一场多么可怕的灾难啊！”

“Indeed,” said Mayor Maynard. “The hard work of all the mushers and dogs would have been for nothing. And many more people would have lost their lives to diphtheria. But I have now delivered the package into the hands of Dr. Welch and Nurse Morgan, who will give the lifesaving serum to their patients!” “确实如此，”梅纳德市长说。“所有赶狗人和狗的努力都将白费，而且还有更多人会因为白喉而丧命。但现在我已经把这个包裹交到了韦尔奇博士和摩根护士手中，他们会把救命的血清给他们的病人！”

Everyone clapped and cheered. Oki and his uncle cheered the loudest. 大家鼓掌欢呼起来。奥基和他的叔叔欢呼得最响亮。

Jack and Annie looked at each other. “Time to go,” said Jack. 杰克和安妮互相看了看。“该走了，”杰克说。

“Okey dokey,” said Annie. “好的，”安妮说。

The reporter was asking more questions as Jack and Annie pulled away from the crowd, heading back to the tree house. They hadn’t gone far down the boardwalk when they heard barking. They both turned. 记者继续提问，而杰克和安妮则从人群中拉开了距离，朝树屋走去。他们还没走远，就听到一阵狗叫声。两人同时转过身来。

“Balto?” said Annie. “巴尔托？”安妮说。

The beautiful black husky was bounding after them. 那只美丽的黑毛哈士奇正朝他们跑来。

“Balto!” said Annie. She knelt in the blue dawn and reached out her arms. “巴尔托！”安妮说。她跪在蓝色的黎明中，张开双臂朝他伸去。

Balto nearly knocked her over as he licked her face. His dark eyes were twinkling. He seemed to be smiling. 巴尔托舔她的脸时差点把她撞倒。他黑亮的眼睛闪着光，好像在笑。

“He remembers us, Jack!” said Annie. “Balto remembers us!” “他记得我们，杰克！”安妮说。“巴尔托记得我们！”

“Yep,” said Jack. “But that’s okay. He won’t tell anyone.” “是啊，”杰克说。“不过没关系，他不会告诉别人的。”

Annie laughed. 安妮笑了。

“Balto!” Gunnar Kaasen shouted. “Come!” “巴尔托！”冈纳·卡森喊道。“来！”

The crowd barely glanced at Jack and Annie. 人群几乎没看一眼杰克和安妮。

“Balto!” called Gunnar. “巴尔托！”冈纳喊道。

“Go on, Balto,” Annie said. “You’re their hero. They need you.” “去吧，巴尔托，”安妮说，“你是他们的英雄，他们需要你。”

Balto gave her one more lick. Then he left them and trotted back toward the cheering crowd. His head and tail were held high, his fur fringed with sunlight. 巴尔托又舔了她一下。然后他离开了他们，小跑着回到欢呼的人群中。他的头和尾巴高昂着，毛发被阳光照得闪闪发亮。

Jack and Annie turned away and headed down the boardwalk. Soon they left Front Street and walked along the creek. They crossed the bridge and the frosty field to the bare tree with the tree house. 杰克和安妮转过身，沿着木板路走下。不久他们离开了前街，沿着溪流走过去。他们穿过桥和结着霜的田野，来到那棵光秃秃的树下，树上有个树屋。

In his thick fur boots and mittens, Jack clumsily climbed the rope ladder. Annie followed him. When they looked out the window, they were nearly blinded by the dazzling reflection of sunlight on the snow and the ice-covered sea. 穿着厚毛皮靴子和手套的杰克笨手笨脚地爬上了绳梯。安妮跟在他后面。当他们从窗户往外看时，雪地和结冰的海面上阳光反射出耀眼的光芒，几乎让他们睁不开眼。

“Mission accomplished,” said Annie. “任务完成，”安妮说。

“I guess we helped save lives,” said Jack. “我想我们确实救了一些人的命，”杰克说。

“Yep,” said Annie. “Now let’s go home and get warm.” She picked up the Pennsylvania book and pointed to a picture of the Frog Creek woods. “是啊，”安妮说。“现在我们回家去暖和暖和吧。”她拿起那本宾夕法尼亚的书，指着一张蛙溪镇森林的图片。

“I wish we could go there,” she said. “我希望我们能去那里，”她说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
