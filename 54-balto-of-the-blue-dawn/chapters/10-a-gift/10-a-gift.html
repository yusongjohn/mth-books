Jack felt warm, wonderfully warm. He was wearing his shorts and T-shirt again. No time at all had passed in Frog Creek. It was still twilight on a summer night. 杰克感觉暖暖的，特别暖和。他又穿上了短裤和T恤。在蛙溪镇，一点时间都没有过去。夏夜的黄昏依然挂在天空上。

“Nice,” said Annie. “真不错，”安妮说。

“Really nice,” said Jack. He reached into his pocket and took out the tiny box, empty of gold dust now. He placed it on the floor of the tree house, next to the guide to the Territory of Alaska. “真的很好，”杰克说。他把手伸进口袋，拿出那个小盒子，现在盒子里已经没有金粉了。他把盒子放在树屋的地板上，放在阿拉斯加地区指南的旁边。

Annie reached into her pocket and pulled out the shimmering dark blue box, empty of stardust. She put it beside the gold box on the floor. 安妮把手伸进她的口袋，拿出那个闪着暗蓝色光芒的盒子，现在盒子里已经没有星尘了。她把盒子放在地板上，放在那个金盒子的旁边。

“That was a good trip,” said Annie. “这次旅行真不错，”安妮说。

“Yeah, a little cold, a little stressful,” said Jack, “but all in all, very good.” “是啊，有点冷，有点紧张，”杰克说，“但总的来说，非常好。”

“The best part, of course, was the dogs,” said Annie. “当然，最棒的部分当然是那些狗，”安妮说。

“Totally,” said Jack. “Come on, let’s go. Let’s go home.” He started down the rope ladder. “完全正确，”杰克说。“走吧，我们走。回家去吧。”他开始顺着绳梯往下爬。

“Huskies are great dogs,” said Annie, following him. “哈士奇是很好的狗，”安妮跟着他说。

“Yep,” said Jack. “Strong and brave.” “没错，”杰克说。“强壮又勇敢。”

“And really smart, too. Like Saint Bernards. Remember Barry in Switzerland?” Annie said as she stepped off the ladder. “而且特别聪明，就像圣伯纳犬一样。还记得在瑞士的巴里吗？”安妮说着，从梯子上跳了下来。

“Of course,” said Jack. He smiled and shook his head, remembering the giant crazy puppy high in the mountains. “当然记得，”杰克说。他微笑着摇头，回想起高山中那只巨大的疯狗。

“And remember when Morgan turned Teddy into a terrier?” said Annie. “还记得摩根把泰迪变成了一只猎犬吗？”安妮说。

“Of course,” Jack said. “We’d better walk our bikes now. It’s getting dark fast.” “当然记得，”杰克说。“我们现在最好推着自行车走一走了，天很快就黑了。”

“Okay,” said Annie. She and Jack started pushing their bicycles over leaves and grass. “好的，”安妮说。她和杰克开始推着自行车，穿过树叶和草地。

The woods were filled with the sounds of summer. Bullfrogs croaked from the hidden creek. Crickets chirped in the grass. An owl hooted from a tree branch. Then came another sound: Yip! 树林里充满了夏天的声音。牛蛙从隐蔽的小溪里呱呱叫着。蟋蟀在草丛中唧唧叫着。一只猫头鹰从树枝上发出“喔喔”的叫声。接着又传来另一种声音：汪！

“What was that?” said Annie, stopping. “那是什么？”安妮停了下来，问。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “我不知道，”杰克说。

Yip! 汪！

“It sounds like a dog,” said Annie. “听起来像是一只狗，”安妮说。

“No way,” said Jack. “That’s too much of a coincidence!” “没门，”杰克说。“这太巧合了！”

Yip. Yip! 汪！汪！

“Coincidence or not, there’s a dog around here somewhere,” said Annie. She parked her bike against a tree. “不管是不是巧合，这里肯定有一只狗，”安妮说。她把自行车停在了一棵树旁。

“Seriously?” said Jack. “真的吗？”杰克说。

“Shhh—” said Annie. “嘘——”安妮说。

The leaves rustled. Yip. Yip! 树叶沙沙作响。汪！汪！

In the dark, Annie got down on her hands and knees. “Where are you?” she whispered. 在黑暗中，安妮跪在地上。“你在哪儿？”她小声说。

Yip, yip, yip! 汪，汪，汪！

“I’ve got you!” said Annie, and she lifted a wiggling little black-and-white puppy out of the greenery. “我找到你了！”安妮说，她从绿丛中抱起了一只扭来扭去的黑白小 puppy。

“I don’t believe this,” said Jack. “我简直不敢相信，”杰克说。

“Look—look!” said Annie. She stood up and carried the puppy over to Jack. “Here, pet him.” “看—看！”安妮说。她站起来，把小狗抱到杰克面前。“来，摸摸他。”

Jack reached out, and the next thing he knew, his hand was wet from puppy licks. “Oh, man!” he said, laughing. 杰克伸出手，下一秒，他就发现自己的手被小狗舔得湿湿的。“哦，天哪！”他笑着说。

“Can you push my bike?” Annie asked. “I’ll carry him to the street so we can get a good look at him.” “你能帮我推一下我的自行车吗？”安妮问。“我要抱着他走到街上，这样我们就能好好看看他。”

Annie took off with the puppy. 安妮带着小狗跑开了。

Jack tried to steer two bikes through the woods. Annie’s bike twisted and fell over, but Jack finally got a good grip and wheeled both bikes out to the sidewalk. 杰克试着在树林里操控两辆自行车。安妮的自行车扭来扭去，摔倒了，但杰克终于稳住了，把两辆自行车都推到了人行道上。

Annie was standing under a streetlamp, cradling the puppy in her arms. 安妮站在路灯下，用双臂抱着小狗。

“Let me see him,” said Jack. He parked the bikes and looked at the puppy under the light. The tiny dog had beautiful big brown eyes. His curly black-and-white fur was soft and shiny. “让我看看他，”杰克说。他把自行车停好，然后在灯光下仔细看着小狗。这只小狗有一双漂亮的大棕色眼睛。他卷曲的黑白毛发又软又亮。

“Ohh,” said Jack, stroking the furry little head. “He’s really cute.” “哦～”杰克一边抚摸着小狗毛茸茸的小脑袋，一边说。“他真的很可爱。”

“Let’s get him home,” said Annie. “I’m sure he’s hungry and thirsty.” “我们带他回家吧，”安妮说。“我肯定他现在又饿又渴。”

Jack grabbed their bikes again and started down the sidewalk with Annie and the puppy. “What breed do you think he is?” he asked. 杰克又抓起他们的自行车，和安妮还有小狗一起沿着人行道走下去。“你觉得他是什么品种的狗？”他问。

“I think he’s a mix,” said Annie. “我觉得他是一只混血狗，”安妮说。

“A mix of what, I wonder,” said Jack. “混血？混什么血呢，我好奇，”杰克说。

“Lots of dogs! All sizes, big, small, and in between. Saint Bernard, terrier, husky,” said Annie. “He’s a mix of all the dogs we’ve ever loved.” “很多狗啊！各种大小，大的、小的，还有中间的。圣伯纳犬、梗犬、哈士奇，”安妮说。“他是一只混血狗，把我们曾经喜欢过的所有狗狗都混在一起了。”

Jack nodded. “Cool,” he said. “I hope Mom and Dad will let us keep him.” 杰克点点头。“太酷了，”他说。“我希望爸爸妈妈能让我们把他留下来。”

“They will,” said Annie. “They said we could get a dog when we found the right one. And this is definitely the right one.” “他们会的，”安妮说。“他们说过，当我们找到合适的狗狗时，就可以领养一只。而这只狗狗肯定就是那只合适的。”

“Yeah, but what if he’s just lost?” said Jack. “是啊，但是万一他只是迷路了呢？”杰克说。

“Don’t worry. We’ll ask around and put up signs,” said Annie. “But I have a feeling he’s not lost. I think he was actually in the woods waiting for us. I think he’s a gift.” “别担心，我们会四处问问，还会贴一些寻狗启事，”安妮说。“不过我觉得他不是迷路了。我觉得他其实一直在森林里等着我们。我觉得他是一份礼物。”

“A gift? From who?” said Jack. “一份礼物？是谁送的？”杰克说。

“Maybe from Merlin and Morgan,” said Annie. “A gift to thank us.” “可能是梅林和摩根送的，”安妮说。“一份礼物，为了感谢我们。”

Jack smiled. He liked that idea. “So what do you think we should name him?” he said. 杰克笑了。他喜欢这个想法。“那你觉得我们应该给他取什么名字呢？”他说。

“Hmm…” Annie thought for a moment. “What about Oki?” “嗯……”安妮想了一会儿。“要不叫奥基怎么样？”

“Oki?” said Jack. “Like the boy we just met?” “奥基？”杰克说。“就是我们刚认识的那个小男孩吗？”

“Yeah, I like that name,” said Annie. “Plus, it’ll remind us of all the huskies and Balto.” “对，我喜欢这个名字，”安妮说。“而且它还会让我们想起那些哈士奇和巴尔托。”

“Yeah…,” said Jack. “But, hey, just promise me if he asks to go outside or eat or play with a toy, you won’t say ‘Okey dokey, Oki.’ ” “对……，”杰克说。“不过，嘿，你答应我，如果他想出去玩，或者吃东西，或者玩玩具，你不要说‘奥基，行吗？’。”

Annie laughed. “I promise,” she said. 安妮笑了。“我答应你，”她说。

They climbed the steps to their front porch. Then Annie stopped at the screen door. “Ready to meet the parents?” she asked the puppy. 他们爬上了台阶，来到门廊前。然后安妮在纱门那儿停了下来。“准备好去见爸爸妈妈了吗？”她问小狗。

Yip. Yip! 汪！汪！

“Okey dokey, Oki,” said Annie. “Let’s go inside.” “奥基，行吗？”安妮说。“我们进去吧。”
