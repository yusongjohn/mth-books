“Stay with me, boy!” Uncle Joe called after Oki. But the man’s voice was lost in the excited barking of the huskies in the yard. “和我在一起，孩子！”乔叔叔在奥基身后喊道。但男人的声音被院子里哈士奇兴奋的吠叫淹没了。

“Excuse me, sir,” Annie said. “What if we go with him?” “请问，先生，”安妮说，“如果我们和他一起去呢？”

“Annie—” said Jack, shaking his head. “安妮——”杰克说，摇了摇头。

The man turned and looked at Annie and Jack as if noticing them for the first time. “Who are you?” he said. 那人转过身，好像第一次注意到安妮和杰克。“你们是谁？”他说。

“Jack and Annie,” said Annie. “We’re friends of Oki’s, and—and—we’re champion mushers.” “杰克和安妮，”安妮说。“我们是奥基的朋友，而且——而且——我们是雪橇比赛的冠军。”

What? thought Jack. 什么？杰克心想。

Oki’s uncle scowled. “Champion mushers? How can that be? You are very young.” 奥基的叔叔皱起眉头。“雪橇比赛的冠军？这怎么可能？你们还很小。”

“We are young,” said Annie. “But we have great skills. Right, Jack?” She quickly added in a whisper, “Gold dust.” “我们确实很小，”安妮说。“但我们有很棒的技能。对吧，杰克？”她赶紧小声补充道：“金粉。”

Oh, right, thought Jack. He nodded. 哦，对，杰克心想。他点了点头。

“We heard that lives were in danger here,” said Annie. “So we came from the United States to help.” “我们听说这里有人的生命有危险，”安妮说。“所以我们从美国来这里帮忙。”

“Help how?” said Oki’s uncle. “怎么帮忙？”奥基的叔叔问。

“Take a team of sled dogs and help get the medicine here,” said Annie. “Right, Jack?” “带一队雪橇犬来这里，帮我们拿到药，”安妮说。“对吧，杰克？”

Jack took a deep breath. He was a little nervous about losing their way and freezing to death, but he nodded again. 杰克深吸了一口气。他有点担心迷路和冻死，但他又点了点头。

“We really want to help you,” Annie said to Oki’s uncle. “我们真的很想帮你们，”安妮对奥基的叔叔说。

The man stared at them for a moment, his face lined with grief. “I don’t know what to do,” he said. “I lost my wife and my two children seven years ago in the flu epidemic. Nome lost more than a thousand people then. I fear it is about to happen again.” Tears started down the man’s craggy face. 那个男人盯着他们看了片刻，脸上满是悲伤。“我不知道该怎么办，”他说。“七年前，我失去了我的妻子和两个孩子，他们死于流感大流行。当时，诺姆镇有好多人死去了，超过一千人。我担心，这可能会再次发生。”泪水从男人布满皱纹的脸上滑落下来。

Jack was stunned. More than a thousand people died? 杰克震惊了。“超过一千人死了？”

“That won’t happen again,” Jack vowed. “We’ll do everything we can.” “这不会再次发生，”杰克发誓。“我们会尽一切努力。”

“Thank you. But please leave my nephew here,” said the man. “Do not let him go. Oki and his mother and sister are all I have.” “谢谢你们。但请留下我的侄子，”男人说。“不要让他离开。奥基和他的妈妈、姐姐是我全部的依靠。”

“Don’t worry. We won’t let him go,” said Jack. “别担心，我们不会让他离开，”杰克说。

“Take my team. They are good dogs,” said Oki’s uncle. “They have traveled the trail hundreds of times, delivering mail with me.” “带上我的狗队，”奥基的叔叔说。“它们是好狗，已经和我一起走这条路线几百次了，一直帮我送信。”

“We promise we’ll take good care of them,” said Annie. “我们保证会好好照顾它们，”安妮说。

“Thank you.” The man bowed his head. “Thank you.” Then he turned and hobbled with his crutch back into his room. “谢谢。”男人低头鞠了一躬。“谢谢。”然后他转身，拄着拐杖一瘸一拐地回到房间里。

Annie took a deep breath. “This is a good plan,” she said to Jack. “Right?” 安妮深吸了一口气。“这是一个好计划，”她对杰克说。“对吧？”

“Right,” he said. He hoped it was a good plan. He felt terrible for Oki and his uncle. “对，”他说。他希望这真的是个好计划。他为奥基和他的叔叔感到非常难过。

“Gold dust?” said Annie. “金粉？”安妮说道。

Jack took off his mitten, reached into his pocket, and pulled out the tiny gold box. He pried open the lid and stared at the glittering dust inside. 杰克摘下他的手套，伸手进他的口袋，拿出那个小小的金盒子。他撬开盒盖，盯着里面闪闪发光的金粉。

“It’s so beautiful,” breathed Annie. “太美了，”安妮轻声说道。

Jack poured a bit of the gold dust into the palm of his bare hand. “We wish to be champion dog mushers to save lives,” he whispered. Then he tossed the dust into the air. The room filled with a sparkling gold light. The light gathered around Jack and Annie, then quickly faded away. 杰克把一点金粉倒在自己光秃秃的掌心。“我们希望成为冠军雪橇犬驾驶员，拯救生命，”他小声说道。然后他把金粉撒向空中。房间里充满了闪闪发光的金色光芒。光芒围绕着杰克和安妮，然后迅速消失了。

Jack felt a rush of energy. 杰克感到一阵能量涌上全身。

“Wow!” said Annie, grinning. “I’m ready to hit the trail. Are you?” “哇！”安妮说道，咧嘴笑着。“我准备出发了，你呢？”

“Yep!” said Jack. “Sure am.” He laughed. “当然！”杰克说道。“我准备好了。”他笑了。

“Quick, before Oki leaves with the dogs,” said Annie. She started for the door. “快点，奥基要带着狗离开了，”安妮说道。她朝门口走去。

“Wait,” said Jack. “The magic works for twelve hours.” He looked at a clock on the wall. It was eight-thirty. “We have to be back by eight-thirty tomorrow morning. No later.” “等一下，”杰克说道。“魔法只能持续十二个小时。”他看了看墙上的钟。现在是八点半。“我们必须在明天早上八点半之前回来。不能再晚了。”

“Got it!” said Annie. Then the two of them hurried out of the shack into the freezing night. “明白了！”安妮说道。然后两人赶紧跑出小屋，走进寒冷的夜晚。

The huskies were whining and leaping around the moonlit pen as Oki tried to buckle them into their harnesses. “Be still!” he commanded. “Be still!” 雪橇犬们在月光下围着围栏又叫又跳，奥基试图把它们套上挽具。“安静！”他命令道。“安静！”

“Wait, Oki!” said Jack. “等等，奥基！”杰克说。

Wrestling with a dog, Oki looked up at Jack. 奥基一边和狗搏斗，一边抬头看向杰克。

“Annie and I will look for Gunnar Kaasen!” said Jack. “Your uncle wants us to take the team by ourselves.” “安妮和我会去找冈纳·卡森！”杰克说。“你叔叔让我们自己带这支队伍。”

“By yourselves?” said Oki. “自己？”奥基说。

“Yes!” said Annie. “You have to stay here. He can’t risk losing you.” “是的！”安妮说。“你必须留在这里。他不能冒失去你的风险。”

“But how will you do this?” said Oki. “You don’t know the dogs or the trail.” “但是你们怎么做到呢？”奥基说。“你们不了解这些狗，也不熟悉这条路。”

“We have great skills,” said Jack. “And we trust the dogs. Your uncle told us they’ve traveled the trail hundreds of times.” “我们有很强的技能，”杰克说。“而且我们信任这些狗。你叔叔告诉我们，它们已经沿着这条路走了几百次了。”

“Yes…but—” said Oki. “是的……但是——”奥基说。

“Good! Then our job is just to help them do their job,” said Annie. “太好了！那么我们的任务就是帮助它们完成自己的任务，”安妮说。

“And your job, Oki, is to stay with your uncle and be near your sister and mother,” said Jack. “你的任务，奥基，是留在你叔叔身边，靠近你姐姐和妈妈，”杰克说。

“If you are out in the storm, your uncle will be sick with worry,” said Annie. “And your mother and sister would not want that.” “如果你在暴风雨中，你叔叔会非常担心，”安妮说。“你妈妈和姐姐也不希望这样。”

Oki stared out toward the frozen sea for a moment. He looked very troubled. “I want to go with you,” he said finally. “But I will stay here for the sake of my uncle.” He held the gate open so Jack and Annie could enter the dog pen. 奥基望着结冰的海面看了片刻。他看起来非常烦恼。“我想和你们一起去，”他最后说。“但为了我叔叔，我会留在这里。”他把门打开，让杰克和安妮进入狗舍。

“Come on, guys,” Jack said to the huskies. “Let’s buckle up!” He and Annie pulled off their mittens. Even in the dark, they had no trouble hitching the dogs into their harnesses. “来吧，伙计们，”杰克对哈士奇们说。“系好安全带！”他和安妮摘下手套。即使在黑暗中，他们也很轻松地把狗套上挽具。

“I’ll get the rigging,” said Oki. He grabbed a bundle of lines from the basket of the sled and began untangling them. “我去准备绳索，”奥基说。他从雪橇的篮子里拿出一捆绳子，开始解开它们。

When Jack and Annie finished putting harnesses on all the dogs, they hooked the team to the rigging. They quickly and calmly attached tug lines to the harnesses and fastened neck lines to collars. 当杰克和安妮给所有狗都套上挽具后，他们把狗队连接到绳索上。他们迅速而冷静地将牵引绳系在挽具上，并将颈绳系在项圈上。

“Work with us, guys!” Annie said as they tried to hitch the excited dogs to the towline. “和我们一起努力，伙计们！”安妮说，他们试图把兴奋的狗系在拖绳上。

A lead dog jumped up and licked Jack’s face. 一只领头狗跳起来，舔了舔杰克的脸。

Jack laughed. He knew not to scold the dogs and risk dampening their enthusiasm. The team would need their high spirits to run the tough race ahead. 杰克笑了。他知道不能责骂狗，否则会打击它们的积极性。狗队需要高昂的士气来应对接下来的艰难比赛。

Soon all the huskies were strung to the towline, lined up in pairs. The two lead dogs stood proudly in front, their tails high, their noses sniffing the air. The bells on their collars jingled. 很快，所有的哈士奇都被系在拖绳上，排成一对一对。两只领头狗骄傲地站在前面，尾巴高高翘起，鼻子嗅着空气。它们项圈上的铃铛叮当作响。

Two swing dogs stood behind the leads. Two team dogs stood behind them, and two wheel dogs stood directly in front of the sled. 两只摆动犬站在领头犬后面。两只团队犬站在它们后面，两只轮犬则直接站在雪橇前面。

“You guys are the best!” said Jack. “你们俩真是太棒了！”，杰克说道。

The dogs whined and barked, as if eager to go. 狗们哀叫并吠叫，好像迫不及待地想出发了。

“You are both very good with the dogs,” said Oki. “你们俩和狗相处得非常好，”奥基说道。

“Be firm. Be fair. Give kindness and respect,” said Annie. “That’s how we always treat them. We promise.” “要坚定。要公平。给予关爱和尊重，”安妮说道。“我们一直这样对待它们。我们承诺。”

“Thank you,” said Oki. “谢谢你们，”奥基说道。

Jack and Annie pulled on their mittens. “How far is Solomon?” Jack asked. 杰克和安妮戴上手套。“索洛门还有多远？”杰克问道。

“About thirty-five miles,” said Oki. “In about twenty miles you will pass the roadhouse in Port Safety. Maybe fifteen more, you will come to the roadhouse in Solomon, where you will find Gunnar Kaasen.” “大约三十五英里，”奥基说道。“大约二十英里后，你们会经过安全港的路屋。再走大约十五英里，你们就会到达索洛门的路屋，在那里你们会找到冈纳·卡森。”

“Any idea how long the trip should take?” asked Annie. “你知道这段旅程大概需要多长时间吗？”安妮问道。

“About five hours to get there,” said Oki. “大约五个小时就能到达那里，”奥基说道。

“So around ten to go and come back,” Annie said to Jack. “大概十点出发，十点回来，”安妮对杰克说。

“No problem. Where does the trail start?” Jack asked Oki. “没问题。小路从哪儿开始？”杰克问奥基。

“Just beyond the yard,” said the boy, “where you see tracks in the snow.” “就在院子外面，”男孩说，“在那儿你能看到雪地上的脚印。”

“Got it,” said Jack. He turned to Annie. “How about I drive first? You can sit in the basket, and then we’ll switch?” “明白了，”杰克说。他转身对安妮说，“我要先开车，你坐在雪橇的篮子里，然后我们再换？”

“Okey dokey,” said Annie. The dogs were still as she took her position in the basket of the sled. She sat on the wooden slats and held the side railings. “好的，”安妮说。狗狗们一动不动，她坐到雪橇的篮子里，坐在木板上，抓住了旁边的扶手。

Jack stepped onto the long runners that extended behind the basket. He gripped the two handles of the sled, bent his knees, and leaned forward. He felt as if he’d driven a dogsled all his life. 杰克踩上了后面拖着的长长的滑雪板。他抓住雪橇的两个把手，弯下膝盖，身体向前倾。他感觉好像自己一生都在驾着狗拉雪橇一样。

“I’ll get the snow hook,” said Oki. He picked up a metal hook that anchored the sled to the snow. He put it in the basket in front of Annie. Then he added the sack of dried fish. “Dog food,” he explained. “我去拿雪钩，”奥基说。他捡起一个金属钩子，用来把雪橇固定在雪地上。他把它放在安妮前面的篮子里。然后他又放了一袋干鱼。“这是狗粮，”他解释道。

“Cool,” said Annie. “真酷，”安妮说。

“All set?” Jack asked. “准备好了吗？”杰克问。

“All set,” Annie said. “Oki, please tell your uncle we’ll take good care of his team.” “准备好了，”安妮说。“奥基，请告诉你的叔叔，我们会好好照顾他的狗队。”

“See you in the morning!” said Jack. “明天早上见！”杰克说。

“Good luck!” said Oki. “祝你好运！”奥基说。

“Line out!” Jack called to the dogs, knowing all the right commands to use. Bells jingling, the lead dogs drew the team straight out in front of the pen. “排好队！”杰克对狗叫道，他知道该用哪些正确的口令。铃铛叮当作响，领头的狗把队伍拉得笔直，排在围栏前面。

“Hike!” Jack shouted. All eight huskies bounded forward and pulled the sled into the cold Alaskan night. “出发！”杰克喊道。八只哈士奇立刻向前冲去，把雪橇拉进了寒冷的阿拉斯加夜晚。
