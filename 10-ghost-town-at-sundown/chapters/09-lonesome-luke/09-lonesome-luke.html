9. Lonesome Luke 9. 寂寞卢克

The sun was low in the sky when they reached Rattlesnake Flats. 他们到达响尾蛇滩时，太阳已经低垂。

“Just let us off in front of the hotel,” said Annie. “就在旅馆前面让我们下吧，”安妮说。

“You sure the stage is coming through here?” said Slim. “你确定驿站马车会从这儿经过？”斯利姆说。

“Yup,” said Jack and Annie together. “确定，”杰克和安妮一起说。

In front of the hotel, Slim got down from Dusty. Then he helped Jack and Annie down. 在旅馆前面，斯利姆从达斯蒂身上下来。 然后他帮杰克和安妮下来。

“I hope you’ll come to Laramie and visit me,” said Slim. He winked. “I might be needing some help on my book.” “希望你们能来拉勒米看我，”斯利姆说。他眨了眨眼。 “我写书也许会需要帮手。”

“Sure,” said Annie. “当然，”安妮说。

Slim climbed back on Dusty. He looked down at Jack. 斯利姆又爬上达斯蒂。 他低头看着杰克。

“You know, Shorty,” he said, “you might be short, but you’re mighty tall in the brains.” “你个子也许矮，可脑子却高得很。”

“Thanks,” said Jack. “谢谢，”杰克说。

“And, Smiley,” Slim said. “Your great courage is nothing to smile about.” “还有，笑脸，”斯利姆说。 “你了不起的勇气可不是开玩笑的事。”

“Thanks,” said Annie. “谢谢，”安妮说。

“Good luck with your writing, Slim,” said Jack. “祝你写作顺利，斯利姆，”杰克说。

“I’m grateful to you both for steering me straight,” said Slim. “I promise I’ll thank you someday.” “我感谢你们俩把我引上正路，”斯利姆说。 “我保证有一天会感谢你们。”

“Really?” said Annie. “真的吗？”安妮说。

“A cowboy never goes back on his word,” said Slim. Then he snapped his reins, and Dusty loped down the street. “牛仔从不违背自己的承诺，”斯利姆说。 然后他甩了甩缰绳，达斯蒂沿着街道大步跑去。

“Bye, Slim!” yelled Annie. “再见，斯利姆！”安妮喊道。

Slim Cooley turned one last time. He waved his hat. “So long, pardners!” he called. 斯利姆·库利最后转过一次身，挥了挥帽子。 “再见了，伙计们！”他喊道。

Then he rode off into the sunset. 然后他骑马消失在夕阳里。

Jack let out a deep sigh. “Okay. I’m ready to take my boots off now,” he said. 杰克深深叹了口气。 “好了。 我现在可以脱靴子了，”他说。

“Me too,” said Annie. “我也是，”安妮说。

They sat down on the porch of the hotel. They started pulling off their boots. 他们在旅馆的门廊上坐下。 他们开始脱靴子。

“There!” Jack got them both off. “好啦！”杰克把两只靴子都脱了下来。

He wiggled his toes. He took his sneakers out of his pack and put them on. Annie put hers on, too. 他扭了扭脚趾。 他从背包里拿出运动鞋穿上。 安妮也穿上了她的。

“Man, sneakers never felt so good,” said Jack. “伙计，运动鞋从没这么舒服过，”杰克说。

Suddenly the sound of a piano drifted through the air. 突然，一阵钢琴声从空中飘来。

“Lonesome Luke!” said Annie. “寂寞卢克！”安妮说。

Jack grabbed his pack. He and Annie crept across the porch. They pushed open the swinging door. 杰克抓起背包。 他和安妮悄悄走过门廊。 他们推开弹簧门。

The piano was playing “Red River Valley.” Sitting on the piano stool was the dim but shimmering shape of a cowboy. 钢琴正弹着《红河谷》。钢琴凳上坐着一个模糊却闪着微光的牛仔身影。

Just then the ghost of Lonesome Luke looked at Jack and Annie. He waved a shimmering hand. 就在这时，寂寞卢克的鬼魂看着杰克和安妮。 他挥了挥一只闪着微光的手。

Jack and Annie waved back. 杰克和安妮也朝他挥手。

Then the ghost of Lonesome Luke faded away. Cold air wafted past Jack and Annie. They both shivered. 然后寂寞卢克的鬼魂渐渐消失了。 冷空气从杰克和安妮身边飘过。 他们俩都打了个寒颤。

“Oh, man, let’s go,” breathed Jack. “哦，天哪，我们走吧，”杰克喘着气说。

They leaped and dashed up the dusty road. They ran across the cracked ground and past the graveyard. They ran until they reached the tree with the Magic Tree House in it. 他们跳起来，沿着尘土飞扬的路飞奔。 他们跑过开裂的地面，经过墓地。 他们一直跑到魔法树屋所在的那棵树旁。

Annie grabbed the rope ladder. 安妮抓住绳梯。

She hurried up and Jack followed. They were out of breath when they got inside the tree house. 她赶紧往上爬，杰克跟着。 他们爬进树屋时已经上气不接下气。

Annie grabbed the ancient scroll. She unrolled it. 安妮抓起古老的卷轴，把它展开。

“Yay!” she said. “耶！”她说。

The scroll had one glowing word on it: 卷轴上有一个发光的字：

ECHO 埃科

“We got it right!” said Annie. “我们答对了！”安妮说。

Jack grabbed the Pennsylvania book. He pointed to a picture of the Frog Creek woods. 杰克抓起宾夕法尼亚的书。 他指着一幅蛙溪树林的图画。

“I wish we could go there!” he said. “我希望我们能去那儿！”他说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 绝对静止。
