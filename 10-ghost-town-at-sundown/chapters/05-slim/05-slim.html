5. Slim 5. 斯利姆

“We’re not horse thieves!” said Annie. “我们不是偷马贼！”安妮说。

“Well then, what are you doing with my horse here?” he said. “那你们拿着我的马在这儿干什么？”他说。

“Some bad guys came through town with his mother,” said Annie. “They left him behind because he was too slow.” “有些坏蛋带着他妈妈穿过镇子，”安妮说。 “他们因为他太慢，就把他丢下了。”

“Yup, must be the rustlers that stole my last five mustangs,” the cowboy said. “没错，一定是偷走我最后五匹野马的那些盗马贼，”牛仔说。

“Who are you?” said Jack. “你是谁？”杰克说。

“I’m a mustang herder,” the cowboy said. “我是野马牧人，”牛仔说。

“They rode through town. Then Sunset showed up all alone,” said Annie. “We’re taking him to his mother.” “他们骑马穿过镇子。 “后来日落孤零零地出现了，”安妮说。 “我们正带他去找妈妈。”

“Sunset?” the cowboy said. “日落？”牛仔说。

“Yup,” Annie smiled. “I named him.” “对，”安妮笑了。 “是我给他起的名。”

The cowboy put away his six-shooter. “Well, you’re pretty brave to try and rescue him, Smiley,” he said. 牛仔收起了左轮手枪。 “嗯，你挺勇敢，还想救它，笑脸，”他说。

“Thanks,” said Annie. “谢谢，”安妮说。

Jack cleared his throat. “A mustang needs his family,” he said. “The bond between a mare and her young is very strong.” 杰克清了清嗓子。 “野马需要家人，”他说。 “母马和幼驹之间的纽带非常牢固。”

The cowboy looked at Jack. “Whoa, you’re pretty smart to know that, Shorty.” 牛仔看着杰克。 “哇，你知道这个，还挺聪明嘛，矮个子。”

“Shorty?” said Jack. “矮个子？”杰克说。

“Every cowpoke’s got to have a nickname,” said the cowboy. “每个牛仔都得有个外号，”牛仔说。

“What’s yours?” said Annie. “你叫什么？”安妮说。

“Slim,” said the cowboy. “My name is Slim Cooley. And this is Dusty.” He patted his horse. “斯利姆，”牛仔说。 “我叫斯利姆·库利。 这是达斯蒂。”他拍了拍自己的马。

“That fits,” said Annie. “挺配的，”安妮说。

Jack agreed. Slim was slim. And Dusty was dusty. 斯利姆很瘦。 达斯蒂身上也满是灰尘。

“So tell me,” Slim said. “How did you two brave, smart young’uns end up in Rattlesnake Flats?” “那么告诉我，”斯利姆说。 “你们两个勇敢又聪明的小家伙，怎么会到响尾蛇滩来的？”

Jack caught his breath. He didn’t know how to explain it. 杰克屏住呼吸。 他不知道该怎么解释。

“Um … the stagecoach,” said Annie. “We begged the driver to let us off. But I think we made a mistake.” “呃……驿站马车，”安妮说。 “我们求车夫让我们下车。 “可我觉得我们弄错了。”

Slim looked around. “I’ll say,” he said. 斯利姆四周看了看。 “可不是嘛，”他说。

“When the next stage comes through, we’re leaving,” said Annie. “等下一班驿站马车来，我们就走，”安妮说。

“I see,” said Slim. “Well, I’m going to take my colt now and find those rustlers. You didn’t hear where they were headed, did you?” “明白了，”斯利姆说。 “好吧，我现在要带走我的小马驹，去找那些偷马贼。 你们没听见他们往哪儿去吗？”

“They said they were going to camp over the rise,” said Jack. “他们说要到山岗那边扎营，”杰克说。

“Hmm, must be over yonder,” said Slim. He looked at a low rise in the distance. The sun was a red ball above it. “嗯，一定是在那边，”斯利姆说。 他望向远处一座低矮的山岗。 太阳像个红球挂在山岗上方。

“Better get going before dark,” he said. “最好天黑前动身，”他说。

“Can we go with you?” said Annie. “我们能跟你一起去吗？”安妮说。

“No, we have to stay here,” Jack said quickly. Now that Slim could help Sunset, Jack wanted to look for the answer to the riddle. Plus, he still wanted to take off his boots. “不行，我们得留在这儿，”杰克赶快说。 既然斯利姆能帮助日落，杰克就想去找谜底。 而且，他还想把靴子脱掉。

“Shorty’s right to be scared,” Slim said to Annie. “This is no job for young’uns.” “矮个子害怕是对的，”斯利姆对安妮说。 “这可不是小孩子干的活。”

“Scared?” said Jack. “害怕？”杰克说。

“Oh, please! I want to go,” said Annie. “哦，求你了！ 我想去，”安妮说。

Slim looked at Jack. “And what do you want, Shorty?” 斯利姆看着杰克。 “那你呢，矮个子，你想怎样？”

For starters, he wanted Slim to stop calling him Shorty. And he wanted Slim to think he was brave. 首先，他希望斯利姆别再叫他矮个子。 而且他希望斯利姆觉得他很勇敢。

“Sure, I want to go,” said Jack. “当然，我想去，”杰克说。

“What about your stagecoach?” asked Slim. “那你们的驿站马车怎么办？”斯利姆问。

“It’s not coming until tomorrow,” Annie said quickly. “要到明天才会来，”安妮赶紧说。

“Well … ” Slim scratched his chin. “I reckon I could use some brave, smart help. But you have to do everything I say.” “这个……”斯利姆挠了挠下巴。 “我想我能用得上又勇敢又聪明的帮手。 可你们必须照我说的每件事去做。”

“We will!” said Annie. “Can I ride Sunset?” “我们会的！”安妮说。 “我能骑日落吗？”

“I wouldn’t say ‘yes’ to many kids, Smiley, but you seem to have a knack with horses,” said Slim. “Now, hang on tight to his mane, I’ll just pull him along behind me.” “换了别的孩子，我可不会答应，笑脸，可你好像挺有驯马的本事，”斯利姆说。 “现在，抓紧它的鬃毛，我就把它牵在我后面。”

Slim slipped the rope off the post. Then he held his hand out to Jack. 斯利姆把绳子从柱子上解下来。 然后他向杰克伸出手。

“Put your foot in the stirrup, Shorty. And grab my hand,” said Slim. “把脚放进马镫里，矮个子。 抓住我的手，”斯利姆说。

Jack did as Slim said. Slim pulled him onto the front of his saddle. 杰克照斯利姆说的做了。 斯利姆把他拉上马鞍前部。

Jack held on to the saddle horn. 杰克抓住鞍桥。

“Sit tight,” said Slim. “It’s not far.” “坐好了，”斯利姆说。 “不远。”

Slim snapped his reins. Dusty took off with Sunset right behind him. 斯利姆抖了抖缰绳。 达斯蒂跑了起来，日落紧跟在后面。

Jack bounced up and down. His boots hurt. The sunlight blinded him. 杰克上下颠簸着。 阳光刺得他睁不开眼。

“Giddy-up!” said Slim. “驾！”斯利姆说。

“Giddy-up!” said Annie. “驾！”安妮说。

The horses galloped across the prairie, dust flying from their hooves. 马儿们飞奔过草原，蹄下扬起尘土。

“Ah-choo!” Jack sneezed as he bounced along into the setting sun. “阿嚏！”杰克一边打着喷嚏，一边颠簸着朝夕阳奔去。
