7. Ghost Story 7. 鬼故事

Jack bounced in the saddle. He felt the cool night wind against his face. 杰克在马鞍上颠簸着。 他感到凉爽的夜风迎面吹来。

He couldn’t tell where they were going. But he trusted Dusty to follow the others. 他分不清他们在往哪儿走。 可他相信达斯蒂会跟上其他人。

Finally Dusty caught up with the herd as they began to slow down. 终于，达斯蒂赶上了开始放慢速度的马群。

Jack snapped his reins. Dusty came up beside Slim and Annie. 杰克抖了抖缰绳。 达斯蒂来到斯利姆和安妮身边。

“Howdy!” said Slim. “你好呀！”斯利姆说。

“Howdy!” said Jack. “你好！”杰克说。

“Howdy!” said Annie. “Are you okay?” “你好！”安妮说。 “你还好吗？”

Jack pushed his glasses into place. “Yup,” he said. “You?” 杰克推了推眼镜。 “嗯，”他说。 “你呢？”

“Yup,” she said. “嗯，”她说。

“That was some good riding, Shorty!” said Slim. “骑得真不错啊，矮个子！”斯利姆说。

“Thanks,” said Jack, smiling. He even liked being called “Shorty” now. “谢谢，”杰克笑着说。 他现在甚至喜欢别人叫他“矮个子”了。

“Where we headed, boss?” Jack asked Slim. “我们去哪儿，老板？”杰克问斯利姆。

“Blue Canyon,” said Slim. “Okay with you?” “蓝峡谷，”斯利姆说。 “你们觉得行吗？”

“Yup,” said Jack. “行，”杰克说。

“This way!” said Slim. He slapped his horse and they all speeded up again. “这边走！”斯利姆说。 他拍了拍马，他们又加快了速度。

Slim steered the herd to the left. Soon he led them through a deep, narrow pass. 斯利姆把马群往左带。 很快，他带着它们穿过一条又深又窄的山道。

Finally they came to a boxy open space surrounded by walls of rock and lit by moonlight. 最后他们来到一块四四方方的空地，四周是石壁，月光洒在上面。

“We’ll corral the mustangs here in Blue Canyon,” Slim said. “我们就在蓝峡谷这儿把野马圈起来，”斯利姆说。

He got off his horse. He helped Jack down. Annie slipped off Sunset. 他下了马。 他帮杰克下了马。 安妮从日落背上下来。

“Take him to his ma,” Slim told Annie. “带他去找妈妈，”斯利姆对安妮说。

Annie led Sunset to the mare. In the moonlight, the two mustangs rubbed against one another and neighed. 安妮把日落领到母马身边。 月光下，两匹野马互相摩擦着，发出嘶鸣。

As Jack patted Dusty’s damp neck, he remembered the last two rules: praise and reward. 杰克拍着达斯蒂潮湿的脖子，想起最后两条规则：表扬和奖励。

“Thanks,” he whispered to Dusty. “You were great. You were super great.” “谢谢，”他对达斯蒂轻声说。 “你真棒。 “你简直太棒了。”

Slim unsaddled Dusty, then handed Jack his saddle bags. 斯利姆给达斯蒂卸下马鞍，然后把鞍囊递给杰克。

“Take those over to that grassy spot. We’ll camp there,” he said. “把那些拿到那边有草的地方。 我们在那儿扎营，”他说。

As Jack carried the saddlebags, his boots felt stiff and tight. His legs were sore and wobbly. But he didn’t mind. 杰克扛着鞍囊时，靴子又硬又紧。 他的腿又酸又软。 可他并不介意。

He threw down the saddlebags and his backpack. Then he flopped himself down. He was very tired. Annie joined him. 他把鞍囊和背包扔到地上。 然后他一屁股坐下。 安妮也坐到他旁边。

“They seem so happy to be free and together again,” she said, gazing at the moonlit mustangs. “他们又能自由地待在一起了，看起来好幸福，”她说，望着月光下的野马群。

“Yup,” said Jack. “是啊，”杰克说。

He lay back, using his backpack as a pillow. He looked up at the stars. 他仰面躺下，把背包当枕头。 他抬头看着星星。

“If we just had the answer to the riddle, everything would be perfect,” he said. “要是我们能找到谜底，一切就完美了，”他说。

“Yup,” said Annie. “是啊，”安妮说。

“Hey, Slim,” he called. “I have a question for you.” “嘿，斯利姆，”他喊道。 “我有个问题要问你。”

“Shoot,” said Slim. “说吧，”斯利姆说。

“Do you know the answer to this riddle?” Jack asked. “Out of the blue, my lonely voice calls out to you. Who am I? Am I?” “你知道这个谜语的答案吗？”杰克问。 突然之间，我孤独的声音向你呼唤。 我是吗？”

Slim was silent for a moment, then said, “Sorry, Shorty, don’t know that one.” 斯利姆沉默了一会儿，然后说：“抱歉，矮个子，这个我不知道。”

Jack’s heart sank. “That’s okay,” he said. “We don’t either.” 杰克的心沉了下去。 “没关系，”他说。 “我们也不知道。”

“I have a question, too,” said Annie. “Why does the piano in the hotel play by itself?” “我也有个问题，”安妮说。 “旅馆里的钢琴为什么会自己弹起来？”

“I do know the answer to that one,” said Slim. “那个我倒知道答案，”斯利姆说。

“What is it?” said Annie. “是什么？”安妮说。

“It’s Lonesome Luke,” said Slim. “He’s a ghost of a cowboy who wanders the prairie.” “是寂寞卢克，”斯利姆说。 “他是个游荡在大草原上的牛仔鬼魂。”

Jack sat straight up. 杰克一下子坐直了。

“I saw him! I saw him!” he said. “I just remembered! He scared the rustlers! If he hadn’t come, I never would have gotten away!” “我看见他了！ 我看见他了！”他说。 “我刚想起来！ 他吓跑了偷马贼！ “要不是他来了，我根本逃不掉！”

“Oh, yeah?” Slim chuckled. “Well, lucky for us, Lonesome Luke sometimes likes to help folks out.” “是吗？”斯利姆轻笑道。 “嗯，算我们走运，寂寞卢克有时喜欢帮人一把。”

Slim threw his saddle down next to Jack and Annie and sat against it. 斯利姆把马鞍扔在杰克和安妮旁边，靠着它坐下。

“Years ago, Lonesome Luke had a gal who he was just crazy about,” said Slim. “She couldn’t take the Wild West, though. So she went back east.” “多年前，寂寞卢克有个他爱得发狂的姑娘，”斯利姆说。 “可她受不了蛮荒西部。 于是她回东部去了。”

“What happened then?” asked Jack. “后来呢？”杰克问。

“Luke went loco. Every night he’d show up at the hotel and play the piano. He played ‘Red River Valley’ over and over. “卢克疯了。 每天晚上他都会出现在旅馆，弹钢琴。 他一遍又一遍地弹《红河谷》。

“Then one night he just vanished into the prairie and was never seen alive again. His bones were found a year later. But folks say his ghost returns to the hotel piano to play ‘Red River Valley.’ It goes like this … ” “后来有一天晚上，他消失在大草原上，从此再没人活着见过他。 一年后人们发现了他的尸骨。 可人们说，他的鬼魂会回到旅馆的钢琴前弹《红河谷》。曲子是这样的……”

Slim took out a harmonica. He began to play a song. It was the same sad song Jack and Annie had heard in the hotel. 斯利姆拿出一把口琴。 他开始弹一首曲子。 正是杰克和安妮在旅馆里听过的那支悲伤的歌。

Jack lay back down and listened to the lonesome tune. A coyote howled in the distance. The horses stirred in the dark. 杰克重新躺下，听着那支孤寂的曲子。 远处有一只丛林狼在嚎叫。 马匹在黑暗中骚动着。

I better take some notes, thought Jack. 我最好记点笔记，杰克想。

But he didn’t write a word before he fell asleep. He didn’t even take off his boots. 可他还一个字都没写就睡着了。 他甚至连靴子都没脱。
