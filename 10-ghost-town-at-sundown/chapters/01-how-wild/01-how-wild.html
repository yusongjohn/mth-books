1. How Wild? 1. 有多野？

Jack and Annie were sitting on the porch of their house. Annie was gazing down the street at the Frog Creek woods. Jack was reading a book. 杰克和安妮坐在自家房子的门廊上。 安妮正盯着街对面的蛙溪树林看。 杰克正在看书。

“I have a feeling we should check the woods again,” said Annie. “我觉得我们应该再去树林里看看，”安妮说。

“Why?” said Jack without looking up. “为什么？”杰克头也不抬地说。

“A rabbit’s hopping by,” said Annie. “有只兔子跳着经过呢，”安妮说。

“So? We’ve seen rabbits before.” “那又怎样？ 我们以前见过兔子。”

“Not like this one,” said Annie. “不像这只，”安妮说。

“What do you mean?” Jack stood up and looked with her. “你什么意思？”杰克站起来，跟她一起看。

He saw a rabbit with very long legs hopping down their street. Soon the rabbit left the sidewalk and headed into the woods. 他看见一只腿很长的兔子正沿着他们的街跳过去。 很快，兔子离开人行道，朝树林走去。

“He’s a sign,” said Annie. “他是个征兆，”安妮说。

“A sign of what?” said Jack. “什么征兆？”杰克说。

“That Morgan’s back,” said Annie. She jumped off the porch. “Come on!” “说明摩根回来了，”安妮说。 她从门廊上跳了下来。 “快点！”

“But what about dinner?” Jack said. “Dad said it would be ready soon.” “可晚饭怎么办？”杰克说。 “爸爸说很快就好了。”

“Don’t worry,” said Annie. “You know when we leave in the tree house, time stops.” “别担心，”安妮说。 “你知道的，我们坐树屋离开时，时间会停住。”

She ran across their yard. 她跑过院子。

Jack pulled on his backpack. “Back in ten minutes!” he called through the screen door. Then he followed Annie. 杰克背上背包。 “十分钟就回来！”他对着纱门喊道。 然后他跟上了安妮。

They hurried down their street and into the Frog Creek woods. The sun was setting above the trees. 他们急忙顺着街跑进蛙溪树林。 太阳正落在树梢上方。

“There he is!” said Annie. “他在那儿！”安妮说。

The rabbit was standing in a ray of sunlight. When he saw them, he took off. 那只兔子正站在一束阳光里。 它一看见他们，就突然跑开了。

Jack and Annie followed the rabbit until he vanished behind the tallest tree. 杰克和安妮跟着兔子，直到它消失在那棵最高的树后面。

“I told you! See?” said Annie, panting. She pointed up at the tree. “我就说嘛！ 看见了吧？”安妮喘着气说。 她指着那棵树。

Morgan le Fay was waving to them from the magic tree house high in the branches. 摩根·勒菲正站在高高的树枝上的魔法树屋里，朝他们挥手。

Jack and Annie waved back to her. As always, Jack was very happy to see the enchantress librarian again. 杰克和安妮也朝她挥手。 和往常一样，杰克很高兴再次见到这位女魔法师图书管理员。

“Come on up!” she called. “上来吧！”她喊道。

Annie and Jack started up the rope ladder. They climbed up to the tree house. 安妮和杰克开始往绳梯上爬。 他们爬上了树屋。

“We followed a strange rabbit here,” said Annie. “Is he your friend?” “我们跟着一只奇怪的兔子来到这儿，”安妮说。 “他是你的朋友吗？”

“Perhaps,” said Morgan. She smiled mysteriously. “I have many odd friends.” “也许吧，”摩根说。 她神秘地笑了笑。 “我有很多古怪的朋友。”

“Including us,” said Annie. “也包括我们，”安妮说。

Morgan laughed. “That’s right.” 摩根笑了。 “没错。”

“How are you?” said Jack. “你们好吗？”杰克说。

“I’m still having problems with Merlin,” said the enchantress, “which leaves me little time to do my real work. But soon you both will become Master Librarians, and that will be a big help to me.” “我跟梅林还有些麻烦，”女魔法师说，“这让我几乎没时间做真正的工作。 不过很快你们俩都会成为大师级图书管理员，那会对我帮助很大。”

Jack smiled. He was going to be a Master Librarian who traveled through time and space. It was almost too good to believe. 杰克笑了。 他就要成为一个穿越时空的大师级图书管理员了。 这简直好得让人不敢相信。

“Are you ready to solve another riddle?” asked Morgan. “你们准备好再解一道谜语了吗？”摩根问。

“Yes!” said Jack and Annie together. “好！”杰克和安妮一起说。

“Good,” said Morgan. “First, you’ll need this for research … ” “很好，”摩根说。 “首先，你们需要这个来做研究……”

She pulled a book from her robe and handed it to Jack. It was the book that would help them on their journey. 她从长袍里抽出一本书，递给杰克。 那是能在旅途中帮助他们的书。

The title of the book was Days of the Wild West. On the cover was a picture of a western town on a prairie. 书名是《蛮荒西部的日子》。 封面上是大草原上的一座西部小镇。

“Oh, wow,” said Annie. “The Wild West!” “哦，哇，”安妮说。 “蛮荒西部！”

Jack took a deep breath. Just how wild? he wondered. 杰克深吸一口气。到底有多野呢？ 他想。

Morgan reached into the folds of her robe again and pulled out a scroll. She handed it to Annie. 摩根又把手伸进长袍的褶层里，抽出一卷轴。 她把它递给安妮。

“Read this when the tree house lands,” she said. “等树屋落地后再读，”她说。

“Is it the riddle?” asked Jack. “是谜语吗？”杰克问。

“Yes,” said Morgan. “Then you’ll only have two more to solve. Are you ready to go?” “是的，”摩根说。 “然后你们就只剩下两道谜要解了。 你们准备好出发了吗？”

Jack and Annie nodded. Annie pointed to the picture on the cover of the Wild West book. 杰克和安妮点点头。 安妮指着《狂野西部》书封面上的图画。

“I wish we could go there,” she said. “我希望我们能去那儿，”她说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

“Good-bye!” said Morgan. “Good luck!” “再见！”摩根说。 “祝你们好运！”

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

Jack squeezed his eyes shut. 杰克紧紧闭上眼睛。

The tree house spun faster and faster. 树屋转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 绝对静止。

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开眼睛。

Morgan le Fay was gone. 摩根·勒菲不见了。

A fly buzzed around his head. 一只苍蝇在他头边嗡嗡飞。
