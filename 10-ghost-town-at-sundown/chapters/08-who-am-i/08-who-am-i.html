8. Who Am I? 8. 我是谁？

A fly buzzed by Jack’s ear. He slapped it away. He opened his eyes. 一只苍蝇在杰克耳边嗡嗡飞过。 他把它拍开。 他睁开眼睛。

The sun was high above the canyon walls. He had slept a long time. 太阳高高挂在峡谷壁上方。 他睡了很久。

Slim and Annie were sitting by a fire, drinking from tin cups. 斯利姆和安妮正坐在火旁，用铁皮杯子喝着东西。

“Coffee? Biscuit?” Annie asked Jack. “咖啡？ 饼干？”安妮问杰克。

“Where did you get them?” said Jack. “你们从哪儿弄来的？”杰克说。

“A cowboy always carries biscuits and a canteen of coffee,” said Slim. “牛仔总是带着饼干和一壶咖啡，”斯利姆说。

He walked over and gave Jack a biscuit and a cup of coffee. 他走过来，给了杰克一块饼干和一杯咖啡。

“It’s hard as a rock,” Slim said. “And bitter as muddy river water. But a cowboy takes what he can get.” “硬得像石头，”斯利姆说。 “而且苦得像浑浊的河水。 可牛仔有什么吃什么。”

Jack took a bite and a sip. 杰克咬了一口，又喝了一小口。

The biscuit was very hard and the coffee was very bitter. But that was okay with Jack. Since cowboys didn’t mind, he didn’t mind, either. 饼干非常硬，咖啡非常苦。 可这对杰克来说没问题。 既然牛仔都不在乎，他也不在乎。

“I’ll saddle up Dusty,” Slim said, “and take you back to town to catch your stage.” “我给达斯蒂备上鞍，”斯利姆说，“再送你们回镇子去赶马车。”

“Then what will you do?” said Annie. “那你接下来怎么办？”安妮说。

“Head south with my herd,” said Slim. “Sell ’em. Then ride across the plains and round up more mustangs.” “带着我的马群往南走，”斯利姆说。 “把它们卖掉。 然后骑马穿过平原，再去聚集更多野马。”

While Slim saddled Dusty, Jack took out his notebook and pencil. He wrote: 斯利姆给达斯蒂上鞍时，杰克拿出笔记本和铅笔，写道：

图中文字：Cowboy breakfast

bitter coffee

hard biscuits 图中文字：牛仔早餐：苦咖啡和硬饼干

“Hey, Shorty,” called Slim. “What are you doing?” “嘿，矮个子，”斯利姆喊道。 “你在干什么？”

“Taking notes,” said Jack. “记笔记，”杰克说。

“What for?” “为什么？”

“He likes writing things down,” said Annie. “他喜欢把事情写下来，”安妮说。

“Oh, yeah?” said Slim. “Me too. In fact, I first came out west to write a book. But one thing led to another. The next thing I know, I’m a mustang herder.” “是吗？”斯利姆说。 “我也是。 其实，我当初来西部就是为了写一本书。 可事情一环扣一环。 接下来，我就成了野马牧人。”

“Slim, you should write your book,” said Annie. “And let the mustangs go free.” “斯利姆，你应该写你的书，”安妮说。 “再放野马自由。”

“Think so?” said Slim. “你这么觉得？”斯利姆说。

They looked at the grazing wild horse. 他们看着那匹正在吃草的野马。

“I know so,” said Annie. “我知道你会写好的，”安妮说。

“Yup,” said Jack. “Your book should be about the Wild West, Slim.” “是啊，”杰克说。 “你的书应该写狂野西部，斯利姆。”

Slim kept staring at his herd. “Maybe you’re right,” he said. “I could settle in Laramie and write there. Wouldn’t have to chase after rustlers anymore.” 斯利姆一直盯着他的马群。 “也许你说得对，”他说。 “我可以在拉拉米安家，在那儿写作。 就不用再追那些偷马贼了。”

Slim turned back to Jack and Annie. “Yup, I think I’ll be a writer. Let’s go. Before I change my mind,” he said. 斯利姆转过身看着杰克和安妮。 “对，我想我会当个作家。我们走吧。 趁我还没改主意，”他说。

“Yay!” said Annie. “I’ll go tell them.” She jumped up and ran to the mustangs. “耶！”安妮说。 “我去告诉它们。”她跳起来，朝野马跑去。

Jack packed his backpack, while Slim packed his saddlebags. 杰克收拾背包，斯利姆则收拾鞍囊。

Then Slim and Jack climbed onto Dusty. They rode over to Annie, who was stroking Sunset’s neck. 然后斯利姆和杰克爬上达斯蒂。 他们骑到达正在抚摸日落脖子的安妮身边。

“I told him he’s as free as the wind now,” said Annie. “我告诉他，他现在像风一样自由了，”安妮说。

“Sounds good,” said Slim. “Give me your hand, Smiley.” “听起来不错，”斯利姆说。 “把手给我，笑脸。”

Slim pulled Annie onto Dusty. She sat in front of Jack. 斯利姆把安妮拉上达斯蒂。 她坐在杰克前面。

Slim snapped his reins. Dusty started off. 斯利姆抖了抖缰绳。 达斯蒂出发了。

The sun was hot as Dusty climbed out of the canyon. When they reached the top, they peered down at the canyon floor. 太阳火辣辣的，达斯蒂爬出峡谷。 到达顶部时，他们俯视着峡谷底部。

The mustangs pranced playfully, their coats shining in the hazy light. 野马欢快地腾跳着，皮毛在朦胧的光线里发亮。

“They’ll find their way out soon,” Slim said. “Then cut across the prairie. Yell goodbye to your pal, Smiley.” “他们很快就会找到出路的，”斯利姆说。 “然后穿过草原。 “跟你的朋友喊再见吧，笑脸。”

“Stay with your mother, Sunset!” shouted Annie. “Good-bye!” “跟妈妈待在一起，日落！”安妮喊道。 “再见！”

Out of the blue, a voice called, “Bye!” 突然，一个声音喊道：“再见！”

Annie gasped. “Who said that?” she asked. “The ghost?” 安妮倒抽一口气。 “谁在说话？”她问。 “是鬼吗？”

“Nope,” said Jack. “It’s just an echo. It’s caused by sound bouncing off the canyon walls.” “不是，”杰克说。 “那只是回声。 是声音从峡谷石壁上弹回来造成的。”

Slim cupped his hands around his mouth. “Who am I?” he shouted. 斯利姆把手拢成杯状围在嘴边。 “我是谁？”他喊道。

“Am I?” came the distant voice. “我是吗？”远处传来回声。

“Oh, man,” Jack said softly. “That’s the answer … ” “哦，天哪，”杰克轻声说。 “那就是答案……”

“To Morgan’s riddle!” said Annie. “摩根的谜语的答案！”安妮说。

“Echo!” she and Jack said together. “回声！”她和杰克一起说。

Jack looked at Slim. “You knew the answer last night,” he said. 杰克看着斯利姆。 “你昨晚就知道答案了，”他说。

Slim just smiled and snapped his reins. “Let’s go, pardners,” he said. 斯利姆只是笑了笑，抖了抖缰绳。 “走吧，伙计们，”他说。
