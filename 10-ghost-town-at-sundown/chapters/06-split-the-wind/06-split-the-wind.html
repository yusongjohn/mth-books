6. Split the Wind 6. 乘风而去

The sky was dark by the time they got to the rise. The wind was cool, almost cold. 他们到达山岗时，天已经黑了。 风很凉，几乎算得上冷。

“Whoa,” said Slim. “吁，”斯利姆说。

Dusty slowed to a halt. 达斯蒂慢慢停了下来。

“They’re camped down there,” Slim said in a low voice. “In that patch of trees.” “他们在下面扎营，”斯利姆压低声音说。 “在那片树林里。”

Jack saw a campfire at the bottom of the slope. He saw the horses gathered in a dark clump. One let out a loud whinny. 杰克看见斜坡底下有一堆营火。 他看见马匹聚成黑压压的一簇。 一匹马大声嘶叫起来。

“Hear that?” said Slim. “The mare. She senses Sunset is nearby.” “听见了吗？”斯利姆说。 “是母马。 它感觉到日落就在附近。”

The mare whinnied again. 母马又嘶叫了一声。

“Sounds like she’s tied to a tree,” said Slim. “I think the rest of the herd are loose.” “听上去它被拴在树上，”斯利姆说。 “我想其余的马群都没拴着。”

“What’s our plan?” whispered Jack. “我们的计划是什么？”杰克小声问。

“Smiley, you stay here and guard Sunset,” said Slim. “笑脸，你留在这儿看守日落，”斯利姆说。

“Right,” said Annie. “好，”安妮说。

“Shorty, you and I ride down near their camp,” said Slim. “You keep Dusty quiet while I cut the mare loose.” “矮个子，你和我骑到靠近他们营地的地方，”斯利姆说。 “你让达斯蒂别出声，我去把母马解开。”

How do you keep a horse quiet? wondered Jack. 怎样才能让马安静下来呢？杰克想。

“Once the mare’s loose, she’ll break for Sunset,” said Slim. “Then you and Sunset take off, Smiley.” “母马一松开，就会朝日落冲过去，”斯利姆说。 “然后你和日落就出发，笑脸。”

“Got it,” said Annie. “明白了，”安妮说。

“Then we’ll split the wind,” said Slim. “然后我们就风驰电掣地跑，”斯利姆说。

What’s that mean? wondered Jack. 那是什么意思？杰克想。

“Till we get to Blue Canyon,” said Slim. “一直到蓝峡谷，”斯利姆说。

Where’s that? wondered Jack. 那是哪儿？杰克想。

“All set? Any questions?” asked Slim. “都准备好了？ 有问题吗？”斯利姆问。

“Nope,” said Annie cheerfully. “没有，”安妮兴高采烈地说。

Yup, about a million, thought Jack. 有，大概一百万个，杰克想。

“Okay, pardners,” said Slim. “See ya soon, Smiley. Come on, Shorty.” “好了，伙计们，”斯利姆说。 “回头见，笑脸。

“Have fun,” said Annie. “玩得开心，”安妮说。

Fun? thought Jack. Is she nuts? Our lives are at stake. 好玩？杰克想。 她疯了吗？ 我们的性命正处在危急关头。

Slim snapped his reins. Dusty started down the rise. Their way was lit by a nearly full moon and a million stars. 斯利姆抖了抖缰绳。 达斯蒂开始往山岗下走。 将近满月和无数星星照亮了他们的路。

Maybe now I can ask Slim some questions, thought Jack. 也许现在我能问斯利姆一些问题了，杰克想。

But just then voices came from the rustlers’ camp. They were mean voices, followed by mean laughter. 可就在这时，偷马贼营地里传来了声音。 那是凶狠的声音，接着是凶狠的笑声。

A chill went through Jack. 杰克觉得脊背发凉。

Dusty halted. 达斯蒂停住了。

“This is far enough,” whispered Slim. He slipped off of Dusty. “这就够远了，”斯利姆低声说。 他从达斯蒂身上下来。

“Keep him here,” Slim whispered to Jack, “and keep him quiet.” “让他待在这儿，”斯利姆对杰克低声说，“让他保持安静。”

“Wait—” whispered Jack. He needed more information. “等等——”杰克小声说。 他还需要更多消息。

But Slim was gone. 可斯利姆已经走了。

Jack gripped the reins and held his breath. He hoped Dusty wouldn’t do anything. 杰克抓紧缰绳，屏住呼吸。 他希望达斯蒂别乱动。

For a moment Dusty was still. But then he snorted and began walking. 有一会儿，达斯蒂一动不动。 可接着他喷了喷鼻息，开始往前走。

Oh, no! thought Jack. He tried to think of the rules on how to treat a horse. 哦，不！杰克想。 他努力想对待马的那些规矩。

He remembered: a soft hand, a firm voice. 他记起来了：温柔的手，坚定的声音。

He patted Dusty softly. 他轻轻拍着达斯蒂。

“Whoa!” he said firmly. To his surprise, Dusty froze and was quiet. “吁！”他坚定地说。 出乎他的意料，达斯蒂僵住了，不再出声。

Jack remembered another rule: a sunny attitude. He patted Dusty again. “Don’t worry,” he whispered. “Everything’s going to be fine.” 杰克又想起另一条规则：阳光般的态度。 他又拍了拍达斯蒂。 “别担心，”他轻声说。 “一切都会没事的。”

Just then a loud whinny came from the herd of mustangs. They began moving up the moonlit slope. 就在这时，野马群里传来一声响亮的嘶鸣。 他们开始沿着月光下的山坡往上走。

“Hey! The horses!” a rustler shouted. “嘿！ 马！”一个偷马贼喊道。

A gun went off. Jack ducked. 枪响了。杰克猛地低下头。

“Come on, Shorty!” came Slim’s voice. “快点儿，矮个子！”传来斯利姆的声音。

Jack looked up. Slim was riding the mare! 杰克抬起头。 斯利姆正骑着那匹母马！

Jack was shocked. He had thought that Slim was coming back to ride Dusty. 杰克惊呆了。 他原以为斯利姆会回来骑达斯蒂。

Instead, Slim rode right past him! As he got close to Annie, she took off on Sunset. 相反，斯利姆径直从他身边骑了过去！ 他刚靠近安妮，安妮就骑着日落冲了出去。

The mare galloped after Sunset. And the band of mustangs galloped after the mare. 母马飞奔着追赶日落。 野马群也跟着母马飞奔而去。

Bang! Bang! 砰！砰！

Jack snapped the reins. “Go, Dusty!” he said. 杰克用力甩了一下缰绳。 “跑啊，达斯蒂！”他说。

Dusty leaped after the mustangs. Jack nearly fell off. He clutched the reins in one hand and the saddle horn in the other. 达斯蒂跃起，追着野马群。 杰克差点掉下去。 他一只手抓紧缰绳，另一只手抓住鞍桥。

Bang! Bang! 砰！砰！

The rustlers were on their horses now. They were getting closer. 偷马贼现在已经上了马。 他们越来越近了。

“Hurry!” Jack cried. “快点！”杰克喊道。

Dusty cleared the rise in an awkward leap. Jack started to slip out of the saddle. He let go of the reins and tried to hold on to the saddle horn, but his weight pulled him down. He closed his eyes as he fell to the ground. 达斯蒂笨拙地一跃，腾空越过了土丘。 杰克开始从马鞍上往下滑。 他松开缰绳，想抓住鞍桥，可身体的重量把他往下拽。 他闭上眼睛，摔到了地上。

Bang! Bang! 砰！砰！

Oh, man, thought Jack, this is the end. 哦，天哪，杰克想，这下完了。

He opened his eyes. Dusty was looking at him. Jack scrambled up and tried to climb back into the saddle. It was hard without Slim’s help. 他睁开眼睛。 达斯蒂正看着他。 杰克爬起来，试着重新爬上马鞍。 没有斯利姆帮忙，这可不容易。

As Jack struggled, he heard shouts from the rustlers. Their horses gave high-pitched neighs. 杰克挣扎时，听见偷马贼在喊叫。 他们的马发出尖厉的嘶鸣。

Jack looked back. 杰克回头看。

A shimmering white figure was moving across the top of the rise! The rustlers’ horses were panicking and backing away. 一个闪着白光的身影正越过山脊顶部！ 盗马贼的马惊慌失措，直往后退。

Jack didn’t have time to think about what he was seeing. He knew it might be his only chance to escape. Using all his strength, he pulled himself into the saddle. 杰克来不及去想自己看见了什么。 他知道这也许是他逃走的唯一机会。 他使出全身力气，把自己拉上马鞍。

“Go, Dusty, go!” he shouted. “跑啊，达斯蒂，跑！”他喊道。

Dusty took off at full gallop over the prairie. Jack held on for dear life as they split the wind. 达斯蒂在草原上全速疾驰而去。 他们风驰电掣地飞奔，杰克死死抓着不放。
