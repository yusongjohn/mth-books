10. Echo from the Past 10. 来自过去的回声

Jack and Annie looked outside. 杰克和安妮向外面看。

The sun had slipped behind the trees of the Frog Creek woods. 太阳已经滑到蛙溪树林的树后。

Annie still held the ancient scroll. She put it in the corner, next to the scroll from their ocean trip. 安妮还拿着那卷古老的轴。 她把它放在角落里，挨着那次海洋之旅的卷轴。

“Just two more to go,” she whispered. “只剩两个了，”她小声说。

“Yup,” said Jack. He unzipped his pack. He pulled out Days of the Wild West. He put it on top of a stack of books. “是啊，”杰克说。 他拉开背包的拉链。 他拿出《狂野西部的日子》。 他把它放在一摞书的最上面。

“Ready?” he said. “准备好了吗？”他说。

Annie was staring at the books. Her mouth dropped open. 安妮正盯着那些书。 她张大了嘴。

“What’s wrong?” asked Jack. “怎么了？”杰克问。

Annie just kept staring. 安妮还是一直盯着。

“Have you gone nuts?” said Jack. “你傻了吗？”杰克说。

Annie pointed at the Wild West book. 安妮指着《狂野西部》这本书。

“Read the cover,” she said. “读封面，”她说。

Jack picked the book up. He read the title aloud: “Days of the Wild West.” He looked at Annie. “So?” 杰克拿起书。 他大声读出书名：《蛮荒西部的日子》。他看着安妮。 “那又怎样？”

“Keep reading,” said Annie. “继续读，”安妮说。

The author’s name was below the title. It was in smaller letters. Jack read: “Slim Cooley.” 作者的名字在书名下面。 字比较小。 杰克念道：“斯利姆·库利。”

Jack gasped. His mouth dropped open. He and Annie stared at the words for a long moment. 杰克倒抽一口气。 他张大了嘴。 他和安妮盯着那些字看了很长一会儿。

图中文字：DAYS of the WILD WEST 图中文字：西部狂野时代

“Oh, man,” whispered Jack. “We were using Slim’s book. The book he wrote after he left us!” “哦，天哪，”杰克轻声说。 “我们用的是斯利姆的书。 是他离开我们之后写的那本！”

Jack and Annie shook their heads with wonder. 杰克和安妮惊讶地摇了摇头。

Jack opened Slim’s book. He looked at the title page. At the bottom of the page, he read: Texas Press, Dallas, 1895. 杰克打开斯利姆的书。 他看了看书名页。 在页面底部，他读到：得克萨斯出版社，达拉斯，1895年。

Jack turned the page. He read the dedication: 杰克翻过一页。 他读了献词：

WITH THANKS TO SMILEY AND SHORTY,
TWO STRANGERS WHO CHANGED MY LIFE

Jack looked at Annie. “Slim dedicated his book to us,” he said. 杰克看着安妮。 “斯利姆把他的书献给了我们，”他说。

“Yup,” said Annie. She smiled. “是啊，”安妮说。她笑了。

Jack placed Slim’s book back on the stack of books. 杰克把斯利姆的书放回那摞书上。

Then he and Annie left the tree house and climbed down the ladder. 然后他和安妮离开树屋，爬下梯子。

As they started through the woods, the trees were alive with bird sounds. The air felt soft and moist. 他们走进树林时，树上满是鸟叫声。 空气摸上去柔软而潮湿。

“Frog Creek seems so peaceful,” said Jack. “No rattlers, no rustlers, no ghosts.” “蛙溪显得那么宁静，”杰克说。 “没有响尾蛇，没有偷马贼，没有鬼。”

“Yeah, but no Slim Cooley, either,” said Annie sadly. “是啊，可也没有斯利姆·库利了，”安妮难过地说。

“I know,” said Jack. “But when we read his book, it’s like he’s still talking to us.” “我知道，”杰克说。 “可我们读他的书时，就好像他还在跟我们说话。”

“Oh, right,” said Annie. “You mean it’s like an echo from the past?” “哦，对，”安妮说。 “你是说，就像来自过去的回声？”

“Yeah,” said Jack softly. “Wow.” “是啊，”杰克轻声说。 “哇。”

Just then, out of the blue, a voice called, “Jack! Annie!” 就在这时，不知从哪儿传来一个声音：“杰克！安妮！”

“It’s Dad!” said Annie. “是爸爸！”安妮说。

“Coming!” she and Jack shouted. “来了！”她和杰克喊道。

Then they ran all the way home, through the long shadows of the setting sun. 然后他们一路跑回家，穿过夕阳拉长的影子。

Here are the words to RED RIVER VALLEY,the traditional cowboy song that Lonesome Luke played on the piano. 下面是寂寞卢克用钢琴弹奏的那首传统牛仔歌曲《红河谷》的歌词。

From this valley they say you are going. I shall miss your sweet face and bright smile. For they say you are taking the sunshine That has brightened my pathway awhile. 人们说你就要离开这山谷。我会想念你甜蜜的脸庞和明亮的笑容。因为他们说你带走了阳光，那阳光曾照亮我的小路片刻。

Refrain (after each verse):



Come and sit by my side if you love me. Do not hasten to bid me adieu. For remember the Red River Valley And the cowboy who loved you so true. 如果你爱我，就来坐在我身边。不要急着向我道别。请记住红河谷，还有真心爱你的牛仔。

There never could be such a longing In the heart of a poor cowboy’s breast. As dwells in this heart you are breaking While I wait in my home in the West. 可怜牛仔的胸怀里，从不会有这样的思念，就像住在这颗你正在伤害的心里，而我在西部的家里等待。

Do you think of this valley you’re leaving,

Oh, how lonely and dreary it will be?

Do you think of the kind hearts you’re grieving,

And the pain you are causing to me?



 你可曾想到你正离开的山谷，啊，它将会多么孤独凄凉？你可曾想到那些你让它们伤心的善良心灵，还有你给我带来的痛苦？

From this valley they say you are going. I will miss your bright eyes and sweet smile. For they say you are weary and tired And must find a new range for a while. 人们说你就要离开这山谷。我会想念你明亮的眼睛和甜蜜的笑容。因为他们说你已经疲倦劳累，必须另找一片牧场待上一阵。
