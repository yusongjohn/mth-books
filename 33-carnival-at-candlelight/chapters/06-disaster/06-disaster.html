6. Disaster 6. 灾难

“W e have to save all of Venice?” said Jack. “That’s a big responsibility. What do we have to save Venice from?” “我们必须拯救整个威尼斯吗？”杰克说。 “这可是一个很大的责任。 我们要拯救威尼斯免于什么？”

“Well, if Neptune’s going to help us, it must have something to do with water,” said Annie. “嗯，如果海王要帮我们的话，一定和水有关，”安妮说。

“Yeah, like the water that’s flooding the alleys,” said Jack. “对，就像正在淹没小巷的水，”杰克说。

“But the woman at the café told us not to worry about that,” said Annie. “但是咖啡馆里的女士告诉我们不用担心那件事，”安妮说。

“I’m still worried,” said Jack. “Let’s look up floods in our Venice book.” He found floods in the index. He turned to the right page and read: “我还在担心，”杰克说。 “我们去威尼斯的书里查一下洪水吧。”他找到了书的目录里的洪水。 他翻到右边的那一页开始读：

Most of the time, high water in Venice is not serious. But if several conditions are present at the same time, a flood disaster can occur. 大多数时候，威尼斯的水位很高并不严重。但如果同时出现几种情况，就可能发生洪水灾害。

“A flood disaster! That must be it!” said Annie. “So what are the conditions?” “一场洪水灾害！ “一定是这个！”安妮说。 “那条件是什么呢？”

“It lists them here,” said Jack. He read: “它们在这里列出来了，”杰克说。他读道：

A high tide 高潮
Strong winds from the south 从南方吹来的强风
Heavy flow of water from the mountains 从山上流下来的水很多
Severe storms at sea 海上暴风雨

“There’s a high tide tonight—the passenger with the bird mask in the gondola told us that,” said Annie. “今晚有大潮——坐船的戴鸟面具的乘客告诉我们的，”安妮说。

“Yeah, and winds from the south—the weather vane told us that,” said Jack. “对，还有从南方来的风——风向标告诉我们的，”杰克说。

“And water’s flowing down from the mountains—the woman at the café told us that,” said Annie. “还有水从山上流下来——咖啡馆的女士告诉我们的，”安妮说。

“Yeah, and storms out at sea—I saw lightning when we crossed the water,” said Jack. “对，还有海上的暴风雨——我们过海的时候，我看到闪电，”杰克说。

“All the conditions are here,” said Annie. “所有的条件都具备了，”安妮说。

Jack and Annie looked around. Water was now flowing steadily from the alleyways into the small square. It had risen above their ankles. 杰克和安妮环顾四周。 现在，水正稳定地从小巷流进广场。 水已经漫到了他们的脚踝。

“I get it now,” said Jack. “The water will just keep getting higher and higher until it destroys the whole city. And no one’s paying attention!” “我现在明白了，”杰克说。 “水会一直上涨，直到把整个城市都毁掉。 而且，没人注意！”

“Neptune’s the only one who can help us stop the flood,” said Annie. “只有海神能帮助我们阻止洪水，”安妮说。

“But Neptune’s not real,” said Jack. “I mean, he’s a character in mythology, and mythology’s not real life. It’s—” “但海神不是真的，”杰克说。 “我是说，他只是神话里的角色，神话不是现实生活。它——”

“Okay, okay,” said Annie. “Let’s just take one step at a time. At midnight two guys will tell us the time, and then we should climb to the top of their tower, right?” “好吧，好吧，”安妮说。 “我们一步一步来吧。 午夜时分，有两个人会告诉我们时间，然后我们应该爬到他们塔顶，对吧？”

“Right,” said Jack. “对，”杰克说。

“So what we have to do now is find those two guys!” said Annie. “那么我们现在要做的就是找到那两个人！”安妮说。

“Let’s go back to the waterfront,” said Jack. “The man with the dog said everyone in Venice would be there for the fireworks.” “我们回水边去吧，”杰克说。 “那个牵着狗的人说，威尼斯所有人都会在那里看烟花。”

Jack put away their book. Then he and Annie retraced their steps over the footbridge and back through the alleys. Lots of seaweed was floating in the narrow lanes between buildings. Water is definitely flowing in from the sea, Jack thought. 杰克把他们的书收了起来。 然后他和安妮又沿着人行桥走回小巷。 很多海藻在建筑物之间的狭窄小巷里漂浮着。 水肯定是从海里流进来了，杰克想。

When they got back to Saint Mark’s Square, people were streaming toward the waterfront. Jack and Annie walked with the crowd. Everyone was talking and laughing as they looked up at the sky over the water, waiting for the fireworks to begin. No one paid attention to the damp winds or the seawater spilling over the side of the canal, soaking their shoes. 当他们回到圣马可广场时，人们正朝水边涌去。 杰克和安妮跟着人群走。 大家一边看着水面上的天空，一边谈笑，等着烟花开始。 没人注意潮湿的风，也没人注意从运河边溢出来的海水，浸湿了他们的鞋子。

“Excuse me!” Annie shouted. “Can anyone tell us the time?” “对不起！”安妮喊道。 “有人能告诉我们现在几点吗？”

No one answered, for just then the first explosion of fireworks shook the night. The crowd cheered as blue and red showers exploded in the sky. 没人回答，因为就在这时，第一朵烟花在夜空中炸开。 人群欢呼起来，蓝色和红色的烟花在天空中绽放。

In the distance, a clock started to chime. Jack counted the bongs. 远处，一座钟开始敲响。 杰克数着钟声。

“Twelve!” he said. “It’s midnight now, according to that clock.” “十二点！”他说道。 “根据那个钟，现在是午夜了。”

More fireworks exploded over the waterfront, and another clock began clanging. This time, Jack counted only eleven bongs. He shook his head. “This is crazy!” he muttered. 更多的烟花在港口上空炸开，另一个钟开始发出轰鸣声。 这次，杰克只数到了十一个钟声。 他摇了摇头。 “这太疯狂了！”他小声说。

Jack looked around at the crowd. “Can anyone tell us the real time?” he yelled. “Is it midnight yet? Can anyone tell us?” 杰克环顾四周的人群。 “有人能告诉我们现在几点吗？”他大声喊道。 “现在是半夜了吗？” 有人能告诉我们吗？“

No one answered—not even one man, much less two. Everyone was oohing and aahing over the dazzling fireworks. 没有人回答——连一个人都没有，更别说两个了。 大家都惊叹着看着那耀眼的烟花。

Another clock began to sound. This one was much louder than the first two. 另一个钟开始敲响了。 这个声音比前两个响得多。

BONG! BONG!

“This is hopeless!” said Jack. “这太糟糕了！”杰克说。

BONG! BONG!

“We’ll never know the right time,” he said. “我们永远也找不到正确的时间，”他说。

BONG! BONG!

“Jack, look over there—” said Annie. “杰克，快看那边——”安妮说。

BONG! BONG!

“We’ll never find the two men with the tower,” said Jack. “我们永远找不到那两个有塔的人了，”杰克说。

BONG! BONG!

“Jack, look—” said Annie. “杰克，快看——”安妮说。

BONG! BONG!

“All of Venice is about to drown in a flood,” said Jack. “整个威尼斯马上就要被洪水淹没了，”杰克说。

BONG! BONG!

“And everyone’s just cheering for fireworks!” said Jack. “而且大家还在为烟花欢呼呢！”杰克说。

BONG! BONG!

“JACK! LOOK!” said Annie. She pointed toward the clock tower in Saint Mark’s Square. “杰克！ “看！”安妮说。 她指着圣马可广场上的钟楼。

BONG! BONG!

Jack saw a huge bell on top of the tower. Two bronze statues were holding a club and striking the bell. 杰克看到钟楼顶部有一个巨大的钟。 两个青铜雕像拿着一根棒子，正在敲钟。

BONG! BONG!

The statues were of two men. 这两个雕像是两个男人。

BONG! BONG!

“At midnight two men will tell you the time,” said Annie. “午夜时分，两个人会告诉你们时间，”安妮说。

BONG! BONG！

The two men struck the bell for the twelfth time and then stopped. 那两个人敲响了钟，敲了第十二下后停了下来。

“Come on!” cried Jack. “We have to climb that tower!” “快点！”杰克喊道。 “我们必须爬上去！”

More fireworks thundered over the canal as Jack and Annie pushed their way back through the crowd. They ran into Saint Mark’s Square and splashed their way to the tall tower with the two men on top. They ran to the arched entrance of the tower and stepped inside. The air was damp and musty. 更多的烟花在运河上空炸开，杰克和安妮挤开人群往回跑。 他们跑进了圣马可广场，蹚着水朝那座塔楼奔去，塔楼顶部站着两个人。 他们跑到塔楼的拱形入口处，走了进去。 空气又湿又闷。

“Stairs!” said Jack. He ran to a dark, winding stairway and started up. Annie followed. They climbed and climbed until they reached the top of the tower. “楼梯！”杰克说。 他跑向一条黑暗的、弯曲的楼梯，开始往上爬。安妮跟了上去。 他们一直往上爬，直到到达塔顶。

Jack was breathing hard as he pushed open a heavy door that led out onto the bell terrace. The two statues were frozen on either side of the bronze bell. 杰克喘着粗气，推开了一扇沉重的门，门后是钟楼的露台。 两座雕像静静地站在青铜钟的两侧。

As soon as Jack and Annie stepped onto the terrace, the wind blew their hats off their heads. The air was filled with cracking and hissing sounds as more fireworks burst through the sky. Everyone on the waterfront was clapping and cheering. 杰克和安妮一踏上露台，风就吹掉了他们头上的帽子。 更多的烟花在天空中炸开，发出噼啪和嘶嘶的声音，空气里充满了这些声音。 水边的所有人都在鼓掌和欢呼。

“What’s the next thing Merlin tells us to do to find Neptune?” Annie shouted. “梅林告诉我们接下来要做什么才能找到海王？”安妮大声喊道。

Jack pulled out Merlin’s letter. Holding on to it tightly as it flapped in the wind, he read aloud: 杰克拿出梅林的信。 紧紧抓着它，任凭它在风中飘动，他大声读了起来：

The King of the Jungle will carry you there, 丛林之王会把你带到那里。

Not over land, but high in the air. 不是走陆地，而是飞上天空。

“The King of the Jungle is a lion,” said Annie. “So it sounds like we need to find a flying lion!” “丛林之王是一只狮子，”安妮说。 “那听起来我们得找一只会飞的狮子了！”，

“Right,” said Jack. “But where?” “对，”杰克说。 “但在哪里呢？”，

“How about that one?” said Annie. She was pointing over the terrace railing. “那边那只怎么样？”，安妮说。 她正指着露台的栏杆。

Jack looked down. Standing on the wide ledge below was the stone statue of a lion. Growing out of the lion’s back were two powerful-looking carved wings. 杰克往下看。 在下方宽阔的石阶上，站着一只狮子的石像。 狮子背上长出两只看起来非常有力的雕刻翅膀。

“But that’s just a statue,” cried Jack. “How can a statue take us anywhere?” “但这只是个雕像，”杰克喊道。 “一个雕像怎么可能带我们去任何地方呢？”

Annie grinned. “I think it’s time to use a little more magic,” she said. 安妮露出了笑容。 “我想是时候用一点更多魔法了，”她说。
