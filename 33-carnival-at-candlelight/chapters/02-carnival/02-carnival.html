2. Carnival 2. 游乐园

Annie laughed. 安妮笑了。

Jack opened his eyes. He and Annie were wearing purple and pink outfits with huge ruffled collars. On their heads were funny hats. On their feet were red slippers with big bows. 杰克睁开了眼睛。 他和安妮穿着紫色和粉色的衣服，领子上有很多大大的褶皱。 他们头上戴着滑稽的帽子。 他们脚上穿着红色的拖鞋，上面还有大大的蝴蝶结。

“Who are we?” Annie asked. “我们是谁？”安妮问。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. He didn’t feel like a wise and brave helper in his red slippers. He felt stupid. “我不知道，”杰克说。 他穿着红色拖鞋，觉得自己一点都不聪明勇敢，反而很傻。

Together Jack and Annie looked out the tree house window. They had landed in a small tree inside a walled garden. The sky was gray. Jack couldn’t tell if it was morning or evening, but the air felt damp and heavy, as if a storm was about to break. 杰克和安妮一起从树屋的窗户往外看。 他们降落在一个围墙花园里的小树上。天空是灰色的。 杰克分不清是早上还是晚上，但空气又湿又重，好像马上就要下暴雨了。

“I guess this is Venice,” he said. “我想我们到了威尼斯，”他说。

“Let’s check our book,” said Annie. She opened their research book and read aloud: “我们来看看我们的书吧，”安妮说。 她打开他们的研究书，大声读起来：

Today the city of Venice (say VEN-iss) is one of the most popular tourist spots in the world. It lies in a lagoon of the Adriatic Sea. Instead of roads, Venice has waterways called canals. People glide along the canals in shallow boats called gondolas (GON-duh-luz). A man called a gondolier (gon-duh-LEER) stands in the back of the boat and uses a long oar to propel the gondola through the water. 今天，威尼斯（发音为VEN-iss）是世界上最受欢迎的旅游景点之一。它位于亚得里亚海的一个泻湖中。威尼斯没有道路，而是有被称为运河的水道。人们乘坐一种叫贡多拉（GON-duh-luz）的浅底小船在运河上滑行。船尾站着一个叫贡多拉船夫（gon-duh-LEER）的人，他用一根长桨划船，让贡多拉在水中前进。

“That sounds like fun,” said Jack. “那听起来很有趣，”杰克说。

“Yep,” said Annie. She closed the book. “Let’s go.” “是啊，”安妮说。 她合上了书。 “我们走吧。”

“Wait,” said Jack. “We don’t even know what our mission is yet.” He took out Merlin’s letter and unfolded it. He read aloud: “等等，”杰克说。 “我们甚至还不知道我们的任务是什么。”他拿出梅林的信，把它展开。他大声读起来：

图中文字：Dear Jack and Annie of Frog Creek,

Your mission will require great patience and a bit of magic. The Grand Lady of the Lagoon is in terrible danger. The only one who can help you save her from disaster is the Ruler of the Seas. To locate him, follow these directions:

When waters rise beneath the moon,

Visit the Grand Lady of the Lagoon.

To find out where you need to go,

Seek out a painter named Tiepolo.

At midnight two men will tell you the time.

To the top of their tower hurry and climb.

The King of the Jungle will carry you there,

Not over land, but high in the air.

An angel of gold will show you the way

To the sea by night, and home by day. 图中文字：亲爱的弗罗格克里克的杰克和安妮，你们的任务需要极大的耐心和一点点魔法。泻湖的贵妇人正处于可怕的危险中。唯一能帮助你们救她脱离灾难的人是海洋之王。要找到他，请按照以下指示行动：当月光下水位上涨时，去拜访泻湖的贵妇人。要弄清楚你们需要去哪里，去找一位名叫提埃波洛的画家。午夜时分，有两个人会告诉你们时间。赶快爬上他们的塔顶。丛林之王会带你们去那里，不是走陆地，而是高高在空中。一位金色的天使会指引你们的路，夜晚通往大海，白天回到家中。

Jack pushed his glasses into place. “Hmm,” he said. 杰克把眼镜推到正确的位置上。 “嗯，”他说。

“I wonder who the Grand Lady is,” said Annie. “And why do we need to save her?” “我想知道那位大女士是谁，”安妮说。 “为什么我们需要救她？”

“I’m wondering lots of stuff,” said Jack. “What about the King of the Jungle? We’re in a city, not a jungle. And what about that angel? And—” “我在想很多事情，”杰克说。 “那丛林之王呢？ 我们是在城市里，不是在丛林里。 那那个天使呢？还有—”，

“Let’s just take one thing at a time,” said Annie. “What are we supposed to do first?” “我们一次只做一件事，”安妮说。 “我们第一步应该做什么？”

Jack looked back at the letter and read: 杰克回头看了看那封信，读道：

When waters rise beneath the moon, 当月光下水面开始上涨时。

Visit the Grand Lady of the Lagoon. 去拜访沼泽的贵夫人吧。

“So let’s start by finding the Grand Lady,” said Annie. She headed down the tree house ladder. “那我们先从找到贵夫人开始吧，”安妮说。 她走下树屋的梯子。

Jack put Merlin’s letter into his backpack, along with Morgan’s research book and Teddy and Kathleen’s book of magic rhymes. Then he climbed down the rope ladder and caught up with Annie. 杰克把梅林的信放进背包里，还放上了摩根的研究书，以及泰迪和凯瑟琳的魔法童谣书。 然后他顺着绳梯爬下来，追上了安妮。

Jack and Annie walked over a pebbly path as the sky darkened. Good, thought Jack. It’s night. He didn’t want anyone to see him in his silly outfit—especially the red slippers with the bows. 杰克和安妮走过一条布满鹅卵石的小路，天空渐渐变暗了。 很好，杰克心想。天黑了。 他不想让任何人看到他穿着那套傻乎乎的衣服——尤其是那双带蝴蝶结的红色拖鞋。

“That must be the way out,” said Annie. She led Jack to a wooden gate in the garden wall and pushed it open. “这一定是出去的路，”安妮说。 她带杰克来到花园墙边的一扇木门旁，把它推开。

Jack and Annie stepped through the gate onto a quiet, empty walkway. Next to the walkway was a narrow lane of water. 杰克和安妮穿过大门，走上一条安静、空荡荡的小路。 小路旁边是一条狭窄的水道。

“I guess that’s a canal,” said Jack. “我想那是一条运河，”杰克说。

“And that must be a gondola,” said Annie. She pointed to a long, curved black boat rounding the corner. In the last light of day, the gondola was gliding silently up the canal toward them. “那一定是一艘贡多拉，”安妮说。 她指着一艘长长的、弯曲的黑色小船，正绕过拐角。 天色渐暗，小船悄无声息地沿着运河朝他们滑来。

“Yikes,” whispered Annie. “天哪，”安妮小声说。

There were two people in the boat—a gondolier and a passenger. Each wore a black cloak, white gloves, and a ghostly white mask. The masks had long, pointy noses shaped like bird beaks. The passenger sat in the middle of the boat, holding a lantern. The gondolier stood in the back, pushing a long oar through the water. 船上坐着两个人，一个是船夫，一个是乘客。 他们每个人都穿着黑色斗篷，戴着白色手套，还戴着一副苍白的面具。 面具的鼻子又长又尖，形状像鸟喙。 乘客坐在船中间，手里拿着一盏灯。 船夫站在船尾，用一根长桨划着水。

“They look a lot weirder than us,” said Annie. “他们看起来比我们奇怪多了，”安妮说。

“No kidding,” said Jack. “可不是嘛，”杰克说。

“Hello!” the passenger shouted. “你好！”乘客喊道。

The person’s voice was muffled behind the white mask. “Do you need help?” 那个人的声音被白色面具遮住了。 “你需要帮忙吗？”

“Yes!” Annie shouted back. “Can you take us to the Grand Lady of the Lagoon?” “需要！”安妮喊道。 “你能带我们去沼泽女主人那里吗？”

“Yes, of course. She is back that way!” answered the masked passenger. “Come.” “可以，当然可以。 她就在那边！”戴面具的乘客回答道。 “来吧。”

“Great!” said Annie. She grabbed Jack’s hand and pulled him over to the gondola. The gondola rocked a bit as she and Jack climbed aboard and took seats between the gondolier and the passenger. “太好了！”安妮说。 她抓住杰克的手，把他拉到贡多拉旁边。 安妮和杰克爬上去坐下，坐在船夫和乘客之间，贡多拉轻轻晃了一下。

The gondolier pushed the gondola away from the landing. His long oar made soft splashes in the water as the boat moved up the canal. 船夫把船从码头上推开。 他长长的船桨在水中轻轻划出水花，船沿着运河慢慢向前。

Jack cleared his throat. “Um, excuse me,” he said. “Why are you wearing bird masks?” 杰克清了清嗓子。 “呃，打扰一下，”他说。 “你们为什么戴着鸟面具？”

“For Carnival, of course,” said the passenger. “That is why you are wearing clown costumes, no?” “为了狂欢节，当然了，”乘客说。 “那就是你们为什么穿着小丑服装，对吗？”

“Oh, yeah, sure,” said Jack. “哦，对啊，当然了，”杰克说。

As the gondola slid through the canal, Jack snuck their research book out of his backpack. 当船沿着运河滑行时，杰克偷偷从背包里拿出他们的研究书。

“Oh, boy, a carnival!” Annie whispered to Jack. “I hope it has a roller coaster.” “哦，天哪，一个狂欢节！”，安妮小声对杰克说。 “希望里面有过山车。”

“I don’t think they had roller coasters two hundred sixty years ago,” whispered Jack. “我觉得两百六十年前他们还没有过山车，”杰克小声说。

Jack looked up carnival in the index. He found the page. Then he and Annie silently read together: 杰克在目录里查找了游乐园。 他找到了那一页。 然后他和安妮悄悄地一起读起来：

For many centuries, Carnival has been the most famous yearly festival of Venice. For Carnival, people disguise themselves as anything they want to be. Rich, poor, male, female, young, and old—all are equal during Carnival. 很多很多年以前，威尼斯最著名的年度节日就是狂欢节。在狂欢节期间，人们可以把自己打扮成任何他们想成为的样子。富人、穷人、男人、女人、年轻人和老人都在狂欢节期间变得平等。

“Look, that’s us,” whispered Annie. She pointed to a picture of a colorful costume with a white ruffled collar and red slippers with bows. “看，那是我们，”安妮小声说。 她指着一张彩色服装的图片，图片上是白色的蕾丝领子和红色的带蝴蝶结的拖鞋。

“Yeah, and that’s them,” whispered Jack. He pointed to a picture of a person dressed in a black cloak and a white mask with a bird’s beak. “对，那是他们，”杰克小声说。 他指着一张图片，图片上是一个穿着黑色斗篷、戴着鸟嘴白面具的人。

Jack closed the book and put it away. The gondola people didn’t seem so weird anymore. But he still wondered why a grand lady was in terrible danger at Carnival. 杰克合上书，把它收了起来。 那些坐船的人看起来不再那么奇怪了。 但他还是想知道，为什么一位高贵的女士在嘉年华上遇到了危险。

As the boat glided around a bend, Jack caught his breath. He saw dozens of gondolas rocking on the choppy waters of a wide, open canal. They were all decorated with ribbons and flowers. Candlelight from their lanterns danced on the dark, rippling water. 当船绕过一个弯道时，杰克屏住了呼吸。 他看到许多小船在宽阔的运河里摇晃，水面波涛起伏。 每艘船上都装饰着丝带和花朵。 灯笼里的烛光在黑暗的水面上摇曳。

“Look, that must be Carnival over there,” said Annie, pointing. “看，那边一定是嘉年华，”安妮指着说。

In the distance, thousands of candles twinkled along the shoreline. Sounds of laughter, clapping, and shouting floated over the water. 在远处，岸边有成千上万的蜡烛闪烁着微光。 笑声、掌声和喊声从水面上飘来。

“Hold on tightly!” said the masked person in the front of the gondola. “The tide is high tonight!” As their gondola joined the fleet of boats heading toward Carnival, the wind picked up. The waves grew taller. “抓紧了！”坐在船头戴面具的人说道。 “今晚潮水涨得很高！”当他们的船加入前往嘉年华的船队时，风突然变大了。 海浪变得越来越高。

Jack and Annie held on to the sides of the boat. Jack heard a faint rumble of faraway thunder. He saw a zigzag flash of lightning in the distant sky. Is a storm coming? he wondered nervously. Is the storm part of the disaster that’s going to happen to the Grand Lady of the Lagoon? 杰克和安妮紧紧抓住船的两边。 杰克听到远处传来一声微弱的雷声。 他看到远处天空中一道闪电划出锯齿形的光。 是不是要来风暴了？ 他紧张地想。 这场风暴是不是会带来发生在湖中女士身上的灾难？

“This is going to be fun, isn’t it?” said Annie cheerfully. “这会很有趣，对吧？”安妮开心地说。

“Sure,” said Jack. He tried to shake off his worries as the wind and tide helped push the gondola toward the twinkling candles of Carnival. “当然，”杰克说。 他努力甩掉心中的担忧，风和潮水一起推着小船，朝嘉年华闪烁的蜡烛靠近。
