9. The Painting 9. 那幅画

The gondolier silently helped Jack and Annie into the boat. Then he untied the gondola and pushed off from the landing. 船夫默默地帮杰克和安妮上了船。 然后他解开了船的绳子，从码头划开。

As the boat glided through the shallow waters, Jack looked back at Venice. Bathed in early sunlight, the Grand Lady of the Lagoon did seem timeless. 当船滑过浅水时，杰克回头看向威尼斯。 沐浴在清晨的阳光下，这位湖畔的贵妇似乎真的穿越了时间。

The gondola moved around the bend and up the narrow canal near the walled garden. The gondolier tied the boat to a striped pole. He then offered his gloved hand to Annie to help her out of the boat. Annie climbed out and the gondolier offered his hand to Jack. 船绕过弯道，沿着靠近围墙花园的小运河向前行驶。 船夫把船绑在一根有条纹的杆子上。 然后，他伸出戴着手套的手，帮安妮从船上下来。 安妮爬下来后，船夫又伸出他的手，帮杰克。

As the gondolier helped Jack onto the landing, the boat rocked in the water. Jack tripped, pulling the glove off the gondolier’s hand. 当船夫帮杰克登上码头时，船在水里晃了一下。 杰克被绊了一下，把船夫手上的手套扯了下来。

“Oh, sorry,” said Jack. As he handed the glove back, he gasped. On the gondolier’s finger was a pale blue glass ring. “对不起，”杰克说。 当他把手套还回去时，他惊叫了一声。 船夫手指上戴着一枚淡蓝色的玻璃戒指。

Before Jack could say anything, the gondolier pulled his glove back on and pushed the boat away from the landing. 杰克还没来得及说话，船夫就重新戴上了手套，把船从码头上推开。

“Hey—hey!” Jack sputtered. “Teddy? Kathleen? Wait! Come back!” “嘿——嘿！”杰克结结巴巴地说。 “泰迪？凯瑟琳？ 等一下！回来！

Neither of the masked people looked back at Jack and Annie. 戴面具的两个人没有回头看杰克和安妮。

“Teddy and Kathleen? Where?” said Annie. “泰迪和凯瑟琳？ 在哪里？”安妮说。

“His glove—it came off! There was a blue glass ring on his finger!” said Jack. “他的手套——掉下来了！ “他手指上戴着一个蓝色的玻璃戒指！”杰克说。

Jack and Annie watched the gondola disappear into a blaze of sunlight shining on the water. Had it just glided around the bend? Or had it vanished altogether? 杰克和安妮看着热气球消失在阳光照耀的水面上。 它刚刚滑过弯道了吗？ 还是完全消失了？

“Are you sure it was them?” said Annie. “你确定是他们吗？”安妮说。

“Well, I guess anybody could wear a glass ring,” said Jack. “But still…” “嗯，我想任何人都可能戴着一个玻璃戒指，”杰克说。 “但还是……”

“Maybe Morgan and Merlin told them to watch over us,” said Annie. “也许摩根和梅林告诉他们要守护我们，”安妮说。

“Yeah, to make sure we’d be safe,” said Jack. “对，为了确保我们安全，”杰克说。

“And be patient and follow instructions,” said Annie. “还要有耐心，听从指示，”安妮说。

“Right,” said Jack. “Well, Venice wasn’t destroyed by a flood. So I guess we passed our first test.” “对，”杰克说。 “好吧，威尼斯并没有被洪水摧毁。 所以我想我们通过了第一个考验。”

“I think we did,” said Annie. “我想我们通过了，”安妮说。

With Lorenzo’s canvas tucked under his arm, Jack led the way into the walled garden. Annie followed him to the rope ladder. 杰克把洛伦佐的画布夹在胳膊下，带头走进了带围墙的花园。 安妮跟着他走到绳梯那里。

When they climbed into the tree house, Jack pulled Merlin’s letter from his backpack. He unfolded it and pointed to the words Frog Creek. 他们爬进树屋后，杰克从背包里拿出梅林的信。 他展开信，指着上面写着‘蛙溪镇’的字。

“I wish we could go there!” he said. “真希望我们能去那里！”他说。

“Good-bye, Grand Lady of the Lagoon!” said Annie. “再见了，沼泽湖的女主人！”安妮说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 绝对一动不动。

A nippy wind rustled the Frog Creek trees. Jack and Annie were wearing their jeans and jackets again. It was dawn. 一阵凉风轻轻摇晃着蛙溪镇的树木。 杰克和安妮又穿上了他们的牛仔裤和夹克。天刚刚亮。

Annie sighed. “I wish we’d had a little more time to visit Venice,” she said. 安妮叹了口气。 “真希望我们能多一点时间参观威尼斯，”她说。

“I’m glad Lorenzo gave us his painting to finish,” said Jack. “That’ll be like living our trip all over again.” “我很高兴洛伦佐给了我们他的画，让我们完成它，”杰克说。 “那会感觉就像重新经历一次我们的旅行。”

“Cool,” said Annie. “太酷了，”安妮说。

“We’d better leave Morgan’s research book here,” said Jack. He pulled the book out of his backpack and put it on the floor. “And this.” He took out Teddy and Kathleen’s book of magic rhymes. “我们最好把摩根的研究书留在这里，”杰克说。 他从背包里拿出书，放在了地上。 “还有这个。”他拿出了泰迪和凯瑟琳的魔法押韵书。

“Wait,” said Annie. “Don’t you think we should take the book of rhymes with us? For safekeeping?” “等等，”安妮说。 “你不觉得我们应该带上这本押韵书吗？为了安全起见？”

Jack nodded. “We can’t use them in Frog Creek,” he said. “We’ll just keep it safe till we go on our next mission.” 杰克点了点头。 “我们在蛙溪镇不能使用它们，”他说。 “我们只是在下次任务前把它保管好。”

“That’s what I was thinking,” said Annie. “Come on. Hurry, before Mom and Dad wake up.” “我也是这么想的，”安妮说。 “快点。 快点，不然爸爸妈妈就醒了。”

Jack put the book of rhymes back into his pack. Carrying Lorenzo’s canvas, Annie climbed down the ladder. Jack followed. 杰克把这本押韵书放回了他的背包里。 安妮抱着洛伦佐的画布，顺着梯子爬了下来。杰克跟在后面。

As they walked through the chilly dawn woods, Annie held up Lorenzo’s canvas. The shimmering light and waters of Venice looked just like they had in real life. “So what should we put in our painting?” she asked. 他们穿过寒冷清晨的树林时，安妮举着洛伦佐的画布。 威尼斯闪烁的灯光和水面看起来和现实生活中一模一样。 “那我们画些什么呢？”她问。

“Gondolas, of course,” said Jack, “with people in costumes.” “当然是贡多拉，”杰克说，“上面还有穿着戏服的人。”

“Wearing black capes and bird masks,” said Annie, “and fancy dresses and wigs.” “穿着黑色斗篷和鸟面具，”安妮说，“还有华丽的裙子和假发。”

“And we could paint the clock tower in the background,” said Jack, “with the two men striking the bell.” “我们还可以在背景里画钟楼，”杰克说，“上面有两个男人在敲钟。”

“And the watchtower, too,” said Annie, “with the gold angel on top.” “还有瞭望塔，”安妮说，“上面有金色的天使。”

“And the ruler’s palace,” said Jack. “还有统治者的宫殿，”杰克说。

“And the old man and Rosa walking along the canal,” said Annie, “and, of course, Lorenzo.” “还有那个老人和罗莎沿着运河走路，”安妮说，“当然还有洛伦佐。”

“And the lion flying across the sky,” said Jack, “and Neptune’s spear rising out of the water.” “还有飞过天空的狮子，”杰克说，“还有从水里升起的海王的长矛。”

“With Neptune himself peeking out!” said Annie. “We’ll show just the top of his head and his mysterious eyes.” “海王本人还偷偷探出头来呢！”安妮说。 “我们只画出他的头和神秘的眼睛。”

“That’s a lot of stuff for one painting,” said Jack. “一幅画里要画这么多东西，”杰克说，“还真有点多。”

“We didn’t even put ourselves in yet,” said Annie. “我们甚至还没画上自己呢，”安妮说。

“We’ll paint ourselves on the back of the lion,” said Jack, “wearing our clown suits and red slippers.” “我们要把自己画在狮子的背上，”杰克说，“穿着我们的小丑服和红色拖鞋。”

“Yeah, with huge smiles on our faces,” said Annie, “like we’re thinking, Wow! Wow! Wow!” “是的，脸上还要带着大大的笑容，”安妮说，“就像我们在想：哇！哇！ 哇！”，

Jack laughed. 杰克笑了。

A chilly dawn breeze swept through the bare trees. The bells of a Frog Creek church began to chime. Jack and Annie took off running for home. 一阵凉爽的晨风穿过光秃秃的树梢。 蛙溪镇教堂的钟声开始响起来了。 杰克和安妮赶紧往家跑。
