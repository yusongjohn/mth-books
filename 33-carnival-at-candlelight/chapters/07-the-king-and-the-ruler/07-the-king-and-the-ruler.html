7. The King and the Ruler 7. 国王与统治者

“Oh, yeah. Of course,” whispered Jack. He’d forgotten all about Teddy and Kathleen’s book of magic rhymes! “哦，对啊。 当然，”杰克小声说。 他完全忘记了泰迪和凯瑟琳的魔法童谣书！

Jack pulled the book out of his backpack. He and Annie studied the table of contents. “ Make a Stone Come Alive,” said Annie. “ Make Metal Soft. Turn into Ducks. Fly Through the Air— that’s it!” 杰克从背包里拿出那本书。 他和安妮研究了目录。 “让石头活起来，”安妮说。 “让金属变软，变成鸭子。 飞过天空——就是这个！”，

“No,” said Jack. “Go back to Make a Stone Come Alive.” “不，”杰克说。 “回到‘让石头活起来’那里。”

“Why?” said Annie. “为什么？”安妮说。

“Because the lion’s supposed to carry us,” said Jack, “and he already has wings. But he’s made of stone. So what we need to do is make him come alive.” “因为狮子应该带我们走，”杰克说，“而且他已经长出翅膀了。” 但他是由石头做的。 所以我们需要做的，就是让他活过来。”

“Oh, right,” said Annie. “哦，对啊，”安妮说。

“But then what?” said Jack. “Where will we go?” “那之后呢？”杰克说。 “我们要去哪儿呢？”

“Merlin’s letter said an angel of gold would show us the way, remember?” said Annie. “梅林的信上说，一位金色的天使会指引我们方向，还记得吗？”安妮说。

“Angel of gold?” said Jack. “Where are we going to find that? And how will we find Neptune? How will he help us save Venice?” “金色的天使？”杰克说。 “我们到哪儿去找那东西呢？ 安妮问：“我们怎么才能找到海王星呢？” 他怎么帮我们拯救威尼斯呢？

“Patience” said Annie. “If we need more magic, we’ll go back to the book.” “耐心点。”安妮说。 “如果我们需要更多魔法，我们就回到书里。”

“Okay. But let’s hurry,” said Jack. He opened Teddy and Kathleen’s book to the second rhyme. He took a deep breath. He looked down at the lion on the ledge. Then he read in a loud, clear voice: “好吧。” “不过我们得快点，”杰克说。 他翻开泰迪和凯瑟琳的书，翻到第二首童谣。 他深吸了一口气。 他低头看着石台上那只狮子。 然后他用响亮清晰的声音读起来：

Stone so silent, cold, and hard, 石头沉默、冰冷又坚硬。

Cum-matta-lie, cum-matta-skaard! 库姆-马塔-利，库姆-马塔-斯卡ard！

A cracking sound seemed to come from deep within the lion’s body. As Jack and Annie peered down at the statue, the lion’s stone mane ruffled into a mass of shaggy fur. His stone back softened into a sleek golden coat. His stone wings stretched into long, luminous feathers. 似乎有一声裂响从狮子身体深处传来。 当杰克和安妮低头看着雕像时，狮子的石制鬃毛变得蓬松起来，像一团乱蓬蓬的毛发。 他的石头后背变得柔软，变成了一身光滑的金色毛皮。 他的石头翅膀伸展开来，变成了长长的、闪闪发光的羽毛。

“Wow,” breathed Annie. Jack couldn’t speak. Before their eyes, the statue had turned into a living lion with magnificent golden wings. The lion shook his mane and yawned. He had huge, sharp teeth and a long pink tongue. His ears twitched. His tufted tail swayed back and forth. “哇，”安妮惊叹道。 杰克说不出话来。 在他们眼前，雕像变成了一个活生生的狮子，有着华丽的金色翅膀。 狮子摇晃着鬃毛，打了个哈欠。 他有又大又尖的牙齿，还有一条长长的粉红色舌头。 他的耳朵动了动。 他蓬松的尾巴来回摆动。

The lion crouched and leapt off the ledge like a cat. He spread his wings and caught a strong current of wind. His wings flapped, and he began circling above the square. 狮子蹲下身，像猫一样从台阶上跳了下去。 他展开翅膀，抓住了一阵强风。 他的翅膀拍打，开始在广场上空盘旋。

“Here! Here!” Annie shouted. She waved wildly. “在这儿！ 在这儿！”安妮喊道。她挥舞着双手。

The winged lion turned and flew back toward the tower. He glided silently onto the terrace, landing just a few feet away from Jack and Annie. He stared at them with his golden eyes. 长着翅膀的狮子转过身，飞回了塔楼。 他悄无声息地滑翔到露台上，落在杰克和安妮不远处。 他用金色的眼睛盯着他们。

“You have to help us save Venice from a flood disaster,” Jack said. “你们必须帮助我们拯救威尼斯，免受洪水灾害，”杰克说。

“Can you carry us to Neptune?” said Annie. “你能带我们去见海神吗？”安妮说。

The lion padded toward them. He kept staring straight at them. He tilted his tremendous head and let out a long growl, as if he was trying to answer. 狮子慢慢地朝他们走来。 他一直直直地盯着他们。 他倾斜着巨大的头，发出一声长长的吼叫，好像他正试图回答。

“We have to climb on your back,” said Jack. “我们必须爬到你背上，”杰克说。

“I hope we won’t hurt you,” said Annie. “希望我们不会伤害你，”安妮说。

The lion let out another growl, but he didn’t sound angry. He sounded as if he was telling them to hurry. He crouched down so they could climb on. 狮子又发出一声吼叫，但听起来并不生气。 他听起来像是在催促他们快点。 他蹲下身，让他们爬上去。

“I’ll go first,” Jack said to Annie. “I’ll hold on to his mane, and you hold on to me.” Jack slipped off his backpack and dropped it onto the terrace. “我先上，”杰克对安妮说。 “我会抓住他的鬃毛，你抓住我。”杰克取下背包，把它扔到了阳台上。

“Take the book of rhymes,” said Annie. “拿上这本押韵书，”安妮说。

“Got it,” said Jack. He tucked the book under his arm and carefully climbed onto the lion’s back. “明白了，”杰克说。 他把书夹在腋下，小心地爬上了狮子的背。

Annie climbed on behind Jack and wrapped her arms tightly around his waist. Jack twisted his fingers into the lion’s mane. The mane felt surprisingly soft. 安妮从后面爬到杰克身上，紧紧地环住他的腰。 杰克把手指插进狮子的鬃毛里。 鬃毛竟然出奇地柔软。

“Okay, we’re ready,” said Jack. “好了，我们准备好了，”杰克说。

The lion stood up. He trembled slightly. Then he leapt off the terrace. 狮子站了起来。 他微微颤抖了一下。 然后他从阳台上跳了下去。

“Ahh!” Jack yelled. The rhyme book slipped from under his arm and fell down into the flooded square below. “啊！”杰克喊道。 韵律书从他胳膊下滑落，掉进了下面被水淹没的广场里。

“Oh, no! Our book!” cried Jack. “哦，不！ 我们的书！”杰克喊道。

“Hang on!” cried Annie. “抓紧！”安妮喊道。

The lion flapped his great wings and rose through the sky. Jack pressed his knees into the lion’s warm back and clung to his mane. 狮子扇动着巨大的翅膀，飞向天空。 杰克把膝盖压在狮子温暖的背上，紧紧抓住它的鬃毛。

The lion flew toward the fireworks. A shower of red sparks was opening like an umbrella. Booms and whistles filled the night. Fiery bits rained down through the darkness, whistling into the canal. 狮子飞向烟花。 一 shower 红色的火花像伞一样张开。 爆炸声和口哨声充满了夜晚。 火光四溅，穿过黑暗，呼啸着落进运河里。

“Help! We’re heading straight into the fireworks!” cried Annie. “我们正冲向烟花！”安妮喊道。

The lion dipped and turned away from the fireworks. The red showers gave way to bursts of blue and green. 狮子低下头，转身避开烟花。 红色的雨点变成了蓝色和绿色的烟花。

“Which way do we go?” shouted Annie. “我们该往哪走？”安妮喊道。

As the lion flew back toward the square, Jack saw the gold weather vane on top of the watchtower. It was in the shape of an angel. 当狮子飞回广场时，杰克看见了钟楼顶部的金色风向标。 它形状像一个天使。

“The angel of gold!” shouted Jack. “金色的天使！”杰克喊道。

The weather vane was no longer pointing north with the winds. The angel was turning slowly around and around, pointing in all directions. 风向标不再随着风指向北方了。 天使慢慢地转着圈，指向各个方向。

“Fly closer to the angel!” shouted Annie. “飞得更靠近天使一点！”安妮喊道。

The lion turned and flew toward the watchtower. As they drew near the golden angel, Jack called out, “Which way do we go? Which way?” 狮子转身，朝瞭望塔飞去。 当他们靠近金色天使时，杰克喊道：“我们该往哪走？哪边？”

The weather vane turned in a full circle and then came to a complete stop. The angel was pointing toward the southeast, over the choppy water. 风向标转了一圈，然后完全停了下来。 天使正指着东南方向，那里的水面波涛汹涌。

“To the sea!” Annie called over the wind. “去海里！”安妮顶着风喊道。

The lion turned and soared into the wind, his strong wings shining like gold. 狮子转身，迎着风飞去，他强壮的翅膀闪闪发亮，像金子一样。

“Oh, wow!” cried Annie. “哦，哇！”安妮喊道。

The flying lion glided past the fireworks and over the wide canal. Leaving Venice behind, he flew high over the stormy seas. 飞着的狮子从烟花旁滑过，飞过了宽阔的运河。 离开威尼斯后，他飞过汹涌的海面，飞得很高。

Jack gripped the lion’s mane with all his might. The lion sailed in and out of thick, fast-moving clouds. He flew above crashing waves and past bolts of lightning. He flew through screaming winds and pelting rain. 杰克用尽全力抓着狮子的鬃毛。 狮子在厚厚的、快速移动的云层中穿梭进出。 他飞过翻腾的海浪，飞过一道道闪电。 他飞过呼啸的狂风，飞过倾盆大雨。

In the middle of the sea, far from land, the lion began circling above the water. “What’s he doing?” cried Jack. 在大海的中央，远离陆地的地方，狮子开始在水面上盘旋。 “他在干什么？”杰克喊道。

“Looking for Neptune!” shouted Annie. “我们在找海王星！”安妮喊道。

“But Neptune’s not real!” said Jack. “但海王星不是真的！”杰克说。

“I know!” shouted Annie. “We’ll have to use our imaginations! Like Lorenzo! Try to imagine Neptune!” “我知道！”安妮喊道。 “我们必须用想象力！就像洛伦佐那样！ 试着想象海王星！

Jack tried to imagine Neptune, but he was too scared to think clearly. 杰克试着想象海王尼普顿，但他太害怕了，想不清楚。

“Neptune!” shouted Annie. “Rise from the water! Save Venice, Neptune! Help us!” Annie’s voice was lost in the wind. “尼普顿！”安妮喊道。 “从水中升起！ 拯救威尼斯，尼普顿！ 帮帮我们！”安妮的声音被风吹散了。

Jack wrapped his arms around the lion’s neck. He buried his face in the lion’s mane. He tried desperately to imagine Neptune. 杰克把双臂环住了狮子的脖子。 他把脸埋进了狮子的鬃毛里。 他拼命地试着想象海王尼普顿。

The lion let out a roar. With his hands under the lion’s throat, Jack felt as if he himself were roaring. The lion roared again. The roaring made Jack feel stronger and calmer. The details of Tiepolo’s painting came into his mind. 狮子发出一声怒吼。 杰克用手托住狮子的喉咙，感觉好像自己在怒吼。 狮子又吼了一声。 吼声让杰克感觉更强大、更平静了。 提埃波洛画作的细节浮现在他的脑海中。

In his imagination, Jack saw Neptune, Ruler of the Seas, with his white beard and long hair, his strong arms and shoulders. He saw a lovely lady who was Venice, the Grand Lady of the Lagoon…. He saw Neptune giving the Grand Lady a gift…. 在他的想象中，杰克看到了海神尼普顿，他有着白色的胡须和长长的头发，强壮的双臂和肩膀。 他看到了一位美丽的女士，她是威尼斯，潟湖的高贵女士…… 他看到海神尼普顿送给高贵女士一份礼物……

“I see something!” cried Annie. “我看到了什么！”，安妮喊道。

Jack opened his eyes and sat up. “Where?” he cried. 杰克睁开了眼睛，坐了起来。 “在哪里？”他喊道。

“In the water!” said Annie. “在水里！”，安妮说道。

Clinging to the lion’s mane, Jack peered down into the darkness below. Lightning flashed over the sea. Jack saw a huge three-pronged spear rising out of the foaming, churning waves. 杰克紧紧抓着狮子的鬃毛，低头看向下面的黑暗。 闪电划过海面。 杰克看到一支巨大的三叉戟从翻腾的浪花中升起。

The sea below the spear began to heave and billow. Lightning flashed again, and Jack saw a great mass of swirling seaweed rising from the waves. Not seaweed—hair! Jack realized. A man’s giant head and neck appeared above the surface of the water. Then the man’s massive shoulders, chest, and arms rose above the storm-tossed sea. The giant loomed high above them, as tall as a mountain. 三叉戟下方的海水开始翻滚和鼓荡。 闪电再次划过，杰克看到一团巨大的海藻从波浪中升起。不是海藻——是头发！ 杰克意识到。 一个男人巨大的头和脖子出现在水面上。 接着，这个男人巨大的肩膀、胸部和手臂也从风暴肆虐的海面上升了起来。 这个巨人高高耸立在他们上方，像一座山一样高。

“Neptune!” cried Annie. “海神！”安妮喊道。

The lion let out a roar and then another and another. 狮子发出一声吼叫，接着又一声，又一声。

Lit by flashes of lightning, Neptune’s face looked as if it had been weathered by thousands of years of wind and sand and waves. He had deep-set eyes, craggy cheeks, a white beard, and tangled hair hanging to his shoulders. 闪电的光芒一闪一闪，照亮了海神的面孔，看起来仿佛经历了几千年的风吹、沙打和海浪冲刷。 他有着深陷的眼窝、粗糙的脸颊、雪白的胡须，还有缠绕着垂到肩膀上的乱发。

“Neptune, save Venice from the flood!” cried Jack. “海神，救救威尼斯，别让洪水淹了它！”，杰克喊道。

“Please!” called Annie. “Save the Grand Lady of the Lagoon!” “求求你了！”，安妮喊道。 “救救潟湖的女王吧！”，

Neptune looked at them for a moment. Then, with his powerful arms, he lifted his spear and thrust it down into the waves. When the spear pierced the surface of the water, the sea made a gurgling sound—and then a long slurp, as if water were flowing down a drain. 海神看了他们一会儿。 然后，他用强有力的手臂举起长矛，猛地刺向海浪中。 当长矛刺入水面时，海水发出咕噜咕噜的声音，接着又发出长长的吸水声，就像水被排进下水道一样。

The thunder and lightning stopped. The storm-tossed waves grew calm. The wind died to a gentle breeze. The clouds parted, and the stars shined brightly. 雷声和闪电停了下来。 风暴中的海浪渐渐平静下来。 风也变成了轻柔的微风。 乌云散开了，星星闪闪发亮。

Neptune raised his spear. He nodded to Jack and Annie and the lion. 海神尼普顿举起了他的长矛。 他朝杰克、安妮和狮子点了点头。

“Thanks!” cried Annie. “谢谢！”安妮喊道。

“Thanks!” shouted Jack. The lion roared again. “谢谢！”杰克大声说。 狮子又吼了一声。

Then Neptune began to sink back into the sea. His long arms … huge shoulders … thick neck … craggy face … floating hair—all disappeared. The prongs of his spear sank below the surface of the water. 接着，尼普顿开始沉入海中。 他长长的胳膊……宽阔的肩膀……粗壮的脖子……布满皱纹的脸……漂浮的头发——全都消失了。 他的长矛尖端沉入了水下。

The Ruler of the Seas was gone. Only a shimmering whirlpool swirled in the moonlight. 海的统治者消失了。 只剩下漩涡在月光下闪闪发光地旋转着。
