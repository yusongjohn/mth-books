1. A Book of Magic 1. 一本魔法书

Dawn was breaking in the Frog Creek woods. Jack saw a light shining up ahead. He ran toward it. He ran so fast, he couldn’t hear his feet hitting the ground. He couldn’t feel the frosty winter air. 黎明正在蛙溪镇的森林中渐渐来临。 杰克看到前方有一束光。他朝光跑去。 他跑得非常快，连脚踩在地上都听不见了。 他感觉不到寒冷的冬风。

As Jack got closer to the light, he could see the magic tree house at the top of the tallest oak. A girl and boy were looking out the window. The girl had dark, wavy hair and sea-blue eyes. The boy had tousled red hair and a big grin on his face. As the two kids waved at him, Jack felt incredibly happy. 当杰克靠近那束光时，他看见了最高的橡树顶端的神奇树屋。 一个女孩和一个男孩正望着窗外。 女孩有一头深色的波浪头发，眼睛是海蓝色的。 男孩的头发是红色的，有点凌乱，脸上带着灿烂的笑容。 当两个孩子向他挥手时，杰克感到非常开心。

“Jack, wake up!” “杰克，醒醒！”

Jack opened his eyes. His sister, Annie, was standing beside his bed. She was wearing her winter jacket. It was barely light outside. 杰克睁开了眼睛。 他的姐姐安妮正站在他的床边。 她穿着她的冬装外套。 外面几乎天还没亮。

“I just had a dream about the tree house,” she said. “我刚刚梦到了树屋，”她说。

“Really?” Jack said sleepily. “真的吗？”杰克迷迷糊糊地说。

“I dreamed we were running through the woods at dawn,” said Annie, “and when we got to the tree house, Teddy and Kathleen were there waiting for us.” “我梦见我们在黎明时分跑过树林，”安妮说，“当我们跑到树屋时，泰迪和凯瑟琳已经在那儿等我们了。”

Jack sat up. “I just had the same dream!” he said. 杰克坐了起来。 “我刚刚也做了同样的梦！”他说。

“Meet you downstairs,” said Annie. “我们在楼下见。”安妮说。

Annie left Jack’s room. Jack jumped out of bed, put on his glasses, and threw on his clothes. He grabbed his winter jacket and his backpack. Then he slipped quietly down the stairs and out the front door. 安妮离开了杰克的房间。 杰克跳下床，戴上眼镜，然后匆忙穿上衣服。 他抓起他的冬衣和背包。 然后他悄悄地走下楼梯，从前门走出去。

Annie was waiting on the porch. The February air was chilly. Frost sparkled in the grass as the sun rose over the Frog Creek woods. 安妮正在门廊上等着。 二月的空气有点冷。 太阳升起时，霜花在草地上闪闪发亮，照耀着蛙溪镇的森林。

“Ready?” asked Annie. “准备好了吗？”安妮问。

Jack nodded and zipped his jacket. Without another word, he and Annie hurried up their street and headed into the woods. They ran through the long shadows of early morning, between the bare winter trees. Then they stopped. 杰克点点头，拉上了他的夹克。 他没有再说什么，就和安妮一起快步走上他们的街道，朝森林里跑去。 他们跑过清晨长长的影子，穿过光秃秃的冬树之间。 然后他们停了下来。

The tree house was back, just as Jack had seen it in his dream! It was high in the tallest oak tree, shining in the cold morning light. 树屋又出现了，和杰克在梦中看到的一模一样！ 它高高地挂在最高的橡树上，在寒冷的晨光中闪闪发亮。

“Wow,” breathed Jack. “Dreams can come true.” “哇，”杰克惊叹道。 “梦想可以成真。”

“Yep,” said Annie. “Teddy! Kathleen!” “对，”安妮说。 “泰迪！凯瑟琳！”

No one answered. 没有人回答。

“I guess only part of this dream came true,” Annie said sadly. She grabbed the rope ladder and started up. Jack followed. Annie climbed into the tree house. “Oh, wow!” she said. “我想只有部分梦想成真了，”安妮伤心地说。 她抓住绳梯开始往上爬。杰克跟了上去。 安妮爬进了树屋。 “哦，哇！”她说。

“What is it?” said Jack. “怎么了？”杰克说。

“They’re here!” said Annie in a loud whisper. Jack climbed in behind her. Their friends Teddy and Kathleen, apprentices to Morgan le Fay, were sitting under the tree house window. Wrapped in heavy woolen cloaks, they were both fast asleep. “他们在这里！”安妮大声小声地说。 杰克从后面爬了进去。 他们的朋友泰迪和凯瑟琳，是摩根·勒·法伊的学徒，正坐在树屋的窗户下面。 他们披着厚重的羊毛斗篷，都睡着了。

“Hey, sleepyheads!” said Annie. “Wake up!” “嘿，睡懒觉的！”安妮说。 “醒醒！”

Kathleen blinked and yawned. Teddy rubbed his eyes. When he saw Jack and Annie, he gave them a wide grin and leapt to his feet. “Hello!” he said. 凯瑟琳眨了眨眼，打了个哈欠。 泰迪揉了揉眼睛。 当他看到杰克和安妮时，他朝他们咧嘴笑了一下，然后跳了起来。 “你好！”他说。

“Hello!” cried Annie. She threw her arms around Teddy. “We both dreamed you were here.” “你好！”安妮喊道。 她张开双臂抱住泰迪。 “我们都梦到你在这里。”

“Ah, then our magic worked!” said Teddy. “Kathleen suggested we send dreams to let you know we were here, and it seems our magic sent us to dreamland as well.” “啊，那我们的魔法起作用了！”，泰迪说。 “凯瑟琳建议我们发送梦境，让你知道我们在这里，看来我们的魔法也让我们进入了梦乡。”

“But now we are all awake,” said Kathleen. “And I am very glad to see you.” She stood up, drawing her cloak around her. Her blue eyes sparkled like seawater in the dawn light. “但现在我们都醒了，”凯瑟琳说。 “我很高兴见到你。”她站起来，把斗篷裹在身上。 她的蓝眼睛在黎明的光线下闪闪发亮，就像海水一样。

“I’m glad to see you, too,” Jack said shyly. “我也很高兴见到你，”杰克害羞地说。

“Are you taking us on another Merlin Mission?” said Annie. “你们要带我们去另一个梅林任务吗？”安妮说。

“Not exactly,” said Teddy. “Merlin has a most important mission for you. But this time, we will not be going along.” “不完全是，”泰迪说。 “梅林给你一个非常重要的任务。” “但这次，我们不会和你们一起去。”

“Oh, no!” said Annie. “What if we need your magic to help us?” “哦，不！”安妮说。 “如果我们需要你的魔法来帮助我们怎么办？”

Teddy and Kathleen looked at each other and smiled. Then Kathleen turned back to Jack and Annie. “Morgan thinks you may be ready to use magic on your own,” she said. 泰迪和凯瑟琳互相看了看，然后笑了。 然后凯瑟琳转身面对杰克和安妮。 “摩根认为你可能已经准备好自己使用魔法了，”她说。

“Really?” said Jack. “真的吗？”杰克说。

“Yes,” said Teddy, “but Merlin is very cautious about sharing magic powers with mortals, even with two as worthy as you. He is also wary of magic being used outside the realm of Camelot. Nevertheless, Morgan has convinced Merlin to let you prove yourselves. You will be tested on four missions.” “是的，”泰迪说，“但梅林对把魔法力量分享给凡人非常谨慎，即使是像你们这样值得信赖的两个人也不例外。 他也很担心魔法被用在卡梅洛特以外的地方。 不过，摩根已经说服梅林让你证明自己。 你将接受四项任务的考验。”

“But we don’t know any magic,” said Jack. “但我们什么魔法都不会，”杰克说。

“Remember what I told you on our last adventure?” said Teddy. “If we all work together—” “还记得上次冒险时我告诉你的吗？”泰迪说。 “如果我们大家齐心协力——”

“Anything is possible!” said Annie. “But you just said you weren’t coming with us.” “任何事情都有可能！”安妮说道。 “但是你刚才说你不和我们一起去。”

“That is true,” said Kathleen. “And that is why we bring you this.” She reached into a pocket of her cloak and pulled out a small handmade book. She gave the book to Annie. “那是真的，”凯瑟琳说道。 “这就是为什么我们给你带来这个。”她伸手到斗篷的口袋里，拿出了一本小的手工书。 她把这本书给了安妮。

The cover of the book was made of rough brown paper. Written on it in neat, simple handwriting was the title: 这本书的封面是用粗糙的棕色纸做的。 上面用整齐、简单的 handwriting 写着标题：

图中文字：10

Magic Rhymes for

Annie and Jack from

Teddy and Kathleen 图中文字：安妮和杰克从泰迪和凯瑟琳那里收到的10个魔法韵文

“You made this for us?” said Annie. “这是你们做的吗？”安妮说。

“Yes,” said Kathleen. “One line of each rhyme is in Teddy’s language, and one is in mine, the language of the Seal People.” “是的，”凯瑟琳说。 “每句押韵诗里有一行是泰迪的语言，另一行是我的语言，也就是海豹族的语言。

Annie opened the book to the table of contents. She and Jack skimmed the list of rhymes, and Jack read some of the entries aloud: 安妮翻开书，来到了目录页。 她和杰克快速浏览了押韵的列表，杰克还大声读了一些条目：

“Fly Through the Air. Make Metal Soft. “飞上天空，让金属变软。

Turn into Ducks—” 变成鸭子——”

Annie giggled. “These are so cool!” she said. “Let’s all turn into ducks!” 安妮咯咯地笑了。 “这些太酷了！”她说道。 “我们都变成鸭子吧！”

“Not now,” said Kathleen. “You must use these rhymes very sparingly. There are only ten rhymes in the book, and each can only be used once. They are meant to last you for four journeys.” “现在不行，”凯瑟琳说。 “你们必须非常少地使用这些押韵句。 这本书里只有十个押韵句，每个只能用一次。 它们可以陪你完成四次冒险。”

“Four?” said Jack. “四次？”杰克说。

“Aye,” said Teddy. “Merlin has agreed that if you can use our magic wisely on four missions, he will teach you the secrets that will allow you to make magic on your own.” “是的，”泰迪说。 “梅林同意了，如果你能明智地在四次任务中使用我们的魔法，他就会教你一些秘密，让你自己也能施法。”

“Oh, boy!” said Annie. “哦，天哪！”安妮说。

Jack put the book of magic rhymes in his backpack. “So where are we going on our first mission?” he asked. 杰克把那本魔法韵律书放进他的背包里。 “那么，我们的第一次任务要去哪里呢？”他问。

“This research book from Morgan will tell you,” said Teddy. He took out a book and handed it to Jack. The cover showed a bright, colorful city surrounded by water. “摩根给的这本研究书会告诉你，”泰迪说。 他拿出一本书递给杰克。 封面显示着一座被水环绕的明亮多彩的城市。

Jack read the title aloud: 杰克大声读出书名：

A VISIT TO VENICE, ITALY. 参观意大利威尼斯。

“I’ve heard of Venice,” said Annie. “Last year Aunt Gail and Uncle Michael went there on vacation.” “我听说过威尼斯，”安妮说。 “去年盖尔阿姨和迈克尔叔叔去那里度假了。”

“Aye, ’tis a city that has long welcomed travelers,” said Teddy. “But you and Jack will travel to the Venice of two hundred sixty years ago.” “啊，那是一座早已欢迎旅行者的城市，”泰迪说。 “但你和杰克要前往二百六十年前的威尼斯。”

“What will we do there?” asked Jack. “我们在那里做什么呢？”杰克问。

“Merlin has prepared careful directions for you,” said Teddy. He pulled a letter out of a pocket in his cloak and gave it to Jack. “Read this when you get to Venice.” “梅林已经为你准备好了详细的路线图，”泰迪说。 他从斗篷口袋里拿出一封信，递给杰克。 “到了威尼斯再读这封信。”

“Okay,” said Jack. He put Merlin’s letter and Morgan’s research book into his backpack. “好的，”杰克说。 他把梅林的信和摩根的研究书放进背包里。

“Wait a minute,” said Annie. “If we take the tree house to Venice, how will you guys get back to Camelot?” “等一下，”安妮说。 “如果我们把树屋带到威尼斯，你们怎么才能回到卡梅洛特呢？”，

Teddy and Kathleen smiled and held up their hands. They each wore a ring made of pale blue glass. “These magic rings belong to Morgan,” said Kathleen. “They will take us home.” 泰迪和凯瑟琳笑了，举起手来。 他们每个人手上都戴着一个由淡蓝色玻璃制成的戒指。 “这些魔法戒指是摩根的，”凯瑟琳说。 “它们会带我们回家。”

“Remember,” Teddy said to Jack and Annie. “Follow Merlin’s directions carefully. If you prove yourselves to be wise and brave helpers, he will call for you again soon.” “记住，”泰迪对杰克和安妮说。 “仔细听从梅林的指示。 如果你们证明自己是聪明又勇敢的助手，他很快就会再次召唤你们。”

Kathleen nodded. “Good-bye now,” she said to Jack and Annie. “Good luck.” 凯瑟琳点了点头。 “现在再见，”她对杰克和安妮说。 “祝你们好运。”

Kathleen and Teddy raised their glass rings to their lips. Together they whispered words too softly to be heard, then blew on the rings. 凯瑟琳和泰迪把玻璃戒指举到嘴边。 他们一起轻声说出一些几乎听不见的话，然后对着戒指吹了一口气。

Before Jack and Annie’s eyes, the two young sorcerers began to fade into the cool morning air. In an instant, they had disappeared completely. 杰克和安妮眼前，两个年轻的巫师开始慢慢消失在清晨的空气中。 转眼间，他们完全消失了。

“They’re gone,” breathed Jack. “他们不见了，”杰克低声说。

“I guess it’s time for us to go, too,” said Annie. “我想我们也该走了，”安妮说。

Jack took a deep breath. Then he pointed at the cover of the Venice book. “I wish we could go there!” he said. 杰克深吸了一口气。 然后他指着威尼斯书的封面。 “真希望我们也能去那里！”他说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都安静下来了。

Absolutely still. 绝对安静。
