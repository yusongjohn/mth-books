CHAPTER ELEVEN

Breaking the Spell

11. Breaking the Spell 11. 打破咒语

A crescent moon hung low in the dark purple sky. A few stars still twinkled. Jack and Annie didn’t move for a moment, breathing in the clean, fresh air. 一弯新月低低地挂在深紫色的天空中。 还有一些星星在闪烁。 杰克和安妮愣住了，一会儿才吸了一口清新的空气。

Jack broke the silence. “It looks like we made it to Camelot before break of day.” 杰克打破了沉默。 “看起来我们赶在天亮之前到达了卡梅洛特。”

“Yep. Where did we land exactly?” said Annie. “是啊。 我们到底降落在哪里了？”安妮说。

Jack turned on their flashlight. He pointed it out the window. The light shone on trees, their branches heavy with golden apples. 杰克打开了他们的手电筒。 他把手电筒指向窗外。 光亮照在树上，树枝上挂满了金黄色的苹果。

“I’ll bet we’re in the orchard where we landed the last time we were here,” said Jack. “我敢打赌，我们一定又降落在了上次来这里时那片果园里，”杰克说。

“Turn off the light,” whispered Annie. “I think I see something in the distance.” “关掉灯，”安妮小声说。 “我觉得我看到远处有什么东西。”

Jack switched off the flashlight. 杰克关掉了手电筒。

A different kind of light was shining in the dark orchard—a flickering light that moved toward them. Jack heard leaves crunching, twigs cracking, feet running, and voices whispering. 一种不同的光在黑暗的果园里闪烁着——一种忽明忽暗的光，朝他们这边移动过来。 杰克听见了踩碎树叶的声音，树枝断裂的声音，奔跑的脚步声，还有低声说话的声音。

“Jack? Annie?” a boy called out. “杰克？ 安妮？”一个男孩喊道。

“Is that you?” a girl said. “是你们吗？”一个女孩说。

“Yes!” cried Annie. She scrambled down the rope ladder. “是的！”安妮喊道。 她赶紧顺着绳梯爬了下来。

Jack gathered up the jewel, the flower, the feather, and the panda bread. He put them in his pack and carefully climbed down after Annie. 杰克捡起了宝石、花朵、羽毛和熊猫面包。 他把它们放进背包里，小心地在安妮后面爬下来。

Teddy and Kathleen stood in a circle of lantern light, dressed in their long dark cloaks. They hugged Jack and Annie. “Welcome!” said Teddy. “We have been walking the grounds all night, waiting for you. Finally we saw a light above the orchard!” 泰迪和凯瑟琳站在灯笼的光圈里，穿着他们长长的深色斗篷。 他们拥抱了杰克和安妮。 “欢迎！”泰迪说。 “我们整晚都在园子里走来走去，等着你们。 最后我们在果园上方看到了一束光！”

“Our flashlight,” said Annie. “我们的手电筒，”安妮说。

“And then we saw your light!” said Jack. “然后我们看到了你们的光！”杰克说。

“We’re so glad you’re here!” said Kathleen. “我们非常高兴你们来了！”凯瑟琳说。

“We are, too!” said Annie. “我们也一样！”安妮说道。

“Did you translate the last lines of the rhyme?” asked Jack. “你翻译了那首诗的最后几句吗？”杰克问道。

“We did, indeed,” said Kathleen. “我们确实翻译了，”凯瑟琳说道。

“And did you bring the four things to help us break the spell?” asked Teddy. “你们带来那四样东西帮助我们打破咒语了吗？”泰迪问道。

“We did, indeed!” said Annie. “我们确实带来了！”安妮说道。

“They’re all in my backpack,” said Jack. “它们都在我的背包里，”杰克说。

“Wonderful,” said Kathleen. “We knew if anyone could find them, it would be you two.” “太棒了，”凯瑟琳说。 “我们知道，如果有人能找到它们，一定是你们两个。”

“You have brought the four special things, and now we are all together. It is time to undo the spell,” said Teddy. “你们带来了四件特别的东西，现在我们终于都到齐了。 是时候解除这个咒语了，”泰迪说。

“Yay!” said Annie. “太棒了！”安妮说。

“Yay, indeed,” said Teddy. “Come along! Before the sun rises!” “确实太棒了，”泰迪说。 “快点走吧！ 在太阳升起之前！"

Teddy and Kathleen started back the way they’d come, and Jack and Annie followed. Together, they wove between the trees in the fresh dawn air. 泰迪和凯瑟琳开始往他们来的路上走，杰克和安妮跟在后面。 他们一起在清晨的新鲜空气中穿梭在树之间。

Finally they all emerged from the orchard. Kathleen shined the lantern on the door of a wooden cottage. 最后，他们都从果园里走了出来。 凯瑟琳用灯笼照向一座木屋的门。

“Merlin’s garden house,” said Jack. “梅林的花园小屋，”杰克说。

“Yes,” said Teddy. “This is where we brought Penny from her little nest in the castle. We hoped that she might come back to life in Merlin’s favorite place. But alas, she has not done so.” He sighed. “是的，”泰迪说。 “这是我们将彭妮从城堡里的小巢穴带到这里的地方。” 我们希望她能回到梅林最喜欢的地方重新活过来。 但唉，她并没有这么做。”他叹了口气。

Teddy opened the door to the cottage, and they all stepped in. Jack shivered. It was damp and chilly inside, like a tomb. 泰迪打开小屋的门，他们全都走了进去。杰克打了个寒战。 里面又潮湿又寒冷，就像一座坟墓。

Kathleen placed the lantern on a table in the middle of the room. 凯瑟琳把提灯放在房间中间的桌子上。

“There she is,” Teddy said sadly. “她在那里，”泰迪难过地说。

Penny stood on the table, lit by lantern light. The little penguin was as gray and lifeless as stone. 彭妮站在桌子上，被提灯的光亮照着。 那只小企鹅灰扑扑的，像石头一样没有生气。

“Penny?” said Jack. The last time he’d seen her, she’d been so cute, so warm and … alive. “彭妮？”杰克说。 最后一次见到她时，她看起来那么可爱，那么温暖，那么……有生命。

“Poor Penny,” said Annie. She touched the penguin’s beak and the top of her head. “可怜的彭妮，”安妮说。 她碰了碰企鹅的喙和头顶。

“Bring forth the four things you found to break the spell,” said Teddy. “拿出你找到的四样东西来打破这个咒语，”泰迪说。

Jack reached into his backpack. “Here—this is the first thing we found. It’s from India,” he said. He took out the emerald cut in the shape of a rose. He placed the jewel in front of Penny, where it glowed in the lantern light. 杰克伸手进了他的背包。 “这个是我们找到的第一样东西，”他说。 它来自印度，”他说。 他拿出了一颗形状像玫瑰的绿宝石。 他把宝石放在彭妮面前，在灯笼的光下，宝石闪闪发光。

“The emerald rose stands for love,” Annie said. “The kind of love that rises above our faults and mistakes.” “这颗绿宝石玫瑰代表爱，”安妮说。 “一种超越我们缺点和错误的爱。”

Jack pulled out the dried yellow and white flower and placed it next to the emerald rose. “This is from the Swiss Alps,” he said. 杰克拿出一朵干枯的黄色和白色花朵，把它放在翡翠玫瑰旁边。 “这是来自瑞士阿尔卑斯山，”他说。

“The glacial buttercup stands for being like a little kid,” Annie said. “The joyful, open spirit of childhood.” “这种冰川毛茛代表像小孩子一样，”安妮说。 “童年时那种快乐、开朗的精神。”

“This is from Abraham Lincoln, a great president of our country,” Jack said next. He took out the goose-feather quill and put it next to the flower. “这是来自亚伯拉罕·林肯，我们国家的一位伟大总统，”杰克接着说。 他拿出一支鹅毛笔，把它放在花朵旁边。

“This quill pen stands for the deepest kind of hope,” said Annie. “The hope that one day all our sorrows will make sense.” “这支鹅毛笔代表最深的一种希望，”安妮说。 “希望有一天，我们所有的悲伤都能说得通。”

“And this is from China,” said Jack. He took out the panda bread and put it next to the feather. “这是来自中国，”杰克说。 他拿出熊猫面包，把它放在羽毛旁边。

“This is called panda bread,” said Annie. “It stands for protecting animals, for loving them and speaking up for them because they can’t speak for themselves.” “这叫熊猫面包，”安妮说。 “它代表保护动物，爱护它们，为它们发声，因为它们自己无法为自己说话。”

All four things were lit by the circle of glowing lantern light, along with Penny. 这四个东西都被发光灯笼的光圈照亮，还有彭妮也被照亮了。

“Teddy, stand closer, you’re the spell caster,” said Kathleen. “泰迪，站得近一点，你是施法者，”凯瑟琳说。

Teddy stood in front of the table. He closed his eyes and took a deep breath. Then he raised his arms and exclaimed, “All four things are here with me, the one who cast the spell. Spell be broken! Penny, come back to life!” 泰迪站在桌子前面。 他闭上眼睛，深吸了一口气。 然后他举起手臂，大声喊道：“这四样东西都和我在一起，施法的人。咒语被打破了！” “佩妮，回来吧！”

Teddy opened his eyes. Everyone stared at Penny, waiting for something to happen. 泰迪睁开了眼睛。 大家都盯着彭妮，等着看会发生什么。

They waited and waited. 他们等啊等啊。

“Please, Penny … come back to life,” whispered Teddy. “求你了，彭妮……回来吧，”泰迪小声说。

They all waited another long moment. 他们又等了好久好久。

Slowly Teddy lowered his arms. “It’s not working,” he said quietly. “The spell to break the spell, it doesn’t work.” He picked up the small stone penguin and cradled her in his arms. “So you won’t come back to us,” he said. “You’ll never come back to us.” A tear fell from Teddy’s eyes, then another. 泰迪慢慢地放下了手臂。 “没用的，”他轻声说。 “解除咒语的咒语，没用的。”他捡起那只小石企鹅，把她抱在怀里。 “所以你不会回到我们身边了，”他说。 “你永远都不会回到我们身边了。”泰迪的眼泪掉了下来，接着又是一滴。

“Oh, Teddy,” said Annie. “哦，泰迪，”安妮说。

Teddy wiped his face on the sleeve of his cloak. “Don’t look at stupid me weeping,” he said. 泰迪用斗篷的袖子擦了擦脸。 “别看傻乎乎的我哭，”他说。

“It’s okay to cry,” said Jack. “哭也没关系，”杰克说。

“Are you sad because you think Merlin will banish you from Camelot?” said Annie. “你是因为觉得梅林会把你赶出卡梅洛特才难过的吗？”安妮说。

“No—no.” Teddy shook his head. “I’m sad because … well, what you’ve shown us—what you said to us just now—makes me terribly sad. The emerald makes me think how much Merlin loves Penny, and your flower makes me remember the joy she brought to everyone. And your gray feather makes me think how desperately Kathleen and I have been hoping we could undo the spell and bring Penny back to life. And your panda bread makes me think how I was supposed to protect Penny while Merlin was gone. But I didn’t care properly for her. I caused her harm instead…. I—I can’t change that now. I can’t, not ever, and I’m so sorry.” As Teddy wept, his tears fell onto the stone penguin. “不——不。”泰迪摇了摇头。 “我难过是因为……嗯，你们刚才展示给我们看的，刚才对我们的那些话，让我非常难过。 翡翠让我想到梅林有多爱彭妮，而你的花让我想起她给大家带来的快乐。 而你的灰色羽毛让我想到凯瑟琳和我多么希望能解除咒语，把彭妮带回人世。 而你的熊猫面包让我想到我本应该在梅林离开时好好保护彭妮。 但我没有好好照顾她。 我反而伤害了她…… 我——我现在没办法改变这件事。 我永远都无法改变，真的很抱歉。” 泰迪哭泣着，眼泪落在石头企鹅上。

“Teddy,” said Annie, “please don’t …” But then she stopped. “泰迪，”安妮说，“请别……” 但接着她停了下来。

Peep.

“Hush, Teddy,” Kathleen said. “Stop crying. Listen.” “安静，泰迪，”凯瑟琳说。 “别哭了。听我说。”

Peep.

Teddy and the others looked at Penny. As they watched, the baby penguin’s body turned from dull gray to soft white. She tilted her head. Her eyes blinked. 泰迪和其他人看着彭妮。 他们看着的时候，小企鹅的身体从暗灰色变成了柔软的白色。 她歪了歪头。她眨了眨眼睛。

Peep.
