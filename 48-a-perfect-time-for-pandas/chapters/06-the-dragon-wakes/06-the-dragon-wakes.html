CHAPTER SIX

The Dragon Wakes

6. The Dragon Wakes 6. 龙醒来了

Jack put on his brakes and jumped off his bike. Annie did the same. A huge boulder crashed down the mountain slope beside them. The boulder slammed onto the road right in front of them. They looked around wildly. Rocks were sliding and tumbling. Trees were whipping back and forth and then breaking apart. Branches, rocks, and dirt cascaded down the slope. 杰克踩住刹车，跳下了自行车。 安妮也做了同样的事。 一块巨大的石头从山坡上滚了下来，就在他们旁边。 那块石头重重地砸在了他们面前的道路上。 他们惊慌地四处张望。 石头不停地滑落和翻滚。 树木来回摇晃，然后断裂开来。 树枝、石头和泥土从山坡上倾泻而下。

As Jack tried to figure out what to do, another boulder tumbled down—and then another! Jack pulled Annie off the road. They scrambled into a rocky crevice and huddled down. Clods of dirt hit their bike helmets. 杰克正努力想该怎么办，又一块大石头滚了下来——接着又是一块！ 杰克把安妮从路上拉了回来。 他们慌慌张张地钻进一块岩石缝隙里，缩成一团。 泥土块砸在他们的自行车头盔上。

The ground finally stopped shaking and rumbling. For a moment, neither Jack nor Annie said anything. Then Jack uttered one word: “Earthquake.” 地面终于停止了震动。 有一会儿，杰克和安妮谁都没说话。 然后杰克说了一个词：‘地震。’

“I know,” Annie said hoarsely. “Like—like that earthquake we lived through in San Francisco.” ‘我知道，’安妮沙哑地说。 ‘就像——就像我们在旧金山经历过的那次地震。’

“Except there, buildings crumbled around us,” said Jack. “Here, it’s mountains.” ‘不过那次，周围的建筑物都倒塌了，’杰克说。 “这里，是山。”

“I think it’s stopped,” said Annie. “Let’s look.” “我觉得它停了，”安妮说。 “我们去看看。”

Annie started to stand up, but Jack pulled her back down. “Wait!” The ground had started shaking again. “Aftershock!” he said. “Watch out—more stuff might fall.” 安妮开始站起来，但杰克把她拉了回去。 “等一下！”地面又开始摇晃了。 “余震！”他说。 “小心——还可能有东西掉下来。”

No sooner had Jack said that than another enormous boulder crashed to the ground. Then another one tumbled down. The earth stopped trembling again, but the air was so thick with dust and grit that it made Jack’s eyes burn. 杰克话音刚落，又一块巨大的石头砸到了地上。 接着又有一块滚了下来。 地面再次停止了颤抖，但空气中充满了灰尘和碎屑，让杰克的眼睛感到刺痛。

“I wonder if the pandas are all right,” said Annie. “我真希望熊猫们没事，”安妮说。

“Oh, man!” said Jack. “Bing-Bing!” He pictured the giant panda in her yard, trapped under a rock or a tree! “哦，天哪！”杰克说。 “蹦蹦！”他想象着那只大熊猫在院子里，被一块石头或一棵树压住了！

“We’ve got to go back!” said Annie. “我们必须回去！”安妮说。

“I know!” said Jack. They both scrambled out to the road. “Where are our bikes?” “我知道！”杰克说。 他们俩赶紧爬到路上。 “我们的自行车去哪儿了？”

Jack and Annie looked around. As the dust settled, they saw massive damage up and down the highway. Fallen rocks, mud, and trees blocked both the way to Wolong Town and the way back to the panda center. A boulder the size of a car had crushed both their bikes. 杰克和安妮环顾四周。 随着尘土落定，他们看到高速公路上到处都是严重的损坏。 掉落的石头、泥浆和树木堵住了去往卧龙镇的路，也堵住了返回熊猫中心的路。 一块像汽车一样大的石头把他们的两辆自行车都压坏了。

Jack and Annie took off their helmets and dropped them to the ground. “We can’t go back to the panda center now,” said Annie. “We can’t go to town. We can’t go anywhere.” 杰克和安妮摘下头盔，把它们扔在地上。 “我们现在不能回熊猫中心了，”安妮说。 “我们不能去镇上。 我们哪儿也去不了。”

“No kidding,” said Jack. “You’d have to be King Kong to get over this stuff.” “真没办法，”杰克说。 “你得是金刚才能翻过这些东西。”

“King Kong,” Annie repeated. Then she looked at Jack and grinned. “金刚，”安妮重复了一遍。 然后她看着杰克，露出了笑容。

“What?” he said. “怎么了？”他说。

“King Kong. No problem,” she said. “We can do it.” “金刚。” “没问题，”她说。 “我们可以做到。”

“Do what?” said Jack. “做什么？”杰克说。

“Turn into King Kongs!” said Annie. “Well, not quite as big as King Kong, maybe, but we could be five times bigger than we are now.” “变成金刚！”安妮说。 “嗯，可能不会像金刚那么大，但我们可以变得比现在大五倍。”

“Ohhhh,” said Jack. He’d forgotten all about their magic potion! “哦——，”杰克说。 他完全忘记了他们的魔法药水！

“It’s perfect,” said Annie. “It’s the only way we can get out of here.” “太完美了，”安妮说。 “这是我们现在唯一能出去的办法。”

“Yeah …,” said Jack, letting out a long breath. He reached into his backpack and took out the small bottle that held the potion. “嗯……，”杰克说，长长地叹了一口气。 他伸手到背包里，拿出装着魔药的小瓶子。

“Five times our size,” said Annie. “You’re almost five feet tall. So you’ll be almost twenty-five feet tall. And I’ll be just a little shorter.” “我们的五倍大，”安妮说。 “你们几乎有五英尺高。 所以你们会几乎有二十五英尺高。 而我只会比你们矮一点点。”

“Right,” said Jack. “对，”杰克说。

“But we can only use it once,” said Annie. “And we only have an hour. And we have to choose which way to go: back to Wolong Town or back to the panda center. If we go to town, we can keep looking for the special food to save Penny—if the restaurants are still standing.” “但我们只能用一次，”安妮说。 “我们只有一小时。 我们必须选择走哪条路：回到卧龙镇，还是回到熊猫中心。” 如果我们去镇上，就能继续寻找能救彭妮的特殊食物——如果餐馆还在的话。”

“Plus we can leave in the tree house whenever we’re ready,” said Jack. “而且，我们随时可以坐树屋离开，”杰克说。

“Right,” said Annie. “But we’d have to leave Bing-Bing and the rest of the pandas behind. And we wouldn’t know what happened to them, and we wouldn’t be able to help.” “对，”安妮说。 “但我们得把蹦蹦和剩下的熊猫们留在后面。 我们不知道他们怎么样了，也无法帮助他们。”

Jack stared at the bottle. It was a terrible choice. Then he thought of something. “Remember on our last mission, you said that when we do the right thing, it feels like it somehow helps Penny—even if it seems to take us away from our goal?” 杰克盯着瓶子。 这是一个可怕的决定。 然后他想到了一件事。 “还记得上次任务时，你说当我们做正确的事时，感觉好像以某种方式帮助了彭妮——即使这看起来让我们偏离了目标？”

Annie smiled. “Yep, and everything worked out on that mission, didn’t it?” 安妮微笑了。 “对，那次任务一切都顺利，不是吗？”

Jack nodded. 杰克点了点头。

“Panda center!” they said together. “熊猫中心！”他们异口同声地说。

“You take a sip first,” said Annie. “你先喝一口，”安妮说。

Jack popped the tiny cork out of the bottle. He tilted his head back and took a quick sip. He licked his lips, then held the bottle out to Annie. 杰克把瓶子里的小木塞拔了出来。 他仰起头，快速喝了一口。 他舔了舔嘴唇，然后把瓶子递给了安妮。

Just as Annie took the bottle from Jack, the ground trembled with another aftershock. 就在安妮从杰克手里接过瓶子时，地面又因为余震而颤抖起来。

Jack started to shake. 杰克开始发抖。

“Yikes!” cried Annie. “天哪！”安妮喊道。

The aftershock ended, and the ground stopped trembling. But Jack kept shaking. 余震结束了，地面不再颤抖了。 但杰克还在发抖。

Annie yelled something, but Jack couldn’t understand what she was saying. His body was still quaking. He had started to grow! 安妮大声喊了什么，但杰克听不懂她在说什么。 他的身体还在发抖。 他开始变高了！
