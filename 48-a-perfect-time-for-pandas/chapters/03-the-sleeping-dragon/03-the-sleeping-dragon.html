CHAPTER THREE

The Sleeping Dragon

O h, brother, thought Jack, hurrying after Annie. By the time he entered the bike shop, she was already talking with the man who worked there. “Good news!” Annie said, rushing over to Jack. “He says we can get to the panda center on mountain bikes! And it’s only thirty yuan a day per bike!” 哦，天哪，杰克一边跑一边想，赶紧追上安妮。 等他走进自行车店时，她已经和那里的工作人员聊上了。 “好消息！”安妮一边跑一边对杰克说。 “他说我们可以骑山地自行车去熊猫中心！ 而且每辆自行车每天才三十元！

“Did he say how long it takes to get there?” asked Jack. “他說要多久才能到那裡嗎？”杰克問。

“Not too long,” said Annie. She clapped her hands. Her eyes were shining. “Jack! I can’t believe this!” “不要太久，”安妮说。 她拍了拍手。 她的眼睛闪闪发亮。 “杰克！ 我简直不敢相信！”

Annie was so excited about the panda center that Jack just couldn’t say no. He reached into his pocket and took out three twenty-yuan notes and gave them to the shopkeeper. 安妮对熊猫中心太兴奋了，杰克根本没法拒绝。 他把手伸进口袋，拿出三张二十元的纸币，给了店主。

The man handed Jack and Annie their helmets. “It’s a beautiful ride up the mountain, but be very careful,” he said as Annie put her helmet on. 那个人把头盔递给了杰克和安妮。 “爬山的路很美，但要非常小心，”他说，当安妮戴上头盔时。

“Thanks! See you later!” said Annie. She grabbed a blue mountain bike and steered it out of the store. “谢谢！ 再见啦！“安妮说。 她抓起一辆蓝色的山地自行车，把它从店里开出来。

“Be very careful!” the man called after her again. “一定要非常小心！“那人又在她身后喊道。

Very careful of what? wondered Jack. But tucking his helmet under his arm, he grabbed a red bike and followed Annie. 非常小心什么？杰克心想。 但把头盔夹在胳膊下，他抓起一辆红色的自行车，跟着安妮走了。

“So the bike guy told me to go up this street and turn left onto the highway,” Annie said. “Then we just stay on it until we come to the panda center.” “那个卖自行车的人告诉我，要沿着这条街走，然后左转上高速公路，”安妮说。 “然后我们一直沿着它走，直到到达熊猫中心。

“Cool. How far away is it?” asked Jack as he put on his helmet. He snapped the strap under his chin. “太酷了。 “它有多远？”杰克一边戴上头盔一边问道。 他把头盔的带子系在下巴下面。

“Not far, just five or six miles,” said Annie. “不远，就五到六英里，”安妮说。

“What?” said Jack. “什么？”杰克说。

“Don’t worry, we can do it. Easy.” Annie pushed off and pedaled away. “To panda paradise!” she shouted, zooming up the street. “别担心，我们能行的。 很简单。”安妮蹬上自行车，骑走了。 “去熊猫天堂！”她一边喊着，一边飞快地沿着街道骑了上去。

“Five or six miles,” muttered Jack. “Up a mountain. Great.” He got on his bike and followed Annie. She reached the end of the street and turned left onto the highway. Jack hurried to catch up. As he pedaled up the highway, he glanced at the scenery. “五到六英里，”杰克小声嘟囔着。 “上山。 太棒了。”他骑上自行车，跟在安妮后面。 她走到街的尽头，然后左转上了高速公路。 杰克赶紧跑过去追上。 他骑着车沿着高速公路前进，一边骑一边看着周围的风景。

On one side of the road was a steep mountain slope. It was covered with fir trees and bushes with purple and pink flowers. On the other side of the road was a steep drop down into a river valley. The river sparkled with sunlight as it rushed through green farmland. More cloud-covered mountains loomed in the distance. 公路的一边是一片陡峭的山坡。 山坡上长满了冷杉树，还有开着紫色和粉色花朵的灌木。 公路的另一边是一处陡峭的悬崖，一直延伸到河谷里。 河水在绿色的农田间奔流，阳光照在河面上闪闪发光。 远处还有几座被云雾笼罩的高山若隐若现。

A blue bus suddenly roared past. Jack almost lost his balance. “Good grief!” he said. “That was close!” 一辆蓝色的公交车突然呼啸着从旁边驶过。 杰克差点失去了平衡。 “天哪！”他说道。 “刚才可真险！”

Then a car that looked like a taxi flew by. “This is definitely not safe!” Jack said aloud. How could he have let Annie talk him into riding bikes on a highway? They should have taken a bus or a taxi, or better yet, not gone at all! 然后一辆看起来像出租车的车飞驰而过。 “这 definitely 不安全！”杰克大声说道。 他怎么能听任安妮说服他，在高速公路上骑自行车呢？ 他们应该坐公交车或出租车，或者最好根本就不要去！

“Annie!” Jack shouted. She was so far ahead that he could barely see her. “Annie!” Jack yelled again as loudly as he could. “Slow down! Annie! Slow down!” “安妮！”杰克喊道。 她已经骑得远远的，他几乎看不见她了。 “安妮！”杰克又尽可能大声地喊了一遍。 “慢点！安妮！ 慢点！”

Annie rounded a sharp curve and disappeared from sight. 安妮转过一个急弯，消失在视线之外。

Jack stood up on his pedals and pumped with all his might. He imagined Annie veering into the path of a vehicle or driving off the cliff! 杰克站起来，用尽全力踩着踏板。 他想象着安妮突然转向车辆的行驶路线，或者直接冲下悬崖！

When Jack finally came around the curve, he saw Annie up ahead, waiting beside the road. A big grin lit up her face. “Thank goodness, you’re okay!” she called. “I was worried about you!” 当杰克终于转过弯时，他看见安妮在前方，正站在路边等着。 她脸上露出灿烂的笑容。 “谢天谢地，你没事！”她喊道。 “我刚才很担心你！”

Jack rode to her and stopped his bike. 杰克骑到她身边，停下自行车。

“This road isn’t safe, you know,” said Annie. “你知道这条路不安全，”安妮说。

“You’re telling me?” said Jack, panting. “你是在跟我说吗？”杰克喘着气说。

“But I can understand how that dragon felt. Can’t you?” said Annie. “It’s so beautiful!” “但我能理解那条龙的感受。 你不能吗？”安妮说。 “太美了！”，

Jack caught his breath and looked around. “Yeah, it is,” he said. “But how much farther do we have to go? We can’t forget about our mission.” 杰克停下来喘了口气，环顾四周。 “是的，”他说。 “但是我们还得走多远呢？ 我们不能忘记我们的任务。”

“I don’t think it’s much farther. Come on, we’ll hurry,” said Annie. She climbed back onto her bike. “我觉得不远了。 加油，我们会快点的，”安妮说。 她爬回自行车上。

“I’ll go first,” said Jack. “Let’s stay together and keep as close as we can to the side of the road. I’m serious, Annie.” He gripped his handlebars and started pedaling again. “我先走，”杰克说。 “我们待在一起，尽量靠近路边走。 我是认真的，安妮。”他握紧车把，又开始骑了起来。

Leading the way, Jack hugged the side of the road. He glanced back a couple of times to make sure Annie was doing the same. As they struggled uphill, huffing and puffing, a light rain started to fall. Mist gradually descended over the valley and the highway as they biked higher and higher up the mountain. 杰克带头骑车，紧贴着路边走。 他回头看了好几次，确保安妮也这么做。 当他们努力向上骑，喘着粗气时，开始下起小雨了。 随着他们骑得越来越高，山谷和高速公路渐渐被雾气笼罩。

More vehicles roared by, but Jack tried to ignore them. He couldn’t believe they were biking up a mountain on a Chinese highway in foggy weather! 更多的车辆呼啸而过，但杰克努力不去理会它们。 他简直不敢相信，他们竟然在中国的高速公路上骑车爬山，而且还是在雾天！

Finally Jack and Annie came to a sign that said: 终于，杰克和安妮来到一个写着字的牌子前：

CONSERVATION AND RESEARCH CENTER FOR THE GIANT PANDA 保护与研究中心——大熊猫

“Yay! We’re here!” said Annie. “太好了！” “我们到了！”安妮说。

The rain had stopped. Jack was wet, exhausted, and out of breath. “I hope you’re—you’re happy,” he said. 雨停了。 杰克浑身湿透了，累得气喘吁吁。 “我希望你——你很开心，”他说。

Annie gave Jack a big smile. “I am,” she said. “Totally.” 安妮给杰克一个大大的微笑。 “我很开心，”她说。 “非常开心。”

The panda center was nestled between two tall mountains. It was on the far side of a narrow river. A small bridge crossed the river to the entrance gate. Near the gate a group of grown-ups was waiting to go inside. 熊猫中心坐落在两座高山之间。 它位于一条狭窄河流的对岸。 一座小桥横跨河流，通向入口大门。 靠近大门的地方，一群大人正在等待进入。

Jack and Annie rode to the parking lot and parked their bikes next to a small blue bus. They took off their helmets and hung them from their handlebars. 杰克和安妮骑车来到停车场，把自行车停在一辆小蓝巴士旁边。 他们摘下头盔，挂在车把上。

“Oh, no!” cried Annie. She pointed to the panda center sign. At the bottom it said: “哦，不！”安妮喊道。 她指着熊猫中心的招牌。 在招牌底部写着：

NO ONE UNDER 18 ADMITTED UNLESS ACCOMPANIED BY AN ADULT “18岁以下者未经成人陪同不得进入”

“I don’t believe it,” said Jack. “We came all this way for nothing?” He had to admit he was secretly relieved. Now they could get back to their mission. “我不相信，”杰克说。 “我们一路来到这里，难道是白跑一趟？”他不得不承认，他其实暗暗松了口气。 现在他们可以回到原来的任务上去了。

“I can’t accept this,” said Annie. “We have to get in there.” “我不能接受这个，”安妮说。 “我们必须进去。”

“Your attention, please, everyone!” a woman in a uniform called to the group waiting outside the gate. “请大家注意，”一位穿制服的女士对在门口等待的小组喊道。

“Let’s go listen,” said Annie. She ran across the bridge. “我们去听听吧，”安妮说。 她跑过了桥。

“Annie!” Jack called. He sighed, and then hurried after her. They stood at the back of the little crowd. “安妮！”杰克喊道。 他叹了口气，然后赶紧追了上去。 他们站在小人群的后面。

“My name is Dr. Ling,” the young Chinese woman said to the group. “Welcome to the largest giant panda reserve in the world. This is a place for scientists and animal protectors.” “我的名字是Dr. Ling，”这位年轻的中国女人对大家说。 “欢迎来到世界上最大的大熊猫保护区。 这里是科学家和动物保护者的地方。”

“That’s me,” Annie said, “animal protector.” “那是我，”安妮说，“动物保护者。”

Several people looked at Annie, as if they were wondering who she was. 好几个人看着安妮，好像在想她是谁。

Oh, brother, thought Jack. 哦，天哪，杰克心想。

“Good,” Dr. Ling said to Annie. “Pandas are one of the most endangered species in the world. For at least three million years, they have lived in China’s dense bamboo forests. Now those same forests are being cut down to make room for farms and roads.” “很好，”林博士说。 林博士对安妮说。 “熊猫是世界上最濒危的动物之一。” 至少有三百万年的时间，它们一直生活在中国茂密的竹林中。 现在，这些竹林却被砍伐，用来给农田和道路让路。”

“How many pandas are left?” asked Annie. “还剩多少只熊猫？”安妮问。

“Throughout the world, only about sixteen hundred in the wild,” said Dr. Ling. “Today the species is threatened with extinction.” “全世界野生熊猫大约只剩下一千六百只，”林博士说。 “如今，这种动物正面临灭绝的威胁。”

“That’s terrible!” Annie said. “这太可怕了！”安妮说。

“Shhh,” said Jack. “嘘——，”杰克说。

But Annie didn’t shhh. “Can we save them, Dr. Ling?” she asked. 但安妮没有嘘——。 “我们能救救它们吗，林博士？”她问。

“We hope so. We’re working hard to do just that at the panda center,” said Dr. Ling. “We are responsible for about two hundred pandas here. Many were rescued after an injury or illness. And many more were born here. Last year we had sixteen new babies.” “我们希望如此。 我们在熊猫中心努力做到这一点，”林博士说。 “我们在这里负责照顾大约两百只熊猫。 许多熊猫在受伤或生病后被救了过来。 还有更多熊猫是在这里出生的。 去年我们有十六个新宝宝。

“Wow!” said Annie. “哇！”安妮说。

“While the panda center is not a place for tourists,” said Dr. Ling, “we do allow volunteers to come in and help us. So without further ado, let’s all get suited up!” “虽然熊猫中心不是游客可以去的地方，”博士说。 灵，“我们确实允许志愿者进来帮助我们。 那么，不用再拖延了，让我们都穿上装备吧！”

Annie started walking with the crowd. “Annie, come back,” Jack whispered loudly. 安妮跟着人群开始走路了。 “安妮，回来，”杰克大声地小声说。

But Annie just signaled to Jack to follow her. Then she stepped boldly into the Conservation and Research Center for the Giant Panda. 但安妮只是朝杰克做了个手势，让他跟着她。 然后她大胆地走进了大熊猫保护与研究中心。
