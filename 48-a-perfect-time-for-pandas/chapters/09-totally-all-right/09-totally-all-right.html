9. Totally All Right 9. 完全没问题

“Helicopter?” said Jack. “直升机？”杰克说。

“Yes, I received a call on my cell phone from the military,” said Dr. Ling. “They’re bringing medical supplies and food.” “是的，我接到军方打来的电话，”林博士说。 “他们要带来医疗物资和食物。”

“That’s great,” said Annie. “太好了，”安妮说。

“We need to get our pandas to other reserves,” said Dr. Ling. “Until the road is cleared, we will send some by helicopter to Wolong Town. From there, a van will take them to a safe reserve.” “我们需要把我们的熊猫送到其他保护区，”林博士说。 “直到道路清理完毕，我们会用直升机把一些送到卧龙镇。” 从那里，一辆车会带他们去一个安全的保护区。”

“That sounds like a good plan,” said Jack. “那听起来是个好主意，”杰克说。

Dr. Ling smiled. “I am glad you think so, because I would like to ask you to accompany these three cubs on their first helicopter flight. Do you think your grandmother would mind?” 灵博士笑了。 “我很高兴你这么想，因为我想请你陪同这三个小熊，进行他们第一次的直升机飞行。 你觉得你奶奶会介意吗？

“No problem. We’ve been in choppers before,” Annie said casually. “Antarctica.” “没问题。” “我们以前坐过直升机，”安妮随意地说。 “南极。”

“Really?” said Dr. Ling. She looked surprised. “Well, good. I’ll be right back. I need to speak to the helicopter pilot and make a quick call to our staff in Wolong Town.” “真的吗？”林博士说。 她看起来很惊讶。 “嗯，很好。 我马上回来。 我需要和直升机飞行员谈谈，还要给我们在卧龙镇的工作人员打个电话。”

Dr. Ling started to leave the nursery, then stopped. “Why, I just realized—I don’t even know your names,” she said. 博士。 凌博士开始离开育婴室，然后停了下来。 “哦，我刚刚意识到——我甚至还不知道你们的名字，”她说。

“Jack and Annie,” said Annie. “杰克和安妮，”安妮说。

Dr. Ling nodded. “Thanks, Jack and Annie,” she said, and then left. 凌博士点了点头。 “谢谢你们，杰克和安妮，”她说，然后离开了。

“This is so cool,” Jack said to Annie. “I was wondering how we’d get back to Wolong Town and complete our mission.” “这太酷了，”杰克对安妮说。 “我在想我们怎么才能回到卧龙镇，完成我们的任务。”

Annie smiled at Jack. “Remember—things always seem to work out when we just do the next right thing,” she said. 安妮对杰克笑了笑。 “记住——当我们只是做下一件正确的事时，事情总会顺利解决的，”她说。

“Funny how that works,” said Jack. “真有趣，事情就是这样发生的，”杰克说。

“And this way, we won’t have to leave the cubs yet,” said Annie. She wiped milk off Roly’s mouth with her hand and kissed the top of his head. All three cubs had dropped their bottles and were nodding off to sleep. “这样我们就不需要现在离开小熊们了，”安妮说。 她用手擦掉罗利嘴上的牛奶，然后亲了亲他的头顶。 三个小熊都放下了奶瓶，开始打瞌睡了。

“Can you stay with the kids while I say good-bye to Bing-Bing?” said Jack. “你能陪孩子们一会儿，让我跟冰冰道个别吗？”杰克说。

“Sure,” said Annie. “Say good-bye for me, too.” “当然可以，”安妮说。 “也替我告别。”

“I will.” Jack hurried out the door. He dashed along the path to Bing-Bing’s house, sidestepping rubble and glass. “我会的。”杰克赶紧跑出门口。 他沿着小路冲向蹦蹦的家，一边跑一边躲开碎石和玻璃。

Jack found Master Lee sitting with Bing-Bing on the concrete floor. He was quietly feeding her bits of bread from a small, round loaf. For a moment, the earthquake disaster seemed far away. 杰克发现李师傅正和蹦蹦坐在水泥地上。 他正安静地从一个小小的圆面包里掰下一点面包屑喂她。 有一会儿，地震灾害似乎离得很远。

“Bing-Bing loves a snack,” Master Lee said, smiling at the panda, “even when the world is turned upside down.” “蹦蹦喜欢吃点心，”李师傅微笑着对熊猫说，“即使世界被颠倒过来。”

“I know,” said Jack, kneeling beside them. “I gave her some bamboo in the woods. What’s she eating now?” “我知道，”杰克跪在他们旁边说。 “我在树林里给她了一些竹子。 她现在在吃什么呢？

“It’s a special treat,” said Master Lee. “Here. You can give it to her.” He handed Jack the loaf. “这是特别的礼物，”李师傅说。 “给。” 你可以把它给她。”他把面包递给杰克。

The bread was very hard. Jack struggled to break off a piece. Bing-Bing opened her mouth wide, and Jack placed the bread on her pink tongue. As the giant panda chewed, she stared at Jack with her wise and gentle gaze. 面包非常硬。 杰克努力掰下一块。 蹦蹦张开嘴，杰克把面包放在她粉红色的舌头上面。 当这只大熊猫咀嚼时，她用聪明而温柔的眼神看着杰克。

“I thought she ate bamboo,” said Jack. “我以为她吃竹子，”杰克说。

“She does,” said Master Lee. “This is bamboo, but it’s ground up and mixed with molasses, grains, and vitamins. It’s very healthy. We call it panda bread.” “她吃，”李师傅说。 “这是竹子，但它被磨碎了，和糖浆、谷物和维生素混合在一起。 这非常健康。 我们叫它熊猫面包。

Jack turned his head to look at Master Lee. “And it’s baked with love?” he said. 杰克转过头看向李师傅。 “它是用爱烤制的吗？”, 他说。

“Yes …,” said Master Lee, looking puzzled. “是……,” 李师傅说，看起来很困惑。

Jack gave him a radiant smile. 杰克给了他一个灿烂的微笑。

“Is everything all right?” asked Master Lee. “一切都好吗？”李师傅问。

“Yes. Everything’s all right,” said Jack. “Totally all right.” “是的。 一切都很好，”杰克说。 “完全没问题。”

“Good … I’m glad,” said Master Lee. “It has been a very difficult day.” “好……我很高兴，”李师傅说。 “今天过得非常艰难。”

“It has,” agreed Jack. “Annie and I have to take the cubs to Wolong Town, in a helicopter. Would it be okay if I took a little panda bread with me? To show Annie?” “是啊，”杰克同意道。 “安妮和我得坐直升机，把幼崽送到卧龙镇。 如果我带一点熊猫面包给安妮看看，可以吗？

“Yes, certainly,” said Master Lee. He gave Jack a small hunk of the panda bread. “But don’t try to eat it,” he said. “It’s very—” “可以，当然可以，”李师傅说。 他给了杰克一小块熊猫面包。 “但别试着吃它，”他说。 “它非常——”

“Tough,” finished Jack. “I know—tough as wood.” He put the bread in his backpack. Then he stroked Bing-Bing’s fur. “Bye, beautiful,” he said. He turned to Master Lee. “Bing-Bing is really lucky to have you in her family, Master Lee.” “坚硬，”杰克补充道。 “我知道——像木头一样坚强。”他把面包放进背包里。 然后他抚摸着冰冰的毛发。 “再见，漂亮的小家伙，”他说。 他转向李师傅。 “冰冰能有你这个家人真是太幸运了，李师傅。”

The panda keeper nodded shyly. “Thank you,” he said. 熊猫饲养员害羞地点了点头。 “谢谢，”他说。

Jack and the giant panda exchanged one last look, then Jack hurried outside. 杰克和大熊猫交换了最后一眼，然后杰克赶紧跑出去了。

Leaping over the rubble, Jack ran back to the nursery. On his way, he saw that the helicopter had landed on the kindergarten playground. Soldiers in green-and-tan uniforms were unloading boxes. 跳过废墟，杰克跑回育婴室。 在路上，他看到直升机已经降落在幼儿园的操场上。 穿着绿棕色制服的士兵们正在卸下箱子。

Jack found Dr. Ling and Annie waiting for him. They were holding Roly and Poly. “Jack, if you take the smallest cub, I will help you all get settled in the helicopter,” said Dr. Ling. 杰克找到了林博士。 林博士和安妮正在等他。 他们手里拿着罗利和波利。 “杰克，如果你带走最小的幼崽，我会帮助你们所有人顺利登上直升机，”林博士说。

“Sure!” Jack picked up Little Guy. The cub was drowsy and cuddly. “当然！”杰克抱起了小家伙。 小熊宝宝困了，软绵绵的。

“Let’s go!” said Dr. Ling. “我们走吧！”林博士说。

Dr. Ling, Jack, and Annie left the nursery and carried the little pandas outside. Night was falling as they stood in the playground and watched the soldiers finish unloading food and medicine. 博士 林博士、杰克和安妮离开了育婴室，把小熊猫们抱到了外面。 天黑了，他们站在操场上看士兵们把食物和药品卸下来。

“Until you can get all the pandas out of here, how will you take care of them?” asked Annie. “在你们把所有熊猫都带出这里之前，怎么照顾它们呢？”安妮问。

“We will set up tents and carry water from the stream and get by as best we can,” said Dr. Ling. “I believe people here in China and around the world will help us. I’ve already had a call from Pandas International. They promised to send powdered milk for the young ones, and they will write about our situation and raise money to help the pandas.” “我们会搭帐篷，从溪流里取水，尽我们最大的努力活下去，”林博士说。 “我相信，中国和世界各地的人们都会帮助我们。 我已经给熊猫国际打过电话了。 他们承诺会给小熊猫送奶粉，还会写文章介绍我们的情况，为熊猫筹钱。”

“Cool,” said Jack. “Hey, in that case …” He reached into his pocket and pulled out the rest of their money. “We’d like to give something for the pandas, too.” He handed their Chinese currency to Dr. Ling. “太酷了，”杰克说。 “嘿，这样的话……”他把手伸进口袋，拿出他们剩下的钱。 “我们也想为熊猫做点什么。”他把他们的人民币递给林博士。

“Thank you!” she said. “All of this province will need help in the days to come. I have heard from the staff in Chengdu that this has been one of the most damaging earthquakes of all time. They think many people have been killed. Whole towns have been destroyed.” “谢谢！”她说。 “这个地方在接下来的日子里都需要帮助。 我从成都的工作人员那里听说，这可能是有史以来破坏最严重的地震之一。 他们认为很多人已经被杀死了。 整个城镇都被毁掉了。

“That’s terrible,” said Jack. “太可怕了，”杰克说。

“What about your family?” said Annie. “你家人都好吗？”安妮说。

“My parents are safe in Beijing,” said Dr. Ling. “But in the face of this disaster, everyone in our nation is one big family now. Oh, look, the pilot is waving to us.” “我父母现在在北京很安全，”林博士说。 “但是面对这场灾难，我们国家的每个人现在都像一个大家庭。” 哦，快看，飞行员在向我们挥手。”

In the twilight, Jack, Annie, and Dr. Ling each carried a cub to the helicopter. They climbed up the steps and entered the small cabin. Jack and Annie sat down and buckled their seat belts. Dr. Ling handed Roly to Annie. Annie hugged Roly and Poly while Jack cradled Little Guy in his arms. 在暮色中，杰克、安妮和博士 灵每人都抱着一只小熊猫走向直升机。 他们爬上台阶，走进了小舱室。 杰克和安妮坐下来，系好了安全带。博士。 灵把罗利交给安妮。 安妮一边抱着罗利和波利，一边杰克把小家伙抱在怀里。

“You will need this,” said Dr. Ling. She pulled a small flashlight from her pocket and gave it to Annie. “It will be dark when you arrive. Wolong Town is without power, too.” “你们会需要这个，”博士灵说。 她从口袋里拿出一个小手电筒，给了安妮。 “你们到达时会很黑的。 卧龙镇也没有电了。”

“Thanks,” said Jack. “谢谢，”杰克说。

“When you land, staff members from our office in Wolong Town will be waiting for you,” said Dr. Ling. “Have a safe trip, and thank you for everything. Good-bye, Jack and Annie.” “你们落地后，我们沃尔龙镇办公室的工作人员会来接你们，”林博士说。 “祝你们一路平安，感谢你们做的一切。 再见了，杰克和安妮。”

“Good luck rebuilding the panda center,” said Jack. “It’s an amazing place.” “祝你们好运，重建熊猫中心，”杰克说。 “这是一个非常棒的地方。”

Dr. Ling climbed out of the helicopter and waved. The military pilot hopped aboard. “The ride will be very noisy,” he told Jack and Annie, putting headphones over their ears. Then he took his seat. The two soldiers sat behind him. 林 博士从直升机里爬出来，挥手示意。 军人飞行员跳上了直升机。 “飞行过程中会很吵，”他对杰克和安妮说，把耳机戴在他们的耳朵上。 然后他坐到了自己的座位上。 两个士兵坐在他后面。

A moment later, the engine roared and the blades started to spin. The helicopter shook; then it lifted off the ground and buzzed away from the panda center, through the darkening sky. 过了一会儿，引擎轰鸣起来，螺旋桨开始转动。 直升机摇晃了一下；然后它离开了地面，嗡嗡地飞离了熊猫中心，穿过逐渐变暗的天空。
