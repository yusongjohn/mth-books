CHAPTER TEN

Panda Bread

10. Panda Bread 10. 熊猫面包

The helicopter flew over the broken bridge, over the blocked highway, and over the crumbling mountains of the Wolong Reserve. Soon it roared over Wolong Town. 直升机飞过了被毁坏的桥，飞过了被堵住的高速公路，飞过了卧龙保护区崩塌的山。 不久后，它轰鸣着飞过了卧龙镇。

The pilot landed in a large field lit by emergency spotlights. Two other helicopters were also in the field. Tents had been set up, and people were carrying the injured on stretchers to the helicopters. 飞行员降落在一片被应急探照灯照亮的大田里。 还有两架其他直升机也在田里。 搭起了帐篷，人们正用担架把伤者抬到直升机上。

When the blades completely stopped turning, Jack and Annie carried the sleeping cubs out of the cabin and down the steps to the field. 当扇叶完全停止转动时，杰克和安妮把睡着的小熊猫从小木屋里抱出来，沿着台阶走到田野里。

“Jack! Annie!” someone called. Four women dressed in dark coveralls rushed to greet them. “We work for the panda center. Thank you for bringing the cubs to us!” one said. “杰克！ “安妮！”有人喊道。 四个穿着深色工装服的妇女急忙跑过来迎接他们。 “我们是熊猫中心的工作人员。 谢谢你们把小熊猫带来给我们！”其中一个人说。

Annie kissed each of the little pandas. “Be good now,” she said. 安妮亲了每个小熊猫一下。 “现在要乖哦，”她说。

Then she and Jack handed them over to the women. “We really love them,” said Annie. 然后她和杰克把小熊猫交给了那些妇女。 “我们真的很喜欢它们，”安妮说。

“I am so glad,” said one of the women. “We will get them to a safe new home very soon. Later the others will join them there.” “我太高兴了，”其中一位女士说道。 “我们很快就能让他们住进一个安全的新家。 之后其他人也会去那里和他们会合。”

“We must help you, too,” said another woman. “Go with Mrs. Chang to our office, and she will locate your grandmother.” “我们也必须帮助你们，”另一位女士说道。 “和Mrs. 去我们的办公室，她会找到你的祖母。”

“Oh, that’s okay …,” said Jack. “We know exactly where she is.” He started to back up. “哦，那没关系……，”杰克说。 “我们知道她确切的位置。”他开始往后退。

Annie did the same. “That’s right,” she said. She pointed to the street. “We’re going to meet her in a restaurant in town.” 安妮也这样做。 “没错，”她说。 她指着街道说。 “我们要在镇上的一家餐厅里见她。”

“Good-bye,” said Jack. “再见，”杰克说。

“Good-bye,” said Annie. “再见，”安妮说。

Before any of the women could speak, Jack grabbed Annie’s hand and started to run. They ran across the busy field, weaving around the tents and helicopters, doctors, nurses, and military people. When they came to the street, they stopped to catch their breath. 在任何一位女士说话之前，杰克抓住了安妮的手，开始跑起来。 他们穿过繁忙的田野，绕过帐篷、直升机、医生、护士和军人。 当他们来到街道上时，他们停下来喘口气。

“Ready to go home?” asked Jack. “准备回家了吗？”杰克问。

“No, wait,” said Annie. “We still have to find the special food.” “不，等等，”安妮说。 “我们还得找到那种特别的食物。”

“No, actually, we don’t,” said Jack. “不，其实我们不用，”杰克说。

“What?” said Annie. “什么？”安妮说。

“I said we don’t have to look for the special food anymore,” said Jack. “我说我们不用再找那种特别的食物了，”杰克说。

“But why? What do you mean?” said Annie. “但是为什么？ 你是什么意思？”安妮说。

“We … don’t … have … to … look … for … it,” Jack said. “我们……不……用……再……找……它了，”杰克说。

Annie stared at him. “Are you saying we have it?” she asked. 安妮盯着他看。 “你是说我们已经有了吗？”她问。

Jack nodded, then pulled the hunk of panda bread out of his backpack. “It’s called panda bread! When I went to say good-bye to Bing-Bing, Master Lee was feeding it to her.” 杰克点点头，然后从背包里拿出一块熊猫面包。 “它叫熊猫面包！ 当我去跟蹦蹦道别时，李老师正在喂它吃面包。”

“But what makes you think—” began Annie. “但是你怎么会想—”安妮说道。

Jack held up his hand, interrupting her. “Panda bread is a healthy food,” he said. “It’s grainy and good, baked with love, tough as wood, round in shape, and the color of sand. And it’s given to those who have lost their land—the pandas at the center!” 杰克举起手，打断了她的话。 “熊猫面包是一种健康的食物，”他说。 “它粗糙又美味，充满爱意地烤制，像木头一样坚硬，形状圆圆的，颜色像沙子。 而且它只送给那些失去家园的人——动物园里的熊猫！”

“Perfect!” breathed Annie. “太棒了！”安妮轻声说。

Jack held up the panda bread and looked at it. “This is why I think pandas will survive,” he said. 杰克举起熊猫面包，仔细看着它。 “这就是为什么我认为熊猫会存活下来，”他说。

“Why?” asked Annie. “Because of panda bread?” “为什么？”安妮问。 “是因为熊猫面包吗？”

“Nope,” said Jack. He put the bread into his pack. “I think they’ll survive because people take responsibility for them. People make healthy bread for them and carry them to safety and raise money for them. And people speak up for them because they can’t speak up for themselves.” “不是，”杰克说。 他把面包放进背包里。 “我想它们会活下来的，因为人们会为它们负责。 人们为它们做健康的面包，把它们带到安全的地方，还为它们筹钱。 而且人们会为它们发声，因为它们自己无法发声。”

“That’s it,” said Annie. “That’s what panda bread stands for! It stands for people protecting and saving animals.” “就是这样，”安妮说。 “这就是熊猫面包的意义！ 它代表人们保护和拯救动物。”

“Exactly,” said Jack. “And now we have to go save Penny.” “没错，”杰克说。 “现在我们必须去救彭妮了。”

“Great,” said Annie. “I think the tree house is nearby.” She looked around. “Yes! It’s there, in that line of trees. See it?” “太好了，”安妮说。 “我觉得树屋就在附近。”她环顾四周。 “对！ 就在那排树后面，看见了吗？”

“Yep,” said Jack. “Let’s go!” “看见了，”杰克说。 “我们走吧！”

When they came to the grove of trees, Jack switched on their small flashlight. The beam of light guided them to the rope ladder hanging in the dark. 当他们来到那片树林时，杰克打开了他们的小手电筒。 光束引导他们来到黑暗中挂着的绳梯前。

“Wait! Our volunteer clothes!” said Annie. “等一下！ “我们的志愿者衣服！”安妮说道。

They took off their coveralls and left them at the bottom of the tree. Then they climbed up the rope ladder into the tree house. 他们脱下工作服，把它们留在树的底部。 然后他们沿着绳梯爬进树屋里。

“Is everything still here?” asked Annie. “一切还在吗？”安妮问。

Jack shined the flashlight into a corner of the tree house. The light fell on the emerald rose, the white and yellow flower, and the gray goose feather. “It’s all here,” he said. 杰克用手电筒照向树屋的一个角落。 光线落在翡翠玫瑰、白色和黄色的花，以及灰色的鹅毛上。 “一切都在这里，”他说。

“Add the fourth thing,” said Annie. “再添第四样东西，”安妮说。

Jack pulled out the panda bread from his backpack and placed it on the floor next to the feather. 杰克从背包里拿出熊猫面包，把它放在羽毛旁边的地面上。

“Good,” said Annie. “很好，”安妮说。

Roaring sounds filled the night. Jack and Annie looked out the window and saw another helicopter coming in. 夜晚响起了轰隆隆的声音。 杰克和安妮看向窗外，看到另一架直升机飞了过来。

“Remember our helicopter ride in Antarctica?” said Annie. “When you hid Penny in your jacket?” “你还记得我们在南极乘坐直升机的经历吗？”安妮说。 “就是你把小便便藏在夹克里的那次？”

“I totally remember it,” said Jack. “We couldn’t let anyone see her and she kept peeping, so I had to keep pretending to cough.” He thought about the penguin’s big eyes and her fuzzy little head. He thought about Teddy and Kathleen and the wonder and beauty of Camelot. Suddenly he couldn’t wait to be there and help bring Penny back to life. “Are you ready to go to Camelot?” he asked Annie. “我完全记得，”杰克说。 “我们不能让任何人看到她，而且她一直发出声音，所以我只好一直假装咳嗽。”他想起了企鹅那双大眼睛和她毛茸茸的小脑袋。 他想起了泰迪和凯瑟琳，还有卡梅洛特的奇妙与美丽。 突然间，他迫不及待想去那里，帮助把小便便救回来。 “你准备好去卡梅洛特了吗？”他问安妮。

“Absolutely!” she said. “当然！”她说。

Jack reached into his backpack again and took out their note from Teddy and Kathleen. He pointed to the word Camelot. “I wish we could go there, ” he said. 杰克又把手伸进他的背包，拿出泰迪和凯瑟琳给他们的便条。 他指着上面的卡梅洛特这个词。 “我希望我们能去那里，”他说。

A blast of light— 一道强光—

a roar of wind— 一阵狂风—

a rumble of thunder— 一声雷响—

and all was quiet. 然后一切归于平静。
