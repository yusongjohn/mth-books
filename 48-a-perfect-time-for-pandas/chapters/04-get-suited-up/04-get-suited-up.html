CHAPTER FOUR

Get Suited Up! 第四章穿好装备！

“I don’t believe her!” Jack grumbled to himself. He had no choice but to follow Annie into the panda center. When he walked through the gate, he saw a group of low buildings with tree-covered mountains looming behind them. “我不信她！”杰克小声嘟囔着。 他别无选择，只能跟着安妮走进熊猫中心。 当他穿过大门时，他看到一排低矮的建筑，背后是覆盖着树木的高山。

As Dr. Ling led the group along a stone path, Jack saw Annie talking to an elegant older woman with snow-white hair. He quickly caught up with them. He pulled on Annie’s sleeve, but she kept talking to the woman. “I’ve always loved them,” Annie said. “You too?” 当林博士 带领大家沿着石板路走时，杰克看到安妮正和一位头发雪白、优雅的老妇人交谈。 他很快就追上了他们。 他拉了拉安妮的袖子，但安妮还在和那个女人说话。 “我一直都很喜欢它们，”安妮说。 “你也是吗？”

“My goodness, yes,” the woman said. “I’ve come all by myself from New York City to see them.” “天哪，是的，”那个女人说。 “我一个人从纽约市赶来就是为了看它们。”

“That’s great,” said Annie. “We’ve come all the way from Pennsylvania! What’s your name?” “太棒了，”安妮说。 “我们是从宾夕法尼亚州一路赶来的！ 你叫什么名字？

“Sylvia,” the woman answered. “And yours?” “西尔维亚，”那个女人回答。 “那你的呢？”

“I’m Annie. This is my brother, Jack. Do you mind if we tag along with you, Sylvia?” said Annie. “我是安妮。” 这是我的哥哥，杰克。 安妮问道：「我们能和你一起去吗，西尔维亚？」

“Of course not,” said Sylvia. “I’d be delighted.” 西尔维亚说：「当然不介意。」 「我非常乐意。」

“Great.” Annie smiled at Jack. 「太好了。」安妮对杰克笑了笑。

Dr. Ling led the group into one of the buildings and gave each person canvas coveralls, paper shoes, and rubber gloves. “All volunteers must wear these over their clothes,” she said. “That way, we’ll know you’re here to help us.” 博士。 凌博士带领大家走进了一栋建筑，给每个人发了帆布工装裤、纸鞋和橡胶手套。 她说道：「所有志愿者都必须在衣服外面穿上这些。」 “这样，我们就知道你是来帮我们的。”

“We’re going to get caught,” Jack whispered to Annie. “我们会被抓到的，”杰克对安妮小声说。

“Don’t worry,” she said. “We actually are with an adult now.” “别担心，”她说。 “其实我们现在和一个大人在一起。”

Jack shook his head. But he took off his pack, and he and Annie each pulled on large, bulky coveralls. They rolled up their sleeves and pant legs. Then they slid the paper shoes over their sneakers and tugged on the rubber gloves. 杰克摇了摇头。 但他取下了背包，他和安妮各自穿上了一件又大又笨重的防护服。 他们卷起了袖子和裤腿。 然后他们把纸鞋套在运动鞋上，又戴上了橡胶手套。

“We’ll all go into the nursery first,” said Dr. Ling. “我们先都去育婴室，”林博士说。

Annie grabbed Jack’s arm. She looked like she might explode with happiness. 安妮抓住了杰克的手臂。 她看起来高兴得快要爆炸了。

Dr. Ling opened a door at the back of the room and herded everyone into the panda nursery. “We have only one newborn panda cub now. As you walk by the incubator, take a quick peek.” 博士。 玲在房间后面打开一扇门，把大家都赶进了熊猫育婴室。 “我们现在只有一只刚出生的熊猫宝宝。 你们走过孵化器时，快偷偷看一眼。”

The group paraded slowly past the incubator. Almost everyone who peeked at the baby murmured with surprise. Jack quickly found out why—the newborn didn’t look like a panda at all. It looked more like a fuzzy pink mouse. 大家慢慢地排着队走过孵化器。 几乎每个人都偷偷看了一眼宝宝，惊讶地小声说了几句。 杰克很快就明白了原因——这只新生的宝宝看起来一点都不像熊猫。 它看起来更像一只毛茸茸的粉红色小老鼠。

“Incredible,” said Jack. “太不可思议了，”杰克说。

“It’s so teeny!” whispered Annie. “它好小呀！”安妮小声说。

“Yes,” Sylvia whispered back. “I’ve read that newborn pandas weigh less than half a pound. But they can grow into two-hundred-and-fifty-pound bears.” “是的，”西尔维亚小声回答。 “我读到过，刚出生的熊猫体重不到半磅。 但它们可以长成重达250磅的熊。”

“Whoa,” whispered Jack. He and Annie walked with Sylvia out of the nursery. “哇，”杰克小声说。 他和安妮跟着西尔维亚走出了育婴室。

“Now let’s go meet some of our panda keepers!” said Dr. Ling. She led the group over a stone walkway beside bamboo woods. Near a goldfish pond, several men in blue coveralls were waiting for them. “现在我们去见见我们的熊猫饲养员吧！”林博士说。 她带领大家走过竹林旁边的石板路。 在金鱼池附近，几位穿着蓝色工装裤的男士正在等他们。

“These gentlemen are the panda keepers,” said Dr. Ling. “You volunteers will work in pairs in the different panda houses. Each pair will spend a little time helping take care of a panda.” She pointed to Jack and Annie. “You two go with Master Lee. He’s Bing-Bing’s keeper. He’ll take you to her house. I hope your grandmother doesn’t mind.” “这些男士是熊猫饲养员，”林博士说。 “你们这些志愿者将两人一组，在不同的熊猫馆里工作。” 每一对都会花一点时间帮忙照顾一只熊猫。”她指着杰克和安妮。 “你们两个和李师傅一起去。 他是冰冰的饲养员。 他会带你们去她的家。 我希望你奶奶不会介意。”

Sylvia smiled. “ Oh! I’m not—” she started to say. 西尔维亚笑了。 我不是—”她开始说。

“Bye, Grandma!” Annie said with a laugh. “再见，奶奶！”安妮笑着说道。

Sylvia laughed, too, as if she thought Annie was making a joke, and she waved good-bye. 西尔维亚也笑了，好像觉得安妮在开玩笑，然后她挥手告别。

“See you later!” Annie called. Then she grabbed Jack by the arm again and pulled him along after Master Lee. “再见啦！”安妮喊道。 然后她又抓住杰克的手臂，把他拉到李师傅后面跟着走。

Master Lee was quiet and very serious-looking. Without a word, he led Jack and Annie to Bing-Bing’s house. The panda’s house was a giant cage with a door that opened into a yard. The yard was surrounded by a rock wall. 李师傅很安静，看起来非常严肃。 他没有说话，就带着杰克和安妮走向宾宾的家。 熊猫的家是一个巨大的笼子，门打开后通向一个院子。 院子被一块岩石墙围了起来。

“Bing-Bing lives by herself here,” Master Lee said. “She is an eight-year-old adult who joined us when she was quite small.” “宾宾就住在这里，”李师傅说。 “她是一个八岁的成年人，很小的时候就加入了我们。”

When they entered the cage, Jack saw leaves and bamboo stalks scattered across the concrete floor. But there was no panda inside. 当他们走进笼子时，杰克看到水泥地上散落着树叶和竹子。 但里面没有熊猫。

“Where’s Bing-Bing now?” said Annie. “宾宾现在在哪里？”安妮说。

“Somewhere in her yard,” said Master Lee. He grabbed two brooms from the corner. “在她家院子里的某个地方，”李师傅说。 他从角落里拿起了两把扫帚。

Jack and Annie looked through the bars at the yard. They saw trees and bushes, but no sign of a giant panda. 杰克和安妮透过栏杆看向院子里。 他们看到了树木和灌木，但没有看到一只大熊猫。

“She must have found a good hiding place,” said Annie. “她一定找到了一个很好的藏身之处，”安妮说。

“Yes. I do not think you will see her today,” Master Lee said matter-of-factly. “是的。 我想你今天不会看到她了，”李师傅不慌不忙地说。

“Oh, no,” said Annie. “哦，不，”安妮说。

“Can we look for her?” asked Jack. “我们可以去找她吗？”杰克问。

“No, Bing-Bing is very shy. We have to respect that,” said Master Lee. “I am going to get fresh bamboo now. You can clean her house by removing yesterday’s uneaten bamboo stalks and sweeping the floor.” He handed brooms to Jack and Annie. “不，蹦蹦很害羞。” “我们必须尊重这一点，”李师傅说。 “我现在要去摘新鲜的竹子。” 你可以打扫她的房子，把昨天没吃完的竹子去掉，然后扫地。” 他把扫帚递给了杰克和安妮。

“Is bamboo Bing-Bing’s favorite food?” asked Jack. “竹子是冰冰最喜欢吃的食物吗？” 杰克问。

“Yes, all pandas eat bamboo for breakfast, lunch, and dinner,” said Master Lee. “是的，熊猫早餐、午餐和晚餐都吃竹子，”李师傅说。

“Wow, they must really love it,” said Annie. “哇，他们一定特别喜欢竹子，”安妮说。

“Can humans eat bamboo, too?” Jack asked. “人类也能吃竹子吗？”杰克问。

“If they tried, they would break their teeth,” Master Lee answered without smiling. “Only a creature with superstrong jaws can eat bamboo.” “如果他们试一下，牙齿肯定会断，”李师傅不带笑地回答。 “只有拥有超级强壮下颚的生物才能吃竹子。”

“So it’s very tough,” said Jack, growing excited. “Is it as tough as wood?” “所以它非常坚硬，”杰克说着，越来越兴奋了。 “它和木头一样坚硬吗？”

“Well, yes …,” said Master Lee. “嗯，是的……，”李师傅说。

“And I’ll bet it’s really healthy, too!” said Annie. “而且我敢打赌它一定非常健康！”，安妮说。

“Uh, no …,” said Master Lee. “Bamboo is not very nutritious. That’s why a panda must eat a lot of it. Some pandas at the center eat eighty pounds a day.” “呃，不……，”李师傅说。 “竹子的营养不太高。” 这就是为什么熊猫必须吃很多竹子。 中心的一些熊猫一天能吃八十磅呢。”

“Oh, okay,” said Annie, her shoulders sagging. “And I guess it’s not baked with love, either. Is it?” “哦，好吧，”安妮说，肩膀垂了下来。 “而且我想它应该也没用爱来烘烤吧。是吗？”

Master Lee stared at Annie. He looked confused. “No … of course not,” he said. 李师傅盯着安妮看。 他看起来很困惑。 “不……当然不是，”他说。

“Darn,” said Annie. “真糟糕，”安妮说。

Jack was embarrassed. Like the waiter in the restaurant, Master Lee must think we’re really weird, he thought. 杰克感到很尴尬。 就像餐厅里的服务员一样，李师傅一定觉得我们真的很奇怪，他想。

“Well!” said Master Lee. “Shall we get to work now?” “好吧！”，李师傅说。 “我们现在开始工作吧？”，

“Sure,” said Annie. “当然可以，”安妮说。

“Sweep up the stalks, as well as the panda waste,” said Master Lee. “Then discard everything there.” He pointed to a trash bin in the cage. “把秸秆和熊猫的粪便都打扫干净，”李师傅说。 “然后把那里的一切都扔掉。”他指着笼子里的垃圾桶说。

“Panda waste?” said Jack. “熊猫的垃圾？”杰克说。

“Yes,” said Master Lee. Seeing Jack’s expression, he added, “It’s not bad, I promise you. Their droppings look like little dry straw baskets.” “是的，”李师傅说。 看到杰克的表情，他又补充道，“我向你保证，这不坏。 它们的粪便看起来像小小的干草篮子。

Jack looked around. He saw what he thought Master Lee was talking about. It didn’t look that bad. 杰克环顾四周。 他看到了他以为李师傅在说的东西。 看起来好像没那么糟糕。

“I will be back,” said Master Lee. “I am going to get fresh bamboo from the farmer’s truck.” Master Lee then left through the door at the back of the cage. “我会回来的，”李师傅说。 “我要去农民的卡车那儿拿新鲜的竹子。”李师傅说完，从笼子后面门走了出去。

“Darn,” said Jack, “we struck out.” “真糟糕，”杰克说，“我们失败了。”

“No special food here,” said Annie. She looked out in the yard. “And no panda here, either. I really want to see Bing-Bing!” “这里没有特别的食物，”安妮说。 她朝院子里看去。 “这里也没有熊猫， 我真的好想看看兵兵！”

“How did this happen?” said Jack. “We should be working on our mission. Not stuck in a cage picking up panda poop.” “这是怎么发生的？”杰克说。 “我们应该在完成我们的任务， 而不是被困在笼子里捡熊猫的粪便。”

Annie giggled. “Don’t worry, we’ll leave soon,” she said. “Let’s just do our job first. It’s nice to help out here.” 安妮咯咯地笑了。 “别担心，我们很快就走，”她说。 “我们先完成我们的任务吧。” 在这里帮忙真开心。

“Yeah, sure,” said Jack. “Sweep fast.” “嗯，好吧，”杰克说。 “快扫。”

Jack and Annie began sweeping up old bamboo stalks and panda droppings. Jack filled a dustpan. As he dumped everything into the trash bin, Annie gasped. 杰克和安妮开始扫起旧的竹子和熊猫粪便。 杰克把扫帚装满了灰尘。 当他把所有东西倒进垃圾桶时，安妮惊叫起来。

“I see her!” she whispered. “She’s up in a tree!” “我看到她了！”，她小声说。 “她在树上！”，
