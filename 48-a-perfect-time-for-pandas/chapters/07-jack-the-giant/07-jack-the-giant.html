CHAPTER SEVEN

Jack the Giant

7. Jack the Giant 7. 巨人杰克

Jack felt as if someone was gripping his head—and pulling him up, and up, and up! He looked down and watched his arms and hands stretch out. His legs grew longer, his feet bigger. 杰克感觉好像有人抓住了他的头——把他往上拉，往上拉，往上拉！ 他低头看去，看到自己的手臂和手正在伸长。 他的腿变得越来越长，脚也变得越来越大。

Jack rocked back and forth, then stood still. He had stopped stretching and growing. His shoes, clothes, and backpack had all grown bigger to fit his new body. 杰克前后摇晃了一下，然后站住了。 他停止了伸展和生长。 他的鞋子、衣服和背包都变大了，刚好适合他新的身体。

“Jack!” Annie’s voice sounded distant. “杰克！”安妮的声音听起来很远。

Peering through the dust, Jack looked around for Annie. 杰克透过灰尘四处张望，寻找安妮。

“Jack! Down here!” “杰克！在这下面！”

Jack looked down. Annie was standing next to him. She only came up to his knees! “I dropped the bottle and it broke!” she said. “I didn’t get to drink the potion!” 杰克低头看去。 安妮正站在他旁边。 她才到他的膝盖那么高！ “我把瓶子掉了，它碎了！”她说。 “我没喝到魔药！”

“Oh, no!” Jack boomed. Even his voice was bigger. “I’m so sorry.” “哦，不！”杰克大声说道。 就连他的声音都变得更大了。 “对不起。”

“You’re huge!” said Annie. “How does it feel? Is it fun?” “你变得好大呀！”安妮说。 “感觉怎么样？好玩吗？”

“Not yet,” said Jack. “还没呢，”杰克说。

The earth trembled again with another aftershock. Annie lost her balance and fell. Jack leaned over and picked her up with both hands. He placed her on one of his giant shoulders. 大地再次因余震而颤抖。 安妮失去平衡，摔倒了。 杰克弯下腰，用两只手把她抱了起来。 他把她放在自己巨大的肩膀上。

“Whoa,” said Annie. “This is cool! Now I’m taller than you! I can be your lookout.” “哇，”安妮说。 “这太酷了！ 现在我比你高了！ 我可以当你的瞭望员。”

From their new height, Jack and Annie could see over the rubble to the vast devastation. Trees down in the valley had been uprooted. The river was wild and raging. The mountaintops were shrouded in dark clouds. 从他们新的高度，杰克和安妮可以看到废墟之外，那片巨大的破坏景象。 山谷里的树木都被连根拔起。 河水狂野地翻滚着。 山顶被浓浓的乌云笼罩着。

“It’s starting to rain again,” said Annie. “又要开始下雨了，”安妮说。

“That’s the least of our problems,” said Jack. “I just hope the earthquake didn’t destroy the panda center.” “这是我们遇到的最不严重的问题，”杰克说。 “我只是希望地震没有毁掉熊猫中心。”

“I know! Let’s hurry! We only have an hour!” said Annie. “我知道！我们快点走！ 我们只剩下一个小时了！”安妮说。

Annie held on to Jack’s head, and Jack held on to Annie’s legs. He lifted one giant foot and stepped over the boulder that had crushed their bikes. Picking his way through the rubble, he headed back to the panda center. He stepped over fallen debris, power lines, and deep cracks in the road. His enormous sneakers crushed twigs and brush. 安妮抓住了杰克的头，杰克抓住了安妮的腿。 他抬起一只巨大的脚，跨过了压碎他们自行车的石头。 他小心地穿过废墟，朝熊猫中心走去。 他跨过了倒塌的碎片、电线和道路上深深的裂缝。 他巨大的运动鞋踩碎了树枝和杂草。

Jack stepped over huge mounds of mud as if they were anthills. He kicked away boulders as if they were soccer balls. He tossed aside fallen trees as if they were broken branches. He leapt over a river of water coursing down from the mountain as if it were a rain puddle. 杰克跨过了巨大的泥堆，就好像那是蚂蚁山一样。 他踢开石头，就像它们是足球一样。 他把倒下的树扔到一边，就像它们是折断的树枝一样。 他跳过从山上流下来的河水，就像那是水坑一样。

“This is incredible!” said Jack. “这太神奇了！”杰克说。

“Watch out!” said Annie. A boulder was rolling down the wet road toward them. “小心！”安妮说。 一块石头正沿着湿漉漉的路朝他们滚来。

Jack spread his legs wide, and the boulder went between them and kept rolling downhill. 杰克张开双腿，石头从他们中间滚过，继续往山下滚去。

Jack and Annie laughed. “Now are you having fun?” she said. 杰克和安妮笑了起来。 “现在你玩得开心了吗？”她说。

Jack nodded. “Maybe a little bit.” 杰克点了点头。 “可能有点吧。”

Suddenly a tree crashed across the road. Jack stumbled over it. He fell to the ground just as a wall of mud came cascading down the mountain slope. The black ooze was filled with rock fragments and plants. 突然，一棵树横跨在了路上。 杰克被它绊了一下。 就在泥墙从山坡上倾泻而下的时候，他摔倒在了地上。 黑色的泥浆里混着碎石和植物。

Annie! thought Jack. He lifted his head out of the mud before it smothered him. He reached around and felt Annie’s kicking feet. He pulled her out of the thick, wet goo. 安妮！杰克心想。 他把头从泥浆里抬起来，以免被它埋住。 他伸手一摸，感觉到了安妮踢动的脚。 他把她从又厚又湿的泥浆里拉了出来。

“You okay?” Jack shouted. “你没事吧？”杰克喊道。

“Yes!” sputtered Annie. “But we’re sliding over the cliff!” “是的！”安妮结结巴巴地说。 “但我们正在滑下悬崖！”

Annie was right. With the force of a tidal wave, the mudslide was pushing them across the highway toward the cliff! 安妮说得对。 带着海啸般的巨大力量，泥石流正推着他们穿过公路，朝悬崖冲去！

Jack clutched Annie under one of his huge arms. Then he struggled through the grimy ooze until they were clear of the mudslide. Covered with mud from his hair to his shoes, Jack felt heavy and uncomfortable. He even had mud in his mouth. He put Annie down and sat on the road. 杰克用他强壮的一只手臂紧紧搂住安妮。 然后他挣扎着穿过肮脏的泥浆，直到他们脱离了泥石流。 从头发到鞋子，杰克全身都沾满了泥，他感觉又重又不舒服。 他嘴里甚至也有泥。 他把安妮放下来，坐在了路上。

“At least we’re—we’re safe,” Annie said. “至少——至少我们安全了，”安妮说。

Jack coughed, gagging on mud. 杰克咳嗽起来，被泥巴呛得直恶心。

“You look like a giant swamp monster,” Annie said. “你看起来像个巨大的沼泽怪物，”安妮说。

Jack couldn’t talk. As the rain fell harder, he threw back his head and let rainwater wash his face and fill his mouth. He choked and spit and coughed until his throat was clear. With help from the pelting rain, he washed the mud off his bare arms and his shirt, jeans, and sneakers. Finally he looked at Annie. 杰克说不出话来。 雨下得更大了，他仰起头，让雨水冲刷他的脸，流进他的嘴里。 他呛得咳嗽，吐出泥巴，直到喉咙通畅了。 在暴雨的帮助下，他洗掉了自己光着的胳膊上的泥巴，还有他的衬衫、牛仔裤和运动鞋上的泥巴。 最后，他看着安妮。

“How long do you think I’ve been a giant now?” he asked hoarsely. “你猜我现在当巨人有多久了？”他沙哑地问。

“I don’t know,” she said. “It seems like a long time.” “我不知道，”她说。 “感觉已经很久了。”

“We’d better get moving. We only have an hour,” he said. “我们最好赶快动身。” “我们只有一小时，”他说。

“What’ll we do if you’re still a giant when we get to the center?” asked Annie. “如果我们到中心时你还是巨人怎么办？”安妮问。

“I’ll hide outside,” said Jack, “until I’m small again.” He figured the last thing the staff needed now was to see a twenty-five-foot-tall kid. “我会躲到外面去，”杰克说，“直到我变回小个子。”他想，现在工作人员最不需要看到的就是一个二十五英尺高的小孩。

Slowly they both stood up, soaking wet, but cleaner. Jack lifted Annie back onto his shoulder. He began striding uphill again, sloshing through mud puddles and stepping over crushed rocks. Jack walked through the falling rain, never stopping. But by the time they reached the panda center, he was so tired he could barely take another step. 他们俩慢慢地站了起来，浑身湿透，但看起来干净了一些。 杰克把安妮抬回自己的肩膀上。 他又开始往山上走，踩着泥坑，跨过碎石子。 杰克在雨中走着，一刻也没停。 但等他们到达熊猫中心时，他已经累得几乎走不动了。

With Annie on his shoulder, Jack stood on the bank of the river and they stared across at the damage wrought by the earthquake. 安妮在杰克的肩膀上，杰克站在河边，他们看着地震造成的破坏。

The parking lot was filled with mud. The center’s sign had been knocked down. Portions of the bridge had collapsed into the river, and fallen brush and debris were piled up on the other side of the entrance gate. 停车场里全是泥巴。 中心的大牌子被撞倒了。 桥的一部分塌进了河里，掉落的树枝和杂物堆在入口大门的另一边。

The slopes that surrounded the center were now gray and bare. Landslides had stripped them of foliage. 围绕中心的山坡现在变得灰秃秃的。 山体滑坡把上面的植物都刮掉了。

“If it’s this bad outside the center,” said Jack, “I wonder how bad it is inside.” “如果外面的情况这么糟糕，”杰克说，“我想里面一定更糟糕。”

“How do we even get inside?” said Annie, staring down at the raging river. “我们怎么才能进去呢？”安妮盯着汹涌的河水，说道。

“Don’t worry,” said Jack. “I’m pretty sure I’m tall enough to wade across.” He took a deep breath, then stepped into the river. The cold water swirled around his waist. The current nearly knocked him over. Slowly and carefully, he stepped around a huge rock that had rolled into the river. Suddenly Jack felt his body start to quake. “别担心，”杰克说。 “我确定我长得足够高，可以蹚过去。”他深吸了一口气，然后走进了河里。 冰冷的水漫到了他的腰部。 水流差点把他冲倒了。 他小心翼翼地绕过一块滚到河里的大石头。 突然，杰克感觉自己的身体开始发抖。

Another aftershock, he thought. He paused, but his body kept shaking. As Jack shook, he started to grow smaller. In an instant he had shrunk back to his normal size. 他又觉得是一次余震。 他停了下来，但身体还在发抖。 杰克一边发抖，一边开始变小。 转眼间，他又恢复了原来的大小。

He and Annie plunged into the swiftly churning water. Jack grabbed the branch of a fallen tree. Clinging to the branch, he looked around frantically for Annie. She was holding on to a log. “Here!” shrieked Jack. He reached out his hand, and Annie grabbed it. As the water swirled around them, he pulled her over to him. 他和安妮掉进了湍急的水流中。 杰克抓住了一棵倒下的树的树枝。 紧紧抓着树枝，他焦急地四处寻找安妮。 她紧紧抓着一根木头。 “在这儿！”杰克喊道。 他伸出手，安妮抓住了它。 当水流在他们周围旋转时，他把她拉到了自己身边。

“Can we get to the bridge?” Annie cried. “我们能到桥上吗？”安妮喊道。

“Try!” yelled Jack. They both let go of the branch and thrashed through the water until they grabbed on to a piece of the wrecked bridge. “试试看！”杰克大喊。 他们俩松开了树枝，在水中拼命挣扎，直到抓住了桥残骸的一块碎片。

Jack and Annie hoisted themselves up onto the slab of concrete. Jack pointed to the part of the bridge that still stood at the edge of the ravine. He took another deep breath and leapt over the gap onto the broken bridge. Annie followed. 杰克和安妮把自己拉上了那块混凝土板。 杰克指着桥上仍然屹立在峡谷边缘的部分。 他深吸了一口气，跳过了那个缺口，落在了那座破桥上。安妮跟了上去。

Jack and Annie clung to a piece of bridge railing and stared at the entrance gate of the panda center. Muddy rocks, branches, and leaves were piled on the other side. 杰克和安妮紧紧抓着桥栏杆的一段，盯着熊猫中心的大门看。 另一边堆满了泥巴的石头、树枝和树叶。

“I think we can climb over,” said Annie. She led the way, stepping onto the railing next to the gate. “我想我们可以爬过去，”安妮说。 她带头走了，踩着栏杆站在了大门旁边。

Jack followed her. They climbed over the gate and kept climbing over the heap of branches, brush, and rocks that blocked the entrance. When they reached the top of the wreckage, they looked down. 杰克跟在她后面。 他们爬过了大门，接着又爬过了挡住入口的树枝、灌木和石头堆。 当他们爬到废墟的顶部时，他们往下看了看。

Staff members were rushing around with buckets, shovels, and first-aid kits. When Master Lee and Dr. Ling caught sight of Jack and Annie, they both stopped dead in their tracks and gaped at them. 工作人员们拿着桶、铲子和急救包到处跑来跑去。 当李师傅和林博士 看到杰克和安妮时，他们都猛地停住了脚步，惊讶地盯着他们。

“Hi there,” Annie called. “Can we help?” “你好，”安妮喊道。 “我们需要帮忙吗？”
