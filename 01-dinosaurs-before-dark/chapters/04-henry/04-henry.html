4. Henry 4. 亨利

Jack gasped as Annie held out her hand. 安妮伸出手，杰克倒抽了一口气。

Oh, brother. She was always trying to make friends with animals. But this was going too far. 真受不了。 她总是想跟动物交朋友。 可这回太过分了。

“Don’t get too close to him, Annie!” Jack shouted. “别靠它太近，安妮！”杰克喊道。

But Annie touched the Pteranodon’s crest. She stroked his neck. She was talking to him. 可安妮还是摸了摸无齿翼龙的冠，又抚摸它的脖子。 她在跟它说话。

What in the world was she saying? 她到底在说什么？

Jack took a deep breath. Okay. He would go down, too. It would be good to examine the creature. Take notes. Like a scientist. 杰克深吸一口气。好吧。 他也要下去。 好好观察一下这只生物，做做笔记，也不错。 像个科学家一样。

Jack started down the rope ladder. 杰克开始往下爬绳梯。

When he got to the ground, Jack was only a few feet away from the creature. 到了地上，杰克离那生物只有几步远。

The creature stared at Jack. His eyes were bright and alert. 那生物盯着杰克。 它的眼睛又亮又机灵。

“He’s soft, Jack,” said Annie. “He feels like Henry.” “它软软的，杰克，”安妮说。 “摸起来像亨利。”

Jack snorted. “He’s no dog, Annie.” 杰克哼了一声。 “它可不是狗，安妮。”

“Feel him, Jack," said Annie. “摸摸它，杰克，”安妮说。

Jack didn’t move. 杰克没动。

“Don’t think, Jack. Just do it.” “别想了，杰克。动手就行。”

Jack stepped forward. He put out his arm. Very cautiously. He brushed his hand down the creature’s neck. 杰克往前走了一步。 他伸出胳膊。非常小心。 他的手轻轻顺着那生物的脖子往下摸。

Interesting. A thin layer of fuzz covered the Pteranodon’s skin. 有意思。 无齿翼龙的皮肤上覆着一层薄薄的绒毛。

“Soft, huh?” said Annie. “软软的吧？”安妮说。

Jack reached into his backpack and pulled out a pencil and a notebook. 杰克伸手进背包，拿出一支铅笔和一个笔记本。

He wrote: 他写道：

FUZZY SKIN 皮肤有绒毛

“What are you doing?” asked Annie. “你在干什么？”安妮问。

“Taking notes,” said Jack. “We’re probably the first people in the whole world to ever see a real live Pteranodon.” “做笔记，”杰克说。 “我们大概是全世界头一回看见活生生的无齿翼龙的人。”

Jack looked at the Pteranodon again. The creature had a bony crest on top of his head. The crest was longer than Jack’s arm. 杰克又看了看无齿翼龙。 这生物头顶上有一道骨质的冠。 那冠比杰克的胳膊还长。

“I wonder how smart he is,” Jack said. “不知道它有多聪明，”杰克说。

“Very smart,” said Annie. “非常聪明，”安妮说。

“Don’t count on it,” said Jack. “His brain’s probably no bigger than a bean.” “别指望了，”杰克说。 “它的脑子大概也就一颗豆子那么大。”

“No, he’s very smart. I can feel it,” said Annie. “I’m going to call him Henry.” “不，它非常聪明。 我能感觉到，”安妮说。 “我要叫它亨利。”

Jack wrote in his notebook: 杰克在笔记本上写道：

SMALL BRAIN? 脑子很小？

Jack looked at the creature again, “Maybe he’s a mutant,” he said. 杰克又看了看那生物。“也许它是个变种，”他说。

The creature tilted his head. 那生物歪了歪头。

Annie laughed. “He’s no mutant, Jack.” 安妮笑了。 “它不是变种，杰克。”

“Well, what’s he doing here then? Where is this place?” said Jack. “那它在这儿干什么？ 这到底是哪儿？”杰克说。

Annie leaned close to the Pteranodon. 安妮凑近无齿翼龙。

“Do you know where we are, Henry?” she asked softly. “你知道我们在哪儿吗，亨利？”她轻声问。

The creature fixed his eyes on Annie. His long jaws were opening and closing. Like a giant pair of scissors. 那生物盯着安妮。 它长长的嘴巴一张一合。 像一把巨大的剪刀。

“Are you trying to talk to me, Henry?” asked Annie. “你是在跟我说话吗，亨利？”安妮问。

“Forget it, Annie.” Jack wrote in his notebook: “算了吧，安妮。”杰克在笔记本上写道：

MOUTH LIKE SCISSORS? 嘴巴像剪刀？

“Did we come to a time long ago, Henry?” asked Annie. “Is this a place from long ago?” Suddenly she gasped. “Jack!” “我们是不是来到很久以前了，亨利？”安妮问。 “这是很久以前的地方吗？”忽然，她倒抽一口气。 “杰克！”

He looked up. 他抬起头。

Annie was pointing toward the hill. On top stood a huge dinosaur! 安妮正指向小山。 山顶上站着一头巨大的恐龙！
