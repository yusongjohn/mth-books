5. Gold in the Grass 5. 草丛里的金子

"Go! Go!” said Jack. He threw his notebook into his pack. He pushed Annie toward the rope ladder. “走！快走！”杰克说。 他把笔记本扔进背包。 他把安妮推向绳梯。

“Bye, Henry!” she said. “再见，亨利！”她说。

“Go!” said Jack. He gave Annie a big push. “走！”杰克说。 他用力推了安妮一把。

“Quit it!” she said. But she started up the ladder. Jack scrambled after her. “别推了！”她说。 可她还是开始往上爬梯子。 杰克连忙跟着爬上去。

They tumbled into the tree house. 他们跌进了树屋。

They were panting as they looked out the window at the dinosaur. He was standing on the hilltop. Eating flowers off a tree. 他们喘着气，从窗口看着那头恐龙。 它站在山顶上。 正在吃树上的花。

“Oh, man,” whispered Jack. “We are in a time long ago!” “天哪，”杰克轻声说。 “我们真的来到很久以前了！”

The dinosaur looked like a huge rhinoceros. Only he had three horns instead of one. Two long ones above his eyes and one on his nose. He had a big shield-like thing behind his head. 那头恐龙看起来像一头巨大的犀牛。 只不过它有三只角，不是一只。 两只长角在眼睛上方，一只在鼻子上。 它脑袋后面有一块像盾牌一样的东西。

“Triceratops!” said Jack. “三角龙！”杰克说。

“Does he eat people?” whispered Annie. “它吃人吗？”安妮轻声问。

“I'll look it up.” Jack grabbed the dinosaur book. He flipped through the pages. “我查查。”杰克抓起恐龙书。 他飞快地翻着书页。

“There!” he said. He pointed to a picture of a Triceratops. He read the caption: “找到了！”他说。 他指着一张三角龙的图画。 他读了说明文字：

The Triceratops lived in the late Cretaceous period. This plant-eating dinosaur weighed over 12,000 pounds. 三角龙生活在晚白垩纪。这种吃植物的恐龙体重超过一万二千磅。

Jack slammed the book shut, “Just plants. No meat.” 杰克把书啪地合上。“只吃植物。不吃肉。”

“Let’s go see him,” said Annie. “我们去看看它，”安妮说。

“Are you nuts?” said Jack. “你疯了吗？”杰克说。

“Don’t you want to take notes about him?” asked Annie. “We’re probably the first people in the whole world to ever see a real live Triceratops.” “你不想给它做笔记吗？”安妮问。“我们大概是全世界头一回看见活生生的三角龙的人。”

Jack sighed. She was right. 杰克叹了口气。她说得对。

“Let’s go,” he said. “走吧，”他说。

He shoved the dinosaur book into his pack. He slung it over his shoulder and started down the ladder. 他把恐龙书塞进背包。 他把包挎到肩上，开始往下爬梯子。

On the way down, Jack stopped. 往下爬的时候，杰克停住了。

He called up to Annie, “Just promise you won’t pet him.” 他朝上面的安妮喊：“答应我，你别摸它。”

“I promise.” “我答应。”

“Promise you won’t kiss him.” “答应我，你别亲它。”

“I promise.” “我答应。”

“Promise you won’t talk to him.” “答应我，你别跟它说话。”

“I promise.” “我答应。”

“Promise you won’t-” “答应我，你别——”

“Go! Go!” she said. “走！快走！”她说。

Jack went. Annie followed. 杰克下去了。安妮跟着。

When they stepped off the ladder, the Pteranodon gave them a kind look. 他们从梯子上下来时，无齿翼龙朝他们友善地看了一眼。

Annie blew a kiss at him. “Be back soon, Henry,” she said cheerfully. 安妮朝它飞了一个吻。 “一会儿就回来，亨利，”她高兴地说。

“Shush!” said Jack. And he led the way through the ferns. Slowly and carefully. “嘘！”杰克说。 他带着路，穿过蕨类植物。 慢慢地，小心地。

When he reached the bottom of the hill, he kneeled behind a fat bush. 到了山脚下，他跪在一丛很厚的灌木后面。

Annie knelt beside him and started to speak. 安妮跪在他旁边，正要开口。

“Shush!” Jack put his finger to his lips. “嘘！”杰克把手指放在嘴唇上。

Annie made a face. 安妮做了个鬼脸。

Jack peeked out at the Triceratops. 杰克偷偷朝三角龙望去。

The dinosaur was incredibly big. Bigger than a truck. He was eating the flowers off a magnolia tree. 那头恐龙大得吓人。 比卡车还大。 它正在吃玉兰树上的花。

Jack slipped his notebook out of his pack. He wrote: 杰克从背包里抽出笔记本，写道：

EATS FLOWERS 吃花

Annie nudged him. 安妮捅了捅他。

Jack ignored her. He studied the Triceratops again. He wrote: 杰克没理她。 他又仔细看了看三角龙。他写道：

EATS SLOWLY 吃得很慢

Annie nudged him hard. 安妮用力捅了他一下。

Jack looked at her. 杰克看着她。

Annie pointed to herself. She walked her fingers through the air. She pointed to the dinosaur. She smiled. 安妮指了指自己。 她用手指在空中走了几步。 她指了指恐龙。她笑了。

Was she teasing? 她是在逗他吗？

She waved at Jack. 她朝杰克挥了挥手。

Jack started to grab her. 杰克伸手去抓她。

She laughed and jumped away. She fell into the grass. In full view of the Triceratops! 她笑着跳开了。 她摔进了草丛里。 正好被三角龙看得一清二楚！

“Get back!” whispered Jack. “回来！”杰克低声说。

Too late. The big dinosaur had spotted Annie. He gazed down at her from the hilltop. Half of a magnolia flower was sticking out of his mouth. 太晚了。 那头大恐龙已经看见安妮了。 它从山顶上低头看着她。 半朵玉兰花还从它嘴里伸出来。

“Oops,” said Annie. “哎呀，”安妮说。

“Get back!” Jack shouted at her. “回来！”杰克朝她喊。

“He looks nice, Jack.” “它看起来很友好，杰克。”

“Nice? Watch out for his horns, Annie!" “友好？ 小心它的角，安妮！”

“No. He’s nice, Jack.” “不。它很友好，杰克。”

Nice? 友好？

But the Triceratops just gazed calmly down at Annie. Then he turned and loped away. Down the side of the hill. 可三角龙只是平静地低头看着安妮。 然后它转过身，大步跑开了。 跑下了山坡。

“Bye!” said Annie. She turned back to Jack. "See?” “再见！”安妮说。 她转过身对着杰克。 “看见了吧？”

Jack grunted. But he wrote in his notebook: 杰克哼了一声。 可他还是在笔记本上写道：

NICE 友好

“Come on. Let’s look around some more,” said Annie. “来吧。 我们再四处看看，”安妮说。

As Jack started after Annie, he saw something glittering in the tall grass. He reached out and picked it up. 杰克跟着安妮走，忽然看见高草里有什么东西在闪光。 他伸手把它捡了起来。

A medallion. A gold medallion. 一枚徽章。一枚金徽章。

A letter was engraved on the medallion. A fancy M. 徽章上刻着一个字母，一个花体的M。

"Oh, man. Someone came here before us,” Jack said softly. “天哪。 有人比我们先来过这儿，”杰克轻声说。
