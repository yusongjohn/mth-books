6. Dinosaur Valley 6. 恐龙谷

“Annie, look at this!” Jack called. “Look what I found!” “安妮，看这个！”杰克喊道。 “看我找到了什么！”

Annie had gone up to the hilltop. 安妮已经走到山顶上了。

She was busy picking a flower from the magnolia tree. 她正忙着从玉兰树上摘一朵花。

“Annie, look! A medallion!” “安妮，快看！一枚徽章！”

But Annie wasn’t paying attention to Jack. She was staring at something on the other side of the hill. 可安妮没听杰克说话，她正盯着山丘另一边的什么东西看。

“Oh, wow!” she said. “哇！”她说。

“Annie!” “安妮！”

Clutching her magnolia flower, she took off down the hill. “Annie, come back!” Jack shouted. 她攥着木兰花，冲下了山丘。 “安妮，回来！”杰克喊道。

But Annie had disappeared.

 可安妮已经不见了。

“I’m going to kill her,” Jack muttered. “气死我了。”杰克嘀咕道。

He stuffed the gold medallion into his jeans pocket. 他把金色徽章塞进牛仔裤口袋。

Then he heard Annie shriek. 接着他听见安妮尖叫了一声。

“Annie?” “安妮？”

Jack heard another sound as well. A deep, bellowing sound. Like a tuba. “Jack! Come here!” Annie called. 杰克还听见了别的声音。 一种低沉的轰鸣，像大号一样。 “杰克！ 快过来！”安妮喊道。

“Annie!” “安妮！”

Jack grabbed his backpack and raced up the hill. 杰克抓起背包，冲上山丘。

When he got to the top, he gasped. 跑到山顶，他倒抽了一口气。

The valley below was filled with nests. Big nests made out of mud. And the nests were filled with tiny dinosaurs! 下面的山谷里全是窝。 用泥巴堆成的大窝。 窝里全是小小的恐龙！

Annie was crouching next to one of the nests. And standing over her was a gigantic duck-billed dinosaur! 安妮蹲在一个窝旁边，她头顶上站着一只巨大的鸭嘴龙！

“Don’t panic. Don’t move,” said Jack. He stepped slowly down the hill toward Annie. “别慌，别动。”杰克说。 他慢慢走下山丘，朝安妮走去。

The huge dinosaur was towering above Annie. Waving her arms. Making her tuba sound. 那只巨大的恐龙高高挡在安妮上方，挥着前肢。 发出大号一样的叫声。

Jack stopped. He didn’t want to get too close. 杰克停住了。 他不想靠得太近。

He knelt on the ground. “Okay. Move toward me. Slowly,” he said. 他跪在地上。 “好。朝我这边来。 慢慢来。”他说。

Annie started to stand up.

 安妮开始站起来。

“Don’t stand. Crawl,” said Jack. “别站起来。 爬过来。”杰克说。

Clutching her flower, Annie crawled toward Jack. 安妮攥着花，朝杰克爬过去。

The duck-billed dinosaur followed her. Still bellowing. 那只鸭嘴龙跟着她，还在吼。

Annie froze. 安妮僵住了。

“Keep going,” Jack said softly. “接着走。”杰克轻轻说。

Annie started crawling again. 安妮又开始爬。

Jack inched farther down the hill. Until he was just an arm’s distance from Annie. 杰克一点点往山下挪。 直到离安妮只有一臂远。

He reached out—and grabbed her hand. 他伸手——抓住了她的手。

He pulled Annie toward him. 他把安妮拉到身边。

“Stay down,” he said. He crouched next to her. “Bow your head. Pretend to chew.” “别起来。”他说。 他蹲在她旁边，“低下头。假装嚼东西。”

“Chew?” “嚼东西？”

“Yes. I read that’s what you do if a mean dog comes at you.” “对。 我看书上说，要是恶狗冲过来，就该这么做。”

“She’s no dog, Jack,” said Annie. “她可不是狗，杰克。”安妮说。

“Just chew,” said Jack. “你就嚼。”杰克说。

Jack and Annie both bowed their heads. And pretended to chew. 杰克和安妮都低下头。 假装嚼东西。

Soon the dinosaur grew quiet. 很快，恐龙安静下来了。

Jack raised his head. 杰克抬起头。

“I don’t think she’s mad anymore,” he said. “我觉得她不生气了。”他说。

“Thanks, Jack, for saving me,” said Annie. “谢谢你救了我，杰克。”安妮说。

"You have to use your brain,” said Jack. “You can’t just go running to a nest of babies. There’s always a mother nearby.” “你得动脑子。”杰克说。 “不能一看见宝宝窝就往上冲。 附近肯定有妈妈。”

Annie stood up. 安妮站了起来。

“Annie!” “安妮！”

Too late. 太晚了。

Annie held out her magnolia flower to the dinosaur.

 安妮把木兰花递给恐龙。

“I’m sorry I made you worry about your babies,” she said. “对不起，让你担心宝宝了。”她说。

The dinosaur moved closer to Annie. She grabbed the flower from her. She reached for another. 恐龙朝安妮走近。 她从安妮手里抢走了花。 又伸手要另一朵。

“No more," said Annie. “没了。”安妮说。

The dinosaur let out a sad tuba sound. 恐龙发出一声难过的大号声。

“But there are more flowers up there," Annie said. She pointed to the top of the hill. “I’ll get you some.” “不过上面还有花。”安妮说。 她指了指山顶。 “我去给你摘。”

Annie hurried up the hill. 安妮赶紧往山上跑。

The dinosaur waddled after her. 恐龙摇摇摆摆地跟在后面。

Jack quickly examined the babies. Some were crawling out of their nests. 杰克赶紧看了看那些宝宝。 有的正从窝里往外爬。

Where were the other mothers? 别的妈妈呢？

Jack took out the dinosaur book. He flipped through the pages. 杰克拿出恐龙书。 他一页页翻着。

He found a picture of some duck-billed dinosaurs. He read the caption: 他找到一张鸭嘴龙的图片。 他读了说明：

The Anatosauruses lived in colonies. While a few mothers babysat the nests, others hunted for food. 鸭嘴龙成群住在一起。 几个妈妈照看窝，别的妈妈去找吃的。

So there must be more mothers close by. 所以附近一定还有别的妈妈。

“Hey, Jack!” Annie called. “嘿，杰克！”安妮喊道。

Jack looked up. Annie was at the top of the hill. Feeding magnolia flowers to the giant Anatosaurus! 杰克抬起头。 安妮在山顶上。 正在给那只巨大的鸭嘴龙喂木兰花！

“She’s nice, too, Jack,” Annie said. “她也很好，杰克。”安妮说。

But suddenly the Anatosaurus made her terrible tuba sound. Annie crouched down and started to chew. 可突然，那只鸭嘴龙发出可怕的大号声。 安妮蹲下来，开始嚼东西。

The dinosaur barged down the hill. 恐龙冲下了山丘。

She seemed afraid of something. 她好像在害怕什么。

Jack put the book down on top of his pack. He hurried up to Annie. 杰克把书放在背包上。 他赶紧跑向安妮。

“I wonder why she ran away,” said Annie. “We were starting to be friends.” “她怎么跑了？”安妮说。 “我们都快成朋友了。”

Jack looked around. What he saw in the distance almost made him throw up. 杰克四处看了看。 远处的景象差点让他吐出来。

An enormous ugly monster was coming across the plain. 一只又大又丑的怪兽正穿过平原走来。

He was walking on two big legs. And swinging a long, thick tail. And dangling two tiny arms. 它用两条大腿走路。 甩着一条又长又粗的尾巴。 还垂着两只小小的前肢。

He had a huge head. And his jaws were wide open. 它的头很大。 嘴巴张得大大的。

Even from far away Jack could see his long, gleaming teeth. 就算隔得很远，杰克也能看见它又长又亮的牙齿。

“Tyrannosaurus rex!” whispered Jack. “霸王龙！”杰克小声说。
