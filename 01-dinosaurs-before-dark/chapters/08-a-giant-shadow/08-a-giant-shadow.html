8. A Giant Shadow 8. 巨大的影子

Jack jumped behind the magnolia tree. 杰克跳到木兰树后面。

His heart was beating so fast he could hardly think. 他的心跳得好快，几乎没法想事情。

He peeked out at the giant monster. The horrible-looking creature was opening and closing his huge jaws. His teeth were as big as steak knives. 他偷偷看那只巨大的怪兽，那可怕的家伙正一张一合着大嘴。 它的牙像菜刀那么大。

Don’t panic. Think. 别慌。动脑子。

Jack peered down at the valley. 杰克朝山谷望去。

Good. The duck-billed dinosaurs were sticking close to their nests. 还好，那些鸭嘴龙都紧挨着自己的窝。

Good. The monster still didn’t seem to know he was there. 还好，那只怪兽好像还不知道他在这儿。

Don’t panic. Think. Think. Maybe there’s information in the book. 别慌。动脑子。 想。 也许书里有用得上的信息。

Jack opened the dinosaur book. He found Tyrannosaurus rex. He read: 杰克打开恐龙书。 他找到了霸王龙。他读道：

Tyrannosaurus rex was the largest meat-eating land animal of all time. If it were alive today, it would eat a human in one bite. 霸王龙是有史以来最大的陆上食肉动物。 要是它活到今天，一口就能把人吃掉。

Great. The book was no help at all. 太好了。 这本书一点忙都帮不上。

Okay. He couldn’t hide on the other side of the hill. The Anatosauruses might stampede. 好吧。 他不能躲到山丘另一边。 那些鸭嘴龙可能会受惊乱冲。

Okay. He couldn’t run to the tree house. The Tyrannosaurus might run faster. 好吧。 他也不能跑向树屋。 霸王龙也许跑得更快。

Okay. Maybe he should just wait. Wait for the monster to leave. 好吧。 也许他该等一等。 等那只怪兽离开。

Jack peeked around the tree. 杰克从树后探出头。

The Tyrannosaurus had wandered closer to the hill. 霸王龙已经走到离山丘更近的地方。

Something caught Jack’s eye. Annie was coming down the rope ladder! Was she nuts? What was she doing? 有样东西吸引了杰克的注意。 安妮正顺着绳梯往下爬！她疯了吗？ 她在干什么？

Jack watched Annie hop off the ladder. 杰克看着安妮跳下梯子。

She went straight to the Pteranodon. She was talking to him. She was flapping her arms. She pointed at Jack, at the sky, at the tree house. 她径直走向无齿翼龙。 她在跟它说话。 她拍打着胳膊。 她指了指杰克，指了指天空，又指了指树屋。

She was nuts! 她真的疯了！

“Go! Go back up the tree!” Jack whispered. “Go!” “回去！ 快爬回树上去！”杰克小声说。 “回去！”

Suddenly Jack heard a roar. 突然，杰克听见一声吼叫。

The Tyrannosaurus rex was looking in his direction. 霸王龙正朝他这边看。

Jack hit the ground. 杰克趴到地上。

The Tyrannosaurus rex was coming toward the hill. 霸王龙正朝山丘走来。

Jack felt the ground shaking. 杰克感到地面在晃。

Should he run? Crawl back into Dinosaur Valley? Climb the magnolia tree? 他该跑吗？ 爬回恐龙谷？ 爬上木兰树？

Just then a giant shadow covered Jack. He looked up. 就在这时，一个巨大的影子罩住了杰克。他抬头看。

The Pteranodon was gliding overhead. The giant creature sailed down toward the top of the hill. 无齿翼龙正在头顶滑翔。 那只巨大的生物朝山顶飞下来。

He was coming straight for Jack. 它正朝杰克直冲过来。
