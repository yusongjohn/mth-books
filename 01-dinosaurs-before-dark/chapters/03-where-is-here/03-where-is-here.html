3. Where Is Here? 3. 这是哪儿？

Jack looked out the window. 杰克往窗外看。

He looked down at the picture in the book. 他又低头看了看书里的画。

He looked back out the window. 再往窗外看。

The world outside and the world in the picture—they were exactly the same. 窗外的世界和画里的世界——一模一样。

The Pteranodon was soaring through the sky. The ground was covered with ferns and tall grass. There was a winding stream. A sloping hill. And volcanoes in the distance. 无齿翼龙正在天上飞。 地上长满了蕨类植物和高高的草。 有一条弯弯的小溪。还有一座斜斜的小山。 远处还有火山。

“Wh-where are we?” stammered Jack. “这——这是哪儿？”杰克结巴着说。

The Pteranodon glided down to the base of their tree. The creature coasted to a stop. And stood very still. 无齿翼龙滑翔下来，落到他们这棵树下。 那生物滑行着停了下来。 然后一动不动地站着。

“What happened to us?” said Annie. She looked at Jack. He looked at her. “我们怎么了？”安妮说。 她看着杰克。杰克看着她。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “I was looking at the picture in the book-” “我不知道，”杰克说。 “我当时在看书里的画——”

“And you said, 'Wow, I wish I could see a Pteranodon for real,’ ” said Annie. “然后你说，‘哇，真希望能亲眼看看无齿翼龙，’”安妮说。

“Yeah. And then we saw one. In the Frog Creek woods,” said Jack. “Yeah. And then the wind got loud. And the tree house started spinning,” said Annie. “对。 然后我们就看见了一只。 就在蛙溪镇的树林里，”杰克说。 “对。 然后风变得好大声。 树屋开始旋转，”安妮说。

“And we landed here,” said Jack. “然后我们就落到这儿了，”杰克说。

“And we landed here,” said Annie. “然后我们就落到这儿了，”安妮说。

“So that means...” said Jack. “所以那就是说……”杰克说。

“So that means... what?” said Annie. “所以那就是说…… 什么？”安妮说。

“Nothing,” said Jack. He shook his head. “None of this can be real.” “没什么，”杰克说。 他摇了摇头。 “这些都不可能是真的。”

Annie looked out the window again. “But he’s real,” she said. “He’s very real.” 安妮又往窗外看。 “可它是真的，”她说。 “它真的是真的，”

Jack looked out the window with her. The Pteranodon was standing at the base of the oak tree. Like a guard. His giant wings were spread out on either side of him. 杰克也跟她一起往窗外看。 无齿翼龙站在橡树底下。像个卫兵。 它巨大的翅膀向两边张开。

“Hi!” Annie shouted. “嗨！”安妮喊道。

“Shush!” said Jack. “We’re not supposed to be here.” “嘘！”杰克说。 “我们不该在这儿的。”

“But where is here?” said Annie. “可这是哪儿啊？”安妮说。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “我不知道，”杰克说。

“Hi!” Annie called again to the creature. “嗨！”安妮又朝那生物喊了一声。

The Pteranodon looked up at them.

 无齿翼龙抬头看着他们。

“Where is here?" Annie called down. “这是哪儿啊？”安妮朝下喊。

“You’re nuts. He can’t talk,” said Jack. “But maybe the book can tell us.” Jack looked down at the book. He read the words under the picture: “你疯了。 它不会说话，”杰克说。 “不过也许书能告诉我们。”杰克低头看书。 他读了图画下面的字：

This flying reptile lived in the Cretaceous period. It vanished 65 million years ago. 这种会飞的爬行动物生活在白垩纪。 它在六千五百万年前就消失了。

No. Impossible. They couldn’t have landed in a time 65 million years ago. 不。不可能。 他们不可能落到六千五百万年前去。

“Jack,” said Annie. “He’s nice.” “杰克，”安妮说。 “它很友好。”

“Nice?” “友好？”

“Yeah, I can tell. Let’s go down and talk to him.” “对，我能感觉到。 我们下去跟它说说话。”

“Talk to him?” “跟它说话？”

Annie started down the rope ladder. 安妮开始往下爬绳梯。

“Hey!” shouted Jack. “嘿！”杰克喊道。

But Annie kept going. 可安妮还是往下爬。

“Are you crazy?” Jack called. “你疯了吗？”杰克喊道。

Annie dropped to the ground. She stepped boldly up to the ancient creature. 安妮跳到了地上。 她大胆地走到那只古老的生物跟前。
