7. Ready, Set, Go! 7. 预备，开始，跑！

“Run, Annie! Run!” cried Jack, “To the tree house!” “跑，安妮！ 跑！”杰克喊道，“回树屋！”

They dashed down the hill together. Through the tall grass, through the ferns, past the Pteranodon, and right to the rope ladder. 他们一起冲下山丘。 穿过高草，穿过蕨类，经过无齿翼龙，一直跑到绳梯前。

They scrambled up. Seconds later they tumbled into the tree house. 他们爬了上去，几秒后就滚进了树屋。

Annie leaped to the window. 安妮跳到窗边。

“He’s going away!” she said, panting. “它走了！”她喘着气说。

Jack pushed his glasses into place. He looked through the window with her. 杰克把眼镜扶正。 他和她一起往窗外看。

But then the monster stopped and turned around. 可那只怪兽停住了，转过身来。

“Duck!” said Jack. “趴下！”杰克说。

The two of them hunched down. 他们俩赶紧低下身子。

After a long moment, they raised their heads. They peeked out again. “Coast clear,” said Jack. 过了好一会儿，他们才抬起头。 又偷偷往外看。 “安全了。”杰克说。

“Yay,” whispered Annie. “耶。”安妮小声说。

“We have to get out of here,” said Jack. “我们得离开这儿。”杰克说。

“You made a wish before,” said Annie. “你刚才许过愿。”安妮说。

“I wish we could go back to Frog Creek,” said Jack. “我希望我们能回到蛙溪镇。”杰克说。

Nothing happened. 什么也没发生。

“I wish-” “我希望——”

“Wait. You were looking at a picture in the dinosaur book. Remember?” “等等。 你当时在看恐龙书上的图画。记得吗？”

The dinosaur book. 恐龙书。

Jack groaned. “Oh, no. I left the book and my pack on the hill. I have to go back.” 杰克唉了一声。 “糟了。 我把书和背包落在山上了。 我得回去。”

“Oh, forget it,” said Annie. “哎呀，别管了。”安妮说。

“I can’t,” said Jack. “The book doesn’t belong to us. Plus my notebook’s in my pack. With all my notes.” “不行。”杰克说。 “那本书不是我们的。 而且我的笔记本在背包里。 里面全是我的笔记。”

“Hurry!” said Annie. “快点！”安妮说。

Jack hurried down the rope ladder. 杰克赶紧顺着绳梯爬下去。

He leaped to the ground. 他跳到地上。

He raced past the Pteranodon, through the ferns, through the tall grass, and up the hill. 他跑过无齿翼龙，穿过蕨类，穿过高草，冲上山丘。

He looked down. 他往下看。

There was his pack, lying on the ground. On top of it was the dinosaur book. 背包就躺在地上。 上面放着那本恐龙书。

But now the valley below was filled with Anatosauruses. All standing guard around the nests. 可现在山谷里全是鸭嘴龙。 都围着窝站岗。

Where had they been? Did fear of the Tyrannosaurus send them home? 它们刚才去哪儿了？ 是怕霸王龙，才跑回家的吗？

Jack took a deep breath. 杰克深吸一口气。

Ready! Set! Go! 预备！开始！ 跑！

He charged down the hill. He leaped to his backpack. He scooped it up. 他冲下山丘。 他跳到背包旁，一把抄起来。

A terrible tuba sound! Another! Another! All the Anatosauruses were bellowing at him. 一声可怕的大号声！又一声！ 又一声！ 所有鸭嘴龙都在朝他吼。

Jack took off. 杰克拔腿就跑。

He raced up to the hilltop. 他冲上山顶。

He started down the hill. 开始往山下跑。

He stopped. 他停住了。

The Tyrannosaurus rex was back! And he was standing between Jack and the tree house! 霸王龙又回来了！ 它正挡在杰克和树屋中间！
