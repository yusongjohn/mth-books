2. The Monster 2. 怪物

Jack crawled through a hole in the tree house floor. 杰克从树屋地板上的洞口爬了进去。

Wow. The tree house was filled with books. Books everywhere. Very old books with dusty covers. New books with shiny, bright covers. 哇。 树屋里全是书。到处都是书。 有很旧的书，封面落满了灰。 也有新书，封面又亮又鲜艳。

“Look. You can see far, far away,” said Annie. She was peering out the tree house window. “看。 能看得好远好远，”安妮说。 她正从树屋的窗口往外看。

Jack looked out the window with her. Down below were the tops of the other trees. In the distance he saw the Frog Creek library. The elementary school. The park. 杰克也跟她一起往窗外看。下面是其他树的树梢。 远处，他看见了蛙溪镇图书馆。 还有小学，还有公园。

Annie pointed in the other direction. 安妮指向另一个方向。

“There’s our house,” she said. “那是我们家，”她说。

Sure enough. There was their white wooden house with the green porch. Next door was their neighbor’s black dog, Henry. He looked very tiny. 果然。 那是他们家白色的木头房子，还有绿色的门廊。 隔壁是邻居家的黑狗亨利。 它看起来小小的。

“Hi, Henry!” shouted Annie. “嗨，亨利！”安妮喊道。

“Shush!” said Jack. “We’re not supposed to be up here.” “嘘！”杰克说。 “我们不该上来的。”

He glanced around the tree house again. 他又朝树屋里扫了一眼。

“I wonder who owns all these books,” he said. He noticed bookmarks were sticking out of many of them. “不知道这些书都是谁的，”他说。 他发现很多书里都夹着书签。

“I like this one,” said Annie. She held up a book with a castle on the cover. “我喜欢这本，”安妮说。 她举起一本封面上有城堡的书。

“Here’s a book about Pennsylvania,” said Jack. He turned to the page with the bookmark. “这儿有一本关于宾夕法尼亚的书，”杰克说。他翻到夹着书签的那一页。

“Hey, there’s a picture of Frog Creek in here,” said Jack. “It’s a picture of these woods!” “嘿，这里面有蛙溪镇的画，”杰克说。 “画的就是这片树林！”

“Oh, here’s a book for you,” said Annie. She held up a book about dinosaurs. A blue silk bookmark was sticking out of it. “哦，这本适合你，”安妮说。 她举起一本关于恐龙的书。 一根蓝色丝绸书签从里面伸出来。

“Let me see it.” Jack set down his backpack and grabbed the book from her. “让我看看。”杰克放下背包，从她手里拿过书。

“You look at that one, and I’ll look at the one about castles,” said Annie. “No, we better not,” said Jack. “We don’t know who these books belong to.” “你看那本，我看这本城堡的，”安妮说。 “不行，我们还是别看了，”杰克说。 “我们不知道这些书是谁的。”

But even as he said this, Jack opened the dinosaur book to where the bookmark was. He couldn’t help himself. 可话虽这么说，杰克还是把恐龙书翻到了书签夹着的地方。他忍不住。

He turned to a picture of an ancient flying reptile. A Pteranodon. 他翻到一张古代会飞的爬行动物的图画。是一只无齿翼龙。

He touched the huge bat-like wings. 他摸了摸那巨大的、像蝙蝠一样的翅膀。

“Wow,” whispered Jack. “I wish I could see a Pteranodon for real.” “哇，”杰克轻声说。 “真希望能亲眼看看无齿翼龙。”

Jack studied the picture of the odd-looking creature soaring through the sky. 杰克仔细看着画里那个模样奇特的生物在天上飞。

“Ahhh!” screamed Annie. “啊——！”安妮尖叫起来。

“What?” said Jack. “怎么了？”杰克说。

“A monster!” Annie cried. She pointed to the tree house window. “有怪物！”安妮喊道。 她指向树屋的窗口。

“Stop pretending, Annie,” said Jack. “别装了，安妮，”杰克说。

“No, really!” said Annie. “不是装的，是真的！”安妮说。

Jack looked out the window. 杰克往窗外看。

A giant creature was gliding above the treetops! He had a long, weird crest on the back of his head. A skinny beak. And huge bat-like wings! 一只巨大的生物正从树梢上空滑过！ 它后脑勺上有一道又长又怪的冠。还有一张细细的喙。 还有巨大的、像蝙蝠一样的翅膀！

It was a real live Pteranodon! 那是一只活生生的无齿翼龙！

The creature curved through the sky. He was coming straight toward the tree house. He looked like a glider plane! 那生物在天空中拐了个弯。 它正朝树屋直冲过来。 它看起来像一架滑翔机！

The leaves trembled. 树叶抖了起来。

Suddenly the creature soared up. High into the sky. Jack nearly fell out the window trying to see it. 忽然，那生物冲上了天。 飞得高高的。 杰克为了看清它，差点从窗口摔出去。

The wind picked up. It was whistling now. 风大了起来。 风开始呼啸。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

“What’s happening?" cried Jack. “怎么回事？”杰克喊道。

“Get down!” shouted Annie. “趴下！”安妮喊道。

She pulled him back from the window. 她把他从窗口拉了回来。

The tree house was spinning. Faster and faster. 树屋在旋转。 越转越快。

Jack squeezed his eyes shut. He held on to Annie. 杰克紧紧闭上眼睛。 他紧紧抓住安妮。

Then everything was still. 然后，一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止。

Jack opened his eyes. Sunlight slanted through the window. There was Annie. The books. His backpack. 杰克睁开眼睛。 阳光斜斜地照进窗口。安妮还在。 书还在。他的背包还在。

The tree house was still high up in an oak tree. But it wasn’t the same oak tree. 树屋还在一棵橡树高高的树上。 可那不是原来那棵橡树了。
