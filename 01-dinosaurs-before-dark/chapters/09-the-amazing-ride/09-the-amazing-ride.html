9. The Amazing Ride 9. 神奇的飞行

The Pteranodon coasted down to the ground. 无齿翼龙滑落到地上。

He stared at Jack with his bright, alert eyes. 它用明亮、机灵的眼睛盯着杰克。

What was Jack supposed to do? Climb on? “But I’m too heavy,” thought Jack. 杰克该怎么办？爬上去？ “可是我太重了。”杰克想。

Don’t think. Just do it. 别想了。快做。

Jack looked at the Tyrannosaurus. 杰克看了看霸王龙。

He was starting up the hill. His giant teeth were flashing in the sunlight. Okay. Don’t think. Just do it! 它开始往山上走。 巨大的牙齿在阳光下闪着光。好。 别想了。快做！

Jack put his book in his pack. Then he eased down onto the Pteranodon’s back. 杰克把书放进背包。 然后他轻轻坐到无齿翼龙背上。

He held on tightly. 他抓得紧紧的。

The creature moved forward. He spread out his wings-and lifted off the ground! 那只生物往前走。 它张开翅膀——飞离了地面！

They teetered this way. Then that. 它们这边晃一下，那边晃一下。

Jack nearly fell off. 杰克差点掉下去。

The Pteranodon steadied himself, then rose into the sky. 无齿翼龙稳住身子，然后升上天空。

Jack looked down. The Tyrannosaurus was chomping the air and staring up at him. 杰克往下看。 霸王龙正对着空气乱咬，抬头盯着他。

The Pteranodon glided away. 无齿翼龙滑翔着离开了。

He sailed over the hilltop. 它飞过山顶。

He circled over the valley. Over all the nests filled with babies. Over all the giant duck-billed dinosaurs. 在山谷上空盘旋。 飞过所有装满宝宝的窝。 飞过所有巨大的鸭嘴龙。

Then the Pteranodon soared out over the plain-over the Triceratops who was grazing in the high grass. 接着无齿翼龙飞向平原——飞过那只正在高草里吃草的三角龙。

It was amazing! It was a miracle! 太神奇了！简直是奇迹！

Jack felt like a bird. As light as a feather. 杰克觉得自己像一只鸟。 轻得像一根羽毛。

The wind was rushing through his hair. The air smelled sweet and fresh. He whooped. He laughed. 风从他头发里穿过。 空气闻起来又香又新鲜。他欢呼了一声。 他笑了。

Jack couldn’t believe it. He was riding on the back of an ancient flying reptile! 杰克简直不敢相信。 他正骑在一只远古飞行动物的背上！

The Pteranodon sailed over the stream, over the ferns and bushes. 无齿翼龙飞过小溪，飞过蕨类和灌木。

Then he carried Jack down to the base of the oak tree. 然后它把杰克带到橡树底下。

When they came to a stop, Jack slid off the creature’s back. And landed on the ground. 停稳后，杰克从它背上滑下来。 落到了地上。

Then the Pteranodon took off again and glided into the sky. 接着无齿翼龙再次起飞，滑进天空。

“Bye, Henry,” whispered Jack. “再见，亨利。”杰克小声说。

“Are you okay?” Annie shouted from the tree house. “你没事吧？”安妮从树屋里喊道。

Jack pushed his glasses into place. He kept staring up at the Pteranodon. “Jack, are you okay?” Annie called. 杰克把眼镜扶正。 他仍盯着天上的无齿翼龙。 “杰克，你没事吧？”安妮喊道。

Jack looked up at Annie. He smiled. 杰克抬头看安妮。他笑了。

“Thanks for saving my life,” he said. “That was really fun.” “谢谢你救了我的命。”他说。 “那真好玩。”

“Climb up!" said Annie. “快爬上来！”安妮说。

Jack tried to stand. His legs were wobbly. 杰克想站起来。 他的腿在发抖。

He felt a bit dizzy. 他有点晕。

“Hurry!” shouted Annie. “He’s coming!” “快点！”安妮喊道。 “它来了！”

Jack looked around. The Tyrannosaurus was heading straight toward him! 杰克四处看了看。 霸王龙正朝他直冲过来！

Jack bolted to the ladder. He grabbed the sides and started up. 杰克冲向梯子。 他抓住两边，开始往上爬。

“Hurry! Hurry!” screamed Annie. “快点！ 快点！”安妮尖叫道。

Jack scrambled into the tree house. 杰克爬进了树屋。

“He’s coming toward the tree!” Annie cried. “它朝树冲过来了！”安妮喊道。

Suddenly something slammed against the oak tree. The tree house shook like a leaf. 突然，什么东西撞上了橡树。 树屋像树叶一样直晃。

Jack and Annie tumbled into the books. 杰克和安妮摔进了书堆里。

“Make a wish!” cried Annie. “快许愿！”安妮喊道。

“We need the book! The one with the picture of Frog Creek!” said Jack. “Where is it?” “我们需要那本书！ 有蛙溪镇图画的那本！”杰克说。 “在哪儿？”

He pushed some books aside. He had to find that book about Pennsylvania. 他把一些书推到一边。 他得找到那本关于宾夕法尼亚的书。

There it was! 找到了！

He grabbed it and tore through it, looking for the photograph of the Frog Creek woods. 他抓过来，飞快翻着，寻找蛙溪镇树林的照片。

He found it! Jack pointed to the picture. 找到了！ 杰克指着那张图画。

The wind began to moan. Softly at first. 风开始呜咽。起先很轻。

“Hurry!” Jack yelled. “快点！”杰克喊道。

The wind picked up. It was whistling now. 风变大了。 现在发出了呼啸声。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 转得越来越快。

Jack closed his eyes. He held on tightly to Annie. Then everything was still. 杰克闭上眼睛。 他紧紧抓住安妮。 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止。
