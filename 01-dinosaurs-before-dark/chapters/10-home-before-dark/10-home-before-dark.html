10. Home Before Dark 10. 天黑前回家

A bird began to sing. 一只鸟开始唱歌。

Jack opened his eyes. He was still pointing at the picture of the Frog Creek woods. 杰克睁开眼睛。 他仍指着蛙溪镇树林的图画。

He peeked out the tree house window. Outside he saw the exact same view. 他从树屋窗口往外看。 外面的景色一模一样。

“We’re home,” whispered Annie. “我们回家了。”安妮小声说。

The woods were lit with a golden late-afternoon light. The sun was about to set. 树林沐浴在金色的傍晚阳光里。 太阳快落山了。

No time had passed since they’d left. “Ja-ack! An-nie!” a voice called from the distance. 他们离开后，时间一点也没过。“杰——克！ 安——妮！”远处有人在喊。

“That’s Mom,” said Annie, pointing. “是妈妈。”安妮指着说。

Jack saw their mother far away. She was standing in front of their house. She looked very tiny. 杰克看见远处的妈妈。 她站在家门前。 看上去小小的。

“An-nie! Ja-ack!” she called. “安——妮！ 杰——克！”她喊道。

Jack still felt dazed. He just stared at Annie. 杰克仍觉得晕乎乎的。 他只是盯着安妮。

“What happened to us?” he said. “我们刚才怎么了？”他说。

“We took a trip in a magic tree house,” said Annie simply. “我们坐神奇树屋去旅行了。”安妮说得很简单。

“But it’s the same time as when we left,” said Jack. “可现在的时间和我们走的时候一样。”杰克说。

Annie shrugged. 安妮耸了耸肩。

“And how did it take us so far away?” said Jack. “And so long ago?” “那它怎么把我们带得那么远？”杰克说。 “还那么久以前？”

“You just looked at a book and said you wished we could go there,” said Annie. “And the magic tree house took us there.” “你只是看了一本书，说希望能去那儿。”安妮说。 “然后神奇树屋就把我们带去了。”

“But how?" said Jack. “And who built this magic tree house? Who put all these books here?” “可是怎么做到的？”杰克说。 “还有，谁建了这座神奇树屋？ 这些书又是谁放这儿的？”

A magic person? 一个魔法师？

“Oh, look,” said Jack. “I almost forgot about this.” He reached into his pocket and pulled out the gold medallion. “Someone lost this back there... in dinosaur land. Look, there’s a letter M on it.” “哦，看。”杰克说。 “差点忘了这个。”他伸进口袋，拿出那枚金色徽章。 “有人把它丢在那儿了……在恐龙的世界。 看，上面有个字母M。”

Annie’s eyes got round. “You think M stands for magic person?” she said. 安妮的眼睛瞪圆了。 “你觉得M是魔法师的意思？”她说。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “I just know someone went to that place before us.” “我不知道。”杰克说。 “我只知道，有人比我们先去过那个地方。”

“Ja-ack! An-nie!” came the distant cry again. “杰——克！ 安——妮！”远处又传来喊声。

Annie poked her head out the window. “Come-ing!” she shouted. 安妮把头探出窗口。 “来——啦！”她喊道。

Jack put the gold medallion back in his pocket. 杰克把金色徽章放回口袋。

He pulled the dinosaur book out of his pack. And put it back with all the other books. 他从背包里拿出恐龙书，放回其他书中间。

Then he and Annie took one last look around the tree house. 然后他和安妮最后看了一眼树屋。

“Good-bye, house,” whispered Annie. “再见，树屋。”安妮小声说。

Jack slung his backpack over his shoulder. He pointed at the ladder. Annie started down. Jack followed. 杰克把背包挎上肩。 他指了指梯子。 安妮先往下爬，杰克跟着。

Seconds later they hopped onto the ground and started walking out of the woods. 几秒后，他们跳到地上，开始往树林外走。

“No one’s going to believe our story,” said Jack. “没人会相信我们的故事。”杰克说。

“Dad won’t believe it,” said Jack. “爸爸不会信。”杰克说。

“He’ll say it was a dream,” said Annie. “他会说那是做梦。”安妮说。

“Mom won’t believe it,” said Jack. “妈妈不会信。”杰克说。

“She’ll say it was pretend.” “她会说那是假装的。”

“My teacher won’t believe it," said Jack. “我的老师也不会信。”杰克说。

“She’ll say you’re nuts,” said Annie. “她会说你疯了。”安妮说。

“We better not tell anyone,” said Jack. “我们最好谁也别告诉。”杰克说。

“I already said that,” said Annie. “我早就说过了。”安妮说。

Jack sighed. “I think I’m starting to not believe it myself,” he said. They left the woods and started up the road toward their house. 杰克叹了口气。 “我觉得我自己都快不信了。”他说。 他们走出树林，沿着路往家走。

As they walked past all the houses on their street, the trip to dinosaur time did seem more and more like a dream. 走过街上一家家房子时，那趟恐龙时代的旅行越来越像一场梦。

Only this world and this time seemed real. 只有这个世界、这个时间才像是真的。

Jack reached into his pocket. He clasped the gold medallion. 杰克把手伸进口袋。 他握住那枚金色徽章。

He felt the engraving of the letter M. It made Jack’s fingers tingle. 他摸到上面刻着的字母M。 杰克的手指一阵发麻。

Jack laughed. Suddenly he felt very happy. 杰克笑了。 突然，他觉得特别开心。

He couldn’t explain what had happened today. But he knew for sure that their trip in the magic tree house had been real. 他说不清今天到底发生了什么。 可他很确定，他们坐神奇树屋的旅行是真的。

Absolutely real. 绝对是真的。

“Tomorrow," Jack said softly, “we’ll go back to the woods.” “明天，”杰克轻轻说，“我们再去树林。”

“Of course,” said Annie. “当然。”安妮说。

“And we’ll climb up to the tree house," said Jack. “然后我们再爬上树屋。”杰克说。

“And we’ll see what happens next,” said Jack. “Of course,” said Annie. “Race you!” “再看看接下来会发生什么。”杰克说。 “当然。”安妮说。“我们赛跑！”

And they took off together, running for home. 于是他们一起跑起来，朝家奔去。
