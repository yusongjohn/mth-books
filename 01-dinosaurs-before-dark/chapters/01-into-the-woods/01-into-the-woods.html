1. Into the Woods 1. 走进树林

“Help! A monster!” said Annie. “救命！ 有怪物！”安妮说。

“Yeah, sure,” said Jack. “A real monster in Frog Creek, Pennsylvania.” “Run, Jack!” said Annie. She ran up the road. “是啊，才怪，”杰克说。 “蛙溪镇、宾夕法尼亚真有怪物。” “快跑，杰克！”安妮说。她顺着路跑了上去。

Oh, brother. 真受不了。

This is what he got for spending time with his seven-year-old sister. Annie loved pretend stuff. But Jack was eight and a half. He liked real things. 这就是他跟七岁的妹妹待在一起的结果。安妮就爱假装玩。 可杰克已经八岁半了。 他喜欢真的东西。

“Watch out, Jack! The monster’s coming! Race you!” “No, thanks,” said Jack. “小心，杰克！ 怪物来了！ 比比谁跑得快！” “不用了，谢谢，”杰克说。

Annie raced alone into the woods. 安妮一个人冲进了树林。

Jack looked at the sky. The sun was about to set. 杰克抬头看了看天。 太阳快要落山了。

“Come on, Annie! It’s time to go home!” “快点，安妮！ 该回家了！”

But Annie had disappeared. 可安妮已经不见了。

Jack waited. 杰克等着。

No Annie. 没有安妮。

“Annie!” he shouted again. “安妮！”他又喊了一声。

“Jack! Jack! Come here!” “杰克！杰克！ 过来！”

Jack groaned. “This better be good,” he said. 杰克哼了一声。 “最好真有什么事，”他说。

Jack left the road and headed into the woods. The trees were lit with a golden late-afternoon light. 杰克离开大路，走进了树林。 傍晚的金光照在树上。

“Come here!” called Annie. “过来！”安妮喊道。

There she was. Standing under a tall oak tree. “Look,” she said. She was pointing at a rope ladder. 她就在那儿。 站在一棵高大的橡树下。 “看，”她说。 她正指着一架绳梯。

The longest rope ladder Jack had ever seen. 那是杰克见过最长的绳梯。

“Wow,” he whispered. “哇，”他轻声说。

The ladder went all the way up to the top of the tree. 梯子一直通到树顶。

There—at the top—was a tree house. It was tucked between two branches. 那儿——树顶上——有一座树屋。 它卡在两根树枝中间。

“That must be the highest tree house in the world,” said Annie. “那一定是世界上最高的树屋，”安妮说。

“Who built it?” asked Jack. “I’ve never seen it before.” “谁建的？”杰克问。 “我以前从没见过。”

“I don’t know. But I’m going up,” said Annie. “我不知道。 可我要上去，”安妮说。

“No. We don’t know who it belongs to," said Jack. “不行。 我们不知道这是谁的，”杰克说。

“Just for a teeny minute,” said Annie. She started up the ladder. “Annie, come back!” “就一小会儿，”安妮说。 她开始往上爬梯子。 “安妮，回来！”

She kept climbing. 她还是往上爬。

Jack sighed. “Annie, it’s almost dark. We have to go home.” 杰克叹了口气。 “安妮，天快黑了。 我们得回家了。”

Annie disappeared inside the tree house. 安妮钻进树屋，不见了。

“An-nie!” “安——妮！”

Jack waited a moment. He was about to call again when Annie poked her head out of the tree house window. 杰克等了一会儿。 他正要再喊，安妮从树屋的窗口探出头来。

“Books!” she shouted. “书！”她喊道。

“What?” “什么？”

“It’s filled with books!" “里面全是书！”

Oh, man! Jack loved books. 哇，太棒了！杰克超爱书。

He pushed his glasses into place. He gripped the sides of the rope ladder, and up he went. 他把眼镜往上推了推。 他抓住绳梯两边，往上爬去。
