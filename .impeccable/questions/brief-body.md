Scope: the whole viewer (sidebar, chapter board, reading column, pager). Mode: Read.

Audience: one adult English learner reading Magic Tree House at a desk in long sessions; English first, Chinese only when stuck. Job: read, keep good sentences, revisit them.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every book is a departure and its time is already in its title (Before Dark, At Dawn, Past Noon, On Sunday). The viewer is a timetable around a calm reading column; it refuses the generic e-reader (grey sidebar, white page, blue accent).

OWN-WORLD: Timetable ink #15212b frame, cool timetable-stock ground #f4f3ee, one signal yellow #f4c21b spent only on "where you are" and "what you kept", route blue-grey for the line. Condensed signage grotesk (Barlow Condensed, self-hosted) for board, timetable and labels with tabular figures; a reading serif (system Charter/Iowan) for English; PingFang for Chinese.

STORY: The reader sees where they are on the line (book, time, stop N of M), reads English uninterrupted, kept sentences carry a yellow tab, and the express mode stops only at kept sentences.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: ink timetable of 55 rows (No. / time from title / destination), current row a yellow bar, its chapters listed as stops. Top of main: ink board with chapter title large left, book + time + stop count right on one line, and a route line of chapter stops below (current stop yellow, stops with kept sentences ticked). Below: 66ch English column, Chinese under each line, controls as board switches at the board's right.
