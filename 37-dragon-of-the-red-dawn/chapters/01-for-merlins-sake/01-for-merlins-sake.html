1. For Merlin’s Sake 1. 为了梅林的缘故

Tap, tap, tap. 叩，叩，叩。

Jack was dreaming that a white bird was pecking at his window. Tap … tap. A red bird appeared and pecked with the white bird. Tap … tap. 杰克正在做梦，一只白鸟正在啄他的窗户。叩……叩。 一只红鸟出现了，和白鸟一起啄。叩……叩。

“Jack, wake up!” said Annie. “杰克，醒醒！”安妮说。

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开了眼睛。

“They’re here!” said Annie. “他们来了！”安妮说。

“Who? The birds?” said Jack. “谁？ 是鸟吗？”杰克说。

“No! Teddy and Kathleen!” Annie rushed to the window and waved outside. “They’re tossing pebbles at our windows.” “不是！ 是泰迪和凯瑟琳！”安妮冲到窗边，向外挥手。 “他们在往我们窗户上扔小石子。”

“Teddy and Kathleen!” Jack jumped out of bed and joined Annie at the window. “是泰迪和凯瑟琳！”杰克从床上跳起来，跑到安妮身边，一起站在窗前。

The two young enchanters of Camelot were standing in Jack and Annie’s front yard. They were dressed in long, dark cloaks. They smiled and waved up at Jack and Annie. 卡梅洛特的两位年轻魔法师正站在杰克和安妮家的前院里。 他们穿着长长的、深色的斗篷。 他们朝杰克和安妮微笑着挥手。

“Merlin must have sent them!” said Jack. “梅林一定派他们来的！”杰克说。

Teddy made a walking motion with his fingers and pointed toward the Frog Creek woods. 泰迪用手指比划出走路的动作，然后指向蛙溪镇的树林。

Annie nodded eagerly. “They want us to meet them at the tree house!” she said to Jack. “Hurry and get dressed! Before Mom and Dad wake up!” 安妮兴奋地点头。 “他们想让我们在树屋那儿见他们！”她对杰克说。 “快点穿上衣服！ 在爸爸妈妈醒来之前！”，

Annie started out of Jack’s room. When she got to the door, she turned. “Oh, and don’t forget to bring the Wand of Dianthus!” 安妮从杰克的房间走了出去。 她走到门口时，转过身来。 “哦，还有，别忘了带上迪安瑟斯魔杖！”，

Jack threw on his clothes. He grabbed his backpack and peeked inside. The wand was there. Jack put his pack on his back. Then he slipped quietly downstairs and out the door. 杰克赶紧穿上衣服。 他抓起背包，往里面看了一眼。 魔杖就在里面。 杰克把背包背在了背上。 然后他悄悄地溜下楼，走出了门。

Annie was standing on the front porch. “Let’s go!” she said. 安妮正站在前门廊上。 “我们走吧！”她说。

Jack and Annie ran across their yard and dashed up the sidewalk. 杰克和安妮跑过他们的院子，冲上了人行道。

“I wonder why they came for us!” said Annie. “我不知道他们为什么来找我们！”安妮说。

“I wonder where we’re going!” said Jack. “我不知道我们要去哪儿！”杰克说。

“I wonder everything! ” said Annie. “我什么都不知道！”安妮说。

Jack and Annie crossed the street and hurried into the Frog Creek woods. The early-March trees looked weary from winter, gray and brown with no leaves on them yet. 杰克和安妮穿过街道，赶紧跑进蛙溪镇的树林里。 三月初的树木看起来因为冬天而疲惫不堪，灰灰的、棕棕的，还没有叶子。

“Look—” said Annie, out of breath. “They’re waiting for us!” “看——”安妮气喘吁吁地说。 “它们在等我们！”

Jack looked up. Teddy and Kathleen were waving from the window of the magic tree house. 杰克抬头看了看。 泰迪和凯瑟琳正从魔法树屋的窗户里挥手。

Jack grabbed the rope ladder and started up. Annie followed. When Jack and Annie climbed inside the tree house, they threw their arms around Teddy and Kathleen. 杰克抓住绳梯开始往上爬，安妮跟在后面。 当杰克和安妮爬进树屋时，他们立刻张开双臂抱住了泰迪和凯瑟琳。

“We’re so glad to see you!” cried Annie. “我们太高兴见到你们了！”安妮喊道。

“And we are happy to see you, also,” said Kathleen. The sea girl’s lovely water-blue eyes sparkled. “我们也非常高兴见到你们，”凯瑟琳说。 海姑娘漂亮水蓝色的眼睛闪闪发亮。

“Indeed,” said Teddy. “It has been too long.” “确实如此，”泰迪说。 “已经太久了。”

“What’s our mission this time?” asked Jack. “Where’s Merlin sending us?” “这次我们的任务是什么？”杰克问。 “梅林要我们去哪？”

Teddy glanced at Kathleen. “I fear Merlin does not even know we are here,” said Teddy. “We have come not at his bidding, but for his sake.” 泰迪看了看凯瑟琳。 “我担心梅林甚至都不知道我们在这里，”泰迪说。 “我们不是听从他的命令来的，而是为了他。”

“What does that mean?” asked Jack. “这是什么意思？”杰克问。

“Merlin is not well,” said Kathleen. “He complains that he is getting old and feeble and that life is full of sorrows. He does not eat or sleep.” “梅林身体不好，”凯瑟琳说。 “他抱怨自己变老了，变得虚弱了，还说生活中充满了悲伤。” 他不吃也不睡。”

“Oh, no!” said Annie. “哦，不！”安妮说。

“All of Camelot wishes to help him,” said Teddy. “But no one knows quite how.” “整个卡梅洛特都希望帮助他，”泰迪说。 “但没人知道该怎么做。”

“What can we do to help?” asked Jack. “我们能做些什么来帮忙呢？”杰克问。

Teddy picked up a book from the corner of the tree house. “Throughout the ages, people all over the world have sought the secrets of happiness,” he said. “Morgan wants you to search for four of these secrets to share with Merlin. She believes that the first one might be found here.” 泰迪从树屋角落捡起了一本书。 “在漫长的历史中，世界各地的人都在寻找幸福的秘密，”他说。 “摩根希望你去寻找这四个秘密中的四个，然后和梅林分享。 她认为第一个秘密可能就在这里。”

Jack took the book from Teddy. He read the title aloud. 杰克从泰迪那里拿起了书。 他大声读出了书名。

图中文字：A Journey to Old Japan 图中文字：去往古老日本的旅程

“Oh, wow, we’ve been to Japan before!” said Annie. “哦，哇，我们以前去过日本！”，安妮说。

“Before we met you,” Jack said to Teddy and Kathleen. “We had an adventure with ninjas.” “在遇见你们之前，”，杰克对泰迪和凯瑟琳说， “我们和忍者一起经历过一场冒险。”

“Yes, Morgan told us,” said Teddy. “But she said that on that journey, you visited the countryside. This time you must travel to the capital city.” “是的，摩根告诉过我们，”泰迪说。 “但她说过，在那次旅程中，你们去了乡村。 这次你们必须去首都城市。”

“Are you guys coming with us?” asked Annie. “你们要和我们一起去吗？”安妮问。

“I am afraid not,” said Kathleen. “We must return to Camelot now to help Morgan. Since Merlin has fallen ill, she has taken on much of his work.” “恐怕不能，”凯瑟琳说。 “我们必须现在返回卡梅洛特去帮助摩根。” 自从梅林生病以来，她承担了他很多工作。”

“You have the wand, do you not?” asked Teddy. “你有魔杖，对不对？”泰迪问。

“Yep,” said Jack. He reached into his backpack and took out the Wand of Dianthus. The spiraled wand was shaped like a unicorn’s horn. “对，”杰克说。 他把手伸进背包，拿出了一根迪安瑟斯魔杖。 那根螺旋形的魔杖形状像独角兽的角。

“With the help of the wand, you will make your own magic,” said Teddy. “有了这根魔杖，你们就能创造自己的魔法，”泰迪说。

“That’s what Merlin said when he gave it to us,” said Annie. “这是梅林把它交给我们的时候说的，”安妮说。

“But he didn’t say how,” said Jack. “但他没说怎么创造，”杰克说。

“It is very simple,” said Teddy. “The wand has three rules. First, it only works for the good of others. The wand can never be used for selfish reasons.” “这非常简单，”泰迪说。 “这支魔杖有三条规则。” 第一条是，它只能用来帮助别人。 这支魔杖绝不能用来满足自私的愿望。”

“Second, the wand works only after you have tried your very hardest without its help,” said Kathleen. “Do not attempt to use its magic too quickly.” “第二条是，只有在你非常努力尝试过，但没有它的帮助仍无法完成时，它才会起作用，”凯瑟琳说。 “不要急着使用它的魔法。”

“And third, the wand only works with a command of five words,” said Teddy. “So you must choose your words carefully.” “第三条是，魔杖只有在你说出五个字的指令时才会起作用，”泰迪说。 “所以，你必须仔细选择你说的话。”

“Can we review all that, please?” asked Jack. “请让我们再复习一遍这些规则好吗？”杰克问。

“Don’t worry, I’ve got it,” said Annie. “We have to go. We have to help Merlin as soon as we can.” “别担心，我记住了，”安妮说。 “我们必须出发了。” “我们必须尽快帮助梅林。”

“If the tree house takes us to Japan, how will you go back to Camelot?” Jack asked Teddy and Kathleen. “如果树屋带我们去了日本，你们怎么才能回到卡梅洛特呢？”杰克问泰迪和凯瑟琳。

Teddy and Kathleen held up their hands. They each wore a sparkling blue ring. “Our magic rings will take us home,” said Kathleen. 泰迪和凯瑟琳举起了手。 他们每人手上都戴着一个闪闪发光的蓝色戒指。 “我们的魔法戒指会带我们回家，”凯瑟琳说。

“And this book from Camelot’s library will bring you back home to Frog Creek,” said Teddy, “after you have completed your mission.” He picked up another book lying in a corner. It was the book about Pennsylvania that Jack and Annie had used on their first magic tree house adventures. “而且这本来自卡梅洛特图书馆的书会带你们回到蛙溪镇，”泰迪说，“在你们完成任务之后。”他捡起角落里另一本书。 那是杰克和安妮第一次进行魔法树屋冒险时用过的宾夕法尼亚州的书。

“Thanks,” said Jack. “谢谢，”杰克说。

“Good-bye,” said Annie. “Take good care of Merlin.” “再见，”安妮说。 “要好好照顾梅林。”

“We will try,” said Kathleen. She and Teddy raised their magic rings to their lips. They whispered words too softly for Jack and Annie to hear, then blew on the rings. As they blew, the young sorcerers began to fade into the cool morning air. In a moment, they had disappeared completely. “我们试试看，”凯瑟琳说。 她和泰迪把魔法戒指举到嘴边。 他们低声念出一些话，杰克和安妮听不见，然后对着戒指吹气。 当他们吹气时，年轻的巫师开始逐渐消失在清晨的凉爽空气中。 片刻之后，他们完全消失了。

Silence filled the tree house. 树屋里充满了寂静。

Annie turned to Jack. “Ready?” she said. 安妮转向杰克。 “准备好了吗？”，她说。

Jack nodded. He pointed to the cover of the Japan book. “I wish we could go there! ” he said. 杰克点了点头。 他指着日本书的封面。 “我希望我们能去那里！”他说。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
