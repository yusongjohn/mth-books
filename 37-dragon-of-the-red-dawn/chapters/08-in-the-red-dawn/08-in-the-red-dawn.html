8. In the Red Dawn 8. 在红日黎明

“A re you—are you okay?” Annie asked Jack. She was still holding the wand. Her pigtails blew in the bright wind. “你——你没事吧？”安妮问杰克。 她仍然握着那根魔杖。 她的两个小辫子在明亮的风中飘动。

“Yeah, yeah, but what happened?” said Jack in a daze. He was freezing and out of breath. “Where are we?” “是啊，是啊，但是发生了什么？”杰克迷迷糊糊地说。 他冷得发抖，喘不过气来。 “我们这是在哪里？”

“I don’t know,” whispered Annie. “我不知道，”安妮小声说。

Jack shielded his eyes from the brilliant light of the red dawn and looked around. Pink clouds floated through the air like piles of cotton candy. Through a gap in the clouds, he saw hills shrouded in black smoke below. Beneath the smoke, flames rose from the city of Edo. 杰克用手遮住眼睛，避开红日黎明耀眼的光芒，然后环顾四周。 粉红色的云朵在空中飘动，就像一堆堆棉花糖。 云朵中间出现了一个缺口，他看见下面的山丘被黑烟笼罩着。 黑烟之下，火焰从江户城升起。

“I think we’re on Mount Fuji,” said Annie. “我想我们是在富士山，”安妮说。

“Mount Fuji?” said Jack. “That’s crazy! Why are we here?” He stopped to catch his breath. He felt dizzy and light-headed. “Edo’s burning! We should be there!” “富士山？”杰克说。 “那太疯狂了！ 我们怎么会在这里？”他停下来喘口气。 他感到头晕晕的，轻飘飘的。 “江户城在燃烧！ 我们应该去那里！”

“Maybe the wand didn’t understand,” said Annie. “Maybe it was trying to save us by taking us far away from the fire.” “也许魔杖没听懂，”安妮说。 “也许它想通过把我们带到远离火的地方来救我们。”

Suddenly a great mass of thick clouds piled up, ringing the mountaintop like a wall. The clouds swirled and whirled and tumbled. They changed color, from rose to gold to gray to white. 突然，一大团厚厚的云朵堆积起来，像一堵墙一样环绕着山顶。 云朵旋转、翻滚、翻腾。 它们的颜色不断变化，从玫瑰色变成金色，再变成灰色，最后变成白色。

“What’s going on?” cried Jack. “怎么回事？”杰克喊道。

The head of a gigantic monster rose from the bubbling clouds! 一团巨大的怪物的头从翻腾的云中升了起来！

“AHHH!” Jack and Annie screamed. They grabbed each other and crouched down on the rocky ledge. “啊——！”杰克和安妮尖叫起来。 他们紧紧抓住彼此，蹲在岩石边缘。

The monster had spiky eyebrows and long, curled whiskers. It had the horns of a deer, the forked tongue of a snake, and the fiery breath of a dragon. Through the swirling clouds, Jack and Annie could see the dragon’s snake-like body curling through the clouds and down the mountainside. Its back was covered with shiny scales. Its spine had a row of shark-like fins. 这个怪物有尖尖的眉毛和长长的卷曲胡须。 它有鹿的角，蛇的分叉舌头，还有龙的喷火能力。 在翻滚的云层中，杰克和安妮看到龙蛇一样的身体在云层中盘旋，然后向下爬到山腰。 它的背上覆盖着闪亮的鳞片。 它的脊背上有一排像鲨鱼一样的鳍。

The dragon reached out its claws. They were like the claws of an eagle—only a thousand times bigger! The claws gripped the side of the mountain. 龙伸出它的爪子。 它们就像老鹰的爪子——只不过大了整整一千倍！ 爪子抓住了山的侧面。

Jack made himself as small as he could. He covered his head. But Annie jumped to her feet. “I get it!” she cried. “I know what’s happening! Thank you for coming!” 杰克让自己尽可能地缩成一团。 他捂住了自己的头。 但安妮跳了起来。 “我明白了！”她喊道。 “我知道发生了什么！ 谢谢你们来！”

“Annie, get down!” Jack shouted. “安妮，下来！”杰克喊道。

“Jack, it’s the Cloud Dragon!” said Annie. “The puppet show—remember? The wand sent her here!” “杰克，是云龙！”安妮说道。 “木偶戏——还记得吗？ “魔杖把她送到了这里！”

“What? Why?” cried Jack. “什么？为什么？”杰克喊道。

“She makes rain! Don’t you remember? Rain!” cried Annie. “ She commands the rain clouds!” “她让下雨了！ 你不记得了吗？ 下雨了！”安妮喊道。 “她控制着雨云！”

The dragon lowered her giant head, stretching it over the mountain ledge. Her scales glittered honey-gold in the dawn light. She was still, very still, as if she were waiting for something. 巨龙低下她巨大的头，把它伸到山崖上方。 在黎明的阳光下，她的鳞片闪烁着蜂蜜般的金色。 她一动不动，非常安静，仿佛在等待着什么。

“Come on! We have to climb on her back!” shouted Annie. “快点！ 我们必须爬到她背上！”安妮喊道。

“Why?” cried Jack. “为什么？”杰克喊道。

“We have to go with her!” said Annie. “The wand brought us to the dragon! Now it’s up to us to show her what to do!” “我们必须跟她走！”安妮说。 “魔杖把我们带到了巨龙这里！” 现在就看我们的了，得教她怎么做！

“Okay! Okay!” said Jack. “好吧！好吧！”杰克说。

Annie climbed onto the back of the Cloud Dragon. She sat between two of the dragon’s shark-like fins. Jack climbed on behind her. He gripped the fin in front of him as if he were holding on to the horn of a saddle. 安妮爬上了云龙的背上。 她坐在龙那像鲨鱼一样的两个鳍之间。 杰克爬到她后面。 他紧紧抓住面前的鳍，就像抓住了马鞍上的角一样。

“Fly over the fire!” yelled Annie. “Make rain!” “飞过火堆！”安妮喊道。 “下大雨！”，

“Lots of it!” shouted Jack. “下很多很多！”杰克喊道。

The Cloud Dragon slid off the mountain ledge. Jack trembled with cold as the monster slithered through the freezing sea of clouds, like a snake through the grass. 云龙从山崖上滑了下来。 杰克冷得发抖，那怪物在冰冷的云海中滑行，就像蛇在草丛中穿行一样。

Above Edo, Jack looked down. Billowing black smoke and red flames shot into the dawn sky. 杰克在江户上方低头望去。 滚滚的黑烟和红色火焰冲向黎明的天空。

“Now! Rain now!” Jack said. The Cloud Dragon reared back her head. Great black clouds flowed from her mouth. The clouds spread across the sky. There was a crack of thunder and a flash of lightning. Then rain began to pour down on the city. “现在！ 下雨了！”杰克说。 云龙扬起她的头。 大量黑色的云朵从她嘴里涌出。 云朵在天空中蔓延开来。 突然传来一声雷响，一道闪电划破天际。 然后，雨开始倾泻在城市上。

The dragon twisted her head this way and that. More clouds blew from her mouth, and more rain fell on the burning city of Edo. 龙扭着她的头，这边看看，那边看看。 她嘴里又吹出更多的云，更多的雨落在燃烧的江户城上。

As the dragon slithered through the sky, breathing out mountains of storm clouds, rain fell on the flat lands of the rice fields, on the Imperial Garden, the samurai castles, the fish market, and the Great Bridge. 龙在天空中滑行，吐出成山的乌云，雨水落在稻田的平地上，落在皇宫花园、武士城堡、鱼市和大桥上。

Rain fell on the floating world of inns and stages, teahouses, temples, and farms. 雨水落在旅馆和戏台漂浮的世界，落在茶馆、寺庙和农田上。

Rain fell on the lumberyard and the pine forest and the canals. 雨水落在木料场、松树林和运河上。

Slowly the rain washed away all the smoke and flames. Even after the fires of Edo no longer flickered, the Cloud Dragon breathed more black clouds, and the rain kept falling, falling steadily, soaking the gardens and fields, filling the shallow rivers and dried-up ponds. 慢慢地，雨水冲走了所有的烟和火焰。 即使江户的火已经熄灭，云龙仍然吐出更多的黑云，雨水继续下着，下得稳稳的，浸湿了花园和田野，填满了浅浅的河流和干涸的池塘。

“The fire’s out!” Jack shouted. “火灭了！”杰克喊道。

“Take us to Basho’s!” Annie yelled to the dragon. “带我们去芭蕉的！”安妮对龙大喊。

The Cloud Dragon twisted her giant body. She slid through the dark clouds. Then she reared up—until Jack and Annie couldn’t hold on any longer and they were falling backward, somersaulting through the air! 云龙扭动着她巨大的身体。 她滑进了黑暗的云层里。 然后她猛地扬起身子——直到杰克和安妮再也抓不住了，他们向后倒下，在空中翻滚着掉下去！

SPLASH! SPLASH! 扑通！扑通！

Jack sank to the bottom of the river. He flailed his arms and struggled to the surface. His head bobbed up—he spewed out water and gasped for air. He had lost his glasses. He dove back down and grabbed them from the river bottom, then swam back to the surface. Treading water, he wiped his glasses, then put them on. 杰克沉到了河底。 他挥舞着双臂，努力游到水面。 他的头浮了上来——他吐出水，急促地喘着气。 他弄丢了眼镜。 他再次潜入水中，从河底捡起了眼镜，然后游回水面。 在水里踩着水，他擦了擦眼镜，然后戴上。

“Hi!” Annie yelled. She was holding the wand in the air, above the water. “你好！”安妮大喊。 她举着魔杖，高高悬在水面上方。

“Hi!” Jack yelled back. “你好！”杰克大声喊道。

Jack and Annie swam toward the bank of the river. They swam until their feet could touch bottom. Then they dragged themselves out of the water and collapsed on the muddy bank. 杰克和安妮朝河岸游去。 他们一直游到脚能碰到河底。 然后他们把自己从水里拖出来，倒在泥泞的河岸上。

They had lost their socks and sandals. Their silk kimonos clung to their bodies. Dripping wet, they gasped for breath and looked up at the sky. 他们失去了袜子和凉鞋。 丝绸和服紧紧贴在他们身上。 浑身湿透的他们喘着粗气，抬头看向天空。

Big drops of rain splashed on their faces. There was no sign of the Cloud Dragon. But the cool rain kept falling, bathing the floating world of Edo. 大雨的水滴砸在他们脸上。 天空中没有云龙的踪影。 但是凉爽的雨一直下着，淋湿了江户漂浮的世界。
