7. Clang, Clang, Clang! 7. 咚，咚，咚！

Clang, clang, clang! 咚，咚，咚！

Jack opened his eyes. The sound of bells filled the night—not the gentle ringing of the temple bells but a harsh clanging. 杰克睁开了眼睛。 夜晚响起了钟声——不是寺庙里温柔的钟声，而是刺耳的撞击声。

Jack smelled smoke. He and Annie threw off their mosquito nets and stumbled to the door. 杰克闻到了烟味。 他和安妮掀开蚊帐，跌跌撞撞地跑向门口。

Basho was standing in his yard, looking at the dawn sky. It was black with smoke. The bells kept clanging. 巴沙站在他的院子里，看着黎明的天空。 天空被烟遮得漆黑一片。 钟声还在不断响起。

“Is there a fire?” asked Jack. “着火了吗？”杰克问。

“Yes,” said Basho. “It must be very big, for the bells do not stop ringing from the watch-tower. This is what we have feared most. I must go and help the firefighters.” “是的，”巴肖说。 “火一定很大，因为瞭望塔上的钟一直响个不停。 这就是我们最害怕的事情。 我得去帮消防员了。

“We’ll help, too,” said Jack. “我们也想帮忙，”杰克说。

“No, stay here,” said Basho. He pulled on his socks and sandals, then grabbed a wooden bucket by the door. “If the fire gets close, wade into the river, where you will be safe.” “不，你们待在这里，”巴肖说。 他穿上袜子和拖鞋，然后拿起门边的一个木桶。 “如果火靠近了，就蹚进河里，那里你们会很安全。

“But we want to help!” said Annie. “但我们想帮忙！”安妮说。

“Yes, wait for us!” said Jack. He and Annie pulled on their socks and sandals. “是的，等我们一下！”杰克说。 他和安妮穿上袜子和凉鞋。

“Come, then,” said Basho. “But if the fire begins to spread, you must promise to return here to the river.” “那快过来吧，”巴肖说。 “但如果火开始蔓延，你们必须答应回到这里，回到河边。”

“We promise!” said Annie. “我们答应！”安妮说。

“Then bring the other bucket and follow me,” said Basho. “那把另一个桶拿来，然后跟着我，”巴肖说。

“I’ll get it,” said Jack. He hurried into the hut and grabbed the wooden bucket by the hearth. It was heavy, even without water in it. Jack hugged the bucket to his chest and rushed back outside. “我来拿，”杰克说。 他赶紧跑进小屋，拿起壁炉边的木桶。 即使里面没有水，它也很重。 杰克把桶抱在胸前，赶紧跑回外面。

Jack and Annie followed Basho through the pine forest. They passed a farmhouse where two small children stood outside, looking at the fiery sky. 杰克和安妮跟着巴肖穿过了松树林。 他们经过了一座农舍，两个小孩站在外面，看着火红的天空。

“Our father says the lumberyard near the river is burning!” the boy shouted to Basho. “我们爸爸说河边的木材厂着火了！”男孩朝巴肖喊道。

“He has gone to help fight the fire!” said the girl. “Great piles of wood are burning!” “他已经去帮忙灭火了！”女孩说。 “一大堆木材正在燃烧！”

The fire bells kept clanging as Basho, Jack, and Annie rushed past the temple and across the narrow footbridge. 火铃铛不停地响着，巴肖、杰克和安妮匆匆跑过庙宇，穿过狭窄的木桥。

They hurried up the winding dirt path until they came to the shopping market. In the smoky red dawn, people were pushing carts piled high with goods. They were running away from the fire. 他们沿着蜿蜒的土路小跑，直到来到集市。 在烟雾弥漫的红色黎明中，人们推着装满货物的购物车。 他们正逃离火灾。

But Basho, Jack, and Annie ran toward the fire. The air grew hotter and smokier near the teahouses and the performance stages. Sparks flew through the sky. Tiles on rooftops were catching fire and crashing to the ground. 但巴肖、杰克和安妮却朝火场跑去。 空气在茶馆和表演舞台附近变得又热又烟雾缭绕。 火星在天空中飞舞。 屋顶上的瓦片着了火，然后掉落到地上。

Basho led Jack and Annie farther through the smoke until they came to the lumberyard. The fire roared as it burned piles of logs. Flames rose high into the sky. 巴肖带着杰克和安妮穿过烟雾，直到他们来到木材厂。 火苗燃烧着木头堆，发出轰隆轰隆的声音。 火焰升到天空中很高很高的地方。

Firefighters were passing buckets of water up a line from the river to the fire. Others waved huge fans to beat back the windblown flames, while the most daring worked with hooks and axes, trying to separate the burning timber. 消防员们正沿着从河里到火场的绳索传递水桶。 其他人挥舞着大扇子，试图挡住被风吹来的火焰，而最勇敢的人则用钩子和斧头努力把燃烧的木材分开。

“Help the bucket brigade!” Basho said to Jack and Annie. “Get water from the river!” “帮帮忙，水桶队！”巴肖对杰克和安妮说。 “去河里取水！”

Basho rushed to help the men beating back the fire with fans while Jack and Annie hurried down to the river. Jack filled their wooden bucket. With water inside, it was so heavy he couldn’t lift it. 巴肖赶紧跑去帮忙，用扇子帮那些人挡住火势，而杰克和安妮则赶快跑下河去。 杰克把水桶装满。 水桶里装了水后，实在太重了，他根本抬不起来。

“Do it together!” said Annie. “一起抬！”安妮说。

“Right!” said Jack. “对！”杰克说。

Using all their strength, Jack and Annie carried their bucket up the bank of the river. As they stumbled along, they tried not to spill the water. Jack could hardly breathe in the smoke-filled air. His throat and eyes burned. His face felt red-hot. Finally, when he thought he couldn’t take another step, they got to the bucket brigade. They gave their bucket to the person at the end, who gave them an empty bucket. “Get more!” he said. 用尽全力，杰克和安妮一起把水桶抬上河岸。 他们跌跌撞撞地走着，努力不让水洒出来。 杰克在充满烟雾的空气中几乎喘不过气来。 他的喉咙和眼睛都火辣辣地疼。 他的脸感觉像被火烧一样热。 终于，当他觉得自己再也走不动的时候，他们到了水桶接力队那里。 他们把水桶递给队尾的人，那个人又给了他们一个空水桶。 “再来一桶！”他说。

Jack and Annie hurried back to the river with the empty bucket. They filled it with water and then struggled back up the bank. 杰克和安妮赶紧拿着空水桶跑回河边。 他们装满水，然后又吃力地爬回岸边。

Over and over, Jack and Annie hauled buckets of water back and forth from the river to the line of firefighters. Everyone worked hard to battle the great blaze. But the flames kept shooting into the sky. Eventually the fire leapt over the river, and timber on the far bank began to burn. 一遍又一遍，杰克和安妮把一桶桶水从河边运到消防员的队伍那里。 每个人都努力工作，试图扑灭这场大火。 但是火焰还是不断冲向天空。 最后，火势跳过了河，对岸的树木开始燃烧起来。

“Oh, no!” cried a woman. “All of Edo will catch fire now!” “哦，天哪！”一个女人喊道。 “整个江户现在都会着火！”

“The rice-storage houses will burn down!” said a man. “The harvest will be destroyed!” “储存大米的房子会被烧毁！”一个人说。 “收成会被毁掉！”

Several people began weeping. Jack felt like crying, too. Right in front of his eyes, the beautiful floating world of Edo was about to go up in flames. “This is hopeless!” he said to Annie. 几个人开始哭泣。 杰克也想哭。 就在他眼前，美丽的江户浮世即将被火焰吞噬。 “这太绝望了！”他对安妮说。

“No, it’s not!” Annie said. “The wand! We can use the wand!” “不，不是的！”安妮说。 “魔杖！ 我们可以用魔杖！”

“Of course!” cried Jack. “But it’s in my bag! Back at Basho’s house!” “当然！”杰克喊道。 “但它在我的包里！ 回到巴肖的家！

“We have to get it!” said Annie. She shouted to Basho, “Basho, we’re going back to your castle!” “我们必须拿到它！”安妮说。 她朝巴肖喊道：“巴肖，我们要回到你的城堡！”

“Yes, run for safety!” called Basho. “Jump into the river!” “对，快去安全的地方！”巴肖喊道。 “跳进河里！”

“Right!” cried Jack. “对！”杰克喊道。

“Be careful!” Annie shouted to Basho. Then she and Jack started running as fast as they could. They raced past the market. They ran across the footbridge, past the temple and the farmhouse, and through the grove of pines to Basho’s house. “小心！”安妮对巴肖喊道。 然后她和杰克尽全力开始奔跑。 他们飞快地跑过了市场。 他们跑过人行桥，经过神庙和农舍，穿过松树林，跑到巴肖的家。

Jack and Annie ran inside the tiny hut. Jack grabbed his bag and pulled out the wand. He waved it through the air. “Do something to make the fire stop!” he shouted. 杰克和安妮跑进了那间小屋。 杰克抓起他的包，拿出魔杖。 他挥舞着魔杖在空中划过。 “快做点什么，让火停下来！”他喊道。

Jack held his breath and waited. 杰克屏住呼吸，等待着。

“Let me try!” said Annie. She took the wand and waved it. “Stop the fire over Edo now! ” she shouted. “让我来试试！”安妮说。 她拿起魔杖，挥舞起来。 “现在立刻熄灭江户的火！”她喊道。

Jack and Annie waited again. 杰克和安妮又等了一会儿。

“It’s not working!” cried Jack. “We must be doing something wrong.” “这不管用！”杰克喊道。 “我们一定哪里做错了。”

“But this is for the good of everyone!” said Annie. “但这是为了大家好！”安妮说。

“I know, I know!” said Jack. “我知道，我知道！”杰克说。

“And we’ve tried our hardest!” said Annie. “Everybody has!” “我们已经尽力了！”安妮说。 “大家都尽力了！”

“Five words!” said Jack. “We have to use five words!” “五个词！”杰克说。 “我们必须用五个词！”

“Oh, right!” said Annie. She waved the wand through the air again. “Put. Out. The. Fire!” she yelled. “哦，对了！”安妮说。 她又挥舞着魔杖。 “熄灭。 火。 ！”她喊道。

“One more word!” cried Jack. “再说话就完蛋了！”杰克喊道。

“Please! ” shouted Annie. “求求你！”安妮喊道。

Jack and Annie were blasted by a blinding light. Jack felt himself shooting through brightness, then darkness, then back into light. An icy wind blew. The air was crystal-clear. Early sunlight flashed on rock. 杰克和安妮被一道刺眼的强光击中了。 杰克感觉自己穿过一片明亮，然后是黑暗，接着又回到光明。一阵冰冷的风吹来。 空气非常清澈。 清晨的阳光闪现在岩石上。

Jack and Annie were standing on the ledge of a mountain. 杰克和安妮正站在山崖上。
