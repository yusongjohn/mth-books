3. Her Imperial Majesty 3. 她的帝国陛下

“Thanks for your help, Josef,” said Annie. “谢谢你的帮助，约瑟夫，”安妮说。

“Yeah, thanks a lot,” said Jack. “是啊，非常感谢，”杰克说。

“You’re very welcome,” said Josef. “I hope you will enjoy yourselves at the party.” “不用谢，”约瑟夫说。 “希望你们在派对上玩得开心。”

“ We will,” said Annie. “Bye.” “我们会的，”安妮说。 “再见。”

As the coachman climbed back into his carriage, Annie turned to Jack. “This is so cool!” she said. 当车夫爬回他的马车时，安妮转向杰克。 “这太酷了！”，她说。

Not really, thought Jack. He didn’t know how he was supposed to act with Her Imperial Majesty whatever-the-rest-of-her-name-was! His hands felt clammy. “What do we do when we meet her? What do we say?” he asked. 其实不是，杰克心想。 他不知道该怎么和那位不管她全名是什么的皇太后相处！ 他的手心感觉湿漉漉的。 “我们见到她的时候该怎么办？ 我们该说什么？”他问。

“ We just watch what other people do,” said Annie, “and we copy them. Come on.” “我们只是观察别人怎么做，”安妮说，“然后模仿他们。来吧。”

Jack and Annie walked over to the sweeping stairway. They started up the steps, following the other party guests. The women all wore glittering jewels and gowns with giant hoopskirts. The men wore wigs with white curls flowing down over the collars of their long coats. All the clothes were made of silk and satin and velvet in rich colors and patterns. 杰克和安妮走到那条长长的楼梯前。 他们开始走上台阶，跟着其他派对的客人走。 女人们都戴着闪闪发光的珠宝，穿着裙摆巨大的蓬蓬裙。 男人们戴着假发，白色的卷发从他们长外套的领子上垂下来。 所有的衣服都是用丝绸、缎子和天鹅绒做的，颜色和图案都很漂亮。

“Oh, brother,” said Jack. “哦，天哪，”杰克说。

“What?” said Annie. “什么？”安妮说。

“There’s that kid who yelled at me,” Jack said. “就是那个朝我大喊大叫的小孩，”杰克说。

The small boy from the blue coach was standing at the top of the stairs. He wore a lilac-colored coat with gold braid. A tiny sword hung from his side. 那辆蓝色马车里的小男孩正站在楼梯的最上面。 他穿着一件淡紫色的外套，上面有金色的花纹。 他腰间挂着一把小剑。

“A sword?” said Jack. “That’s ridiculous. He can’t be more than four or five years old.” “一把剑？”杰克说。 “这太荒谬了。 他最多才四五岁而已。”

The boy turned and caught sight of Jack and Annie. A big smile crossed his round face. He waved at them. 那个男孩转过身，看见了杰克和安妮。 他圆圆的脸上露出一个大大的笑容。他朝他们挥手。

“He’s cute,” said Annie. She waved back. “他真可爱，”安妮说。她也朝他挥手。

“Not really,” said Jack. “其实不然，”杰克说。

A man grabbed the boy’s hand and pulled him into the palace. 一个男人抓住男孩的手，把他拉进了宫殿。

“And he thought I looked funny,” said Jack. “How’s my wig now?” “他认为我看起来很滑稽，”杰克说。 “我的假发现在怎么样了？”

Annie giggled. “It’s crooked again,” she said. “And your ears are poking out. Hold on.” 安妮咯咯地笑了。 “又歪了，”她说。 “而且你的耳朵露出来了。抓紧了。”

Jack and Annie stopped on the stairs. Annie grabbed both sides of Jack’s wig and gave it a good yank. 杰克和安妮停在了楼梯上。 安妮抓住了杰克假发的两边，用力一拉。

“Move along, children. You’re holding up the line!” a woman behind them said. “快走吧，孩子们。 你们挡着路了！”后面的一位女士说。

Annie picked up her hoopskirt, and she and Jack hurried up the stairs. At the top, Jack pulled their invitation out of his pocket again. He led Annie into the palace and showed the invitation to a guard in a red uniform. 安妮提起她的蓬蓬裙，她和杰克赶紧跑上楼梯。 到了楼梯顶端，杰克又从口袋里拿出邀请函。 他带安妮走进宫殿，把邀请函展示给一位穿红色制服的卫兵。

“Follow the line through the lantern room and into the Great Rosa Room,” the guard said. “跟着队伍穿过灯笼室，进入伟大的罗莎大厅，”卫兵说。

Jack saw a line of party guests walking slowly through a candlelit room. He and Annie quickly joined the line. The room was filled with sounds of whispering and the rustling of silk. 杰克看到一队参加派对的客人正慢慢地穿过一个点着蜡烛的房间。 他和安妮很快加入了队伍。 房间里充满了窃窃私语和丝绸摩擦的声音。

A young girl in a white dress with red roses was standing near Jack and Annie. When the line moved, Jack waited for the girl to go ahead. 一个穿着白色连衣裙、上面绣着红玫瑰的年轻女孩正站在杰克和安妮旁边。 当队伍向前移动时，杰克等着女孩先走。

The girl smiled. “I’m not in line,” she said in a soft voice. “I’m waiting for my brother.” 女孩微笑了。 “我不在队伍里，”她用轻柔的声音说。 “我在等我哥哥。”

Jack nodded, and he and Annie stepped forward. Jack craned his neck to get a better view inside the Great Rosa Room. He couldn’t see Her Imperial Majesty yet, but he could see part of the fancy room with its red velvet chairs and gold-trimmed, glossy white walls. 杰克点了点头，他和安妮向前走了一步。 杰克伸长脖子，想更好地看看大罗莎房间里面。 他还没看到大罗莎陛下，但他能看到房间里的一部分，那里有红色天鹅绒椅子和镶着金边的光滑白色墙壁。

Another guest entered the Great Rosa Room, and Jack and Annie took a step closer to the door. Now Jack could see Her Imperial Majesty. She was a tall, plump woman dressed in a blue silk gown with ruffles. To Jack’s surprise, the little kid with the sword was sitting on her lap! A long row of older children stood behind her. 另一位客人走进了大罗莎房间，杰克和安妮又向门口走近了一步。 现在杰克能看见她的帝国陛下啦。 她是个又高又胖的女士，穿着一件蓝色丝绸长裙，裙摆有很多褶边。 让杰克惊讶的是，那个拿着剑的小孩正坐在她的腿上！ 她身后站着一排年纪较大的孩子。

Jack turned to Annie. “Who do you think those kids are?” he asked. 杰克转向安妮。 “你觉得那些孩子是谁呀？”他问。

Annie shrugged. 安妮耸了耸肩。

“They are the children of Her Imperial Majesty,” said the girl who had spoken to Jack earlier. “The imperial children.” “他们是帝国陛下的孩子，”之前和杰克说话的那个女孩说。 “帝国的孩子们。”

“Thanks,” said Annie. “谢谢，”安妮说。

“The imperial children look pretty unfriendly,” Jack whispered to Annie. The little boy with the sword was the only one smiling. “那些皇室的孩子看起来一点都不友好，”杰克小声对安妮说。 拿着剑的小男孩是唯一在笑的。

“Well, it must be really hard to just stand there wearing stiff clothes and wigs while people are being received,” Annie whispered back. “嗯，站在那里穿着僵硬的衣服和假发，看着人们被接待，一定真的很困难，”安妮小声回答。

“Prepare yourselves. You are next,” a servant at the door said to Jack and Annie. “做好准备。 轮到你们了，”门口的仆人对杰克和安妮说。

Oh, no! thought Jack. He’d been too busy looking at the imperial children to watch the guests in front of him. “What do we do when we meet Her Imperial Majesty?” he asked Annie frantically. 哦，不！杰克心想。 他一直在看那些皇室的孩子，都没注意前面的客人。 “我们见到皇太后的时候该怎么做？”他焦急地问安妮。

“I don’t know! I forgot to watch!” she said. Annie turned to the girl in the white dress. “Um— excuse me? Can you tell us what to do when we meet Her Imperial Majesty?” she asked. “我不知道！ 我忘了看！”她说。 安妮转向穿白裙子的女孩。 “呃——请问一下？ 你能告诉我们，当我们见到她的帝国陛下时该怎么做吗？”她问。

The girl leaned close to Annie and whispered, “Enter the room and announce your names. Then walk to the middle of the room. You must curtsy; he must bow. Next go directly up to Her Imperial Majesty and do the same.” 女孩靠近安妮，低声说：“走进房间，报上你们的名字。 然后走到房间中间。 你必须行屈膝礼；他必须鞠躬。 接下来直接走到她的 Imperial Majesty 面前，做同样的动作。

“Got it,” said Annie. “明白了，”安妮说。

“Oh, and remember—” the girl said to Jack, “do not rise from the second bow until Her Imperial Majesty tells you to. Do not even look up. Rise only when she says ‘rise.’ Then step backward out of the room.” “哦，还有——”女孩对杰克说，“在第二次鞠躬后，不要起身，除非她的 Imperial Majesty 让你起身。 甚至不要抬头看。 只在她说了‘起立’的时候起立。然后退后一步走出房间。”

“Backward?” said Jack. “退后？”杰克说。

“Yes. You must never turn your back on Her Imperial Majesty,” said the girl. “It would be considered very bad manners.” “对。” 你永远不能背对她的 Imperial Majesty，”女孩说。 “这会被认为是非常不礼貌的行为。”

“Thanks!” said Jack. He was grateful to the girl for giving them such important information. “谢谢！”杰克说。 他很感激女孩给了他们这么重要的信息。

The servant at the door turned and nodded to Jack and Annie. The two of them stepped into the Great Rosa Room. 门口的仆人转过身，向杰克和安妮点头。 他们两人走进了伟大的罗莎房间。

“Annie of Frog Creek!” Annie said loudly. “蛙溪镇的安妮！”安妮大声说。

“Jack, also of Frog Creek!” said Jack. “杰克，来自蛙溪镇的杰克！”杰克说道。

Jack and Annie walked slowly to the center of the room; Her Imperial Majesty and the imperial children watched them closely. The little boy with the sword waved at them. Annie curtsied and Jack gave a low bow. 杰克和安妮慢慢地走到房间中央；帝国女王和皇室的孩子们仔细地看着他们。 拿着剑的小男孩朝他们挥手。 安妮行了个屈膝礼，杰克则深深地鞠了一躬。

Then Jack and Annie walked closer to Her Imperial Majesty. She had a double chin, a high forehead, and lots of teeny blond curls. Jack smiled at her, but her pale face stayed very serious. 然后杰克和安妮走近了帝国女王。 她有双下巴，高高的额头，还有许多又小又金黄的卷发。 杰克朝她笑了笑，但她苍白的脸色依然非常严肃。

Annie curtsied again, and Jack gave a second bow. As he bowed, he remembered that it was bad manners to rise or look up before Her Imperial Majesty told him to. 安妮再次行了个屈膝礼，杰克也再次鞠躬。 他鞠躬时，想起了在帝国女王允许之前，起身或抬头看都是不礼貌的行为。

Jack stared at the shiny buckles on his shoes, waiting to hear Her Imperial Majesty say “rise.” 杰克盯着自己鞋子上闪亮的扣子，等着听帝国女王说“起立”。

Maybe I’m not bowing low enough, Jack thought. He bent over a few inches more. To his horror, the silver flute slipped out of his coat pocket! It clattered to the floor. As Jack grabbed it, his hat fell off. 也许我没有弯得足够低，杰克心想。 他又往下弯了几英寸。 令他惊恐的是，那支银色的笛子从他的外套口袋里滑了出来！ 它叮叮当当地掉到了地上。 当杰克去捡它时，他的帽子掉了下来。

The imperial children snickered. 帝国的孩子们咯咯地笑了起来。

Clutching the flute, Jack reached for his hat with his other hand. But when he grabbed his hat, his wig fell off! Jack reached for his wig, but powder got in his nose, and he sneezed. As he sneezed, he slipped on the polished floor and fell to his knees. Gripping his wig, his hat, and his flute, Jack scrambled back up to his bowing position. But he still hadn’t heard Her Imperial Majesty tell him to rise! 紧紧抓着笛子，杰克用另一只手去够他的帽子。 但当他拿起帽子时，他的假发却掉了下来！ 杰克去够他的假发，但粉末进了他的鼻子，他打了个喷嚏。 当他打喷嚏时，他滑倒在光滑的地板上，跪倒在地。 杰克抓着他的假发、帽子和长笛，赶紧爬回鞠躬的位置。 但他还是没听到陛下让他起身！

The imperial children laughed loudly. Jack even heard Her Imperial Majesty laughing! She probably can’t speak because she’s laughing so hard, thought Jack. He didn’t know what to do. His face was burning hot. You’ve got to get out of here! he told himself. 皇室的孩子们大声地笑了起来。 杰克甚至听到陛下也在笑！ 她可能因为笑得太厉害，说不出话了，杰克心想。 他不知道该怎么办。 他的脸烧得通红。 你必须赶紧离开这里！他对自己说。

Still bowing, Jack began backing up with little steps—until he bumped into a wall. 仍然鞠躬着，杰克开始小步往后退——直到他撞上了一面墙。

As the imperial children howled with laughter, Jack turned his head and saw Annie peeking out from a doorway, giggling. He backed over to her. 当皇室的孩子们笑得前仰后合时，杰克转过头，看见安妮正从一扇门里探出头来，咯咯地笑着。 他往后退到她身边。

Annie grabbed his coattails and pulled him out of the Great Rosa Room. 安妮抓住他的大衣下摆，把他从伟大的罗莎房间拉了出来。

The imperial children clapped and cheered. Jack heard one of the girls say between gulps of laughter, “Who was that fool?” 皇室的孩子们鼓掌欢呼。 杰克听到其中一个女孩在笑声中说：“那是谁啊，傻瓜？”

“That was Jack of Frog Creek!” answered the small boy in his high voice. And they all laughed loudly again. “那是蛙溪镇的杰克！”那个小男孩用高高的声音回答道。 他们又大声地笑了起来。
