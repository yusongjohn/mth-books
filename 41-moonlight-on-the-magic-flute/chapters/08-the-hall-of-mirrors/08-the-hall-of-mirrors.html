8. The Hall of Mirrors 8. 镜子大厅

Wolfie took off running toward the palace. 狼菲朝宫殿跑去。

“The whole world?” Jack said to Annie. “I don’t think so.” “全世界？”杰克对安妮说。 “我想不是吧。”

Annie smiled. “Come on, let’s go back inside,” she said. 安妮笑了。 “快点，我们回屋里去，”她说。

Jack and Annie hurried through the moonlit garden back to the summer palace. They followed Wolfie up the stairway that led to the terrace. 杰克和安妮赶紧穿过月光下的花园，回到夏天的宫殿。 他们跟着沃尔菲沿着通往露台的楼梯走上去。

“I’m here, Nan!” Wolfie called. “我在这里，纳恩！”沃尔菲喊道。

Nan ran across the terrace and grabbed him. “Oh, Wolfie! Where were you?” she asked. 纳恩跑过露台，一把抓住了他。 “哦，沃尔菲！ 你去哪儿了？”她问。

“I was in the garden! Oh, Nan, the most amazing thing—” said Wolfie. “我在花园里！ 哦，纳恩，最神奇的事——”沃尔菲说。

“Not now, Wolfie, we don’t have time,” Nan said. She brushed off his jacket and straightened his wig. “现在不行，沃尔菲，我们没时间，”南说。 她拍掉他外套上的灰尘，整理好他的假发。

“I love music, Nan! Jack and Annie made me love music again!” Wolfie said, pointing at Jack and Annie. “我爱音乐，南！ 杰克和安妮让我重新爱上了音乐！”沃尔菲指着杰克和安妮说。

“I’m glad, good, come now,” said Nan. “ We have to hurry to the Hall of Mirrors! Papa’s there waiting for us!” Nan pulled Wolfie toward the door. “我很高兴，快点，现在就走，”南说。 “我们必须赶快去镜厅！ 爸爸在那里等我们！”南拉着沃尔菲朝门口走去。

“Jack, Annie, come with us!” Wolfie yelled over his shoulder. “杰克、安妮，跟我们一起来！”沃尔菲回头喊道。

“In a minute! We’ll be right there!” said Annie. “等一下！ 我们马上过去！”安妮说。

As Wolfie and Nan went inside, Annie tried to smooth her clothes. The lace on her petticoat was ripped. The bottom of her dress was dirty. Her bows had come untied. The hoop of her skirt was bent. “I’m a mess,” she said to Jack. 当沃尔菲和南走进去时，安妮试着把她的衣服弄平整。 她衬裙上的蕾丝被撕破了。 她裙子的下摆弄脏了。 她的蝴蝶结松开了。 她裙子的裙撑被弄弯了。 “我一团糟，”她对杰克说。

“Me too,” he said. His jacket was dirty and his pants were torn. He found his wig and hat where he’d left them and scrunched them back onto his head. “But we have to look for the artists now,” he said. “我也是，”他说。 他的夹克很脏，裤子也破了。 他找到了自己留下的假发和帽子，又把它们弄皱后戴回头上。 “但我们现在得去找那些艺术家了，”他说。

“What about Wolfie?” said Annie. “那沃尔夫呢？”安妮说。

“Forget it. We don’t have time to hang out with Wolfie,” said Jack. “算了吧。 我们没时间陪沃尔夫玩，”杰克说。

“But we told him we’d come,” said Annie. “但我们答应过他要来的，”安妮说。

“ We can’t spend our whole lives chasing after Wolfie!” said Jack. “Because of him, we’ve already used up our only chance to make magic. And we haven’t even started our mission yet!” “我们不能把一辈子都花在追着沃尔夫跑上！”杰克说。 “就因为他，我们已经用掉了唯一一次施展魔法的机会。 我们还没开始我们的任务呢！”

“Okay, okay,” said Annie. “But we should at least say good-bye to him and Nan.” “好吧，好吧，”安妮说。 “但我们至少应该跟他说声再见，还有纳恩。”

Jack sighed. “Okay. A quick good-bye,” he said. 杰克叹了口气。 “好吧。” “快点道别，”他说。

Jack and Annie walked across the terrace and stepped into the palace. 杰克和安妮穿过露台，走进了宫殿。

“Excuse me,” Annie said to a servant, “where is the Hall of Mirrors?” “请问，”安妮对一名仆人说，“镜子大厅在哪里？”

The servant frowned at their appearance. But he pointed to a door on the right. “Pass through the next three rooms, then through the Great Rosa Room, into the Hall of Mirrors.” 仆人看到他们的样子，皱起了眉头。 但他指着右边的一扇门。 “穿过接下来的三间房间，然后穿过伟大的罗莎房间，就能进入镜子大厅了。”

“Thanks!” Jack and Annie hurried through the four rooms until they came to a large door. They opened it and peeked into the Hall of Mirrors. “谢谢！”杰克和安妮赶紧穿过四间房间，直到他们来到一扇大门口。 他们打开门，偷偷 peek 进入镜子大厅。

Mirrors hung on the walls. The room was filled with party guests sitting in rows of chairs. Her Imperial Majesty and the imperial children sat in the front row. Wolfie was standing near the front of the room with Nan and their father. 墙壁上挂满了镜子。 房间里坐满了参加聚会的客人，他们一行一行地坐在椅子上。 皇太后和皇室的孩子们坐在第一排。 沃尔菲和南以及他们的爸爸站在房间的前面。

Jack wanted to leave as quickly as possible. But Wolfie saw them and called out, “Jack! Annie! Come in!” 杰克想尽快离开。 但沃尔菲看见他们，喊道：“杰克！安妮！ 进来吧！”

Jack started to slip away. But Annie pushed the door open and stepped inside. Oh, brother, thought Jack, following her. 杰克开始悄悄溜走。 但安妮推开门走了进去。 天啊，杰克心想，跟着她走了进去。

“Watch me!” cried Wolfie. Then Wolfie shot away from his family and hopped in front of the crowd. “看我的！”沃尔菲喊道。 然后沃尔菲从家人身边跳开，蹦到人群前面。

Oh, no! thought Jack. What’s he doing? Why doesn’t someone grab him? 哦，天哪！杰克心想。 他要干什么？ 为什么没人抓住他？

Wolfie faced the audience. He placed his hand over his heart and bowed. Then he swept back his coattails and climbed up on a bench in front of an odd-looking piano. His short legs didn’t even reach the floor. 沃尔菲面对着观众。 他把手放在胸口，鞠了一躬。 接着他把外套下摆向后一甩，爬到一架奇怪的钢琴前面的长凳上。 他那短小的腿甚至够不到地面。

Wolfie closed his eyes and bowed his head close to the keyboard. With just one finger he began tapping out some musical notes. 沃尔菲闭上眼睛，把头靠近键盘。 他用一根手指开始在键盘上敲出一些音符。

Why’s everyone watching this little kid pretend to play the piano? Jack wondered. Then he realized something amazing: the simple tune Wolfie was picking out on the keyboard was the same tune the magic flute had played in the garden. 为什么大家都看着这个小孩假装弹钢琴呢？杰克想知道。 然后他突然意识到一件神奇的事情：沃尔菲在键盘上弹出的简单曲调，和魔法笛子在花园里吹出的曲调是一样的。

Everyone in the room seemed to hold their breath as Wolfie played. He went from tapping with one finger to tapping with two fingers and then three. As Wolfie kept tapping out notes, he didn’t look like a silly six-year-old anymore. The expression on his face was thoughtful and dreamy. 房间里所有人都屏住了呼吸，看着沃尔菲弹琴。 他从用一根手指敲击，变成了用两根手指，接着又变成了三根手指。 沃尔菲继续敲击琴键，弹出音符时，他看起来不再是个傻乎乎的六岁小孩了。 他脸上的表情变得认真又梦幻。

Suddenly Wolfie was playing with all his fingers. His small hands flew over the black and white keys as he added to the tune of the magic flute. 突然，沃尔菲用上了所有手指弹琴。 他小小的双手在黑白琴键上飞快地移动，为魔法笛子的曲调增添了新的音符。

Jack was stunned. He couldn’t believe a little kid could make such beautiful music. The sounds from the piano were sometimes light and playful,and at other times heavy and forceful. The slow parts of Wolfie’s music made Jack want to close his eyes. The lively parts made him want to bounce up and down. 杰克惊呆了。 他简直不敢相信，一个小孩子竟然能弹出这么美妙的音乐。 钢琴发出的声音有时轻快活泼，有时又沉重有力。 沃尔菲音乐中缓慢的部分让杰克想闭上眼睛。 欢快的部分则让他想跳上跳下。

Wolfie ended his concert with a great flourish. Then he stood up and bowed. 沃尔菲用一个精彩的结尾结束了他的音乐会。 然后他站起来鞠躬。

The guests jumped to their feet, smiling and laughing, clapping and yelling, “Bravo! Bravo!” 客人们立刻站起来，笑着、喊着，鼓掌欢呼：“太棒了！太棒了！”

Wolfie kept bowing. The clapping and cheering would die down, but then it would start up again. As long as people clapped, Wolfie bowed. 沃尔菲不停地鞠躬。 鼓掌和欢呼声会停下来，但很快又会再次响起。 只要有人鼓掌，沃尔菲就继续鞠躬。

Finally Wolfie’s father stepped up to the piano. Then Wolfie seemed to turn into a little kid again. “Papa!” he said. He grabbed his father and buried his face in his father’s coat. Wolfie’s dad had tears on his cheeks as he hugged his son. 最后，沃尔菲的父亲走上钢琴。 然后沃尔菲好像又变回了一个小孩子。 “爸爸！”他说。 他抓住了父亲，把脸埋进父亲的外套里。 沃尔菲的爸爸抱着儿子，眼里流下了泪水。

All the guests talked excitedly about the performance they’d just seen: 所有客人都兴奋地谈论着他们刚看到的表演：

“I couldn’t believe my ears!” “我简直不敢相信自己的耳朵！”，

“I couldn’t believe my eyes!” “我简直不敢相信自己的眼睛！”，

“How did he do it? He is so young and small!” “他是怎么做到的？ 他这么小，怎么可能做到呢！”，

As the party guests kept praising Wolfie, Nan walked over to Jack and Annie. “Thank you for bringing Wolfie back for his concert,” she said. 当派对的客人们一直夸奖沃尔菲时，南走到杰克和安妮身边。 “谢谢你们把沃尔菲带回来参加他的音乐会，”她说。

“Has he been playing a long time?” Annie asked. “他已经演奏很久了吗？”安妮问。

“Papa has been teaching him since he was three,” said Nan. “And now Wolfie is even starting to write his own music. He tells Papa he hears tunes in his head—like the tune he played tonight. I have never heard that one before.” “爸爸从他三岁就开始教他了，”娜娜说。 “现在沃尔菲甚至开始写自己的音乐了。 他告诉爸爸，他脑子里能听到旋律——就像今晚演奏的那首旋律。 我以前从来没听过这首。”

Annie smiled at Jack, and he smiled back at her. “Cool,” Annie said. 安妮对杰克笑了笑，杰克也回她一笑。 “酷，”安妮说。

A hush fell over the crowd. Her Imperial Majesty had stepped forward. She took Wolfie’s hands in hers. “Thank you for your brilliant performance, Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart,” she said. 人群安静了下来。 她的至高皇太后向前走了一步。 她握住了沃尔菲的手。 “谢谢你精彩的表演，沃尔夫冈·阿玛多伊斯·莫扎特，”她说。

As the crowd clapped wildly again, Annie looked at Jack. “Mozart!” she said. 当人群再次热烈鼓掌时，安妮看向杰克。 “莫扎特！”，她说。

Jack was confused. Mozart? He knew the name Mozart. Their piano teacher loved the music of Mozart. Their parents loved it, too. In fact, they’d once taken Annie and Jack to hear a concert of Mozart’s music. Jack couldn’t believe that this strange little kid was the world-famous Mozart. 杰克感到困惑。莫扎特？ 他知道莫扎特这个名字。 他们的钢琴老师很喜欢莫扎特的音乐。 他们的父母也一样喜欢。 事实上，他们曾经带安妮和杰克去听过莫扎特音乐的音乐会。 杰克简直不敢相信，这个奇怪的小孩竟然就是世界著名的莫扎特。

The applause stopped as Her Imperial Majesty spoke to the crowd. “ We have witnessed a great event here tonight. I know we will all remember it in the years to come, when our young Wolfgang Mozart brings joy to all the world with his music.” 掌声停了下来，因为她的 Imperial Majesty 对人群说话了。 “今晚我们在这里见证了一件大事。 我知道，将来在我们的年轻沃尔夫冈·莫扎特用他的音乐给全世界带来欢乐时，我们都会记住这件事。”

“Oh, man,” Jack whispered. He looked at Annie. “Did you hear that?” “哦，天啊，”杰克小声说。 他看着安妮。 “你听到吗？”

Annie smiled at him and nodded. 安妮对他笑了笑，并点了点头。

“Thank you again for your help,” Nan said to them. She started to leave, then turned back. “Oh, I asked Papa your question about the artists living here. He said he was talking about another palace in Vienna. I am sorry.” “再次感谢你们的帮助，”南对他们说。 她开始离开，然后又转过身来。 “哦，我问过爸爸关于这里居住的艺术家的问题。” 他说他是在说维也纳的另一座宫殿。对不起。”

“That’s okay,” said Annie. “No problem.” “没关系，”安妮说。 “没问题。”

As Nan headed for Wolfie and her father, Annie looked at Jack. “ We found our great artist,” she said. “He was with us all along.” 当南走向沃尔菲和她的父亲时，安妮看着杰克。 “我们找到了那位伟大的艺术家，”她说。 “他一直和我们在一起。”
