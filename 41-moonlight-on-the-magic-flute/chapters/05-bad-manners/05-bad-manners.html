5. Bad Manners 5. 不好的礼仪

A servant rang a bell. Other servants began carrying in large silver bowls, platters, and trays. 一个仆人按响了铃铛。 其他的仆人开始端着大大的银碗、盘子和托盘进来。

“It is time for dinner now,” said Nan. “ We must go, Wolfie. We have to find Papa.” She helped her little brother put his wig back on. “现在是吃晚饭的时间了，”纳恩说。 “我们必须走了，沃尔菲。 我们要去找爸爸。”她帮她的小弟弟把假发重新戴上。

“But I want to stay with Jack and Annie,” whined Wolfie. “I want to play with them in the garden.” “但是我想和杰克、安妮一起待着，”沃尔菲抱怨道。 “我想和他们在花园里玩。”

“Not now,” said Nan. She grabbed Wolfie’s hand. “It was nice to talk to you,” she said to Jack and Annie. “And I hope we can see you perform again someday, Jack.” “现在不行，”纳恩说。 她抓住了沃尔菲的手。 “很高兴和你聊天，”她对杰克和安妮说。 “希望有一天能再看你表演，杰克。”

Jack forced a smile. “Uh … yeah, thanks. Hey, let us know what your dad—I mean, your papa— says about the brilliant artists living in the palace,” he said. “Maybe you can meet us later by those double doors?” 杰克勉强笑了笑。 “呃……谢谢。 嘿，告诉我们你爸爸——我是说你爸爸——对住在宫殿里的天才艺术家们怎么说？ “也许你之后可以到那扇双门那里来找我们？”

“Yes, after dinner, we will meet you,” said Nan. “是的，晚饭后我们会去找你们，”南说。

“And then we will play in the garden!” said Wolfie. “然后我们会去花园里玩！”沃尔菲说。

“No, you and I will have other things to do, Wolfie, remember?” said Nan. “Come along now.” She pulled her little brother away. “不，你和我之后还有别的事情要做，沃尔菲，还记得吗？”南说。 “快点走吧。”她把她的小弟弟拉走了。

“But I want to play with Jack the Clown!” Wolfie wailed. “可是我想和小丑杰克一起玩！”沃尔夫哭喊着。

Jack and Annie watched Wolfie and Nan disappear into the crowd. 杰克和安妮看着沃尔夫和南消失在人群中。

“I am not a clown,” Jack said darkly. “我不是小丑，”杰克阴沉地说。

“Don’t worry about him,” said Annie. “He really admires you.” “别担心他，”安妮说。 “他真的很崇拜你。”

“Lucky me,” said Jack. “我真幸运，”杰克说。

The bell sounded again. People started moving toward the dinner tables. 钟声又响了。 人们开始朝晚餐桌那边走。

“Where should we sit?” said Annie. “我们应该坐在哪儿？”安妮说。

“Anywhere,” said Jack, “as long as we’re out of the way.” He still wanted to hide from the imperial children. “What about that table at the end of the room? Near the door?” “ anywhere，” 杰克说，“只要别被那些皇室的孩子发现就行。”他还是想躲开那些皇室的孩子。 “房间尽头靠近门的那张桌子怎么样？”

“Sure.” “好啊。”

Jack led the way to the table at the far end of the room. “Let’s grab seats while we can,” he said to Annie. 杰克带路，走到房间最远端的那张桌子前。 “趁现在赶紧找个座位，”他对安妮说。

As grown-ups hovered near the table talking and laughing, Jack and Annie sat down in two chairs. Steaming platters of food lined the middle of the table: beef stew, mashed potatoes, sausages, dumplings, cabbage, spiced apples, and gingerbread. 大人在桌子旁边聊天、笑，杰克和安妮坐在两张椅子上。 一排排冒着热气的盘子摆在桌子中间：炖牛肉、土豆泥、香肠、饺子、卷心菜、香料苹果和姜饼。

Jack’s mouth watered. He hadn’t realized how hungry he was. “Okay, here’s our plan,” he said to Annie. “We’ll eat first. Then Nan will tell us where all the artists live, and we’ll get to work.” 杰克的嘴开始流口水了。 他没意识到自己有多饿。 “好了，这是我们的计划，”他对安妮说。 “我们先吃饭。” 然后南奶奶会告诉我们所有艺术家住在哪里，我们就可以开始工作了。”

“Excuse me, young man and young lady,” someone said in a snippy tone. “打扰一下，年轻人和小姐，”有人用尖刻的语气说道。

Jack and Annie turned around in their chairs. An elderly couple stood glaring at them. 杰克和安妮转过身来。 一对年长的夫妇站在那里瞪着他们。

“The empress herself designed the seating plan,” the man said. “And I can assure you these are not your seats.” “女皇亲自设计了座位安排，”那人说。 “我可以向你保证，这些不是你们的座位。”

“Furthermore,” said the woman, “no one sits until Her Imperial Majesty is seated.” “此外，”女人说，“在女皇陛下就座之前，没人可以坐下。”

Jack and Annie jumped up from their chairs. 杰克和安妮从椅子上跳了起来。

“Oops!” said Annie. “哎呀！”安妮说。

“Sorry!” said Jack. “对不起！”杰克说。

“Our mistake,” said Annie. “Excuse us.” “是我们错了，”安妮说。 “请原谅。”

Jack and Annie walked away from the table. “That was bad manners,” Annie said to Jack. 杰克和安妮走开了，离开桌子。 “这是不礼貌的行为，”安妮对杰克说。

“Who, us or them?” said Jack. “是咱们还是他们？”杰克说。

“Us,” Annie said. “I wonder where our seats are.” “是咱们，”安妮说。 “我想知道我们的座位在哪里。”

“I have a feeling we’re not a part of the empress’s seating plan,” said Jack. His face felt hot again. His wig itched like crazy. “我有种感觉，我们不在女皇的座位安排里，”杰克说。 他的脸又感觉热了起来。 他的假发痒得要命。

Suddenly a hush fell over the room. The harp and violin music stopped. Everyone stopped talking. 突然，房间里一片寂静。 竖琴和小提琴的音乐停了下来。 每个人都停止了说话。

Her Imperial Majesty had entered through the main doors. The imperial children followed her. Her Majesty led them to the table in the middle of the room. Everyone else stood silently, waiting for the imperial family to sit down. 她的皇家 Majesty 从正门走了进来。 皇室的孩子们跟在她后面。 她 Majesty 带领他们走到房间中央的桌子旁。 其他人都静静地站着，等着皇室一家人坐下。

“ We have to get out of here now,“ Jack whispered to Annie. “Soon we’ll be the only ones left standing.” “我们现在必须马上离开这里，”杰克对安妮小声说。 “很快我们就会是唯一剩下站着的人了。”

“Like musical chairs,” said Annie. “就像音乐椅游戏，”安妮说。

“Right,” said Jack. “Forget dinner. Let’s go look for where the artists are staying. We can’t wait for Nan to ask her dad.” “对，”杰克说。 “忘了晚饭吧。 我们去瞧瞧艺术家们住在哪里。 我们不能等南去问她爸爸。”

As all the guests sat down, Jack and Annie hurried toward the door. 当所有客人都坐下来时，杰克和安妮赶紧朝门口跑去。

“Jack the Clown!” came a high voice. “小丑杰克！”，一个高高的声音传来。

Jack looked over his shoulder. Wolfie was waving from one of the tables. 杰克回头看了看。 沃尔菲正从一张桌子旁朝他们挥手。

Annie waved back. 安妮也朝他挥了挥手。

“Don’t wave! Keep going,” said Jack. He grabbed Annie’s hand and pulled her out of the party room. They hurried into yet another elegant room with red velvet furniture and gold-trimmed walls. “别挥手！ 继续往前走，”杰克说。 他抓住安妮的手，把她从派对房间拉了出来。 他们匆匆跑进另一个华丽的房间，房间里摆满了红色天鹅绒家具，墙壁上镶着金色装饰。

“Keep going,” said Jack. He led the way into another fancy room. “继续往前走，”杰克说。 他带他们走进了另一个华丽的房间。

“Jack and Annie, wait!” came a cry. “杰克和安妮，等等！”有人喊道。

“I hear Wolfie!” said Annie. “我听到沃尔菲了！”安妮说。

“Darn!” Jack quickly closed the door behind them. “Keep moving!” he said. “该死！”杰克赶紧在他们身后关上门。 “继续前进！”他说。

“ We can’t,” said Annie. “That’s mean. We should wait for him.” “我们不能，”安妮说。 “这太坏了。 我们应该等他。”

“But he’ll slow down our mission!” said Jack. “When are we going to get started?” “但他会让我们任务变慢的！”杰克说。 “我们什么时候才能开始？”

“Calm down,” said Annie. “We’ll just tell him we can’t hang out with him now because we have something important that we have to do.” “冷静点，”安妮说。 “我们只告诉他我们现在不能和他一起玩，因为我们有重要的事情要做。”

Jack heaved a sigh. “Okay.” 杰克叹了口气。 “好吧。”

“Jack! Annie!” “杰克！安妮！”

Jack opened the door. 杰克打开了门。

Wolfie ran right into him. “There you are!” the little boy said, smiling. “I was looking for you!” 沃尔菲直接撞了上去。 “找到你了！”小男孩笑着说。 “我一直在找你！”

“No kidding,” said Jack. “真吗？”杰克说。

“Yes!” Wolfie said. “Are you leaving?” “是的！”沃尔菲说。 “你要离开了吗？

“Not yet, we have to do something important,” said Jack. “And you can’t come with us.” “还没呢，我们必须做件重要的事，”杰克说。 “你不能跟我们一起去。”

Wolfie’s smile vanished. 沃尔菲的笑容消失了。

“Sorry,” said Jack. “对不起，”杰克说。

“But I want you to come to the garden with me,” said Wolfie. “但是我想让你和我一起去花园，”沃尔菲说。

“Not now,” said Jack. “Wolfie, listen to me. Listen carefully. Annie and I have to do something really, really important. And we have to do it all by ourselves.” “现在不行，”杰克说。 “沃尔菲，听我说。仔细听好。 安妮和我必须做一件非常、非常重要的事情。 而且我们必须自己完成。”

Wolfie’s bottom lip started to quiver. Oh, no, thought Jack. He’s going to cry. 沃尔菲的下嘴唇开始颤抖。 哦，不，杰克心想。 他要哭了。

A tear ran down the little boy’s cheek. 小男孩的眼泪顺着脸颊流了下来。

“Aww, Wolfie, don’t cry,” Annie said gently. “啊呀，沃尔菲，别哭，”安妮温柔地说。

“Wolfie! Wolfie!” someone called. Nan burst into the room. “Wolfie, what are you doing? Papa is very upset!” “沃尔菲！ 沃尔菲！”有人喊道。 南冲进房间。 “沃尔菲，你在干什么？ 爸爸现在非常生气！”

“I want to play with Jack and Annie,” said Wolfie. Another big tear ran down his cheek. “我想和杰克和安妮一起玩，”沃尔菲说。 又有一滴大眼泪从他的脸颊滑下来。

“Please, Wolfie,” Nan begged. “You know you have a big responsibility tonight. You have to—” “求你了，沃尔菲，”南恳求道。 “你知道今晚你有很重要的责任。你必须——”

“No!” wailed Wolfie, stamping his foot. “No, no, no! I never get to play!” “不！”沃尔菲哭喊着，用脚跺地。 “不，不，不！ 我从来没机会玩！”，

“Wolfie, stop!” said Nan. “Please do not be this way. Not now, of all times! You will kill Papa!” “沃尔菲，住手！”南说。 “请不要这样。” 现在不行，什么时候都不行！ 你会害死爸爸的！

“Wolfie! Wolfie!” came a deep voice. “沃尔菲！ “沃尔菲！”一个低沉的声音喊道。

“He’s in here, Papa!” Nan called. “Come, Wolfie.” She took Wolfie’s hand, but he pulled away from her. “他在这里，爸爸！”南喊道。 “来吧，沃尔菲。”她抓住沃尔菲的手，但他挣脱了她的手。

“No! I want to play!” shouted Wolfie. And he charged out of the room. “不！ 我想玩！”沃尔菲喊道。 然后他冲出了房间。

“Wolfie?” A portly man in a wig burst through another door. “Where is he? Where is my son?” “沃尔菲？”一个戴着假发的胖男人从另一扇门冲了出来。 “他在哪儿？我儿子在哪儿？”

“Oh, Papa!” said Nan. “Wolfie ran away!” “哦，爸爸！”南南说道。 “沃尔菲跑掉了！”

“Ran away?” The man threw his hands in the air. “ We must find him!” he cried. “跑掉了？”那人双手举到空中。 “我们必须找到他！”他喊道。

“Papa, calm down!” said Nan. “爸爸，冷静点！”南说。

“ We will fail utterly without him!” cried her papa. And he rushed out of the room. “没有他，我们肯定彻底失败！”她爸爸喊道。 他冲出了房间。

“Papa! Papa!” yelled Nan. And she hurried after her father. “爸爸！ 爸爸！”南喊道。 她赶紧追上父亲。
