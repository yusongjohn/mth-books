7. Follow Me 7. 跟我来

“Stand back!” Wolfie shouted at the leopard. “I have a sword! And I’m not afraid to use it!” “退后！”沃尔菲对豹子大喊。 “我有剑！ 我一点都不怕用它！”

“Oh, brother,” whispered Jack. There was no way Wolfie’s tiny sword could ward off the huge leopard. But if Jack and Annie rushed in to rescue Wolfie, the leopard might leap up and attack him. “哦，天哪，”杰克小声说。 沃尔菲那把小小的剑根本不可能挡住那只大豹子。 但如果杰克和安妮冲进去救沃尔菲，豹子可能会跳起来攻击他。

Annie nudged Jack’s arm. She pointed at the pocket of his coat. She held up her hands, pretending to play a flute. 安妮戳了戳杰克的胳膊。 她指着他的外套口袋。 她举起双手，假装在吹奏长笛。

Jack had forgotten all about their magic flute! He remembered Teddy’s words: Playing this flute will deliver you from danger. 杰克完全忘记了他们那支神奇的长笛！ 他想起了泰迪的话：吹奏这支长笛能让你脱离危险。

But what can the flute do? Jack wondered. How can it help us? He reached into his pocket and pulled out the flute anyway. 但这支长笛到底能做什么呢？杰克想知道。 它怎么才能帮到我们呢？ 他还是把手伸进口袋，把长笛拿了出来。

“You play—just blow over the mouthpiece,” whispered Annie. “I’ll make up a song. Remember, whatever I sing will come true.” “你来吹吧——只要对着笛口吹气就行，”安妮小声说。 “我会编一首歌。” 记住，我唱的任何歌都会成真。”

Jack nodded and held up the magic flute. It glistened in the light of the moon. Jack wasn’t sure he was holding it right, but he hoped it wouldn’t matter. He closed his eyes and blew gently across the mouthpiece. 杰克点点头，拿起了那支魔法笛子。 月光下，笛子闪闪发亮。 杰克不确定自己拿对了，但他希望这不会影响什么。 他闭上眼睛，轻轻吹过笛子的吹口。

Music streamed from the silver instrument! The pure sound wafted through the air like a feather on the wind. The melody was simple, yet beautiful. 音乐从那支银色的乐器中流淌出来！ 纯净的声音像风中的羽毛一样飘散在空气中。 旋律简单，却非常美丽。

Annie started singing, making up a song: 安妮开始唱歌，编了一首歌：

Leopard, hey, leopard,

Listen to our sound. 豹子，嘿，豹子，听我们的声音。

The leopard turned its head and looked at Jack and Annie. It pricked up its spotted ears. 猎豹转过头，看着杰克和安妮。 它竖起了身上有斑点的耳朵。

Follow me, follow me,

Follow me and the clown. 跟我来，跟我来，跟我来，还有小丑。

Clown? thought Jack. Is that me? He didn’t love Annie’s choice of words, but he didn’t have time to worry about it. The leopard rose to its feet and began walking toward Jack and Annie. 小丑？杰克心想。 那是我吗？ 他不喜欢安妮选择的那些话，但他没时间去担心这件事。 豹子站了起来，开始朝杰克和安妮走来。

Jack was so scared that he almost turned and ran away. But he didn’t dare. He knew that as soon as he stopped playing the flute, the magic would end. 杰克非常害怕，差点转身就跑掉了。 但他不敢。 他知道，只要他停止吹笛子，魔法就会结束。

Annie tugged on Jack’s sleeve, and they started walking slowly down the path, back toward the palace. The leopard padded silently after them as Annie kept singing: 安妮拽了拽杰克的袖子，他们开始慢慢地沿着小路往回走，朝宫殿走去。 豹子悄无声息地跟在他们后面，而安妮继续唱着歌：

Wolfie, hey, Wolfie,

Jump down, jump down …

Follow me, follow me,

Follow me and the clown. 沃尔菲，嘿，沃尔菲，跳下来，跳下来……跟着我，跟着我，跟着我和小丑。

Without a word, Wolfie bravely hopped down from the tree. He followed the leopard, Jack, and Annie. They all walked down the path between the trees. Jack had no idea where they were going. He only knew that he should keep playing, Annie should keep singing, and they should all keep moving. Wolfie一声不响，勇敢地从树上跳了下来。 他跟着猎豹，杰克和安妮。 他们沿着树间的路走了下去。 杰克一点也不知道他们要去哪儿。 他只知道他应该继续玩，安妮应该继续唱歌，他们都要继续前进。

Jack heard branches breaking. He heard grunting and snorting. The giant bear lumbered out from behind some trees. But Jack kept playing, and Annie kept singing: 杰克听见树枝被折断的声音。 他听见了咕哝和哼哧的声音。 那只大熊从几棵树后面 lumbered 出来。 但杰克继续玩，安妮继续唱歌：

Bear, hey, bear,

No need to frown.

Follow me, follow me,

Follow me and the clown. 熊，嘿，熊，不用皱眉头。跟着我，跟着我，跟着我跟小丑走。

The bear followed Jack, Annie, the leopard, and Wolfie down the path. The moonlight grew brighter and brighter. The magic music was making the night as bright as day! 那只熊跟着杰克、安妮、猎豹和沃尔夫沿着小路走下去。 月光变得越来越亮了。 魔法音乐让夜晚亮得像白天一样！

Next the hyena’s laugh rippled through the air. WHOOP-WHOOP! The hyena slunk out from behind a tree trunk. 接下来，鬣狗的笑声在空中回荡。WHOOP-WHOOP！ 鬣狗从一棵树后面溜了出来。

Jack kept playing, and Annie kept singing: 杰克继续演奏，安妮继续唱歌：

Hyena, hey, hyena,

We’re glad you’ve been found.

Follow me, follow me,

Follow me and the clown. 鬣狗，嘿，鬣狗，我们很高兴找到你了。跟我来，跟我来，跟我来，还有小丑。

The hyena joined the parade. 鬣狗加入了游行。

More creatures appeared: the gazelle and the ostrich and the peacock. Annie sang to them as Jack played the magic flute: 更多的动物出现了：羚羊、鸵鸟和孔雀。 安妮一边唱，杰克一边吹着魔法笛子：

Hey, gazelle! Hey, ostrich!

Hey, bird with a crown!

Follow me, follow me,

Follow me and the clown. 嘿，羚羊！嘿，鸵鸟！嘿，戴冠的鸟！跟我来，跟我来，跟我和小丑来。

The animals all joined the parade. Jack heard Wolfie laughing with joy. He looked back and saw the little boy waving his arms as if he were conducting the magic music, a big grin on his face. 所有动物都加入了游行。 杰克听到沃尔菲开心地笑了。 他回头一看，看见那个小男孩挥舞着双臂，好像在指挥着神奇的音乐，脸上露出灿烂的笑容。

Jack kept playing, and Annie kept singing: 杰克继续演奏，安妮继续唱歌：

All creatures who fly,

Fly after this tune.All creatures who walk,

Walk under the moon.

All creatures who crawl,

Crawl over the ground.

Follow me, follow me,

Follow me and the clown. 所有会飞的生物，都跟着这个旋律飞。所有会走的生物，都在月光下走。所有会爬的生物，都爬过地面。跟着我，跟着我，跟着我和小丑。

Baboons and bunnies, snakes and squirrels, lizards and foxes—all the ordinary and extraordinary creatures in the woods followed Jack and Annie. On the other side of the wide square, candles twinkled in the rear windows of the palace. Jack wondered where he should lead all the wild creatures. Where was the zoo? How could he and Annie get the animals back into their cages? 狒狒和兔子，蛇和松鼠，蜥蜴和狐狸——森林里所有普通和奇特的动物都跟着杰克和安妮走。 广场的另一边，宫殿后窗的烛光在远处闪烁。 杰克想知道他应该带这些野兽去哪里。 动物园在哪里？ 他和安妮怎么才能把这些动物送回笼子里呢？

But Annie had another idea, as she sang: 但安妮又有另一个主意，因为她唱道：

To your forests and plains,

Where you’re all free to roam,

To your lands near and far,

Go home now, go home …

Go home now, go home …

Go home now, go home … 到你的森林和原野， 你可以在那里自由地奔跑， 到你近处和远处的家园， 回家吧，回家…… 回家吧，回家…… 回家吧，回家……

As Annie sang the last words over and over, the creatures began to vanish into thin air. The leopard, bear, hyena, ostrich, gazelle, peacock, baboon, and crane all disappeared. Soon, the only animals left in the woods were the ones that belonged there. 当安妮一遍又一遍地唱完最后一句时，这些动物开始消失在空气中。 豹子、熊、鬣狗、鸵鸟、羚羊、孔雀、狒狒和鹤全都消失了。 很快，森林里只剩下那些本来就应该在这里的动物了。

Annie stopped singing, Jack stopped playing, and Wolfie stopped waving his hands. The bright light faded to silver moonlight, and the cats, squirrels, and bunnies scampered off into the dark. The garden became quiet and peaceful again, except for the chirping of crickets. 安妮停止了唱歌，杰克停止了演奏，沃尔菲也停止了挥手。 明亮的光渐渐变成了银色的月光，猫、松鼠和小兔子都跑进了黑暗中。 花园再次变得安静和平和，除了蟋蟀的鸣叫。

“Where did the wild animals go?” asked Wolfie. “野动物们去哪儿了？”沃尔菲问道。

“Home,” Annie said simply. “回家了，”安妮简单地说。

Jack put the flute back in his pocket and heaved a sigh. “Good work,” he said to Annie. “But did you have to keep calling me a clown?” 杰克把笛子放回口袋里，叹了口气。 “干得好，”他对安妮说。 “但你非要一直叫我小丑吗？”

Annie giggled. “Sorry, but ‘clown’ sounds good with so many other words.” 安妮咯咯地笑了。 “对不起，但‘小丑’这个词和很多其他词搭配起来听起来很好。”

“I’m glad they went home,” said Wolfie. “I wanted them to be free.” “我很高兴他们回家了，”沃尔菲说。 “我想让他们获得自由。”

“Listen, Wolfie,” said Jack. “Don’t ever, ever again try to free animals from a zoo. Someone could have gotten hurt!” “听着，沃尔菲，”杰克说。 “永远、永远不要再试图从动物园里放走动物了。 有人可能会受伤的！”

“I’m sorry,” said Wolfie. “I promise I’ll never do it again. But how did you make them follow you?” “对不起，”沃尔菲说。 “我保证以后再也不这样做了。 但是你是怎么让他们跟着你的？

“It wasn’t us,” said Annie. “It was our music.” “不是我们，”安妮说。 “这是我们的音乐。”

“Was it magic?” asked Wolfie. “这是魔法吗？”沃尔菲问道。

“Yes, as a matter of fact it was,” said Annie. “是的，事实上，它就是魔法，”安妮说。

“Music is magic,” said Wolfie thoughtfully. “I love music.” “音乐就是魔法，”沃尔菲若有所思地说。 “我爱音乐。”

“Cool,” said Jack. “酷毙了，”杰克说。

“I really love it!” said Wolfie. “我真的超级喜欢！”沃尔菲说。

“Uh … good,” said Jack. “呃……不错，”杰克说。

“I love it more than anything!” said Wolfie. He whirled around, clapping his hands and dancing for joy. “我比任何东西都更爱它！”沃尔菲说。 他转了个圈，拍着手，高兴地跳起舞来。

What a weird kid, thought Jack. 真奇怪的孩子，杰克心想。

As Wolfie twirled, the palace clock sounded: bong, bong, bong, bong, bong, bong, bong. 当沃尔菲转圈时，宫殿的钟敲响了：咚，咚，咚，咚，咚，咚，咚。

“Seven,” said Annie. “Seven o’clock.” “七，”安妮说道。 “七点整。”

Wolfie stopped twirling and stumbled dizzily. “Oh, no! I must go!” he cried. “I will be late!” Wolfie grabbed Jack and Annie. “Come with me! You have to come with me!” 沃尔菲停止了旋转，摇摇晃晃地摔倒了。 “哦，不！” “我必须去！”他喊道。 “我要迟到了！”沃尔菲抓住了杰克和安妮。 “跟我来！ 你必须跟我来！”

“Uh, okay,” said Jack. Why is Wolfie so frantic? he wondered. “呃，好吧，”杰克说。 为什么沃尔菲这么着急？他想知道。

Wolfie pulled Jack and Annie toward the palace. “Hurry! I cannot be late!” he cried. 沃尔菲拉着杰克和安妮朝宫殿走去。 “快点！ 我不能迟到！”他喊道。

“Late for what?” said Jack. “迟到干什么？”杰克说。

Before Wolfie could answer, someone called his name. “Wolfie! Where are you?” It was Nan. She was standing on the back terrace of the palace. “Wolfie!” she called again. 沃尔菲还没来得及回答，有人叫了他的名字。 “沃尔菲！ 你在哪里？”是南。 她正站在宫殿后院的阳台上。 “狼菲！”她又喊了一遍。

“Coming!” shouted Wolfie. “Poor Nan! She is waiting for me! Papa is waiting for me! The whole world is waiting for me!” “来了！”狼菲喊道。 “可怜的南妮！ 她在等我！ 爸爸在等我！ 全世界都在等我！”，
