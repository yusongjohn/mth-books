2. Put On Your Wig! 2. 戴上你的假发！

Jack opened his eyes. He looked down at his clothes. He was wearing a blue velvet coat, a long vest, and knee-length pants. His shoes were black with shiny buckles. 杰克睁开了眼睛。 他低头看了看自己的衣服。 他穿着一件蓝色的天鹅绒外套、一件长背心和一条及膝的裤子。 他的鞋子是黑色的，上面有闪亮的扣子。

Jack looked up at Annie and burst out laughing. Her pigtails had changed into thick, cigar-shaped curls. She was wearing a pink dress covered with lace and bows. There was a big hoop underneath her skirt to hold it out. The hoop, the skirt, and lots of petticoats were all bunched up around Annie. 杰克抬头看着安妮，突然笑出声来。 她的麻花辫变成了又粗又长的卷发。 她穿着一条满是蕾丝和蝴蝶结的粉色连衣裙。 她的裙子下面还有一个大圆环，用来把裙子撑开。 圆环、裙子和很多衬裙全都堆在安妮的周围。

“You look like you’re standing in a basket,” said Jack. “你看起来像是站在一个篮子里，”杰克说。

“You look like an old woman,” said Annie. She pointed at Jack’s head. “你看起来像一个老奶奶，”安妮说。 她指着杰克的头。

Jack reached up and lifted off a black velvet hat. But something else was on his head, too. He pulled off a white wig with curls on the side and a ponytail in the back. 杰克伸手向上，摘下了一顶黑色天鹅绒帽子。 但他的头上还有别的东西。 他摘下了一顶白色的假发，假发的一侧有卷发，后面还有一条马尾辫。

“ A wig?” said Jack. His nose tickled. He sneezed, and a cloud of white powder puffed from his wig. “It’s covered in powder! I can’t wear this!” “假发？”杰克说。他的鼻子发痒。 他打了个喷嚏，假发里飘出了一团白色的粉末。 “它上面全是粉末！ 我不能戴这个！”

“Yes, you can,” said Annie. “If I have to wear this “—she shook her dress—”you have to wear that.” “可以的，”安妮说。 “如果我必须穿这个——”她摇晃着裙子——“那你必须穿那个。”

“But why are we dressed this way?” asked Jack. “但我们为什么穿成这样？”杰克问。

“Because we’re going to a fancy party in a palace a long time ago,” said Annie. “因为我们要去很久以前的一座宫殿参加一个盛大的聚会，”安妮说。

“Okay, but where’s the palace?” said Jack. “好吧，但宫殿在哪里？”杰克说。

Jack and Annie went to the window and looked out. They had landed in a row of trees that lined a cobblestone street. At the far end of the street, glittering horse-drawn coaches were parked outside tall iron gates. 杰克和安妮走到窗户前，朝外看。 他们降落在一条两旁种满树木的街道上，街道铺着鹅卵石。 在街道的尽头，闪闪发光的马车停在高大的铁门前。

“I wonder if the palace is beyond those gates,” said Annie, pointing. “我想宫殿可能就在那扇门后面，”安妮指着说。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “I wish we had a research book to help us.” “我不知道，”杰克说。 “我希望我们有一本研究书来帮我们。”

“Merlin wants us to rely on our wits and talents,” said Annie. “梅林希望我们依靠自己的智慧和才能，”安妮说。

“Yeah,” said Jack, “whatever that means.” “对，”杰克说，“不管那是什么意思。”

Bells rang in the distance: bong, bong, bong, bong, bong. 远处传来钟声：当、当、当、当、当。

“Five bongs,” said Annie. “What time’s the party?” “五下钟声，”安妮说。 “聚会几点开始？”

Jack looked at their invitation and read aloud, “‘Five o’clock in the evening.’” 杰克看了看他们的邀请函，大声读道：“‘傍晚五点。’”

“Oh, no! We’re going to be late!” said Annie. “Put on your wig!” “哦，不！ 我们要迟到了！”安妮说。 “戴上你的假发！”

Jack crammed his wig back onto his head and put on his hat. He pushed the silver flute and invitation into a deep pocket in his coat. 杰克把他的假发重新戴回头上，然后戴上帽子。 他把银色的笛子和邀请函塞进外套的深口袋里。

Annie squashed her hoopskirt and squeezed through the opening in the tree house floor. “It’s hard to climb down a rope ladder in this dress!” she said. 安妮把她的蓬蓬裙挤扁，从树屋地板上的开口处挤了过去。 “穿这身裙子，顺着绳梯往下爬可真难！”她说。

“Be careful! Go slow!” said Jack. “小心点！ 慢慢来！”杰克说。

“But we have to hurry!” said Annie. She jumped off the ladder and landed on the ground. “但我们得快点！”安妮说。 她从梯子上跳了下来，落在了地上。

Jack hurried down the ladder. “You okay?” he asked. 杰克赶紧顺着梯子爬了下来。 “你没事吧？”他问。

“I’m fine,” said Annie, standing up. “Just got a little dirty.” She brushed off her dress. “Let’s ask one of those guys about the palace.” She pointed toward the coach drivers outside the iron gates. “Hurry!” “我没事，”安妮站起来说。 “只是弄脏了一点。”她擦了擦裙子。 “我们问问那些人关于宫殿的事吧。”她指着铁门外的马车夫。 “快点！”

Annie started to run. Her skirt swung wildly from side to side like a giant bell. 安妮开始跑起来。 她的裙子像一个大铃铛一样，左右摇摆。

“Slow down!” said Jack, rushing to catch up with her. “You can’t run in that big skirt—you look ridiculous. Besides, we need to discuss our mission before we go to this party.” “慢点走！”杰克喊道，赶紧去追她。 “你穿着这么大的裙子不能跑——你看起来太滑稽了。 再说，我们去参加这个派对之前，得先讨论一下我们的任务。”

“It’s simple,” said Annie. “We’re looking for a brilliant—” “很简单，”安妮说道。 “我们正在寻找一位杰出的—”

“I know—artist,” finished Jack. “But how do we find this person?” “我知道—艺术家，”杰克补充道。 “但我们怎么找到这个人呢？”

“We’ll get to that part soon enough,” said Annie. “First let’s find the party.” “我们很快就会知道的，”安妮说道。 “首先，我们得找到宴会。”

Jack and Annie walked toward the fancy coaches parked near the gates. “Where’s our invitation?” asked Annie. 杰克和安妮朝停在大门附近的豪华马车走去。 “我们的邀请函在哪里？”安妮问道。

“Here.” Jack pulled the invitation out of his pocket. “在这里。”杰克从口袋里拿出邀请函。

“Excuse me,” said Annie, walking up to the driver of a golden coach pulled by two white horses. “We’re looking for the summer palace.” Jack showed their invitation to the man. “打扰一下，”安妮走到一辆由两匹白马拉着的金色马车前，对司机说道。 “我们在找夏天的宫殿。”杰克把邀请函展示给那个人看。

The driver nodded approvingly. “Ah, so you are guests of the imperial family!” he said. “But why are you arriving on foot? Where is your coach?” 司机点了点头，表示赞同。 “啊，原来你们是皇室的客人！”他说。 “但是，你们为什么步行而来？ 你们的马车在哪里？”

“Um, our driver let us out back there,” said Annie, pointing down the street. “嗯，我们的司机刚刚在那边让我们下车，”安妮说，指着街的尽头。

“I fear he let you out too soon,” said the driver. “You still have quite a distance to go.” “我担心他让你们下得太早了，”司机说。 “你们还有很远的路要走。”

“ We do?” said Annie. “我们还有很远的路要走吗？”安妮说。

“Yes,” said the driver. “I have already delivered my employer and his family to the palace. I was waiting here until the party ended. But if you like, I can take you there. Young nobles should always arrive by coach.” “是的，”司机说。 “我已经把我雇主和他的家人送到皇宫了。” 我在这里一直等到聚会结束。 但如果你愿意，我可以带你去那里。 年轻贵族应该一直坐马车到达。

“Oh, thanks!” said Annie. “哦，谢谢！”，安妮说道。

Jack straightened his shoulders and tried to look noble. 杰克挺直了肩膀，努力看起来像个贵族。

“My name is Josef,” said the man. “Come, let me help you up.” Josef held out his hand. Annie took it, and Josef helped her up onto a cushioned leather seat. “我的名字是约瑟夫，”那个人说。 “来，我帮你起来。”约瑟夫伸出了手。 安妮抓住了他的手，约瑟夫把她扶到了一个有垫子的皮质座位上。

Josef helped Jack up, too. Then the friendly driver climbed onto a bench behind the milk white horses. 约瑟夫也帮杰克爬了上来。 然后，那个友好的司机爬到了一辆白色奶牛马后面的长凳上。

“Wow, I feel like Cinderella going to the ball,” Annie said to Jack. “哇，我感觉自己像灰姑娘去参加舞会，”安妮对杰克说。

Josef snapped the reins. The horses took off, and the guards opened the gates. The coach clattered over the cobblestones into an immense square. 约瑟夫猛地拉紧缰绳。 马儿飞快地跑起来，守卫们打开了城门。 马车在鹅卵石路上颠簸着，驶入了一个巨大的广场。

The square was bathed in the last light of day. Monks in brown robes strolled around a huge fountain. Soldiers in uniforms rode on horseback. On the far side of the square was a long building. It had bright yellow walls and dozens of windows that flashed with the light of the setting sun. 广场沐浴在一天中最后的阳光里。 穿着棕色长袍的僧侣们在一座巨大的喷泉周围漫步。 穿着制服的士兵骑着马在广场上巡逻。 广场的另一边是一座长长的建筑。 它有着明亮的黄色墙壁，还有许多窗户在夕阳的映照下闪闪发光。

“Is that the summer palace, sir?” Annie called to Josef. “先生，那是夏天的宫殿吗？”安妮对约瑟夫喊道。

“Yes, indeed,” Josef said over his shoulder. “And there is much more that you cannot see! Behind the palace is a garden of nearly three hundred acres. It has beautiful flowers, orchards, fountains, and a zoo.” “是的，确实如此，”约瑟夫一边说一边回头看向他们。 “还有许多你无法看到的东西！ 宫殿后面有一个占地近三百英亩的花园。 那里有美丽的花朵、果园、喷泉，还有一个动物园。”

“A zoo?” said Jack. “动物园？”杰克问。

“Hello, boy and girl!” came a high little voice. A closed blue coach passed them. A small boy wearing a white wig was calling from a window. He pointed at Jack and laughed. “Your wig is crooked!” he shouted in his high voice. “你好，男孩和女孩！”一个高高的小声音传来。 一辆蓝色的马车从他们身边驶过。 一个戴着白色假发的小男孩从窗户里朝他们喊叫。 他指着杰克，笑了起来。 “你的假发歪了！”他用他那高高的声音喊道。

Before the boy could say more, someone pulled him back from the window, and the blue coach rumbled away. 就在男孩想说更多话的时候，有人把他从窗户边拉了回来，那辆蓝色的马车咕噜咕噜地开走了。

“What an annoying kid,” said Jack. “Is my wig crooked?” “真是个烦人的小孩，”杰克说道。 “我的假发歪了吗？”

“A little.” Annie lifted Jack’s hat off. She tried to straighten his wig as they lurched and swayed on the cushioned seat. “There,” she said. “That’s better.” “有一点。”安妮摘下了杰克的帽子。 她试图在他们坐在软垫座位上颠簸摇晃时把他的假发整理好。 “那儿，”她说。 “这样就好多了。”

Josef drove the horses to the front of the palace. The coach stopped near a staircase that led up to a terrace. Josef helped Jack and Annie down from their seat. 约瑟夫把马赶到宫殿的前面。 马车停在一座通往露台的台阶旁边。 约瑟夫帮杰克和安妮从座位上下来。

“I can tell this is your first visit to the palace,” said Josef. “At the door, you must show the guard in the red uniform your invitation. He will direct you to the receiving line.” “我能看出这是你第一次来宫殿，”约瑟夫说。 “在门口，你必须向穿红制服的卫兵出示邀请函。 他会带你去接待队列。”

“The receiving line? What’s that?” said Jack. “接待队列？ 那是什么？”杰克问。

“That is where you will wait to be introduced,” said Josef. “那就是你要等待被介绍的地方，”约瑟夫说。

“Introduced to who?” asked Jack. “被介绍给谁？”杰克问。

“To Her Imperial Majesty Maria Theresa. She is Archduchess of Austria; Queen of Hungary, Croatia, and Bohemia; and Empress of the Holy Roman Empire,” said Josef. “被介绍给神圣罗马帝国的女皇玛丽亚·特蕾莎。 她是奥地利大公；匈牙利、克罗地亚和波希米亚的女王；以及神圣罗马帝国的女皇，”约瑟夫说。

“Oh, right,” said Annie. “哦，对了，”安妮说。

Yeah, right, thought Jack. Help! 杰克心想，没错，真需要帮忙！
