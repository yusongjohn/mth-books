1. To the Summer Palace 1. 前往夏宫

Jack was sitting at the computer in the living room. He was doing research on penguins for a school project. His mom and dad were cooking in the kitchen. Jack could smell bread baking in the oven and spaghetti sauce bubbling on top of the stove. 杰克正坐在客厅的电脑前。 他正在为学校的项目研究企鹅。 他的妈妈和爸爸正在厨房里做饭。 杰克能闻到烤面包的香味，还能听到炉子上意大利面酱咕嘟咕嘟的声音。

“Jack!” Annie burst into the living room. “Come with me!” she said. “杰克！”安妮冲进客厅。 “跟我来！”她说。

“What’s going on?” said Jack. “怎么了？”杰克问。

“They’re here!” said Annie. “他们来了！”安妮说。

“Teddy? Kathleen?” asked Jack. “泰迪？ 凯瑟琳？”杰克问。

Annie nodded, her eyes shining. 安妮点点头，眼睛闪闪发亮。

“Oh, man!” Jack whispered. He jumped up from his chair and grabbed his jacket and backpack. “Mom, Dad—we’ll be back soon!” he called. “天啊！”杰克小声说。 他从椅子上跳起来，抓起外套和背包。 “妈妈，爸爸——我们很快就回来！”他喊道。

“Dinner’s in thirty minutes,” their mom called from the kitchen. “三十分钟后开饭，”妈妈从厨房里喊道。

“No problem!” said Annie. Then she and Jack headed out the front door into the cool spring air. “没问题！”安妮说。 然后她和杰克从正门走出去，走进了凉爽的春日空气里。

“Where did you see them?” said Jack. “你在哪里看到他们的？”杰克说。

“At the edge of the woods!” said Annie. “I was riding my bike home from my piano lesson. When they saw me, they waved.” “在树林边！”安妮说。 “我骑着自行车从钢琴课回家， 他们看到我时，朝我挥手了。”

“You didn’t stop and talk to them?” said Jack. “你没停下来和他们说话吗？”杰克问。

“No, I pointed to the house,” said Annie, “to let them know I had to get you first.” “没有，我指着房子，”安妮说，“告诉他们我得先去接你。”

“Oh! Thanks!” said Jack. “We’d better hurry!” 谢谢！”杰克说。 “我们最好快点！”

“I wonder where they’re sending us!” said Annie as they crossed their yard and headed up the sidewalk. “I wonder what our next mission is! Hey, did you bring the Wand of Dianthus?” “我不知道他们要把我们送到哪里！”安妮一边穿过院子，一边沿着小路往树屋的方向走，说道。 “我不知道我们的下一个任务是什么！ 嘿，你带了迪安瑟斯的魔杖吗？

“Yep, it’s in my backpack!” said Jack. “带了，它在我的背包里！”杰克说道。

Jack and Annie ran into the Frog Creek woods. They hurried through the shadowy light of late afternoon, until they came to the tallest tree. Jack looked up. The tree house was back. The two young enchanters from Camelot were looking out the window. 杰克和安妮跑进了蛙溪镇的森林里。 他们匆匆穿过傍晚昏暗的光线，直到来到最高的那棵树前。杰克抬头看了看。 树屋又回来了。 来自卡梅洛特的两位年轻魔法师正从窗户里往外看。

“Hi!” yelled Jack and Annie. “你好！”杰克和安妮喊道。

“Hello!” Teddy and Kathleen shouted. “你好！”泰迪和凯瑟琳喊道。

Annie grabbed the rope ladder and climbed up into the tree house. Jack followed. 安妮抓住绳梯爬进了树屋。杰克跟了上去。

“We’re so glad you’re back!” said Annie. She hugged Teddy and Kathleen. Jack hugged them, too. “我们很高兴你回来！”安妮说道。 她拥抱了泰迪和凯瑟琳。 杰克也拥抱了他们。

“How’s Penny?” Jack asked. He’d been missing the little penguin they had given Merlin on their last adventure. “佩妮怎么样了？”杰克问道。 他一直想念上次冒险时送给梅林的小企鹅。

“Oh, Penny and Merlin have become the best of friends,” said Kathleen. “She has brought him much joy and laughter.” “哦，佩妮和梅林已经成为最好的朋友了，”凯瑟琳说道。 “她给梅林带来了许多快乐和笑声。”

“Cool,” said Jack. He wasn’t surprised. Penny had made him really happy, too. “太酷了，”杰克说道。 他一点都不惊讶。 佩妮也让他非常开心。

“What do you want us to do now?” said Annie. “我们现在该做什么呢？”安妮说。

“On your last missions, you found secrets of happiness to help Merlin,” said Kathleen. “在你上次的任务中，你找到了幸福的秘密来帮助梅林，”凯瑟琳说。

Jack and Annie nodded. 杰克和安妮点了点头。

“Now, on your next mission, Merlin wants you to help bring happiness to millions of people,” said Kathleen. “现在，在你们下一次任务中，梅林希望你们帮助给数百万人带来幸福，”凯瑟琳说。

“Whoa,” said Jack. “That’s a big job.” “哇，”杰克说。 “这可是一个大任务。”

Teddy and Kathleen laughed. 泰迪和凯瑟琳笑了。

“How do we do that?” asked Jack. “我们该怎么做呢？”杰克问。

“Simple,” said Teddy. “You must seek out a brilliant artist—” “简单，”泰迪说。 “你必须找到一位杰出的艺术家——”

“Do you mean like a painter?” asked Annie. “你是说像画家那样的人吗？”安妮问。

“It could be,” said Teddy. “But it could also be any person who uses passion and imagination to create something beautiful.” “可能是，”泰迪说。 “但也可以是任何用热情和想象力创造出美丽事物的人。”

“Merlin wants you to help put that artist on the right path,” said Teddy, “to share his or her gifts with the world.” “梅林希望你帮忙让那位艺术家走上正确的道路，”泰迪说，“把他的或她的礼物分享给全世界。”

“Oh, cool!” said Annie. “Where do we start?” “哦，太酷了！”安妮说。 “我们从哪里开始？”

Kathleen took a creamy white envelope from her cloak. It was sealed with melted red wax. In fancy writing, it said: For Jack and Annie of Frog Creek. “‘ Tis a royal invitation,” said Kathleen. 凯瑟琳从她的斗篷里拿出一封奶油白色的信封。 它用融化的红色蜡封着。 用漂亮的字写着：献给蛙溪镇的杰克和安妮。 “这是皇家邀请函，”凯瑟琳说。

Jack took the envelope. He carefully broke the seal and pulled out a thick card with gold edges and gold script. He read aloud: 杰克拿起了信封。 他小心地拆开封口，拿出一张厚卡片，卡片边缘和上面的字都是金色的。他大声读道：

图中文字：You are invited to a party

at the Summer Palace

October 13, 1762

five o'clock in the evening 图中文字：你被邀请参加1762年10月13日晚上五点在颐和园举行的聚会

“A party at a summer palace! In 1762!” said Annie. “夏天宫殿里的聚会！ “在1762年！”安妮说。

“Yes,” said Teddy. “The palace is in Vienna, Austria. It is one of the most splendid palaces in all the world.” “是的，”泰迪说。 “宫殿在奥地利的维也纳。 它是世界上最宏伟的宫殿之一。”

“That sounds like fun,” said Annie. “听起来很有趣，”安妮说。

“Indeed, it should be,” said Kathleen. “But you must watch your manners. And beware of unexpected dangers.” “确实应该这样，”凯瑟琳说。 “但你必须注意礼貌。 还要提防意想不到的危险。”

“What kind of dangers?” asked Jack. “什么危险？”杰克问。

“I do not know,” said Kathleen. “But Merlin believes you will need magic to keep safe. Did you bring the Wand of Dianthus?” “我不知道，”凯瑟琳说。 “但梅林认为你需要魔法来保护自己。 你带了迪安瑟斯的魔杖吗？”

“Yes,” said Jack. He reached into his backpack and pulled out the silvery wand. It was shaped like the horn of a unicorn. “带了，”杰克说。 他伸手到背包里，拿出那根银色的魔杖。 它形状像独角兽的角。

Kathleen took the wand from Jack. She closed her eyes and spun it through the air, as if she were spinning a baton. In a blur of light and movement, the wand changed into a small silver flute. 凯瑟琳从杰克手里拿走了魔杖。 她闭上眼睛，把魔杖在空中转了个圈，就像在转一根接力棒。 在一阵光和运动的模糊中，魔杖变成了一支小小的银色笛子。

“Wow,” said Annie. “哇，”安妮说。

“A flute?” asked Jack. “是笛子吗？”杰克问。

“A magic flute,” said Teddy. “Playing this flute will deliver you from danger.” “是魔法笛子，”泰迪说。 “吹响这支笛子会把你从危险中解救出来。”

“But Jack and I don’t know how to play the flute,” said Annie. “但是杰克和我不会吹笛子，”安妮说。

“Do not worry,” said Kathleen. “If the time is right, the flute will make its own music.” “不用担心，”凯瑟琳说。 “如果时机合适，笛子会自己发出音乐。”

“Just blow over the mouthpiece,” said Teddy. “And while one of you plays, the other must make up a song. Whatever you sing will come true.” “只要对着喇叭吹就行了，”泰迪说。 “当一个人在演奏时，另一个人必须编一首歌。 你们唱的歌会成真。”

“Cool!” said Annie. “太酷了！”安妮说。

“But when your song has ended, the flute’s magic will end, too,” said Kathleen. “You can only use the flute’s magic once, so choose the time wisely.” “但当你们的歌结束时，笛子的魔法也会结束，”凯瑟琳说。 “你们只能使用一次笛子的魔法，所以要选好时机。”

“Okay, got it!” said Annie. “好的，明白了！”安妮说。

“Are you ready to go?” asked Kathleen. “你们准备好了吗？”凯瑟琳问。

“Uh, wait,” said Jack. “Party invitation … magic flute … Is that all? Don’t you have a research book for us?” “呃，等等，”杰克说。 “派对邀请函……魔法笛子……就这些吗？ “你们不是有一本研究书给我们吗？”

“On this journey, Merlin wants you to rely only on your wits and your talents,” said Teddy. “在这次旅程中，梅林希望你们只依靠自己的智慧和才能，”泰迪说。

“Oh. Okay,” said Jack, though he wasn’t sure he had many talents. “好的，”杰克说，尽管他不确定自己有多少才能。

“Tell the magic tree house where to go by pointing to the words on the invitation,” said Kathleen. “通过指向邀请函上的文字，告诉神奇树屋要前往哪里，”凯瑟琳说。

Annie pointed to the words Summer Palace. But before she made her wish, she looked at Teddy and Kathleen. “I hope we see you again very soon,” she said. “Say hi to Merlin and Morgan when you go back to Camelot.” 安妮指向了文字“夏宫”。 但在她许愿之前，她看了看泰迪和凯瑟琳。 “希望我们很快能再见到你们，”她说。 “回到卡梅洛特时，记得跟梅林和摩根打个招呼。”

“And Penny, too,” said Jack. “还有彭妮，”杰克说。

Kathleen and Teddy both smiled. 凯瑟琳和泰迪都笑了。

“Yes, we will,” said Teddy. “是的，我们会去的，”泰迪说。

Annie took a deep breath. “Okay. I wish we could go there!” she said. “To the summer palace!” 安妮深吸了一口气。 “好吧。 我希望我们能去那里！”她说。 “去夏天的宫殿！”

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
