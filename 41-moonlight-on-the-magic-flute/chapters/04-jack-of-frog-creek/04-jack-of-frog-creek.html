4. Jack of Frog Creek! 4. 蛙溪镇的杰克！

Annie was laughing so hard that she could barely stand up. “What—what happened to you in there?” she said. “After I curtsied, I left. When I looked back in the room, you were still bowing!” 安妮笑得实在太厉害了，几乎站不起来。 “你——你里面发生了什么？”她说。 “我行了个屈膝礼，然后就离开了。” 当我回头看向房间时，你还在鞠躬！

“She didn’t say ‘rise’!” said Jack. “她没说‘起来’！”杰克说。

“Yes, she did, dummy!” said Annie. “是的，她做到了，笨蛋！”安妮说。

“Well, I didn’t hear her!” said Jack. He turned away from Annie and started walking as fast as he could. He strode through one elegant room into another. He didn’t know where he was. These rooms all look the same, he thought angrily, withstupid velvet furniture and stupid gold-trimmed walls. “好吧，我没听见她！”杰克说。 他转身离开安妮，尽可能快地走开了。 他大步走进一个华丽的房间，又走进另一个房间。 他不知道自己在哪里。 这些房间看起来都一模一样，他生气地想，愚蠢的天鹅绒家具，愚蠢的镶金墙壁。

“Jack, wait!” Annie called. She hurried after him. “杰克，等等！”安妮喊道。 她赶紧跟了上去。

Jack kept walking, desperate to get far away from the scene of his disaster. Finally he came to a door that led outside. He opened it and stepped out onto a long marble terrace overlooking the back of the palace. Bright silvery light from a full moon shone on a huge garden. 杰克继续往前走，拼命想离开灾难发生的地方。 终于，他来到一扇通向外面的门前。 他打开门，走了出去，站在一个长长的大理石露台上，露台正对着宫殿的后方。 满月发出明亮的银色光芒，照在一片巨大的花园上。

Jack stood in the chilly air, trying to calm down. He took a deep breath. He desperately wanted to run back to the tree house and go home. 杰克站在寒冷的空气中，努力让自己冷静下来。 他深吸了一口气。 他非常想跑回树屋，然后回家。

“Jack!” Annie slipped out to join him. “I’m sorry I called you a dummy,” she said. “Are you okay?” “杰克！”安妮溜出来，走到他身边。 “对不起，我刚才叫你笨蛋，”她说。 “你还好吗？”

“I didn’t hear her say to rise,” said Jack. “And the flute fell out of my coat and my hat came off … and my wig … and I sneezed and I slipped…. Come on, let’s leave. We can go down these stairs.” “我没听到她说要起床，”杰克说。 “而且笛子从我的外套里掉出来了，我的帽子掉了……我的假发……我打了个喷嚏，然后滑了一跤…… 快点，我们走吧。 “我们可以从这些楼梯下去。”

“No, we can’t leave now,” said Annie. “ We have a mission from Merlin. Don’t feel bad. Hardly anyone saw what you did.” “不，我们现在不能离开，”安妮说。 “我们从梅林那里接到了任务。不要难过了。” 几乎没人看到你做了什么。”

“Yeah, except Her Imperial Majesty and all those imperial kids,” said Jack. “They all laughed at me.” “是啊，除了她的帝国陛下和那些帝国的孩子们，”杰克说。 “他们都嘲笑我。”

“They weren’t being mean,” said Annie. “You were funny. Here, give me your wig.” “他们并不是故意的，”安妮说。 “你刚才真搞笑。” 来，把你的假发给我。

Jack handed Annie his wig, and she put it back on his head and straightened it. 杰克把他的假发递给安妮，安妮把它戴回他头上并整理好。

“Your hat,” she said. “你的帽子，”她说。

Jack gave her his hat, and she placed it over his wig. “Hide the flute,” said Annie. 杰克把他的帽子给了她，她把它戴在了他的假发上。 “把笛子藏好，”安妮说。

Jack stuck the silver flute back into his pocket. “I don’t know what we need this flute for,” he said. “There’s nothing dangerous here. I don’t get this whole mission.” 杰克把那支银色的笛子又放回了口袋里。 “我不知道我们为什么需要这支笛子，”他说。 “这里没什么危险的。 我完全不明白这次任务的意义。”

“We’ll figure it out. Let’s just go back in,” said Annie. She pulled Jack back inside the palace. “我们一定能想明白。” “我们还是回去吧，”安妮说。 她把杰克拉回了宫殿里面。

“So what do we do now?” asked Jack. “那我们现在该怎么办呢？”杰克问。

“We should find the party we were invited to,” said Annie. “That must be what Merlin wanted us to do.” She pointed across the room. People were streaming through a set of doors into another room. Loud party noises came from inside: the chatter of guests, tinkling china, and harp and violin music. “我们应该去找我们被邀请参加的聚会，”安妮说。 “这一定是梅林想让我们做的事。”她指着房间的另一边。 人们正从一扇门涌入另一个房间。 房间里传来热闹的聚会声音：客人们的谈话声、瓷器叮当声，还有竖琴和小提琴的音乐声。

Jack pulled back. But Annie took him by the arm. “Don’t worry, I’ll bet there are hundreds of people in there,” she said. “We’ll get lost in the crowd.” 杰克往后退了一步。 但安妮抓住了他的手臂。 “别担心，我敢打赌里面肯定有几百个人，”她说。 “我们会被人群淹没的。”

“But what about all those kids and Her Imperial Majesty?” Jack asked. “但是那些孩子们和她的陛下呢？”杰克问。

“They don’t care about us,” said Annie. “They have too many other guests to think about. Come on.” Annie led Jack through the double doors into the party room. “他们才不会管我们呢，”安妮说。 “他们有太多其他客人要照顾，根本不会想到我们。” 快点。”安妮带着杰克穿过双门，走进了派对房间。

“Whoa,” breathed Jack. He and Annie stopped and stared. “哇，”杰克惊叹道。 他和安妮停下了脚步，目不转睛地望着。

The party room was the size of a football field. Grand paintings covered the towering ceiling. Everything was trimmed in gold. The glossy white walls and tall mirrors reflected the glow of at least a thousand candles. 派对房间有足球场那么大。 巨大的画作覆盖着高高的天花板。 一切都镶着金边。 光滑的白色墙壁和高大的镜子反射出至少一千支蜡烛的光芒。

Musicians played harps and violins while hundreds of guests stood around long dinner tables, chatting and laughing. Women fanned themselves, their diamonds and rubies flashing in the candlelight. The air smelled of perfume, powder, and roses. 音乐家们弹着竖琴和小提琴，数百位客人站在长长的晚餐桌旁，聊天、欢笑。 女人们用扇子扇风，她们的钻石和红宝石在烛光下闪闪发光。 空气里弥漫着香水、粉饼和玫瑰的香味。

“Okay! Let’s talk about our mission,” said Annie. “ We have to help a brilliant artist get on the right path to bring joy to the world.” “好！ 我们来谈谈我们的任务，”安妮说。 “我们必须帮助一位才华横溢的艺术家走上正确的道路，为世界带来欢乐。”

“Yeah, but first we have to find one …,” said Jack. “是啊，但首先我们得找到他……，”杰克说。

“Right, so let’s look around,” said Annie. “对，那我们四处看看吧，”安妮说。

Jack and Annie started drifting together through the huge room. Jack gazed at the grownups. Dressed in their finery, they all looked pretty much the same. What does a brilliant artist look like? Jack wondered. 杰克和安妮一起漂浮着穿过那个大房间。 杰克看着那些大人。 他们穿着华丽的衣服，看起来都差不多。 一个杰出的艺术家是什么样子的呢？杰克想知道。

“Jack of Frog Creek!” came a high little voice. “蛙溪镇的杰克！”一个高高的小声音说道。

Oh, no! thought Jack. He whirled around. 哦，不！杰克心想。 他猛地转过身。

The boy with the tiny sword was grinning up at him. “I have been looking all over for you!” he said. 那个拿着小剑的男孩正冲着他笑。 “我一直在找你！”他说。

“Hi!” said Annie. “What’s your name?” “你好！”安妮说。 “你叫什么名字？”

“Wolfie,” said the boy. “沃尔菲，”男孩说。

“That’s a funny name,” said Annie. “这个名字真有趣，”安妮说。

“So is Jack of Frog Creek!” said Wolfie. His eyes shone as he looked up at Jack. “Are you a clown?” he asked. “杰克来自蛙溪镇的名字也很有趣！”沃尔菲说。 他抬头看着杰克，眼睛闪闪发亮。 “你是小丑吗？”他问。

Annie giggled. 安妮咯咯地笑起来。

“Yeah, that’s right, I’m a clown,” said Jack. “是的，没错，我是个小丑，”杰克说。

“How old are you, Wolfie?” said Annie, changing the subject. “沃尔菲你几岁了？”安妮说，她改变了话题。

“Six!” the little boy said. “六岁！”小男孩说。

“Six?” said Jack. This kid looked more like a four-year-old, he thought, five at the most. “六岁？”杰克说。 这个孩子看起来更像是四岁，他想，最多五岁。

“And I’m eleven,” a girl said. “我十一岁，”一个女孩说。

For the first time, Jack noticed the girl standing behind Wolfie. She wore a white dress with red roses. She was the girl who had helped them in line! 第一次，杰克注意到女孩站在沃尔菲后面。 她穿着一件白色连衣裙，上面有红色的玫瑰。 她就是之前在队伍里帮助过他们的人！

“Hi!” said Annie. “你好！”安妮说。

“Hello again,” the girl said in a soft, lovely voice. “I am Wolfie’s sister.” “很高兴再次见到你，”女孩用柔和、可爱的声音说。 “我是沃尔菲的姐姐。”

Jack felt his face grow red. The girl must have seen him make a fool of himself. 杰克感觉自己的脸变得通红。 那个女孩一定看到他出丑了。

“My name is Nannerl,” said the girl. “我叫纳内尔，”女孩说。

“Nan-nerl?” said Annie, trying to pronounce her name. “纳-内尔？”安妮试着念她的名字。

The girl smiled. “You can call me Nan if you like,” she said. “Wolfie and I enjoyed your performance in the Great Rosa Room, Jack. You must be very proud. Her Imperial Majesty does not laugh easily.” 女孩笑了。 “如果你愿意的话，可以叫我南，”她说。 “沃尔菲和我看了你在大罗莎房间的表演，杰克。 你一定非常自豪。 她的皇太后陛下不轻易笑。”

Jack shrugged and scratched his wig. He wondered if the girl was joking. But she looked serious, so he decided not to tell her he hadn’t meant to be funny. 杰克耸了耸肩，挠了挠他的假发。 他想知道女孩是不是在开玩笑。 但是她看起来很严肃，所以他决定不告诉她自己其实不是故意搞笑的。

“Do you call your mother ‘Her Imperial Majesty’?” Annie asked Nan. “你们叫你妈妈‘她的帝国陛下’吗？”安妮问南。

Nan looked confused. “No.” 南看起来很困惑。 “不。”

“ We call her Mama!” said Wolfie. “我们叫她妈妈！”沃尔菲说。

“But you just said Her Imperial Majesty doesn’t laugh much,” said Annie. “但是你刚才说‘她的帝国陛下’不太爱笑，”安妮说。

“Oh, Her Imperial Majesty is not our mother!” Nan said. “Our mother is back home in Salzburg. We are just visiting the palace.” “哦，‘她的帝国陛下’不是我们的妈妈！”南说。 “我们的妈妈在萨尔茨堡的家里。 我们只是来宫殿参观的。”

“Then why was Wolfie sitting on Her Imperial Majesty’s lap?” asked Annie. “那为什么沃尔菲坐在‘她的帝国陛下’的腿上？”安妮问。

“Because she likes me very much!” Wolfie piped up. “因为她非常喜欢我！”沃尔菲插话道。

Oh, brother, thought Jack. 哦，天哪，杰克心想。

“Wolfie, be modest,” said Nan, shaking her head. “Actually, Wolfie jumped in her lap when we presented ourselves. I tried to grab him, but Her Imperial Majesty wanted him to stay with her.” “沃尔菲，要谦虚一点，”南说，摇了摇头。 “其实，当我们自我介绍时，沃尔菲跳到了她的腿上。 我试图抓住他，但她的皇室 Majesty 她想让他留在她身边。”

“Were all those other kids imperial children?” Annie asked. “那些其他的孩子都是皇室的孩子吗？”安妮问。

“Oh, yes,” said Nan. “Papa taught me all their names before we came here: Leopold, Ferdinand, Maximilian, Joseph, Maria Antonia, Maria Caroline, Maria Josepha, Maria Amalie, Maria Elisabeth, Maria Christina, Maria Johanna, and Maria Anna.” “哦，是的，”南说。 “爸爸在我们来这里之前就教过我他们的名字：利奥波德、费迪南德、马克西米利安、约瑟夫、玛丽亚·安东尼亚、玛丽亚·卡罗琳娜、玛丽亚·约瑟法、玛丽亚·阿马莉亚、玛丽亚·伊丽莎白、玛丽亚·克里斯蒂娜、玛丽亚·约翰娜和玛丽亚·安娜。”

“Hmm,” said Jack. “Maria’s a popular name around here.” “嗯，”杰克说。 “玛丽亚在这里是个很受欢迎的名字。”

Nan laughed. Jack liked making her laugh. 南笑了。 杰克喜欢让她笑。

“Hey, look at me!” said Wolfie. He pulled off his wig. He pretended to sneeze and then fell to the floor. “I’m Jack the Clown!” he said. “嘿，看我！”，沃尔菲说。 他摘下了假发。 他假装打喷嚏，然后摔倒在地板上。 “我是杰克小丑！”，他说。

“Ha-ha. Very funny,” said Jack, forcing a smile. He’d had just about enough of Wolfie. “哈哈哈。 真有趣，”杰克说着，勉强笑了笑。 他对沃尔菲已经忍无可忍了。

“Nan, we have a question for you,” said Annie. “Do you know if there are any brilliant artists here at the party?” “南，我们有个问题要问你，”安妮说。 “你知道派对上有没有特别厉害的艺术家吗？”

“I haven’t really been in Vienna long enough to meet many people,” said Nan. “But Papa told me that artists do live—” “我还没在维也纳待很久，认识的人不多，”南说。 “但是爸爸告诉我，艺术家确实住在这里——”

“Wait!” Wolfie broke in. He jumped to his feet. “I know someone here who is very brilliant.” “等等！”沃尔菲插话道。 他跳了起来。 “我知道这里有人特别厉害。”

“Who?” asked Annie. “谁？”安妮问。

“Me!” said Wolfie. And he bowed. “我！”沃尔菲说。然后他鞠了一躬。

“Wolfie,” said Nan, shaking her head. “沃尔菲，”南摇着头说。

“Right,” said Jack. He turned back to Nan. “What were you about to say?” “对，”杰克说。 他转身对南说。 “你刚才想说什么？”

But Wolfie broke in again. “Nan and I are both brilliant!” he exclaimed in his high voice. “Our papa teaches us math, history, writing, reading, geography, music, drawing, riding, fencing, and dancing.” Wolfie raised his arms over his head and did a little dance step. 但沃尔菲又插话了。 “南和我都很棒！”他用高高的声音喊道。 “我们的爸爸教我们数学、历史、写作、阅读、地理、音乐、绘画、骑马、击剑和跳舞。”沃尔菲把双臂举过头顶，跳了一个小舞步。

Annie laughed. 安妮笑了。

“Wolfie, stop!” said Nan. “沃尔菲，停下！”南说。

Yeah, cool it, kid, thought Jack. 是啊，冷静点，孩子，杰克心想。

“Do you want to go outside and play in the garden?” Wolfie asked Jack and Annie. “ We can all dance!” “你们想出去到花园里玩吗？”沃尔菲问杰克和安妮。 “我们都可以跳舞！”

“I don’t think so, Wolfie,” said Jack. “But thanks.” He turned back to Nan. “So! What were you going to tell us about artists?” “我不这么认为，沃尔菲，”杰克说。 “但谢谢。”他转身面对南娜。 “所以！ 你们要告诉我们关于艺术家的事情吗？

“Oh, yes,” said Nan. “Papa told me that Her Imperial Majesty often invites artists to live in the palace and work here.” “哦，是的，”南说。 “爸爸告诉我，她的陛下经常邀请艺术家到宫殿里住，并在这里工作。”

“Perfect!” said Jack. “太棒了！”杰克说。

“Do you think any of them are here at the party now?” asked Annie. “你觉得他们现在有谁在派对上吗？”安妮问。

“I don’t know,” said Nan. “The palace is very big. Papa told us that more than fifteen hundred people live here. Why are you looking for brilliant artists?” “我不知道，”纳恩说。 “宫殿非常大。 爸爸告诉我们，这里住着一千五百多个人。 你们为什么在找杰出的艺术家？

“Oh,” said Annie, “we’re on a mission to—” “哦，”安妮说，“我们正在执行一项任务——”

Jack interrupted her. “… to party with brilliant artists,” he finished. “That’s our mission.” Then he laughed because he knew he sounded stupid. 杰克打断了她。 “……和杰出的艺术家一起聚会，”他接着说。 “这就是我们的任务。”然后他笑了，因为他知道自己听起来很傻。

Nan laughed, too. “I see,” she said. “All right then, at dinner I will ask Papa where the artists stay.” 南也笑了。 “我明白了，”她说。 “那好吧，晚饭时我会问爸爸那些艺术家住在哪儿。”

“Thank you!” said Jack. Finally, he thought, they were starting their Merlin mission! “谢谢你！”杰克说。 终于，他想，他们开始执行梅林任务了！
