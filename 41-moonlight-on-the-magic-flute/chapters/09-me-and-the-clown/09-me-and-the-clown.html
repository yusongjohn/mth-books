9. Me and the Clown 9. 我和小丑

“Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart,” said Annie. “沃尔夫冈·阿玛多伊斯·莫扎特，”安妮说。

“Yeah,” Jack sighed. “And right now he’s just a little kid.” “是的，”杰克叹了口气。 “而现在他只是一个小孩。”

“So I guess we did our mission,” said Annie. “Remember what Wolfie said to Nan? He said we made him love music again. So I guess we put him back on the path to giving his gifts to the world.” “所以我想我们完成任务了，”安妮说。 “还记得沃尔菲对南娜说过的话吗？ 他说我们让他重新爱上了音乐。 所以我想我们把他送回了继续把他的天赋献给世界的道路上。”

“Yep, and now we can leave,” said Jack. “Whew.” He was ready to get out of his fancy clothes and wig and go home and have a good dinner. “是啊，现在我们可以离开了，”杰克说。 “哇，”他准备好脱掉他的华丽衣服和假发，回家吃顿好吃的晚餐了。

“Let’s go say good-bye to Wolfie,” said Annie. “我们去跟沃尔菲道别吧，”安妮说。

As Jack and Annie started toward Wolfie, Her Imperial Majesty was laughing at something he had said. “You are truly a young magician, my little Wolfgang Mozart!” she said. 当杰克和安妮朝沃尔菲走去时，她的帝国陛下正在笑他刚才说的话。 “你真是个年轻的小魔法师，我的小沃尔夫冈·莫扎特！”，她说。

“No, not me,” said Wolfie. “Jack and Annie are the true magicians!” “不，不是我，”沃尔菲说。 “杰克和安妮才是真正的小魔法师！”

“Who?” said Her Imperial Majesty. “谁？”女皇陛下说。

“Over there,” said Wolfie, pointing across the room. “Jack! Annie!” he called. “在那里，”沃尔夫指了指房间的另一边。 “杰克！ 安妮！”他喊道。

All eyes turned to Jack and Annie. 所有人的目光都转向了杰克和安妮。

“Look! It’s Jack of Frog Creek!” said one of the imperial children. “看！ 他是来自蛙溪镇的杰克！”一个皇室的孩子说。

Jack froze, horrified. 杰克吓得僵在原地。

“Jack is a clown,” said Wolfie. “And he plays magic music on his flute. And Annie sings beautiful magic songs. I just heard them.” “杰克是个小丑，”沃尔夫说。 “而且他用笛子演奏魔法音乐。” 安妮唱着美妙的魔法歌曲。 我刚听到他们。”

“Really?” said Her Imperial Majesty. She raised her eyebrows. “Well, Jack and Annie. Perhaps you will perform for all of us here as well?” “真的吗？”女王陛下说道。 她挑起了眉毛。 “嗯，杰克和安妮。” 也许你们也会在这里为大家表演一下吧？

Everyone was silent, waiting for an answer from Jack and Annie. 大家都沉默了，等着杰克和安妮给出答案。

“Um, well, you see …,” Jack began. “呃，你看啊……，”杰克开始说道。

“Sure,” said Annie with a big smile. “We’d love to perform for you.” “当然，”安妮带着灿烂的微笑说道。 “我们很乐意为你表演。”

Oh, no! thought Jack. 哦，不！杰克心想。

“Yes! Sing for us! Play for us!” said Wolfie, clapping his hands. He ran to Jack and Annie and pulled them toward the front of the room. “太好了！给我们唱一首歌！ 给我们演奏一下吧！”沃尔菲边说边鼓掌。 他跑到杰克和安妮面前，把他们拉到房间前面。

Annie leaned close to Jack. “You play; I’ll sing,” she whispered. 安妮靠近杰克。 “你来演奏；我来唱歌，”她小声说。

“But the flute won’t make magic anymore,” Jack said out of the corner of his mouth. “但笛子已经不会再产生魔法了，”杰克小声说。

“Do the best you can,” whispered Annie. She smiled at the crowd. “尽你最大的努力，”安妮小声说。 她对观众微笑了。

Jack could hardly breathe. This is worse than facing the leopard and bear! he thought. 杰克几乎喘不过气来。 这比面对豹子和熊还要糟糕！他心想。

“Me, then you,” Annie said to him. “我先来，然后是你，”安妮对他说。

Jack reached into his pocket and pulled out the silver flute. Annie started singing: 杰克把手伸进口袋，拿出了一支银色的笛子。 安妮开始唱歌：

We came in a coach,

In a wig and a gown.We came to Vienna,

Me and the clown. 我们坐大巴来的，戴了假发和长袍。我们来到维也纳，还有小丑陪着。

Annie turned to Jack. As everyone watched him, he held the instrument in a playing position. He desperately hoped a little magic still lingered in the flute. He blew air over the mouthpiece. 安妮转向杰克。 当大家看着他时，他把乐器摆成演奏的姿势。 他拼命希望笛子里还有一点点魔法。 他对着笛子的吹口吹气。

No sound came out. All anyone could hear was Jack’s huffing and puffing. 笛子却一点声音也没有发出。 只能听到杰克喘粗气的声音。

Wolfie giggled. “See! Jack’s a clown!” he said. 沃尔菲咯咯地笑了。 “看！ 杰克是个小丑！”，他说。

Everyone laughed. 大家都笑了。

Okay, thought Jack, if they want a clown, I’ll be a clown. Jack put on a goofy face. He looked into the end of the flute, as if searching for its missing sound. He jerked back his head, as if something had shot out of the flute and hit him in the eye. 好吧，杰克心想，如果他们想要一个小丑，那我就当个小丑。 杰克装出一个滑稽的脸。 他把脸凑到笛子的尽头，好像在寻找笛子丢失的声音。 他猛地把头缩回来，好像有什么东西从笛子里射出来，打中了他的眼睛。

Everyone laughed harder. 大家笑得更厉害了。

Jack liked hearing their laughter this time. He made a big show of wiping his eye. Then he gave Annie a silly grin. 杰克这次喜欢听他们笑的声音。 他夸张地擦了擦眼睛。 然后他对着安妮露出一个傻笑。

Annie shook her head and sang: 安妮摇摇头，开始唱歌：

My brother’s a fool,

He’s always around.

Why is it always

Me and the clown? 我哥哥是个傻瓜，他总是缠着我。为什么总是我跟那个小丑在一起？

Jack pretended to bonk her on the head with his flute. He tried playing again, but again no sound came out. He turned the flute upside down and shook it, as if he were trying to shake out some music. 杰克假装用他的笛子敲了安妮的头。 他又试着吹了一次，但还是没有声音出来。 他把笛子倒过来摇晃，好像在努力把音乐摇出来。

Annie rolled her eyes, to show she thought Jack was hopeless. Then she nodded at him, motioning for him to walk with her. As they moved away from the front of the room, Annie sang the last verse of her song: 安妮翻了个白眼，表示她觉得杰克一点希望也没有。 然后她点点头，示意他跟着她走。 当他们离开房间前面的时候，安妮唱了她歌曲的最后一段：

It’s time to go,

It’s time to leave town.

Good-bye from me, and

Good-bye from the clown. 该走了，该离开小镇了。我向你告别，小丑也向你告别。

Crossing the room, Jack put the flute to his lips again. As he followed Annie to the door, he looked at the crowd and winked. Then he started to make up his own flute sounds. “Tweetle-tweetletweetle!” he sang. “Toot-toot-ah-roo!” 杰克穿过房间，又把笛子放在嘴边。 跟着安妮走到门口时，他看了看人群，眨了眨眼。 然后他开始自己编一些笛子的声音。 “嘀嗒嘀嗒嘀嗒！”他唱道。 “嘟嘟啊噜！”

The audience laughed. When Jack saw Nan laughing hard, it spurred him on, and he did a little dance step. “Peep-peep-peep-ah-lee!” he sang, pretending to play the flute. “Peek-peek-a-boo!” 观众们笑了起来。 当杰克看到南笑得很厉害时，他更加卖力了，还跳了一个小舞步。 “唧唧唧啊咧！”他唱着，假装在吹笛子。 “peek-peek-a-boo！”

At the door, Jack and Annie stopped. Jack held the flute to his side and bowed. Annie curtsied at the same time. The audience laughed and clapped. Together Jack and Annie waved to the crowd. 在门口，杰克和安妮停了下来。 杰克把长笛靠在身侧，鞠了一躬。 安妮同时行了个屈膝礼。 观众们笑了，还鼓起掌来。 杰克和安妮一起向人群挥手告别。

“Bye, Wolfie!” shouted Annie. She blew kisses to him. “Keep making music!” “再见了，沃尔菲！”安妮喊道。 她向他飞吻。 “继续演奏音乐吧！”，

“Have a great life, Wolfie!” shouted Jack. “祝你生活愉快，沃尔菲！”杰克喊道。

“Bye, Jack! Bye, Annie!” yelled Wolfie. “I promise I will never forget you!” “再见了，杰克！ 再见了，安妮！”沃尔菲大喊。 “我保证我永远不会忘记你！”

Jack bowed very low, and Annie curtsied again. 杰克深深地鞠了一躬，安妮又行了一个屈膝礼。

“Rise,” said Her Imperial Majesty, laughing. “起来吧，”她的 Imperial Majesty 微笑着说。

Jack and Annie straightened up, waved one last time, and slipped out the door. 杰克和安妮站直了身子，最后一次挥手，然后溜出了门。
