6. Under the Moon 6. 月光下

“Good grief,” breathed Jack. “That family has problems.” “天哪，”杰克叹了一口气。 “那个家庭有麻烦。”

“Maybe we should help them,” said Annie. “也许我们应该帮帮他们，”安妮说。

“No, we can’t,” said Jack. “Right now we have our own problem. We have to start working on our mission. Darn! I should have asked Nan’s dad about the artists in the palace.” “不，我们不能，”杰克说。 “现在我们有自己的问题要解决。 我们必须开始完成我们的任务。真烦人！ 我应该问问南的爸爸关于宫殿里的艺术家的事。”

“I think he’s too upset to think about that now,” said Annie. “我想他现在太伤心了，不可能想到这些，”安妮说。

“You’re right,” said Jack. “So we need to find some friendly people who work here and ask them.” “你说得对，”杰克说。 “所以我们需要找一些在这里工作、对我们友好的人问问他们。”

“Let’s go look for help,” said Annie. “我们去寻找帮助吧，”安妮说。

As Jack and Annie started toward the door, Nan rushed into the room again. “Has Wolfie come back?” she asked frantically. 当杰克和安妮朝门口走去时，南又冲进了房间。 “沃尔菲回来了吗？”她焦急地问。

“I’m afraid not,” said Annie. “恐怕不行，”安妮说。

“Oh, dear! Papa will die if Wolfie doesn’t return soon!” said Nan, near tears. “Can you help me find him, please?” “哦，天哪！ 如果沃尔菲不尽快回来，爸爸就会死的！”纳恩快哭出来了。 “你能帮我找到他吗？”

“Well, we’d really like to,” said Jack, “but—” “嗯，我们真的很想帮忙，”杰克说，“但是——”

“Please!” said Nan again. “Please?” “求求你了！”纳恩又说了一遍。 “求求你？”

Jack sighed. “Okay,” he said. “Sure.” 杰克叹了口气。 “好吧，”他说。 “当然。”

“Thank you!” said Nan. “The palace is very large. I’ll go this way, and you go that way. Surely one of us will find him!” She hurried out of the room. “谢谢！”南说。 “宫殿非常大。 我走这边，你走那边。 我们当中肯定有人能找到他！”她急忙走出房间。

“I’ll bet I know where Wolfie is,” said Annie. “The garden. Remember he kept talking about the garden?” “我敢打赌我知道沃尔菲在哪里，”安妮说。 “花园。 还记得他一直在说那个花园吗？

“Oh, yeah. I think the garden is below that terrace where I went after everyone laughed at me,” said Jack. “Let’s go check.” “哦，对了。 我想花园就在那座露台下面，就是我被大家嘲笑之后去过的那个地方，”杰克说。 “我们去看看吧。”

Jack led Annie back through one of the elegant palace rooms and into the party room. Everyone was still seated, eating dinner and talking loudly. No one noticed as Jack and Annie hurried through the double doors and slipped into the room off the terrace. 杰克带着安妮穿过宫殿里的一间漂亮房间，走进了宴会厅。 大家都还坐在那里，一边吃晚饭，一边大声说话。 没人注意到杰克和安妮匆匆穿过双门，溜进了露台旁边的房间。

Jack opened the door, and he and Annie stepped outside. The air was chilly, but it felt good. Jack pulled off his hat and his wig. He gave his head a good scratch. 杰克打开门，他和安妮走了出去。 空气有点冷，但感觉很好。 杰克摘下他的帽子和假发。 他挠了挠头。

Annie looked down at the moonlit garden. “Do you think that’s the garden Wolfie was talking about?” she said. 安妮低头看向月光下的花园。 “你觉得这是沃尔菲说的那个花园吗？”她说。

The silver light of the full moon shone on a large square with flower beds and fountains. Woods bordered the square. Cricket sounds filled the night. 满月银白色的光芒照在一片大广场上，那里有花坛和喷泉。 树林围着广场。 蟋蟀的声音充满了夜晚。

“Probably,” said Jack. “Wolfie!” “大概吧，”杰克说。 “沃尔菲！”

There was no answer. 没有回应。

“Let’s go down and look for him,” said Annie. “我们下去找他吧，”安妮说。

Leaving his wig and hat behind, Jack followed Annie down the stone stairway. As they started across the square, a strange sound came from the woods: AI-YEE! 杰克留下了他的假发和帽子，跟着安妮沿着石阶走下去。 当他们开始穿过广场时，树林里传来了一种奇怪的声音：AI-YEE！

“What was that?” said Jack. “那是什么？”杰克说。

Two creatures scampered out from the trees. 两只生物从树后慌慌张张地跑了出来。

“Ahh!” said Jack. “啊！”杰克说。

“Don’t worry,” said Annie. “It’s just a squirrel and a cat.” “别担心，”安妮说。 “那只是松鼠和一只猫。”

The squirrel dashed over the flower beds and disappeared. The cat ran after it. 松鼠冲过花坛，然后消失了。 猫追了上去。

“Yeah, but what was that noise from the woods?” said Jack. “I’ve never heard a squirrel or a cat make a sound like that.” “是啊，但是树林里那声音是什么？”杰克说。 “我从来没听过松鼠或猫发出这样的声音。”

Another sound came from behind the trees: WHOOP-WHOOP-WHOOP! 又一个声音从树后传来：WHOOP-WHOOP-WHOOP！

“What was that?” Jack said. “那是什么？”杰克说。

“An owl maybe?” said Annie. “可能是一只猫头鹰？”安妮说。

“I’ve never heard an owl make a noise like that!” said Jack. “Have you?” “我从来没听过猫头鹰发出那样的声音！”杰克说。 “你听过吗？”

KER-LOO! KER-LOO! KER-LOO! KER-LOO!

“Yikes! What was that?” said Annie. “天哪！ “那是什么？”安妮说。

“I don’t know. Those woods sound like a jungle,” said Jack. “我不知道。” “那些树林听起来像丛林，”杰克说。

“Wolfie!” yelled Annie. “沃尔菲！”安妮喊道。

There was no answer. Wind shook the treetops. The cricket chirps grew louder. 没有回应。 风吹得树顶摇晃。 蟋蟀的叫声越来越响了。

“This place feels really weird. Let’s go back inside,” said Jack. “I don’t think Wolfie’s out here.” “这里感觉真的很奇怪。 我们回屋里去吧，”杰克说。 “我觉得沃尔菲不在这里。

“Wait a minute,” said Annie. “I think I just heard him.” “等一下，”安妮说。 “我觉得我刚刚听见他了。

Jack and Annie both listened. A faint little cry came from the woods. “Annie! Jack!” 杰克和安妮都竖起耳朵听。 一片微弱的哭声从树林里传来。 “安妮！杰克！

“Wolfie is out there!” said Annie. “Wolfie!” She picked up her hoopskirt. She hurried across the square and disappeared down a path that led into the woods. “沃尔菲在外面！”安妮喊道。 “沃尔菲！”她捡起了她的裙撑。 她匆匆穿过广场，沿着一条通往树林的小路消失不见了。

“Annie, wait!” Jack called. He started after her, but then— “安妮，等等！”杰克喊道。 他开始追赶她，但然后——

WHOOP-WHOOP-WHOOP! WHOOP-WHOOP-WHOOP！

Jack froze. He was sure the whooping sound wasn’t coming from an owl. 杰克僵在了那里。 他确信这声音不是来自猫头鹰。

EEE-EEE!

Was that Annie screaming? Jack wondered. “Annie?” he yelled. He dashed across the moonlit garden and ran down the path to the woods. He stopped between the trees. “Annie?” he called’ again. 那是安妮在尖叫吗？杰克心想。 “安妮？”他大喊。 他冲过月光下的花园，沿着小路跑向树林。 他停在了两棵树之间。 “安妮？”他再次喊道。

EEE-EEE! Something leapt down from a tree branch and landed on the path. “Ahhh!” said Jack, jumping back. It looked like a baboon! EEE-EEE! The baboon bounded away! EEE-EEE! 有什么东西从树上跳下来，落在了小路上。 “啊啊！”杰克跳了回去，说道。 看起来像是一只狒狒！EEE-EEE! 狒狒蹦蹦跳跳地跑开了！

KER-LOO! KER-LOO! Jack looked up. Overhead a huge crane glided through the air. KER-LOO! KER-LOO! KER-LOO! KER-LOO! 杰克抬头看了看。 头顶上，一只巨大的起重机在空中滑翔。KER-LOO! KER-LOO!

AI-YEE! A crowned peacock stood in the moonlight, fanning its green and gold tail feathers. AI-YEE! 一只戴着王冠的孔雀站在月光下，扇动着绿色和金色的尾羽。

What’s going on? Jack thought. This is crazy! 发生什么事了？杰克想。 这太疯狂了！

AI-YEE! the peacock cried again. AI-YEE！ 孔雀又叫了一声。

Jack heard a rustling in the bushes. Something was moving through the woods, grunting, snorting, and growling. A huge bear lumbered onto the moonlit path! Standing on its hind legs, the bear looked about ten feet tall. It growled. 杰克听到灌木丛里有沙沙声。 有什么东西在树林里穿行，发出咕哝、喷气和咆哮的声音。 一只巨大的熊慢悠悠地走上被月光照亮的小路！ 熊用后腿站着，看起来有十英尺高。它发出低吼声。

“Hi?” Jack croaked. “你好？”杰克小声说。

The bear growled again. It stepped toward Jack, raking the air with its huge claws. 熊又吼了一声。 它朝杰克走来，用巨大的爪子划着空气。

Jack began backing away. Then he turned and ran as fast as he could. As he wound his way between the trees, he could hear the bear crashing through the woods behind him. 杰克开始往后退。 然后他转身，拼命地跑了起来。 他一边在树之间穿行，一边能听到熊在后面穿过树林的声音。

WHOOP-WHOOP-WHOOP!

A dog-like animal bounded across Jack’s path. Jack recognized it. It was a laughing hyena—a creature he and Annie had seen on the plains of Africa! What’s going on? Jack wondered again. Where are these animals coming from? 一只像狗的动物跳到了杰克的路中间。 杰克认出了它。 那是只笑嘻嘻的鬣狗——他和安妮曾在非洲大草原上见过这种动物！这是怎么回事？ 杰克又在想事情。 这些动物是从哪儿来的呢？

“Jack!” yelled Annie. “Over here!” “杰克！”安妮喊道。 “在这儿！”

Annie was hiding behind a tree. Jack ran over and crouched beside her. He could hear the bear growling and thrashing through the brush. 安妮正躲在一棵树后面。 杰克跑过去，蹲在她旁边。 他能听到那只熊在灌木丛中咆哮和挣扎。

“A huge bear’s chasing me!” Jack said. “有一只大熊在追我！”，杰克说。

“I know! He was chasing me, too!” said Annie. “我知道！ 它也在追我！”，安妮说。

“I saw a baboon, a hyena, and a crane!” sputtered Jack. “What’s happening?” “我看到一只狒狒、一只鬣狗和一只鹤！”杰克结结巴巴地说。 “发生什么事了？”

“I don’t know!” whispered Annie. “Look!” “我不知道！”安妮小声说。 “看！”

From behind the tree, they watched an ostrich prance through the moonlight. Behind the ostrich, a gazelle stepped softly over the path in the woods, looking from side to side. Then the peacock strutted back into sight, fanning its luminous feathers. 他们躲在树后，看着一只鸵鸟在月光下蹦蹦跳跳地穿过树林。 鸵鸟后面，一只羚羊轻手轻脚地走过林间小路，左右张望。 接着，孔雀骄傲地走了出来，展开它闪闪发光的羽毛。

“It’s like a zoo in these woods,” said Jack. “这树林里好像一个动物园，”杰克说。

“Oh, wow, that’s it!” said Annie. “哦，哇，就是这样！”安妮说。

“What do you mean?” said Jack. “你是什么意思？”杰克说。

“The zoo! That’s where they’re from!” said Annie. “Remember our coach driver said the palace had a zoo!” “动物园！ “那就是他们来自的地方！”安妮说道。 “记住我们的马车夫说过宫殿里有个动物园！”

“But zoos have cages!” said Jack. “The animals don’t just roam around free!” “但动物园里有笼子！”杰克说。 “动物们不会到处自由地跑来跑去！”

“Jack! Annie!” Wolfie’s voice was close by. “杰克！ 安妮！”沃尔菲的声音就在附近。

“That’s Wolfie again!” said Annie. “Oh, wow. I’ll bet Wolfie let the animals out of their cages.” “那是沃尔菲又来了！”安妮说。 “哦，哇。 我敢打赌沃尔菲把动物们从笼子里放了出来。”

“Help!” Wolfie called. “救命！”沃尔菲喊道。

“Oh, man, that kid’s crazy,” said Jack. “天啊，那个小孩疯了，”杰克说。

“I know, but we have to help him!” said Annie. “我知道，但我们得帮助他！”安妮说。

Annie picked up her skirt. She and Jack crept from their hiding place and walked cautiously down the path. 安妮提起她的裙子。 她和杰克从藏身的地方悄悄爬出来，小心翼翼地沿着小路走下去。

“Jack! Annie! Help me!” “杰克！安妮！ 救救我！”

Jack and Annie kept going, stepping in and out of the shadowy moonlight. Suddenly Annie gasped and pointed. Wolfie was sitting in the crook of a tree up ahead. 杰克和安妮继续往前走，时而走进月光，时而躲进阴影里。 突然，安妮惊叫起来并指了指。 沃尔菲正坐在前方一棵树的树杈上。

A large spotted animal was crouched below him. 一只身上有斑点的大动物正悄悄地蹲在他下面。

“A leopard!” whispered Jack. “是一只豹子！”杰克小声说。

The leopard looked up at Wolfie and growled. 豹子抬头看着沃尔菲，发出低吼声。
