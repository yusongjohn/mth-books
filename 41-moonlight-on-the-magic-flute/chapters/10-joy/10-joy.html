10. Joy 10. 快乐

“Go, go!” said Jack. “快点，快点！”杰克说。

Jack and Annie ran through the Great Rosa Room, through the next three rooms, and into the room off the back terrace. Then they turned and hurried to the huge party room, where the thousand candles had nearly burned out. They ran to the door that led out to the front entrance. 杰克和安妮跑过了伟大的罗莎房间，跑过了接下来的三个房间，然后跑到了后院露台旁边的房间。 然后他们转身，赶紧跑向那个巨大的宴会厅，那里的一千支蜡烛几乎快要燃尽了。 他们跑到通向前门的那扇门前。

“ We have to leave. Good night!” Annie said to the guard. “Thanks for everything!” “我们必须离开了。” “晚安！”安妮对守卫说。 “谢谢你们做的一切！”

The guard opened the door, and they ran outside. 守卫打开了门，他们跑到了外面。

“Keep going!” said Jack. “继续往前走！”杰克说。

They hurried down the sweeping, curved stairway that led to the giant square. 他们匆匆跑下那条弯曲的楼梯，一直通向巨大的广场。

A line of coaches was parked below, waiting to pick up guests. Jack and Annie saw Josef standing by his coach. The moonlight shone on his two milk white horses. 下面停着一排马车，正等着接客人。 杰克和安妮看见约瑟夫站在他的马车旁。 月光照亮了他那两匹牛奶白色的马。

“Josef!” shouted Annie. “约瑟夫！”安妮喊道。

Jack and Annie ran over to the coachman. 杰克和安妮跑向马车夫。

“Ah, my young friends!” he said. “How was your evening?” “啊，我的年轻朋友们！”他说。 “你们晚上过得怎么样？”

“Great,” said Annie. “But we have to go home now. Do you have time to take us back to the gate?” “很好，”安妮说。 “但我们现在得回家了。 你有时间送我们回大门吗？”

“Indeed, it is early,” said Josef. “I can take you now and then return for my employer and his family.” “确实，现在还早，”约瑟夫说。 “我现在可以送你们，然后再去接我的雇主和他的家人。”

Josef gave Annie his hand and helped her into the coach. Then he helped Jack. Josef climbed up onto his bench and snapped the reins, and the two horses clomped over the cobblestones. 约瑟夫递给安妮他的手，帮她上了马车。 然后他又帮了杰克。 约瑟夫爬上他的座位，扯了扯缰绳，两匹马踏着鹅卵石走了起来。

“So tell me now, young lady,” said Josef, “did you have a good time at the party? What did you see and what did you do?” “现在告诉我，小姑娘，”约瑟夫说，“你在派对上玩得开心吗？” 你看到了什么，然后做了什么？

“I had a great time!” said Annie. “I was received by Her Imperial Majesty. I saw a room lit with a thousand candles. I made good friends, met some zoo animals, heard a great concert, and saw a silly clown.” “我玩得可开心了！”安妮说。 “我受到了她的皇室 Majesty 的接见。 我看到一个房间被一千支蜡烛照亮。 我交到了好朋友，见到了一些动物园里的动物，听了一场精彩的音乐会，还看到一个傻乎乎的小丑。

“Excellent,” said Josef. “And you, young man?” “太棒了，”约瑟夫说。 “那你呢，年轻人？”

“I did all those things, too,” said Jack. “But the clown was the best part. He knew how to make great use of his wits and his talents.” “我也做了所有这些事，”杰克说。 “不过小丑是最精彩的部分。” 他知道怎么好好利用自己的聪明才智和才能。

Annie laughed. Josef drove the coach through the palace gate and stopped on the cobblestone street. “Where do you need to go?” he asked. 安妮笑了。 约瑟夫驾驶着马车穿过宫殿的门，停在鹅卵石街道上。 “你们要去哪儿？”他问。

“Oh, this is perfect,” said Annie. “ We can get out right here. Thank you.” “哦，这太完美了，”安妮说。 “我们在这里就可以下车了。谢谢。”

Jack and Annie climbed out of the coach and looked up at Josef. 杰克和安妮从马车里爬出来，抬头看着约瑟夫。

“Thank you, Josef!” said Annie. “谢谢您，约瑟夫！”安妮说。

“Yeah, thanks a lot for the ride,” said Jack. “是啊，非常感谢你送我们一程，”杰克说。

“You two are very mysterious,” said Josef. “You appeared out of the twilight and now you disappear into the moonlight.” “你们俩真是神秘莫测，”约瑟夫说。 “你从暮色中出现，现在又消失在月光里。”

“We’re magicians,” said Annie. “我们是魔法师，”安妮说。

“It would seem so,” said Josef, smiling. He tipped his hat. “Well, I had better return now. Good night, my young friends.” “看起来是这样，”约瑟夫微笑着说。 他摘下帽子行了个礼。 “那么，我最好现在回去了。 晚安，我的年轻朋友们。”

“Good night, Josef,” said Jack and Annie. “晚安，约瑟夫，”杰克和安妮说。

Josef flicked the reins, and the milk white horses clomped back toward the palace gates. 约瑟夫抽了下缰绳，乳白色的马踏着蹄子往宫殿大门走去。

“Nice guy,” said Jack. “Let’s go.” “挺不错的人，”杰克说。 “我们走吧。”

Jack and Annie ran to the trees that lined the cobblestone street. “There!” said Annie. She grabbed the rope ladder. 杰克和安妮跑到铺着鹅卵石的街道两旁的树那里。 “在那里！”安妮说。 她抓住了绳梯。

Annie started up to the tree house. Jack followed her. When they got inside, they looked out the window at the full moon shining over Vienna, Austria. 安妮开始往上爬树屋。 杰克跟在她后面。 他们一进到里面，就从窗户往外看，看到奥地利的维也纳上空满月高照。

“Good-bye, Wolfie,” said Annie. “再见了，沃尔菲，”安妮说。

“Good luck, kid,” said Jack. “祝你好运，孩子，”杰克说。

Then Jack picked up the envelope from their invitation. He pointed at the words Frog Creek. “ I wish we could go there!” he said. 然后杰克从他们的邀请函上捡起了信封。 他指着上面写着的‘蛙溪镇’。 “我希望我们能去那里！”他说。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。

Jack opened his eyes. “Ahhh,” he sighed with relief. They were back in the Frog Creek woods. They were wearing their own clothes again. Jack was holding the magic flute. 杰克睁开了眼睛。 “啊——，”他松了口气。 他们又回到了蛙溪镇的森林里。 他们又穿上了自己的衣服。 杰克手里拿着那支魔法笛子。

“Ready?” asked Annie. “准备好了吗？”安妮问。

“Totally” said Jack. “完全准备好了。”杰克说。

Jack carefully laid the flute in a corner of the tree house. Then he and Annie climbed down the rope ladder and started for home. 杰克小心地把长笛放在树屋的一个角落里。 然后他和安妮顺着绳梯爬下来，开始回家。

Jack was in such a good mood, he nearly bounced through the woods. The sun was going down, dappling the ground with lovely light. The air was soft and smelled like new leaves. Jack knew a good dinner was waiting for them at home. 杰克心情这么好，差点在树林里蹦跳着走。 太阳正在下山，把地面照得斑斑点点，非常漂亮。 空气很柔软，还散发着新叶子的香味。 杰克知道家里正等着他们吃一顿美味的晚餐。

When Jack and Annie came out of the woods, they took off running down the street. They crossed their yard, scrambled onto the porch, and banged through the front door. 当杰克和安妮从树林里出来时，他们立刻沿着街道跑了起来。 他们穿过院子，爬上了门廊，然后冲进了前门。

“We’re back!” Annie called. “我们回来了！”安妮喊道。

“Just in time,” their dad said from the kitchen. “Dinner’s in a few minutes.” “刚好及时，”他们的爸爸从厨房里说道。 “晚餐马上就好。”

“Quick, before we eat …,” Jack said to Annie. He led her over to the computer. He sat down and typed in one word: Mozart. “快点，吃饭前……，”杰克对安妮说。 他带她走到电脑前。 他坐下来，输入了一个词：莫扎特。

There were 48,400,207 entries. 一共有48,400,207条记录。

“Whoa!” said Jack. He clicked the first one. He read aloud: “哇！”杰克说。 他点击了第一条。他大声读出来：

Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart was the most famous child musician in history. He performed all over Europe for many years. As Mozart grew up, he composed more than six hundred pieces of music. For over two hundred years, his music has brought joy to the world. 沃尔夫冈·阿玛多伊斯·莫扎特是历史上最著名的儿童音乐家。他在欧洲各地表演了很多年。随着莫扎特长大，他创作了六百多首音乐作品。两百多年来，他的音乐给世界带来了欢乐。

“ Yes!” said Annie. “对！”安妮说。

As Jack scrolled down, three words leapt out at him. He gasped. “Listen to this!” he said. And then he read to Annie: 杰克往下翻的时候，有三个词突然跳进了他的眼睛。他惊讶地喊了一声。 “你听这个！”他说。 然后他给安妮读起来：

Mozart’s last great opera was calledThe Magic Flute. 莫扎特最后一部伟大的歌剧叫做《魔笛》。

Annie smiled at Jack. “Wolfie kept his promise,” she said. “He never forgot us.” 安妮对杰克笑了笑。 “沃尔菲信守了他的承诺，”她说。 “他从来没有忘记我们。”
