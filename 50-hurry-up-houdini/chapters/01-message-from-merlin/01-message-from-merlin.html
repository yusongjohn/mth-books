1. Message from Merlin 1. Message from Merlin

Early one summer evening, Jack lay on the couch, reading a book about the history of horses. 一个夏天的傍晚，杰克躺在沙发上，读着一本关于马的历史的书。

“Jack, could you run and get Annie from Jenny’s house, please?” their mom called from the kitchen. “We’re grilling hot dogs soon.” “杰克，能请你去珍妮家把安妮叫过来吗？”妈妈从厨房里喊道。 “我们很快就去烤热狗。”

“Sure,” said Jack. He closed his book, hopped off the couch, and headed out the front door. As he started down the porch steps, Annie charged into the yard. “好的，”杰克说。 他合上书，从沙发上跳下来，然后走出了前门。 当他开始走下门廊的台阶时，安妮冲进了院子。

“It’s back!” she said, out of breath. “它回来了！”她气喘吁吁地说。

“You’re kidding!” said Jack. “你是在开玩笑吗？”杰克说。

“No! I’m serious!” said Annie. “On my way home from Jenny’s, I checked the woods. I had a funny feeling it was back—and it was!” “不是！ 我可是认真的！”安妮说。 “我从珍妮家回家的时候，去森林里看了看。 我有种奇怪的感觉，它又回来了——而且真的回来了！”

“Are Merlin and Penny there?” asked Jack. “梅林和彭妮在那儿吗？”杰克问。

“I don’t think so,” said Annie. “I shouted hello, but no one answered. I wanted to come get you before I climbed up!” “我想不在吧，”安妮说。 “我喊了‘你好’，但没人回答。” “我想在爬上去之前来接你！”

“Thanks,” said Jack. “Wait here. I’ll tell Mom.” He dashed back into the house. “Mom, can Annie and I do something for about twenty minutes?” he called. “谢谢，”杰克说。 “等在这里。” “我要告诉妈妈。”他冲回了房子。 “妈妈，安妮和我能做点事情大约二十分钟吗？”他喊道。

“Okay, twenty minutes, no more,” said their mom. “The grill’s almost ready.” “好吧，二十分钟，不能再多了，”他们的妈妈说道。 “烧烤架快准备好了。”

“Thanks!” said Jack. He looked at his wristwatch. It was 5:42. So they had to be home by two minutes after six. He grabbed his backpack and hurried out of the house. Annie was waiting on the sidewalk. “谢谢！”杰克说。 他看了看手腕上的手表，是5点42分。 所以他们必须在六点零二分之前回家。 他抓起书包，赶紧跑出了房子。 安妮正在人行道上等着。

“Twenty minutes! No more!” Jack called, running toward her. “二十分钟！ “不行了！”杰克喊道，朝她跑过去。

“No problem!” Annie said, and they hurried up the sidewalk together. “没问题！”安妮说，然后他们一起快步走上人行道。

“I’ll bet Merlin wants us to find another secret of greatness,” said Jack. “我敢打赌梅林是想让我们找到另一个伟大的秘密，”杰克说。

“I know,” said Annie. “I can’t wait!” “我知道，”安妮说。 “我等不及了！”

They crossed the street and headed into the Frog Creek woods. They ran through the shadowy light until they came to the tallest oak. 他们穿过街道，朝蛙溪镇的森林走去。 他们跑过斑驳的光影，直到来到最高的橡树前。

“Merlin? Penny?” Annie called. “梅林？ 佩妮？”安妮喊道。

There was no answer. The woods were still, strangely still. Not a leaf moved. 没有回应。 树林里静悄悄的，奇怪地静悄悄的。一片叶子都没动。

“Let’s go up,” said Jack. He grabbed the rope ladder and started climbing. Annie followed. When they scrambled into the tree house, there was no sign of Merlin or Penny. “我们上去吧，”杰克说。 他抓住绳梯开始爬。安妮跟了上去。 当他们爬进树屋时，梅林和彭妮的踪影全无。

“Not here,” said Annie. “他不在这里，”安妮说。

“Nope,” said Jack. “But everything else is.” “没错，”杰克说。 “但其他东西都在。”

He pointed to a piece of paper lying on the floor in the corner. On the paper he’d written the secret of greatness they’d learned on their last mission: 他指着角落地板上的一张纸。 纸上写着他们上次任务中学到的伟大秘密：

HUMILITY

Sitting on top of the paper were two things—a small gold ring and a tiny bottle. Annie picked up the ring and held it out to Jack. “The Ring of Truth,” she said. “You get to wear it this time.” 纸的上面放着两样东西——一枚小小的金戒指和一个小小的瓶子。 安妮捡起戒指，递给杰克。 “真相之戒，”她说。 “这次你来戴吧。”

“Thanks,” said Jack. He took the gold ring and slipped it onto his finger. “谢谢，”杰克说。 他拿起金戒指，把它戴在手指上。

“Just make sure you keep checking to see if it’s glowing,” said Annie. “只是要记得一直检查它是不是在发光，”安妮说。

“Don’t worry, I will,” said Jack. According to Merlin, the Ring of Truth would glow only when Jack and Annie discovered a true secret of greatness. “别担心，我会的，”杰克说。 根据梅林的说法，真相之戒只有在杰克和安妮发现一个关于伟大真正的秘密时才会发光。

Annie picked up the tiny bottle. She and Jack stared at its swirling contents. “Wow,” she breathed. “Merlin’s magic mist.” 安妮捡起了那个小瓶子。 她和杰克盯着瓶子里旋转的内容物。 “哇，”她轻声说。 “梅林的魔法雾气。”

Jack repeated the words of Camelot’s magician: “Mist gathered at first light on the first day of the new moon on the Isle of Avalon.” 杰克重复了卡梅洛特的魔术师的话：‘新月的第一天，黎明时分，阿瓦隆岛上的雾气开始聚集。’

“Good memory,” said Annie. “记忆力真好，”安妮说。

“Who wouldn’t remember that?” said Jack. “If we take a sniff, we can be great at anything we choose for one hour.” “谁不会记住这个呢？”杰克说。 “如果我们闻一闻，我们可以在一小时内变得非常擅长我们选择的任何事情。”

“That’s so cool,” said Annie. “这太酷了，”安妮说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. He had loved becoming a great horse trainer on their last mission. “I wonder what talent we’ll have this time. And where will we go?” “是啊，”杰克说。 他上次执行任务时，特别喜欢成为一名出色的马术教练。 “我不知道这次我们会有什么样的才能。 这次又会去什么地方呢？”

Annie pointed at a booklet that was lying in the shadows. She picked it up and handed it to Jack. 安妮指着躺在阴影里的一本小册子。 她捡起来，递给杰克。

“Coney Island, I think,” she said. “我想是康尼岛，”她说。

Jack read the title: 杰克读着标题：

图中文字：CONEY ISLAND

TOURIST GUIDE 图中文字：康尼岛旅游指南

“Coney Island. Isn’t that in New York?” said Annie. “康尼岛。 那不是在纽约吗？”安妮说。

“Yep. Look, there’s a note inside,” said Jack. He pulled out a piece of parchment from the booklet. He read aloud: “对。” 看，里面有一张纸条，”杰克说。 他从手册里拿出一张羊皮纸。他大声读道：

Dear Jack and Annie, 亲爱的杰克和安妮。

Thank you for successfully completing your first mission to find a secret of greatness. Now on your new mission, I would like you to learn a second secret—this time, from the Great Houdini. 谢谢你们成功完成第一次任务，找到伟大秘密。 现在在你们的新任务中，我想让你们学习第二个秘密——这次是从伟大的胡迪尼那里。

—M.

“Oh, man, the Great Houdini!” said Jack. “哦，天哪，伟大的胡迪尼！”，杰克说。

“What did he do?” said Annie. “他做了什么？”，安妮说。

“Dad told me about him,” said Jack. “He could escape from anything! Chains, ropes, locks, handcuffs, prisons. He was amazing!” “爸爸跟我讲过他，”杰克说。 “他能从任何地方逃出来！ “链条、绳子、锁、手铐、监狱。他太厉害了！”

“Was he a criminal?” said Annie. “他是罪犯吗？”安妮问。

Jack laughed. “No, he was a performer—the greatest escape artist who ever lived,” he said. “He performed his act all over the world. I can’t believe we’re actually going to meet him!” 杰克笑了。 “不，他是一个表演者——世界上最伟大的逃脱大师，”他说。 “他曾在世界各地表演他的节目。 我简直不敢相信我们真的要见到他了！”

“So where do we find a person like that?” asked Annie. “那我们去哪儿找这样的人呢？”安妮问。

“Coney Island!” said Jack, holding up the booklet. “康尼岛！”杰克说着，举起那本小册子。

“Oh, yeah, duh,” said Annie. “So make the wish and let’s go!” “哦，对啊，真是笨。”安妮说。 “那就许个愿，我们出发吧！”

“Okay!” said Jack. He put Merlin’s message and the magic mist into his backpack. Then he pointed to the words Coney Island on the booklet. “I wish we could go there!” “好！”杰克说。 他把梅林的信息和魔法雾气放进背包里。 然后他指着小册子上写着康尼岛的字。 “我希望我们能去那里！”

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 绝对静止了。
