6. Jolly Jack and the Amazing Annie 6. 愉快的杰克和神奇的安妮

As the orchestra played, Mr. Wilson guided Jack and Annie through the backstage area. “Watch out, kids,” he said. “Like I said before, it’s a tough crowd out there tonight.” 当乐队演奏时，先生。 威尔逊带杰克和安妮穿过后台区域。 “小心点，孩子们，”他说。 “就像我之前说的，今晚外面的人可不好对付。”

Jack smiled confidently. No crowd is too tough for us, he thought. 杰克自信地笑了笑。 他心想，没有哪群人是咱们对付不了的。

Hank and Butch set the table with Jack and Annie’s props center stage behind the closed curtain. The stage was lit with a soft rosy light. 汉克和巴奇把杰克和安妮的道具摆上舞台，放在幕布后面中央位置。 舞台上洒下柔和的粉红色灯光。

“Places!” called Mr. Wilson. “各就各位！”威尔逊先生喊道。

Jack and Annie nodded and calmly took their places in front of the table. They heard a long drumroll, then a cymbal crash. They heard Mr. Dewey speaking to the audience from in front of the curtain: 杰克和安妮点点头，平静地走到桌子前面站好。 他们听到了一阵长长的鼓声，接着是镲片的巨响。他们听到了先生 德威从幕布前对观众说话：

“Ladies and gentlemen! Welcome to the Great Houdini show! I have a very special and wonderful surprise for you this evening. As you may know, the Bambini Brothers were scheduled to be our opening act. But instead, it will be your great good fortune to welcome a truly remarkable pair of young illusionists. These master magicians have performed in theaters all around the world, and tonight they are here to perform for you! Ladies and gentlemen, I give you Jolly Jack and the Amazing Annie!” “女士们先生们！ 欢迎来到伟大的胡迪尼表演！ 今晚我有一个非常特别又精彩的惊喜要送给你们。 你们可能知道，巴姆比尼兄弟本来是我们的开场表演。 但是，这将是你难得的好运，欢迎一对真正了不起的小魔术师。 这些大师级的魔术师曾在世界各地的剧院表演过，今晚他们将为你演出！ 女士们先生们，我请出快乐的杰克和神奇的安妮！”，

A trumpet blasted, the curtain rose, and the crowd clapped and whistled as Jack and Annie stepped into a yellow spotlight. 一声小号响起，幕布拉开，杰克和安妮走进黄色的聚光灯下，观众们鼓掌欢呼。

Looking out at the audience, Jack chuckled like a seasoned performer. His heart pounded, but not with fear. It pounded with an excitement that made him want to do his best. 杰克看着观众，像一个经验丰富的表演者一样笑了起来。 他的心跳加速了，但不是因为害怕。 他的心跳充满了兴奋，让他想尽自己最大的努力表演。

“Good evening, friends!” Jack shouted over the applause. “They call me Jolly Jack because nothing brings me greater joy than entertaining folks like you! And they call my sister Amazing Annie because—” Jack held his gloved hand out toward Annie. “晚上好，朋友们！”杰克在掌声中大声喊道。 “他们叫我快乐的杰克，因为没有什么比给像你们这样的人带来欢乐更让我开心的了！ 他们叫我神奇的安妮，因为——”杰克伸出戴着手套的手朝安妮伸去。

“I love to amaze you with my amazing ability to amaze you!” Annie shouted. “我超喜欢用我超厉害的本领来让你吃惊！”安妮喊道。

The cymbals crashed. The crowd laughed. 铙钹响了起来。 人群笑了起来。

“Do not let our youthful appearance fool you!” Annie went on. “My brother and I have traveled the globe to discover mysteries both ancient and modern!” “别被我们年轻的样子骗了！”安妮继续说道。 “我和我哥哥已经环游世界，去发现古代和现代的各种秘密！”

Jack took off his top hat and turned it toward the crowd. “I direct your attention to my hat,” he said. “As you can see, the inside is quite ordinary. But strange things happen with this hat, especially when I touch it with a wand given to us by an Arabian princess.” 杰克摘下他的礼帽，把它转向人群。 “请大家注意我的帽子，”他说。 “你们可以看到，里面非常普通。 但用一根来自阿拉伯公主的魔杖碰这顶帽子时，奇怪的事情就会发生。”

Jack looked around. “Oh, dear, I seem to have forgotten my wand.” 杰克环顾四周。 “哦，天哪，我好像忘记带我的魔杖了。”

Annie stepped forward. She swept her right arm in front of her—and presto! A wand appeared in her hand! Jack knew that when she waved her arm, she had simply allowed one of the hidden wands to slip from her sleeve. 安妮向前走了一步。 她举起右臂，向前一挥——哇！ 一根魔杖出现在她手中！ 杰克知道，当她挥动手臂时，她只是让袖子里隐藏的魔杖滑了出来。

The cymbals crashed. 铜锣响了起来。

The crowd cheered. 人群欢呼起来。

Annie grinned triumphantly and bowed. 安妮得意地笑了笑，然后鞠了一躬。

While Annie was distracting everyone, Jack casually put his hat upside down on the velvet tabletop, over one of the hidden trapdoors. As he set the hat down, he pressed on the trapdoor and it flapped open. 当安妮吸引大家注意力的时候，杰克随意地把他的帽子倒着放在天鹅绒桌面上，盖住了其中一个隐藏的暗门。 当他放下帽子时，他按了按暗门，暗门“啪”地一声打开了。

“Thank you, Amazing Annie!” Jack called. “谢谢，神奇的安妮！”杰克喊道。

Annie handed Jack the wand, and he stepped back and waved it around the brim of the top hat. 安妮把魔杖递给杰克，他往后退了一步，挥舞着魔杖绕着礼帽的帽檐转了一圈。

The crowd grew quiet. 人群安静了下来。

In a deep, mysterious voice, Jack intoned, “Zoom-bally-win-doo-chee-gone-hay! Which means, Oh, marvelous hat, what do you have for me today?” 杰克用低沉而神秘的声音说道：“Zoom-bally-win-doo-chee-gone-hay!” 这意味着，“哦，神奇的帽子，今天你有什么惊喜给我吗？”

Jack tapped the hat, stepped back from the table, and turned to Annie. “My dear, may I ask you to check my hat, please?” he said. “I have a feeling it holds a surprise.” 杰克敲了敲帽子，然后退到桌子旁边，转身面对安妮。 “亲爱的，我可以请你帮我检查一下我的帽子吗？”他说。 “我有种感觉，它里面藏着一个惊喜。”

There was a drumroll as Annie slowly reached into the hat. Jack knew she was pushing down on the top of the hat, which also had a secret flap. 安妮慢慢地把手伸进帽子时，传来一阵鼓声。 杰克知道她正在按压帽子的顶部，帽子上还有一个秘密的翻盖。

A small white rabbit suddenly leapt from the secret compartment, through the hat, and into Annie’s arms! 一只小白兔突然从秘密抽屉里跳出来，穿过帽子，跳进了安妮的怀里！

The orchestra played triumphant music, and the audience cheered. 乐队演奏着胜利的音乐，观众们欢呼起来。

Two more rabbits jumped out! Each time, Annie caught the rabbits and pretended to be surprised. 又有两只兔子跳了出来！ 每次，安妮都会接住兔子，假装很惊讶。

The cymbals crashed. 铙钹响了起来。

The audience laughed and clapped. 观众们笑了，鼓起掌来。

Annie handed the rabbits to Hank and Butch, who carried them offstage. Then she took off her own hat. “Ladies and gentlemen!” she shouted. “As you can see, my hat is as empty as my brother’s was.” She showed the inside of the hat to the audience. “But it also holds wonders.” 安妮把兔子交给了汉克和巴奇，他们把兔子带下了舞台。 然后她摘下了自己的帽子。 “女士们先生们！”她喊道。 “你们可以看到，我的帽子和我哥哥的一样空空如也。”她把帽子的内侧展示给观众看。 “但它也藏着奇迹哦。”

Annie set her hat on the table beside Jack’s, carefully placing it on top of the second hidden door. “Oh, I’m going to need another wand!” she said. This time, she swept her left arm in front of her, and presto! The second wand appeared in her hand. 安妮把帽子放在桌子旁边杰克的帽子上，小心地放在第二个隐藏的门上面。 “哦，我需要另一根魔杖！”她说。 这次，她挥动左臂，哗啦一下！ 第二根魔杖出现在她手中。

As the crowd applauded, Annie waved the wand over her hat. In a strange voice, she said, “Zoom-bally-hula-hula-hi-ho-hay! Which means, Oh, wondrous hat, send new friends our way!” 当人群鼓掌时，安妮用魔杖在帽子上挥了挥。 用一种奇怪的声音，她说：“Zoom-bally-hula-hula-hi-ho-hay！ 意思是，哦，神奇的帽子，给我们带来新朋友吧！

Jack reached into the hat, pushed down on the secret flap, then gently lifted out a dove. As the crowd cheered and clapped, he reached in again and took out the second dove. Jack handed the birds to Annie. They perched on her fingers as she lifted her hands into the air. The birds flapped their wings, bowing to the audience. 杰克把手伸进帽子，按了按秘密的夹层，然后轻轻地把一只鸽子拿出来。 当人群欢呼鼓掌时，他又一次把手伸进去，拿出第二只鸽子。 杰克把鸟递给了安妮。 它们停在她的手指上，当她把手举到空中时。 鸟儿扇动翅膀，向观众鞠躬。

The cymbals crashed. 铙钹响了起来。

The crowd cheered and clapped. 人群欢呼鼓掌。

While Annie distracted the crowd with the birds, Jack scooped the deck of cards off the table and stepped into the shadows. Behind his back, he divided the deck equally. Then he hid the two halves in the palms of his hands. 当安妮用鸟儿吸引人群的注意力时，杰克把桌上的牌堆捡起来，走进了阴影里。 他背对着人群，把牌堆平均分成两份。 然后他把两半牌藏在了手心里。

Annie gave the doves to Hank and Butch. Then she turned back to the crowd. “I believe my brother has something to share with you now!” Annie shouted. “Jolly Jack?” 安妮把鸽子交给了汉克和巴奇。 然后她转身面对人群。 “我相信我哥哥现在有东西要和你们分享！”安妮喊道。 “快乐的杰克？”

Jack took Annie’s place in the spotlight. “Are there any cardplayers in the house?” he called. 杰克走到聚光灯下，站在安妮的位置上。 “屋里有会打牌的人吗？”他喊道。

Many in the audience raised their hands. Some laughed loudly and teased each other. 许多观众举起了手。 有些人大声笑起来，互相取笑。

“Well then, my friends,” said Jack, “my strong advice to you tonight is never play cards with a magician!” As he spoke, he secretly gripped the edge of the card stack in his right hand. Using his thumb, he expertly inched the top card up until it was just behind his fingers. “那么，我的朋友们，”杰克说，“我今晚给你的忠告是，千万不要和魔术师打牌！”他说着，悄悄用右手握住了牌堆的边缘。 他用拇指巧妙地将最上面的一张牌一点点向上推，直到它刚好藏在手指后面。

All the while, Jack kept talking: “But oddly, some folks are foolish enough to challenge me to a game. Therefore, I never travel anywhere without a deck, but I prefer my cards to remain invisible until I am ready to use them!” 与此同时，杰克继续说道：“但奇怪的是，有些人竟然愚蠢到敢向我挑战游戏。 因此，我到哪儿都带着一副牌，但我更喜欢在准备好使用它们之前，让它们保持隐形！”

Jack then raised his right hand and flipped out the card, pretending to pluck it out of the air. The audience gasped. Cymbals clashed. 杰克然后举起右手，把卡片翻出来，假装从空中摘下来。 观众们惊呼起来。铜锣叮当作响。

“Of course, you can’t play a card game with only one card!” Jack said. He reached into the air with his left hand. “Ah, good! Here is another … and another … and another!” “当然啦，你不可能只用一张卡片玩牌游戏！”杰克说。 他用左手伸向空中。 “啊，太好了！ 这里还有另一个……还有一个……还有一个！”

The drummer pounded his drums each time Jack appeared to snatch a card from the air. He produced card after card. “What’s this? Another! Another!” Finally, he said, “My goodness! I seem to have gathered an entire deck. Who wants to play a game?” 每当杰克出现去从空中抢走一张牌时，鼓手就敲他的鼓。 他一张又一张地拿出卡片。 “这是什么？另一个！ “另一个！”他终于说，“天哪！” 我好像把一副完整的牌都拿来了。 谁想玩个游戏？

The cymbals crashed. The crowd cheered and Jack bowed. 铙钹猛然响了起来。 人群欢呼起来，杰克鞠了一躬。

Annie stepped into the spotlight, holding up three large rings. “Once upon a time, in a palace in China, we learned the ancient art of magic rings,” she said. 安妮走进了聚光灯下，手里举着三个大环。 “从前，在中国的一座宫殿里，我们学会了古老的魔法戒指技艺，”她说。

Jack knew that one of the rings had a tiny opening in it. Annie hid the opening between her thumb and index finger so no one could see it. 杰克知道其中一个戒指上有一个小小的开口。 安妮把开口藏在拇指和食指之间，这样没人能看见。

“These are three solid rings,” Annie told the crowd. “There is no way on earth to link them together!” She banged the rings against one another, making a show of trying to fit them together. Each time she failed. “这三个戒指都是实心的，”安妮对人群说。 “根本不可能把它们连在一起！”她把戒指互相敲击，做出努力把它们连在一起的样子。 每次她都失败了。

Jack stepped forward. “But deep in the palace of the emperor,” he said, “a Chinese sage taught us a magical song.” Jack waved his wand over the first ring and sang, “Hong-hong-hong-hong!” 杰克向前走了一步。 “但是，在皇帝的宫殿深处，”他说，“一位中国智者教给我们一首神奇的歌。” 杰克把魔杖挥过第一个环，唱道：“洪——洪——洪——洪！”

Annie banged the rings together again. This time she slipped the solid rings through the hidden opening in the other ring. Her hands moved so swiftly no one could see what she was doing. 安妮又把环撞在一起。 这次她把实心的环穿过了另一个环中隐藏的开口。 她的手动作如此迅速，没人能看清她在做什么。

“Once again, the magic song has worked!” she shouted, holding up the three connected rings. “魔法歌曲再次生效了！”她高举着三个连在一起的环喊道。

Cymbals crashed. 铜锣轰然作响。

As the crowd applauded, Annie easily disconnected the rings. Then she tossed the two solid ones high into the air. When they came down, she caught them so they slipped perfectly through the tiny opening of the third ring. 当人群鼓掌时，安妮轻松地把环分开了。 然后她把两个实心的环高高抛向空中。 他们下来时，她接住了他们，让他们完美地从第三个戒指的小开口滑了过去。

Annie held up the connected rings, and the orchestra played triumphant music. As she bowed, the audience clapped and cheered wildly. 安妮举起连接在一起的戒指，乐队奏起了胜利的音乐。 当她鞠躬时，观众们热烈地鼓掌欢呼。

Jack joined her in the spotlight. “You have been most agreeable and welcoming tonight!” he shouted. “We will think of you fondly for years to come. But now we must leave you. During intermission, you can prepare your minds and nerves, in anticipation of the most amazing escape artist of all time: the Great Houdini!” 杰克也走到聚光灯下，和她一起。 “今晚你们今晚表现得非常友好和热情！”他喊道。 “我们会一直怀念你们的。 但现在我们必须离开了。 中场休息时，你们可以准备好你们的头脑和神经，期待最了不起的逃脱艺术家：伟大的胡迪尼！”

Trumpets sounded. Everyone cheered and stamped their feet. 号角响起了。 每个人都欢呼着，跺着脚。

Jack and Annie each bowed with a flourish. They raised their top hats to the crowd. Then, laughing and waving, they ran offstage, and the curtain came down. 杰克和安妮各自优雅地鞠了一躬。 他们把礼帽举到观众面前。 接着，他们一边笑着一边挥手，跑下了舞台，幕布缓缓落下。
