10. Best Escape Act Ever 10. 有史以来最棒的逃脱表演

Annie grinned at Jack. “Done!” she whispered. 安妮对杰克露出了笑容。 “完成了！”她小声说。

“Done!” Jack agreed. “Now we can leave.” “完成了！”杰克同意了。 “现在我们可以离开了。”

“Harry and I have to go, too,” said Bess. “I want to get him out of here before he attracts the wrong types.” “哈利和我也要走，”贝丝说。 “我想在他引来错误的人之前把他带出去。”

“Hooligans and crazy fans?” asked Annie. “恶作剧的人和疯狂的粉丝？”安妮问。

“You got it,” said Bess. “Harry!” she called. “Time’s up!” “你明白了，”贝丝说。 “哈里！”她喊道。 “时间到了！”

Harry excused himself from the crowd of people and walked back to the bench. “Thanks, sweetheart,” he said. “I couldn’t escape.” He winked at Bess, then looked at Jack and Annie. “They think I do truly magical things. But it’s not true. It’s all just entertainment. It’s all tricks.” 哈利从人群中走了出来，回到长椅旁。 “谢谢，宝贝，”他说。 “我逃不掉。”他朝贝丝眨了眨眼，然后看着杰克和安妮。 “他们以为我真的会做魔法的事情。 但其实不是真的。 那都只是娱乐，都是把戏。”

“Harry’s never seen anyone do a trick he couldn’t do himself,” said Bess. “哈利从来没见过有人表演的把戏是他自己做不到的，”贝丝说。

“That’s a fact,” said Harry. “Nothing. Ever.” “这是事实，”哈利说。 “什么都没有。从来没有。”

“Amazing,” said Annie. “太神奇了，”安妮说。

“Amazing, yes, but it’s also a great sadness,” said Harry, frowning. “The world’s lost its wonder for me. I would be so happy just once to see a bit of magic I could never do or never explain.” “确实很神奇，但这也让我感到非常难过，”哈利皱着眉头说。 “这个世界对我来说已经失去了奇迹。” “只要能亲眼看到一点我永远做不到、也永远解释不了的魔法，我就满足了。”

“Really?” said Jack. “真的吗？”杰克说。

“Yes, but it’s not going to happen,” Harry said with a sigh. “Well, I’m ready, my love. Let’s go home.” “是的，但这件事不会发生，”哈利叹了口气说。 “好吧，亲爱的，我们回家吧。”

“Yes, let’s,” said Bess, standing. She looked at Jack and Annie. “Which way are you two going?” “好啊，”贝丝站起来说。 她看着杰克和安妮。 “你们俩要往哪个方向走？”

“Just down that way,” Jack said. And then he had a great idea. He smiled at Annie. “Let’s walk them as far as the Japanese Tea Garden.” “就往那边走，”杰克说。 然后他突然有了一个好主意。 他微笑着看着安妮。 “我们陪他们走到日本茶花园那边吧。”

Annie’s eyes lit up. She seemed to know what Jack was thinking. “Good plan!” she said. 安妮的眼睛亮了起来。 她似乎知道杰克在想什么。 “好主意！”她说。

“Sure, let’s all go together,” said Bess. “当然，我们一起走吧，”贝丝说。

Harry, Bess, Jack, and Annie all stepped down from the terrace and started up the broad avenue. The band was playing “After the Ball Is Over.” The crowd had thinned out. The lights were starting to dim. 哈利、贝丝、杰克和安妮都从露台走下来，沿着宽阔的大道开始前行。 乐队正在演奏《舞会结束后》。人群已经渐渐散去。 灯光也开始暗下来。

“What a fantastic park,” said Harry. “Did you try many rides? The pirate ship? Or the navy ship sailing the deep ocean? The Arctic trip?” “这真是个棒极了的公园，”哈利说。 “你试过很多游乐项目吗？海盗船？ 或者是那艘在深海航行的海军船？还是北极之旅？”

“No, but we’ve done all those things before,” said Jack. “不，但我们以前都做过这些事，”杰克说。

“Did you get to experience the cyclone attraction, too?” said Bess. “The volcano? The big fire?” “你有没有也去体验过龙卷风游乐项目呀？”贝丝说。 “火山？那个大火山？”

“Actually we’ve experienced all those things, too,” said Jack. “其实我们也体验过所有这些项目，”杰克说。

“Really?” said Bess. “So you must have been in this park a long time?” “真的吗？”贝丝说道。 “所以你一定在这个公园待了很久吗？”

“No, I was talking about real life,” Jack said matter-of-factly. “My sister and I travel in a magic tree house to different times and places and have lots of adventures.” “不，我是说现实生活，”杰克一本正经地说。 “我和我姐姐乘坐神奇的树屋，去不同的时间和地方，经历了很多冒险。”

Annie grinned. “Jack’s telling the truth,” she said. “We’ve been all through time.” 安妮露出了笑容。 “杰克说的是实话，”她说。 “我们已经穿越过时间了。”

Harry and Bess chuckled. “Sounds like a good act,” said Harry. 哈利和贝丝笑了起来。 “听起来是个不错的表演，”哈利说。

“It does, doesn’t it?” said Bess delightedly. “Where else do you perform your act, besides Coney Island?” “是啊，不是吗？”贝丝高兴地说。 “除了康尼岛，你们还在哪里表演这个节目？”

“We don’t really perform this act for anyone,” said Annie. “It’s usually a secret.” “我们其实不会给别人表演这个节目，”安妮说。 “这通常是个秘密。”

“But we’ll show it to you, ” said Jack. “但我们会给你表演，”杰克说。

“Lucky us,” said Bess, winking at Harry. “我们可真幸运，”贝丝说，朝哈里眨了眨眼。

“The tree house is parked in the Japanese Tea Garden,” said Annie. “You want to see it? It’ll only take a minute.” “树屋停在日式茶花园里，”安妮说。 “你想去看看吗？ 只用一分钟。”

“Sure, why not?” said Harry. “当然，为什么不呢？”哈利说。

Bess and Harry followed Jack and Annie over to the wooden gate that led to the Japanese Tea Garden. They followed them through the gateway, up the pebble path, and over the small arched bridge to the cluster of trees. 贝丝和哈利跟着杰克和安妮走到通往日式茶花园的木头大门那里。 他们跟着他们穿过大门，沿着鹅卵石小路，走过小拱桥，来到一片树丛前。

“There,” said Annie. She pointed up at one of the trees. “在那里，”安妮说。 她指着其中一棵树。

“A tree house! Well, how about that?” said Harry. He sounded genuinely surprised. “I never knew that was here.” “树屋！ 哇，真没想到。”哈利说。 他真的听起来很惊讶。 “我从来不知道这里还有这个。”

“That’s because it’s only been here for a few hours,” said Annie. “那是因为它只在这里待了几个小时，”安妮说。

“You want to watch us take off?” asked Jack. “你想看我们起飞吗？”杰克问。

“Take off?” said Bess. “起飞？”贝丝说。

“Yep, the tree house is going to take us back home to Pennsylvania now,” said Jack, “to a time more than a hundred years from today.” “对，树屋现在要带我们回到宾夕法尼亚州的家，”杰克说，“回到比今天还要一百多年后的时间。”

“In the future,” Annie added. “在未来，”安妮补充道。

Bess and Harry laughed. “You two might be the looniest kids in the world,” said Bess. 贝丝和哈利笑了。 “你们两个可能是世界上最疯癫的孩子，”贝丝说。

“Or the best actors!” said Harry. “或者是最棒的演员！”哈利说。

“Wait and see …,” said Annie. She held out her hand. “Bye, Harry. It’s been great knowing you.” “等着瞧吧……，”安妮说。 她伸出了手。 “再见，哈利。 认识你真的很开心。”

Harry shook Annie’s hand, and then he shook Jack’s. Bess grabbed each of them and gave them big hugs. “You two kiddos are great!” she said, giggling. “You’re just my kind of screwballs.” 哈利握了握安妮的手，然后又握了握杰克的手。 贝丝一把抱住他们，给了他们大大的拥抱。 “你们这两个小家伙真棒！”，她说着，咯咯地笑起来。 “你们就是我这种人喜欢的疯子。”

They all laughed. Then Jack and Annie climbed up the rope ladder. Inside the tree house, Annie picked up the Pennsylvania book, and she and Jack looked out the window. “Bye!” They both waved to Harry and Bess. 他们全都笑了。 然后杰克和安妮爬上了绳梯。 在树屋里，安妮拿起了一本宾夕法尼亚的书，她和杰克一起看向窗外。 “再见！”他们向哈利和贝丝挥手告别。

The Houdinis waved back. 胡迪尼一家也挥手回应了。

“I wish we could go home,” Annie whispered as she pointed to a picture of Frog Creek. “我希望我们能回家，”安妮指着一张蛙溪镇的图片，小声说道。

“Behold a miracle!” Jack shouted down to Bess and Harry. He clapped three times. One! Two! THREE! “看啊，奇迹发生了！”杰克朝贝丝和哈利大声喊道。 他鼓了三次掌。一！ 二！三！

Then the wind started to blow. 然后，风开始吹了起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都安静下来了。

Absolutely still. 非常非常安静。

The Frog Creek woods were quiet and peaceful. The early-evening light filtered into the tree house. Jack and Annie were wearing their own clothes again. 蛙溪镇的森林安静又平和。 傍晚的阳光照进了树屋。 杰克和安妮又穿上了自己的衣服。

“Now that was the best escape act ever,” said Annie. “这真是最棒的逃脱表演了，”安妮说。

Jack smiled. “I wanted to make Harry happy. We showed him something he’ll never be able to explain.” 杰克笑了。 “我想让哈利开心。 我们给他看了他永远也解释不清的东西。”

Jack looked at his watch. As always, no time had passed in Frog Creek while they’d been on their mission. “Six minutes to get home,” said Jack. 杰克看了看他的手表。 和往常一样，他们在执行任务的时候，蛙溪镇的时间一点都没过去。 “回家还有六分钟，”杰克说。

“Yep, but first we have to write down our new secret of greatness,” said Annie. “对，不过在那之前，我们得先把新的伟大秘密写下来，”安妮说。

Jack pulled out a pencil and picked up the paper that was still lying on the floor. And under the word HUMILITY, he wrote: 杰克拿出一支铅笔，捡起了还躺在地上的那张纸。 在“谦逊”这个词下面，他写下了：

HARD WORK

“Hard work,” said Annie. “That’s so simple.” “努力工作，”安妮说。 “这太简单了。”

“Yep,” said Jack. “Sometimes the truth is amazingly simple.” He placed the Ring of Truth on top of the paper. “And I’ll leave this, too”—he took the little bottle from his backpack and placed it beside the ring—“for next time.” “是啊，”杰克说。 “有时候，真相竟然如此简单。”他把真理之戒放在了纸的上面。 “而且这个我也留下”—他从背包里拿出小瓶子，把它放在戒指旁边—“等下次再来的时候用。”

“Mist gathered at first light on the first day of the new moon on the Isle of Avalon,” said Annie. “新月第一天清晨，第一缕阳光照在阿瓦隆岛时，薄雾就出现了，”安妮说。

Jack closed his backpack. “Okay, that’s it! Let’s go! Mom’s hot dogs are waiting!” 杰克合上了他的背包。 “好了，就这样吧！我们走吧！ 妈妈做的热狗正等着我们呢！”

“Greatest in the world!” said Annie. The two of them climbed down from the ladder and headed through the trees. “世界上最好的！”安妮说。 他们两个人从梯子上爬下来，穿过树林走了出去。

“You know what?” said Annie. “你知道吗？”安妮说。

“What?” asked Jack. “什么？”杰克问。

“You know how we were supposed to learn a secret of greatness from the Great Houdini?” Annie said. “你知道我们本来应该从伟大的霍迪尼那里学到伟大秘密的方法吗？”安妮说。

“Yep,” said Jack. “对，”杰克说。

“And we kept waiting for Harry to say or do something to make the ring glow?” said Annie. “我们一直在等哈利做点什么，让戒指发光吗？”安妮说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “是的，”杰克说。

“But in the end, we learned the secret from Bess, not Harry,” said Annie. “但最后，我们是从贝丝那儿学到秘密的，而不是哈利，”安妮说。

“You’re right,” said Jack. “你说得对，”杰克说。

“So I think Bess was the Great Houdini that Merlin sent us to find,” said Annie. “所以我觉得贝丝就是梅林派我们去找的伟大的霍迪尼，”安妮说。

“Why do you think Bess was great?” asked Jack. “你为什么觉得贝丝很厉害？”杰克问。

“Well, she was kind and funny and friendly,” said Annie. “因为她善良、有趣、友好，”安妮说。

“Yeah …,” said Jack, “like you.” “是啊……”杰克说，“就像你一样。”

“Me?” said Annie. “Huh.” She looked a little embarrassed. “Well, also she was a loyal person. She was loyal to Harry.” “我？”安妮说。 “嗯。”她看起来有点不好意思。 “嗯，她也是一位忠诚的人。 她对哈利很忠诚。”

“That’s true,” said Jack. “这倒是真的，”杰克说。

“She was loyal like you are,” said Annie. “她像你一样忠诚，”安妮说。

“Me?” said Jack. “我？”杰克说。

“Yep, you came right back when you realized I needed help,” said Annie. “Even though I’d made you really mad.” “对，你一意识到我需要帮助，就立刻回来了，”安妮说。 “尽管我让你非常生气。”

“Well, you were loyal, too,” said Jack. “You didn’t hold a grudge because I’d left you.” “好吧，你也是忠诚的，”杰克说。 “你没有因为我不辞而别而记仇。”

“Me, loyal?” said Annie. “And kind and funny and friendly? You think I’m all that? Seriously? Me?” “我，忠诚？”安妮说。 “还有善良、有趣、友好？” 你认为我有那么好吗？真的吗？ 我？

“Okay, forget it,” said Jack, grinning. “好吧，算了吧，”杰克笑着说。

“No, I’m going to remember all those things,” said Annie. “And the next time you’re mad at me, I’m going to remind you how great I am. How loyal and kind and funny—” “不，我会记住所有的事情，”安妮说。 “下次你生我的气时，我会提醒你我有多棒。 多么忠诚、善良和有趣——”

“Okay, okay, that’s enough!” said Jack. “Bye!” He took off running. He ran out of the Frog Creek woods, across the street, and down the sidewalk. “好了好了，够了！”杰克说。 “再见！”他飞快地跑开了。 他从蛙溪镇的树林里跑出来，穿过街道，沿着人行道一路跑下去。

“Wait! Slow down! You can’t escape from me!” called Annie. She ran after Jack as fast as she could and caught up with him before he reached home. “等一下！慢点跑！ 你逃不掉的！”，安妮喊道。 她尽自己最大的努力追赶杰克，在他到家之前追上了他。
