5. Together 5. 一起行动

Jack whirled around and started back up Surf Avenue. The sidewalk was so crowded, he had to jump off the curb into the street and trot alongside the old-timey cars and the horses and buggies. 杰克猛地转身，开始沿着冲浪大道往回走。 人行道上挤满了人，他不得不跳下人行道，跑到街上，和那些老式的汽车、马车和马车夫并肩前行。

When Jack reached the theater, people were still making their way inside. He squeezed through the crowd, trying not to push anyone too hard. “Excuse me, excuse me,” he said. He tried to sneak his way past the ticket taker, but the man grabbed him. “Ticket, sonny?” 当杰克到达剧院时，人们还在陆续往里面走。 他挤进人群，尽量不把别人推得太狠。 “对不起，对不起，”他说。 他试图偷偷溜过检票员，但那个人抓住了他。 “票呢，小伙子？”

“I don’t have a ticket,” said Jack. “I just need to get backstage fast.” “我没有票，”杰克说。 “我只需要快点去后台。”

“Beat it, kid,” said the ticket taker. “滚开，小孩，”检票员说。

“Wait!” said Jack. He pointed at the chalkboard announcing their act. “I’m Jolly Jack. See?” “等一下！”杰克说。 他指着黑板，上面写着他们要表演的节目。 “我是快乐的杰克。看见了吗？”

“I don’t see nothing jolly about you!” said the ticket seller. “Beat it now or I’ll call the cops. Next!” “我什么都没看见，你一点都不快乐！”售票员说。 “现在走开，不然我就叫警察了。下一个！”

Jack backed away from the door. He pulled out his pocket watch. It was ten minutes to eight! Annie had to go on at eight-fifteen! He paced up and down the sidewalk, desperately looking for another entrance. 杰克往后退到门边。 他掏出他的怀表。 离八点还有十分钟！ 安妮要在八点十五分出发！ 他在人行道上来回走着，焦急地寻找另一个入口。

There must be a door somewhere that leads backstage! he thought. He peered down a long, narrow alley beside the theater. The alley was dark, except for the glow of light from high windows above a row of garbage cans. 一定有个门能通往后台！他心想。 他朝剧院旁边一条又长又窄的小巷望去。 小巷很暗，只有高处窗户透出的光亮照在一堆垃圾桶上。

Jack hurried down the alley. A rat skittered out from behind a can. Jack kept going until he rounded the corner. He found the back entrance, but when he tried the handle, it was locked. He banged and banged on the door, but no one answered. 杰克赶紧朝小巷走去。 一只老鼠从垃圾桶后面窜了出来。 杰克继续往前走，直到转过拐角。 他找到了后门，但当他试着开门时，门却上锁了。 他不停地敲门，但没人回应。

The windows! Jack thought. He hurried back into the alley. To reach the row of windows, he had to step from a packing crate onto the top of a garbage can. Jack stood on his toes and looked through a lit window. He saw a hallway, but no one was there. He carefully stepped from one garbage can to another until he reached the next window. 窗户！杰克心想。 他赶紧跑回小巷里。 要到达那排窗户，他必须从一个纸箱上跳到垃圾箱的顶部。 杰克踮起脚尖，透过一扇亮着的窗户往外看。 他看到一条走廊，但那里没有人。 他小心翼翼地从一个垃圾箱跳到另一个垃圾箱，直到到达下一扇窗户。

Jack looked through the glass into the Bambinis’ dressing room. He saw Annie dressed in an oversized tuxedo. She was staring at a piece of paper. 杰克透过玻璃看向巴姆比尼家的更衣室。 他看到安妮穿着一件 oversized 的晚礼服。 她正盯着一张纸看。

Jack pounded on the window. “Annie! Annie!” he shouted, panting. 杰克用力敲打窗户。 “安妮！” 安妮！”他喘着气喊道。

When Annie saw him, her face lit up. “Hi!” she yelled. She put down the paper and jumped onto a chair. She lifted a latch and then pushed the window up. “Thanks for coming back!” she cried. “I am so sorry, Jack!” 安妮看到他时，脸上露出了笑容。 “你好！”她大喊。 她放下报纸，跳上了一把椅子。 她抬起插销，然后把窗户推开。 “谢谢你回来！”她喊道。 “对不起，杰克！”

“Don’t worry about it,” he said. “别担心，”他说。

“Are you coming in?” asked Annie. “你要进来吗？”安妮问。

“Yeah. Hold on.” Jack jumped down and picked up the packing crate. He placed it on top of the garbage can. Then he carefully climbed onto the can and the crate. Clutching the windowsill, he hoisted himself through the window. “嗯。” “抓紧了。”杰克跳下来，捡起了那个纸箱。 他把它放在垃圾桶上面。 然后他小心翼翼地爬上了垃圾桶和纸箱。 他抓住窗台，把自己拉进了窗户。

Annie helped him step down onto a chair, and then onto the floor. 安妮帮他踩着椅子，然后走到地板上。

Jack reached into his bag and pulled out the small bottle. “You’ll need this before you go … onstage,” he said, out of breath. “Mist gathered at first light … on the first day … of the new moon … on the Isle of Avalon.” 杰克把手伸进包里，拿出了一小瓶东西。 “在你上台之前，你得先用这个……”他喘着气说。 “第一缕阳光出现时，迷雾就会聚集……在新月第一天……在阿瓦隆岛……”

“Thanks!” said Annie, taking the bottle. “When I realized I didn’t have it, I didn’t know what to do.” “谢谢！”安妮接过瓶子说。 “当我意识到自己没带它的时候，我都不知道该怎么办。”

“That’s what I figured,” said Jack. “这就是我想的，”杰克说。

Annie grabbed the paper from the table. “Look at this,” she said, handing it to Jack. “The Bambini Brothers must have left it.” 安妮从桌子上抓起了那张纸。 “看这个，”她说，把它递给杰克。 “巴姆比尼兄弟一定把它留下了。”

Jack and Annie looked at the paper together. “It looks like a list of tricks,” said Jack. He read: 杰克和安妮一起看着这张纸。 “看起来像是一份魔法把戏的清单，”杰克说。他读道：

Instant Wand 瞬间魔杖

Three Bunnies 三只兔子

Miracle Doves 奇迹鸽子

Cards from Thin Air 凭空出现的卡片

Chinese Rings 中国环

“Yeah, I think I have all the props for them,” said Annie. “See? There are two wands, three rabbits, two doves, a pack of cards, and three big rings.” “是的，我想我已经有所有道具了，”安妮说。 “看？ 有两根魔杖，三只兔子，两只鸽子，一副牌，还有三个大环。”

“Yeah, it’s all here …,” said Jack. “是的，都在这儿了……，”杰克说。

“But what do I do with it?” said Annie. “但是我要怎么用它们呢？”安妮说。

“Maybe you’ll find out when you use the magic mist,” said Jack. “也许当你使用魔法雾的时候，你会找到答案，”杰克说。

“Oh, yes, I bet I will!” said Annie. “哦，是的，我敢打赌我会的！”安妮说。

“Yeah,” Jack said. “是的，”杰克说。

“Now I can’t wait to find out what’s going to happen!” said Annie. “现在我迫不及待想看看接下来会发生什么！”安妮说。

“Bet you’ll have a good time,” said Jack. “你一定会玩得很开心，”杰克说。

“What are you thinking?” asked Annie. “Should I still perform all by myself? Or do you think maybe we should perform together?” “你在想什么？”安妮问。 “我是不是还得一个人表演？ 还是你觉得我们该一起表演？”

Jack stared at her a moment, then sighed. “Together,” he said softly. 杰克盯着她看了片刻，然后叹了口气。 “一起表演，”他轻声说。

“Yay!” said Annie. “We’ll be a great team. I promise.” She held up the bottle. “Should we use this right now? Do you want to?” “太好了！”安妮说。 “我们会成为一支很棒的团队。 我保证。”她举起了瓶子。 “我们现在就用这个好吗？你想用吗？”

“Hmm,” said Jack. “Yeah. Then we’ll be able to figure everything out. But remember, the magic only works for one hour.” He looked at his watch. “It’s five after eight. If we start the magic now, we’ll be great stage magicians until five after nine.” “嗯，”杰克说。 “对啊。 那样的话，我们就能弄清楚一切了。 但记住，魔法只管用一个小时。”他看了看手表。 “八点零五分。 如果我们现在开始魔法，我们就会一直是很棒的舞台魔术师，直到九点零五分。”

“No problem!” said Annie. “We go on at eight-fifteen for thirty minutes. That means we’ll leave the stage at eight-forty-five.” “没问题！”安妮说。 “我们八点十五分上台，表演三十分钟。 这意味着我们会在八点四十五分离开舞台。”

There was a knock on the door. Mr. Wilson poked his head inside, and the noise of the audience rushed into the room like a lion’s roar. Jack heard stamping and whistling and shouting. 有人敲了敲门。先生。 威尔逊把头探了进去，观众的喧闹声像狮子的吼叫一样涌进了房间。 杰克听见了踩踏声、口哨声和喊叫声。

“You go on in ten minutes!” Mr. Wilson said. “你十分钟后进去！”，威尔逊先生说。

“Thanks,” said Annie. “谢谢，”安妮说。

The door closed. Jack looked at Annie in a panic. “Did you hear that crowd?” he said. 门关上了。 杰克惊慌地看着安妮。 “你听到那群人了吗？”他说。

“Don’t think about it,” said Annie. “Quick, let’s use the magic!” She pulled the cork out of the bottle. “Ready?” “别去想它，”安妮说。 “快，我们用魔法吧！”她拔出了瓶子的软木塞。 “准备好了吗？”

Jack nodded. 杰克点了点头。

Annie held the bottle under her nose. “We wish to be two great stage magicians!” she said, and she took a deep breath. “Ahhh!” Then she handed the bottle to Jack. 安妮把瓶子举到鼻子下面。 “我们希望成为两位伟大的舞台魔术师！”她说，然后深吸了一口气。 “啊——！”接着，她把瓶子递给了杰克。

Jack closed his eyes and inhaled the mist. It smelled of dewy grass, sparkling lake water, and wild thyme. He stuck the cork back into the bottle and put the bottle into the pocket of his pants. 杰克闭上眼睛，吸入了雾气。 雾气闻起来有露水草、闪闪发光的湖水和野生百里香的味道。 他把软木塞重新塞回瓶子里，然后把瓶子放进裤子的口袋里。

Jack gave Annie a big grin. He felt like a new person. The crowd wasn’t scary anymore. In fact, he could hardly wait to get on the stage and show them what he could do. 杰克对安妮露出了一个大大的笑容。 他感觉自己像换了个人。 人群不再可怕了。 其实，他迫不及待地想上台，向大家展示他能做什么。

“Okay!” Jack said, clapping his hands. “I’d better put on my costume!” He grabbed the extra tuxedo and slipped behind a dressing curtain. “好！”杰克说，拍了拍手。 “我最好穿上戏服！”他抓起那套备用的西装，溜到更衣幕布后面。

As he changed his clothes, Jack realized that everything was slightly too big. But it didn’t bother him. Not at all. He rolled up his pants cuffs and the sleeves of his shirt and jacket, and then stepped out from behind the curtain. 当他换衣服时，杰克意识到所有东西都稍微大了一点。 但这根本没让他烦。一点都不烦。 他卷起了裤子的裤脚和衬衫、外套的袖子，然后从幕布后面走了出来。

“You look just like a real magician!” said Annie. “你看起来就像一个真正的魔术师！”安妮说。

“Of course,” said Jack, looking in the mirror and straightening his bow tie, “for that is exactly who I am. And so are you.” He looked around the room. In the corner was a table on a pedestal. The top was covered with black velvet trimmed with gold fringe. “当然，”杰克看着镜子，整理了一下领结，“因为我就是真正的魔术师。 你也是。”他环顾了一下房间。 角落里有一张放在高台上的桌子。 顶部铺着黑色天鹅绒，边缘装饰着金色流苏。

“Now let us prepare for our show. First we’ll put the rabbits and doves in the hidden compartments of that table,” he said. “现在，让我们开始准备我们的表演。 首先，我们要把兔子和鸽子放进那张桌子的隐藏隔间里，”他说。

“Yep,” said Annie. “No problem.” “嗯，”安妮说。 “没问题。”

Jack and Annie began preparing their props quietly and expertly, as if they’d done it a thousand times before. Jack picked up the rabbit cage and set it on the table. He pressed a small trapdoor in the middle of the velvet-covered tabletop, and the door flapped down. 杰克和安妮开始安静而熟练地准备他们的道具，就好像他们以前做过一千次一样。 杰克拿起兔子笼子，把它放在桌子上。 他按了按天鹅绒桌面上中间的小门，门“啪”地一声向下打开。

“Here you go, bunnies,” said Jack. He lifted the three small rabbits out of their cage and placed them comfortably in one of the table’s hidden compartments. “See you soon.” “来吧，小兔子们，”杰克说。 他把笼子里的三只小兔子轻轻抱出来，放进桌子的一个隐藏隔间里。 “很快再见。”

While Jack took care of the rabbits, Annie handled the birds. She opened a second small door on the tabletop and carefully placed the two white doves into another secret compartment. “They look happy in there,” she said. 杰克照顾兔子的时候，安妮负责处理鸟儿。 她在桌面上打开第二个小门，小心地把两只白鸽放进另一个秘密隔层里。 “它们在里面看起来很开心，”她说。

Jack set the deck of cards in the center of the table. Annie hid the two wands inside the sleeves of her tuxedo jacket. 杰克把一副牌放在桌子中央。 安妮把两根魔杖藏在她晚礼服夹克的袖子里。

Jack pulled his pocket watch from his pants. “Eight-fifteen,” he said. 杰克从裤子口袋里拿出他的怀表。 “八点十五分，”他说。

Annie smiled. “We have fifty minutes of magic left,” she said. 安妮笑了。 “我们还有五十分钟的魔法时间，”她说。

Jack slipped the watch into the pocket of his tuxedo jacket. Annie arranged the silver rings on top of the table. 杰克把手表放进他晚礼服的口袋里。 安妮把银戒指摆放在桌子的最上面。

A knock came at the door and Mr. Wilson looked into the room. 有人敲了敲门，然后是梅林先生。 威尔逊先生朝房间里看了一眼。

“Ready? Jolly Jack? Amazing Annie?” the stage manager asked. “准备好了吗？快乐的杰克？ 神奇的安妮？”舞台经理问道。

This time, the roar of the crowd did not alarm Jack in the least. In fact, it thrilled him. “Yes, indeed, Mr. Wilson! Thank you!” he said with a jolly laugh. 这一次，观众的欢呼声一点都没让杰克感到害怕。 事实上，这让他感到非常兴奋。 “是的，确实如此，威尔逊先生！ 谢谢！”他开心地笑着说。

“If you’d be so kind, Mr. Wilson, please set our table center stage,” said Annie. “如果你能帮个忙，威尔逊先生， 请把我们的桌子搬到舞台中央，”安妮说。

“Hank! Butch!” Mr. Wilson shouted. Two stagehands immediately appeared. They picked up the table and carried it out of the dressing room. When the stage crew was gone, Jack and Annie pulled on their white gloves. “汉克！巴奇！”先生 威尔逊喊道。 两名舞台工作人员立刻出现了。 他们捡起桌子，把它从更衣室里搬了出来。 当舞台工作人员离开后，杰克和安妮戴上了白色手套。

“You sure you feel okay about doing this?” Annie asked. “你确定你做这件事感觉没问题吗？”安妮问。

“Totally.” Jack smiled at her. He tightened his gloves and wiggled his fingers. Then he put on his top hat. “Let’s go, my dear,” he said, leading the way out the door. “It’s showtime!” “当然没问题。”杰克对她笑了笑。 他收紧了手套，然后活动了一下手指。 然后他戴上了他的礼帽。 “走吧，亲爱的，”他说着，带头走出了门。 “开始表演了！”，
