8. Master of Escape 8. 逃脱大师

“You … you?” breathed Annie. “你……你？”安妮喘着气说。

“You’re the Great Houdini?” said Jack. He couldn’t believe it! “你就是伟大的胡迪尼？”杰克说。 他简直不敢相信！

“Just give us a moment to set up our show!” Houdini shouted to the audience. “Sit tight, and we’ll be back quick as a wink! In the meantime, how about a nice hand for these funny little magicians?” “给我们一点时间布置我们的表演！”霍迪尼对观众喊道。 “坐好，我们马上回来，快得像眨一下眼睛！ 与此同时，大家给这几位有趣的小小魔术师鼓个掌吧？

Music played, Jack and Annie bowed, the crowd roared enthusiastically, and the red curtain came down. Bess rushed over to Jack and Annie. “Thanks for helping us out!” she said. 音乐响起，杰克和安妮鞠躬，观众热烈欢呼，红色的幕布缓缓落下。 贝丝急忙跑到杰克和安妮面前。 “谢谢你们帮我们一把！”她说。

“We had no idea you had a comedy act,” Harry said. “我们根本不知道你们还有一个喜剧表演，”哈利说。

“We had no idea you were the Great Houdini!” said Annie. “我们根本不知道你们就是伟大的霍迪尼！”安妮说。

“That’s what we figured,” said Bess. “Forgive us for having a little fun with you two.” “我们早就猜到了，”贝丝说。 “请原谅我们刚才和你们开的这个小玩笑。”

“Harry! Hurry!” Mr. Dewey said. The theater owner led Houdini back to his dressing room. “哈里！快点！”先生。 德威说。 剧院老板带胡迪尼回到了更衣室。

“Thanks again, you crazy kids, for covering for us!” said Bess, hugging Jack and Annie. “谢谢你们，你们这两个疯孩子，帮我们顶了这个缸！”贝丝一边说，一边拥抱杰克和安妮。

“Everyone, please clear the stage!” Mr. Wilson said. “We have to reset!” “大家请离开舞台！”威尔逊先生说。 “我们必须重新布置！”

“Stick around and watch us!” Bess said to Jack and Annie. She guided them to a couple of chairs just offstage. “Sit here! I have to help Harry.” She hurried back to the dressing room. “留下来，看我们表演吧！”贝丝对杰克和安妮说。 她带他们走到舞台边的几张椅子前。 “坐这儿！ 我得去帮哈里。”她赶紧跑回更衣室。

As crowd noises filled the theater, Annie turned to Jack. “Can you believe it?” she said. 剧场里人声鼎沸，安妮转头看向杰克。 “你能相信吗？”她说。

“No … I can’t—I can’t believe it …,” said Jack. He was in a daze. “不……我不能——我不能相信……”，杰克说。他整个人都愣住了。

“Maybe we’ll learn the secret of greatness from him now,” said Annie hopefully. “也许我们现在能从他那里学到伟大的秘密了，”安妮满怀希望地说。

“Oh, right,” said Jack. He pulled off his gloves and looked at the Ring of Truth on his finger. He hoped it would soon start to glow. “哦，对了，”杰克说。 他摘下手套，看着手指上的真理之戒。 他希望它很快就能开始发光。

While the Houdinis prepared for the show, the stagehands found all the rabbits and doves and put them back in their cages. They picked up the cards and rings and hauled the table away. 当胡迪尼一家为表演做准备时，舞台工作人员找到了所有的兔子和鸽子，把它们重新关回笼子里。 他们捡起卡片和戒指，把桌子搬走了。

“Set Harry’s cabinet center stage!” Mr. Wilson called. “把哈利的柜子放在舞台中央！”威尔逊先生喊道。

Hank and Butch carried a wooden cabinet onto the stage. Black fabric covered three sides of it, and a black curtain hung in front. 汉克和巴奇把一个木柜抬到了舞台上。 黑色的布料盖住了它的三面，黑色的幕布挂在前面。

Just as the audience began to stamp their feet, Mr. Dewey led the Houdinis out of their dressing room. Bess was wearing white tights and a short pleated dress. Her delicate face was framed by a ring of dark curls. Harry was dressed in an elegant black suit, his hair combed back neatly. 就在观众开始跺脚的时候，先生。 德威带领胡迪尼一家从更衣室走了出来。 贝丝穿着白色紧身裤和一条短褶裙。 她精致的脸蛋被一圈深色的卷发环绕着。 哈利穿着一套优雅的黑色西装，头发整齐地向后梳着。

The curtain rose, and the orchestra played a rousing tune. 幕布拉开，乐队奏响了一首激动人心的曲子。

Bess stood beside the cabinet while Harry stepped into a spotlight. For a long moment, he gazed intensely at the audience. When the noisy crowd grew quiet, he spoke in a voice that carried all the way to the back of the theater. 贝丝站在柜子旁边，而哈利走进了聚光灯下。 他久久地凝视着观众，眼神专注。 当嘈杂的人群安静下来时，他用一个能传到剧院最后面的声音说话。

“Ladies and gentlemen, I, Harry Houdini, would like to make a bet with you! I’ll bet a thousand dollars there is no lock that can hold me captive tonight! They don’t call me the Master of Escape for nothing!” “女士们先生们，我是哈利·胡迪尼，我想和你们打个赌！ 我赌一千美元，今晚没有任何锁能把我关住！ 他们叫我‘逃脱大师’可不是没有原因的！

From that moment on, Harry Houdini was the Master of Escape. As the orchestra played, he performed easily, with humor and confidence. Several times during his act, he invited volunteers onstage to make sure all his locks were securely locked and all his knots were tightly tied. 从那一刻起，哈利·胡迪尼就成了逃脱大师。 当乐队开始演奏时，他轻松地表演，充满幽默和自信。 在表演过程中，他好几次邀请观众上台，确保所有的锁都牢固地锁好了，所有的绳结都紧紧地系好了。

During his first trick, Houdini asked the audience volunteers to check three pairs of handcuffs that Bess had locked around his wrists. Then he stepped inside his cabinet. 在第一个魔术中，胡迪尼请观众志愿者检查贝丝锁在他手腕上的三副手铐。 然后他走进了柜子里。

Bess closed the black curtain. “Let’s count to ten together!” she called to the crowd. 贝丝拉上了黑色的幕布。 “我们一起数到十吧！”她对人群喊道。

Everyone counted: “One! Two! Three! …” 大家开始数数：“一！二！ 三！……”

On the count of ten, Houdini threw open the curtain—his handcuffs dangling from his hand! 数到十的时候，霍迪尼猛地拉开帷幕——他的手铐从他手上晃荡着！

Jack and Annie gasped. “How did he do that?” Jack said. 杰克和安妮惊呼起来。 “他是怎么做到的？”杰克说。

Harry did the trick again with a new level of difficulty. This time, Bess not only locked handcuffs around Houdini’s wrists, she also wrapped chains around his ankles and secured them with padlocks. Again, excited volunteers made certain that all the locks were truly locked. 哈利又表演了这个魔术，难度更高了。 这次，贝丝不仅用手铐锁住了霍迪尼的手腕，还用铁链缠住了他的脚踝，并用挂锁固定好了。 再次，兴奋的志愿者们确认所有的锁都确实锁好了。

When the black curtain closed, Bess asked everyone to count again. On the count of ten, Harry threw open the curtain—freed from his shackles and handcuffs. The crowd clapped and cheered. 当黑色帷幕再次落下时，贝丝让大家再次数数。 十、一！哈利猛地拉开窗帘——他挣脱了手铐和脚镣。 观众们鼓掌欢呼。

“And now,” he said, “the Master of Escape will share with you his most thrilling and dangerous escape to date!” “现在，”他说，“逃脱大师将和你们分享他迄今为止最惊险刺激、最危险的一次逃脱！”

Houdini dashed offstage. The orchestra played scary-sounding music as Hank and Butch brought out a huge milk can and placed it inside the cabinet. Audience members whispered to each other as the stagehands filled the giant can with buckets of water. 霍迪尼迅速跑下舞台。 汉克和巴奇抬出一个巨大的牛奶罐，把它放进柜子里，同时乐队演奏出令人毛骨悚然的音乐。 当舞台工作人员往大罐子里倒水时，观众们互相小声议论着。

Harry then reappeared dressed in an old-fashioned black bathing suit. Bess put the three pairs of handcuffs around his wrists again. After volunteers checked the locks, Houdini climbed inside the milk can. 接着，哈利穿着一件老式的黑色泳衣重新出现。 贝丝又用三副手铐把他手腕绑了起来。 志愿者检查完锁之后，霍迪尼爬进了牛奶罐里。

Jack and Annie stood up to watch. 杰克和安妮站起来观看。

Houdini lowered himself into the water until he was completely inside the can. Water splashed over the sides. Bess then closed the lid, padlocked it, and pulled the black curtain. 胡迪尼把自己慢慢放进水里，直到完全钻进罐子里。 水从罐子的两边溅了出来。 贝丝接着关上盖子，用锁链锁好，然后拉上黑色的窗帘。

While the can was out of sight, the orchestra played tick-tock, tick- tock. Jack could hardly breathe as one minute went by … two minutes … Someone in the audience cried out with alarm. 当罐子看不见的时候，乐队开始演奏滴答、滴答的声音。 杰克几乎喘不过气来，一分钟过去了……两分钟……观众席上有人惊叫起来。

How does Harry keep from drowning? Jack wondered anxiously. How can he undo handcuffs in such a tight space? 哈利是怎么不被淹死的？ 杰克焦急地想。 他怎么能在这么狭小的空间里解开手铐？

Suddenly Houdini threw back the curtain. He was standing in front of the milk can, dripping wet! He had escaped again! He stepped to the side and pointed to the lid of the can—it was still locked firmly in place! 突然，胡迪尼掀开了窗帘。 他站在牛奶罐子前面，浑身湿透了！ 他又逃出来了！ 他走到一旁，指着罐子的盖子——它仍然牢牢地锁着！

Bess brought Harry a red robe, and the audience cheered. The orchestra played triumphant music. 贝丝给哈利带来了一件红色的长袍，观众们欢呼起来。 乐队演奏了胜利的音乐。

The whole time Houdini had been performing his tricks, Jack had listened carefully to everything he’d said: 在整个胡迪尼表演魔术的过程中，杰克一直在仔细听他说的每一句话：

“Ladies and gentlemen, my brain is the key that sets me free!” “女士们先生们，我的大脑是我获得自由的钥匙！”，

“Ladies and gentlemen, my chief task is to conquer fear!” “女士们先生们，我最重要的任务是战胜恐惧！”，

“Ladies and gentlemen, an old trick in a new dress is always a pleasant change!” “女士们先生们，一个老把戏穿上新衣服总是令人愉快的改变！”，

Every time Houdini said something that sounded meaningful, Jack checked the Ring of Truth. He kept hoping that some secret of greatness would be revealed. But no matter what Harry said, the gold ring didn’t glow. 每次胡迪尼说出听起来有意义的话时，杰克都会去检查真理之环。 他一直希望会有一些伟大的秘密被揭示出来。 但是不管哈利说什么，那枚金戒指都没有发光。

After the milk can trick, the stage crew replaced the can with a steamer trunk. 牛奶罐的把戏之后，舞台工作人员用一个蒸汽箱替换了那个罐子。

“Finally, for old times’ sake, I close with the act that first made me famous,” said Houdini. “最后，为了纪念旧时光，我要以最初让我成名的节目结束演出，”胡迪尼说道。

The drummer played a drumroll. Then Houdini shouted: “The world-famous original Metamorphosis in three seconds! For this amazing feat, I will share the spotlight with the one and only Mrs. Houdini!” 鼓手敲响了鼓点。 然后胡迪尼大喊：“三秒钟完成世界著名的原版《变形术》！ 为了这个惊人的壮举，我要和唯一无二的胡迪尼夫人一起分享聚光灯！”，

Harry took Bess’s hand, and she curtsied for the crowd. 哈利牵起贝丝的手，她向观众鞠了一躬。

“Years ago, I met this beautiful gal right here on Coney Island,” Houdini said. “I love her with all my heart. But now she must become my prisoner.” “多年前，我在康尼岛这里遇到了这位美丽的姑娘，”胡迪尼说。 “我全心全意地爱着她。 但现在她必须成为我的囚犯。”

The audience laughed as a stagehand brought out a short, thick rope. Harry quickly tied Bess’s hands behind her back. “Dear one, I must now ask you to step into this bag.” Bess stepped into a large canvas bag, and Houdini tied the bag at the top. 观众们笑了，因为一个舞台工作人员搬出了一根又短又粗的绳子。 哈利迅速把贝丝的手绑在她背后。 “亲爱的，我必须请你现在走进这个袋子里。”贝丝走进了一个大画布袋，胡迪尼在袋子顶部系紧了它。

With Hank and Butch’s help, Houdini lifted Bess into the steamer trunk that had been placed inside the cabinet. Then Hank and Butch closed the trunk, wrapped it with a rope, and locked it with padlocks. After volunteers checked to see that the trunk was securely bound and locked, Houdini shouted, “Behold a miracle!” 在汉克和巴奇的帮助下，胡迪尼把贝丝抬进了放在柜子里的蒸汽箱里。 然后汉克和巴奇关上箱子，用一根绳子裹住箱子，用锁头锁了起来。 在志愿者检查箱子是否被牢固地绑好并锁上之后，胡迪尼大喊道：“看啊，奇迹发生了！”

Remaining inside the cabinet with the trunk, Harry closed the black curtain. As the audience watched and waited, three loud claps rang out from behind the curtain. 胡迪尼留在柜子里和箱子一起，哈利拉上了黑色的幕布。 当观众们看着并等待时，幕布后响起了三声响亮的掌声。

The curtain was thrown open. Bess stood in front of the roped and locked trunk, completely free! She quickly cut the rope around the trunk, unlocked the padlocks, untied the sack—and out jumped the Great Houdini! 幕布被猛地拉开。 贝丝站在被绳子绑着并锁上的箱子前，完全自由了！ 她迅速剪断了树干上的绳子，解开锁链，解开麻袋——然后，伟大的霍迪尼跳了出来！

Everyone leapt to their feet, applauding and roaring, “Bravo! Bravo!” 大家都跳了起来，鼓掌欢呼：“太棒了！太棒了！”

Jack and Annie clapped and shouted with them as the Houdinis bowed again and again. 杰克和安妮也和他们一起鼓掌大喊，看着霍迪尼们一次次鞠躬。

“Ladies and gentlemen, let me assure you that I have shown you no supernatural powers tonight!” Harry shouted. “Please know that magic is all tricks! Everything you saw tonight was illusion!” “女士们先生们，请相信我，今晚我向你们展示的没有任何超自然的力量！”哈利大声喊道。 “请记住，魔法其实都是把戏！ 你们今晚看到的一切都是幻象！

And Harry Houdini is the greatest Master of Illusion in the world, Jack thought. 杰克心想，哈利·霍迪尼是世界上最伟大的幻象大师。

Jack’s only disappointment was that all during the Great Houdini show, the Ring of Truth had not glowed once. Not even the tiniest bit. 杰克唯一的失望是，在伟大的霍迪尼表演过程中，真理之环一次都没有发光。 甚至一点点都没有。
