2. Leaving Again 2. 再次出发

“No one’s here!” Annie shouted down. “这里没人！”安妮朝下面喊。

Jack thought about going home. Then he thought about all the books in the tree house. 杰克想过回家。 可他又想起树屋里那些书。

He started up the ladder. When he was nearly to the tree house, he saw light in the distant sky. Dawn was starting to break. 他开始往上爬梯子。 快到树屋时，他看见远处的天上有光。 天开始亮了。

He crawled through a hole in the floor and took off his backpack. 他从地板上的洞口爬进去，放下背包。

It was dark inside the tree house. 树屋里很黑。

Annie was shining her flashlight on the books scattered about. 安妮正用手电照着散落一地的书。

“They’re still here,” she said. “书还在。”她说。

She stopped the light on a dinosaur book. It was the book that had taken them to the time of the dinosaurs. 灯光停在一本恐龙书上。 就是那本带他们去恐龙时代的书。

“Remember the Tyrannosaurus?” asked Annie. “还记得霸王龙吗？”安妮问。

Jack shuddered. Of course he remembered! How could anyone forget seeing a real live Tyrannosaurus rex? 杰克打了个寒战。 他当然记得！ 谁能忘掉亲眼看见一只活生生的霸王龙呢？

The light fell on a book about Pennsylvania. A red silk bookmark stuck out of it. 灯光落到一本讲宾夕法尼亚的书上。 一条红色丝绸书签从里面伸出来。

“Remember the picture of Frog Creek?” said Annie. “还记得蛙溪镇的那张图吗？”安妮说。

“Of course,” said Jack. That was the picture that had brought them home. “当然记得。”杰克说。 就是那张图把他们送回了家。

“There’s my favorite,” said Annie. “这本是我最喜欢的。”安妮说。

The light was shining on a book about knights and castles. There was a blue leather bookmark in it. 灯光照在一本讲骑士和城堡的书上。 书里夹着一条蓝色的皮书签。

Annie turned to the page with the bookmark. There was a picture of a knight on a black horse. He was riding toward a castle. 安妮翻到书签那一页。 上面有一幅画：一个骑士骑着黑马。 他正朝一座城堡奔去。

“Annie, close that book,” said Jack. “I know what you’re thinking.” “安妮，把书合上。”杰克说。 “我知道你在想什么。”

Annie pointed at the knight. 安妮指着那个骑士。

“Don’t, Annie!” “别这样，安妮！”

“We wish we could see this guy for real,” Annie said. “真希望能亲眼看看这个人。”安妮说。

“No, we don’t!” shouted Jack. “我们才不希望！”杰克大喊。

They heard a strange sound. 他们听见一种奇怪的声音。

“Neeee-hhhh!”

It sounded like a horse neighing. 听起来像马在叫。

They both went to the window. 他们一起走到窗边。

Annie shined the flashlight down on the ground. 安妮把手电往下照向地面。

“Oh no,” whispered Jack. “糟了。”杰克小声说。

“A knight!” said Annie. “一个骑士！”安妮说。

A knight in shining armor! Riding a black horse! Through the Frog Creek woods! 一个穿着闪亮盔甲的骑士！ 骑着一匹黑马！ 就在蛙溪镇的树林里！

Then the wind began to moan. The leaves began to tremble. 接着风开始呜呜地响。 叶子开始发抖。

It was happening again. 又来了。

“We’re leaving!” cried Annie. “Get down!” “我们要出发了！”安妮喊。 “趴下！”

The wind moaned louder. The leaves shook harder. 风呜得更响了。 叶子晃得更厉害了。

And the tree house started to spin. It spun faster and faster! 树屋开始旋转。 转得越来越快！

Jack squeezed his eyes shut. 杰克把眼睛闭得紧紧的。

Then everything was still. 然后，一切都静了。

Absolutely still. 彻底安静。

Jack opened his eyes. He shivered. The air was damp and cool. 杰克睁开眼睛。他打了个哆嗦。 空气又湿又凉。

The sound of a horse’s whinny came again from below. 下面又传来马的嘶叫声。

“Neeee-hhhh!”

“I think we’re here,” whispered Annie. She was still holding the castle book. “我想我们到了。”安妮小声说。 她还拿着那本城堡书。

Jack peeked out the window. 杰克往窗外偷看。

A huge castle loomed out of the fog. 一座巨大的城堡从雾里显现出来。

He looked around. The tree house was in a different oak tree. And down below, the knight on the black horse was riding by. 他朝四周看了看。 树屋到了另一棵橡树上。 下面，那个骑黑马的骑士正从旁边经过。

“We can’t stay here,” said Jack. “We have to go home and make a plan first.” He picked up the book about Pennsylvania. He opened it to the page with the red silk bookmark. He pointed to the photograph of the Frog Creek woods. “I wish—” “我们不能待在这儿。”杰克说。 “得先回家，再做个计划。”他拿起那本讲宾夕法尼亚的书。 他翻到夹着红丝书签的那一页。 他指着蛙溪镇树林的照片。 “我希望——”

“No!” said Annie. She yanked the book away from him. “Let’s stay! I want to visit the castle!” “不行！”安妮说。 她一把把书从他手里抢了过去。 “我们留下吧！ 我想去看看城堡！”

“You’re nuts. We have to examine the situation,” said Jack. “From home.” “你疯了。 我们得先把情况弄清楚。”杰克说。 “回家再弄。”

“Let’s examine it here!” said Annie. “就在这儿弄清楚！”安妮说。

“Come on.” He held out his hand. “Give it.” “快点。”他伸出手。 “给我。”

Annie gave Jack the book. “Okay. You can go home. I’m staying,” she said. She clipped the flashlight to her belt. 安妮把书给了杰克。 “好吧。你可以回家。 我留下。”她说。 她把手电筒夹到腰带上。



“Wait!” said Jack. “等一下！”杰克说。

“I’m going to take a peek. A teeny peek,” she said. And she scooted down the ladder. “我去看一眼。 就看小小一眼。”她说。 然后她顺着梯子溜了下去。

Jack groaned. Okay, she had won. He couldn’t leave without her. Besides, he sort of wanted to take a peek himself. 杰克哼了一声。 好吧，她赢了。 他不能丢下她自己走。 再说，他自己也有点想看一眼。

He put down the book about Pennsylvania. 他放下宾夕法尼亚那本书。

He dropped the castle book into his pack. He stepped onto the ladder. And headed down into the cool, misty air. 把城堡书丢进背包。 踏上梯子。 往下走进清凉、雾蒙蒙的空气里。
