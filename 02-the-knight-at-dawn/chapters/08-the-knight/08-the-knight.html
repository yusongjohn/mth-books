8. The Knight 8. 骑士

Water filled Jack’s nose and covered his head. His glasses fell off. He grabbed them just in time. He coughed and flailed his arms. 水灌进杰克的鼻子，淹没了他的头。 他的眼镜掉了。 他刚好一把抓住。 他一边咳嗽，一边乱挥胳膊。

“Jack!” Annie was calling from above. “杰克！”安妮在上面喊。

“I’m in … the moat!” said Jack, gasping for air. He tried to tread water and put his glasses back on. With his backpack, his shoes, and his heavy clothes, he could hardly stay afloat. “我掉进……护城河了！”杰克喘着气说。 他一边踩水，一边把眼镜戴回去。 背包、鞋子，还有湿透的厚衣服，让他几乎浮不起来。

SPLASH!

“Hi! I’m here!” Annie sputtered. “嘿！ 我在这儿！”安妮一边吐水一边说。

Jack could hear her nearby. But he couldn’t see her. 杰克听见她就在附近。 可他看不见她。

“Which way’s land?” Annie asked. “岸在哪边？”安妮问。

“I don’t know! Just swim!” “我不知道！游就行了！”

Jack dog-paddled through the cold black water. 杰克用狗刨式在又冷又黑的水里游。

He heard Annie swimming, too. At first it seemed as if she was swimming in front of him. But then he heard a splash behind him. 他也听见安妮在游。 一开始，他觉得她好像在前面游。 可接着他又听见身后扑通一声。

“Annie?” he called. “安妮？”他喊。

“What?” Her voice came from in front. Not behind. “干吗？”她的声音是从前面传来的。不是后面。

Another splash. Behind. 又是扑通一声。在后面。

Jack’s heart almost stopped. Crocodiles? He couldn’t see anything through his water-streaked glasses. 杰克的心几乎停了。鳄鱼？ 透过沾满水的眼镜，他什么也看不见。

“Annie!” he whispered. “安妮！”他小声喊。

“What?” “干吗？”

“Swim faster!”

“But I’m here! I’m over here! Near the edge!” she whispered. “可我就在这儿！我在这边！ 就在岸边！”她小声说。

Jack swam through the dark toward her voice. He imagined a crocodile slithering after him. 杰克朝着她的声音，在黑暗里游过去。 他想象着一条鳄鱼正从后面滑过来。

Another splash! Not far away! 又是扑通一声！就在不远处！

Jack’s hand touched a wet, live thing. 杰克的手碰到一个湿漉漉的、活的东西。

“Ahhhh! ” he cried. “啊啊啊！”他叫起来。

“It’s me! Take my hand!” said Annie. “是我！ 抓住我的手！”安妮说。

Jack grabbed her hand. She pulled him to the edge of the moat. They crawled over an embankment onto the wet grass. 杰克抓住她的手。 她把他拉到护城河边上。 他们爬过堤岸，爬上湿湿的草地。

Safe! 安全了！

Another splash came from the moat waters. 护城河里又传来扑通一声。

“Oh, man,” Jack said. “哇，天哪。”杰克说。

He was shivering all over. His teeth were chattering. He shook the water off his glasses and put them back on. 他浑身直打哆嗦。 牙齿也在打战。 他甩掉眼镜上的水，重新戴上。

It was so misty he couldn’t see the castle. He couldn’t even see the moat, much less a crocodile. 雾太大了，他看不见城堡。 连护城河都看不见，更别说鳄鱼了。

“We … we made it,” said Annie. Her teeth were chattering, too. “我……我们成功了。”安妮说。 她的牙齿也在打战。

“I know,” said Jack. “But where are we?” He peered at the foggy darkness. “我知道。”杰克说。 “可咱们在哪儿？”他朝雾蒙蒙的黑暗里张望。

Where was the drawbridge? The windmill? The hawk house? The grove of trees? The tree house? 吊桥呢？风车呢？ 鹰舍呢？ 小树林呢？树屋呢？

Everything had been swallowed up by the thick, soupy darkness. 所有东西都被浓得像糊糊一样的黑暗吞没了。

Jack reached into his wet backpack and pulled out the flashlight. He pushed the switch. No more light. 杰克伸进湿湿的背包，掏出手电筒。 他按了开关。灯不亮了。

They were trapped. Not in a dungeon. But in the still, cold darkness. 他们被困住了。不是困在地牢里。 而是困在静悄悄的、冷冰冰的黑暗里。

“Neeee-hhhh!”

A horse’s whinny. 一声马嘶。

Just then the clouds parted. A full moon was shining in the sky. A pool of light spread through the mist. 就在这时，云分开了。 一轮满月照在天上。 一片光在雾里铺开。

Then Jack and Annie saw him just a few feet away. The knight. 接着杰克和安妮看见他就在几步远的地方。那个骑士。

He sat on the black horse. His armor shone in the moonlight. A visor hid his face. But he seemed to be staring straight at Jack and Annie. 他坐在那匹黑马上。 他的盔甲在月光下发亮。 面甲遮住了他的脸。 可他好像正直直地盯着杰克和安妮。
