9. Under the Moon 9. 月光下

Jack froze. 杰克愣住了。

“It’s him,” Annie whispered. “是他。”安妮小声说。

The knight held out his gloved hand. 骑士伸出戴着手套的手。

“Come on, Jack,” Annie said. “来吧，杰克。”安妮说。

“Where are you going?” said Jack. “你去哪儿？”杰克说。

“He wants to help us,” said Annie. “他想帮咱们。”安妮说。

“How do you know?” “你怎么知道？”

“I can just tell,” said Annie. “我就是感觉得到。”安妮说。

Annie stepped toward the horse. The knight dismounted. 安妮朝那匹马走过去。 骑士下了马。

The knight picked Annie up and put her on the back of his horse. 骑士把安妮抱起来，放上马背。

“Come on, Jack,” she called. “来吧，杰克。”她喊。

Jack moved slowly toward the knight. It was like a dream. 杰克慢慢朝骑士走过去。 像在做梦一样。

The knight picked him up, too. He placed Jack on the horse, behind Annie. 骑士也把他抱了起来。 他把杰克放在马上，坐在安妮后面。

The knight got on behind them. He slapped the reins. 骑士自己坐到他们后面。 他拍了拍缰绳。

The black horse cantered beside the moonlit water of the moat. 黑马沿着月光下的护城河小跑起来。

Jack rocked back and forth in the saddle. The wind blew his hair. He felt very brave and very powerful. 杰克在马鞍上前后晃着。 风吹着他的头发。 他觉得自己好勇敢，好厉害。

He felt as if he could ride forever on this horse, with this mysterious knight. Over the ocean. Over the world. Over the moon. 他觉得自己可以永远骑着这匹马，和这个神秘的骑士一起。越过大海。 越过全世界。越过月亮。

A hawk shrieked in the darkness. 黑暗里，一只老鹰尖叫了一声。

“There’s the tree house,” said Annie. She pointed toward a grove of trees. “树屋在那儿。”安妮说。 她指向一片小树林。

The knight steered the horse toward the trees. 骑士把马转向那片树林。

“See. There it is,” Annie said, pointing to the ladder. “看见了吧。 就在那儿。”安妮指着梯子说。

The knight brought his horse to a stop. He dismounted and helped Annie down. 骑士把马停住。 他下了马，把安妮抱下来。

“Thank you, sir,” she said. And she bowed. “谢谢您，骑士大人。”她说。接着她鞠了一躬。

Then Jack. “Thank you,” he said. And he bowed also. 然后是杰克。 “谢谢您。”他说。 他也鞠了一躬。

The knight got back on his horse. He raised his gloved hand. Then he slapped the reins and rode off through the mist. 骑士重新上了马。 他举起戴着手套的手。 接着他拍了拍缰绳，骑着马消失在雾里。

Annie started up the tall ladder, and Jack followed. They climbed into the dark tree house and looked out the window. 安妮开始爬那架高高的梯子，杰克跟在后面。 他们爬进黑黑的树屋，朝窗外看。

The knight was riding toward the outer wall. They saw him go through the outer gate. 骑士正朝外墙骑去。 他们看见他穿过了外门。

Clouds started to cover the moon again. For a brief moment, Jack thought he saw the knight’s armor gleaming on the top of a hill beyond the castle. 云又开始把月亮遮住。 有那么一小会儿，杰克觉得自己看见骑士的盔甲在城堡外一座小山头上发亮。

The clouds covered the moon completely. And a black mist swallowed the land. 云把月亮完全遮住了。 黑色的雾吞没了整片土地。

“He’s gone,” whispered Annie. “他走了。”安妮小声说。

Jack shivered in his wet clothes as he kept staring at the blackness. 杰克穿着湿衣服直发抖，还盯着那片黑暗。

“I’m cold,” said Annie. “Where’s the Pennsylvania book?” “我好冷。”安妮说。 “宾夕法尼亚那本书呢？”

Jack heard Annie fumble in the darkness. He kept looking out the window. 杰克听见安妮在黑暗里摸来摸去。 他还在朝窗外看。

“I think this is it,” said Annie. “I feel a silk bookmark.” “我看就是这本。”安妮说。 “我摸到一根丝绸书签。”

Jack was only half-listening. He was hoping to see the knight’s armor gleam again in the distance. 杰克只听进去一半。 他还盼着再看见骑士的盔甲在远处发亮。

“Okay. I’m going to use this,” said Annie. “Because I think it’s the right one. Here goes. Okay. I’m pointing. I’m going to wish. I wish we could go to Frog Creek!” “好。 我要用这本。”安妮说。 “因为我觉得就是这本。开始了。 好。我指着了。 我要许愿了。 我希望我们能去蛙溪镇！”

Jack heard the wind begin to blow. Softly at first. 杰克听见风开始刮起来。起先轻轻的。

“I hope I pointed to the right picture in the right book,” said Annie. “希望我指的是对的书、对的图。”安妮说。

“What?” Jack looked back at her. “Right picture? Right book?” “什么？”杰克回头看她。 “对的图？对的书？”

The tree house began to rock. The wind got louder and louder. 树屋开始摇晃。 风越来越大。

“I hope it wasn’t the dinosaur book!” said Annie. “希望不是那本恐龙书！”安妮说。

“Stop!” Jack shouted at the tree house. “停下！”杰克朝树屋大喊。

Too late. 太晚了。

The tree house started to spin. It was spinning and spinning! 树屋开始旋转。 它转啊转啊！

The wind was screaming. 风在尖叫。

Then suddenly there was silence. 然后忽然静了下来。

Absolute silence. 一片寂静。
