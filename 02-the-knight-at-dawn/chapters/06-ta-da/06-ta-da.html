6. Ta-da! 6. 嗒哒！

In the torchlight, Jack saw three huge men standing over him. 火把光里，杰克看见三个大块头男人站在他头顶上。

One with very squinty eyes held the torch. One with a very red face held Jack. And one with a very long mustache held on to Annie. 一个眼睛眯成一条缝的人举着火把。 一个脸很红的人抓着杰克。 还有一个留着长长胡子的人抓着安妮。

Annie was kicking and yelling. 安妮又踢又叫。

“Stop!” said the one with the very long mustache. “停下！”长胡子那个人说。

“Who art thou?” said the one with the very red face. “汝是何人？”红脸那个人说。

“Spies? Foreigners? Egyptians? Romans? Persians?” said the squinty-eyed one. “间谍？外国人？ 埃及人？罗马人？ 波斯人？”眯眯眼那个人说。

“No, you dummies!” said Annie. “才不是，你们这帮笨蛋！”安妮说。

“Oh, brother,” Jack muttered. “真受不了。”杰克嘟囔着。

“Arrest them!” said Red-face. “把他们抓起来！”红脸说。

“The dungeon!” said Squinty-eyes. “关进地牢！”眯眯眼说。

The guards marched Jack and Annie out of the armory. Jack looked back frantically. Where was his backpack? 卫兵押着杰克和安妮走出了军械库。 杰克慌慌张张地回头看。 他的背包呢？

“Go!” said a guard, giving him a push. “走！”一个卫兵推了他一把。

Jack went. 杰克只好走。

Down they marched, down the long, dark hallway. Squinty, Annie, Mustache, Jack, and Red. 他们往下走，穿过长长的、黑黑的走廊。 眯眯眼、安妮、大胡子、杰克，还有红脸。

Down a narrow, winding staircase. 走下一截又窄又弯的楼梯。

Jack heard Annie shouting at the guards. “Dummies! Meanies! We didn’t do anything!” 杰克听见安妮冲卫兵大喊。 “笨蛋！坏蛋！ 我们什么都没做！”

The guards laughed. They didn’t take her seriously at all. 卫兵们笑了。 他们根本没把她当回事。

At the bottom of the stairs was a big iron door with a bar across it. 楼梯底下有一扇大铁门，门上横着一根门闩。

Squinty pushed the bar off the door. Then he shoved at the door. It creaked open. 眯眯眼把门闩推开。 接着他用力推门。门嘎吱一声开了。

Jack and Annie were pushed into a cold, clammy room. 杰克和安妮被推进一间又冷又潮的屋子。

The fiery torch lit the dungeon. There were chains hanging from the filthy walls. Water dripped from the ceiling, making puddles on the stone floor. It was the creepiest place Jack had ever seen. 火把照亮了地牢。 脏兮兮的墙上挂着铁链。 水从天花板滴下来，在石地板上积成小水洼。 这是杰克见过最吓人的地方。

“We’ll keep them here till the feast is done. Then turn them over to the Duke,” said Squinty. “He knows how to take care of thieves.” “先把他们关在这儿，等宴会结束。 然后再交给公爵。”眯眯眼说。 “他知道该怎么收拾小偷。”

“There will be a hanging tomorrow,” said Mustache. “明天就要绞死他们。”大胡子说。

“If the rats don’t get them first,” said Red. “要是老鼠不先把他们吃掉的话。”红脸说。

They all laughed. 他们都笑了。

Jack saw that Annie had his backpack. She was quietly unzipping it. 杰克看见安妮拿着他的背包。 她正悄悄拉开拉链。

“Come on, let’s chain the two of ’em,” said Squinty. “来，把这两个人锁上。”眯眯眼说。

The guards started toward them. Annie whipped her flashlight out of the pack. 卫兵朝他们走过来。 安妮猛地从背包里抽出手电筒。

“Ta-da!” she yelled. “嗒哒！”她大喊。

The guards froze. They stared at the shiny flashlight in her hand. 卫兵们愣住了。 他们盯着她手里亮晶晶的手电筒。

Annie switched the light on. The guards gasped in fear. They jumped back against the wall. 安妮打开了灯。 卫兵们吓得倒抽一口冷气。 他们往后一跳，撞到了墙上。

Squinty dropped the torch. It fell into a dirty puddle on the floor, sputtered, and went out. 眯眯眼把火把掉了。 火把掉进地上脏脏的水洼里，噼啪响了几下，灭了。

“My magic wand!” Annie said, waving the flashlight. “Get down. Or I’ll wipe you out!” “这是我的魔杖！”安妮挥着手电筒说。 “趴下。 不然我就把你们消灭掉！”

Jack’s mouth dropped open. 杰克惊得嘴巴都合不拢。

Annie fiercely pointed her light at one, then the other. Each howled and covered his face. 安妮狠狠地把灯光对准这个，又对准那个。 每个人都哇哇叫着，捂住自己的脸。

“Down! All of you! Get down!” shouted Annie. “趴下！你们全都趴下！ 趴下！”安妮喊道。

One by one, the guards lay down on the wet floor. 卫兵一个接一个地趴在湿漉漉的地上。

Jack couldn’t believe it. 杰克简直不敢相信。

“Come on,” Annie said to him. “Let’s go.” “快走。”安妮对他说。 “咱们走。”

Jack looked at the open doorway. He looked at the guards quaking on the ground. 杰克看了看开着的门口。 他又看了看地上抖成一团的卫兵。

“Hurry!” said Annie. “快点！”安妮说。

In one quick leap, Jack followed her out of the terrible dungeon. 杰克一下子跳起来，跟着她逃出了可怕的地牢。
