10. One Mystery Solved 10. 解开一个谜

The air was warm. 空气暖暖的。

It was dawn. Far away a dog barked. 天亮了。 远处有一只狗在叫。

“I think that’s Henry barking!” Annie said. “We did come home.” “我看那是亨利在叫！”安妮说。 “我们真的回家了。”

They both looked out the tree house window. 他们俩一起朝树屋窗外看。

“That was close,” said Jack. “好险。”杰克说。

In the distance, streetlights lit their street. There was a light on in their upstairs window. 远处，路灯照亮了他们的街道。 楼上他们家的窗户亮着灯。

“Uh-oh,” said Annie. “I think Mom and Dad are up. Hurry!” “糟了。”安妮说。 “我看爸爸妈妈已经起来了。快点！”

“Wait.” In a daze, Jack unzipped his backpack. He pulled out the castle book. It was quite wet. But Jack placed it back with all the other books. “等一下。”杰克恍恍惚惚地拉开背包拉链。 他抽出那本城堡书。书已经很湿了。 可杰克还是把它放回其他书中间。

“Come on! Hurry!” said Annie, scooting out of the tree house. “快点！ 快点！”安妮说着，钻出了树屋。

Jack followed her down the ladder. 杰克跟着她爬下梯子。

They reached the ground and took off between the gray-black trees. 他们一落地，就从灰黑色的树中间跑了出去。

They left the woods and ran down their deserted street. 他们跑出树林，沿着空荡荡的街道往前跑。

They got to their yard and crept across the lawn. Right up to the back door. 他们跑到自家院子，悄悄穿过草坪。 一直走到后门前。

Jack and Annie slipped inside the house. 杰克和安妮溜进了房子。

“They’re not downstairs yet,” whispered Annie. “他们还没下楼。”安妮小声说。

“Shhh,” said Jack. “嘘。”杰克说。

He led the way up the stairs and down the hall. No sign of his mom or dad. But he could hear water running in the bathroom. 他在前面带路，走上楼梯，走过走廊。 没看见爸爸妈妈的影子。 可他听见浴室里有水声。

Their house was so different from the dark, cold castle. So safe and cozy and friendly. 他们的家跟那座又黑又冷的城堡太不一样了。 又安全，又暖和，又亲切。

Annie stopped at her bedroom door. She gave Jack a smile, then disappeared inside her room. 安妮在自己卧室门口停住。 她朝杰克笑了笑，然后走进房间，不见了。

Jack hurried into his room. He took off his damp clothes and pulled on his dry, soft pajamas. 杰克赶紧进了自己的房间。 他脱掉湿衣服，换上干干软软的睡衣。

He sat down on his bed and unzipped his backpack. He took out his wet notebook. He felt around for the pencil, but his hand touched something else. 他坐到床上，拉开背包拉链。 他拿出湿湿的笔记本。 他摸着找铅笔，可手碰到了别的东西。

Jack pulled the blue leather bookmark out of his pack. It must have fallen out of the castle book. 杰克从背包里抽出那根蓝色皮书签。 它一定是从那本城堡书里掉出来的。

Jack held the bookmark close to his lamp and studied it. The leather was smooth and worn. It seemed ancient. 杰克把书签拿到台灯跟前，仔细看起来。 皮子又滑又旧。 看起来好古老。

For the first time Jack noticed a letter on the bookmark. A fancy M. 杰克第一次注意到书签上有一个字母。一个花体的M。

Jack opened the drawer next to his bed. He took out the gold medallion. 杰克打开床边的抽屉。 他拿出那枚金勋章。

He looked at the letter on it. It was the same M. 他看了看上面的字母。 也是一样的M。

Now this was an amazing new fact. 这可是一个了不起的新事实。

Jack took a deep breath. One mystery solved. 杰克深深吸了一口气。 一个谜解开了。

The person who had dropped the gold medallion in the time of the dinosaurs was the same person who owned all the books in the tree house. 在恐龙时代丢掉这枚金勋章的人，就是树屋里那些书的主人。

Who was this person? 这个人是谁呢？

Jack placed the bookmark next to the medallion. He closed the drawer. 杰克把书签放在勋章旁边。 他关上抽屉。

He picked up his pencil. He turned to the least wet page in his notebook. And he started to write down this new fact. 他拿起铅笔。 他翻到笔记本里最不湿的那一页。 然后开始记下这个新事实。

But before he could draw the M, his eyes closed. 可还没画出那个M，他的眼睛就闭上了。

He dreamed they were with the knight again. All three of them riding the black horse through the cool, dark night. Beyond the outer wall of the castle. And up over a moonlit hill. 他梦见他们又和那个骑士在一起。 三个人骑着黑马，穿过凉爽的黑夜。 越过城堡的外墙。 再爬上一座洒满月光的小山。

Into the mist. 钻进雾里。
