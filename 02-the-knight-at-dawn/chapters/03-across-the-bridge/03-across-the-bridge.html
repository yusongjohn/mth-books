3. Across the Bridge 3. 过桥

Annie was under the tree, looking across the foggy ground. 安妮在树下，望着雾蒙蒙的地面。

“The knight’s riding toward that bridge, I think,” said Annie. “The bridge goes to the castle.” “骑士好像正朝那座桥骑过去。”安妮说。 “那座桥通向城堡。”

“Wait. I’ll look it up,” said Jack. “Give me the flashlight!” “等一下。 我查查看。”杰克说。 “把手电给我！”

He took the flashlight from her and pulled the castle book out of his pack. He opened it to the page with the leather bookmark. 他从她手里拿过手电筒，从背包里抽出城堡书。 他翻到夹着皮书签的那一页。

He read the words under the picture of the knight: 他读了骑士图画下面的字：

This is a knight arriving for a castle feast. Knights wore armor when they traveled long and dangerous distances. The armor was very heavy. A helmet alone could weigh up to forty pounds. 这是一个来参加城堡宴会的骑士。骑士在长途旅行时会穿上盔甲，因为旅途又长又危险。盔甲非常重，一个头盔就能重达四十磅。

Wow. Jack had weighed forty pounds when he was five years old. So it’d be like riding a horse with a five-year-old on your head. 哇。 杰克五岁的时候才四十磅重。 那就好像骑马时，头上还坐着一个五岁的小孩。

Jack pulled out his notebook. He wanted to take notes, as he’d done on their dinosaur trip. 杰克拿出笔记本。 他想像上次恐龙之旅那样做笔记。

He wrote: 他写道：

图中文字：heavy head 图中文字：头痛

What else? 还有什么？

He turned the pages of the castle book. He found a picture that showed the whole castle and the buildings around it. 他翻着城堡书。 他找到一幅图画，画着整座城堡和周围的房子。

“The knight’s crossing the bridge,” said Annie. “He’s going through the gate .… He’s gone.” “骑士在过桥了。”安妮说。 “他正走进大门……他进去了。”

Jack studied the bridge in the picture. 杰克仔细看图里的桥。

He read: 他读道：

A drawbridge crossed the moat. The moat was filled with water, to help protect the castle from enemies. Some people believe crocodiles were kept in the moat. 一座吊桥横跨护城河。护城河里装满了水，用来帮助城堡抵御敌人。有些人相信护城河里养了鳄鱼。

Jack wrote in his notebook: 杰克在笔记本上写道：

图中文字：crocodiles in moat? 图中文字：护城河里有鳄鱼吗？

“Look!” said Annie, peering through the mist. “A windmill! Right over there!” “看！”安妮说着，朝雾里张望。 “一座风车！ 就在那边！”



“Yeah, there’s a windmill in here, too,” said Jack, pointing at the picture. “对，书里也有一座风车。”杰克指着图画说。

“Look at the real one, Jack,” said Annie. “Not the one in the book.” “看真的那座，杰克。”安妮说。 “别看书里的。”

A piercing shriek split the air.

“Yikes,” said Annie. “It sounded like it came from that little house over there!” She pointed through the fog. “哎呀。”安妮说。 “声音好像是从那边那座小房子里传来的！”她朝雾里指着。

“There’s a little house here, ” said Jack, studying the picture. He turned the page and read: “这儿也有一座小房子。”杰克看着图画说。 他翻过一页，读道：

The hawk house was in the inner ward of the castle. Hawks were trained to hunt other birds and small animals. 鹰舍在城堡的内院里。人们训练鹰去捕捉其他鸟和小动物。

Jack wrote in his notebook: 杰克在笔记本上写道：

图中文字：hawks in hawk house 图中文字：Hawk House 的鹰

“We must be in the inner ward,” said Jack. “我们一定是在内院里。”杰克说。

“Listen!” whispered Annie. “You hear that? Drums! Horns! They’re coming from the castle. Let’s go see.” “听！”安妮小声说。 “你听见了吗？鼓声！ 号角！ 是从城堡里传来的。我们去看看。”

“Wait,” said Jack. He turned more pages of the book. “等一下。”杰克说。 他又翻了几页书。

“I want to see what’s really going on, Jack. Not what’s in the book,” said Annie. “我想看真正正在发生的事，杰克。 不是书里写的。”安妮说。

“But look at this!” said Jack. “可是你看这个！”杰克说。

He pointed to a picture of a big party. Men were standing by the door, playing drums and horns. 他指着一幅盛大宴会的图画。 男人们站在门边，敲鼓、吹号。

He read: 他读道：

Fanfares were played to announce different dishes in a feast. Feasts were held in the Great Hall. 用号角宣布宴会的不同菜肴。宴会是在大厅里举行的。

“You can look at the book. I’m going to the real feast,” said Annie. “你可以看书。 我要去看真正的宴会。”安妮说。

“Wait,” said Jack, studying the picture. It showed boys his age carrying trays of food. Whole pigs. Pies. Peacocks with all their feathers. Peacocks? “等一下。”杰克说着，还在看那幅图。 图上有和他差不多大的男孩，端着一盘盘食物。整头猪。 馅饼。 还带着羽毛的孔雀。孔雀？

Jack wrote: 杰克写道：

图中文字：they eat peacocks? 图中文字：他们吃孔雀？

He held up the book to show Annie. “Look, I think they eat—” 他把书举起来给安妮看。 “看，我觉得他们吃——”

Where was she? Gone. Again. 她人呢？不见了。 又跑了。

Jack looked through the fog. 杰克朝雾里看。

He heard the real drums and the real horns. He saw the real hawk house, the real windmill, the real moat. 他听见了真正的鼓声和号角。 他看见了真正的鹰舍、真正的风车、真正的护城河。

He saw Annie dashing across the real drawbridge. Then she vanished through the gate leading to the castle. 他看见安妮冲过真正的吊桥。 接着她钻进城堡的大门，不见了。 4. 进入城堡
