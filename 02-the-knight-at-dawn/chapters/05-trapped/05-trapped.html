5. Trapped 5. 被困住了

“Come on!” cried Annie. “快来！”安妮喊。

Jack raced behind her. 杰克在后面猛跑。

Were they being followed? 有人在追他们吗？

“Here! Quick!” Annie dashed toward a door off the hallway. She pushed the door open. The two of them stumbled into a dark, cold room. The door creaked shut behind them. “这儿！ 快点！”安妮朝走廊边的一扇门冲过去。 她推开门。 两人踉踉跄跄进了一间又黑又冷的屋子。 门在他们身后嘎吱一声关上。

“Give me the flashlight,” said Annie. Jack handed it to her, and she switched it on. “把手电给我。”安妮说。 杰克递给她，她打开了。

Yikes! A row of knights right in front of them! 呀！ 一排骑士就在他们面前！



Annie flicked off the light. 安妮啪地关了灯。

Silence. 一片安静。

“They aren’t moving,” Jack whispered. “他们没动。”杰克小声说。

Annie turned the light back on. 安妮又把灯打开。

“They’re just suits,” Jack said. “那只是盔甲。”杰克说。

“Without heads,” said Annie. “还没有头。”安妮说。

“Let me have the flashlight a second,” said Jack. “So I can look in the book.” “把手电给我用一下。”杰克说。 “我要查查书。”

Annie handed him the flashlight. He pulled out the castle book. He flipped through the pages until he found what he was looking for. 安妮把手电筒递给他。 他抽出城堡书。 他一页页翻，直到找到要找的内容。

Jack put the book away. “It’s called the armory,” he said. “It’s where armor and weapons are stored.” 杰克把书收好。 “这叫军械库。”他说。 “是存放盔甲和兵器的地方。”

He shined the flashlight around the room. 他把手电筒照向屋子四周。

“Oh, man,” whispered Jack. “哇，天哪。”杰克小声说。

The light fell on shiny breastplates, leg plates, arm plates. On shelves filled with helmets and weapons. On shields, spears, swords, crossbows, clubs, battle-axes. 灯光照到亮闪闪的胸甲、腿甲、臂甲。 照到摆满头盔和兵器的架子。 还有盾牌、长矛、剑、弩、棍棒、战斧。

There was a noise in the hall. Voices! 走廊里有动静。有人说话！

“Let’s hide!” said Annie. “我们躲起来！”安妮说。

“Wait,” said Jack. “I’ve got to check on something first.” “等一下。”杰克说。 “我得先确认一件事。”

“Hurry,” said Annie. “快点。”安妮说。

“It’ll take just a second,” said Jack. “Hold this.” He handed Annie the flashlight. “只要一会儿。”杰克说。 “拿着这个。”他把手电筒递给安妮。

He tried to lift a helmet from a shelf. It was too heavy. 他想从架子上举起一顶头盔。太沉了。

He bent over and dragged the helmet over his head. The visor slammed shut. 他弯下腰，把头盔拖到自己头上。 面甲啪地关上了。

Oh, forget it. It was worse than having a five-year-old on your head. More like having a ten-year-old on your head. 唉，算了吧。 这比头上坐着一个五岁小孩还糟糕。 更像头上坐着一个十岁小孩。

Not only could Jack not lift his head, he couldn’t see anything, either. 杰克不但抬不起头，还什么都看不见。

“Jack!” Annie’s voice sounded far away. “The voices are getting closer!” “杰克！”安妮的声音听起来好远。 “说话声越来越近了！”

“Turn off the flashlight!” Jack’s voice echoed inside the metal chamber. “把手电关掉！”杰克的声音在铁头盔里嗡嗡回响。

He struggled to get the helmet off. 他拼命想把头盔摘下来。

Suddenly he lost his balance and went crashing into other pieces of armor. 忽然他失去平衡，撞到了别的盔甲上。

The metal plates and weapons clattered to the floor. 金属甲片和兵器稀里哗啦掉到地上。

Jack lay on the floor in the dark. 杰克躺在黑暗的地板上。

He tried to get up. But his head was too heavy. 他想站起来。 可是头太沉了。

He heard deep voices. 他听见低沉的说话声。

Someone grabbed him by the arm. The next thing he knew, his helmet was yanked off. He was staring into the blazing light of a fiery torch. 有人抓住了他的胳膊。 接下来，他的头盔被人猛地拽了下来。 他眼前是一把火把，火光亮得刺眼。
