7. A Secret Passage 7. 秘密通道

Annie and Jack raced back up the winding stairs and down the long hallway. 安妮和杰克飞快跑上弯弯曲曲的楼梯，再冲进长长的走廊。

They hadn’t gone far when they heard shouting behind them. 他们没跑多远，就听见后面有人喊。

Dogs barked in the distance. 远处有狗在叫。

“They’re coming!” Annie cried. “他们追来了！”安妮喊道。

“In here!” said Jack. He shoved open a door off the hallway and pulled Annie into a dark room. “进这儿！”杰克说。 他推开走廊边上的一扇门，把安妮拉进一间黑屋子。

Jack pushed the door shut. Then Annie shined her flashlight around the room. There were rows of sacks and wooden barrels. 杰克把门推上。 接着安妮用手电照了照屋子。 里面一排排都是袋子和木桶。

“I’d better look in the book,” said Jack. “Give it to me!” “我最好先查查书。”杰克说。 “把它给我！”

Annie gave him the flashlight and his backpack. He pulled out the book and started tearing through it. 安妮把手电筒和他的背包递给他。 他抽出那本书，飞快地翻了起来。

“Shhh!” said Annie. “Someone’s coming.” “嘘！”安妮说。 “有人来了。”

Jack and Annie jumped behind the door as it creaked open. 门嘎吱一声开了，杰克和安妮赶紧躲到门后。

Jack held his breath. A light from a torch danced wildly over the sacks and barrels. 杰克屏住呼吸。 火把的光在袋子和木桶上乱晃。

The light disappeared. The door slammed shut. 光消失了。 门砰地关上了。

“Oh, man,” whispered Jack. “We have to hurry. They might come back.” “哇，天哪。”杰克小声说。 “咱们得快点。 他们说不定还会回来。”

His hands were trembling as he flipped through the pages of the castle book. 他翻着那本城堡书，手直发抖。

“Here’s a map of the castle,” he said. “Look, this must be the room we’re in. It’s a storeroom.” Jack studied the room in the book. “These are sacks of flour and barrels of wine.” “这儿有一张城堡地图。”他说。 “看，咱们一定就在这间屋子里。 这是储藏室。”杰克仔细看了看书上的那间屋子。 “这些是面粉袋和葡萄酒桶。”

“Who cares? We have to go!” said Annie. “Before they come back!” “谁管这个？ 咱们得走了！”安妮说。 “趁他们还没回来！”

“No. Look,” said Jack. He pointed at the map. “Here’s a trapdoor.” “别急。看。”杰克说。 他指着地图。 “这儿有一扇活板门。”

He read aloud: 他大声读了出来：

This door leads from the storeroom through a secret passage to a precipice over the moat. 这扇门从储藏室通过一条秘密通道通向护城河上方的悬崖。

“What’s a precipice?” said Annie. “悬崖是什么？”安妮说。

“I don’t know. We’ll find out,” said Jack. “But first we have to find the door.” “我不知道。 咱们到了就知道了。”杰克说。 “不过得先找到那扇门。”

Jack looked at the picture carefully. Then he shined the flashlight around the room. 杰克仔细看了看图。 然后他用手电照了照屋子。

The floor of the room was made up of stones. The trapdoor in the picture was five stones away from the door to the hall. 屋子的地板是一块块石头铺成的。 图上的活板门离通向走廊的门隔着五块石头。

Jack shined the light on the floor and counted the stones. “One, two, three, four, five.” 杰克把手电照向地面，数起石头来。 “一、二、三、四、五。”

He stamped on the fifth stone. It was loose! 他在第五块石头上跺了跺脚。它是松的！

He put the flashlight on the floor. He worked his fingers under the thin sheet of stone and tried to lift it. 他把手电筒放在地上。 他把手指伸进那块薄薄的石板底下，试着把它抬起来。

“Help,” Jack said. “帮帮忙。”杰克说。

Annie came over and helped him lift the stone square out of its place. Underneath was a small wooden door. 安妮走过来，帮他把那块方石板抬出来。 底下是一扇小小的木门。

Jack and Annie tugged on the rope handle of the door. The door fell open with a thunk. 杰克和安妮拽着门上的绳子把手。 门咚的一声翻开了。

Jack picked up the flashlight and shined it on the hole. 杰克拿起手电筒，照向那个洞。

“There’s a little ladder,” he said. “Let’s go!” “这儿有一小截梯子。”他说。 “走吧！”

He clipped on the flashlight and felt his way down the small ladder. Annie followed. 他把手电筒夹好，摸索着走下小梯子。安妮跟在后面。

When they both reached the bottom of the ladder, Jack shined the light around them. 两个人都下到梯子底，杰克用手电四周照了照。

There was a tunnel! 有一条地道！

He crouched down and began moving through the damp, creepy tunnel. The flashlight barely lit the stone walls. 他蹲下身子，钻进又潮又吓人的地道。 手电筒几乎照不亮石墙。

He shook the light. Were the batteries running down? 他晃了晃手电。 电池是不是快没电了？

“I think our light’s dying!” he said to Annie. “我看咱们的灯快灭了！”他对安妮说。

“Hurry!” she called from behind. “快点！”她在后面喊。

Jack went faster. His back hurt from crouching. 杰克走得更快了。 蹲着走，他的背好疼。

The light got dimmer and dimmer. 灯光越来越暗。

He was desperate to get out of the castle before the batteries died completely. 他拼命想在电池彻底没电前逃出城堡。

Soon he reached another small wooden door. The door at the end of the tunnel! 很快，他走到了另一扇小木门前。 地道尽头的那扇门！

Jack unlatched the door and pushed it open. 杰克拉开门闩，把门推开。

He poked his head outside. 他把头探到外面。

He couldn’t see anything in the misty darkness. 雾蒙蒙的黑暗里，他什么也看不见。

The air felt good. Cool and fresh. He took a deep breath. 空气真舒服。又凉又新鲜。 他深深吸了一口气。

“Where are we?” whispered Annie behind him. “What do you see?” “咱们在哪儿？”安妮在他身后小声问。 “你看见什么了？”

“Nothing. But I think we’ve come to the outside of the castle,” said Jack. “I’ll find out.” “什么也没有。 不过我想咱们已经到城堡外面了。”杰克说。 “我去看看。”

Jack put the flashlight in his pack. He put the pack on his back. He stuck his hand out the door. He couldn’t feel the ground. Just air. 杰克把手电筒放进背包。 他把背包背好。 他把手伸出门外。 他摸不到地面。只有空气。

“I’m going to have to go feet first,” he said. “我得脚先出去。”他说。

Jack turned around in the small tunnel. He lay down on his stomach. He stuck one leg out the door. Then the other. 杰克在窄窄的地道里转过身。 他趴了下来。 他把一条腿伸出门外。接着伸出另一条。

Jack inched down, bit by bit. Until he was hanging out the door, clinging to the ledge. 杰克一点点往下挪。 直到整个人挂在门外，紧紧抓着石沿。

“This must be the precipice!” he called to Annie. “Pull me up!” “这一定就是悬崖了！”他朝安妮喊。 “把我拉上去！”

Annie reached for Jack’s hands. “I can’t hold you!” she said. 安妮伸手去抓杰克的手。 “我抓不住你！”她说。

Jack felt his fingers slipping. Then down he fell. 杰克觉得手指在往下滑。 接着他就掉了下去。

Down through the darkness. 掉进黑暗里。

SPLASH! 扑通！
