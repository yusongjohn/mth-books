4. Into the Castle 4. 进入城堡

“I’m going to kill her,” muttered Jack. “我真想掐死她。”杰克嘟囔。

He threw his stuff into his pack and moved toward the drawbridge. He hoped no one would see him. 他把东西扔进背包，朝吊桥走去。 他希望没人看见自己。

It was getting darker. It must be night. 天越来越黑。一定是晚上了。

When he got to the bridge, he started across. The wooden planks creaked under his feet. 到了桥边，他开始过桥。 木板在他脚下嘎吱作响。

He peered over the edge of the bridge. Were there any crocodiles in the moat? He couldn’t tell. 他朝桥边往下看。 护城河里有鳄鱼吗？他看不清。

“Halt!” someone shouted. A guard on top of the castle wall was looking down. “站住！”有人喊。 城堡墙头上，一个卫兵正往下看。

Jack dashed across the bridge. He ran through the castle gate and into the courtyard. 杰克冲过桥。 他跑进城堡大门，进了院子。

From inside the castle came the sounds of music, shouting, and laughter. 城堡里面传来音乐、喊叫和笑声。

Jack hurried to a dark corner and crouched down. He shivered as he looked around for Annie. 杰克赶紧跑到一个黑暗的角落蹲下来。 他一边发抖，一边四处找安妮。

Torches lit the high wall around the courtyard. The courtyard was nearly empty. 火把照亮了院子四周高高的墙。 院子里几乎没人。

Two boys led horses that clopped over the gray cobblestones. 两个男孩牵着马，马蹄在灰色的石板路上嘚嘚地响。

“Neeee-hhhh!”

Jack turned. It was the knight’s black horse! 杰克转过身。 那是骑士的黑马！

“Psssst!”

He peered into the darkness. 他朝黑暗里看。

There was Annie. 安妮在那儿。

She was hiding behind a well in the center of the courtyard. She waved at him. 她躲在院子中央一口井的后面。她朝他招手。

Jack waved back. He waited till the boys and horses disappeared inside the stable. Then he dashed to the well. 杰克也朝她招手。 他等到男孩和马都进了马厩，不见了。 然后冲到井边。

“I’m going to find the music!” whispered Annie. “Are you coming?” “我要去找那音乐！”安妮小声说。 “你来吗？”

“Okay,” Jack said with a sigh. “好吧。”杰克叹着气说。

They tiptoed together across the cobblestones. Then they slipped into the entrance of the castle. 他们一起踮着脚，走过石板路。 然后溜进城堡门口。

Noise and music came from a bright room in front of them. They stood on one side of the doorway and peeked in. 前面一间明亮的屋子里传来喧闹和音乐。 他们站在门口一侧，往里偷看。

“The feast in the Great Hall!” whispered Jack. He held his breath as he stared in awe. “大厅里的宴会！”杰克小声说。 他屏住呼吸，看得入了迷。

A giant fireplace blazed at one end of the noisy room. Antlers and rugs hung on the stone walls. Flowers covered the floor. Boys in short dresses carried huge trays of food. 吵闹的大厅一头，壁炉里火烧得旺旺的。 石墙上挂着鹿角和地毯。 地上铺满了花。 穿着短袍的男孩端着巨大的食物托盘。

Dogs were fighting over bones under the tables. 桌子底下，狗在抢骨头。

People in bright clothes and funny hats strolled among the crowd. Some played funny-shaped guitars. Some tossed balls in the air. Some balanced swords on their hands. 穿着鲜艳衣服、戴着滑稽帽子的人在人群里走来走去。 有的弹着奇形怪状的吉他。 有的把球抛到空中。 有的把剑立在手上保持平衡。

Men and women dressed in capes and furs sat at long, crowded wooden tables. 穿着披风、皮毛的男男女女，坐在又长又挤的木桌旁。

“I wonder which one is the knight,” said Jack. “不知道哪个才是那个骑士。”杰克说。

“I don’t know,” whispered Annie. “But they’re eating with their fingers.” “我不知道。”安妮小声说。 “不过他们在用手抓着吃。”

Suddenly, someone shouted behind them. 忽然，身后有人喊了一声。

Jack whirled around. 杰克猛地转过身。

A man carrying a tray of pies was standing a few feet away. 一个端着一盘馅饼的男人，就站在几步远的地方。

“Who art thou?” he asked angrily. “汝是何人？”他生气地问。

“Jack,” squeaked Jack. “杰克。”杰克尖声说。

“Annie,” squeaked Annie. “安妮。”安妮尖声说。

Then they ran as fast as they could down a dimly lit hallway. 然后他们拼命顺着一条灯光昏暗的走廊跑。
