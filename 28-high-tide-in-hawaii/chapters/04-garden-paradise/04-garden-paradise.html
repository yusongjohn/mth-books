4. Garden Paradise 4. 花园天堂

Jack heard pounding noises. He imagined Boka and Kama were building a ship. 杰克听见了敲打的声音。 他想象波卡和卡玛正在造船。

He opened his eyes. Only he and Annie were still in the hut. A piece of cloth covered the doorway. Jack sat up and shook Annie. 他睁开眼睛。 小屋里只剩他和安妮。 一块布遮住了门口。 杰克坐起来，摇晃安妮。

“Wake up!” he said. “醒醒！”他说。

She opened her eyes. 她睁开眼睛。

“I think they’re building a ship outside,” said Jack. “Come on, let’s go.” “我觉得他们在外面造船，”杰克说。 “来吧，我们去看看。”

Annie jumped up. 安妮跳了起来。

“Don’t forget your lei,” she said. “别忘了你的花环，”她说。

They put on their flower wreaths. Jack lifted the cloth over the doorway, and they stepped out into the warm sunlight. 他们戴上花环。 杰克掀起门口的布帘，他们走进温暖的阳光里。

Boka, Kama, and their parents smiled at Jack and Annie. They were all working. But no one was building a ship. 波卡、卡玛和他们的父母对杰克和安妮微笑。 他们都在干活。 可没有人在造船。

Boka was pounding a wide strip of bark with a wooden club. Kama was using a stone to pound something that looked like a fat sweet potato. Their parents were weaving grass mats. 波卡正用木棒敲打一条宽宽的树皮。 卡玛正用石头砸着一样像胖红薯的东西。 他们的父母正在编织草垫。

“What are you making?” Jack asked. “你们在做什么？”杰克问。

“I’m making tapa,” said Boka. “First I beat the bark of the mulberry tree into thin sheets. Then my father pastes the sheets together to make cloth for us.” “我在做树皮布，”波卡说。 “首先，我把桑树的树皮敲成薄片。 然后我父亲把薄片粘在一起，给我们做布。”

“This is the root of a taro plant,” said Kama. She pointed at the squashed white vegetable. “When you add fruit to it, you get poi.” “这是芋头的根，”卡玛说。 她指着捣烂的白色蔬菜。 “加上水果，就成了芋泥。”

“Great,” said Jack. “By the way, do you ever build ships?” “很好，”杰克说。 “顺便问一下，你们造过船吗？”

“Ships?” asked Boka. “What for?” “船？”波卡问。 “做什么用？”

Jack shrugged. “To sail away?” he said. 杰克耸耸肩。 “去航海离开吗？”他说。

“Why would we do that?” asked Kama. “我们为什么要那样做？”卡玛问。

“Good question,” said Jack, smiling. “好问题，”杰克微笑着说。

“Can I help?” Annie asked Kama. “我能帮忙吗？”安妮问卡玛。

“Sure,” said Kama. While she showed Annie how to pound the taro root, Jack slipped back into the hut. He pulled out his notebook and quickly added to his Hawaii list: “当然，”卡玛说。 她教安妮怎么捣芋头根时，杰克溜回小屋。 他抽出笔记本，很快在夏威夷清单上补充：

图中文字：tapa-bark pounded into cloth

taro root-pounded for poi

ship-? 图中文字：用布包裹着捣碎的木薯根，用来做波伊船？

Jack heard Kama ask her parents if they could play now. 杰克听见卡玛问父母现在能不能去玩。

“We’ve finished our chores,” said Kama. “May we take Jack and Annie to the ocean?” “我们的家务做完了，”卡玛说。 “我们可以带杰克和安妮去海边吗？”

“For wave riding,” said Boka. “去驾浪，”波卡说。

Jack held his breath. He half hoped their parents would say no. 杰克屏住呼吸。 他真有点希望父母说不。

“Yes, go have a good time with your friends,” said their father. “好，去和朋友们好好玩吧，”他们的爸爸说。

“Come on, Jack!” Annie called. “来呀，杰克！”安妮喊道。

Jack put his notebook away. He pulled on his pack and joined the others outside. 杰克收起笔记本。 他背上背包，到外面和大家会合。

“We’ll be back in a little while,” said Kama. “我们一会儿就回来，”卡玛说。

“Don’t forget to eat breakfast!” said her mother. “别忘了吃早饭！”她妈妈说。

“We won’t,” said Kama. “不会忘的，”卡玛说。

Where will we get breakfast? wondered Jack. 我们去哪儿吃早饭？杰克想。

He and Annie followed Kama and Boka. They passed villagers hard at work. Some carried firewood or water. Others were cutting grass or stripping bark from trees. Everyone smiled and waved. 他和安妮跟着卡玛和波卡。 他们走过正在忙着干活的村民。 有人扛着柴火或水。 有人在割草或剥树皮。 大家都微笑着挥手。

“Hungry?” Kama asked Jack and Annie. “饿了吗？”卡玛问杰克和安妮。

“Sure,” they said. “饿，”他们说。

Kama and Boka went into the grove of palms near the huts. They climbed up two slanting tree trunks, using their hands and feet to push themselves up. At the top, they shook the palm leaves. 卡玛和波卡走进小屋附近的棕榈树丛。 他们手脚并用，爬上两根倾斜的树干。 到了顶上，他们摇晃棕榈叶。

“Watch out!” Kama shouted. “小心！”卡玛喊道。

Jack and Annie jumped back as big, round coconuts fell to the ground. 杰克和安妮往后跳开，又大又圆的椰子掉到地上。

Kama and Boka slid down the trees. They each picked up a coconut. Then they found rocks and began to whack the hard shells. They whacked and whacked until their coconuts cracked into halves. 卡玛和波卡滑下树来。 他们每人拿起一个椰子。 然后他们找来石头，开始猛敲硬壳。 他们敲啊敲，直到椰子裂成两半。

Kama shared hers with Annie. Boka shared his with Jack. 卡玛和安妮分着吃她的。 波卡和杰克分着吃他的。

Jack drank the fresh, sweet milk inside the coconut. “Mmm!” he said. 杰克喝着椰子里新鲜香甜的汁。 “嗯！”他说。

“Interesting?” asked Boka. “有意思？”波卡问。

“No. Mmm means good!” said Jack. “不。 嗯的意思是好吃！”杰克说。

Everyone laughed. 大家都笑了。

Then Kama picked bananas off a banana tree and gave them to Jack and Annie. Jack peeled his and took a bite. It was the best banana he’d ever eaten. 然后卡玛从香蕉树上摘下香蕉，递给杰克和安妮。 杰克剥开自己的，咬了一口。 那是他吃过最好吃的香蕉。

When breakfast was over, they all headed into the flowery meadow. The sky was the bluest blue Jack had ever seen. The grass was the greenest green. The flowers and birds sparkled like jewels. 早饭结束后，他们一起走进开满花的草地。 天空是杰克见过最蓝的蓝。 草是最绿的绿。 花和鸟像宝石一样闪闪发光。

Hawaii is like a garden paradise, Jack thought. 夏威夷真像一座花园天堂，杰克想。

He wanted to look up Hawaiian birds and flowers in the research book. As the others kept walking, he stopped and pulled out the book. 他想在研究书里查查夏威夷的鸟和花。 其他人继续往前走时，他停下，抽出书。

“Jack! Come look!” Annie shouted. She was standing at the edge of a cliff with Boka and Kama. “杰克！ 快来看！”安妮喊道。 她正和波卡、卡玛站在悬崖边。

Jack put away the book and hurried to join the others. He looked down at a beach fifty feet below. 杰克收起书，赶紧和大家会合。 他向下望去，五十英尺下是一片海滩。

There were no people. Only seashells and seaweed lay on the glistening white sand. Big, foamy waves crashed against the shore. 那儿没有人。 闪闪发亮的白沙上，只有贝壳和海藻。 又大又起泡沫的浪拍打着海岸。

“Wow!” said Annie. “哇！”安妮说。

Uh-oh, thought Jack. 糟了，杰克想。
