6. Shake-up 6. 震动

“Jack!” cried Annie. She ran to him. “Are you okay?” “杰克！”安妮喊着向他跑来。 “你没事吧？”

Jack just nodded. He put on his wet glasses. He felt shaky and mad at himself. I never should have tried to stand! he thought. 杰克只是点点头。 他戴上湿眼镜。 他觉得自己直发抖，还生自己的气。 我不该试着站起来的！他想。

Kama picked up Jack’s surfboard from the shallow water and brought it over to him. 卡玛从浅水里捞起杰克的冲浪板，拿给他。

“I told you not to stand,” she said, laughing. “You fell hard.” “我告诉过你别站起来，”她笑着说。 “你摔得好重。”

It’s not funny, thought Jack. I nearly drowned! 这一点都不好笑，杰克想。我差点淹死！

“The best thing to do is to go right back out,” said Boka. “最好的办法是马上再出去，”波卡说。

“You go,” said Jack. His eyes and nose burned from the salt water. “I’ll stay here.” He walked over to his pack, picked it up, and took out the research book. “你们去吧，”杰克说。 他的眼睛和鼻子被盐水刺得发疼。 “我待在这儿。”他走到背包旁，拿起来，取出研究书。

“Come on, Jack!” said Annie. “Try it again! Stay on your belly this time!” “来呀，杰克！”安妮说。 “再试一次！ 这次趴着就行！”

“No, this time I’m going to read about surfing first,” he said. “不，这次我要先读关于冲浪的内容，”他说。

“Aw, you should just try it again,” said Annie. “Not read about it!” “哎呀，你就该再试一次，”安妮说。 “别光读！”

She ran to him and pulled the book out of his hands. Jack jerked it away from her. He slipped and fell onto the sand. 她跑到他身边，从他手里把书抽走。 杰克猛地把它从她手里夺了过来。 他脚下一滑，摔倒在沙子上。

Kama and Boka laughed again. 卡玛和波卡又笑了。

“Why are you laughing?” Jack snapped. “You don’t even know how to read!” “你们笑什么？”杰克厉声说。 “你们甚至不会阅读！”

Boka and Kama looked hurt. 波卡和卡玛看起来很受伤。

“Jack!” said Annie. “That was mean. Say you’re sorry.” “杰克！”安妮说。 “那样太刻薄了。 快说对不起。”

Jack opened his book and pretended to read it. He did feel sorry, but he was too upset to say so. 杰克打开书，假装在读。 他确实觉得抱歉，可他太烦躁了，说不出口。

“Fine, stay here,” said Annie. She went back to Boka and Kama. “Let’s go.” “好吧，那就待在这儿，”安妮说。 她回到波卡和卡玛身边。 “走吧。”

As Jack sat alone on the beach, he looked up from his book. He watched the other kids paddling through the water. 杰克独自坐在沙滩上，从书上抬起头。 他看着其他孩子在水里划。

“I don’t care,” he muttered. “I’m never going back out in those waves.” “我不在乎，”他嘀咕道。 “我再也不回那些浪里去了。”

Morgan didn’t send us here to surf anyway, he thought. She told us to build a ship. But how the heck are we supposed to do that? 反正摩根也不是派我们来冲浪的，他想。 她让我们造一艘船。 可我们到底该怎么做呢？

Jack heaved an angry sigh. Now he was cross with Morgan. He turned to the back of the book and searched the index for “ship.” 杰克愤怒地叹了口气。 现在他对摩根生气了。 他翻到书的后面，在索引里查找“船”。

Suddenly Jack heard a rumbling from under the sand. The ground started to shake. It shook so hard, the book flew out of Jack’s hands! 忽然杰克听见沙子下面传来隆隆声。 地面开始震动。 震得太厉害，书从杰克手里飞了出去！

Jack bounced up and down on the beach. Shells were jumping up and down, too. Rocks tumbled down from the cliff. 杰克在沙滩上颠上颠下。 贝壳也上下跳着。 岩石从悬崖上滚落。

It’s an earthquake! thought Jack. 地震了！杰克想。

The rumbling stopped. 隆隆声停了。

The shaking stopped. 震动停了。

Jack looked around. Everything was normal again, except some rocks rolled around at the bottom of the cliff. 杰克环顾四周。 一切又恢复正常了，只是悬崖底下有些石头滚来滚去。

Jack looked out to sea. Kama, Boka, and Annie were past the breakers. They were sitting on their surfboards, laughing and talking. 杰克向海上望去。 卡玛、波卡和安妮已经过了碎浪带。 他们坐在冲浪板上，又笑又聊。

Everything seemed okay. But Jack felt sure that something was wrong. He grabbed the Hawaii book from the sand. He looked up “earthquake.” He read: 一切似乎都没事。 可杰克确信有什么不对劲。 他从沙子上抓起夏威夷那本书。 他查找“地震”。他读道：

Earthquakes in Hawaii have been known to cause tsunamis (soo-NAH-meez), which used to be called “tidal waves.” An earthquake can cause water out at sea to be set in motion. The water grows higher and higher as it moves toward land. Just before the tsunami strikes, water may pull away from the shore. Then it returns in a gigantic wave that crashes over the land and washes everything away. 夏威夷的地震有时会引起海啸（soo-NAH-meez），以前人们称之为“潮汐波”。地震会让海水开始运动，海水越靠近陆地就越高。海啸来临前，海水可能会退向海里，然后以巨大的波浪冲上海岸，把一切冲走。

Oh, man! thought Jack. A tsunami might be coming! 天啊！杰克想。 海啸可能要来了！
