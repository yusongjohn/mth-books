7. Ride for Your Lives! 7. 为了你们的生命，快跑！

Jack had to find out more about tsunamis quickly. He read as fast as he could: 杰克必须赶快了解海啸的知识。 他尽可能快地读起来：

A tsunami can strike a few hours—or a few minutes!—after an earthquake. It depends on the strength of the earthquake and where it took place. After earthquakes, it is safest for islanders to seek higher ground. 海啸可能在地震后几小时，甚至几分钟就发生！这取决于地震的强度和发生的位置。地震后，岛上的居民最好尽快跑到高处，这样最安全。

We have to get to higher ground now! thought Jack, dropping the book. 我们现在必须赶快去高地！ 杰克想着，把书扔下了。

He ran down to the edge of the ocean. Boka, Kama, and Annie were still paddling out beyond the waves. Jack forgot all about their fight. 他跑到了海边。 波卡、卡玛和安妮还在海浪之外划着船。 杰克完全忘记了他们之间的争吵。

“Hey, you guys!” he yelled. “嘿，你们这些人！”他喊道。

They didn’t hear him. 他们没听到他。

Jack went into the shallow water. “Hey, you guys!” he yelled. “Come back!” 杰克走进浅水区。 “嘿，你们 guys！”他喊道。 “回来！”

They still didn’t hear him. 他们还是没听到他。

Jack ran to his surfboard, grabbed it, and ran into the ocean. He fought the breaking waves. Once he was past them, he threw himself on his board and paddled wildly. 杰克跑到他的冲浪板前，抓起它，然后跑进海洋。 他与冲破的海浪搏斗。 他一过去，就跳上冲浪板，拼命地划起来。

The wave swells grew as he paddled. He could hardly see Annie, Boka, or Kama over them. Jack paddled faster and faster, trying to reach them. 他划得越快，海浪就越高。 他几乎看不见安妮、波卡和卡玛了。 杰克划得越来越快，努力想追上他们。

“Hey!” he yelled. “ Hey!” “嘿！”他喊道。 “嘿！”，

Boka looked back at him. He gave Jack a friendly wave, then turned away again. 波卡回头看了他一眼。 他朝杰克友好地挥了挥手，然后又转身离开了。

I have to get them to come to me! Jack thought frantically. “HELP! HELP!” he yelled at the top of his lungs. 我得让他们来找我！ 杰克急得团团转。 “救命！ 救命！”他大声喊道。

The three kids jerked around. They paddled quickly toward Jack with worried faces. 三个孩子猛地转过身来。 他们担心地快速划船朝杰克驶来。

“What’s wrong?” Annie cried when they got closer. “Are you in trouble?” “怎么了？”安妮靠近时喊道。 “你遇到麻烦了吗？”

“We all are!” said Jack. “A tsunami might be coming! There was an earthquake when I was on the beach!” “我们都会的！”杰克说。 “可能要来海啸了！ 我上次在海滩的时候发生了地震！

“We’d better ride in fast!” said Boka. “我们最好赶快骑上去！”波卡说。

“Stay on your bellies!” said Kama. “It’s safer!” “趴下！”卡玛说。 “这样更安全！”

“Here comes a wave!” cried Boka. “来波浪了！”波卡喊道。

They all started paddling. 他们都开始划桨。

The swell of the wave picked them up. They were all swept forward! 海浪涌上来，把他们卷了起来。 他们被冲得向前飞去！

Jack gripped the sides of his board as he zoomed along with the others. Suddenly he dropped down as the wave curled under. It felt like a roller coaster! But he stayed on his board as the wave carried him to shore. 杰克紧紧抓住冲浪板的两边，和其他人一起飞快地向前冲。 突然，海浪卷到下面，他一下子掉了下去。 感觉就像坐过山车！ 但海浪把他带到岸边时，他一直没离开冲浪板。

Jack rolled off into the shallow water. He snatched up his board and ran onto the sand. Boka and Kama were waiting. 杰克从冲浪板上滚下来，掉进了浅水里。 他一把捡起冲浪板，跑上了沙滩。 波卡和卡玛正在等。

“Good riding, Jack!” said Boka. “骑得真棒，杰克！”波卡说。

“Where’s Annie?” asked Jack. “安妮在哪里？”杰克问。

Boka pointed. Annie was in the shallow water, pulling her board in. As they watched, something very weird began to happen to the ocean. 波卡指了指。 安妮在浅水里，正把她的冲浪板拉回来。 当他们看着的时候，海洋里发生了一件非常奇怪的事情。

The water around Annie started to pull away. 安妮周围的水开始被吸走。
