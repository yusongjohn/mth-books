3. Sleepover 3. 过夜

“Aloha,” said Jack and Annie. “阿罗哈，”杰克和安妮说。

“Where did you come from?” a pretty woman asked. “你们从哪儿来？”一位漂亮的妇人问。

“Frog—” Annie started. “蛙——”安妮开口说。

But Jack broke in. “From over the mountains,” he said quickly. He pointed at the mountains looming in the distance. 可是杰克打断了她。 “从山那边来，”他赶紧说。 他指着远处耸立的群山。

“We are glad you have come to visit us,” the woman said. “我们很高兴你们来拜访，”妇人说。

Everyone smiled and nodded. 大家都微笑着点头。

They’re all amazingly nice, Jack thought. 他们都特别好，杰克想。

The music started again. As people began to dance, Kama took Annie’s hand. 音乐又响了起来。 人们开始跳舞时，卡玛拉住安妮的手。

“Sit and talk with us,” she said. “坐下来跟我们聊聊，”她说。

She and Boka led Jack and Annie to the edge of the clearing. They sat cross-legged in the grass. Kama picked up a wooden bowl. She held it out. 她和波卡领着杰克和安妮走到空地边缘。 他们盘腿坐在草地上。 卡玛拿起一只木碗。她递了过来。

“Please eat,” she said. “请吃，”她说。

“What is it?” asked Annie. “这是什么？”安妮问。

“It is poi,” said Kama. She scooped some poi out of the bowl and licked it off of her fingers. “这是芋泥，”卡玛说。 她从碗里舀了些芋泥，用手指舔着吃。

“You eat it with your hands? Cool,” said Annie. She stuck her fingers in the bowl and licked off the poi. “Mmm … good.” “用手吃？ 酷，”安妮说。 她把手指伸进碗里，把芋泥舔掉了。 “嗯……好吃。”

Jack stuck his finger in the bowl, too. The gooey mixture felt like peanut butter. But when he licked it off his finger, it had a weird taste—both bitter and sweet. 杰克也把手指伸进碗里。 那黏糊糊的东西摸起来像花生酱。 可当他从手指上舔下来时，味道怪怪的——又苦又甜。

“Hmm,” he said, but he made a face. “嗯，”他说，可他做了个鬼脸。

“He doesn’t like it,” Kama said to Boka. “他不喜欢，”卡玛对波卡说。

“No, no,” said Jack. “It’s … ” He tried to think of something polite.… “It’s very interesting.” “不，不，”杰克说。 “它……”他想找句礼貌的话……“它很有意思。”

Kama and Boka giggled. Then they stuck their fingers in the bowl and ate some poi. 卡玛和波卡咯咯笑了。 然后他们把手指伸进碗里，吃了些芋泥。

“Interesting!” they exclaimed. They cracked up laughing. Jack and Annie laughed with them. “真有意思！”他们大声说。 他们大笑起来。 杰克和安妮也跟他们一起笑。

“Now tell us about your home over the mountains,” said Kama, “this place you call ‘Frog.’ ” “现在跟我们说说山那边的家吧，”卡玛说，“那个你们叫‘蛙’的地方。”

Kama’s friendly smile made Jack want to tell her the truth. 卡玛友好的笑容让杰克想告诉她真相。

“It’s actually called Frog Creek,” he said. “It’s very far away—much farther than just over the mountains. We traveled here in a magic tree house.” “其实叫蛙溪，”他说。 “非常远——比翻过那些山还要远得多。 我们是坐魔法树屋来这儿的。”

Kama’s and Boka’s eyes got huge. They smiled even bigger smiles than before. 卡玛和波卡的眼睛睁得大大的。 他们笑得比刚才更灿烂了。

“That sounds fun!” said Kama. “听起来好玩！”卡玛说。

“You are so lucky!” said Boka. “你们真幸运！”波卡说。

Jack and Annie laughed. 杰克和安妮笑了。

“Yeah, we are,” said Jack. He felt great telling their new friends about the tree house. He and Annie had never talked about it with their friends back home. “是啊，我们是，”杰克说。 跟新朋友讲树屋的事，他感觉好极了。 他和安妮从没跟家里的朋友说过这件事。

“Can you stay here tonight?” asked Kama. “你们今晚能住这儿吗？”卡玛问。

Jack shrugged. “Sure, we can stay at least one night,” he said. 杰克耸耸肩。 “当然，我们至少能住一晚，”他说。

Kama hurried over to the pretty woman. They spoke together for a moment. Then Kama returned to Jack and Annie. 卡玛赶紧走到那位漂亮妇人那儿。 她们交谈了一会儿。 然后卡玛回到杰克和安妮身边。

“Our mother invites you to sleep at our house,” she said. “我们妈妈邀请你们到家里睡觉，”她说。

“Great,” said Annie. “Thanks.” “太好了，”安妮说。 “谢谢。”

Jack and Annie stood up. In the gray twilight, they followed Kama and Boka through the village. They wove between small huts with steep roofs until Kama stopped in front of one. 杰克和安妮站了起来。 在灰蒙蒙的暮色里，他们跟着卡玛和波卡穿过村子。 他们在陡屋顶的小屋间穿行，直到卡玛在一间屋前停下。

“This is our house,” she said. “这是我们的家，”她说。

The hut had no door—just a wide entrance that opened into one large room. 小屋没有门——只有一个宽宽的入口，通向一个大房间。

Kama and Boka led Jack and Annie inside. In the dim light, Jack could barely see the dried-grass walls and the woven-grass mats on the dirt floor. 卡玛和波卡领着杰克和安妮进去。 在昏暗的光线里，杰克几乎看不清干草墙和泥土地上的草垫。

“Where do we sleep?” he asked. “我们睡哪儿？”他问。

“Here!” said Boka. “这儿！”波卡说。

He and Kama lay down on the mats. Annie pulled off her lei and shoes. Then she lay down, too. 他和卡玛在垫子上躺了下来。 安妮摘下花环，脱掉鞋子。 然后她也躺下了。

“Oh,” said Jack. “Okay.” “哦，”杰克说。 “好吧。”

He took off his shoes and wreath of flowers. He used his backpack as a pillow when he lay down. The warm wind rustled the palm leaves outside. Music drifted in from the party. 他脱掉鞋子和花环。 他躺下时，把背包当枕头用。 温暖的风沙沙吹动外面的棕榈叶。 音乐从聚会上飘进来。

“The ocean is calling,” said Kama. “大海在呼唤，”卡玛说。

Jack could barely hear the waves in the distance. 杰克几乎听不见远处的浪声。

“Tomorrow we will take you wave riding,” said Boka. “明天我们带你们去驾浪，”波卡说。

“You mean surfing?” said Annie. “你是说冲浪吗？”安妮说。

“Yes,” said Kama. “对，”卡玛说。

“Cool,” said Jack. But he wasn’t sure he meant it. Surfing actually seemed pretty scary. “酷，”杰克说。 可他不确定自己是不是真心这么想。 冲浪其实听起来挺吓人的。

Kama seemed to hear his thoughts. “Don’t worry,” she said. “We’ll have fun.” 卡玛似乎听懂了他的心思。 “别担心，”她说。 “我们会玩得很开心。”

“No kidding,” said Annie. “没错，”安妮说。

Soon Jack heard steady breathing. The other kids had fallen asleep. 很快，杰克听见了平稳的呼吸声。 其他孩子已经睡着了。

Oh darn, we forgot to ask them about building a ship, he thought. I guess we’ll have to do that tomorrow.… 糟糕，我们忘了问他们造船的事，他想。 看来我们明天得去做那件事了……

Jack closed his eyes and yawned. Soon he, too, was fast asleep. 杰克闭上眼睛，打了个哈欠。 很快他也睡着了。
