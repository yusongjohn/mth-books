2. Aloha! 2. 阿罗哈！

Jack opened his eyes. A gentle wind brushed against his skin. It smelled sweet and fresh. 杰克睁开眼睛。 一阵轻风拂过他的皮肤。 闻起来又甜又新鲜。

Annie looked out the window. “Nice!” she said. 安妮望向窗外。 “真棒！”她说。

Jack looked out, too. The tree house had landed on top of a tall palm tree. The palm tree was at the edge of a flowery meadow. 杰克也望出去。 树屋落在一棵高大的棕榈树顶上。 那棵棕榈树长在开满花的草地边上。

On one side of the meadow, a cliff dropped down to the beach and ocean. On the other side of the meadow were the rooftops of a small village. 草地一边，悬崖直落海滩和大海。 草地另一边是小村庄的屋顶。

Beyond the village were tall gray mountains. Misty clouds hid their peaks. Waterfalls gushed down their sides. 村庄远处是高高的灰山。 薄雾般的云遮住了山顶。 瀑布从山坡上倾泻而下。

“I told you I loved Hawaii!” said Annie. “Don’t you?” “我就说我爱夏威夷吧！”安妮说。 “你呢？”

“I have to learn about it first,” said Jack. He pushed his glasses into place and opened their research book. He read aloud: “我得先了解一下，”杰克说。 他把眼镜推正，打开研究书。他大声读道：

Hawaii is a chain of islands in the Pacific Ocean. The largest island is Hawaii, which gives its name to the whole group. The islands were formed millions of years ago by volcanoes. The volcanoes erupted under the ocean. Over time, their craters rose above the water. 夏威夷是太平洋上的一串岛屿。最大的那个岛叫夏威夷，整个群岛的名字就是从这里来的。这些岛屿是几百万年前由火山形成的。火山在海底喷发，随着时间的推移，它们的火山口逐渐升到水面上。

“Wow,” said Annie. “We’re on the top of a volcano.” “哇，”安妮说。 “我们在一座火山顶上。”

“Yeah,” said Jack. He read on: “是啊，”杰克说。他继续读：

The volcanic rock crumbled and turned to soil. Over millions of years, wind and birds dropped seeds on the islands. Plants and trees began to grow, and birds and insects made their homes. 火山岩碎裂变成了土壤。经过数百万年，风和鸟把种子带到岛上。植物和树木开始生长，鸟儿和昆虫也在这里安了家。

“Cool,” said Jack. He took out his notebook and pencil and wrote: “酷，”杰克说。 他拿出笔记本和铅笔写道：

图中文字：wind and birds brought seeds 图中文字：风和鸟带来了种子

He read some more: 他又读了一些：

About two thousand years ago, people first came to Hawaii. They came in canoes from other islands in the Pacific. They rowed for thousands of miles across the ocean, guided only by the wind and stars. 大约两千年前，人们第一次来到夏威夷。他们乘着独木舟，从太平洋其他岛屿出发。他们划着船，穿越几千英里的海洋，只依靠风和星星指引方向。

“Hey, listen,” said Annie. “嘿，听，”安妮说。

Jack put down the book and listened. Sounds of music and laughter floated on the breeze. 杰克放下书，听着。 音乐和笑声随着微风飘来。

“There must be a party in that village,” said Annie. “Let’s go.” “那个村子里一定有聚会，”安妮说。 “我们去吧。”

“What about building that ship?” asked Jack. “那造船的事呢？”杰克问。

“We’ll figure that out later,” said Annie. “Let’s meet some people at the party. Maybe they can help us.” “以后再想，”安妮说。 “我们先去聚会上认识些人。 也许他们能帮我们。”

She started down the ladder. 她开始顺着梯子往下爬。

Jack heard a whoop of laughter in the distance. The party does sound fun, he thought. He packed up his things and followed Annie down to the ground. 杰克听见远处传来一声欢笑。 聚会听起来确实好玩，他想。 他收拾好东西，跟着安妮下到地上。

The sun was low in the sky. They walked through the meadow toward the village. Everything was bathed in a golden red glow. 太阳低低地挂在天上。 他们穿过草地走向村子。 一切都沐浴在金红色的光辉里。

“Oh, man,” breathed Jack. “天啊，”杰克轻声说。

There was beauty everywhere: purple flowers shaped like bells, white flowers that looked like stars, tall, feathery ferns, green spiky plants, big orange-and-black butterflies, and tiny yellow birds. 到处都是美景：像铃铛一样的紫花，像星星一样的白花，又高又像羽毛的蕨类，带刺的绿植物，大大的橙黑蝴蝶，还有小小的黄鸟。

When they got close to the village, they saw an open area filled with people. Jack and Annie slipped behind a palm tree. They peeked out at the party. 走近村子时，他们看见一片开阔地里满是人。 杰克和安妮溜到一棵棕榈树后面。 他们偷偷朝聚会望去。

There were about fifty people, including grown-ups, teenagers, and little kids. They were all barefoot and wore wreaths of flowers around their necks. 大约有五十个人，有大人、少年，还有小孩子。 他们都光着脚，脖子上戴着花环。

A woman was chanting. Her words rose and fell like waves. She chanted about a volcano goddess named Pele. 一个女人正在吟唱。 她的话像波浪一样起伏。 她吟唱着火山女神佩勒的故事。

While she chanted, other people played music. Some blew on pipes that looked like flutes. Others shook gourds that sounded like baby rattles. Some hit sticks together to make clicking sounds. 她吟唱时，其他人在奏乐。 有人吹着像笛子一样的管乐器。 有人摇着听起来像婴儿拨浪鼓的葫芦。 有人敲击木棍发出咔嗒声。

Most of the villagers were dancing to the music. They stepped from side to side. They swayed their hips and waved their hands. 大多数村民正随着音乐跳舞。 他们左右来回地迈步。 他们扭动着胯部。

“They’re doing the hula,” whispered Annie. She smiled and waved her hands, too. “他们在跳草裙舞，”安妮小声说。 她也微笑着挥动双手。

“Don’t get carried away,” whispered Jack. “别得意忘形，”杰克小声说。

He took out their book and found a picture of Hawaiians dancing. He read: 他拿出书，找到一幅夏威夷人跳舞的画。他读道：

The early Hawaiians had no written language. They told stories with hula dancing. The hula is a blend of dancing and chanting poetry. 早期的夏威夷人没有书写语言。他们用夏威夷舞蹈来讲故事。夏威夷舞蹈是一种舞蹈和吟唱诗歌的结合。

Jack pulled out his notebook. He started a list about early Hawaii: 杰克抽出笔记本。 他开始列一份关于早期夏威夷的清单：

图中文字：no written language

stories with hula 图中文字：没有文字的故事，只有夏威夷草裙舞

Suddenly Jack heard loud laughter and clapping. He looked up. Annie was gone! 忽然杰克听见响亮的笑声和掌声。他抬起头。 安妮不见了！

Jack peeked out from behind the tree. Annie was doing the hula with the dancers! But no one seemed surprised. Everyone just smiled at her as they kept dancing. 杰克从树后探出头。 安妮正和舞者们一起跳草裙舞！ 可好像没有人感到惊讶。 大家只是对她微笑，继续跳舞。

A girl caught sight of Jack. She looked about Annie’s age. She had long, shiny black hair and a big, friendly smile. 一个女孩看见了杰克。 她看上去和安妮差不多大。 她有长长的、闪亮的黑发，还有大大的、友善的笑容。

“Come do the hula!” she called to him. “来跳呼啦舞吧！”她对他喊道。

“No way,” Jack breathed. “不行，”杰克低声说。

He slipped behind the tree again. But the girl danced over to him and took his hand. 他又溜到树后面。 可那女孩跳到他身边，拉住他的手。

“Join us!” she said. “加入我们吧！”她说。

“No thanks,” said Jack. “不用了，谢谢，”杰克说。

The girl didn’t let go. She pulled Jack into the open. The music got louder. The dancers and musicians nodded and smiled at Jack. 女孩没有松手。 她把杰克拉到开阔处。 音乐更响了。 舞者和乐手们向杰克点头微笑。

Jack stood still. He didn’t know how to do any kind of dance, let alone the hula! He stared at the ground, clutching his backpack and notebook until the music and dancing ended. 杰克站着不动。 他什么舞都不会跳，更别说呼啦舞了！ 他盯着地面，紧紧抓着背包和笔记本，直到音乐和舞蹈结束。

The Hawaiians gathered around Jack and Annie. They all had friendly, open faces. 夏威夷人围到杰克和安妮身边。 他们都有友善、坦诚的面孔。

“Who are you?” the young girl asked. “你们是谁？”年轻女孩问。

“I’m Annie,” said Annie. “This is my brother, Jack.” “我是安妮，”安妮说。 “这是我哥哥杰克。”

“I’m Kama,” the girl said. “This is my brother, Boka.” She pointed to a boy in the crowd who looked about Jack’s age. “我是卡玛，”女孩说。 “这是我哥哥波卡。”她指向人群中一个看上去和杰克差不多大的男孩。

The boy stepped forward. He grinned a big grin just like his sister’s. He pulled off his wreath of red fluffy flowers. He put it around Annie’s neck. 男孩走上前来。 他咧嘴一笑，笑容跟姐姐一模一样。 他摘下那圈红红的、毛茸茸的花环。 他把它套到安妮脖子上。

“A lei to welcome you,” Boka said. “送你一个花环，欢迎你们，”波卡说。

Kama then pulled off her lei and put it around Jack’s neck. 卡玛然后摘下自己的花环，套到杰克脖子上。

“Aloha, Jack and Annie!” everyone said. “阿罗哈，杰克和安妮！”大家都说。
