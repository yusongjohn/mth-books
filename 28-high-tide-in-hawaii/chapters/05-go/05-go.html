5. GO! 5. 出发！

Boka looked at Jack and grinned. 波卡看着杰克，咧嘴一笑。

“Ready?” he asked. “准备好了吗？”他问。

“I’m ready!” said Annie. “Where do we get our surfboards?” “我准备好了！”安妮说。 “我们去哪儿拿冲浪板？”

“Down there,” said Kama. She pointed to a rocky path that led to the beach. “在下面，”卡玛说。 她指向通往海滩的一条岩石小路。

“Let’s go,” said Annie. “走吧，”安妮说。

Annie, Boka, and Kama started down the path. Jack followed, moving slowly and carefully. 安妮、波卡和卡玛开始沿小路往下走。 杰克跟着，慢慢地、小心地移动。

When he stepped onto the beach, Jack slipped off his shoes. He dug his toes into the dry, warm sand. It felt as soft as silk. 踏上沙滩时，杰克脱掉鞋子。 他把脚趾埋进干燥温暖的沙子里。 它摸起来像丝绸一样软。

“Actually, I wouldn’t mind just taking a walk on the beach,” he said to the others. “其实，我倒不介意就在海滩上走走，”他对其他人说。

But no one seemed to hear. They had all walked over to a row of wooden surfboards propped against the rocks. 可好像没人听见。 他们都走到一排靠在岩石上的木制冲浪板旁。

Boka picked out a long board and lugged it over to Jack. “For you,” he said. 波卡挑出一块长板，吃力地拖到杰克身边。 “给你，”他说。

Jack took the board and looked up at it. It was as tall as his dad. 杰克接过冲浪板，抬头看着它。 它跟他爸爸一样高。

“Isn’t this a little big for me?” he asked. “这对我是不是有点太大了？”他问。

Boka shook his head. He chose a board for Annie. Then he and Kama grabbed a couple for themselves. 波卡摇摇头。 他给安妮选了一块板。 然后他和卡玛也给自己抓了两块。

Jack took a deep breath. “I’d like to read a little about surfing first,” he said. He put his board down and pulled out the research book. 杰克深吸一口气。 “我想先读一点关于冲浪的内容，”他说。 他放下板，抽出研究书。

“What is that? ” asked Kama. “那是什么？”卡玛问。

“It’s a book,” said Jack. “It tells you about things.” “是一本书，”杰克说。 “它告诉你关于事物的事。”

“How does it talk?” said Kama. “它怎么说话？”卡玛说。

“It doesn’t talk,” said Annie. “You read it.” “它不说话，”安妮说。 “你读它。”

Kama looked confused. 卡玛看起来很困惑。

“Jack, forget the book now,” Annie said. “Let’s just do what Boka and Kama tell us.” She headed for the ocean, lugging her board. “杰克，现在别管书了，”安妮说。 “我们就按波卡和卡玛说的做。”她拖着冲浪板朝大海走去。

Jack sighed and put the book away. He left his pack in the sand, picked up his board, and followed the others. 杰克叹了口气，收起书。 他把背包留在沙子上，拿起冲浪板，跟上其他人。

They all stopped at the edge of the water. 他们都在水边停下。

“First we need to get past the breaking waves,” said Kama. “Then we’ll show you what to do next.” “首先我们得穿过拍岸的浪，”卡玛说。 “然后我们再教你们下一步做什么。”

Together they waded into the cool, shallow water. The waves don’t seem all that big, Jack thought hopefully. 他们一起走进凉爽、浅浅的海水。 浪看起来也没那么大，杰克满怀希望地想。

But as he waded deeper into the ocean, the breaking waves began to look bigger and bigger. When the first wave hit him, Jack leaned against it, lifting his board. He nearly fell over. 可当他往海里走得更深，碎浪开始显得越来越大。 第一道浪打来时，杰克侧身顶着它，举起冲浪板。 他差点摔倒。

Kama, Boka, and Annie moved farther out into the ocean. Jack watched as a wave loomed over them. They all threw their boards over the wave and dove into it. 卡玛、波卡和安妮继续往海里走得更远。 杰克看着一个浪头赫然朝他们压过来。 他们都把冲浪板抛过浪头，然后一头扎进水里。

Jack struggled forward. The next time a big wave came toward him, he threw his board over it. Holding his glasses tightly, he ducked under. 杰克费力地往前走。 下一次大浪朝他涌来时，他把板抛过浪头。 他紧紧抓住眼镜，潜到水下。

When Jack stood up again, he wiped the water from his eyes and glasses. His surfboard was close by. He grabbed it before another wave came. 杰克再次站起来时，擦去眼睛和眼镜上的水。 他的冲浪板就在旁边。 他在另一道浪打来前抓住了它。

Jack kept fighting his way forward. By the time he got past all the breakers, the water was up to his chest. 杰克继续奋力向前冲。 等他穿过所有拍岸的浪时，水已经到胸口了。

“We’ll paddle out to catch a big wave!” said Boka. “我们要划出去抓一个大浪！”波卡说。

Jack frowned. “But—” 杰克皱起眉。 “可是——”

“Don’t worry, Jack,” said Kama. “It will be fun!” “别担心，杰克，”卡玛说。 “会很好玩的！”

Boka and Kama pulled themselves onto their boards. They lay on their bellies and began paddling with their hands out to sea. 波卡和卡玛爬上冲浪板。 他们趴在板上，开始用手向大海划去。

Jack and Annie lay down on their boards, too. Paddling over the gentle waves, Jack relaxed. Now, this was something he could do all day. 杰克和安妮也趴到自己的板上。 在温和的海浪上拨水时，杰克放松了下来。 这个他倒是可以整天做。

“When I say go, paddle fast back toward the shore!” said Kama. “我说出发时，就赶快往岸边划！”卡玛说。

“When do we stand up?” said Annie. “我们什么时候站起来？”安妮说。

“When you start toward the shore!” said Boka. “Stand up with one foot forward. Stretch out your arms to keep your balance!” “当你们开始向岸边冲的时候！”波卡说。 “一只脚在前站起来。 伸出胳膊来保持平衡！”

“But don’t try to stand up the very first time!” said Kama. “Just ride your board on your belly!” “可第一次别试着站起来！”卡玛说。 “就趴在板上冲就行！”

“I see one coming now!” said Boka. “我看见一个浪来了！”波卡说。

“Wait, wait!” said Jack. Everything was happening too fast. He had questions. “等等，等等！”杰克说。 一切发生得太快了。他还有问题要问。

“Go!” Kama shouted. “出发！”卡玛喊道。

Jack saw a big wave rolling toward them. Before he knew it, Boka, Kama, and Annie were paddling quickly toward the shore. Jack paddled like crazy to keep up. 杰克看见一道大浪朝他们滚来。 他还没反应过来，波卡、卡玛和安妮已经飞快地朝岸边划去。 杰克拼命拨水，好跟上。

Suddenly the wave lifted him and swept him forward! Jack zoomed toward the shore with amazing speed. Out of the corner of his eye, he saw Boka and Kama— and Annie!—all standing up. 忽然浪把他托起来，向前冲去！ 杰克以惊人的速度朝岸边飞快冲去。 他眼角瞥见波卡和卡玛——还有安妮！——都站了起来。

Jack wanted to be like them. In a flash, he went up on his knees. He put his left foot forward and stood up! For one second he felt like a soaring bird—then he lost his balance! 杰克想像他们一样。 一瞬间，他跪了起来。 他把左脚迈到前面，站了起来！ 有那么一秒，他觉得自己像只飞翔的鸟——然后就失去了平衡！

Jack fell into the water, grabbing his glasses just in time. The wave crashed down on top of him! Water filled his mouth and went up his nose. His board and his lei were swept away. 杰克掉进水里，及时抓住了眼镜。 浪头砸在他身上！ 水灌进他的嘴，冲进鼻子。 他的冲浪板和花环被冲走了。

Jack twisted and turned in the churning water. When his head bobbed up above the water, he choked and coughed. 杰克在汹涌的水里扭来扭去。 当他的头浮出水面时，他呛住了，直咳嗽。

Another big wave crashed down on him, and he went under again. When he came up, he plunged forward, desperately trying to get to shore. 又一道大浪砸下来，他又沉了下去。 浮上来时，他拼命向前扑，想游到岸边。

Again and again, Jack was thrown down and slammed by breaking waves. But each time, he got up and hurled himself closer to shore. 一次又一次，杰克被碎浪打倒、撞击。 可每一次，他都站起来，用力朝岸边扑去。

Finally, Jack dragged himself out of the ocean. Feeling bruised and battered, he fell onto the sand. 终于，杰克把自己拖出了大海。 他又疼又累，一下子倒在沙滩上。
