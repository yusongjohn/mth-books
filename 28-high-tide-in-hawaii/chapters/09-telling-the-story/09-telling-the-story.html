9. Telling the Story 9. 讲述故事

Finally, the hugging and crying and laughing died down. The villagers started walking back to their huts. 终于，拥抱、哭泣和笑声渐渐平息了。 村民们开始走回小屋。

Jack and Annie followed Boka, Kama, and their parents. 杰克和安妮跟着波卡、卡玛和他们的父母。

“We felt the ground shaking,” said Boka and Kama’s father. “We knew a big wave might follow!” “我们感觉到地面在晃，”波卡和卡玛的父亲说。 “我们知道大浪可能会跟着来！”

“Jack saved us!” said Boka. “He read in a book and found out about the big waves.” “杰克救了我们！”波卡说。 “他在书里读到了，发现了大浪的事。”

“What’s a book?” asked his mother. “书是什么？”他妈妈问。

“Show them,” Annie said to Jack. “给他们看看，”安妮对杰克说。

Jack reached into his pack and took out their research book. 杰克把手伸进背包，取出研究书。

“It tells about the big waves in here,” he said. “Books give lots of information.” “这里面讲大浪的事，”他说。 “书能告诉我们很多信息。”

“Ah,” said Boka and Kama’s mother. “A book is a good thing.” “啊，”波卡和卡玛的妈妈说。 “书是好东西。”

“Books tell stories, too,” said Annie. “书也讲故事，”安妮说。

“That is impossible,” said Kama. “The book cannot move its feet or wave its hands. It cannot sing or chant.” “那不可能，”卡玛说。 “书不能动脚，也不能挥手。 它不能唱歌或吟唱。”

“That’s true,” said Jack, smiling. “这话不假，”杰克微笑着说。

“Now we should do the hula,” Boka said to Annie, Kama, and Jack, “and tell our story.” “现在我们该跳草裙舞，”波卡对安妮、卡玛和杰克说，“把我们的故事讲出来。”

“I’ll watch,” said Jack, stepping away. “我看着，”杰克说着往后退开。

Boka and Kama’s father called for music. 波卡和卡玛的父亲招呼大家奏乐。

The villagers gathered around. A man started to play a pipe. A teenage boy knocked two sticks together. Some women began shaking rattles. 村民们围了过来。 一个男人开始吹管乐器。 一个少年敲击两根木棍。 几个女人开始摇响铃。

Boka, Kama, and Annie waved their hands in time to the music. They stepped from side to side. They swayed their hips. 波卡、卡玛和安妮随着音乐挥动双手。 他们左右来回地迈步。 他们扭动着胯部。

Kama chanted about going out into the water. She, Boka, and Annie waved their hands to show how they paddled out to sea. 卡玛吟唱着他们如何出海。 她、波卡和安妮挥手表示他们如何划向大海。

Kama chanted about how Jack had warned them. She and the others waved their hands to show how they rode their surfboards to shore. 卡玛吟唱杰克如何警告他们。 她和其他人挥手表示他们如何乘冲浪板回到岸上。

Then Jack surprised himself. He waved his hands to show how he rode his surfboard like a bird soaring through the air. The next thing he knew, he was stepping from side to side. He was swaying his hips. He was doing the hula! 然后杰克自己都觉得意外。 他挥手表示自己如何像鸟儿翱翔一样驾着冲浪板。 接下来他知道的是，他正在左右迈步。 他扭动着胯部。 他在跳草裙舞！

Kama chanted about how the water had pulled away from the shore—and how they had climbed to safety—and how the giant wave had crashed against the cliff. 卡玛吟唱着海水怎样从岸边退去——他们怎样爬到了安全的地方——还有巨浪怎样撞上悬崖。

As Kama chanted the story, all the villagers joined in the dance. The tall grasses swayed. The palm trees swayed. And all the hula dancers swayed, too. 卡玛吟唱故事时，所有村民都加入了舞蹈。 高高的草在摇摆。 棕榈树在摇摆。 所有草裙舞者也在摇摆。

When the story ended, everyone clapped. 故事结束时，大家都鼓掌。

“Thanks for helping us,” Boka said to Jack and Annie. “谢谢你们帮助我们，”波卡对杰克和安妮说。

“We’re a good team,” said Annie. “我们是很棒的一队，”安妮说。

“We are best friends,” said Kama. “我们是最好的朋友，”卡玛说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “I’m sorry I said mean things.” “是啊，”杰克说。 “对不起，我说了难听的话。”

“We’re sorry we laughed at you,” said Boka. “对不起，我们刚才笑话你了，”波卡说。

“I’m sorry I grabbed the book,” said Annie. “对不起，我抢了书，”安妮说。

“Our mother says friendship is like riding the waves,” said Kama. “Sometimes you ride low, gentle waves. Sometimes you ride high, rough ones.” “妈妈说友谊就像驾浪，”卡玛说。 “有时你乘的是低而温柔的浪。 有时候你会遇上又高又猛的浪。”

Annie gasped. She looked at Jack. She repeated Morgan’s rhyme: 安妮倒抽一口气。 她看着杰克。 她重复摩根的韵诗：

To find a special magic, 去寻找一种特别的魔法。

build a special kind of ship 造一种特别的船

that rides the waves, 它驾驭着波浪。

both high and low, 无论高低起伏。

on every kind of trip. 在每一种旅程上。

“Friend ship! That’s the ship!” said Jack. 那就是那艘船！”杰克说。

“And we built it!” said Annie. “而且我们造好了！”安妮说。

She and Jack burst out laughing. 她和杰克大笑起来。

Boka and Kama looked a little confused, but they laughed, too. 波卡和卡玛看起来有点困惑，可他们也笑了。

“We have to go back to our own home now,” Annie said to Boka and Kama. “我们现在得回自己的家了，”安妮对波卡和卡玛说。

“It’s time to say good-bye,” said Jack. “该说再见了，”杰克说。

“We never say good-bye,” said Kama. “We say aloha when we greet our friends. And we say aloha when we leave them.” “我们从不说再见，”卡玛说。 “迎接朋友时我们说阿罗哈。 离开朋友时我们也说阿罗哈。”

“Friends are always together,” said Boka, “even when they are far apart.” “朋友永远在一起，”波卡说，“就算相隔很远。”

“Have a good journey in your magic tree house,” said Kama. “祝你们在魔法树屋里旅途愉快，”卡玛说。

“Thanks,” said Jack and Annie. They waved to all the villagers. “ Aloha!” “谢谢，”杰克和安妮说。 他们向所有村民挥手。 “阿罗哈！”

“Aloha!” everyone called back. “阿罗哈！”大家回应道。

Then Annie and Jack started through the meadow. Tiny yellow birds and orange-and-black butterflies flitted about them. 然后安妮和杰克开始穿过草地。 小小的黄鸟和橙黑相间的蝴蝶在他们周围轻快地飞来飞去。

At the edge of the meadow, they came to the grove of palm trees. They climbed up the rope ladder into the tree house. 在草地边缘，他们来到棕榈树丛。 他们爬上绳梯，进了树屋。

Out the window, Jack saw the tall mountains, the small village, the flowery meadow, and the ocean. The water was peaceful again. 窗外，杰克看见高高的山、小村庄、开满花的草地，还有大海。 海水又平静了。

“I still have my lei,” said Annie. “我还戴着花环呢，”安妮说。

She took it off. Though the red flowers were wet, they were still a little fluffy. 她摘了下来。 红花虽然湿了，却还带着一点蓬松。

“It’s proof that we found the special magic,” said Jack. “The magic of friendship.” “这是我们找到特别魔法的证据，”杰克说。 “友谊的魔法。”

Annie put the lei on the floor next to the play scrolls, the twig, and the corn seeds. Then she picked up the Pennsylvania book. 安妮把花环放在地板上，旁边是戏剧卷轴、小树枝和玉米种子。 然后她拿起宾夕法尼亚那本书。

“Ready?” she asked. “准备好了吗？”她问。

Jack sighed. “I love Hawaii,” he said. 杰克叹了口气。 “我爱夏威夷，”他说。

“Finally, you admit it,” said Annie. She pointed at a picture of the Frog Creek woods. “I wish we could go home now.” “你终于承认了，”安妮说。 她指着一幅蛙溪树林的画。 “但愿我们现在能回家。”

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
