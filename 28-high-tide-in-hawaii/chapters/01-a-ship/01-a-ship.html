1. A Ship? 1. 一艘船？

Jack and Annie were sitting on their porch, reading books. Jack was reading about gorillas. Annie was reading about Pilgrims. 杰克和安妮坐在门廊上读书。 杰克在读关于大猩猩的书。 安妮在读关于清教徒的书。

Suddenly Annie closed her book. She looked up into the sunset. 忽然安妮合上了书。 她抬头望向落日。

“Hey!” Annie said with a smile. “嘿！”安妮笑着说。

Jack looked over at her. 杰克看向她。

“It’s back!” she said, jumping up. “它回来了！”她跳起来说。

“Oh, man,” breathed Jack. He knew she was talking about the magic tree house. Annie could always tell when it was back. “天啊，”杰克轻声说。 他知道她说的是魔法树屋。 安妮总能感觉到它什么时候回来。

Jack closed his book and stood up. 杰克合上书，站了起来。

“We’re going to the woods!” he called through the screen door. “There’s something we have to check on!” “我们去树林！”他隔着纱门喊道。 “有件事我们得去看看！”

“Be back before dark!” their mom said. “天黑前回来！”妈妈说。

“We will!” said Jack. “我们会的！”杰克说。

He picked up his backpack. Then he and Annie headed across the yard. When they got to the sidewalk, they started running. They ran up their street and into the Frog Creek woods. 他拿起背包。 然后他和安妮穿过院子。 走到人行道上时，他们开始跑起来。 他们沿着街道跑进蛙溪树林。

In the last light of day, they hurried between the trees. Finally, they came to the tallest oak. They held their breath as they looked up. 在一天最后的光里，他们在树木间匆匆穿行。 终于，他们来到最高的那棵橡树下。 他们屏住呼吸，抬头望去。

The magic tree house was back. 魔法树屋回来了。

“Good going,” said Jack. “干得好，”杰克说。

“Thanks!” said Annie. “太好了！”安妮说。

She started up the ladder. Jack followed. It was nearly dark inside. But the sun-dried wood smelled like a summer day. 她开始往上爬。杰克跟着。 里面几乎全黑了。 可晒干的木头闻起来像夏日一样。

“What kind of special magic will we look for this time?” said Jack. “这次我们要寻找什么样的特别魔法？”杰克说。

They glanced around the tree house. They saw the scrolls they’d brought back from Shakespeare’s theater. They saw the twig from the mountain gorillas and the pouch of corn seeds from the first Thanksgiving. 他们环顾树屋。 他们看见从莎士比亚剧院带回的卷轴。 他们看见山地大猩猩的小树枝，还有第一次感恩节带回的玉米种子袋。

“There!” said Annie. She pointed to a book in the corner. A piece of paper was sticking out of it. “那儿！”安妮说。 她指向角落里的一本书。 一张纸从里面露了出来。

Jack picked up the book. Then he pulled out the paper and read: 杰克拿起书。 然后他抽出纸读道：

Dear Jack and Annie, 亲爱的杰克和安妮。

Good luck on your fourth journey to find a special magic. This secret rhyme will guide you: 祝你们第四次寻找特别魔法的旅程好运。 这首秘密韵诗会指引你们：

To find a special magic, 去寻找一种特别的魔法。

build a special kind of ship 造一种特别的船

that rides the waves, 它驾驭着波浪。

both high and low, 无论高低起伏。

on every kind of trip. 在每一种旅程上。

Thank you, 谢谢你们。

          Morgan

Jack looked at Annie. 杰克看着安妮。

“A ship?” he said. “一艘船？”他说。

She shrugged. “Yep. I guess we have to build a ship. Where do we go to build it?” 她耸耸肩。 “对。 我猜我们得造一艘船。 我们去哪儿造呢？”

She and Jack looked at the book’s cover. It showed palm trees, a beach, and a beautiful ocean. The title was: 她和杰克看着书的封面。 上面画着棕榈树、沙滩和美丽的大海。书名是：

A VISIT TO OLD HAWAII 《古夏威夷之旅》

“Oh, wow!” said Annie. “I love Hawaii!” “哦，哇！”安妮说。 “我爱夏威夷！”

“How do you know you love it?” Jack asked. “We’ve never been to Hawaii.” “你怎么知道你爱它？”杰克问。 “我们从没去过夏威夷。”

“Well, we’re going now!” said Annie. She pointed at the cover. “We wish we could go there!” “嗯，我们现在就去！”安妮说。 她指着封面。 “但愿我们能去那儿！”

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
