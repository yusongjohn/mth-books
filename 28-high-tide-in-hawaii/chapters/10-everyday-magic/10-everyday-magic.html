10. Everyday Magic 10. 日常魔法

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开眼睛。

The sun was setting beyond the woods. No time at all had passed in Frog Creek. 太阳正在树林外落下。 在蛙溪，时间一点都没有过去。

“Welcome back,” said a soft, lovely voice. “欢迎回来，”一个温柔动听的声音说。

Morgan le Fay was in the magic tree house. 摩根·勒菲就在魔法树屋里。

“Morgan!” cried Annie. She threw her arms around the enchantress. “摩根！”安妮喊道。 她张开双臂拥抱女魔法师。

Jack hugged Morgan, too. 杰克也拥抱了摩根。

“Look, Morgan,” said Annie. “We have proof we found four special kinds of magic!” “看，摩根，”安妮说。 “我们有证据证明找到了四种特别的魔法！”

“Yes, I see,” said Morgan. “是的，我看见了，”摩根说。

Morgan picked up the play scrolls that Shakespeare had given Jack and Annie in old England. 摩根拿起莎士比亚在古英格兰送给杰克和安妮的戏剧卷轴。

“You found the magic of theater,” she said. “你们找到了戏剧的魔法，”她说。

Morgan picked up the twig from a mountain gorilla in the African cloud forest. 摩根拿起非洲云雾林山地大猩猩的小树枝。

“And the magic of animals,” she said. “还有动物的魔法，”她说。

Morgan picked up the pouch of corn seeds from their trip to the first Thanksgiving. 摩根拿起第一次感恩节之旅带回的玉米种子袋。

“And the magic of community,” she said. “还有共同体的魔法，”她说。

Finally, Morgan picked up the wreath of flowers from Kama and Boka. 最后，摩根拿起卡玛和波卡送来的花环。

“And you discovered the magic of friendship,” she said. “而且你们发现了友谊的魔力，”她说。

Morgan looked at Jack and Annie for a long moment. “Listen carefully to what I’m about to tell you,” she said. 摩根久久地看着杰克和安妮。 “仔细听我接下来要说的话，”她说。

“Yes?” They both leaned forward. “好？”他们俩都凑上前。

“You are now Magicians of Everyday Magic,” said Morgan. “You have learned to find the magic in things you encounter on earth every day. There are many other forms of everyday magic. You never have to look far to find it. You only have to live your life to the fullest.” “你们现在是日常魔法的魔法师了，”摩根说。 “你们已经学会，在每天遇见的事物里找到魔力。 日常魔法还有许多其他形式。 你们永远不必找得很远。 你只要尽情地生活就好。”

Jack and Annie nodded. 杰克和安妮点点头。

“Soon you will be called upon to use your knowledge of Everyday Magic in the realm of fantasy.” 很快你们就会被召唤，在幻想的领域里运用日常魔法的知识。”

“The realm of fantasy?” said Jack. “幻想的领域？”杰克说。

“Are we going back to Camelot?” said Annie. “我们要回卡米洛特吗？”安妮说。

Before Morgan could answer, a shout came from the distance. “Jack! Annie!” 摩根还没来得及回答，远处传来一声喊叫。 “杰克！安妮！”

“Our dad’s calling,” said Annie. “爸爸在叫我们，”安妮说。

“You must go home now,” said Morgan gently. “Rest—and get ready to test your powers. Your most exciting challenges are yet to come.” “你们现在必须回家了，”摩根轻声说。 “休息吧——准备好去考验你们的能力。 你们最激动人心的挑战还在后头。”

“Good-bye, Morgan,” said Annie and Jack. “再见，摩根，”安妮和杰克说。

They hugged the enchantress. Then Jack took the Hawaii book out of his pack and gave it to Morgan. He put on his backpack and followed Annie down the ladder. 他们拥抱了女魔法师。 然后杰克从背包里取出夏威夷那本书，交给摩根。 他背上背包，跟着安妮爬下梯子。

When they stepped onto the ground, there was a great roar above them. Jack and Annie looked up. A swirl of sparkling light lit the top of the tree. 他们踏到地面时，头顶传来巨大的轰鸣。 杰克和安妮抬头望去。 一圈闪闪发光的光亮照亮了树顶。

Then the light was gone. The tree house was gone. Morgan le Fay was gone, too. 然后光消失了。 树屋消失了。 摩根·勒菲也消失了。

Jack and Annie didn’t speak for a long moment. Then Jack broke the silence. 杰克和安妮好一会儿没有说话。 然后杰克打破了沉默。

“Our most exciting challenges are yet to come?” he said. “What do you think Morgan meant by that?” “我们最激动人心的挑战还在后面？”他说。 “你觉得摩根那是什么意思？”

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “我不知道，”安妮说。

“It sounds a little scary,” said Jack. “听起来有点吓人，”杰克说。

“That’s okay. We can handle it,” said Annie. She smiled. “We’re Magicians of Everyday Magic.” “没关系。 我们能应付，”安妮说。她笑了。 “我们是日常魔法的魔法师。”

Jack smiled. “Yeah,” he said. “I guess we are.” 杰克笑了。 “是啊，”他说。 “我想我们就是。”

They walked out of the woods as the sun was setting. Down the street their mom and dad were standing on their front porch. They waved at Jack and Annie. 他们走出树林时，太阳正在落下。 街那边，爸爸妈妈正站在前门廊上。 他们向杰克和安妮挥手。

Jack felt a surge of happiness. There’s another kind of everyday magic, he thought, the magic of family. 杰克心里涌起一股幸福。 还有另一种日常魔法，他想，那就是家的魔法。

In that moment, it seemed the best magic of all. 在那一刻，它似乎是所有魔法中最好的。
