8. Dogs for an Hour 8. 变成狗一小时

“Ready?” said Jack. “准备好了吗？”杰克说。

Annie nodded. “Ready,” she said. 安妮点了点头。 “准备好了，”她说。

Jack took a sip of the potion and handed the bottle to Annie. She took a sip and put the bottle down on the ground. They looked at each other. 杰克喝了一口药水，然后把瓶子递给了安妮。 她喝了一口，然后把瓶子放在地上。 他们互相看了看。

“Okay. Together,” said Jack. “One, two, three …” “好吧。 一起，”杰克说。 “一，二，三……”

“Turn us into Saint Bernard dogs!” they shouted into the cold wind. “让我们变成圣伯纳犬吧！”他们对着寒冷的风喊道。

Nothing happened. 什么也没发生。

Then suddenly, Jack was hurled face-first into the snow. The world went black, and Jack felt his body shaking uncontrollably. 然后突然，杰克被猛地扑倒，脸朝下摔在了雪里。 世界一片漆黑，杰克感觉自己的身体不受控制地颤抖起来。

When the shaking stopped, Jack opened his eyes. He wasn’t dizzy. He wasn’t scared or worried or tired. He felt enormously happy. He looked down at himself. He was covered with thick red and white fur. He had four furry legs with big paws. 颤抖停止后，杰克睁开了眼睛。他不再头晕了。 他不再害怕，也不再担心，也不再累了。 他感到非常非常开心。 他低头看了看自己。 他身上覆盖着厚厚的红白相间的毛发。 他有四条毛茸茸的腿，还有大大的爪子。

Something moved behind Jack. He turned and saw a fluffy tail waving. He leapt toward it. The tail moved away! Jack realized the tail was his! Still, it was fun to try to catch it. He turned in a circle, around and around and around. 杰克背后有什么东西在动。 他转过身，看到一条毛茸茸的尾巴在摇来摇去。 他朝那条尾巴跳了过去。 尾巴动起来了！ 杰克意识到那条尾巴是他自己的！ 不过，试着去抓它还是很有趣的。 他转着圈，一圈又一圈又一圈。

Jack noticed fat snowflakes floating like feathers through the air. He stopped chasing his tail and bounced around clumsily, trying to eat the swirling flakes. He snapped at the air, until he heard another dog barking. 杰克注意到胖乎乎的雪花像羽毛一样在空中飘动。 他停止了追逐自己的尾巴，笨拙地蹦来蹦去，试图吃掉那些旋转的雪花。 他对着空气猛咬，直到他听到另一只狗在叫。

Jack understood the dog’s language right away: “JACK! JACK!” 杰克立刻明白了那只狗的语言：‘杰克！杰克！’

Jack looked at the Saint Bernard running toward him through the snow. She was a little smaller, with smoother fur and bright, lively eyes. She barked, “WOW! WOW! CAN YOU BELIEVE THIS?” 杰克看着那只圣伯纳犬穿过雪地朝他跑来。 她小一点，毛发更光滑，眼睛又亮又活泼。她叫道：‘哇！’ 哇！ 你能相信吗？“

Jack and Annie laughed with short bursts of loud panting. Their tongues hung out of their mouths, and their breath billowed into the cold. The world was alive with exciting sounds Jack had never heard before. The sounds weren’t loud, but they were clear and distinct. 杰克和安妮发出短促而响亮的喘气声，笑了起来。 他们的舌头从嘴里伸出来，呼出的热气在寒冷中飘散开来。 这个世界充满了杰克以前从未听过的激动人心的声音。 声音不大，但很清楚，也很特别。

Along with the HEH-HEH-HEH! of their heavy breathing, Jack heard the swish- swish of the wind-driven snow and the crick- crack of ice on the mountain slopes. 还有那呼哧呼哧的声音！ 伴随着他们沉重的呼吸声，杰克听到了风吹雪地的沙沙声，还有山上的冰层发出的咔咔声。

“SMELL!” barked Annie. She and Jack lifted their big black noses into the air and sniffed. “闻一闻！”安妮喊道。 她和杰克抬起了他们又大又黑的鼻子，使劲地闻了闻。

Strong scents wafted from all directions. Jack stepped forward, sniffing this way and that, his nostrils quivering. The smells changed with every step and every turn of his head. The sky had a scent. The distant chimney smoke, craggy rocks, and mountain slopes—all had different scents. 各种香味从四面八方飘来。 杰克向前走，这儿闻闻，那儿嗅嗅，鼻子不停地抽动。 他每走一步，每转一次头，闻到的气味都不一样。 天空也有味道。 远处的烟囱烟、崎岖的岩石和山的斜坡，都有不同的气味。

Jack smushed his nose into the newly fallen snow. The snow held a dozen different smells, all wonderful and amazing. Jack licked the snow like ice cream. It was so cold, it felt hot! 杰克把鼻子埋进新落的雪里。 雪里有十几种不同的味道，每一种都又棒又神奇。 杰克像吃冰淇淋一样舔着雪。 它冷得让人感觉像热的一样！

“JACK! JACK!” Annie barked again. “杰克！” “杰克！”安妮又大声喊道。

Jack looked up at her, his nose covered with snow. 杰克抬头看着她，鼻子上全是雪。

Annie stood perfectly still, her paw up. Jack sniffed the air. The wind now carried different smells that stood out from all the others: a whiff of fish and crackers mixed with wet fur. 安妮一动不动地站着，爪子举了起来。 杰克闻了闻空气。 现在，风里带着一些特别明显的气味：一点鱼和饼干的味道，混着湿漉漉的毛发味。

Amid the sounds of wind and ice, Jack heard distant barking. 在风声和冰声中，杰克听到了远处的狗叫声。

“BARRY!” Jack barked. He had been so busy discovering what it was like to be a dog, he’d almost forgotten Barry! “巴里！”杰克大声叫道。 他一直忙着体验当一只狗的感觉，差点把巴里给忘了！

Jack and Annie started yapping and bumping against each other. They bolted into the wind and the flying snow, scrambling over rocks and plowing through snowdrifts. Jack’s four legs were nimble and strong. The freezing cold didn’t bother him at all. He didn’t feel a bit stiff. None of his muscles ached. 杰克和安妮开始又叫又撞地玩耍起来。 他们冲进风雪中，翻过岩石，冲过雪堆。 杰克的四条腿又灵活又有力。 寒冷的天气一点也影响不了他。 他一点都不僵硬。 他的肌肉一点也不疼。

Jack and Annie ran this way and that, through the fresh mountain air, following sound and scent. As Barry’s barking grew louder, they slowed down. 杰克和安妮在新鲜的山风中跑来跑去，跟着声音和气味前进。 当巴里的吠叫越来越响时，他们放慢了脚步。

And then they saw him! 然后他们看见他了！

“BARRY!” Jack barked. “巴里！”杰克大声叫道。

Barry stood on a small ridge above them, his tail high in the air. When he saw Jack and Annie, he looked surprised. He sniffed the air. He cocked his head to the side. 巴里站在他们上方的一条小山脊上，尾巴高高举在空中。 当他看到杰克和安妮时，他看起来很惊讶。 他闻了闻空气。 他把头转向了一边。

“IT’S US!” barked Annie. “是我们！”安妮喊道。

Barry began yelping with glee. He leapt off the ridge, half tumbling, half running down the slope to greet them. 巴里高兴地大叫起来。 他从山脊上跳了下来，一边翻滚，一边跑下斜坡去迎接他们。

Barry’s giant head pushed against Jack and Annie. His eyes shone in the snowy afternoon light. As they all nudged each other and sniffed, three tails wagged in the air. Barry yowled and whined, “YOU’RE DOGS! HOW? HOW?” 巴里的大头撞上了杰克和安妮。 他的眼睛在雪后的下午阳光下闪闪发亮。 当他们互相推搡并嗅来嗅去时，三条尾巴在空中摇来摇去。 巴里尖叫着哭喊，“你们是狗！怎么了？ 怎么了？

“MAGIC!” barked Annie. “魔法！”安妮喊道。

Barry sneezed, “OH! OKAY!” Then he went down on his front paws and barked, “LET’S PLAY!” The three of them started play fighting. They stood on their back legs and pushed each other around. Jack nipped at Barry’s fur. Annie nibbled his ears. 巴里打了个喷嚏，“哦！ 好吧！”然后他用前爪趴下，喊道：“我们来玩吧！”他们三个开始打闹玩耍。 他们用后腿站立，互相推来推去。 杰克咬了咬巴里的毛。 安妮咬了他的耳朵。

Jack, Annie, and Barry bounced and barked and fell and fought, until finally they all collapsed into the snow and rolled over onto their backs, sticking their paws in the air. 杰克、安妮和巴里蹦蹦跳跳、汪汪大叫，摔倒了，还打起了架，直到最后他们都倒在了雪地里，翻过身来，把爪子伸向空中。

Rubbing his furry back against the crunchy snow felt wonderful to Jack. He wiggled and rocked from side to side. Then he and Annie and Barry stood up and gave their wet bodies good shakes. They shook their heads, too, flinging drool and snow on each other. 杰克把毛茸茸的后背贴在咯吱咯吱响的雪地上，感觉特别棒。 他左右摇晃着，扭来扭去。 然后他和安妮、巴里站了起来，用力抖了抖身上湿漉漉的身体。 他们也摇了摇头，朝对方吐口水和扔雪球。

But Jack didn’t mind drool a bit now. He was completely happy to be a big, furry dog playing in a snowstorm in the mountains. Even though the wind whipped against him and it was hard to see, he felt no fear. He was warm, his body was sturdy and strong, and he could smell and hear a whole new world of scents and sounds. 但杰克现在一点都不在意口水了。 他非常开心，变成了一只毛茸茸的大狗，在山里的暴风雪中玩耍。 虽然风刮得他睁不开眼，也很难看清东西，但他一点都不害怕。 他感觉很暖和，身体又结实又强壮，还能闻到、听到一个全新的世界里的各种气味和声音。

Relaxed and happy, Jack decided to give Barry some advice: “LISTEN, MAN,” he barked, “IF YOU WANT TO STAY WITH THE MONKS, YOU HAVE TO GET SERIOUS AND ACT RESPONSIBLE!” 放松又开心的杰克决定给巴里一点建议：‘听着，伙计，’他叫道，‘如果你想和修道士们在一起，你得认真一点，表现得负责任一些！’

Barry turned his giant head from side to side, listening to Jack. 巴里把巨大的脑袋左右转了转，听着杰克的话。

Jack barked: “DO NOT KNOCK PEOPLE OVER, AND WHEN THEY SAY, SIT, YOU SIT! SAME WITH COME AND STAY.” 杰克叫道：‘不要把人撞倒，当他们说坐下时，你就坐下！’ ‘坐下’和‘待着’也是一样。’

Barry looked curious. “HUH!” he barked. 巴里看起来很好奇。 “哇！”他喊道。

“DON’ T CHEW THINGS LIKE BOOKS,” Jack barked. “AND DON’ T CRY LIKE A PUPPY WHEN PEOPLE LEAVE YOU. YOU’ RE A BIG, FUNNY DOG. BUT YOU COULD BE A GREAT DOG!” “别像小狗一样咬书！”杰克喊道。 “别在人们离开你时像小狗一样哭哭啼啼。 你是一只又大又搞笑的狗。 但你可以成为一只很棒的狗！”

Before Barry could answer, Jack felt a distant tremor, and he heard a whumph! He knew that sound! He sprang to his feet and lifted a paw, listening. Annie and Barry scrambled to their feet and listened, too. 在巴里能回答之前，杰克感觉到远处传来一阵震动，还听到“砰”的一声！ 他认得这个声音！ 他跳了起来，抬起爪子，仔细听着。 安妮和巴里也赶紧站起来，听着。

Jack heard the sounds of rumbling and ice cracking and snow sliding. 杰克听到了隆隆声、冰裂声和雪滑动的声音。

Barry barked and bolted in the direction of the noises. Jack and Annie followed him down the pass. The three of them swerved around rocks and threaded their way along a narrow path. Then they stopped and whimpered and whined. 巴里冲着声音的方向大声吠叫，然后飞快地跑过去。 杰克和安妮跟着他穿过山口。 他们三个人绕过石头，沿着狭窄的小路穿行。 然后他们停了下来，呜咽着，哀叫着。

A pile of avalanche snow blocked their way. With barely a pause, Barry barreled on, his paws and powerful chest plowing through the snow, clearing a passage. 一堆雪崩的雪挡住了他们的路。 几乎没有停顿，巴里继续冲了上去，他的爪子和强壮的胸膛在雪中开路，清理出一条通道。

Jack and Annie plowed after Barry. Then they all stopped. A black hat was lying on top of the snow ahead of them! A knapsack with buckles lay next to it! 杰克和安妮也跟着巴里冲了过去。 然后他们全都停了下来。 他们前面的雪上放着一顶黑色的帽子！ 帽子旁边还放着一个带扣子的背包！

Barry, Jack, and Annie yelped and quivered as they sniffed the hat and the knapsack. Jack smelled wool, metal, and leather. 杰克、安妮和巴里闻到帽子和背包的味道时，吓得大叫起来，浑身发抖。 杰克闻到了羊毛、金属和皮革的味道。

“DIG! DIG!” Barry barked. “挖！ 挖！”巴里大声喊道。

Jack, Annie, and Barry swiped at the snow with their huge paws. Bucketfuls of snow flew behind them. Whimpering and whining, they dug deeper and deeper. The more snow they cleared, the more smells Jack inhaled—hair, wool, blood. 杰克、安妮和巴里用他们巨大的爪子刨着雪。 雪块从他们身后飞溅出去。 他们一边呜咽着，一边越挖越深。 他们清理的雪越多，杰克闻到的味道就越多——头发、羊毛、血液。

Barry yelped. He had uncovered a face: a young man’s face with brown hair matted against white skin, with bloody scratches and bluish lips. Jack remembered being caught in the avalanche and how the dogs had saved him. He barked at Barry, telling him to lick the man’s face. 巴里尖叫起来。 他挖到了一张脸：一个年轻人的脸，棕色的头发粘在苍白的皮肤上，脸上有血迹，嘴唇发青。 杰克记得自己曾被雪崩困住，还有那些狗救了他的命。 他对着巴里大叫，让它去舔那个男人的脸。

Barry swiped his warm tongue across the man’s eyes and nose and mouth. Barry snuffled and whined and kept licking. He frantically licked the man’s ears and hair. 巴里用温暖的舌头舔了那个男人的眼睛、鼻子和嘴巴。 巴里闻了闻，呜咽着，还在不停地舔。 他疯狂地舔着那个男人的耳朵和头发。

Jack and Annie stood back, watching Barry work to save the avalanche victim. Barry’s Saint Bernard instincts had taken over. He kept licking and licking, until the man’s eyelids fluttered open. The man blinked. Then he blinked again. He was alive! He tried to speak, but no sound came from his mouth. 杰克和安妮站在一旁，看着巴里努力地救那个雪崩的受害者。 巴里的圣伯纳犬本能接管了身体。 他不停地舔啊舔，直到那个男人的眼皮微微睁开。那个男人眨了眨眼。 然后他又眨了一次。他活过来了！ 他试着说话，但嘴里发不出任何声音。

Then something else seemed to grab Barry’s attention. The dog jumped up and held his nose in the air. He barked, “STAY!” to Jack and Annie. “I’LL BE BACK! DIG!” Then he scrambled down the snowy passage and disappeared. 然后，似乎有别的东西吸引了巴里的注意。 狗跳了起来，把鼻子举到空中。 他对着杰克和安妮叫道：“STAY！” “我马上回来！ DIG！”然后他沿着雪地里的通道爬下去，消失不见了。

“BARRY!” Annie barked. She started to go after him. “巴里！”安妮叫道。 她开始追他。

“HE TOLD US TO STAY!” Jack barked. “KEEP DIGGING!” “他让我们待着！”杰克叫道。 “继续挖！”

Jack and Annie dug deeper and deeper into the snow. They uncovered the rest of the man’s body—his blue, white, and red uniform with brass buttons, and his leather boots. They began licking his hands. They licked and licked until his fingers trembled. Jack and Annie barked with joy. 杰克和安妮在雪里挖得越来越深。 他们发现了那人的身体——他穿着蓝白红三色的制服，上面有铜纽扣，还有一双皮靴。 他们开始舔他的手。 他们不停地舔，直到他的手指颤抖起来。 杰克和安妮高兴地叫了起来。

Suddenly Jack felt a shock pass through his body. It shot through his head, chest, back, and four legs. He and Annie were hurled away from the man in the snow. The world went black, and Jack felt his whole body shaking wildly. 突然，杰克感觉一股电流穿过他的身体。 电流冲进了他的头、胸、背和四条腿。 他和安妮被猛地甩开了雪地里的那个人。 世界一片漆黑，杰克感觉全身在剧烈颤抖。

Finally Jack was still. He opened his eyes. In a daze, he saw the French soldier lying in the snow nearby. Jack wasn’t a Saint Bernard anymore. He was a person again. 最后，杰克终于不动了。 他睁开了眼睛。 他迷迷糊糊地看到附近雪地里躺着一名法国士兵。 杰克不再是圣伯纳犬了。 他又变回了人。

Annie was a person, too. “I guess the magic hour just ended,” she said. 安妮也变回了人。 “我想魔法时间刚刚结束了，”她说。
