6. Good Dog? 6. 好狗吗？

Barry dragged Jack out the door of the dining hall into the yard. The air was cold and damp. The sky was gray, and the mountain peaks were shrouded in mist. 巴里把杰克从餐厅的门拽到院子里。 空气又冷又潮湿。 天空灰蒙蒙的，山峰被雾气笼罩着。

“Barry, sit!” said Annie. “巴里，坐下！”安妮说。

But Barry didn’t sit. Instead, he put his nose to the ground and pulled Jack all around the yard. He smelled the snow and the rock walls. Then he raised his head and closed his eyes, sniffing the air. 但巴里没有坐下。 相反，他把鼻子贴到地上，把杰克拉得在院子里转了一圈。 他闻到了雪和岩石墙的味道。 然后他抬起头，闭上眼睛，闻着空气。

What does he smell? Jack wondered. Jack couldn’t smell anything in the cold mountain air. 他闻到了什么味道呢？杰克想知道。 杰克在寒冷的山间空气中什么也闻不到。

Annie ran to Jack and Barry and scratched Barry behind his ear. “Come on, Barry! Let’s get to work!” she said with enthusiasm. 安妮跑到杰克和巴里身边，挠了挠巴里耳朵后面。 “来吧，巴里！ 我们开始工作吧！”她兴奋地说。

The big dog opened his eyes and wagged his tail. His whole body seemed to be wagging. 大狗睁开眼睛，摇起了尾巴。 他的全身都在摇晃。

“Now, Barry, to start with, you have to learn three commands,” Annie said. “Come, sit, and stay.” “现在，巴里，首先你要学会三个指令，”安妮说。 “来，坐下，待着。”

Barry sneezed as if to say, No problem! 巴里打了个喷嚏，好像在说，没问题！

Annie turned to Jack. “When he obeys a command, we give him lots of praise. We should act really positive and upbeat. That’s what my book said.” 安妮转向杰克。 “当他听从指令时，我们要给他很多表扬。 我们应该表现得非常积极和兴奋。 我的书上就是这样说的。”

Jack rolled his eyes. 杰克翻了翻白眼。

“Positive,” Annie repeated. “Upbeat. Let’s start with come. I’ll hold him. You walk away and then stop and say, ‘Come, Barry!’ ” “积极向上，”安妮重复道。 “充满活力。 我们先从‘来’开始。我会牵着它。 你走开，然后停下来，说‘来，巴里！’

Jack sighed and handed Annie the leash. She struggled to keep Barry with her as Jack walked about twenty feet away. Jack turned and said, “Come, Barry!” Annie let go of the leash. Barry charged toward Jack. When he reached him, he jumped up and put his paws on Jack’s shoulders. Jack fell backward into the snow. 杰克叹了口气，把牵引绳递给安妮。 她努力让巴里跟在身边，而杰克则走开了大约二十英尺远。 杰克转身说：“来，巴里！”安妮松开了牵引绳。 巴里冲向杰克。 当他靠近时，他跳起来，用爪子按在杰克的肩膀上。 杰克向后倒进雪里。

“Oww!” said Jack. “哎哟！”杰克说。

“Good dog!” Annie said, clapping. “好狗狗！”安妮边拍手边说。

Barry leaned over and slurped his tongue across Jack’s face and ears. 巴里弯下身子，伸出舌头舔了舔杰克的脸和耳朵。

“Yuck! Stop, stop!” said Jack. He scrambled to his feet before Barry could give him more sloppy kisses. “真恶心！ 停下，停下！”杰克说。 他赶紧站起来，还没等巴里给他更多的湿漉漉的亲吻。

Annie ran over and patted Barry as she repeated, “Good dog, good dog.” 安妮跑过来，一边拍着巴里，一边重复道："好狗狗，好狗狗。"

Barry’s eyes were bright and happy. He panted with short quick breaths, as if he were laughing. 巴里的眼睛又亮又开心。 他喘着气，呼吸又短又快，好像在笑一样。

“Good dog?” said Jack. “You’ve got to be kidding.” “好狗狗？”杰克说道。 “你该不会是开玩笑吧。”

“Yes! He did what you said! You said ‘come,’ and he came!” said Annie. “Come on, Jack. Positive! Upbeat!” “是的！ 他照你的话做了！ “你说了‘来’，他就来了！”，安妮说。 “快点，杰克。积极点！ 充满活力！”

“Oh, sorry,” said Jack, wiping his ears with his sleeve. “I love your slobber, Barry. It’s my favorite thing.” “哦，对不起，”，杰克说，用袖子擦着耳朵。 “我超爱你的口水，巴里。 这是我最喜欢的东西。”

“Okay, say that all over again, but use a happy voice,” said Annie. “好吧，再重新说一遍，但要用快乐的语气，”，安妮说。

“Don’t be ridiculous,” said Jack. “Come on, let’s move along.” “别胡说八道，”杰克说。 “来吧，我们走吧。”

“Right,” said Annie. “I think he’s got come. Here, take the leash back, and we’ll do sit. Ready, Barry? Sit!” “对，”安妮说。 “我想他来了。 拿着牵引绳，我们来坐下吧。准备好了吗，巴里？ 坐下！

Annie pushed down on Barry’s rear end, and Barry sat. “Good dog!” Annie said. “Now pull the leash up, Jack, so he lifts his head.” 安妮按住巴里的屁股，巴里就坐下了。 “好狗！”，安妮说。 “现在，杰克，把牵引绳拉上来，让他抬起头来。”

Jack tugged on the leash, pulling Barry’s head up. The Saint Bernard looked into Jack’s eyes. Then he leapt up and licked Jack’s face with his huge pink tongue. Jack yelped and fell backward again into the snow. 杰克拉了拉牵引绳，把巴里的头提了起来。 圣伯纳犬看着杰克的眼睛。 然后他跳起来，用他巨大的粉红色舌头舔了杰克的脸。 杰克尖叫着，又向后倒进雪里。

Before Barry could pounce on him, Jack scrambled to his feet, nearly tripping on his robe. “Oh, man!” he said to Barry. “What is wrong with you?” 在巴里扑向他之前，杰克赶紧站起来，差点被他的长袍绊倒。 “哦，天哪！”他对巴里说。 “你哪里不对劲？”

“Nothing’s wrong with him, Jack,” said Annie. “He just likes you!” “他没什么不对劲，杰克，”安妮说。 “他只是喜欢你！”

“Well, tell him not to like me so much,” said Jack. “那告诉他别这么喜欢我，”杰克说。

“Come on, Jack. Be positive,” Annie said. “来吧，杰克。 “要保持积极，”安妮说。

“No. I’m done with training,” said Jack. “You can do it by yourself. I’m going inside to read about flora and fauna in our research book.” “不。” “我受够训练了，”杰克说。 “你可以自己做到的。 我要回屋里去，看看我们的研究书，读一读关于动植物的内容。”

“But it’s easier with two of us,” said Annie. “Just one more command? Please?” “但是两个人一起更容易，”安妮说。 “再教我一个指令好吗？求你了？”

Annie looked hopefully at Jack. Barry looked at Jack, too. His tail had stopped wagging. 安妮满怀希望地看着杰克。 巴里也看着杰克。 他的尾巴已经不再摇晃了。

“Oh, okay,” said Jack, sighing. “One more command.” “哦，好吧，”杰克叹了口气说。 “再下一条指令。”

“Yay, thanks!” said Annie. “太好了，谢谢！”安妮说。

Barry barked and wagged his tail. 巴里叫了起来，摇着尾巴。

“Let’s try the stay command,” said Annie. “We’ll teach Barry to go down on all fours and stay—like all the dogs in the dining hall this morning.” “我们试试‘待着’这个指令吧，”安妮说。 “我们要教巴里四脚着地趴下，就像今天早上餐厅里所有的狗一样。”

“He’ll never be that calm,” said Jack. “他永远不会那么安静的，”杰克说。

Annie handed him the leash. “Here. Now try to gently pull Barry’s head down while I press between his shoulder blades. See, I memorized all this stuff. Okay, Jack?” 安妮把牵引绳递给他。 “给。” 现在试着轻轻拉巴里的头向下，同时我用手按在他肩膀之间。 看，我把这些都记住了。好吧，杰克？

“Yeah, yeah,” Jack said. He knelt in front of Barry and gently pulled down on the leash. “是啊，是啊，”杰克说。 他跪在巴里面前，轻轻地拉了拉牵引绳。

Annie pushed Barry’s shoulders with the palms of her hands. Barry didn’t move. “I can’t find his shoulder blades,” said Annie. “Try this, Jack. Pull on his front legs. Pull them out in front of him.” 安妮用手掌推了推巴里的肩膀。 巴里一动不动。 “我找不到他的肩胛骨，”安妮说。 “试试这个，杰克。 拉他的前腿。 把他前面的腿拉出来。”

Jack grabbed Barry’s forelegs and pulled. Barry jumped up and put his paws on Jack’s shoulders again, knocking him into the snow for the third time. Then the giant dog flopped onto Jack’s chest, pinning him to the ground. 杰克抓住巴里的前腿，拉了拉。 巴里跳起来，又把爪子搭在杰克的肩膀上，第三次把他推到了雪里。 然后那只大狗扑到杰克的胸口，把他压在地上。

“Get off!” gasped Jack. “I can’t breathe!” But Barry’s brown eyes just stared happily at Jack. His cold, wet nose sniffed Jack’s face. “Get-off-me-now-please!” commanded Jack. “快下来！”杰克喘着气说。 “我喘不过气了！”但巴里的棕色眼睛只是开心地盯着杰克。 他冰冷、湿漉漉的鼻子嗅了嗅杰克的脸。 “现在立刻下来！”杰克命令道。

Annie laughed. “You really love Jack, don’t you, Barry?” she said. 安妮笑了。 “你真的很喜欢杰克，对吧，巴里？”她说。

Barry answered, YES! with a big sneeze right in Jack’s face. Then he bounded to his feet. 巴里回答，YES！ 然后他对着杰克的脸打了个大喷嚏。 然后他一下子跳了起来。

“Eww, Jack!” said Annie, laughing. “哇，杰克！”安妮笑着说。

“Gross!” cried Jack, wiping his face with the rough sleeve of his robe. “Okay, that’s it.” He scrambled to his feet. “I’m done. I’m so done with this. See you guys inside.” “真恶心！”杰克一边用长袍粗糙的袖子擦脸，一边喊道。 “行了，就这样吧。”他赶紧站起来。 “我受够了。 我真受够了。 你们待在里面吧。”

“Wait, we’ll go with you,” said Annie. “Come on, Barry. We’ll do more training later. But you did a really great job! What a good dog!” “等等，我们和你一起去，”安妮说。 “来吧，巴里。 我们待会儿再训练。” 但是你做得真的很好！好样的，好狗！

“Oh, brother,” said Jack. “哦，哥哥，”杰克说。

Barry sneezed again and gave his body a shake. Annie held the door, and the big dog trotted into the monastery, his head held high. 巴里又打了个喷嚏，然后抖了抖身子。 安妮扶着门，那只大狗神气地走进修道院。
