5. He’s All Yours 5. 他全都是你的

“Come on, hurry,” said Annie, pulling on her shoes. “快点，快点，”安妮一边说，一边穿鞋子。

“But we have to look in our book,” said Jack, “and try to figure out—” “但我们得先看看我们的书，”杰克说，“然后试着弄清楚——”

“Let’s look after we talk to Brother Michael,” said Annie. “He’s leaving soon. And I have a feeling that he—or someone else here—is going to help us.” “我们先跟迈克尔兄弟谈谈，再看书，”安妮说。 “他很快就走了。 我觉得他——或者这里的某个人——会帮我们的。”

“Let’s hope,” said Jack. “希望如此，”杰克说。

Still sore from the avalanche, Jack limped a little as he followed Annie out of their room. They passed all the closed doors lining the long torch-lit hallway, until finally they came to the parlor. From the dining hall, they could hear the clinking of silverware and china, but no voices. 因为雪崩还疼着，杰克跟着安妮走出房间时，走路有点一瘸一拐。 他们经过长长的、有火炬照明的走廊两侧一排排关着的门，直到最后来到客厅。 从餐厅里，他们能听到银餐具和瓷器叮叮当当的声音，但听不到任何人的说话声。

Jack and Annie peered into the room and saw about forty monks sitting on benches on either side of a long table. Brother Michael waved from one end. The other monks didn’t even look up. They all kept their heads bowed as they silently finished eating their noontime meal. 杰克和安妮往房间里张望，看到大约四十个修士坐在一张长桌的两边，坐在长凳上。 迈克尔修士从桌子的一端朝他们挥手。 其他修士甚至没有抬头看他们。 他们全都低着头，默默地吃完中午的餐点。

Jack and Annie crossed the dining hall, weaving among a dozen Saint Bernards resting with their heads on their paws. None of them had Barry’s crazy energy. 杰克和安妮穿过餐厅，在十几只圣伯纳犬中间穿行，这些狗都趴在地上，头放在爪子上休息。 它们之中没有一只像巴里那样精力充沛。

Jack and Annie sat at the end of a bench across from Brother Michael. One of the other monks served them bread and butter, bowls of porridge, and cups of hot tea. 杰克和安妮坐在长椅的尽头，正对着迈克尔神父。 其他一位修士给他们端来了面包和黄油、碗里的粥，还有热茶。

Jack took a sip of the milky, sweet tea. Then he leaned across the table to Brother Michael. “Excuse me,” he whispered, “but is there another place in these mountains—a place not too far from here—where we might find flowers?” 杰克喝了一口这牛奶味甜的茶。 然后他趴在桌子上，对迈克尔神父说。 “对不起，”他小声说，“但是这些山里还有别的地方吗？一个离这儿不太远的地方，我们可能会找到花吗？”

Brother Michael looked thoughtful. Then he shook his head. “Certainly not now. It has been a very cold spring. But a month from now, at a lower altitude, you will definitely find flowers.” 迈克尔神父看起来若有所思。 然后他摇了摇头。 “现在肯定没有。 今年春天特别冷。 但一个月后，在海拔更低的地方，你们一定能找到花。”

“Not until a month from now?” said Jack. “要等到一个月后吗？”杰克说。

“I am afraid not,” said Brother Michael. He smiled curiously at Jack. “You must love flowers very much.” “恐怕是这样，”迈克尔兄弟说。 他好奇地朝杰克笑了笑。 “你一定非常喜欢花。”

Jack didn’t know what to say. How could he possibly explain that they had to find a white and yellow flower and live its meaning for an hour to help break a spell to save Merlin’s penguin, who’d been turned to stone by a young sorcerer in Camelot? 杰克不知道该说什么。 他怎么才能解释，他们必须找到一朵白花和黄花，然后花一小时体会它的含义，才能打破一个咒语，拯救被卡梅洛特的一个年轻巫师变成石头的梅林的企鹅呢？

“Um … yes, I do like them,” said Jack. “嗯……是的，我确实喜欢花，”杰克说。

“Perhaps you could go on a climbing trip with Father Laurent if you come back this way in the summer,” said Brother Michael. “He also loves plants and flowers and is an expert on the flora of the Alps.” “如果你夏天再来这里，也许可以和劳伦特神父一起去爬山旅行，”迈克尔兄弟说。 “他也很喜欢植物和花，还是阿尔卑斯山植物方面的专家。”

Jack nodded. He picked up a chunk of bread and took a small bite. That would be great, he thought, except we can’t wait! His only hope was that their research book could help them somehow. 杰克点了点头。 他拿起一块面包，咬了一小口。 这会很棒，他想，只是我们不能等！ 他唯一的希望是他们的研究书能以某种方式帮上忙。

By now, all the monks were getting up from the table. The Saints quietly followed them out of the dining hall. As Jack ate his bread and watched everyone leave, Barry bounded out of the kitchen. He grabbed the last bite of bread right out of Jack’s hand and gulped it down! 到这时候，所有修道士都从桌边站了起来。 圣徒们默默地跟着他们走出了餐厅。 当杰克吃着面包，看着大家离开时，巴里从厨房里蹦了出来。 他一把从杰克手里抢走了最后一口面包，一口吞了下去！

“Hey!” said Jack. “嘿！”杰克说。

Annie burst out laughing. “Barry! You thief!” she cried. 安妮突然笑出声来。 “巴里！” “你这个小偷！”她喊道。

Without a word, Brother Michael grabbed Barry by the collar. But the dog broke loose from the monk and ran around the room. He went down on his forelegs. He barked his happy, booming bark, as if daring them to catch him. Brother Michael一声不吭，抓住了Barry的衣领。 但那只狗挣脱了修道士，绕着房间跑了起来。 它用前腿跪了下来。 它发出欢快、响亮的吠声，仿佛在挑战他们来抓它。

Annie laughed again. 安妮又笑了起来。

Brother Michael sighed. “I apologize for the theft of your bread,” he said to Jack. “May I get you some more?” Brother Michael叹了口气。 “我对偷走你们的面包表示道歉，”他对杰克说。 “我可以再给你拿一些吗？”

“No, no, it’s okay,” said Jack. “不用了，不用了，没关系，”杰克说。

Barry’s barks shook the dining hall. 巴里的吠叫震得餐厅都在晃。

“Excuse me, I will take him to the kitchen,” Brother Michael said. He grabbed Barry by the collar again. This time he held on tight and pulled him into the kitchen. “对不起，我要带他去厨房，”迈克尔兄弟说。 他又一把抓住巴里的领子。 这次他紧紧抓住，把巴里拽进了厨房。

The dining hall was empty now, except for Jack and Annie. “Listen, we can’t waste any more time,” said Jack, rising from the table. “I’m going back to our room to read about flora and fauna of the Alps.” 现在餐厅里只剩下杰克和安妮。 “听着，我们不能再浪费时间了，”杰克说完，从桌边站起来。 “我要回我们的房间，去读关于阿尔卑斯山动植物的书。”

“I’ll go with you,” said Annie. “我跟你一起去，”安妮说。

As Jack and Annie were leaving, Brother Michael came out of the kitchen alone. “I apologize for Barry,” he said, frowning. “He will be leaving us tomorrow.” 杰克和安妮正要离开时，迈克尔神父独自从厨房里走了出来。 “我对巴里刚才的行为表示歉意，”他皱着眉头说。 “他明天就要离开我们了。”

“Why? Where’s he going?” asked Annie. “为什么？ “他去哪儿了？”安妮问。

“Down to a house in the valley,” said the monk. “去山谷里的一座房子，”和尚说。

“But he seems so happy here,” said Annie. “但他在这里看起来很开心，”安妮说。

“Oh, he is very happy here,” said Brother Michael. “Too happy. It will break his heart to leave.” “哦，他在这里非常开心，”迈克尔兄弟说。 “太开心了。 离开的话，他会伤心欲绝。”

“So why does he have to leave?” said Annie. “那他为什么必须离开呢？”安妮说。

“Because he has a very independent spirit, and none of us has the time to give him the extra training he needs,” said Brother Michael. “因为他有一颗非常独立的心，而我们谁也没有时间给他额外的训练，”迈克尔兄弟说。

“Well, what if I try to train him?” said Annie. “好吧，我要试着训练他。”安妮说。

“What?” Jack looked at her in surprise. “什么？”杰克惊讶地看着她。

“No, really. I can do it,” Annie said. She turned back to the monk. “You can give that job to me. I know about training dogs. And I’m really good with animals. Right, Jack?” “不，真的可以。” 安妮说：“我能行。” 她转身面对那个修士。 “你可以把那份工作交给我。” 我知道怎么训练狗。 我和动物们相处得特别好。对吧，杰克？

“Yeah, but—” said Jack. “是的，但是——”杰克说。

“Please, let me try,” Annie said to Brother Michael. “请让我试试，”安妮对迈克尔兄弟说。

“You are very kind. However—” Brother Michael was interrupted by howling from the kitchen. The monk shook his head. “Listen to him! He will cry night and day if he has to leave us. And Father Laurent shall miss him terribly.…” He paused and looked at Annie. “Perhaps we should give him one more chance, if you really think you can handle him. Shall I put him in your charge today, and we will see what training you can give him?” “你非常善良。” 然而——”迈克尔兄弟被厨房里传来的嚎叫声打断了。 那个修士摇了摇头。 “听他说话！ 如果他必须离开我们，他就会哭个不停。 而且劳伦特神父会非常非常想念他。…“他停顿了一下，看着安妮。 “也许我们应该再给他一次机会，如果你真的觉得你能对付他的话。 今天我把他交给你照顾，看看你能给他什么样的训练？

“Yes!” said Annie. “好！”安妮说。

“Good. Then I will bring him to you,” said Brother Michael, and he headed back to the kitchen. “很好。 然后我会带他来找你，”兄弟迈克尔说，然后他回到厨房去了。

“Are you nuts?” Jack whispered to Annie. “You don’t know how to train a crazy dog like that.” “你疯了吗？”杰克低声对安妮说。 “你不知道怎么训练这样一只疯狗。

“Actually, I do,” said Annie. “For your information, I just read a book on training dogs. I even wrote a book report about it.” “其实我知道，”安妮说。 “告诉你吧，我刚刚读了一本关于训练狗的书。 我甚至还写了一篇读书报告呢。”

“But you haven’t had any practice,” said Jack. “And even if you had, we don’t have time now. What about saving Penny? Did you forget our mission?” “但是你从来没实践过，”杰克说。 “就算你实践过，我们现在也没有时间。 救出佩妮怎么办？ 你忘记我们的任务了吗？”

“No. I was thinking this would give us an excuse to stay here longer,” said Annie. “不。” “我在想这会给我们一个理由，让我们在这里多待一会儿，”安妮说。

“Yeah … okay,” said Jack. “But stay for what? Brother Michael said there are no flowers around here now. We’d have to stay for a month.” “嗯……好吧，”杰克说。 “但是等什么呀？ 迈克尔哥哥说，现在这里附近没有花。 “我们得在那里待一个月。”

“Okay, okay,” said Annie. “Listen, what about the magic potion? We can change into anything we want for an hour.” “好吧，好吧，”安妮说。 “听着，那瓶魔法药水呢？ 我们可以变成我们想变成的东西一小时。”

“Yeah. So … ?” said Jack. “对。” “所以呢？”杰克说。

“Well … do you think we could wish to change into something like … like official finders of white and yellow flowers?” asked Annie. “那……你觉得我们能不能许愿变成……变成专门寻找白色和黄色花朵的人？”安妮问。

“No, that’s—” said Jack. “不，这太——”杰克说。

“Stupid, yeah,” said Annie. “So let’s think.” “是啊，很蠢。”安妮说。 “那我们得好好想想。”

Before they had time to think, Brother Michael came out of the kitchen. He was carrying monks’ robes and had Barry on a leash. 还没等他们想好，迈克尔神父就从厨房出来了。 他拿着修士的长袍，还牵着巴里的一条狗绳。

The huge dog dragged Brother Michael over to Jack and Annie, swinging his tail and barking with excitement. 那只大狗把迈克尔神父拽到了杰克和安妮面前，摇着尾巴兴奋地叫着。

“You can work with him in the enclosed yard,” said Brother Michael. He pointed to a door off the dining hall. “And you may want to wear these over your clothes, as it’s quite cold outside.” He handed them the monks’ robes. “你们可以在围起来的院子里和他一起工作，”迈克尔神父说。 他指着餐厅旁边的门。 “你们可能想把这件衣服穿在衣服外面，因为外面很冷。”他把修道士的长袍递给了他们。

Jack and Annie pulled on the robes and tied the rope belts around their waists. Then they lifted their hoods over their heads. 杰克和安妮穿上长袍，把绳子腰带系在腰上。 然后他们把兜帽拉到头上。

“Perfect!” said Annie. “Much warmer!” “太完美了！”安妮说。 “暖和多了！”

“Good,” said Brother Michael. “He’s all yours!” He handed Barry’s leash to Jack. Jack gripped it with both hands as the big dog tugged on the other end. “很好，”迈克尔修士说。 “他现在归你了！”他把巴里的狗绳递给了杰克。 杰克用两只手握紧狗绳，而大狗则在另一端使劲拉。

“All of us will be gone from the monastery for the afternoon,” said Brother Michael. “If you take a walk, you must not let Barry off the leash. Whatever you do, do not let him run freely outside. I am certain he would dash off to explore the mountains and get lost in the snow.” “今天下午，我们所有人都会离开修道院，”迈克尔修士说。 “如果你们去散步，一定不能让巴里挣脱狗绳。” 不管你做什么，都不要让他在外面乱跑。 我确定他会跑出去探索山里，然后在雪地里迷路。

“Don’t worry. We’ll keep him in the yard,” said Annie. “别担心。 我们会让他待在院子里，”安妮说。

“I am very grateful for your help,” said Brother Michael. “Perhaps this will even assist you with your research about the Alps.” “非常感谢你们的帮助，”迈克尔兄弟说。 “也许这还能帮到你研究阿尔卑斯山。

“I’ll bet it will,” said Annie. “Well, good-bye. Good luck!” “我敢打赌会的，”安妮说。 “好吧，再见。祝你好运！

Brother Michael smiled. “I fear you are the ones who will need luck today,” he said. “Be good, Barry.” Then the rosy-faced monk hurried off to catch up with the others. 迈克尔兄弟微笑了。 “我担心今天需要好运的人是你们，”他说。 “要乖，巴里。”然后，红脸的修道士匆匆忙忙地去追上其他人。

“Okay! Let’s get started!” said Annie. “Come on, Barry!” She threw open the door to a snowy yard surrounded by a rock wall. “好的！ “让我们开始吧！”安妮说。 “快点，巴里！”她猛地推开一扇门，门外是一个被岩石墙围起来的雪地院子。

Barry instantly lurched toward the yard. 巴里立刻朝院子冲了过去。

“Arrgh!” cried Jack as the dog nearly yanked his arms off. “啊！”杰克喊道，因为那只狗差点把他手臂拽下来。
