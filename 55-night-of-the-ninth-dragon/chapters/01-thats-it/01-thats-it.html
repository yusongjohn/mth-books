1. That’s It? 1. 那就是它吗？

The day was cloudy and windy. Jack was sitting on the front porch, using a yellow marker to highlight a book called Caring for Your New Puppy. 那天天气阴沉，风很大。 杰克坐在前廊上，用一支黄色的记号笔标出一本书，书名叫《照顾你的新小狗》。

“Fetch, Oki!” Annie shouted, throwing a ball across their yard. “去捡球，奥基！”安妮喊道，把球扔到了他们的院子里。

Yip, yip! A scruffy black-and-white puppy raced after the ball and caught it in his mouth. “Bring it back!” Annie called. But Oki dashed away from her instead. 汪汪！ 一只又脏又乱的黑白小狗飞快地追着球，用嘴叼住了球。 “把球带回来！”安妮喊道。 但奥基却转身跑开了。

“He never does what you say,” Jack said with a laugh. “他从来都不听你的话，”杰克笑着说。

“Yes, he does,” said Annie. “He fetched! He just decided not to bring it back.” She ran after Oki and wrestled the ball away from him. “Ready to go to the dog park now?” she called to Jack. “是的，他听了，”安妮说。 “他去拿了！ 他只是决定不把它带回来。”她追着奥基跑过去，把球从他手里抢了过来。 “现在准备去狗狗公园了吗？”她对杰克喊道。

“Yep.” Jack highlighted a paragraph about puppy dental care. Then he closed the book and dropped it into his pack along with his yellow marker. “All set.” “准备好了。”杰克用黄色记号笔标出一段关于小狗牙齿护理的段落。 然后他合上书，把它和黄色记号笔一起放进背包里。 “都准备好了。”

“Did you pack supplies?” said Annie. “你带好用品了吗？”安妮说。

“Just his leash and water bowl,” said Jack. “And some puppy treats.” He pulled the leash out of his pack. “只带了他的狗绳和水碗，”杰克说。 “还有一些小狗零食。”他从背包里拿出牵引绳。

“Wait. Let’s see if he’ll walk with us on his own,” said Annie. “等等。 我们看看他会不会自己和我们一起去，”安妮说。

“If I know him, he’ll take off,” said Jack. “如果我了解他的话，他会跑掉的，”杰克说。

“Come on, give him a chance. I’ve been working with him,” said Annie. “Watch. Oki! Come!” The little dog ran to Jack and Annie. “来吧，给他一个机会。 我一直在和他一起工作，”安妮说。 “看。奥基！ “来！”小狗狗跑向杰克和安妮。

“See?” said Annie as they started down the sidewalk. “看到了吗？”安妮边说边和杰克一起沿着人行道走下去。

“Do you really think he’ll stay with us?” said Jack, putting the leash back in his pack. “你真的觉得他会一直和我们在一起吗？”杰克说着，把狗绳放回了自己的背包里。

“Sure,” said Annie. “当然可以，”安妮说。

“Hey, do you have any money?” Jack asked. “嘿，你有没有带钱？”杰克问。

“What for?” said Annie. “干什么？”安妮说。

“I thought we could stop at the pet store,” said Jack. “We need to get him a dog toothbrush and toothpaste. I was just reading that canine tooth care is very important. You have to—” Before Jack could finish, Oki yipped and dashed away down the sidewalk. “我想我们可以去宠物店停一下，”杰克说。 “我们需要给他买牙刷和牙膏。 我刚刚读到，狗狗的牙齿护理非常重要。 你必须—”杰克还没说完，奥基就汪汪叫着跑下人行道了。

“Oki, wait! Oki!” shouted Annie. “Stay!” “奥基，等等！ “奥基！”安妮喊道。 “留下！”

But the puppy kept running. He crossed the street, bounded over the curb, and disappeared into the Frog Creek woods. 但是小狗一直跑着。 它穿过街道，跳过了路沿，消失在蛙溪镇的树林里。

“I knew it!” Jack said. “我知道了！”杰克说。

“Oki!” Annie shouted. She and Jack raced into the woods after the puppy. Tree branches were waving in the wind. Dry autumn leaves shook and rustled. “奥基！”安妮喊道。 她和杰克冲进树林追赶小狗。 树枝在风中摇摆。 干枯的秋叶摇晃着，发出沙沙声。

“Oki!” yelled Jack. “奥基！”杰克喊道。

“You were right!” wailed Annie. “We should have put him on the leash!” “你没错！”安妮哭喊着。 “我们应该把它拴上绳子的！”

“Don’t worry, we’ll find him,” said Jack. “别担心，我们会找到他，”杰克说。

“Oki!” they called. “Oki!” “奥基！”他们喊道。 “奥基！”，

Yip, yip! 汪，汪！

“Did you hear that?” said Jack. “你听到吗？”杰克说。

“Yes!” Annie and Jack took off running between the trees. They followed the yipping sounds—until they found the puppy at the base of a giant oak tree. “听到了！”安妮和杰克在树之间跑了起来。 他们跟着小狗的叫声跑——直到他们在一棵巨大的橡树下找到了小狗。

The magic tree house was nestled high in the treetop. The rope ladder was swaying from side to side. 神奇的树屋静静地藏在树顶高处。 绳梯在两边摇晃着。

“Good boy, Oki!” Annie said. She picked up the puppy and giggled as he licked her face. “How did you know it was back?” “好样的，奥基！”安妮说。 她抱起小狗，笑嘻嘻地让他舔她的脸。 “你怎么知道它回来了？”

“Teddy?” Jack called, looking up at the tree house. “Teddy?” “泰迪？”杰克朝树屋喊道。 “泰迪？”

There was no answer, and no one looked down from the window. 没有人回答，窗户里也没有人往下看。

“Let’s climb up,” said Annie. “There must be a message inside.” “我们上去吧，”安妮说。 “里面一定有信息。”

Yip? Yip? 咦？咦？

“Yes, you’re coming with us!” Annie said to Oki. “Here, get in Jack’s bag.” Jack took off his backpack, and Annie lowered the puppy inside. “Is that going to be too heavy?” “对，你要和我们一起去！”安妮对奥基说。 “来，钻进杰克的包里。”杰克摘下背包，安妮把小狗放了进去。 “这会不会太重了？”

“No, he doesn’t weigh much,” said Jack, pulling on the pack. “Let’s go, buddy.” Jack carried Oki up the rope ladder, and Annie followed. “不会，他不重，”杰克一边说，一边拉起背包。 “我们走吧， buddy。”杰克背着奥基爬上绳梯，安妮跟在后面。

When they had all climbed into the tree house, Jack took off his pack and set it on the floor. Oki scampered out and began sniffing every corner. 当他们都爬进树屋后，杰克脱下背包放在地上。 奥基蹦蹦跳跳地跑出来，开始嗅遍每个角落。

“I don’t see anything here,” said Annie, looking around. “我这里什么也没看到，”安妮环顾四周说。

“Me neither,” said Jack. There was no message from Merlin, no book from Morgan. “There’s nothing here to tell us what to do.” “我也没看到，”杰克说。 梅林没有留任何信息，摩根也没有带来任何书。 “这里什么也没有，告诉我们该做什么。”

Yip, yip, yip! Oki was looking out the window, barking at the woods. 咦，咦，咦！ 奥基正趴在窗边，对着树林汪汪大叫。

Jack looked up and saw a scrap of paper floating on the wind. “Hey, is that our note?” he said. 杰克抬头一看，发现一张纸片被风吹着飘在空中。 “嘿，那是我们的便条吗？”他说。

“It must have blown out of the window!” said Annie. “I’ll get it.” She hurried back down the rope ladder. Oki kept barking as Annie chased after the paper and finally snatched it from the ground. “肯定是从窗户里被吹出去的！”安妮说。 “我去拿回来。”她赶紧顺着绳梯爬下来。 安妮追着纸片跑，奥基还在不停地汪汪叫，最后安妮终于从地上捡起了那张纸。

Annie read the note to herself and smiled. 安妮自己读了读便条，然后露出了微笑。

“What does it say?” Jack called. “上面写着什么？”杰克喊道。

“Good news!” said Annie. “好消息！”安妮说。

“What? What is it?” said Jack. “什么？” “这是什么？”杰克说。

“See for yourself!” said Annie. She scrambled back up the ladder and handed Jack the scrap of paper. “Our favorite place to visit.” “自己看看吧！”安妮说。 她赶紧爬回梯子，把那张纸条递给了杰克。 “我们最喜欢去的地方。”

Jack looked at the old-fashioned handwriting: 杰克看着那古老的手写体：

Dear Jack and Annie, 亲爱的杰克和安妮。

Please come to Camelot. 请到卡梅洛特来。

“That’s it?” said Jack. “就这些吗？”杰克说。

“It’s good news, right?” said Annie. “这是好消息，对吧？”安妮说。

“Yeah, but it doesn’t look like Merlin or Morgan’s handwriting, or Teddy’s….I don’t understand. Who wrote this? And why?” said Jack. “是的，但看起来不像梅林或摩根，或者泰迪的字迹……我不明白。谁写的这封信？ “为什么？”杰克说。

“It doesn’t matter,” said Annie. “It’s an invitation to Camelot! I love visiting Camelot, don’t you?” “没关系，”安妮说。 “这是去卡梅洛特的邀请函！ 我喜欢去卡梅洛特，你不也喜欢吗？

“Of course,” said Jack. They had visited the kingdom many times. He loved its orchards and the Great Hall and Morgan’s magnificent library. Most of all, he loved their friends in Camelot. “But something’s weird.” “当然，”杰克说。 他们已经去过那个王国很多次了。 他喜欢那里的果园、大礼堂和摩根那座宏伟的图书馆。 他最喜欢的是卡梅洛特的朋友。 “但是有些奇怪。”

“Don’t worry, Jack. Let’s just go,” said Annie. “Get ready, Oki!” She pointed at the word Camelot on the note. “I wish we could go there!” “别担心，杰克。 “我们走吧，”安妮说。 “准备好了，奥基！”她指着纸条上写着卡梅洛特的那句话。 “真希望我们能去那里！”，

Yip! Yip!

Jack grabbed Oki and held him tightly. 杰克抓住奥基，紧紧地抱着他。

The wind blew harder. 风刮得更猛了。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转起来。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后，一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
