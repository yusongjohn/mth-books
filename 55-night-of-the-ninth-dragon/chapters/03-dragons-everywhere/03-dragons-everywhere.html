3. Dragons Everywhere 3. 到处都是龙

“Oh, wow…a secret garden,” whispered Annie. She put Oki down and unhooked his leash, and the puppy dashed into the lush greenery. “哦，哇……一个秘密花园，”安妮小声说。 她把奥基放下来，解开他的项圈，小狗立刻冲进了茂密的绿丛中。

Guinevere led Jack and Annie through the doorway and into the walled garden. As they followed her down a stone path, they passed tangles of cream-colored lilies, butter-yellow daffodils, blue periwinkles, and pale-pink roses. 桂妮薇尔带杰克和安妮穿过门口，走进了有围墙的花园里。 当他们跟着她沿着石板路走时，他们经过了一丛丛奶油色的百合花、淡黄色的郁金香、蓝色的海葵花和淡粉色的玫瑰花。

“From her plants, Morgan makes inks and paints for the books of our scribes,” explained Guinevere, “and dyes for the wool of the castle weavers.” “从她的植物中，摩根为我们的抄写员制作墨水和颜料，”桂妮薇尔解释道，“还为城堡的织工制作羊毛染料。”

“Cool,” said Annie. “真酷，”安妮说。

Guinevere pointed to raised earth beds in the center of the garden. “She grows flowers and herbs for healing everyday illnesses and injuries,” she said. 桂妮薇尔指着花园中央隆起的土床。 “她种植花朵和草药，用来治疗日常的疾病和伤痛，”她说。

“Like what?” asked Jack. “比如什么？”杰克问。

“Nettles for a cold,” said Guinevere, “and mint for headaches.” She pointed to a fishpond surrounded by yellow-green grasses and white flowers. “Lemon-grass for fever, and yarrow to stop bleeding.” “荨麻治疗感冒，”桂妮薇尔说，“薄荷治疗头痛。”她指着一个被黄绿色草和白色花朵环绕的鱼塘。 “柠檬草治疗发烧，鼠尾草可以止血。”

Jack shivered. The air felt tingly and alive. Why did he imagine they were being watched? Did he hear breathing? Whispering? The flutter of wings? Or was it just the wind blowing through the garden? 杰克打了个寒战。 空气感觉有点刺痒，还充满生机。 为什么他觉得他们好像被人盯着看？ 他听到呼吸声了吗？听到耳语了吗？ 翅膀扑扇的声音？ 还是只是风吹过花园？

Oki started barking. He bounced up and down, yelping at something in the shrubbery. When Jack and Annie hurried to him, they found a small bronze statue of a dragon. 奥基开始吠叫起来。 他蹦蹦跳跳地来回跑，对着灌木丛里的什么东西大声吠叫。 当杰克和安妮匆忙跑过去时，他们发现了一座小青铜龙雕像。

Jack laughed. “It’s not a real dragon, Oki,” he said. “It’s just a statue.” 杰克笑了。 “这不是真正的龙，奥基，”他说。 “这只是一个雕像。”

Annie started to pick up the puppy. But Oki dashed to a patch of pink roses and barked again. Jack and Annie followed him and found a statue carved from pink granite. “Another dragon!” said Annie. 安妮开始捡起小狗。 但是奥基冲到一片粉红色的玫瑰丛中，又大声吠叫起来。 杰克和安妮跟着他，发现了一座由粉红色花岗岩雕刻而成的雕像。 “又有一条龙！”，安妮说道。

Oki kept bounding around the garden, yelping at more and more hidden dragon statues: a clay dragon tucked behind clay pots, an emerald dragon hidden among the ivy, a marble dragon between white stones, a silver dragon in tall silvery grass, a glass dragon and a dragon made from seashells in the shadows of the rock wall. 奥基继续在花园里蹦跳，对着越来越多隐藏在角落里的龙雕像吠叫：一条藏在陶土花盆后面的陶土龙，一条藏在常春藤中的翡翠龙，一条夹在白色石头之间的大理石龙，一条在高高的银色草地里的银色龙，还有在岩石墙阴影中的一条玻璃龙和一条由贝壳制成的龙。

“Dragons everywhere!” said Jack. “到处都是龙！”，杰克说道。

Annie picked up the puppy and tried to calm him. “Don’t worry, Oki,” she said, “they’re just garden decorations.” 安妮抱起小狗，试图安抚他。 “别担心，奥基，”她说，“它们只是花园装饰而已。”

“No,” the queen said. “These dragons are far more than garden decorations.” She looked very serious. “Perhaps you have heard of King Arthur’s father, Uther Pendragon?” “不，”女王说道。 “这些龙远不止是花园装饰。”她看起来非常严肃。 “也许你听说过亚瑟王的父亲，尤瑟·潘德拉贡？”

“I’ve read about him,” said Jack. “Doesn’t Pendragon mean ‘Chief Dragon’?” “我读过关于他的书，”杰克说。 “Pendragon不是‘首席龙’的意思吗？”

The queen nodded. “Indeed, it does,” she said. “The emblem on King Uther’s flag and shield was a dragon. And now a dragon adorns Arthur’s flag and shield as well. The dragon is the symbol of the ruler of Camelot.” 女王点了点头。 “确实如此，”她说。 “国王尤瑟的旗帜和盾牌上的标志是一条龙。 现在亚瑟的旗帜和盾牌上也有一条龙。 龙是卡梅洛特统治者的象征。”

“Got it,” said Annie. “明白了，”安妮说。

“Before he died, Uther Pendragon ordered his craftsmen to create nine dragon statues. Each statue was to be made from a different material,” said the queen. “在他去世前，尤瑟·潘德拉贡命令他的工匠制作了九座龙雕像。 每座雕像都要用不同的材料制成，”女王说。

“And those statues are the ones here in the garden?” said Jack. “这些雕像就是花园里的那些吗？”杰克问道。

“Yes,” said the queen. “Merlin placed a spell on each of them. As long as the dragons are here in Morgan’s garden, the kingdom’s enchanters can unlock the portals to any of the nine mythical realms of Camelot.” “是的，”女王说。 “梅林给每个雕像施了魔法。 只要龙还在摩根的花园里，王国的法师们就能打开通往卡梅洛特九个神话王国的传送门。”

“Wow,” said Annie. “哇，”安妮说。

“What are the nine mythical realms?” asked Jack. “We’ve never heard of them.” “那九个神话王国是什么？”杰克问。 “我们从来没听说过它们。”

“They are lands that cannot be traveled to directly from mortal worlds,” said the queen. “And actually, you do know about some of them. With Merlin’s help, you have made journeys to four of the mythical realms.” She pointed to the silver dragon in the grass. “That dragon opens the portal to the realm of Timeless Treasures. I believe Merlin sent you there one Christmas.” “它们是无法直接从凡人世界到达的地方，”女王说。 “实际上，你们对其中一些是知道的。 在梅林的帮助下，你们已经去过四个神话王国。”她指着草地上那只银龙。 “那条龙会打开通往永恒宝藏之境的门户。” 我相信梅林在圣诞节那天派你们去那儿的。

“He did,” said Annie. “That was our mission to find the Water of Memory and Imagination.” “他做到了，”安妮说。 “我们的任务就是找到记忆与想象之水。”

“And the marble dragon opens the portal to the Land-Behind-the-Clouds,” said Guinevere. “而且那颗大理石龙会打开云后之地的传送门，”桂妮薇尔说。

“Where we met the Ice Wizard!” said Jack. “我们在那里遇到了冰法师！”杰克说。

“The bronze dragon is the gateway to the realm of the Raven King,” said the queen. “And the sea-shell statue to the realm of the Sea Serpent.” “青铜龙是进入乌鸦王领地的入口，”女王说。 “而海螺雕像则是进入海蛇领地的入口。”

“Where we changed into seals,” said Annie. “我们在那里变成了海豹，”安妮说。

“And met Kathleen,” said Jack. “还遇到了凯瑟琳，”杰克说。

“There are five realms you have not visited,” said the queen. “Each of the other dragons unlocks a portal to one of them. But the most important dragon is the gold dragon, for it unlocks the portal to the most magical realm of all: the Isle of Avalon. And that is where Morgan and Merlin and Teddy and Kathleen are right now. A fortnight ago, they journeyed to Avalon for a respite.” “你们还没有去过五个王国，”女王说。 “其他的每条龙都能打开通往其中一个王国的传送门。 但最重要的是金龙，因为它能打开通往最神奇王国的传送门：阿瓦隆岛。 而安妮、摩根、梅林和泰迪现在就在那里。 两周前，他们前往阿瓦隆岛去休息一下。

“What’s a respite?” asked Annie. “什么是休息？”安妮问。

“It’s like a vacation, right?” said Jack. “就像度假，对吧？”杰克说。

“Yes, but in this case, a vacation with a bit of magic,” said the queen. “Morgan and Merlin go to Avalon to rest and renew themselves. There is a vast garden there, filled with the ancient secrets of healing. It is the only place where Morgan can grow plants that mend all wounds.” “是的，但这次的度假带有一点魔法，”女王说。 “摩根和梅林去阿瓦隆岛是为了休息和恢复精力。 那里有一个巨大的花园，里面充满了古老的治愈秘密。 这是唯一一个摩根可以种植能治愈所有伤口的植物的地方。

“Cool,” said Annie. “真酷，”安妮说。

“So when will they come back to Camelot?” asked Jack. “那他们什么时候会回到卡梅洛特呢？”杰克问。

The queen sighed. “The ninth statue—the gold dragon—occupies the most important place in the garden,” she said. “There, surrounded by those rosebushes.” She pointed toward clusters of bright yellow roses in the far corner of the garden. 女王叹了口气。 “第九座雕像——金龙——在花园里占据最重要的位置，”她说。 “在那里，在那些玫瑰丛旁边。”她指着花园角落里一丛亮黄色的玫瑰。

Jack and Annie hurried over to the rosebushes. But there was no dragon. They looked back at Guinevere. “Um…I don’t see a dragon here,” said Jack. 杰克和安妮赶紧跑向玫瑰丛。 但那里没有龙。 他们又看向桂妮薇尔。 “呃……我这里没看到龙，”杰克说。

“No,” said the queen. “That is because the ninth dragon has been stolen.” “不，”女王说。 “那是因为第九条龙被偷走了。”

“Oh, no!” said Annie. “哦，天哪！”安妮说。

“When the invaders plundered the castle, they found their way into this garden,” said the queen. “Since they were after gold, the theft of the gold dragon was likely a simple act of greed. But when they took it from its place in the garden, the portal to Avalon closed. Without the ninth dragon, Merlin, Morgan, Teddy, and Kathleen are locked out of Camelot forever.” “当入侵者洗劫城堡时，他们找到了进入这个花园的路，”女王说。 “因为他们想要黄金，偷走黄金龙很可能只是出于贪婪。 但当他们把它从花园里的位置拿走时，通往阿瓦隆的门户就关闭了。 没有第九条龙，梅林、摩根、泰迪和凯瑟琳将永远被锁在卡梅洛特之外。”

“Forever?” breathed Jack. “永远？”杰克低声说。

“Yes,” said Guinevere. “Avalon is where they are, and Avalon is where they will remain—unless we find the ninth dragon.” “是的，”桂妮薇尔说。 “他们现在在阿瓦隆，他们也将一直留在那里——除非我们找到第九条龙。”

“We have to find it,” said Annie. “We have to help them all get back!” “我们得找到它，”安妮说。 “我们必须帮助他们全都回去！”

“Yes,” said the queen. “And there is another reason to find the gold statue. I told you that I returned to the castle yesterday after I received terrible news. A messenger came from the east to tell me that King Arthur had been mortally wounded.” “对，”女王说。 “还有另一个理由要找到那尊金雕像。 我告诉过你，我昨天收到可怕的消息后回到了城堡。 一个信使从东方来告诉我，亚瑟王受了致命的伤。

“Mortally wounded? What does that mean?” Annie asked. “致命的伤？ “那是什么意思？”安妮问。

Oki whined as if he understood. 奥基抱怨着，好像他明白了。

Guinevere took a deep breath. “An arrow pierced the king’s armor and plunged deep into his chest, close to his heart. If King Arthur does not journey to Avalon soon, he will die.” 桂妮薇尔深吸了一口气。 “一支箭射穿了国王的铠甲，深深扎进他的胸口，靠近他的心脏。” “如果亚瑟王不尽快前往阿瓦隆，他就会死去。”
