7. Run for Your Lives! 7. 快跑，为了你们的生命！

Oki barked furiously at the three men on horseback. 奥基对着那三个骑马的人狂吠。

“Oki! Quiet!” Jack commanded. “奥基！ 安静！”，杰克命令道。

“Tell them your sister and your parents have a disease,” said Annie. “告诉他们，你姐姐和你的父母得了病，”安妮说。

“What?” said Jack. He could barely hear Annie above Oki’s barks. “Quiet!” he said. But the puppy kept barking. “什么？”杰克说。 他几乎听不清安妮在奥基的吠叫中说的话。 “安静！”，他说。 但是小狗一直叫个不停。

“I have a terrible disease, Jack,” Annie said. “Here, take the reins.” She pushed them into Jack’s hands. Then she grabbed his backpack and started digging through it. “杰克，我得了重病，”安妮说。 “来，拿着缰绳。”她把缰绳塞进杰克手里。 然后她抓起他的背包，开始翻找。

“What are you doing?” he asked. “你在干什么？”他问。

“I have a disease,” Annie said, keeping her head down. “我得了病，”安妮说，低着头。

“What do you mean?” said Jack. “你是什么意思？”杰克说。

Annie said something, but Jack couldn’t hear her. 安妮说了一些话，但杰克听不见。

Oki barked louder as the riders galloped closer and closer. As they pulled up in front of the cart, Jack saw that the men had grimy faces, bristly beards, and knotted hair down to their shoulders. 随着骑手们越来越近，奥基的叫声也越来越响亮。 当他们停在马车前时，杰克看到那些人的脸上满是污垢，长着粗硬的胡须，头发乱糟糟地垂到肩膀上。

“Halt!” the biggest man ordered. “停住！”那个最高大的人命令道。

Jack pulled on the reins to stop the oxen. Oki barked and growled from the cart. 杰克拉住缰绳，让牛停下来。 奥基从马车上跳出来，大声吠叫并咆哮。

“Quiet!” Jack ordered the puppy. He was gripping the reins so tightly his knuckles were white. “安静！”杰克命令小狗。 他握着缰绳的力道太大，手背都变得发白了。

The huge rider glared at him with small, mean eyes. “Who are you?” he snarled. 那个高大的骑手用一双又小又凶的眼睛盯着他。 “你是谁？”他咆哮道。

“I’m Jack and she’s my sister, Annie,” said Jack. He tried to keep his voice steady. “我是杰克，她是我的姐姐安妮，”杰克说道。 他努力让自己的声音保持平稳。

Annie didn’t look up. She was still hunched over the backpack. Jack had no idea what she was doing. 安妮没有抬头看。 她仍然弯着腰看着背包。 杰克完全不知道她在干什么。

The rider glanced toward the back of the cart. “Who lies there?” he asked. 骑手朝马车后面看了一眼。 “那里躺着谁？”他问。

“Tell them about our disease, ” Annie said, keeping her head down. “告诉他们我们的病，”安妮说，低头看着地面。

“Um…our mother and father…they’re sick,” said Jack. “And my sister—she’s sick, too.” “呃……我们的爸爸妈妈……他们生病了，”杰克说。 “还有我姐姐——她也生病了。”

“Bad disease!” hissed Annie. “是种很严重的病！”安妮低声说。

“They all have a bad disease!” Jack had to shout to be heard above Oki’s barking. “他们全都得了很严重的病！”杰克不得不大声喊，才能盖过奥基的吠叫。

“What disease?” growled the rider. “什么病？”骑手低声吼道。

“I’m sorry, what?” said Jack. “对不起，什么？”杰克说。

“What is their bad disease?” the man said. “他们的坏病是什么？”那人说。

“Well, it, uh…it’s—” Jack stammered. “嗯，那个……它是——”杰克结结巴巴地说。

“Yellow fever!” Annie shouted. She threw off her hood and looked up at the men. “Run for your lives!” “黄热病！”安妮喊道。 她摘下兜帽，抬头看着那些男人。 “快跑吧，你们的命要紧！”

Jack nearly fell off the cart. Annie looked terrible! Her face was covered with bright yellow spots! 杰克差点从车上摔下来。 安妮看起来糟透了！ 她的脸上布满了亮黄色的斑点！

The men pulled back. Grunting and growling, they spurred their horses past the cart. The horses broke into a gallop, and the thieves thundered away. 那些男人往后退开了。 他们一边咕哝着，一边催促着马匹从马车旁疾驰而过。 马儿开始奔跑，小偷们也飞快地逃走了。

“Well, how rude was that?” said Annie, laughing. “哎呀，那也太不礼貌了吧？”安妮笑着说。

“What happened to you?” cried Jack, staring at her. “你怎么了？”杰克惊讶地看着她喊道。

Annie held up the yellow marker from Jack’s backpack, the one he’d used to highlight the puppy book. 安妮举起了一块黄色的标记笔，那是杰克背包里的，他用它来标出那本小狗书的。

Jack burst out laughing, too. “Oh, man, that was quick thinking.” 杰克也忍不住笑起来。 “哦，天哪，你真是机灵。”

“Thank you!” said Annie. “谢谢你！”安妮说。

Jack looked back at the king and queen. Arthur had slept through the whole incident. Guinevere was sitting up, holding Oki. The puppy was still barking at the fleeing horsemen. 杰克回头看向国王和王后。 亚瑟整个事件过程中都睡着了。 桂妮薇尔正坐着，抱着奥基。 那只小狗还在对着逃跑的骑士们汪汪叫。

“What happened?” the queen asked. “Oki was barking so loudly, I could not hear. Where did those men go?” “发生了什么？”女王问道。 “奥基叫得太响了，我听不见。 那些人去哪儿了？”

“Annie saved us, Your Majesty,” said Jack. “She tricked them. I don’t think we’ll be seeing them again.” “是安妮救了我们，陛下，”杰克说。 “她骗了他们。 我想我们不会再见到他们了。”

“What did you do, Annie?” said the queen. “你做了什么，安妮？”女王说。

Annie turned around and grinned. Guinevere cried out in alarm. 安妮转过身，咧嘴笑了。 桂妮薇尔惊叫起来。

“It’s okay!” said Annie, laughing. “I just marked my face with this!” She held up Jack’s highlighter. “I told them we all had yellow fever.” “没关系！”安妮笑着说。 “我只是用这个在脸上画了点东西！”她举起了杰克的荧光笔。 “我告诉他们我们大家都得了黄热病。”

“Oh. Oh!” said Guinevere. “I see!” Then she started to laugh, too. “哦。 哦！”桂妮薇尔说。 “我明白了！”然后她也开始笑了。

“All right! Let’s get this show back on the road!” said Jack. Their success had lifted his spirits. He shook the reins. “Hike!” “好吧！ 让我们重新出发吧！”杰克说。 他们的成功让他心情好了起来。 他摇动了缰绳。 “前进！”

The oxen started forward, and the queen settled back in the cart. Jack pulled his notebook out of his pack and handed it to Annie. “Read the riddle again,” he said. “We’ll figure it out.” 牛开始向前走，女王则在马车里坐稳了。 杰克从背包里拿出他的笔记本，递给安妮。 “再读一遍谜题，”他说。 “我们会想出办法的。”

Annie read aloud: “Moon so bright. Munith Mor night. Curtain of white.” 安妮大声读道：“月亮如此明亮。穆尼特·莫尔之夜。 白色的窗帘。”

“Curtain of white,” Jack broke in. “That’s probably just a white curtain, right? Like a window curtain inside a house?” “白色的窗帘，”杰克插话道。 “那可能只是白色的窗帘，对吧？ 像房子里的窗帘一样？”

“Maybe,” said Annie. “Or a curtain in front of a closet?” “也许吧，”安妮说。 “或者像衣柜前的窗帘？”

“Maybe,” said Jack. “Keep reading.” “也许吧，”杰克说。 “继续读下去。”

Annie read more: “Hides from sight.” 安妮接着读：“隐藏起来。”

“Wait,” said Jack. “Does that mean the curtain hides from sight? So it’s a hidden curtain?” “等一下，”杰克说。 “这意思是窗帘把东西藏起来了？” 所以这是个隐藏的窗帘？

“Maybe,” said Annie. “Or maybe…the curtain is hiding something else?” “也许吧，”安妮说。 “或者……窗帘后面藏着别的东西？”

“Yes!” said Jack. “That makes more sense. Keep reading.” “对！”杰克说。 “这样更有道理。继续读下去。”

Annie read on: “Magic for flight. Do you think magic for flight is what the curtain is hiding?” 安妮继续读道：“飞行的魔法。 你觉得飞行的魔法就是窗帘后面藏着的东西吗？

“Yes!” said Jack again. “I’ll bet the robbers hid the gold dragon behind a white curtain.” “对！”杰克又说了一遍。 “我敢打赌，强盗们把金龙藏在了白色的窗帘后面。”

“That’s it!” said Annie. “就是这样！”安妮说。

“But I wonder where they are,” said Jack. “但是我想知道他们现在在哪里，”杰克说。

“Who? The robbers?” asked Annie. “谁？” “强盗？”安妮问。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “What if we run into them when we find the place with the curtain?” “对，”杰克说。 “如果我们找到挂着窗帘的地方时碰上他们怎么办？”

“Well, we could try the yellow marker trick again,” said Annie. “那我们可以再试试黄色标记的技巧，”安妮说。

“But if it’s nighttime?” said Jack. “但如果那时是晚上呢？”杰克问。

“Hmm, maybe not,” said Annie. “Let’s just find the white curtain first.” “嗯，可能不行，”安妮说。 “那我们先找到那块白色的窗帘吧。”

“Okay,” said Jack. “Read the last lines.” “好，”杰克说。 “读最后一行。”

Annie read aloud: “Before dawn’s light. Or lose the fight.” 安妮大声读道：“在黎明之前。” “否则就会输掉战斗。”

“That means we have to hurry,” said Jack. “那意味着我们必须赶快，”杰克说。

“Yep,” said Annie. “Hurry.” “对，”安妮说。 “快点。”

Dark clouds covered the sky as the cart creaked and rattled over the weedy path. The air was heavy with impending rain. Jack glanced back at the king and queen. The king was shivering as Guinevere wiped his forehead with a cloth. 乌云遮住了天空，马车在长满杂草的小路上吱呀作响，颠簸前行。 空气里弥漫着即将到来的雨的气息。 杰克回头看了看国王和王后。 国王正在发抖，桂妮薇尔用布擦着他的额头。

The queen looked up and caught Jack’s gaze. “He is very weak now,” she said. “But he never gives up. No matter how great the struggle, he and his knights never give up. They keep going, without food and rest, through wind and hail and freezing cold.” 王后抬起头，看见了杰克的目光。 “他现在非常虚弱，”她说。 “但他从不放弃。” 不管遇到多大的困难，他和他的骑士们从不放弃。 他们继续前进，没有食物，没有休息，穿越风雨和刺骨的寒冷。

“We do that, too,” said Annie. “我们也是这样做的，”安妮说。

“We never give up,” said Jack, sitting a little straighter. “我们从不放弃，”杰克说，坐得更直了一些。

“Thank you, Jack and Annie,” said Guinevere. “谢谢你们，杰克和安妮，”桂妮薇尔说道。

The oxen pulled the cart past deserted farms, thatched huts, and clusters of orchards. As they traveled farther across the countryside, there were fewer and fewer trees. Just before sunset, they came to an open plain that stretched from the base of a mountain. There was no road or path to follow. 牛拉着车经过荒废的农场、茅草小屋和成片的果园。 当他们继续向乡村深处前进时，树越来越少。 日落之前，他们来到了一片开阔的平原，一直延伸到山脚下。 这里既没有路，也没有小径可以走。

“Your Majesty, could this be the moorlands at the foot of Munith Mor that Cafelle talked about?” asked Annie. “陛下，这会不会是卡弗尔提到的，位于蒙斯莫尔山脚下的沼泽地呢？”安妮问道。

“Yes, it could be,” said Guinevere. “是的，可能是那里，”桂妮薇尔说。

“So we keep going,” said Jack. “Hike!” “那我们就继续前进，”杰克说。 “出发！”

The oxen started across the moorlands. Riding over the scrubby land toward the mountains, Jack searched for any sign of a human settlement. He looked in vain for a hut, a fort, a tent, a lighted window, or chimney smoke. Where will we ever find a white curtain? he wondered. 牛开始穿过沼泽地。 杰克骑着马，穿越着长满灌木的土地，朝山的方向前进，他四处寻找有人类居住的迹象。 他徒劳地寻找着小屋、堡垒、帐篷、亮着灯光的窗户或烟囱冒出的烟。 我们到底在哪里才能找到白色的窗帘呢？他心想。

As the sky darkened to dusk, the wind blew harder and a thin drizzle started to fall. Jack pulled off his cloak and held it out to the queen. “Your Majesty, please give this to King Arthur,” he said. 随着天空渐渐变暗，风也吹得更猛烈了，细雨开始落下。 杰克脱下斗篷，递给了女王。 “陛下，请把这个交给亚瑟王，”他说。

“Thank you.” Guinevere took Jack’s cloak and covered the sleeping king and Oki. Annie pulled her cloak off, too. “And for you, Your Majesty,” she said. “谢谢。”桂妮薇尔接过杰克的斗篷，盖在睡着的国王和奥基身上。 安妮也脱下了自己的斗篷。 “陛下，这是给您的，”她说。

“No, Annie, you must keep yourself warm,” said Guinevere. “不，安妮，你得让自己暖和点，”桂妮薇尔说。

“Please take it,” said Annie. “I’m fine. Really.” “请收下吧，”安妮说。 “我没事的。真的。”

The queen took the cloak and wrapped it around her shoulders. 女王拿过斗篷，披在自己肩上。

“You children are very kind,” she said. “你们这些孩子真善良，”她说。

“No problem,” said Jack, though he was freezing. The drizzle had quickly become a windblown rain. “没问题，”杰克说，尽管他冷得发抖。 雨很快变成了被风吹着的雨。

“Your Majesty, can you tell us about the Isle of Avalon?” asked Annie. “陛下，您能告诉我们关于阿瓦隆岛的事吗？”安妮问。

“I have never been there, Annie,” said Guinevere. “But Arthur has. He tells me it is a large island in a vast, hidden lake surrounded by mountains. It is covered with green pastures and groves of apple trees.” “我从来没去过那里，安妮，”桂妮薇尔说。 “但亚瑟去过。 他告诉我，那是一个位于一个巨大、隐藏湖泊中的大岛，四周被群山环绕。 那里有绿色的草地和苹果树丛。”

“Apple trees?” said Jack, shivering in the gloomy rain. “苹果树？”杰克在阴暗的雨中发抖着说。

“In truth, Avalon means ‘apples,’ ” said the queen. “But the isle has trees of all kinds, as well as flowers, birds, butterflies, and animals, both magical and real.” “事实上，阿瓦隆的意思是‘苹果’，”女王说。 “但这个岛上有各种各样的树，还有花、鸟、蝴蝶和动物，既有魔法的，也有真实的。”

Oki barked. “Yes, like you, ” the queen said, smiling at the puppy. “Arthur says that swans pull boats and mermaids swim in the lake.” 奥基汪汪叫。 “是的，就像你，”女王对小狗笑着说。 “亚瑟说天鹅能拉船，人鱼会在湖里游泳。”

“Mermaids!” exclaimed Annie. “Really?” “人鱼！”安妮惊呼道。 “真的吗？”，

“Yes, and Avalon has sunshine and gentle breezes and clear, starry nights all year long, and the air smells of lilac and lavender,” said the queen. As she spoke, her words seemed to soothe the elements: the rain clouds passed, the wind died down, and the air grew warmer. The nearly full moon was rising over the moorlands, shining brightly in the early-evening sky. “是的，而且阿瓦隆全年都有阳光和轻柔的微风，还有清澈的星空之夜，空气里还飘着丁香和薰衣草的香味，”女王说。 她说着，她的话似乎安抚了大自然：雨云散去了，风也停了，空气变得温暖起来。 将近满月的月亮正在沼泽地上升起，在傍晚的天空中明亮地照耀着。

“Sounds like heaven,” said Jack. “听起来像天堂，”杰克说。

“Our most beautiful dreams and happiest thoughts come from Avalon,” said Guinevere. “There is singing and poetry and playacting, dancing and games and running and swimming. I remember when Arthur journeyed there once with Merlin. He—” “我们最美丽梦想和最快乐的想法都来自阿瓦隆，”桂妮薇尔说。 “那里有唱歌、诗歌和戏剧表演，有跳舞、游戏、奔跑和游泳。” 我记得亚瑟曾经和梅林一起去过那里。他—

The queen’s story was interrupted by a sudden jolt. The cart jerked forward violently, and Jack and Annie were thrown from the bench onto the wet grass. 王后的讲述被突然的一阵颠簸打断了。 手推车猛地向前冲去，杰克和安妮从长椅上被甩了出去，摔在湿漉漉的草地上。
