2. Hail, Your Majesty! 2. 臣民们向您致敬！

The tree house was dark with shadows. 树屋被阴影笼罩，一片漆黑。

Yip! 咦！

Jack clutched Oki. He and the puppy both shivered in the damp, chilly air. 杰克紧紧抱着奥基。 他和小狗都因为潮湿寒冷的空气而发抖。

“Hey, our clothes didn’t change,” said Annie. She and Jack were still wearing their jeans, T-shirts, and sneakers. “嘿，我们的衣服没变，”安妮说。 她和杰克仍然穿着牛仔裤、T恤和运动鞋。

“Right…,” said Jack. “对……，”杰克说。

“Maybe the person who invited us didn’t know how to magically change our clothes,” said Annie. “也许邀请我们的人不知道怎么用魔法变我们的衣服，”安妮说。

“Who is that person?” said Jack. He looked out the window with Annie and Oki. “And where did we land, exactly?” “那个人是谁？”杰克说。 他和安妮、奥基一起看向窗外。 “我们到底降落在哪儿了？”

The tree house was in a forest of ancient-looking oak trees with gnarled roots and sprawling branches. The shadowy woodland was eerily quiet. No birds sang. No squirrels scurried over the ground. 树屋位于一片看起来古老的大橡树林中，树根盘结，树枝四处伸展。 这片阴暗的森林异常安静。没有鸟儿唱歌。 也没有松鼠在地面上奔跑。

“Are we sure this is Camelot?” asked Jack. “I wish we had a book to give us information.” “我们确定这是卡梅洛特吗？”杰克问道。 “但愿我们有一本书能给我们提供信息。”

“Let’s go down and get information firsthand,” said Annie. “我们下去亲自获取信息吧，”安妮说。

“Okay,” said Jack. He picked up Oki and put him inside his backpack. Then he pulled on the pack and followed Annie down the rope ladder to the forest floor. “好，”杰克说。 他捡起奥基，把它放进背包里。 然后他背上背包，跟着安妮沿着绳梯下到森林地面。

“So…which way?” Jack asked, looking around at the thick, towering oaks. Here and there, a shaft of sunlight shot through the treetops. “所以……往哪儿走？”杰克环顾四周，看着那些高大茂密的橡树问道。 阳光从树顶间时而射下一道道光束。

Oki sniffed the air and barked excitedly. 奥基闻了闻空气，兴奋地叫了起来。

“He wants to lead us somewhere,” said Annie. “他想带我们去什么地方，”安妮说。

“Okay, but let’s put him on the leash this time,” said Jack. Annie lifted the puppy and the leash out of his pack. But before she could fasten the leash to Oki’s collar, the puppy jumped from her arms and dashed off. “好吧，不过这次我们得把他的项圈拴上，”杰克说。 安妮从背包里拿出小狗和狗绳。 但就在她还没来得及把狗绳拴在奥基的项圈上时，小狗突然从她怀里跳了出去，飞快地跑开了。

“Oki! Come back!” said Jack. “奥基！ 回来！”杰克喊道。

“Stop!” cried Annie. “停下！”安妮喊道。

“Oh, brother, not again!” said Jack, breaking into a run. “Oki!” “哦，天哪，又来了！”杰克说着，撒腿就跑。 “奥基！”

“Oki, stop!” Annie yelled. “奥基，停下！”安妮喊道。

Racing through the woods, Jack and Annie chased Oki around one huge tree and then another. They chased him through pools of light and shade, and over fallen leaves, twigs, and acorns, until the puppy dashed from the dappled dark into a bright wheat field. 杰克和安妮在树林里狂奔，围着一棵大树又一棵大树追赶奥基。 他们追着他穿过光斑和阴影，跨过落叶、树枝和橡果，直到小狗冲出斑驳的黑暗，跑进一片明亮的麦田里。

“Gotcha!” Annie said, grabbing Oki. “抓住你了！”安妮说着，一把抓住了奥基。

“You’ve got to stick with us, buddy,” said Jack as Annie attached the leash to the puppy’s collar. “你得跟紧我们， buddy，”杰克说，安妮把绳子系在小狗的项圈上。

“Where are we now?” said Annie. “我们现在在哪里？”安妮说。

Tall yellow stalks of wheat blocked the view of the land beyond the field. All Jack could see was a faraway mountain range. 高高的黄色麦秆挡住了田地外的景色。 杰克只能看到远处的一片山脉。

“I don’t know. Let’s keep going,” said Jack. “我不知道。” “我们继续往前走，”杰克说。

With Oki pulling on his leash, Jack and Annie began pushing their way through the wheat. Morning sunlight shimmered on the tasseled stalks as they swayed in the wind. 奥基牵着绳子，杰克和安妮开始穿过麦田往前走。 早晨的阳光照在麦穗上，随着风轻轻摇摆。

“Oh, wow, look over there!” said Annie, pointing. Beyond the wheat, red flags waved from cone-shaped towers. Dragons decorated the flags. “King Arthur’s castle!” “哦，哇，快看那边！”安妮指着说。 麦田的另一边，红色的旗帜从锥形的塔楼上飘扬着。 旗帜上画着龙。 “这是亚瑟王的城堡！”

“Yes!” said Jack. He and Annie and Oki pushed through the stalks until they came to an open field where cut grass was piled into haystacks. “对！”杰克说。 他和安妮还有奥基穿过麦秆，直到来到一片空地，那里堆满了干草垛。

“Yay!” said Annie. “Let’s go, Oki!” The puppy pulled on his leash, and they all started running across the field toward the outer wall of the castle. When they drew close, a trumpet sounded. A guard appeared between two turrets. “太棒了！”安妮说。 “走吧，奥基！”小狗拽着绳子，他们全都开始跑过田野，朝城堡外壁跑去。 当他们靠近时，一声号角响了。 一名卫兵出现在两座箭塔之间。

The guard aimed a crossbow at them. 卫兵用弩箭对准了他们。

“Annie, stop!” Jack yelled. “安妮，停下！”杰克喊道。

Oki barked wildly. Annie scooped him into her arms. “We come in peace!” she called to the guard. 奥基疯狂地吠叫起来。 安妮把他抱进了怀里。 “我们是来和平的！”她对卫兵喊道。

“We have an invitation!” Jack shouted. “我们有邀请函！”杰克大喊。

The guard scowled and kept his crossbow aimed directly at them. 守卫皱起眉头，一直把弩箭对准他们。

“Jack! Annie!” someone called. A golden-haired woman in a green cape was hurrying through the gate in the outer wall. As she ran toward Jack and Annie, the guard slowly lowered his weapon. “杰克！ 安妮！”有人喊道。 一位金发女人穿着绿色斗篷，正匆匆穿过外城墙的门。 当她朝杰克和安妮跑来时，守卫慢慢放下了武器。

“Queen Guinevere!” said Jack. “桂妮薇尔女王！”，杰克说道。

“Oh, wow!” said Annie. “哦，哇！”，安妮说道。

“Hello! Hello!” Guinevere said when she reached Jack and Annie. “你好！ 你好！”，桂妮薇尔女王走到杰克和安妮面前说道。

“Hail, Your Majesty!” said Jack, bowing. “向您致敬，陛下！”，杰克鞠了一躬。

“Hail, Your Majesty!” said Annie as Oki tried to wriggle out of her arms. “This is Oki, our new puppy.” “万岁，陛下！”安妮说，奥基试图从她怀里挣脱出来。 “这是奥基，我们的新小狗。”

“Hello, Oki.” Though she smiled, Guinevere looked worried. Her golden hair was tangled, and her long gown was torn. “I am very glad to see all three of you,” she said. “你好，奥基。”虽然她微笑着，但桂妮薇尔看起来很担心。 她的金发凌乱不堪，长长的裙子也撕破了。 “我很高兴见到你们三个，”她说。

“Did you send the tree house for us, Your Majesty?” said Annie. “陛下，你们是为我们派来了树屋吗？”安妮说。

“Yes. Long ago, Morgan explained its magic to me,” said the queen. “She told me how to send for you if I ever needed your help.” “是的。 很久以前，摩根向我解释了它的魔法，”女王说。 “她告诉我，如果我需要你们的帮助，该如何召唤你们。”

“So…does that mean you need our help now?” said Jack. “那么……这意味着你现在需要我们的帮助吗？”杰克说。

“I will explain everything,” said Guinevere. “But it is not safe outside our castle walls. Come.” She beckoned for Jack and Annie to follow her back toward the gate. “我会解释一切，”桂妮薇尔说。 “但是城堡墙外不安全。 来。”她示意杰克和安妮跟着她往回走，朝城门方向去。

Not safe? Why? Jack wondered as he and Annie hurried after the queen. 不安全？为什么？ 杰克一边和安妮赶紧跟在女王后面，一边想。

Guinevere led them through the arched gateway. In the outer yard, Jack saw no sign of knights or servants—and no plowmen or stonecutters or carpenters working on the grounds. 桂妮薇尔领他们穿过拱形的城门。 在院子外面，杰克没有看到骑士或仆人——也没有工人在地面上干活，比如犁地的、凿石的或做木工的。

“Excuse me, Your Majesty, but are Merlin and Morgan here?” asked Annie. “And Teddy and Kathleen?” “陛下，对不起，但梅林和摩根在这里吗？”安妮问。 “泰迪和凯瑟琳呢？”

“No, they left a fortnight ago,” said Guinevere. “Only a small number of us are here in Camelot.” She waved at a guard beside the gate in the inner wall, and he raised the iron grid that sealed off the main entrance of the castle. “不，他们两周前就离开了，”桂妮薇尔说。 “只有少数人在这里的卡梅洛特。”她朝内墙旁边的守卫挥了挥手，守卫抬起了封住城堡主入口的铁栅栏。

Jack and Annie followed the queen across a wide courtyard. As Jack looked around, he saw that the stables and forge seemed to be empty. No grooms or blacksmiths were at work. “Where is everyone, Your Majesty?” he asked. 杰克和安妮跟着女王穿过一个宽阔的庭院。 杰克环顾四周，发现马厩和铁匠铺似乎都是空的。 没有马夫或铁匠在工作。 “陛下，大家都在哪儿呢？”他问。

“Ten days ago, enemy invaders came ashore in the distant east, and the king and his knights set out to defend the kingdom and push them back,” the queen said. “After anxious days with no news of the battle, we received word that several bands of the invaders had slipped past the king’s army and were heading toward Camelot. Before they arrived to plunder the castle, I led everyone who was still here to a hill fort hidden in the west. Yesterday I returned with only a few guards.” “十天前，敌军从遥远的东方登陆，国王和他的骑士们出发去保卫王国并把他们赶回去，”女王说。 “在焦急地等待了几天，没有战斗的消息后，我们收到了消息，说敌军的几个小队已经绕过了国王的军队，正朝卡梅洛特赶来。 在他们到达城堡抢劫之前，我带领这里剩下的所有人躲到了西边一个隐藏的山丘堡垒里。 昨天我只带着少数几个卫兵回来了。”

“Why did you come back?” Annie asked. “你为什么回来呢？”安妮问。

The queen sighed. Jack thought she looked as if she might cry. But then she steadied herself and spoke in a strong voice. “Because at the fort, I received terrible news.” 女王叹了口气。 杰克觉得她看起来好像要哭了。 但接着她稳了稳情绪，用坚定的声音说话了。 “因为在堡垒里，我收到了可怕的消息。”

“What news, Your Majesty?” asked Jack. “什么消息，陛下？”杰克问道。

“I will explain,” said the queen. “But first I must take you to a secret place.” Guinevere led them into the Great Hall, the main room of the palace. Beneath the vaulted ceiling, tables and benches were overturned. Broken earthenware platters and moldy crusts of bread littered the floor. Tapestries had been torn from the walls. “我会解释的，”女王说。 “但首先我必须带你们去一个秘密的地方。”桂妮薇尔带他们走进了大宴会厅，宫殿里的主要房间。 在高高的天花板下，桌子和长凳都被打翻了。 破碎的陶制盘子和发霉的面包屑散落在地上。 挂毯从墙上被撕了下来。

Jack remembered what the Great Hall had been like when he and Annie had attended a celebration there: warm, bright, and filled with music. “What happened?” he asked the queen. 杰克记得他和安妮以前在蛙溪镇的礼堂参加庆祝活动时，那里是温暖、明亮，到处都是音乐声。 “发生了什么？”他问女王。

“The invaders were looking for gold,” she said. “They stole gold candlesticks and goblets from the chapel, and gold plates and bowls from the hall.” The queen led Jack and Annie from the chilly hall into the castle kitchen and pantry. There was no clatter of cooks or scullions at work in the kitchen. No fire burned in the huge stone hearth. The wind whistled through the open windows. A rat scurried across the floor. “入侵者在找黄金，”女王说。 “他们从教堂偷走了镶金的烛台和酒杯，还从大厅偷走了镶金的盘子和碗。”女王带着杰克和安妮离开了寒冷的礼堂，走进了城堡的厨房和储藏室。 厨房里没有厨师和帮厨在忙碌的声音。 巨大的石壁炉里没有火在燃烧。 风从开着的窗户里呼啸而过。 一只老鼠在地板上窜来窜去。

“Whoa,” said Annie, stepping out of the way. Oki barked and squirmed in her arms. “哇，”安妮一边说，一边闪到一边。 奥基在她怀里叫着，扭来扭去。

Without even glancing at the rat, Guinevere led them out of the kitchen, across an inner courtyard, and up a dark stairway. 连看都没看那只老鼠一眼，桂妮薇尔就带他们离开了厨房，穿过一个内院，走上了一条昏暗的楼梯。

“Were Merlin and Morgan scared, too?” asked Jack, climbing after the queen. “Is that why they left?” “梅林和摩根也害怕吗？”杰克一边问，一边爬着跟在女王后面。 “这就是他们离开的原因吗？”

“Oh, no, Merlin and Morgan left with Teddy and Kathleen before word came of the invaders,” Guinevere said. “I believe they know nothing about what has happened to Camelot.” “哦，不，梅林和摩根在得知入侵者消息之前，就和泰迪、凯瑟琳一起离开了，”桂妮薇尔说。 “我相信他们什么都不知道，卡梅洛特发生了什么。”

“Can you send a message to them, Your Majesty?” asked Annie. “你能给他们捎个信吗，陛下？”安妮问。

“I am afraid that is impossible,” the queen said. She took a torch from the wall and hurried down a narrow passageway. Jack and Annie followed her to a heavy wooden door at the end. The queen opened the door and led them into a torch-lit room. “恐怕这不可能，”女王说。 她从墙上取了一支火把，匆忙走进一条狭窄的通道。 杰克和安妮跟着她来到尽头的一扇厚重木门前。 女王打开门，带他们走进了一个被火光照亮的房间。

Inhaling the sweet, woodsy smell of books, Jack recognized the room at once. “Morgan’s library!” he murmured. Shelves covered the walls from floor to ceiling. The shelves were filled with books from all times and places. 杰克闻到书本散发出的甜美木质香味，立刻认出了这个房间。 “这是摩根的图书馆！”，他小声说道。 书架从地板一直延伸到天花板，覆盖了整面墙。 书架上摆满了来自不同时代和地方的书籍。

“Fortunately, the thieves were not interested in books,” said the queen. “They did not steal a single one.” “幸运的是，那些盗贼对书不感兴趣，”女王说道。 “他们连一本书都没偷走。”

“Good, I’m glad,” said Jack. Books were more valuable to him than gold. “太好了，我很高兴，”杰克说。 对杰克来说，书比黄金更有价值。

“Excuse me, Your Majesty,” said Annie, “but where did Morgan and Merlin go? And Teddy and Kathleen? And why can’t you send word to them?” “陛下，打扰了，”安妮说，“但是摩根和梅林去哪儿了？ 泰迪和凯瑟琳呢？ 而且你为什么不能给他们送个信呢？“

“I’ll explain in a moment,” said the queen. She crossed the room and opened another heavy wooden door. Damp, fragrant air rushed in from a lush garden surrounded by tall stone walls. “我马上解释，”女王说。 她穿过房间，打开另一扇沉重的木门。 潮湿而芬芳的空气从被高大石墙环绕的茂密花园中涌了进来。

“The answers to all your questions are here,” said Queen Guinevere, “in Morgan le Fay’s secret garden.” “你所有问题的答案都在这里，”桂妮薇尔女王说，“在摩根勒菲的秘密花园里。”
