9. Fire Dragon 9. 火龙

Jack followed Annie over the wet rocks to the waterfall. In the moonlight, he could barely make out a hollow space in the cliff behind it. 杰克跟着安妮跨过湿漉漉的石头，来到瀑布前。 在月光下，他勉强看清楚了瀑布后面悬崖上一个空洞的地方。

“That looks like a cave!” said Annie. “那看起来像一个洞穴！”安妮说。

“A cave that hides from sight behind a curtain of white,” said Jack. “一个藏在白色帘幕后面的洞穴，”杰克说。

Yip! Yip! Oki popped his head out of Jack’s backpack. 咦！咦！ 奥基把头从杰克的背包里探了出来。

The little dog kept barking as Jack and Annie slipped behind the waterfall and made their way inside the cave. 小狗一边叫，一边陪着杰克和安妮溜到瀑布后面，走进了洞穴。

The air in the cramped cave smelled of wet rocks and mold. Jack had to bend down to keep his head from hitting the ceiling, and Oki tumbled out of his backpack. 狭小的洞穴里空气潮湿，散发着岩石和霉味。 杰克得弯下腰，以免头撞到天花板，奥基从他的背包里滚了出来。

Before Jack could catch him, the puppy dashed off. 杰克还没来得及抓住他，小狗就跑开了。

“Oki!” cried Annie. “奥基！”安妮喊道。

Yip! Yip! 汪！汪！

Oki’s barking came from deep within the cave. “We’re coming!” called Annie. She and Jack moved their hands along the cave wall to guide their way. As they followed the sound of Oki’s barks and whines, the cave became more like a tunnel. The ceiling was so low that Annie and Jack had to get on their hands and knees and crawl in the blackness toward Oki’s cries. 奥基的吠声从洞穴深处传来。 “我们来了！”安妮喊道。 她和杰克沿着洞穴的墙壁摸索着前进，指引自己的方向。 随着他们跟着奥基的吠叫和呜咽声前进，洞穴变得越来越像一条隧道。 天花板非常低，安妮和杰克不得不趴在地上，爬向奥基的哭喊声。

Suddenly Jack heard a flapping and squeaking off to the side. “Watch out for bats,” he called. 突然，杰克听到旁边有扑棱和吱吱的声音。 “小心蝙蝠，”他喊道。

“Yikes. You think spiders could be in here, too?” said Annie. “天哪。 你觉得这里也可能有蜘蛛吗？”安妮说。

“Probably yes,” said Jack. “可能有，”杰克说。

“Oh, no,” said Annie. “哦，不，”安妮说。

“Keep going,” said Jack. “Just keep your head down.” “继续前进，”杰克说。 “只要低头小心点。”

Close by, Oki yelped furiously. 附近，奥基疯狂地叫了起来。

Jack felt around blindly for the puppy. But instead of fur, his hand touched a cold, smooth object. He quickly drew back, afraid he might have touched a snake or some other creature. 杰克盲目地四处摸索着找小狗。 但他摸到的不是毛发，而是一个冰冷光滑的物体。 他赶紧缩回手，害怕自己可能碰到了蛇或者其他什么动物。

“Gotcha!” said Annie. “I got Oki!” “抓住了！”，安妮说。 “我抓住奥基了！”，

“Good,” said Jack. “很好，”，杰克说。

“Hey, there’s something else here,” said Annie. “嘿，这里还有别的东西，”，安妮说。

“What?” said Jack. “什么？”，杰克说。

“It’s heavy, but it feels like a plate,” said Annie. “它很重，但感觉像一个盘子，”，安妮说。

“Actually, I felt something, too,” said Jack. He reached out and touched the cold object again. He picked it up and ran his fingers over it. “I think I found a goblet,” he said. “其实，我也感觉到了什么，”，杰克说。 他伸出手，再次碰了碰那个冰冷的物体。 他捡起来，用手指摸了摸它。 “我觉得我找到了一个杯子，”他说。

“I just found something else,” said Annie. “Oh, wow…it’s too heavy to lift with one hand.” “我刚刚找到了别的东西，”安妮说。 “哦，哇……太重了，一只手抬不起来。”

“Where is it?” said Jack, reaching toward the sound of Annie’s voice. “在哪里？”杰克说，朝安妮的声音方向伸出手去。

“Here,” said Annie. She guided his hand to the object. “在这里，”安妮说。 她引导他的手去触碰那个东西。

When Jack tried to pick the object up, he almost dropped it. It was very heavy. Jack ran his fingers over its surface…. Is that a head? A snout? he wondered. Wings? Claws? “ Oh, man, we found it!” he whispered. 当杰克试图拿起那个东西时，他差点把它掉下去了。 它非常重。 杰克用手指摸着它的表面……这是个头吗？一个鼻子？ 他想知道。翅膀？ 爪子？ “天啊，我们找到了！”，他小声说。

“The dragon?” asked Annie. “是龙吗？”安妮问。

“Yes, I think so!” said Jack. “Let’s get it outside!” “是的，我想是！”，杰克说。 “我们把它弄出去吧！”

“Okay, I’ll carry Oki,” said Annie. “好吧，我来背奥基，”安妮说。

Jack stuffed the heavy object in his backpack. Crawling and then crouching, he and Annie made their way back through the cave, until they came to the entrance. The waterfall showered them with spray as they climbed over the rocks to the ground. 杰克把那个重物塞进了他的背包里。 爬行然后蹲下，他和安妮一路爬回洞穴，直到来到入口处。 他们爬过岩石来到地面时，瀑布的水花洒在他们身上。

Jack set down his backpack and pulled out the object. In the moonlight, the ninth dragon was magnificent. Its golden wings were raised behind its back, and its eyes seemed to be gazing into the far distant hills. 杰克放下背包，拿出那个物体。 月光下，第九条龙显得非常壮观。 它金色的翅膀在背后展开，眼睛似乎正凝视着远处的山丘。

“Your Majesties!” Annie called. “We found the ninth dragon!” She put Oki on the ground, and they all hurried to show Arthur and Guinevere the golden statue. But when they reached the cart, there was no sign of the king or queen. “陛下们！”安妮喊道。 “我们找到第九条龙了！”她把奥基放在地上，他们赶紧跑去向亚瑟和桂妮薇尔展示那尊金色的雕像。 但当他们到达马车时，国王和王后却不见了踪影。

“Where are they?” said Jack. “他们去哪儿了？”杰克说。

Oki yelped and dashed into the shadows. 奥基叫了一声，然后冲进了阴影里。

“There they are!” said Annie, pointing to a cluster of rocks. “他们在那儿！”安妮指着一堆岩石说。

Guinevere was kneeling on the grass, holding Arthur in her arms. Oki whined and pawed the ground. 桂妮薇尔正跪在草地上，怀里抱着亚瑟。 奥基呜咽着，用爪子刨着地面。

“We found it!” Jack shouted as he and Annie ran to them. “我们找到了！”杰克一边喊着，一边和安妮朝他们跑过去。

Jack placed the gold dragon statue next to Arthur. “See, we found the ninth dragon!” he said. But Arthur and Guinevere were both still. 杰克把金龙雕像放在亚瑟旁边。 “你看，我们找到了第九条龙！”，他说。 但亚瑟和桂妮薇尔都仍然一动不动。

“What—what’s wrong?” said Annie. “怎、怎么了？”安妮说。

“The king is dying,” the queen said in a hollow voice. “国王要死了，”女王用空洞的声音说。

Oki let out a howl. 奥基发出一声嚎叫。

“He can’t be,” said Jack. Not now. “What about the water, the healing water from Avalon? Wait!” Jack ran to the cart and grabbed the leather flask and brought it back to the queen. “他不会死的，”杰克说。现在不行。 “那水呢，从阿瓦隆来的治愈之水呢？ 等一下！”杰克跑向马车，拿起皮制水壶，又跑回去递给女王。

“There is no more,” said Guinevere. “已经没有了，”桂妮薇尔说道。

Jack turned the leather flask upside down. Not a drop of the healing water was left. 杰克把皮制酒壶倒了个底朝天。 一点治愈水都不剩了。

“Listen,” said Annie. “听着，”安妮说。

Jack heard a faint whinnying sound and the thunder of horse hooves. Peering across the moonlit moor, he saw riders in the distance galloping toward them. 杰克听到一声微弱的嘶鸣声，还有马蹄的轰隆声。 他望向月光照亮的沼泽，看到远处有骑手正朝他们疾驰而来。

“The robbers,” said Guinevere. “是强盗，”桂妮薇尔说。

“Oh, no,” said Annie. “哦，天哪，”安妮说。

“They’re coming back for their treasure,” said the queen. “他们回来取他们的财宝了，”女王说。

Jack started to tremble. The king was dying. The enemy was coming. 杰克开始发抖。 国王快要死了。 敌人要来了。

“There is no hope,” Guinevere said softly. “没有希望了，”桂妮薇尔轻声说。

Jack took a deep breath. “Yes, there is,” he said. “There’s hope.” 杰克深吸了一口气。 “有，”他说。 “还有希望。”

“There is?” said Annie. “真的吗？”安妮说。

“Yes. We can save the king,” said Jack. “是的。 我们可以救出国王，”杰克说。

“We can?” said Annie. “真的可以吗？”安妮说。

“Yes. We have to keep going,” said Jack. “是的。” “我们必须继续前进，”杰克说。

The thundering of the horses’ hooves grew louder. 马蹄声越来越响了。

“Keep going? How?” said Annie. “继续前进？怎么继续？”安妮说。

“We just have to keep going,” said Jack. “We have to do the next right thing.” “我们只能继续前进，”杰克说。 “我们必须做接下来正确的事。”

“But what’s that? What’s the next right thing?” said Annie. “但是那是什么？ 接下来正确的事是什么？”安妮说。

“I don’t know!” said Jack. “我不知道！”杰克说。

“Wait a minute,” said Annie. “I just remembered! Our stones from Cafelle!” “等一下，”安妮说。 “我刚想起来！ 我们从卡法勒得到的石头！

“Yes!” said Jack. He and Annie reached into their pockets and pulled out the red stones. “But what do we do with them?” Jack said. “是的！”杰克说。 他和安妮把手伸进口袋，拿出了红色的石头。 “那我们拿它们干什么呢？”杰克说。

“We use them with hope and strength! And imagination!” said Annie. “That’s what Cafelle told us to do!” “我们用它们，带着希望和力量！” “还有想象力！”安妮说道。 “这就是卡弗尔告诉我们要做的事！”

Jack clasped his stone. He stared at the dragon statue and tried to feel hopeful and strong and use his imagination. “Long live King Arthur and Queen Guinevere!” he said. 杰克握紧了他的石头。 他盯着龙的雕像，努力让自己感到充满希望、坚强，并运用自己的想象力。 “国王亚瑟和王后桂妮薇尔万岁！”他说。

“Long live goodness! And kindness!” said Annie. “愿善良永存！ 愿仁慈永存！”安妮说。

“And courage!” said Jack. “愿勇敢永存！”杰克说。

“And patience!” said Annie. “愿耐心永存！”安妮说。

“And never giving up!” said Jack. “永不放弃！”杰克说。

As Jack and Annie shouted their words, the gold dragon began to glow as if it were lit from within. It glowed brighter and brighter and grew warmer and warmer. The statue glowed so brightly that it looked like it was on fire. 当杰克和安妮喊出这些话时，金色的龙开始发光，就像从内部被点燃了一样。 它越来越亮，也越来越热。 雕像亮得仿佛要着火了。

“Long live Camelot!” said Jack. “And Morgan’s library and all the books!” “卡梅洛特万岁！”杰克说。 “还有摩根的图书馆和所有书籍！”

“Long live the castle gardens and flowers!” said Annie. “And the Great Hall! And music!” “愿城堡的花园和花朵万岁！”安妮说道。 “还有大礼堂！还有音乐！”

“Long live myths and legends!” shouted Jack. “From all times and places!” “愿神话和传说万岁！”杰克喊道。 “来自所有时代和地方！”

Jack heard thunder, but it wasn’t the thunder of horse hooves. Jack felt trembling, but it wasn’t the trembling of his body. The rumbling and quaking came from the statue. The very air around the gold dragon quivered. 杰克听到雷声，但那不是马蹄声的雷声。 杰克感到颤抖，但那不是他身体的颤抖。 隆隆声和震动来自雕像。 黄金龙周围的空气都在颤动。

“Long live Morgan and Merlin and Teddy and Kathleen!” shouted Jack. “愿摩根、梅林、泰迪和凯瑟琳万岁！”杰克喊道。

“And all the knights and ladies and children and horses and oxen of Camelot!” said Annie. “还有卡梅洛特的所有骑士、女士、孩子们、马儿和牛！”安妮说道。

“Long live Annie!” said Jack. “安妮万岁！”杰克说道。

“Long live Jack!” shouted Annie. “And Oki!” “杰克万岁！”安妮喊道。 “还有奥基！”

Hissing, cracking, screaming sounds rocked the night. A mighty explosion and a great blast shook the earth. With a thunderous roll, a billowing cloud erupted from the top of Munith Mor. The cloud burst into flame, and with a deafening roar, a gigantic fire-red dragon rose from the flames and seemed to spread its wings over the world. 嘶嘶声、爆裂声和尖叫声震撼着夜晚。 一声巨大的爆炸和猛烈的冲击震动了大地。 伴随着雷鸣般的滚动声，一团浓烈的云雾从穆尼特莫尔的顶部喷涌而出。 云雾突然爆燃，伴随着震耳欲聋的咆哮声，一条巨大的火红色巨龙从火焰中升起，仿佛展开双翼覆盖了整个世界。
