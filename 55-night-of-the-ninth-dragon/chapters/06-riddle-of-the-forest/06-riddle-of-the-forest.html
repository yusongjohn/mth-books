6. Riddle of the Forest 6. 森林之谜

Cafelle turned to face the king. She began to speak in a clear voice: 卡法尔转身面对国王。 她用清晰的声音开始说话：

“Moon so bright. “月亮如此明亮。

Munith Mor night. 穆尼特·莫夜。

Curtain of white. 白色的帘幕。

Hides from sight. 隐藏了视线。

Magic for flight. 魔法助飞翔。

Before dawn’s light. 在黎明之前。

Or lose the fight.” 否则就失败。”

Jack wrote down her words as fast as he could. When Cafelle finished, she and her dog both stood silent and still in the doorway again. 杰克赶紧写下她的话。 卡费尔说完后，她和她的狗又安静地站在门口。

“Thank you, Cafelle,” said the king. “谢谢你，卡费尔，”国王说。

The seer turned to him. “What did I say?” she whispered. She seemed to be in a daze. 预言者转向他。 “我刚才说了什么？”她小声问。 她看起来好像在发呆。

Jack read Cafelle’s riddle aloud: “Moon so bright. Munith Mor night. Curtain of white. Hides from sight. Magic for flight. Before dawn’s light. Or lose the fight.” 杰克大声读出卡费尔的谜语：“月亮如此明亮。穆尼特·莫的夜晚。 白色的窗帘。藏起视线。 魔法带来飞行。 在黎明之前。 或者输掉战斗。

“Oh…I see…,” said Cafelle. She looked troubled. “哦……我明白了……，”卡法勒说。 她看起来很烦恼。

Jack was troubled, too. What did the last two lines mean? 杰克也感到烦恼。 最后两句话是什么意思？

“Munith Mor is the highest mountain in the west,” said Guinevere. “It seems we should go there first?” “Munith Mor是西边最高的山，”桂妮薇尔说道。 “我们是不是应该先去那里？”

“Yes,” said Cafelle. “ ’ Tis not far from the sea. You can see it from the cart path. Continue on your way and journey over the moorlands.” “是的，”卡费尔说道。 “它离海不远。 你从马车路上就能看到它。 继续往前走，穿过沼泽地。

“Good,” said Guinevere. “Jack and Annie, you will decipher the rest of the riddle as we travel.” The queen stood and helped the king rise. Jack and Annie and Oki stood up, too. “We must leave at once, Cafelle,” said Guinevere. “Not only must we travel to Munith Mor to look for the gold dragon, but we must then take it back to the castle garden in order for its magic to work.” “好，”桂妮薇尔说。 “杰克和安妮，我们在旅行途中你们要解开谜语的其余部分。”女王站起身，扶起国王。 杰克、安妮和奥基也站了起来。 “我们必须马上离开，卡法尔，”桂妮薇尔说。 “我们不仅要前往穆尼特莫寻找金龙，之后还要把它带回城堡花园，这样它的魔法才能生效。”

“But, Your Majesty…,” said Jack. “可是，陛下……，”杰克说。

“Yes, Jack?” said the queen. “是的，杰克？”女王说。

“The last two lines…,” he said. The others looked at him as he read the last two lines again: “Before dawn’s light. Or lose the fight.” “最后两句……，”他说。 其他人看着他，他又读了一遍最后两句：‘在黎明之前。’ 或者输掉战斗。”

“Ah,” the queen said. “That seems to say we will lose the fight if we do not arrive in Avalon before dawn.” Guinevere took a deep breath. “So we will do all we need to do by dawn tomorrow—travel to Munith Mor, find the gold dragon, and return it to the castle garden. Thank you for your help, Cafelle.” She reached out and took the blind woman’s hands. “啊，”女王说道。 “那似乎是在说，如果我们不能在黎明前到达阿瓦隆，我们就会输掉这场战斗。”桂妮薇尔深吸了一口气。 “那么，我们明天黎明前必须完成所有事情——前往穆尼特莫尔，找到金龙，再把它送回城堡的花园。 谢谢你帮了我们，卡法尔。”她伸出手，握住了这位盲眼女人的手。

“My lady, you are trembling,” Cafelle said. “我的女士，你在发抖，”卡菲勒说。

“I am fine,” said Guinevere. Her voice wavered, but she stood tall. “我没事，”桂妮薇尔说。 她的声音有些颤抖，但她挺直了身子。

Is she worried we don’t have enough time? Jack thought. 她担心我们时间不够吗？杰克想。

“Thank you, and farewell,” said Guinevere. Then she helped the king toward the door. “Come, Jack and Annie.” “谢谢你们，再见，”桂妮薇尔说。 然后她帮助国王朝门口走去。 “来吧，杰克和安妮。”

“Thank you, Cafelle,” said Annie. “谢谢你，卡法尔。”安妮说。

Jack put away his notebook and picked up his backpack. Then he, Annie, and Oki followed the king and queen outside into the cool, windy air. Arthur held on to Guinevere’s arm as they walked away from the stone hut and through the gate. Oki scampered ahead of them, yapping at windblown leaves. 杰克收起他的笔记本，拿起他的背包。 接着，他、安妮和奥基跟着国王和王后走出了门，来到凉爽的、有风的空气里。 亚瑟边走边握着桂妮薇尔的手臂，他们离开了石头小屋，穿过大门。 奥基蹦蹦跳跳地跑在他们前面，对着被风吹来的树叶汪汪叫。

Jack glanced back at Cafelle and Kee standing in the doorway of the hut. The dog could not hear and the woman could not see, but together the pair seemed to hear and see more than others could, Jack thought. He raised his hand in farewell, and Kee whined good- bye. 杰克回头看了看站在小屋门口的卡法尔和基。 狗听不见，女人也看不见，但杰克想，这对搭档似乎能听见和看见别人无法听见和看见的东西。 他举起手告别，基呜呜地叫着说再见。

“Wait! Children!” called Cafelle. “等一下！ “孩子们！”卡法勒喊道。

Jack and Annie hurried back to her. The seer reached into the pocket of her dress. “Take this,” she said, and she handed them each a small red stone. 杰克和安妮赶紧跑回去找她。 预言家伸手到她裙子的口袋里。 “拿着这个，”她说，然后每人给了他们一块小红石头。

“What is it?” asked Jack. “这是什么？”杰克问。

“Rocks from the mountains of fire before time,” said the blind woman. “来自时间之前火山山脉的石头，”盲眼女人说。

“Lava rocks? Like from volcanoes?” Jack wondered aloud. He rubbed his fingers over his stone. “What do we do with them?” “是熔岩石头吗？ 就像火山喷发出来的那种吗？”杰克大声地问。 他用手指摸了摸自己的石头。 “我们拿它们怎么办呢？”

“Use them to save the king,” said the blind woman. “用它们去救国王，”盲眼女人说。

“Use them how?” asked Jack. “怎么用？”杰克问。

“With hope and courage,” said Cafelle. “用希望和勇气，”卡法尔说。

“And…?” said Jack. “还有……？”杰克说。

“With imagination,” said the seer. “用想象力，”预言家说。

“Got it! Thanks!” said Annie. “明白了！ 谢谢！”安妮说。

Jack didn’t understand. “But what do we do with—” 杰克不明白。 “但是我们拿——”

“Annie? Jack?” Guinevere called from the forest. “安妮？” “杰克？”桂妮薇尔从森林里喊道。

“Coming, Your Majesty!” Annie called back. “来了，陛下！”安妮喊道。

“Please hurry!” the queen called. “请快点！”女王喊道。

“Go now,” said Cafelle. “现在就走，”卡法勒说。

“We have to go, Jack!” said Annie. She grabbed him and pulled him away. “Bye! Thank you, Cafelle! Thanks, Kee!” she called back to them. “Thanks for everything!” “我们得走了，杰克！”安妮说。 她抓住他，把他拉走了。 “再见！ 谢谢你们，卡法勒！ 谢谢，基！”她回头对他们喊道。 “谢谢你们的一切！”

“But I don’t understand,” Jack said to Annie. “What do we do with these stones?” “但是我不明白，”杰克对安妮说。 “我们拿这些石头怎么办？”

“Use our imaginations!” said Annie. “用我们的想象力！”安妮说。

“But how, exactly?” said Jack. “但是具体怎么做呢？”杰克说。

“Don’t worry! We’ll figure it out. We have to hurry! Come on!” said Annie. And she took off running through the forest. “别担心！ 我们会想出来的。我们必须赶快！ 快点！”安妮说。 然后她就跑着穿过森林。

Jack looked back at the hut. Cafelle and Kee had gone inside. 杰克回头看向小屋。 卡费尔和基基已经进去了。

“Hurry, Jack!” called Annie. “快点，杰克！”安妮喊道。

“Darn,” said Jack, annoyed. He crammed his stone into a pocket of his jeans and ran after the others. “真烦人，”杰克不高兴地说。 他把石头塞进牛仔裤的口袋里，然后追上其他人。

Jack caught up with everyone at the footbridge. Annie picked up Oki and carried him across the stream. The queen helped Arthur into the back of the cart. The king trembled as he drank deeply from the leather flask. Again, Jack worried about running out of the healing water. 杰克在桥下追上了大家。 安妮抱起奥基，把他抬过了小溪。 女王帮亚瑟爬进了马车的后部。 国王一边从皮制酒壶里痛饮，一边颤抖着。 又一次，杰克担心治愈水会用完。

When he finished drinking, Arthur lay back on the hay and closed his eyes. 喝完后，亚瑟躺在干草上，闭上了眼睛。

“We must hurry,” Guinevere said quietly to Jack and Annie, “or all will be lost.” “我们必须快点，”桂妮薇尔轻声对杰克和安妮说，“否则一切都会失去。”

“Yes, Your Majesty,” said Jack. “是，陛下，”杰克说。

“Keep him warm,” Annie whispered to Oki. She put the puppy next to Arthur, and the little dog cuddled against the king again. “让他暖和点，”安妮对奥基小声说。 她把小狗放在亚瑟旁边，小狗狗又依偎在国王身边。

“On to Munith Mor,” the queen said. Then she hoisted herself into the back of the cart. “前往穆尼特莫尔，”女王说。 然后她把自己抬上了马车的后部。

Jack and Annie climbed onto the drivers’ bench. Annie picked up the reins. 杰克和安妮爬上了车夫的座位。 安妮捡起了缰绳。

“Hike!” she said. “出发！”，她说。

In the failing light, the yoked oxen began lumbering up the road toward the distant mountain peaks. 在逐渐暗淡的光线中，套着轭的牛开始沿着道路缓慢地向远处的山峰前进。

“Hurry!” Annie said, shaking the reins. “快点！”安妮说，摇着缰绳。

But the oxen kept their slow pace as the cart creaked and bumped over the rough ground. How will we ever make it to the mountain, find the gold dragon, and return to the castle before dawn? Jack wondered. And what does Cafelle expect us to do with the red stones? 但牛车依旧缓慢前行，车轮在崎岖的地面颠簸着发出嘎吱声。 我们怎么才能在天亮前赶到山里，找到金龙，再回到城堡呢？杰克心想。 卡法尔勒又指望我们拿红色石头做什么呢？

“We need more information,” he said to Annie. “We should have learned more about the stones.”

“We had to hurry,” said Annie. “I know, but you lack patience,” said Jack, quoting Cafelle. “我知道，但你缺乏耐心，”杰克引用卡法尔勒的话说。

“Well, you don’t act quickly enough,” said Annie. “And you worry too much.” “好吧，你动作也太慢了，”安妮说。 “你又太担心了。”

Both of them were silent for a moment. 他们俩沉默了一会儿。

“Read the riddle again,” said Annie. “再读一遍谜语，”安妮说。

Jack pulled out his notebook and read: “Moon so bright.” He stopped. 杰克拿出他的笔记本，读道：‘月亮如此明亮。’他停了下来。

“Keep going,” said Annie. “继续读，”安妮说。

Jack read the next line: “Munith Mor night.” He paused again. 杰克读下一行：‘穆尼特莫夜。’他又停了下来。

“Keep going, keep going!” said Annie. “继续读，继续读！”安妮说。

“No! Let’s discuss each line,” said Jack. “不！” 让我们逐句讨论，”杰克说。

“But we know what those lines mean,” said Annie. “Plus we need to think about the whole riddle.” “但我们知道这些线是什么意思，”安妮说。 “而且我们得考虑整个谜题。”

“The way to figure out the whole riddle is piece by piece, line by line,” said Jack. He looked back at his notebook. “ Moon so—” “解开整个谜题的方法是一点一点，一行一行，”杰克说。 他回头看了看自己的笔记本。 “月亮所—”

Jack’s reading was interrupted by a gasp from Annie. He looked up. “What?” 杰克正在读书，突然被安妮的一声惊呼打断了。他抬起头。 “什么？”

“Wolf pack!” she whispered. “狼群！”她小声说。

In the distance, three horsemen in black cloaks were riding across a burnt field, heading toward the road. 在远处，三个穿着黑色斗篷的骑手正骑着马，穿过一片烧焦的田野，朝路上走来。

“Oh, man.” Jack’s hands shook as he stuffed his notebook into his pack. He looked back at the queen. “Invaders ahead!” he said. “天啊。”杰克的手在发抖，他赶紧把笔记本塞进背包里。 他又回头看了看女王。 “前方有入侵者！”他说。

Guinevere nodded and covered the king’s face with his ragged cloak. Then she slumped down in the hay beside him and tucked her golden hair under the hood of her own cloak. 桂妮薇尔点点头，用她破旧的斗篷盖住了国王的脸。 然后她坐在干草堆上，挨着他，把金色的头发藏在自己斗篷的兜帽下。

Jack could barely breathe as he and Annie watched the riders leave the field and start galloping down the road toward them. 杰克和安妮看着那些骑手离开田野，朝他们这边飞驰而来，杰克几乎喘不过气来。
