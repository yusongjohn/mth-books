5. Cafelle and Kee 5. 卡费尔和基

“Westward, toward the mountains,” said Queen Guinevere. She was sitting next to the sleeping king, with her hand resting on his chest. “Cafelle lives not far from here, where the wood borders the heath.” “向西，朝山的方向走，”桂妮薇尔女王说。 她坐在熟睡的国王旁边，手放在他的胸口上。 “卡费尔住在不远的地方，就在树林和荒原交界处。”

“Got it,” said Annie. “Haw!” she called to the oxen, using the sled dog command for “Turn left!” “明白了，”安妮说。 “哈！”她对牛叫道，用雪橇犬的口令表示“向左转！”，

The heavy animals moved slowly toward the distant cloud-covered mountains. A cool, moist wind rippled through the wild grasses on either side of the rutted road. 这些又重又慢的动物缓缓地朝远处被云雾笼罩的山脉移动。 一股凉爽潮湿的风吹过坑洼不平的道路上两边的野草。

“Where are the invaders now, Your Majesty?” asked Jack, rocking from side to side on the drivers’ bench. “入侵者现在在哪里，陛下？”杰克在驾驶座上左右摇晃着问道。

“We hope that our army is defeating them in the east,” said the queen. “But small bands may still be roaming the kingdom, like packs of wolves.” “我们希望我们的军队正在东部击败他们，”女王说道。 “但小股的入侵者可能仍在王国各地游荡，就像一群群狼一样。”

“Like packs of wolves?” said Jack. His eyes darted around the countryside, looking for bands of invaders. “就像一群群狼？”杰克说道。 他的眼睛四处张望，寻找入侵者的踪迹。

The hills and fields were covered with shadows of clouds scudding across the sun. The shadows swept over abandoned huts and farmlands with charred trees and blackened fields. 山丘和田野上布满了云影，它们在阳光下飞快地掠过。 这些影子掠过了废弃的小屋和农田，那里树木焦黑，田野一片漆黑。

“What happened here, Your Majesty?” Annie asked. “Did the wolf packs of invaders burn everything?” “这里发生了什么事，陛下？”安妮问。 “入侵的狼群烧毁了一切吗？”

“No, the fires were set by those who live here,” answered the queen. “Rumors of the enemy caused farmers to abandon their crops and hide in the hills. Before they fled, some burned their timber, barley, and flax to keep them from the invaders.” “不，是这里的人放的火，”女王回答道。 “敌人即将来临的谣言让农夫们丢下庄稼，躲进了山里。 在他们逃跑之前，有些人烧掉了木材、大麦和亚麻，以防被入侵者抢走。”

“When will everyone come back?” asked Jack. “大家什么时候才能回来呢？”杰克问。

“When they know their king has returned,” the queen said. “Until then, no one will have the courage.” “当他们知道他们的国王已经回来时，”女王说。 “在那之前，没人会有勇气。”

So everything depends on King Arthur, Jack thought, which means everything depends on us. 所以一切都要靠亚瑟王，杰克想，这意味着一切都要靠我们。

As the oxen pulled the cart toward the wooded hills and plains at the base of the mountains, the bright air grew colder and the wind stronger. The lonely road grew wilder, until it was no more than a weedy path, overgrown with thistles and brambles. 当牛拉着车朝山脚下长满树木的山丘和平原前进时，空气变得越来越冷，风也变得越来越强。 这条孤单的路越来越荒野了，直到它变成了一条长满杂草的小径，到处都是蓟和荆棘。

“Stop here,” Guinevere said finally. “在这里停下，”桂妮薇尔终于说道。

“Whoa!” said Annie. “哇！”安妮说道。

The oxen stopped moving, and the cart jolted to a halt. 牛停下了脚步，马车猛地停了下来。

Guinevere gently shook her husband’s shoulder. “Wake, my lord,” she said. “Wake up.” 桂妮薇尔轻轻摇晃着她丈夫的肩膀。 “醒醒，我的老爷，”她说。 “醒一醒。”

“Yes?” the king said, lifting his head. “是吗？”国王说道，抬起了头。

“We are near Cafelle’s,” said Guinevere. “We must walk a short way now.” “我们快到卡费勒家了，”桂妮薇尔说道。 “我们现在得走一小段路了。”

Arthur sat up and looked around. Then he ran his fingers through his hair. “Yes,” he said faintly. “We must walk from here.” 亚瑟坐起来，四处张望。 然后，他用手梳理了一下头发。 “是的，”他轻声说道。 “我们必须从这里步行前进。”

“First, drink this,” said the queen, lifting the flask to the king’s lips. Jack wondered how much of the healing water from Avalon was left. He hoped they had enough for the whole journey. “首先，喝这个，”女王说，把酒壶举到国王的嘴唇边。 杰克想知道还有多少来自阿瓦隆的治愈之水。 他希望他们有足够的水量完成整个旅程。

Annie climbed down from the cart bench and grabbed Oki. After Arthur drank from the flask, Guinevere helped him out of the back of the cart. King Arthur took the queen’s arm, and the two of them started walking slowly down a path. 安妮从马车座位上爬下来，抓起了奥基。 亚瑟喝完酒壶里的水后，桂妮薇尔帮他从马车后面下来。 亚瑟国王挽起女王的手臂，两人慢慢地沿着小路走起来。

As Jack and Annie followed the royal couple through the shifting shadows of the forest, silver leaves on the interlacing tree branches rustled in the wind. Jack smelled wood smoke. Soon they crossed a sagging footbridge over a stream and came to a clearing with a small stone hut. A fence of woven sticks ringed the yard. Chimney smoke swirled through the afternoon air. 杰克和安妮跟着王室夫妇穿过森林中不断变化的阴影，交织的树枝上银色的叶子在风中沙沙作响。 杰克闻到了木头燃烧的气味。 不久后，他们走过一座摇摇欲坠的木桥，来到一条小溪边，眼前出现了一片空地，空地上有一座小石屋。 院子周围围着一圈用树枝编织成的篱笆。 烟囱冒出的烟在下午的空气中盘旋。

The queen opened the gate, and she and Arthur crossed to the hut. Guinevere knocked on the door. 女王打开了门，她和亚瑟一起走向小屋。 桂妮薇尔敲了敲门。

A moment later, an old woman wrapped in a shawl stood in the doorway. She had a long white braid and luminous skin worn smooth by weather and time, like an ancient stone. The woman’s eyes were closed. With a start, Jack realized Cafelle was blind. At her side was a tall white dog with light blue eyes. 片刻之后，一位披着披肩的老人站在了门口。 她有一条长长的白色发辫，皮肤光滑，像被岁月和天气磨砺过的古老石头一样闪着光。 女人的眼睛是闭着的。 杰克突然意识到，卡法尔是盲人。 她身边有一只高大的白狗，眼睛是浅蓝色的。

Oki didn’t bark or whine. The peaceful gaze of the white dog seemed to keep him calm. 奥基没有吠叫，也没有呜咽。 那只白狗平静的眼神似乎让他保持冷静。

“Greetings, Cafelle. We have come to—to seek your help,” rasped King Arthur. “你好，卡法尔。 我们是来——来请求你的帮助，”亚瑟王沙哑地说。

Cafelle bowed her head. “Welcome, Your Majesty,” she said. “And the queen has come with you this time, yes?” 卡法尔低下头。 “欢迎你，陛下，”她说。 “这次，王后也和你一起来了，对吗？”

“Yes, Cafelle.” Guinevere touched the blind woman’s arm. “对，卡法尔。”桂妮薇尔轻触了盲女人的手臂。

“Welcome, dear lady,” said Cafelle. “And there are others with you as well. Children?” “欢迎你，亲爱的女士，”卡弗勒说道。 “还有其他人和你一起来了，是孩子吗？”

“Yes. Jack and Annie,” said the queen. “是的。 杰克和安妮，”女王说道。

“Annie.” The seer reached out, and Annie took her hand. “I see you well, Annie,” Cafelle said with her eyes still closed. “You love all animals. You speak your mind and are quick and courageous. Sometimes, though, you move too swiftly. You lack patience.” “安妮。”先知伸出手，安妮握住了她的手。 “我看得见你，安妮，”卡法勒说，她的眼睛仍然闭着。 “你热爱所有动物。 你敢说敢做，又机敏又勇敢。 不过，有时候你行动太快了。 你缺乏耐心。”

“That’s right, that’s me,” Annie said with a smile. “没错，就是我，”安妮微笑着说道。

“And Jack?” Cafelle said, turning to him. “那杰克呢？”卡法尔转向他问道。

Jack took the seer’s hand. 杰克握住了预言者的手。

“You are very thoughtful and intelligent, Jack,” she said. “You love books and knowledge. Sometimes, though, you worry too much and you do not act quickly enough.” “你非常体贴而且聪明，杰克，”她说。 “你热爱书籍和知识。 不过，有时候你太担心了，行动也不够快。”

“Yes, that’s me,” said Jack. “是的，就是我，”杰克说道。

Oki barked. “And this is our dog, Oki,” said Annie. She held out the puppy, and the blind woman patted his head. 奥基叫了起来。 “这是我们的狗，奥基，”安妮说道。 她抱出小狗，盲眼女人摸了摸他的头。

“Oki, you are a very happy and lively dog! Very curious and willful,” the seer said with a smile. She pointed to the blue-eyed dog at her side. “And this is my Kee. She is old, like me. She cannot hear, and I cannot see. So we must rely on one another.” “奥基，你是一条非常快乐又活泼的狗！ “非常好奇又任性，”先知微笑着说道。 她指着身边那只蓝眼睛的狗。 “这是我的基基。 她和我一样老。 她听不见，我看不见。 所以我们必须互相依靠。

“Cool,” said Annie. “酷，”安妮说。

Cafelle then held both her hands out to King Arthur, and he took them in his. “Come inside, my lord,” she said. “You have been wounded.” 然后，卡法尔把她的双手伸向亚瑟王，他握住了她的手。 “请进来，我的大人，”她说。 “你受伤了。”

“Yes…I have,” the king said. “是的……我受伤了，”国王说。

Cafelle and Kee stepped back into the stone hut. The others followed, stepping onto a worn wooden floor. 卡法尔和基回到了石屋里面。 其他人也跟着进来了，踩在磨损的木地板上。

Inside, Jack could barely see anything. There were no windows and no lanterns. After Cafelle closed the door, the fire from the hearth and two candles were all that illuminated the one-room hut. Once Jack got used to the dim light, he was amazed at how easily Cafelle moved about with Kee at her side. The blind woman pulled a wooden bench close to the hearth, added kindling and logs to the fire, and poked it with a stick until flames shot into the air. 屋内，杰克几乎什么也看不见。 这里没有窗户，也没有灯笼。 卡法尔关上门后，壁炉里的火光和两支蜡烛的光，就是唯一照亮这个单间小屋的东西。 一旦杰克适应了昏暗的光线，他惊讶地发现卡法尔在基的陪伴下，行动竟然如此自如。 这位盲眼女人把一张木椅搬到壁炉旁边，往火堆里添了柴火和木头，然后用一根棍子拨弄火堆，直到火苗猛地窜起。

“Pray, sit down,” Cafelle said, turning back to her visitors. “请坐下来，”卡法尔说，转身面对她的客人。

Jack and Annie joined Arthur and Guinevere on the bench, and Cafelle sat on a low stool near the blazing fire. Kee sat beside her. 杰克和安妮加入了亚瑟和桂妮薇尔，坐在长凳上，卡费尔坐在一个矮凳上，靠近熊熊燃烧的火堆。 基坐在她旁边。

“Tell me, what do you seek to know, my lord?” Cafelle asked King Arthur. “告诉我，陛下，您想了解什么？”卡费尔问亚瑟国王。

The king leaned forward into the half circle of firelight. “The ninth dragon…was stolen…from Morgan’s garden,” he said. 国王向前倾身，进入火光形成的半圆中。 “第九条龙……被偷走了……从摩根的花园里，”他说。

“Oh,” Cafelle said. “I understand. You need the ninth dragon, my lord, to cross to Avalon.” “哦，”卡费尔说。 “我明白了。 陛下，您需要第九条龙，才能前往阿瓦隆。”

“I do indeed, wise friend,” he said. “确实如此，智慧的朋友，”他说。

Cafelle nodded. Without another word, she stood up and opened the door. Kee joined her at the threshold, and the two stood together, facing the woods outside. Kee’s silky white fur glistened in the silver light as the dog looked deep into the forest. 卡费尔点了点头。 她没有再说一句话，站起身来，打开了门。 基基走到门口，和她站在一起，面对着外面的森林。 基基丝滑的白色毛发在银色的光线下闪闪发亮，狗看着森林深处。

“Write down what she says,” Annie whispered. “把她说的话写下来，”安妮小声说。

Jack reached under his cloak and took off his backpack. He pulled out his pencil and notebook and opened to a blank page, ready to write down the seer’s prophecy. 杰克伸手到他的斗篷下面，取下了他的背包。 他拿出铅笔和笔记本，翻开一页空白的纸，准备写下预言师的预言。

For a long moment, all was quiet except for the gentle rustling of the trees. Gradually, though, the sounds of leaves and wind grew louder and louder until it seemed as if all the forest was shouting its secrets to the blind woman. 很长一段时间，除了树叶轻轻的沙沙声，一切都很安静。 渐渐地，树叶和风的声音变得越来越大，仿佛整片森林都在向那个盲女人大声喊出它的秘密。

Then the force of the wind lessened…the whispering and rustling grew softer and softer…until a hush fell over the forest. 然后，风的力量减弱了……树叶和风的沙沙声也变得越来越轻……直到森林里一片寂静。

No one moved as they waited for Cafelle to tell them what the leaves had said. 没有人动，他们等着卡法尔告诉他们树叶说了什么。
