10. All Good 10. 所有人都很好

The dragon’s eyes were flaming. Its horns and claws and scaly skin flashed in the moonlight. Its open batlike wings began moving against the night sky, sweeping back and forth, back and forth. Jack, Annie, Oki, and Guinevere huddled with the king in the raging windstorm. In a flash of fire and smoke, a portal opened before them. Colors swirled—red, black, yellow, and green. Images from mythic realms of the otherworld passed by Jack’s eyes. Flying stags, gossamer-winged fairies, one-eyed ice wizards, spider queens, raven kings, water horses, wolf shadows, sea serpents, snow giants, and fallen knights all flew by, ghostly and real at the same time. And then nothing. Nothing at all. Only darkness. 巨龙的眼睛燃烧着火焰。 它的角、爪子和带鳞的皮肤在月光下闪闪发光。 它展开像蝙蝠一样的翅膀，开始在夜空中扇动，来回、来回。 杰克、安妮、奥基和桂妮薇尔在狂风暴雨中和国王挤在一起。 一道火光和烟雾闪过，一个传送门在他们面前打开了。 颜色开始旋转——红色、黑色、黄色和绿色。 来自其他世界神话王国的图像从杰克眼前飞过。 飞舞的鹿、薄纱翅膀的仙女、独眼的冰法师、蜘蛛女王、渡鸦国王、水马、狼影、海蛇、雪巨人和倒下的骑士全都飞过，既像幽灵又像真实。然后什么也没有了。 什么也没有了。只有黑暗。

Annie’s voice broke through the black silence. “Jack. Jack,” she said. 安妮的声音打破了黑色的寂静。 “杰克。杰克，”她说。

Jack opened his eyes. He was sitting in soft wet grass, still clasping the red stone Cafelle had given him. Annie sat beside him, and Guinevere and Arthur lay nearby. 杰克睁开了眼睛。 他正坐在柔软的湿草地上，仍然紧握着卡弗尔给他的那块红色石头。 安妮坐在他旁边，桂妮薇儿和亚瑟躺在附近。

Jack heard water splashing. It sounded as if fish were jumping, but when he sat up, he couldn’t see anything through a golden mist that was rising from a lake. The breeze was soft and smelled of lavender and lilac. “What happened?” he said. 杰克听到水花四溅的声音。 听起来像是鱼在跳跃，但当他坐起来时，他什么也看不见，因为湖面上升起了金色的薄雾。 风很轻柔，还带着薰衣草和紫罗兰的香味。 “发生了什么？”他说。

“We were pulled into the portal, and it brought us to Avalon,” said Annie. “我们被吸进了传送门，它把我们带到了阿瓦隆，”安妮说。

Yip! Oki popped up beside her. 耶！ 奥基突然出现在她旁边。

“Right, Oki?” Annie asked. “对吧，奥基？”安妮问。

“I believe you are right,” answered the queen. “我想你是对的，”女王回答。

Jack and Annie turned around. Guinevere was sitting up, her golden hair tumbling around her shoulders. She looked dazed but happy. “How are you both?” she said. 杰克和安妮转过身。 桂妮薇尔坐了起来，她金色的头发散落在肩膀周围。 她看起来有点晕但很开心。 “你们俩怎么样？”她说。

“I think we’re good,” said Annie. “我想我们没事，”安妮说。

“All good,” said Jack. He slipped the red stone back into his pocket. “我们都很好，”杰克说。 他把那块红色的石头又放回口袋里。

Guinevere smiled at them. Then she reached out and tenderly touched the king’s shoulder. “Wake, my lord,” she said. 桂妮薇尔微笑着看着他们。 然后她伸出手，温柔地碰了碰国王的肩膀。 “醒醒，我的领主，”她说。

King Arthur opened his eyes and stared up at the pale sky. Oki scampered over the grass and licked the king’s cheek. Arthur laughed softly and stroked the puppy’s fur. 亚瑟国王睁开了眼睛，抬头看着苍白的天空。 奥基在草地上蹦蹦跳跳，舔了舔国王的脸颊。 亚瑟轻声笑了，抚摸着小狗的毛。

“Where’s the dragon?” said Jack. “龙在哪里？”杰克说。

“You’re sitting beside him,” said Annie. “你正坐在他旁边，”安妮说。

Jack looked down and saw the gold statue shining in the dewy grass. When he wrapped his hand around it, the statue was still warm. “What about the giant living, breathing dragon?” he asked. 杰克低头一看，发现金色的雕像在露水草地上闪闪发光。 当他用手握住它时，雕像还是暖暖的。 “那条巨大的、会呼吸的龙呢？”他问。

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “我不知道，”安妮说。

“Everyone saw a giant dragon, right?” said Jack. “It wasn’t just me?” “大家都看到一条巨大的龙，对吧？”杰克说。 “不只是我看到的吧？”

“Yes, I saw it,” said Guinevere. “是的，我看到了，”桂妮薇尔说。

“And I,” said the king, sitting up. Arthur’s voice was hoarse, but his eyes were clear. Color had returned to his face. “我也看到了，”国王坐起来说。 亚瑟的声音沙哑，但他的眼睛很清醒。 他的脸上又有了血色。

“Oh, wow, Your Majesty! Are you going to be okay now?” asked Annie. “哦，哇，陛下！ 安妮问道：‘你现在会没事了吧？’

The king took a deep breath. “Yes, Annie. I believe my wound has already begun to heal.” 国王深吸了一口气。 “是的，安妮。” “我认为我的伤口已经开始愈合了。”

“I’m really glad,” Annie said. “我真的很高兴，”安妮说。

“Me too,” said Jack. “我也是，”杰克说。

Suddenly Oki let out a yelp. Then the puppy dashed to the shore of the lake and barked wildly. High, beautiful singing was coming from across the mist-covered water. 突然，奥基发出一声尖叫。 然后小狗冲到湖边，疯狂地吠叫起来。 高亢、优美的歌声从雾气笼罩的水面上传来。

“Who’s singing?” Jack asked. “谁在唱歌？”杰克问。

“Mermaids,” said the king. “人鱼，”国王说。

“Mermaids?” said Annie. “人鱼？”安妮说。

Jack just smiled. In this strange new world, nothing seemed impossible. 杰克只是笑了笑。 在这个奇怪的新世界里，似乎没有什么是不可能的。

“Look!” said Annie. “看！”安妮说。

Slowly, a cinnamon-colored boat with a white sail glided out of the haze. At the helm of the boat was a tall bearded man in a blue robe. A small penguin stood beside him. 慢慢地，一艘肉桂色的小船，带着白色的帆，从雾中滑了出来。 船头站着一位身穿蓝色长袍、留着长胡子的高个子男人。 他身边站着一只小企鹅。

“Merlin! Penny!” breathed Annie. She and Jack jumped up and ran to the edge of the water. “梅林！ 彭妮！”安妮惊叹道。 她和杰克跳起来，跑到水边。

When Merlin saw Jack and Annie, his eyes grew wide with astonishment. 当梅林看到杰克和安妮时，他惊讶得眼睛睁得大大的。

Peep, chirped Penny. “咕咕！”佩妮叫道。

“Greetings!” called Jack. “你好！”杰克喊道。

“Greetings, indeed!” said the magician. “How on earth do you children come to be here in Avalon?” “真高兴见到你！”魔术师说道。 “天哪，你们这些孩子是怎么来到阿瓦隆的？”

“Magic!” said Annie. “魔法！”安妮说道。

Merlin landed the small sailboat, and he and Penny came ashore. Oki rushed forward and nuzzled the baby penguin’s fuzzy head. Penny flapped her wings and made squeaking sounds as if she was giggling. 梅林把小帆船停好，然后他和彭妮上岸了。 奥基冲了过去，用鼻子蹭了蹭小企鹅毛茸茸的脑袋。 彭妮扑扇着翅膀，发出咯咯的声音，好像在笑。

“New friends!” Guinevere said. “新朋友！”桂妮薇尔说。

Merlin cried out in amazement when he saw the king and queen walking toward him. “Your Majesties!” said the magician. “What has happened? Why are you all here in Avalon?” 梅林看到国王和王后朝他走来，惊讶地叫出声来。 “陛下们！”魔术师说道。 “发生了什么事？ 你们为什么都来到阿瓦隆？”

“Soon after you left Camelot, word came of enemy forces in the east,” said the king. As he began telling Merlin the story of the invaders, a snow-white unicorn pranced out of the golden haze. Teddy and Kathleen were on its back. “你们离开卡梅洛特不久后，就传来东部有敌军的消息，”国王说道。 当他开始给梅林讲述入侵者的故事时，一只雪白色的独角兽从金色的雾气中蹦跳了出来。 泰迪和凯瑟琳骑在它的背上。

“Dianthus!” Annie cried. “Teddy! Kathleen!” “迪安瑟！”安妮喊道。 “泰迪！凯瑟琳！”

“Hi!” yelled Jack, and he and Annie ran away from the shore to greet their friends. “你好！”杰克大喊，他和安妮从岸边跑过去迎接他们的朋友。

Teddy and Kathleen shouted with wonder and joy when they saw Jack and Annie. The young enchanters jumped off the unicorn and threw their arms around them. Oki yapped and leapt joyfully around Dianthus. 当泰迪和凯瑟琳看到杰克和安妮时，他们惊喜又开心地大喊起来。 年轻的魔法师们从独角兽身上跳下来，张开双臂拥抱他们。 奥基叫着，快乐地在迪安瑟斯周围跳来跳去。

“Hi, Dianthus! We missed you!” said Annie. She lifted Oki up to the unicorn. Dianthus sniffed the puppy, and Oki licked him. “你好，迪安瑟斯！ 我们很想你！”安妮说。 她把奥基举到独角兽面前。 迪安瑟斯闻了闻小狗，奥基舔了他。

“Why have you come here?” asked Kathleen. “你们为什么来这里？”凯瑟琳问。

“King Arthur was wounded,” said Jack. “亚瑟王受伤了，”杰克说。

“Wounded? Badly?” Teddy asked. “受伤了？ 伤得严重吗？”泰迪问。

“Yes, but he’s better now,” said Jack. “See?” They looked back at the king and queen, who were talking with Merlin near the lake. “是的，但他现在好多了，”杰克说。 “看到了吗？”他们回头看向国王和王后，国王和王后正在湖边和梅林谈话。

“And we hope Morgan can make him even better,” said Annie. “That’s why we came here.” “我们希望摩根能让他变得更好，”安妮说。 “这就是我们来这里的原因。”

“Where is Morgan?” asked Jack. “摩根在哪里？”杰克问。

Teddy peered into the mist. “She’s…there!” he said, pointing toward the water. 泰迪朝雾中望去。 “她……在那里！”他说着，指向水面。

Morgan le Fay was emerging from the mist in a small boat pulled by a flock of swans. 摩根·法正在雾中乘着一艘由一群天鹅拉着的小船出现。

“Morgan, look who’s here!” called Teddy. “摩根，看看是谁来了！”泰迪喊道。

Jack and Annie waved at the enchantress. “Hi, Morgan!” Annie called. 杰克和安妮向女巫挥手。 “你好，摩根！”安妮喊道。

Morgan looked bewildered to see them. Her swan boat glided to the sandy beach, and she stepped ashore. “How? Why?” she said. 摩根看到他们时，看起来很困惑。 她的天鹅船滑到了沙滩上，她走下船。 “怎么了？为什么？”她说。

Carrying Oki in her arms, Annie ran to Morgan. Jack followed. They told her the whole story, from Oki finding the tree house in the Frog Creek woods to coming to Avalon through the dragon’s portal. 安妮抱着奥基跑向摩根，杰克跟在后面。 他们告诉她整个故事，从奥基在蛙溪镇的森林里发现树屋，到他们通过龙的传送门来到阿瓦隆。

“Astonishing,” said Morgan when they finished. “And the king and queen are here, too?” “令人惊讶，”他们讲完后，摩根说道。 “国王和王后也在这里吗？

“Yes! There!” Annie pointed farther down the shore, where Merlin was still talking with the royal couple. “是的！ 在那里！安妮指着更远处的岸边，梅林还在和王室夫妇谈话。

Morgan crossed to King Arthur. “Welcome back to the Isle of Avalon, Your Majesty,” she said. 摩根走到亚瑟王面前。 “陛下，欢迎回到阿瓦隆岛，”她说。

The king smiled and nodded. The enchantress gazed at him for a long moment, then put her hand on his chest and closed her eyes. Finally she smiled and looked at him again. “You will soon be completely healed,” she said. “But you and the queen must remain here for a short while, until you are restored to your strongest self.” 国王微笑着点了点头。 女巫注视了他很久，然后把手放在他的胸口，闭上了眼睛。 最后，她微笑着再次看着他。 “你很快就会完全康复，”她说。 “但你和王后必须在这里待一会儿，直到你们恢复到最强壮的状态。”

“Thank you, Morgan,” said Guinevere. “And Jack and Annie? May they stay in Avalon with us?” “谢谢你，摩根，”桂妮薇尔说。 “杰克和安妮呢？ 他们可以和我们留在阿瓦隆吗？”

“I fear they must return to Camelot,” said Merlin, stepping forward. “Tonight will be the last night of the full moon. Before the sun rises on Camelot tomorrow, they must restore the gold dragon to its rightful place in the garden, or its power will be lost forever.” “我担心他们必须返回卡梅洛特，”梅林说着，向前走了一步。 “今晚将是满月的最后一个夜晚。 在明天太阳升起照耀卡梅洛特之前，他们必须把金龙送回花园里它原本的位置，否则它的力量将永远消失。”

“But the dragon is not in its place now,” said Teddy. “So how can they return, if we cannot?” “但现在龙不在它该在的地方，”泰迪说。 “那如果我们做不到，他们怎么回来呢？”

“I imagine they will return in the same fashion that they have traveled here,” said Morgan. “With their own special magic.” “我想他们会以和他们来到这里相同的方式返回，”摩根说。 “用他们自己的魔法。”

“Oh!” said Jack. He felt in his pocket for the piece of volcanic rock. “With Cafelle’s stones?” “哦！”杰克说。 他摸了摸口袋，寻找那块火山岩石。 “用卡弗莱的石头？”

Morgan smiled and nodded. “Yes. I am certain that the magic stones, along with your words of strength and hope, will again call forth the dragon’s power to open the portal. When I see Cafelle in Camelot, I will tell her of your courage.” 摩根微笑着点点头。 “是的。 我确信，这些魔法石头，加上你坚强和充满希望的话语，会再次唤醒巨龙的力量，打开传送门。 当我见到卡梅洛特的卡费尔时，我会告诉她你的勇敢。”

“And I will tell her that you saved my life,” King Arthur said. “我还会告诉她，你救了我的命，”亚瑟王说。

“We shall be grateful to you both forever,” said Queen Guinevere. “我们俩将永远感激你们，”桂妮薇尔王后说。

“Please tell my knights I will return soon,” said King Arthur, “and together we will restore the kingdom.” “请告诉我的骑士们，我很快就会回来，”亚瑟王说，“我们一起将恢复王国。”

“Yes, Your Majesty,” said Jack. “是的，陛下，”杰克说。

“It is time,” said Merlin. “是时候了，”梅林说。

Everyone grew quiet. Annie and Jack took the red stones from their pockets. Annie held Oki, and Jack picked up the heavy statue of the ninth dragon. Then, with all his heart and all his good feelings for their friends, Jack whispered, “Long live Camelot.” 大家都安静了下来。 安妮和杰克从口袋里拿出了红色的石头。 安妮抱着奥基，杰克捡起了第九条龙的巨大雕像。 然后，用他全部的心和对朋友们的美好感情，杰克小声说：“卡梅洛特万岁。”

“Long live Camelot,” echoed Annie. “卡梅洛特万岁，”安妮也跟着说。

Grasping their stones, Jack and Annie whispered these words over and over with hope and strength, until the air crackled and a rushing wind bent the trees. Clouds billowed and spun into swirling white masses, then changed their colors from white to pink to red. And then from the churning crimson sky, the ninth dragon took shape and spread its great wings. In a rush of air and burst of light, Jack and Annie and Oki were lifted off the Isle of Avalon and returned to the castle of Camelot. 握着他们的石头，杰克和安妮满怀希望和力量一遍又一遍地低声念着这些话，直到空气发出噼啪声，一阵狂风吹弯了树木。 云朵翻滚着，旋转成一团团白色的漩涡，然后从白色变成了粉色，又变成了红色。 接着，从翻滚的红色天空中，第九条龙逐渐成形，展开它巨大的翅膀。 在一阵风和一道强光中，杰克、安妮和奥基被从阿瓦隆岛抬了起来，回到了卡梅洛特的城堡。
