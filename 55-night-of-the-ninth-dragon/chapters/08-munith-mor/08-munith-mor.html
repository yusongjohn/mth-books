8. Munith Mor 8. 莫尼特·莫

The oxen bellowed. Oki barked wildly. 牛发出低沉的吼声。 奥基疯狂地叫了起来。

“Jack! Annie!” Guinevere cried. As she climbed out of the back of the cart, Oki was barking, but King Arthur slept on. “Are you all right?” “杰克！ 安妮！”桂妮薇尔喊道。 当她从马车后面爬出来时，奥基还在叫，但亚瑟王继续睡着。 “你没事吧？”

“Yes, Your Majesty,” Annie said, standing up with Jack. “是的，陛下，”安妮说，站起来和杰克一起站着。

“The rope snapped in two,” said Jack. “绳子断成两截了，”杰克说。

“I see,” said the queen. The wooden pole that had been lashed to the oxen’s yoke had fallen to the ground. The rope was frayed and broken. The oxen were no longer connected to the cart. “我明白了，”女王说。 那根绑在牛轭上的木杆掉到了地上。 绳子已经磨损断裂了。 牛不再和马车连在一起了。

“Can we fix it?” Annie asked hopefully. “我们能修好吗？”安妮满怀希望地问。

“No, I fear not,” the queen said, holding the pieces of the rope. She took a deep breath. “Nothing can be fixed now. Even if we get to Munith Mor and find the gold dragon statue, we will never get back to Camelot’s garden by dawn.” “恐怕不能，”女王说，手里拿着绳子的碎片。 她深吸了一口气。 “现在什么都无法修复了。” 即使我们到了穆尼特莫，找到了金龙雕像，我们也无法在天亮前回到卡梅洛特的花园。”

“Oh,” said Annie. “哦，”安妮说。

No one said anything for a moment. 大家一时都没有说话。

Finally Jack broke the silence. “We never give up,” he said. “We have to do something.” 最后，杰克打破了沉默。 “我们从不放弃，”他说。 “我们必须做点什么。”

“Like what?” said Annie. “做什么呢？”安妮问。

“We’ll have to walk,” said Jack. “我们必须走路，”杰克说。

“The king cannot walk, Jack,” said the queen. “He is too weak.” “国王不能走路，杰克，”女王说。 “他太虚弱了。”

“Then we’ll pull him in the cart,” said Jack. “The pole is still attached. Annie and I can pick it up and pull the cart to Munith Mor.” “那我们就把他装到推车上，”杰克说。 “杆子还连着。 安妮和我可以把它捡起来，把推车拉到穆尼特·莫尔那里。”

“Sure,” said Annie. “It’s not that far. We’re almost there. We can do it.” “当然可以，”安妮说。 “没多远。 我们快到了。我们能做到。”

Guinevere took a deep breath. “Yes,” she said. “Thank you, children. I will pull, too.” 桂妮薇尔深吸了一口气。 “是的，”她说。 “谢谢你们，孩子们。 我也会帮忙拉。”

“Should we take the yoke off the oxen first?” Annie asked. “Maybe they’ll return home on their own.” “我们先给牛卸下轭吗？”安妮问。 “也许它们会自己回家。”

“Yes, of course,” said the queen. “是的，当然可以，”女王说。

With Guinevere’s help, Jack and Annie lifted the heavy yoke from the necks of the two oxen and set it on the ground. 在桂妮薇尔的帮助下，杰克和安妮把沉重的轭从两头牛的脖子上卸下，放在了地上。

“Go home. You’re free,” Jack said. “回家吧。 你们自由了，”杰克说。

Neither ox moved. 两头牛都没有动。

“Go back home to Camelot,” said Annie. “That’s what we need you to do now. Don’t worry about us.” “回到卡梅洛特去吧，”安妮说。 “这就是我们现在需要你们做的。 不用为我们担心。”

The heavy animals snorted. Then they both began lumbering back across the moor. 那些笨重的动物们哼哧哼哧地喘着气。 然后它们都开始慢吞吞地往沼泽地那边走回去。

“Good job! Bye, guys! Thanks!” Annie called after them. “干得好！再见啦，伙计们！ 谢谢！”，安妮在后面喊道。

“Come on, let’s get going,” said Jack. He picked up his pack. As he placed it in the back of the cart, he saw that the king was still sleeping in the hay under his cloak. Oki was lying on the king’s chest. “Good dog. Stay with him,” Jack whispered. “快点，我们该出发了，”杰克说道。 他捡起了自己的背包。 当他把它放进马车后面时，他看到国王仍然穿着斗篷，睡在干草堆上。 奥基正躺在国王的胸口上。 “好狗， 待在他身边，”杰克小声说。

Jack hurried to Annie and Guinevere. “Ready?” he said. 杰克赶紧跑到安妮和桂妮薇尔面前。 “准备好了吗？”他说。

“Ready,” they both answered. “准备好了，”她们两个回答。

Jack and Annie stood at one end of the pole, and Guinevere stood at the other. “Okay, team!” said Jack. “Grab and lift!” 杰克和安妮站在木杆的一端，桂妮薇尔站在另一端。 “好了，团队！”杰克说。 “抓住它，把它抬起来！”

All of them wrapped their arms around the wooden pole. “Heave!” said Annie. Puffing and panting, they lifted the pole off the ground. 他们所有人都把手臂绕在木杆上。 “用力！”安妮说。 他们喘着气，把木杆从地上抬了起来。

“Onward!” said Jack. Gripping the pole, they struggled together, trying to move the cart forward. But it didn’t budge. “前进！”杰克说。 抓住柱子，他们一起努力，试着把推车往前推。 但是推车一动不动。

“Wait, I’ll push,” said Jack. He went to the back of the cart and pushed. He pushed so hard that his feet slipped, and he fell to his knees. But he scrambled up and tried again. “等一下，我来推，”杰克说。 他走到推车后面，开始推。 他推得非常用力，结果脚一滑，摔倒在膝盖上。 但他又爬起来，再次尝试。

As Jack pushed, Annie and Guinevere pulled from the front. The cart rocked forward, then slipped back. Jack pushed as hard as he could. Finally the wheels creaked and moaned, and the cart began rolling over the ground. 杰克在后面推，安妮和桂妮薇尔在前面拉。 推车向前一晃，又滑回原地。 杰克用尽全力去推。 终于，轮子发出吱呀声，推车开始在地面上滚动起来。

“Yay! Keep going,” called Annie. “太棒了！”， “继续往前走，”安妮喊道。

Keep going echoed in Jack’s mind as he pushed the cart across the moor. Even the squeak of the wheels seemed to say Keep going, keep going. “继续往前走”在杰克的脑海中回响，他推着推车穿过了沼泽地。 就连车轮发出的吱呀声似乎也在说：“继续往前走，继续往前走。”

Jack kept pushing while Annie and Guinevere kept pulling, until at last the cart rolled to a stop at the foot of the mountains. 杰克继续推着车，而安妮和桂妮薇尔则继续拉着车，直到最后，推车终于停在了山脚下。

“We’re here,” Jack said, panting. He ran to the front of the cart and helped Annie and Guinevere lower the pole to the ground. “我们到了，”杰克喘着气说。 他跑到马车前面，帮安妮和桂妮薇尔把杆子放到底下。

Then they all looked up at Munith Mor looming overhead. 然后他们都抬头看着头顶上高耸的蒙尼特莫尔。

A stream of moonlit water splashed over the ridges of the mountainside. The shimmering white waterfall spilled into a pool and then coursed down a narrow streambed. Except for the soft rushing sound of the water, the night was still. 一道月光照亮的水流溅过山腰的山脊。 那道闪闪发光的白色瀑布流进一个水池，然后沿着狭窄的溪流向下奔腾。 除了水声轻轻流淌的声音，夜晚非常安静。

“Are we in Avalon?” a deep voice rasped. King Arthur was sitting up in the hay. “我们到阿瓦隆了吗？”一个低沉的声音沙哑地说。 亚瑟王从干草堆上坐了起来。

The queen hurried to him. “We are very close,” she said. 王后赶紧走到他身边。 “我们离那里非常近了，”她说。

“Help…help me down,” he said. “帮…帮我下来，”他说。

As Guinevere helped Arthur from the back of the cart, Annie put Oki on the ground. Then she turned to Jack. “We have to solve Cafelle’s riddle now,” she said. 当桂妮薇尔帮亚瑟从马车后边下来时，安妮把奥基放在了地上。 然后她转向杰克。 “我们现在必须解开卡法尔的谜题，”她说。

Jack pulled his notebook out of his backpack and turned to the riddle. “There’s not enough light,” he said. 杰克从背包里拿出他的笔记本，翻到谜题那一页。 “这里光线不够，”他说。

“Don’t worry, we can remember,” said Annie. “ Moon so bright. Munith Mor night.” “别担心，我们可以记住，”安妮说。 “月亮这么亮。 穆尼特·莫尔的夜晚。”

“Then Curtain of white. Hides from sight,” said Jack. “然后白色的窗帘。 藏起视线，”杰克说。

“Yes! Magic for flight,” said Annie. “对！ 魔法带来飞行，”安妮说。

“Got it,” said Jack. He repeated the whole riddle: “Moon so bright. Munith Mor night. Curtain of white. Hides from sight. Magic for flight. Before dawn’s light. Or lose the fight.” “明白了，”杰克说。 他重复了整个谜语：‘月亮这么亮。穆尼特·莫尔的夜晚’ 白色的窗帘。躲藏起来，看不见。 魔法带来飞行的力量。 在黎明之前。 否则就会输掉战斗。

“The moon is bright, and we’re at Munith Mor,” said Annie. “So where’s the curtain?” “月亮很亮，我们到了穆尼特莫尔，”安妮说。 “所以窗帘在哪里呢？”

They looked around at the barren landscape. 他们环顾四周，看着这片荒凉的景色。

“That’s the big question,” said Jack. “这就是最大的问题，”杰克说。

“Hey, look at that waterfall!” said Annie. “Doesn’t it kind of look like a curtain?” “嘿，快看那条瀑布！”安妮说。 “它看起来是不是有点像窗帘？”

Jack stared at the sparkling torrent flowing over the side of the mountain. It did look like a curtain, he thought. 杰克盯着从山边流下来的闪闪发光的瀑布。 看起来真像一块窗帘，他想。

“Yes! A curtain of white!” Jack said. “对！ 一块白色的窗帘！”杰克说。

“Hides from sight!” said Annie. “The waterfall’s hiding something!” “藏起来的东西！”安妮说。 “瀑布后面藏着什么东西！”

“Magic for flight,” said Jack. “飞行的魔法，”杰克说。

“The gold dragon statue!” said Annie. “那尊金龙雕像！”安妮说。

“Behind the waterfall!” said Jack. “在瀑布后面！”杰克说。

“That’s the hidden place!” said Annie. “那就是隐藏的地方！”安妮说。

“Yes!” said Jack. He turned to the king and queen. “The gold dragon is somewhere behind that waterfall,” he said. “是的！”杰克说。 他转向国王和王后。 “那条金龙就在那道瀑布后面 somewhere，”他说。

“We’re going to check it out. You guys stay here,” Annie said to the queen. “I mean, Your Majesties stay here.” “我们要去查看一下。 你们 guys 待在这里，”安妮对王后说。 “我的意思是，陛下们待在这里。”

Guinevere smiled. “Pray, be careful,” she said. 桂妮薇尔微笑了。 “请小心，”她说。

“We will,” said Annie. As she and Jack started toward the waterfall, Oki ran along beside them. “我们会的，”安妮说。 当她和杰克朝瀑布走去时，奥基在他们旁边跑着。

“No! Go back!” Jack commanded. “不！” “回去！”杰克命令道。

Oki barked at him. 奥基朝他叫了起来。

“Let’s just put him in your bag,” said Annie. She picked up the puppy and lowered him into Jack’s backpack. “There you go.” “我们干脆把他放进你的包里吧，”安妮说。 她抱起小狗，把它放进杰克的背包里。 “好了。”

“Come on!” said Jack. And they headed toward the shimmering curtain of white. “快点！”杰克说。 他们朝那道闪闪发光的白色帘幕走去。

“Wait, I need to wash off the yellow marks!” said Annie. She ran to the streambed and splashed water on her face. When she finished, she hurried back to Jack. “All set,” she said. “等一下，我得把脸上的黄色痕迹洗掉！”安妮说。 她跑到溪边，用水泼在脸上。 她说完，赶紧跑回杰克身边。 “准备好了，”她说。

Jack and Annie climbed up to a rocky ledge and gazed at the cascade. Oki sneezed in Jack’s pack. “Hang in there, buddy,” said Jack. “Okay. See that rock formation jutting out? See how the water shoots over it and then splashes down on the rocks below? It looks like there’s an opening of some kind behind the waterfall…doesn’t it?” 杰克和安妮爬到一块岩石平台上，望着瀑布。 奥基在杰克的背包里打了个喷嚏。 “坚持住， buddy，”杰克说。 “好的。 看到那块突出的岩石了吗？ 看到水从上面流下来，然后溅到下面的岩石上吗？ 看起来瀑布后面好像有个入口……不是吗？

“Yes!” Annie said. “Let’s go!” “是的！”安妮说。 “我们走吧！”
