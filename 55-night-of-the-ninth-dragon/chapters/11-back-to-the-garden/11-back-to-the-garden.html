11. Back to the Garden 11. 回到花园

Y ip? said Oki. 咦？奥基说。

“Wow,” said Annie. “哇，”安妮说。

“Yes,” said Jack. In the first light of dawn, he could see the tangles of vines and flowers, the fishpond, and the stone wall. They were back in Morgan le Fay’s secret garden. “对，”杰克说。 在黎明的第一缕光中，他能看到缠绕的藤蔓和花朵、鱼塘，还有石墙。 他们又回到了摩根·勒·法伊的秘密花园。

Oki leapt from Annie’s arms and scurried among the patches of ivy, wild grasses, and shrubs, yapping and yipping at the dragons made of glass and granite, marble, clay, bronze, silver, and seashells. 奥基从安妮的臂弯里跳下来，在常春藤、野草和灌木丛中穿梭，对着由玻璃、花岗岩、大理石、黏土、青铜、银和贝壳制成的龙吠叫和欢叫。

“The ninth dragon goes behind those yellow rosebushes,” said Annie. “第九条龙在那些黄色玫瑰丛后面，”安妮说。

“Right,” said Jack. “And let’s leave Cafelle’s magic stones there, too.” “对，”杰克说。 “还有，我们把卡费尔的魔法石头也留在那儿吧。”

Jack carefully placed the statue between the bushes and stone wall. Then he and Annie set Cafelle’s stones at its feet. The dragon glistened and shone as it caught the light of the rising sun. 杰克小心地把雕像放在灌木和石墙之间。 然后他和安妮把卡费尔的石头放在雕像的脚下。 龙在阳光照耀下闪闪发光。

“Good-bye,” Jack whispered. “再见，”杰克小声说。

“Long live Camelot,” said Annie. “卡梅洛特万岁，”安妮说。

Yip! said Oki. “耶！”，奥基说。

“Let’s go,” said Jack. And the three of them left Morgan’s garden. They walked through the library and down the musty hallway, down the steep steps and through the rocky passage to the outer courtyard. “我们走吧，”杰克说。 他们三人离开了摩根的花园。 他们穿过图书馆，走过发霉的走廊，走下陡峭的台阶，穿过岩石小路，来到外院。

Sir Lamorak and Sir Tristan were standing by the entrance gate. 拉莫拉克爵士和特里斯坦爵士正站在入口的门前。

“Hello, sirs!” Annie called from behind them. “你们好，爵士们！”，安妮从他们身后喊道。

The knights whirled around. When they saw Jack and Annie, they lowered their lances. 骑士们转过身来。 他们看到杰克和安妮时，放下了长矛。

“How—how did you get inside?” stammered Sir Lamorak. “怎—怎么进来的？”拉莫拉克爵士结结巴巴地说。

“Where are the king and queen?” said Sir Tristan. “国王和王后在哪里？”特里斯坦爵士说。

“They’re safe in Avalon!” said Jack. “他们在阿瓦隆很安全！”杰克说。

“What happened?” said Sir Lamorak. “发生了什么？”拉莫拉克爵士说。

“It’s a long story,” said Annie. “But just know that the king will be completely well soon. And when he is, he and Guinevere will come back to the castle.” “这是一个很长的故事，”安妮说。 “但你要知道，国王很快就会完全康复。 等他康复后，他和桂妮薇尔就会回到城堡。”

“And the king said to tell you that he’ll work with his knights and all his subjects to restore order to the kingdom,” said Jack. “而且国王说要告诉你们，他会和骑士们以及所有子民一起努力，恢复王国的秩序，”杰克说。

The two knights shook their heads in wonder. 两位骑士惊讶地摇了摇头。

“Bye for now,” said Annie. “Hold down the fort!” “再见啦，”安妮说。 “守住阵地！”

Jack, Annie, and Oki headed out the castle entrance gate and started through the hay field. They ran over the dry grass, then pushed their way through the tall wheat stalks waving in the wind. From the field, they headed into the shadowy forest, and Oki led the way around the ancient oaks, until they came to the magic tree house. 杰克、安妮和奥基从城堡的入口门走了出去，开始穿过干草田。 他们跑过干草，然后挤着穿过随风摇摆的高高的麦秆。 从田里，他们走进了阴暗的森林，奥基带领他们绕过古老的橡树，直到他们来到神奇的树屋前。

“Let’s go, buddy!” said Jack. “我们走吧， buddy！”杰克说。

He lifted the puppy and put him into his pack. Then he and Annie headed up the rope ladder. When they climbed into the tree house, Annie grabbed the Pennsylvania book and pointed at a picture of Frog Creek. 他抱起小狗，把它放进背包里。 然后他和安妮爬上了绳梯。 当他们爬进树屋时，安妮抓起了宾夕法尼亚的书，指着一张蛙溪镇的图片。

“I wish we could go there!” she said. “我希望我们能去那里！”她说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来了。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转起来。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。

The day was cloudy and cool. No time had passed in the Frog Creek woods. The wind shook the tree leaves. 那天天气阴沉沉的，凉凉的。 在蛙溪镇的森林里，时间一点都没过去。 风摇晃着树上的叶子。

Yip! Oki popped his head out of Jack’s pack. 咦！ 奥基从杰克的背包里探出头来。

“We’re home,” said Annie. “我们到家了，”安妮说。

“Home, sweet home,” said Jack. “Right, little buddy.” “家，甜蜜的家，”杰克说。 “对，小家伙。”

Yip! 汪！

Jack climbed down the rope ladder, and Annie followed. Then they started through the Frog Creek woods. 杰克顺着绳梯爬下来，安妮跟着下来。 然后他们开始穿过蛙溪镇的树林。

“Should we put Oki on the ground?” said Annie. “我们应该把奥基放在地上吗？”安妮说。

“Nope. Keep him in the pack,” said Jack. “不用， 把他留在背包里，”杰克说。

“All the way to the dog park?” asked Annie. “一路到狗狗公园吗？”安妮问。

“Dog park? Are you serious?” said Jack. “You still want to go to the dog park? Aren’t you worn out?” “狗狗公园？ “你当真吗？”杰克说。 “你还是想去狗狗公园吗？” 你不是累了吗？

“No, not really,” said Annie. “不，其实没那么累，”安妮说。

“Really not really?” said Jack. “真的不是真的吗？”杰克说。

“Really not really,” said Annie. “真的不是真的，”安妮说。

“But we did so much today,” said Jack. “但我们今天做了很多事情，”杰克说。

“Not really,” said Annie. “Oki just led us to the magic tree house. And then we took a little journey to Camelot’s secret garden, met a blind seer, scared away robbers, hauled a broken cart across a moor, solved a riddle, explored a hidden cave, found a stolen statue, faced a dragon bigger than a mountain, landed on the Isle of Avalon, saw a unicorn, heard some mermaids, and used magic stones to take the gold dragon back to the garden to help the king and queen and our Camelot friends return to the castle.” She shrugged. “And then we came home to Frog Creek.” “不是真的，”安妮说。 “奥基只是带我们去了神奇的树屋。 然后我们去了一趟卡梅洛特的秘密花园，遇见了一位盲眼先知，吓跑了强盗，把一辆坏掉的马车拉过了沼泽，解开了一道谜题，探索了一个隐藏的洞穴，找到了一尊被偷走的雕像，面对了一条比山还大的龙，降落在阿瓦隆岛上，看到了独角兽，听到了一些人鱼，还用魔法石头把金龙带回了花园，帮助国王、王后和我们的卡梅洛特朋友们返回了城堡。”她耸了耸肩。 “然后我们回到了蛙溪镇。”

Jack nodded. “Okay. What you’re saying is Oki just took us on a little detour on our way to the dog park.” 杰克点了点头。 “好的。 你意思是奥基只是在我们去狗狗公园的路上带我们绕了个小路。”

“That’s what I’m saying,” said Annie. “So, do you want to go there now?” “这就是我想说的，”安妮说。 “那么，你现在想去那里吗？”

“Sure, why not?” said Jack as they headed out of the woods. “And let’s stop by the pet store.” “当然，为什么不呢？”杰克边说边和他们一起走出了树林。 “顺便去宠物店看看吧。”

“Right. You want to buy a toothbrush and toothpaste for Oki’s dental care,” said Annie. “对。” “你想给奥基买牙刷和牙膏，让他注意牙齿护理，”安妮说。

“I do,” said Jack. “是的，”杰克说。

“Cool,” said Annie. “You know what? I love our ordinary lives.” “太酷了，”安妮说。 “你知道吗？ 我最喜欢我们平凡的生活。”

“Me too,” said Jack. “Long live Frog Creek.” “我也是，”杰克说。 “蛙溪镇万岁。”
