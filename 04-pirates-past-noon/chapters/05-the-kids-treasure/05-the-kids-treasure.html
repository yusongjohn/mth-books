5. The Kid’s Treasure 5. 基德的宝藏

Jack and Annie watched in horror. 杰克和安妮吓得看着。

The gold-greedy pirates seemed to have lost their minds. 这些贪金子的海盗好像疯了一样。

Jack nudged Annie. Together they started to back slowly away from the pirates. Toward the tree house. 杰克轻轻碰了碰安妮。 他们一起慢慢往后退，离开那些海盗。 朝树屋那边退。

“Halt!” Cap’n Bones shouted. He aimed his pistols at them. “Not another step, lubbers!” “站住！”骨头船长喊道。 他把枪口对准他们。 “再敢走一步试试，旱鸭子！”

Jack and Annie froze. 杰克和安妮愣住了。

Cap’n Bones grinned his black-toothed grin. “Tell old Bones where the rest is,” he growled. “Or prepare to meet thy doom.” 骨头船长咧开黑牙笑了。 “告诉老骨头，剩下的在哪儿。”他低吼。 “不然就等着完蛋吧。”

“What—what rest?” said Annie. “什——什么剩下的？”安妮说。

“The rest of the treasure!” roared Cap’n Bones. “I know it’s on this island. I have a map!” “剩下的宝藏！”骨头船长吼道。 “我知道就在这座岛上。我有地图！”

He reached into a belt pouch and pulled out a torn piece of paper. He waved it at Jack and Annie. 他伸进腰带上的袋子，掏出一张撕破的纸。 他冲杰克和安妮晃了晃。

“Is that a treasure map?” asked Jack. “那是藏宝图吗？”杰克问。

“Aye, it’s the map telling about Kidd’s treasure.” “对，这张图说的是基德的宝藏。”

“Which kid’s treasure? Not us kids,” said Annie. “We don’t know anything about a kid’s treasure.” “哪个小孩的宝藏？ 不是我们小孩的吧。”安妮说。 “我们根本不知道什么小孩的宝藏。”

“Why don’t you read the map?” said Jack. “你为什么不自己看地图？”杰克说。

“You read it!” Cap’n Bones shoved the map in Jack’s face. “你来念！”骨头船长把地图怼到杰克脸上。

Jack stared at the strange marks on the paper. 杰克盯着纸上那些奇怪的记号。

“What does that mean?” asked Jack. “那是什么意思？”杰克问。

“What does what mean?” asked Cap’n Bones. “什么什么意思？”骨头船长问。

“Those words.” Jack pointed at the words at the bottom of the map. “那些字。”杰克指着地图底下的字。

“Well, it means … ” Cap’n Bones’ good eye squinted at the writing. He frowned. He coughed. He rubbed his nose. “这个嘛，意思是……”骨头船长那只好眼睛眯起来看那些字。他皱起眉头。 他咳嗽了一声。 他揉了揉鼻子。

“Aw, leave him alone,” Pinky growled at Jack. “哎呀，别为难他了。”平奇冲杰克低吼。

“You know he can’t read,” said Stinky. “你知道他不认字。”臭臭说。

“Shut up!” Cap’n Bones roared at his men. “闭嘴！”骨头船长冲手下吼道。

“Jack and I can read,” Annie piped up. “杰克和我会认字。”安妮插嘴说。

“Shhh,” said Jack. “嘘。”杰克说。

“Cap’n, make ’em read the map!” said Stinky. “船长，让他们念地图！”臭臭说。

Cap’n Bones gave Jack and Annie a dark look. “Read it,” he growled. 骨头船长阴沉地看了杰克和安妮一眼。 “念。”他低吼。

“Then will you let us go?” said Jack. “那你就会放我们走吗？”杰克说。

图中文字：The gold doch lie beneath the whales eye. 图中文字：金豆子在鲸鱼眼睛下面。

The pirate squinted his good eye. “Aye, lubber. When the treasure’s in me hands, I’ll let you go.” 海盗眯起那只好眼睛。 “好，旱鸭子。 等宝藏到了我手里，我就放你们走。”

“Okay,” said Jack. “I’ll read it to you.” He looked at the map. “It says, The gold doth lie beneath the whale’s eye.” “好吧。”杰克说。 “我念给你听。”他看着地图。 “上面写着：金子就藏在鲸鱼的眼睛下面。”

“Heh?” Cap’n Bones scowled. “What’s that supposed to mean, lubber?” “啊？”骨头船长沉下脸。 “那是什么意思，旱鸭子？”

Jack shrugged. 杰克耸耸肩。

“Hang it! Take ’em back to the ship!” shouted Cap’n Bones. “They can rot there till they’re ready to tell us how to find Kidd’s treasure!” “该死！ 把他们带回大船去！”骨头船长喊道。 “让他们在那儿待着发霉，直到愿意告诉我们怎么找到基德的宝藏！”

Jack and Annie were tossed into the rowboat. 杰克和安妮被扔进了小船。

Waves splashed the sides. The sky ahead was dark with thunderclouds. A strong wind had started to blow. 浪花拍打着船帮。 前方的天空乌云密布。 大风刮了起来。

“Row, dogs, row!” said Cap’n Bones. “划啊，狗东西，划啊！”骨头船长说。

Pinky and Stinky began rowing toward the big ship. 平奇和臭臭开始朝大船划去。

“Look!” Annie said to Jack. She pointed to the shore. “看！”安妮对杰克说。 她指着岸边。

Polly the parrot was flying over the sand. 鹦鹉波莉正在沙滩上空飞。

“She wants to help us,” whispered Annie. “她想帮我们。”安妮小声说。

Polly started to fly out over the waves. But the winds were too strong. She turned around and flew back to the island. 波莉想朝海浪上空飞去。 可风太大了。 她掉过头，飞回了岛上。
