8. Dig, Dogs, Dig 8. 挖啊，狗东西，挖啊

Pinky and Stinky tied their rope around the big rock. 平奇和臭臭把绳子绑在大石头上。

The wind howled. The two pirates pulled. And pulled. And pulled. 风呼啸着。 两个海盗拉啊。拉啊。 还在拉。

“They need help!” said Jack. “他们需要帮忙！”杰克说。

“Aghh, let the dogs do the work!” growled Cap’n Bones. “哼，让这些狗东西自己干！”骨头船长吼道。

“You’re not very nice to them,” said Annie. “你对他们可不太好。”安妮说。

“Who cares?” roared Cap’n Bones. “谁在乎？”骨头船长吼道。

“Cap’n! We got it!” shouted Pinky. “船长！ 拉动了！”平奇喊道。

They started pulling the rock across the sand. 他们开始把石头往沙子上拖。

“Now let’s dig where the rock was,” said Jack. “All of us!” “现在在石头原来的地方挖吧。”杰克说。 “大家都来挖！”

But Cap’n Bones ignored his suggestion. 可骨头船长根本不理他。

“Dig, you dogs!” he shouted. “挖啊，你们这些狗东西！”他喊道。

Pinky and Stinky started to dig. The wind blew even harder. There was going to be a thunderstorm. 平奇和臭臭开始挖。 风刮得更猛了。 雷暴雨就要来了。

“Oww! I got sand in me eyes!” Pinky whined. “哎哟！ 沙子进眼睛了！”平奇哼哼着说。

“Oww! Me back hurts!” Stinky cried. “哎哟！ 我的背疼死了！”臭臭叫道。

“Dig!” shouted Cap’n Bones. He held Jack and Annie with one hand. With the other he pulled out the gold medallion. “挖！”骨头船长喊道。 他一只手抓着杰克和安妮。 另一只手掏出那枚金徽章。

He tossed it at the two pirates. It fell into the hole. 他把它扔向那两个海盗。 金徽章掉进了坑里。

“Dig for more of these, you swine!” he said. “再挖出更多这样的东西来，你们这些猪！”他说。

Squawk! 嘎！

“Look!” Annie said. “看！”安妮说。

Polly was back! She was circling above them! 波莉回来了！ 她在他们头顶上转圈！

“Go back!” she squawked. “回去！”她叫道。

Stinky and Pinky looked up at the parrot. They scowled. 臭臭和平奇抬头看着鹦鹉。他们皱起了眉。

“Dig!” shouted Cap’n Bones. “挖！”骨头船长喊道。

“A big storm is comin’, Cap’n!” said Pinky. “大暴风雨要来了，船长！”平奇说。

“Go back!” said Polly. “回去！”波莉说。

“The bird’s an omen, Cap’n!” shouted Stinky. “这只鸟是个预兆，船长！”臭臭喊道。

“Dig, you dogs!” cried Cap’n Bones. “挖啊，你们这些狗东西！”骨头船长叫道。

“Go back!” squawked Polly. “回去！”波莉叫道。

“The bird’s warning us!” shouted Pinky. “We’ve got to get to the ship before it’s too late!” “这只鸟在警告我们！”平奇喊道。 “我们得赶在来不及之前回到大船上去！”

The two pirates threw down their shovels. They started running toward the rowboat. 两个海盗扔下铲子。 他们朝小船跑去。

“Mutineers! Come back!” shouted Cap’n Bones. He dragged Jack and Annie down the beach as he ran after his men. “Stop!” “反叛的家伙！ 给我回来！”骨头船长喊道。 他拖着杰克和安妮，沿着海滩去追手下。 “站住！”

But the pirates kept running. They got to the rowboat and pushed it into the sea. 可海盗们还是接着跑。 他们跑到小船那儿，把它推进海里。

“Wait!” cried Cap’n Bones. “等等！”骨头船长叫道。

Pinky and Stinky jumped into the boat. They started rowing. 平奇和臭臭跳上了船。 他们开始划起来。

“Wait!” Cap’n Bones let go of Jack and Annie. He ran into the water. “Wait, you dogs!” “等等！”骨头船长松开了杰克和安妮。 他冲进水里。 “等等，你们这些狗东西！”

He hauled himself into the rowboat. 他爬上了小船。

Then the three pirates disappeared into the spray of the waves. 然后三个海盗消失在浪花里。

“Go back!” squawked Polly. “回去！”波莉叫道。

“She means us!” said Annie. “她说的是我们！”安妮说。

Just then the storm broke over the island. The wind howled. Rain fell in buckets. 就在这时，暴风雨砸到了岛上。风呼啸着。 雨像用桶往下倒一样。

“Let’s go!” cried Annie. “快走！”安妮叫道。

“Wait! I have to get the medallion!” shouted Jack. He ran to the hole dug by the pirates. He looked down in it. “等一下！ 我得去拿徽章！”杰克喊道。 他跑到海盗挖的坑那儿。 他往坑里看。

Even in the dreary light, the medallion was shining. 就算光线这么暗，徽章还是在发亮。

Big, fat raindrops were falling into the hole, washing away the sand. 又大又重的雨点打进坑里，把沙子冲走了。

Jack saw a patch of wood. 杰克看见一块木头。

Then the rain cleared away more sand. And Jack saw the top of an old trunk. 雨又冲走了更多沙子。 杰克看见一只旧箱子的盖子。

He stared. Was it Captain Kidd’s treasure chest? 他盯着看。 这是基德船长的藏宝箱吗？

“Hurry, Jack!” cried Annie. She was halfway up the tree house ladder. “快点，杰克！”安妮叫道。 她已经爬到树屋梯子的半当中了。

“I found it! I found it!” cried Jack. “I found the treasure chest!” “我找到了！ 我找到了！”杰克叫道。 “我找到藏宝箱了！”

“Forget the treasure chest!” said Annie. “We have to go now! The storm’s getting worse!” “别管什么藏宝箱了！”安妮说。 “我们现在就得走！ 暴风雨越来越大了！”

Jack kept staring at the chest. Was there gold inside? Silver? Precious gems? 杰克还盯着那只箱子。 里面有金子吗？银子吗？ 还有珍贵的宝石？

“Come on!” Now Annie was shouting from the tree house window. “快点啊！”这会儿安妮在树屋窗口喊了。

But Jack couldn’t tear himself away. He brushed the rest of the muddy sand off the chest. 可杰克就是舍不得离开。 他把箱子上剩下的泥沙拂掉。

“Jack, forget the treasure chest!” cried Annie. “Let’s go!” “杰克，别管藏宝箱了！”安妮叫道。 “快走！”

“Go back!” squawked Polly. “回去！”波莉叫道。

Jack looked at the parrot. She was perched on the black rock. 杰克看着那只鹦鹉。 她停在那块黑石头上。

He stared into her wise eyes. He thought he knew her—knew her from somewhere else. 他盯着她那双聪明的眼睛。 他觉得自己认识她——在别的地方见过她。

“Go back, Jack,” she said. She sounded like a person. “回去吧，杰克。”她说。 她说话的声音像人一样。

Okay. It was definitely time to go. 好吧。 现在真的该走了。

Jack took one last look at the treasure chest. He clutched the gold medallion. Then he took off, running toward the tree house. 杰克最后看了一眼藏宝箱。 他抓紧那枚金徽章。 然后他拔腿就跑，朝树屋冲去。

His socks and rain boots were still there. He quickly pulled the boots on. He shoved the socks into his backpack. 他的袜子和雨靴还在那儿。 他赶紧把靴子套上。 他把袜子塞进背包。

The rope ladder was dancing wildly in the wind. Jack grabbed it. 绳梯在风里狂甩。杰克一把抓住它。

The ladder swayed as Jack climbed. He was tossed this way and that. But he held on tight. 杰克往上爬的时候，梯子晃来晃去。 他被甩来甩去。 可他还是抓得紧紧的。

At last he pulled himself into the tree house. 终于，他爬进了树屋。

“Let’s go!” he cried. “走吧！”他喊道。

Annie was already holding the Pennsylvania book. She pointed to the picture of Frog Creek. 安妮已经拿着那本宾夕法尼亚的书了。 她指着蛙溪镇的那张图。

“I wish we could go there!” she shouted. “我希望我们能去那儿！”她喊道。

The wind was already blowing hard. But now it blew even harder. 风本来就刮得很猛。 这会儿刮得更猛了。

The tree house started to spin. It spun faster and faster. 树屋开始转起来。 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
