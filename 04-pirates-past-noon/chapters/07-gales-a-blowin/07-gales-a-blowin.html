7. Gale’s a-Blowin’ 7. 大风刮起来了

“So the treasure must be under that rock,” said Annie. “所以宝藏一定在那块石头下面。”安妮说。

“Right,” said Jack. “Now we just have to get back to the island. We’ll show Cap’n Bones where the treasure is. Then while all the pirates are digging, we’ll sneak up to the tree house.” “对。”杰克说。 “现在我们只要回到岛上就行了。 我们告诉骨头船长宝藏在哪儿。 等海盗们都在挖的时候，我们就偷偷溜回树屋。”

“And make a wish to go home,” said Annie. “然后许愿回家。”安妮说。

“Right.” Jack poked his head out of the round window of the cabin. “Cap’n Bones, sir!” he shouted. “对。”杰克把头伸出船舱的圆窗。 “骨头船长，长官！”他喊道。

The pirates took up the cry. “Cap’n Bones! Cap’n Bones!” 海盗们跟着喊起来。 “骨头船长！骨头船长！”

“Hey?” came a horrible voice. “干什么？”一个可怕的声音传来。

Cap’n Bones stuck his ugly face through the window. His good eye glared at Jack. “What do you want, lubbers?” 骨头船长把那张难看的脸伸进窗户。 他那只好眼睛瞪着杰克。 “想干什么，旱鸭子？”

“We’re ready to tell the truth, sir,” said Jack. “我们准备说实话了，长官。”杰克说。

“Go ahead,” growled Cap’n Bones. “说。”骨头船长低声吼道。

“We know where Captain Kidd’s treasure is.” “我们知道基德船长的宝藏在哪儿。”

“Where?” “在哪儿？”

“We can’t just tell you. We have to show you,” said Annie. “我们不能光说。 得带你去看。”安妮说。

Cap’n Bones gave Annie and Jack a long hard look. 骨头船长狠狠地盯着安妮和杰克，看了好一会儿。

“You’ll need a rope,” said Jack. “你们得带根绳子。”杰克说。

“And shovels,” said Annie. “还得带铲子。”安妮说。

Cap’n Bones growled. Then he roared at his men, “Get some rope and shovels!” 骨头船长咕哝了一声。 然后他对手下吼道：“去拿绳子和铲子来！”

“Aye, aye, Cap’n!” “是，船长！”

“Then throw these lubbers in the boat! We’re going back to the island!” “再把这两个旱鸭子扔进小船！ 我们回岛上去！”

“Aye, aye, Cap’n!” “是，船长！”

Back in the rowboat, Jack saw the sky had grown even darker with clouds. The waves were bigger. The wind was howling. 回到小船上，杰克看见天上的云更黑了。 浪更大了。 风在呼啸。

“Gale’s a-blowin’!” said Pinky. “大风刮起来了！”平奇说。

“You’ll see a gale if I don’t get me gold today, by thunder!” Cap’n Bones shouted. “Row, dogs, row!” “要是我今天拿不到金子，你们才知道什么叫大风呢，真要命！”骨头船长喊道。 “划啊，狗东西，划啊！”

The three pirates battled the waves, until the rowboat reached the island. They all piled onto the shore. 三个海盗跟大浪搏斗，小船终于靠上了岛。 他们全挤上了岸。

Cap’n Bones grabbed Jack and Annie. 骨头船长抓住杰克和安妮。

“Okay, lubbers,” he said. “Now show us where the treasure is.” “好了，旱鸭子。”他说。 “现在带我们去看宝藏在哪儿。”

“There,” said Annie. She pointed at the black rock near the tip of the island. “那儿。”安妮说。 她指着靠近岛尖的那块黑石头。

“Under that rock,” said Jack. “就在那块石头下面。”杰克说。

Cap’n Bones dragged Jack and Annie down the beach to the black rock. 骨头船长拖着杰克和安妮，沿着海滩走到黑石头那儿。

“Get to work!” Cap’n Bones said to Pinky and Stinky. “动手干活！”骨头船长对平奇和臭臭说。

“What about you?” said Annie. “那你呢？”安妮说。

“Me? Work?” Cap’n Bones chuckled. “我？ 干活？”骨头船长咯咯笑了。

Jack gulped. How could they get away from him? 杰克咽了口唾沫。 他们要怎么才能甩掉他呢？

“Don’t you think you should help your friends?” he said. “你不觉得该帮帮你的朋友吗？”他说。

Cap’n Bones gave Jack a horrible grin. “Nay. I’m going to hold you two—till there’s treasure in me hands!” 骨头船长朝杰克露出一个可怕的笑。 “才不要。 我得抓住你们俩——直到金子到我手里！”
