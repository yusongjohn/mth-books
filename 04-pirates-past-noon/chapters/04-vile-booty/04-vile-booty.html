4. Vile Booty 4. 讨厌的战利品

Jack started to run across the hot sand. He ran as fast as he could. 杰克开始在滚烫的沙子上跑。 他拼命跑。

But the pirates ran faster. 可海盗跑得更快。

Before Jack knew it, the biggest pirate had grabbed him! 杰克还没反应过来，那个最大的海盗已经抓住他了！

Jack struggled. But the pirate had huge, strong arms. He held on to Jack and laughed a mean, ugly laugh. He had a shaggy black beard. A patch covered one eye. 杰克拼命挣扎。 可那个海盗的胳膊又粗又壮。 他抓着杰克，发出难听又凶狠的笑声。 他留着乱蓬蓬的黑胡子。 一只眼睛上蒙着眼罩。

Jack heard Annie yelling. He saw her coming down the rope ladder. 杰克听见安妮在喊。 他看见她顺着绳梯往下爬。

“Stay where you are!” Jack shouted. “待在那儿别动！”杰克喊道。

But Annie kept coming. “Leave him alone, you bully!” she cried. 可安妮还是下来了。 “放开他，你这个坏蛋！”她喊道。

The other two pirates laughed meanly. They were dirty and ragged. 另外两个海盗阴狠狠地笑着。 他们又脏又破。

Annie charged up to the biggest pirate. “Let him go!” she said. She hit the pirate with her fist and kicked him. 安妮冲到那个最大的海盗面前。 “放开他！”她说。 她挥拳打他，还踢他。

But the pirate just growled. Then he grabbed her, too. And with his giant hands, he held Jack and Annie as if they were two kittens. 可那个海盗只是低吼了一声。 接着把她也抓住了。 他那双大手抓着杰克和安妮，就像抓着两只小猫。

“No one escapes Cap’n Bones!” he roared. His breath was terrible. “谁也逃不出骨头船长的手！”他吼道。 他的口气难闻极了。

“Let go!” Annie shouted into his face. “放手！”安妮冲着他的脸喊。

But Cap’n Bones just smiled. All his teeth were black. 可骨头船长只是笑了笑。 他满口牙都是黑的。

Annie fell silent. 安妮不说话了。

Cap’n Bones laughed loudly. Then he turned to the other two. 骨头船长大声笑起来。 然后转向另外两个。

“Find out what’s in their house, you dogs,” he said. “去看看他们屋里有什么，你们这些狗东西。”他说。

“Aye, aye, Cap’n!” they answered. And they started up the ladder to the tree house. “是，船长！”他们答道。 他们顺着梯子爬上树屋。

“What do you spy, Pinky?” shouted Cap’n Bones. “看见什么了，平奇？”骨头船长喊道。

“Books, Cap’n!” Pinky shouted down. “书，船长！”平奇朝下喊。

“Aghh, books,” growled Cap’n Bones. He spit on the sand. “I want gold, you dogs!” “啊，书。”骨头船长低吼。 他往沙子上吐了口唾沫。 “我要的是金子，你们这些狗东西！”

“Dogs are nicer than you,” said Annie. “狗都比你强。”安妮说。

“Shhh,” said Jack. “嘘。”杰克说。

“What about you, Stinky?” Cap’n Bones roared. “你呢，臭臭？”骨头船长吼道。

“Just books, Cap’n!” shouted Stinky. “只有书，船长！”臭臭喊道。

“Aghh, books,” said Cap’n Bones. He spit on the sand again. “I hate books! Keep looking, dogs! Find me something good!” “啊，书。”骨头船长说。 他又往沙子上吐了口唾沫。 “我讨厌书！ 继续找，狗东西！ 给我找点好东西！”

Cap’n Bones grabbed Jack’s backpack. “What’s in here?” he said. 骨头船长一把抓过杰克的背包。 “这里面是什么？”他说。

“Nothing—” Jack quickly opened the pack. “Just paper, a pencil, a book.” “没什么——”杰克赶紧打开背包。 “就几张纸、一支铅笔，还有一本书。”

“Another book!” roared Cap’n Bones. “That’s vile booty!” “又是书！”骨头船长吼道。 “这战利品恶心死了！”

A gleeful shriek pierced the air. 一声高兴的尖叫刺破了空气。

Cap’n Bones froze. “What’s that?” he shouted. 骨头船长愣住了。 “那是什么？”他喊道。

“Look, Cap’n! Look!”

Pinky leaned out the tree house window. He held the medallion. It glimmered in the sunlight. 平奇从树屋窗口探出身来。 他手里拿着那枚徽章。 它在阳光下闪闪发亮。

Oh brother, thought Jack. 完了，杰克想。

“Throw it down!” cried Cap’n Bones. “扔下来！”骨头船长喊道。

“It’s not yours!” shouted Annie. “那不是你的！”安妮喊道。

Cap’n Bones dropped Jack and Annie. He caught the medallion as it fell. 骨头船长放下杰克和安妮。 徽章掉下来时，他一把接住。

“Gold! Gold! Gold!” he cried. Cap’n Bones threw back his head and laughed horribly. “金子！金子！ 金子！”他叫道。 骨头船长把头往后一仰，发出可怕的大笑。

He grabbed two of his pistols. He shot them into the air. Pinky and Stinky howled like wolves. 他抓起两把手枪。 对着天空开了两枪。 平奇和臭臭像狼一样嚎叫起来。
