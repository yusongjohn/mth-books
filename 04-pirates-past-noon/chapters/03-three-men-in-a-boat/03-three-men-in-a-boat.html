3. Three Men in a Boat 3. 小船上的三个男人

“We’ve come to the time of pirates!” Jack said. “我们来到海盗的时代了！”杰克说。

“Pirates?” squeaked Annie. “Like in Peter Pan?” “海盗？”安妮尖声说。 “跟《彼得·潘》里的一样？”

Jack flipped to the picture that showed the parrot, the sea and the ship. 杰克翻到那页画着鹦鹉、大海和船的图画。

He read the caption under the picture: 他念了图画下面的说明：

Three hundred years ago, pirates raided Spanish treasure ships in the Caribbean Sea. 三百年以前，海盗在加勒比海袭击了西班牙的宝船。

He grabbed his notebook and pencil from his pack. He wrote: 他从背包里拿出笔记本和铅笔，写了下来：

图中文字：pirates in the Caribbean 图中文字：加勒比海盗

He turned to the next page. There was a picture of a pirate flag. He read: 他翻到下一页。 上面有一幅海盗旗的画。他念道：

The skull-and-crossbones flag was called the Jolly Roger. 骷髅和交叉骨头的旗帜叫做杰克·拉戈（Jolly Roger）。

“Let’s go!” said Annie. “走吧！”安妮说。

“Wait!” said Jack. “I want to make a drawing of the flag.” “等等！”杰克说。 “我想把这面旗画下来。”

He propped the pirate book in the sand. 他把海盗书支在沙子上。

He started drawing the Jolly Roger flag. 他开始画那面骷髅旗。

“Don’t copy the picture in the book,” said Annie. “Look at the real thing.” “别照着书上的画抄。”安妮说。 “看真的那面旗。”

But Jack pushed his glasses into place and kept drawing. 可杰克把眼镜推正，继续画。

“Jack, some pirates are getting into a rowboat,” said Annie. “杰克，有几个海盗正在上小船。”安妮说。

Jack kept drawing. 杰克继续画。

“Jack, the boat’s leaving the big ship,” said Annie. “杰克，小船离开大船了。”安妮说。

“What?” Jack looked up. “什么？”杰克抬起头。

“Look.” Annie pointed. “看。”安妮指着。

Jack looked. He saw the rowboat coming toward the shore. 杰克一看。 他看见小船正朝岸边划来。

“Run!” said Annie. She started running toward the tree house. “跑！”安妮说。 她朝树屋跑去。

Jack jumped up. His glasses fell off. 杰克跳了起来。 眼镜掉了。

“Hurry!” Annie called back to him. “快点！”安妮回头喊他。

Jack went down on his knees. He felt for his glasses. Where were they? 杰克跪了下去。 他摸来摸去找眼镜。眼镜哪儿去了？

Jack saw something glinting in the sand. He reached for it. It was his glasses. He snatched them up. 杰克看见沙子里有东西在闪光。 他伸手去拿。 是他的眼镜。 他一把抓起来。

Then he threw his notebook and pencil into his pack. He put the pack on his back. 然后把笔记本和铅笔扔进背包。 他背上背包。

He grabbed his boots and his socks. And he took off running. 抓起靴子和袜子。 撒腿就跑。

“Hurry! They’re coming!” Annie was at the top of the rope ladder. “快点！ 他们来了！”安妮已经爬到绳梯顶上了。

Jack looked back at the sea. The pirates were closer to the shore. 杰克回头看海。 海盗离岸更近了。

Suddenly Jack saw the pirate book. In all the confusion he had forgotten it. It was still propped in the sand. 忽然，杰克看见了那本海盗书。 这一通忙乱，他把它给忘了。 书还支在沙子上。

“Oh man, I forgot the book!” he said. He dropped his socks and boots below the tree house. “天哪，我把书忘了！”他说。 他把袜子和靴子扔在树屋下面。

“Come on, Jack!” Annie shouted. “快点，杰克！”安妮喊道。

“I’ll be right back!” Jack called. “I’ve got to get the book!” “我马上回来！”杰克喊道。 “我得把书拿回来！”

“Jack, forget it!” “杰克，别管它了！”

But Jack was already running toward the water. 可杰克已经朝水边跑去了。

Jack grabbed the book. 杰克抓起那本书。

“Come back!” Annie shouted. “回来！”安妮喊道。

Jack shoved the book into his backpack. 杰克把书塞进背包。

Suddenly a giant wave carried the rowboat right onto the beach. 忽然，一个大浪把小船直接冲上了沙滩。

“Run, Jack!” shouted Annie. “跑啊，杰克！”安妮喊道。

Three big pirates splashed onto the sand. 三个大块头海盗踩着水上了沙滩。

They had knives in their teeth. 他们牙齿里咬着刀。

They had pistols in their belts. 腰带上别着手枪。

They charged toward Jack. 他们朝杰克冲过来。

“Run, Jack, run!” cried Annie. “跑，杰克，快跑！”安妮叫道。
