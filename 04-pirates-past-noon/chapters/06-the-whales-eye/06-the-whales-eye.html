6. The Whale’s Eye 6. 鲸鱼的眼睛

The rowboat tossed from side to side. The waves were huge. Salty spray stung Jack’s eyes. He felt seasick. 小船左右摇晃。 浪非常大。 咸咸的浪花刺痛杰克的眼睛。他觉得晕船了。

“Hold ’er steady!” shouted Cap’n Bones. “把船稳住！”骨头船长喊道。

He pointed at the sea. “Or we’ll be meat for those evil brutes!” 他指着海面。 “不然我们就成那些坏东西的晚餐了！”

Dark fins cut through the water. Sharks. One zoomed right by the boat. Jack could have reached out and touched it. 黑色的鳍划开水面。是鲨鱼。 一条鲨鱼紧贴着小船冲了过去。 杰克一伸手就能摸到它。

He shuddered. 他打了个寒战。

Soon the rowboat pulled alongside the ship. 很快，小船靠上了大船。

The air was filled with wild fiddle music and bagpipes playing. And Jack heard jeers, shouts, and ugly laughter. 空气里全是狂乱的提琴声和风笛声。 杰克还听见嘲笑、叫喊和难听的笑声。

“Hoist ’em aboard!” Cap’n Bones shouted to his men. “把他们吊上来！”骨头船长对手下喊道。

Annie and Jack were hauled onto the deck. 安妮和杰克被拽上了甲板。

The ship creaked and moaned. It rolled from side to side. Ropes flapped and snapped in the wild wind. 大船吱吱呀呀地响。 船左右摇晃。 绳子在狂风里啪啪作响。

Everywhere they looked, Jack and Annie saw pirates. 杰克和安妮四处一看，满眼都是海盗。

Some were dancing. Some were drinking. Many were fighting. With swords. Or with their fists. 有的在跳舞。 有的在喝酒。 好多都在打架。用剑打。 或者用拳头打。

“Lock ’em in my cabin!” Cap’n Bones ordered. “把他们关进我的船舱！”骨头船长下令。

A couple of pirates grabbed Jack and Annie. And threw them in the ship’s cabin. Then locked the door. 两个海盗抓住了杰克和安妮。 把他们扔进了船上的船舱。 然后把门锁上了。

The air inside the cabin was damp and sour-smelling. A shaft of gray light came through a small round window. 船舱里的空气又潮又酸，很难闻。 一道灰蒙蒙的光，从一扇小圆窗照进来。

“Oh man,” said Jack. “We’ve got to figure out how to get back to the island.” “天哪。”杰克说。 “我们得想办法回到岛上去。”

“So we can get into the tree house and go home,” said Annie. “这样才能进树屋，回家去。”安妮说。

“Right.” Jack suddenly felt tired. How would they ever get out of this mess? “对。”杰克突然觉得好累。 他们要怎么才能摆脱这个烂摊子呢？

“We better examine the book,” he said. “我们最好查查这本书。”他说。

He reached into his pack and pulled out the pirate book. 他把手伸进背包，拿出那本海盗书。

He flipped through the pages. 他一页一页地翻着。

He looked for information to help them. 他在找能帮上忙的资料。

“Look,” he said. “看。”他说。

He found a picture of pirates burying a treasure chest. “This might help.” 他找到一张图，画着海盗在埋宝箱。 “这个说不定有用。”

Together they read the words under the picture. 他们一起读图下面的字。

Captain Kidd was a famous pirate. It is said that he buried a treasure chest on a deserted island. The chest was filled with gold and jewels. 基德船长是一位著名的海盗。据说他在一个荒岛上埋了一个宝箱。宝箱里装满了金币和珠宝。

“Captain Kidd!” said Jack.

“So that’s the kid that Bones keeps talking about,” said Annie. “原来骨头船长一直说的那个小孩，就是他呀。”安妮说。

“Right,” said Jack. “对。”杰克说。

Annie looked out the round window. 安妮朝圆窗外看去。

“And Captain Kidd’s treasure is buried somewhere on the island,” she said. “基德船长的宝藏就埋在这座岛上的某个地方。”她说。

Jack took out his notebook and pencil. He wrote: 杰克拿出笔记本和铅笔。他写道：

图中文字：Captain Kidd's treasure on island 图中文字：基德船长的宝藏在岛上

“Ja-ack,” Annie said. “杰——克。”安妮说。

“Shhh, wait a minute,” he said. “I’m thinking.” “嘘，等一下。”他说。 “我在想呢。”

“Guess what I see?” said Annie. “你猜我看见什么了？”安妮说。

“What?” Jack asked. He looked back at the book. “什么？”杰克问。 他又低头看书。

“A whale.” “一条鲸鱼。”

“Neat,” he said. Then he looked up. “A whale? Did you say … a whale?” “真酷。”他说。 然后他抬起头。 “一条鲸鱼？ 你刚才说……一条鲸鱼？”

“A whale. A huge whale. As big as a football field.” “一条鲸鱼。一条好大的鲸鱼。 跟足球场一样大。”

Jack jumped up and looked out the window with her. 杰克跳起来，跟她一起往窗外看。

“Where?” Jack asked. All he could see was the island. And stormy waves. And shark fins. “在哪儿？”杰克问。 他只能看见那座岛。还有汹涌的海浪。 还有鲨鱼鳍。

“There!” said Annie. “那儿！”安妮说。

“Where? Where?” “哪儿？哪儿？”

“There! The island is shaped like a giant whale!” “那儿！ 这座岛的形状就像一条大鲸鱼！”

“Oh man,” whispered Jack. Now he could see it. “天哪。”杰克小声说。 这下他看出来了。

“See the whale’s back?” said Annie. “看见鲸鱼的背了吗？”安妮说。

“Yep.” The slope of the island looked like the back of a whale. “看见了。”岛的斜坡看起来就像鲸鱼的背。

“See his spout?” said Annie. “看见它喷水的地方了吗？”安妮说。

“Yep.” The palm tree that held the tree house looked like the spout of the whale. “看见了。”那棵托着树屋的棕榈树，看起来就像鲸鱼喷出的水柱。

“See his eye?” said Annie. “看见它的眼睛了吗？”安妮说。

“Yep.” A big black rock looked like the eye of the whale. “看见了。”一块大黑石头看起来就像鲸鱼的眼睛。

“The gold doth lie beneath the whale’s eye,” whispered Jack. “Wow.” “金子就藏在鲸鱼的眼睛下面。”杰克小声说。
