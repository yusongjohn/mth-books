9. The Mysterious M 9. 神秘的M

Drip, drip. 滴答，滴答。

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开眼睛。

Rain was dripping from the leaves of the tree. 雨从树叶上滴下来。

They were back in Frog Creek. The rain was softer. The wind was gentler. The air was sweeter. 他们回到蛙溪镇了。 雨更小了。 风更轻了。 空气更香了。

“Oh man.” Jack sighed. “That was close.” He was still holding the gold medallion. “天哪。”杰克叹了口气。 “好险啊。”他还握着那枚金徽章。

“Polly’s gone,” said Annie sadly. “I was hoping she might come back with us.” “波莉不见了。”安妮难过地说。 “我还希望她能跟我们一起回来呢。”

“No magic creature has ever come back with us,” said Jack. “从来没有魔法生物跟我们一起回来过。”杰克说。

He pulled off his backpack. It was damp with rain and saltwater. 他取下背包。 背包被雨水和海水打湿了。

Jack took out the pirate book. He put it on the stack of books. On top of the dinosaur book. And the knight book. And the mummy book. 杰克拿出那本海盗书。 他把它放回那一摞书上。 放在恐龙书上面。 还有骑士书上面。 还有木乃伊书上面。

Then Jack put the gold medallion beside the bookmark with the letter M. 然后杰克把金徽章放在带M字母的书签旁边。

Next he went down onto his knees. And ran his finger over the shimmering M on the floor. “We didn’t find any M’s on this trip,” he said. 接着他跪了下来。 用手指摸过地板上那闪闪发亮的M。 “这趟旅行我们一个M也没找到。”他说。

“Or the M person,” said Annie. “那个M也没找到。”安妮说。

Squawk! 嘎！

“Polly!” Annie cried. “波莉！”安妮叫道。

The parrot swooshed into the tree house. She perched on the stack of books. 鹦鹉呼地飞进树屋。 她停在那一摞书上。

Polly looked straight at Jack. 波莉直直地看着杰克。

“What—what are you doing here?” he asked her. “你——你怎么会在这儿？”他问她。

Slowly Polly raised her bright green wings. They grew bigger and bigger until they spread out like a huge green cape. 波莉慢慢张开她那亮绿色的翅膀。 翅膀越来越大，最后像一件巨大的绿披风一样展开。

Then, in a great swirl of colors—in a blur of feathers and light—in a flapping and stretching and screeching—a new being took shape. 接着，在一片旋转的色彩里——羽毛和光混成一团——扑棱着，伸展着，尖叫着——一个新的生命出现了。

Polly was not a parrot any longer. In her place was an old woman. A beautiful old woman with long white hair and piercing eyes. 波莉不再是一只鹦鹉了。 她的位置上站着一位老奶奶。 一位漂亮的老奶奶，白头发长长的，眼睛亮亮的。

She wore a green feathered cape. She perched on the stack of books. And she was very calm and very still. 她穿着一件绿色的羽毛披风。 她坐在那一摞书上。 她非常安静，一动也不动。

Neither Jack nor Annie could speak. They were too amazed. 杰克和安妮都说不出话来。 他们太吃惊了。

“Hello, Jack. Hello, Annie,” the old woman said. “My name is Morgan le Fay.” “你好，杰克。 你好，安妮。”老奶奶说。 “我叫摩根勒菲。”
