1. Too Late! 1. 来晚了！

Jack stared out his bedroom window. The rain kept falling. And falling. 杰克望着卧室窗外。 雨一直下啊，下啊。

“The TV said it would stop by noon,” said Annie, his seven-year-old sister. “电视上说中午就会停的。”七岁的妹妹安妮说。

“It’s already past noon,” said Jack. “现在都过中午了。”杰克说。

“But we have to go to the tree house,” said Annie. “I have a feeling the M person will be there today.” “可我们得去树屋啊。”安妮说。 “我有种感觉，那个M人今天会在那儿。”

Jack pushed his glasses into place and took a deep breath. He wasn’t sure he was ready to meet the M person yet. The mysterious person who had put all the books in the magic tree house. 杰克把眼镜推正，深深吸了口气。 他还不确定自己准备好见那个M人了。 就是那个把书都放进神奇树屋的神秘人。

“Come on,” said Annie. “走吧。”安妮说。

Jack sighed. “Okay,” he said. “You get our raincoats and boots. I’ll get the medallion and bookmark.” 杰克叹了口气。 “好吧。”他说。 “你去拿雨衣和雨靴。 我去拿徽章和书签。”

Annie ran to get their rain gear. 安妮跑去拿雨具。

Jack reached into his drawer. He took out the medallion. 杰克把手伸进抽屉。 他拿出徽章。

It was gold. The letter M was engraved on it. 是金的。 上面刻着字母M。

Then he took out the bookmark. It was made of blue leather. It had the same M on it. 接着他又拿出书签。 是蓝色皮革做的。 上面也有同样的M。

Both M’s matched the M that was on the floor of the tree house. 两个M都跟树屋地板上的M一模一样。

Jack put the medallion and bookmark into his backpack. Then he threw in his notebook and pencil. Jack liked to take notes about important things. 杰克把徽章和书签放进背包。 又把笔记本和铅笔扔了进去。 杰克喜欢把重要的事情记下来。

“I got our rain stuff!” called Annie. “雨具拿到了！”安妮喊道。

Jack picked up his pack and went downstairs. Annie was waiting by the back door. She was putting on her boots. “Meet you outside,” she said. 杰克拿起背包，下了楼。 安妮在后门等着。 她正在穿雨靴。 “外面见。”她说。

Jack pulled on his raincoat and boots. Then he put on his backpack and joined her. 杰克穿上雨衣和雨靴。 然后背上背包，去跟她会合。

The wind was blowing hard. 风刮得很猛。

“Ready! Set! Go!” shouted Annie. “预备！注意！ 跑！”安妮喊道。

They kept their heads down and charged into the rainy wind. 他们低着头，冲进了风雨里。

Soon they were in the Frog Creek woods. 很快，他们就进了蛙溪镇的树林。

Tree branches swayed, flinging rainwater everywhere. 树枝摇来摇去，雨水甩得到处都是。

“Yuck!” said Annie. “恶心！”安妮说。

They splashed through puddles. Until they came to the tallest oak tree in the woods. 他们踩着水坑往前走。 一直走到树林里最高的那棵橡树下。

They looked up. 他们抬头望去。

Tucked between two branches was the tree house. It looked dark and lonely against the stormy sky. 树屋就卡在两根树枝中间。 在暴风雨的天空下，它显得又黑又孤单。

Hanging from the tree house was a rope ladder. It was blowing in the wind. 树屋下面垂着一架绳梯。 绳梯在风里晃来晃去。

Jack thought of all the books up there. He hoped they weren’t getting wet. 杰克想起了上面那些书。 他希望书没有被淋湿。

“The M person’s been there,” said Annie. “那个M人来过了。”安妮说。

Jack caught his breath. “How can you tell?” he said. 杰克倒吸了一口气。 “你怎么知道？”他说。

“I can feel it,” she whispered. “我感觉得到。”她小声说。

She grabbed the rope ladder and started up. Jack followed. 她抓住绳梯往上爬。杰克跟了上去。

Inside the tree house it was chilly and damp. 树屋里又冷又潮。

But the books were dry. They were all neatly stacked along the wall. Just the way they had been the day before. 不过书是干的。 它们整整齐齐地靠墙码着。 跟昨天一模一样。

Annie picked up a castle book on top of one stack. It had taken them to the time of castles. 安妮拿起一摞书最上面那本城堡书。 就是这本书带他们去过城堡的时代。

“Remember the knight?” she said. “还记得那个骑士吗？”她说。

Jack nodded. He would never forget the knight who had helped them. 杰克点点头。 他永远忘不了那个帮过他们的骑士。

Annie put down the castle book. She picked up the next book on the stack. 安妮放下城堡书。 又拿起下面那一本。

It was the dinosaur book that had taken them to the time of dinosaurs. 是那本恐龙书，就是它带他们去过恐龙时代。

“Remember?” she said. “还记得吗？”她说。

Jack nodded. 杰克点点头。

He’d never forget the pteranodon who had saved him from the Tyrannosaurus rex. 他永远忘不了那只把他从霸王龙嘴里救下来的无齿翼龙。

Then Annie held up a book about ancient Egypt. 接着安妮举起一本关于古埃及的书。

“Meow,” she said. “喵。”她说。

Jack smiled. The Egypt book had taken them to the time of pyramids. A black cat had come to the rescue there. 杰克笑了。 埃及这本书带他们去过金字塔的时代。 在那儿，一只黑猫救了他们。

“And here’s the book about home,” Annie said. “还有这本回家的书。”安妮说。

She held up the book with the picture of their hometown in it. 她举起那本印着他们家乡图画的书。

Frog Creek, Pennsylvania. 宾夕法尼亚州，蛙溪镇。

Jack smiled again. The Pennsylvania book had brought them back home at the end of each of their adventures. 杰克又笑了。 每次冒险结束，都是这本宾夕法尼亚的书把他们带回家。

Jack sighed. Okay. He still had two main questions. 杰克叹了口气。好吧。 他还有两个大问题。

Who was the M person who had put all the books here? 把这些书放在这儿的M人到底是谁？

And did the knight, the pteranodon, and the cat all know the M person? 还有，那个骑士、无齿翼龙和黑猫，是不是都认识这个M人？

Finally Jack reached into his backpack. He took out the gold medallion and the leather bookmark. He placed them on the floor. Right over the spot where the M glowed faintly in the wood. 最后，杰克把手伸进背包。 拿出金徽章和皮书签。 他把它们放在地板上。 正好放在木头上隐隐发亮的那个M上面。

Rain blew into the tree house. 雨被吹进了树屋。

“Brr!” said Annie. “It’s not very cozy today.” “好冷啊！”安妮说。 “今天这儿可不太舒服。”

Jack agreed with her. It was too wet and cold. 杰克同意她的话。 又湿又冷。

“Look.” Annie pointed to an open book lying in the corner. “I don’t remember a book being open.” “看。”安妮指着角落里一本摊开的书。 “我不记得有书是打开的。”

“Me neither,” said Jack. “我也不记得。”杰克说。

Annie picked up the book. She stared at the picture on the page. 安妮拿起那本书。 她盯着书页上的图画。

“Wow, this place looks great.” She showed the picture to Jack. “哇，这地方看起来好棒。”她把图画给杰克看。

He saw a sunny beach. A big green parrot sitting in a palm tree. And a ship sailing on a blue sea. 他看见一片阳光沙滩。 一只绿色的大鹦鹉坐在棕榈树上。 还有一艘船在蓝色的大海上航行。

Another gust of rainy wind blew into the tree house. 又一阵夹着雨的风吹进树屋。

Annie pointed to the picture. “I wish we were there instead of here,” she said. 安妮指着那幅画。 “真希望我们在那儿，而不是在这儿。”她说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “But where is there?” “是啊。”杰克说。 “可是‘那儿’是哪儿啊？”

“Too late!” came a squawk. “来晚了！”传来一声尖叫。

Jack and Annie turned quickly. 杰克和安妮猛地转过身。

Sitting on a branch outside the window ledge of the tree house was a green parrot. Exactly like the parrot in the picture. 树屋窗台外面的树枝上，蹲着一只绿色的鹦鹉。 跟画里的那只一模一样。

“Too late!” the parrot squawked again. “来晚了！”鹦鹉又叫了一声。

“A talking parrot!” said Annie. “Is your name Polly? Can I call you Polly?” “会说话的鹦鹉！”安妮说。 “你叫波莉吗？ 我能叫你波莉吗？”

Suddenly the wind started to whistle. 忽然，风呼呼地吹了起来。

“Oh no! Now we’re in big trouble!” said Jack. “糟了！ 这下我们麻烦大了！”杰克说。

The wind blew harder. 风刮得更猛了。

The leaves shook. 叶子直抖。

The tree house started to spin. Faster and faster! 树屋开始转了起来。 越转越快！

Jack squeezed his eyes shut. Then everything was still. 杰克紧紧闭上眼睛。 然后，一切都静了下来。

Absolutely still. 完全静下来了。

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开眼睛。

“Too late!” squawked Polly. “来晚了！”波莉叫道。
