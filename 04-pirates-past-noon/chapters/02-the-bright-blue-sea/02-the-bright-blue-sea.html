2. The Bright Blue Sea 2. 湛蓝的大海

Jack felt hot sunlight streaming into the tree house. 杰克感觉到热乎乎的阳光照进树屋。

He smelled saltwater. 他闻到了海水的咸味。

He heard the sound of waves. 他听见了海浪的声音。

He and Annie looked out the window. 他和安妮向窗外望去。

The tree house was in a palm tree. Beyond was a bright blue sea. A tall sailing ship was on the horizon. It was just like the picture in the book. 树屋停在一棵棕榈树上。 外面是一片湛蓝的大海。 远处的海平线上有一艘高大的帆船。 跟书里的画一模一样。

“Too late!” squawked Polly. “来晚了！”波莉叫道。

“Look!” said Annie. “快看！”安妮说。

Polly was flying in circles above the tree house. Then she swooped down to the ocean. 波莉在树屋上空一圈圈地飞。 然后她朝大海俯冲下去。

“Come on, let’s follow her! Let’s go in the water!” said Annie. She took off her raincoat and dropped it on the floor. “走，咱们跟着她！ 下水去！”安妮说。 她脱掉雨衣，扔在地板上。

“Wait, we have to study the book first,” said Jack. He started to reach for the book. But Annie grabbed it. “等等，我们得先看看书。”杰克说。 他伸手去拿那本书。 可安妮一把抢了过去。

“You can read it on the beach,” she said. Without even looking at the cover, she shoved the book into Jack’s backpack. “你到沙滩上再看嘛。”她说。 她连封面都没看，就把书塞进了杰克的背包。

He sighed. Actually, the water did look wonderful. 他叹了口气。 说真的，那海水看起来确实很棒。

“Okay,” Jack said. He took off his raincoat, too. “好吧。”杰克说。 他也脱掉了雨衣。

“Come on!” Annie handed Jack his backpack, then started down the ladder. “走吧！”安妮把背包递给杰克，然后开始下梯子。

Jack folded the raincoat and put it next to the stack of books. He put on his backpack. Then he went down the ladder. 杰克把雨衣叠好，放在那摞书旁边。 他背上背包。 然后顺着梯子爬下去。

As soon as Annie hit the sand, she ran toward the ocean. Jack watched her wade into the water. She was still wearing her rain boots. 安妮脚一踩到沙子，就朝大海跑去。 杰克看着她走进水里。 她还穿着雨靴呢。

“Your boots, Annie,” called Jack. “你的靴子，安妮！”杰克喊道。

She shrugged. “They’ll dry out,” she said. 她耸耸肩。 “会干的。”她说。

Jack took off his boots and socks. He put them beside his pack. Then he rolled up his jeans. And ran across the hot sand into the waves. 杰克脱掉靴子和袜子。 把它们放在背包旁边。 然后把牛仔裤卷起来。 跑过滚烫的沙子，冲进浪里。

The water was warm and clear. Jack could see shells and tiny fishes. 海水又暖又清。 杰克能看见贝壳和小鱼。

He shielded his eyes against the sun. And peered out at the sea. 他用手挡住阳光。 朝远处的海面望去。

The tall sailing ship seemed a bit closer. 那艘高大的帆船好像近了一点。

“Where’s Polly?” said Annie. “波莉呢？”安妮说。

Jack glanced around. No sign of Polly. Not in the palm trees. Not on the sunlit sand. Not over the bright blue sea. 杰克四处看了看。没有波莉的影子。 棕榈树上没有。 阳光下的沙滩上也没有。 湛蓝的大海上也没有。

When Jack looked out at the sea again, the ship seemed even closer. Now Jack could see its flag. 杰克再朝海上望去，那艘船更近了。 这下杰克能看见船上的旗子了。

As he stared at the ship’s flag, a chill went through him. 他盯着那面旗，心里忽然一凉。

The flag was black. With a skull and crossbones. 旗是黑的。 上面有骷髅头和交叉的骨头。

“Oh man,” he breathed. He started out of the water. “天哪。”他小声说。 他开始往岸上走。

“What’s wrong?” said Annie. She splashed after him. “怎么了？”安妮说。 她踩着水跟上他。

Jack ran to his backpack. Annie followed. 杰克跑向背包。安妮跟了过去。

He grabbed the book from his backpack. He looked at the cover. For the first time, he and Annie read the title of the book. 他从背包里抽出那本书。 他看了看封面。 他和安妮第一次看清了书名。

“Yikes!” said Annie. “哎呀！”安妮说。

“Pirates of the Caribbean,” Jack read aloud. “加勒比海盗。”杰克大声念道。
