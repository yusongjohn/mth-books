10. Treasure Again 10. 又是宝藏

Annie found her voice first. “The M person,” she whispered. 安妮先说出了话。 “那个M。”她小声说。

“Yes. I’m the M person,” said Morgan. “对。 我就是那个M。”摩根说。

“Wh-where are you from?” asked Jack. “你——你从哪儿来？”杰克问。

“Have you ever heard of King Arthur?” said Morgan. “你们听说过亚瑟王吗？”摩根说。

Jack nodded. 杰克点点头。

“Well, I am King Arthur’s sister,” said Morgan. “嗯，我是亚瑟王的姐姐。”摩根说。

“You’re from Camelot,” said Jack. “I’ve read about Camelot.” “你来自卡米洛特。”杰克说。 “我读过卡米洛特的故事。”

“What did you read about me, Jack?” said Morgan. “你读到的我是什么样的，杰克？”摩根说。

“You—you’re a witch.” “你——你是个女巫。”

Morgan smiled. “You can’t believe everything you read, Jack.” 摩根笑了。 “书上写的不一定都是真的，杰克。”

“But are you a magician?” said Annie. “那你是魔法师吗？”安妮说。

“Most call me an enchantress. But I’m also a librarian,” said Morgan. “大多数人叫我女魔法师。 可我也是图书管理员。”摩根说。

“A librarian?” said Annie. “图书管理员？”安妮说。

“Yes. And I’ve come to the 20th century, your time, to collect books. You are lucky to be born in a time with so many books.” “对。 我来到二十世纪，也就是你们的时代，来收集书。 你们能出生在有这么多书的时代，真幸运。”

“For the Camelot library?” asked Jack. “是给卡米洛特图书馆用的吗？”杰克问。

“Precisely,” said Morgan. “I travel in this tree house to collect words from many different places around the world. And from many different time periods.” “正是。”摩根说。 “我坐着这间树屋去旅行，收集世界各地的文字。 还有很多不同时代的文字。”

“Did you find books here?” said Jack. “你在这儿找到书了吗？”杰克说。

“Oh yes. Many wonderful books. I want to borrow them for our scribes to copy.” “哦，找到了。 好多好多好书。 我想借回去，让我们的抄书人抄下来。”

“Did you put all the bookmarks in them?” said Jack. “书里的书签都是你放的吗？”杰克说。

“Yes. You see, I like the pictures in the books. Sometimes I want to visit the scenes in the pictures. So all the bookmarks mark places I wish to go.” “对。 你看，我喜欢书里的图画。 有时候我想去图画里的那些地方看看。 所以这些书签标的，都是我想去的地方。”

“How do you get there?” asked Annie. “你怎么去那儿呢？”安妮问。

“I placed a spell on the tree house,” said Morgan. “So when I point to a picture and make the wish, the tree house takes me there.” “我给树屋下了咒语。”摩根说。 “所以我一指着图画许愿，树屋就会带我去那儿。”

“I think you dropped this in dinosaur times,” said Jack. “我想这是你在恐龙时代掉的。”杰克说。

He handed the gold medallion to Morgan. 他把金徽章递给摩根。

“Oh, thank you! I wondered where I’d lost it,” she said. She put the medallion into a hidden pocket in her cape. “哦，谢谢你！ 我还在想它掉哪儿了。”她说。 她把徽章放进披风里一个暗口袋。

“So can anybody work the spell?” asked Annie. “Anybody who tries it?” “那谁都能用这个咒语吗？”安妮问。 “谁试都能用吗？”

“Oh dear, no! Not just anybody,” Morgan said. “You two are the only ones besides me to do it. No one else has ever even seen my tree house before.” “哎呀，不行！ 不是谁都行。”摩根说。 “除了我，就只有你们两个做到过。 别人连我的树屋都没看见过。”

“Is it invisible?” asked Annie. “它是隐形的吗？”安妮问。

“Yes,” said Morgan. “I had no idea it would ever be discovered. But then you two came along. Somehow you hooked right into my magic.” “是的。”摩根说。 “我根本没想到它会被人发现。 可后来你们两个出现了。 不知怎么的，你们正好接上了我的魔法。”

“H-how?” asked Jack. “怎——怎么接上的？”杰克问。

“Well, for two reasons, I think,” explained Morgan. “First, Annie believes in magic. So she actually saw the tree house. And her belief helped you to see it, Jack.” “嗯，我想有两个原因。”摩根解释说。 “第一，安妮相信魔法。 所以她真的看见了树屋。 她的相信，也帮你看见了，杰克。”

“Oh man,” said Jack. “天哪。”杰克说。

“Then you picked up a book, Jack. And because you love books so much, you caused my magic spell to work.” “然后你拿起了一本书，杰克。 因为你太爱书了，我的魔法咒语就生效了。”

“Wow,” said Annie. “哇。”安妮说。

“You can’t imagine my dismay when you started to take off for dinosaur times. I had to make a very quick decision. And I decided to come along.” “你们朝着恐龙时代飞走的时候，我有多着急，你们想象不到。 我得马上做个决定。 我就决定跟你们一起去。”

“Oh, so you were the pteranodon!” said Annie. “哦，所以无齿翼龙就是你！”安妮说。

Morgan smiled. 摩根笑了。

“And the cat and the knight and Polly!” said Annie. “还有那只猫、那个骑士，还有波莉！”安妮说。

“Yes,” said Morgan softly. “是的。”摩根轻轻地说。

“You were all these things to help us?” asked Jack. “你变成这些，都是为了帮我们吗？”杰克问。

“Yes, but I must go home now. The people in Camelot need my help.” “是的，可我现在得回家了。 卡米洛特的人们需要我帮忙。”

“You’re leaving?” whispered Jack. “你要走了？”杰克小声说。

“I’m afraid I must,” said Morgan. “恐怕必须走了。”摩根说。

She picked up Jack’s backpack and handed it to him. Jack and Annie picked up their raincoats. It had stopped raining. 她拿起杰克的背包递给他。 杰克和安妮拿起雨衣。 雨已经停了。

“You won’t forget us, will you?” asked Annie, as they put their raincoats on. “你不会忘了我们吧？”安妮一边穿雨衣一边问。

“Never,” said Morgan. She smiled at both of them. “You remind me too much of myself. You love the impossible, Annie. And you love knowledge, Jack. What better combination is there?” “永远不会。”摩根说。 她朝他们两个笑了笑。 “你们太像我自己了。 你爱那些不可能的事，安妮。 你爱知识，杰克。 还有比这更好的搭配吗？”

Morgan le Fay touched Annie’s forehead gently. And then Jack’s. She smiled. 摩根勒菲轻轻摸了摸安妮的额头。然后又摸了摸杰克的。 她笑了。

“Good-bye,” she said. “再见。”她说。

“Good-bye,” said Annie and Jack. “再见。”安妮和杰克说。

Annie left the tree house first. Jack followed. They climbed down the rope ladder for the last time. 安妮先离开树屋。杰克跟着下去。 他们最后一次爬下绳梯。

They stood below the oak tree and looked up. 他们站在橡树下，抬头看。

Morgan was looking out the window. Her long white hair blew in the breeze. 摩根正从窗口往外看。 她长长的白头发在风里飘着。

Suddenly the wind began to blow. 突然，风刮了起来。

The leaves began to shake. 叶子开始摇晃。

A loud whistling sound filled the air. 一阵很响的呼啸声充满了空气。

Jack covered his ears and squeezed his eyes shut. 杰克捂住耳朵，紧紧闭上眼睛。

Then everything was silent. 然后一切都静了。

Absolutely silent. 完全静了。

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开眼睛。

The tree house was gone. 树屋不见了。

All gone. 全没了。

Absolutely gone. 彻底没了。

Annie and Jack stood a moment, staring up at the empty oak tree. Listening to the silence. 安妮和杰克站了一会儿，盯着空空的橡树。 听着那片安静。

Annie sighed. “Let’s go,” she said softly. 安妮叹了口气。 “走吧。”她轻轻地说。

Jack just nodded. He felt too sad to speak. As they started walking, he put his hands into his pockets. 杰克只是点点头。 他难过得说不出话。 他们开始走的时候，他把手伸进口袋。

He felt something. 他摸到一样东西。

Jack pulled out the gold medallion. “Look!” he said. “How did—?” 杰克拿出那枚金徽章。 “看！”他说。 “怎么会——？”

Annie smiled. “Morgan must have put it there,” she said. 安妮笑了。 “一定是摩根放进去的。”她说。

“But how?” “可是怎么放的？”

“Magic,” said Annie. “I think it means she’ll be coming back.” “魔法。”安妮说。 “我想这是说她还会回来。”

Jack smiled. He clutched the medallion as he and Annie took off through the wet, sunny woods. 杰克笑了。 他握紧徽章，和安妮一起跑进又湿又亮的树林。

As they walked, the sun shined through the woods. And all the wet leaves sparkled. 他们走着的时候，阳光照进树林。 所有湿叶子都在闪光。

Everything, in fact, was shining. 其实，所有东西都在发亮。

Leaves, branches, puddles, bushes, grass, vines, wild flowers—all glittered like jewels. 叶子、树枝、水坑、灌木、草、藤蔓、野花——全都像宝石一样闪闪发光。

Or gleamed like gold. 或者像金子一样发亮。

Annie had been right, thought Jack. 安妮说得对，杰克想。

Forget the treasure chest. 别管什么藏宝箱了。

They had treasure at home. A ton of it. Everywhere. 家里就有宝藏。多得是。 到处都是。
