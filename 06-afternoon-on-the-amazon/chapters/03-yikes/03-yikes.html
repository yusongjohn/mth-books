3. Yikes! 3. 呀！

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开眼睛。

The air was hot and steamy. 空气又热又湿。

“It looks like we landed in some bushes,” said Annie. “看起来我们落在灌木丛里了。”安妮说。

She was peeking out of the tree house window. Peanut was peeking out of Annie’s pocket. 她正从树屋的窗户往外偷看。 花生从安妮的口袋里探出头来。

Jack peeked out of the tree house, too. 杰克也从树屋往外偷看。

They had landed in a sea of shiny green leaves. Outside there were flowers, bright butterflies, and birds. Just as in the book. 他们落在了一片亮闪闪的绿叶海洋里。 外面有鲜花、鲜艳的蝴蝶，还有小鸟。 就像书里写的一样。

“That’s strange,” said Jack. “I wonder why we didn’t land in a tree. The way we always do.” “真奇怪。”杰克说。 “真奇怪，我们怎么没落在树上。 以前总是那样的。”

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “But let’s hurry and find the thing for Morgan. So we can get back home before we meet any big bugs.” “我不知道。”安妮说。 “不过咱们快点找到给摩根的那件东西吧。 这样就能在碰到大虫子之前回家。”

“Wait. This seems weird,” said Jack. “I don’t understand why we landed in bushes. I’d better read about this.” “等等——”杰克喘着气说。 这看着有点怪。”杰克说。 “我不明白我们为什么会落在灌木丛里。 我最好先读读这个。”

“Oh, come on,” said Annie. “We don’t even need the ladder. We can just climb out the window.” “哎呀，快点嘛。”安妮说。 “我们根本用不着梯子。 我们只要从窗户爬出去就行。”

Annie put Peanut in her pocket. She stuck one leg out the window. 安妮把花生放进口袋。 她把一条腿伸出窗外。

“Wait!” Jack grabbed Annie’s other leg. He read: 林冠下面是林下叶层，再往下就是森林地面。

The rain forest is in three layers. Thick treetops, often over 150 feet in the air, make up the top layer. This is called the forest canopy. Below the canopy is the understory, then the forest floor. 雨林分为三层。高大的树冠层，常常高达150英尺，是顶层，叫做森林树冠层。树冠层下面是灌木层，再下面是森林地面。

“Get back in here!” cried Jack. “We’re probably more than 150 feet above the ground! In the forest canopy!” “快回来！”杰克大喊。 “我们大概离地面有一百五十多英尺！ 在森林的树冠层！”

“Yikes!” said Annie. She slipped back into the tree house. “呀！”安妮说。 她赶紧缩回树屋里。

“We have to use the ladder,” said Jack. He got on his hands and knees. He moved leaves away from the hole in the floor. He looked down. “我们得用梯子。”杰克说。 他双手双膝着地趴了下来。 他把树叶从地板上的洞口挪开。

The ladder seemed to fall between the branches of a giant tree. But Jack couldn’t see beyond that. 绳子梯子好像是从一棵大树的枝丫间垂下去的。 可再往下，杰克就什么也看不见了。

“I can’t tell what’s down there,” he said. “Be careful.” “我看不清下面是什么。”他说。 “小心点。”

Jack put the rain forest book in his backpack. Then he stepped onto the rope ladder. 杰克把雨林的书放进背包。 然后他踩上了绳梯。

He started down. Annie followed with Peanut in her pocket. 他开始往下爬。 安妮跟着他，口袋里装着花生。

Jack pushed through the leaves. 杰克挤着穿过树叶。

He came to the understory below the canopy. 他来到了林冠下面的林下叶层。

He looked down at the forest floor. It was very far away. 他低头看着森林地面。 地面离得好远。

“Oh, man,” whispered Jack. “天哪。”杰克小声说。

This world was completely different from the one above the treetops. 这个世界和树顶上面完全不一样。

Now that they were out of the sun, it was cooler. It was also damp and very quiet. 这会儿他们离开了阳光，凉快了一些。 这里也很潮湿，而且非常安静。

Jack shivered. It was the spookiest place he had ever seen. 杰克打了个哆嗦。 这是他见过的最阴森的地方。
