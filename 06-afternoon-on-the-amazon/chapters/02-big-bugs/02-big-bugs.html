2. Big Bugs 2. 大虫子

“Wow,” said Jack, picking up the book. “The ninja book was open yesterday. Now this one. Who opened them?” “哇。”杰克拿起书说。 “昨天打开的是忍者那本书。现在又是这一本。 是谁把它们打开的？”

Jack closed the book and looked at the cover. 杰克合上书，看了看书的封面。

It showed a picture of a green forest. The trees were very tall and close together. 封面上是一片绿色的森林。 树木又高又密，挤在一起。

On the cover were the words The Rain Forest. 封面上写着：《雨林》。

“Oh, wow,” said Jack. “哦，哇。”杰克说。

“Oh, no,” said Annie.

“What’s wrong?” said Jack. “怎么了？”杰克说。

“I learned about the rain forest in school,” said Annie. “It’s filled with big bugs and spiders.” “我在学校里学过雨林。”安妮说。 “里面全是大虫子和蜘蛛。”

“I know,” said Jack. “Half of them have never even been named.” “我知道。”杰克说。 “它们有一半甚至从来都没被起过名字。”

“It’s creepy,” said Annie. “好吓人。”安妮说。

“It’s neat,” said Jack. He wanted to take lots of notes in the rain forest. Maybe he could even name some unknown bugs. “那很酷。”杰克说。 他想在雨林里记下好多笔记。 说不定他还能给一些未知的虫子起名字。

“Neat? Yuk,” said Annie. She shivered. “很酷？恶心。”安妮说。 她打了个哆嗦。

“I don’t get it,” said Jack. “You weren’t afraid of dinosaurs.” “我不明白。”杰克说。 “你都不怕恐龙。”

“So?” “那又怎样？”

“You weren’t afraid of the castle guards or the mummy’s ghost.” “城堡卫兵和木乃伊的鬼魂你都不怕。

“So?” “那又怎样？”

“You weren’t afraid of pirates or ninjas.” 海盗和忍者你也不怕。

“So?” “那又怎样？”

“You’re not afraid of really scary things. But you’re afraid of little bugs and spiders. That doesn’t make sense.” “真正可怕的东西你都不怕。 可你却怕小虫子和蜘蛛。 这说不通。”

“So?” “那又怎样？”

Jack sighed. “Listen,” he said. “We have to go there. To help Morgan. That’s why the book was left open.” 杰克叹了口气。 “听着，”他说。 “我们得去那儿。为了帮摩根。 所以这本书才被打开着。”

“I know that,” said Annie, frowning. “我知道。”安妮皱着眉说。

“Plus, the rain forests are being cut down,” said Jack. “Don’t you want to see one before it’s too late?” “而且，雨林正在被砍伐。”杰克说。 “你不想在一切都太晚之前去看看吗？”

Annie took a deep breath and slowly nodded. 安妮深吸一口气，慢慢点了点头。

“Okay, then, let’s go,” said Jack. “那好，咱们走吧。”杰克说。

He opened the book again. He pointed to a picture that showed blue sky, green leaves, and bright flowers. 他又打开了那本书。 他指着一幅画，上面有蓝天、绿叶和鲜艳的花。

“I wish we could go there,” he said. “我希望我们能去那儿。”他说。

The wind began to blow. 风开始刮起来。

Squeak.

“Stay here, Peanut,” said Annie as she put the mouse in her pocket. “待在这儿，花生。”安妮一边说，一边把小老鼠放进口袋。

The wind picked up. The tree house started to spin. 风又大了起来。 树屋开始旋转。

Jack squeezed his eyes shut. 杰克紧紧闭上眼睛。

The wind was whistling now. The tree house was spinning faster and faster. 风现在呼呼地响了起来。 树屋转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后，一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 绝对静止。

Wild sounds broke the silence. 狂野的声音打破了寂静。

Screeeeeech! 嘎——！

Buzzzzzzz! 嗡嗡嗡！

Chirp! Chirp! 啾！啾！
