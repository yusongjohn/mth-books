5. Pretty Fish 5. 漂亮的鱼

Squeak. 吱。

Annie patted the little mouse in her pocket. 安妮轻轻拍了拍口袋里的小老鼠。

“It’s okay, Peanut. The ants can’t get us in the river. We’re safe,” she said. “没事的，花生。 蚂蚁到不了河里来。 我们安全了。”她说。

“Maybe safe from the ants,” said Jack. “But where is this canoe going?” “也许躲开了蚂蚁。”杰克说。 “可这独木舟要漂到哪儿去？”

Jack and Annie stared at the river. Branches spread over the water. Vines and mosses hung down from them. 杰克和安妮盯着河面。 树枝伸展在水面上。 藤蔓和苔藓从上面垂下来。

“We’d better look this up,” said Jack. He pulled the rain forest book out of his backpack and flipped through it. “我们最好查查看。”杰克说。 他从背包里抽出雨林的书，一页页翻着。

Soon he found a picture of a river. He read: 很快他找到一幅河的图画。他读道：

The Amazon River stretches over 4,000 miles from the mountains of Peru, across Brazil, to the Atlantic Ocean. The river basin contains over half of the rain forests in the world. 亚马逊河从秘鲁的山脉流经巴西，一直延伸到大西洋，全长超过4000英里。这条河的流域包含了世界上超过一半的雨林。

Jack looked at Annie. “We’re on the Amazon River,” he said. “It’s more than four thousand miles long!” 杰克看着安妮。 “我们在亚马孙河上。”他说。 “它有四千多英里长！”

“Wow,” Annie whispered. She looked at the river. She trailed her hand through the water. “哇。”安妮小声说。 她看着河水。 她把手拖在水里。

“I have to make some notes—” Jack said. He pulled his notebook out of his pack. He wrote: “我得记点笔记——”杰克说。 他从包里拿出笔记本，写道：

图中文字：The Amazon rain forest is 图中文字：亚马逊雨林是

“Jack, look at those pretty fish with the teeth,” said Annie. “杰克，快看那些长着牙齿的漂亮鱼。”安妮说。

“What?” Jack glanced up from his writing. “什么？”杰克从书写中抬起头。

Annie was pointing at some blue fish swimming near the boat. The fish had red bellies and razor-sharp teeth. 安妮正指着船边游着的几条蓝色的鱼。 那些鱼有红色的肚子和像剃刀一样锋利的牙齿。

“Watch it!” cried Jack. “Those aren’t pretty fish. They’re piranhas! They’ll eat anything! Even people!” “当心！”杰克喊道。 “那些可不是漂亮的鱼。它们是食人鱼！ 它们什么都吃！连人也不例外！”

“Yikes,” whispered Annie. “呀。”安妮小声说。

“We better get back on shore,” said Jack, putting the books in his backpack. “我们最好回到岸上去。”杰克一边说，一边把书放进背包。

“How?” said Annie. “We can’t go in the water now. And we don’t have any paddles.” “怎么回去？”安妮说。 “我们现在不能下水。 而且我们也没有桨。”

Jack tried to stay calm. “We need a plan,” he said. 杰克努力保持镇定。 “我们得想个办法。”他说。

Jack stared at the river. The canoe would soon float under some vines. 杰克盯着河面。 独木舟很快就要漂到一些藤蔓下面了。

“I’ll grab a vine,” said Jack. “And pull us to shore.” “我去抓一根藤蔓，”杰克说。 “然后把我们拉到岸边。”

“Good idea,” said Annie. “好主意。”安妮说。

As they glided under the branches, Jack stood up. 当他们滑到树枝下面时，杰克站了起来。

The canoe rocked. He nearly fell out. 独木舟晃了晃。 他差点掉出去。

“Balance the canoe,” said Jack. “把独木舟平衡住。”杰克说。

Annie leaned to one side. Jack reached—he missed! 安妮往一边倾斜。 杰克伸手一抓——没抓住！

The canoe floated under more branches. 独木舟又漂到更多树枝下面。

Jack reached for another thick vine. 杰克又伸手去抓另一根粗藤蔓。

He grabbed it! 他抓住了！

It was cold and scaly. It wiggled and jerked! 它又冷又滑，还带着鳞片。 它扭动着，猛地一抽！

“Ahhh!” Jack screamed and fell back into the canoe. “啊啊啊！”杰克尖叫一声，跌回独木舟里。

The vine was alive! 那根藤蔓是活的！

It was a long green snake! 那是一条长长的绿蛇！

The snake fell from the tree. It splashed into the water and swam away. 蛇从树上掉下来。 它扑通一声掉进水里，游走了。

“Oh, man,” said Jack. “天哪。”杰克说。

He and Annie stared in horror at each other. 他和安妮惊恐地互相盯着。

“What now?” said Annie, making a face. “现在怎么办？”安妮做了个鬼脸说。

“Well … ” Jack looked at the river. There were no vines up ahead. But there was a big branch floating on the water. “嗯……”杰克看着河面。 前面已经没有藤蔓了。 可水面上漂着一根大树枝。

“Grab that branch near you,” said Jack. “Maybe we can use it for a paddle.” “抓住你旁边那根树枝。”杰克说。 “也许我们可以拿它当桨用。”

The canoe floated closer to the branch. Annie reached for it. 独木舟漂近了那根树枝。 安妮伸手去抓。

Suddenly the branch rose into the air! 忽然，那根树枝腾空而起！

It was a crocodile! 那是一条鳄鱼！

“Help!” screamed Annie, and she fell back into the canoe. “救命！”安妮尖叫着，跌回独木舟里。

The crocodile opened and closed its huge, long jaws. Then it moved past the canoe and swam up the river. 鳄鱼张开又合上它又大又长的嘴巴。 然后它从独木舟旁游过，朝上游游去。

“Oh, man,” whispered Jack. “天哪。”杰克小声说。

A screeching sound split the air. 一声尖利的叫声划破空气。

Jack and Annie jumped. 杰克和安妮吓了一跳。

“Help!” said Jack. “救命！”杰克说。

He expected to see another terrible creature. 他以为又会看见一只可怕的动物。

But all he saw was a small brown monkey, hanging by its tail from a tree. 可他只看见一只棕色的小猴子，正倒挂在树上甩着尾巴。
