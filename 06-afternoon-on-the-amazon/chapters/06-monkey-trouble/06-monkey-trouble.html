6. Monkey Trouble 6. 猴子捣乱

Squeak!Squeak! Peanut poked her head out from Annie’s pocket. She seemed to be yelling at the monkey. 吱！吱！ 花生从安妮的口袋里探出头来。 她好像在冲着猴子大喊大叫。

“Don’t worry, Peanut,” said Annie. “He’s just a little monkey. He won’t hurt us.” “它只是一只小猴子。 它不会伤害我们的。”

But suddenly the monkey grabbed a big red fruit hanging from the tree. He hurled it at the canoe. 可忽然，猴子抓住了树上挂着的一颗大红果子。 它把果子朝独木舟扔了过来。

“Watch it!” shouted Jack. “小心！”杰克喊道。

The fruit fell into the water with a splash. 果子扑通一声掉进水里。

The monkey screeched even louder. 猴子叫得更响了。

He grabbed another fruit. 它又抓起一颗果子。

“Don’t throw things at us!” shouted Annie. “别朝我们扔东西！”安妮喊道。

But the monkey hurled the red fruit right at them. 可猴子把红果子径直朝他们扔了过来。

Jack and Annie ducked again. And the fruit splashed into the water. 杰克和安妮又一次低下头躲开。 果子又扑通一声掉进水里。

“Stop that!” Annie shouted. “别扔了！”安妮喊道。

But the monkey only waved his arms and screeched again. 可那只猴子只是挥着胳膊，又尖声叫了起来。

“Oh, brother,” said Jack. “I don’t believe this.” “哎呀，真是的。”杰克说。 “真不敢相信。”

The monkey grabbed a third fruit and hurled it at Jack and Annie. It landed inside the canoe with a thump. 猴子又抓起第三个果子，朝杰克和安妮扔过来。 果子砰的一声落进了独木舟里。

Annie grabbed the fruit. She stood up and threw it back at the monkey. 安妮抓住果子。 她站起来，把果子扔回给猴子。

She missed. The canoe rocked. Annie almost fell out. 她没打中。 安妮差点掉出去。

The monkey screeched even louder. 猴子叫得更响了。

“Go away!” Annie shouted. “You’re the meanest thing in the world!” “走开！”安妮喊道。 “你是世界上最坏的东西！”

The monkey stopped screeching. 猴子停止了尖叫。

He looked at Annie. Then he swung away. Into the forest. 它看着安妮。 然后它荡走了，进了森林。

“I think I hurt his feelings,” said Annie. “我觉得我伤了它的心。”安妮说。

“Who cares?” said Jack. “He shouldn’t throw things.” “管它呢？”杰克说。 “它不该乱扔东西。”

“Uh-oh,” said Annie. “It’s raining now.” “哎呀。”安妮说。 “现在下雨了。”

“What?” Jack looked up. A raindrop hit him in the eye. “什么？”杰克抬起头。 一滴雨打在他的眼睛上。

“Oh, no. I don’t believe this,” Jack said. “哦，不。 真不敢相信。”杰克说。

“What’d you expect?” said Annie. “It is the rain forest.” “你指望怎样？”安妮说。 “这儿可是雨林。”

A gust of wind blew the canoe. 一阵风吹得独木舟晃起来。

Thunder rolled in the sky. 雷声在天空中滚过。

“A river’s a bad place to be in a storm,” said Jack. “We have to get back to shore. Right now.” “暴风雨里待在河里可不好。”杰克说。 “我们得马上回到岸上。”

“But how?” said Annie. “We can’t wade or swim. The piranhas, the snake, and the crocodile will get us.” “我们不能蹚水，也不能游泳。 食人鱼、蛇，还有鳄鱼会把我们吃掉的。”

Screeching split the air again. 尖叫声再次划破空气。

“Oh, no,” said Jack. The bratty monkey was back. “哦，不。”杰克说。 那只讨厌的猴子又回来了。

This time, the monkey was pointing a long stick at the canoe. 这一次，猴子正拿着一根长棍子指着独木舟。

Jack crouched down. Was the monkey going to hurl the stick at them? Like a spear? 杰克蹲了下来。 像一支矛？

Annie jumped up and faced the monkey. 安妮跳起来，面对着那只猴子。

“Watch it! He’s nuts,” said Jack. “小心！”杰克喊道。 它疯了。”杰克说。

But the monkey just stared at Annie. And Annie just stared back at him. 可猴子只是盯着安妮。 安妮也盯着它。

After a long moment, the monkey seemed to smile. 过了好一会儿，猴子好像笑了。

Annie smiled back. 安妮对它笑了笑。

“What’s going on?” said Jack. “怎么回事？”杰克说。

“He wants to help us,” Annie said. “它想帮我们。”安妮说。

“Help us how?” said Jack. “要怎么帮我们？”杰克说。

The monkey held out the long stick. 猴子伸出那根长棍子。

Annie grabbed the other end. 安妮抓住了另一头。

The monkey pulled on the stick. The canoe started floating toward him. 猴子拉着棍子。 独木舟开始朝它漂过去。

The monkey pulled the canoe all the way to the bank of the river. 猴子把独木舟一直拉到了河岸边。
