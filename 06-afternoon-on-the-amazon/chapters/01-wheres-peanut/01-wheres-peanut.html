1. Where’s Peanut? 1. 花生在哪儿？

“Hurry, Jack!” shouted Annie. “快点，杰克！”安妮喊道。

Annie ran into the Frog Creek woods. 安妮跑进了蛙溪镇的树林。

Jack followed her. 杰克跟在她后面。

“It’s still here!” Annie called. “它还在这儿！”安妮叫道。

Jack caught up with Annie. She stood beside a tall oak tree. 杰克追上了安妮。 她站在一棵高大的橡树旁。

Jack looked up. The magic tree house was shining in the afternoon sunlight. 杰克抬起头。 魔法树屋在午后的阳光里闪闪发亮。

“We’re coming, Peanut!” Annie called. “我们来了，花生！”安妮喊道。

She grabbed the rope ladder and started up. 她抓住绳梯，开始往上爬。

Jack followed. They climbed and climbed. Finally they climbed into the tree house. 杰克跟了上去。 他们爬啊，爬啊。 他们终于爬进了树屋。

“Peanut?” said Annie. “花生？”安妮说。

Jack took off his backpack. He looked around. 杰克放下背包，四处看了看。

Sunlight slanted across a stack of books—books about ninjas, pirates, mummies, knights, and dinosaurs. 阳光斜照在一摞书上——忍者、海盗、木乃伊、骑士和恐龙的书。

The letter M shimmered on the wooden floor. M for Morgan le Fay. 字母M在木地板上闪着微光。 M代表摩根·勒菲。

“I don’t think Peanut’s here,” said Jack. “我觉得花生不在这儿。”杰克说。

“I wonder where she is,” said Annie. “不知道她去哪儿了。”安妮说。

“How do you know Peanut’s a she?” asked Jack. “你怎么知道花生是母的？”杰克问。

“I just know it,” said Annie. “我就是知道。”安妮说。

“Oh, brother,” said Jack. “哎呀，真是的。”杰克说。

Squeak!

Annie laughed. “Look, Jack!” 安妮也跟着它笑。 “快看，杰克！”

A small pink sock was moving across the floor. Yesterday Annie had turned her sock into a bed for Peanut. 一只粉红色的小袜子正从地板上挪过去。 昨天安妮把她的袜子变成了花生的床。

Annie picked up the tiny lump. 安妮捡起了那个小东西。

Squeak.

A brown-and-white mouse peeked out of the sock. She looked from Annie to Jack with her big eyes. 一只棕白相间的小老鼠从袜子里探出头来。 她睁着大眼睛，看看安妮，又看看杰克。

Jack laughed. “Hi, Peanut,” he said. 杰克笑了。 “嗨，花生。”他说。

“Will you help us again today?” asked Annie. “你今天还会再帮我们吗？”安妮问。

In old Japan, Peanut had helped them when they’d gotten lost. 在古代日本，花生曾在他们迷路时帮过忙。

“We have to find three more things for Morgan,” said Annie. “我们还得再给摩根找三样东西。”安妮说。

Jack pushed his glasses into place. “First we have to find a clue that tells us where to begin,” he said. 杰克把眼镜往上推了推。 “首先我们得找到一条线索，告诉我们从哪儿开始。”他说。

“Guess what,” said Annie. “你猜怎么着。”安妮说。

“What?” said Jack. “什么？”杰克说。

“We don’t have to look very far.” She pointed at a corner of the tree house. 她指着树屋的一个角落。

In the shadows was an open book. 阴影里放着一本翻开的书。
