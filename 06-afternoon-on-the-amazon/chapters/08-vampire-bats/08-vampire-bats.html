8. Vampire Bats? 8. 吸血蝙蝠？

“Wait—” said Jack, panting. “I think we got away.” “等等——”杰克喘着气说。 “我觉得我们逃掉了。”

Jack and Annie stopped running and caught their breath. 杰克和安妮停下来，喘了口气。

“Where are we?” said Jack. “我们在哪儿？”杰克说。

“Where’s the monkey?” said Annie, looking back at the forest. “Do you think the jaguar caught him?” “猴子呢？”安妮回头望着森林说。 “你觉得美洲豹抓住它了吗？”

“No, monkeys are fast,” said Jack. “不会，猴子跑得很快。”杰克说。

Of course, jaguars are fast, too, Jack thought. But he didn’t want to tell Annie that. 当然，美洲豹也很快，杰克想。 可他不想告诉安妮这个。

“I hope he’s okay,” said Annie. “希望它没事。”安妮说。

Squeak. Peanut peeked out of Annie’s pocket. 花生从安妮的口袋里探出头来。

“Peanut! I almost forgot you!” said Annie. “Are you okay?” “花生！ 我差点把你忘了！”安妮说。 “你还好吗？”

The mouse just stared at Annie with her big eyes. 小老鼠只是睁着大眼睛盯着安妮。

“She looks scared,” said Jack. “Poor Peanut.” “她看起来很害怕。”杰克说。 “可怜的花生。”

“Poor monkey,” said Annie. She looked around at the forest. “可怜的猴子。”安妮说。 她朝森林四周看了看。

“We’d better check the book,” Jack said. “我们最好查查书。”杰克说。

He pulled out the book. He turned the pages, searching for help. 他抽出书。 他翻着书页，想找点办法。

He stopped at a picture of a scary creature. 他停在一幅可怕生物的图画上。

“Oh, man. What’s this?” he said. “天哪。 这是什么？”他说。

Jack read the writing below the picture. It said: 杰克读了图画下面的文字。上面写着：

Vampire bats live in the Amazon rain forest. At night, they quietly bite their victims and suck their blood. 吸血蝙蝠住在亚马逊雨林里。到了晚上，它们悄悄地咬住受害者，吸它们的血。

“Vampire bats?” said Jack. He felt faint. 他觉得头都晕了。

“Vampire bats?” said Annie. “吸血蝙蝠？”安妮说。

Jack nodded. “After dark.” 杰克点了点头。 “天黑以后。”

Annie and Jack looked around. The rain forest seemed to be getting even darker. 安妮和杰克四处看了看。 雨林好像变得更暗了。

“Yikes,” said Annie. She looked at Jack. “Maybe we should go home.” “呀。”安妮说。 她看着杰克。 “也许我们该回家了。”

Jack nodded. For once he agreed with her. 杰克点了点头。 就这一次，他同意了她的想法。

“But what about our mission?” said Annie. “What about Morgan?” “可我们的任务怎么办？”安妮说。 “那摩根怎么办？”

“We’ll come back,” said Jack. “We’ll have to be prepared.” “我们会再来的。”杰克说。 “我们得做好准备。”

“So we’ll come back tomorrow?” Annie asked. “那我们明天再来？”安妮问。

“Right. Now which way is the tree house?” said Jack. “对。 现在树屋在哪边？”杰克说。

“This way,” said Annie, pointing. “这边。”安妮指着说。

“That way,” said Jack, pointing in the opposite direction. “那边。”杰克说着，指向相反的方向。

They looked at each other. “We’re lost,” they said together. 他们互相看了看。 “我们迷路了。”他们一起说。

Squeak.

“Don’t worry, Peanut.” Annie started to pat the mouse again. But then she stopped. 安妮又开始轻轻拍那只老鼠。 可接着她停了下来。

Squeak. Squeak. Squeak.

“Jack, I think Peanut wants to help us,” said Annie. “杰克，我觉得花生想帮我们。”安妮说。

“How?” “怎么帮？”

“The way she helped us in the time of ninjas—” “就像她在忍者时代帮我们那样——”

Annie placed the mouse on the leafy forest floor. “Take us to the tree house, Peanut.” 安妮把小老鼠放在铺满叶子的森林地面上。 “带我们去树屋，花生。”

The mouse took off. 小老鼠跑开了。

“Where’d she go?” said Annie. “I don’t see her!” “她去哪儿了？”安妮说。 “我看不见她！”

“There!” said Jack. He pointed to leaves rustling on the ground. “那儿！”杰克说。 他指着地上沙沙作响的树叶。

A streak of white passed over the leaves. 一道白色从树叶上掠过。

“Yes, there!” said Annie. “对，那儿！”安妮说。

Jack and Annie followed the moving leaves. The streak of white appeared. And disappeared. 杰克和安妮跟着那些晃动的落叶。 又消失了。

Suddenly Jack stopped. 忽然杰克停住了。

The forest floor was still. There was no sign of Peanut. 森林地面静止不动。 已经看不见花生的踪影了。

“Where is she?” asked Jack. “她在哪儿？”杰克问。

He kept staring at the ground. 他一直盯着地面。

“Jack!” “杰克！”

Jack glanced around. Annie was standing on the other side of a nearby tree. She was pointing up. 杰克四周扫了一眼。 安妮正站在附近一棵树的另一边。 她正指着上面。

Jack looked up. 杰克抬起头。

The tree house. 他们终于爬进了树屋。

“Oh, whew,” Jack said softly. “哦，呼。”杰克轻轻地说。

“She saved us again,” said Annie. “She’s running up the ladder. All by herself. Look.” “她又救了我们一次。”安妮说。 全靠她自己。 快看。”

Annie pointed at the rope ladder. 安妮指着绳梯。

Peanut was climbing up one of the ropes. 花生正顺着其中一根绳子往上爬。

“Let’s go,” Jack said. “咱们走。”杰克说。

Annie started up the ladder. Then Jack. 安妮先爬上梯子，然后是杰克。

They followed Peanut all the way up to the canopy of the rain forest. 他们跟着花生，一直爬到雨林的树冠层。
