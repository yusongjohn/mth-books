7. Freeze! 7. 别动！

Jack and Annie jumped out of the canoe. 杰克和安妮跳出独木舟。

The rain was starting to fall harder. 雨开始下得更猛了。

The monkey took off. He swung from tree to tree, heading up the riverbank. 猴子跑开了。 它从一棵树荡到另一棵树，沿着河岸往上走。

He screeched and beckoned to Jack and Annie. 它尖声叫着，向杰克和安妮招手。

“He wants us to follow him!” said Annie. “它要我们跟着它！”安妮说。

“No! We have to find the special thing. Then go home!” said Jack. 没什么可怕的。”杰克喊道。 我们得找到给摩根的那件特别的东西。 然后再回家！”杰克说。

“He wants to help us!” said Annie. She took off after the monkey. “它想帮我们！”安妮说。 她跟着猴子追了上去。

The two of them vanished into the rain forest. 他们两个消失在雨林里。

“Annie!” “安妮！”

Thunder shook the sky. 雷声震动了天空。

“Oh, brother,” said Jack. “哎呀，真是的。”杰克说。

He dashed after Annie and the monkey. Into the dark forest. 他冲过去追安妮和猴子。 闯进了黑暗的森林。

The forest seemed surprisingly dry. 森林里出人意料地干爽。

Jack looked up. It was still raining. But the treetops acted like a huge umbrella. 杰克抬起头。 雨还在下。 可树冠就像一把巨大的伞。

“Annie?” called Jack. “安妮？”杰克喊道。

“Jack! Jack!” cried Annie. “杰克！ 杰克！”安妮喊道。

“Where are you?” “你在哪儿？”

“Here!” “这儿！”

Jack hurried in the direction of Annie’s voice. 杰克朝着安妮声音的方向赶过去。

Soon he found the monkey. He was screeching and swinging from a tree. 很快他找到了那只猴子。 它正尖声叫着，在树上荡来荡去。

Annie was kneeling on the forest floor. She was playing with an animal that looked like a giant kitten. 安妮跪在森林地面上。 她正在跟一只看起来像巨型小猫的动物玩。

“What’s that?” Jack said. “那是什么？”杰克说。

“I don’t know, but I love it!” said Annie. “我不知道，可我太喜欢了！”安妮说。

Annie batted the animal’s paws. It had gold fur and black spots. 安妮拍打着那只动物的爪子。 它有金色的毛和黑色的斑点。

“I’d better find out what it is,” said Jack. He pulled out the rain forest book and flipped through it. “我最好查查看它是什么。”杰克说。 他抽出雨林的书，一页页翻着。

“Oh, it’s so cute,” said Annie. “噢，它好可爱。”安妮说。

Jack found a picture of an animal with gold fur and black spots. He read: 杰克找到一幅金色皮毛、带黑斑的动物图画。他读道：

The jaguar is the biggest predator in the western hemisphere. 美洲豹是西半球最大的捕食者。

“Forget cute,” Jack said. “That must be a baby jaguar. It’s going to grow up and be the biggest predator in—” “先别说可爱了。”杰克说。 “那一定是美洲豹的幼崽。 它长大后会成为最大的捕食者——”

“What’s a predator?” asked Annie. “什么是捕食者？”安妮问。

GRRR! There was a terrible growl. 传来一声可怕的低吼。

Jack whirled around. 杰克猛地转过身。

The mother jaguar was coming out from behind a tree. She was creeping over the dead leaves—right toward Annie. 美洲豹妈妈正从树后走出来。 它正从枯叶上慢慢爬过来——直直地朝安妮走来。

“Freeze!” whispered Jack. “别动！”杰克低声说。

Annie froze. But the jaguar kept moving slowly toward her. 安妮僵住了。 可美洲豹还是慢慢朝她走来。

“Help,” said Jack weakly. “救命。”杰克虚弱地说。

Suddenly the monkey swooped down from his tree. He grabbed the jaguar’s tail! 突然，猴子从树上猛冲下来。 它抓住了美洲豹的尾巴！

The cat roared and spun around. 那只大猫吼了一声，转过身来。

Annie jumped up. 安妮跳起来，面对着那只猴子。

The monkey pulled the jaguar’s tail again. Then he let go and took off. 猴子又扯了一下美洲豹的尾巴。 然后它松开爪子，跑掉了。

The jaguar sprang after him. 美洲豹朝它扑了过去。

“Run, Annie!” cried Jack. “快跑，安妮！”杰克喊道。

Jack and Annie took off through the rain forest. They ran for their lives! 杰克和安妮拔腿就跑，穿过雨林。 他们拼命地跑！
