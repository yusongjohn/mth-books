2. Kah and Ko 2. 卡和科

The warm air burst with sound. 温暖的空气里突然爆发出各种声音。

Kah-ko!

Eee-eee!

Akkk-awkk!

“What’s going on?” said Jack. “怎么回事？”杰克说。

He and Annie looked out the window. 他和安妮向窗外望去。

The sky was lit by an orange glow as the sun went down. 太阳落下时，天空被一片橙光点亮。

The tree house was in a tree by a stream at the edge of a forest. 树屋在森林边缘、小溪旁的一棵树上。

The wild screeching and cawing came from the forest’s tall, leafy trees. 那刺耳的尖叫和呱呱声来自森林里高大茂密的树冠。

Just then, two creatures leaped onto the windowsill. 就在这时，两只小动物跳上了窗台。

“Aaah!” Jack and Annie yelled, jumping back. “啊！”杰克和安妮大叫着往后跳。

Annie burst out laughing. 安妮突然笑了出来。

Arf! Arf! Teddy barked. 汪！汪！ 泰迪叫着。

Two small monkeys peered at them. Their dark faces were framed with light gray fur. They looked as if they were wearing tiny parkas. 两只小猴子盯着他们看。 它们深色的脸被浅灰色的毛围在中间。 它们看起来就像穿着小小的派克大衣。

“Hi,” said Annie. “I’m Annie. He’s Jack. And he’s Teddy. What are your names?” “嗨，”安妮说。 “我是安妮。他是杰克。 还有他是泰迪。 你们叫什么名字？”

Kah- ko, kah- ko, the monkeys chattered. “咔科，咔科，”猴子们叽叽喳喳地叫着。

“Cool,” said Annie. She turned to Jack. “Her name is Kah. His name is Ko.” “酷，”安妮说。 她转向杰克。 “她叫卡。他叫科。”

“Oh, brother,” said Jack. “哎呀，真是的。”杰克说。

“I bet he is her brother,” said Annie. “我猜他是她弟弟，”安妮说。

Kah and Ko whooped as if laughing at Annie’s joke. Their yellow eyes twinkled. 卡和科大声叫着，好像在笑安妮的笑话。 它们黄色的眼睛闪闪发亮。

“We came to get a gift from the forest,” said Annie. “Do you know where we can find it?” “我们来拿森林的礼物，”安妮说。 “你们知道在哪儿能找到吗？”

The monkeys nodded and chattered. Then they started down the tree. 猴子们点点头，叽叽喳喳地叫着。 然后它们开始往树下爬。

Using their long tails and arms, they swung from branch to branch. They jumped to the ground and looked up. 它们用长尾巴和长胳膊，从一根树枝荡到另一根。 它们跳到地上，抬起头看。

“Coming! Bring Teddy, Jack,” Annie said. Then she started down the ladder. “来了！ 带上泰迪，杰克，”安妮说。 然后她开始往梯子下爬。

Jack quickly flipped through Wildlife of India. He found a picture of the gray monkeys. He read: 杰克飞快地翻着《印度野生动物》。 他找到一张灰色猴子的图片。他读道：

This monkey is called a langur (say lun-GOOR). The word langur means “having a long tail.”

Jack pulled his notebook and pencil out of his backpack. He wrote: 杰克从背包里拿出笔记本和铅笔。他写道：

图中文字：langur means long tail 图中文字：langur 是长尾巴

Annie’s laughter came from below. It blended with the sounds of the forest. 下面传来安妮的笑声。 它和森林里的声音融在了一起。

Arf! Arf! barked Teddy. 汪！汪！ 泰迪叫着。

“Okay, okay,” said Jack. “好了，好了，”杰克说。

He put the book, his notebook, and Teddy into his pack. Then he hurried down the ladder. 他把书、笔记本和泰迪放进背包。 然后赶紧顺着梯子爬下去。

Annie was playing with the langurs on the stream bank. 安妮正在溪岸上和长尾猴一起玩。

Jack put Teddy on the ground. 杰克把泰迪放到地上。

Kah bounded over to Jack and grabbed his hand. The langur’s paw felt like a tiny human hand. 卡跳到杰克身边，抓住了他的手。 长尾猴的爪子摸起来像一只小小的人手。

Kah pulled Jack toward the forest. Ko pulled Annie, and Teddy scampered after them. 卡拉着杰克往森林走。 科拉着安妮，泰迪蹦蹦跳跳地跟在后面。

The langurs climbed the huge, leafy trees. Then they began swinging from branch to branch, like kids on a jungle gym. 叶猴爬上高大茂密的树。 接着它们开始从一根树枝荡到另一根，像孩子在攀爬架上玩。

Annie dashed beneath the swinging monkeys. Teddy ran after her. 安妮在荡来荡去的猴子下面飞奔。 泰迪跟在她后面跑。

“Wait, wait!” Jack called, hurrying after them all. “Annie, slow down! We don’t know anything about this place.” “等等，等等！”杰克喊着，匆匆跟上他们。 “安妮，慢点！ 我们对这儿一无所知。”

The langurs slowed down, as if they understood Jack’s words. Jack caught up with Annie. They walked on through the forest. 叶猴放慢了速度，好像听懂了杰克的话。 杰克追上了安妮。 他们继续穿过森林。

“This is so amazing,” said Annie. “这儿太神奇了，”安妮说。

Jack agreed. 杰克也这么觉得。

The sunset gave the trees a fiery glow. 夕阳把树木染成火红的光。

The hot air smelled sweet. 热空气闻起来香香的。

Blue peacocks spread their tails. 蓝色的孔雀展开了尾巴。

Yellow birds flew from tree to tree. 黄色的鸟儿在树间飞来飞去。

Small deer ate red flowers in a clearing. 小鹿在林中空地上吃着红花。

“It’s like paradise,” said Annie. “这儿简直像天堂。”安妮说。

“Yeah, but don’t forget the title of our book: Wildlife of India, ” said Jack. “ ‘ Wildlife’ means scary animals, too.” “是啊，可别忘了我们书的名字：《印度野生动物》，”杰克说。 “‘野生动物’也包括可怕的动物。”

Jack noticed long, deep gashes in a tree as they walked by. He stopped. 他们走过时，杰克注意到树上有又长又深的裂口。他停了下来。

“What happened there?” he said. “那儿怎么了？”他说。

Annie shrugged and kept walking. 安妮耸耸肩，继续走。

Jack pulled the book out of his pack. There was a picture of a tree with gashes. 杰克从背包里抽出那本书。 书上有一张带抓痕的树的图片。

He read aloud: 他大声读了出来：

Tigers sharpen their claws on tree trunks. They leave big gashes in the bark.

“What?” said Annie. She stopped and looked back at the tree. “什么？”安妮说。 她停下来，回头看着那棵树。

“See what I mean?” said Jack. “Tigers live here. And one of them just came this way.” “明白我的意思了吧？”杰克说。 “这儿有老虎。 而且有一只刚从这边走过。”
