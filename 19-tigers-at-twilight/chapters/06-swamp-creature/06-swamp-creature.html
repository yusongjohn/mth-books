6. Swamp Creature 6. 沼泽生物

Caw! Caw! 呱！呱！

Awk! Awk! Awk! 啊！啊！ 啊！

Jack slowly came out of his dream. He opened his eyes with a start. 杰克慢慢从梦里醒过来。 他猛地睁开了眼睛。

He was surrounded by hazy sunlight. 他四周都是雾蒙蒙的阳光。

Where am I? he thought in a panic. 我在哪儿？ 他惊慌地想。

Then he remembered—he was in India, on an elephant’s back! 然后他想起来——他在印度，在大象背上！

He sat up. Through the haze, he saw that Saba was standing on a muddy stream bank. 他坐了起来。 透过薄雾，他看见萨芭正站在泥泞的溪岸上。

Jack yawned. Where was Annie? 杰克打了个哈欠。安妮呢？

The other elephants were upstream. They sprayed water on each other with their trunks. 其他大象在上游。 它们用鼻子互相喷水。

Teddy, Kah, and Ko were at the edge of the forest. Teddy sniffed the tall grass. The langurs ate flowers. 泰迪、卡和科在森林边缘。 泰迪嗅着高高的草。 叶猴在吃花。

“Good morning!” called Annie. “早上好！”安妮喊道。

She was sitting on a big black rock downstream. She was barefoot and soaking wet. 她坐在下游一块大黑石头上。 她光着脚，全身湿透。

“Hi,” said Jack. “How did you get down?” “嗨，”杰克说。 “你怎么下来的？”

“Teddy and I slid off Saba into the mud,” said Annie. “Try it. But throw down your sneakers and backpack first.” “泰迪和我从萨巴身上滑进泥里，”安妮说。 “你也试试。 不过先把运动鞋和背包扔下来。”

Annie went to Saba’s side. Her feet were buried in mud up to her ankles. 安妮走到萨巴身边。 她的脚陷在泥里，一直没到脚踝。

Jack threw his things to Annie. Then he patted Saba’s rough, wrinkled skin. 杰克把东西扔给安妮。 然后他拍了拍萨巴粗糙起皱的皮肤。

“Thanks for the ride,” he said softly. “谢谢你载我们，”他轻声说。

The elephant touched him one last time with her trunk. 大象最后一次用鼻子碰了碰他。

Jack slid down her side—feet first—and fell into the mud. He caught himself with his hands. They sank into the mud past his wrists. His glasses were spattered, too. 杰克脚先着地，从她身侧滑下去，摔进了泥里。 他及时用手撑住了自己。 手陷进泥里，过了手腕。 他的眼镜上也溅上了泥点。

“Wash off in the stream,” Annie said. “到溪里洗掉。”安妮说。

She put Jack’s pack and shoes on the rock while Jack waded into the cool water. 她把杰克的背包和鞋子放在石头上，杰克则蹚进了凉水里。

He washed the mud off his hands and feet. He rinsed off his glasses. Then he looked around. 他洗掉手上和脚上的泥。 他把眼镜冲洗干净。 然后环顾四周。

Saba had joined the rest of the herd. The elephants looked beautiful in the morning mist. 萨巴已经加入了象群的其余成员。 大象们在晨雾里显得很美。

Everything looked beautiful. 一切看起来都很美。

Yellow and blue water birds spread their wings. Mossy hanging vines swayed in the breeze. Huge white flowers floated on top of the stream. 黄蓝相间的水鸟展开了翅膀。 长满苔藓的垂藤在微风里摇摆。 巨大的白花漂在溪面上。

Then Jack saw a strange sight. It looked like a horn and two ears sticking out of the water. One ear flicked away a fly. 接着杰克看见一个奇怪的景象。 那看起来像一只角和两只耳朵露出水面。 一只耳朵轻轻一甩，赶走了一只苍蝇。

“There’s a weird creature out here,” he called to Annie. “It looks like it has a horn.” “外面有个奇怪的家伙，”他对安妮喊道。 “好像长着角。”

Annie waded into the stream. 安妮蹚进小溪。

“I better check the book,” said Jack. “我最好查查书，”杰克说。

He hurried to his pack, wiping his wet hands on his T-shirt. He pulled out the India book. 他赶紧走向背包，在T恤上擦了擦湿手。 他抽出那本印度书。

There was a picture of a horn sticking out of the water. He read: 书上有一张角从水里伸出来的图片。他读道：

The one-horned rhinoceros, or “rhino,” washes in a forest stream. Rhinos are not usually dangerous. But because they do not see well, they sometimes charge at things by mistake. A loud noise will usually stop them.

Jack felt sorry for the rhinos. Too bad animals can’t wear glasses, he thought. He read more: 杰克为这些犀牛感到难过。 可惜动物不能戴眼镜，他想。他又往下读：

The Indian rhino is a very endangered animal. This means that there are not many left. People called poachers kill them and sell their body parts as medicine and good-luck charms.

Jack started to take out his notebook. 杰克开始拿出笔记本。

Just then, a slurpy, sloshing sound came from the water. 就在这时，水里传来咕嘟咕嘟、哗啦哗啦的声音。

“Whoa!” said Annie. “哇！”安妮说。

Jack looked up. 杰克抬起头。

The rhino rose from the stream. He looked like an ancient swamp creature. 犀牛从溪里站了起来。 它看起来像一头古老的沼泽生物。

“Oh, man!” said Jack. “天哪！”杰克说。

The rhino peered at Annie with his tiny eyes. 犀牛用小小的眼睛盯着安妮。

Then he snorted and lowered his head. His horn pointed right at Annie. 接着它喷了喷鼻息，低下头。 它的角正好对准安妮。

“Make a loud noise!” Jack yelled. “弄出大声响！”杰克大喊。

Annie clapped her hands and shouted, “We come in peace!” 安妮拍着手喊道：“我们是来和平的！”

The rhino stopped. He grunted. Then he sank back into the water. 犀牛停住了。它哼了一声。 然后它又沉回水里。

Annie laughed. 安妮也跟着它笑。

“Whew,” said Jack. “I better take some notes about that big guy.” “呼，”杰克说。 “我最好记点关于那大家伙的笔记。”

Arf! Arf! Teddy barked from the edge of the forest. 汪！汪！ 泰迪从森林边缘叫着。

“And I better get Teddy,” said Annie. “我也得去找泰迪，”安妮说。

She hurried out of the stream and ran to get the dog. Jack pulled out his notebook and wrote: 她赶紧走出小溪，跑去找狗。 杰克拿出笔记本写道：

图中文字：one-horned rhino

an endangered animal

needs glasses 图中文字：独角犀牛是一种濒危动物，需要戴眼镜

“Jack!” shouted Annie. She was racing toward him, with Teddy at her heels. “Come quick!” “杰克！”安妮喊道。 她正朝他跑来，泰迪紧跟在她脚边。 “快来！”

“What’s wrong?” he said. “怎么了？”他说。

“We found something terrible!” Annie was close to tears. “Really terrible!” “我们发现了很可怕的事！”安妮快要哭了。 “糟透了！”
