4. Swing Time 4. 荡秋千时刻

Annie grabbed a vine. 安妮抓住一根藤条。

Jack looked back at the python. The giant snake was still winding its way up the tree. It had almost reached their branch. 杰克回头看那条蟒蛇。 那条巨蛇还在往树上缠。 它差不多已经爬到他们那根树枝了。

Jack took a deep breath. Then he grabbed a vine, too. 杰克深吸一口气。 然后他也抓住一根藤条。

“Lean back, like Kah and Ko did,” said Annie. “往后仰，像卡和科那样。”安妮说。

Jack and Annie leaned back. 杰克和安妮向后仰去。

“One, two, three—go!” said Annie. “一、二、三——走！”安妮说。

They swung out of the tree. 他们荡出了树。

Jack felt his stomach drop. Air rushed by. Leaves and branches slapped at him. 杰克觉得胃往下一沉。 叶子和树枝拍打着他。

Suddenly, the forest shook with a great roar. 突然，森林里响起一声巨吼。

Like a flame, a tiger leaped up from the bushes! 像一道火焰，一只老虎从灌木丛中跃起！

His yellow eyes blazed. His teeth shone like daggers. His claws barely missed Jack and Annie! 它黄色的眼睛闪着光。 它的牙齿亮得像匕首。 它的爪子差一点就抓到杰克和安妮！

“AAAHHH!” they yelled. “啊啊啊！”他们大叫。

The tiger crashed back down into the bushes. 老虎又猛地摔回灌木丛里。

Jack and Annie swung into the langurs’ tree. 杰克和安妮荡进了叶猴的树。

Jack threw one leg around the trunk. He let go of his vine and held on tightly to a branch. 杰克把一条腿缠到树干上。 他松开藤蔓，紧紧抓住一根树枝。

“Oh, man!” he said. He was in shock. “天哪！”他说。他吓呆了。

The langurs patted him, as if to make sure he was okay. 长尾猴拍拍他，好像要确认他没事。

“Wow, that was fun,” said Annie, sitting on a big branch. “哇，真好玩，”安妮坐在一根大树枝上说。

“Fun? Are you nuts?” said Jack. “好玩？ 你疯了吗？”杰克说。

“The swinging was fun,” said Annie. “The tiger was scary.” “荡藤条很好玩，”安妮说。 “老虎很吓人。”

Just then, the tree began to shake. Branches snapped below. 就在这时，树开始晃起来。 下面的树枝突然折断了。

“Oh, no!” said Jack. “哦，不！”杰克说。

“Can tigers climb trees?” asked Annie. “老虎会爬树吗？”安妮问。

“Probably,” said Jack. He hugged the trunk and squeezed his eyes shut. “很可能。”杰克说。 他抱紧树干，紧紧闭上眼睛。

From below came loud sounds of chewing, smacking, and crunching. 下面传来大声的咀嚼声、吧唧声和嘎吱声。

Teddy growled. 泰迪低吼着。

Jack groaned. 杰克哼了一声。

“Now the tiger’s eating the tree,” he said. “现在老虎在吃树了，”他说。

Annie burst into laughter. 安妮突然大笑起来。

Kah and Ko whooped as if they were laughing, too. 卡和科也谁谁叫着，好像在笑。

Arf! Arf! barked Teddy. 汪！汪！ 泰迪叫着。

“What?” said Jack, opening his eyes. “什么？”杰克睁开眼睛说。

“Look!” Annie pointed at the twilight. “看！”安妮指向暮色。

A thick gray tube was waving in the air. 一根粗粗的灰色管子在空中晃来晃去。

“Another snake?” said Jack, horrified. “又是一条蛇？”杰克吓得不行。

“No! An elephant trunk!” said Annie. “别高兴得太早。”杰克说。 大象鼻子！”安妮说。

The trunk wiggled near Jack and Annie, as if it were sniffing them. Then it picked leaves from the tree and disappeared. 象鼻在杰克和安妮身边扭来扭去，好像在闻他们。 然后它从树上摘了些叶子，又消失了。

“Let’s go see!” said Annie. “咱们去看看！”安妮说。

With Teddy still in his backpack, Jack followed Annie down to a lower branch. 泰迪还在背包里，杰克跟着安妮爬到较低的树枝。

They peered out at the twilight forest. 他们朝暮色中的森林望去。

In the gray gloom, they saw a herd of elephants. 在灰蒙蒙的幽暗里，他们看见一群大象。

One stood beneath their tree, eating leaves. Others munched grass. 一头站在他们树下，吃着叶子。 其他大象正大声嚼着草。

“Hey, I’ve got a really cool idea,” said Annie. “嘿，我有个超酷的主意，”安妮说。
