7. Trapped! 7. 被困住了！

Jack threw his things into his pack and followed Annie to the forest edge. 杰克把东西扔进背包，跟着安妮跑到森林边缘。

Teddy stayed close to them, whining. Kah and Ko bounced around, chattering nervously. 泰迪紧跟在他们身边，呜呜叫着。 卡和科蹦来蹦去，紧张地叫着。

As Jack got closer, he saw a tiger. The tiger was lying on his side, completely still. 杰克走近时，看见一只老虎。 老虎侧卧着，一动不动。

His eyes were closed. His front paw was caught in a trap. 它的眼睛闭着。 它的前爪被夹在捕捉器里。

“Is he dead?” said Jack. “它死了吗？”杰克说。

“No, he’s still breathing,” Annie said. A tear ran down her cheek. “He’s worn out from struggling. He must have gotten caught last night. That’s the sad sound we heard.” “没有，他还在呼吸，”安妮说。 一滴眼泪顺着她的脸颊流下来。 “它挣扎得筋疲力尽了。 它一定是昨晚被夹住的。 那就是我们听到的伤心声音。”

“What can we do?” said Jack. “我们能做什么？”杰克说。

“We have to free him!” said Annie. She started toward the tiger. “我们得救他！”安妮说。 她朝老虎走去。

“Wait! Wait!” Jack grabbed her. “Tigers eat people, you know.” He took a deep breath. “Let’s see what the book says first.” “等等，等等！”杰克喊着，匆匆跟上他们。 等等！”杰克一把抓住她。 “老虎吃人的，你知道。”他深吸一口气。 “先看看书上怎么说。”

“Hurry,” said Annie. “快点。”安妮说。

Jack opened their India book. He found a chapter called “Tiger Traps.” He read: 杰克打开他们的印度书。 他找到一章叫“老虎陷阱”。他读道：

Poachers catch Indian tigers with steel traps. This is against the law. After trapping a tiger, they kill it and sell the body parts for money. Like the rhino, the tiger is a very endangered species. If the killing does not end, they both face extinction. Extinction means that someday there may be no Indian tigers or rhinos left on earth.

“Oh, man, we do have to save him,” said Jack. “天哪，我们真的得救他，”杰克说。

Under the writing was a picture of a steel trap used to catch tigers. Jack studied it. It looked horrible and deadly. 这段文字下面有一幅抓老虎用的钢夹图。 它看起来又可怕又致命。

“Okay,” he said. He showed the picture to Annie. “Here’s the plan. I’ll push down on this part. The trap will spring open. Then you pull his leg out. Got it?” “好吧，”他说。 他把图片给安妮看。 “计划是这样。 我按下这个部分。 夹子会弹开。 然后你把他的腿拉出来。明白了吗？”

“Got it,” said Annie. “Sit, Teddy.” “明白了。”安妮说。 “坐下，泰迪。”

The little dog sat. 小狗坐下了。

The langurs watched silently as Jack and Annie moved closer to the tiger. 叶猴静静看着杰克和安妮靠近老虎。

He was the most majestic creature Jack had ever seen. His huge head was a dark orange color. He had perfect black-and-white stripes around his wide face. 它是杰克见过的最威严的生物。 它巨大的头是深橘色的。 它宽阔的脸上有完美的黑白条纹。

The leg in the ugly steel trap was bleeding. 夹在那丑陋钢夹里的腿正在流血。

Slowly, silently, Jack pushed down the lever. 杰克慢慢地、悄悄地按下杠杆。

He raised the bar off the tiger’s leg. 他把横杆从老虎腿上抬起来。

The tiger kept sleeping. 老虎继续睡着。

Slowly, silently, Annie freed the tiger’s leg. She stroked his fur gently. 慢慢地，静静地，安妮把老虎的腿解放出来。 她轻轻抚摸着它的毛。

“Get well,” she whispered. “快点好起来。”她低声说。

The tiger didn’t move. 老虎一动不动。

Slowly, silently, Jack and Annie stood up. 慢慢地，静静地，杰克和安妮站了起来。

They turned around. They started tiptoeing back toward the langurs. 他们转过身。 他们开始踮着脚走回长尾猴那边。

Koo-koo-koo! warned Kah and Ko. 咕——咕——咕！ 卡和科警告道。

Jack and Annie turned back. 杰克和安妮转回头。

The tiger was on his feet. He stared right at them. His eyes seemed to glow. 老虎已经站起来了。 它正好盯着他们。 它的眼睛好像在发光。

Jack looked about wildly. How could they escape? 杰克慌乱地四处张望。 他们怎么逃？

The tiger snarled at Jack and Annie. 老虎对着杰克和安妮咆哮。

Then slowly, silently, he started toward them. 然后它慢慢地、静静地朝他们走来。
