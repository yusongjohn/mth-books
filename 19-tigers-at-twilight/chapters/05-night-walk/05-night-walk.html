5. Night Walk 5. 夜行

“Uh-oh,” said Jack. “What is it?” “呃哦，”杰克说。 “什么主意？”

“I know how to escape the tiger,” said Annie. “Our book said tigers don’t attack elephants, right?” “我知道怎么躲开老虎。”安妮说。 “书上说老虎不会攻击大象，对吧？”

“Yeah,” said Jack. “是啊，”杰克说。

“So we should travel through the forest on the back of an elephant,” said Annie. “那我们就该骑在大象背上穿过森林。”安妮说。

Jack nodded slowly. 杰克慢慢点了点头。

“That is a cool idea,” he said. “But—” “这主意真酷，”他说。 “可是——”

“No buts. I’ll get on first,” said Annie. “别找借口。 我先上去，”安妮说。

She climbed down the tree until she was close to the elephant’s back. She carefully lowered herself off a branch. When her feet rested on the elephant’s back, she let go of the branch. Then she slowly sat down. 她顺着树往下爬，直到靠近大象的背。 她小心翼翼地从树枝上往下放自己。 等脚撑在大象背上，她才松开树枝。 然后她慢慢坐了下去。

The elephant let out a low rumbling sound and shifted her weight. 大象发出低沉连续的响声，移动了一下身体重心。

“Don’t worry, it’s just me,” Annie said softly. She patted the huge creature’s back. “Thanks, Saba.” “别怕，只是我，”安妮轻声说。 她拍了拍这头巨兽的背。 “谢谢，萨巴。”

“Saba?” said Jack. “萨巴？”杰克说。

“That’s her name,” said Annie. “She just told me.” “那是她的名字，”安妮说。 “她刚才告诉我的。”

“Yeah, right,” said Jack. “是啊，才怪，”杰克说。

Arf! Arf! barked Teddy. 汪！汪！ 泰迪叫着。

“Come on, Jack,” said Annie. “It’s not scary.” “来吧，杰克，”安妮说。 “一点不可怕。”

Jack sighed and slowly climbed down the tree. When he was above Saba, he lowered himself off the branch. 杰克叹了口气，慢慢顺着树往下爬。 到了萨巴上方，他从树枝上往下滑。

He put both feet on the elephant. Then he carefully sat down in front of Annie. 他把两只脚都踩在大象身上。 然后小心翼翼地坐在安妮前面。

Saba rumbled again. 萨巴又咕哝了一声。

“Tell her not to worry,” said Annie. “Pat her head.” “告诉她别担心，”安妮说。 “拍拍她的头。”

“Don’t worry, Saba,” Jack said. He patted the elephant’s head. Her skin was rough and wrinkled. “别担心，萨巴，”杰克说。 他拍了拍大象的头。 它的皮肤粗糙，布满皱纹。

The elephant curled her trunk back and rested it on Jack’s head. 大象把鼻子向后卷，搁在杰克头上。

“Hi,” he said in a small voice. “嗨，”他小声说。

Saba flapped her ears. 萨芭拍了拍耳朵。

Kah and Ko swung to the ground in front of Saba. They chattered at her. She waved her trunk at them. The langurs began bounding through the forest. 卡和科荡到萨巴前面的地上。 它们对着她叽叽喳喳。 她朝它们甩了甩鼻子。 长尾猴开始在森林里蹦跳着往前走。

Saba followed. 萨巴跟了上去。

The rest of the herd followed in line. Saba walked with a calm, rolling motion. Jack felt as if he were riding over ocean waves. 象群其余的成员排成一行跟在后面。 萨芭走着，带着平缓起伏的动作。 杰克觉得自己好像在海浪上骑行。

A full moon was rising above the trees. 一轮满月正升到树梢上方。

“Where are we going?” Jack asked. “我们去哪儿？”杰克问。

“Just relax,” said Annie. “Kah and Ko know where to go.” “放轻松，”安妮说。 “卡和科知道该去哪儿。”

Arf! Arf! Teddy barked from Jack’s backpack. 汪！汪！ 泰迪从杰克的背包里叫着。

“You relax, too,” Jack said to the little dog. “你也放松点，”杰克对小狗说。

Fireflies blinked. The moon lit a path between the trees as the elephants marched on. 萤火虫闪着光。 月亮照出一条树间的小路，大象继续往前走。

From a distance came a low growl. 远处传来一声低低的吼叫。

Is that the tiger? Jack wondered. 是那只老虎吗？杰克心想。

The elephants paid no attention. They kept walking through the warm woods. They marched slowly under hanging vines and through misty clearings. 大象毫不理会。 他们继续穿过温暖的树林。 它们慢慢走过垂下的藤蔓，穿过雾蒙蒙的林中空地。

Kah and Ko bounded ahead of them, two moon shadows leading the way. 卡和科在前面蹦跳着，两个月光影子带路。

“We’re going far from the tree house,” said Jack. “我们离树屋越来越远了，”杰克说。

“Don’t worry,” Annie said. “别担心，”安妮说。

Suddenly, a long roar split the night. 突然，一声长长的咆哮划破了夜色。

A chill went down Jack’s spine. 一阵寒意顺着杰克的脊背往下走。

The roar came again. It turned into a yowling. The yowling turned into steady moaning. It sounded as if the whole forest were moaning. 吼声又来了。 那咆哮变成了号叫声。 号叫又变成了持续的呻吟。 听起来整片森林都在呻吟。

“That’s a really sad sound,” said Annie sleepily. “那声音真伤心，”安妮昏昏欲睡地说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “是啊，”杰克说。

But the elephants all marched on. 可大象们仍继续往前走。

Jack rocked in sleepy rhythm with Saba’s walk. He could hear Teddy snoring in his backpack. 杰克随着萨芭走路的节奏昏昏沉沉地摇着。 他能听见泰迪在背包里打呼噜。

Soon Jack’s head rested on Saba’s back. He began drifting in and out of dreams—dreams of rocking in a boat under the dark treetops. 很快，杰克的头靠在了萨巴的背上。 他开始半梦半醒——梦见自己在黑暗的树冠下，坐在一条摇晃的小船上。
