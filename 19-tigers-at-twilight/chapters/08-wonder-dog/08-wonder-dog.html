8. Wonder Dog 8. 神奇狗

The huge tiger limped closer and closer to Jack and Annie. 那头巨大的老虎一瘸一拐，越来越靠近杰克和安妮。

Jack clapped his hands. 杰克拍起手来。

“We come in peace!” he shouted. “我们是来和平的！”他大喊。

But the tiger didn’t turn away. His eyes blazed. His lip curled. 可老虎并没有转开。 它的嘴唇卷了起来。

Arf! Arf! Teddy barked fiercely at Annie. 汪！汪！ 泰迪冲着安妮猛叫。

“Teddy says run and hide!” said Annie. “泰迪说快跑去躲起来！”安妮说。

She grabbed Jack’s hand and pulled him over to the bank. 她抓住杰克的手，把他拉到岸边。

“Wait—what about Teddy?” he cried. “等等——泰迪怎么办？”他喊道。

“Don’t worry!” Annie said. “别担心！”安妮说。

She pulled Jack down behind the black rock. 她把杰克拉到黑色岩石后面蹲下。

“What about Teddy?” Jack asked again. “泰迪呢？”杰克又问。

“He’s okay—he told me!” said Annie. “他没事——他告诉我的！”安妮说。

Jack heard Teddy’s barks turn to fierce growls. 杰克听见泰迪的叫声变成了凶猛的咆哮。

ARF! ARF! GRRR! GRRRR!

The growls grew louder and louder. 咆哮声越来越大。

“That doesn’t sound like Teddy,” said Jack. “那听起来不像泰迪，”杰克说。

Then suddenly, there was silence. A strange silence. 然后突然一片寂静。 一种奇怪的寂静。

“Teddy?” Annie asked. Now she sounded worried. “泰迪？”安妮问。 现在她听起来担心了。

Annie raised her head. She and Jack both peered over the rock. 安妮抬起头。 她和杰克都从岩石上方探出头。

Teddy stood tall and brave in the grass. 泰迪高大勇敢地站在草丛里。

The tiger was limping away. He disappeared between the trees. 老虎一瘸一拐地走开了。 它消失在树丛间。

All the forest seemed to hold its breath—until Annie broke the silence. 整座森林都好像屏住了呼吸——直到安妮打破了沉默。

“Teddy, you’re a wonder dog!” she said. “泰迪，你真是神奇狗！”她说。

The langurs clapped and jumped up and down. 叶猴拍着手，跳上跳下。

Arf! Arf! Teddy was just like a small scruffy dog again. He wagged his tail and ran to Annie and Jack. 汪！汪！ 泰迪又变回一只毛茸茸的小狗了。 它摇着尾巴，跑向安妮和杰克。

Annie scooped him into her arms. 安妮把他抱进怀里。

“You saved us!” she said. “你救了我们！”她说。

“How did you drive away that tiger?” asked Jack, rubbing Teddy’s head. “Did you turn into a wild dog?” “你是怎么把那只老虎赶走的？”杰克一边问，一边揉着泰迪的头。 “你变成野狗了吗？”

Teddy just panted and licked them both. 泰迪只是喘着气，舔了舔他们俩。

Jack pushed his glasses into place and looked back at the forest. 杰克推了推眼镜，回头望着森林。

“Well, I guess we won’t be getting a thank-you gift from that tiger,” he said. “嗯，我想我们是别指望那只老虎送感谢礼物了。”他说。

Annie laughed. 安妮也跟着它笑。

“I guess not,” she said. “I wonder where our gift is.” “我想没有，”她说。 “不知道我们的礼物在哪儿。”

“And I wonder where the tree house is,” said Jack. “也不知道树屋在哪儿，”杰克说。

Kah and Ko chattered at Jack. Then they bounded down the bank, waving their arms. 卡和科对着杰克叽叽喳喳。 然后它们挥着胳膊蹦下河岸。

“They want us to follow them again,” said Annie. “Come on.” “它们又想让我们跟着，”安妮说。 “走吧。”

She and Jack grabbed their things off the rock. They hurried down the stream after the langurs. 她和杰克从岩石上拿起东西。 他们沿着小溪赶紧跟上叶猴。

The water shimmered in the early light. Silver fish leaped into the air. 水面在晨光里闪闪发亮。 银色的鱼跃出水面。

Teddy bounded ahead with Kah and Ko. Soon they disappeared around a bend. 泰迪和卡、科一起在前面蹦跳。 很快，它们就在一个弯道处消失了。

Jack and Annie followed them. 杰克和安妮跟着他们。

When they went around the bend, they saw a man sitting cross-legged on a rock. The langurs sat close to him. 他们绕过弯道，看见一个人盘腿坐在石头上。 叶猴紧挨着他坐着。

The man’s eyes were closed. 那人的眼睛闭着。

He had long white hair and a long white beard. His skin was brown. 他有长长的白发和长长的白胡子。 他的皮肤是棕色的。

He looked very peaceful. 他看起来非常平静。
