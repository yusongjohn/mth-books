3. Life or Death 3. 生死关头

“Tigers?” said Annie. “Cool.” “老虎？”安妮说。 “酷。”

Jack read more: 杰克继续往下读：

A wild tiger eats almost 5,000 pounds of fresh raw meat a year.

“Oh, not so cool,” said Annie. Jack went on: “哦，没那么酷了，”安妮说。杰克接着读：

Tigers usually leave elephants alone. And like many smaller cats, tigers often avoid wild dogs.

Teddy growled. 泰迪低低地吼了一声。

“Wild dogs, not a shrimp like you,” Jack said to Teddy. “A tiger would eat you in a minute.” “是野狗，不是你这么丁点儿大的小家伙。”杰克对泰迪说。 “老虎一转眼就能把你吃掉。”

Teddy growled again. 泰迪又吼了一声。

Just then, Kah and Ko began hooting. Koo-koo-koo! 就在这时，卡和科开始叫了起来。咕——咕——咕！

The peacocks cried Kok! Kok! 孔雀叫着：咯！咯！

The small deer made short barking sounds and stamped their hooves. 小鹿发出短促的吠叫声，还跺着蹄子。

“What’s going on?” said Annie. “怎么回事？”安妮说。

“We better put Teddy in my pack,” said Jack, “to keep him safe.” “我们最好把泰迪放进我的背包里，”杰克说，“好保护他。”

Jack slipped the dog into his pack. Teddy’s head poked out the top. 杰克把狗塞进背包。 泰迪的头从背包口探出来。

“All set?” Jack asked the little dog. “都准备好了吗？”杰克问那只小狗。

Teddy growled again. 泰迪又吼了一声。

This time, a deep, fierce growl answered back. It seemed to surround them. 这一次，一声低沉凶猛的咆哮回应了过来。 那声音好像从四面八方围住他们。

Jack’s hair stood on end. 杰克吓得汗毛直竖。

“Yikes!” said Annie. “哎呀！”安妮说。

“A tiger!” said Jack. “老虎！”杰克说。

Arf! Arf! Teddy barked. 汪！汪！ 泰迪叫着。

Kah and Ko screeched at Jack and Annie from their tree. 卡和科在树上对着杰克和安妮尖叫。

“They want us to join them!” said Annie. “Come on!” She grabbed a branch and climbed up. “它们想让我们跟它们一起上去！”安妮说。 “快来！”她抓住一根树枝往上爬。

Jack’s hands were shaking as he put his backpack on. He grabbed a branch and pushed off the ground. He pulled himself into the tree. 杰克双手发抖，背上背包。 他抓住一根树枝，蹬地跳了上去。 他把自己拉上了树。

Another growl shook the forest. 又一声吼叫震动了整片森林。

“Oh, man,” said Jack. “天哪。”杰克说。

Koo-koo-koo! The langurs climbed higher up the tree. 叶猴往树上爬得更高。

Jack and Annie followed them, climbing from branch to branch. 杰克和安妮跟着它们，从一根树枝爬到另一根。

The sky above was no longer glowing. The bright orange had faded to a twilight gray. 头顶的天空不再发光了。 那片明亮的橙红色已经褪成了黄昏的灰。

Jack looked down. He couldn’t see the ground at all. 杰克往下看。 他根本看不见地面。

He listened for another scary roar. 他听着，等着下一声可怕的吼叫。

Only the cries of frightened forest creatures filled the air. 空气里只剩受惊的林中动物的叫声。

“Maybe the tiger’s gone,” said Annie. “也许老虎走了，”安妮说。

Jack glanced at Kah and Ko. The langurs cuddled together. Their dark faces looked worried. 杰克瞥了一眼卡和科。 长尾猴们挤在一起依偎着。 它们黑黑的脸上带着担忧。

“And maybe not,” said Jack. “也许没有，”杰克说。

“How can we get through the forest without running into him?” said Annie. “我们怎么才能穿过森林，还不碰上它呢？”安妮说。

“That’s a problem,” said Jack. “And it’s getting dark. Soon we won’t be able to see anything.” “这可麻烦了，”杰克说。 “天也越来越黑了。 很快我们就什么也看不见了。”

Kah and Ko hooted again. They pointed down the tree trunk. 卡和科又叫了起来。 它们指着下面的树干。

Arf! Arf! Teddy barked from Jack’s pack. 汪！汪！ 泰迪从杰克的背包里叫着。

“Do they see the tiger?” Jack asked, his heart thumping again. He couldn’t see anything but leaves and branches. “它们看到老虎了吗？”杰克问，心又怦怦跳了起来。 除了叶子和树枝，他什么也看不见。

Then, far below, he saw the tree trunk move! 然后，在远远的下方，他看见树干在动！

“A snake!” said Annie. “蛇！”安妮说。

The snake was slithering around the trunk. It had black-and-tan markings. The snake’s body was as thick as the tree trunk! 那条蛇正绕着树干蜿蜒向上。 它身上有黑棕相间的斑纹。 那条蛇的身体跟树干一样粗！

“A python,” breathed Jack. “是蟒蛇。”杰克屏着气说。

The python kept curling up the tree trunk. 蟒蛇继续沿着树干往上盘。

“Is it poisonous?” asked Annie. “它有毒吗？”安妮问。

Jack pulled out their book. By the last light of day, he found a picture of a python. He read aloud: 杰克抽出他们的书。 借着最后的一丝天光，他找到一张蟒蛇的图片。他大声读道：

The python is not a poisonous snake.

“Whew,” said Annie. “呼。”安妮说。

“Not so fast,” said Jack. He read more: “别高兴得太早。”杰克说。

To kill its prey, the python squeezes it to death, then swallows it whole. A python can swallow an animal the size of a full-grown deer.

“Oh, yuck!” said Annie. “哦，恶心！”安妮说。

“This is more than just yuck, Annie,” said Jack. “This is life or death.” “这可不仅仅是恶心，安妮，”杰克说。 “这可是生死攸关的，”

Kah and Ko chattered at Jack and Annie. 卡和科对着杰克和安妮叽叽喳喳。

“Not now,” said Jack. “We have to think.” “现在不行，”杰克说。 “我们得想办法。”

The langurs grabbed thick vines. They leaned back. Then they jumped out of the tree! 叶猴抓住粗藤条，往后倾。 然后它们跳出了树！

The langurs swung through the air like trapeze artists. They swung over bushes and tall grass and landed in another tree. 长尾猴像空中飞人一样在空中荡过去。 它们荡过灌木和高草，落在另一棵树上。

They screeched at Jack and Annie and waved their arms. 它们冲着杰克和安妮尖叫，挥舞着胳膊。

“I know what they’re saying,” said Annie. “They want us to copy them!” “我知道它们在说什么，”安妮说。 “它们想让我们照着做！”
