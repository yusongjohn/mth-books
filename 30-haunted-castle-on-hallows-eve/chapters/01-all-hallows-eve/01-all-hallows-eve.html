1. All Hallows Eve 1. 所有圣徒节

“Maybe I should be a vampire instead of a princess,” said Annie. “也许我应该当一个吸血鬼，而不是公主，”安妮说。

She and Jack were sitting on their front porch. A cool breeze rustled the trees. Autumn

leaves twirled to the ground. 她和杰克正坐在他们家的门廊上。 一阵凉爽的微风吹动了树木。 秋天的树叶旋转着落到了地上。

“But you already have your princess costume,” said Jack. “Besides, you were a vampire last Halloween.” “但你已经有公主的服装了，”杰克说。 “再说，你去年万圣节还当过吸血鬼呢。”

“I know, but I want to wear my big teeth again,” said Annie. “我知道，但我想再穿我的大牙齿，”安妮说。

“So wear your big teeth and be a vampire-princess,” said Jack. He stood up. “I’m going to go put on my ghoul makeup now.” “那就穿上你的大牙齿，当一个吸血鬼公主吧，”杰克说。他站了起来。 “我要去涂我的幽灵妆了。”

KRAW!

“Oh, man!” said Jack. “哦，天哪！”杰克说。

A giant black bird swooped down to the ground. The bird strutted through the fallen leaves. Its feathers glistened in the golden afternoon light. 一只巨大的黑鸟俯冲到地面。 那只鸟在落叶间踱步。 它的羽毛在金色的下午阳光下闪闪发光。

“Wow, is that a crow?” asked Annie. “哇，那是一只乌鸦吗？”安妮问。

“It’s too big for a crow,” said Jack. “I think it might be a raven.” “它太大了，不是乌鸦。”杰克说。 “我觉得它可能是一只渡鸦。”

“A raven? ” said Annie. “ Cool.” “是一只渡鸦？”安妮说。

The raven lifted its sleek head and stared at them with bright eyes. Jack held his breath. 渡鸦抬起了光滑的头，用明亮的眼睛盯着他们。 杰克屏住了呼吸。

The bird hopped forward. It flapped its great black wings and lifted into the air. Then he glided into the autumn sky and headed toward the Frog Creek woods. 那只鸟向前跳了一步。 它拍打着巨大的黑色翅膀，飞了起来。 然后，它滑翔着飞向秋天的天空，朝蛙溪镇的森林飞去。

Annie jumped up. “It’s a sign! Morgan’s back!” she said. 安妮跳了起来。 “这是一个信号！ 摩根要回来了！”她说。

“I think you’re right!” said Jack. “Let’s go!” “我想你是对的！”杰克说。 “我们走吧！”

Jack and Annie hurried across their front yard, crunching through the leaves. They ran up their street and into the Frog Creek woods. 杰克和安妮赶紧穿过他们的前院，踩着落叶走过去。 他们跑上街道，跑进了蛙溪镇的树林里。

When they came to the tallest oak, they saw the rope ladder swaying in the wind. The magic tree house was waiting for them. 当他们来到最高的橡树前，他们看到绳梯在风中摇晃。 神奇的树屋正等着他们。

“Just as we thought,” Annie said, smiling. “正如我们所想，”安妮笑着说。

Jack followed her up the ladder. When they climbed inside the tree house, they saw no sign of Morgan le Fay, the enchantress from the kingdom of Camelot. 杰克跟着她爬上了梯子。 当他们爬进树屋时，他们没看到摩根·勒·法伊，那位来自卡梅洛特王国的女巫。

“That’s weird,” said Jack, looking around. “这太奇怪了，”杰克环顾四周说。

The wind blew hard again, shaking the tree branches. A huge yellow leaf fluttered through the open window and came to rest at Jack’s feet. 风又猛烈地吹了起来，摇晃着树枝。 一片巨大的黄色叶子从敞开的窗户飘进来，落在杰克的脚边。

“Oh, man,” he said. “Look at this.” “哦，天哪，”他说。 “看看这个。”

“What?” said Annie. “什么？”安妮说。

Jack picked up the leaf. There was writing on it. The letters were curvy and old-fashioned. 杰克捡起了那片叶子。 上面有字。 那些字母弯弯的，看起来很古老。

“Wow,” whispered Annie. “What does it say?” “哇，”安妮小声说。 “上面写着什么？”

Jack held the leaf up to the tree house window. In the fading light, he read aloud: 杰克把叶子举到树屋的窗户前。 天色渐暗时，他大声读起来：

图中文字：To Jack and Annie of Frog Creek,

On $ \underline{\text{all}} $ Hallows Eve,

look for me

in the heart of the oak.

—M. 图中文字：致弗罗格克里克的杰克和安妮，在万圣节之夜，到橡树的心里来找我。——M。

“M!” said Annie. “Morgan never signs her messages with M. …” “M！”安妮说。 “摩根从不用自己的名字首字母M来署名……”

“Right …,” said Jack. “But …” “对……，”杰克说。 “但是……”

“Merlin does!” they said together. “梅林会！”他们异口同声地说。

“Like when he sent us the invitation to spend Christmas in Camelot,” said Annie. She pointed to the Royal Invitation that still lay in the corner of the tree house. “比如他之前寄给我们邀请函，邀请我们在卡梅洛特过圣诞节，”安妮说。 她指着树屋里角落里仍然放着的那张皇家邀请函。

“Now he’s inviting us on Halloween!” said Jack. “Halloween was called ‘All Hallows Eve’ a long time ago.” “现在他邀请我们去万圣节了！”杰克说。 “很久以前，万圣节叫做‘诸圣节前夕’。”

“I know,” said Annie. “We have to go!” “我知道，”安妮说。 “我们必须去！”

“Of course,” said Jack. There was no way they could turn down an invitation from the master magician of all time. “But how do we get there?” “当然，”杰克说。 他们不可能拒绝有史以来最伟大魔法师的邀请。 “但是我们怎么去那儿呢？”

“I’ll bet our invitation will take us,” said Annie, “like when we went to King Arthur’s castle on Christmas Eve.” “我想我们的邀请函会带我们去，”安妮说，“就像我们圣诞节前夜去亚瑟王城堡时那样。”

“Good idea,” said Jack. He pointed to the fancy writing on the leaf. “I wish we could go— uh—” “好主意，”杰克说。 他指着叶子上漂亮的字迹。 “我希望我们能去——呃——”

“To where this leaf invitation came from!” said Annie. “去这张叶子邀请函来的那个地方！”安妮说。

“Right!” said Jack. “对！”杰克说。

The wind began to blow. 风开始吹起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
