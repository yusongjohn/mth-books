5. Ghosts! 5. 幽灵！

“AHHH!” yelled Teddy. “啊——！”泰迪喊道。

“AHHH!” yelled Jack. “啊——！”杰克喊道。

“AHHH!” yelled Annie. “啊——！”安妮喊道。

Still screaming, they all ran toward the door. The bone came after them. 他们还在尖叫，全都朝门跑过去。 那块骨头追了上来。

Teddy led the way as they tore through the archway and scrambled up the winding staircase. 泰迪带头，他们冲过拱门，沿着螺旋楼梯往上爬。

Jack looked behind them. 杰克回头看了一眼。

“It’s still coming!” he shrieked. “它还在追！”他尖叫道。

“AHHH!” they all screamed again. “啊——！”他们又一起尖叫起来。

On the next landing, Teddy charged into a nearby room. 下一站在附近的一间房间，泰迪冲了进去。

“Make haste!” he yelled. “快点！”他喊道。

He pulled Jack and Annie into the room and slammed the door behind them. Out of breath, they all leaned against the door, panting and trembling. 他拉着杰克和安妮冲进房间，然后砰地一声把门关上。 他们气喘吁吁地靠在门上，喘着气，浑身发抖。

“Safe—” Teddy gasped. “Safe from the bone!” Then he started laughing. “安全了——”泰迪喘着气说。 “安全了，不用再害怕骨头怪了！”然后他开始笑起来。

Jack laughed, too. He laughed out of sheer terror. He couldn’t stop. 杰克也笑了。 他因为太害怕了，笑得停不下来。

“Listen, guys! Listen!” said Annie. “I hear a noise!” “听我说，大家！ 听我说！”安妮说。 “我听到声音了！”

Teddy stopped laughing. Jack clapped his hand over his mouth. He listened. He heard a faint clicking sound, but he couldn’t see anything. 泰迪停止了笑。 杰克用手捂住嘴巴。他听了听。 他听到一种微弱的咔哒声，但他什么也没看到。

Teddy used the fire from his lantern to light torches near the door. Then they all looked about. 泰迪用他的提灯里的火点燃了门附近的火把。 然后他们都四处张望。

“Looks like a nursery,” said Teddy. “看起来像婴儿房，”泰迪说。

The torchlight showed a kids’ room. The room had three small beds. Wooden toys were scattered across the floor. A long white curtain fluttered from an open window. 火把的光亮显示这是一个孩子的房间。 这个房间有三张小床。 木制玩具散落在地板上。 一条长长的白色窗帘从开着的窗户里飘了出来。

The clicking noise seemed to be coming from a dark corner. 咔哒咔哒的声音似乎是从一个黑暗的角落传来的。

“What is that?” whispered Annie. She started toward the noise. “那是什么？”安妮小声说道。 她朝声音的方向走了过去。

Jack and Teddy followed her. Teddy held up his lantern. His light shone on a child-sized spinning wheel. It sat in the corner near a basket of wool and a tall, dusty mirror. 杰克和泰迪跟在她后面。 泰迪举起了他的提灯。 灯光照到了一个儿童大小的纺车。 它坐在角落里，靠近一篮羊毛和一面又高又灰尘的镜子。

The spinning wheel was spinning thread. But no one was touching it. It was spinning all by itself. 纺车正在纺线。 但没有人碰它。 它自己在转圈呢。

“Look!” whispered Annie. “看！”安妮小声说。

She pointed to a low table near the spinning wheel. On the table was a chessboard. Large wooden chess pieces sat on the squares of the board. 她指着纺车旁边的一张矮桌子。 桌子上放着一个棋盘。 棋盘上摆着很大的木头棋子。

But some of the pieces weren’t just sitting! 但是有些棋子不是静静地坐着！

As Jack, Annie, and Teddy watched, a horse piece slid slowly from one square to another. Then a queen piece did the same! 杰克、安妮和泰迪看着，一个马棋子慢慢地从一个格子滑到另一个格子。 接着，一个王后棋子也做了同样的事！

“Yikes!” said Annie. “天哪！”安妮说。

“Ghosts!” said Teddy. “是鬼魂！”泰迪说。

“Let’s get out of here!” said Jack. “我们快离开这里！”杰克说。

They bolted across the room. Teddy threw open the door. The white bone was hanging in the air, right outside the door! 他们冲过房间。 泰迪猛地推开房门。 那块白色的骨头悬在空中，就在门外面！

“AHHH!” they all screamed. “啊——！”他们都尖叫起来。

Teddy slammed the door shut. They huddled together, afraid to leave and afraid to stay. Jack’s heart was beating wildly. He couldn’t breathe. 泰迪猛地把门关上。 他们挤在一起，既不敢离开，又不敢留下。 杰克的心跳得飞快。 他喘不过气来。

“I—I thought you weren’t afraid of ghosts!” he said to Teddy, gasping. “我……我以为你不怕鬼！”他喘着气对泰迪说。

“Yes, well, I believe I just discovered that I am!” said Teddy. “是的，嗯，我想我刚刚发现我就是！”泰迪说道。

“What’ll we do?” said Jack. “我们该怎么办？”杰克说道。

“A rhyme—a rhyme,” said Teddy. He gave Annie his lantern. He threw out his arms and started a rhyme: “一个押韵词——一个押韵词，”泰迪说道。 他把他的手电筒给了安妮。 他张开双臂，开始念起押韵词：

Spirits of the earth and air! 大地和空气的精灵！

He looked at Jack and Annie. “Quick, what rhymes with air?” 他看着杰克和安妮。 “快点，什么和air押韵？”

“Bear!” said Jack. “熊！”杰克说道。

Teddy shook his head. “I fear a bear might make things worse.” 泰迪摇了摇头。 “我担心熊会让事情变得更糟。”

Jack tried hard to think of a better word to rhyme with air. 杰克努力想找个和air押韵的更好词语。

“Wait a minute!” said Annie. “I get it now! I get it!” She grinned at Jack and Teddy. “等一下！”安妮说。 “我明白了！ 我明白了！”她对杰克和泰迪笑了笑。

Has she lost her mind? Jack wondered. 她是不是疯了？杰克心想。

“Remember what old Maggie said?” asked Annie. Then she recited: “还记得老玛吉说过的话吗？”安妮问。然后她背诵起来：

“Where is the girl

who spins wool into thread?” “那个把羊毛纺成线的女孩在哪里？”

Annie pointed at the spinning wheel in the corner. “There she is!” she said. “She’s spinning at that wheel.” 安妮指着角落里的纺车。 “她就在那儿！”她说。 “她在那个纺车旁边转圈呢。”

Annie recited more: 安妮接着背诵道：

“Where are the boys

who play chess before bed?” “睡前玩国际象棋的男孩们去哪儿了？”

Annie pointed at the chess table. “There they are!” she said. “They’re probably her brothers! They’re playing chess!” 安妮指着国际象棋桌。 “他们在那儿呢！”她说道。 “他们可能是她的哥哥们！ 他们在下国际象棋！”

She recited more: 她继续背诵道：

“Where is the hound

who waits to be fed?” “那只等着吃饭的猎犬去哪儿了？”

Annie threw open the door to the nursery. 安妮猛地推开育婴室的门。

The bone was still hanging in the air. Jack and Teddy jumped back in fear. 骨头还在空中挂着。 杰克和泰迪吓得跳了回来。

“Don’t be afraid!” said Annie. “It’s just a dog—a hound! He’s carrying a bone in his mouth. Don’t you see? The girl, the boys, the hound—they’re all here! They’re just invisible!” “别害怕！”安妮说。 “那只是只狗——一只猎犬！ 他嘴里叼着一根骨头，难道你没看见吗？ 女孩、男孩、猎犬——他们都在这儿！ 他们只是看不见而已！”，
