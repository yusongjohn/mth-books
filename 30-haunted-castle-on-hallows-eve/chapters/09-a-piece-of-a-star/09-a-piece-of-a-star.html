9. A Piece of a Star 9. 一颗星星的碎片

Teddy dropped down to a ledge. Jack and Annie followed. Hidden in the shadows, they crouched together, their dark feathers touching. They peered out at the moonlit lair of the Raven King. 泰迪跳到一块突出的岩石上。 杰克和安妮跟了上去。 藏在阴影中，他们蹲在一起，黑色的羽毛相互触碰。 他们朝月光照亮的乌鸦王的藏身之处望去。

The king’s giant nest was tucked under a rocky overhang. It was made of mud, twigs, and long strips of tree bark. Two raven sentries were guarding its dark entrance. 乌鸦王的巨大巢穴藏在一块岩石的遮蔽处。 它是由泥土、树枝和长长的树皮条做成的。 两只渡鸦哨兵守卫着它黑暗的入口。

Okay, Jack softly croaked, what’s the plan? 好吧，杰克轻声说道，有什么计划？

Listen carefully, Teddy answered. In tiny queeks and caws of raven whispers, he spelled out a plan: I’ll distract the guards. Annie, you keep watch at the entrance. Jack, you go into the nest and get the diamond. Then both of you head back to the castle and wait for me there. 仔细听，泰迪回答道。 他用渡鸦的低声咕哝和叫声，拼出一个计划：我会引开守卫。 安妮，你在入口处守望。 杰克，你去鸟巢里拿到那颗钻石。 然后你们两个回到城堡，在那里等我。

What about the Raven King? croaked Jack. 那乌鸦王呢？杰克咕哝道。

I sense he’s not here, croaked Teddy. I see no legions of bodyguards. But we should hurry before he returns. 我感觉他不在这里，泰迪咕哝道。 我没看到大批的保镖。 但我们得在他回来之前赶快行动。

Jack had lots more questions about the plan. But before he could ask them, Teddy lifted off his perch and flew toward the entrance. 杰克对这个计划还有好多问题想问。 但还没等他问出来，泰迪就从栖木上飞起来，朝入口飞去。

Let’s go! cried Annie, rising into the air. 我们走吧！安妮喊道，飞了起来。

Jack was in a panic. He fluffed out his feathers and croaked, Wait, you guys! 杰克慌了神。 他抖了抖羽毛，嘎嘎叫道：‘等等，你们这些家伙！’

But it was too late! Teddy was already dive-bombing the raven sentries! 但已经太晚了！ 泰迪已经冲向了乌鸦哨兵，开始俯冲轰炸！

“ARK-ARK-ARK!” “ARK-ARK-ARK！”，

The two sentries left their watch and flew at Teddy with short shrieks. They chased him high into the sky. 两只哨兵离开了岗位，发出短促的尖叫声朝泰迪飞去。 他们追着他飞向高空。

Annie zipped to the entrance of the nest. Come on, Jack! she croaked. 安妮迅速飞到鸟巢的入口。杰克，快过来！ 她咕哝道。

Jack jumped off the ledge and flew on to the giant nest. Without thinking, he stepped through the entrance. 杰克从悬崖上跳下来，飞到了巨大的鸟巢上。 他没有多想，就走进了入口。

He jerked his head from side to side. With his raven sight, he saw walls packed with dried mud, animal fur, vines, and sticks. The floor was covered with moss. 他左右转着头看。 凭借乌鸦的视力，他看见墙壁上堆满了干泥、动物毛发、藤蔓和树枝。 地板上铺满了青苔。

Jack took a few steps forward. He stopped. He saw no sign of the Raven King. He cocked his head from side to side, listening. All was quiet. 杰克向前走了几步。他停下了。 他没有看到乌鸦王的踪影。 他左右转着头，仔细听着。一切都很安静。

Jack looked around the nest. One part of the nest’s wall looked different. It was black and shiny. He stepped toward it. He touched it with his beak. It wasn’t a wall at all. It was a curtain of feathers. 杰克环顾着鸟巢。 鸟巢的一面墙看起来有点不一样。 那面墙是黑色的，还闪着光。 他朝那面墙走了过去。 他用喙碰了碰它。 那根本不是墙。 那是一片羽毛做的帘子。

Jack pushed through the feather curtain. Moonlight shone into the space behind it. Heaps of gold and silver coins glittered in the cool light. Pale pearls, emeralds, and rubies shone and sparkled. 杰克穿过羽毛帘子。 月光从帘子后面照了进来。 金子和银币堆在一起，在凉爽的光线下闪闪发亮。 苍白的珍珠、翡翠和红宝石闪闪发光。

Amid all the treasure was a blue-white crystal. It was no bigger than a marble. But it shone with a light all its own, like a piece of a star. 在所有这些宝藏中，有一块蓝白色的水晶。 它只有弹珠那么大。 但它发出自己独特的光芒，就像星星的一部分。

Jack knew at once the stone was the Diamond of Destiny. His raven heart thumping, he walked to the diamond and nudged it with his beak. As the diamond tilted, it shot forth beams of brilliant light. 杰克立刻知道这块石头就是命运之钻。 他的乌鸦心脏怦怦直跳，他走到钻石前，用喙轻轻碰了碰它。 当钻石倾斜时，它射出了耀眼的光束。

“JAH! JAH!” Annie was calling to him from outside. “CREE-GRO!” They’re coming! “JAH! JAH！”安妮从外面朝他喊道。 “克里格罗！”他们来了！

Jack carefully picked up the diamond with his beak. He felt a surge of strength and courage. 杰克小心翼翼地用喙捡起了那颗钻石。 他感到一阵力量和勇气涌上心头。

Annie called another warning. But Jack wasn’t afraid at all. He calmly walked out of the Raven King’s nest back into the night. 安妮又喊出了一个警告。 但杰克一点也不害怕。 他冷静地走出了渡鸦国王的巢穴，重新走进了夜色中。

More sentries had been alerted. They were flying toward the mountaintop, cawing madly in alarm. 更多的守卫已经被警觉了。 他们正飞向山顶，疯狂地叫着，发出警报。

“KRAK! KRAK! KRAK!” “克拉克！克拉克！ 克拉克！”

Jack saw Annie perched on the ledge. Hurry, Jack! Hurry! she croaked. 杰克看到安妮坐在山崖上。快点，杰克！ 快点！

Annie flew off the mountain. Holding the diamond in his beak, Jack gracefully flapped his wings and lifted into the air after her. 安妮从山上飞了下去。 杰克用喙叼着钻石，优雅地拍打着翅膀，飞到她后面。

As he and Annie sailed down from the mountaintop, a chorus of KRAKs split the night. Thousands of roosting ravens rose into the sky like a giant black cloud. Their beating wings rumbled like thunder. 当杰克和安妮从山顶滑翔而下时，一阵阵“克啦”的声音划破了夜晚的寂静。 成千上万只栖息的渡鸦飞上天空，像一团巨大的黑云。 它们拍打翅膀的声音像雷声一样隆隆作响。

The cloud of ravens covered the light of the moon. The night was completely black. 渡鸦的黑云遮住了月亮的光。 夜晚变得一片漆黑。

“SPREE! SPREE!” Annie croaked. Fly! Fly! “斯普里！” “SPREE！”安妮嘶哑地喊道。飞！ 飞！

She and Jack glided down through the dark sky toward the duke’s castle. The wing beats of the raven army still thundered above the mountaintop. But none of the ravens were chasing them. 她和杰克在漆黑的天空中滑翔着，朝公爵的城堡飞去。 渡鸦军队的翅膀仍在山顶上空轰鸣。 但没有一只渡鸦在追赶他们。

They don’t know what to do without their king, Jack thought. He wondered where their king was. But with the Diamond of Destiny in his beak, he felt no fear. 他们不知道没有国王该怎么办，杰克想。 他想知道他们的国王在哪里。 但因为嘴里叼着命运之 diamond，他一点也不害怕。

The farther Jack and Annie flew from the mountain, the more distant became the sound of the raven soldiers’ wings. 杰克和安妮飞得越远，乌鸦士兵翅膀的声音就越远。

The duke’s castle came into sight. Jack saw the light of Teddy’s lantern in the nursery. But he didn’t want to stop flying just yet. Instead, he swooped over the castle keep, over the courtyard, the gatehouse, and the bridge, over the candlelit cottages and oak forest. Annie flew with him. 公爵的城堡出现在了视野中。 杰克看见泰迪的提灯在育婴室里亮着。 但他还想再飞一会儿。 于是，他俯冲过城堡的塔楼，飞过庭院、门房和桥，飞过那些点着蜡烛的小屋和橡树林。 安妮和他一起飞。

Finally they both glided smoothly back to the castle and landed on the window ledge of the nursery. The Diamond of Destiny was safe! 最后，他们平稳地滑翔回到城堡，落在育婴室的窗台上。 命运之钻安全了！
