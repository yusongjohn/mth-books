Chapter 4 The Castle 4. 城堡

The castle was still and silent. 城堡静悄悄的，没有一点声音。

No candles burned in its windows. 窗户里没有点燃蜡烛。

No guards stood at its gate-house. 城门旁没有守卫站着。

No archers patrolled the tops of its walls. 城墙顶部也没有弓箭手巡逻。

Hello! 你好！

Teddy shouted. 泰迪喊道。

No one answered. 没有人回答。

Not very well protected, eh? 保护得不太周到，对吧？

Said Teddy. 泰迪说。

Our mission should be easy. 我们的任务应该很容易。

Yeah! 对！

Said Annie. 安妮说。

Jack didn't say anything. 杰克什么也没说。

He would have felt happier if guards had been protecting the castle. 如果有人守卫着城堡，他应该会感觉更开心一些。

That would have seemed more normal. 那样看起来会更正常一些。

Come along, said Teddy. 快点来，”泰迪说。

Jack and Annie followed Teddy over a wooden bridge to the gate-house. 杰克和安妮跟着泰迪走过一座木桥，来到门房前。

Teddy held up his lantern before the arched double doors. 泰迪在拱形双门前举起了他的灯笼。

Cobwebs sparkled in the misty light. 蛛网在朦胧的灯光下闪闪发亮。

Hello! 你好！

May we enter? 我们可以进去吗？

He called. 他喊道。

Silence! 安静！

They all stared at the heavy wooden doors. 他们全都盯着那扇沉重的木门。

Never fear. 别害怕。

I shall get us in. 我会想办法进去的。

Said Teddy. 泰迪说。

The boy's sorcerer put down his lantern. 那个男孩的巫师放下了他的提灯。

He took a deep breath. 他深吸了一口气。

He rubbed his hands together. 他搓了搓手。

Then he stretched out his arms and shouted, 然后他张开双臂，大声喊道，

Open ye to oaken doors! 打开橡木大门！

He looked at Jack and Annie. 他看着杰克和安妮。

Quick! 快！

What rhymes with doors? 什么词和“门”押韵？

Uh, floors? 呃，是地板吗？

Said Jack. 杰克说。

Good! 好！

Said Teddy. 泰迪说。

He opened his arms again and yelled, Open ye to oaken doors! 他张开双臂，大喊道：“打开你们的橡木门！”

Or we will not mop up your floors! 否则我们就不帮你们打扫地板了！

Nothing happened. 什么也没发生。

Teddy looked at Jack and Annie. 泰迪看着杰克和安妮。

Bad rhyme, he said. 这是个糟糕的押韵，他说。

Annie frowned and nodded. 安妮皱起眉头，点了点头。

Are you sure they're even locked? 你确定它们真的锁上了吗？

Said Jack. 杰克说。

Let's see, said Annie. 让我们看看，安妮说。

She pushed on one door. 她推了其中一扇门。

Jack pushed on the other. 杰克推了另一扇门。

Slowly the doors creaked open. 门慢慢吱呀地打开了。

Ah, brilliant! 啊，太棒了！

Said Teddy with a laugh. 泰迪笑着说道。

Shall we? 我们进去吧？

He held out his hand and waved Jack and Annie through the open doorway. 他伸出一只手，示意杰克和安妮穿过敞开的门。

The castle gatehouse was cold and empty. 城堡的门楼又冷又空荡荡的。

Jack could see his breath in the sharp air. 杰克在刺骨的空气中看见自己呼出的白气。

He heard a creak. 他听见了一声吱呀声。

They all turned to look. 他们都转过身来看。

The heavy doors moved by themselves and closed with a thump. 沉重的门自己动了起来，然后砰地一声关上了。

They all stared at the doors for a moment. 他们都盯着门看了好一会儿。

Then Teddy broke the silence. 然后泰迪打破了沉默。

Interesting! 真有趣！

He said cheerfully. 他说得很开心。

Jack tried to smile. 杰克试着笑了笑。

Indeed! 确实！

Interesting! 真有趣！

He said. 他说。

He shivered. 他打了个寒战。

He couldn't tell if it was from cold or from fright. 他分不清这是因为冷还是因为害怕。

Now, he wondered, now are we entering the tunnel of fear? 现在，他想知道，我们现在是不是正进入恐惧的隧道？

Onward, said Teddy. 向前走，”泰迪说。“

He led them through the empty gatehouse onto the shadowy castle grounds. 他带他们穿过空荡荡的门房，来到阴暗的城堡地面上。

There were no signs of life anywhere. 到处都没有生命的迹象。

Jack thought of the old woman's rhyme. 杰克想起了那位老妇人的童谣。

Where's the girl who spins wool into thread? 纺羊毛成线的女孩在哪里？

Where are the boys who play chess before bed? 睡觉前下棋的男孩们在哪里？

Where's the hound who waits to be fed? 等着吃饭的猎犬在哪里？

Jack wondered what the rhyme could mean. 杰克想知道这首童谣到底是什么意思。

What girl? 什么女孩？

What boys? 什么男孩？

What hound? 什么猎犬？

Teddy crossed the courtyard to the entrance of a large building. 泰迪穿过庭院，来到一座大建筑物的入口处。

Jack and Annie quickly followed him. 杰克和安妮迅速跟了上去。

Teddy held up his lantern so they could see inside. 泰迪举起他的提灯，好让他们能看见里面。

There were rows of clean, empty stalls. 里面是一排排干净、空荡的马厩。

Saddles and bridles hung from pegs on the walls. 马鞍和缰绳挂在墙上的钉子上。

Hay was piled in the corners. 干草堆在角落里。

Must be the stables, said Jack. 一定是马厩，杰克说。

But no horses, said Annie. 但没有马，安妮说。

No matter, it is orderly, said Teddy. 没关系，这里很整齐，泰迪说。

Onward! 前进！

He led them to the open doorway of another building. 他带他们走到另一座建筑的敞开门口。

Teddy's lantern shone on a brick oven, a stone hearth, baskets of apples, and strings of onions 泰迪的提灯照见了一个砖砌的烤炉、石砌的壁炉、一篮篮苹果，还有串串洋葱

hanging from the rafters. 挂在横梁上。

The kitchen, said Jack. 厨房，杰克说。

But no cooks or servants, said Annie. 但没有厨师或仆人，安妮说。

No matter, it is orderly here, too, said Teddy. 没关系，这里也很整洁，泰迪说。

Onward! 前进！

As they wandered through the moonlit courtyard, Jack looked to his right and his left. 当他们穿过月光下的庭院时，杰克看了看右边和左边。

He glanced behind them. 他回了一下头。

If there are ghosts, he thought, what do they look like? 如果有鬼魂，他想，它们看起来会是什么样子呢？

Halloween ghosts in sheets? 万圣节穿床单的鬼魂？

See through people like in the movies? 能看到穿透明衣服的人，就像电影里那样吗？

He stopped. 他停下了。

Hey, guys! 嘿，大家！

He whispered loudly. 他大声地小声说。

Wait a minute, wait a minute. 等一下，等一下。

What is it, said Annie. 怎么了？

Jack pushed his glasses into place. 杰克把眼镜推到正确的位置。

Are we just going to keep wandering from building to building, he said? 我们就这样一直从一栋楼走到另一栋楼吗？

What's our strategy here? 我们这里的策略是什么？

Strategy? 策略？

asked Teddy. 泰迪问。

What means we should make a plan? 策略的意思是我们应该制定一个计划？

said Annie. 安妮说。

Indeed, said Teddy. 确实，泰迪说。

Excellent idea! 好主意！

A plan, yes. 制定一个计划，没错。

He grinned. 他咧嘴笑了。

How do we do that? 我们怎么做到呢？

Well, first we ask ourselves, where exactly are we going? 嗯，首先我们要问问自己，我们到底要去哪儿？

said Jack. 杰克说。

Teddy looked about. 泰迪四处张望。

He pointed to a tower rising above the courtyard. 他指着庭院上方的一座塔楼。

There, he said, the keep. 就在那儿，他说，是城堡。

It is where the family lives, the Duke and Duchess. 那是伯爵和伯爵夫人的家。

Great, said Jack. 太好了，杰克说。

Now what will we do when we get there? 那我们到了那里之后该做什么呢？

Climb the stairs to each floor, said Teddy. 去爬每一层楼的楼梯，泰迪说。

Have a look around. 四处看看。

And if we see anything that's not orderly, we'll tidy it up, said Annie. 如果我们看到有什么地方不整齐，我们就把它整理好，安妮说。

Excellent, said Teddy. 太棒了，泰迪说。

And then asked Jack. 然后杰克问了。

We leave, said Teddy, our mission done. 我们离开了，泰迪说，我们的任务完成了。

Jack nodded. 杰克点了点头。

This wasn't much of a plan or a mission, he thought. 他想，这并不是一个好计划，也不是什么重要的任务。

But he liked the leaving part. 但他喜欢离开的部分。

He hoped that happened before any ghosts showed up. 他希望在任何鬼魂出现之前就发生。

Okay, he said. 好吧，他说。

Holding his lantern to light their way, Teddy led them to the entrance of the Castle Keep. 泰迪举着提灯照亮前方，带领他们走向城堡的入口。

He pushed open a wooden door, and they all stepped inside. 他推开了一扇木门，他们全都走了进去。

Dark figures loomed against the stone walls. 黑暗的影子在石墙上若隐若现。

Ah! 啊！

Jack cried. 杰克喊道。

He jumped back, bumping into Annie. 他往后跳，撞到了安妮。

Annie laughed. 安妮笑了。

It's only our shadows, she said. 只是我们的影子，她说。

Jack felt silly. 杰克觉得有点傻。

Right. 对，对。

Sorry, sorry, he said. 对不起，对不起，他说。

He took a deep breath. 他深吸了一口气。

Okay, let's find the stairway. 好，我们去找楼梯。

Aye, said Teddy. “啊，”泰迪说。

He started walking slowly down a dark passageway. 他开始沿着一条黑暗的通道慢慢走。

Jack and Annie followed close behind. 杰克和安妮紧跟在后面。

The air was heavy and damp. 空气又重又潮湿。

Jack's heart was pounding. 杰克的心跳得很快。

Now, he wondered, now are we in the tunnel of fear? 现在，他想，我们现在在恐惧隧道里吗？

A moaning sound came through the passageway, then a loud bang. 传来一阵呜呜的声音，接着“砰”的一声巨响。

Yikes! 天哪！

Said Annie. 安妮说。

We and Jack grabbed each other. 我们和杰克赶紧抓住了彼此。

Teddy laughed. 泰迪笑了。

"'Twas only the shudders banging,' he said. “只是地板在震动而已，”他说。

"'What about that moaning?' asked Jack. “那刚才的呜呜声呢？”杰克问。

"'Twas only the wind blowing through the crannies,' said Teddy.' “那是风吹进缝隙的声音，”泰迪说。

Jack took another deep breath and kept going. 杰克又深吸了一口气，继续往前走。

Soon they came to a twisting stairway. 不久后，他们来到了一条弯弯曲曲的楼梯前。

"'The stairs,' said Annie. “‘楼梯，’安妮说。

"'Good,' thought Jack. “‘好，’杰克心想。

Climbing the stairway was a solid part of their plan. 爬楼梯是他们计划中很重要的一部分。

"'Shall we?' said Teddy. “‘我们上去吧？’泰迪说。

"'Indeed upward,' said Jack, trying to sound like Teddy.' “‘确实要向上，’杰克说，努力模仿泰迪的声音。

Teddy held up his lantern and started up the steep stone stairs. 泰迪举起他的提灯，开始走上陡峭的石阶。

Jack and Annie followed. 杰克和安妮跟着走。

They climbed around and around the twisting stairway. 他们绕着螺旋楼梯爬呀爬呀。

When they came to the first landing, Teddy led them to the doorway of a room. 当他们来到第一层平台时，泰迪带他们来到一间房间的门口。

They peeked in. 他们偷偷往里瞧。

They saw rows of helmets, breastplates, gauntlets, shields, spears, and swords. 他们看见一排排头盔、胸甲、手套、盾牌、长矛和剑。

"'The armour-room,' said Jack. “这是盔甲室，”杰克说。

"'Aye,' said Teddy. “对，”泰迪说。

"'Everything seems in order here,' said Annie.' “这里看起来一切都很整齐，”安妮说。

Jack nodded. 杰克点了点头。

He liked the order in the room. 他喜欢房间里井井有条的样子。

It made him feel safer. 这让他感觉更安全了。

"'Shall we keep going?' said Teddy. “我们继续前进吧？”泰迪说。

"'Of course,' said Jack. “当然可以，”杰克说。

He was starting to feel a lot braver now.' 他现在感觉勇敢了许多。

They returned to the stairs and began climbing again. 他们回到楼梯，又开始往上爬。

On the third floor they peered through an arched entrance into a huge room. 到了三楼，他们透过一个拱形入口，望进了一个很大的房间。

Teddy used the candle from his lantern to light torches on either side of the doorway. 泰迪用他手电筒里的蜡烛，把门口两边的火把点着了。

In the flickering light Jack saw high ceiling and walls hung with tapestries. 在忽明忽暗的灯光下，杰克看见了高高的天花板，墙上挂着很多挂毯。

"'It's the Great Hall,' he said, where they have feasts and stuff. “这是宴会厅，”他说，“他们在这里举办宴会和各种活动。

"'Let's look around,' said Annie. “我们四处看看吧，”安妮说。

"'See if anything seems out of order.' “看看有没有什么不对劲的地方。

As the three of them slowly walked forward, Jack kept an eye out for ghosts. 当他们三个人慢慢向前走时，杰克一直留意着有没有鬼魂。

He held up his lantern. 他举起了手电筒。

It shone on a long banquet table. 手电筒的光照射在一张长长的宴会桌上。

"'Aha,' he said. “啊哈，”他说。

The table was littered with breadcrumbs, candle-wax, and the petals of dead flowers. 桌子上到处都是面包屑、蜡烛蜡和死花的花瓣。

The floor near the table was messy too, with bits of food and meat-bones. 桌子附近的地板也很乱，到处都是食物残渣和肉骨头。

"'Finally we found something to put in order,' said Teddy. “终于找到一些可以整理的东西了，”泰迪说。

Shall we?' 我们走吧？

Jack caught sight of a straw broom in the corner. 杰克在角落里看到了一把稻草扫帚。

"'Sure, I'll sweep,' he said. “好的，我会打扫卫生，”他说。

"'I'll clear the table,' said Annie. “我会清理桌子，”安妮说。

"'I will scrape the wax,' said Teddy.' “我要把蜡刮掉，”泰迪说。

Jack grabbed the straw broom and began sweeping the floor around the table. 杰克抓起扫帚，开始清扫桌子周围的地板。

He swept up apple-peelings, fish-bones, bits of eggshells, and old cheese. 他扫起了苹果皮、鱼骨头、蛋壳碎片和陈旧的奶酪。

As he swept everything into a neat pile, he felt good. 当他把所有东西扫成一堆时，他感觉很好。

They were finally carrying out their mission. 他们终于开始执行任务了。

We're bringing order to the castle just like Merlin told us to,' he thought. “我们正像梅林告诉我们的那样，把城堡变得井井有条，”他心想。

Soon we can leave.' 很快我们就能离开了。

Suddenly Annie screamed. 突然，安妮尖叫起来。

Jack dropped his broom and whirled around. 杰克扔掉了他的扫帚，转过身来。

"'Look,' Annie cried, her eyes wide. “看，”安妮喊道，她的眼睛睁得大大的。

She pointed toward a stone hearth at the other end of the great hall. 她指着大厅另一端的一个石壁炉。

In front of the hearth, a large, white bone hovered in the air. 壁炉前，一根又大又白的骨头漂浮在空中。

It bobbed up and down, then it began floating straight toward them. 它上下晃动了一下，然后开始朝他们直直地飘过来。
