Queek.

12. A New Day 12. 新的一天

A little croak came from the cage on the floor. 地板上的笼子里传来一声小小的蛙叫。

“Teddy!” cried Annie. “泰迪！”安妮喊道。

“We almost forgot you!” said Jack. “我们差点把你给忘了！”杰克说。

Queek, Teddy croaked again. 奎克，泰迪又叫了一声。

“Let me change him back,” Annie said to Jack. “让我把他变回来，”安妮对杰克说。

“Okay, but let me get out of the way first,” said Jack. He handed Annie the hazel twig. Then he quickly stepped over to the window. “好吧，不过让我先躲开，”杰克说。 他把榛树枝递给了安妮。 然后他迅速走到窗边。

Annie moved closer to Teddy’s cage. She closed her eyes and thought for a moment. Then she waved the wand over the cage and said: 安妮走近了泰迪的笼子。 她闭上眼睛，想了一会儿。 然后她挥动魔杖，念叨着说：

“O hazel twig from hazel tree!

Make him Teddy! Set him free!” “榛树枝，来自榛树！让他变成泰迪！放他自由！”

There was a mighty roar, a blast of heat, and a blaze of light! Then the cage was gone, and Teddy was a boy again, sitting on the floor. 突然传来一声巨响，一阵热浪，还有耀眼的光芒！ 接着笼子不见了，泰迪又变回了男孩，坐在地板上。

“Yay!” said Annie. “太棒了！”安妮说。

“Nicely done,” said Teddy. “Thanks.” “做得很好，”泰迪说。 “谢谢。”

“Welcome back!” said Jack. He and Annie helped Teddy stand up. “欢迎回来！”杰克说。 他和安妮帮泰迪站起来。

Teddy shook his arms and legs. “ Ahhh! ’Tis good to be human again!” he said. “And now we must help the duke’s family. Where’s the diamond?” 泰迪晃了晃他的手臂和腿。 “太好了，又能做人了！”他说。 “现在我们必须帮助公爵一家。 钻石在哪里？

“We lost it!” said Annie. “我们把它弄丢了！”安妮说。

“Yeah, I had it in my beak,” said Jack. “But I must have dropped it when Annie changed us back into ourselves.” “是的，我原本是用嘴叼着的，”杰克说。 “但当我把你们变回原来的样子时，我可能把它掉下来了。”

“Don’t worry,” said Teddy, “it must be here somewhere.” “别担心，”泰迪说，“它一定在这里的某个地方。”

The three of them got on their hands and knees and started crawling around the floor of the nursery. There was no sign of the diamond. Suddenly Jack heard Teddy gasp. 他们三个人趴在地上，开始在育婴室的地板上爬来爬去。 地板上没有任何钻石的踪迹。 突然，杰克听到泰迪倒抽一口气。

“Oh, my,” Teddy whispered. “Look.” He was staring at the corner. “哦，天哪，”泰迪小声说。 “看。”他正盯着角落。

The Diamond of Destiny was rising from the basket of wool by the spinning wheel. 命运之钻正从纺车旁的羊毛篮子里升起来。

“The ghost girl must have hidden it when the Raven King came!” whispered Annie. “那个女鬼一定是在乌鸦王来的时候把它藏起来的！”安妮小声说。

The diamond moved slowly toward Jack and stopped in front of him. Jack held out his hand, and the diamond settled into his palm. 钻石慢慢朝杰克移动，然后在他面前停了下来。 杰克伸出一只手，钻石落在了他的掌心里。

“Thank you,” Jack said to the ghost girl. “I’ll put it back in its place now.” “谢谢你，”杰克对女鬼说。 “我现在就把它放回原位。”

Carefully holding the diamond, Jack crossed the room. Annie pulled the tapestry aside, and Jack opened the golden door of the cabinet. 杰克小心地拿着钻石，穿过房间。 安妮把挂毯拉开，杰克打开了柜子上的金色门。

He looked one last time at the shining stone. “I really felt brave when I was carrying this diamond,” he said softly. 他最后一次看了看那块闪闪发光的石头。 “当我拿着这块钻石的时候，我真的感觉很勇敢，”他轻声说道。

“Jack,” said Annie, “you were pretty brave just now without it.” “杰克，”安妮说，“你刚才没有它的时候就已经很勇敢了。”

“Indeed you were,” said Teddy. “确实如此，”泰迪说。

Jack smiled. He carefully put the Diamond of Destiny back in its place and closed the golden door. Then Annie covered the cabinet once more with the tapestry. 杰克笑了。 他小心地把命运之钻放回原位，然后关上了金色的门。 接着，安妮再次用挂毯把柜子盖上。

The air in the nursery grew warmer. A girl began to take shape beside Teddy. She wore a white nightgown. She had dark curly hair. She was about Teddy’s age. 育婴室里的空气变得越来越热了。 一个女孩开始在泰迪旁边逐渐成形。 她穿着一件白色的睡衣。 她有一头深色的卷发。 她的年龄和泰迪差不多大。

At the chess table, two boys took shape, too. They looked just alike. They were twins about Annie’s age. 在棋桌旁，两个男孩也逐渐显现出来。 他们看起来一模一样。 他们是安妮年龄的双胞胎兄弟。

At first the children were a bit pale and cloudy. Slowly they grew more and more visible, until they were solid and rosy-cheeked. 一开始，孩子们看起来有点苍白和模糊。 慢慢地，他们越来越清晰，直到变得结实而脸颊红润。

At the same time, a large brown dog became visible by the door. He barked and ran to the girl. 与此同时，一只大棕狗在门口也变得清晰可见。 他叫了一声，然后跑向那个女孩。

“Oliver!” she cried. She hugged him. Then she looked at Jack, Annie, and Teddy. She gave them a big smile. “Hello,” she said. “奥利弗！”她喊道。她抱住了他。 然后她看了看杰克、安妮和泰迪。 她给了他们一个大大的微笑。 “你好，”她说。

“Hi!” said Annie. “Are the three of you the only people in this castle?” “你好！”安妮说。 “你们三个是这座城堡里唯一的人吗？”

“Oh, no, everyone else is here, too,” the girl said, “but they were all asleep when the Raven King stole the diamond. We were supposed to be asleep, too. But sometimes we like to sneak out of bed and play. We were playing hide-and-seek when I found the secret door behind the tapestry. I wanted to see the diamond better, so I put it on the window ledge to catch the moonlight. Then Tom and Henry started to play chess—” She pointed to the boys. “哦，不，其他人也在这里，”女孩说，“但当乌鸦国王偷走钻石时，他们都睡着了。” 我们本来也该睡觉了。 但有时候我们喜欢偷偷从床上溜出来玩。 我们在玩捉迷藏时，我发现了挂毯后面的秘密门。 我想看得更清楚些，所以我把钻石放在窗台上，好让月光照到它。 然后汤姆和亨利开始下棋——”她指着那两个男孩。

“Gwendolyn started spinning,” said Tom. “And Oliver went down to the great hall to look for scraps.” “格温多林开始纺线，”汤姆说。 “奥利弗则下到大厅去找剩饭。”

“That’s when the Raven King swooped down to the window and stole the diamond,” said Gwendolyn. “Before we could even go tell our mother and father, we began to fade away.” “就是那时，渡鸦国王飞到窗边，偷走了钻石，”格温多林说。 “在我们甚至还没来得及告诉爸爸妈妈之前，我们就开始慢慢消失不见了。”

“Mother! Father!” said Tom, as if he’d just remembered their parents. “We must wake them, Gwendolyn!” “妈妈！ “爸爸！”汤姆说，好像他刚刚才想起来他们的父母。 “我们必须叫醒他们，格温多林！”

“I know,” she said. “We shall go upstairs and wake them at once. Since they were sleeping, I suspect they never even knew they were invisible!” “我知道，”她说。 “我们要上楼去，立刻叫醒他们。” 因为他们正在睡觉，我猜他们甚至不知道自己是隐形的！“

Gwendolyn took her brothers’ hands, and the three of them started out of the nursery. At the door, she looked back at Jack, Annie, and Teddy. “Thank you for helping us,” she said, “whoever you are.” 格温多林牵起她兄弟们的手，三个人一起离开了育婴室。 到了门口，她回头看了看杰克、安妮和泰迪。 “谢谢你们帮助我们，”她说，“不管你们是谁。”

The duke’s children then slipped out of the nursery. Oliver grabbed his bone and bounded after them. 公爵的孩子们然后悄悄离开了育婴室。 奥利弗抓起他的骨头，飞快地追了上去。

Jack handed the hazel twig to Teddy. 杰克把榛树枝递给了泰迪。

“Listen,” Jack said. “I don’t think this is something that kids should play with—even sorcerer kids. You’d better give it back to your cousin.” “听着，”杰克说。 “我觉得这东西孩子们不该玩，就算是法师的孩子也不该玩。” 你最好把它还给你的表弟。”

“Aye, perhaps that is a good plan,” said Teddy. He grinned impishly as he slipped the twig back into his pocket. Then he gestured toward the door. “Shall we?” “啊，也许这是个好主意，”泰迪说。 他狡黠地笑了笑，然后把树枝悄悄放回口袋里。 接着，他朝门做了个手势。 “我们走吧？”

Jack and Annie nodded. 杰克和安妮点了点头。

Teddy picked up his lantern and blew out the candle. Then he led them all out of the castle nursery into the hallway. As they started down the stairs, servants rushed by. 泰迪拿起他的提灯，吹灭了蜡烛。 然后他带他们所有人从城堡的婴儿室走到了走廊里。 当他们开始下楼时，仆人们匆匆跑过。

“Ring the bells!” one said. “敲响钟声！”一个人喊道。

“Bring water for the duke and duchess!” said another. “给公爵和公爵夫人带水来！”另一个人说。

“We’re getting a late start today!” said a third. “今天我们出发得有点晚！”第三个人说。

Jack, Annie, and Teddy kept winding down the stairs, past the great hall, past the armor room, down to the entrance of the keep. 杰克、安妮和泰迪一直沿着楼梯往下走，经过大厅，经过铠甲室，来到城堡入口处。

As they stepped into the courtyard, bright sunlight shone on the castle towers. The bells began to ring. Roosters crowed. Horses neighed. 当他们走进庭院时，明亮的阳光照在城堡的塔楼上。 钟声开始响起。公鸡打鸣。 马儿嘶叫。

Servants were making a big cooking fire. A blacksmith was pounding his anvil. A milkmaid was hauling her pails. 仆人们正在生起一个大火堆。 铁匠正在敲打他的铁砧。 挤奶女工正拉着她的桶。

In the bright daylight, Jack, Annie, and, Teddy walked through the busy courtyard. They passed through the gatehouse and crossed the wooden bridge. When they got to the other side, they looked back. 在明亮的 daylight 中，杰克、安妮和泰迪穿过繁忙的庭院。 他们穿过门房，走过木桥。 到了另一边，他们回头看了看。

Archers now stood guard on top of the castle walls. 现在，弓箭手站在城堡墙顶上守卫。

Teddy waved to them. Then he looked at Jack and Annie. “Order has returned to the castle!” he said. “Our mission is done!” 泰迪朝他们挥手。 然后他看着杰克和安妮。 “城堡恢复秩序了！”他说。 “我们的任务完成了！”

Laughing, they ran through the patch of trees toward the small village. As they hurried along the dirt path past the cottages, they saw villagers in their doorways. They were all staring in the direction of the ringing castle bells. 他们笑着，跑过那片树林，朝小村庄跑去。 当他们沿着泥路跑过小屋时，看见村民们站在门口。 他们都朝城堡方向望去，那里正在响着钟声。

Maggie, the old woman, grinned toothlessly at the three of them. “The bells are ringing again,” she said in a creaky voice. 玛吉，那位老妇人，毫无牙齿地对三人露出了笑容。 “钟声又响起来了，”她用沙哑的声音说道。

“Yes!” said Jack. “The boys and the girl and the hound are all back! There’s nothing to be afraid of anymore. The whole castle is alive and well!” “是啊！”杰克说。 “男孩、女孩和猎犬都回来了！ 现在再也没有什么好害怕的了。 整个城堡都活了过来，而且很好！”

Jack, Annie, and Teddy left the village and headed for the woods. As they walked through the fallen leaves, sunlight filtered down through the tree branches. 杰克、安妮和泰迪离开了村庄，朝森林走去。 当他们穿过落叶时，阳光透过树枝照了进来。

Merlin’s words echoed in Jack’s mind: You are about to enter a tunnel of fear. Proceed onward with courage, and you will come out into the light. 梅林的话在杰克的脑海中回响：你们即将进入一个充满恐惧的隧道。 鼓起勇气继续前进，你们就会走到光明之中。

Jack looked around. The forest was bright with the most beautiful golden light he had ever seen. 杰克环顾四周。 森林里亮着最美丽的金色光芒，这是杰克见过最亮的光。
