8. SPREE! SPREE! 8. 斯普里！斯普里！

“CRA-JAH! CRA-AWK-NEE!” Teddy croaked. “克啦-贾！” 克啦-奥克-尼！”泰迪嘶哑地喊道。

Teddy was speaking Raven now, but Jack understood him perfectly. Teddy had said, Sorry, Jack and Annie! 泰迪现在在跟拉文说话，但杰克完全听懂他的话。 泰迪说：‘对不起，杰克和安妮！’

Annie stepped forward. She fluttered to the window ledge and perched with Teddy. 安妮向前走了一步。 她飞到窗台上，和泰迪一起坐了下来。

“GRA-QUORK!” she croaked. That’s okay! This is fun! “GRA-QUORK！”她咕哝道。没关系！ 这真好玩！

“QUORK?” Jack squeaked. Fun? “QUORK？”杰克惊叫道。好玩吗？

“GRO-JAH!” croaked Annie. “KAH-SPREE!” Come on, Jack! Let’s fly! “GRO-JAH！”安妮咕哝道。 “KAH-SPREE！”快点，杰克！我们飞吧！

Annie and Teddy lifted off the ledge and disappeared into the moonlit mist. 安妮和泰迪从窗台上飞了起来，消失在月光下的雾气中。

This can’t be real, thought Jack. It can’t be real! 这不可能是真的，杰克心想。这不可能是真的！

He looked at his feathers and claws. He stretched out his right wing, then his left. He flapped them both. Before he knew what was happening, he lifted clumsily off the floor and landed on the window ledge. 他看了看自己的羽毛和爪子。 他先伸展了右边的翅膀，然后是左边的。 他把两只翅膀都扇动起来。 就在他还没反应过来的时候，他笨拙地从地上飞了起来，落在了窗台上。

Jack saw Annie and Teddy flying around in the moonlight. They were zipping about like acrobats—diving and tumbling through the air. 杰克看到安妮和泰迪在月光下飞来飞去。 他们像杂技演员一样在空中穿梭——俯冲、翻滚。

“AWK-NEE CAW!” Jack croaked. Annie, come back! “啊克-尼卡！”杰克嘎嘎叫道。安妮，回来！

“SPREE! SPREE!” she called. Fly! Fly! “斯普里！ 斯普里！”她喊道。飞！ 飞！

“AWK-NEE!”

Annie rose from a dive. In one easy swoop, she glided up and sat beside Jack on the window ledge. 安妮从一次跳水中跃起。 她轻松地一跃而上，滑翔着落在杰克旁边的窗台上。

This is so much fun, Jack! she croaked. Don’t just sit here! 杰克，这太好玩了！她喊道。 别只是坐在这儿！

Teddy flew by them. I’m off to the mountain-top! he croaked. Fly with me! 泰迪从他们身边飞过。 我要去山顶了！他喊道。 和我一起飞吧！

Come on, Jack! croaked Annie. She took off after Teddy, swooping through the cool night air. 快点，杰克！安妮喊道。 她紧随泰迪之后起飞，在凉爽的夜空中俯冲飞行。

Oh, man! Fear clutched Jack’s small raven heart. I have definitely entered the tunnel of fear now, he thought. Merlin’s words echoed in his mind: “Proceed onward with courage, and you will come out into the light.” 哦，天哪！ 恐惧紧紧抓住了杰克那颗小小的乌鸦之心。 我肯定已经进入恐惧的隧道了，他想。 梅林的话在他脑海中回响：‘鼓起勇气继续前进，你就会来到光明之中。’

Jack looked out at the night. He closed his eyes. He jumped off the ledge. 杰克望向夜晚。 他闭上了眼睛。 他跳下了悬崖。

Jack was falling! He opened his eyes and flapped. His wings lifted him up. He steadied himself. He hovered in the cold night air, his eyes darting from right to left. He looked down. He nearly fainted! The castle courtyard was far below! 杰克在下坠！ 他睁开了眼睛，开始拍打翅膀。 他的翅膀把他托了起来。 他稳住了自己。 他在寒冷的夜空中悬停，眼睛左右张望。他向下看。 他差点晕倒了！ 城堡的庭院远在下方！

Jack flapped wildly. He glided. He flapped again. He glided. Flapping and gliding, he climbed higher and higher into the sky. 杰克疯狂地拍打翅膀。他滑翔了。 他又拍打了一下。他滑翔了。 一边拍打翅膀一边滑翔，他越飞越高，飞向天空。

Finally Jack caught up with Annie and Teddy. They were circling in the air, waiting for him. 最后，杰克追上了安妮和泰迪。 他们正在空中盘旋，等着他。

“RARK!” Jack croaked. Onward! “RARK！”杰克嘶哑地喊道。继续前进！

The three of them flew together through the moonlit night, heading for the nest of the Raven King. Except for the swooshing of their wings, they made no noise. 他们三个一起飞过月光下的夜晚，朝乌鸦国王的巢穴飞去。 除了翅膀划过空气的嗖嗖声，他们没有发出任何声音。

They soared up the side of the mountain, past hemlocks and tall pines. They flew through long, misty clouds. 他们飞过山的一侧，经过冷杉和高大的松树。 他们飞过了长长的、雾蒙蒙的云层。

As they glided toward the mountain’s peak, Teddy let out a low croak: Raven troops! 当他们滑翔向山顶时，泰迪低声叫道：乌鸦部队！

Jack peered through the night. He couldn’t believe his eyes. In the white moonlight, he saw thousands of ravens roosting on rocky ledges! 杰克透过黑夜望去。 他简直不敢相信自己的眼睛。 在白色的月光下，他看见成千上万的乌鸦栖息在岩石的悬崖上！

Jack, Annie, and Teddy kept flying. They soared above the troops, higher and higher, toward the craggy peak of the mountain. When they reached the top, Teddy let out a squawk. 杰克、安妮和泰迪继续向前飞。 他们飞在乌鸦部队的上方，越来越高，朝山顶的崎岖峰顶飞去。 他们到达顶部时，泰迪发出一声尖叫。

There it is! he croaked. The nest of the Raven King! 就是那里！他嘶哑地喊道。 乌鸦王的巢穴！
