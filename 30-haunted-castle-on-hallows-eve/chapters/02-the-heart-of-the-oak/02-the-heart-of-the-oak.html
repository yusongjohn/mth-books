2. The Heart of the Oak 2. 橡树的心

Jack opened his eyes. A chilly wind blew into the tree house. Oak leaves swirled outside the window. 杰克睁开了眼睛。 一股冷风吹进了树屋。 橡树叶在窗外飞舞。

“Look, we have our costumes,” said Annie. “I’m not a princess or a vampire.” “看，我们有我们的服装，”安妮说。 “我不是公主，也不是吸血鬼。”

Jack looked at their clothes. He was wearing a knee-length tunic and tights. Annie was wearing a long dress with an apron. 杰克看了看他们的衣服。 他穿着一件及膝的长袍和紧身裤。 安妮穿着一条长裙和围裙。

“Camelot costumes,” Jack said softly. “卡梅洛特的服装，”杰克轻声说。

They looked out the window together. They were high in a huge oak tree in a thick forest. 他们一起望向窗外。 他们正坐在一棵巨大的橡树上，周围是茂密的森林。

The afternoon sun was low in the autumn sky. 下午的太阳在秋天的天空中低低地挂着。

“So what do we do now?” said Jack. “那我们现在该做什么呢？”杰克说。

“The invitation says we’re supposed to meet Merlin in the heart of the oak,” said Annie. “邀请函上说，我们该在橡树的心脏处见梅林，”安妮说道。

“Yeah, but what does that mean?” said Jack, scowling. “The heart of an oak?” “是啊，但这是什么意思呢？”杰克皱着眉头说。 “橡树的心脏？”

“Let’s go down and try to figure it out,” said Annie. “我们下去看看，试着弄清楚吧，”安妮说。

She carefully placed their invitation in a corner of the tree house. Then she and Jack climbed down the rope ladder. They stepped onto the leafy ground. In the fading daylight, they began circling the base of the giant oak. 她小心地把邀请函放在树屋的一个角落里。 然后她和杰克顺着绳梯爬了下来。 他们踏上了长满树叶的地面。 在逐渐消失的 daylight 中，他们开始绕着那棵巨大的橡树转圈。

They walked all the way around, until they came to the rope ladder again. 他们一直绕到再次来到绳梯前。

“We’re back where we started,” said Jack. “We never found the heart.” “我们又回到了起点，”杰克说。 “我们从来没找到过树心。”

“Wait a minute,” said Annie. “What’s that?” She pointed to a long, thin crack in the bark of the tree trunk. A sliver of light seemed to be coming from the crack. “等一下，”安妮说。 “那是什么？”她指着树干树皮上的一条又长又细的裂缝。 有一丝光亮似乎从裂缝里透出来。

Jack touched the bark around the light. He pushed. The crack got bigger. 杰克碰了碰光亮周围的树皮。他推了推。 裂缝变得更大了。

“It’s a secret door!” said Jack. He pushed harder. Creak. A tall, narrow door swung into the tree. Light streamed from inside. “这是一个秘密门！”杰克说。他用力推了推。 吱呀一声。 一扇又高又窄的门在树里打开了。 光从里面流了出来。

“We found it,” whispered Annie, “the heart of the oak.” “我们找到了，”安妮小声说，“橡树的心。”

Jack nodded. 杰克点了点头。

“Let’s go in,” said Annie. They slipped through the narrow doorway into the bright hollow of the tree trunk. “我们进去吧，”安妮说。 他们从狭窄的门缝溜进树干里明亮的树洞中。

Jack couldn’t believe his eyes. The round room was lit with hundreds of candles. Shadows danced on the curved brown walls. 杰克简直不敢相信自己的眼睛。 这个圆形的房间被数百支蜡烛照亮了。 影子在弯曲的棕色墙壁上跳舞。

This isn’t possible! thought Jack. The heart of the oak seemed much bigger than the tree itself! 这不可能！杰克心想。 橡树的心似乎比树本身还要大！

“Welcome,” said a deep, whispery voice. “欢迎，”一个低沉而轻柔的声音说道。

They turned around and saw an old man sitting in a carved wooden chair. He had a long white beard and wore a red cloak. 他们转过身，看见一位老人正坐在一把雕刻过的木椅上。 他有一把长长的白胡子，穿着一件红色的斗篷。

“Hi, Merlin,” said Annie. “你好，梅林，”安妮说。

“Hello, Annie. Hello, Jack. It is good to see you again,” the magician said. “I am grateful for the help you gave us on Christmas Eve in Camelot. Now Morgan and I believe you might be able to help us again.” “你好，安妮。你好，杰克。 很高兴再次见到你们，”这位魔术师说。 “我非常感谢你们在圣诞节前夕在卡梅洛特对我们的帮助。 现在摩根和我相信你也许能再次帮助我们。”

“We’d love to!” said Annie. “我们非常愿意帮忙！”安妮说。

“The whole future of our kingdom depends upon your success,” said Merlin. “我们王国的整个未来都取决于你们的成功，”梅林说。

“Are you sure you want us?” Jack asked. “I mean, we’re just kids.” “你确定你想让我们帮忙吗？”杰克问。 “我是说，我们只是孩子。”

“You have passed many tests for Morgan,” said Merlin. “Are you not Master Librarians and Magicians of Everyday Magic?” “你已经通过了很多测试，摩根。”梅林说道。 “你们不是日常魔法的图书管理员和魔法师吗？”

Jack nodded. “Yes, we are,” he said. 杰克点了点头。 “是的，我们是。”他说。

“Good. You will need all your skills on this mission,” said Merlin. “You will also need a helper and guide from our world, the world of magic and legend.” “很好。 这次任务你们需要运用所有技能，”梅林说道。 “你们还需要一位来自我们世界、魔法和传说世界的助手和向导。”

“Are you coming with us?” asked Annie. “你要和我们一起去吗？”安妮问道。

“No,” the magician said. “Your guide shall be one much younger than I. He is in my library now. Yesterday he brought me some books I had requested from Morgan’s library.” “不，”这位魔法师说道。 “你的向导将是一个比我年轻得多的人。 他现在在我的图书馆里。 昨天他从摩根的图书馆里给我带来了我请求的几本书。

Merlin rose from his chair. “Come,” he said, leading them to a door in the curved wall. He opened it and stepped into another room. Jack and Annie followed him. 梅林从椅子上站起来。 “来，”他说着，领他们走到曲墙上的一个门前。 他打开门，走进了另一个房间。 杰克和安妮跟了上去。

The musty room was filled with scrolls and ancient-looking books. Sitting on the floor was a boy about eleven or twelve years old. He was reading by the light of a lantern. 这个有点发霉的房间里摆满了卷轴和看起来很古老的书。 地板上坐着一个大约十一二岁的小男孩。 他正借着提灯的光读书。

“Your helper and guide,” Merlin said to Jack and Annie. “你的助手和向导，”梅林对杰克和安妮说。

The boy looked up. He had a friendly, freckled face and dark, twinkly eyes. He broke into a big grin. 男孩抬头看了看。 他有一张友好的、有雀斑的脸，还有又黑又闪亮的眼睛。 他咧开嘴笑起来。

“Arf, arf!” he said. “汪，汪！”他说。

“Teddy!” cried Annie. “泰迪！”安妮喊道。

Jack couldn’t believe it! Their helper was the young sorcerer who was training as Morgan’s apprentice! 杰克简直不敢相信！ 他们的助手竟然是正在摩根门下学习的年轻法师！

Merlin, for once, looked surprised. “You already know each other?” he asked. 梅林这一次看起来也惊讶了。 “你们已经互相认识了？”他问。

“Yes, we met a while ago when I accidentally changed myself into a dog!” said Teddy. “是的，我们之前见过，当时我意外把自己变成了一只狗！”泰迪说道。

“Morgan wanted to teach Teddy a lesson,” explained Annie. “So she sent him with us on four tree-house journeys before she changed him back into a boy. He saved us on the Titanic. And he saved us from a buffalo stampede!” “摩根想教泰迪一个教训，”安妮解释道。 “所以她让我们带着他经历了四次树屋之旅，之后又把他变回了男孩。 他在泰坦尼克号上救了我们。 他还救了我们免于被野牛群踩踏！”

“And from a tiger in India,” said Jack, “and a forest fire in Australia.” “还有在印度的一只老虎，”杰克说，“还有在澳大利亚的一场森林大火。”

“Wondrous journeys, indeed,” said Merlin. “I am glad you are already friends. Your friendship may help you on this mission.” “真是奇妙的旅程，”梅林说道。 “我很高兴你们已经是朋友了。 你们的友谊可能会帮助你们完成这次任务。”

“What is our mission?” asked Annie. “我们的任务是什么？”安妮问道。

“We are now in one of the outlying realms of Camelot,” said Merlin. “Beyond these woods lies the castle of a duke.” “我们现在在卡梅洛特的外围领地，”梅林说道。 “穿过这些树林，就是一位公爵的城堡。”

Merlin leaned forward, as if he were about to tell them something really scary. “It will be your mission,” he said, “to bring order to the duke’s castle.” 梅林向前倾身，好像要告诉他们一些非常可怕的事情。 “你们的任务，”他说，“是让公爵的城堡恢复秩序。”

Merlin sat back in his chair. His gaze was calm, but a fierce light shone in his eyes. 梅林坐回他的椅子上。 他的目光平静，但眼中闪烁着强烈的光芒。

Bring order to a castle? thought Jack. Is that all? 让一座城堡恢复秩序？杰克心想。 就只是这样吗？

“Who messed it up, sir?” asked Annie. “是谁搞乱了城堡，先生？”安妮问道。

“You will find out soon enough,” said Merlin. “你们很快就会知道了，”梅林说道。

“We accept our assignment gladly,” said Teddy. “The mission will be done without fail!” “我们很乐意接受这个任务，”泰迪说。 “任务一定会圆满完成！”，

Merlin fixed his gaze on Teddy. “Perhaps,” he said. “But a warning, my boy: you are hasty and careless with your magic rhymes. On this mission, you must choose all your words wisely.” 梅林盯着泰迪看。 “也许吧，”他说。 “但是，我的孩子：你使用魔法押韵时太匆忙、太粗心了。 这次任务中，你必须谨慎选择每句话。”

“Indeed I will,” said Teddy. “我一定会做到的，”泰迪说。

Merlin turned to Jack and Annie. “And a warning to you, too,” he said. “You are about to enter a tunnel of fear. Proceed onward with courage, and you will come out into the light.” 梅林转向杰克和安妮。 “我也要给你们一个警告，”他说。 “你们即将进入一个充满恐惧的隧道。 继续前进，勇敢一点，你们就会走到光明中。”

Tunnel of fear? Jack thought. 恐惧隧道？杰克想。

Merlin picked up the lantern and handed it to Teddy. “The duke’s castle lies to the east. Go quickly,” he said. “Order must be restored as soon as possible.” 梅林拿起提灯，递给泰迪。 “公爵的城堡在东边。 快去吧，”他说。 “必须尽快恢复秩序。”

Teddy nodded at Merlin. Then he turned to Jack and Annie. “To the duke’s castle!” he said, and he led them out of the heart of the oak. 泰迪朝梅林点头。 然后他转向杰克和安妮。 “去公爵的城堡！”他说，然后带领他们走出了橡树的中心。
