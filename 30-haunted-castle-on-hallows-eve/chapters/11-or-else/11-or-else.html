11. Or Else! 11. 否则！

Jack and Annie were too stunned to speak. 杰克和安妮太震惊了，说不出话来。

Staring at the bizarre creature, Jack remembered the story of the Raven King—how the king had wanted to be a bird, how he had stolen a spell from the Wizard of Winter, how the spell had worked only halfway and left him half bird and half human. 杰克盯着那个奇怪的生物，想起了关于乌鸦国王的故事——国王想变成一只鸟，他偷走了冬之巫师的咒语，但这个咒语只生效了一半，让他变成了一半鸟一半人。

The Raven King jumped from the window to the floor. One by one, his raven bodyguards swooshed into the room after him. At least twenty ravens came through the window. Soon Jack and Annie were surrounded by dark wings, sharp beaks, and bright eyes. 乌鸦国王从窗户跳到了地板上。 一个接一个，他的乌鸦卫兵嗖地飞进了房间。 至少有二十只乌鸦从窗户飞了进来。 很快，杰克和安妮就被漆黑的翅膀、尖锐的鸟喙和闪亮的眼睛包围了。

Once his guards were in place, the Raven King twisted his head from side to side, looking from Jack to Annie. “Where are the two ravens who stole my diamond?” he asked in a raspy voice. 他的卫兵们站好位置后，乌鸦国王左右扭动着头，从杰克看向安妮。 “那两个偷走我钻石的乌鸦在哪里？”他用沙哑的声音问道。

“What diamond?” asked Annie. “什么钻石？”安妮问。

“What … what ravens?” asked Jack, his voice trembling. He wished desperately that he still had the Diamond of Destiny to give him strength and courage. “什么……什么乌鸦？”杰克颤抖着声音问。 他非常希望能再次拥有命运之钻，让自己变得勇敢和坚强。

“The ravens that came to this castle after raiding my treasure room,” said the Raven King. “Where are they hiding?” “这些在袭击我的宝库后来到这座城堡的渡鸦，”渡鸦国王说道。 “他们躲在哪里？”

Jack tried to imagine that he was still holding the diamond. “We don’t know anything about them,” he said in a low, steady voice. Pretending to hold the diamond actually made him feel brave. 杰克试着想象自己仍然拿着那颗钻石。 “我们对他们一无所知，”他用低沉而平稳的声音说道。 假装拿着钻石竟然让他感觉勇敢了起来。

“You don’t know anything about them?” said the Raven King. “你们对它们一无所知？”渡鸦国王说道。

“No,” said Jack. “You must have the wrong castle.” “是的，”杰克说道。 “你们一定找错了城堡。”

“Ah, the wrong castle,” said the Raven King. “啊，找错了城堡，”渡鸦国王说道。

“Yes,” said Jack. “是的，”杰克说道。

“Perhaps you’re right,” said the Raven King. “But are you certain you haven’t seen them? They look very much like this little one here.” “也许你是对的，”乌鸦国王说。 “但你确定你没看到他们吗？ 他们看起来和这里这个小家伙非常像。”

The Raven King threw his cape over his shoulder and held up an iron birdcage. A raven was held captive inside. 乌鸦国王把斗篷甩到肩上，举起一个铁制的鸟笼。 一只乌鸦被关在笼子里。

“JAH, AWK-NEE!” the raven croaked. “JAH, AWK-NEE!”乌鸦嘎嘎叫道。

“Teddy!” cried Annie. “泰迪！”安妮喊道。

“His name is Teddy?” said the Raven King. “How charming. I’ve caught a Teddy. I think he’ll make a wonderful pet, don’t you?” “他的名字是泰迪？”乌鸦国王说道。 “真迷人。 我抓到了一只泰迪。 我觉得他一定会成为一只很棒的宠物，你不觉得吗？

Jack was horrified to see Teddy trapped in the Raven King’s cage. “It’s not charming,” he said. “It’s cruel. You better let him go, or else!” 杰克看到泰迪被困在乌鸦王的笼子里，感到非常震惊。 “这并不迷人，”他说。 “这是残忍的。 你最好放了他，否则的话！

“Yeah, let him go,” said Annie. “Or else.” “对，放了他，”安妮说。 “否则的话。

“Or else?” said the Raven King. “Or else what?” With backward jerks of his head, he laughed a raspy laugh. “否则的话？”乌鸦王说。 “否则会怎样？”他一边用头往后仰，一边发出刺耳的笑声。

As the king laughed, Jack glanced at the floor under the window. He saw the hazel twig. He moved toward it. 当国王大笑时，杰克朝窗户下方的地板看了一眼。 他看见了一根榛树枝。 他朝它走了过去。

The Raven King caught sight of him. His laugh stopped abruptly. “ CREE! CAW!” he croaked to one of his bodyguards. 乌鸦国王发现了他。 他的笑声突然停了下来。 “嘎！”他对着其中一个卫兵嘶哑地喊道。

Jack dashed for the twig. But before he could grab it, the king’s bodyguard had swooped across the floor and picked up the twig in his beak. As the raven carried it to the top of the window, Jack noticed that one of his tail feathers was bent. 杰克冲向那根树枝。 但就在他还没碰到它的时候，国王的卫兵已经飞过地板，用喙叼起了那根树枝。 当乌鸦把树枝带到窗户顶部时，杰克注意到他的一根尾羽是弯的。

“Jack, look, it’s Rok!” said Annie. She called up to the bird. “Rok! Rok!” “杰克，看，是罗克！”，安妮说道。 她朝那只鸟喊道。 “咯！咯！”，

From his perch above the window, the raven looked down at Annie. 从窗子上方的栖木上，渡鸦低头看着安妮。

“Rok, it’s me, Annie,” she said. “I helped you when the people in the village threw stones at you. Remember?” “咯，是我，安妮，”她说。 “当村里人向你扔石头时，我帮过你。还记得吗？”，

“What nonsense,” croaked the Raven King. “Bring me the stick, bird.” “胡说八道，”渡鸦国王嘶哑地说。 “把树枝给我，鸟。”

Rok didn’t move. Gripping the hazel twig in his beak, he stared down at Annie. 罗克没有动。 他用喙紧紧抓住榛树枝，低头盯着安妮。

“Give the twig to Jack, Rok,” she said. “So he can turn Teddy back into a boy.” “把树枝给杰克，罗克，”她说。 “这样他就能把泰迪变回男孩。”

“So that ugly little stick is a magic wand, is it?” said the Raven King. “Bring it to me, bird. Now!” “所以那根丑陋的小树枝是一根魔法棒，是吗？”乌鸦国王说道。 “把它带来给我，鸟。现在！”

“Don’t do it, Rok,” said Annie. “Don’t let him boss you around anymore.” “别这么做，洛克，”安妮说道。 “别再让他指挥你了。”

The raven stared at Annie for a moment with his dark brown eyes. He looked at the Raven King. He looked back at Annie. Then he swooped down to Jack and dropped the hazel twig at his feet. 乌鸦用他深棕色的眼睛盯着安妮看了片刻。 他看了看乌鸦国王。 他又看了看安妮。 然后他俯冲下来，把榛树枝丢在杰克的脚边。

Jack grabbed it. 杰克捡起了它。

“Traitor!” the Raven King shrieked at Rok. “You’ll pay for this!” He lunged toward the raven. Rok tried to escape, but the king grabbed him by the throat. “叛徒！”乌鸦国王对着洛克尖叫道。 “你得付出代价！”他朝乌鸦冲了过去。 罗克想逃跑，但国王抓住了他的喉咙。

Jack had to save Rok! He pointed the twig at the Raven King’s back and shouted: 杰克必须救罗克！ 他把树枝指向乌鸦国王的后背，大喊：

“O hazel twig from hazel tree! “来自榛树的榛树枝！

Make him what he wanted to be!” 让他变成他想成为的样子！”

A deafening wind roared through the room. A blinding light flashed. Then all was clear. 一阵震耳欲聋的风席卷了整个房间。 一道刺眼的光闪了一下。 然后一切变得清晰起来。

The Raven King had vanished. His cape lay on the floor. Rok hopped away, unharmed. 乌鸦国王消失了。 他的披风躺在地板上。 罗克蹦蹦跳跳地逃走了，没有受伤。

From under the feathered cape came a hoarse cry. Awk. 披风下面传来一声沙哑的叫声。‘啊呜。’

Annie lifted the cape and uncovered a tiny raven. “Ohh!” she said softly. 安妮拿起披风，发现下面藏着一只小乌鸦。 “哦～”她轻声说道。

The bird stretched out his scrawny neck. Awk, he croaked again. 那只鸟伸出了瘦瘦的脖子。 ‘啊呜，’他又叫了一声。

“Hello yourself,” Annie said, smiling. She stroked the downy feathers on the creature’s head. Then she looked up at Jack. “How did you come up with that rhyme?” “你自己也一样，”安妮微笑着说。 她抚摸着那只小动物头上的绒毛。 然后她抬头看向杰克。 “你是怎么想到那个押韵的句子的？”

“It just came to me,” said Jack. “这句诗突然就出现在我脑海里，”杰克说。

“I knew I had to save Rok. But I didn’t want to hurt the Raven King. I think I actually felt sorry for him.” “我知道我必须救出罗克。 但我不想伤害渡鸦王。 我想我真的有点同情他。”

“So you helped him finally get what he wanted,” said Annie. “You turned him into a baby raven.” “所以你最终帮助他得到了他想要的东西，”安妮说。 “你把他变成了一只小渡鸦。”

“Yeah,” Jack said. “Now he can live his whole life as a bird.” “是的，”杰克说。 “现在他可以整个一生都作为一只鸟生活。”

Rok flew up to the window ledge. He looked around at the other ravens. It was clear he had become their new leader. 罗克飞到了窗台上。 他环顾四周，看着其他的渡鸦。 很明显，他已经成为了它们的新首领。

“GRO! GRO!” Rok croaked. “GRO! GRO!” 罗克嘶哑地叫道。

He stepped aside. The raven troops began to leave the nursery, one by one. Two of them escorted the new member of their flock as he timidly flapped his small wings. 他让开了。 渡鸦们开始一个接一个地离开育雏室。 其中有两只护送着新加入的成员，他胆怯地扇动着小小的翅膀。

Rok was the last to leave. He stared at Annie and Jack with a long gaze. Then he lifted off the window ledge and flew away into the light of the silver dawn. 罗克是最后一个离开的。 他用长时间的目光盯着安妮和杰克。 然后他从窗台上飞起，飞向银色黎明的光芒中。
