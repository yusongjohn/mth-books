6. Merlin’s Diamond 6. 梅林的钻石

Jack and Teddy were speechless. They kept staring at Annie as she got down on her knees and talked to the invisible dog. 杰克和泰迪说不出话来。 他们一直盯着安妮，看她跪下来和看不见的狗说话。

“Hi, you,” she said in a soft voice. “Are you hungry?” “你好，”她用轻柔的声音说道。 “你饿吗？”

The bone dropped toward the floor. It flipped over, then rocked from side to side. 骨头掉向了地板。 它翻了个身，然后左右摇晃起来。

“See,” Annie said to Jack and Teddy. “Now he’s rolling on his back with his bone in his mouth. Poor thing.” “你看，”安妮对杰克和泰迪说。 “现在他倒着滚，嘴里还叼着骨头。真可怜。”

“Poor thing?” said Jack. “真可怜？”杰克说。

“We have to help him,” said Annie. She stood up. “We have to help them, too—the girl and her brothers.” “我们必须帮助他，”安妮说。她站了起来。 “我们也要帮助他们——那个女孩和她的兄弟们。”

She hurried across the room. Jack and Teddy followed. Annie stopped at the small spinning wheel. 她赶紧穿过房间。 杰克和泰迪跟了上去。 安妮停在了那台小纺车前。

“We can’t see you,” said Annie, “but we’re not afraid of you. We want to help you. Can you hear me?” “我们看不见你，”安妮说，“但我们不怕你。 我们想帮助你。你能听到我说话吗？

The spinning wheel stopped spinning. 纺车停止了转动。

“She can hear us!” Annie said to Jack and Teddy. Annie turned back to the ghost girl. “What happened to you and your brothers and your dog and everyone else in the castle? How did you all become invisible?” “她能听到我们！”安妮对杰克和泰迪说。 安妮转身面对那个女鬼。 “你们和你的兄弟、你的狗，还有城堡里的其他人到底怎么了？ 你们是怎么变得看不见的？”

Jack felt a wave of cold air whoosh past him. 杰克感到一阵冷风吹过他。

“I think she’s moving,” said Annie. “我觉得她在动，”安妮说。

“Aye,” said Teddy, “to the looking glass. See?” “对，”泰迪说，“她正往镜子里去。看见了吗？”

An invisible finger had begun to write something in the thick dust of the mirror. Four words slowly appeared: 一只看不见的手开始在镜子厚厚的灰尘上写些什么。 四个字慢慢地出现了：

图中文字：Diamond of Destiny stolen

Diamond of Destiny stolen 图中文字：命运之钻被偷 命运之钻被偷

“I can’t believe it!” said Teddy. “This must be the secret castle that guards the Diamond of Destiny!” “我简直不敢相信！”泰迪说。 “这一定是守护命运之钻的秘密城堡！”

“What’s that? ” said Jack. “那是什么？”杰克问。

“A magic diamond that belongs to Merlin,” said Teddy. “It was set in the handle of the very sword King Arthur pulled from the stone many years ago.” “属于梅林的一颗魔法钻石，”泰迪说。 “很久以前，亚瑟王从石头中拔出宝剑时，这颗钻石就被镶在剑柄上。”

“Oh, I know that story,” said Annie. “That’s how Arthur became king!” “哦，我知道这个故事，”安妮说。 “这就是亚瑟如何成为国王的！”

“Yes!” said Teddy. “And someday the Diamond of Destiny will give the same strength and power to the next rightful ruler of Camelot.” “对！”泰迪说。 “总有一天，命运之钻会给卡梅洛特的下一位合法统治者带来同样的力量和权力。”

“That’s what Merlin must have meant when he said the future of Camelot depends on us,” said Annie. “这就是梅林说未来取决于我们时的意思，”安妮说。

“Indeed,” said Teddy. “确实如此，”泰迪说。

“Wait, wait,” said Jack. “I’m confused. What does the Diamond of Destiny have to do with invisible kids and dogs?” “等一下，等一下，”杰克说。 “我有点困惑。 命运之钻和看不见的孩子和狗有什么关系？”

“After Arthur became king, Merlin gave the diamond to a noble family of Camelot,” said Teddy. “The name of the family was kept secret. As long as the family kept the diamond safe, they would have good fortune. But should they fail to protect it, they would fade from life.” “亚瑟成为国王后，梅林把钻石交给了卡梅洛特的一个贵族家庭，”泰迪说。 “这个家庭的名字一直被保密。 只要一家人把钻石保管好，他们就会有好运。 但如果他们没能保护好它，他们就会从生活中消失。

“Oh! So the family let the diamond get stolen,” said Annie. “And now they’ve all turned into ghosts!” 所以这家人让钻石被偷走了，”安妮说。 “现在他们全都变成了鬼魂！”，

“Precisely,” said Teddy. “没错，”泰迪说。

“I wonder where the diamond was kept,” said Jack. “我真想知道那颗钻石被藏在哪里了，”杰克说。

“Good question,” said Teddy. “Certainly some special hiding place, perhaps in one of the towers.” “好问题，”泰迪说。 “肯定有个特别的藏身之处，可能在其中一个塔里。”

“Guys, look!” said Annie. She pointed to the wall near the mirror. “大家看！”安妮说。 她指着镜子旁边的墙。

A long, heavy tapestry had been pulled aside to reveal a small door in the stone wall. The door was slowly swinging open. 一块又长又重的挂毯被拉到一边，露出石墙上的一个小门。 门缓缓地打开了。

“The ghost girl!” said Annie. “She’s showing us the diamond’s secret hiding place!” “幽灵女孩！”，安妮说道。 “她要带我们去看钻石的秘密藏身处！”，

The three of them hurried to the stone wall and looked inside a tiny cabinet. The walls of the cabinet were made of gold and ivory. But the cabinet was empty. 他们三个人赶紧跑到石墙前，往里面的小柜子里看。 柜子的墙壁是用金子和象牙做的。 但是柜子里什么也没有。

Annie looked around. “Ghost girl?” she said. “Who stole the Diamond of Destiny from its hiding place?” 安妮四处张望。 “幽灵女孩？”，她说。 “谁偷走了命运之钻的藏身处？”，

Letters began to appear on the mirror again. 字母又开始出现在镜子上了。

In the thick dust, the invisible finger wrote: 在厚厚的灰尘里，看不见的手指写下了：

图中文字：The Raven 图中文字：渡鸦

“Oh, no,” whispered Teddy. “Please, no.” Jack felt a fresh wave of fear. “Oh-no-please-no- what? ” he said. “哦，不，”泰迪小声说。 “求求你，别这样。”杰克感到一阵新的恐惧涌上心头。 “哦不，请别这样，什么？”他说。

“Wait,” said Teddy, pointing at the mirror. The finger wrote one more word in the dust: “等等，”泰迪指着镜子说。 手指在灰尘里又写了一个词：

图中文字：king 图中文字：国王

“Just as I feared,” said Teddy in a hushed voice. “The Raven King!” “正如我所害怕的，”泰迪压低声音说。 “乌鸦王！”
