7. One, Two, Three! 7. 一，二，三！

“So that’s why Merlin sent for those books!” said Teddy. “所以这就是为什么梅林要那些书的原因！”泰迪说。

“What books? Who’s the Raven King?” said Jack. “什么书？ 谁是渡鸦王？”杰克说。

“Now it all makes sense,” said Teddy. “现在一切都说得通了，”泰迪说。

“Who’s the Raven King?” said Jack. “谁是渡鸦王？”杰克说。

“But I wonder how he found the Diamond of Destiny,” said Teddy. “但我想知道他是怎么找到命运之钻的，”泰迪说。

“Teddy, who’s the Raven King?” Jack nearly shouted. “泰迪，谁是渡鸦王？”杰克几乎喊了出来。

“He’s a terrifying creature who comes from the Otherworld,” said Teddy. “I read all about him in one of the books I brought Merlin from Morgan’s library. As a boy, the Raven King longed to be a bird so he could fly. He stole a spell from the Wizard of Winter, but he didn’t have the magic to make it work properly. So the spell only worked halfway. It made him half bird and half human.” “他是一个来自异世界的可怕生物，”泰迪说。 “我在从摩根图书馆带给梅林的一本书里读到过关于他的所有事情。 小时候，渡鸦王渴望变成一只鸟，这样他就能飞了。 他从冬之巫师那里偷了一个咒语，但他没有足够的魔法让咒语正常生效。 所以这个咒语只生效了一半。 它让他变成了一半鸟一半人。

“Oh, man,” said Jack. “哦，天哪，”杰克说。

“Now he commands a huge army of ravens who treat him as their king,” said Teddy. “现在他指挥着一大群渡鸦，这些渡鸦把他当作他们的国王，”泰迪说。

“Why would he steal the Diamond of Destiny?” asked Annie. “他为什么要偷命运之钻呢？”安妮问。

“I don’t know,” said Teddy, “but we must get it back! For the sake of Camelot’s future!” “我不知道，”泰迪说，“但我们必须把它拿回来！ 为了卡梅洛特的未来！

“And for these ghost kids, too,” said Annie, “and the ghost dog!” “还有这些鬼孩子的安全，”安妮说，“还有那只鬼狗！

She looked around the room. “Don’t worry!” she called. “We’ll help all of you! We’ll get the Diamond of Destiny back!” 她环顾了一下房间。 “别担心！”她喊道。 “我们会帮你们所有人！ 我们会把命运之钻夺回来！”

“We will?” asked Jack. “How? We don’t know where this crazy raven man lives or anything.” “我们会吗？”杰克问。 “怎么弄？” 我们不知道这个疯狂的渡鸦男人住在哪里或者任何事情。”

“Look! More writing,” whispered Teddy. “She heard you.” “看！ 更多的文字，”泰迪小声说。 “她听见你了。”

Three more words slowly appeared in the mirror dust: 镜子里的灰尘中又慢慢出现了三个字：

图中文字：nest on mountaintop 图中文字：山顶上的巢

Jack felt cold air whoosh by him again. The curtain covering the window moved aside. A patch of moonlight shone on the floor. 杰克又感觉到一阵冷风吹过他身边。 遮住窗户的窗帘被吹到一边去了。 一束月光照在地板上。

Jack, Annie, and Teddy walked to the window and looked out. In the distance, a craggy mountain rose into the moonlit sky. 杰克、安妮和泰迪走到窗户前，朝外看去。 在远处，一座崎岖的山峰耸立在月光下的天空中。

“Ah!” whispered Teddy. “So there dwells the Raven King! I had thought his nest was in the Otherworld.” “啊！”泰迪小声说。 “所以，乌鸦王就住在这里！ 我还以为他的巢穴在另一个世界呢。”

“It might as well be,” said Jack. “We’ll never be able to get to the top of that mountain.” “那也差不多，”杰克说。 “我们永远都爬不到那座山的山顶。”

“Aye,” said Teddy, “no mere mortal can climb that steep rock.” “啊，”泰迪说，“没有凡人能爬过那块陡峭的岩石。”

“How will we get the diamond back?” cried Annie. “我们怎么把钻石拿回来？”安妮喊道。

“I said no mere mortal,” said Teddy. “I am more than a mortal, remember? I’m a sorcerer.” “我说的是没有凡人，”泰迪说。 “你忘了，我可不只是凡人，我是个巫师，”安妮说。

“Yeah, but your rhymes never work,” said Annie. “是啊，但你的押韵从来都不管用，”安妮说。

“True, but I have more than just rhymes,” said Teddy. He pulled a twig out of his pocket. “See?” “没错，但我不仅仅有押韵，”泰迪说。 他从口袋里拿出一根树枝。 “看到了吗？”

“What’s that?” said Jack. “那是什么？”杰克说。

“’Tis an enchanted hazel twig,” said Teddy. “Its magic is strong enough to change me into anything I want.” “那是被施了魔法的榛树枝，”泰迪说。 “它的魔法足够强大，可以把我变成我想变成的任何东西。”

“Oh, wow,” said Annie. “哦，哇，”安妮说。

“Did Morgan give you that?” asked Jack. “摩根给你的吗？”杰克问。

“No,” said Teddy. “Morgan and Merlin do not even know I have it. A forest sprite, a cousin of my mother’s, gave it to me, in case I was ever in urgent need.” “没有，”泰迪说。 “摩根和梅林甚至都不知道我有它。 我妈妈的一位森林精灵亲戚给了我，以防我有紧急需要。”

“So what do you want to change into?” asked Annie. “那你想要变成什么？”安妮问。

“Why, a raven, of course!” Teddy said. “当然是乌鸦！”泰迪说。

Teddy’s crazy, thought Jack. 泰迪疯了，杰克心想。

But Annie didn’t seem to think so. “What a cool idea,” she said. 但安妮似乎不这么认为。 “真是个酷主意，”她说。

They’re both crazy, thought Jack. 他们俩都疯了，杰克心想。

“’Tis cool indeed,” said Teddy. He held up the twig. “确实很酷，”泰迪说。 他举起了那根树枝。

“Wait a minute!” said Jack. “Do you have a plan? I mean, what’re you going to do once you change into a raven?” “等一下！”杰克说。 “你有计划吗？ 我是说，你变成渡鸦之后打算做什么？

“I shall fly up to the mountain nest,” said Teddy. “Find the diamond. Bring it back. Mission done.” “我要飞到山上的巢穴，”泰迪说。 “找到钻石，把它带回来。 任务完成。”

“And what do we do?” asked Annie. “那我们该怎么办呢？”安妮问。

“Wait here for me. I’ll be back as soon as I can,” said Teddy. He climbed up onto the window ledge. His moon shadow stretched across the floor. “你在这里等我。 我尽快回来，”泰迪说。 他爬上了窗台。 他的月亮影子铺满了地板。

“Good luck!” said Annie. “祝你好运！”安妮说。

“Thank you!” said Teddy. He raised his hazel twig. “谢谢！”泰迪说。 他举起了榛树枝。

“Stop!” said Jack. “Can we discuss your ‘plan’ a little more?” “停住！”杰克说。 “我们能不能再仔细讨论一下你的‘计划’？”

But Teddy was already sweeping his hazel twig through the air. He waved it from the top of his head to the tips of his toes. 但是泰迪已经用榛树枝在空中扫来扫去。 他从头顶一直扫到脚尖。

“Teddy, stop!” said Jack. “泰迪，停下！”杰克说。

But Teddy started his rhyme: 但泰迪开始念他的顺口溜：

“O hazel twig from hazel tree! “榛树枝来自榛树！

Make me a raven—” 让我变成一只乌鸦——”

“Quick,” he said, “a word that rhymes with tree?” “快点，”他说，“一个和tree押韵的词？”

“Wait!” said Jack. “等一下！”杰克说。

“Doesn’t rhyme with tree,” said Teddy. “不和tree押韵，”泰迪说。

“Three!” said Annie. “Three！”安妮说。

“Brilliant!” said Teddy. He started again: “太棒了！”泰迪说。他再次开始：

“O hazel twig from hazel tree! “一根榛树枝，来自榛树！

Make me a raven one, two, three!” 让我变成乌鸦，一，二，三！”，

He waved the twig wildly. 他疯狂地挥舞着树枝。

“Careful!” said Jack. He ducked and covered his head. “小心！”杰克说。 他蹲下身子，护住头部。

Suddenly he heard a roar. He felt a blast of heat. Then he heard a strange screech. 突然，他听到了一声咆哮。 他感到一阵热浪扑面而来。 然后，他听到了一声奇怪的尖叫声。

Jack looked up. Teddy’s hazel twig had fallen to the floor. Jack saw Teddy’s shadow on the floor, too. But it was no longer the shadow of a boy. 杰克抬头看了看。 泰迪的榛树枝掉到了地板上。 杰克也看到泰迪的影子在地板上。 但那已经不是男孩的影子了。

A chill went through Jack. 杰克感到一阵寒意。

A large raven was perched on the window-sill. Moonlight shone on its sleek blue-black wings, its shaggy throat feathers, its thick neck and big beak. 一只大乌鸦停在窗台上。 月光照在它光滑的蓝黑色翅膀上，照在它蓬松的喉咙羽毛上，照在它粗壮的脖子和大嘴巴上。

A second raven stood beneath the window. It looked like the first, only smaller. 另一只乌鸦站在窗子下面。 它看起来和第一只很像，只是更小一些。

Where’s Annie? Jack wondered wildly. He tried calling her name. But a terrible croak came from the back of his throat: “AWK-NEE!” 安妮去哪儿了？ 杰克疯狂地想着。 他试着喊她的名字。 但他的喉咙里发出一声可怕的呱叫：“AWK-NEE！”

Jack felt as if he were caught in a terrible nightmare. With jerky turns of his head, he peered down at his own body. 杰克感觉好像掉进了一个可怕的噩梦里。 他扭动着头，低头看着自己的身体。

His arms had turned into jet-black wings. His legs were spindly twigs that ended in four long, skinny toes with curved claws. 他的手臂变成了漆黑的翅膀。 他的腿细得像树枝，末端有四个又长又细的脚趾，脚趾上还有弯弯的爪子。

Teddy had accidentally changed them all into ravens. One, two, three. 泰迪不小心把它们全都变成了渡鸦。一，二，三。
