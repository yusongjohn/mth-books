10. Where Is It? 10. 它在哪里？

Jack and Annie perched on the ledge and peered into the nursery. Teddy’s lantern and hazel twig were still on the floor. But there was no sign of Teddy. 杰克和安妮坐在窗台上，朝育婴室里张望。 泰迪的提灯和榛树枝还在地板上。 但泰迪却不见了。

Teddy’s not here yet, croaked Annie. Let’s go ahead and put the diamond back in its place. 泰迪还没来呢，安妮咕哝道。 我们先把钻石放回原位吧。

Jack didn’t move. He didn’t want to give up the diamond quite yet. It still made him feel incredibly brave. 杰克没有动。 他还不想那么快放弃这块钻石。 它仍然让他感到非常勇敢。

Jack? croaked Annie. Let’s put it back in its hiding place. I’ll move the tapestry. 杰克？安妮咕哝道。 我们把它放回藏起来的地方吧。 我去移动那幅挂毯。

Annie flapped to the long tapestry hanging on the wall. Fluttering in the air, she took its edge in her beak. She tried to pull it aside, but it was too heavy. She let go. 安妮飞到了墙上挂着的那幅长长的挂毯前。 她在空中扑腾着，用喙叼住了挂毯的边缘。 她试着把它拉开，但太重了。她只好放手了。

I can’t move it, she croaked, not as long as I’m a raven. I guess we’ll have to wait for Teddy to change us back into ourselves. 我动不了它，她咕哝道，只要我还是一只渡鸦。 我想我们必须等泰迪把我们变回原来的样子了。

She flapped to the window ledge and landed beside Jack. Jack was relieved. The longer he could hold on to the diamond, the better. 她扑扇着翅膀飞到窗台上，落在杰克旁边。 杰克松了口气。 他能握住这颗钻石的时间越长越好。

Hey! croaked Annie. Maybe we could use Teddy’s magic hazel twig ourselves! I can come up with better rhymes than him anyway. It won’t hurt to try. 嘿！安妮咕哝道。 也许我们自己也可以用泰迪的魔法榛树枝！ 反正我编的押韵诗比他好。 试一下又不会受伤。

Jack shook his head. But Annie didn’t notice. She hopped down to the hazel twig under the window. She carefully picked it up with her beak. 杰克摇了摇头。 但安妮没有注意到。 她跳到窗户下的榛树枝下。 她小心地用喙捡起那根树枝。

She fluttered back up to the ledge beside Jack. Then she moved her head from side to side, passing the twig over Jack’s feathered head, his body, his wings, and his claws. The twig passed over her feathery body and wings as well. 她扑扇着翅膀飞回杰克旁边的窗台上。 然后她左右摇晃着头，把树枝依次从杰克的羽毛头部、身体、翅膀和爪子上拂过。 树枝也从她自己的羽毛身体和翅膀上拂过。

With the twig still in her beak, she made a deep croaking sound. 她仍然用喙叼着树枝，发出一声深沉的咯咯声。

“HA-HA-REE-REE!

JAH-JAH-AWK-NEE!”

O hazel twig from hazel tree! 榛树上的榛树枝啊！

Make him Jack, and make me, me! 让他变成杰克，让我变成我！

There was a mighty roar and a flash of light and a blast of heat! 突然传来一声巨响，一道强光，还有一阵热浪！

Then Jack heard Annie giggle. “Yippee! I made the magic work for us. Look.” 然后，杰克听到安妮咯咯地笑起来。 “太棒了！ 我让魔法为我们效力了。看。”

Jack looked down at his arms and legs and feet. “Ohh, man,” he breathed. 杰克低头看了看自己的手臂、腿和脚。 “哦，天哪，”他轻声说。

Awk-nee and Jah were gone. Annie and Jack were back. 奥克尼和杰克不见了。 安妮和杰克回来了。

Jack wiggled his fingers and toes. He felt his face: his mouth, his nose, his ears. He loved having his own body back! 杰克动了动手指和脚趾。 他摸了摸自己的脸：嘴巴、鼻子、耳朵。 他太高兴了，又能拥有自己的身体了！

“Teddy’s going to be so surprised,” said Annie. “泰迪会非常惊讶的，”安妮说。

“He acts like he’s the only kid who can do magic.” She looked around the nursery. “Hi! We’re back!” she called to the invisible children. “Guess what? We’ve got the diamond!” “他表现得好像只有他一个人能使用魔法。”她环顾了一下育婴室。 “你好！ 我们回来啦！“她对那些看不见的孩子喊道。 “猜猜看？ 我们拿到钻石了！“

“The diamond! Where is it?” said Jack. “I must’ve dropped it when you changed us!” “钻石！ 它在哪里？”杰克说。 “我一定是你们变我们的时候把它掉下来了！”

Suddenly they heard a swoosh and a flapping at the window. 突然，他们听到窗户外面传来嗖的一声和扑棱棱的声音。

“Teddy!” cried Annie. She and Jack whirled around. “泰迪！”安妮喊道。 她和杰克猛地转过身。

But Teddy wasn’t there. 但泰迪不在那儿。

Instead, perched on the ledge of the nursery window was a horrifying creature. He was part human and part raven. He had silky feathers for hair, a beak for a nose, sharp claws, and a billowing feathered cape that glistened in the moonlight like shiny black armor. 相反，一只可怕的生物正停在育婴室窗户的窗台上。 他一半是人，一半是渡鸦。 他有 silky 的羽毛当头发，喙当鼻子，锋利的爪子，还有一件在月光下闪闪发光，像黑色盔甲一样的羽毛斗篷。

“Good evening,” said the Raven King. “晚上好，”渡鸦国王说道。
