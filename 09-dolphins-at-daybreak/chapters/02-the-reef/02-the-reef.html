2. The Reef 2. 珊瑚礁

A breeze blew through the window. Sea gulls cried. Waves lapped the shore. 一阵微风吹进窗户。海鸥在叫。 海浪轻拍着岸边。

Annie picked up the riddle scroll. She unrolled it. Together she and Jack read the riddle: 安妮拿起谜语卷轴。她把它展开。 她和杰克一起读着谜语：

Rough and gray as rock,

I’m plain as plain can be.

But hidden deep inside

There’s great beauty in me.

What am I? 又粗糙又灰暗，像石头一样，我平平无奇。但在我内心深处，藏着巨大的美丽。我是什么？

“Let’s go find the answer,” said Annie. “我们去找答案吧，”安妮说。

She and Jack looked out the window. The tree house wasn’t in a tree. It was on the ground. 她和杰克朝窗外望去。 树屋不在树上。 它在地面上。

“Why is the ground pink?” said Jack. “地面为什么是粉红色的？”杰克说。

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “But I’m going out there.” “我不知道，”安妮说。 “可我要出去看看。”

“I’m going to do a little research first,” said Jack. “我先做点研究，”杰克说。

Annie climbed out of the tree house. 安妮爬出了树屋。

Jack picked up the ocean book and flipped through it. 杰克拿起海洋书，翻了起来。

He found a picture of a pink island surrounded by water. He read: 他找到一幅被水环绕的粉色岛屿的图。他读道：

This is a coral reef. Corals are tiny sea animals. After they die, their skeletons remain. Over time, the reef builds up from stacks of coral skeletons. 这是一个珊瑚礁。珊瑚是微小的海洋动物。它们死后，骨骼会留下来。随着时间的推移，珊瑚礁由一层层珊瑚骨骼堆积而成。

“Oh, man, tiny skeletons,” said Jack. He pulled out his notebook and wrote: “天哪，小小的骨骼，”杰克说。 他拿出笔记本写道：

图中文字：Millions of coral skeletons 图中文字：数以百万计的珊瑚骨骼

“Jack! Jack! Come look at this,” cried Annie. “杰克！杰克！ 快来看这个，”安妮喊道。

“What is it?” “那是什么？”

“I don’t know. But you’ll love it!” she said. “我不知道。 可你会喜欢的！”她说。

Jack threw his notebook and the ocean book into his pack. He climbed out the window. 杰克把笔记本和海洋书扔进背包。 他爬出窗户。

“Is it the answer to the riddle?” he called. “它是谜语的答案吗？”他喊道。

“I don’t think so. It doesn’t look very plain,” said Annie. “我可不这么想。 “看起来不太普通，”安妮说。

She was standing at the edge of the water. Beside her was a strange-looking machine. 她正站在水边。 她身边有一台样子奇怪的机器。

Jack hurried over the bumpy coral to get a better look. 杰克急忙越过高低不平的珊瑚，想看得更清楚。

The machine was half on the reef and half in the clear blue water. It looked like a huge white bubble with a big window.

“Is it a special kind of boat?” asked Annie. “这是一种特殊的船吗？”安妮问。

Jack found a picture of the machine in the ocean book. He read: 杰克在海洋书里找到了这台机器的图。他读道：

Scientists who study the ocean are called oceanographers. Sometimes they travel in small diving vessels called submersibles, or “mini-subs,” to study the ocean floor. 研究海洋的科学家叫做海洋学家。有时他们会乘坐一种叫做潜水器或者“迷你潜水器”的小船，去研究海底。

“It’s a mini-sub,” said Jack. He pulled out his notebook. “这是一艘迷你潜艇，”杰克说。 他拿出笔记本。

“Let’s get inside it,” said Annie. “我们进去看看吧，”安妮说。

“No!” said Jack. Actually, he did want to see what the sub looked like inside. But he shook his head. “We can’t. It’s not ours.” “不！”杰克说。 其实，他也想看看潜艇里面是什么样。 可他摇了摇头。 “我们不能。这不是我们的。”

“Just a teeny peek,” said Annie. “It might help us figure out the riddle.” “就看一小眼，”安妮说。 “也许能帮我们解开谜语。”

Jack sighed. “Okay. But we have to be careful. Don’t touch anything,” he said. 杰克叹了口气。 “好吧。 但我们得小心。 什么都别碰，”他说。

“Don’t worry,” said Annie. “别担心，”安妮说。

“And take off your shoes so they won’t get wet,” said Jack. “而且脱掉鞋子，免得弄湿，”杰克说。

He and Annie slipped off their shoes and socks and threw them toward the tree house. 他和安妮脱掉鞋子和袜子，把它们扔向树屋。

Then they stepped carefully over the sharp coral. 然后他们小心翼翼地踩过锋利的珊瑚。

Annie turned the handle on the hatch of the mini-sub. It opened. 安妮转动迷你潜艇舱口上的把手。舱口打开了。

She and Jack climbed inside. The hatch slammed shut. 她和杰克爬了进去。 舱口砰地关上了。

The mini-sub was tiny. Two seats faced the big window. In front of the seats was a computer built into a control panel. 迷你潜艇很小。 两个座位面向大窗户。 座位前面有一台嵌在控制台里的电脑。

Annie sat down. 安妮坐了下来。

Jack opened the ocean book and read more on the mini-sub page: 杰克打开海洋书，继续读迷你潜艇那一页：

Mini-subs have strong hulls to keep air in and protect those aboard from water pressure. Computers are used to guide the mini-sub through the ocean. 小型潜水器有坚固的外壳，用来保持空气在内，并保护里面的人不受水压的影响。电脑用来引导小型潜水器在海洋中前进。

“Oops,” said Annie. “哎呀，”安妮说。

“What’s wrong?” Jack looked up. “怎么了？”杰克抬起头。

Annie was waving her hands in front of the computer. Now the screen showed a map. 安妮正对着电脑挥手。 这时屏幕上显示出一张地图。

“What’s going on?” said Jack. “怎么回事？”杰克说。

“I just pressed a few keys—” said Annie. “我只按了几个键——”安妮说。

“What? I said not to touch anything!” said Jack. “什么样的帮助？”杰克说。 “我说了什么都别碰！”杰克说。

An air blower came on. The mini-sub jerked backward. 一台鼓风机启动了。 迷你潜艇猛地向后一冲。

“Get out!” said Jack. “出去！”杰克说。

He and Annie scrambled for the hatch. Jack grabbed the handle. 他和安妮慌忙爬向舱口。 杰克抓住把手。

But they were too late. 可已经太晚了。

The mini-sub slid off the reef. 迷你潜艇滑离了珊瑚礁。

Then it dove silently down into the deep. 然后它静静地潜进深海。
