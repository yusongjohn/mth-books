6. C-R-A-C-K 6. 咔——啦——咔

The octopus hugged the mini-sub. Each of its eight arms had two rows of suckers. The suckers stuck to the window. 章鱼抱住了迷你潜艇。 它的八条手臂每条都有两排吸盘。 吸盘粘在了窗户上。

The mini-sub stopped. 迷你潜艇停了下来。

The octopus stared at Jack and Annie with huge, human-like eyes. 章鱼用巨大的、像人一样的眼睛盯着杰克和安妮。

“I don’t think it wants to hurt us,” whispered Annie. “It’s just curious.” “我觉得它不想伤害我们，”安妮低声说。 “它只是好奇而已。”

“I—I’m going to research it,” said Jack. “我——我要查查它，”杰克说。

His hands shook as he flipped through the pages of the ocean book. 他翻着海洋书的书页，手在发抖。

He found a picture of an octopus and read aloud: 他找到一幅章鱼的图，大声读道：

The octopus tends to be a gentle, shy creature. Sometimes, though, curiosity gets the best of it and it comes out of hiding. 章鱼通常是一种温和、害羞的动物。不过，有时候好奇心会占上风，它就会从藏身的地方出来。

“Aw. See, I told you, he’s shy,” said Annie. She yelled to the octopus, “Hi! I’m Annie! He’s Jack!” “噢。 看，我跟你说过，它害羞，”安妮说。 她对章鱼喊道：“嗨！我是安妮！ 他是杰克！”

“Oh, brother,” moaned Jack. He read further: “唉，”杰克呻吟道。他继续读：

But the octopus has huge strength. Each of its arms, or tentacles, has many suckers, which act like rubber suction cups. It is nearly impossible to free an object from their grasp. 但是章鱼力气很大。它的每条胳膊，或者说触手，上面都有很多吸盘，就像橡皮做的吸盘一样。几乎不可能从它的抓握中把东西挣脱出来。

“Oh, great,” said Jack. “We’ll never get rid of this thing.” “哦，太好了，”杰克说。 “我们永远也摆脱不了这家伙了。”

Just then, Jack felt a drop hit his arm. Water. He looked up at the ceiling. 就在这时，杰克感到一滴水打在他胳膊上。是水。

“Uh-oh,” said Annie. “糟糕，”安妮说。

A thin crack ran along the ceiling. Smaller cracks branched out from it. 一道细细的裂缝沿着天花板延伸。 更小的裂缝从那条缝上分了出去。

Water dripped from the cracks. 水从裂缝里滴下来。

“We found the cracks,” said Annie. “我们找到裂缝了，”安妮说。

“The octopus better let go! Before the whole ceiling breaks!” said Jack. “章鱼最好放开！ 趁整个天花板还没裂开！”杰克说。

“Let go, please. Please!” Annie shouted at the octopus. “放开吧，求求你。 求求你了！”安妮对着章鱼喊。

The creature blinked, as if trying to understand her. 那生物眨了眨眼，好像在试着理解她。

“Please! Please! Please!” she shouted.

“Come on, Annie,” said Jack. “It doesn’t care if you’re polite.”

The octopus blinked at Jack. 章鱼对杰克眨了眨眼。

“Get out of here!” Jack yelled at it. “Now! Beat it! Scram! Go!” “走开！”杰克对它大喊。 “现在！走开！ 滚开！走！”

The octopus shot a cloud of black liquid into the water and disappeared into the dark cloud. Its long tentacles trailed through the water. 章鱼向水中喷出一团黑色液体，消失在那团黑云里。 它长长的触手拖过水中。

The mini-sub started to rise slowly again. 迷你潜艇又开始慢慢上升。

“You hurt his feelings,” Annie said. “你伤了它的感情，”安妮说。

“I don’t think so … ” Something bothered Jack. “我可不这么想……”有件事让杰克心里不安。

He looked back at the ocean book. He read to himself: 他又看了看海洋书。 他默默地读道：

The octopus squirts black ink to escape its enemies. One of its main enemies is the shark. 章鱼喷出黑色墨汁来躲避敌人。它的主要敌人之一是鲨鱼。

“Oh, no,” said Jack. “哦，不，”杰克说。

“What’s wrong?” asked Annie. “怎么了？”安妮问。

Jack looked out the window. The water was growing clear again. 杰克朝窗外望去。 海水又变得清澈起来。

A shadowy figure moved toward the mini-sub. 一个影子朝迷你潜艇游来。

“What is that?” whispered Annie. “那是什么？”安妮低声说。

The fish was way bigger than the dolphins. And it had a very weird head. 那条鱼比海豚大得多。 而且它的头非常怪异。

Jack could feel his heart nearly stop. 杰克觉得自己的心跳几乎停了。

“A hammerhead shark,” he breathed. “We’re really in trouble now.” “是一条锤头鲨，”他屏住呼吸说。 “我们现在真的有麻烦了。”
