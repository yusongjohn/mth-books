7. Remain Calm 7. 保持冷静

The shark swam behind the coral. 鲨鱼游到了珊瑚后面。

“Where did it go?” said Annie, peering out the window. “它去哪儿了？”安妮说，透过窗户张望。

“It doesn’t matter,” said Jack. “We have to get to the top.” “没关系，”杰克说。 “我们得浮到水面。”

“More water’s coming in,” said Annie. “更多水进来了，”安妮说。

“Yeah, I know. Come on … come on … faster!” Jack ordered the mini-sub. “是啊，我知道。 “快点……快点……再快一点！”杰克命令迷你潜艇。

“Even more water’s coming in,” said Annie, “lots more!” “水进得更多了，”安妮说，“多得多！”

Jack looked up. The water wasn’t dripping now—it was spurting. 杰克抬起头。 水现在不是在滴了——而是在往外喷。

“A few seconds, a few seconds,” said Jack. “几秒钟，几秒钟，”杰克说。

Suddenly, the mini-sub burst out of the water. It bobbed on the waves like a cork. The ocean sparkled all around it. 突然，迷你潜艇冲出了水面。 它像软木塞一样在浪上上下浮动。 大海在四周闪闪发光。

“Safe!” shouted Annie.

Jack felt the water rising around his bare feet. 杰克感到水漫到了他光着的脚上。

“Uh—not really … ” he said. “呃——不完全是……”他说。

“Oops,” said Annie. “The octopus must have made cracks in the bottom, too.” “哎呀，”安妮说。 “章鱼一定也把底部弄出裂缝了。”

The water was up to their ankles now. 水现在已经没到他们的脚踝了。

Jack looked out. He saw the reef in the distance. 杰克朝外看。 他看见远处的珊瑚礁。

“The sub can make it. It doesn’t look that far,” he said. “潜艇能到的。 看起来没那么远，”他说。

“Go, go, go,” said Annie. “走，走，走，”安妮说。

She pressed one of the steering keys. 她按下其中一个转向键。

Suddenly, the screen went blank. 突然，屏幕变成了空白。

“What’s happening?” said Jack. “怎么回事？”杰克说。

Annie pressed the key again. Then Jack pressed the other pictures. Nothing happened. 安妮又按了一次那个键。 然后杰克按了其他图画。什么都没发生。

“It’s dead,” said Annie. “它死机了，”安妮说。

“Oh, great,” said Jack. “哦，太好了，”杰克说。

Now the water was up to their knees. 现在水已经到了他们膝盖。

“I guess we’ll have to swim,” said Jack. He took a deep breath. “我想我们得游泳了，”杰克说。 他深深吸了一口气。

“Right,” said Annie. “It’s a good thing we had swimming lessons this summer.” “对，”安妮说。 “幸好我们今年夏天上过游泳课。”

“Right,” said Jack. “And it’s a bad thing we just saw a shark.” “对，”杰克说。 “不幸的是我们刚看见了一条鲨鱼。”

Jack quickly found the picture of the shark in the book. 杰克很快在书里找到了鲨鱼的图。

He read aloud: 他大声读道：

If you ever see a shark in the water, don’t splash. Swim calmly away. 如果你在水里看到鲨鱼，不要扑腾。要镇定地游开。

Jack closed the book. 杰克合上书。

“We better do the breast stroke,” said Annie. “So we won’t splash.” “我们最好游蛙泳，”安妮说。 “这样就不会拍水。”

“Yep, and stay close,” said Jack. “对，而且要靠近，”杰克说。

“Very close,” said Annie. Her eyes were wide. But she seemed very calm. “非常靠近，”安妮说。 她的眼睛睁得大大的。 可她看上去非常镇静。

Jack took a deep breath. He tried to be calm, too. He calmly took off his glasses. He calmly put them and the book into his pack. He calmly put his pack on his back. 杰克深深吸了一口气。 他也努力保持冷静。 他镇定地摘下眼镜。 他冷静地把它们和书放进背包。 他冷静地背上背包。

Annie opened the hatch. 安妮打开舱盖。

“Be calm,” Annie said. She slipped out of the mini-sub. “保持冷静，”安妮说。 她溜出了迷你潜艇。

“Help,” Jack said calmly. He held his nose. “救命，”杰克镇定地说。他捏住了鼻子。

Then he calmly lowered himself into the ocean. 然后他镇定地让自己沉入海里。
