5. Two Eyes 5. 两只眼睛

“What’s a ship’s log?” said Annie. “什么是航海日志？”安妮说。

“It’s a diary of an ocean trip,” said Jack. “是海上旅行的日记，”杰克说。

He peered at the computer screen and read a log entry: 他盯着电脑屏幕，读了一条航海日志：

MONDAY, JULY 5 星期一，7月5日

“Hey, that was just last week,” said Jack. He read further: “嘿，那就是上周，”杰克说。他继续读：

COLLECTED ROCK AND SHELL SAMPLES 收集了岩石和贝壳样本
MAPPED OCEAN FLOOR 绘制好的海底地图
FOUND TINY CRACK IN HULL 在船体上发现了一条小裂缝

“This is like your notebook,” said Annie. “这像你的笔记本，”安妮说。

“Yeah, the oceanographer was writing notes on the computer,” said Jack. “是啊，海洋学家在电脑上记笔记，”杰克说。

Jack and Annie read further: 杰克和安妮继续往下读：

TUESDAY, JULY 6 星期二，7月6日
CRACK HAS WIDENED 裂缝已经扩大了
MUST RETURN TO REEF SOON 必须尽快返回珊瑚礁

“A crack where?” said Annie. “裂缝在哪儿？”安妮说。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. He read further: “我不知道，”杰克说。他继续读：

WEDNESDAY, JULY 7 星期三，7月7日
MORE TINY CRACKS 更多的小裂缝
CANNOT BE FIXED 无法修复
HEADING BACK TO REEF TODAY 今天返回珊瑚礁

“Uh-oh. This doesn’t sound good,” said Jack. He read further: “哎呀。 这听起来不妙，”杰克说。他继续读：

THURSDAY, JULY 8 星期四，7月8日
DEFECTIVE SUB 有缺陷的子部件
RETURN TO REEF 返回珊瑚礁
LEAVE FOR HELICOPTER TO TRANSPORT 乘直升机离开
TO JUNKYARD 去废品场

“ ‘Defective’ means broken, right?” said Annie. “‘有缺陷’就是坏了的意思，对吧？”安妮说。

“Yep,” said Jack. “没错，”杰克说。

“So this sub is broken, right?” said Annie. “所以这艘潜艇坏了，对吧？”安妮说。

“Yep,” said Jack. “And it was waiting to be taken away by a helicopter. To a junkyard.” “没错，”杰克说。 “它正等着被直升机运走。运到废品场去。”

“Yikes,” said Annie. “哎呀，”安妮说。

“Now we really have to get back,” said Jack. “现在我们真的得回去了，”杰克说。

“Let’s try pressing the waves picture,” said Annie. “我们试试按波浪的图画，”安妮说。

She pressed the waves picture on the computer screen. 她按下电脑屏幕上波浪的图画。

The mini-sub began to rise slowly. 迷你潜艇开始慢慢上升。

“Oh, good,” said Jack. “哦，好，”杰克说。

The sub went past a small coral mountain. It went past schools of fish and waving plants. 潜艇经过一座小珊瑚山。 它驶过一群群鱼和摇摆的水草。

“Oh!” gasped Annie.

Jack gasped, too. 杰克也倒抽一口气。

Two eyes were staring out from behind a giant sea plant. They looked human—except they were as big as golf balls. 两只眼睛正从一株巨大的海生植物后面盯着他们。 它们看起来像人的眼睛——只不过有高尔夫球那么大。

The sub moved past the giant plant. Jack breathed a sigh of relief. 潜艇驶过那株巨大的植物。 杰克松了口气。

“What—? Whose—?” sputtered Annie. “谁的——？”安妮结结巴巴地说。

“Don’t ask,” said Jack. “别问，”杰克说。

They stared back at the plant. 他们盯着那株植物看。

Just then, a long arm came out from behind it. 就在这时，一条长长的手臂从后面伸了出来。

Then another arm came out. 然后又一条手臂伸了出来。

Then another—and another—and another—and another—and another—and another! 然后又一条——又一条——又一条——又一条——又一条——又一条！

Jack and Annie stared in horror at a giant octopus. 杰克和安妮惊恐地盯着一只巨大的章鱼。

“It’s coming after us,” said Annie. “它在追我们，”安妮说。

Slowly, the octopus crept through the water. Its eight arms reached for the mini-sub. 章鱼慢慢地在水中爬行。 它的八条手臂朝迷你潜艇伸来。
