4. Fish City 4. 鱼的城市

There were fish everywhere: floating over the swaying sea grass, eating on the white sandy bottom, peeping out of coral caves. 到处都是鱼：在摇曳的海草上漂着，在白色沙底上吃东西，从珊瑚洞穴里往外偷看。

Some kinds of coral looked like blue fingers or lacy fans. Others looked like deer antlers or lettuce leaves or mushrooms or trees. 有些珊瑚看起来像蓝色的手指，或带花边的扇子。 还有的看起来像鹿角、生菜叶、蘑菇或树木。

Jack read in the book: 杰克在书里读道：

Coral reefs are only found in warm, tropical waters. Nearly 5,000 different species of fish live around coral reefs in the Indian and Pacific Oceans. 珊瑚礁只生长在温暖的热带水域。印度洋和太平洋的珊瑚礁周围生活着近5000种不同的鱼类。

Jack pulled out his pencil and notebook. 杰克拿出铅笔和笔记本。

He started to write a list. 他开始列清单。

图中文字：Coral Reef Research

warm water over 5,000 kinds of fish 图中文字：珊瑚礁研究温暖水域有超过5000种鱼

“Look!” said Annie. “看！”安妮说。

The sub floated past a huge starfish. Then a pink jellyfish. Then a blue sea horse. 潜艇漂过一只巨大的海星。 接着是一只粉红色的水母。 接着是一匹蓝色的海马。

Jack added to his list: 杰克在清单上又加了几条：

图中文字：jellyfish sea horses 图中文字：水母和海马

“What is that? ” said Annie. “那是什么？”安妮说。

Jack saw a creature that looked like a giant pancake with a long tail. 杰克看见一个生物，看起来像一块带着长尾巴的巨大薄饼。

“A stingray!” said Jack. He put that on his list as well. “是一条黄貂鱼！”杰克说。 他也把那个加进了清单。

“And that?” said Annie.

She pointed at the biggest shell Jack had ever seen. It was as big as a footstool. 她指着杰克见过的最大的贝壳。 它有脚凳那么大。

“I’ll have to check on that one,” said Jack. He turned the pages of the ocean book. When he got to the page about clams, he read aloud: “那个我得查一查，”杰克说。 他翻着海洋书的书页。 翻到关于蛤蜊的那一页时，他大声读了起来：

The giant clam of the coral reef is three feet wide and weighs up to 200 pounds. 珊瑚礁上的巨型蛤蜊有三英尺宽，重达200磅。

“Wow,” said Annie. “哇，”安妮说。

“No kidding,” said Jack. He added “giant clam” to his list. “可不是开玩笑，”杰克说。 他把“巨蛤”加进清单。

“Dolphins!” cried Annie. “海豚！”安妮叫道。

Jack looked up. Two dolphins were peering in the window. They tapped their noses against the glass. 杰克抬起头。 两只海豚正透过窗户往里看。 它们用鼻子轻轻敲着玻璃。

Their eyes were bright. They seemed to be smiling. 它们的眼睛亮亮的。 它们好像在微笑。

Jack laughed. “It’s like we’ re in a fish tank—and they’re looking at us, ” he said. 杰克笑了。 “就好像我们在鱼缸里——而它们在看我们，”他说。

“Their names are Sukie and Sam,” said Annie. “Sister and brother.” “它们叫苏琪和山姆，”安妮说。 “姐姐和弟弟。”

“You’re nuts,” said Jack. “你疯了，”杰克说。

“Here’s a kiss for you, Sukie,” Annie said. She pressed her lips to the glass as if she were kissing the dolphin’s nose. “这是给你的一个吻，苏琪，”安妮说。 她把嘴唇贴在玻璃上，好像在亲海豚的鼻子。

“Oh, brother,” said Jack. “唉，”杰克说。

But the dolphin opened her mouth and tossed her head. She seemed to be laughing. 可那只海豚张开嘴，甩了甩头。 她好像在笑。

“Hey, I know the answer to the riddle—dolphins!” said Annie. “They’re gray and plain. But they have great beauty inside.” “嘿，我知道谜语的答案了——海豚！”安妮说。 “它们是灰色的，外表普通。 可它们内心有巨大的美。”

“You forgot the ‘rough as a rock’ part,” said Jack. “Dolphin skin looks smooth and slippery.” “你忘了‘像石头一样粗糙’那一部分，”杰克说。 “海豚的皮肤看起来又滑又光。”

“Oh, right,” said Annie. “哦，对，”安妮说。

The dolphins flipped their tails. They swam off into the light blue water. 海豚们甩了甩尾巴。 它们游进了浅蓝色的海水里。

“Wait! Don’t go!” called Annie. “Sukie!” “等——等等。我来了！”杰克说。 别走！”安妮喊道。 “苏琪！”

But the dolphins were gone. 可海豚们走了。

“It’s time for us to go, too,” said Jack. He was afraid someone might be looking for the mini-sub. “我们也该走了，”杰克说。 他担心可能有人在找这艘迷你潜艇。

“But we haven’t solved the riddle,” said Annie. “可我们还没解开谜语，”安妮说。

Jack studied the bright underwater world. 杰克仔细观察着明亮的水下世界。

“I don’t see the answer,” he said. “There’s nothing plain at all out there.” “我看不见答案，”他说。 “外面根本没有什么普通的东西。”

“Then maybe the answer’s in the mini-sub,” said Annie. “那么也许答案就在迷你潜艇里，”安妮说。

They looked around the tiny space. 他们环顾这个小小的空间。

“I’ll check the computer,” said Jack. He studied the row of pictures at the top of the screen. “我查查电脑，”杰克说。 他研究着屏幕上方那一排图画。

He pressed the book picture. 他按下书的图画。

The words SHIP’S LOG flashed onto the screen. 屏幕上闪现出“航海日志”几个字。
