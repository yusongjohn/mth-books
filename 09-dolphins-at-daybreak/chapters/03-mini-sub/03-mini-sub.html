3. Mini-Sub 3. 迷你潜艇

“You’ve really done it now, Annie!” said Jack. “安妮，这下你可闯祸了！”杰克说。

“Sorry, sorry. But look out the window!” Annie said. “Look!” “对不起，对不起。 可看看窗外！”安妮说。 “看！”

“Forget it! We have to figure this out!” Jack stared at the computer. He saw a row of pictures at the top of the screen. “别管那个！ 我们得把这个搞清楚！”杰克盯着电脑。 他看见屏幕上方有一排图片。

“What did you do?” he asked. “你做了什么？”他问。

“I just pressed the ON button,” said Annie. “我只按了开关键，”安妮说。

“The screen lit up. And I pressed the starfish.” “屏幕亮了。 然后我按了海星。”

“That must be the command to go under the water,” said Jack. “那一定是潜入水下的指令，”杰克说。

“Yeah. Then the map came on,” said Annie. “是啊。 “然后地图亮了，”安妮说。

“Okay, okay. The map shows the reef,” said Jack. “Look! There’s the mini-sub on the map! It’s moving away from the reef.” “好吧，好吧。 地图显示的是珊瑚礁，”杰克说。 “看！ 地图上有迷你潜艇！ 它正离开珊瑚礁。”

“It’s like a video game,” said Annie. “I bet I know what to do.”

Annie pressed a key with an arrow pointing right. The mini-sub on the screen moved right. The real mini-sub turned to the right, also. 安妮按了一个箭头朝右的键。 屏幕上的迷你潜艇向右移动。 真正的迷你潜艇也向右转了。

“Great!” said Jack with relief. “You press the arrows to steer the mini-sub. So now we can go back.” “太好了！”杰克松了口气说。 “你按箭头就能驾驶迷你潜艇。 所以现在我们可以回去了。”

“Oh, no, not right away,” said Annie. “It’s so beautiful down here.” “哦，不，别马上回去，”安妮说。 “下面太美了。”

“We have to get back to the reef,” said Jack. His eyes were still glued to the computer screen. “What if the owners find it gone?” “我们得回到珊瑚礁，”杰克说。 他的眼睛仍然盯着电脑屏幕不放。 “要是主人发现它不见了怎么办？”

“Look out the window,” said Annie. “Just for one teeny second.” “看看窗外，”安妮说。 “就看那么小小的一秒。”

Jack sighed. He pushed his glasses into place and looked up. “Oh, man,” he said softly. 杰克叹了口气。 他把眼镜往上推了推，抬起头。 “哦，天哪，”他轻声说。

Outside the glass was a strange world of bright moving color. 玻璃外面是一个色彩鲜艳、不断移动的奇异世界。

It looked like another planet. 它看起来像另一个星球。

The mini-sub was moving past red, yellow, and blue coral—past little coral mountains, valleys, and caves—past fishes of every color and size. 迷你潜艇正驶过红色、黄色和蓝色的珊瑚——驶过小小的珊瑚山、山谷和洞穴——驶过各种颜色和大小的鱼。

“Can’t we stay a little while? The answer to Morgan’s riddle must be here,” said Annie. “我们就不能多待一会儿吗？ 摩根谜语的答案一定在这儿，”安妮说。

Jack nodded slowly. She might be right, he thought. Besides, when would they ever get to visit a place like this again? 杰克慢慢点了点头。 她也许是对的，他想。 再说，他们什么时候还能再来这样的地方呢？
