10. The True Pearl 10. 真正的珍珠

Dawn light slanted into the tree house. 黎明的光线斜斜照进树屋。

No time at all had passed since they’d left. Day was breaking. 从他们离开到现在，时间一点也没过去。天正破晓。

Jack rolled up the ancient scroll. He tucked it into the corner. 杰克卷起那卷古老的卷轴。 他把它塞到角落里。

“We solved the first riddle,” he said. “Three more to go.” “我们解开了第一个谜语，”他说。 “还剩三个。”

“I don’t see another scroll,” said Annie. “Maybe tomorrow we’ll get the next riddle.” “我没看见另一卷卷轴，”安妮说。 “也许明天我们会得到下一个谜语。”

“That’s okay,” said Jack. “I think I need to rest—and dry out.” “没关系，”杰克说。 “我想我需要休息——还得把衣服弄干。”

His T-shirt and shorts were still soaked. His backpack, too. Only his shoes and socks were dry. 他的 T 恤和短裤还是湿的。 他的背包也是。 只有他的鞋子和袜子是干的。

“And this needs to dry out, too,” said Annie. She put the wet ocean book in a patch of sunlight. “这个也需要晾干，”安妮说。 她把湿湿的海洋书放在一小片阳光下。

Then Jack and Annie climbed down the ladder. 然后杰克和安妮爬下梯子。

They walked through the woods, through leafy shadows and golden light. 他们穿过树林，穿过树叶的阴影和金色的光。

They left the woods and started down their street. 他们离开树林，开始沿着街道走。

“You know, we should have found the answer to the riddle right away,” said Jack. “The oyster was on the reef all along.” “你知道，我们本来一开始就该找到谜语的答案，”杰克说。 “那只牡蛎一直都在珊瑚礁上。”

“I know, but we wouldn’t have had so much fun,” said Annie. “我知道，可那样我们就没那么好玩了，”安妮说。

“Fun?” said Jack. “You call being squeezed by an octopus and chased by a shark fun?” “好玩？”杰克说。 “你把被章鱼缠住、被鲨鱼追赶叫做好玩？”

“Don’t forget the dolphins,” Annie said simply. “别忘了海豚，”安妮只是这样说。

Jack smiled. “Right,” he said. The dolphins made up for everything. They were fun. 杰克笑了。 “对，”他说。 海豚弥补了一切。它们真有趣。

“I guess they were the true pearl in the oyster,” said Annie. “我想它们才是牡蛎里真正的珍珠，”安妮说。

“Yep … ” said Jack. “I wonder what Sam is doing right now.” “嗯……”杰克说。 “真不知道山姆现在在干什么。”

“Sam?” Annie grinned at him. “You’re nuts,” she said. “山姆？”安妮朝他咧嘴一笑。 “你疯了，”她说。

They climbed their steps and went into their house. 他们爬上台阶，走进屋子。

“We’re back!” Annie shouted. “我们回来了！”安妮喊道。

“Did you get your shoes wet?” their mom called. “你们把鞋子弄湿了吗？”妈妈喊道。

“Not one bit,” called Jack. Then he and Annie slipped up the stairs to change their clothes. “一点也没有，”杰克喊道。 然后他和安妮悄悄上楼去换衣服。
