1. Master Librarians 1. 图书大师

Jack stared out the kitchen window. 杰克盯着厨房窗外。

The sun was not up yet. But the sky was growing lighter. 太阳还没升起。 但天空正渐渐亮起来。

Jack had been awake for a long time. He had been thinking about the dream he’d had—the dream about Morgan le Fay. 杰克已经醒了很长时间。 他一直在想自己做过的那个梦——关于摩根·勒菲的梦。

The tree house is back, Morgan had said. I’m waiting. 树屋回来了，摩根说过。我在等你们。

Jack wished that dreams were real. He missed Morgan’s magic tree house. 杰克真希望梦是真的。 他想念摩根的魔法树屋。

“Jack!” His little sister Annie appeared in the doorway. “We have to go to the woods now!” she said. “杰克！”他的小妹妹安妮出现在门口。 “我们现在就得去树林！”她说。

“Why?” Jack asked. “为什么？”杰克问。

“I had a dream about Morgan!” exclaimed Annie. “She said the tree house is back and she’s waiting for us!” “我梦见摩根了！”安妮喊道。 “她说树屋回来了，她在等我们！”

“That was my dream,” said Jack. “那是我的梦，”杰克说。

“Oh, wow,” said Annie. “She told you, too? So it must be important.” “哇，”安妮说。 “她也告诉你了？ 那这一定很重要。”

“But dreams aren’t real,” said Jack. “可梦不是真的，”杰克说。

“Some dreams aren’t. But this one is,” said Annie. “I can just feel it.” She opened the back door. “ I’ll see you later!” “有些梦不是。 可这个是，”安妮说。 “我就是感觉到了。”她打开后门。

“Wait—wait. I’m coming!” said Jack.

He raced up the stairs. Having the same dream must mean something, he thought.

He grabbed his backpack and threw his notebook and pencil into it. 他抓起背包，把笔记本和铅笔扔进去。

Then he ran downstairs. 然后他跑下楼。

“We’ll be back soon, Mom!” Jack called into the living room. “我们很快回来，妈妈！”杰克朝客厅喊道。

“Where you going so early?” his dad called. “这么早你去哪？”他爸爸喊道。

“Just for a quick walk!” said Jack. “就出去走一会儿！”杰克说。

“It rained last night,” called his mom. “Don’t get your shoes wet.” “昨晚下雨了，”妈妈喊道。 “别把鞋子弄湿了。”

“We won’t!” “我们不会的！”

Jack slipped out the door. Annie was waiting for him. 杰克溜出门外。 安妮在等他。

“Let’s go!” she said. “走吧！”她说。

The sky was pale gray. The air felt freshly washed. 天空是浅灰色的。 空气像刚被清洗过一样清新。

Jack and Annie ran up their quiet street to the Frog Creek woods. 杰克和安妮跑上安静的街道，朝蛙溪树林奔去。

They headed between the trees. Soon they came to the tallest oak in the woods. There was a wooden house high in the treetop. 他们钻进树丛之间。 很快他们来到树林里最高的那棵橡树旁。 树顶上有一座木头房子。

“It is back!” whispered Jack. “真的回来了！”杰克低声说。

Someone looked out the window of the tree house—a lovely old woman with long white hair. Morgan le Fay. 有人从树屋窗口望出来——一位美丽的老妇人，留着长长的白发。摩根·勒菲。

“Come up,” called the magical librarian. “上来吧，”这位神奇的图书管理员喊道。

Jack and Annie climbed up the rope ladder and into the tree house. 杰克和安妮爬上绳梯，进了树屋。

In the dawn light, they stared at Morgan le Fay. She looked beautiful in a red velvet robe. 在黎明的光线里，他们盯着摩根·勒菲。 她穿着红色天鹅绒长袍，看上去很美。

Jack pushed his glasses into place. He couldn’t stop smiling. 杰克把眼镜往上推了推。 他忍不住一直笑。

“We both had dreams about you!” said Annie. “我们俩都梦见你了！”安妮说。

“I know,” said Morgan. “我知道，”摩根说。

“You do?” “真的吗？”

“Yes, I sent them to you,” said Morgan, “because I need your help.” “是的，是我把梦送给你们的，”摩根说，“因为我需要你们的帮助。”

“What kind of help?” said Jack. “什么样的帮助？”杰克说。

“Merlin the Magician has been up to his tricks again,” said Morgan. “So I haven’t had any time to collect books for Camelot’s library.” “梅林巫师又在搞他的鬼把戏了，”摩根说。 “所以我一直没有时间去为卡米洛特的图书馆收集书籍。”

“Can we collect them for you?” asked Annie. “我们能替你收集吗？”安妮问。

“Yes, but in order to gather books through time you must be Master Librarians,” said Morgan. “可以，但为了穿越时间去收集书籍，你们必须成为图书大师，”摩根说。

“Oh, well,” Annie said sadly. “哦，唉，”安妮伤感地说。

“But you can become Master Librarians,” said Morgan, “if you pass the test.” “但你们可以成为图书大师，”摩根说，“如果你们通过考试的话。”

“Really?” said Annie. “真的？”安妮说。

“What kind of test?” Jack asked. “什么样的考验？”杰克问。

“You must show that you know how to do research,” said Morgan, “and show that you can find answers to hard questions.” “你们必须证明自己会做研究，”摩根说，“还要证明你们能找到难题的答案。”

“How?” said Annie. “怎么找？”安妮说。

“By solving four riddles,” said Morgan. She reached into the folds of her robe and pulled out a rolled-up paper. “通过解开四个谜语，”摩根说。 她把手伸进长袍的褶皱里，抽出一张卷起来的纸。

“The first riddle is written on this ancient scroll,” she said. “This book will help you find the answer.” “第一个谜语就写在这张古老的卷轴上，”她说。 “这本书会帮你们找到答案。”

She held out a book. On the cover were the words Ocean Guide. 她拿出一本书。 封面上写着“海洋指南”。

“This is where you have to go,” said Morgan. “这就是你们要去的地方，”摩根说。

“The ocean! Oh, boy!” said Annie. She pointed at the cover. “I wish we—” “大海！ 哦，太棒了！”安妮说。 她指着封面。 “我希望我们——”

“Stop!” Jack grabbed Annie’s hand. “How will we know if we’ve found the right answer to the riddle?” he asked Morgan. “停下！”杰克抓住安妮的手。 “我们怎么知道找到的谜底对不对？”他问摩根。

“You will know,” Morgan said mysteriously. “I promise you will know.” “你们会知道的，”摩根神秘地说。 “我保证你们会知道的。”

Jack let go of Annie’s hand. She pointed again at the cover and finished her wish: “I wish we could go there.” 杰克松开了安妮的手。 她又指着封面，说完了愿望：“我希望我们能去那儿。”

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

“Are you coming with us, Morgan?” Jack said. “你跟我们一起去吗，摩根？”杰克说。

Before Morgan could answer, the tree house started to spin. 摩根还没来得及回答，树屋就开始旋转。

Jack squeezed his eyes shut. 杰克紧紧闭上眼睛。

The tree house spun faster and faster. 树屋转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静了下来。

Absolutely still. 完全静止。

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开眼睛。

Morgan le Fay was gone. 摩根·勒菲不见了。

Only the ancient scroll and the ocean book were left in her place. 她的位置上只留下了那卷古老的卷轴和那本海洋书。
