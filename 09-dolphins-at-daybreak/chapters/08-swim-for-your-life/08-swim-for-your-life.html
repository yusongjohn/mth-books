8. Swim for Your Life! 8. 拼命游！

Jack moved his arms slowly. He moved his legs slowly. He gently pushed the water out of his way as he did the breast stroke. 杰克慢慢移动双臂。 他慢慢移动双腿。 他轻轻拨开海水，做着蛙泳。

Calm, calm, he told himself. 冷静，冷静，他对自己说。

Annie swam beside him. They headed for the reef. 安妮在他旁边游。 他们朝珊瑚礁游去。

All was calm. 一切都很平静。

Then Jack saw something out of the corner of his eye. 接着杰克用余光看见了什么。

A dark fin was zigzagging through the water. It was heading toward them. 一道黑鳍正在水中曲折前进。 它正朝他们游来。

Jack wanted to splash. He wanted to yell. But he remembered: calm. 杰克想扑腾水。 他想大喊。 可他记住了：冷静。

I better not tell Annie, he thought. She’ll stay calmer if she doesn’t know. 我最好别告诉安妮，他想。 如果她不知道，她会更冷静。

He began to swim faster—then faster. Annie went faster, too. 他开始游得更快——然后更快。 安妮也游得更快了。

They both swam as fast, and as calmly, as they could. 他们俩都尽可能又快又冷静地游。

Sometimes Annie went even faster than Jack, which made him swim faster. And faster. 有时安妮甚至比杰克游得还快，这让他也游得更快。更快。

Jack was so scared that he wasn’t tired at all. He was swimming for his life—and for Annie’s life, too. 杰克害怕得一点也不觉得累。 他是在为自己的命游——也为安妮的命游。

He didn’t look back to see if the shark was still there. He didn’t want to know. 他没有回头看鲨鱼是否还在。 他不想知道。

He just kept his eye on the tree house in the distance. And he kept swimming. 他只把目光盯着远处的树屋。 他继续游。

Jack and Annie swam and swam and swam. 杰克和安妮游啊游啊游。

It took forever for the tree house to get just a little closer. 树屋要靠近一点点都花了好久。

Jack realized the reef was farther away than he had thought. 杰克意识到珊瑚礁比他想的要远。

He kept swimming, but his arms and legs felt heavy. 他继续游，可胳膊和腿都觉得沉。

Annie was struggling, too. 安妮也在苦苦挣扎。

“Float!” she said. “Float!” “漂着！”她说。 “漂着！”

Jack and Annie turned onto their backs. They floated the way they had learned in swimming class. 杰克和安妮翻过身，仰面躺着。 他们按照游泳课上学过的方法漂着。

We’ll just rest for a minute, Jack thought. Then we’ll keep going. 我们就休息一分钟，杰克想。 然后我们继续游。

But the more Jack floated, the more tired he felt. Soon he was too tired even to float. He started to sink. 可杰克越漂就越觉得累。 很快他就累得连漂着都做不到了。 他开始往下沉。

Then he felt something. 然后他感觉到了什么。

His heart stopped. Something pushed at him in the water. 他的心跳停了。 有什么东西在水里推他。

It was slippery and alive. 它滑溜溜的，而且是活的。

Had the hammerhead caught up with them? 锤头鲨已经追上他们了吗？

Jack shut his eyes and waited for the worst. He waited and waited. Finally, he opened his eyes. 杰克闭上眼睛，等着最坏的情况发生。 他等啊等。 终于，他睁开眼睛。

In front of him was a shiny gray head—a dolphin’s head! 在他面前是一个闪亮的灰色脑袋——海豚的头！

The dolphin pushed Jack with its nose. It made happy clicking sounds. 海豚用鼻子推着杰克。 它发出快乐的咔咔声。

“Hooray!” cried Annie. “万岁！”安妮喊道。

Jack looked over at her. 杰克朝她看去。

She was clinging to the fin of another dolphin! Her dolphin was moving through the water. 她正紧紧抓着另一只海豚的鳍！ 她的海豚正穿过海水游着。

Jack grabbed the fin of his dolphin. 杰克抓住自己那只海豚的鳍。

Then the two dolphins swam smoothly through the water, pulling Jack and Annie toward the reef. 接着两只海豚平稳地游过水面，把杰克和安妮拉向珊瑚礁。
