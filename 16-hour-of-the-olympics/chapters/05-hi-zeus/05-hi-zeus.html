5. Hi, Zeus 5. 你好，宙斯

“This is the very first day of the games,” Plato told Jack, “the day of the chariot races.” “这是运动会的第一天，”柏拉图告诉杰克，“也就是战车比赛的日子。”

“Oh, wow,” whispered Jack. “哇，”杰克低声说。

He couldn’t believe he was going to see a chariot race. The modern Olympic Games didn’t have chariot races. 他简直不敢相信自己就要看到战车比赛了。 现代奥运会没有战车赛。

They walked toward the race track. Plato pointed to a large building near the road. 他们朝跑道走去。 柏拉图指着路边一座大建筑。

“That is the gymnasium,” he said. “It is where our athletes train. They practice running and throwing the javelin and discus.” “那是体育馆，”他说。 “我们的运动员就在那里训练。 他们练习跑步，还练习投掷标枪和铁饼。”

“We have a gymnasium at our school in Frog Creek,” said Jack. “We call it a gym.” “我们蛙溪学校也有体育馆，”杰克说。 “我们管它叫健身房。”

“People all over the world copy us Greeks,” Plato said. “全世界的人都在学我们希腊人，”柏拉图说。

“Wait,” said Jack. “I have to take notes for Annie.” “等等，”杰克说。 “我得给安妮记笔记。”

He pulled out his notebook and wrote: 他抽出笔记本写道：

图中文字：Ancient Greeks invented gyms 图中文字：古希腊人发明了健身房

“Okay, we can go,” said Jack. He tucked his notebook under his arm. “好了，我们可以走了，”杰克说。 他把笔记本夹到胳膊底下。

As they moved along, Plato pointed to a beautiful tree nearby. 他们往前走时，柏拉图指着附近一棵美丽的树。

“The olive tree is our sacred tree,” he said. “The winners of the games will wear crowns made from its branches.” “橄榄树是我们的圣树，”他说。 “比赛的获胜者会戴上用它的树枝做成的花冠。”

“Oh, wow,” said Jack. And he wrote: “哇，”杰克说。他写道：

图中文字：Olive tree is sacred 图中文字：橄榄树是神圣的

Next they passed a beautiful statue of a winged lady. 接着他们走过一座美丽的带翅膀的女子雕像。

“Who’s that?” said Jack. “那是谁？”杰克说。

“She’s Nike, the goddess of victory,” said Plato. “她是胜利女神尼刻，”柏拉图说。

Jack quickly wrote: 杰克赶紧写道：

图中文字：Nike is goddess of victory 图中文字：Nike是胜利女神

“Nike is important to the games,” said Plato. “But the most important Olympic god is in there.” “尼刻对运动会很重要，”柏拉图说。 “但最重要的奥林匹斯神就在那里面。”

He took Jack to a brick building with huge columns. They stepped through the door. It was a temple. Jack gasped. 他带杰克来到一座有巨大柱子的砖石建筑。 他们走进门去。里面是一座神殿。 杰克倒吸一口气。

In front of them loomed the biggest statue he had ever seen. 他们面前赫然耸立着他见过的最大的一座雕像。

The statue was at least two stories high. It was a bearded man sitting on a throne. 那座雕像至少有两层楼高。 那是一个坐在王座上、留着胡子的男人。

“This is the temple of Zeus. And that is a statue of Zeus himself,” said Plato. “The Olympic Games are played in his honor. He is the chief god of the Greek gods and goddesses.” “这是宙斯神殿。 而那就是宙斯本人的雕像，”柏拉图说。 “奥林匹克运动会就是为了向他致敬而举办的。 他是希腊众神里最主要的神。”

“Oh, man,” whispered Jack. “天哪，”杰克低声说。

“Yesterday all the athletes came here,” said Plato. “They swore to Zeus that they had trained for ten months. And they promised to obey the rules of the games.” “昨天所有运动员都来过这里，”柏拉图说。 “他们向宙斯发誓，自己已经训练了十个月。 他们还承诺遵守比赛规则。”

The statue of the mighty Greek god stared down at Jack. 这座强大的希腊神明雕像俯视着杰克。

Jack felt very small. 杰克觉得自己好渺小。

“Hi, Zeus,” he said. His voice was small, too. “你好，宙斯，”他说。 他的声音也很小。

Suddenly, trumpet sounds came from outside. 忽然，外面响起了喇叭声。

“The hour has come,” said Plato. “We must hurry. The Olympic parade begins!” “时候到了，”柏拉图说。 “我们必须快点。 奥林匹克游行要开始了！”
