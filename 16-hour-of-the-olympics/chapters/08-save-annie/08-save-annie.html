8. Save Annie! 8. 救安妮！

The guards tried to pull Annie away from the chariot race. 卫兵想把安妮从战车赛场拉开。

Annie looked surprised. Then she looked angry. 安妮看上去很惊讶。 接着她生气了。

“Let go of me!” she shouted. “放开我！”她喊道。

Jack rushed down the steps of the viewing stand. 杰克冲下看台的台阶。

The guards were having a hard time pulling Annie through all the people. 卫兵费了好大劲，才把安妮从人群里往外拉。

“Leave her alone!” Jack yelled. “放开她！”杰克大喊。

His voice was lost in the noise of the race. 他的声音淹没在比赛的喧闹里。

He pushed his way through the crowd. 他挤着穿过人群。

“Leave her alone!” he kept shouting. “Leave her alone!” “放开她！”他不停地喊。 “放开她！”

Finally, Jack reached Annie and the guards. He tried to grab her, but a guard blocked his way. 终于，杰克赶到安妮和卫兵身边。 他想抓住她，可一名卫兵挡住了他的路。

“Let her go!” yelled Jack. “I promise I’ll take her home!” “放她走！”杰克喊道。 “我保证带她回家！”

More guards arrived. The crowd began to shout, “Arrest her! Arrest her!” 更多卫兵赶到了。 人群开始喊：“抓住她！抓住她！”

The guards kept pulling Annie away. 卫兵继续把安妮往外拖。

“Jack! The story!” cried Annie. “杰克！ 故事！”安妮喊道。

Of course! thought Jack. The poet’s story! This is definitely our darkest hour! 对了！杰克想。 诗人的故事！ 这无疑是我们最黑暗的时刻！

He reached into his bag and pulled out the poet’s scroll. 他伸手进袋子，抽出诗人的卷轴。

He held the story up to the sky. 他把那篇故事举向天空。

“Save Annie!” he shouted. “救安妮！”他喊道。

But Jack’s voice was again lost in the roar of the race as the four-horse chariots barreled through the dust. 可杰克的声音又一次淹没在比赛的轰鸣里，四马战车在尘土中飞驰而过。

Jack looked around wildly for someone—or something—to help them. 杰克疯狂地四处张望，想找人或什么东西来帮他们。

Then suddenly the crowd fell silent. 接着人群忽然安静下来。

All heads turned to watch as a huge white horse galloped out of the dust. 所有人都转过头去，只见一匹巨大的白马从尘土中飞奔出来。

The crowd murmured with excitement and wonder. 人群又是兴奋又是惊叹，低声议论着。

The white horse was the most beautiful animal Jack had ever seen. 那匹白马是杰克见过的最美丽的动物。

He was pulling an empty chariot. 它拉着一辆空战车。

And he was galloping straight toward Jack. 它正径直朝杰克飞奔而来。
