1. Just One More 1. 再来一次

“You awake?” Annie’s voice came out of the dark. “你醒了吗？”安妮的声音从黑暗中传来。

“Yep,” said Jack from his bed. “嗯，”杰克在床上说。

“Get up,” said Annie. “We have to get to the tree house before sunrise.” “快起来，”安妮说。 “我们得在日出前赶到树屋。”

“I’m ready,” said Jack. “我准备好了，”杰克说。

He threw back his covers and jumped out of bed. He was wearing his jeans and T-shirt. 他把被子往后一掀，跳下了床。 他穿着牛仔裤和T恤。

“You slept in your clothes?” asked Annie. “你穿着衣服睡觉？”安妮问。

“I didn’t want to waste any time,” said Jack. He pulled on his backpack. “我不想浪费时间，”杰克说。 他背上背包。

Annie laughed. 安妮笑了。

“You must really be excited about going to ancient Greece,” she said. “你一定是对去古希腊特别兴奋，”她说。

“Yep,” said Jack. “嗯，”杰克说。

“Do you have your secret library card?” asked Annie. “你带上秘密图书管理员卡了吗？”安妮问。

“Yeah, do you?” said Jack. “带了，你呢？”杰克说。

“Sure. Put it in your backpack,” she said. She handed her card to him. “I’ll carry the flashlight.” “当然。 放进你的背包里，”她说。 她把卡片递给了他。 “我拿手电筒。”

“All set,” said Jack. “准备好了，”杰克说。

They tiptoed downstairs and out the door. 他们踮着脚走下楼，出了门。

Outside the air was fresh and cool. 外面空气清新凉爽。

“There’s no moon,” said Annie. “Just stars.” “没有月亮，”安妮说。 “只有星星。”

She turned on her flashlight. 她打开了手电筒。

“Ta-da!” she said. “Let’s go.” “瞧！”她说。 “走吧。”

They followed the beam of light across their yard and up the street. 他们跟着那道光柱穿过院子，走上街道。

Jack was thrilled to be going to ancient Greece. But something worried him. 杰克为即将去古代希腊而兴奋不已。 可有件事让他担心。

“What do you think will happen after we go to Greece?” he asked Annie. “Is this our last mission ever?” “我们去了希腊之后会怎样？”他问安妮。 “这会是我们最后一次任务吗？”

“Oh, I hope not,” said Annie. “What do you think?” “哦，希望不是，”安妮说。 “你觉得呢？”

“I don’t know. Let’s ask Morgan,” said Jack. “我不知道。 “我们去问摩根吧，”杰克说。

“Hurry!” said Annie. “快点！”安妮说。

They started running. The flashlight beam flew in front of them, lighting the way. 他们开始跑起来。 手电筒的光束在他们前面飞舞，照亮了前路。

They slowed to a walk when they got to the Frog Creek woods. The thick woods were pitch-black. 到了蛙溪树林时，他们放慢脚步，改成了走。 密林里一片漆黑。

Annie shined the flashlight upward as they walked between the trees. Finally, they found the magic tree house. 他们在树间走着时，安妮把手电筒往上照。 终于，他们找到了魔法树屋。

“We’re here!” Annie called. “我们到了！”安妮喊道。

“Go on up,” said Jack. “你先上去，”杰克说。

Annie grabbed the rope ladder and started up. Jack followed. 安妮抓住绳梯往上爬。杰克跟在后面。

Annie shined the flashlight around the tree house. 安妮用手电在树屋里四处照。

Morgan le Fay was sitting at the window. She covered her eyes when the light hit her face. 摩根·勒菲正坐在窗边。 光线照到她脸上时，她用手遮住了眼睛。

“Turn off the light, please, Annie,” she said softly. “请把灯关掉，安妮，”她轻声说。

Annie turned it off. 安妮关掉了手电。

“Welcome,” Morgan said in the dark. “Are you ready for your next mission?” “欢迎，”摩根在黑暗中说。 “准备好执行下一次任务了吗？”

“Yes!” said Annie. Then her voice got quiet. “This isn’t our last one ever, is it?” “是的！”安妮说。 接着她的声音变轻了。 “这不会是我们最后一次了吧？”

“Ask me that question after this mission,” said Morgan. “等这次任务结束后再问我这个问题，”摩根说。

“We want to go on more,” said Jack. “我们还想继续去，”杰克说。

“You’re very brave to say that,” said Morgan. “You’ve had three very hard journeys as Master Librarians.” “你能这么说真勇敢，”摩根说。 “作为首席图书管理员，你们已经经历了三次非常艰难的旅程。”

“Oh, they weren’t so hard,” said Jack. “哦，也没那么难，”杰克说。

“You risked your life to save the lost story of Hercules,” said Morgan. “你冒着生命危险去救回了赫拉克勒斯的失传故事，”摩根说。

“It was nothing,” said Annie. “没什么，”安妮说。

“And the Chinese story of the silk weaver,” said Morgan. “And the Irish story of the serpent monster Sarph. Thank you.” “还有中国织女的故事，”摩根说。 “还有爱尔兰蛇怪萨尔夫的故事。谢谢你们。”

“You’re welcome,” said Jack and Annie. “不客气，”杰克和安妮说。

“Now,” said Morgan, “for the last story … ” “现在，”摩根说，“最后一个故事……”

Jack heard a rustling sound. 杰克听到一阵沙沙声。

“Here is the title,” Morgan said. “You can shine your light on it, Annie.” “这是标题，”摩根说。 “你可以把手电照上去，安妮。”

Annie turned the flashlight back on. She shined it on the paper. 安妮又打开了手电筒。 她把手电照在那张纸上。

“Wow, is that Greek?” asked Jack. “哇，那是希腊语吗？”杰克问。

“It certainly is,” said Morgan. “确实是，”摩根说。

She reached into her robe again and pulled out a book. 她又把手伸进袍子，掏出一本书。

“For your research,” she said. “给你们做研究用，”她说。

Jack took the book from her. Annie shined her flashlight on the cover. They read the title: A Day in Ancient Greece. 杰克从她手里接过书。 安妮把手电照在封面上。 他们读出书名：《古希腊的一天》。

“Now, what must you always remember?” asked Morgan. “那么，你们必须永远记住什么？”摩根问。

“Our research book will guide us,” said Jack. “我们的研究书会指引我们，”杰克说。

“But in our darkest hour, only the lost story can save us,” said Annie. “但在最危急的时刻，只有失传的故事才能救我们，”安妮说。

Morgan nodded. “And you must show your secret library cards to the wisest person you meet,” she said. 摩根点了点头。 “而且你们必须把秘密图书管理员卡拿给遇见的最有智慧的人看，”她说。

“Don’t worry. We will. Bye!” said Annie. “别担心。我们会的。 再见！”安妮说。

Jack shivered with excitement as he pointed at the book’s cover. 杰克指着书的封面，兴奋得微微发抖。

“I wish we could go there,” he said. “我希望我们能去那里，”他说。

“And I wish we could go on lots of other missions!” Annie added. “我还希望我们能去很多很多其他任务！”安妮补充道。

The wind began to blow. 风开始刮起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 转得越来越快。

Then everything was silent. 然后一切都静了下来。

Absolutely silent. 绝对安静。
