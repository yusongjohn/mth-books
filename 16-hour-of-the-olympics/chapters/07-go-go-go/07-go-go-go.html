7. Go! Go! Go! 7. 冲啊！冲啊！冲啊！

Jack stared in horror at Annie. She must have borrowed a costume from the theater! 杰克惊恐地盯着安妮。 她一定是从剧场借了一套戏服！

He remembered Plato’s words: A girl will get in terrible trouble if she tries to attend the Olympic Games. 他想起柏拉图的话：女孩要是想参加奥林匹克运动会，就会惹上大麻烦。

Jack shook his head at her and pointed his finger, as if to say, Get out of here! 杰克朝她摇头，还用手指着，像是在说：快离开这儿！

But Annie just waved at him again. 可安妮又朝他挥了挥手。

Jack kept shaking his head at Annie. He even shook his fist. 杰克不停地对安妮摇头。 他甚至还挥了挥拳头。

Annie turned back to watch the race. 安妮转回去看比赛。

“It’s not a joke!” Jack shouted. “这不是开玩笑！”杰克喊道。

Plato turned and looked at him. 柏拉图转过身看着他。

“Of course not,” said Plato. “We take the games very seriously.” “当然不会，”柏拉图说。 “我们对运动会非常认真。”

Jack felt his face grow hot. He glared at Annie’s back. 杰克觉得自己的脸发烫了。 他瞪着安妮的背影。

Just then the trumpet sounded. 就在这时，号角响了。

“The chariots are taking their places,” said Plato. “战车正在就位，”柏拉图说。

Jack saw the dozens of chariots lined up on the race track. Each chariot was pulled by four horses. 杰克看见几十辆战车在跑道上排好了队。 每辆战车由四匹马拉着。

Jack glanced back at Annie. She was looking up at him, pointing at the chariots. 杰克回头瞥了安妮一眼。 她正抬头看着他，指着那些战车。

The trumpet sounded again. 号角又响了。

The horses took off! 马匹冲了出去！

The crowd went wild. They were cheering and screaming and stamping their feet. 人群沸腾了。 他们欢呼、尖叫、跺着脚。

Clouds of dust rose up as the chariots raced around the track. 战车绕着跑道飞驰，扬起一团团尘土。

Annie turned back to watch the race. She began jumping up and down. 安妮又转回去看比赛。 她开始上下蹦跳。

“Go! Go! Go!” she shouted. “加油！加油！ 冲啊！”她喊道。

A few people began staring at the strange small soldier with the high-pitched voice. 有几个人开始盯着那个声音尖细、模样古怪的矮小士兵。

Jack couldn’t take it anymore. He had to get Annie away before it was too late! 杰克再也受不了了。 他必须在太晚之前把安妮带走！

He shoved his notebook into his bag. 他把笔记本塞进包里。

“I have to go!” he shouted to Plato. “我得走了！”他对柏拉图喊道。

The philosopher looked surprised. 这位哲学家看上去很惊讶。

Jack was afraid to tell him that Annie had broken the rules. 杰克不敢告诉他安妮已经犯规了。

“I had a great time. But I have to go home now,” said Jack. “Thanks for everything!” “我玩得很开心。 可我现在得回家了，”杰克说。 “谢谢你的一切！”

“Have a safe journey,” said Plato. “一路平安，”柏拉图说。

Jack waved and started down the steps. 杰克挥了挥手，开始走下台阶。

As he climbed down, he saw Annie pull off her helmet. 他往下爬时，看见安妮摘下头盔。

Her pigtails flew up and down as she jumped and shouted, “Go! Go! Go!” 她又跳又喊“加油！加油！ 冲啊！”，辫子上下飞舞。

Her soldier’s cape fell off. 她的士兵披风掉了下来。

Now lots of people were staring at her. Someone shouted for the guards. 现在很多人盯着她看。 有人喊卫兵。

Annie was too busy cheering to notice anything. 安妮忙着加油，什么都没注意到。

Jack moved as quickly as he could. 杰克尽快往前冲。

But two big guards got to Annie first. 可两个大块头卫兵先到了安妮身边。
