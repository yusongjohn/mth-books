9. Fly Away Home 9. 飞回家

The white horse stopped at the low wall by the edge of the track. 白马在跑道边缘的矮墙边停了下来。

“He’s come for us!” cried Annie. “它来接我们了！”安妮喊道。

The guards stared in awe at the horse. 卫兵们敬畏地盯着那匹马。

Annie broke free and dashed over to Jack. He grabbed her hand and they ran to the horse. 安妮挣脱开来，冲向杰克。 他抓住她的手，一起跑向那匹马。

The guards shouted and started after them. 卫兵大喊着追过来。

But they were too late. Jack and Annie had already climbed over the wall and into the waiting chariot. 可已经太晚了。 杰克和安妮已经翻过墙，进了等候的战车。

“Go! Go! Go!” Annie cried to the huge white horse. “跑！跑！ 跑！”安妮对那匹巨大的白马喊道。

The horse reared and pawed the air. 那匹马扬起前蹄，扒着空气。

The crowd stepped back from the wall. 人群从墙边往后退。

The guards froze. 卫兵愣住了。

Jack looked up to where Plato was now standing. Plato smiled and waved at him. 杰克抬头看向柏拉图此刻站着的地方。 柏拉图对他微笑并挥手。

Then the white horse leaped forward, pulling the chariot behind him. 接着白马向前一跃，拖着战车冲了出去。

Jack couldn’t even wave back at Plato. All he could do was hold on tight as the horse galloped beside the Olympic racers. 杰克甚至没法向柏拉图挥手回应。 他唯一能做的，就是抓紧，任那匹马在奥林匹克赛手旁边飞奔。

Jack bounced up and down. Dust and sand got in his eyes. He squeezed them shut and crouched down in the chariot. 杰克上下颠簸着。 灰尘和沙子进了他的眼睛。 他挤上眼睛，蹲进战车里。

He didn’t know where they were going. But it didn’t matter. The white horse was in charge. 他不知道他们要去哪里。 可那已经不重要了。 那匹白马才是做主的。

Jack heard the thundering noise of the racing horses and chariots. He heard the screaming crowd. 杰克听见赛马和战车雷鸣般的声响。 他听见人群的尖叫。

He felt sand blowing in his face and the hard bumping and rattling of the chariot. 他感到沙子打在脸上，还有战车猛烈的颠簸和咔嗒声。

Suddenly, he was thrown backward. He heard a swoosh of wind, then … 忽然，他被向后甩了出去。 他听见一阵风声呼啸，然后……

Silence. 下面，奥林匹克的观众目瞪口呆地看着。

“Oh, wow!” cried Annie. “哇！”安妮喊道。

Jack opened his eyes. All he saw was blue sky. He pushed his glasses into place and looked around. 杰克睁开眼睛。 他只看见蓝天。 他把眼镜推正，四处张望。

“Help!” he cried. “救命！”他喊道。

The white horse had grown giant feathery wings and was pulling their chariot into the sky. 白马长出了巨大的羽毛翅膀，正拖着他们的战车飞向天空。

Jack gripped the railing of the chariot and held on for his life. 杰克死死抓住战车的栏杆，拼命抓牢。

“To the tree house!” shouted Annie. “去树屋！”安妮喊道。

Below, the Olympic crowd watched in stunned silence. 下面，奥林匹克的观众目瞪口呆地看着。

The winged horse left the games behind and flew over the temple of Zeus, over the statue of Nike, over the sacred olive tree and the gymnasium. 长着翅膀的马把赛场甩在身后，飞过宙斯神庙，飞过尼克雕像，飞过圣橄榄树和体育馆。

On they went: over the poet’s house, the Greek theater, and the field of white tents. 他们继续飞：越过诗人的房子、希腊剧场，还有白色帐篷的营地。

Finally, the winged horse coasted to the ground near the olive grove. 最后，长翅膀的马滑翔到橄榄树林附近的地面上。

The wheels of the chariot bumped onto the grass. Then slowly, slowly, they came to a stop. 战车的轮子磕上了草地。 然后慢慢地、慢慢地，它们停了下来。

Jack and Annie stepped out of the chariot. Jack’s legs were so wobbly he could hardly walk. 杰克和安妮走下战车。 杰克的腿抖得几乎走不了路。

Annie rushed to the horse and stroked his neck. 安妮冲到马身边，抚摸它的脖子。

“Thank you,” she whispered. “谢谢你，”她轻声说。

Jack patted the horse’s long white neck too. 杰克也拍了拍马长长的白脖子。

“Thanks,” he said. “That was the best ride of my life.” “谢谢，”他说。 “这是我这辈子最棒的一次骑行。”

The horse snorted and pawed the ground. 马打了个响鼻，用蹄子刨着地。

“Come on, Annie. We have to go before they find us,” said Jack. “来吧，安妮。 我们得在他们找到我们之前走，”杰克说。

“I don’t want to leave him,” said Annie. “He’s the most beautiful horse in the whole world.” “我不想离开它，”安妮说。 “它是全世界最美丽的马。”

Her eyes filled with tears. 她眼里满是泪水。

“We have to,” said Jack. “我们必须走，”杰克说。

The horse put his head down and touched Annie’s forehead with his soft nose. Then he gave her a gentle push toward the tree house. 那匹马低下头，用柔软的鼻子碰了碰安妮的额头。 然后他轻轻把她推向树屋。

Annie sniffled but started walking. Jack took her hand as they walked through the olive grove to the rope ladder of the tree house. 安妮抽了抽鼻子，但还是迈步走了。 杰克拉着她的手，穿过橄榄树林，走向树屋的绳梯。

“You first,” Jack said. “你先上，”杰克说。

Annie started up the ladder. Jack followed. 安妮开始爬梯子。杰克跟在后面。

When they were inside, Annie hurried to the window. Jack grabbed the Pennsylvania book. 进去后，安妮赶紧跑到窗边。 杰克抓起宾夕法尼亚的书。

He pointed to a picture of the Frog Creek woods and said, “I wish—” 他指着蛙溪树林的图画说：“我希望——”

“Look!” said Annie. “看！”安妮说。

Jack looked out the window. The horse had spread his great feathery wings. He was rising from the field. 杰克朝窗外望去。 那匹马展开了巨大的羽毛翅膀。 它正从田野上升起。

The white horse flew high into the blue Olympian sky. 白马高高飞入奥林匹亚的蓝天。

Then he disappeared behind the clouds. 然后它消失在云层后面。

“Bye!” called Annie. “再见！”安妮喊道。

A tear rolled down her cheek. 一滴眼泪顺着她的脸颊滑落。

Jack pointed again at the Pennsylvania book. 杰克又指着宾夕法尼亚的书。

“I wish we could go there,” he said. “我希望我们能去那里，”他说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

The tree house began to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 绝对静止。
