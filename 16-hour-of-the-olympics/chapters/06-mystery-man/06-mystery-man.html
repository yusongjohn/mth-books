6. Mystery Man 6. 神秘人

Plato and Jack hurried past the crowds standing at the sides of the race track. Everyone was shouting and cheering. 柏拉图和杰克匆匆穿过站在跑道两侧的人群。 人人都在喊叫、欢呼。

“I have seats next to the judges,” said Plato. He pointed to a tall stand with rows of benches. “我的座位就在裁判旁边，”柏拉图说。 他指着一座有一排排长凳的高看台。

Plato led Jack through the crowd and up the steps to their seats. 柏拉图领着杰克穿过人群，走上台阶来到他们的座位。

“Wow, thanks,” said Jack. “哇，谢谢，”杰克说。

He had a great view. 他看得一清二楚。

The Olympic parade had already started. Musicians playing pipes were at the front. Behind them marched the Olympic athletes—the best in all of Greece. 奥林匹克游行已经开始了。 前面有吹笛子的乐手。 走在他们后面的是奥林匹克运动员——全希腊最优秀的人。

Jack sighed as he watched the parade going around the track. Annie would really love this, he thought. 杰克看着队伍绕着跑道行进，叹了口气。 安妮一定会超喜欢这个，他想。

“The athletes in front are the runners,” said Plato. “Foot races are the oldest event of the Olympics.” “前面那些运动员是赛跑选手，”柏拉图说。 “赛跑是奥运会最古老的项目。”

Jack pulled out his notebook and wrote: 杰克抽出笔记本写道：

图中文字：oldest event is foot races 图中文字：最古老的活动是赛跑

“Behind the runners are the boxers,” said Plato. “They’re wearing special gloves and bronze helmets.” “跑者后面是拳击手，”柏拉图说。 “他们戴着特制手套和青铜头盔。”

Jack wrote: 杰克写道：

图中文字：boxers wear gloves and helmets 图中文字：拳击手戴拳套和头盔

“Behind them are the wrestlers,” said Plato. “他们后面是摔跤选手，”柏拉图说。

And Jack wrote: 杰克又写道：

图中文字：wrestlers 图中文字：摔跤手

When Jack looked up again, he saw a soldier staring up at him from the sidelines. 杰克再抬起头时，看见一个士兵在边线上抬头盯着他。

The soldier was dressed like the actor from the outdoor theater. He had on a long cape and a red-crested helmet that covered most of his face. 那个士兵穿得像露天剧场里的演员。 他穿着长披风，戴着盖住大半张脸的红冠头盔。

But something was strange. This soldier was short— really short. 可有什么不对劲。 这个士兵很矮——真的很矮。

“Here come the discus and javelin throwers,” said Plato, “and then the men in armor.” “铁饼和标枪选手过来了，”柏拉图说，“然后是穿着盔甲的人。”

“What do they do?” asked Jack. “他们干什么？”杰克问。

“They race wearing full armor,” said Plato. “他们穿着全套盔甲赛跑，”柏拉图说。

Jack smiled to himself. He knew Annie would think that was funny. 杰克暗自笑了笑。 他知道安妮会觉得这很好笑。

He wrote in his notebook: 他在笔记本上写道：

图中文字：some runners race in armor 图中文字：一些跑步者穿着盔甲比赛

Jack finished writing. 杰克写完了。

He looked back at the short soldier. 他回头看那个矮士兵。

“In a moment, the chariot races will begin,” said Plato. “Winning a chariot race is the greatest honor of the games.” “马上，战车比赛就要开始了，”柏拉图说。 “赢得战车比赛是运动会的最高荣誉。”

Jack just nodded. He was still studying the short soldier, who seemed to be looking back at him. 杰克只是点了点头。 他还在打量那个矮小的士兵，那士兵好像也在回看他。

Suddenly, a small hand came out from the soldier’s cape. The hand gave a little wave. 突然，一只小手从士兵的披风里伸出来。 那只手轻轻挥了挥。

Jack gasped. It was Annie’s hand waving! 杰克倒吸一口气。 那是安妮的手在挥！

The short soldier was Annie. 那个矮士兵就是安妮。
