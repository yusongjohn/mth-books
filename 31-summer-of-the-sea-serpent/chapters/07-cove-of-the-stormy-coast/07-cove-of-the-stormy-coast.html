7. Cove of the Stormy Coast 7. 风暴海岸湾

“Seals?” asked Annie. “海豹？”安妮问。

Jack looked at Teddy. “Did she say ‘change into seals’?” he asked. 杰克看着泰迪。 “她说‘变成海豹’吗？”他问。

“Indeed!” said Teddy. “That’s what selkies do. They are people on land and seals in the sea.” “确实如此！”泰迪说。 “这就是海豹人做的事。” 他们在陆地上是人，在海里是海豹。

“You’re a seal?” Jack asked Kathleen. “你是海豹吗？”杰克问凯瑟琳。

“Sometimes,” said the selkie, smiling. “有时候是，”水妖精微笑着说。

Jack just stared at her. He couldn’t believe the beautiful girl was sometimes a seal. 杰克只是盯着她看。 他简直不敢相信，那个漂亮的女孩有时竟然是一只海豹。

“Whenever I come ashore and dry off in the sun, my seal skin falls away,” said Kathleen. “Then I am as human as—well, as I am right now.” “每当我上岸，躺在太阳下晒干，我的海豹皮肤就会脱落，”凯瑟琳说。 “那我就是一个人类——嗯，就像我现在这样。”

“Oh, I get it!” said Annie. “You were one of the seals we met in the water!” “哦，我明白了！”安妮说。 “你就是我们在水里遇到的那些海豹之一！”

“Of course,” said Kathleen, “and Teddy was the other.” “当然，”凯瑟琳说，“泰迪是另一个。”

Jack looked at Teddy. “Y-you?” he stammered. “How … ?” 杰克看着泰迪。 “你……你？”他结结巴巴地说。 “怎……怎么……？”

Teddy grinned. “I am a magician, remember?” he said. 泰迪咧嘴笑了。 “我是个魔术师，你忘了吗？”他说。

Kathleen laughed. “Yes, but this time, ’twas my magic that did the work,” she said. She pointed to two shiny gray skins lying on the sand. “I gave him a seal skin and spoke a selkie spell.” 凯瑟琳笑了。 “是的，但这一次，是我的魔法起作用了，”她说。 她指着沙子上躺着的两片闪亮的灰色皮肤。 “我给了他一张海豹皮，然后念了一个塞利基的咒语。”

“You turned Teddy into a seal?” asked Jack. “你把泰迪变成海豹了？”杰克问。

“Aye,” said Kathleen, “and I shall do the same for you. I am sure my sisters will not mind if we borrow two more of their skins.” The selkie started toward a large pile of rocks. “是的，”凯瑟琳说，“我也会把你变成一样。” 我确信我的姐妹们不会介意我们再借两张她们的皮。”海豹人开始朝一堆大石头走去。

Teddy watched Kathleen go, then looked back at Jack and Annie. “She has powerful magic indeed,” he said. 泰迪看着凯瑟琳离开，然后回头看向杰克和安妮。 “她确实有强大的魔法，”他说。

“No kidding,” said Annie. “真不敢相信，”安妮说。

Jack was speechless. He couldn’t believe he and Annie were about to become seals. 杰克说不出话来。 他不敢相信他和安妮马上就要变成海豹了。

Kathleen came out from behind the rocks carrying two shiny skins. “Take these,” she said, handing them to Jack and Annie. The skins looked like scuba divers’ wet suits with hoods. 凯瑟琳从石头后面走出来，拿着两张闪亮的皮。 “拿着这些，”她说，把它们递给杰克和安妮。 这些皮套看起来就像潜水员的湿衣，还有带兜帽的。

“Pull them on over your clothes,” said Kathleen. “Like this.” She and Teddy picked up their own seal skins from the sand and began pulling them on. Jack and Annie did the same. “把它们套在你们的衣服上，”凯瑟琳说。 “就像这样。”她和泰迪从沙子里捡起自己的海豹皮套，开始套上。 杰克和安妮也这样做。

Jack stretched the skin up over his legs and shorts, then over his arms and T-shirt. He felt as if he were wearing a thick layer of rubber. 杰克把皮套拉过他的腿和短裤，然后拉过他的手臂和T恤。 他感觉就像穿着一层厚厚的橡胶。

“Before we cover our heads and faces, we must wade into the water,” said Kathleen. “Follow me.” “在我们盖住头和脸之前，我们必须蹚进水里，”凯瑟琳说。 “跟我来。”

Jack waded clumsily into the water with the others. This is crazy, he thought. You can’t become a seal just by putting on a seal costume. 杰克和其他人笨拙地蹚进水里。 这太疯狂了，他想。 你不能只是穿上海豹服装，就变成海豹。

Kathleen stopped them when they were waist deep. “Slip on your hoods,” she said. “I will say a few words in selkie language. Then we shall all dive in.” 凯瑟琳在他们 waist deep 的时候拦住了他们。 “戴上你们的兜帽，”她说。 “我要用海豹语说几句话。 然后我们全都跳下去。”

Teddy grinned at Jack and Annie. “We have flown together through the sky,” he said. “Now we shall swim together in the deep, eh?” 泰迪对杰克和安妮露出微笑。 “我们曾经一起飞过天空，”他说。 “现在我们要一起在深水中游泳，对吧？”

Jack nodded, but he still didn’t believe this could possibly work. 杰克点了点头，但他仍然不相信这有可能成功。

“Quickly!” said Kathleen. “Cover your heads and faces! Dive as soon as I’ve spoken the spell!” “快点！”凯瑟琳说。 “盖住你们的头和脸！ 念完咒语后马上跳下去！

Jack pulled on his hood. He stretched it down over his forehead, then over his face—his glasses, nose, and chin. He couldn’t see or talk. He wanted to rip the hood off, but Kathleen’s voice stopped him: 杰克拉上了他的兜帽。 他把兜帽往下拉，盖住额头，然后盖住脸——他的眼镜、鼻子和下巴。 他看不见也说不出话。 他想把兜帽扯下来，但凯瑟琳的声音阻止了他：

An-ca-da-tro-a-day-mee! 安-卡-达-特罗-阿-日-米！

Ba-mi-hu-no-nay-hah-nee! 巴-米-胡-诺-奈-哈-尼！

Jack heard one splash—then two more. He quickly dove into the sea. 杰克听到一声水花声——接着又来了两声。 他迅速跳入海中。

As soon as the seawater covered his head and body, Jack felt the seal skin melt into his own. His chest expanded to the size of a barrel! His arms and legs vanished and flippers took their place! 海水一盖住杰克的头和身体，他就感觉到海豹的皮肤融化进了自己的皮肤里。 他的胸脯膨胀得像一个大桶！ 他的手臂和腿消失了，取而代之的是鳍！

Jack shot through the water like a torpedo. 杰克像一枚鱼雷一样在水里飞速前进。

When he moved his front flippers, he turned right or left. When he moved his back flippers, he zoomed forward. 当他动前鳍时，他就能向右或向左转弯。 当他动后鳍时，他就飞快地向前冲。

Jack rolled and swerved in and out of schools of fish and jungles of sea grass. He dove down to the murky depths of the cove. Then he shot back up to the surface. In his smooth seal body, he could swim ten times faster than he ever had in his human body! And he could see and hear perfectly! 杰克在鱼群和海草丛林中翻滚、穿梭。 他潜到海湾深处的浑浊水域里。 然后他又猛地冲回水面。 他光滑的海豹身体让他游得比以前当人的时候快十倍！ 他看得清清楚楚，听得一清二楚！

Jack rose and fell and rose and fell through the deep. Two seals appeared beside him. Bubbles came from their mouths. They made gurgling, clicking sounds. Jack could understand exactly what their seal sounds meant. 杰克在深深的水里上下起伏，又上下起伏。 两只海豹出现在他旁边。 它们的嘴里冒出了泡泡。 它们发出咕噜咕噜和咔哒咔哒的声音。 杰克完全明白它们的海豹叫声是什么意思。

Hey, Jack! It’s me! 嘿，杰克！是我！

’Tis me, too! 我也是，梅林！

Hi, guys! Jack called to Annie and Teddy. He heard trilling sounds on his other side. He saw a third seal. It was Kathleen, swimming gracefully toward him. 嗨，你们俩！ 杰克朝安妮和泰迪喊道。 他听到另一边传来清脆的声音。 他看到第三个海豹。 那是凯瑟琳，优雅地朝他游来。

Hello, Jack! 你好，杰克！

Hello, Kathleen! Jack clicked back to her. 你好，凯瑟琳！ 杰克又回到了她身边。

He wanted to tell the selkie how much fun he was having. But when he opened his mouth, a school of tiny fish swam down his throat. Before he knew it, Jack had swallowed all the fish whole! But he didn’t mind. He laughed a seal laugh full of bubbles. 他想告诉海豹女孩他玩得有多开心。 但他刚张开嘴，一群小鱼就游进了他的喉咙。 还没等他反应过来，杰克就把所有小鱼都吞了下去！ 但他一点也不在意。 他发出海豹般的笑声，满是泡泡。

Onward, Kathleen! clicked Teddy. Lead us to the Jellyfish Cave! 向前冲，凯瑟琳！泰迪说道。 带我们去水母洞！
