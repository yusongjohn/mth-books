9. The Sword of Light 9. 光之剑

Annie pointed to the sword handle. Jack nodded. But he was running out of air. He swam back up to the surface. Annie followed him. 安妮指着剑柄。杰克点了点头。 但他快要没气了。 他游回水面。 安妮跟着他上来。

Their heads bobbed above the water, and they gasped. “Did you see it?” cried Annie. “The handle of the sword?” 他们的头浮在水面上，惊呼起来。 “你看到那东西了吗？”安妮喊道。 “剑柄？”

“Yes! The blade must be buried in the sand!” said Jack. “看到了！ “剑刃一定埋在沙子里了！”杰克说。

“We have to pull it out!” said Annie. “And we have to hurry!” “我们必须把它拉出来！”安妮说。 “而且要快！”

“Right!” said Jack. “对！”杰克说。

He and Annie took giant gulps of air. Then they swam back down to the bottom of the cove. Jack got to the sword first. He grabbed the handle and pulled. The sword didn’t budge. He pulled again. The sword still didn’t move. 他和安妮大口吸了口气。 然后他们又游回了海湾的底部。 杰克第一个拿到了剑。 他抓住剑柄，用力一拉。 剑一点都没动。他又拉了一次。 剑还是没有动。

Annie grabbed one side of the handle. She and Jack pulled together. Jack felt the sword move a tiny bit. His lungs felt like they were about to burst. 安妮抓住了剑柄的一边。 她和杰克一起用力拉。 杰克感觉到剑动了一点点。 他的肺好像要爆炸了一样。

Gripping his side of the handle with both hands, Jack pulled with all his might. Suddenly the flashing blade slipped out of the sand! 杰克用双手紧紧抓住自己那边的剑柄，使出全身的力气拉。 突然，那把闪闪发光的剑从沙子里滑了出来！

Jack and Annie carried the sword up through the water as fast as they could. They burst into the air, clinging to the Sword of Light and gasping for air. 杰克和安妮赶紧把剑举过水面，拼命往上爬。 他们冲出水面，紧紧抓着光之剑，大口喘着气。

“We got it!” Jack called to Kathleen and Teddy. “我们拿到了！”杰克朝凯瑟琳和泰迪喊道。

The seals circled around them, splashing and barking joyfully. 海豹们围在他们身边，欢快地拍水，发出叫声。

Barrh! Barrh! 巴尔！巴尔！

“Lead us to the rocks!” Annie shouted. “带我们去岩石那儿！”安妮大喊。

Jack and Annie each held on to the sword’s handle with one hand. Paddling with their free hands, they dragged the sword after the seals. 杰克和安妮各自用一只手紧紧抓住剑柄。 他们用另一只手划水，拖着剑跟在海豹后面。

As they swam, the fog began to lift, and the sky turned from gray to blue. By the time they reached the rocky island, light from the late-afternoon sun was again glinting off the strange green shoreline. 他们游泳时，雾气开始散去，天空也从灰色变成了蓝色。 等他们游到那块岩石小岛时，傍晚的阳光又开始在那片奇怪的绿色海岸线上闪闪发光。

“Hold the sword while I get out!” Jack shouted to Annie. “拿着剑，我出去！”杰克对安妮喊道。

Jack climbed onto the rocky island. He took the sword from Annie, then carefully pulled it up onto the rocks and laid it on its side. 杰克爬上了岩石小岛。 他从安妮手里接过剑，然后小心地把它拉到岩石上，平放在那里。

Annie climbed out of the water. Nearby, Teddy and Kathleen poked up their seal heads. They were all silent as they stared in wonder at the sword. 安妮爬出了水里。 附近，泰迪和凯瑟琳把它们的海豹脑袋探了出来。 他们都安静地盯着剑，满脸惊奇。

“Wow,” breathed Annie. “哇，”安妮轻声说。

Jack nodded. The mighty sword reflected the burning light of the setting sun. Its blade glowed as if it, too, were on fire. 杰克点了点头。 这把强大的剑反射着落日的火光。 它的剑刃闪闪发光，仿佛也在燃烧。

“Now what do we do?” Jack said. “我们现在该怎么办？”杰克说。

“We have to get the sword to Merlin fast, ” said Annie. “The sun’s about to go down.” “我们必须赶快把剑送到梅林那里，”安妮说。 “太阳快要落山了。”

“Right,” said Jack. “Before nightfall. Right.” “对，”杰克说。 “在天黑之前。对。”

Suddenly Teddy and Kathleen barked a warning. Jack and Annie looked up. 突然，泰迪和凯瑟琳大声喊了起来。 杰克和安妮抬头看去。

The water had begun to ripple. The ripples started at the edge of the cove and swelled toward the center. Waves crashed against the small rocky island, sending up great showers of spray. 水开始泛起涟漪。 涟漪从海湾边缘开始，向中心扩散。 海浪撞在那座小岩石岛上，溅起了大片水花。

“What’s going on?” said Jack. “发生什么事了？”杰克说。

“Maybe a bad storm’s coming!” said Annie. “可能要来一场暴风雨了！”安妮说。

Barrh! Barrh! Kathleen and Teddy tried to haul themselves onto the rock, but the waves pushed them away. 巴尔！巴尔！ 凯瑟琳和泰迪努力想爬到岩石上，但海浪把他们推了回去。

“We’ve got to help them!” said Annie. She and Jack tried to help the seals out of the rough water. Their hands slipped over the sleek seal skin, and they nearly toppled into the water themselves. “我们必须帮他们！”安妮说。 她和杰克试着把海豹从汹涌的海水中救出来。 他们的手在光滑的海豹皮上打滑，差点自己也掉进水里。

The waves kept rolling and splashing. Barrh! Barrh! The two seals were swept away from the rocky island. 海浪继续翻滚着，溅起水花。巴尔！ 巴尔！ 两只海豹被冲离了岩石小岛。

“Look!” cried Annie. “The land is moving!” “看！”安妮喊道。 “土地在动！”

Jack looked at the strange green shoreline surrounding the cove. It was moving! It shifted from side to side, then slithered forward! 杰克看着环绕着海湾的奇怪绿色海岸线。它在动！ 它左右移动，然后向前滑行！

A great roaring noise rocked the cove. A monstrous head rose above the surface of the water. 一声巨大的咆哮声震撼了海湾。 一个巨大的头颅从水面上升了起来。

The green hills were not hills at all. They were the coils of a giant sea serpent’s body! 那些绿色的山丘根本就不是山丘。 它们是巨大海蛇身体的 coils！
