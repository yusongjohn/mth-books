2. The Water Knight 2. 海上骑士

A salty breeze blew into the tree house. Seagulls screeched overhead. Jack and Annie looked out the tree house window. 一股咸咸的海风吹进了树屋。 海鸥在头顶上尖叫着飞过。 杰克和安妮从树屋的窗户往外看。

They were high in the branches of a gnarled old tree. The tree was on a sea cliff beneath snowcapped mountains. The mountains overlooked a rocky coast. There was no sign of human life anywhere. 他们正站在一棵古老扭曲的树的高处枝桠间。 这棵树位于一座海崖上，海崖下是白雪皑皑的山脉。 这些山脉俯瞰着一块崎岖的海岸。 这里完全没有人类生活的迹象。

“It looks wild and lonely here,” said Annie. “这里看起来又荒野又孤独，”安妮说。

“Really lonely,” said Jack. “I wonder where Merlin and Teddy are.” “真的很孤独，”杰克说。 “我真想知道梅林和泰迪去哪儿了。”

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “They were in the trunk of our tree last time. Let’s look for them in this one.” She climbed down the rope ladder. “我不知道，”安妮说。 “上次他们就在我们树的树干里。 我们在这棵树里找找看吧。”她爬下了绳梯。

Jack crammed the seashell in his pocket and followed her. 杰克把贝壳塞进口袋，然后跟着她。

“Merlin?” Annie called. “Teddy?” “梅林？”安妮喊道。 “泰迪？”

Jack and Annie circled the gnarled tree trunk, but there was no sign of an entrance into the magical chamber of Merlin the magician. They circled the tree again. Jack tapped the bark in several places. 杰克和安妮绕着那棵扭曲的树干转了一圈，但没有发现通往梅林法师魔法密室的入口。 他们又绕着树转了一圈。 杰克在好几个地方敲了敲树皮。

“I have a feeling no one lives inside this tree,” said Annie. “我觉得这棵树里没人住，”安妮说。

“I think you’re right,” said Jack. “我想你是对的，”杰克说。

“Maybe they’re down by the water,” said Annie. “也许他们是在水边，”安妮说。

Annie and Jack walked a few feet to the edge of the sea cliff and looked down at the rugged coast. They saw three coves, separated from each other by rocky cliffs. The cliffs were filled with shadowy crags and the dark mouths of caves. 安妮和杰克走到海崖边，走了几步，然后向下看去，看到了崎岖的海岸。 他们看见了三个海湾，彼此之间由岩石峭壁隔开。 峭壁上布满了阴暗的岩壁，还有黑暗的洞口。

In the first cove, sunlit water flowed from the open sea through a small space between the cliffs, then washed onto a pebbly shore. 在第一个海湾里，阳光照耀的海水从开放的海中，通过峭壁之间的一小块空隙流进来，然后冲刷到布满鹅卵石的岸边。

The second cove was smaller, but looked much like the first. 第二个海湾更小，但看起来和第一个很像。

The third cove was different. The farthest away, it was surrounded by a ring of bright green hills. A thin white mist hung over its milky-green waters. 第三个海湾不一样。 它离得最远，被一圈翠绿色的山丘环绕着。 一层薄薄的白雾笼罩着它那乳白色带绿的水面。

“I don’t see any sign of Merlin or Teddy,” said Jack. “I guess we’ll have to get started without them.” “我没看到梅林或泰迪的任何踪迹，”杰克说。 “我想我们得在他们没来之前开始行动了。”

“Read the beginning of Merlin’s rhyme again,” said Annie. “再读一遍梅林的咒语开头部分，”安妮说。

Jack took the shell from his pocket and read aloud: 杰克从口袋里拿出贝壳，大声读道：

Before night falls on this long summer day, 在这一天漫长的夏日天黑之前。

A shining sword must find its way 一把闪亮的剑必须找到它的去路

Into your hands and out of the gloom— 进入你的手中，走出黑暗——

Or Camelot’s king shall meet his doom. 否则卡梅洛特的国王将面临毁灭。

Jack looked up at the sky. The sun was almost directly overhead. “It must be around noon now,” he said. 杰克抬头看向天空。 太阳几乎正挂在头顶上。 “现在应该差不多是中午了，”他说。

“We don’t have much time,” said Annie. “What do we do first?” “我们没剩多少时间了，”安妮说。 “我们第一步该做什么？”

Jack looked back at the rhyme and read aloud: 杰克回头看了看那首诗，大声读道：

To begin your quest for this Sword of Light, 开始你的寻剑之旅，寻找这把光之剑。

Call for the help of the Water Knight. 呼唤水之骑士的帮助。

“Oh, easy,” said Annie. “哦，这很简单，”安妮说。

“It is?” said Jack. “是吗？”杰克说。

“Sure,” said Annie. “If he’s a Water Knight, he’s probably down by the water.” She started down the steep, rocky hill that led to the nearest cove. “当然，”安妮说。 “如果他是水之骑士，他可能就在水边。”她开始沿着通往最近海湾的陡峭岩石小路往下走。

Jack stuffed the shell back in his pocket. “But who is the Water Knight?” he yelled as he followed Annie. 杰克把贝壳又放回口袋里。 “但是水之骑士是谁？”他一边跟着安妮一边喊道。

“It doesn’t matter,” she yelled back. “We just have to go down to the water and call for his help.” “这不重要，”她回喊道。 “我们只需要去到水边，请求他的帮助。”

They climbed over large boulders toward the cove. The boulders were slippery, but Jack’s sneakers kept him from sliding. A damp breeze blew from the sea. It made his skin and clothes feel clammy. 他们爬过巨大的岩石，朝海湾走去。 岩石很滑，但杰克的运动鞋让他没有滑倒。 一阵潮湿的海风吹来。 让他的皮肤和衣服都感觉湿漉漉的。

When they got to the shore, Jack wiped the mist off his glasses and looked around. The wide beach was covered with silvery pebbles, shells, and rows of sea foam. Sandpipers and gulls picked at tangled ribbons of soggy seaweed. 当他们到达岸边时，杰克擦掉眼镜上的雾气，环顾四周。 宽广的海滩上铺满了银色的鹅卵石、贝壳和一排排海浪泡沫。 沙鸻和海鸥在缠结的湿海藻丝带间翻找食物。

“The tide must be out,” said Jack. He studied the cliffs above the cove. “I don’t know how a knight can get down here. A horse could never make it over all those rocks.” “潮水一定退下去了，”杰克说。 他仔细观察着海湾区上方的悬崖。 “我不知道骑士怎么才能下来这里。 一匹马永远不可能翻过那些岩石。”

“Let’s just do what the rhyme tells us,” said Annie, “and see what happens.” “我们还是按照童谣说的去做吧，”安妮说，“然后看看会发生什么。”

Annie stretched out her arms. She closed her eyes. She raised her head toward the sky and shouted: “O Water Knight, whoever you are, come here and help Jack and Annie!” 安妮伸出了她的双臂。 她闭上了眼睛。 她仰起头朝天空喊道：‘水之骑士，不管你是谁，请过来帮杰克和安妮！’

“Oh, brother,” Jack muttered to himself. “哦，天哪，”杰克小声对自己说。

Suddenly they heard the wild cries of seagulls. “Jack, look!” said Annie. She pointed toward the middle of the cove. 突然，他们听到了海鸥狂野的叫声。 “杰克，快看！”安妮说。 她指着海湾的中央。

Seabirds were screeching and flapping above a giant swirl of spray and foam. The swirl was spinning just above the surface of the water, headed toward the beach! 海鸟在一大团水花和泡沫上方尖叫着飞来飞去。 那团水花和泡沫在水面上方旋转着，正朝海滩飞去！

“Wow!” said Annie. She took off running across the sand. “哇！”安妮说。 她立刻跑过沙滩。

“Come back!” shouted Jack. “回来！”杰克喊道。

“No! Come look!” said Annie. “不！” 快来看！”安妮说。

Jack hurried to the edge of the surf. 杰克赶紧跑到浪花的边缘。

Through the blur of spinning spray and foam, he saw the silver helmet of a knight appear above the water. He saw silver breast armor. Then a strange creature burst through the surface, carrying the knight on its back. 在飞溅的水花和泡沫模糊的视野中，他看见一位骑士的银色头盔浮出水面。 他看见了银色的胸甲。 接着，一个奇怪的生物从水面冲了出来，骑士骑在它的背上。

The creature had a horse’s head and neck and front legs. But instead of back legs, it had a long, silvery fish’s tail! With the Water Knight on its back, the horse creature half galloped and half swam through the cove. Seagulls shrieked wildly overhead, following the pair toward the shore. 这个生物有马的头、脖子和前腿。 但它没有后腿，而是有一条长长的、银色的鱼尾！ 骑士骑在它背上，这个马形生物一边奔跑一边游动，穿过海角。 海鸥在头顶上疯狂地尖叫着，跟着这对搭档朝岸边飞去。

As the knight drew close to the edge of the water, he looked straight at Jack and Annie. He raised his gloved hand and beckoned to them. 当骑士靠近水边时，他直直地看向杰克和安妮。 他戴上手套，朝他们挥了挥手，示意他们过来。

“Okay, we’re coming!” cried Annie. She started pulling off her sneakers. “好啊，我们这就来！”安妮喊道。 她开始脱下自己的运动鞋。

“Wait—let’s think about this!” cried Jack. “等等——我们得想清楚！”杰克喊道。

“We don’t have time!” said Annie. “He wants to help us. He’s like the stag that came for us in Camelot.” “我们没时间了！”安妮说道。 “他想帮助我们。 他就像在卡梅洛特时来接我们的那头鹿。”

“No, he isn’t,” said Jack. “He’s much weirder!” “不对，他不是，”杰克说。 “他要奇怪得多！”

But Annie tossed her sneakers onto the rocks and splashed through the shallow water. The knight held out his hand and helped her onto the strange horse creature. The creature slapped its fish’s tail against the water, sending up a fountain of spray. 但安妮把运动鞋扔到了石头上，然后踩着浅水冲了过去。 骑士伸出他的手，帮她爬上了这个奇怪的马形生物。 这个生物用鱼尾巴拍打水面，溅起了一股水花。

“Come on, Jack!” Annie yelled. “We can’t waste time!” “快点，杰克！”安妮喊道。 “我们不能浪费时间！”

Annie’s right, Jack thought. They had to find the Sword of Light before nightfall. He pulled off his sneakers and tossed them up onto the rocks near Annie’s. Then he stepped into the cold water and waded out to the knight. 安妮说得对，杰克心想。 他们必须在天黑之前找到光明之剑。 他脱下运动鞋，把它们扔到安妮的鞋子旁边的小石头上。 然后他走进冰冷的水里，蹚水走到骑士那里。

Annie helped pull Jack up onto the horse creature. He sat on its scaly tail and clung to Annie while she held on tightly to the Water Knight’s tunic. 安妮帮忙把杰克拉上了马形生物。 他坐在它鳞片覆盖的尾巴上，紧紧抓着安妮，而安妮则紧紧抓住水骑士的外衣。

The silvery fish’s tail slapped the water. A shower of spray rained down on Jack. He closed his eyes. “Onward,” he said faintly. 银色的鱼尾巴拍打着水面。 水花像雨一样洒在杰克身上。 他闭上了眼睛。 “继续前进，”他轻声说道。

The Water Knight turned away from the shore. With more slaps of its tail, the horse creature began galloping and swimming across the cove. The seagulls shrieked wildly as they swooped after them. 水骑士转身离开了岸边。 随着尾巴一次次拍打水面，那匹马一样的生物开始在海湾里奔跑和游泳。 海鸥们尖叫着，俯冲着追赶他们。

Bumping up and down, Jack clung desperately to Annie. He kept his eyes squeezed shut and tried hard not to fall off. 上下颠簸着，杰克紧紧抓住安妮。 他努力睁不开眼睛，拼命不想掉下去。

As they sped across the cove, the Water Knight guided his steed steadily over each ripple and wave. The bumpy ride soon turned into a smooth one. 当他们飞速穿过海湾时，水骑士稳稳地引导着他的坐骑，越过每一个涟漪和波浪。 颠簸的旅程很快就变得平稳了。

“This is great!” cried Annie. “太棒了！”安妮喊道。

Jack opened his eyes. With the wind and spray whipping his face and hair, he began to feel more excited than scared. 杰克睁开了眼睛。 随着风和浪花拍打着他的脸和头发，他开始感到兴奋多过害怕。

“I’ll bet he’s taking us to the Sword of Light!” cried Annie. “We’ll be done with our mission in no time!” “我敢打赌他正带我们去光之剑！”安妮喊道。 “我们马上就能完成任务了！”

That would be too easy, thought Jack. But as they sped over the waves, he grew hopeful. Maybe she’s right. Maybe itwillbe easy, he thought. Not all our missions have to be hard.But what about the rest of the stuff in the rhyme? What about—? 杰克心想，这未免太容易了。 但随着他们飞快地在海浪上前进，他开始感到希望。 也许她说得对。 他心想，也许这真的很容易。 不是所有任务都必须很难。但那首诗里还有别的内容呢？比如——？

Before Jack could finish his thought, the strange horse creature stopped and reared up. Jack and Annie tumbled over its fish’s tail and splashed into the cold water. 杰克还没说完，那个奇怪的马形生物停下了，然后猛地扬起了前蹄。 杰克和安妮从它的鱼尾上翻滚过去，扑通一声掉进了冰冷的水里。

They sank for a moment and then bobbed back up to the surface, frantically treading water. They looked up at the Water Knight and his horse. 他们沉了一小会儿，然后又浮上水面，拼命地踩水。 他们抬头看向水骑士和他的马。

The knight lifted his arm into the air. He pointed to a pile of boulders near the base of a nearby cliff. Then he spread the fingers of his gloved hand in a gesture of farewell. 骑士抬起手臂，指向空中。 他指着附近悬崖底部的一堆巨石。 然后，他张开戴着手套的手指，做出告别的姿势。

“Bye! Thanks!” shouted Annie. “再见！ 谢谢！”安妮喊道。

The horse creature slapped its fish’s tail and took off in a fountain of spray and foam. With the gulls circling above them, the mysterious pair whirled toward the passage between the cliffs that led out to the sea. In a moment, they vanished into the open water beyond. 那头马形生物拍打着鱼尾，溅起一片水花，然后飞快地冲了出去。 海鸥在他们头顶盘旋，那对神秘的伙伴朝悬崖之间的通道飞去，那里通往大海。 转眼间，他们就消失在了前方的开阔水域中。
