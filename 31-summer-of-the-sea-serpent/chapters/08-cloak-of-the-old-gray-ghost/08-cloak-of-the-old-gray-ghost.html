8. Cloak of the Old Gray Ghost 8. 老灰鬼的斗篷

The four seals paddled with ease through the sunlit waters of the Selkie Cove and into the Jellyfish Cave. The water in the cave was cold and murky. But in his seal body, Jack was warm; and with his seal eyes, he could see clearly. 四只海豹轻松地在阳光照耀的塞尔基湾水中游动，然后游进了水母洞。 洞里的水又冷又浑浊。 但以海豹的身体，杰克感到温暖；以海豹的眼睛，他看得一清二楚。

As they swam farther and farther into the cave, the jellyfish began to appear. At first there were just a few. Then there were hundreds … then thousands … pink jellyfish, purple jellyfish, orange and chocolate-colored jellyfish … jellyfish as big as umbrellas and as small as pennies … jellyfish shaped like bells, saucers, parachutes, mushrooms, cannonballs … jellyfish as bright as candle flames and jellyfish as clear as glass. 当他们游得越来越深，水母开始出现了。 最开始只有几只。 接着变成了数百只……然后是数千只……粉红色的水母，紫色的水母，橙色和巧克力色的水母……像伞一样大的水母，像硬币一样小的水母……像铃铛、盘子、降落伞、蘑菇、炮弹形状的水母……像蜡烛火焰一样明亮的水母，还有像玻璃一样透明的水母。

Some of the jellyfish pulsed in and out as they swam. Others silently drifted by, their long stinging tentacles trailing behind them. As Jack swam among the jellyfish, he wasn’t afraid at all. His tough seal skin protected him completely. 有些水母在游泳时一缩一胀地进进出出。 其他的则悄无声息地飘过，长长的带刺触手在它们身后拖着。 杰克在水母中间游动时，一点也不害怕。 他坚韧的海豹皮肤把他保护得严严实实。

Finally Kathleen led Jack, Annie, and Teddy through a narrow passage of the cave and into the milky-green waters of the third cove. 最后，凯瑟琳带领杰克、安妮和泰迪穿过洞穴的一条狭窄通道，进入了第三个海湾那牛奶般绿色的水域。

The four seals poked their heads above the surface of the water and took deep gulps of air. Jack’s whiskers twitched as he looked around the Cove of the Stormy Coast. 四只海豹把头探出水面，深深吸了一口气。 杰克环顾着风暴海岸海湾，他的胡须微微颤动。

The cove was completely silent. It was lit with a hazy, warm light. The water was flat calm, without a single ripple. Circling the cove were strange green hills that shimmered in the afternoon light. Snowcapped mountains loomed above the hills. Jack could see the tree with the magic tree house on a distant sea cliff. 海湾里完全安静。 这里被一种朦胧而温暖的光照射着。 水面平静得没有一丝涟漪。 环绕着海湾的是些奇怪的绿色山丘，在下午的阳光下闪闪发光。 雪白的山峰耸立在山丘之上。 杰克能看到远处海崖上那棵有魔法树屋的树。

Climb onto those rocks and dry off! Kathleen barked. 爬到那些岩石上，把身子晾干！凯瑟琳喊道。

They all swam to a small rocky island in the middle of the cove. Jack hoisted his blubbery, tear-shaped body out of the water. He flopped beside the others and puffed and groaned. The seal body that had felt so graceful underwater now felt heavy and awkward. 他们全都游到了海湾中央的一座小岩石岛上。 杰克把自己肥嘟嘟、像泪滴一样的身体从水里拉了出来。 他扑通一声躺在其他人旁边，喘着气，呻吟着。 刚才在水里感觉那么灵活的海豹身体，现在却变得又重又笨拙。

Jack’s skin began to feel tighter and tighter in the sunlight. Almost before he knew it, the skin had slipped from his body like old wrapping paper. He was human again—lying on the rock in his shorts and T-shirt. He sat up and pushed his glasses into place. 杰克的皮肤在阳光下开始变得越来越紧。 几乎就在他意识到之前，皮肤已经像旧的包装纸一样从他身上滑落了。 他又变回人类了——穿着短裤和T恤，躺在岩石上。 他坐起来，把眼镜推到正确的位置。

“That was great!” said Annie. “太棒了！”安妮说。

Jack looked at her. The same magic had happened to Annie and the others. They were all human again. 杰克看着她。 同样的魔法也发生在安妮和其他人身上。 他们都变回人类了。

“Yeah, it was,” Jack said happily. He looked around. “And I don’t see any signs of a winter storm here.” “是啊，确实很棒，”杰克开心地说。他环顾四周。 “不过，这里好像没有暴风雪的迹象。”

“No, but still, I do not like the looks of this place,” said Teddy. The boy sorcerer frowned as he peered around at the cove. “It gives me the quivers.” “没有，不过我还是不太喜欢这个地方，”泰迪说。 那个男孩巫师皱着眉头，环顾着这个海湾。 “它让我浑身发抖。”

Jack glanced anxiously at Teddy. If Teddy was afraid, something must be wrong. Teddy never acted as if he were afraid of anything. 杰克紧张地看了看泰迪。 如果泰迪害怕了，一定有什么不对劲。 泰迪从不表现得好像害怕任何东西。

“Well, the day wears on,” Teddy said, looking up at the sinking sun. “Let us hurry ashore to find the sword, so we can leave this cove as quickly as possible.” “好吧，天色越来越晚了，”泰迪说，抬头看着逐渐落下的太阳。 “我们赶紧上岸去找那把剑，这样我们就能尽快离开这个海湾了。”

“I fear our search may be difficult,” said Kathleen. “Look.” “我担心我们的搜索可能会很困难，”凯瑟琳说。 “看。”

A dense gray fog was rolling down from the mountains. As they watched, the fog hid the sea cliff where their tree house had landed. Within moments, the fog had completely covered the green hills. Then it swept over the windless waters of the cove. 一团浓密的灰色雾气正从山上滚下来。 当他们看着时，雾气遮住了他们树屋降落的海崖。 转眼间，雾就完全覆盖了绿色的山丘。 接着，它席卷了这个没有风的海湾的水面。

“Oh, dear,” said Kathleen. “The Cloak of the Old Gray Ghost is upon us.” “哦，天哪，”凯瑟琳说。 “老灰鬼的斗篷已经笼罩了我们。”

“The Cloak of the Old Gray Ghost?” asked Annie. “老灰鬼的斗篷？”安妮问。

“Aye, that’s what we selkies call a very thick fog,” said Kathleen. “对，我们海豹人就是这样称呼非常浓的雾的，”凯瑟琳说。

“And that’s a line in Merlin’s rhyme!” said Annie. “ Dive ’ neath the Cloak of the Old Gray Ghost!” “这可是梅林童谣里的一句话！”安妮说。 “潜入老灰鬼的斗篷之下！”

Jack breathed a sigh of relief. “Gray Ghost” wasn’t a ghost at all! It was just another name for fog. “So I guess we just go ashore and look for the sword under the fog somewhere,” he said. 杰克松了一口气。 “老灰鬼”根本就不是鬼！ 这只不过是雾的另一个名字。 “所以我想我们只能上岸，在雾中某处寻找那把剑，”他说。

“The rhyme says we dive,” said Teddy. “So perhaps we do not go ashore at all.” “韵文说我们要跳水，”泰迪说。 “所以也许我们根本不用上岸。”

“Oh, right,” said Jack. “Does that mean we get to turn back into seals?” Shivering in the cold fog, he was eager to slip back into his warm, protective seal body. “哦，对，”杰克说。 “这意思是我们可以变回海豹吗？”他冷得发抖，在浓雾中，他迫不及待想回到温暖、保护他的海豹身体里。

“I fear we cannot all be seals,” Kathleen said, “for how could we grasp a heavy sword with our flippers?” “我担心我们不能都变成海豹，”凯瑟琳说，“因为我们的鳍怎么才能握住一把沉重的剑呢？”

“You and Teddy be the seals, then, and look for the sword,” said Annie. “Jack and I can swim down and grab it after you show us where it is.” “那你就和泰迪当海豹，去寻找那把剑，”安妮说。 “杰克和我可以游下去，在你们告诉我们剑在哪里之后去捡起它。”

Jack was about to say he’d rather be a seal. But before he could speak, Teddy piped up. “Excellent plan!” he said. 杰克正要说他宁愿当一只海豹。 但还没等他说话，泰迪就插话了。 “好主意！”，他说。

“Indeed,” said Kathleen. “Your friends are very brave.” She turned and smiled warmly at Jack. “确实如此，”凯瑟琳说。 “你的朋友非常勇敢。”她转过身，对杰克温暖地笑了笑。

“Uh, sure, no problem,” he said. “呃，当然，没问题，”他说。

“Let us make haste,” said Teddy. Hidden by the fog, he and Kathleen pulled their seal skins back on. A moment later, Jack heard Teddy call out, “Farewell, friends!” Then Kathleen spoke her selkie spell. Her words were followed by two splashes. “让我们赶快行动，”泰迪说。 隐藏在雾中，他和凯瑟琳又穿上了他们的海豹皮。 片刻之后，杰克听到泰迪喊道：“再见，朋友们！”然后凯瑟琳念出了她的海豹咒语。 她的话之后跟着两声水花声。

“What do we do now?” Jack asked. “我们现在该怎么办？”杰克问。

“We wait for them to find the sword,” said Annie. “我们等他们找到那把剑，”安妮说。

“I hope they hurry,” said Jack, shivering. “我希望他们快点，”杰克说着，打了个寒战。

“Me too,” said Annie. “我也是，”安妮说。

They listened for the barking of the seals. They listened and listened … . 他们听着海豹的叫声。 他们听着，听着……

“I wonder what time it is,” said Annie. “我想知道现在几点了，”安妮说。

“Impossible to tell,” said Jack. “没法知道，”杰克说。

“They’d better—” “他们最好——”

“Shh!” said Jack. “嘘！”杰克说。

He heard a faint seal bark, then another and another. But in the thick fog, he couldn’t tell where the barks were coming from. “Where are they?” he said. 他听到一声微弱的海豹叫声，接着又是一声，又是一声。 但是浓雾中，他分不清声音是从哪里传来的。 “他们在哪儿？”他说。

“I think they’re over there! ” said Annie. “我觉得他们在那边！”安妮说。

Splash! Annie had jumped in. Jack couldn’t see her in the fog. 扑通！ 安妮跳进去了。 杰克在浓雾中看不见她。

“Annie, where are you?” he shouted. “安妮，你在哪儿？”他喊道。

“Here!” Annie yelled through the ghostly mist. “Come on!” “在这儿！”安妮穿过幽灵般的雾气喊道。 快点！

Jack put his glasses down on the rocks. Then he slowly lowered himself into the water. As he started after Annie, his human body felt thin and frail compared to his powerful seal body. He couldn’t swim nearly as fast. He couldn’t hold his breath underwater for nearly as long. And he was freezing cold. 杰克把眼镜放在了岩石上。 然后他慢慢地把自己放进了水里。 当他开始追赶安妮时，他的人类身体和他强大的海豹身体相比，显得又瘦又弱。 他游得没有那么快了。 他也不能在水下憋那么久的气了。 而且他冷得发抖。

The seal barks grew louder and louder. Barrh! Barrh! 海豹的叫声越来越响亮了。巴尔！

Jack didn’t see their two friends until he nearly bumped into them. Teddy and Kathleen were swimming in a tight circle, barking excitedly. 杰克差点撞上他们，才看到他们的两个朋友。 泰迪和凯瑟琳正在一个圈子里游泳，兴奋地叫着。

“Did you find the sword?” Annie shouted at them. “Is it here?” “你们找到剑了吗？”安妮朝他们喊道。 “它在这里吗？”

The seals barked and dove under the water. Jack and Annie took deep breaths and followed. 海豹们大声叫着，然后潜入水中。 杰克和安妮深吸一口气，跟了上去。

The seals swam quickly to the sandy bottom of the cove. They circled a shimmering object sticking out of the sand. 海豹们快速游向海湾底部的沙地。 它们绕着一个从沙子里冒出来的闪闪发光的东西转了一圈。

It was the golden handle of a sword. 那是一把剑的金色剑柄。
