4. Web Walk 4. 网上行走

Jack grabbed Annie’s hand. “Whatever you do, don’t look up,” he said. 杰克抓住了安妮的手。 “无论发生什么，都不要抬头看，”他说。

Annie looked up. 安妮抬头看了。

“AHHHHH!” she screamed. She started to leap off the ledge into the churning waters. But then another wave crashed wildly against the walls. “啊啊啊！”她尖叫起来。 她开始从岩石边缘跳进翻腾的水中。 但就在这时，另一波巨浪猛烈地撞向墙壁。

Jack held Annie back. “Don’t jump!” he shouted. “You’ll drown!” 杰克拉住安妮。 “别跳！”他喊道。 “你会淹死的！”

Above the sound of the rushing waters, a loud whisper echoed around the cave: Stay! Stay! Stay! The Spider Queen was staring down at them with her eight red eyes. 在湍急的水流声之上，一个响亮的低语在洞穴中回荡：留下！留下！ 留下！ 蜘蛛女王正用她那八只红色的眼睛盯着他们看。

As Jack and Annie stared back in horror, the giant spider shot a web strand as thick as a rope straight toward them. Jack and Annie ducked. The strand stuck to the wall. 当杰克和安妮惊恐地回望时，那只巨大的蜘蛛朝他们射出了一根像绳子一样粗的蛛丝。 杰克和安妮迅速蹲下身。 那根丝线粘在了墙上。

“What’s she doing?” cried Annie. “她在干什么？”安妮喊道。

“I don’t know!” said Jack. “我不知道！”杰克说。

He and Annie looked back up at the Spider Queen. She’d crawled a few feet closer to the crack in the wall. She stared down at them for a moment with her glowing eyes, then shot out another thick rope strand. 他和安妮抬头看向蜘蛛女王。 她爬了几英尺，离墙上的裂缝更近了。 她用发光的眼睛盯着他们看了片刻，然后又射出了一根粗壮的丝线。

“Watch out!” shouted Jack. “小心！”杰克大喊。

He and Annie ducked. 他和安妮赶紧蹲下。

Thwack! The second web rope stuck to the ledge just a few feet away from the first. 啪！ 第二根蛛网绳粘在了离第一根只有几英尺远的岩石边缘上。

“Oh, no! Look!” shrieked Annie. She pointed up at the cave ceiling. “哦，不！” “看！”安妮尖叫道。 她指着洞穴的天花板。

The monster spider was zigzagging between the two strands of her web. She was heading straight toward them. 那只怪物蜘蛛在她的蛛丝之间来回穿梭。 她正直直地朝他们走来。

Jack and Annie screamed and pressed themselves against the wall. “We have to leave!” cried Annie. But before they could make a move, another wave crashed into the cave! The water swirled with ferocious force around the chamber. 杰克和安妮尖叫着，把自己贴在了墙上。 “我们必须离开！”安妮喊道。 但就在他们准备行动时，又一波巨浪冲进了洞穴！ 水流以凶猛的力量在洞穴里旋转着。

“We can’t leave!” cried Jack. “我们不能离开！”杰克喊道。

“We can’t stay!” cried Annie. “我们不能待在这里！”安妮喊道。

Wait! Wait! Wait! whispered the Spider Queen. 等一下！等一下！ 等一下！蜘蛛女王低声说。

The giant spider kept zigzagging between the two lines, spinning more web, coming closer and closer and closer to Jack and Annie. 那只巨大的蜘蛛在两条线之间来回穿梭，织出更多的网，越来越接近杰克和安妮。

They watched in horror, unable to speak or move. But just when she came close enough to touch them, the Spider Queen turned and scurried back up to the ceiling, leaving a giant web ladder behind her. 他们惊恐地望着，无法说话或移动。 但就在她靠近到可以碰到他们时，蜘蛛女王转过身，迅速爬回天花板，留下了一条巨大的网梯在身后。

The Spider Queen stared down at Jack and Annie with her eight red eyes. Climb! Climb! Climb! she whispered from her spot on the ceiling. 蜘蛛女王用她那八只红色的眼睛盯着杰克和安妮。爬上去！ 爬上去！爬上去！ 她从天花板上低声说。

“I think she wants to help us!” said Jack. “我想她想帮我们！”杰克说。

“No! She wants to trap us!” said Annie. “不！” 她想抓住我们！”安妮说。

The spider whispered again: Climb! Climb! Climb! 蜘蛛又小声地说：“爬上去！爬上去！” 爬上去！

Something about her voice made Jack feel certain the Spider Queen wanted to help. “She doesn’t want to hurt us!” he said. “She wants to help us escape! Besides, we don’t have any choice!” 安妮的声音让杰克确信，蜘蛛女王是想帮忙的。 “她不想伤害我们！”他说。 “她想帮助我们逃出去！ 再说，我们别无选择！

The water was rising higher and higher over their ledge. It was swirling above their ankles now. 水位一直在他们脚下的石台上不断上涨。 现在水已经漫到了他们的脚踝。

“We have to climb her web!” said Jack. “I’ll go first!” “我们必须爬她的网！”杰克说。 “我先来！”，

He reached up and grabbed one of the spider ropes. It felt damp and sticky. He pulled himself onto the bottom strands of the web ladder. 他伸手抓住了一根蜘蛛绳。 它感觉又湿又粘。 他把自己拉到了网梯的最下面几根绳子上。

“Grab on!” he shouted to Annie above the roar of the water. “We have to get to that crack in the ceiling!” “抓紧！”他在水声中朝安妮大喊。 “我们必须赶到天花板上的那个裂缝那儿！”

Annie grabbed one of the web ropes. “Eww!” she said. “It’s gross!” 安妮抓住了一根网绳。 “哇！”她说。 “好恶心！”

“Just climb!” said Jack. “快爬上去！”杰克说。

Holding tightly to the sticky strands, Jack and Annie began making their way up the Spider Queen’s web ladder. The web swayed and stretched, but it was sturdy enough to hold their weight. Its stickiness kept them from slipping and falling. 紧紧抓住粘稠的丝线，杰克和安妮开始沿着蜘蛛女王的网梯往上爬。 网在摇晃和拉伸，但足够结实，能承受他们的重量。 它的粘性让他们不会滑倒或摔下来。

Crawling and climbing, they moved higher and higher above the crashing waters. As they drew near the crack in the ceiling, Jack kept his eyes on the Spider Queen. She was watching them carefully. 爬呀爬，他们越爬越高，越过了翻腾的水流。 当他们接近天花板上的裂缝时，杰克一直盯着蜘蛛女王。 她正仔细地观察着他们。

Finally Jack reached the ceiling crack. He swung to one side of the web ladder, putting himself between Annie and the giant spider. 终于，杰克到达了天花板的裂缝。 他荡到网梯的一侧，把自己放在安妮和那只大蜘蛛之间。

“You go first,” he said. “你先走，”他说。

“Okay,” said Annie. She grabbed the rocky edge of the opening and stuck her head out of the crack. “There’s nowhere to go but into the water!” she called. “好，”安妮说。 她抓住洞口的岩石边缘，把头从裂缝里探了出来。 “除了跳进水里，没有别的路可走！”她喊道。

“How far down is it?” said Jack. “水有多深？”杰克问。

“Pretty far!” said Annie. “But I think we can make it!” “很深！”安妮说。 “但我觉得我们能过去！”

“Wait—” said Jack. “等等——”杰克说。

But Annie was already squeezing herself through the crack. 但安妮已经挤着自己穿过裂缝了。

“Annie, be careful!” said Jack. “安妮，小心点！”杰克说。

Splash! 扑通！

“Oh, man,” said Jack. He gripped the edge of the crack. Then he glanced back at the Spider Queen. “哦，天哪，”杰克说。 他抓住裂缝边缘。 然后他朝蜘蛛女王看了一眼。

Her glowing red eyes peered out at him from the shadows. Go! Go! Go! she whispered. 她发着红光的眼睛从阴影中盯着他。走！ 走！走！ 她小声说。

Jack smiled at her. “Thanks!” he said. 杰克对她笑了笑。 “谢谢！”他说。

Go! the Spider Queen whispered again. 走！ 蜘蛛女王再次小声说。

Jack pulled himself out of the darkness onto a narrow rocky ledge. The bright sun sparkled on the blue water of the small cove below. 杰克从黑暗中爬出来，到了一块狭窄的岩石边缘。 明亮的阳光在下面小海湾的蓝色水面上闪闪发光。

“Come on!” Annie shouted. She was bobbing up and down on the gentle waves. “快点！”安妮喊道。 她在轻柔的海浪上上下起伏。

Jack took off his glasses. He pinched his nose and closed his eyes. Then he jumped off the ledge. 杰克摘下了他的眼镜。 他捏了捏鼻子，闭上了眼睛。 然后他跳下了岩石边缘。
