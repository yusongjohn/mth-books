5. Barrh! Barrh! 5. 巴尔！巴尔！

Jack splashed into the blue sunlit sea. He sank to the bottom and then bobbed back up. He coughed and pushed his hair out of his eyes. 杰克扑进了阳光照耀的蓝色海里。 他沉到水底，然后又浮了上来。 他咳嗽了一下，把头发从眼睛上拨开。

Annie was treading water nearby. “Hey!” she called. 安妮在附近踩水。 “嘿！”，她喊道。

“Hey!” Jack sputtered. “嘿！”，杰克呛着说。

“I was wrong! You were right!” Annie said. She sounded thrilled. “The Spider Queen just wanted to help us!” “我错了！ 你才是对的！”，安妮说。 她听起来非常兴奋。 “蜘蛛女王只是想帮助我们！”，

“Yeah,” said Jack. He shook the seawater off his glasses and put them back on. “是啊，”，杰克说。 他把海水从眼镜上抖掉，又戴了回去。

“She must be really lonely!” said Annie. “She probably feels like she has to hide in that cave because she’s so scary-looking!” “她一定很孤单！”安妮说。 “她可能觉得因为看起来太吓人，所以必须躲进那个洞里！”

“Maybe,” said Jack. He looked around the second cove. Purple shadows stretched over a rocky seashore beneath the cliffs. The sun had moved farther across the sky. “也许吧，”杰克说。 他环顾了第二个海湾。 紫色的影子在悬崖下的岩石海岸上拉得很长。 太阳已经移动到了天空更远的地方。

“We’d better hurry!” said Jack. “What do we do now?” “我们最好快点！”杰克说。 “我们现在该怎么办？”

“Read the rhyme!” said Annie. “读那首诗！”安妮说。

Jack pulled the shell out of his pocket. Treading water, he read the next line in Merlin’s poem: 杰克从口袋里掏出贝壳。 在水里踩着水，他读了梅林诗中的下一行：

And swim with a selkie clothed in green. 并且用一件绿色的海豹皮游泳。

“What’s a selkie?” said Annie. “什么是selkie？”安妮说道。

“Who knows?” said Jack. “谁知道呢？”杰克说道。

He looked at the cliffs and the shore. Is a selkie a fish? A person? What? he wondered. Then Jack saw two dark shapes speeding like fat torpedoes below the surface of the water. They were coming straight toward him and Annie. 他看了看悬崖和海岸。 塞尔基是鱼吗？是人吗？ 什么？他想知道。 接着，杰克看到两个黑色的影子，像胖乎乎的鱼雷一样，在水下快速游动。 它们正朝杰克和安妮冲过来。

“Watch out!” Jack shouted. “小心！”杰克喊道。

“Yikes!” Annie yelled. “天哪！”安妮喊道。

Jack and Annie swam out of the way as the creatures moved swiftly past them. Suddenly two sleek gray heads popped above the surface. They had wide snouts and long white whiskers. They had tiny, wrinkly ears and big, dark eyes. 杰克和安妮赶紧游开，那些生物迅速从他们身边游过。 突然，两只光滑的灰色脑袋从水面上冒了出来。 它们有宽宽的鼻子和长长的白色胡须。 它们有小小的、皱巴巴的耳朵和大大的、黑黑的眼睛。

“Seals!” cried Annie. “海豹！”安妮喊道。

The two seals turned their heads like periscopes. When they saw Jack and Annie, they opened their mouths, showing their small, pointed teeth. They seemed to be smiling. 两只海豹像潜望镜一样转着头。 当它们看到杰克和安妮时，张开嘴巴，露出它们小小的尖牙齿。 它们看起来好像在微笑。

“Hi, guys!” said Annie. “嗨，大家！”安妮说道。

Barrh! Barrh! the seals barked. Then they rolled through the water and bumped Jack and Annie with their noses. They barked again joyfully, then darted toward the shore. 海豹们叫了起来。 接着，它们在水里翻滚着，用鼻子撞了撞杰克和安妮。 它们又高兴地叫了一声，然后朝岸边游去。

“Come on!” cried Annie. “Let’s play with them!” “快点！”安妮喊道。 “我们和它们一起玩吧！”

“We don’t have time to play!” said Jack. “我们没时间玩了！”杰克说。

But Annie had already started after the seals, swimming toward the rocky beach. 但安妮已经跟着海豹游过去，朝岩石海滩游去。

“Annie! Stop!” called Jack. “We have to hurry and find the selkie! And the Sword of Light! Before nightfall! Or King Arthur will meet his doom ….” Jack’s voice trailed off. “安妮！ 停下！”杰克喊道。 “我们必须赶快找到海豹人！ 还有光之剑！在天黑之前！ 或者亚瑟王将面临他的厄运……”杰克的声音渐渐消失了。

Annie didn’t hear him. She and the two seals had reached the shore and were getting out of the water. The seals clumsily hauled their chubby bodies onto a big rock and flopped down. Annie climbed onto the rock, too. 安妮没听到他说话。 她和两只海豹已经到达了岸边，正从水里爬出来。 海豹们笨拙地把胖乎乎的身体拖上一块大石头，然后扑通一声倒了下去。 安妮也爬上了岩石。

“Annie, come on!” shouted Jack. It could be as late as four o’clock now, he thought. And there was still a lot they had to do before nightfall. “安妮，快点！”杰克喊道。 他想，现在可能已经到四点了。 而且在天黑之前，他们还有很多事情要做。

“Let’s rest for a minute!” Annie called. She sat near the seals and patted their shiny heads as if they were big dogs. The seals barked. “我们休息一分钟吧！”安妮喊道。 她坐在海豹旁边，轻轻拍着它们闪亮的脑袋，好像它们是大狗一样。海豹们“汪汪”地叫了起来。

Actually, Jack wanted to rest, too. He felt really tired. Maybe we could rest for a minute on the rock with the seals, he thought, then search for the selkie. 其实，杰克也想休息一下。 他感觉非常累。 他想，也许我们可以和海豹一起在岩石上休息一分钟，然后再去找_selkie_。

“Well, okay,” he shouted. “But just for a quick minute!” “好吧，”他喊道。 “但只休息一会儿！”

Jack headed for shore. By the time he dragged himself out of the water, the seals were lying on their backs with their eyes closed. Their white whiskers twitched as they slept in the warm sunlight. 杰克朝岸边走去。 等他把自己从水里拖出来时，海豹们已经躺在背上，眼睛闭着睡觉了。 它们白色的胡须在温暖的阳光下轻轻颤动。

“Shhh, they’re napping,” said Annie. She lay down beside the seals and closed her eyes, too. “The sun feels really good, Jack. Come on. Lie down with us for a second.” “嘘——它们在打盹，”安妮说。 她躺在海豹旁边，也闭上了眼睛。 “太阳照在身上真舒服，杰克。来吧。 和我们一起躺一会儿。”

“Oh, brother,” Jack muttered. But the afternoon sunlight did feel good. He climbed onto the warm rock and lay down next to Annie and the seals. “哦，天哪，”杰克小声嘟囔道。 但下午的阳光确实让人感觉很舒服。 他爬到温暖的岩石上，躺在安妮和海豹旁边。

“Okay, just for a quick second,” he said. “好吧，就一会儿，”他说。

Jack closed his eyes. The sunshine felt good on his tired arms and legs. The gentle sea breeze felt especially clean and fresh after the Cave of the Spider Queen. The next thing he knew, he had faded into a deep, peaceful sleep …. 杰克闭上了眼睛。 阳光照在他累得发酸的胳膊和腿上，感觉真好。 在蜘蛛女王洞穴之后，海风温柔地吹来，感觉格外清新。 他接下来知道的下一件事是，他已经进入了深深的、平静的睡眠……
