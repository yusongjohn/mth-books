1. Summer Solstice 1. 夏至

Jack was sitting on the porch reading the newspaper. It was a warm summer day, but the porch was shady and cool. 杰克正坐在门廊上读报纸。 那天是温暖的夏日，但门廊阴凉而凉爽。

Annie poked her head out of the screen door. “Hey, Mom says she’ll drive us to the lake this afternoon,” she said. 安妮从纱门探出头来。 “嘿，妈妈说她今天下午要开车带我们去湖边，”她说。

Jack didn’t raise his eyes from the weather page. “Did you know today is the summer solstice?” he said. 杰克没有抬头看天气版。 “你知道今天是夏至吗？”他说。

“What’s that?” asked Annie. “那是什么？”安妮问。

“It’s the official first day of summer,” said Jack. “There’s more daylight today than on any other day of the year.” “那是夏天的正式第一天，”杰克说。 “今天白天比一年中其他任何一天的白天都要长。”

“Cool,” said Annie. “太酷了，”安妮说。

“Starting tomorrow, the days will get shorter and shorter,” said Jack. “从明天开始，白天会越来越短，”杰克说。

A loud screech came from overhead. 头顶传来一声尖锐的叫声。

“Look,” said Annie, “a seagull!” “看，”安妮说，“一只海鸥！”

Jack looked up. A large white gull was circling in the bright noon sky. “What’s he doing here?” asked Jack. “The ocean’s two hours away.” 杰克抬头看。 一只大白海鸥在明亮的正午天空中盘旋。 “他怎么会在这里？”杰克问。 “海洋离这儿还有两个小时。”

The gull swooped down and screeched again. 海鸥俯冲下来，又尖声叫了起来。

“Maybe he’s a messenger from Morgan or Merlin,” said Annie. “Maybe one of them sent him to tell us the tree house is finally back.” “也许他是摩根或梅林派来的信使，”安妮说。 “也许他们其中一个人派他来告诉我们树屋终于回来了。”

Jack’s heart began to pound. He put down the newspaper. “You think so?” he asked. 杰克的心开始怦怦直跳。 他放下报纸。 “你是这么想的吗？”他问。

Jack and Annie hadn’t seen the magic tree house since their Merlin Mission to a haunted castle last Halloween. Jack had begun to worry that the tree house might never come back. 杰克和安妮自从去年万圣节完成梅林任务，去 haunted castle（闹鬼的城堡）以来，就没再见过神奇的树屋了。 杰克开始担心树屋可能再也回不来了。

“Look, he’s flying toward the woods,” said Annie. “看，他正飞向树林，”安妮说。

Jack jumped up. “Okay,” he said. “Let’s go.” 杰克跳了起来。 “好吧，”他说。 “我们走吧。”

“We’ll be back soon, Mom!” Annie called. Then she and Jack dashed across their yard. They ran down their street and into the Frog Creek woods. “我们很快就回来，妈妈！”安妮喊道。 然后她和杰克冲过他们的院子。 他们跑下街道，进入蛙溪镇的树林。

The shady woods were dappled with sunlight. The air smelled fresh and clean. Jack and Annie hurried past the leafy trees, until they came to the tallest oak. High in the oak branches, the magic tree house was waiting for them. 阴凉的树林里洒满了阳光。 空气闻起来新鲜又干净。 杰克和安妮匆匆穿过茂密的树木，直到他们来到最高的橡树前。 在橡树高高的枝叶间，神奇的树屋正等着他们。

“Wow,” Jack and Annie said together. The tree house looked exactly the same as when they’d last seen it. “哇，”杰克和安妮异口同声地说。 树屋看起来和他们上次见到它时一模一样。

Annie grabbed the rope ladder and started up. Jack followed. When they climbed inside the tree house, no one was there. 安妮抓住绳梯开始往上爬，杰克跟在后面。 当他们爬进树屋时，里面一个人也没有。

“Look, our Royal Invitation is still here,” said Annie. She picked up the card that had taken them to Camelot on Christmas Eve. “看，我们的皇家邀请函还在这里，”安妮说。 她捡起了那张在圣诞节前夜带他们去卡梅洛特的卡片。

“And our leaf from Merlin is still here,” said Jack. He picked up the yellow autumn leaf that had sent them on their Halloween mission. “我们的叶子还在，”杰克说。 他捡起了那片黄色的秋叶，它曾带他们去完成万圣节的任务。

“This is new,” said Annie. She picked up a pale blue seashell. The shell was shaped like a small fan. There was writing on it. “这个是新的，”安妮说。 她捡起了一只淡蓝色的贝壳。 这个贝壳的形状像一把小扇子。 上面有字。

“Hey, this looks like Merlin’s handwriting!” said Annie. “We must be going on another mission for Merlin!” She read aloud the message from the magician: “嘿，这看起来像是梅林的字迹！”，安妮说。 “我们必须又去执行梅林的另一个任务了！”，她大声读出这位魔法师留下的信息：

图中文字：To Jack and Annie of Frog Creek: On this summer solstice, journey to a land lost in mist, to a time even before Camelot. Follow my rhyme to complete your mission.

-M. 图中文字：给弗罗格克里克的杰克和安妮：在这个夏至日，去到被薄雾笼罩的失落之地，去到甚至在卡米洛特之前的时代。跟着我的韵律完成你们的任务。-M.

Annie looked up. “What rhyme?” she said. 安妮抬起头。 “什么押韵？”，她说。

“Let me see.” Jack took the shell from her and turned it over. On the other side was a poem. Jack read the poem aloud: “让我看看。” 杰克从她手里接过贝壳，把它翻过来。 贝壳的另一面是一首诗。 杰克大声读出这首诗：

Before night falls on this long summer day, 这一天漫长的夏日即将夜幕降临。

A shining sword must find its way 一把闪亮的剑必须找到它的去向。

Into your hands and out of the gloom— 进入你的手中，走出黑暗——

Or Camelot’s king shall meet his doom. 否则卡梅洛特的国王将面临毁灭。

To begin your quest for this Sword of Light, 为了开始寻找这把光明之剑的旅程。

Call for the help of the Water Knight. 请召唤水之骑士的帮助。

Then pass through the Cave of the Spider Queen— 然后穿过蜘蛛女王的洞穴——

“Spider Queen?” Annie interrupted. She frowned. Spiders were about the only thing she was afraid of. “蜘蛛女王？”安妮打断了。她皱起了眉头。 蜘蛛是她唯一害怕的东西。

“Don’t think about it now,” said Jack. “Let’s keep going.” He read on: “现在不要去想它，”杰克说。 “我们继续往前走。”他继续读下去：

… pass through the Cave of the Spider Queen …穿过蜘蛛女王的洞穴

And swim with a selkie clothed in green. 和穿着绿色衣服的海豹人一起游泳。

Enter the Cove of the Stormy Coast, 进入风暴海岸的海湾。

Dive ’neath the Cloak of the Old Gray Ghost— 潜入老灰鬼的斗篷之下—

Jack stopped reading. “Old Gray Ghost?” he said. 杰克停下了阅读。 “老灰鬼？”他说。

“Don’t think about it now,” said Annie. “Keep going.” “现在别去想它，”安妮说。 “继续读。”

Jack read more: 杰克继续读：

Answer a question with love, not fear. 用爱来回答问题，而不是用恐惧。

With rhyme and sword, your home is near. 用韵律和剑，你的家就在附近。

Both Jack and Annie were silent for a moment. “That’s a lot to do before nightfall,” Jack finally said. 杰克和安妮都沉默了一会儿。 “在天黑之前要完成这么多事情，”杰克终于说道。

“Yeah,” said Annie, “and I’m a little worried about the spider part.” “是啊，”安妮说，“我对蜘蛛的部分有点担心。”

“And the ghost part,” said Jack. “还有幽灵的部分，”杰克说。

“Hey,” said Annie, “if we’re going on another Merlin Mission, I’ll bet Teddy will come with us! He can help us get through the scary parts.” “嘿，”安妮说，“如果我们又要执行梅林任务，我敢打赌泰迪会和我们一起去！ 他可以帮助我们度过可怕的环节。”

“Right,” said Jack. Just hearing Teddy’s name made him feel braver. “对，”杰克说。 只是听到泰迪的名字，他就感觉勇敢了一些。

“So,” said Annie. “Onward?” Onward was Teddy’s favorite word. “所以，”安妮说。 “向前？”向前是泰迪最喜欢的词。

“Onward!” said Jack. He pointed to the handwriting on the pale blue shell. “I wish we could go to the time before Camelot!” “向前！”杰克说。 他指着那个淡蓝色贝壳上的笔迹。 “我希望我们能去卡梅洛特之前的时代！”，

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止。
