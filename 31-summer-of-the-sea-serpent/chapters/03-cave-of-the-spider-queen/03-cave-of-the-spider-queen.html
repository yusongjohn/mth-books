3. Cave of the Spider Queen 3. 蜘蛛女王的洞穴

Gentle waves rippled through the cove as Jack and Annie swam to the foot of the cliff. They pulled themselves onto the boulders. Soaking wet, they sat for a moment in the warm sunlight and caught their breath. 波光粼粼的海浪轻轻荡漾，杰克和安妮游到悬崖脚下。 他们爬上了岩石。 浑身湿透的他们坐在温暖的阳光下，喘了口气。

“That was so cool!” said Annie. “太酷了！”安妮说道。

“Yeah. But why—” Jack gasped, “why did he dump us way out here? What do we do now?” “是啊。 但是为什么——”杰克喘着气说，“他为什么要把我们扔到这么远的地方？” 我们现在该怎么办？”

“Check the rhyme,” said Annie. “What happens after we call for the help of the Water Knight?” “检查一下韵文，”安妮说。 “我们请求水之骑士的帮助之后会发生什么呢？”

Jack reached into his pocket and pulled out the seashell. He read aloud: 杰克把手伸进口袋，拿出了一只贝壳。他大声读道：

Call for the help of the Water Knight. 请求水之骑士的帮助。

Then pass through the Cave of the Spider Queen. 然后穿过蜘蛛女王的洞穴。

“Oh. Right,” said Annie. She took a deep breath. “Spider Queen.” “对，”安妮说。 她深吸了一口气。 “蜘蛛女王。”

“Sorry,” Jack said softly. “But don’t worry, maybe the Spider Queen is just a person. Maybe ‘Spider Queen’ is her nickname.” “对不起，”杰克轻声说。 “不过别担心，也许蜘蛛女王只是个普通人。” 也许‘蜘蛛女王’只是她的昵称。”

“But what if she’s half a person and half a spider?” said Annie. “Like the Raven King was half a man and half a raven?” “但是，如果她一半是人，一半是蜘蛛呢？”安妮说。 “就像乌鸦王一半是人，一半是乌鸦吗？”

Jack shuddered at the memory of the monster on their last Merlin Mission. “Don’t think about that,” he said. “Time’s running out. Let’s just find this cave.” 杰克一想到上次梅林任务中遇到的怪物，就不由得打了个寒战。 “别想那件事，”他说。 “时间不多了。 我们赶紧找到这个洞穴吧。”

Annie nodded and smiled bravely. “Okay, you’re right,” she said. 安妮点了点头，勇敢地笑了笑。 “好吧，你说得对，”她说。

They stood up and started climbing barefoot around the craggy curves at the base of the cliff. As they rounded a corner, they both gasped. 他们站起来，光着脚在悬崖底部崎岖的曲线上爬来爬去。 当他们转过一个弯时，两人都惊叫了起来。

In front of them was the mouth of a cave. The mouth was covered with thick, sticky white ropes. The ropes were woven tightly together in a crisscross cobweb pattern. 他们面前是一个山洞的入口。 入口处覆盖着厚厚的、粘稠的白色绳子。 这些绳子紧紧地编织在一起，形成了一张纵横交错的蜘蛛网。

“If that’s a spiderweb, we’re in big trouble,” said Annie. “如果那是蜘蛛网，我们就有大麻烦了，”安妮说。

Jack tried to sound calm. “Um, the size of the web doesn’t actually tell us the size of the spider,” he said. “Plus, I once read that no spider on earth is bigger than a dinner plate.” 杰克试图让自己看起来很冷静。 “嗯，其实网的大小并不能告诉我们蜘蛛的大小，”他说。 “再说，我以前读过，世界上没有蜘蛛比盘子还大。”

“Yeah, and no horse on earth has a giant fish’s tail either,” said Annie. “是啊，世界上也没有马长着巨大的鱼尾巴。”安妮说。

Good point, thought Jack. “Let’s just concentrate on finding the Sword of Light before nightfall,” he said. 好主意，杰克心想。 “我们先把注意力放在找到光明之剑上，天黑之前一定要找到，”他说。

Jack picked up a stone the size of a softball. He hurled it toward the mouth of the cave. The stone sailed through the giant cobweb and into the cave, pulling the sticky rope-like strands down with it. 杰克捡起一块大小像棒球的石头。 他把石头朝洞口扔了过去。 石头穿过巨大的蜘蛛网，飞进了洞里，把粘糊糊的绳子一样的蛛丝也带了下去。

Jack turned to Annie. “Ready?” he said. 杰克转身看向安妮。 “准备好了吗？”他说。

She didn’t move. 她没有动。

Jack took her hand. “Don’t worry, I won’t let any monster spiders get you,” he said. He nodded toward the mouth of the cave. “Onward?” 杰克牵起了她的手。 “别担心，我不会让任何怪物蜘蛛碰到你的，”他说。 他朝洞口点了点头。 “向前？”

“Onward,” Annie repeated in a small voice. Then together she and Jack stepped over the fallen web and entered the Cave of the Spider Queen. “向前，”安妮用很小的声音重复道。 然后，她和杰克一起跨过那张倒下的网，走进了蜘蛛女王的洞穴。

Inside the cave, the walls were black and shiny. The ground was wet and slippery under their bare feet. 洞里的墙壁又黑又亮。 他们光着脚，脚下地面又湿又滑。

“Yikes!” Annie said, jumping back. A pale pink crab scuttled sideways across the rocky floor. “天哪！”安妮说道，跳了回去。 一只淡粉色的螃蟹在岩石地面上横着爬行。

“Don’t be afraid,” said Jack. “That’s not a spider.” “别害怕，”杰克说道。 “那不是蜘蛛。”

“I know,” said Annie. “Sorry.” “我知道，”安妮说。 “对不起。”

As they went deeper and deeper into the cave, it grew darker and darker. Finally Jack saw a faint light coming from a wide, arched passage. “That way,” he said. 当他们越来越深入洞穴时，周围变得越来越暗。 最后，杰克看到一道微弱的光从一个宽阔的拱形通道里透出来。 “那边，”他说。

They stepped through the arch into a round chamber with a high ceiling. There were several large cracks in the ceiling that let in beams of sunlight. The misty light shined on mossy green ledges and on a green, spongy-looking floor. Silver droplets dripped from above, ker- plopping into tiny pools. Squeaks and chirps came from crannies and hiding places in the walls. 他们穿过拱门，走进了一个圆形的房间，天花板很高。 天花板上有几道大裂缝，让阳光的光束照了进来。 朦胧的光线照在长满青苔的绿色石架上，也照在看起来像海绵一样的绿色地面上。 银色的水滴从上面滴落，扑通扑通地掉进小水坑里。 墙壁的缝隙和隐蔽处传来了吱吱声和叽叽声。

“What’s that noise?” asked Annie. “那是什么声音？”安妮问。

“Probably just teeny cave crickets or baby bats,” said Jack. “可能是小蟋蟀或者小蝙蝠，”杰克说。

“No, that noise,” said Annie, “the whispering noise.” “不是那个声音，”安妮说，“是那个窃窃私语的声音。”

Jack listened. Then he heard it: a low whispering. He couldn’t make out the words. It just sounded like whisper-whisper-whisper-whisper. The hair stood up on the back of his neck. Now he began to feel scared. 杰克听了听。 然后他听到了：一种低低的窃窃私语声。 他听不清说的是什么。 听起来就像‘嘘——嘘——嘘——嘘——’。 他脖子后面的头发都竖起来了。 现在他开始感到害怕了。

“This place is really creepy,” said Annie. “这个地方真的很吓人，”安妮说。

“No kidding,” said Jack. “But we don’t have to stay here long. The rhyme says we just have to pass through the cave. So let’s hurry and pass through it.” “别开玩笑了，”杰克说。 “但我们不用在这里待太久。 韵文说我们只需要穿过这个洞穴。 所以让我们赶紧穿过它。”

Jack and Annie walked through the ghostly green light of the chamber. The spongy floor squished beneath their bare feet. As they searched for an exit from the cave, they both kept an eye out for the Spider Queen. 杰克和安妮穿过了洞穴里幽灵般的绿色光线。 松软的地板在他们赤裸的脚底下发出挤压的声音。 当他们在寻找洞穴的出口时，他们都留意着寻找蜘蛛女王。

“Hey, look at the starfish,” said Annie. She pointed to a bright orange starfish clinging to the rocky ceiling. “How’d he get up there?” “嘿，看那只海星，”安妮说。 她指着岩石天花板上紧紧贴着的一只亮橙色的海星。 “他是怎么上去的？”

Before Jack could answer, a wave crashed into the chamber. Water splashed over Jack and Annie. 杰克还没来得及回答，一阵巨浪冲进了洞穴。 水花溅到了杰克和安妮身上。

“Yikes!” said Annie. She and Jack jumped onto a mossy ledge jutting out from the wall. “天哪！”安妮说道。 她和杰克跳到了从墙上突出来的长满青苔的岩石上。

The wave washed back out. There was a moment of quiet. Then another wave surged into the chamber. It splashed against the cave walls, soaking Jack and Annie again. 巨浪退了回去。 有一瞬间的安静。 接着又有一波浪涌进了洞穴。 它撞在洞壁上，再次把杰克和安妮淋湿了。

“Oh, man,” said Jack. “The tide must be coming in! Soon this whole cave will be flooded!” “哦，天啊，”杰克说道。 “潮水一定是涨起来了！ 很快，这个整个洞穴都会被水淹没！

The wave receded. For a moment all was quiet again. 波浪退去了。 有一会儿，又恢复了安静。

“We’d better leave now! ” said Jack. “Quick! Go back the way we came in!” “我们最好现在就离开！”杰克说。 “快！ “回去我们来的路！”

Jack and Annie jumped down from the ledge. But before they could escape, another wave crashed into the cave! This one swept them off their feet and pulled them down into the foamy water. 杰克和安妮从岩石上跳了下来。 但就在他们能逃出去之前，另一波巨浪冲进了洞穴！ 这一波把他们掀翻在地，拉进了泡沫般的水里。

Jack grabbed Annie’s hand. Fighting the swirling current, they climbed back up onto the mossy ledge. The water churned and gurgled around the chamber. 杰克抓住了安妮的手。 他们奋力对抗着旋转的水流，爬回了长满青苔的岩石上。 水在洞穴里翻滚着，咕嘟咕嘟地响着。

“We can’t go back the way we came in,” said Jack. “The waves will just keep knocking us down, and we’ll get caught in the current!” “我们不能从我们进来的路回去，”杰克说。 “海浪会一直把我们打倒，我们会被水流卷走！”，

“Maybe we can get out through that crack!” cried Annie. She pointed to the widest crack in the cave ceiling. It was high above the swirling water. “也许我们可以从那个裂缝出去！”，安妮喊道。 她指着洞穴顶部最宽的裂缝。 那裂缝在旋转的水面上方很高处。

“It’s too high!” said Jack. “We can’t get up there!” He looked frantically around the flooded cave, searching for another way out. Suddenly he froze in horror. “太高了！”，杰克说。 “我们上不去！”，他慌乱地环顾着被水淹没的洞穴，寻找另一条出路。 突然，他吓得一动不动。

Clinging to a ledge near the ceiling crack was the Spider Queen. She had eight red glowing eyes. She had eight long, hairy legs. And she was much bigger than a dinner plate. 紧贴着天花板裂缝旁边的岩石，蜘蛛女王正紧紧抓着不放。 她有八只红色发光的眼睛。 她有八条又长又毛茸茸的腿。 而且她比一个餐盘还要大得多。

The Spider Queen was bigger than Jack. 蜘蛛女王比杰克还要大。
