10. The Ancient Question 10. 古老的问题

“AHHH!” screamed Jack and Annie. “啊！”杰克和安妮尖叫道。

The giant sea serpent arched its long neck into the sky. Its scaly green skin glistened in the late sunlight. Staring at Jack and Annie, its eyes burned like bright yellow lamps. 巨大的海蛇把长长的脖子弯向天空。 它鳞片状的绿色皮肤在夕阳下闪闪发亮。 它盯着杰克和安妮，眼睛像明亮的黄色灯泡一样燃烧。

Jack and Annie were frozen with terror. 杰克和安妮被吓得动弹不得。

The monster opened its mouth. Inside were hideous fangs and a purple forked tongue. The serpent made a terrible hissing sound! 怪物张开了嘴巴。 里面长着可怕的獠牙和一条紫色的分叉舌头。 蛇发出可怕的嘶嘶声！

Jack and Annie huddled together on the rock. Frantic seal barks came from far away. 杰克和安妮在岩石上紧紧地抱在一起。 远处传来海豹惊慌的叫声。

“Teddy!” yelled Jack. “Kathleen!” “泰迪！”杰克喊道。 “凯瑟琳！”，

“Their magic can’t help us now!” cried Annie. “They’re stuck in their seal—” “他们的魔法现在帮不了我们了！”安妮喊道。 “他们被困在他们的封印里——”

Before she could finish, the sea serpent’s deep voice boomed through the cove: “WHO ARE YOU? AND WHY ARE YOU SSSTEALING THE SSSWORD OF LIGHT?” 她还没说完，海蛇低沉的声音就在海湾中响起：“你是谁？ 你为什么要去偷光明之剑？”

Jack was too stunned to answer. But Annie shouted back at the monster. “We’re Jack and Annie! We’re on a mission for Merlin!” 杰克太震惊了，无法回答。 但安妮朝怪物大声喊道。 “我们是杰克和安妮！ 我们是受梅林的委托来执行任务的！”

“SSSSSSSSS!” the serpent hissed angrily. Its purple tongue flicked as it coiled its body around the rocky island. Then the monster arched its neck and lowered its huge head. “嘶——”海蛇愤怒地嘶嘶作响。 它的紫色舌头一抖一抖的，身体缠绕在岩石小岛上。 然后，怪物扬起脖子，低下它巨大的头。

Again, the ancient voice boomed through the cove: “TO BE WORTHY OF THE SSSWORD, YOU MUSSST ANSSSWER THE QUESSSTION OF THE SSSWORD.” 接着，古老的声音在海湾中响起：‘要配得上这把SSS剑，你们必须回答SSS剑的问题。’

“What is the question?” shouted Annie. “问题是啥？”安妮喊道。

“SSSSSSSSS!” hissed the serpent. It turned away and circled the rocks again. Soon its scaly green body was coiled twice around the little rocky island. “SSSSSSSSS！”蛇嘶嘶地说道。 它转过身，再次绕着岩石转了一圈。 很快，它那带鳞的绿色身体绕着小岛缠了两圈。

It’s going to crush us! Jack thought with horror. Maybe they had strength enough to stab the monster before it squashed them. “Pick up the sword!” he shouted at Annie. 它要压扁我们了！ 杰克吓得想。 也许他们还有力气在被压扁之前刺死怪物。 “捡起这把剑！”他对着安妮喊道。

Together Jack and Annie lifted the mighty sword. They gripped its handle and pointed the gleaming blade at the sea serpent. 杰克和安妮一起抬起了那把巨大的剑。 他们抓住剑柄，把闪闪发光的剑尖指向海蛇。

“Don’t come any closer!” Jack yelled. “别再靠近了！”杰克大喊。

The serpent came closer. Its eyes flashed. Its forked tongue darted in and out. It opened its mouth wide. 海蛇靠近了。它的眼睛闪了一下。 它的分叉舌头来回伸缩。 它张开了嘴巴。

“Wait! Stop!” Annie shouted at the sea serpent. “Give us a chance! Ask us the question!” “等一下！ 停下！”安妮对着海蛇喊道。 “给我们一个机会！” 问我们这个问题！

The serpent closed its mouth. Then it arched its neck, and its huge head dipped down right in front of Jack and Annie. In a low, deep voice, it said, “WHAT ISSS THE PURPOSSSE OF THE SSSWORD? THAT ISSS THE ANCIENT QUESSSTION.” 蛇闭上了它的嘴。 然后它弓起脖子，巨大的头低下来，正好在杰克和安妮面前。 用低沉的声音，它说：“这把剑的目的是什么？“ “那可是个古老的问题。”

“Okay! The purpose of the sword! Just a minute!” shouted Annie. She turned to Jack. “What is the purpose of the sword?” she asked. “好呀！ 这把剑的用途！ “等一下！”安妮喊道。 她转向杰克。 “这把剑的用途是什么？”她问。

“To defeat your enemies?” Jack said. “打败你的敌人？”杰克说。

Annie shook her head. “That doesn’t sound right.” 安妮摇了摇头。 “这听起来不太对。”

“To force them to give up?” Jack said. “To slay them?” “逼他们放弃？”杰克说。 “杀死他们？”

“No, I’m sure that’s not right,” said Annie. “不，我确定这不对，”安妮说。

“SSSSSSSSS!” the serpent hissed at them impatiently. “SSSSSSSSS！”那条蛇不耐烦地朝他们嘶嘶作响。

“Then what?” said Jack. “那是什么？”杰克说。

“I don’t know,” said Annie, “but maybe—maybe it’s not about fighting at all! Look at it!” “我不知道，”安妮说，“但也许——也许这根本不是关于战斗的！看这个！”

Jack stared at the gleaming sword. Its silver blade glowed against the red sunset sky. Staring at the wondrous sword, Jack felt calmer. A strange sense of joy and relief came over him. 杰克盯着那把闪闪发光的剑。 它的银色剑刃在红色的夕阳天空中闪闪发光。 盯着这把神奇的剑，杰克感觉平静了许多。 一种奇怪的快乐和轻松感涌上心头。

“ANSSSWER THE QUESSSTION!” the serpent boomed. “答——答——答——答——答！”，蛇大声喊道。

Jack’s mind became clear. “I think I’ve got it,” he said. “Remember the line from Merlin’s rhyme? Answer a question with love, not fear.” 杰克的头脑变得清晰了。 “我想我知道了，”他说。 “还记得梅林的童谣里的那句话吗？ 用爱回答问题，而不是恐惧。”

“Yes!” said Annie. “That’s it! It’s not about fighting! It’s about not being afraid!” “对！”，安妮说。 “就是这个！ 这不是关于战斗！ 这是关于不害怕！

“ANSSSWER THE QUESSSTION!” the serpent boomed. “答……答……答……问题！”，蛇大声喊道。

Jack looked up at the serpent’s face. As he stared deep into its yellow eyes, he no longer felt any fear. It was the serpent who seemed afraid now. 杰克抬头看着蛇的脸。 当他凝视着它那黄色的眼睛时，他不再感到任何恐惧。 现在看起来像是蛇害怕了。

“The sword should not be used to harm anyone or anything!” Jack shouted. “这把剑不应该用来伤害任何人或任何东西！”杰克喊道。

“That’s right!” said Annie. “It should be used for good only!” “没错！”安妮说。 “它只能用来做善事！”，

The serpent stopped swaying. It flicked its tongue. 蛇停止了摇摆。 它吐出了舌头。

“The sword should not make people afraid!” said Jack. “It should help take away their fear! If they’re not afraid, they’ll stop fighting!” “这把剑不应该让人害怕！”杰克说。 “它应该帮助人们消除恐惧！ 如果他们不害怕，就会停止战斗！

The serpent was very still. 蛇一动不动。

“The purpose of the sword is not fighting!” Jack shouted. “The purpose of the sword is peace!” “这把剑的目的不是战斗！”杰克喊道。 “这把剑的目的是和平！
