6. The Selkie 6. 海豹人

“Wake up, lazybones! You can’t sleep all afternoon!” said a friendly voice. “醒醒，懒骨头！ 你不能整个下午都睡着！”，一个友好的声音说道。

Jack’s eyes shot open. Oh, no! he thought. What time is it? He sat up and looked around. 杰克的眼睛猛地睁开了。哦，不！ 他心想。现在几点了？ 他坐起来，环顾四周。

The seals were gone. Standing over Jack and Annie was a barefoot boy with a happy, freckled face. 海豹们不见了。 站在杰克和安妮面前的是一个赤脚的男孩，脸上布满雀斑，看起来很开心。

“Teddy!” said Jack. For a moment he forgot all about the time. “泰迪！”，杰克说道。 有一瞬间，他完全忘记了时间。

“Teddy! Teddy!” cried Annie. She leaped up and hugged the young sorcerer. “泰迪！ “泰迪！”安妮喊道。 她跳起来，拥抱了这位年轻的法师。

Teddy grinned from ear to ear. He was wearing a brown tunic. His red hair was damp. 泰迪咧嘴笑得合不拢嘴。 他穿着一件棕色的长袍。 他的红头发是湿的。

“You’re finally here!” said Jack, laughing. “你终于来了！”杰克笑着说。

“I have been here for some time,” said Teddy. “Merlin sent me early this morning. I was waiting for you on the beach when Kathleen came along and invited me to go for a swim with her.” “我早就在这里了，”泰迪说。 “梅林今早一早就派我来了。” 我正在海滩上等你，凯瑟琳走过来邀请我和她一起去游泳。”

Teddy turned to a girl standing a little farther down the beach. Like Teddy, she looked to be about thirteen. “Kathleen!” he called. “Come meet my friends!” 泰迪转向站在海滩稍远一点地方的一个女孩。 和泰迪一样，她看起来也大概是十三岁。 “凯瑟琳！”他喊道。 “来见见我的朋友！”

The girl smiled and began walking over the rocks toward Jack, Annie, and Teddy. She wore a green dress that looked as if it had been woven from grass. Her hair fell in black ringlets down to her waist like a dark waterfall. 女孩微笑着，开始沿着岩石朝杰克、安妮和泰迪走来。 她穿着一件绿色的连衣裙，看起来就像是用草编织的。 她的头发是黑色的卷发，一直垂到腰际，像一道黑暗的瀑布。

“These are my good friends Jack and Annie,” Teddy said to the girl. “They come from a faraway land.” “这些都是我的好朋友杰克和安妮，”泰迪对女孩说。 “他们来自一个遥远的国家。”

“Hello, Jack and Annie!” the girl said in a bold, friendly voice. “I am very happy to meet you! My name is Kathleen.” As she spoke, Kathleen’s large eyes sparkled with the blue of the sky and water. “你好，杰克和安妮！”那个女孩用大胆而友好的声音说道。 “我很高兴见到你们！ 我的名字是凯瑟琳。”她说着，凯瑟琳的大眼睛闪烁着天空和水的蓝色。

Jack couldn’t speak. Kathleen was the most beautiful girl he’d ever seen. 杰克说不出话来。 凯瑟琳是他见过的最漂亮的女孩。

“I like your dress,” said Annie. “我喜欢你的裙子，”安妮说道。

Kathleen laughed. “I wove it from sea grass,” she said. “I am not a very good weaver, I am sad to say.” 凯瑟琳笑了。 “我是用海草编织的，”她说。 “我很遗憾地说，我不是一个很好的编织者。”

“Do you live here?” asked Annie. “你住在这里吗？”安妮问道。

“I do,” said Kathleen, “with my nineteen sisters.” “我愿意，”凯瑟琳说，“我和我的十九个姐姐一起。”

“Nineteen sisters!” said Annie. “十九个姐姐！”安妮说。

“Yes,” said Kathleen, tossing back her long curls. “I am the youngest of us all. We live in a cave up in the cliffs.” “是的，”凯瑟琳说着，把她的长卷发甩到脑后。 “我是最小的那个。 我们住在悬崖上的一个洞穴里。”

“Cool,” said Annie. “Is your cave like the Cave of the Spider Queen?” “太酷了，”安妮说。 “你的洞穴像蜘蛛女王的洞穴吗？”

“No, no,” said Kathleen. “It is much more cheerful than Morag’s cave.” “不，不，”凯瑟琳说。 “它比莫拉格的洞穴要快乐得多。”

“So that’s her name,” said Annie. “I’m worried that she might be lonely.” “原来她的名字叫莫拉格，”安妮说。 “我担心她可能会感到孤单。”

“Ah, do not worry,” said Kathleen. “Morag has many friends—bats, crabs, starfish. But it is kind of you to be concerned about her.” She smiled at Annie. “啊，别担心，”凯瑟琳说。 “莫拉有很多朋友——蝙蝠、螃蟹、海星。 但你这么关心她，真的很善良。”她对安妮笑了笑。

Kathleen’s friendly manner finally gave Jack the courage to speak. He cleared his throat. “The Water Knight was really cool, too,” he said. 凯瑟琳友好的态度终于给了杰克说话的勇气。 他清了清嗓子。 “水骑士也很酷，”他说。

“The Water Knight?” said Kathleen. “水骑士？”凯瑟琳说。

“Yeah,” he said, “the guy who carried us across the first cove.” “对，”他说，“就是那个带我们穿过第一个海湾的人。”

Kathleen looked puzzled. 凯瑟琳看起来很困惑。

“His horse had a fish’s tail!” said Annie. “他的马有一条鱼的尾巴！”安妮说。

“How strange,” said Kathleen. “I often swim in that cove, but I’ve never seen or heard of such a knight.” “真奇怪，”凯瑟琳说。 “我经常在那个海湾游泳，但我从来没见过也没听说过这样的骑士。”

“Have you lived here a long time?” asked Annie. “你在这里住很久了吗？”安妮问。

“Always,” said Kathleen. “一直住在这里，”凯瑟琳说。

“Kathleen is a selkie,” said Teddy. “凯瑟琳是个塞利基人，”泰迪说。

“A selkie? ” said Jack and Annie together. “塞利基人？”杰克和安妮一起说。

“Yes,” said Kathleen, laughing. “是的，”凯瑟琳笑着说。

“You’re in Merlin’s rhyme!” said Annie. “It says, Swim with a selkie clothed in green.” “你在梅林的童谣里！”安妮说。 “上面写着，和穿着绿色衣服的塞利基人一起游泳。”

“Merlin’s rhyme?” asked Teddy. “梅林的韵文？”泰迪问。

“Merlin gave us a rhyme to help us find the Sword of Light,” said Jack. “梅林给了我们一首韵文，帮我们找到光明之剑，”杰克说。

The smile left Kathleen’s face. Her beautiful eyes darkened. “You have come in search of the Sword of Light?” she said. “Oh, dear ….” 凯瑟琳脸上的笑容消失了。 她美丽的眼睛暗淡下来。 “你们是来寻找光明之剑的？”她说。 “哦，天哪……”

“What’s wrong?” said Jack. “怎么了？”杰克说。

“Many have passed through here seeking the Sword of Light,” said Kathleen. “But as soon as anyone enters the cove beyond the Jellyfish Cave, terrible winter storms seem to burst out of nowhere. Even in summer, the storms have icy winds and rain. No seekers of the sword have ever survived these gales.” “很多人曾来这里寻找光明之剑，”凯瑟琳说。 “但只要有人进入水母洞后面的海湾，似乎就会突然爆发可怕的冬季风暴。 即使在夏天，风暴也有冰冷的风和雨。 “从来没有人能在这些狂风中活下来。”

“Have you ever been to the cove beyond the Jellyfish Cave?” asked Jack. “你去过水母洞后面的海湾吗？”杰克问。

Kathleen shook her head. “My older sisters have always forbidden me to go there,” she said. “In fact, no selkie has ever dared swim into the Cove of the Stormy Coast.” 凯瑟琳摇了摇头。 “我姐姐们一直禁止我去那里，”她说。 “事实上，从来没有海豹人敢游进风暴海岸湾。”

“Cove of the Stormy Coast?” said Annie. “That’s the next line in Merlin’s rhyme! Enter the Cove of the Stormy Coast!” “风暴海岸湾？”安妮说。 “那是梅林韵文里的下一句！ 进入风暴海岸的海湾！“

“What is Merlin’s rhyme?” asked Teddy. “梅林的咒语是什么？”泰迪问。

“Here,” said Jack. “Look.” “在这里，”杰克说。 “看。”

He handed the seashell to Teddy. 他把贝壳递给了泰迪。

The boy sorcerer quickly read Merlin’s message and rhyme. Then he looked at the sky. “The sun moves on,” he said. “Let us make haste! We must find the sword before nightfall! Or someday Arthur will meet his doom!” 这个男孩法师很快读完了梅林的信息和韵文。 然后他抬头看向天空。 “太阳已经移动了，”他说。 “我们得快点！ 我们必须在天黑前找到那把剑！ 否则有一天亚瑟将面临毁灭！”

“Wait a minute,” said Annie. She looked at Kathleen. “The rhyme says we’re supposed to swim with you. Will you come with us?” “等一下，”安妮说。 她看着凯瑟琳。 “这句诗说我们应该和你一起游泳。” 你和我们一起去吗？

Kathleen looked at them for a long moment. Then she stood up and tossed back her curls. Her eyes were bright. “I have always wanted to explore that cove,” she said. 凯瑟琳盯着他们看了好一会儿。 然后她站了起来，甩了甩她的卷发。 她的眼睛亮晶晶的。 “我一直都想探索那个海湾，”她说。

“Hurray!” said Teddy. “You will be the first selkie to do so! A grand adventure awaits us all! Onward!” “太棒了！”，泰迪说。 “你将是第一个这么做的人鱼！ 一场伟大的冒险正等着我们所有人！前进吧！”，

“Hold on,” said Jack. “What about that Jellyfish Cave?” “抓紧了，”杰克说。 “那条水母洞怎么样？”

“You need not worry about the jellyfish,” said Kathleen. “They cannot harm us.” “你们不用担心水母，”凯瑟琳说。 “它们不会伤害我们。”

“They cannot?” said Jack. “不会吗？”杰克问。

“No,” said the selkie, “not if we all change into seals.” “不会，”水妖说，“只要我们都变成海豹。”
