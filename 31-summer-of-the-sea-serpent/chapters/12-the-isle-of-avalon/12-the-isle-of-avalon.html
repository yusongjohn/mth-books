12. The Isle of Avalon 12. 阿瓦隆岛

“Now what?” Jack asked. “现在怎么办？”杰克问。

“Now I thank you,” said a deep voice. “现在我要感谢你们，”一个深沉的声音说道。

“Merlin!” cried Annie. “梅林！”安妮喊道。

Merlin stepped out of the shadows. He wore his red magician’s cloak. His long white beard shined in the radiant glow of the sword. 梅林从阴影中走了出来。 他穿着红色的魔术师斗篷。 他长长的白色胡须在剑发出的明亮光芒下闪闪发光。

“You brought the Sword of Light out of the gloom just in time,” he said, “before nightfall on the summer solstice.” “你们刚好在夏至日的黄昏前把光之剑从黑暗中带了出来，”他说，“就在夏至日黄昏之前。”

“Why did we have to get it on the summer solstice?” said Jack. “为什么我们偏偏要在夏至日拿到它呢？”杰克问道。

“That is the day when the powers of the Ice Wizard of Winter are weakest,” said Merlin. “这一天，冬之冰巫师的力量最弱，”梅林说。

“The Ice Wizard of Winter?” said Annie. “Does the sword belong to him? Did we just steal it from him?” “冬之冰巫师？”安妮说。 “这把剑是他的吗？ 我们刚刚是不是从他那里偷走了它？”

“No,” said Merlin. “Long ago, the Ice Wizard stole the sword from the Lady of the Lake and brought it to his kingdom high above the North Sea.” Merlin pointed to the snowcapped mountains beyond the rocky coast. “不，”梅林说。 “很久以前，冰巫师从湖中女士那里偷走了剑，带到了他位于北海之上的王国。”梅林指着岩石海岸外那白雪皑皑的山脉。

“The wizard soon discovered that the Sword of Light was useless to him, for the Lady of the Lake had placed a spell upon it that made it powerful only in the hands of worthy mortals. Still, the wizard refused to part with it. He buried it at the bottom of the cove.” “巫师很快发现，光明之剑对他来说毫无用处，因为湖中女士在剑上施了魔法，只有在有德之人的手中，它才会变得强大。 然而，巫师仍然不愿放手。 他把它埋在了海湾区的最深处。

“The Cove of the Stormy Coast,” said Jack. “风暴海岸湾，”杰克说。

“Yes,” said Merlin. “Only recently did the seabirds tell me of the sword’s whereabouts. I knew I needed worthy mortals to retrieve it. So I sent for you on the summer solstice, when the Ice Wizard could send no mighty storms to keep you from finding it. He could only throw the ‘Cloak of the Old Gray Ghost’ over you.” “是的，”梅林说。 “只是最近，海鸟才告诉我剑在哪里。 我知道我需要有德之人去取回它。 所以我特意在夏至那天召唤你们，那时冰巫师无法派出猛烈的风暴阻止你们找到它。” 他只能把‘老灰鬼的斗篷’披在你身上。

“So the Ice Wizard sent the fog,” said Annie. “所以冰巫师派来了雾气，”安妮说。

“And did he put the sea monster in the cove, too?” asked Jack. “他还把海怪放进海角了吗？”杰克问。

Merlin smiled. “No. The serpent serves the Lady of the Lake. Long ago, he secretly took it upon himself to find the sword and guard it. Should any mortals survive the wizard’s storms and gales, they still had to prove themselves worthy by answering the serpent’s question. I believed you two would be able to answer the question wisely. And I was right.” 梅林笑了。 “没有。 蛇为湖中女士效劳。 很久以前，他暗自发誓要找到这把剑并守护它。 如果有人能经受住巫师的风暴和狂风，他们仍必须通过回答蛇的问题来证明自己配得上这把剑。 我相信你们俩能明智地回答这个问题。我没错。

“Your rhyme helped,” said Jack. “你的押韵帮了忙，”杰克说。

He and Annie carefully handed the Sword of Light to Merlin. 他和安妮小心地把光之剑交给了梅林。

“Will you put this sword in a stone now?” asked Annie. “So Arthur can pull it out someday and become king?” “你现在要把这把剑放进石头里吗？”安妮问。 “所以亚瑟有一天可以拔出来，成为国王吗？”

“No, this sword is even more powerful than the sword in the stone,” said Merlin. “This sword has a name—Excalibur.” “不，这把剑比石头里的剑还要强大，”梅林说道。 “这把剑有个名字——艾克苏卡尔布。”

“Excalibur!” said Jack and Annie. “Excalibur!” 杰克和安妮说道。

“I will take it back to the Isle of Avalon now,” said Merlin, “and return it to the Lady of the Lake. Someday after Arthur is king, she will give it to him. The sword will help him face many challenges bravely and wisely. He will—” “我现在就把它带回阿瓦隆岛，”梅林说道，“然后把它归还给湖中仙女。” 有一天亚瑟成为国王后，她会把它交给他的。 这把剑会帮助他勇敢而聪明地面对许多挑战。他会—

Merlin was interrupted by a strange sound from the water below. It sounded like the deep bellow of a foghorn. 梅林被来自下方水中的奇怪声音打断了。 听起来像是雾号深深的轰鸣声。

“What was that?” said Jack. “那是什么？”杰克说。

“Ah, yes, there is one last thing to do,” said Merlin. He raised the sword and pointed it toward the Cove of the Stormy Coast. Like the beam of a giant searchlight, the sword’s light streamed over the black waters. “啊，是的，还有一件事要做，”梅林说。 他举起剑，指向风暴海岸的海湾。 像探照灯的光束一样，剑的光照射在漆黑的水面上。

Merlin moved the beam back and forth, as if he were looking for something. “Ah,” he said. “There he is.” 梅林来回移动光束，好像在寻找什么东西。 “啊，”他说。 “他在那儿。”

The light revealed the gigantic head of the sea serpent. Its yellow lamp-like eyes shined back at them. 光亮揭示了海蛇巨大的头。 它黄色的、像灯一样的眼睛朝他们闪着光。

“He mourns now,” said Merlin, “for he has lost his purpose for being here. ’Tis time we help him home to the waters of Avalon.” “他现在很伤心，”梅林说，“因为他在这里的目的已经失去了。” “是时候帮助他回到阿瓦隆的水域了。”

The magician lifted the sword slightly. The beam made a path to show the monster the passage out of the cove. The giant serpent slid through the water and soon disappeared beneath the waves of the dark summer sea. 这位魔法师稍微抬起了剑。 那道光束照亮了一条路，引导怪物离开这个海湾。 那条巨大的蛇滑入水中，很快便消失在了黑暗夏日海的波浪之下。

“His mission is done now,” said Annie. “他的任务现在已经完成了，”安妮说。

“Yes, and so is yours, my friends,” said Merlin. “You must climb the ladder to your tree house and go home.” “是的，你们的任务也完成了，我的朋友们，”梅林说。 “你们必须爬上梯子回到树屋，然后回家。”

By the light of the sword, Jack and Annie found their way to the rope ladder and climbed up into the tree house. When they looked out the window, they saw Merlin standing in the glow of the Sword of Light. 在剑的光芒下，杰克和安妮找到了通往绳梯的路，爬上了树屋。 当他们从窗户往外看时，看见梅林站在光明之剑的光芒中。

“Bye!” Jack and Annie called. “再见！”杰克和安妮喊道。

The magician raised his arm and spread his fingers in a wave of farewell. Merlin’s gesture stirred something in Jack’s memory, but he wasn’t sure what it was. 魔术师抬起手臂，张开手指，做出告别的手势。 梅林的动作让杰克想起了什么，但他不确定是什么。

“Let’s go now,” Annie said. “我们现在就走，”安妮说。

Jack took the seashell out of his pocket. He pointed to the words Frog Creek. “I wish we could go home!” he said. 杰克从口袋里拿出贝壳。 他指着上面写着的蛙溪镇。 “我希望我们能回家！”他说。

“Wait!” said Annie. “Our shoes! We left them on the beach!” “等等！”安妮说。 “我们的鞋子！ 我们把它们落在海滩上了！”

Too late. 太晚了。

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来了。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转起来。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。

Jack opened his eyes. A warm summer breeze wafted into the tree house. The noon sun shined between the tree leaves. No time at all had passed in Frog Creek. 杰克睁开了眼睛。 一股温暖的夏日微风飘进了树屋。 正午的阳光从树叶间照了进来。 在蛙溪镇，一点时间都没有过去。

“Merlin was the Water Knight,” Jack said. “梅林是水之骑士，”杰克说。

“What?” said Annie. “什么？”安妮说。

“When he said good-bye, Merlin gave us the same wave the Water Knight gave us,” said Jack. “Remember?” Jack raised his hand and imitated Merlin’s gesture. “当他告别时，梅林给我们做了和水之骑士一样的挥手动作，”杰克说。 “还记得吗？”杰克举起手，模仿梅林的动作。

“You’re right!” Annie laughed. “Why didn’t I think of that? He always helps us get started on our missions.” “你没错！”安妮笑了。 “我怎么没想到这一点？ 他总是帮助我们开始任务。”

“And now we have three things from him,” said Jack. He put the pale blue shell on the floor next to the Royal Invitation and the yellow autumn leaf. Then he looked at Annie. “现在我们有他给的三样东西了，”杰克说。 他把那枚淡蓝色的贝壳放在皇家邀请函和那片黄色的秋叶旁边，放在地板上。 然后他看着安妮。

“Home?” he said. “家？”他说道。

She nodded. 她点了点头。

They climbed down the rope ladder and started walking barefoot through the damp, leafy woods. 他们顺着绳梯爬下来，光着脚走进潮湿的、长满树叶的树林里。

“I guess we’ll just have to tell Mom we lost our shoes in a time before Camelot,” said Jack. “我想我们只能告诉妈妈，我们在卡梅洛特之前的时代把鞋子弄丢了，”杰克说道。

“Yeah,” said Annie, “on our way to get the Sword of Light that was stolen by the Ice Wizard of Winter and guarded by a giant sea serpent who served the Lady of the Lake.” “对，”安妮说，“在我们去取被冬之冰巫师偷走、由湖中女士的巨海蛇守护的光明之剑的路上。”

“Right,” said Jack. “A simple explanation.” “没错，”杰克说。 “一个简单的解释。”

“You ready to go swimming at the lake now?” asked Annie. “你现在准备去湖里游泳了吗？”安妮问。

Jack remembered the thrill of being a seal and zooming through the deep waters. “It won’t be the same without Kathleen and Teddy,” he said. “We won’t be seals.” 杰克想起了自己变成海豹时在深水中飞速穿梭的刺激感。 “没有凯瑟琳和泰迪在身边，感觉就不一样了，”他说。 “我们不会变成海豹。”

“We can pretend,” said Annie. “Let’s hurry before Mom decides it’s too late to go.” “我们可以假装，”安妮说。 “我们得快点，不然妈妈会决定现在去晚了。”

They took off running. They ran barefoot through the woods, over sticks and leaves, through the dappled noon light. Then they ran down their street. They were out of breath by the time they reached their yard. 他们立刻跑了起来。 他们光着脚穿过树林，踩过树枝和落叶，穿过斑驳的正午阳光。 然后他们跑到了自己的街上。 他们气喘吁吁地跑到自家院子时，已经累坏了。

“Oh, wow!” said Annie. “Look!” She pointed at their porch. “哦，哇！”安妮说。 “看！”她指着他们的门廊。

Sitting in front of the door were their sneakers. 他们的运动鞋正坐在门前面。

Jack and Annie climbed the porch and picked up their shoes. As Jack turned his over, fine white sand fell out—and a couple of tiny silver pebbles. 杰克和安妮爬上了门廊，捡起了他们的鞋子。 杰克翻过自己的鞋子时，一些细白的沙子掉了出来，还有几颗小小的银色鹅卵石。

“Who—how?” he said. “谁……怎么了？”他说。

A seagull screeched overhead. They looked up. The gull screeched again, then flew away and disappeared into the soft summer light. 一只海鸥在头顶上尖叫。他们抬头看去。 海鸥又尖叫了一声，然后飞走了，消失在柔和的夏日阳光中。

Annie shrugged. “A little leftover magic,” she said. Then she called through the screen door, “Mom! We’re ready!” 安妮耸了耸肩。 “一点点剩下的魔法，”她说。 然后她对着门屏风喊道：“妈妈！我们准备好了！”
