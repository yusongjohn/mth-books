11. Sword and Rhyme 11. 剑与韵律

The sea serpent moved its head very close to Jack and Annie and hissed a long, whispery sssssssss. 海蛇把头靠近杰克和安妮，发出长长的、沙哑的嘶嘶声。

The serpent’s flicking tongue touched the sword for a moment. Jack’s heart nearly stopped. But then the serpent slowly pulled back its head and began to uncoil itself from around the rocky island. 蛇的舌头轻轻碰了一下剑，只持续了一瞬间。 杰克的心脏几乎停跳了。 但接着，蛇慢慢把头缩回去，开始从岩石小岛周围缓缓松开身体。

The monster kept uncoiling its great body, until once again one wide ring surrounded the cove like a circle of green hills. Then the serpent’s head sank beneath the water with barely a ripple. It was impossible to tell where its body started and where it ended. 怪物继续松开它庞大的身体，直到又一个宽大的环形环绕着海湾，就像一圈绿色的山丘。 接着，蛇的头沉入水中，几乎没有激起一点涟漪。 简直无法分辨它的身体是从哪里开始，又在哪里结束。

Jack and Annie lowered the Sword of Light and laid it back on the rock. Then they let out a huge sigh of relief and sat down next to the sword. 杰克和安妮把光之剑放下来，重新放在岩石上。 然后他们长长地松了一口气，坐在剑旁边。

Kathleen and Teddy poked their seal heads up from the calm waters. Barrh! Barrh! they barked. 凯瑟琳和泰迪从平静的水里探出海豹的头。Barrh！ Barrh！他们大声叫道。

Jack and Annie laughed. “It’s safe to come out now!” Annie called. 杰克和安妮笑了。 “现在可以出来了，很安全！”安妮喊道。

The seals hauled themselves onto the rocky island. Then they plopped on their sides. 海豹们把自己拖上了岩石小岛。 然后它们啪地一声躺在了旁边。

“The sword helped us answer the ancient question,” said Jack. “这把剑帮我们回答了古老的问题，”杰克说。

They all looked at the Sword of Light. It glowed brightly, even though the sun had slipped below the horizon and the purple sky was fading into twilight. 他们全都看着光之剑。 即使太阳已经滑落到地平线以下，紫色的天空也渐渐变成了黄昏，它依然闪闪发光。

“We still have to get it out of here before it’s completely dark,” said Annie. “我们还得在天完全黑之前把它带出去，”安妮说。

“I know,” said Jack. “But how?” “我知道，”杰克说。 “但是怎么带呢？”

“Look at Merlin’s rhyme,” said Annie. “看看梅林的韵文，”安妮说。

Jack took the shell from his pocket. He read the last line of Merlin’s rhyme: 杰克从口袋里拿出贝壳。 他读着梅林咒语的最后一行：

With rhyme and sword, your home is near. 用咒语和剑，你的家就在附近。

Jack looked up. “That doesn’t make sense,” he said. 杰克抬头看了看。 “这不太对劲，”他说。

“Perhaps it does,” said Teddy. “也许是对的，”泰迪说。

Jack and Annie turned around. Teddy and Kathleen were standing behind them. Their seal skins had silently slipped off. They were human again. 杰克和安妮转过身。 泰迪和凯瑟琳正站在他们身后。 他们的海豹皮悄无声息地滑落了。 他们又变回人类了。

“Perhaps it calls for a magical rhyme,” said Teddy. “And I am a magician, remember?” “也许需要一个魔法咒语，”泰迪说。 “而且我可是个魔术师，别忘了？”

Annie laughed. “How could we forget?” she said. 安妮笑了。 “我们怎么可能忘记呢？”她说。

Teddy grinned. “I have gotten much better at my rhymes,” he said. “Just watch.” He rubbed his hands together. Then he carefully picked up the Sword of Light. He gripped its handle with both hands. He pointed the silver blade toward the tree house on the distant sea cliff. 泰迪咧嘴笑了。 “我最近的咒语进步了不少，”他说。 “你瞧吧。”他搓了搓手。 然后他小心翼翼地捡起了光明之剑。 他双手紧紧握住剑柄。 他将银色的剑刃指向远处海崖上的树屋。

Teddy took a deep breath. Then he shouted: 泰迪深吸了一口气。然后他大声喊道：

O Sword of Light, now light the night! 哦，光明之剑，现在照亮黑夜！

Teddy paused. Jack grew worried. Teddy always had trouble finishing his rhymes. And even the ones he did finish never worked the way they were supposed to. 泰迪停了下来。 杰克变得担心起来。 泰迪总是很难把他的押韵诗念完。 而且即使他念完了，也从来没有按照应该的方式起作用。

Kathleen stepped close to the young sorcerer. “Say it again,” she said softly. 凯瑟琳走近年轻的法师。 “再念一遍，”她轻声说道。

Teddy called out again: 泰迪再次喊道：

O Sword of Light, now light the night! 哦，光明之剑，现在照亮黑夜！

Kathleen finished the rhyme in selkie language: 凯瑟琳用塞尔基语完成了这首童谣：

Ma-ee-bree-stro-eh-brite!

The sword began to vibrate in Teddy’s hands. There was a roar and a blast of white light. Shimmering beams shot through the dark. The beams wiggled and waved, then streamed together to make a glittering bridge. 剑开始在泰迪的手中震动。 接着传来一声咆哮，一道白光爆发出来。 闪烁的光束穿透了黑暗。 光束扭动着、摇摆着，然后汇聚在一起，形成了一座闪闪发光的桥。

The bridge spanned the purple darkness of dusk. It stretched from the rocky island in the middle of the cove to the sea cliff above the coast. When Teddy lowered the sword, the bridge remained in the sky. 这座桥横跨在黄昏紫色的黑暗中。 它从海湾中央的岩石小岛一直延伸到海岸上方的海崖。 当泰迪放下剑时，桥仍然留在天空中。

“Wow!” whispered Annie. She turned to Kathleen. “What did you say to finish the rhyme?” “哇！”安妮小声说。 她转头看向凯瑟琳。 “你刚才说的是什么，来完成这个押韵？”

“Ma-ee-bree-stro-eh-brite,” Kathleen told her. “Make a bridge, strong and bright.” “马-伊-布里-斯特罗-埃-布赖特，”凯瑟琳告诉她。 “造一座桥，又坚固又明亮。”

“Yes, that is exactly what I was going to say,” said Teddy. “没错，这正是我打算说的，”泰迪说。

“Indeed,” said Kathleen, smiling. She took Teddy’s hand, then turned to Jack and Annie. “ ’ Tis a bright bridge to take you from my world back to yours.” “确实如此，”凯瑟琳微笑着说。 她握了握泰迪的手，然后转向杰克和安妮。 “这是一座明亮的桥，能带你从我的世界回到你们的世界。”

“You mean—we can walk on it?” said Annie. “你是说——我们可以走在上面？”安妮说。

“Try it,” said Teddy. “试试看，”泰迪说。

“Oh, man,” said Jack. He laughed nervously. Then he raised his foot and put it down on the light. It felt solid. He put his other foot on the light and took a step forward. The light felt as firm as a brick pathway. “哦，天哪，”杰克说。 他紧张地笑了笑。 然后他抬起脚，踩在光上。感觉很结实。 他又把另一只脚踩在光上，向前迈了一步。 光感觉像砖铺的小路一样坚实。

Annie stepped onto the light bridge beside Jack. It was wide enough for them to stand side by side. “This is so cool,” she whispered. 安妮走到杰克旁边，踩上了光桥。 桥很宽，他们可以并排站着。 “这太酷了，”她小声说。

“Wait, do not forget this,” said Teddy. He carefully handed Jack and Annie the Sword of Light. “等等，别忘了这个，”泰迪说。 他小心地把光之剑递给杰克和安妮。

Together they gripped the handle of the sword. “What about you guys?” asked Jack. 他们一起握住了剑柄。 “你们呢？”杰克问。

“I must return to my cave now,” said Kathleen, “or my sisters will begin to worry.” “我现在必须回我的洞穴了，”凯瑟琳说，“否则我的姐妹们会开始担心了。”

“And I will see Kathleen back home,” said Teddy, “then return to the future in Camelot.” “我会送凯瑟琳回家，”泰迪说，“然后回到卡梅洛特的未来。”

“After you stay for supper with me and my sisters,” Kathleen reminded Teddy. “你得和我及我的姐妹们一起吃晚饭，”凯瑟琳提醒泰迪。

“Oh … ,” said Jack. He wanted to have supper with the selkies, too. He wanted to spend more time with Kathleen and Teddy, whatever they were doing. “哦……，”杰克说。 他也想和水妖一起吃晚饭。 他想花更多时间陪凯瑟琳和泰迪，不管他们在做什么。

“We’d better get going, Jack,” said Annie. “It’s almost dark.” “我们最好赶紧出发吧，杰克，”安妮说。 “天快黑了。”

“Okay,” said Jack. “好的，”杰克说。

“Good-bye for now,” Kathleen said to them. “And thank you. ’Tis amazing how you defeated the enemy.” “再见啦，”凯瑟琳对他们说。 “谢谢你们。 你们打败敌人真是太神奇了。”

“The sea serpent wasn’t really our enemy,” said Jack. “海蛇其实不是我们的敌人，”杰克说。

“He was like the Spider Queen,” said Annie. “They both seemed really scary until we got to know them.” “他就像蜘蛛女王一样，”安妮说。 “他们一开始看起来都很可怕，直到我们和他们成为朋友。”

“Yeah,” said Jack. “对，”杰克说。

“Will we see you again?” Annie asked Teddy and Kathleen. “我们会再见到你们吗？”安妮问泰迪和凯瑟琳。

“Yes, I have a feeling you will see both of us again soon,” said the selkie. “是的，我觉得很快你们就会再见到我们两个，”水妖说。

“We will find you when you least expect it,” said Teddy with a grin. “Now, my friends, you must go. Night comes quickly upon you. Farewell.” “等你们最不注意的时候，我们就会找到你们，”泰迪笑着说。 “现在，我的朋友们，你们必须走了。” 夜色很快降临了。再见。”

“Farewell,” said Jack and Annie. They turned and started walking up the bright bridge. High above the water, the sword’s light swung over the cove like a swaying lantern. The water below shimmered with sparkling ripples. “再见，”杰克和安妮说道。 他们转身，开始沿着那座明亮的桥往上走。 高高悬在水面上，剑的光芒像一盏摇晃的灯笼一样，掠过海湾。 下面的水面上闪着闪闪发光的波纹。

Jack heard two splashes behind them. He stopped and listened. 杰克听到他们身后传来两声水花声。 他停下了脚步，仔细听。

“Go, go, go,” whispered Annie. “快点，快点，快点，”安妮小声说。

Jack started walking again. He and Annie climbed higher and higher, until they came to the end of the bright pathway. 杰克又开始往前走。 他和安妮越爬越高，直到他们来到那条明亮小路的尽头。

They stepped off the bridge onto the rocky sea cliff above the coves. Clutching the handle of the sword, they looked back. 他们从桥上跳下来，落在海角上方的岩石悬崖上。 握紧剑柄，他们回头望去。

The shining bridge shattered into a million pieces of golden light. Like the sparks of a firecracker, the glittering pieces rained down through the sky. Then they quickly burned out. 那座闪亮的桥碎成了无数金色光点。 就像鞭炮的火星一样，这些闪闪发光的碎片从天空中纷纷落下。 然后它们迅速熄灭了。

The cove below was dark and silent—except for the distant barking of seals. 下面的海角一片黑暗和寂静——除了远处海豹的叫声。
