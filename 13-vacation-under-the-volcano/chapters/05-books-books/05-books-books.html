5. Books? Books? 5. 书？书？

Jack and Annie ran up to the front entrance of the villa. Jack pushed the door open. 杰克和安妮跑到别墅正门前。 杰克推开门。

“Go in. Hurry,” said Annie. “进去。 快点。”安妮说。

They slipped into the main hall. 他们溜进了大厅。

“Hello!” Annie called. “有人吗！”安妮喊道。

There was no answer. The place seemed empty. 没有回答。 这地方好像空无一人。

The main hall had a large opening in the ceiling. Below it was a small stone pool filled with water. Jack looked at it carefully. 大厅的天花板上有一个大开口。 下面是一个装满水的小石池。 杰克仔细地看着它。

“Oh, I bet rain comes through the hole,” said Jack. “Then it lands in that pool so they can use it for their water.” “哦，我猜雨是从那个洞漏进来的。”杰克说。 “然后落进那个池子里，好让他们用水。”

He started to take out his notebook to make a note. 他正要拿出笔记本记一笔。

“There’s no time, Jack!” said Annie. “We have to look in all the rooms for books!” “没时间了，杰克！”安妮说。 “我们得把所有房间都找遍，找书！”

“Okay, okay, calm down,” said Jack. He put away his notebook and followed Annie. “好了好了，冷静点。”杰克说。 他把笔记本收起来，跟着安妮。

“Books? Books?” she said, peering into a room off the hall. She moved to the next room. “Books? Books?” Then she moved on to the next. “书？ 书？”她说着，朝门厅旁的一个房间里张望。 她走到下一个房间。 “书？ 书？”然后她又走到下一个。

Jack trailed behind her. Even though she had already checked the rooms, he took a quick peek in each of them. He wanted to see what a house in Roman times looked like. He’d write notes later. 杰克跟在她后面。 尽管她已经检查过那些房间，他还是朝每个房间快速看了一眼。 他想看看罗马时代的房子是什么样的。 笔记以后再写。

The first two rooms had wooden beds. The walls had pictures painted on them. The floors were covered with tiny pieces of colored stone. 头两个房间里有木床。 墙上画着图画。 地板铺着小小的彩色石片。

The third room had a low table with silver dishes on it. Three sloping couches were placed around the table. The couches were covered with pillows. 第三个房间里有一张矮桌，上面放着银盘子。 三张斜卧榻摆在桌子周围。 沙发上铺着枕头。

“This must be the dining room,” said Jack. “People from Roman times lay down on couches while they ate. Did you know that?” “这一定是餐厅。”杰克说。 “罗马时代的人吃饭时会躺在卧榻上。 你知道吗？”

He looked around for Annie. Where was she? 他四处找安妮。她在哪儿？

“Jack! Come here!” Annie called. “杰克！ 过来！”安妮喊道。

Jack followed her voice. She was in a garden off the dining room. It had a stone patio, palm trees, and grape vines. In the middle was a pond with a mermaid fountain. Goldfish swam in the water. 杰克循着她的声音走去。 她在餐厅旁边的花园里。 那里有石头露台、棕榈树和葡萄藤。 中间有个池塘，还有一座美人鱼喷泉。 金鱼在水里游。

“Look, there’s another room!” said Annie. She moved to the door of a room off the garden. “看，还有一个房间！”安妮说。 她走到花园旁一个房间的门口。

She opened the door and peeked inside. 她打开门往里偷看。

Jack looked with her. Along the walls of the room were long shelves with rolls of paper on them. 杰克也跟着看。 房间的墙壁上有长长的架子，上面放着一卷卷纸。

“Rats!” said Annie. “No books.” She closed the door. “No books in this whole villa. Let’s get out of here.” “可恶！”安妮说。 “没有书。”她关上门。 “整座别墅都没有书。 咱们走吧。”

“Just a minute,” said Jack. “I have an idea.” “等一下。”杰克说。 “我有个主意。”

He pulled out his book on Roman times. He found a section called WRITING. He read: 他抽出那本罗马时代的书。 他找到一节叫“书写”的内容。他读道：

Romans used pens made of small reeds. Their ink came partly from the black ink of octopuses. They wrote their “books” on scrolls of papyrus (puh-PI-rus) paper. 罗马人用小芦苇做的笔写字。他们的墨水部分来自章鱼的黑色墨水。他们把“书”写在纸莎草（puh-PI-rus）纸卷上。

“Aha!” said Jack. “That’s a library of scrolls! I bet our ancient lost story is in there!” “啊哈！”杰克说。 “那是卷轴图书馆！ 我敢打赌我们要找的古代失落故事就在那儿！”
