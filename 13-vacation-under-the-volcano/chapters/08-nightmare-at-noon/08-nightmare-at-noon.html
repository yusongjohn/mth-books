8. Nightmare at Noon 8. 正午的噩梦

In the distance, fire burst from Mount Vesuvius. Burning rocks and fiery ash fell from the sky. 远处，维苏威火山喷出火焰。 燃烧的岩石和炽热的火山灰从天上落下。

The hot, dusty air smelled like rotten eggs as Jack and Annie rushed down the street. In the forum, everyone—shoppers, soldiers, gladiators, fruit sellers—was running in every direction. 又热又灰的空气有一股臭鸡蛋味，杰克和安妮沿街狂奔。 广场上，每个人——购物的人、士兵、角斗士、卖水果的——都在朝四面八方乱跑。

Stalls had collapsed. Carts were sliding. 货摊倒塌了。 马车在滑。

Jack froze. He didn’t know where to go. 杰克愣住了。 他不知道该往哪儿走。

“That way!” shouted Annie. “那边！”安妮喊道。

Jack followed her as they ran past the Temple of Jupiter. Its mighty columns had fallen, and its walls were crumbling. 杰克跟着她跑过朱庇特神庙。 它巨大的柱子已经倒下，墙壁也在崩塌。

They ran past the Public Baths just as its roof caved in. 他们跑过公共浴场，正好屋顶塌了下去。

“Which way now?” shouted Annie. “现在往哪边？”安妮喊道。

“The tree house is in the olive grove!” Jack said as they kept running. “树屋在橄榄林里！”杰克一边跑一边说。

“The olive grove and the bridge are near the street with all those open shops!” said Annie. “Remember the bridge?” “橄榄林和桥就在那些敞开店铺的那条街附近！”安妮说。 “还记得那座桥吗？”

Jack looked up at the erupting mountain. A red-hot cloud billowed over it. Fires burned on its slopes. 杰克抬头望着喷发的火山。 一团火红的云在它上空翻滚。 山坡上燃着火。

“Head in the direction of Mount Vesuvius!” he said. “It was behind us as we came into Pompeii.” “朝维苏威火山的方向跑！”他说。 “我们进庞贝时，它在我们身后。”

“Right!” cried Annie. “对！”安妮喊道。

So while others ran away from Mount Vesuvius, Jack and Annie ran toward it. 于是当别人逃离维苏威火山时，杰克和安妮却朝它跑去。

On the street with the open shops, baskets and broken jars rolled over the cracked stones. 在敞开店铺的那条街上，篮子和碎罐子在裂开的石板路上滚动。

Jack and Annie ran past the bakery and the shoe shop. They ran past the butcher shop and barbershop. All the shops were empty. Their owners had fled. 杰克和安妮跑过面包店和鞋店。 他们跑过肉铺和理发店。 所有店铺都空了。 店主们都逃走了。

The closer they got to the volcano, the more the ground trembled. The darker and dustier it got. 他们离火山越近，地面就晃得越厉害。 天越来越暗，灰尘也越来越多。

“This is just like my nightmare!” cried Annie. “这简直就像我的噩梦！”安妮喊道。

Jack choked on the rotten fumes. His eyes watered. 杰克被难闻的烟气呛到了。他的眼睛流出了泪水。

“Look! The olive grove!” shouted Annie. “The tree house is just over there! Come on!” “看！ 橄榄林！”安妮喊道。 “树屋就在那边！快点！”

Jack could hardly see, but he followed Annie. They left the street and ran to the dried-up stream near the olive grove. 杰克几乎看不见，但他跟着安妮。 他们离开街道，跑向橄榄林附近干涸的小溪。

“Where’s the bridge?” cried Annie. “桥呢？”安妮喊道。

They looked around wildly. The bridge had vanished. 他们慌乱地四处看。 桥不见了。
