7. The Sky Is Falling 7. 天要塌了

The next thing Jack knew, he was lying on the stone patio. The patio stones were trembling. A rumbling sound came from the ground. 接下来杰克知道的事，就是自己躺在石砌的院子里。 露台的石板在颤抖。 地面传来一阵隆隆声。

Jack raised his head. Annie was on the ground, too. 杰克抬起头。 安妮也倒在地上。

“You okay?” said Annie. “你没事吧？”安妮说。

Jack nodded. 杰克点了点头。

Everything was shaking and crashing down around them—pots, plants, the mermaid fountain. Water from the goldfish pond sloshed onto the patio and Jack and Annie. 周围的一切都在晃、在砸下来——罐子、植物、美人鱼喷泉。 金鱼池的水溅到露台上，也溅到杰克和安妮身上。

They both jumped up just as roof tiles began falling into the garden. 他们刚跳起来，屋顶瓦片就开始掉进花园。

“We better get inside!” said Jack. “我们最好进屋！”杰克说。

He grabbed his leather bag. Then he and Annie stumbled into the scroll library. 他抓起皮袋。 接着他和安妮跌跌撞撞地进了卷轴图书室。

Giant cracks split the stone floor as Jack and Annie ran to a window and looked out. 巨大的裂缝裂开石地板，杰克和安妮跑到窗边往外看。

Glowing rocks were bursting through the sky above Mount Vesuvius. The whole top of the mountain had blown off. 发光的岩石正从维苏威火山上空冲破天空。 整座山的山顶都被炸飞了。

“What’s happening?” said Annie. “发生什么事了？”安妮说。

“I’ll check—” said Jack. He pulled out the Roman book. He read aloud from the section about the volcano: “我查查——”杰克说。 他抽出罗马书。 他大声读着关于火山的那一节：

When a volcano erupts, hot melted rock called “magma” is pushed to the surface of the earth. Once it gets outside the volcano, it’s called “lava.” 火山喷发时，炽热的熔融岩石叫做“岩浆”，会被推到地球表面。一旦岩浆离开火山，就叫做“熔岩”。

“Lava! That’s like burning mud!” said Jack. “熔岩！ 那就像燃烧的泥浆！”杰克说。

“It covers everything!” cried Annie. “它会盖住一切！”安妮喊道。

Jack kept reading: 杰克继续读：

There was no running lava from Mount Vesuvius. The magma from the volcano cooled so fast that it froze into small grayish white rocks called pumice (PUM-iss). A pumice rock is very light and has holes like a sponge. 维苏威火山并没有喷出熔岩。火山的岩浆冷却得非常快，变成了小的灰白色岩石，叫做浮石（PUM-iss）。浮石很轻，上面有很多像海绵一样的小孔。

“That doesn’t sound too bad,” said Annie. “听起来还不算太糟。”安妮说。

“Wait, there’s more,” said Jack. He read on: “等等，还有呢。”杰克说。他继续读：

A great cloud of pumice, ash, and burning rock shot miles into the air. When it rained down on Pompeii, it completely buried the town. 一大团火山灰、灰烬和燃烧的岩石射向天空数英里。当它们降落在庞贝时，彻底埋没了这座城市。

“Oh, man,” said Jack. “This is a major disaster!” “天哪。”杰克说。 “这是一场大灾难！”

“It’s getting dark,” said Annie. “天变黑了。”安妮说。

Jack looked out again. A thick black cloud was spreading over the earth like an umbrella. The sun vanished as the sky turned smoky gray. 杰克又向外望去。 一团厚厚的黑云像伞一样铺开，笼罩大地。 太阳消失了，天空变成了烟灰色。

“That must be the cloud of pumice and ash!” said Jack. “那一定就是浮石和火山灰的云！”杰克说。

Just then the ground trembled again. Chunks of plaster from the ceiling fell on the scrolls. 就在这时，地面又震了一下。 天花板上的大块灰泥掉到了卷轴上。

“We have to get out of here!” said Annie. “我们得离开这儿！”安妮说。

They ran from the scroll library into the garden. Ash and pumice began to fall. 他们从卷轴图书室跑进花园。 火山灰和浮石开始往下落。

“We have to cover our heads!” said Jack. “我们得捂住头！”杰克说。

They hurried from the garden into the dining room. 他们从花园匆匆跑进餐厅。

“Look! Pillows!” said Annie. “Let’s put them on our heads!” “看！ 枕头！”安妮说。 “把它们顶在头上！”

They hurried to the couches beside the table and each grabbed a pillow. 他们赶到桌子旁的沙发边，每人抓起一个枕头。

“Tie it around your head with your belt!” said Jack. “用腰带把它绑在头上！”杰克说。

They both pulled off the belts from around their tunics. Then they tied on the pillows, like giant hats. 他们都解下短袍上的腰带。 然后把枕头绑在头上，像顶着巨大的帽子。

A chunk of ceiling crashed down near them. 一块天花板在他们附近砸了下来。

“Let’s get out of here!” said Jack. “咱们离开这儿！”杰克说。

They stepped over pieces of fallen roof tiles and ran into the main hall. They pushed open the front door. 他们跨过掉落的瓦片，跑进大厅。 他们推开前门。

A blast of heat and dust nearly knocked them over. And when they stepped outside, pumice rained down onto their pillow hats. 一股热浪和尘土差点把他们掀倒。 一踏出门，浮石就砸在他们的枕头帽上。

“Run!” cried Annie. “跑！”安妮喊道。

They ran from the vacation villa into the dark, burning streets. 他们从度假别墅跑进黑暗、燃烧的街道。
