6. The End Is Here 6. 末日已至

Jack threw open the door to the room of scrolls. He and Annie rushed in and ran over to the shelves. 杰克猛地推开卷轴室的门。 他和安妮冲进去，跑到架子旁。

Jack pulled out the piece of paper with the Latin title on it: 杰克抽出那张写着拉丁文标题的纸：

Vir Fortissimus in Mundo Vir Fortissimus in Mundo

“Okay,” he said. “We have to find the scroll with this title.” “好。”他说。 “我们得找到这个标题的卷轴。”

They began frantically unrolling scrolls one by one. They were all handwritten in Latin. 他们开始一个接一个地拼命展开卷轴。 它们全是用拉丁文手写的。

“Here it is!” said Annie. “在这儿！”安妮说。

She held up a scroll. The words at the top matched the ones on their paper. 她举起一卷轴。 顶端的字和他们纸上的字对得上。

“Yay,” said Jack. “I wish I could read Latin so we could find out what the story is.” “耶。”杰克说。 “真希望我会读拉丁文，这样就能知道故事讲什么。”

“Don’t think about it now!” said Annie. “Let’s go!” “现在别想这个！”安妮说。 “咱们走！”

Annie handed the scroll to Jack, then started out of the room. 安妮把卷轴递给杰克，然后往外走。

“Come on,” she said. “Bring it!” “快点，”她说。 “把它带上！”

“I just want to check and see what the story’s about,” said Jack. “我只想看看这个故事讲的是什么。”杰克说。

He put the scroll in the leather bag. Then he flipped through the book on Roman times, looking for a picture of the ancient scroll. In the middle of the book, he found a picture of a volcano erupting over a town. 他把卷轴放进皮袋。 然后他翻着那本罗马时代的书，想找那卷古代卷轴的图画。 在书的中间，他找到一幅火山喷发、淹没一座城的图画。

Under the picture was written: 图画下面写着：

For 800 years, Mount Vesuvius was a peaceful mountain, rising above the town of Pompeii. Then, at noon on August 24, A.D. 79, it erupted into a deadly volcano. 800年来，维苏威火山一直是一座平静的山，耸立在庞贝镇上方。然后，在公元79年8月24日中午，它突然爆发，变成了一座致命的火山。

“Oh, no, ” whispered Jack. “August 24, A.D. 79—that’s today! Oh, man, what time is it?” He looked around wildly. “Annie!” “哦，不。”杰克小声说。 “公元79年8月24日——就是今天！ 天哪，现在几点了？”他慌乱地四处看。 “安妮！”

She was gone again. 她又不见了。

“Annie!” “安妮！”

Jack grabbed the leather bag. Then, clutching the book, he tore out of the scroll room. 杰克抓起皮袋。 接着，他攥着那本书，冲出了卷轴室。

“Annie!” he cried. “安妮！”他大喊。

“What?” Annie appeared at the door to the dining room. “什么事？”安妮出现在餐厅门口。

“V-v-volcano!” stuttered Jack. “火——火——火山！”杰克结结巴巴地说。

“What?” said Annie. “什么？”安妮说。

“It’s—it’s coming—a volcano—at noon!” said Jack. “它——它要来了——一座火山——就在中午！”杰克说。

Annie gasped. 安妮倒吸一口气。

“What time is it?” cried Jack. “现在几点了？”杰克喊道。

“So that’ s what the soothsayer meant!” Annie said. “The end is near.” “原来占卜者说的是这个意思！”安妮说。 “末日将近。”

“What time is it?” Jack asked again. He looked around the garden. “现在几点了？”杰克又问。 他朝花园四周看。

He saw something near the mermaid fountain. 他在美人鱼喷泉附近看见了一样东西。

“A sundial!” he said. “That’s how the Romans told time!” “日晷！”他说。 “罗马人就是这样看时间的！”

Jack and Annie raced to the sundial. 杰克和安妮朝日晷跑去。

“What time does it say?” said Annie. “它显示几点？”安妮说。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “我不知道。”杰克说。

His hands shook as he turned the pages of the book. He stopped on a picture of a sundial. It showed examples of different times. Jack looked back and forth from the page to the real sundial in the garden. 他翻书的手在发抖。 他停在一幅日晷的图画上。 上面显示了不同时间的例子。 杰克在书页和花园里真正的日晷之间来回看。

“Here!” he said. He had found the one that matched. Jack read the writing under the picture: “在这儿！”他说。 他找到了匹配的那一个。 杰克读了图画下面的文字：

The shadow on the sundial can hardly be seen at noon. 日晷中午时的影子几乎看不见。

“Oh, man,” he whispered. He looked at Annie. “The end isn’t near; the end is here.” “天哪。”他小声说。 他看着安妮。 “末日不是将近；末日已经到了。”

Just then he heard a terrible blast. It was the loudest sound he had ever heard. 就在这时，他听见一声可怕的巨响。 那是他听过的最响的声音。
