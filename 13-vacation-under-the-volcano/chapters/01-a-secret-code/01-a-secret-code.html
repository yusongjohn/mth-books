1. A Secret Code 1. 秘密密码

Jack reached into his drawer and took out his secret library card. He held the thin piece of wood and ran his finger over its shimmering letters: M L. 杰克把手伸进抽屉，取出他的秘密图书卡。 他拿着那薄薄的木片，用手指摩过那些闪闪发光的字母：M L。

“Master Librarian,” he whispered. “图书大师，”他低声说。

Jack couldn’t believe that he and his sister, Annie, were finally Master Librarians. 杰克简直不敢相信，他和妹妹安妮终于成了图书大师。

He wondered if he should pack the secret card to take on vacation. His family was about to leave for a week in the mountains. 他在想该不该把这张秘密卡片打包带去度假。 他们一家马上要去山里度假一个星期。

Just then Annie stuck her head into Jack’s room. 就在这时，安妮把头探进了杰克的房间。

“Want to check the woods?” she asked. “要去树林里看看吗？”她问。

Every morning, they looked in the Frog Creek woods to see if Morgan le Fay and her magic tree house had returned. 每天早上，他们都会去蛙溪树林里看看摩根·勒菲和她的魔法树屋回来了没有。

“We can’t,” said Jack. “We’re leaving soon.” “我们去不了。”杰克说。 “我们马上就要走了。”

“But what if Morgan’s there?” said Annie. “What if she’s waiting for us?” “可要是摩根在那儿呢？”安妮说。 “嗯，我信。”安妮说。 “要是她在等我们呢？”

“Oh, okay,” said Jack. “Let’s look fast.” “哦，好吧。”杰克说。 “那咱们快点看。”

He grabbed his backpack. He threw in his notebook, his pencil, and his secret library card. Then he followed Annie downstairs. 他抓起背包。 他把笔记本、铅笔和秘密图书卡扔进包里。 接着他跟着安妮下了楼。

“We’ll be back soon!” Annie called. “我们很快就回来！”安妮喊道。

“Don’t go far!” their dad called. “We’re leaving in twenty minutes.” “别走远！”爸爸喊道。 “我们二十分钟后就要出发了。”

“Don’t worry, we’ll be back in ten!” said Annie. “别担心，我们十分钟就回来！”安妮说。

Right, thought Jack. Five minutes to the woods, and five minutes back. Even if Morgan sent them on an adventure, they would return at exactly the same time that they left. 对，杰克想。 去树林五分钟，回来五分钟。 就算摩根派他们去冒险，他们也会在离开的同一时刻回来。

Jack and Annie ran out their front door. In the bright morning sunshine, they hurried across their lawn and down the street. 杰克和安妮跑出前门。 在明亮的晨光里，他们匆匆穿过草坪，沿着街道跑下去。

“I had a nightmare last night,” said Annie. “我昨晚做了个噩梦。”安妮说。

“What was it?” said Jack. “那是什么？”杰克说。

“I dreamed fires were burning,” said Annie. “Everything was smoky and dark, and the ground was shaking. Do you think it was a warning?” “我梦见到处着火。”安妮说。 “到处都是烟，一片漆黑，地面还在晃。 你觉得那是个警告吗？”

“Nah,” said Jack. “Nightmares never come true.” “才不是。”杰克说。 “噩梦从来不会成真。”

They left the sidewalk and headed into the Frog Creek woods. The woods were quiet and peaceful. They walked between sunlit trees until they came to the tallest oak in the woods. 他们离开人行道，走进蛙溪树林。 树林里安静又平和。 他们走在洒满阳光的树间，一直走到树林里最高的那棵橡树下。

“Yay!” said Annie. “耶！”安妮说。

There it was—the magic tree house. And Morgan le Fay was waving from the window. 就在那儿——魔法树屋。 摩根·勒菲正从窗口向他们挥手。

“Hail, Master Librarians,” she said. “向你们致敬，图书大师。”她说。

Jack and Annie bowed playfully. 杰克和安妮开玩笑似的鞠了一躬。

“At your service,” said Annie. “听候吩咐。”安妮说。

“Then come on up!” said Morgan. “那就上来吧！”摩根说。

They grabbed the rope ladder and started up. When they climbed inside the tree house, they found Morgan holding a book and a piece of paper. 他们抓住绳梯往上爬。 爬进树屋后，他们看见摩根手里拿着一本书和一张纸。

“I have an important mission for you,” she said. “Are you ready?” “我有一项重要任务给你们。”她说。 “你们准备好了吗？”

“Yes!” they both answered. “准备好了！”他们一起回答。

Jack’s heart pounded. Ever since Morgan had made them Master Librarians, he had been longing for their first mission. 杰克的心怦怦直跳。 自从摩根任命他们为图书大师以来，他就一直盼着第一次任务。

“You know I collect books for Camelot’s library?” she said. “你们知道我为卡米洛特的图书馆收集图书吗？”她说。

Jack and Annie nodded. 杰克和安妮点了点头。

“Well, there have been many great libraries lost in history,” said Morgan. “And with them, we have lost many wonderful stories.” “历史上有许多伟大的图书馆消失了。”摩根说。 “连同它们一起，我们也失去了许多精彩的故事。”

“That’s sad,” said Annie. “真可惜。”安妮说。

“It is,” said Morgan. “But luckily, with the help of the tree house and you two Master Librarians, some of those stories can be saved. Such as this one—” “是啊。”摩根说。 “不过幸好，在树屋和你们两位图书大师的帮助下，有些故事还能被救回来。 比如这一篇——”

Morgan showed them the piece of paper. There was strange writing on it: 摩根把那张纸给他们看。 上面有奇怪的文字：

Vir Fortissimus in Mundo Vir Fortissimus in Mundo

“Is that a secret code?” said Jack. “那是密码吗？”杰克说。

Morgan smiled. “In a way,” she said. “Actually, it’s the title of a lost story. It’s written in Latin, the language of the ancient Romans of Italy.” 摩根笑了。 “从某种意义上说，是的。”她说。 “其实，这是一个失落故事的标题。 它是用拉丁文写的，那是意大利古罗马人的语言。”

“Ancient Romans?” said Jack. He loved anything to do with ancient Romans. “古罗马人？”杰克说。 凡是跟古罗马人有关的，他都喜欢。

“Yes,” said Morgan. “This story was in a library in a Roman town. I need you to get it before the library becomes lost forever.” “对。”摩根说。 “这个故事在一座罗马小镇的图书馆里。 我需要你们在图书馆永远消失之前把它取回来。”

“No problem!” said Annie. “没问题！”安妮说。

“Do you have your secret library cards?” asked Morgan. “你们带着秘密图书卡了吗？”摩根问。

“Yes,” said Jack. “带了。”杰克说。

“Good. Do not lose them. The right people will know what they mean,” said Morgan. “And, as usual, here is a book to help you.” “很好。别弄丢了。 该认识的人会明白它们的意思。”摩根说。 “而且，像往常一样，这里有一本书来帮助你们。”

Morgan handed Jack a book called Life in Roman Times. The cover showed a Roman town with people wearing tunics and sandals. 摩根递给杰克一本名叫《罗马时代的生活》的书。 封面画着一座罗马城镇，人们穿着短袍和凉鞋。

“That looks great,” said Jack. “看起来真棒。”杰克说。

“And take this—the name of the story I need,” said Morgan. She handed the piece of paper to Jack, and he put it in his pack. “把这个也带上——我需要的那个故事的名字。”摩根说。 她把纸递给杰克，杰克放进了背包。

“Remember,” said Morgan. “Your research book will guide you. But remember: In your darkest hour, only the ancient story can save you. But first you must find it.” “记住，”摩根说。 “你们的研究书会指引你们。 但记住：在你们最黑暗的时刻，只有那个古代故事能救你们。 但首先你们必须找到它。”

Jack and Annie nodded. 杰克和安妮点了点头。

“Go now,” Morgan said softly. “And don’t forget what I just told you.” “现在就去吧。”摩根轻声说。 “别忘了我刚才告诉你们的话。”

“Thanks,” said Jack. He pointed at the cover of the book on Roman times. “I wish we could go there,” he said. “谢谢。”杰克说。 他指着那本罗马时代书的封面。 “我希望我们能去那儿。”他说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

“I almost forgot—I’m going to help you blend in!” Morgan shouted above the wind. “差点忘了——我会帮你们融入当地的！”摩根在风声里喊道。

“What do you mean?” shouted Jack. “你是什么意思？”杰克喊道。

Before Morgan could answer, the tree house started to spin. 还没等摩根回答，树屋就开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster and faster. 它转得越来越快，越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后，一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止。

“Oh, cool,” whispered Annie. “Look at us.” “哦，好酷。”安妮小声说。 “看看我们。”
