5. Bully 5. 霸凌的孩子

The air outside was strangely still. The dark clouds still hovered in the distance. 外面的空气出奇地静止。 远处仍悬着乌云。

“Storm’s coming,” said Will. “暴风雨要来了，”威尔说。

“We have to eat quickly, before it starts to rain,” said Kate. She and Will sat down on the grass. “我们得快点吃，免得开始下雨，”凯特说。 她和威尔坐在草地上。

Annie and Jack sat beside them. 安妮和杰克坐在他们旁边。

Will opened a small burlap sack. He took out four lumpy objects. They looked like dark rocks. 威尔打开一只小粗麻袋。 他拿出四个疙疙瘩瘩的东西。 看起来像深色的石头。

“Hey, there’s one here for each of us!” said Kate. “嘿，我们每人一个！”凯特说。

“One what?” asked Annie, frowning. “一个什么？”安妮皱着眉问。

“Sweet potatoes!” said Will. He gave a potato each to Kate, Annie, and Jack. “红薯！”威尔说。 他给凯特、安妮和杰克各分了一个。

“Um—no thanks,” said Jack, trying to give his back. “We don’t want to take your lunch.” “嗯——不用了，谢谢，”杰克说着，想把红薯还回去。 “我们不想吃掉你们的午饭。”

“We have enough! Keep it!” said Kate. “我们够吃的！ 拿着吧！”凯特说。

“What do you do with it?” asked Annie, holding up her potato. “这要怎么吃？”安妮举起红薯问。

Kate laughed. 凯特笑了。

“Just bite!” she said. “Like this—” “直接咬！”她说。 “像这样——”

Kate and Will bit into their cold sweet potatoes as if they were apples. 凯特和威尔像咬苹果一样咬着冰冷的红薯。

“Cool,” said Annie. She took a big bite out of her potato, too. “酷，”安妮说。 她也在红薯上咬了一大口。

But Jack just held on to his. He didn’t quite feel like eating the cold, brown potato. 可杰克只是拿着自己的。 他不太想吃那冰冷发褐的红薯。

Out of the corner of his eye, he saw Jeb sitting by himself. The big kid didn’t seem to have any lunch at all. 他用眼角余光看见杰布独自坐着。 那个大孩子好像根本没有午饭。

Jack thought he’d try to be friends one more time. 杰克想再试一次交朋友。

“Hey, Jeb,” he called out. “I’m not hungry. You want my sweet potato?” “嘿，杰布，”他喊道。 “我不饿。 你要我的红薯吗？”

Jeb gave Jack a mean look. 杰布恶狠狠地看了杰克一眼。

“I could have brought my own lunch if I wanted to eat,” he said. “要是我想吃，我自己能带午饭来，”他说。

“Oh, sure,” said Jack. “哦，当然，”杰克说。

Jeb narrowed his eyes. 杰布眯起眼睛。

“You making fun again?” he said. “I’m warning you. Do that one more time, and I’ll fight you.” “你又在取笑我？”他说。 “我警告你。 再这样一次，我就跟你打架。”

Jack couldn’t believe it. This kid took everything he said the wrong way! 杰克简直不敢相信。 这孩子把他的每句话都往坏处想！

“Hey!” Annie said. “Leave my brother alone. You’re nothing but a bully, Jeb.” “嘿！”安妮说。 “别找我哥哥的麻烦。 你就是个欺负人的家伙，杰布。”

“Annie, stay out of this,” said Jack. “安妮，别管这事，”杰克说。

But Jeb just laughed. Then he stood up and walked back into the schoolhouse. 可杰布只是笑了笑。 然后他站起来，走回校舍。

Jack felt angry. He hoped they would find the special writing soon so they could leave. 杰克感到生气。 他希望他们快点找到那份特别的文字，好离开这里。

Will seemed to have read Jack’s mind. 威尔好像看穿了杰克的心思。

“Don’t worry about him,” Will said to Jack. “He’s never been to school before.” “别为他担心，”威尔对杰克说。 “他以前从没上过学。”

“Oh, so he’s embarrassed,” said Annie. “哦，所以他觉得难为情，”安妮说。

“Why hasn’t he been to school?” said Jack. “他为什么没上过学？”杰克说。

“Because he has to work in the fields all the time,” said Will. “因为他一直得在地里干活，”威尔说。

“I heard him tell Miss Neely he walked five miles to get here today,” Kate said. “So he must have really wanted to come.” “我听见他告诉尼莉小姐，他今天走了五英里才到这儿，”凯特说。 “所以他一定非常想来。”

“Wow,” said Annie. “How far did you and Will walk?” “哇，”安妮说。 “你和威尔走了多远？”

“Only two,” said Kate. “只有两，”凯特说。

“Two what?” asked Annie. “两什么？”安妮问。

“Miles,” said Kate. “英里，”凯特说。

“Two miles,” Jack repeated. “两英里，”杰克重复道。

The prairie kids nodded. 草原上的孩子们点了点头。

“It must be lonely living out here,” Annie said. “住在这儿一定很寂寞，”安妮说。

Will and Kate nodded again. 威尔和凯特又点了点头。

“Do you live in a sod house?” Jack asked. “你们住在土屋里吗？”杰克问。

“We used to,” said Will. “But it was always dirty. So our pa built us a log cabin.” “以前住过，”威尔说。 “可总是很脏。 所以爸爸给我们盖了小木屋。”

“He cut trees near the creek,” said Kate. “Then he made the cabin by hand.” “他在小溪边砍树，”凯特说。 “然后亲手盖成了木屋。”

Before Annie or Jack could ask another question, thunder cracked in the sky. Then rain started to fall. It fell fast and hard. 安妮或杰克还没来得及再问，天上响起一声雷。 然后雨开始下了。 雨下得又急又猛。

Everyone jumped up. 大家都跳了起来。

“Come in! Come in!” Miss Neely called from the doorway. “进来！ 进来！”尼莉小姐在门口喊道。

They ran back inside. The wind slammed the door behind them with a BANG. 他们跑回屋里。 风砰的一声把门甩上了。
