7. Twister! 7. 龙卷风！

The black clouds had taken on an odd greenish color. They all seemed to be going in different directions. 乌云带上了一种奇怪的淡绿色。 它们似乎都在往不同的方向走。

“Do you think it’s a grasshopper attack?” Annie asked nervously. “你觉得是蝗虫袭击吗？”安妮紧张地问。

“No, I think it’s some more weird weather, like that hailstorm,” said Jack. “Let’s go before it gets worse.” “不，我觉得又是奇怪的天气，像那场冰雹，”杰克说。 “我们走吧，别等它变得更糟。”

As they started back to the tree house, the wind picked up. 他们开始回树屋时，风大了起来。

Jack and Annie looked over their shoulders. The greenish clouds had dipped down close to the prairie. 杰克和安妮回头看。 淡绿色的云已经低低压到草原上。

“I feel like something awful is about to happen,” said Annie. “我觉得可怕的事就要发生了，”安妮说。

“Hurry!” said Jack. “Run!” “快点！”杰克说。 “跑！”

He and Annie started running through the grass. When they got to the ladder of the tree house, they looked back. 他和安妮开始穿过草地奔跑。 跑到树屋梯子旁时，他们回头看。

In the distance, twisting black clouds had dropped out of the storm clouds. They were swirling into a funnel shape. 远处，扭动的黑云从风暴云里落了下来。 它们打着旋，形成漏斗状。

The dark funnel started twisting across the prairie. 黑色漏斗开始在草原上旋转着前进。

Jack’s heart nearly jumped out of his chest. 杰克的心几乎要从胸口跳出来。

“It’s a twister!” he said. “是龙卷风！”他说。

“Oh no!” cried Annie. “哦不！”安妮喊道。

The twister was whirling and tearing across the grass. 龙卷风呼啸着撕过草地。

“Let’s get out of here!” said Jack. He grabbed the rope ladder and started up. “我们离开这儿！”杰克说。 他抓住绳梯开始往上爬。

“Wait!” said Annie. “We have to help Miss Neely and the other kids!” “等等！”安妮说。 “我们得去帮尼莉小姐和其他孩子！”

“They have a storm cellar!” Jack said. “That’s what our book said!” “他们有防风暴地窖！”杰克说。 “书上就是这么写的！”

“Yeah, but it’s only their first day in the dugout! They might not know about it!” said Annie. “There was a rug on the floor!” “对，可这才是他们在地坑屋的第一天！ 他们也许不知道有地窖！”安妮说。 “地板上还铺着地毯呢！”

Annie is right, Jack thought. 安妮说得对，杰克想。

He looked up at the tree house. All they had to do was climb up and leave, and they’ d be safe. 他抬头看着树屋。 他们只要爬上去离开，就安全了。

But what about Miss Neely? What about Will and Kate and Jeb? 可尼莉小姐呢？ 威尔、凯特和杰布呢？

“Okay!” said Jack. He jumped down from the ladder. “Let’s go back!” “好！”杰克说。 他从梯子上跳下来。 “我们回去！”

He and Annie started running back toward the schoolhouse. 他和安妮开始朝校舍跑回去。

They ran as fast as they could across the prairie. 他们以最快的速度穿过大草原。

The roaring sound of the twister followed them. 龙卷风的咆哮声跟在他们后面。

Suddenly, the wind threw them to the ground! 忽然，风把他们刮倒在地！

Jack clutched the tall grass, trying to stand. When he got up, he grabbed Annie’s hand. He pulled her up, too. 杰克紧紧抓住高草，试图站起来。 站起来后，他抓住安妮的手。 他也把她拉了起来。

With all his might, Jack held on to Annie and pulled her along. 杰克用尽全力抓住安妮，拉着她往前跑。

The roaring twister came closer and closer. 咆哮的龙卷风越来越近。

The wind ripped up grass and earth around them. The roar grew deafening. 风把他们周围的草和泥土撕了起来。 咆哮声震耳欲聋。

Jack and Annie could barely stay on their feet. Finally, they reached the dugout. 杰克和安妮几乎站不住。 终于，他们到达了地坑屋。

They tried to open the door, but it wouldn’t budge. 他们想开门，可门纹丝不动。

They banged on the door with their fists. 他们用拳头砸门。

“Let us in!” Annie shouted. “让我们进去！”安妮喊道。

No one opened the door. 没有人开门。

“They can’t hear us!” yelled Jack. “他们听不见我们！”杰克喊道。

But the sound of the twister drowned out his voice. 可龙卷风的声音淹没了他的话。
