10. The Third Writing 10. 第三种文字

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开了眼睛。

Morning light filled the tree house. 晨光洒满树屋。

He and Annie were wearing their own clothes again. 他和安妮又穿上了自己的衣服。

“Home,” said Annie, smiling. “到家了，”安妮笑着说。

Jack looked out the window. 杰克望向窗外。

He saw their nice, cozy house in the distance, their lawn, their sidewalk, their paved street. 他看见远处舒适的家、草坪、人行道和平坦的街道。

“Life here is pretty easy compared to pioneer life,” said Jack. “和拓荒生活比起来，这儿的日子轻松多了，”杰克说。

“We’re lucky,” said Annie. “我们很幸运，”安妮说。

Jack reached into his bag and pulled out the small slate. 杰克伸手进袋子，掏出那块小石板。

“Our third writing,” he said. He added the poem to the list from the Civil War and the letter from the Revolutionary War. “我们的第三种文字，”他说。 他把这首诗放在南北战争的清单和独立战争的信旁边。

“You did just what that poem says you should do,” said Annie. “你正好做了那首诗说的事，”安妮说。

“What do you mean?” Jack asked. “什么意思？”杰克问。

“If at first you don’t succeed, try, try again,” Annie said. “You kept trying to make friends with Jeb. In the end, you did.” “如果一开始不成功，就再试，再试，”安妮说。 “你一直试着和杰布交朋友。 最后你成功了。”

“I guess you’re right,” said Jack. “我想你说得对，”杰克说。

“We have to get only one more special writing for Morgan’s library,” said Annie. “我们只要再为摩根的图书馆找一种特别文字就行了，”安妮说。

“I wonder how that will help save Camelot?” said Jack. “不知道那会怎样帮助拯救卡米洛特？”杰克说。

Annie shrugged. 安妮耸了耸肩。

“It’s a mystery,” she said. “这是个谜，”她说。

She and Jack looked around the tree house. 她和杰克环顾树屋。

“Look—” Annie picked up a piece of paper lying in the corner. She read aloud: “看——”安妮捡起角落里的一张纸。她大声读道：

Come back early Wednesday morning. 星期三一大早再来。

“Wednesday? Man, that’s tomorrow!” said Jack. “星期三？ 天哪，那就是明天！”杰克说。

“So?” said Annie. She started down the rope ladder. “那又怎样？”安妮说。 她开始爬下绳梯。

“Not much time to recover,” said Jack, pulling on his backpack. “没多少时间恢复了，”杰克说着，背上背包。

“Recover from what?” Annie said. “从什么里恢复？”安妮说。

“The twister,” said Jack. “龙卷风，”杰克说。

“Oh yeah, I’d almost forgotten about that,” said Annie. “哦对，我都快忘了，”安妮说。

Jack smiled. 杰克笑了。

Actually, the nightmare of the twister was fading from his memory, too. 其实，龙卷风的噩梦也正从他记忆里淡去。

We must try to hold on to the good memories, Miss Neely had said, and let go of the bad ones. 我们必须努力留住美好的回忆，尼莉小姐说过，放下不好的那些。

The kindness of Will and Kate, making friends with Jeb, the courage of Miss Neely— these memories, Jack thought, he would never forget. 威尔和凯特的善良、和杰布交上朋友、尼莉小姐的勇气——这些回忆，杰克想，他永远不会忘记。
