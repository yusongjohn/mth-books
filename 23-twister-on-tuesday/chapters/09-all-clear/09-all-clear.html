9. All Clear 9. 警报解除

Jeb pushed open the cellar door. Daylight streamed in. He looked out. 杰布推开地窖门。 日光倾泻进来。他向外看。

“All clear,” he said. “警报解除，”他说。

Jeb crawled out of the cellar. Jack followed him, then Annie, Will, Kate, and Miss Neely. 杰布爬出地窖。 杰克跟着他，然后是安妮、威尔、凯特和尼莉小姐。

The sky was gray above them. The twister had torn the roof off the dugout and sucked everything out, even the rug. 他们头顶的天空灰蒙蒙的。 龙卷风掀掉了地坑屋的屋顶，把里面的东西全吸走了，连地毯也不剩。

They all stared in shock at the empty space. 大家都震惊地盯着空荡荡的空间。

Then Miss Neely smiled. 然后尼莉小姐笑了。

“Well, at least we are all safe,” she said. “至少我们都安全了，”她说。

They stepped out of the hut. The air was thick with dust and bits of grass. 他们走出小屋。 空气里满是尘土和草屑。

The twister had cut a wide path of dirt and destruction across the prairie. It was still whirling away on the horizon. 龙卷风在草原上切出一条又宽又脏的破坏带。 它仍在地平线上旋转着远去。

Everyone watched silently as the twister became long and thin, like a piece of rope. Then it vanished completely. 大家静静看着龙卷风变得又细又长，像一根绳子。 然后它完全消失了。

Miss Neely turned to Jack and Annie. 尼莉小姐转向杰克和安妮。

“You saved our lives,” she said. “你们救了我们的命，”她说。

“Thank you!” said Will. “谢谢你们！”威尔说。

“Thank you!” said Kate, hugging Annie. “谢谢你们！”凯特抱着安妮说。

“Jeb actually opened the cellar door,” said Jack. “其实是杰布打开了地窖门，”杰克说。

“Yes! Thank you, too, Jeb!” said Miss Neely. “对！ 也谢谢你，杰布！”尼莉小姐说。

The older boy only scowled. 大男孩只是皱着眉。

“I hope you can get a new school,” said Annie. “希望你们能有一所新学校，”安妮说。

“We will,” said Miss Neely. “Just as we planted our fields again after the grasshopper attack, we’ll build our school again after the twister. If at first you don’t succeed, try, try again.” “会有的，”尼莉小姐说。 “就像蝗虫袭击后我们重新种田一样，龙卷风过后我们也会重建学校。 如果一开始不成功，就再试，再试。”

Jack thought Miss Neely was one of the bravest people he’d ever met. 杰克觉得尼莉小姐是他见过的最勇敢的人之一。

“You’re a good teacher,” he said shyly. “您是一位好老师，”他腼腆地说。

“I love teaching,” she said. “It’s a job that lasts forever. Whatever you teach children today travels with them far into the future.” “我热爱教学，”她说。 “这是一份永远持续的工作。 你今天教给孩子的东西，会陪他们一直走到遥远的未来。”

“That’s true!” said Annie. “说得对！”安妮说。

Jack smiled. 杰克笑了。

“Well, we better leave—again,” he said. “好了，我们又得走了，”他说。

“Bye!” Everyone waved, except Jeb. “再见！”大家挥手，除了杰布。

Jack and Annie started across the prairie, back toward the tree house. 杰克和安妮开始穿过草原，朝树屋走去。

They hadn’t gone far when Jack heard someone shout his name. He turned around. 他们没走多远，杰克听见有人喊他的名字。他转过身。

It was Jeb. 是杰布。

“Wait!” the older boy yelled. He had a fierce look on his face. He started running toward them. “等等！”大男孩喊道。 他脸上带着凶狠的表情。 他开始朝他们跑来。

“Oh no,” Jack breathed. Did Jeb still want to fight? “哦不，”杰克倒吸一口气。 杰布还想打架吗？

“Leave us alone!” Annie said angrily. “别来烦我们！”安妮生气地说。

“Shh, Annie,” said Jack. “Let’s see what he wants.” “嘘，安妮，”杰克说。 “看看他想干什么。”

When Jeb got close to them, he stopped. He looked right at Jack. 杰布靠近他们时停住了。 他直视着杰克。

“Why did you come back?” he asked. “你们为什么回来？”他问。

“We wanted to tell you about the storm cellar,” said Jack. “我们想告诉你们防风暴地窖的事，”杰克说。

“How did you know about that cellar?” Jeb said. “你们怎么知道有那个地窖？”杰布说。

Jack pulled their research book out of his leather bag. 杰克从皮袋里掏出研究书。

“We read about it in this book,” he said. “我们在这本书里读到的，”他说。

Jeb stared at the book. Then he sighed. 杰布盯着那本书。然后他叹了口气。

“I aim to read someday, too,” he said. “That looks like a good book.” “我也打算有一天学会读书，”他说。 “那看起来是本好书。”

Jack didn’t know what to say. He was still afraid of making Jeb mad. So he just nodded. 杰克不知道该说什么。 他还是怕惹杰布生气。 所以他只是点了点头。

“My ma and pa were too poor to go to school,” said Jeb. “They want me to go. But I’m getting started mighty late.” “我爸妈太穷，没上过学，”杰布说。 “他们希望我能上学。 可我开始得太晚了。”

“It’s not too late,” said Jack. “还不晚，”杰克说。

“It’s never too late,” said Annie. “永远都不晚，”安妮说。

Jeb narrowed his eyes. 杰布眯起眼睛。

“If you ever come back through here … ,” he said. “要是你们再路过这儿……，”他说。

“Yes … ?” Jack asked carefully. Was Jeb about to threaten him again? “嗯……？”杰克小心地问。 杰布又要威胁他吗？

“Maybe I’ll be able to read that book of yours,” Jeb said. “也许我就能读你们那本书了，”杰布说。

Jack sighed and smiled. 杰克松了口气，笑了。

“I know you will,” he said. “我知道你会的，”他说。

Jeb smiled back. He had a nice smile. 杰布也笑了。 他有一张很好看的笑脸。

“Thanks for coming back to rescue us,” he said to both Jack and Annie. “Too bad you can’t stay. I reckon we’d all be good friends if you did.” “谢谢你们回来救我们，”他对杰克和安妮说。 “可惜你们不能留下。 要是你们留下，我想我们都会成为好朋友。”

“I reckon we would,” said Annie. “我想也会，”安妮说。

Jeb nodded. Then he turned and ran back to join the others. 杰布点了点头。 然后他转身跑回去加入其他人。

Suddenly, the sun broke through the clouds. The wildflowers danced in a gentle breeze. 忽然，太阳穿过云层露了出来。 野花在轻柔的微风中舞动。

“Ready?” said Annie. “好了吗？”安妮说。

Jack just stood there, staring at the sunlit prairie. 杰克只是站在那儿，望着阳光下的大草原。

“Jack? Ready to go?” Annie asked. “杰克？ 可以走了吗？”安妮问。

In that moment, Jack actually hated to leave. But he nodded slowly and said, “Let’s go.” 那一刻，杰克其实很不想离开。 可他还是慢慢点头说：“我们走吧。”

He and Annie took off through the bright, sparkling grass. They ran to the small grove of trees near the creek. 他和安妮穿过明亮闪亮的草地跑走了。 他们跑向小溪边的那小片树丛。

They climbed up the rope ladder and scrambled into the tree house. 他们爬上绳梯，钻进了树屋。

Annie picked up their Pennsylvania book. 安妮拿起宾夕法尼亚的书。

“I wish we could go there,” she said. “我希望我们能去那里，”她说。

This time the wind did not start to blow. 这一次风没有刮起来。

The tree house simply started to spin on its own. 树屋自己开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
