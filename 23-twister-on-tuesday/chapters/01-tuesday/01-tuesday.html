1. Tuesday! 1. 星期二！

Jack opened his eyes. Sunlight streamed through his window. 杰克睁开了眼睛。 阳光从他的窗户倾泻进来。

“Tuesday!” he whispered. Morgan’s note had told him and Annie to come back to the magic tree house on Tuesday. He could hardly wait to find out where she was sending them today! “星期二！”他小声说。 摩根的纸条告诉他和安妮，星期二再回到魔法树屋来。 他几乎等不及想知道她今天会把他们派到哪里去！

Jack scrambled out of bed. He threw on his clothes. He packed his notebook and pencil into his backpack. Then he headed into the hall. 杰克翻身下了床。 他迅速穿上衣服。 他把笔记本和铅笔装进背包。 然后他朝走廊走去。

Jack bumped into Annie. She was dressed in jeans and a T-shirt. 杰克撞上了安妮。 她穿着牛仔裤和T恤。

“Tuesday!” they both whispered. “星期二！”他们俩都小声说。

Together, they hurried down the stairs. 他们一起匆匆下了楼。

“Mom, Dad, we’re going out for a few minutes!” Jack shouted. “妈妈，爸爸，我们出去几分钟！”杰克喊道。

“Don’t you want breakfast first?” his dad called from the kitchen. “你们不想先吃早餐吗？”爸爸从厨房里喊道。

“When we get back!” said Annie. “回来再吃！”安妮说。

They rushed out the front door. They ran down their street in the bright summer sunlight. 他们冲出前门。 他们在明亮的夏日阳光下沿着街道跑下去。

A warm wind gently shook the trees as Jack and Annie headed into the Frog Creek woods. Soon they came to the tallest tree in the woods. The magic tree house waited for them in the high branches. Jack and Annie grabbed the rope ladder and climbed up. 暖风轻轻摇晃着树叶，杰克和安妮走进了蛙溪树林。 很快他们来到树林里最高的那棵树。 魔法树屋在高高的树枝上等着他们。 杰克和安妮抓住绳梯爬了上去。

Inside the shady tree house, the note from Morgan was still on the floor:



 在阴凉的树屋里，摩根的纸条还在地板上：

Dear Jack and Annie, 亲爱的杰克和安妮：

Camelot is in trouble. To save the kingdom, please find these four special kinds of writing for my library:



 卡米洛特有难了。 为了拯救王国，请为我的图书馆找到这四种特别的文字：

Something to follow 用来遵循的东西

Something to send 用来送出的东西

Something to learn 用来学习的东西

Something to lend



 用来出借的东西

Thank you, 谢谢。

            Morgan



“Okay,” said Jack. “We have the first writing: something to follow. ” He picked up a list from the Civil War. “好，”杰克说。 “我们有了第一种文字：用来遵循的东西。”他捡起南北战争的那份清单。

“And we have the second,” said Annie, “something to send.” She picked up a letter from the Revolutionary War. “我们还有第二种，”安妮说，“用来送出的东西。”她捡起独立战争的那封信。

“Now we need the third,” said Jack, “something to learn.” “现在我们需要第三种，”杰克说，“用来学习的东西。”

“No problem,” said Annie. She grabbed a book lying in the corner. “I hope we’re not going to another war.” “没问题，”安妮说。 她抓起角落里的一本书。 “希望我们不是又要去打仗。”

Jack and Annie looked at the cover. It showed a field of tall green grass. 杰克和安妮看着封面。 上面是一片高高的绿草地。

The title was Life on the Prairie. 书名是《大草原上的生活》。

“The prairie?” said Annie. “We already went to the prairie the time we met Black Hawk.” “大草原？”安妮说。 “我们以前去过大草原，那次还遇见了黑鹰。”

“Yeah,” said Jack, remembering their adventure with the Native American boy. “对，”杰克说，想起他们和那个印第安男孩的冒险。

He opened the book and turned to a picture of an old-fashioned train crossing the prairie. 他打开书，翻到一张老式火车穿过大草原的图画。

“Oh,” he said. “I get it. Trains crossed the prairie after the pioneers came. When we went to the prairie before, Native Americans were the only people who lived there.” “哦，”他说。 “我明白了。 火车是在拓荒者到来之后才穿过大草原的。 我们以前去大草原时，住在那儿的只有印第安人。”

“So we must be going to pioneer time,” said Annie. “这么说我们一定是要去拓荒时代了，”安妮说。

“I think so,” said Jack. “我想是的，”杰克说。

He pointed at the picture that showed the train crossing the prairie. 他指着那幅火车穿过大草原的画。

“I wish we could go there,” he said. “我希望我们能去那里，”他说。

The breeze picked up. 微风加强了。

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
