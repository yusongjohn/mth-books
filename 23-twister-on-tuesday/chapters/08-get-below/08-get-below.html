8. Get Below! 8. 到下面去！

Suddenly, the schoolhouse door blew off its hinges! It went flying through the air! 忽然，校舍的门被风从铰链上刮飞了！ 它飞到了空中！

Jack grabbed Annie and pulled her into the dugout. 杰克抓住安妮，把她拉进地坑屋。

Inside, the benches were overturned. The room was a mess. 里面，长凳都翻倒了。 房间一片狼藉。

Miss Neely and the three kids were pressed against the dirt wall. Kate and Will screamed as the winds whipped around the room. 尼莉小姐和三个孩子紧贴着土墙。 风在房间里抽打时，凯特和威尔尖叫起来。

Miss Neely hugged Kate. Jeb held on to Will. 尼莉小姐抱住凯特。 杰布抓着威尔。

“Get in the cellar!” Jack yelled. “到地窖去！”杰克喊道。

“What cellar?” shouted Miss Neely. “什么地窖？”尼莉小姐喊道。

Together, Jack and Annie pulled the rug off the floor and uncovered the cellar door. 杰克和安妮一起拉开地毯，露出地窖门。

They grabbed the door and tried to open it, but the wind was blowing too hard. 他们抓住门想打开，可风刮得太猛了。

Suddenly, Jeb was beside them. He pulled the door open. A ladder led down into the cellar. 忽然，杰布来到他们身边。 他拉开了门。 一架梯子通向地窖。

One by one, Will, Kate, Miss Neely, and Annie went down the ladder. 威尔、凯特、尼莉小姐和安妮一个接一个爬下梯子。

Jack waited for Jeb to go down. 杰克等着杰布下去。

“Go! Go!” Jeb shouted. “走！走！”杰布喊道。

Jack climbed down into the cellar. 杰克爬进地窖。

Jeb came last. He closed the door behind him, leaving everyone in total darkness. 杰布最后一个下来。 他关上身后的门，大家陷入一片黑暗。

The twister roared fiercely above them. It sounded like a train barreling right through the schoolhouse! 龙卷风在他们头顶猛烈咆哮。 听起来像一列火车正呼啸着穿过校舍！

As the twister roared and howled, Jack couldn’t think. He couldn’t feel. He was totally swept up in the howl of the wind. 龙卷风咆哮怒吼时，杰克无法思考。他什么也感觉不到。 他完全沉浸在风的呼啸里。

Then, just as Jack felt he would disappear in the roar of the twister, the noise died down. 就在杰克觉得自己要消失在龙卷风的咆哮中时，声音渐渐小了。

And then it was silent. 然后一片寂静。

No one spoke for a moment in the blackness. Then Annie broke the silence. 黑暗中，一时没有人说话。 然后安妮打破了沉默。

“Are we still alive?” she whispered. “我们还活着吗？”她小声问。

Everyone laughed. 大家都笑了。

“Yes, I think we are,” said Miss Neely. “是的，我想我们还活着，”尼莉小姐说。
