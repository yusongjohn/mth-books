8. Good Luck, San Francisco! 8. 好运，旧金山！

Jack and Annie ran over the cobblestones. Dynamite blasts echoed behind them. 杰克和安妮跑过鹅卵石路。 炸药的爆炸声在他们身后回荡。

They headed back down the hill. Flames shot across the roofs, traveling from one house to another. 他们继续往山下走。 火焰掠过屋顶，从一栋房子烧到另一栋。

“We’re heading right into the firestorm!” Jack shouted over the noise. “我们正冲进火风暴！”杰克在嘈杂声中喊道。

“We have to keep going,” Annie shouted back, “before the tree house catches fire!” “我们得继续走，”安妮喊回来，“别等树屋着火！”

At the bottom of the hill, thick smoke was rolling through the street. It made Jack’s eyes burn. 到了山脚，浓烟正滚过街道。 烟熏得杰克眼睛生疼。

“Where’s the tree house?” he shouted. “树屋在哪儿？”他喊道。

“Here!” said Annie. “这儿！”安妮说。

Jack followed her voice. 杰克跟着她的声音走。

She was holding on to the rope ladder. 她正抓着绳梯。

“It’s still here!” Jack said with relief. “它还在！”杰克松了口气说。

“Of course. The tree house wouldn’t leave without us,” said Annie. “Don’t you—” “当然。 树屋不会丢下我们自己走，”安妮说。 “难道你——”

“Go! Go!” said Jack. “走！走！”杰克说。

Annie started up the rope ladder. Jack followed. They climbed into the tree house and looked out the window. 安妮开始爬绳梯。杰克跟着。 他们爬进树屋，望向窗外。

All around, buildings were going up in flames. Black smoke seemed to be smothering the city. 四周的建筑物都在起火。 黑烟似乎正把城市窒息。

Jack could scarcely breathe. His throat burned. His eyes were stinging. 杰克几乎喘不过气。 他的喉咙在烧。 眼睛刺痛。

Annie grabbed their Pennsylvania book. She opened it to the picture of Frog Creek and pointed. 安妮抓起宾夕法尼亚的书。 她翻到蛙溪的图画，指着它。

“I wish we could go there,” she said. “Good luck, San Francisco!” “我希望我们能去那里，”她说。 “好运，旧金山！”

“Good-bye, San Francisco!” said Jack. “再见，旧金山！”杰克说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
