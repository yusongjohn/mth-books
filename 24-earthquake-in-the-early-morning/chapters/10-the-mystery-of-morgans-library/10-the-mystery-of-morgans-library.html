10. The Mystery of Morgan’s Library 10. 摩根图书馆的谜

“I can’t believe we took a trip to Morgan’s library,” said Annie. “简直不敢相信我们去了摩根的图书馆，”安妮说。

Jack smiled and nodded. 杰克笑着点头。

“It was incredible, wasn’t it?” said Annie. “太不可思议了，对不对？”安妮说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “对，”杰克说。

A strong breeze gusted through the tree house window. It blew open their research book about San Francisco. Annie reached for the book. 一阵强风吹进树屋的窗户。 风吹开了他们关于旧金山的研究书。 安妮伸手去拿书。

“Jack!” she said. “Look at this!” “杰克！”她说。 “看这个！”

She pointed to a photograph in the research book. It showed a boy and a girl covered with dirt. The boy held a sign. It was the poem about hope. 她指着研究书里的一张照片。 上面是一个男孩和一个女孩，浑身是土。 男孩举着一块牌子。 那是关于希望的诗。

Annie read the caption aloud: 安妮大声读着说明文字：

After the earthquake, while fires raged through the city, two brave children tried to give hope to others. 地震后，当城市里大火熊熊燃烧时，两个勇敢的孩子试图给别人带来希望。

图中文字：THERE IS NO WATER AND STILL LESS SOAP. WE HAVE NO CITY, BUT LOTS OF HOPE. 图中文字：这里没有水，更没有肥皂。我们没有城市，但有很多希望。

Annie laughed. 安妮笑了。

“Those brave children are us!” she said. “That’s the picture Fred took of us before we left San Francisco!” “那两个勇敢的孩子就是我们！”她说。 “那是我们离开旧金山前弗雷德给我们拍的照片！”

Jack laughed and shook his head with amazement. 杰克笑了，惊奇地摇了摇头。

Annie closed the book. 安妮合上书。

“I guess we are two brave kids who try to give hope,” she said. “We just gave some to King Arthur, didn’t we?” “我想我们就是两个努力给人希望的勇敢孩子，”她说。 “我们刚给了亚瑟王一些希望，对不对？”

“King Arthur?” said Jack. “亚瑟王？”杰克说。

“Yeah,” said Annie. She started down the rope ladder. “That was the mystery of Morgan’s library. We had to give four special writings to King Arthur so he could get his hope and courage back and save Camelot.” “对，”安妮说。 她开始爬下绳梯。 “那就是摩根图书馆的谜。 我们得把四种特别文字交给亚瑟王，好让他重新获得希望和勇气，拯救卡米洛特。”

“That wasn’t King Arthur,” said Jack. He threw on his pack and followed Annie. “那不是亚瑟王，”杰克说。 他背上背包，跟着安妮。

“Sure it was,” said Annie, stepping onto the ground. “Didn’t you hear him say, ‘I’m just an ordinary king’? Get it? King.” “当然是他，”安妮说着，踩到地上。 “你没听见他说‘我只是一位普通的国王’吗？明白了吗？

“But King Arthur’s not an ordinary king,” said Jack. “可亚瑟王不是普通的国王，”杰克说。

“Well, he thinks so,” said Annie. “I know it was him. I feel it.” “嗯，他自己这么想，”安妮说。 “我知道就是他。我感觉得到。”

She smiled. Then she started through the Frog Creek woods. 她笑了。 然后她走进蛙溪树林。

Jack stared after her. 杰克盯着她的背影。

King Arthur! 亚瑟王！

As birdsong filled the early-morning woods, Jack thought about their visit to Morgan’s library. He remembered the sad king and how their writings seemed to give him strength. 鸟叫声充满清晨的树林时，杰克想着他们去摩根图书馆的事。 他想起那位悲伤的国王，以及他们的文字怎样似乎给了他力量。

Maybe Annie was right. Maybe they really had helped King Arthur save Camelot. And maybe someday they would go back. 也许安妮是对的。 也许他们真的帮助亚瑟王拯救了卡米洛特。 也许有一天他们还会回去。

“Hurry, Jack!” Annie called. “Before Mom and Dad wake up!” “快点，杰克！”安妮喊道。 “趁爸妈还没醒！”

“Coming!” Jack shouted. And he took off after her, running for home, finally. “来了！”杰克喊道。 他拔腿追她，终于朝家跑去。
