7. Dynamite! 7. 炸药！

“We’d better be careful,” said Annie. “我们最好小心点，”安妮说。

They stepped in their sock feet over the broken cobblestones, trying not to cut themselves. 他们穿着袜子踏过破碎的鹅卵石，尽量不划伤自己。

They headed down the hillside. On their way, they passed policemen carrying stretchers with injured people on them. 他们朝山下走去。 路上，他们遇见抬着伤员担架的警察。

They passed soldiers directing all those trying to escape the fires. 他们遇见指挥逃火人群的士兵。

One man was trying to push a piano down the street. Another man was wearing a bunch of hats, all piled on top of one another. A woman carried her three little dogs in a bag. 一个男人正试图把钢琴推下街。 另一个男人头上叠戴着一堆帽子。 一个女人用袋子装着三只小狗。

“Everyone’s trying to save what’s important to them,” said Annie. “每个人都在努力保住对自己重要的东西，”安妮说。

“Like the librarian,” said Jack. “And us—we’re trying to save this sign.” He clutched the piece of wood. “就像那位图书管理员，”杰克说。 “还有我们——我们正努力保住这块牌子。”他紧紧抓着那块木头。

When they were halfway down the hill, a soldier on a horse galloped in front of them. 走到半山腰时，一个骑马的士兵飞奔到他们面前。

“Get off the street! We’re setting off dynamite!” he shouted. “离开街道！ 我们要引爆炸药！”他喊道。

“Dynamite?” said Jack. “炸药？”杰克说。

“Yikes,” said Annie. “哎呀，”安妮说。

People started running in all directions. Jack and Annie looked around wildly for a safe place to go. Jack saw an alley. 人们开始四面八方地跑。 杰克和安妮慌乱地寻找安全的地方。 杰克看见一条小巷。

“There!” he said. “那儿！”他说。

They ran into the alley and crouched on the ground. 他们跑进小巷，蹲在地上。

Jack reached into his bag for their research book. He looked in the index for the word “dynamite.” He found it. Then he turned to the right page number and read: 杰克伸手进袋子拿研究书。 他在索引里找“炸药”这个词。他找到了。 然后他翻到正确的页码读道：

After the fires started, the mayor had an idea. He thought that if some buildings were destroyed, the sparks would not fly from one wooden roof to the next. He ordered some buildings to be blown up by dynamite. His plan did not work. The firestorm raged from building to building, from street to street. 大火烧起来后，市长想出了一个主意。他认为如果拆掉一些建筑，火星就无法从一个木屋顶飞到另一个木屋顶。他下令用炸药炸掉一些建筑。但他的计划没有成功，火势仍然从一栋楼烧到另一栋楼，从一条街烧到另一条街。

Firestorm, Jack thought. The word sounded terrible. 火风暴，杰克想。 这个词听起来可怕极了。

Just as he put the book away, a huge blast of dynamite shook the ground. 就在他把书收起来时，一声巨大的炸药爆炸震动了地面。

Dust and dirt flew everywhere, even down the alley. 灰尘泥土到处飞扬，甚至飞进小巷。

Jack clutched their sign with one hand. He covered his eyes with the other. Annie did the same. 杰克一只手紧抓着牌子。 另一只手捂住眼睛。 安妮也一样。

Another huge blast rocked the ground. 又一声巨大的爆炸震动了地面。

Jack tasted grit in his mouth. He looked at Annie. She was caked with dust from head to toe. He looked down at himself. He was just as dirty as she was. 杰克嘴里尝到沙砾的味道。 他看着安妮。 她从头到脚都沾满了灰尘。 他低头看自己。 他和她一样脏。

“Hey, would you look at those two!” someone said. “Now, that’ s a story!” “嘿，看看那两个！”有人说。 “这才叫新闻！”

Jack looked up. Betty, the newspaper reporter, and Fred, the photographer, were standing in front of them. 杰克抬起头。 报纸记者贝蒂和摄影师弗雷德站在他们面前。

Even though they were also covered with dirt, Fred was setting up his camera. And Betty was taking notes in her notebook. 尽管他们也满身是土，弗雷德还在架相机。 贝蒂在笔记本上记着。

“Hold up your sign, sonny,” said Betty. “举起你的牌子，小家伙，”贝蒂说。

Too stunned to say anything, Jack held up the sign with the poem about hope. 杰克惊得说不出话，举起了写着希望之诗的牌子。

Fred took a picture. 弗雷德拍了一张。

Another dynamite blast shook the ground. 又一声炸药爆炸震动了地面。

“Come with us! We’re headed for the park!” said Betty. “跟我们来！ 我们去公园！”贝蒂说。

“We can’t. We’re on our way home, to our parents,” said Annie. “我们不能。 我们正要回家找父母，”安妮说。

“Well, get going! And be careful!” said Betty. “Let’s beat it, Fred!” “那就快走！ 小心点！”贝蒂说。 “我们走，弗雷德！”

The photographer grabbed his camera equipment, and the two of them rushed off. 摄影师抓起相机设备，两人匆匆离开了。

“I don’t think Betty and Fred recognized us,” said Annie. “我觉得贝蒂和弗雷德没认出我们，”安妮说。

“I don’t recognize us,” said Jack. “我都不认识我们自己了，”杰克说。

Another blast shook the ground. 又一声爆炸震动了地面。

“Come on,” said Annie. “Let’s beat it!” “快点，”安妮说。 “我们走！”

Jack and Annie jumped up. Jack put their sign in his bag. Then they started back down the hill. 杰克和安妮跳起来。 杰克把牌子放进袋子。 然后他们继续往山下走。
