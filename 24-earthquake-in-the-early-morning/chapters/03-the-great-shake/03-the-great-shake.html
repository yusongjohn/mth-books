3. The Great Shake 3. 大震动

A cloud of dust billowed around Jack. He could hardly breathe. He couldn’t see. But he could hear Annie coughing. 一团尘云在杰克周围翻涌。 他几乎喘不过气。他也看不见。 可他能听见安妮在咳嗽。

Jack opened his mouth to call to her. But dust filled his throat. 杰克张开嘴想叫她。 可灰尘塞满了他的喉咙。

“Jack!” Between coughs, Annie shouted his name. “Jack!” “杰克！”安妮一边咳一边喊他的名字。 “杰克！”

“I’m here!” he said hoarsely. “我在这儿！”他声音嘶哑地说。

“I think I’m in trouble,” she said. “我想我有麻烦了，”她说。

Jack tried to sit up. He hurt all over. His clothes were ripped and covered with dirt. His cap was gone. 杰克试着坐起来。他浑身都疼。 衣服撕破了，满是泥土。帽子也不见了。

“Where are you?” he called. “你在哪儿？”他喊道。

“Here!” said Annie. “这儿！”安妮说。

Jack started to stand. But he fell down again. His legs were like rubber. 杰克开始站起来。 可又摔倒了。 他的腿软得像橡皮。

“Wh-where?” he repeated. Jack cleaned his glasses, then looked around. But he still couldn’t see Annie through the thick haze of dust. “哪——哪儿？”他又问。 杰克擦了擦眼镜，然后四处看。 可厚厚的尘雾中，他还是看不见安妮。

“I fell into the ground!” said Annie. “我掉进地里了！”安妮说。

Jack crawled in the direction of Annie’s voice. 杰克朝安妮声音的方向爬去。

“Keep talking,” he said. “继续说，”他说。

“Here”—she coughed—“here!” “这儿”——她咳着——“这儿！”

Jack felt a ledge with his hands. He looked down into a huge crack in the street. Through the dust, he could see Annie right below him. 杰克用手摸到一个边缘。 他低头看街上一条巨大的裂缝。 透过灰尘，他看见安妮就在他下面。

“You found me!” she said between coughs. “你找到我了！”她一边咳一边说。

“I’ll pull you out,” said Jack. “我把你拉上来，”杰克说。

He grabbed Annie’s hands. He tried to pull her out of the crack, but she was too heavy. 他抓住安妮的手。 他想把她从裂缝里拉出来，可她太重了。

“I can’t do it,” he said. “我拉不动，”他说。

“Bring me something to stand on,” Annie said. “Maybe I can get out by myself.” “给我找点东西站着，”安妮说。 “也许我能自己爬出来。”

Jack stood up and stumbled away from the crack. He gathered an armload of bricks. Then he went back to the crack and handed them down to Annie, one by one. 杰克站起来，跌跌撞撞地离开裂缝。 他抱起一抱砖块。 然后回到裂缝旁，一块一块递给安妮。

Annie carefully stacked the bricks on top of each other. 安妮小心地把砖块一块块叠起来。

“I need more,” she said. “还要更多，”她说。

Jack ran to get more bricks. He was afraid there’d be another earthquake and the crack would close—with Annie inside! 杰克跑去拿更多砖块。 他害怕再来一次地震，裂缝会合上——把安妮关在里面！

He handed the bricks down to her. 他把砖块递给她。

“Hurry!” he said. “快点！”他说。

“I’m hurrying,” she said. “我在快点，”她说。

At last, Annie finished stacking the bricks. She stood on the stack. With her bare hands, she slowly pulled herself up. 终于，安妮叠完了砖块。 她站在砖堆上。 她用空手慢慢把自己拉了上来。

Jack helped her stand. She was covered with dirt. Her stockings were torn. Her knees were skinned. 杰克扶她站稳。 她浑身是土。 长袜撕破了。 膝盖擦破了皮。

“Are you hurt?” Jack asked. “你受伤了吗？”杰克问。

“A little scraped,” said Annie. “How about you?” “擦破了一点，”安妮说。 “你呢？”

“A little shaky,” said Jack. Actually, he was very shaky. “有点发抖，”杰克说。 其实，他抖得很厉害。

“Me too,” said Annie. “我也是，”安妮说。

“I think San Francisco just had a really huge earthquake,” Jack said. He coughed. His throat was clogged with dust. “我想旧金山刚发生了一场很大的地震，”杰克说。他咳了起来。 他的喉咙塞满了灰尘。

Annie coughed, too. 安妮也咳了起来。

“What’s the book say?” she said. “书上怎么说？”她说。

Jack pulled his research book out of his leather bag. His hands were trembling. He could hardly turn the pages. 杰克从皮袋里掏出研究书。 他的手在发抖。 他几乎翻不动页。

“I’ll find it,” said Annie. She took the book from him and found a picture of a torn-up street. “我来找，”安妮说。 她从他手里接过书，找到一张被撕毁的街道的图画。

She read aloud: 她大声读道：

At 5:13 A.M. on April 18, 1906, the people of San Francisco were shaken awake by one of the biggest earthquakes the United States has ever known. Some called it “the Great Shake.” 1906年4月18日早上5点13分，旧金山的人们被有史以来美国最大的地震之一惊醒。有些人称之为“大震动”。

“No wonder we feel shaky,” said Jack. “难怪我们觉得发抖，”杰克说。

“I wonder if a lot of people got hurt,” said Annie. “不知道有没有很多人受伤，”安妮说。

They looked around. Through the dust-filled air, families were stumbling out of their crumbling houses. They all were barefoot and still wore their nightclothes. 他们环顾四周。 透过满是灰尘的空气，一家家从坍塌的房子里跌跌撞撞地走出来。 他们都光着脚，还穿着睡衣。

Some babies and small children were crying. But strangely, the grown-ups were all silent. They just stared at the torn-up street and crumbling houses. 有些婴儿和小孩在哭。 可奇怪的是，大人们都沉默着。 他们只是盯着被撕毁的街道和坍塌的房屋。

“Everyone must be in shock,” said Annie. “大家一定都惊呆了，”安妮说。

“I know how they feel,” said Jack. He gazed at the rubble all around them. He didn’t know what to do. He couldn’t think clearly. “我知道那种感觉，”杰克说。 他看着周围的瓦砾。 他不知道该做什么。 他无法清楚地思考。

Annie looked at the book again. She read aloud: 安妮又看了看书。她大声读道：

Just after the earthquake, broken chimneys, stoves, and lamps caused terrible fires. The fires raged for three days, nearly destroying all of San Francisco. Over 28,000 buildings burned down. 地震刚过，倒塌的烟囱、炉子和灯引发了可怕的火灾。大火燃烧了三天，几乎摧毁了整个旧金山。超过28000栋建筑被烧毁。

“That’s terrible,” breathed Jack. “太可怕了，”杰克倒吸一口气。

In the distance, a cloud of black smoke was rolling through the sky. 远处，一团黑烟正滚过天空。

“The fires are starting!” said Annie. “火灾开始了！”安妮说。
