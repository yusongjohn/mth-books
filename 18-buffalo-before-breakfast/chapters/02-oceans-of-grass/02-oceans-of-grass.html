2. Oceans of Grass 2. 草之海

Early sunlight slanted into the tree house. The cool breeze smelled of wild grass. 清晨的阳光斜斜照进树屋。 凉爽的微风带着野草的气味。

“Oh, man,” said Jack. “These are neat clothes.” “天哪，”杰克说。 “噢，天哪，”杰克说，“这些衣服真棒。”

Their jeans and T-shirts had magically changed. Jack had on a buckskin shirt and pants. Annie wore a fringed buckskin dress. 他们的牛仔裤和T恤魔法般变了样。 杰克穿着鹿皮衬衫和裤子。 安妮穿着带流苏的鹿皮连衣裙。

They both wore soft leather boots and coonskin caps. Jack’s backpack was now a leather bag. 他们俩都穿着软皮靴，戴着浣熊皮帽。 杰克的背包现在成了皮袋子。

“I feel like a mountain man,” he said. “我觉得自己像个山地人，”他说。

“All you’re missing is a mountain,” said Annie. She pointed out the window. “你唯一缺的就是一座山了，”安妮说。 她指着窗外。

Jack and Teddy looked out. 杰克和泰迪往外看。

The tree house sat in a lone tree in a vast golden prairie. The sun was rising in the distance. 树屋坐落在辽阔金色草原上孤零零的一棵树上。 太阳正在远处升起。

Wind whispered through the tall yellow grass. Shh—shh— shh, it said. 风在高高的黄草间低语。 嘘——嘘——嘘，它说。

“We need a gift from the prairie blue,” said Jack. “现在该去找那份来自蓝色大草原的礼物了。”

“I bet that means the sky,” said Annie, looking up. “我敢打赌那是指天空，”安妮抬头说。

“Yep,” said Jack. The sky was growing bluer as they watched. “But how are we supposed to get it?” “对，”杰克说。 他们望着天空，天色渐渐变得更蓝。 “可我们该怎么得到它呢？”

“Just like last time,” said Annie. “We have to wait till someone gives it to us.” “就跟上一次一样，”安妮说。 “我们得等到有人把它送给我们。”

“I don’t see any sign of people out there,” said Jack. “外面看不见有人的迹象，”杰克说。

He opened their book and read aloud. 他打开他们的书大声读。

The Great Plains are in the middle of the United States. Before the 20th century, this vast prairie covered nearly a fifth of America’s land. Some called it “an ocean of grass.” 大平原位于美国中部。20世纪之前，这片广阔的草原几乎覆盖了美国五分之一的土地。有些人称它为“草的海洋”。

Jack pulled out his notebook. 杰克抽出笔记本。

“Come on,” said Annie. “来吧，”安妮说。

She picked up Teddy and carried him down the ladder. 她抱起泰迪，带着它下梯子。

Jack quickly wrote: 杰克飞快地写道：

图中文字：Great Plains - 10ts of land 图中文字：大平原 - 十亿吨土地

“Wow, this is like an ocean of grass,” Annie called from below. “哇，这简直像一片草的海洋，”安妮在下面喊道。

Jack slipped the Great Plains book and his notebook into his leather bag and climbed down. 杰克把大平原书和笔记本塞进皮袋子，爬了下去。

When he stepped onto the ground, the grass came all the way up to his chest. It tickled his nose. 他一踩到地上，草就一直没到他的胸口。 草尖挠得他鼻子发痒。

“Ah-ah-CHOO!” he sneezed. “阿——阿——嚏！”他打了个喷嚏。

“Let’s go swimming in the grass ocean,” said Annie. “我们去草海里游泳吧，”安妮说。

She started off with Teddy under her arm. 她夹着泰迪出发了。

The wind blew gently as Jack hurried after her. All he could see was rolling waves of grass. 风轻轻吹着，杰克急忙跟上她。 他眼前只有连绵起伏的草浪。

They walked and walked and walked. Finally, they stopped to rest. 他们走啊走啊走。 终于，他们停下来休息。

“We could walk for months and never see anything but grass,” said Jack. “我们走上几个月，除了草什么也看不见，”杰克说。

Arf! Arf!

“Teddy says there’s something great up ahead,” said Annie. “泰迪说前面有很棒的东西，”安妮说。

“You can’t tell what he’s saying,” said Jack. “He’s just barking.” “你听不出它在说什么，”杰克说。 “它只是在叫。”

“I can tell,” said Annie. “Trust me.” “我能听出来，”安妮说。 “相信我。”

“We can’t walk all day,” said Jack. “我们不能走一整天，”杰克说。

“Come on,” said Annie. “Just a little farther.” She started walking again. “来吧，”安妮说。 “再走远一点点。”她又开始往前走。

“Oh, brother,” said Jack. “哦，天哪，”杰克说。

But he kept going through the tall, rippling grass. They went down a small slope, then up a small rise. At the top of the rise, Jack froze. 可他还是继续穿过那片高高的、起伏的草。 他们走下一个小斜坡，再爬上一处小高地。 到了高地顶端，杰克愣住了。

“Wow, that is great,” he whispered. “哇，太棒了，”他低声说。

“Told you,” said Annie. “我说对了吧，”安妮说。
