Arf! Arf! Arf!

1. Teddy’s Back! 1. 泰迪回来了！

Jack finished tying his sneakers. Then he looked out his bedroom window. 杰克系好运动鞋。 然后他朝卧室窗外望去。

A small dog stood in the early sunlight. He had floppy ears and scruffy brown fur. 一只小狗站在清晨的阳光里。 它长着耷拉的耳朵和乱蓬蓬的棕色毛。

“Teddy!” said Jack. “泰迪！”杰克说。

Just then, Annie ran into Jack’s room. 就在那时，安妮跑进了杰克的房间。

“Teddy’s back!” she said. “It’s time.” “泰迪回来了！”她说。 “是时候了。”

It was time for their second mission to help free the little dog from a spell. 是时候执行第二次任务，帮助把小狗从咒语中解救出来了。

Jack threw his notebook and pencil into his backpack. Then he followed Annie downstairs and past the kitchen. 杰克把笔记本和铅笔扔进背包。 然后他跟着安妮下楼，经过厨房。

“Where are you two going?” their mom called. “你们俩去哪儿？”妈妈喊道。

“Outside,” said Jack. “外面，”杰克说。

“Breakfast will be ready soon,” she said. “And Grandmother will be here any minute.” “早餐很快就好，”她说。 “而且奶奶随时都会到。”

“We’ll be right back,” said Jack. He loved his grandmother’s visits. She was kind and funny. And she always taught them new things. “我们马上回来，”杰克说。 他很喜欢祖母来做客。 她又和气又有趣。 而且她总是教他们新东西。

Jack and Annie slipped out the front door. Teddy was waiting for them. 杰克和安妮从前门溜了出去。 泰迪在等他们。

Arf! Arf! he barked.

“Hey, where did you go last week?” Jack asked. “嘿，你上周去哪儿了？”杰克问。

The small dog wagged his tail joyfully. 小狗高兴地摇着尾巴。

Then he ran up the sidewalk. 然后它沿人行道跑上去。

“Wait for us!” Annie shouted. “等等我们！”安妮喊道。

She and Jack followed Teddy up the street and into the Frog Creek woods. 她和杰克跟着泰迪沿街跑进蛙溪树林。

They ran between the trees. Wind rattled the leaves. Birds swooped from branch to branch. 他们在树间奔跑。 风吹得树叶沙沙作响。 鸟儿从一根树枝飞扑到另一根树枝。

Teddy stopped at a rope ladder that hung from the tallest oak tree in the woods. At the top of the ladder was the magic tree house. 泰迪停在树林里最高那棵橡树上垂下的绳梯旁。 梯子顶端就是魔法树屋。

Jack and Annie stared up at it. 杰克和安妮抬头盯着它。

“No sign of Morgan,” said Annie. “看不见摩根的踪影，”安妮说。

“Let’s go up,” said Jack. “我们上去吧，”杰克说。

Annie picked up Teddy. She carried him carefully up the ladder. Jack climbed after her. 安妮抱起泰迪。 她小心地抱着它往上爬。 杰克跟在后面爬。

Inside the tree house, Teddy sniffed a silver pocket watch on the floor. Beside it was the note that Morgan had written to Jack and Annie. 树屋里，泰迪嗅着地板上的一只银色怀表。 旁边是摩根写给杰克和安妮的字条。

Annie picked up the note and read it aloud: 安妮捡起字条大声读道：

This little dog is under a spell and needs your help. To free him, you must be given four special things: 这只小狗中了咒语，需要你们的帮助。 要解救它，你们必须得到四样特别的东西：

A gift from a ship lost at sea,
A gift from the prairie blue,
A gift from a forest far away,
A gift from a kangaroo.
Be brave. Be wise. Be careful.

“We’ve got the first special thing,” said Annie, “the gift from a ship lost at sea.” “我们已经有了第一样特别的东西，”安妮说，“来自一艘沉没海船的礼物。”

“Yeah,” said Jack. He picked up the silver pocket watch. “对，”杰克说。 他拿起银色怀表。

The time on the watch was 2:20—the time the Titanic had sunk. 表上的时间是两点二十——泰坦尼克号沉没的时刻。

Jack and Annie stared at the watch. 杰克和安妮盯着那块表。

Arf! Arf! 汪！汪！

Teddy’s barking brought Jack back from his memories. 泰迪的叫声把杰克从回忆里拉了回来。

“Okay,” Jack said. He sighed and pushed his glasses into place. “Now it’s time for the gift from the prairie blue.” “好，”杰克说。 他叹了口气，把眼镜推正。 “现在该找来自草原蓝天的礼物了。”

“What’s that mean?” said Annie. “那是什么意思？”安妮说。

“I’m not sure,” said Jack. He looked around the tree house. “But I bet that book will take us there.” “我不确定，”杰克说。 他在树屋里四处看。 “可我敢打赌那本书会带我们去。”

He picked up a book in the corner. The cover was a picture of a wide prairie. The title was The Great Plains. 他拿起角落里的一本书。 封面是一片辽阔草原的图画。 书名是《大平原》。

“Ready?” Jack said. “准备好了吗？”杰克说。

Teddy yipped and wagged his tail. 泰迪尖叫了一声，摇着尾巴。

“Let’s go,” said Annie. “The sooner we free Teddy, the better.” “走吧，”安妮说。 “我们越早解救泰迪越好。”

Jack pointed at the cover. 杰克指着封面。

“I wish we could go there,” he said. “我希望我们能去那里，”他说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 绝对静止。
