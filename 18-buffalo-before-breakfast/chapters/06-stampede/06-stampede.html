6. Stampede! 6. 狂奔！

“Wow,” whispered Jack and Annie together. “哇，”杰克和安妮一起低声说。

Black Hawk looked silently at the grazing buffalo. 黑鹰静静地看着吃草的野牛。

“Hand me the research book,” said Jack. “把研究书给我，”杰克说。

Annie lifted Teddy out of the bag. Then she slid the book out and gave it to Jack. 安妮把泰迪从袋子里抱出来。 然后她抽出书递给杰克。

He found a picture of a buffalo herd. He read to himself. 他找到一幅野牛群的图画。 他默默读着。

The true name of the buffalo is “bison.” At the beginning of the 1800s, there were 40 million bison on the Great Plains. One hundred years later, there were less than 300. Almost all had been killed by white hunters and soldiers. 野牛的真正名字是“ Bison（ Bison）”。19世纪初，大平原上有4000万头野牛。一百年后，只剩下不到300头。几乎所有的野牛都被白人猎人和士兵杀死了。

Jack looked back at the vast herd. As far as he could see, there was nothing but buffalo. 杰克回头看那片巨大的牛群。 目力所及，除了野牛什么也没有。

Now Jack knew for certain they’d come to the time before the white settlers and soldiers had arrived, before the end of the great buffalo herds. 现在杰克可以肯定，他们来到的是白人殖民者和士兵到来之前、大野牛群消失之前的时代。

“I have been on many hunts,” Black Hawk said, his eyes still on the buffalo. “我参加过很多次狩猎，”黑鹰说，眼睛仍盯着野牛。

“Were you scared?” said Jack. “你害怕过吗？”杰克说。

Black Hawk shook his head. 黑鹰摇了摇头。

“You’re really brave,” said Annie. “你真勇敢，”安妮说。

Black Hawk smiled proudly. 黑鹰骄傲地笑了。

“I will show you how a brave hunter moves,” he said. “我给你们看看勇敢的猎人怎么行动，”他说。

He slid off his pony. 他滑下小马。

“Wait, your grandmother said not to hunt,” said Annie. “Plus, you don’t have your wolf suit.” “等等，你奶奶说不要猎，”安妮说。 “而且，你也没穿你的狼套装。”

“I am not afraid,” said Black Hawk. “我不怕，”黑鹰说。

“I don’t think you should go down there,” said Jack. “There’s no grownups around.” “我觉得你不该下去，”杰克说。 “附近没有大人。”

But Black Hawk wasn’t listening. 可黑鹰没有在听。

He began creeping on all fours toward the buffalo. 他开始四肢着地，悄悄朝野牛爬去。

“I have a feeling something bad is going to happen,” said Annie. “我有种感觉，要出事了，”安妮说。

Jack had the same feeling. He looked back at the book. 杰克也有同样的感觉。 他回头看书。

A bison can weigh two thousand pounds and stand six feet high. If one becomes alarmed by a hunter, he might start running and set off a terrifying stampede. 一只野牛可以重达2000磅，身高6英尺。如果它被猎人吓到，可能会开始奔跑，引发可怕的踩踏。

Jack looked back at Black Hawk. He was creeping closer and closer to the herd. 杰克回头看黑鹰。 他正一点点靠近牛群。

Jack’s heart pounded. He wanted to shout, Come back! But he didn’t want to scare any of the huge, fierce-looking animals. 杰克的心怦怦直跳。 他想喊：回来！ 可他不想吓到那些巨大、看起来很凶猛的动物。

Keeping his eyes on Black Hawk, Jack handed the plains book to Annie. She slid it back into the bag beside Teddy. 杰克眼睛盯着黑鹰，把平原书递给安妮。 她把它塞回袋子，放在泰迪旁边。

Black Hawk stopped just as he was passing the nearest buffalo. His eyes squeezed shut. His nose wrinkled up. His mouth opened. 黑鹰刚经过最近那头野牛时停住了。 他的眼睛紧紧闭上。 他的鼻子皱了起来。

“What’s he doing?” asked Jack. “他在干什么？”杰克问。

“Ah-ah-CHOO!” Black Hawk sneezed. “阿——阿——嚏！”黑鹰打了个喷嚏。

“Uh-oh,” said Annie. “糟了，”安妮说。

The huge buffalo jerked its head up. It made a low, moaning sound. Then it pointed its horns and charged! 那头巨大的野牛猛地抬起头。 它发出低沉的呻吟声。 然后它把犄角对准，冲了过来！

“Watch out!” cried Jack. “小心！”杰克喊道。

Black Hawk threw himself out of the way of the charging buffalo. 黑鹰猛地闪到一边，躲开冲过来的野牛。

A ripple went through the herd as other shaggy animals looked up. 一股波动传过牛群，其他毛茸茸的动物也抬起了头。

Suddenly, Teddy jumped out of Annie’s arms. He landed in the tall grass and ran toward the buffalo. 突然，泰迪从安妮怀里跳了出去。 它落在高草里，朝野牛跑去。

“Teddy!” shouted Annie. “泰迪！”安妮喊道。

The dog tore down the hill. He bounded along the edge of the herd, barking furiously. 那只狗飞奔下山。 它沿着牛群边缘跳跃着，狂怒地叫着。

“Teddy, come back!” cried Annie. “泰迪，回来！”安妮喊道。

She slid off her pony and ran after Teddy. 她滑下小马，追着泰迪跑。

Jack tried to see Black Hawk. 杰克想看见黑鹰。

The boy was still dodging the running buffalo. He looked tired. 那男孩还在躲闪奔跑的野牛。

Jack took a deep breath. 杰克深吸一口气。

“Go to Black Hawk!” he said, nudging Sunlight with his knees. “去黑鹰那儿！”他说着，用膝盖轻推阳光。

The golden pony charged down the slope. He ran between the buffalo. 金色小马冲下斜坡。 它在野牛之间奔跑。

“Black Hawk!” Jack shouted. “黑鹰！”杰克喊道。

Black Hawk started running toward Sunlight. The buffalo swerved behind him. 黑鹰开始朝阳光跑来。 野牛在他身后突然转向。

Sunlight slowed as Black Hawk got near. The boy threw himself over the golden pony’s back. He held on to Jack as Sunlight veered away from the buffalo and ran back up the slope. 黑鹰靠近时，阳光放慢了速度。 男孩扑上金色小马的背。 他抓住杰克，阳光号掉转方向离开野牛，跑回坡上。

“Where’s Annie?” Jack cried as they reached the top. “安妮在哪儿？”他们到达坡顶时杰克喊道。

“There!” said Black Hawk, pointing. “那儿！”黑鹰指着说。

Annie was surrounded by buffalo— calm buffalo. She was patting them and talking to them. The buffalo near her had stopped running, too. 安妮被野牛包围了——平静的野牛。 她正拍着它们，对它们说话。 她附近的野牛也停住了奔跑。

The ones beyond those started to calm down … then others … until all the buffalo had stopped running. They began grazing again as if nothing had happened. 更远一点的那些开始平静下来……然后是另一些……直到所有野牛都停住了奔跑。 它们又开始吃草，好像什么都没发生过。
