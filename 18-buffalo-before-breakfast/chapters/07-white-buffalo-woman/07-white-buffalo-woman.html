7. White Buffalo Woman 7. 白野牛女人

“She has good medicine,” said Black Hawk. “她有好药，”黑鹰说。

“Annie doesn’t have any medicine,” Jack said. “She just has a way with animals.” “安妮没有任何药，”杰克说。 “她只是特别会和动物相处。”

Black Hawk was silent. He climbed back on his waiting pony. Then he rode down toward Annie. 黑鹰沉默着。 他爬回等候的小马。 然后他骑马朝安妮下去。

Jack followed. Annie’s pony trailed behind. 杰克跟上去。 安妮的马跟在后面。

Annie turned to Jack and Black Hawk as they rode up to her. On her face was a look of amazement. 杰克和黑鹰骑到她身边时，安妮转向他们。 她脸上带着惊奇的神情。

“You wouldn’t believe what happened!” she said. “你肯定想不到刚才发生了什么！”她说。

“You stopped the stampede,” said Black Hawk. “你停下了狂奔，”黑鹰说。

“But it wasn’t just me,” said Annie. “可不只是我，”安妮说。

“What do you mean?” asked Jack. “什么意思？”杰克问。

“I was trying to find Teddy,” said Annie, “and I got in the way of the buffalo. I couldn’t escape. So I held up my hands and shouted, ‘Stop!’ Then, out of nowhere, a beautiful lady in a white leather dress came to help me.” “我当时正想找到泰迪，”安妮说，“结果挡了野牛的路。” 我逃不掉。 于是我举起双手喊：“停！”接着，一位穿着白色皮裙的美丽女士突然出现，来帮我。

“You saw a lady in white?” asked Black Hawk. His eyes had grown wide. “你看见一位穿白衣的女士？”黑鹰问。 他的眼睛睁大了。

“Yes!” said Annie. “She held up her hands, and the buffalo stopped running. Then she disappeared.” “是的！”安妮说。 “她举起双手，野牛就停止了奔跑。” 然后她消失了。”

“Where’s Teddy?” said Jack. “泰迪在哪儿？”杰克说。

Annie gasped. 安妮倒吸一口气。

“I don’t know! I forgot about him!” she said. “Teddy! Teddy!” “我不知道！ 我把他给忘了！”她说。 “泰迪！泰迪！”

Arf! Arf! 汪！汪！

The little dog came bounding out of the grass toward them. 小狗从草丛里朝他们蹦跳过来。

Annie scooped him up. Teddy licked her face all over. 安妮一把把他抱起来。 泰迪把她的脸舔了个遍。

“Where did you go?” Annie asked him. “Did you see the beautiful lady, too?” “你去哪儿了？”安妮问它。 “你也看见那位美丽的女士了吗？”

“That lady does not live on this earth,” Black Hawk said softly. “那位女士并不住在这个世界上，”黑鹰轻声说。

“What do you mean?” said Annie. “什么意思？”安妮说。

“You saw the spirit of White Buffalo Woman,” he said. “你看见的是白野牛女人的灵魂，”他说。

“What do you mean, spirit?” said Jack. “You mean like a ghost?” “什么意思，精灵？”杰克说。 “你是说像鬼魂那样？”

Black Hawk turned his pony around. 黑鹰掉转小马。

“Let us go back,” he said. “We must tell Grandmother.” “我们回去吧，”他说。 “我们必须告诉奶奶。”

Annie put Teddy in Jack’s bag. Then she climbed on her pony, and they took off. 安妮把泰迪放进杰克的袋子。 然后她爬上自己的小马，他们出发了。

Behind them, the buffalo grazed peacefully on the plains. 在他们身后，野牛在平原上平静地吃着草。
