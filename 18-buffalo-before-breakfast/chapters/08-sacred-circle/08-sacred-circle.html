8. Sacred Circle 8. 神圣的圆圈

The sun was going down as the three ponies galloped for home. The deep blue sky was streaked with golden red light. 三匹小马朝家疾驰时，太阳正在落下。 深蓝色的天空布满金红色的条纹。

Back at the Lakota camp, the circle of tepees glowed in the setting sun. People were gathered around a large fire. 回到拉科塔营地，圆锥帐篷围成的圆圈在落日中发光。 人们围聚在一大堆火旁。

Black Hawk led Jack and Annie to the camp. They got off their ponies and went over to the fire. 黑鹰领着杰克和安妮到营地。 他们下了小马，走到火边。

Grandmother rose to greet them. 祖母站起来迎接他们。

“You have been gone a long time,” she said. “你们离开很久了，”她说。

Black Hawk looked her bravely in the eye. 黑鹰勇敢地直视她的眼睛。

“Grandmother, I tried to hunt the buffalo alone,” he said. “One charged at me, but Jack saved my life. Then Annie and White Buffalo Woman stopped all the other buffalo from a stampede.” “奶奶，我想独自去猎野牛，”他说。 “有一头朝我冲过来，可杰克救了我的命。 然后安妮和白野牛女人阻止了其他所有野牛狂奔。”

“Let this be a lesson to you,” Grandmother said sternly. “Your pride led you to show off. Showing off made you behave foolishly. Your foolishness frightened a buffalo. He frightened others. One thing always leads to another. Everything is related.” “你要记住这次教训，”祖母严厉地说。 “你的骄傲让你去炫耀。” “炫耀让你表现得很愚蠢。” “你的愚蠢惊吓了一头野牛。” 他吓到了其他的。 “一件事总会引出另一件事。” “万物都是相互关联的。”

“I am sorry,” said Black Hawk. He hung his head. “I have learned.” “对不起，”黑鹰说。他低下了头。 “我学到了。”

Jack felt sorry for Black Hawk. 杰克为黑鹰感到难过。

“I make mistakes sometimes, too,” he said softly. “我有时候也会犯错，”他轻声说。

“Me too,” said Annie. “我也是，”安妮说。

Grandmother looked at Jack and Annie. 奶奶看着杰克和安妮。

“Buffalo Girl and Rides-Like-Wind showed great courage today,” she said. “野牛女孩和御风骑士今天表现出了极大的勇气，”她说。

Jack smiled. He loved his new Lakota name: Rides-Like- Wind. 杰克笑了。 他喜欢自己的新拉科塔名字：骑风者。

“We welcome you to our family,” said Grandmother. “我们欢迎你们加入我们的家庭，”奶奶说。

The evening shadows spread over the camp. Someone began beating a drum. It sounded like a heartbeat. 傍晚的阴影笼罩营地。 有人开始敲鼓。 那声音听起来像心跳。

“Come, sit with us in our circle,” said Grandmother. “来，和我们一起坐在圆圈里，”祖母说。

They sat with her near the warm fire. A cool breeze blew sparks into the gray twilight. 他们和她坐在温暖的火旁。 凉爽的微风把火星吹进灰色的暮色。

An old man held a long pipe up to the sky. He pointed it to the east, the south, the west, and the north. 一位老人把一根长烟斗举向天空。 他把它指向东方、南方、西方和北方。

Then he passed the pipe to the next man in the circle. The man put the pipe to his lips and blew smoke into the golden firelight. Then he passed it on. 然后他把烟斗传给圆圈里下一个人。 那人把烟斗放在唇边，把烟吹进金色的火光里。 然后他继续传下去。

“The smoke from the sacred pipe joins all things to the Great Spirit,” Grandmother said to Jack and Annie. “圣烟斗的烟雾把万物与伟大圣灵连在一起，”祖母对杰克和安妮说。

“The Great Spirit?” asked Annie. “大灵？”安妮问。

“The Great Spirit is the source of all things in the sacred circle of life,” said Grandmother. “It is the source of all spirits.” “伟大圣灵是神圣生命之环中万物的来源，”祖母说。 “它是所有精灵的源头。”

“What spirits?” asked Jack. “什么精灵？”杰克问。

“There are many,” said Grandmother. “Wind spirits, tree spirits, bird spirits. Sometimes they can be seen. Sometimes not.” “有很多，”奶奶说。 “风精灵、树精灵、鸟精灵。 有时看得见。有时看不见。”

“What about the White Buffalo Woman?” said Jack. “Who is she?” “那白野牛女人呢？”杰克说。 “她是谁？”

“She is a messenger of the Great Spirit,” said Grandmother. “He sent her when the people were starving. She brought the sacred pipe so that our prayers could rise to the Great Spirit. He answers by sending us the buffalo.” “她是伟大圣灵的信使，”祖母说。 “他在人们挨饿的时候派她来。” 她带来圣烟斗，好让我们的祈祷升到伟大圣灵那里。 他用送来野牛来回答我们。”

“Why do you think White Buffalo Woman came to me?” asked Annie. “你觉得白野牛女人为什么来找我？”安妮问。

“Sometimes courage can summon help from the beyond,” Grandmother said. “有时候勇气能从另一个世界召唤来帮助，”祖母说。

She pulled a brown-and-white feather from a small buckskin bag. 她从小鹿皮袋里抽出一根棕白相间的羽毛。

She put the feather on the ground in front of Jack and Annie. 她把羽毛放在杰克和安妮面前的地上。

“This is a gift for you,” she said. “An eagle’s feather for your courage.” “这是给你们的礼物，”她说。 “一根鹰羽，表彰你们的勇气。”

Arf! Arf! Teddy wagged his tail. 汪！汪！ 泰迪摇着尾巴。

Jack and Annie smiled at each other. The eagle’s feather was their “gift from the prairie blue.” 杰克和安妮相视而笑。 鹰羽就是他们的“来自草原蓝天的礼物”。

Their mission was complete. 他们的任务完成了。

The chanting and drumbeats grew louder and louder. Then they stopped. 吟唱和鼓声越来越响。 然后他们停下了。

The old man held the pipe up to the sky. 老人把烟斗举向天空。

“All things are related,” he said. “万物相连，”他说。

The pipe-smoking ceremony was over. 抽烟斗的仪式结束了。

The sky was dark and filled with stars. 天空暗了下来，布满星星。

One by one, people rose from the circle and went to their tepees. 人们一个接一个从圆圈里站起来，走进自己的帐篷。

Jack put the eagle’s feather in his bag and yawned. 杰克把鹰羽放进袋子里，打了个哈欠。

“We better go home now,” he said. “我们最好现在回家，”他说。

“You must rest first,” said Grandmother. “You can leave in the dawn.” “你们必须先休息，”奶奶说。 “你们可以天亮时再离开。”

“Good plan,” said Annie. She was yawning, too. “好主意，”安妮说。 她也在打哈欠。

They went with Grandmother and Black Hawk to their tepee. 他们和奶奶、黑鹰一起去他们的帐篷。

Grandmother pointed to two buffalo robes that lay to one side of the still-burning fire. Jack and Annie stretched out on them. Teddy snuggled between them. 奶奶指着仍在燃烧的火旁边的两张野牛皮袍。 杰克和安妮在上面躺直。 泰迪依偎在他们中间。

Grandmother and Black Hawk lay on robes across from them. 奶奶和黑鹰躺在他们对面的皮袍上。

Jack watched as the bluish white smoke rose from the fire. It went up through the tepee hole and into the endless starry sky. 杰克看着蓝白色的烟从火上升起。 它穿过帐篷顶的洞，升进无尽的星空。

Jack listened to the wind blowing through the grass: Shh—shh— shh. 杰克听着风吹过草丛：嘘——嘘——嘘。

It’s the voice of the Great Plains, he thought. Then he drifted off to sleep. 那是大平原的声音，他想。 然后他渐渐睡着了。
