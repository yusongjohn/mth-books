10. Good Medicine 10. 好医药

“We’re home,” said Annie. “我们到家了，”安妮说。

Bright sunlight flooded the tree house. Teddy licked Jack’s and Annie’s faces. They were back in their jeans and T-shirts. 明亮的阳光涌进树屋。 泰迪舔着杰克和安妮的脸。 他们又穿着牛仔裤和T恤了。

“Hey, silly,” Annie said to the dog. “Now we have the second thing to help free you from your spell.” “嘿，小傻瓜，”安妮对那只狗说。 “现在我们有了帮你解开咒语的第二样东西。”

She took the eagle’s feather out of Jack’s backpack. She put it on Morgan’s note, next to the silver pocket watch from the Titanic. 她从杰克背包里取出鹰羽。 她把它放在摩根的字条上，旁边是来自泰坦尼克号的银色怀表。

“Now we have our gift from the prairie blue,” said Jack. “Let your thoughts rise as high as this feather.” “现在我们有了来自草原蓝天的礼物，”杰克说。 “让你的思想像这根羽毛一样高高升起。”

“Hey, I just had a thought!” said Annie. “嘿，我刚想到一件事！”安妮说。

“What?” said Jack. “什么？”杰克说。

“I bet Teddy had something to do with White Buffalo Woman,” she said. “我敢打赌，泰迪和白野牛女人有关系，”她说。

“Why?” asked Jack. “为什么？”杰克问。

“One second Teddy disappeared in the grass. Then White Buffalo Woman appeared,” said Annie. “When White Buffalo Woman disappeared, Teddy appeared.” “上一秒泰迪还在草里消失了。 然后白野牛女人就出现了，”安妮说。 “白野牛女人一消失，泰迪就出现了。”

“Hmm … ” said Jack. He stared at the little dog. “嗯……”杰克说。 他盯着小狗。

Teddy tilted his head and gave Jack a wise look. 泰迪歪着头，给了杰克一个睿智的眼神。

“Well … ” said Jack, “maybe Teddy has good medicine.” “嗯……”杰克说，“也许泰迪有好医药。”

“Now you understand,” said Annie, smiling. “现在你明白了，”安妮笑着说。

“Ja-ack! An-nie!” A call came from the distance. “杰——克！ 安——妮！”远处传来一声呼唤。

Jack and Annie looked out the window of the tree house. 杰克和安妮朝树屋窗外望去。

Their mom and their grandmother were standing on their porch. 他们的妈妈和祖母正站在门廊上。

“Yay, Grandmother’s here!” said Annie. “耶，奶奶来了！”安妮说。

“We’re coming!” they shouted together. “我们来了！”他们一起喊道。

“Let’s put Teddy in your backpack,” said Annie. “So we can take him home with us this time.” “我们把泰迪放进你的背包，”安妮说。 “这样这次我们能带它回家。”

“Okay,” said Jack. “好，”杰克说。

But when they turned around, the little dog was gone. 可他们转过身时，小狗不见了。

“Teddy?” said Annie. “泰迪？”安妮说。

There was no sign of him. 没有任何它的踪影。

“Oh, man, as soon as we turned our backs, he slipped away,” said Jack. “Just like last time.” “噢，天哪，我们一转过身，他就溜走了，”杰克说。 “就跟上一次一样，”安妮说。

“Don’t worry,” said Annie. “He’ll find us again soon. I’m sure of it.” She started down the rope ladder. “别担心，”安妮说。 “他很快还会找到我们的。我非常肯定。” 我确信。”她开始顺着绳梯往下爬。

Jack grabbed his pack and followed. 杰克抓起背包跟上去。

As they started for home, a wind gusted through the trees. 他们往家走时，一阵风猛刮过树林。

Jack stopped for a moment to look at the woods. 杰克停了一会儿，看着树林。

Branches waved their leaves. 树枝摇动着叶子。

Birds left the branches and swooped up into the blue sky. 鸟儿离开树枝，冲上蓝天。

Black Hawk’s grandmother is right, he thought. All things are related. 黑鹰的奶奶说得对，他想。 万物相连。

“Jack!” called Annie. “杰克！”安妮喊道。

“Coming!” said Jack. “来了！”杰克说。

He hurried to catch up with her. 他急忙赶上她。

Together they ran out of the Frog Creek woods … up their street … and into their own grandmother’s arms. 他们一起跑出蛙溪树林……沿街跑上去……扑进自己奶奶的怀里。
