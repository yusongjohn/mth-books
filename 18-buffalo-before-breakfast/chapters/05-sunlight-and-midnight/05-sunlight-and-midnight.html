5. Sunlight and Midnight 5. 日光和午夜

The wolf had yellow eyes and sharp teeth. 那头狼有黄色的眼睛和锋利的牙齿。

Teddy snarled and barked. Annie rushed forward to grab the little dog. 泰迪龇牙低吼着叫起来。 安妮冲上前去抓小狗。

Suddenly the wolf stood up on its hind legs! 突然，那只狼用后腿站了起来！

“Yikes!” said Annie. “呀！”安妮说。

She leaped back. 她往后跳开。

Then she and Jack started to laugh. 然后她和杰克笑了起来。

The fierce wolf was Black Hawk wearing a wolf’s hide! His head came out through a slit near the wolf’s neck. He gave Jack and Annie a little smile. 那只凶猛的狼原来是黑鹰披着狼皮！ 他的头从狼脖子附近的一条缝里伸出来。 他对杰克和安妮微微一笑。

“That’s a great wolf suit,” said Annie. “那件狼套装真棒，”安妮说。

“Why do you wear that?” asked Jack. “你为什么穿那个？”杰克问。

“The wolf is the most powerful hunter of the buffalo,” said Black Hawk. “When I wear his skin, I feel his strength.” “狼是最强有力的野牛猎手，”黑鹰说。 “当我穿上它的皮，我就能感觉到它的力量。”

“Wow,” said Annie. “哇，”安妮说。

Black Hawk looked at his grandmother. 黑鹰看着他的奶奶。

“May I show them the buffalo now?” he said. “我现在可以带他们去看野牛吗？”他说。

“Only show,” said Grandmother. “Do not hunt. We have enough meat today.” “只是展示，”奶奶说。 “不要猎。 我们今天有足够的肉了。”

She looked back at Jack and Annie. 她回头看着杰克和安妮。

“Lakota never take more buffalo than we need,” she said. “拉科塔人从不捕杀超过我们需要的野牛，”她说。

“That’s good,” said Annie. “那很好，”安妮说。

Black Hawk handed his wolf skin to Grandmother. Then he ran to the grazing ponies. 黑鹰把狼皮交给奶奶。 然后他跑向正在吃草的小马。

He climbed on his. Then he herded two ponies, one black and one yellow, over to Jack and Annie. 他爬上自己的那匹。 接着他把两匹小马——一匹黑的，一匹黄的——赶到杰克和安妮面前。

“Hi, Midnight. Hi, Sunlight,” said Annie, naming the ponies. She patted their noses. “嗨，午夜。 嗨，日光，”安妮给马驹起了名字。 她拍了拍它们的鼻子。

“Annie,” whispered Jack. “How are we going to ride without saddles or reins?” “安妮，”杰克低声说。 “我们没有马鞍也没有缰绳，怎么骑马？”

“Just hold on to their manes,” she said, “and grip with your legs. Watch.” “只要抓住它们的鬃毛，”她说，“再用腿夹紧。看着。”

Annie threw her arms around Midnight’s neck. She slung her leg over the pony’s back and pulled herself up. 安妮张开双臂搂住午夜的脖子。 她把腿甩过马背，把自己拉了上去。

“I’ll carry Teddy in the bag,” Annie said. “我把泰迪放在袋子里带，”安妮说。

Jack picked up Teddy and slipped him inside the leather bag. He handed it to Annie, who hung it over her shoulder. Teddy’s head peeked out of the bag. 杰克抱起泰迪，把它塞进皮袋子。 他递给安妮，安妮把它挂在肩上。 泰迪的头从袋子里探出来。

Arf! he barked. 汪！它叫道。

“Giddy-up, Midnight!” said Annie. The pony started to walk away. “驾，午夜！”安妮说。 小马开始走开。

“Wait—” said Jack. “等等——”杰克说。

He turned to Black Hawk. He had just a few questions. 他转向黑鹰。 他还有几个问题。

Black Hawk let out a wild whoop and took off, too. 黑鹰发出一声狂野的高喊，也出发了。

Jack took a deep breath. He threw his arms around Sunlight’s neck. Then he slung his leg over the pony’s back. 杰克深吸一口气。 他张开双臂搂住阳光的脖子。 然后他把一条腿甩过小马的背。

The pony started to move! 小马开始动了！

“Wait—wait!” said Jack. He hopped on one foot, trying to keep up. “等等——等等！”杰克说。 他单脚跳着，想跟上。

The pony stopped. 小马停住了。

Slowly, Jack pulled himself onto Sunlight’s back. He gripped the pony’s mane. Then he carefully reached up and pushed his glasses into place. 慢慢地，杰克把自己拉上阳光的背。 他抓住小马的鬃毛。 然后他小心抬手把眼镜推正。

He looked over his shoulder. Grandmother was watching. 他回头看去。 奶奶在看着。

She nodded at him. 她对他点点头。

Lakota people admire those who do not show fear, Jack remembered. 拉科塔人钦佩不露出害怕的人，杰克想起来了。

He liked Grandmother. He wanted her to admire him. He let out a wild whoop, and Sunlight took off like the wind. 他喜欢奶奶。 他希望她钦佩他。 他发出一声狂野的呼喊，阳光像风一样冲了出去。

The whoop made Jack feel braver. 那声呼喊让杰克觉得更勇敢了。

He held tightly to Sunlight’s mane. They caught up with Black Hawk and Annie, and together they all rode through the tall grass. 他紧紧抓住阳光的鬃毛。 他们追上了黑鹰和安妮，一起穿过高高的草。

Shadows of clouds swept over the plains. They looked like giant dark birds spreading their wings. 云影掠过平原。 它们看起来像展开翅膀的巨大黑鸟。

Black Hawk’s pony stopped at the top of a grassy slope. Sunlight and Midnight halted right behind him. 黑鹰的小马停在长满草的斜坡顶上。 阳光和午夜紧跟在他后面停下。

Jack couldn’t believe his eyes. 杰克不敢相信自己的眼睛。

Before them were thousands and thousands of grazing buffalo. 眼前是成千上万头正在吃草的野牛。
