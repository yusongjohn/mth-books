7. The Moon Man 7. 月球人

Jack’s heart nearly stopped. 杰克的心几乎停止了跳动。

He could hear Annie talking. 他能听见安妮在说话。

“Hi,” she said. “We come in peace.” “嗨，”她说。 “我们为和平而来。”

There was silence. Then Jack heard Annie say, “Thank you. I have to help my brother up now.” 一片寂静。 过了一会儿，杰克听见安妮说：“谢谢。 我现在得把我哥哥扶起来。”

A moment later, Annie rolled Jack onto his back. 片刻之后，安妮把杰克翻过来，让他仰面躺着。

She grabbed his hand and pulled him up. 她抓住他的手，把他拉了起来。

“Thanks,” said Jack, once he was standing. “谢谢，”杰克一站稳就说。

The moon man was a few feet away. His face was hidden by a metal visor. 月球人就在几英尺外。 他的脸藏在金属面罩后面。

He looked like a spaceman. A huge spaceman—with a giant tank on his back. It was as big as a refrigerator. 他看起来像个太空人。 一个巨大的太空人——背上背着一个巨大的罐子。 它有冰箱那么大。

“That’s a jet pack!” said Jack. “I’ve seen pictures of future astronauts flying with those things. It’s like a mini-spaceship. Right?” “那是喷气背包！”杰克说。 “我见过图片，未来的宇航员用那些东西飞行。 就像一艘迷你宇宙飞船。对吧？”

The moon man didn’t answer. 月球人没有回答。

“I don’t think he can hear us,” said Annie. “He’s not hooked up to our radio.” “我觉得他听不见我们说话，”安妮说。 “他没有接到我们的无线电上。”

“Oh, right,” said Jack. “I’ll write him a message!” “哦，对，”杰克说。 “我给他写条信息！”

“Good idea,” said Annie. “好主意，”安妮说。

Jack pulled out his notebook and pencil. He wrote:



 杰克拿出笔记本和铅笔。他写道：

图中文字：We're Jack and Annie. We come in

peace from America. Who are you? 图中文字：我们是杰克和安妮。我们从美国来，和平而来。你是谁？

Jack handed the notebook and his pencil to the moon man. They looked tiny in his big hands. 杰克把笔记本和铅笔递给月球人。 它们在他的大手里显得很小。

The moon man looked down at the message. He looked at the tiny pencil. Then he turned the notebook over. 月球人低头看着那条信息。 他看着那支小小的铅笔。 然后他把笔记本翻了过来。

Jack and Annie watched as the moon man put the pencil to the paper. He was writing something very carefully. 杰克和安妮看着月球人把铅笔放到纸上。 他非常小心地写着什么。

Finally he gave the notebook back to Jack. 终于他把笔记本还给了杰克。

Jack and Annie stared at the marks.



 杰克和安妮盯着那些标记。





“Stars,” said Annie. “He drew stars.” “星星，”安妮说。 “他画了星星。”

“Maybe it’s a space map,” said Jack. “也许这是一张太空地图，”杰克说。

“Space map?” said Annie. “Hey, Jack, map starts with M!” “太空地图？”安妮说。 “嘿，杰克，map是M开头的！”

“Oh, wow,” said Jack. “This must be the fourth M thing!” “哦哇，”杰克说。 “这一定是第四样 M宝物！”

“Let’s ask him what his map means,” said Annie. She turned around. “我们问问他他的地图是什么意思，”安妮说。 她转过身去。

“We’ll never know now,” she said. “现在我们永远不会知道了，”她说。

“Why?” Jack looked up from the map. “为什么？”杰克从地图上抬起头。

“That’s why.” Annie pointed. The moon man was flying over the mountains. “就是因为这个。”安妮指着。 月球人正飞过那些山。

“Thanks!” Annie cried. “谢谢！”安妮喊道。
