3. Open Sesame! 3. 芝麻开门！

“The fourth M thing must be out there,” said Annie. “第四样 M宝物一定在外面，”安妮说。

Beside the door was a button with the word OPEN on it. Annie reached for the button. 门旁边有个写着OPEN的按钮。 安妮伸手去按按钮。

“Wait!” Jack grabbed her hand. “There’s no air on the moon. Remember?” “等等！”杰克抓住她的手。 “月球上没有空气。记得吗？”

“Oh. Right. But we have to go out to find the M thing.” “哦。对。 可我们得出去找那件 M宝物。”

“Let’s see what the book says,” said Jack. “让我们看看书上怎么说，”杰克说。

He pulled the book out of his pack. He flipped through it until he found a page that showed the surface of the moon. He read aloud: 他从背包里拿出书。 他翻着书，直到找到一页画着月球表面的。他大声读道：

It takes fourteen Earth days to equal one day on the moon. No air protects the moon from the sun’s rays, so daytime heat reaches 260 degrees. 在地球上需要十四天的时间，才相当于月球上的一天。月球上没有空气保护它免受阳光照射，所以白天的温度可以达到260度。

Jack looked at Annie. “I told you our blood would boil if we went out there,” he said. 杰克看着安妮。 “我跟你说过，如果出去，我们的血会沸腾，”他说。

“Yuck,” she said. “恶心，”她说。

Jack read from the book again: 杰克又从书上读道：

Moon scientists wear spacesuits, which have controls to keep them from getting too hot or too cold. They have tanks, which provide air for two hours. 月球科学家穿着宇航服，宇航服上有控制装置，可以防止他们太热或太冷。他们还有储气罐，可以提供两个小时的空气。

“Where do we get spacesuits?” asked Annie. She looked around then trotted back down the hall. “Maybe there … ?” “我们去哪儿拿太空服？”安妮问。 她四处看了看，然后小跑着沿走廊往回走。

Jack was studying his map. “Let’s try the spacesuit storeroom.” 杰克在研究他的地图。 “我们试试太空服储藏室。”

“Don’t look at the map,” said Annie. “Look at the real room!” “别光看地图，”安妮说。 “看看真正的房间！”

Jack glanced up. Annie was peering through a doorway off the hall. 杰克抬头看了一眼。 安妮正透过大厅旁的一扇门往里看。

“There’s a ton of space stuff in here!” she said. “这里有好多太空用品！”她说。

Jack went to look. 杰克走过去看。

Bulky white suits hung from hangers. Air tanks, helmets, gloves, and boots sat in neat rows on shelves. 笨重的白色宇航服挂在衣架上。 空气罐、头盔、手套和靴子整整齐齐地排在架子上。

“Wow, it’s like the armor room in a castle,” said Jack. “哇，这像城堡里的盔甲室，”杰克说。

“Yeah, with huge armor,” said Annie. “是啊，还有巨大的盔甲，”安妮说。

“Let’s pick out the smallest stuff,” said Jack. “The suits can go over our clothes.” “我们挑最小的吧，”杰克说。 “宇航服可以套在我们衣服外面。”

Annie found the smallest white suit. And Jack found the next smallest. They stepped into them. 安妮找到了最小的白色宇航服。 杰克找到了第二小的。 他们穿了进去。

Then Annie locked Jack’s air tank into place. 接着安妮把杰克的空气罐锁好。

“Thanks,” he said. And he did the same for her. “谢谢，”他说。 他也帮她扣好。

“Thanks,” she said. “谢谢，”她说。

“Gloves?” said Jack. He and Annie pulled on white gloves. “手套？”杰克说。 他和安妮戴上白色手套。

“Boots?” said Annie. They each pulled on a pair of huge white boots. “靴子？”安妮说。 他们各自穿上一双巨大的白色靴子。

“Helmets?” said Jack. He reached for a helmet. “头盔？”杰克说。 他伸手拿起一顶头盔。

“Wow, they’re pretty light,” he said. “I thought they’d be like knights’ helmets.” “哇，挺轻的，”他说。 “我还以为会像骑士的头盔那样重。”

Jack and Annie put the helmets on. They locked each other’s into place. 杰克和安妮戴上头盔。 他们互相帮对方锁好。

“I can’t move my head right or left,” said Annie. “我没法左右转头，”安妮说。

“Me neither,” said Jack. “Let’s try walking.” “我也是，”杰克说。 “我们试试走路。”

Jack and Annie moved clumsily around the room. Jack felt like a fat snowman. 杰克和安妮笨拙地在房间里走动。 杰克觉得自己像个胖胖的雪人。

“Close your visor,” said Annie. “把面罩关上，”安妮说。

They both closed their see-through visors. Cool air filled Jack’s helmet. 他们都把透明面罩关上了。 凉爽的空气充满了杰克的头盔。

“I CAN BREATHE!” Annie yelled. Her voice boomed in Jack’s ears. “我能呼吸了！”安妮大喊。 她的声音在杰克耳朵里轰鸣。

“Ow! Talk quietly,” Jack said. “We have two-way radios inside our helmets.” “哎呦！ 说话小声点，”杰克说。 “我们头盔里有双向无线电。”

“Sorry,” whispered Annie. “对不起，”安妮轻声说。

Jack put the moon book back in his pack. Then he slung the pack over his shoulder. 杰克把月亮书放回背包。 然后他把背包随手挎到肩上。

“Okay!” he said. “Remember, we only have two hours of air in our tanks. So we need to find the fourth M thing really fast.” “好了！”他说。 “记住，氧气罐里只有两小时的空气。 所以我们得尽快找到第四样 M宝物。”

“I hope we can find it,” said Annie. “希望我们能找到，”安妮说。

“Me too,” said Jack. He knew they could not go home until they did. “我也是，”杰克说。 他知道，找不到就不能回家。

“Let’s go,” said Annie. She gave Jack a little push. “走吧，”安妮说。 她轻轻推了杰克一下。

“Watch it. No goofing off,” he said. “We don’t want to fall over in these suits.” “当心。 别胡闹，”他说。 “我们可不想穿着这些衣服摔倒。”

“Just go—go!” said Annie. She pushed him out of the room. They walked back to the airlock. “快走——走啊！”安妮说。 她把他推出房间。 他们走回气闸。

“Ready?” said Annie. “Open sesame!” She pressed the OPEN button. A door slowly slid closed behind them. A door opened in front of them. “准备好了吗？”安妮说。 “芝麻开门！”她按下了OPEN按钮。 一扇门在他们身后慢慢滑上。 一扇门在他们面前打开。

And Jack and Annie stepped out onto the moon. 杰克和安妮走出门，踏上了月球。
