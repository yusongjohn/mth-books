8. One Star to Another 8. 从一颗星到另一颗星

“Who was that guy?” said Jack. “What does his map mean?” “那家伙是谁？”杰克说。 “他的地图是什么意思？”

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “But let’s see if it works.” “我不知道，”安妮说。 “不过让我们看看它管不管用。”

Jack took a deep breath. “Yeah, we’d better hurry back. I think I’m running out of air. It feels harder to breathe.” 杰克深深吸了一口气。 “对，我们最好赶快回去。 我觉得空气快用完了。 呼吸好像更困难了。”

“For me, too,” said Annie. “我也一样，”安妮说。

“Go slow. Don’t breathe too much,” said Jack. “慢慢走。 别呼吸太重，”杰克说。

He and Annie took long, floating steps toward the moon base. Jack held his breath as if he were underwater. 他和安妮迈着长长的、漂浮般的步子走向月球基地。 杰克屏住呼吸，仿佛自己在水下。

By the time they got to the white dome, he was ready to burst. 等他们走到白色圆顶时，他已经快憋不住了。

Annie pushed a button beside the huge door. It slid open. They hurried into the airlock. The door closed behind them and the door to the hallway opened. 安妮按了大门旁的按钮。门滑开了。 他们急忙走进气闸。 门在他们身后关上，通向走廊的门打开了。

Jack opened the visor of his helmet. He took a long, deep breath—and let it out. “Ahhhh!” 杰克打开头盔的面罩。 他深深吸了一大口气——再吐出来。

“Let’s get out of these suits,” said Annie. “我们脱掉这些衣服吧，”安妮说。

“Good idea.” Jack was dying to free his arms and legs. “好主意。”杰克急着想把胳膊和腿解放出来。

As they moved clumsily into the spacesuit storeroom, Jack felt heavy again. 他们笨拙地走进太空服储藏室时，杰克又觉得自己变重了。

He and Annie unlocked each other’s helmets, gloves, and boots, and pulled everything off. Then they stepped out of their bulky suits. 他和安妮互相解开头盔、手套和靴子，把所有东西都脱下来。 然后他们走出了笨重的宇航服。

“Whew!” Jack said. He took off his glasses and rubbed his eyes. “呼！”杰克说。 他摘下眼镜，揉了揉眼睛。

It was great to be free—even if he no longer felt as light as a feather. 自由的感觉真好——即使他不再觉得自己轻得像羽毛。

“Hurry! Peanut’s waiting!” said Annie. “快点！ 花生在等着呢！”安妮说。

She led the way down the steps to the bright landing chamber. 她带着路走下台阶，来到明亮的着陆舱。

“Yay,” she said softly. “耶，”她轻声说。

Jack was relieved to see the tree house still there. Soon they’d be heading home. He couldn’t wait. 杰克松了口气，看到树屋还在那儿。 很快他们就要回家了。他等不及了。

Jack and Annie crawled through the tree house window. 杰克和安妮爬进树屋窗户。

“We’re back, Peanut!” said Annie. “我们回来了，花生！”安妮说。

Squeak! Peanut ran to the letter M. 吱！ 花生跑到字母 M 上。

“We missed you!” said Annie. She patted the mouse’s head. “We met a moon man.” “我们想你了！”安妮说。 她轻轻拍了拍老鼠的头。 “我们遇见了一个月球人。”

“Sorry, Peanut, but you have to move,” said Jack. “We have to put the map on the M.” “对不起，花生，可你得挪开，”杰克说。 “我们得把地图放在 M 上。”

Annie gently lifted the mouse off the M. 安妮轻轻把老鼠从字母M上抱起来。

Jack tore the star map out of his notebook. He placed it on the M, next to the mammoth bone, the mango, and the moonstone. 杰克从笔记本上撕下星图。 他把它放在字母M上，紧挨着猛犸骨头、芒果和月光石。

He sighed, then sat back on his heels. “Hand me the Pennsylvania book,” he said. They needed the Pennsylvania book to get back home. 他叹了口气，然后蹲坐下来。 “把宾夕法尼亚那本书递给我，”他说。 他们需要宾夕法尼亚那本书才能回家。

There was silence. 一片寂静。

Jack turned and looked at Annie. 杰克转过身看着安妮。

“It’s not working, Jack,” she said. “The book’s not here.” “这没用，杰克，”她说。 “书不在这儿。”

“What?” Was the map the wrong thing? “什么？”地图是不是找错了？

They looked around the tree house. 他们环顾树屋。

“It’s definitely not here,” said Annie. “它肯定不在这儿，”安妮说。

“Oh, no.” Jack’s heart sank. He picked up the star map and stared at it. 杰克的心沉了下去。 他拿起星图盯着看。

Squeak, squeak. Peanut jumped out of Annie’s arms and scurried back to the letter M. 吱吱，吱吱。 花生从安妮怀里跳出来，碎步跑回字母M上。

“I’ve got an idea,” said Jack. He reached into his pack and took out his pencil. “我有个主意，”杰克说。 他伸进背包，拿出铅笔。

“What are you doing?” said Annie. “你在干什么？”安妮说。

“You know how you draw a constellation?” said Jack. “You connect all the stars. What happens if we try that?” “你知道星座是怎么画的吗？”杰克说。 “你把所有星星连起来。 我们要是也试试会怎样？”

He drew a line from one star to another. He kept drawing, until all the stars were connected. 他从一颗星画到另一颗星。 他继续画，直到所有的星都连起来。

“Let me see,” said Annie. “让我看看，”安妮说。

Jack held the paper out so they could both study it. 杰克把纸举着，好让他们俩都能研究。

“It looks like a mouse,” said Annie. “看起来像一只老鼠，”安妮说。

“Yeah,” said Jack.



 “是啊，”杰克说。





“Is there such a thing as a mouse constellation?” said Annie. “有老鼠星座这种东西吗？”安妮说。

“I don’t think so … ” said Jack. “我不这么认为……”杰克说。

Squeak. 吱。

Annie and Jack looked at Peanut. She was standing on the M. 安妮和杰克看着花生。 她正站在 M 上。

“Oh, wow. Jack,” Annie whispered, “I think I know what the fourth thing is—” “哦哇。 杰克，”安妮低声说，“我想我知道第四样东西是什么——”

Jack grinned. “Me too,” he said. “It’s a—” 杰克咧嘴笑了。 “我也知道，”他说。 “是一只——”

“Mouse!” they said together. “老鼠！”他们一起说。

Squeak! Squeak! 吱吱！吱吱！

“Maybe the spell is—Moonstone, mango, mammoth bone, mouse!” said Annie. “也许咒语是——月长石、芒果、猛犸象骨头、老鼠！”安妮说。

Jack touched each M thing in turn as he whispered, “Moonstone, mango, mammoth bone, mouse.” 杰克依次触摸每件M宝物，低声说：“月光石、芒果、猛犸骨头、老鼠。”

“Let’s say it over and over and see what happens,” said Annie. “我们一遍又一遍地念，看看会怎样，”安妮说。

Together, they chanted: 他们一起反复吟诵：

“Moonstone, mango,

mammoth bone, mouse.

Moonstone, mango,

mammoth bone, mouse.” “月光石，芒果，猛犸象牙，老鼠。月光石，芒果，猛犸象牙，老鼠。”

Suddenly, a bright light filled the tree house. 突然，一道亮光充满了树屋。

The light got brighter and brighter and brighter. 光越来越亮，越来越亮，越来越亮。

The brightness was blinding and whirling. 那光亮刺眼，还在旋转。

The air spun with brightness. 空气在光芒中旋转。

Then everything was clear. 然后一切都清晰了。

Peanut the mouse was gone. 老鼠花生不见了。

And Morgan le Fay stood before Jack and Annie. 摩根·勒菲站在杰克和安妮面前。
