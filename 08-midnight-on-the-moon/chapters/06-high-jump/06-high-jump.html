6. High Jump 6. 跳高

“Who is that?” said Jack. “那是谁？”杰克说。

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “But we’ll soon find out!” She started waving. “我不知道，”安妮说。 “不过我们很快就会知道了！”她开始挥手。

“No!” said Jack. He grabbed her arm. “Let’s go back to the base—before he gets here!” “不！”杰克说。 他抓住她的胳膊。 “我们回基地——趁他还没到这儿！”

“Why?” said Annie. “为什么？”安妮说。

“We don’t know who he is!” said Jack. “We don’t know if he’s friendly or mean or what.” “我们不知道他是谁！”杰克说。 “不知道他是友好还是凶恶，还是怎样。”

“But we can’t go back,” said Annie. “We haven’t found the fourth M thing yet. We won’t be able to go home.” “可我们不能回去，”安妮说。 “我们还没找到第四样 M宝物。 我们就回不了家。”

“It doesn’t matter. We can lock the door at the moon base until he goes away,” said Jack. “Then we can get new air tanks!” “没关系。 我们可以把月球基地的门锁上，直到他离开，”杰克说。 “那我们可以换新氧气罐！”

Jack hurried to the moon buggy. “Come on!” He jumped into the driver’s seat. 杰克急忙走向月面车。 “快点！”他跳进驾驶座。

Annie gave a little wave to the dot in the sky. Then she climbed into the moon buggy. 安妮朝天上那个小点挥了挥手。 然后她也爬进了月面车。

The buggy took off. 月面车开动了。

“Careful!” said Annie. “小心！”安妮说。

They bumped over the rocks as Jack turned the buggy around. Then they zoomed toward the pass. 杰克把车掉转方向时，他们颠簸着越过岩石。 然后他们朝山口飞驰而去。

Jack steered around craters and rocks. More than once the buggy nearly tipped over. 杰克绕着陨石坑和岩石驾驶。 车子好几次差点翻倒。

“Whoa! Slow down!” said Annie. “哇！ 慢下来！”安妮说。

They were almost at the mountain pass. Suddenly, a cloud of dust flew up in front of them. The ground trembled. 他们快到山口了。 突然，一团尘云在他们面前扬起。 地面颤抖起来。

“Watch it!” cried Annie. “当心！”安妮喊道。

Jack couldn’t see a thing. 杰克什么也看不见。

He stepped on the brake. The buggy jerked to a stop. 他踩下刹车。 月面车猛地停住。

The dust settled. 尘土落了下来。

A giant rock had fallen into the narrow pass. It was stuck between two walls of rock. They were trapped! 一块巨石落进了狭窄的山口。 它卡在两堵岩壁之间。 他们被困住了！

Jack quickly found a picture of a giant rock in the moon book. He read aloud: 杰克很快在月亮书里找到一幅巨石的图。他大声读道：

Rocks of all sizes crash into the moon from outer space. These rocks are called meteorites. 各种大小的岩石从外太空撞向月球。这些岩石叫做陨石。

“We’re lucky that meteorite didn’t land on us,” said Jack. “真幸运，那块陨石没砸到我们，”杰克说。

“Yeah, and I guess it’s too big to be the M thing,” said Annie. She had climbed out of the moon buggy and was standing by the meteorite. “是啊，而且我觉得它太大了，不可能是那件 M宝物，”安妮说。 她已经爬出月面车，站在陨石旁边。

It was more than twice as tall as she was. 它比她高出两倍还多。

Jack looked at the black sky. The flying thing was nowhere in sight—yet. 杰克看着黑色的天空。 那个飞行物还看不见——暂时。

“We’ll have to jump over it,” Annie said. “我们得跳过去，”安妮说。

“Jump? I don’t think so,” said Jack. “It’s too high.” “跳？ 我不这么认为，”杰克说。 “太高了。”

“I’m going to try anyway,” said Annie. “反正我要试试，”安妮说。

“Wait. Let’s think first,” said Jack. “等到早上吧，”杰克说。 我们先想想，”杰克说。

But Annie was already backing up. 可安妮已经在往后退了。

“One, two, three—go!” she shouted, and took giant, leaping steps toward the meteorite. “一、二、三——冲！”她喊着，迈开巨大的跳跃步子冲向陨石。

When Annie got close to the rock, she pushed off the ground. Then she flew through space and disappeared behind the meteorite. 安妮靠近那块石头时，用力蹬地跳了起来。 然后她飞过空间，消失在陨石后面。

“Annie!” Jack called. “安妮！”杰克喊道。

There was no answer. 没有回答。

“Oh, brother,” Jack said. He backed up and took off toward the rock. He jumped as high as he could. Then he was flying through space. “唉，”杰克说。 他向后退了几步，然后朝那块石头冲去。 他尽可能跳得高高的。 然后他飞过了空间。

Jack hit the ground and fell facedown into the dust. 杰克撞到地面，脸朝下摔进尘土里。

Jack tried to stand. But his suit was too bulky. He tried to roll over. But his suit made even that impossible. 杰克想站起来。 可他的宇航服太笨重了。 他想翻过身来。 可他的宇航服连这也做不到。

“Oh, no,” he groaned. “Not again.”

“Are you here?” asked Annie. “Did you make it?” “你在这儿吗？”安妮问。 “你过来了吗？”

“Yes!” Jack was relieved to hear her voice. But he couldn’t turn his head to see her. He could only hear her over the radio. “是的！”杰克听见她的声音，放了心。 可他没法转头看她。 他只能通过无线电听见她的声音。

“Can you help me up?” he asked. “你能帮我起来吗？”他问。

“Nope,” said Annie. “不行，”安妮说。

“Why not?” “为什么不行？”

“I fell down, too,” she said. “我也摔倒了，”她说。

“Oh, brother,” Jack sighed. “Now we are really in trouble.” “唉，”杰克叹了口气。 “现在我们真的有麻烦了。”

He tried to stand again. And failed. 他又试着站起来。失败了。

“Can you see anything?” he asked. “你能看见什么吗？”他问。

“Just the sky,” said Annie. “Wow, is it weird … ” “只有天空，”安妮说。 “哇，真怪异……”

“I’m worried about our air tanks,” said Jack. “I feel like it’s been two hours.” “我担心我们的氧气罐，”杰克说。 “我觉得好像已经过了两小时了。”

“Ja-ack … ” said Annie. “杰——克……”安妮说。

“And what about that moon man?” said Jack. “Where did he go to?” “还有那个月球人呢？”杰克说。 “他去哪儿了？”

“Jack!” whispered Annie. “杰克！”安妮低声说。

“What?” “什么？”

“He’s here,” she said. “The moon man is here.” “他来了，”她说。 “月球人来了。”

“What?” “什么？”

“He’s standing above me.” “他就站在我上方。”
