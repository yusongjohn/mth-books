9. Morgan 9. 摩根

“Thank you,” Morgan said softly. “You have freed me from the magician’s spell.” “谢谢你们，”摩根轻声说。 “你们把我从魔法师的咒语中解救出来了。”

Jack just stared at her. 杰克只是盯着她看。

“You were Peanut?” Annie said. “你就是花生？”安妮说。

Morgan nodded and smiled. 摩根点点头，笑了。

“Really? You were with us all the time?” said Jack. “On all our missions?” “真的？ 你一直和我们在一起？”杰克说。 “我们所有的任务里？”

Morgan nodded again. 摩根又点了点头。

“Why did we have to go on this mission to find a mouse?” said Jack. “If you were always with us?” “那我们为什么还要执行这个任务去找一只老鼠？”杰克说。 “如果你一直和我们在一起？”

“To break the spell, we had to be on the moon,” said Morgan. “You could have broken it the minute we arrived.” “要解除魔法，我们必须在月球上，”摩根说。 “我们一到就可以把它解除的。”

“Oh, that’s what Peanut—I mean you were trying to say!” said Annie. “We didn’t have to leave the moon base at all.” “哦，原来花生——我是说你——一直想告诉我们这个！”安妮说。 “我们根本不用离开月球基地。”

Morgan nodded, smiling. 摩根点点头，微笑着。

“But the moon man came along to help us,” said Annie. “He drew a constellation of a mouse! Is he a friend of yours?” “可月球人出现来帮我们了，”安妮说。 “他画了一组老鼠星座！ 他是你的朋友吗？”

Morgan shrugged. “Let’s just say we had a little talk. He stopped by the moon base while you were out.” 摩根耸了耸肩。 “这么说吧，我们谈过一小会儿。你们出去的时候，他顺路来过月球基地。” 你们出去的时候，他顺路来过月球基地。

“The same way you had a talk with the ninja master, right?” said Jack. “And the monkey and the sorcerer?” “就像你跟忍者大师谈过话那样，对吧？”杰克说。 “还有那只猴子和那个巫师？”

Morgan nodded. “I always squeaked to the ones who helped you.” 摩根点了点头。 “我总是对那些帮你们的人吱吱叫。”

“But how did they understand you—a mouse?” said Jack. “可他们怎么听得懂你——一只老鼠？”杰克说。

Morgan smiled again. “Certain wise ones understand the language of little creatures,” she said. 摩根又笑了。 “某些智者能听懂小动物的语言，”她说。

“I bet it was you who turned the pages of the books!” said Annie. “To show us where to go next!” “我敢打赌，是你翻开那些书的！”安妮说。 “告诉我们下一步该去哪儿！”

Morgan nodded. 摩根点了点头。

“But who turned you into a mouse?” said Annie. “可是谁把你变成老鼠的？”安妮说。

Morgan frowned. “A certain person who likes to play tricks on me,” she said. “His name is Merlin.” 摩根皱起了眉。 “有个喜欢捉弄我的人，”她说。 “他的名字叫梅林。”

“Merlin!” said Jack. “The greatest magician who ever lived.” “梅林！”杰克说。 “有史以来最伟大的魔法师。”

Morgan sniffed. “He’s not that great,” she said. “He doesn’t even know I have two brave friends who help me.” 摩根哼了一声。 “他没那么伟大，”她说。 “他甚至不知道我有两个勇敢的朋友帮助我。”

“Us?” said Annie shyly. “我们？”安妮害羞地说。

Morgan nodded. “And I thank you both with all my heart.” 摩根点了点头。 “我全心全意地感谢你们俩。”

“You’re welcome,” said Jack and Annie. “不客气，”杰克和安妮说。

Morgan handed Annie the Pennsylvania book. “Are you ready to go home now?” she asked. 摩根把宾夕法尼亚那本书递给安妮。 “你们现在准备好回家了吗？”她问。

“Yes!” said Jack and Annie. “是的！”杰克和安妮说。

Annie pointed to a picture of the Frog Creek woods. “I wish we could go there,” she said. 安妮指着蛙溪树林的图画。 “我希望我们能去那儿，”她说。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster and faster. 它转得越来越快，越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静了下来。

Absolutely still. 完全静止。

But only for a moment. 不过只持续了片刻。
