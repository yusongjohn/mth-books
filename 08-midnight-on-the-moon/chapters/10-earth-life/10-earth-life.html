10. Earth Life 10. 地球生活

The midnight woods woke up. 午夜的树林苏醒了。

A breeze rustled the leaves. 一阵微风把叶子吹得沙沙响。

An owl hooted. 一只猫头鹰叫了一声。

The sounds were soft, but very alive. 这些声音很轻，却充满生机。

Jack opened his eyes. He pushed his glasses into place. 杰克睁开眼睛。 他把眼镜推正。

He smiled. Morgan was still with them. He could see her in the moonlight. Her long white hair was shining. 他笑了。 摩根还和他们在一起。 他能在月光下看见她。 她长长的白发闪着光。

“Morgan, can you and the tree house stay here?” said Annie. “In Frog Creek?” “摩根，你和树屋能留在这儿吗？”安妮说。 “在蛙溪？”

“No, I must leave again, I’m afraid,” said Morgan. “I’ve been gone from Camelot for a long time.” “不，恐怕我又得走了，”摩根说。 “我离开卡米洛特已经很久了。”

She handed Jack his pack. She brushed his cheek. Her hand felt soft and cool. 她把背包递给杰克。 她轻轻抚过他的脸颊。 她的手感觉柔软而凉爽。

“A bit of moondust still on you,” she said. “Thank you, Jack, for your great love of knowledge.” “你身上还沾着一点儿月尘，”她说。 “谢谢你，杰克，感谢你对知识的热爱。”

“You’re welcome,” said Jack. “不客气，”杰克说。

Morgan tugged on one of Annie’s braids. “And thank you, Annie, for your belief in the impossible.” 摩根轻轻拽了拽安妮的一根辫子。 “也谢谢你，安妮，感谢你相信不可能的事。”

“You’re welcome,” said Annie. “不客气，”安妮说。

“Go home now,” said Morgan. “现在回家吧，”摩根说。

Jack smiled. Home was Earth—that bright, colorful world where everything was alive and always changing. 杰克笑了。 家就是地球——那个明亮、多彩的世界，那里一切都活着，并且总在变化。

“Bye, Morgan,” said Annie. She started out of the tree house. “再见，摩根，”安妮说。 她开始走出树屋。

Jack looked back at Morgan. 杰克回头看着摩根。

“Will you come back soon?” he said. “你会很快回来吗？”他说。

“Anything can happen,” said Morgan. “The universe is filled with wonders. Isn’t it, Jack?” “什么都有可能发生，”摩根说。 “宇宙充满了奇迹。不是吗，杰克？”

He smiled and nodded. 他笑着点了点头。

“Go now,” Morgan said softly. “现在走吧，”摩根轻声说。

Jack followed Annie down the rope ladder. He stepped onto the ground. 杰克跟着安妮爬下绳梯。 他踩到了地面。

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

The tree started to shake. 树开始摇晃。

A loud roar filled Jack’s ears. He squeezed his eyes shut. He covered his ears. 一阵响亮的轰鸣充满了杰克的耳朵。 他紧紧闭上眼睛。 他捂住耳朵。

Then everything was silent and still. 然后一切都静了下来。

Jack opened his eyes. The ladder was gone. He looked through the leaves and branches of the giant oak tree. Where the tree house had been was only moonlight now. 杰克睁开眼睛。 梯子不见了。 他透过那棵巨大橡树的叶子和树枝往上看。 树屋曾经在的地方，现在只有月光。

“Bye, Morgan,” he whispered sadly. “再见，摩根，”他伤感地低声说。

“Bye, Peanut,” said Annie. “再见，花生，”安妮说。

Jack and Annie stared at the top of the tree for a long moment. 杰克和安妮久久地盯着树顶。

“Ready?” said Annie. “准备好了吗？”安妮说。

Jack nodded. 杰克点了点头。

They started for home. 他们开始往家走。

The midnight air felt cool and moist. It was filled with the soft sounds of earth life. 午夜的空气凉爽而潮湿。 树林里充满了地球生命柔和的声音。

Jack and Annie left the Frog Creek woods. They started down their street. 杰克和安妮离开了蛙溪树林。 他们开始沿着街道走。

Annie glanced up at the sky. “The moon looks really far away, doesn’t it?” 安妮抬头瞥了一眼天空。 “月亮看起来真的好远，对吧？”

It did, thought Jack. It was. 确实很远，杰克想。它就是很远。

“I wonder how the moon man can be up there all alone,” said Annie. “真不知道月球人怎么能一个人待在上面，”安妮说。

“What do you mean?” said Jack. “什么意思？”杰克说。

“I mean, who helps him put on his spacesuit?” said Annie. “Who helps him get up when he falls down?” “我是说，谁帮他穿太空服？”安妮说。 “他摔倒时谁帮他起来？”

“And who is he?” added Jack. “还有，他是谁？”杰克补充道。

“Who do you think he was?” said Annie. “你觉得他是谁？”安妮说。

“He must be a scientist or an astronaut from Earth,” said Jack. “他一定是来自地球的科学家或宇航员，”杰克说。

“No. I think he’s an alien,” said Annie, “from another galaxy.” “不。 我觉得他是外星人，”安妮说，“来自另一个星系。”

Jack scoffed. “What makes you say that?” 杰克不以为然地哼了一声。 “你凭什么这么说？”

“I just feel it,” said Annie. “我就是感觉到了，”安妮说。

“Wrong,” said Jack. “There’s no proof that aliens exist.” “错了，”杰克说。 “没有证据表明外星人存在。”

“Maybe not now,” said Annie. “But don’t forget—we were in the future.” “也许现在不是，”安妮说。 “但别忘了——我们是在未来。”

“Oh, brother,” said Jack. “唉，”杰克说。

They crossed their yard and climbed their back steps. Annie tiptoed inside the house. Jack followed her. 他们穿过院子，爬上后门台阶。 安妮踮着脚走进屋里。 杰克跟着她。

Before he shut the door, he glanced up at the moon. 关门前，他瞥了一眼月亮。

Was Annie right? he wondered. Could the moon man have come from another galaxy? 安妮说得对吗？他想。 月球人会不会来自另一个星系？

Morgan’s words came back to him: The universe is filled with wonders. Isn’t it, Jack? 摩根的话重新浮现在他脑海里：宇宙充满了奇迹。不是吗，杰克？

“Goodnight, moon man,” Jack whispered. Then he closed the door. “晚安，月球人，”杰克低声说。 然后他关上了门。
