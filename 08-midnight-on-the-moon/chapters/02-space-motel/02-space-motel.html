2. Space Motel 2. 太空旅馆

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开眼睛。

He looked out the window. The tree house had landed inside a large white room. 他朝窗外望去。 树屋降落在一个白色的大房间里。

“What kind of training place is this?” asked Annie. “这是什么样的训练场？”安妮问。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “我不知道，”杰克说。

The room was round. It had no windows. It had white floors and a curved wall lit by bright lights. 房间是圆的。 没有窗户。 它有白色的地板，还有一面被亮光照着的弧形墙。

“Hello!” Annie called. “你好！”安妮喊道。

There was no answer. 没有回答。

Where were all the astronauts and space scientists? Jack wondered. 那些宇航员和太空科学家都去哪儿了？杰克想。

“There’s nobody here,” said Annie. “这儿一个人也没有，”安妮说。

“How do you know?” said Jack. “你怎么知道？”杰克说。

“I just feel it,” said Annie. “我就是感觉到了，”安妮说。

“We’d better find out where we are,” said Jack. “我们最好搞清楚自己在哪儿，”杰克说。

He looked at the page in the moon book. He read the words below the picture of the dome. 他看着月亮书上的那一页。 月球基地于2031年建在月球上。圆顶顶部会滑开，好让宇宙飞船进出。

A moon base was built on the moon in the year 2031. The top of the dome slides open to let spacecrafts enter and leave. 2031年，人们在月球上建了一个月球基地。穹顶的顶部可以滑开，让飞船进出。

“Oh, man—” Jack whispered. “天哪——”杰克低声说。

“What’s wrong?” said Annie. “怎么了？”安妮说。

Jack’s heart pounded with excitement. He could hardly speak. “We’ve landed inside a moon base,” he said. 杰克激动得心跳得厉害。 他几乎说不出话来。 “我们降落在月球基地里了，”他说。

“So … ?” said Annie. “那又怎样……？”安妮说。

“So the moon base is on the moon!” said Jack. “所以月球基地是在月球上的！”杰克说。

Annie’s eyes widened. “We’re on the moon?” she asked. 安妮的眼睛睁大了。 “我们在月球上？”她问。

Jack nodded. “The book says the moon base was built in the year 2031,” he said. “So this book was written after that! Which means this book is from the future!” 杰克点了点头。 “书上说月球基地建于 2031 年，”他说。 “所以这本书是那之后写的！ 也就是说这本书来自未来！”

“Oh, wow,” said Annie. “Morgan must have gone forward in time to borrow it from a future library.” “哦哇，”安妮说。 “摩根一定是去了未来，从未来的图书馆借来了这本书。”

“Right,” said Jack. “And now we’re in the future, on the moon.” “对，”杰克说。 “而现在我们在未来，在月球上。”

Squeak, squeak! 吱吱，吱吱！

Annie and Jack looked at Peanut. The mouse was running around in circles. 安妮和杰克看着花生。 小老鼠正绕着圈跑。

“Poor Peanut,” said Annie. “可怜的花生，”安妮说。

She tried to pick the mouse up. But Peanut hid behind the mango on the letter M. 她想把老鼠抱起来。 可花生躲到了字母 M 上的芒果后面。

“Maybe she’s nervous about being on the moon,” said Annie. “也许她因为在月球上而紧张，”安妮说。

“She’s not the only one,” said Jack. He let out a deep breath, then he pushed his glasses into place. “紧张的可不只是她，”杰克说。 他深深呼出一口气，然后把眼镜推正。

“So what’s a moon base?” asked Annie. “那月球基地是什么？”安妮问。

Jack looked at the book. He read aloud: 杰克看着书。他大声读道：

When scientists visit the moon for short periods, they eat and sleep in the moon base. 当科学家们短时间去月球时，他们会在月球基地里吃饭和睡觉。

“A space motel!” said Annie. “太空旅馆！”安妮说。

“I guess,” said Jack. He read more: “大概是吧，”杰克说。他又读了一些：

The small base has a landing chamber and a room for storing spacesuits. Air and temperature controls make breathing possible. 这个小基地有一个着陆舱和一个存放宇航服的房间。空气和温度控制系统让呼吸成为可能。

“So that’s why we can breathe,” Jack said. “所以我们才能呼吸，”杰克说。

“Let’s explore,” said Annie. “We have to find the fourth thing for Morgan.” “我们去探索吧，”安妮说。 “我们得找到给摩根的第四样东西。”

“No, first we should study this map,” said Jack. He pulled out his notebook. “不，我们得先研究这张地图，”杰克说。 他拿出笔记本。

“You study it,” said Annie. “你研究吧，”安妮说。

Jack copied the map. Then he drew in the tree house.



 杰克把地图画了下来。 然后他画上了树屋。

图中文字：spacesuit

storeroom

rocket entry

landing chamber

airlock

treehouse 图中文字：宇航服储藏室 火箭入口 着陆舱 气闸 树屋

“Okay,” he said. He pointed at the X in his drawing. “We’re here.” “好了，”他说。 他指着自己图上的X。 “我们在这儿。”

Jack looked up. Annie was gone. 杰克抬起头。安妮不见了。

“Oh, brother,” Jack said. As usual, she had left without him. Before they could even make a plan. “唉，”杰克说。 像往常一样，她先走了，没等他。 他们甚至还没来得及制定计划。

Jack put the moon book and pencil into his pack. Carrying his notebook and backpack, he started out the window. 杰克把月亮书和铅笔放进背包。 他拿着笔记本和背包，从窗户往外走。

Squeak! Squeak! 吱吱！吱吱！

Jack looked back at Peanut. The mouse was running back and forth on the M. 杰克回头看着花生。 小老鼠在 M 上来回跑着。

“Stay here and be safe,” said Jack. “We’ll be back soon.” “待在这儿，保证安全，”杰克说。 “我们很快回来。”

Jack swung himself over the window sill. His feet touched the floor of the landing chamber. 杰克翻过窗台。 他的脚碰到了着陆舱的地板。

“Annie!” he called. “安妮！”他喊道。

There was no answer. 没有回答。

Jack looked at his diagram. 杰克看了看他的图解。

It showed only one way to go. Jack walked along the curved white wall to the stairs. 图上只标出一条路可走。 杰克沿着弯曲的白墙走到楼梯旁。

He climbed the steps to a hallway. 他爬上台阶，来到一条走廊。

“Jack—hurry!” Annie was at the end of the hallway, standing in the airlock. She peered out a window in a giant door. “杰克——快点！”安妮站在走廊尽头的密封舱里。 她从一扇大门上的窗户往外看。

Jack hurried toward her. Annie stepped aside so he could look out the window, too. 杰克朝她赶过去。 安妮挪到一旁，好让他也能往窗外看。

“Oh, man,” said Jack. What he saw took his breath away. “天哪，”杰克说。 眼前的景象让他惊叹得屏住了呼吸。

He stared at a rocky gray land. The land was filled with giant craters and tall mountains. The sun was shining. But the sky was ink-black! 他盯着一片布满岩石的灰色地面。 地面上布满巨大的坑和高山。 阳光照耀着。 可天空却是墨黑色的！

“Say hi to the moon,” Annie said softly. “跟月亮打个招呼吧，”安妮轻声说。
