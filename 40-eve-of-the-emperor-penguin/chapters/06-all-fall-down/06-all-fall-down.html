This is unbelievable, said Nancy. 这简直难以置信，南希说。

How did they mess up so badly? 他们怎么会搞成这样？

It's mine, all mine, all. 是我的，全都是我的。

Said Nancy, she seemed near tears. 纳西说，她看起来快要哭了。

You're just little kids. 你们只是小孩子。

Not so little, thought Jack again. 并不那么小，杰克又这样想。

Gee. 哇。

The snowmobile rumbled outside, its engine warming up. 雪地摩托在外面轰隆隆地响，引擎正在预热。

Oh dear, said Nancy. 哦天哪，纳西说。

I've got to lead the group up a safe route to the crater, 我得带大家走一条安全的路去火山口，

or they'll be in trouble. 否则他们会遇到麻烦的。

But Pete should be back here in just a few minutes. 但皮特应该再过几分钟就回来这里了。

Will you be okay by yourselves till then? 在那之前，你们自己能照顾好自己吗？

We'll be fine, don't worry, said Annie. 我们会没事的，别担心，安妮说。

Good, said Nancy. 很好，南希说。

Here, sweeties. 来，宝贝们。

She poured some water into two cups 她往两个杯子里倒了一些水

and gave them to Jack and Annie. 并且把它们给了杰克和安妮。

Drink. 喝吧。

While they drank the water, Nancy spread a blanket 当他们喝水时，南希铺开了毯子

on the floor and turned on the small heater. 在地板上，然后打开了小暖气。

Lie down here, she said. 躺下来，她说。

Just rest. 只是休息一下。

She patted the blanket. 她拍了拍毯子。

Jack and Annie lay down. 杰克和安妮躺了下来。

Nancy covered them with another blanket. 南希又给他们盖了一条毯子。

If you get thirsty, drink more water, she said. 如果你口渴了，就多喝点水，她说。

Thanks, said Annie. 谢谢，安妮说。

Jack was too embarrassed to say anything. 杰克太尴尬了，一句话也说不出来。

He felt like a preschool kid being put down for a nap. 他感觉自己像一个幼儿园的孩子，被要求去午睡。

Okay, Nancy said with a big sigh. 好吧，南希叹了口气说。

You kids nearly gave me a heart attack. 你们差点把我吓出心脏病了。

She repeated half to herself as she left the hut. 她一边离开小屋，一边小声重复着。

Sorry, said Jack. 对不起，杰克说。

But Nancy was gone. 但南希已经不见了。

Soon the roar and rumble of the snowmobiles filled the air 很快，雪地摩托的轰鸣声充满了空气

as Nancy led the scientists and journalists up the mountain. 因为南希带领科学家和记者们爬上了山。

We really messed up our mission this time, 这次我们真的搞砸了任务，

said Jack, lying under the blanket. 杰克躺在毯子下说。

And we were doing so well too, said Annie. 我们之前不是做得很好吗，安妮说。

She sat up. 她坐了起来。

Can I see Morgan's rhyme, please? 我能看看摩根的韵文吗？

Jack pulled the rhyme out of his pocket 杰克从口袋里拿出韵文

and handed it to Annie. 然后把它递给安妮。

Okay, said Annie. 好的，安妮说。

She read aloud. 她大声读了出来。

Then all fall down till you come to the cave 然后所有人都掉下去，直到你来到那个洞穴

of the ancient crown. 那里放着古代的王冠。

I wonder if this counts as falling down, asked Annie. 我想知道这算不算掉下去，安妮问道。

She put the rhyme into her pocket. 她把那句咒语放进了口袋里。

I don't think so, said Jack. 我不这么认为，杰克说。

I don't know what that means. 我不知道这是什么意思。

When there's no ancient crown in Antarctica, 当南极洲没有古代王冠的时候，

it's all science and research and rules 那里全是科学、研究和规则

and helicopters and snowmobiles. 还有直升机和雪地摩托。

It's the real world, his voice trailed off. 那是现实世界，他的声音渐渐消失了。

Well, I know one thing. 嗯，我知道一件事。

I don't want to waste time lying around here, said Annie. “我不想在这里浪费时间躺着，”安妮说。

She threw off the blanket and stood up. 她掀开毯子，站了起来。

At least I can take a few pictures while we wait for Pete. 至少在等皮特的时候，我可以拍几张照片。

You really feel like doing that, said Jack. “你真的想这么做吗？”杰克说。

Not really, but I'm going to try, said Annie. “其实不想，但我打算试试看，”安妮说。

I don't think you should. “我觉得你不应该这么做。”

Said Jack. “杰克说。”

Don't worry, I'll be back soon, said Annie. “别担心，我很快就回来，”安妮说。

Maybe I'll see an ancient crown. 也许我能看到一顶古老的王冠。

Yeah, sure, said Jack. 杰克说：‘是啊，肯定能。’

Annie put on her goggles and ski mask and headed outside. 安妮戴上护目镜和滑雪面罩，然后走出去了。

Jack reached into his pack and pulled out their book. 杰克把手伸进背包，拿出他们的书。

He took off his glove and looked up 他摘下手套，抬头看

ancient crown in the index. ‘古代王冠’在目录里。

He wasn't surprised to find it wasn't there. 他一点也不惊讶地发现它不在那里。

Jack put the book back in his pack 杰克把书放回背包里。

and took out his notebook. 并且拿出了他的笔记本。

He read over his notes. 他仔细看了看自己的笔记。

Go slow, stay with others. 慢慢来，不要离开其他人。

Cracks and ice, never touch wildlife. 注意裂缝和冰面，不要触碰野生动物。

Jack's hand was cold, so he put his glove back on. 杰克的手很冷，所以他又戴上了手套。

He put away his notebook and then laid his head back down 他收起笔记本，然后又把头放回原处

and closed his eyes. 并闭上了眼睛。

He just wanted to sleep. 他只想睡觉。

The warmth from the small heater felt good. 小暖气发出的温暖感觉真好。

The sound of the snowmobiles was fading 雪地摩托的声音渐渐远去

into the distance. 到远处去了。

As he started to fall asleep, 当他开始打瞌睡时，

the words of his notes ran through his mind. 他笔记里的字句在他脑海里回荡。

Go slow, stay with others. 动作要慢，要和大家在一起。

Cracks and ice. 裂缝和冰块。

Oh no, thought Jack. 哦不，杰克心想。

He sat straight up. 他一下子坐了起来。

He tossed off the blanket. 他把毯子扔开了。

He threw on his pack and rushed out of the hut. 他背上背包，冲出了小屋。

The wind was blowing the snow into icy clouds. 风把雪吹成了冰天雪地。

Jack pulled up his ski mask and lowered his goggles. 杰克拉高了他的滑雪面具，戴上护目镜。

Annie, he shouted. 安妮，他喊道。

What? 什么？

Her voice came from the distance. 她的声音从远处传来。

Jack caught sight of her. 杰克看见了她。

She was aiming her camera up the slope 她正把相机对准山坡上方

at the smoking crater of the mountain. 对着那座冒烟的火山口。

You have to come back now. 你现在必须马上回来。

He shouted, walking toward her. 他喊着，朝她走过去。

You shouldn't be walking around by yourself. 你不应该一个人到处乱走。

Okay, okay. 好吧，好吧。

Annie put her camera in her pocket. 安妮把相机放进了口袋里。

Come on, said Jack. 快点，”杰克说。

He took Annie's hand. 他抓住了安妮的手。

They held onto each other 他们紧紧握着对方的手

and walked through the blowing snow toward the hut. 走进了呼啸的雪中，朝小屋走去。

Remember Nancy's rules, said Jack. 记住南希的规则，”杰克说。

There are deep cracks and ah! 这里有很深的裂缝啊！

Before Jack had finished, 杰克还没说完，

the ground beneath him gave way 他脚下的地面突然塌陷了

and he and Annie crashed through a thin layer of snow 杰克和安妮撞破了一层薄薄的雪层

hiding a deep crack in the ice. 露出冰层下深深的裂缝。

Jack and Annie landed on an icy ledge. 杰克和安妮落在了一块结冰的岩石上。

Clumps of snow fell on top of them. 一团团雪落在他们身上。

Silence filled the air. 空气里充满了寂静。

A thin shaft of light came from the opening 一束细长的光从开口处照进来。

they had fallen through. 他们掉下去了。

You okay? 你还好吗？

Jack said. 杰克说。

I think so, said Annie. 我想是的，安妮说。

They both sat up slowly. 他们俩慢慢地坐了起来。

Annie peered over the edge of the ledge. 安妮从岩石边缘探出头去。

Uh oh, she said, look. 呃哦，她说，看。

Jack looked. 杰克看了。

He and Annie were on the ledge of a ravine 他和安妮正坐在峡谷边缘的岩石上

that plunged thousands of feet down into darkness. 那向下坠了数千英尺，掉进了黑暗中。

This must be one of those hidden places in the mountain 这一定是山里那些隐藏的地方之一，

Nancy talked about, said Jack. 南希曾经提到过，杰克说。

The ones made by the lava and hot gases. 那些是由熔岩和热气形成的。

It's incredible, said Annie. 太不可思议了，安妮说。

She reached into her pocket for her camera. 她从口袋里拿出相机。

As soon as Annie moved, Jack heard the ice crack. 安妮一动，杰克就听见冰层裂开了。

Don't move, he said. 不要动，他说。

Annie froze. 安妮吓呆了。

Forget pictures, said Jack. 别看图片了，杰克说。

We're facing serious danger here. 我们正面对着严重的危险。

If we move, the ice might break under us 如果我们动一动，冰面可能会在我们脚下破裂

Got it, said Annie. 明白了，安妮说。

Maybe we should use the wand. 也许我们应该用魔杖。

We can't, said Jack. 我们不能，杰克说。

The wand won't work. 魔杖不管用。

We can only use it for the good of others, 我们只能用它来帮助别人，

not just ourselves. 而不是只帮助自己。

Darn, said Annie. 哎呀，安妮说。

Okay, said Annie. 好吧，安妮说。

The way I see it, if we don't use the wand, 在我看来，如果我们不用魔杖，

we'll be stuck here forever. 我们就永远被困在这里了。

Right, said Jack. 对，杰克说。

So we'll never find the secret of happiness for Merlin, 所以我们永远无法为梅林找到幸福的秘密，

Merlin will fade away completely from sorrow 梅林会因为悲伤而彻底消失

Right, said Jack. 对，杰克说。

So maybe in this case, 那么在这种情况下，

rescuing ourselves isn't just our good, said Annie. 拯救我们自己不仅仅是为了我们好，安妮说。

Our good is also the good of others, like Merlin. 我们好也是为了别人好，就像梅林那样。

Good thinking, said Jack. 想得真好，杰克说。

Let's try it. 我们来试试看吧。

Then he very slowly reached inside 然后他非常缓慢地把手伸了进去

Right, get ready, said Jack. 对，准备好了，杰克说。

What happened, he said. 发生了什么？他说道。

Nothing, said Annie. 什么都没发生，安妮说。

I guess the rules must crack. 我想规则也得破一回。
