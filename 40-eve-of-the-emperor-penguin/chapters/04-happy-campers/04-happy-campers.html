4. Happy Campers 4. 快乐的露营者

Touching down onto a flat, snowy field was an orange and white helicopter. The helicopter had skis on the bottom so it could land on the ice and snow. The bus came to a stop at the edge of the field. 降落到一片平坦的雪地上的是一个橙色和白色的小直升机。 直升机底部装着滑雪板，这样它就能在冰和雪上着陆了。 大巴车停在了田野的边缘。

“We must be going to the volcano in a helicopter!” said Annie. “我们一定是坐直升机去火山了！”，安妮说。

“By air! Just like the rhyme says!” said Jack. “Great!” Jack had always wanted to fly in a helicopter! “乘飞机！ 就像那首童谣里说的一样！”，杰克说。 “太棒了！”，杰克一直想坐直升机飞行！

Nancy stood up. “For those of you getting off the bus now, please remember,” she said, “the helicopter blades are extremely dangerous. Always wait for a signal from the pilot before approaching the chopper.” 南希站了起来。 “现在要下车的朋友们请注意，”，她说，“直升机的旋翼非常危险。 在靠近直升机之前，一定要等飞行员发出信号。”

Everyone watched as the spinning blades slowly came to a stop. The pilot waved from the helicopter window. 大家都看着旋转的叶片慢慢停下来。 飞行员从直升机的窗户里挥手。

“Okay, Pete says we can go!” said Nancy. “好了，皮特说我们可以出发了！”，南希说道。

Jack put their rhyme back into his pocket. He pulled on his backpack. Then, carrying his notebook and pencil, he filed down the aisle after Annie and the others who would join them in the helicopter. They all scrambled off the bus into the dazzling sunlight. 杰克把他们的韵文放回口袋里。 他背上背包。 然后，拿着笔记本和铅笔，他跟着安妮和其他将和他们一起乘坐直升机的人，沿着过道走下来。 他们全都从巴士上跳下来，冲进耀眼的阳光里。

“Jump aboard!” said Nancy. “快上车！”，南希说道。

Jack and Annie followed the four other adults up the steps of the helicopter and squeezed into a small cabin. Sitting in two rows of seats behind the pilot, everyone buckled their seat belts. 杰克和安妮跟着另外四个成年人走上直升机的台阶，挤进了一个小舱室里。 坐在飞行员后面两排座位上，每个人都系好了安全带。

Nancy pulled the door shut and turned the latch. Then she sat next to Pete and put on a set of headphones. “Headphones on, everyone! They’re under your seats,” she said. “They’ll protect your ears from the chopper noise, and also serve as a radio so I can talk to you.” 南希拉上房门，转了门闩。 然后她坐在皮特旁边，戴上了一副耳机。 “大家戴上耳机！ 它们在你们座位下面，”她说。 “它们能保护你们的耳朵不被直升机的噪音干扰，还能当收音机，这样我就能和你们说话了。”

Everyone reached under their seats and took out headphones. Jack and Annie pulled off their hoods. Without taking off their goggles or face masks, they placed the headphones over their ears. The thick pads muffled the sounds around them. 每个人都从座位下面拿出耳机。 杰克和安妮摘下了他们的兜帽。 在不摘下护目镜和面罩的情况下，他们把耳机戴在了耳朵上。 厚厚的耳垫把周围的噪音都隔得远远的。

Jack heard Nancy’s voice over his headphones: “Testing, one, two, three. Can everyone hear me?” 杰克通过耳机听到了南希的声音：‘测试，一，二，三。’ 大家能听到我说话吗？“

Everyone nodded. 大家都点了点头。

“Okay, Pete, take us to Mount Erebus!” “好啦，皮特，带我们去埃里伯斯火山！“

Pete the pilot started the helicopter. Even with his headphones on, Jack heard the roaring of the engine and the spinning of the blades. He held his breath as the chopper trembled and lifted off the ice and snow. 飞行员皮特启动了直升机。 即使戴着耳机，杰克也听到了引擎的轰鸣声和螺旋桨的转动声。 直升机颤抖着，从冰和雪上缓缓升空时，他屏住了呼吸。

The helicopter shook and tilted. Then it buzzed forward through the blue sky. 直升机摇晃着，倾斜了一下。 然后它嗡嗡地向前飞去，穿过蔚蓝的天空。

Annie aimed her camera out the window and took pictures. The Korean photographer did the same, and the Australian journalist scribbled in his notebook. 安妮把相机对准窗外，拍起了照片。 那位韩国摄影师也做了同样的事，而那位澳大利亚记者则在笔记本上飞快地写着。

Jack was too excited to take notes now. This is great, he thought. All the words in the rhyme are coming true. As they flew toward the burning mountain of ice and snow, he tried to remember what words came next. He slipped the rhyme out of his pocket and read: 杰克现在太兴奋了，根本没法做笔记。 太棒了，他心想。 这个韵文里的所有词语都成真了。 当他们飞向那座燃烧的雪山时，他努力回忆接下来的词是什么。 他从口袋里掏出韵文，读了起来：

… you must go

To a burning mountain of ice and snow

On wheels, by air, then all fall down … ……你必须去一座燃烧的冰山和雪山，坐轮子、乘飞机，然后全都掉下来……

“All fall down”? Wait a minute. What does that mean? thought Jack. Does that mean the chopper falls down? Do we fall out of the chopper? “全都倒下”？等一下。 这是什么意思？杰克心想。 这是指直升机倒下吗？ 我们会不会从直升机上掉下去？

As these thoughts swirled through Jack’s mind, Annie turned and gave him a thumbs-up. 当这些想法在杰克脑海里翻腾时，安妮转过身，给了他一个大拇指。

Jack didn’t want to scare her, so he nodded and shoved the rhyme back in his pocket. He watched anxiously out the window as the chopper approached a bright orange-red circle on top of Mount Erebus. 杰克不想吓到她，所以他点了点头，把那句咒语又塞回口袋里。 他紧张地从窗户往外看，直升机正飞向埃里伯斯山顶上一个亮橙红色的圆圈。

“Below is one of the world’s most famous lava lakes,” said Nancy over the headphones. “下面就是世界上最著名的熔岩湖之一，”南希通过耳机说道。

The chopper hovered motionless above the crater of the volcano. The lava lake bubbled and boiled. “That burning lava is miles deep,” said Nancy. “Its temperature is over seventeen hundred degrees Fahrenheit. Can you guys on this side see okay? Pete?” 直升机在火山口上方悬停不动。 熔岩湖咕嘟咕嘟地冒着泡，沸腾着。 “那燃烧的熔岩有好几英里深，”南希说。 “它的温度超过了华氏1700度。 这边的你们看得清楚吗？皮特？

Pete tilted the helicopter to one side and then the other. Everyone but Jack oohed and aahed. Kim Lee and Annie took pictures. 皮特把直升机往一侧倾斜，然后又往另一侧倾斜。 除了杰克，大家都发出“哦哦”和“啊啊”的惊叹声。 金莉和安妮拍了照片。

Go! thought Jack. Before we all fall down! 快！杰克心想。 在我们全都倒下之前！

“Okay, Pete, that’s great,” said Nancy. “Let’s land at the lower field camp now!” “好的，皮特，这很棒，”南希说。 “我们现在降落到下边的营地吧！”，

The helicopter tilted upright and began moving down the side of the volcanic mountain. Jack saw a small orange building sticking up from the snow. Colorful snowmobiles were parked near it. 直升机倾斜着直立起来，开始沿着火山山的侧面下降。 杰克看到一座小橙色的建筑从雪中露出来。 色彩鲜艳的雪地摩托停在它旁边。

Moments later, the chopper touched down onto the slope. It rocked and shuddered, then came to a stop. Whew, thought Jack. They’d landed without falling from the sky into a burning lava lake. But then what could “all fall down” in the rhyme mean? 片刻之后，直升机降落在斜坡上。 它摇晃了一下，然后停了下来。 哇，杰克心想。 他们竟然没有从天上掉进燃烧的熔岩湖里。 但是，那句童谣里的“全都倒下”又是什么意思呢？

“Stay seated till the blades come to a complete stop!” said Nancy. “在叶片完全停止转动之前，请保持坐好！”，南希说道。

Everyone remained in their seats with their seat belts and headphones on. 大家都坐在座位上，系好了安全带，戴好了耳机。

“As you know, we’ll be driving our snowmobiles up to the summit,” said Nancy. “Driving a snowmobile can be very dangerous on these steep, icy slopes. Please remember everything you learned in your snowmobile training yesterday.” “你们知道，我们接下来会开着雪地摩托前往山顶，”，南希说道。 “在这些陡峭的冰坡上驾驶雪地摩托是非常危险的。 请记住昨天雪地摩托训练中学到的所有内容。”

As the others nodded, Annie nodded, too. Jack nudged her. They’d never had any training with snowmobiles! 当其他人点头时，安妮也点了点头。杰克推了推她。 他们从来没有接受过雪地摩托的训练！

“Another warning,” said Nancy. “I know you’ve also been training this week to prevent altitude sickness. But still—it can be very dangerous. So please let me know if you feel any symptoms.” “另一个警告，”南希说。 “我知道你们这周也在训练，以防止高原反应。 但还是——这可能非常危险。 所以如果你们感到任何症状，请告诉我。”

Altitude sickness? Jack wondered. He pulled off his glove again and opened up his backpack. He took out the research book and looked up altitude sickness in the index. He turned to the right page and read: 高原反应？杰克想。 他又摘下手套，打开背包。 他拿出研究书，在目录里查找高原反应。 他翻到正确的那一页，读了起来：

Altitude sickness,also known asmountain sickness,is caused by a lack of oxygen at great heights. Symptoms include headaches, dizziness, and shortness of breath. Climbers traveling up Mount Erebus train for days by climbing to gradually increasing heights. 高原反应，也叫高山病，是因为在高处氧气不足引起的。症状包括头痛、头晕和呼吸困难。攀登埃里伯斯山的登山者会提前几天训练，通过逐渐爬到更高的地方来适应。

Oh, no, thought Jack. 哦，不，杰克心想。

The spinning of the helicopter blades had come to a stop. 直升机叶片的旋转已经停止了。

“Okay, happy campers,” said Nancy. “All clear! Before we drive up to the top, we’ll gather in the hut!” “好了，快乐的露营者们，”南希说。 “没问题！ 在我们开车到山顶之前，我们会先在小屋里集合！”，

Nancy opened the helicopter door. Everyone took off their headphones, undid their seat belts, and followed her down the steps of the chopper. Jack was last as he struggled with putting the research book away, getting his glove back on, and then pulling his pack onto his back. 南希打开了直升机的门。 每个人都摘下了耳机，解开安全带，跟着她走下直升机的台阶。 杰克最后一个到达，因为他一边努力把研究书放好，一边把手套戴回去，然后又把背包背到背上。

“What took you so long?” Annie asked when he got out of the chopper. “你为什么这么晚才来？”安妮问他从直升机里出来的时候。

Jack just shook his head. 杰克只是摇了摇头。

“Have a safe trip back to the station, Pete!” called Nancy. “See you later!” “祝你平安返回车站，皮特！”南希喊道。 “再见啦！”

Pete waved from the window. Then the chopper blades started rotating again. The chopper lifted off the ground and thundered away. 皮特从窗户里挥手告别。 然后直升机的旋翼又开始转动了。 直升机离开了地面，轰隆隆地飞走了。
