8. A Good Story 8. 一个好故事

The snowmobiles and the helicopter were both parked on the slope. The scientists and journalists stood talking with Nancy and Pete. 雪地摩托和直升机都停在坡上。 科学家和记者们站在那里，和南希和皮特聊天。

“Oh, no!” Annie said, grabbing Jack. “What about Penny? We’re not supposed to have a penguin! They’ll take her away!” “哦，不！”安妮说，一把抓住了杰克。 “佩妮呢？ 我们不应该有企鹅！ 他们会把她带走的！”，

Jack put his arms protectively over his parka. “I’ll hide her,” he said. “We’ve got to get her to Merlin.” 杰克保护性地把手臂搭在自己的防寒服上。 “我会把她藏起来，”他说。 “我们必须把她送到梅林那里。”

Jack heard a shout. He turned around. Nancy had caught sight of them. She was waving her arms and running toward them. 杰克听到一声喊叫。他转过身。 南希看到他们了。 她挥舞着双臂，朝他们跑过去。

“Hey, you two!” Nancy called. She dashed across the snow and threw her arms around Annie, then Jack. “嘿，你们两个！”，南希喊道。 她穿过雪地，先扑向安妮，然后是杰克。

Jack held his breath, hoping Penny wouldn’t be squashed. 杰克屏住呼吸，希望佩妮不会被压到。

Peep. 咕噜。

Jack pulled away from Nancy and pretended to cough. He made his coughing end in a sort of peep. 杰克从南希身边拉开，假装咳嗽。 他让咳嗽声最后变成一声咕噜。

“The chopper just got here!” said Nancy. “Pete told me he was delayed by a snow squall! I freaked out! Where have you been? Are you okay?” “直升机刚刚到了！”，南希说。 “皮特告诉我他被一场雪暴耽搁了！我吓坏了！” 你去哪儿了？你还好吗？“

“Don’t worry, we’re fine,” said Annie. “别担心，我们没事，”安妮说。

“Perfect,” said Jack. “We don’t feel a bit sick or anything. We were just getting some fresh air. We’re ready to go now!” Jack started walking toward the chopper. “完美，”杰克说。 “我们一点都不觉得不舒服什么的。 我们只是在呼吸一些新鲜空气。 我们准备出发了！”杰克开始朝直升机走去。

Annie grabbed Nancy’s arm and walked with her. “Did you have fun on the mountain?” Annie asked, trying to keep Nancy’s attention off Jack. “What happened? What did you see?” 安妮抓住南希的手臂，和她一起走。 “你在山上玩得开心吗？”安妮问，试图让南希的注意力不要放在杰克身上。 “发生了什么？ 你看到了什么？”

“We saw lots of things, but I was worried about you guys the whole time,” said Nancy. “Your parents must be frantic back at the station!” “我们看到了很多东西，但一路上我一直在担心你们，”南希说。 “你们的父母一定在车站急坏了！”

“They had their own expedition today,” said Annie. “They study penguins.” “他们今天有自己的探险，”安妮说。 “他们在研究企鹅。”

Peep! 啾！

“What was that?” asked Nancy. “那是什么？”纳西问道。

Jack did his weird cough again. 杰克又做了一次奇怪的咳嗽。

“Are you sure you’re okay, Jack?” Nancy called after him. “你确定你没事吗，杰克？”纳西在他后面喊道。

“I’m perfect,” he said. “我很好，”他说。

As the three of them approached the others, they were greeted with cheers. Nancy must have told everyone that we’re just little kids, Jack thought. 当他们三个人走近其他人时，他们被欢呼声迎接。 杰克想，南希一定告诉了所有人，我们只是小孩子。

“So glad you’re safe, young man!” said Ali, the biologist. He slapped Jack on the back. “真高兴你平安无事，年轻人！”生物学家阿里说。 他拍了拍杰克的后背。

Penny peeped, and Jack coughed. 彭妮小声说了句，杰克咳嗽了一下。

“Sorry you couldn’t make it up to the top,” said Kim, the photographer. “真遗憾你没能爬到山顶，”摄影师金说。

“That’s okay,” said Annie. “We still got a good story.” “没关系，”安妮说。 “我们还是有一个好故事。”

“Did you now?” said Lucy, the space scientist. “是吗？”太空科学家露西说。

“Yes, a really good story!” said Annie. “是的，一个非常好的故事！”安妮说。

“Excellent, but you mustn’t tell a soul,” said Tony, the journalist, “or one of us will steal it!” Tony laughed, and the others laughed with him. “太棒了，但你不能告诉任何人，”记者托尼说，“否则我们中有人会偷走它！”托尼笑了，其他人也跟着他笑了。

“Okay, we’ll keep it a secret.” Annie smiled. “好吧，我们保守这个秘密。”安妮微笑着说。

Pete opened the door of the chopper. 皮特打开了直升机的门。

“Brave little kids first,” said Nancy. “勇敢的小孩先上，”南希说。

Oh, brother, thought Jack. 哦，天哪，杰克心想。

Nancy ushered Jack and Annie toward the helicopter. They scrambled up the steps, climbed aboard, and sat down. 南希把杰克和安妮带到直升机前。 他们爬上了台阶，上了直升机，坐了下来。

As the others climbed in and got settled, Jack loosened his seat belt so he could buckle up without crushing Penny. 当其他人爬上来并坐好后，杰克松开了安全带，这样他就能系上而不压到彭妮。

Peep! 咔嗒！

Jack coughed. But to his relief, Pete started the engine. The rotor blades began to spin. 杰克咳嗽了一下。 但让他松了口气的是，皮特启动了引擎。 螺旋桨开始转动了。

“Headphones!” yelled Nancy. “耳机！”，南希喊道。

Everyone pulled on their headphones. 每个人都戴上了耳机。

Nancy gave Jack and Annie a big smile and a thumbs-up sign as the helicopter lifted off the mountain slope. 直升机起飞时，南希给杰克和安妮露出了一个大大的微笑，还比了个 thumbs-up 的手势。

“I love late spring evenings in Antarctica,” Nancy said to the group. “我最喜欢南极晚春的夜晚，”南希对大家说。

Jack looked out the window. The evening sky was lavender with streaks of pink. 杰克望向窗外。 傍晚的天空是淡紫色的，还有一道道粉色的光带。

“This light always reminds me that we’re in a different world from the world back home,” said Nancy. “这种光线总是让我想起，我们现在所处的世界和我们家乡的那个世界是不一样的，”南希说道。

Jack and Annie smiled at each other. If only the others knew how many different worlds there really were. 杰克和安妮相视一笑。 要是其他人知道其实世界上还有这么多不同的世界就好了。

The chopper swept up through the soft light of the cold sky,

up the slope of the burning mountain,

past the orange-red lake of boiling lava,

over white fields of ice and snow,

until finally it landed at the heliport, where the

red bus was waiting. 直升机穿过寒冷天空中柔和的光线，飞向燃烧的山峰，飞过沸腾的橙红色熔岩湖，飞过白雪皑皑的冰原，最后降落在停机坪上，那里有一辆红色的巴士在等着。

The chopper blades stopped spinning. Pete gave the signal. Then Jack and Annie followed Nancy and the others out of the helicopter. 直升机的旋翼停止了转动。 皮特发出了信号。 然后杰克和安妮跟着南希和其他人一起走出了直升机。

Jack held Penny in place under his parka as he boarded the red bus. He sat with Annie near the back. 杰克在登上红色巴士时，用他的防寒服把佩妮护在下面。 他和安妮坐在靠近车尾的位置。

Nancy took the driver’s seat and started the engine. As the bus rolled along, Jack peeked inside his parka. Penny looked up at him. She blinked a few times, as if she was a little worried. Jack patted her gently until she closed her eyes and fell asleep. 南希坐上了驾驶座，启动了引擎。 巴士缓缓开动时，杰克往防寒服里看了一眼。 佩妮抬头看着他。 她眨了几次眼睛，好像有点担心。 杰克轻轻拍着她，直到她闭上眼睛睡着了。

Jack kept patting the front of his parka to comfort Penny. He looked out the window and patted the baby penguin the whole ride. As Penny snuggled close to Jack, none of his worries of the day mattered anymore: his fear of falling into the lava lake, his dread of altitude sickness, his embarrassment at being caught by Nancy. All the cares and confusions of the day were wiped away by his feelings for the orphan penguin. 杰克不停地拍着自己羽绒服的前面，安慰彭妮。 他看着窗外，一路上都在拍着那只小企鹅。 当彭妮紧紧依偎在杰克身边时，他这一天所有的烦恼都不再重要了：害怕掉进熔岩湖的恐惧，担心高反的焦虑，还有被南希撞见时的尴尬。 他对这只孤儿企鹅的关爱，把这一天所有的烦恼和困惑都一扫而空。

When the red bus stopped at the station, Jack and Annie followed everyone down the aisle and climbed off. As the others stood talking in a group, Jack and Annie started walking away. 当红色的公交车停在车站时，杰克和安妮跟着大家走下过道，下了车。 当其他人站在一块儿聊天时，杰克和安妮开始走开。

“We’re leaving now! Bye, Nancy!” said Annie. “Bye, everyone!” “我们现在要离开了！ 再见，南希！”安妮说。 “再见，大家！”

“Thanks for everything!” called Jack. “谢谢你们的一切！”杰克喊道。

“Oh, no you don’t!” said Nancy. She grabbed them both by the sleeves of their parkas. “I’m not letting you two out of my sight again, not until I hand-deliver you to your parents.” “哦，不行，你别想逃！”南希说。 她抓住了他们的羽绒服袖子。 “我不会再让你们俩离开我的视线，直到我亲手把你们送到你们父母那里。”

“But—but … our parents are still on their penguin expedition,” said Annie. “但是……但是……我们的父母还在做企鹅考察呢，”安妮说。

“Then I’ll take you to where you’re staying,” said Nancy. “Come on.” Clutching their parkas, she started walking them toward the buildings. “You all must be staying at the wildlife quarters, right?” “那我就带你们去你们住的地方，”南希说。 “走吧。”她抓着他们的羽绒服，开始带他们往建筑物那边走。 “你们应该都住在野生动物区对吧？”

“Uh—yes,” said Jack. “呃——是的，”杰克说。

Nancy led Jack and Annie to a building at the edge of the compound. “Well, here you are. Home safe and sound.” 南希带杰克和安妮走到营地边缘的一栋建筑前。 “好吧，你终于来了。” “回家了，安全又平安。”

“Thanks!” said Annie. “谢谢！”安妮说。

“Bye!” said Jack. He was desperate to get away with Penny. “再见！”杰克说。 他非常想和彭妮一起离开。

“Wait—” said Nancy. “等等—”纳西说。

Oh, no. What now? thought Jack. 哦，不。又怎么了？ 杰克想。

“I’m still worried about you guys,” said Nancy. “Are your parents really here to study penguins? I want you to tell me the true story now.” “我仍然担心你们，”纳西说。 “你们的父母真的在这里研究企鹅吗？ 我现在要你告诉我真实的故事。”

Annie heaved a sigh. “Okay. The true story is that Jack and I came alone to Antarctica in a magic tree house—” 安妮叹了口气。 “好吧。 真实的故事是杰克和我独自乘坐魔法树屋来到南极洲——”

“Annie!” said Jack. “安妮！”杰克说。

But Annie kept talking. “It belongs to Morgan le Fay of Camelot. Morgan wants us to find the fourth secret of happiness for Merlin the magician. See, he’s very sad. And as soon as we leave Antarctica, we’re headed for Camelot to cheer him up.” 但安妮继续说着。 “它属于卡梅洛特的摩根勒法伊。 摩根想要我们为梅林这位魔术师找到幸福的第四个秘密。 你看，他现在非常伤心。 等我们一离开南极洲，就要去卡梅洛特让他开心起来。”

Nancy just stared at Annie. Jack held his breath, afraid Nancy would finally have her heart attack. 南希只是盯着安妮看。 杰克屏住呼吸，害怕南希终于会心脏病发作。

But Nancy burst out laughing and shook her head. “Where did that come from?” she said. “You guys are so cute! How do you think up this stuff? Seriously now, tell me the truth.” 但南希突然笑起来，摇了摇头。 “这是从哪儿来的？”她说。 “你们真是太可爱了！ 你们是怎么想出这些点子的？ 说真的，告诉我实话。”

“Well …,” started Jack. “嗯……”，杰克开始说。

“Oh, look!” said Annie. “Mom! Dad!” “哦，看啊！”，安妮说。 “妈妈！爸爸！”

“What?” said Jack. “什么？”杰克说。

“There they are!” said Annie. She pointed to a couple bundled up in parkas, goggles, and ski masks. They were walking toward a building. “在那里！”安妮说。 她指着一对人，他们穿着羽绒服、戴着护目镜和滑雪面具。 他们正朝一座建筑物走去。

“Oh—right!” said Jack. “Mom! Dad!” “哦——对了！”杰克说。 “妈妈！爸爸！”

The couple kept walking and disappeared behind the building. 那对人继续往前走，消失在建筑物后面。

“They didn’t hear us!” said Annie. “We better go! They’ll wonder where we are. Bye, Nancy! Thanks for everything!” “他们没听到我们！”安妮说。 “我们最好走吧！ 他们该会想知道我们去哪儿了。再见，南希！ 谢谢一切！”

“Nancy, you coming with us for coffee?” Tony yelled, standing by the bus. “南希，你要和我们一起去喝咖啡吗？”托尼一边喊着，一边站在公交车旁。

“You should go, Nancy,” said Annie. “We’ll be fine.” “你应该去，南希，”安妮说。 “我们会没事的。”

“Okay,” said Nancy, sighing. “Bye, guys. Run and catch up with your folks.” “好吧，”南希叹了口气说。 “再见，各位。” 跑过去追上你爸妈。

“And take care of that cough of yours, Jack!” said Nancy. “杰克，你得注意你的咳嗽！”南希说。

“Don’t worry, I will!” said Jack. “别担心，我会的！”杰克说。

Then Jack and Annie took off. They ran behind the building. They stopped and peeked back around the corner. They watched Nancy walk off with Tony and the others. 然后杰克和安妮出发了。 他们跑到了建筑物后面。 他们停下来，绕着角落偷偷张望。 他们看着南希和托尼以及其他的人走远了。

“Let’s go,” said Jack. He and Annie hurried away from all the buildings at McMurdo Station. “我们走吧，”杰克说。 他和安妮赶紧离开麦克默多站的所有建筑物。

Jack put his arms around Penny as they crossed the icy slope and ran to the cliff near the seashore. 杰克在他们穿过结冰的斜坡并跑到海边的悬崖时，把手臂环住了彭妮。

The tree house was still there, tucked under the overhang. Annie climbed in through the window. Jack followed her, careful not to let Penny slip out of his parka. 树屋还在那里，藏在遮蔽处下面。 安妮从窗户爬了进去。 杰克跟在她后面，小心不让彭妮从他的羽绒服里滑出去。

Annie pulled Morgan’s rhyme out of her pocket and read the last part: 安妮从口袋里掏出摩根的押韵诗，读到最后部分：

Then speed to Camelot by close of day,

“Let’s go!” said Jack. “Speed to Camelot!” “我们走吧！”杰克说。 “快点去卡梅洛特！”

Annie pointed to the word Camelot and said loudly and clearly: “I wish we could go there!” 安妮指着卡梅洛特这个词，大声清楚地说：‘我希望我们能去那里！’
