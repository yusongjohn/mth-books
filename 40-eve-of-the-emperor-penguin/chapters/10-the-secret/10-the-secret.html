10. The Secret 10. 秘密

“Well done,” Teddy said softly. “做得好，”泰迪轻声说。

“Indeed,” said Kathleen, smiling at Jack and Annie. “确实如此，”凯瑟琳微笑着对杰克和安妮说。

Morgan smiled at them, too. “Thank you for your four secrets,” she said. 摩根也朝他们笑了笑。 “谢谢你们分享四个秘密，”她说。

“You’re welcome,” said Annie. “But we’re not exactly sure what the fourth secret is.” “不用谢，”安妮说。 “但我们还不太确定第四个秘密到底是什么。”

“I told Annie I don’t think it can be a baby penguin,” said Jack. “Because in our time people aren’t allowed to have baby penguins.” “我告诉安妮，我觉得第四个秘密不可能是小企鹅，”杰克说。 “因为在我们那个时代，人们不允许养小企鹅。”

“That is true,” said Morgan. “But people can always take care of someone—or some creature—who needs them.” “这是真的，”摩根说。 “但人们总是可以照顾那些需要他们的人，或者动物。”

“So that’ s the secret?” said Annie. “所以这就是秘密吗？”安妮说。

Morgan nodded. “Taking loving care of another can make one very happy,” she said. “Like the other three secrets, it helps us look outside ourselves. Then we can better see all the gifts the world has to offer.” 摩根点了点头。 “照顾别人可以让人非常快乐，”她说。 “就像其他三个秘密一样，它帮助我们把目光投向自己之外。 然后我们就能更好地看到世界给予我们的所有礼物。”

“Yeah, taking care of Penny really took my attention off myself,” Jack said. “I forgot about a million things I was worried about.” “是啊，照顾小企鹅真的让我把注意力从自己身上移开了，”杰克说。 “我忘记了很多我原本担心的事情。”

“I know you will miss her,” said Morgan. “But I imagine you will see her again someday.” “我知道你会很想她，”摩根说。 “但我想象着，有一天你还会再见到她。”

“So are we going on another mission soon?” asked Annie. “那我们很快就要开始新的任务了吗？”安妮问。

“Return home and rest first, and we will send for you again,” said Morgan. “先回家休息一下，之后我们会再叫你，”摩根说。

“Can you try to make it really soon?” said Annie. “We don’t like long rests.” “你们能尽量快点吗？”安妮说。 “我们不喜欢休息太久。”

Morgan laughed. “We will see,” she said. 摩根笑了。 “我们会看看的，”她说。

“Oh, before we leave, I want to take a picture of you three guys,” said Annie. “It’s for my family project at school. You seem like a family to me. Look this way, please.” “哦，在我们离开之前，我想给你们三个人拍张照片，”安妮说。 “这是我在学校的家庭作业项目。” 你看起来像一个家庭。 请朝这边看。

Annie aimed her camera at Morgan, Teddy, and Kathleen. “Smile!” she said. 安妮把相机对准了摩根、泰迪和凯瑟琳。 “笑一个！”，她说。

“What are you doing?” asked Teddy. “What are you holding?” “你们在干什么？”泰迪问。 “你拿着什么？”

“It’s a camera,” said Annie. “Just smile. Say cheese.” “这是一个相机，”安妮说。 “Just smile. Say cheese.”

“Cheese? Why ‘cheese’?” said Teddy. “Cheese?” Why ‘cheese’?” said Teddy.

Click! Flash! 咔嚓！闪光！

“Got it.” Annie put her camera back into her pocket. “拍到了。”安妮把相机放回口袋里。

“What was that? What did you do?” asked Teddy. “那是什么？ 你做了什么？”泰迪问。

“It’s hard to explain,” said Jack. “It’s like magic. From our time.” “很难解释，”杰克说。 “就像魔法，来自我们的时代。”

“Good-bye now,” said Morgan, smiling. “Have a safe journey home to your family.” “现在再见了，”摩根微笑着说。 “祝你回家路上平安。”

“Thanks,” said Jack. “谢谢，”杰克说。

“See all you guys again soon— real soon, we hope!” said Annie. “希望很快就能再见到你们所有人——真的很快，我们希望！”安妮说。

“We hope so, too!” said Teddy. “我们也希望如此！”泰迪说。

“Good-bye,” said Kathleen. “再见，”凯瑟琳说。

Jack and Annie left the garden cottage and hurried back to the tree house. 杰克和安妮离开了花园小屋，赶紧跑回树屋。

They climbed up the rope ladder. Jack grabbed the Pennsylvania book and opened it to a picture of the Frog Creek woods. 他们爬上了绳梯。 杰克拿起宾夕法尼亚的书，翻到一张蛙溪镇森林的图片。

“Wait, I see Merlin and Penny! Look!” Annie pointed toward Merlin in the distance. He was still walking through the apple orchard with Penny at his side. “等一下，我看到梅林和彭妮！ “看！”安妮指着远处的梅林。 他仍然和彭妮一起穿过苹果园。

“They look like a little family now, too,” said Jack. “现在看起来也像一个小家庭，”杰克说。

“Yeah, they do. I should take their picture,” said Annie. She aimed her camera at Merlin and Penny and took a picture. Then she sighed. “Okay, home now.” “是啊，他们看起来是。” 我应该给他们拍张照片，”安妮说。 她把相机对准梅林和彭妮，拍了一张照片。然后她叹了口气。 “好吧，回家了。”

“Bye, Penny,” Jack said softly. Then he pointed in the book to the words Frog Creek. “ I wish we could go there!” he said. “再见，彭妮，”杰克轻声说。 然后他指着书上的文字蛙溪镇。 “我希望我们能去那里！”他说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切又静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全安静下来。

“We’re home,” Annie said. “That was a great trip.” She and Jack were back in the Frog Creek woods. “我们到家了，”安妮说。 “这次旅行真棒。”她和杰克又回到了蛙溪镇的森林里。

“Yeah. I hope we see Penny on our next mission,” said Jack. “是啊。 希望下次任务能见到彭妮，”杰克说。

“If we see Merlin, we’ll probably see Penny,” said Annie. “I think those two are a team now. At least we have a picture of them—and a picture of Teddy, Kathleen, and Morgan.” “如果我们见到梅林，大概也会见到彭妮，”安妮说。 “我想那两个人现在是一队了。 至少我们有他们俩的照片——还有泰迪、凯瑟琳和摩根的照片。”

Annie held up her camera and clicked through the photo display. “Oh, no!” she said. “I don’t believe it!” 安妮举起她的相机，翻看照片。 “哦，不！”她说。 “我不相信！”

“What?” said Jack. “什么？”杰克说。

“All my pictures are gone!” said Annie. “No Antarctica! No Merlin or Morgan! No penguins!” “我所有的照片都消失了！”安妮说。 “没有南极洲！ 没有梅林或摩根！没有企鹅！”

“Really?” said Jack. “Maybe you can’t keep the pictures you take on a magical trip.” “真的吗？”杰克说。 “也许你在魔法旅行中拍的照片是无法保存的。”

“I think you’re right,” said Annie. “I only have one picture. I took it right before we left home.” She held up her camera to show Jack. It was a picture of him. He had a grin on his face. “我想你是对的，”安妮说。 “我只有一张照片。 我是在我们离开家之前拍的。”她举起相机给杰克看。 这是一张他的照片。 他脸上带着笑容。

“That’s when I saw the streak of light over the woods,” said Jack, “and I knew the tree house was back.” “就在这时，我看见一道光划过树林，”杰克说，“我知道树屋又回来了。”

“Well, at least I captured that moment,” said Annie, sighing. “好吧，至少我捕捉到了那个瞬间，”安妮叹了口气说。

“Yep.” Jack put the Antarctica book on the floor of the tree house and pulled on his pack. Then he climbed down the rope ladder. Annie followed. As they started tramping over the fallen leaves, the woods were growing dark. Jack felt a little cold and hungry. “对。”杰克把南极洲的书放在树屋的地板上，然后背上背包。 然后他顺着绳梯爬了下来。安妮跟在后面。 当他们开始踩着落叶走时，树林变得越来越暗。 杰克感到有点冷，还有点饿。

“So the fourth secret is take care of someone who needs you,” said Annie. “I guess that could mean lots of things. Like take care of a sad person, a baby, a puppy, or a new kid in school …” “所以第四个秘密是照顾需要你的人，”安妮说。 “我想这可能有好多种意思。” 比如照顾一个伤心的人、一个婴儿、一只小狗，或者学校里新来的孩子……

Jack nodded. “Yep,” he said. “And maybe it works the other way, too.” 杰克点了点头。 “是的，”他说。 “也许反过来也是一样的。”

“What do you mean?” said Annie. “你是说啥？”安妮说。

“I think sometimes you can make other people happy by letting them take care of you,” Jack said. “我觉得有时候，你可以让别人开心，方法是让他们来照顾你，”杰克说。

“Oh, right,” said Annie. “It seems to make Mom and Dad happy to take care of us.” “哦，对啊，”安妮说。 “看起来让爸爸妈妈照顾我们，他们会很开心。”

“Like when they tell us to wear scarves and gloves,” said Jack. “比如他们让我们戴围巾和手套的时候，”杰克说。

“And make us dinner,” said Annie. “还有给我们做晚饭的时候，”安妮说。

“And tell us to be home before dark,” said Jack. “而且要告诉我们天黑前回家，”杰克说。

“We better hurry,” said Annie. “我们最好快点，”安妮说。

“Yep, let’s go make Mom and Dad happy—” said Jack, laughing. “没错，让我们去让爸爸妈妈开心——”杰克说着，笑了起来。

“By letting them take care of us!” said Annie. “通过让他们照顾我们！”安妮说。

The wind shook the tree limbs, and leaves fluttered to the ground. Geese honked overhead as Jack and Annie hurried home through the chilly November twilight. 风摇晃着树枝，树叶飘落在地上。 头顶传来鹅群的叫声，杰克和安妮赶紧穿过寒冷的十一月黄昏，跑回家。
