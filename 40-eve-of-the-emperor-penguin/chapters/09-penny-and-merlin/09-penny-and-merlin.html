9. Penny and Merlin 9. 佩妮和梅林

Penny’s head was sticking out of Jack’s jacket. Jack and Annie were wearing their own clothes again—their jeans, jackets, hats, and scarves. All their heavy cold-weather gear for Antarctica was gone. 佩妮的头从杰克的夹克里探出来。 杰克和安妮又穿上了自己的衣服——他们的牛仔裤、夹克、帽子和围巾。 他们去南极用的厚重保暖装备全都不见了。

“We’re in Camelot,” said Annie, looking out the window. “我们在卡梅洛特，”安妮看着窗外说。

Jack took Penny out of his jacket and held her up to the window so she could see the grounds of King Arthur’s castle. 杰克把佩妮从夹克里抱出来，举到窗边，让她看看亚瑟王城堡的庭院。

The tree house had landed in an apple tree in an orchard. In the near distance, castle towers rose into a late-afternoon sky. Several knights on horses were riding away from the castle. 树屋降落在果园里的一棵苹果树上。 在不远处，城堡的塔楼耸立在傍晚的天空中。 几名骑士骑着马，正离开城堡。

“Jack! Annie!” “杰克！安妮！”

Teddy and Kathleen were running through the orchard, between the trees, over fallen golden leaves and apples. 泰迪和凯瑟琳正在果园里奔跑，穿过树丛，踩着掉落在地上的金色树叶和苹果。

Jack and Annie waved at them. 杰克和安妮朝他们挥手。

“Oh, I just thought of something,” said Jack. “We forgot to find out the fourth secret.” “哦，我刚想起来一件事，”杰克说。 “我们忘了找出第四个秘密。”

“What do you mean?” said Annie. “It’s Penny.” “你是什么意思？”安妮说。 “是彭妮。”

“No, I don’t think so,” said Jack. “Remember, Leonardo da Vinci told us a secret of happiness has to be available to everyone. Not many people can have their own baby penguin.” “不，我不这么认为，”杰克说。 “记得吗，达·芬奇告诉我们，幸福的秘密必须人人都能获得。 不是很多人能拥有自己的小企鹅宝宝。”

“Oh, right,” said Annie. “哦，对啊，”安妮说。

“Jack! Annie! Come down!” Teddy and Kathleen called from below. “杰克！安妮！ 下来吧！”泰迪和凯瑟琳从下面喊道。

“We’re coming!” said Annie. “我们这就下来！”安妮说。

Jack put Penny inside his jacket, then carefully climbed down the rope ladder after Annie. 杰克把彭妮放进他的夹克里，然后小心地沿着绳梯爬下来，跟在安妮后面。

“Oh! What did you bring back with you?” said Kathleen. 你们带了什么回来？”凯瑟琳说。

Penny was peeking out of Jack’s jacket again. “A baby penguin for Merlin,” said Annie. 彭妮又从杰克的夹克里探出头来。 “给梅林的一只小企鹅，”安妮说。

“Look at her!” said Kathleen. “She’s beautiful!” “快看她！”凯瑟琳说。 “她真漂亮！”

“Indeed!” said Teddy, petting Penny’s downy head. “确实如此！”泰迪一边说，一边轻轻抚摸彭妮柔软的头。

Peep. 咕咕。

“She wants to help Merlin,” said Annie. “她想帮助梅林，”安妮说。

“Then let us hurry to him at once,” said Teddy. “Come, follow us.” “那我们立刻去帮助他，”泰迪说。 “来吧，跟我们走。”

Kathleen and Teddy led the way between the trees to a small wooden cottage at the edge of the orchard. 凯瑟琳和泰迪在树之间领路，带他们来到果园边缘的一座小木屋前。

“Merlin’s in there? ” asked Jack. “梅林在里面吗？”杰克问。

“’Tis a garden house he always loved as a child,” said Teddy. “Morgan thought he would find comfort there. But alas, he seems to have found nothing but more grief. He will eat nothing, and he has not spoken for days.” “那是他小时候一直喜欢的花园小屋，”泰迪说。 “摩根以为他会在那里找到安慰。 但唉，他似乎只找到了更多的悲伤。 他什么也不吃，而且好几天都没说话了。”

Teddy opened the door to the cottage and ushered Annie and Jack inside. 泰迪打开小屋的门，把安妮和杰克请了进去。

Morgan was sitting by Merlin’s bed. Late-afternoon light slanted through the window onto the magician’s face. He lay very still. His eyes were closed and his hands were folded on his chest. 摩根正坐在梅林的床边。 傍晚的阳光斜斜地透过窗户，照在魔法师的脸上。 他一动不动地躺着。 他的眼睛闭着，双手叠放在胸口。

A chill went through Jack. Merlin hardly looked alive. 杰克感到一阵寒意。 梅林看起来几乎没有生命力了。

Morgan turned around. She, too, looked tired, but her face brightened when she saw Jack and Annie. “Thank goodness you have come!” she said. 摩根转过身来。 她也显得很疲惫，但看到杰克和安妮时，她的脸上露出了笑容。 “谢天谢地你终于来了！”她说。

Annie crossed the room and hugged Morgan. Jack stepped forward with Penny. “Look what we brought for Merlin,” he whispered. 安妮穿过房间，拥抱了摩根。 杰克带着彭妮走上前。 “看我们给梅林带来了什么，”他小声说。

“Oh!” said Morgan. The enchantress gently touched the little penguin. “She is lovely indeed,” she whispered. “Thank you for bringing her.” “哦！”摩根说。 女巫轻轻地摸了摸那只小企鹅。 “她真的很漂亮，”她小声说。 “谢谢你带她来。”

Morgan turned back to Merlin. “Merlin?” she said. “Jack and Annie from Frog Creek are here. They want to talk with you.” 摩根转身面对梅林。 “梅林？”她说。 “杰克和安妮从蛙溪镇来了。 他们想和你说话。”

Annie stepped forward, while Jack stood in the shadows with Penny. “Hi, Merlin!” said Annie. “How are you doing?” 安妮向前走了一步，而杰克则和彭妮一起站在阴影里。 “你好，梅林！”安妮说。 “你过得怎么样？”

The magician didn’t open his eyes. But he nodded to let Annie know he heard her. 这位魔术师没有睁开眼睛。 但他点了点头，让安妮知道他听到了她的话。

“We have four secrets of happiness to share with you,” said Annie. She reached into Jack’s backpack. She pulled out the poem that the poet Basho had given them on their trip to old Japan. “我们有四个关于幸福的秘密要和你分享，”安妮说。 她伸手进了杰克的背包里。 她拿出了一首诗，那是他们在去日本旅行时，诗人芭蕉送给他们的。

“Listen, here’s a poem from a man named Basho,” said Annie. She read the short poem: “听着，这里有一首来自一个叫芭蕉的人写的诗，”安妮说。 她读了这首短诗：

An old pond:

a frog jumps in—

the sound of water. 一个古老的池塘：一只青蛙跳了进去——水的声音。

“The secret of happiness for Basho was that he paid attention to small things in nature,” explained Annie. “对芭蕉来说，幸福的秘密就是他注意到了大自然中的小事情，”安妮解释道。

Merlin nodded slowly. “Nature,” he said in a raspy voice. 梅林慢慢地点了点头。 “大自然，”他用沙哑的声音说。

“That’s right,” said Annie. “And we brought you this, too.” She pulled out the angel picture Leonardo da Vinci had drawn. She held it up to show Merlin. “没错，”安妮说。 “而且我们还带来了这个。”她拿出了一张莱昂纳多·达·芬奇画的天使画。 她把它举起来给梅林看。

“This is an angel drawn by a great genius named Leonardo da Vinci,” said Annie. “这是由一位名叫莱昂纳多·达·芬奇的天才画的天使，”安妮说。

Merlin opened his eyes and peered at the drawing. 梅林睁大了眼睛，仔细看着这幅画。

“Isn’t she beautiful?” said Annie. “The secret of Leonardo’s happiness was that he had great curiosity about everything—angels, art, noses, feathers, flowers, flying. Every day he felt happy when he learned something new.” “她不是很好看吗？”安妮说。 “列奥纳多幸福的秘密是他对一切事物都有极大的好奇心——天使、艺术、鼻子、羽毛、花朵、飞翔。 每天当他学到新东西时，他都会感到非常开心。”

Merlin nodded as he stared at the angel sketch. “Curiosity,” he breathed. 梅林点点头，他盯着那张天使的素描。 “好奇心，”他轻声说。

“Yes. And here’s the third secret,” said Annie. She reached into Jack’s pack and pulled out the nautilus shell they’d been given on their trip to the deep ocean. “是的。 这是第三个秘密，”安妮说。 她伸手到杰克的背包里，拿出他们在深海旅行时得到的鹦鹉螺壳。

“A sea creature once lived inside this shell,” said Annie. “We learned from an ocean scientist that a secret of happiness is having compassion for all living things, from a tiny shell creature to a giant octopus.” “曾经有一种海洋生物住在这个壳里，”安妮说。 “我们从一位海洋科学家那里学到，幸福的一个秘密是对所有生命都怀有同情心，从一只小小的壳生物到一只巨大的章鱼。”

Merlin took the shell from Annie. He cupped his hands around it and closed his eyes. His face softened. “Compassion,” he said. But still he didn’t smile. 梅林从安妮手里拿走了贝壳。 他用手捧着贝壳，闭上了眼睛。 他的表情变得柔和了。 “同情心，”他说。 但他还是没有笑。

Jack sighed. Maybe Merlin isn’t going to get better, he thought. 杰克叹了口气。 他想，也许梅林不会变好了。

“Give him Penny now, Jack,” whispered Annie. “杰克，现在把便士给他，”安妮小声说。

Jack stepped out from the shadows. 杰克从阴影中走了出来。

“Merlin,” he said, “we don’t actually know what the fourth secret of happiness is, but we want to give you something else.” “梅林，”他说，“我们其实不知道幸福的第四个秘密是什么，但我们想给你一些别的东西。”

Merlin looked at Jack. 梅林看着杰克。

Jack held up the tiny penguin. “Her name is Penny,” he said. 杰克举起了那只小企鹅。 “她的名字叫彭妮，”他说。

Merlin just stared at Penny. He looked confused. 梅林只是盯着彭妮看。 他看起来很困惑。

“She’s an orphan,” said Jack. “Her parents were lost in a terrible storm.” “她是个孤儿，”杰克说。 “她的父母在一场可怕的风暴中失踪了。”

Merlin frowned. “She is very small,” he said hoarsely. 梅林皱起了眉头。 “她非常小，”他沙哑地说。

“Yes. And she wants to live with you,” said Annie. “是的。 “她想和你们一起住，”安妮说。

“The emperor of the Cave of the Ancient Crown wants you to take care of her,” said Jack. “He says she is very brave and full of joy.” “洞穴古冠的皇帝要你照顾她，”杰克说。 “他说她非常勇敢，充满快乐。”

Jack set Penny on the floor. Standing alone, the penguin looked tiny and fragile. 杰克把彭妮放在地板上。 独自站着，企鹅看起来又小又脆弱。

“Go to Merlin,” Jack urged Penny. “去梅林那儿，”杰克催促彭妮。

Penny waddled across the floor to Merlin. Her little body rocked stiffly from side to side as she held out her wings to keep her balance. 彭妮摇摇晃晃地走到梅林面前。 她用小身体左右摇摆，展开翅膀保持平衡。

Penny stopped in front of Merlin. The little penguin and the old magician stared at each other for a moment. Merlin’s expression didn’t change. 彭妮停在梅林面前。 小企鹅和年迈的魔法师互相盯着看了片刻。 梅林的表情没有变化。

Peep! said Penny. Peep! Peep! 咕噜！佩妮说。 咕噜！咕噜！

Merlin’s face twitched. Then he began to laugh. At first his laughter seemed more like coughing than laughing, as if he hadn’t laughed in a long time. But then his laughter grew louder. He stood up and scooped the baby penguin into his arms. 梅林的脸抽动了一下。 然后他开始笑起来。 一开始，他的笑声听起来更像是咳嗽而不是笑，好像他已经很久没有笑过了。 但接着，他的笑声变得更大声了。 他站起来，把小企鹅抱进了怀里。

Merlin hugged Penny to his chest, pressing her against his long white beard. His face settled into a warm smile. 梅林把佩妮紧紧地搂在胸前，她贴着梅林长长的白胡子。 他的脸上露出了一丝温暖的微笑。

“It is your destiny to care for her, Merlin,” said Morgan. “The emperor of the Cave of the Ancient Crown has sent her to you. He is very wise.” “照顾她是你命中注定的职责，梅林，”摩根说道。 “古冠洞的皇帝派她来见你。他非常聪明。”

Merlin nodded. Then, cradling Penny in his arms, he walked to the doorway of the cottage and looked out. “The air smells like ripe apples and wood smoke today,” he said. 梅林点了点头。 然后，他抱着彭妮，走到小屋的门口，朝外望去。 “今天的空气闻起来像熟透的苹果和木头燃烧的烟味，”他说。

“Yes, my old friend, it does,” said Morgan. She wiped tears from her eyes. “是啊，我的老朋友，确实是这样，”摩根说道。 她擦去了眼中的泪水。

Merlin turned back and looked at Jack and Annie. “Thank you for bringing—What did you say her name was?” 梅林转身看向杰克和安妮。 “谢谢你们把她带来——你们刚才说她的名字是什么？”

“Penny,” said Annie. “彭妮，”安妮说道。

“Ah, yes, Penny … to me,” said Merlin. “And thank you for your other gifts as well. I will never forget the secrets you have shared with me.” “啊，是的，彭妮……对我来说，”梅林说。 “还有，谢谢你送我的其他礼物。 我永远不会忘记你和我分享的秘密。”

“You’re welcome,” said Jack and Annie. “谢谢，”杰克和安妮说。

Peep. 啾啾。

“Yes, yes,” Merlin murmured to the baby penguin. “You will stay with me. And we will have a happy time together. Let us go into the orchard and I will show you the rising moon.” “是的，是的，”梅林对小企鹅小声说。 “你将和我在一起。 我们会一起度过快乐的时光。 让我们去果园吧，我会给你看升起的月亮。”

Merlin put Penny down on the ground. The baby penguin took tiny quick steps alongside the magician as they walked into the orchard. The two strolled together between the trees, and a round moon rose over the kingdom of Camelot. 梅林把彭妮放在地上。 小企鹅跟在魔术师旁边，小心翼翼地走进果园。 两人在树间漫步，卡梅洛特王国的圆月缓缓升起。
