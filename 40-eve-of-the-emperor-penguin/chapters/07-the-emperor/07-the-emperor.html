7. The Emperor 7. 皇帝

Clutching the wand, Jack lay in the pitch-dark. He pushed up his goggles, but he still couldn’t see anything. 杰克握紧魔杖，躺在伸手不见五指的黑暗中。 他推了推护目镜，但还是什么也看不见。

“You there?” It was Annie’s voice. “你在那儿吗？”是安妮的声音。

“Yes,” said Jack. “在，”杰克说。

“You okay?” “你没事吧？”

“Yes. But we’re really in trouble now,” said Jack. “We fell way down into a dark hole, and the wand doesn’t work.” He struggled to sit up. “是的。” “但我们现在真的遇到麻烦了，”杰克说。 “我们掉进了一个黑暗的洞里，魔杖也不管用了。”他努力坐起来。

“Well, maybe we should try again,” said Annie. “也许我们应该再试一次，”安妮说。

“What’s the point?” said Jack. “We’ll never get out of here now.” “有什么用呢？”杰克说。 “我们现在永远都出不去了。”

Jack and Annie were silent for a moment. 杰克和安妮沉默了一会儿。

“Hey—we’re moving!” Annie said. “嘿——我们在动！”安妮说。

“Moving?” said Jack. They were moving. The ice under them was gliding silently and smoothly through the darkness. “动？”杰克说。他们真的在动。 他们脚下的冰在黑暗中无声地、平稳地滑动着。

“What’s happening?” said Jack. “发生什么事了？”杰克说。

“Maybe we’re not in a hole,” said Annie. “Look, there’s light ahead.” “也许我们不在洞里，”安妮说。 “看，前面有光。”

In the distance they saw a glimmer of light. As they kept moving, the light grew brighter. It grew brighter and brighter, until they slipped out of a dark tunnel into dazzling light. 在远处，他们看到一丝微光。 随着他们继续前进，光变得越来越亮。 光越来越亮，直到他们从黑暗的隧道滑出来，进入耀眼的光明中。

Now Jack could see they were lying on a slab of ice, floating down a narrow river. 现在杰克能看见他们正躺在一块冰上，顺着一条狭窄的河流漂流而下。

“We’re on an ice raft!” said Annie. “我们坐在冰筏上了！”安妮说。

“What’s happening?” said Jack again. “发生什么事了？”杰克又问了一遍。

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “But I think maybe the wand did work.” “我不知道，”安妮说。 “但是我觉得魔杖可能真的起作用了。”

The ice raft floated through light and shadow, past tall frozen cliffs. Then it glided toward an archway in one of the cliff walls. 冰筏在光和影之间漂浮，经过高耸的冰冻悬崖。 然后它滑向了其中一面悬崖墙上的一个拱门。

“Where are we going?” asked Jack. “我们要去哪儿？”杰克问。

The raft floated through the archway into a gigantic cavern. The cavern was like a huge icy cathedral. Its walls shone as if they were covered with silver. 冰筏穿过拱门，进入了一个巨大的洞穴。 这个洞穴就像一座巨大的冰制大教堂。 它的墙壁闪闪发光，仿佛覆盖着银色。

“Ohhh,” whispered Annie. “哦～”，安妮小声说。

“What is this place?” said Jack. “这是什么地方？”杰克说。

“I don’t know. But now I’m sure the wand worked,” said Annie. “我不知道。 但现在我确定魔杖起作用了，”安妮说。

The ice raft kept floating along a thin river inside the cavern, past icy archways and jagged ledges. Jack felt as if they were being watched. He thought he heard whispering and breathing. 冰筏沿着洞穴内的一条细小河流漂浮前进，经过冰制的拱门和尖锐的岩壁。 杰克感觉好像有人在盯着他们。 他觉得自己听到了耳语和呼吸声。

“Look!” said Annie. She pointed to one of the cave openings. Standing on the ledge outside the cave entrance were two penguins. They looked just like the emperor penguins Jack and Annie had seen earlier. “看！”安妮说。 她指着其中一个洞穴入口。 在洞穴入口外的岩石上站着两只企鹅。 它们看起来就像杰克和安妮之前看到的帝企鹅。

Jack and Annie stood up so they could see better. As their raft floated toward the ledge, the penguin in front stepped back into the cave. The other didn’t move. 杰克和安妮站起来，以便看得更清楚。 当他们的冰筏漂向岩石时，前面的那只企鹅退回到洞穴里。 另一个一动不动。

“Oh, man!” said Jack. “天啊！”杰克说。

The penguin wore a glittering crown. 企鹅戴着闪闪发光的王冠。

“The ancient crown,” Jack whispered. “We found it!” “这是古代的王冠，”杰克小声说。 “我们找到了！”

Annie didn’t say anything. She just smiled as the ice raft floated straight toward the emperor penguin. It hit the ledge with a gentle bump. 安妮什么也没说。 她只是微笑着，看着冰筏平稳地朝帝企鹅漂去。 冰筏轻轻撞上了岩石平台。

“Hi,” Annie said simply. “你好，”安妮简单地说。

The penguin made deep murmuring sounds. He didn’t speak in human speech. But somehow Jack and Annie could understand every word: Welcome to the Cave of the Ancient Crown. 企鹅发出低沉的咕哝声。 他不说人类的语言。 但不知怎么的，杰克和安妮能听懂他说的每一句话：欢迎来到古代王冠洞穴。

Annie bowed. The penguin had such dignity, Jack bowed, too. 安妮鞠了一躬。 企鹅如此有尊严，杰克也鞠了一躬。

The emperor spoke again: Come. He beckoned with his wing, then turned. 皇帝又说话了：来。 他用翅膀示意了一下，然后转身。

Annie stepped off the ice raft onto the ledge and followed the emperor into the cave. Jack put the Wand of Dianthus into his pack. He hoisted the pack onto his back and tried to balance his weight so he could step from the raft to the ledge. 安妮从冰筏上跳到岩石平台上，跟着皇帝走进了洞穴。 杰克把迪安瑟斯魔杖放进背包里。 他把背包背到背上，努力调整身体平衡，以便从冰筏上跳到岩石平台上。

“Hurry!” said Annie, poking her head out of the cave. “快点！”安妮说，她把头从洞里探出来。

“I’m coming!” said Jack. He leapt from the raft onto the ledge, then hurried into the cave. “我来了！”杰克说。 他从木筏上跳到岩石平台上，然后赶紧跑进洞里。

The Cave of the Ancient Crown was sparkling. Icicles hung everywhere. Pillars of ice glimmered with blue light. From behind a row of ice pillars came the sound of music—strange music, unlike any Jack had ever heard before. It sounded like the music of a thousand icicle wind chimes. 古代王冠洞闪闪发光。 到处都挂着冰凌。 冰柱在蓝色的光线下闪闪发亮。 一群冰柱后面传来了音乐声——奇怪的音乐，杰克以前从没听过。 那声音听起来像是成千上万冰凌风铃发出的音乐。

The emperor led Jack and Annie around the pillars. 皇帝带着杰克和安妮绕过冰柱。

“Oh, wow,” said Annie. “哦，哇，”安妮说。

Penguin couples were dancing to the strange music on a glittering ice rink. Pink and blue lights spilled over the dancers as they gracefully glided and turned. Some penguins danced silently with their eyes closed. Others touched beaks. A group of baby penguins danced together, hopping and sliding on the ice. 一群企鹅情侣正在闪闪发光的冰场上随着奇怪的音乐跳舞。 粉红色和蓝色的灯光洒在舞者身上，他们优雅地滑动和转身。 一些企鹅闭着眼睛安静地跳舞。 其他的用喙轻轻碰触。 一群小企鹅一起跳舞，蹦跳着在冰面上滑来滑去。

“How—how could this be happening?” stammered Jack. “这—这怎么可能发生？”杰克结结巴巴地说。

“Don’t ask,” whispered Annie. “It’s magic.” “别问，”安妮小声说。 “这是魔法。”

A murmur went through the ballroom as the penguins began to notice Jack and Annie. Though the strange music kept playing, all the dancers came to a stop. None of the penguins seemed alarmed, though. They all looked at their visitors with calm, friendly faces. 当企鹅们开始注意到杰克和安妮时，舞厅里响起了一阵低语。 虽然奇怪的音乐还在继续播放，但所有的舞者都停了下来。 尽管如此，没有一只企鹅看起来感到惊慌。 他们全都用平静友好的表情看着来客。

The emperor spoke to Jack and Annie: Word reached us earlier that you saved one of our own. We have been hoping to meet you. 皇帝对杰克和安妮说：我们之前就听说你们救了我们的一员。 我们一直希望能见到你们。

Puzzled, Jack looked at Annie. 困惑的杰克看着安妮。

“When we first got here, remember?” she whispered. “还记得我们刚到这里的时候吗？”她小声说。

“Oh, right,” said Jack. He’d forgotten all about rescuing the small penguin from the petrel. “哦，对啊，”杰克说。 他完全忘记了自己曾救过那只小企鹅，它被海燕追赶着。

You may stay with us for as long as you wish, said the emperor. You are honored members of our tribe now. 皇帝说：你们可以在这里待多久都行。 现在你们已经是我们部落的荣誉成员了。

“Thank you,” said Annie. “But we can’t stay long. We came to Antarctica to look for a secret of happiness.” “谢谢，”安妮说。 “但我们不能待太久。” 我们来到南极洲是为了寻找幸福的秘密。”

“To save one of our friends,” said Jack. It felt natural to tell the emperor and his tribe the truth. They lived in a world as magical as the world of Camelot. “His name is Merlin. He’s the magician of Camelot, and he’s really sad.” “为了拯救我们的一个朋友，”杰克说。 告诉皇帝和他的部落真相，感觉很自然。 他们生活在一个和卡梅洛特一样神奇的世界里。 “他的名字是梅林。 他是卡梅洛特的魔术师，他真的很伤心。”

The penguins began murmuring and whispering to one another. Jack couldn’t understand what they were saying. From the back of the crowd, a baby penguin waddled forward, the tiniest one in the tribe. 企鹅们开始互相低声议论起来。 杰克听不懂他们在说什么。 人群后面，一只小企鹅摇摇晃晃地走了出来，是部落里最小的一只。

“Oh, look …,” breathed Annie. “哦，看……，”安妮轻声说。

Jack grinned from ear to ear. The baby was a fuzzy gray ball with big dark eyes. It was even smaller than the young penguin they had saved from the petrel. The little penguin waddled up to the emperor. 杰克咧嘴笑得合不拢嘴。 这只小宝宝毛茸茸的，灰灰色的，长着一双大大的黑眼睛。 它甚至比他们之前从信天翁那儿救下来的那只小企鹅还要小。 那只小企鹅摇摇晃晃地走到皇帝面前。

Peep, peep, she said. “叽叽，叽叽，”她说。

The emperor looked down at her. 皇帝低头看着她。

Peep, peep. “叽叽，叽叽。”

She says she wishes to go with you, said the emperor. She wants to help your friend. 她说她想和你们一起去，皇帝说。 她想帮助你的朋友。

“But she’s so little!” said Annie. “What about her mom and dad?” “但她这么小！”安妮说。 “那她的爸爸妈妈呢？”

She is an orphan, said the emperor. Her parents were lost in a terrible storm. But she is very brave and full of joy. I know that she will bring happiness to your friend Merlin. 她说，她是个孤儿。 她的父母在一场可怕的风暴中失踪了。 但她非常勇敢，充满快乐。 我知道她会给你的好朋友梅林带来快乐。

Annie turned to the baby penguin. “Thank you,” she said. Then she leaned over and patted the penguin’s tiny head. “Oh, touch her, Jack. She’s so soft.” 安妮转向小企鹅。 “谢谢你，”她说。 然后她俯下身，轻轻拍了拍企鹅的小脑袋。 “哦，摸摸她，杰克。她这么柔软。”

Jack patted the baby on the head, too. As he touched her soft little feathers, she tilted her head and stared at him with her big eyes. Jack felt a wave of tenderness for the baby penguin. He couldn’t believe she was an orphan. Tears came to his eyes, but he quickly blinked them away and cleared his throat. “Thanks, Penny,” he said. 杰克也摸了摸小企鹅的头。 当他碰到她柔软的小羽毛时，她歪着头，用大眼睛盯着他看。 杰克对这只小企鹅感到一阵温柔。 他简直不敢相信她是个孤儿。 他的眼睛涌出了泪水，但他很快眨掉泪水，清了清嗓子。 “谢谢，彭妮，”他说。

Annie giggled. Jack couldn’t believe he’d just named the little penguin Penny. Usually Annie was the one who named the animals. 安妮咯咯地笑了。 杰克简直不敢相信自己刚刚给这只小企鹅取名叫彭妮。 通常都是安妮给动物们取名字。

Peep, said Penny. 叽叽，彭妮说。

She would like you to pick her up, said the emperor. 皇帝说，她希望你把她抱起来。

“Oh. Okay,” said Jack. He leaned over and held out his arms. Penny snuggled close to his parka. “哦。好的，”杰克说。 他弯下腰，张开双臂。 彭妮紧紧贴着他的羽绒服。

Peep! 啾！

Jack laughed and picked Penny up. He held the little penguin tightly. 杰克笑了，把彭妮抱了起来。 他紧紧地抱着这只小企鹅。

The emperor turned to the crowd. He said something Jack couldn’t understand. The crowd parted to make a path. The emperor nodded at Jack and Annie. Let us go now, he said. As Jack, Annie, and Penny left with the emperor, the penguins flapped their wings against their bodies, applauding. 皇帝转向人群。 他说了一些杰克听不懂的话。 人群让开，为他让出一条路。 皇帝朝杰克和安妮点了点头。 我们现在出发吧，他说。 当杰克、安妮和彭妮跟着皇帝离开时，企鹅们拍打着身体，鼓起掌来。

“Good-bye!” Annie called to the crowd. “再见！”安妮对人群喊道。

Jack smiled and waved. 杰克微笑着挥手。

Peep! said Penny. “啾！”彭妮说道。

The icicle chime music grew softer and softer as Jack and Annie followed the emperor through the cave. 随着杰克和安妮跟着皇帝穿过洞穴，冰锥风铃的音乐渐渐变轻了。

The tall penguin led them outside onto the ledge. 那只高大的企鹅带领他们走出了洞穴，来到一块岩石平台上。

Thank you for your help today, he said. 谢谢你们今天的帮助，他说。

“Thank you for letting Penny come with us,” said Annie. “谢谢你们让彭妮和我们一起来，”安妮说道。

“We promise to take good care of her. We’ll take her to Merlin,” said Jack. “我们答应要好好照顾她。” 杰克说：“我们会带她去梅林那里。”

The emperor touched the orphan’s downy head with the tip of his wing. He leaned close to her and softly murmured something in her ear. 皇帝用翅膀尖轻轻碰了碰孤儿蓬松的头。 他凑近她，轻声在她耳边说了一些话。

Peep, Penny said. “啾——”佩佩说。

The emperor looked up at Jack and Annie. Silently, he bowed to them and they bowed back. Then the emperor penguin turned and walked back into the Cave of the Ancient Crown. 皇帝抬头看着杰克和安妮。 他默默地向他们鞠了一躬，他们也回礼鞠躬。 然后，皇帝企鹅转身走进了古代王冠洞穴。

Jack sighed. For a moment, he hated to leave the enchanted world of the penguins. 杰克叹了口气。 有一瞬间，他不想离开企鹅那神奇的世界。

“Oh, she’s cold, Jack,” said Annie. “哦，她好冷，杰克，”安妮说。

Jack looked down. Penny was shivering in his arms. 杰克低头看了看。 小企鹅在杰克的怀里发抖。

“Put her inside your parka,” said Annie. “把她放进你的羽绒服里，”安妮说。

Jack unzipped his parka and carefully placed the little penguin against his sweater. He pulled the zipper back up. He held Penny close to him. 杰克拉开羽绒服的拉链，小心地把小企鹅靠在自己的毛衣上。 他又把拉链拉上。 他紧紧地把小企鹅搂在怀里。

“Perfect,” said Annie. “Just make sure she can breathe okay.” “太棒了，”安妮说。 “只是要确保她能正常呼吸。”

“Don’t worry,” said Jack, patting his jacket. “I’ll take good care of her.” “别担心，”杰克拍了拍自己的外套。 “我会好好照顾她的。”

“Do you think we can use the wand now to get back?” asked Annie. “你觉得我们现在可以用魔杖回去吗？”安妮问。

Jack nodded. “I think so,” he said. “I can’t think of any other way.” 杰克点了点头。 “我想可以，”他说。 “我想不出其他办法了。”

“Should we tell it to take us to the tree house?” asked Annie. “Or Frog Creek? Or Camelot?” “我们应该告诉它带我们去树屋吗？”安妮问。 “还是去蛙溪镇？还是去卡梅洛特？”

“No, we have to go back to Mount Erebus first,” said Jack. “If Nancy finds out that Pete never picked us up, she really will have a heart attack.” “不，我们必须先回到埃里伯斯山，”杰克说。 “如果南希发现皮特从来没来接我们，她真的会心脏病发作的。”

“Oh, right,” said Annie. “Then we better put on our masks and goggles again.” “哦，对，”安妮说。 “那我们最好再戴上口罩和护目镜。”

Jack pulled up his face mask and pulled down his goggles. “Get the wand out of my pack,” he said. 杰克拉上他的面罩，拉下他的护目镜。 “把魔杖从我的包里拿出来，”他说。

Annie reached into Jack’s backpack and pulled out the Wand of Dianthus. “Okay, ready?” she said. 安妮伸手到杰克的背包里，拿出迪安瑟斯魔杖。 “准备好了吗？”她说。

Jack patted his parka, comforting the baby penguin. “Here we go, Penny,” he said, “on a big adventure.” 杰克拍了拍他的羽绒服，安抚着小企鹅。 “我们出发吧，彭妮，”他说，“去一次大冒险。”

Annie held up the wand, then took a deep breath and said, “TAKE US BACK TO NANCY!” 安妮举起魔杖，然后深吸一口气，说：“带我们回到南茜那里！”

In less than an instant, Jack and Annie and Penny were on the slope of Mount Erebus. 不到一秒钟，杰克、安妮和彭妮就已经站在埃里伯斯火山的山坡上了。
