CHAPTER FIVE Leave Now! 第五章立刻离开！

“E xcuse me, young lady!” Mr. Nicolay said. “This is not a time for play.” “打扰一下，小姐！”尼科拉伊先生说。 “现在不是玩耍的时候。”

“I’m not playing, sir,” said Annie as she and Jack stood at the front of the crowd. “We’re friends of Tad and Willie’s, and Willie just told us to come here to the president’s office. He wants to introduce us to his dad.” “我没在玩耍，先生，”安妮说，她和杰克站在人群的最前面。 “我们是塔德和威尔利的朋友，威尔利刚刚告诉我们来这里去总统办公室。 他想让我们见见他的爸爸。”

Mr. Nicolay scowled. “I’m afraid Mr. Willie misspoke. The president does not have time to meet you now,” he said. “He is in a private meeting with delegates from California, Indiana, and Maine.” 尼科莱先生皱起了眉头。 “恐怕威利先生说错了。 总统现在没有时间见你们，”他说。 “他正在和来自加利福尼亚、印第安纳和缅因的代表进行私人会议。”

“Maybe later, then?” said Annie. “那是不是之后再说？”安妮说。

“Not maybe later,” said Mr. Nicolay. “After this meeting he is scheduled to have a meeting with his generals, and then a meeting with the Department of the Navy.” “不是之后再说，”尼科莱先生说。 “这次会议之后，他要和将军们开会，然后再和海军部开会。”

“Excuse me—” a man in the crowd called out. “打扰一下——”人群中有人喊道。

“But I heard the president say he’d like to meet us!” Jack broke in. “但是我想总统说他想见我们！”杰克插话道。

“I can’t imagine why he said that,” said Mr. Nicolay, shaking his head. “Following all the meetings I just listed, President Lincoln will meet with foreign diplomats, then with a group of senators, and then with reporters from the New York Times.” “我实在想不通他为什么这么说，”尼科莱先生摇着头说。 “我不能想象他为什么这么说，”尼科莱先生摇着头说。 “在列出的所有会议之后，林肯总统将与外国外交官会面，然后与一群参议员会面，接着与《纽约时报》的记者会面。”

“Mr. Nicolay! Listen to me!” someone shouted. “尼科莱先生！ “听我说！”有人喊道。

“So, sir,” Annie interrupted, “you’re saying he’ll have no free time at all today?” “那么，先生，”安妮插话道，“你是说他今天一点空闲时间都没有吗？”

“Oh, he might have a free moment,” said Mr. Nicolay. “But should that miracle occur, the president will go for a horseback ride in the country—and have a private meeting with himself!” “哦，他可能有一会儿空闲时间，”尼科莱先生说道。 “但如果那个奇迹真的发生了，总统会去乡下骑马——然后和他自己进行一次秘密的会面！”

“Got it,” said Annie. She took a deep breath. “Well, maybe you can just answer one question for us. Do you know if the president collects feathers?” “明白了，”安妮说。 她深吸了一口气。 “好吧，也许你可以为我们回答一个问题。 你知道总统收集羽毛吗？“

Mr. Nicolay threw up his hands. “This is no time for silly questions,” he said. “Our country is divided, young lady. We are on the brink of war.” 先生。 尼科莱举起了双手。 “现在不是问傻问题的时候，”他说。 “我们的国家已经分裂了，小姐。 我们正站在战争的边缘。”

“What do you mean, sir?” one of the men in the crowd shouted. “What’s the news from Fort Sumter?” “你是什么意思，先生？”人群中的一位男士喊道。 “福吉特堡有什么消息？”

“Yes! What do you know that we don’t know?” a lady called. “是的！ 你知道些什么是我们不知道的？”一位女士喊道。

Everyone started shouting at once. 大家一下子都开始喊叫起来。

“That’s it! Leave now, everyone!” Mr. Nicolay said. “The president is busy! He works night and day for you and for the unity of this nation!” “就是它！ 现在都离开！”尼科莱先生说。 “总统很忙！ 他日夜不停地为你，为这个国家的团结而努力！”

As the crowd shouted back at the secretary, Jack tugged on Annie’s sleeve. “Let’s get out of here,” he said. 当人群对着秘书喊叫时，杰克拽了拽安妮的袖子。 “我们快离开这里，”他说。

“We should wait for Willie,” said Annie. “我们应该等一下威尔利，”安妮说。

“I don’t think Willie can help us,” said Jack. “Come on. Let’s go back to the tree house and look at our research book. Maybe we can think of something else.” “我觉得威尔利帮不上我们，”杰克说。 “快点。 我们回树屋去看看我们的研究书吧。 也许我们还能想出别的办法。”

“Okay,” said Annie, sighing. “好吧，”安妮叹了口气说。

She and Jack hurried along the hallway, then down the stairs to the first floor. They wove through the crowd, then escaped out the main door. 她和杰克赶紧沿着走廊走，然后下楼到了一楼。 他们穿过人群，然后从正门逃了出去。

“Phew! That place is nuts!” said Jack as they walked between the tall white columns of the White House. “呼！ 那个地方太疯狂了！”杰克一边走，一边看着白宫高大的白色柱子说道。

“Are you sure we shouldn’t wait for Willie?” said Annie. “你确定我们不应该等一下威尔利吗？”安妮说道。

“I’m sure,” said Jack. He hurried down the carriageway. “Even if Willie took us back to the office to meet his dad, we wouldn’t be alone with the president. Lots of other people would be there, too. We couldn’t ask him for a feather. And we sure couldn’t give him any hope. Everyone would laugh.” “我确定，”杰克说。 他赶紧沿着车道跑下去。 “就算威尔利带我们回办公室见他爸爸，我们也不会和总统单独在一起。 那里还会有很多其他的人。 我们不能向他要一根羽毛。 我们肯定也不能给他任何希望。 大家都会笑的。”

“You’re right,” said Annie. “你说得对，”安妮说。

Jack shook his head. “How can the president even think in that place, with Tad jumping on him, his relatives visiting, his secretary yelling—” 杰克摇了摇头。 “总统怎么能在那里思考呢，泰迪一直跳在他身上，他的亲戚们来拜访，他的秘书还在大喊——”

“And a thousand people scheduled to meet with him,” said Annie. “还有上千人要和他见面，”安妮说。

“And another thousand who are trying to meet with him!” said Jack. “还有上千人正试图和他见面！”杰克说。

They had arrived back at the tree house. “Whew. No wonder the president needs to take a ride in the countryside by himself,” Jack said. He grabbed the sides of the ladder. “Let’s go up and look at the book.” 他们已经回到了树屋。 “哇哦。 难怪总统需要独自去乡下散散心，”杰克说。 他抓住了梯子的两侧。 “我们上去看看那本书吧。”

“Wait,” said Annie. “I have a good idea.” “等一下，”安妮说。 “我有个好主意。”

“What?” asked Jack. “什么？”杰克问。

“Right now we really need to have our own meeting with Abraham Lincoln, alone,” said Annie. “Right?” “我们现在真的很需要和亚伯拉罕·林肯单独开个会，”安妮说。 “对吧？”

“Yes … so?” said Jack. “是……所以呢？”杰克说。

“So if that’s the one thing we need, our book can’t really help,” said Annie. “But I know something that can.” “所以如果这就是我们最需要的，我们的书真的帮不上忙，”安妮说。 “但我知道有样东西可以。”

“What?” said Jack. “什么？”杰克说。

Annie reached into her apron pocket. She pulled out the bottle and read the label aloud: “ ‘Take a sip. Make a wish for one thing to help you on your mission. Remember: Trust the magic.’ ” 安妮伸手到她的围裙口袋里。 她拿出瓶子，大声读出标签上的字：“‘喝一口。’” 许一个愿望，帮你完成任务吧。 记住：相信魔法。’ ”

Annie looked up at Jack. “So why don’t we make a wish to have a private meeting with Abraham Lincoln?” 安妮抬头看着杰克。 “那我们为什么不许个愿，和亚伯拉罕·林肯私下见个面呢？”，

“Isn’t it too soon to use our only magic?” said Jack. “我们才刚拿到魔法，现在用是不是太早了？”，杰克说。

“Maybe. But maybe it’s the perfect time,” said Annie. “也许吧。 但也许现在正是最好的时机，”，安妮说。

“So we wish to have a meeting with the president all by ourselves?” said Jack. “那我们许愿，和总统单独见个面？”，杰克说。

“Yep,” said Annie. “对，”，安妮说。

Jack couldn’t think of another plan. “Well … okay,” he said. “Let’s do it.” 杰克想不出其他计划。 “嗯……好吧，”他说。 “我们开始吧。”

“Just remember, we have to trust the magic,” said Annie. “记住，我们必须相信魔法，”安妮说。

Jack nodded. 杰克点了点头。

Annie took the top off the bottle. She raised the bottle to her lips, then swallowed a quick sip of the potion. She handed the bottle to Jack, and he did the same. 安妮拧开了瓶子的盖子。 她把瓶子举到嘴边，然后快速喝了一口药水。 她把瓶子递给杰克，杰克也做了同样的事。

“You can make our wish,” said Annie. “你可以许愿了，”安妮说。

Jack squeezed his eyes shut. “We wish to have a meeting with Abraham Lincoln!” he said. “Alone!” 杰克紧紧闭上眼睛。 “我们希望和亚伯拉罕·林肯见面！”，他说。 “独自一人！”

There was a deafening WHOOSH and a ROAR. The earth shook, like a speeding train passing by. The ground opened, and Jack felt as if he were falling through space, 突然传来一声震耳欲聋的呼啸声，紧接着是一声巨响。 大地震动，就像一列飞驰的火车从旁边经过。 地面裂开了，杰克感觉好像正从空间中坠落。
