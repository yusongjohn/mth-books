CHAPTER THREE Hide! Hide! 第三章藏起来！藏起来！

W illie was waiting at the bottom of the ladder. “Hello,” he said. 威利正等着在梯子下面。 “你好，”他说。

“Willie, this is Jack and Annie,” said Tad. “I told them that you and I have a secret.” He gave Willie a meaningful look. “I told them we’d take them to meet the president. I gave them my word.” “威利，这是杰克和安妮，”塔德说。 “我告诉他们你和我有个秘密。”他意味深长地看了威利一眼。 “我告诉他们我们会带他们去见总统。 我给了他们我的承诺。”

“Oh, you did, did you?” said Willie. “Hello, Jack and Annie.” He shook hands with them. “I apologize for Tad,” he added. “My brother is very high-spirited.” “哦，你做到了，是吗？”威利说。 “你好，杰克和安妮。”他和他们握手。 “我为塔德道歉，”他补充道。 “我哥哥非常调皮。”

“And Willie’s sweet as pie,” said Tad, making a face. “Come on, you all! To the White House!” He saluted, then took huge marching steps across the lawn. “威利可甜了，”塔德说着，做了个鬼脸。 “来吧，你们都！ “去白宫！”他敬了个礼，然后大步跨过草坪。

“So does Tad really know the president?” Annie asked as she and Jack walked with Willie. “泰迪真的认识总统吗？”安妮一边和杰克陪着威尔利走路，一边问。

“He does,” said Willie with a smile, “and so do I.” “他认识，”威尔利微笑着说，“我也是。”

“Oh. Cool,” said Jack. He liked Willie’s kind, mature manner. He wouldn’t mind if Willie introduced them to the president. “Do you think you could introduce us?” “哦。太酷了，”杰克说。 他喜欢威利友善、成熟的样子。 如果威利能带他们去见总统，他一点也不介意。 “你觉得你能带我们去吗？”

“If Tad doesn’t introduce you first,” said Willie, “then I’d be happy to.” “如果塔德不先带你去的话，”威利说，“那我很乐意带你们。”

“That would be great,” said Jack. “那太好了，”杰克说。

As Tad marched ahead of them, Jack, Annie, and Willie walked through the sun-dappled grove of trees. The air was chilly, but it smelled like spring. It looked like spring, too. Tiny buds sprouted from bare branches. Birds flitted from tree to tree, and robins hunted for worms in the green grass. 塔德走在他们前面，杰克、安妮和威利穿过阳光斑驳的树林。 空气有点冷，但闻起来像春天。 看起来也像春天。 树枝上长出了小小的嫩芽。 鸟儿在树之间飞来飞去，知更鸟在绿色的草地上寻找虫子。

“Are your parents visiting the White House today?” Annie asked Willie. “你爸爸妈妈今天去白宫了吗？”安妮问威利。

“You could say that,” said Willie. “可以说是吧，”威利说。

“Look at me!” Tad called. He was running backward up the carriageway. “看我！”塔德喊道。 他倒着沿着车道跑。

“Watch out, Tad!” shouted Willie. “小心，塔德！”威利喊道。

Tad jumped out of the path of a horse-drawn carriage just in time. The carriage stopped in front of the president’s house. The crowd was huge now. As people tried to squeeze through the front doors, some waved pieces of paper at the guard. 塔德刚好及时跳开了马车的路。 马车停在总统家的前面。 现在人群变得非常大。 当人们试图从正门挤进去时，有些人朝卫兵挥舞着纸片。

“Everyone wants to meet the new president!” said Tad. “They all want something from him.” “每个人都想见新总统！”塔德说。 “他们全都想从他那儿得到些什么。”

“Don’t talk that way, Tad,” said Willie. He turned to Jack and Annie. “They’re mostly looking for jobs in the government. They have to take care of their families.” “别那样说话，泰德，”威利说。 他转向杰克和安妮。 “他们大多是在找政府里的工作。 他们得养家糊口。”

Tad led the way past the carriages and between the columns to the crowd at the door. “Allow us to enter!” he shouted to the guard at the door. “Jack and Annie are here! Important friends of the president!” 泰德带头穿过马车，绕过柱子，来到门口的人群中。 “让我们进去！”他对门口的卫兵喊道。 “杰克和安妮来了！ 总统的重要朋友！”

To Jack’s amazement, the guard did as Tad commanded. The man moved people aside and let Tad, Willie, Annie, and Jack walk right into the White House! 让杰克惊讶的是，卫兵照泰德说的做了。 那人让塔德、威尔利、安妮和杰克走进了白宫！

Jack trailed behind as they all passed through the entranceway. From there, they went around a bronze screen and into a wide hall filled with grown-ups. 杰克走在后面，他们全都穿过入口。 从那里，他们绕过一扇青铜屏风，走进了一条挤满成年人的宽大厅。

“Make way!” shouted Tad as he squeezed through the crowd. A few women squealed. Their hoopskirts rocked and swirled. “让开！”塔德喊道，他挤过人群。 几个女人尖叫起来。 她们的裙摆摇晃着旋转着。

“Stop, Tad!” said Willie, grabbing his brother. “Calm yourself!” “停下，塔德！”威尔利说，抓住了他的哥哥。 “冷静点！”

No kidding! thought Jack. He liked Willie a lot, but Tad was too wild and unpredictable. 真别说笑了！杰克心想。 他很喜欢威尔利，但塔德太调皮了，让人捉摸不透。

Tad laughed and broke loose from his big brother. He ran into a room off the hallway. Willie and Annie hurried after him. A moment later, Jack heard someone banging on a piano. 塔德笑了，挣脱了他哥哥的怀抱。 他跑进了走廊旁边的一间房间。 威利和安妮赶紧追了上去。 过了一会儿，杰克听到有人在钢琴上敲敲打打。

Jack followed cautiously. He went through a door into a huge parlor filled with women and girls sipping tea from china cups. 杰克小心翼翼地跟了过去。 他穿过一扇门，走进了一个大客厅，里面有许多妇女和女孩正用瓷器杯子喝茶。

The room had furniture covered in red satin. There was a large portrait of George Washington on the wall by the tall windows. No one was paying any attention to Tad as he pounded away on the piano keys. Even Willie was ignoring him. He was busy introducing Annie to a plump, dark-haired woman sitting on a sofa. 房间里的家具都盖着红色的缎子。 在高大的窗户旁边墙上，挂着一幅乔治·华盛顿的大幅画像。 塔德在钢琴上敲敲打打时，没人注意他。 就连威利也无视他。 他正忙着向坐在沙发上的一个胖乎乎、黑头发的女士介绍安妮。

Why don’t the grown-ups stop this bratty kid? Jack wondered. Where are his parents? The White House guards? The Secret Service? 为什么大人不制止这个调皮的孩子？杰克想。 他的父母在哪里？ 白宫的卫兵？ 特勤局的人？

Tad turned his head and caught Jack frowning at him. He jumped up from the piano bench, rushed over, and grabbed Jack’s hand. “Sorry, mate! I almost forgot! I gave you my word!” he said. “Come along!” 塔德转过头，发现杰克正皱着眉头看他。 他从钢琴凳上跳起来，跑过去，抓住了杰克的手。 “对不起，伙计！我差点忘了！ 我答应过你的！”他说。

Jack tried to free himself from Tad as the boy pulled him out of the parlor. 杰克试图挣脱塔德，而男孩把他从客厅里拉了出来。

“Stop! Let me go! I have to wait for my sister,” said Jack. He looked back and saw Willie and Annie still talking with the woman on the sofa. “停下！让我走！” 我得等我姐姐，”杰克说。 他回头一看，发现威利和安妮还在和沙发上的女人说话。

“They’ll catch up to us,” said Tad. “Come along! I have a secret that you won’t believe!” “他们会追上我们的，”塔德说。 “快点走！ 我有个秘密，你肯定不会相信！

“Please! Leave me alone!” said Jack. “请！ “别管我！”杰克说。

“No! Come with me, or I’ll start screaming,” said Tad. “And I can scream very, very loud.” He had a wild look in his eye. “不！ “跟我走，不然我就开始尖叫，”塔德说。 “而且我还能大声尖叫。”他眼睛里露出了一种狂野的神情。

Oh, no! Jack thought. This kid was totally insane. “Don’t scream, don’t scream, just hold on a second.” He called out, “Annie! Willie!” But neither of them looked up. 哦，不！杰克想。 这个小孩简直疯了。 “别尖叫，别尖叫，再坚持一下。”他喊道，“安妮！” “威利！”但两个人都没有抬头。

“C’mon! It’s now or never!” said Tad. He pulled Jack down the carpeted hallway, toward a wide staircase. “来呀！” “现在不走，就永远别想走了！”泰迪说。 他拉着杰克沿着铺着地毯的走廊走，朝一个宽阔的楼梯走去。

“Let go! Let go of me! I’m serious!” Jack said. “放开！放开我！” “我可是认真的！”杰克说。

Tad let go of him. “Please, please, come up the stairs with me,” he begged. “If you don’t, I’ll …” He opened his mouth wide. 塔德松开了他。 “求求你，求求你，跟我一起上楼，”他恳求道。 “如果你不上来，我就会……”他张大了嘴。

“Fine! I’ll come!” Jack said through his teeth. He let Tad pull him through a group of grown-ups climbing the stairs. When we get to the top, I’ll run back down, Jack thought. Then he can scream as loud as he wants. “好吧！ 我就来！”杰克咬着牙说道。 他让塔德把他拉过一群正在上楼的成年人。 等我们到了顶层，我就跑回去，杰克心想。 然后他就可以大声尖叫了。

As soon as they reached the hallway on the second floor, Jack turned around to run. But the stairway was packed with too many people to escape! 他们一到达二楼的走廊，杰克就转身想跑。 但楼梯上挤满了人，他根本逃不掉！

Tad grabbed Jack by the arm and pulled him to a door off the hall. “The president is right in there, I promise,” he whispered. “Do you want to meet him? Or not?” 塔德抓住杰克的手臂，把他拉到走廊尽头的一扇门前。 “总统就在里面，我向你保证，”他小声说。 “你想见他吗？还是不想？”

“Not,” said Jack. At least not with you, he thought. “不想，”杰克说。 至少现在不想和你一起，他心里想着。

“But you said you did!” said Tad. He threw open the door and pulled Jack inside, then closed the door behind them. “可你刚才说想见他！”塔德说。 他猛地推开房门，把杰克拽了进去，然后关上了门。

Jack looked around. The room was empty of people. It had a huge wooden bed with purple drapes. Flying birds were carved into the black wood. 杰克环顾四周。 房间里一个人也没有。 房间里有一张巨大的木床，上面挂着紫色的窗帘。 飞鸟是刻在黑色木头上的。

This must be the president’s bedroom! Jack thought with awe and horror. He whirled around, but Tad gripped the door handle. 这一定是总统的卧室！ 杰克又惊叹又害怕地想。 他猛地转身，但塔德抓着门把手。

“We can’t stay here, Tad,” Jack whispered furiously. “We’ll get in terrible trouble!” “我们不能待在这里，塔德，”杰克气愤地小声说。 “我们会惹上大麻烦的！”，

“But President Lincoln is there, in his dressing room!” said Tad. He grinned, pointing to a closed door off the bedroom. “I told you I’d take you to him!” “但是林肯总统就在他的更衣室里！”，塔德说。 他咧嘴一笑，指着卧室旁边的一扇关着的门。 “我告诉过你我会带你去见他的！”，

“You are crazy,” Jack whispered. “Move! I’m leaving! Before we get caught!” “你疯了，”杰克小声说。 “快走！我要离开了！” 在我们被抓之前！

Suddenly Tad groaned and fell to the floor. 突然，塔德呻吟了一声，摔倒在地板上。

“Tad?” said Jack. He bent down to check on him. “Tad, are you—” “塔德？”杰克说。 他弯下腰去查看他的情况。 “塔德，你—”

Tad grabbed Jack’s arm and pulled him to the floor, just as the dressing room door opened! 塔德抓住杰克的手臂，把他拉到地上，就在更衣室的门打开的那一刻！

“Hide! Hide!” Tad whispered. He scrambled under the big wooden bed. Jack frantically crawled after him. “藏起来！ “藏起来！”塔德小声说。 他爬到了那张大木床下面。 杰克慌慌张张地爬过去追他。

Jack held his breath as they lay on their stomachs under the bed. His heart was beating so hard that he thought he was going to have a heart attack! Tad covered his mouth and shook with silent laughter. 杰克屏住呼吸，他们趴在床下。 他的心跳得那么快，他以为自己要心脏病发作了！ 塔德捂住嘴，偷偷地笑得发抖。

Two large feet in black socks stopped beside the bed. Jack felt the bed sink down. A pair of hands put a pair of large leather shoes down on the floor. The feet slipped into the shoes. Then the weight lifted off the bed, and the shoes stepped forward. 一双穿着黑袜子的大脚停在床边。 杰克感觉到床慢慢下沉了。 一双手把一双大皮鞋放在了地上。 那双脚滑进了鞋子里。 然后，床上的重量消失了，鞋子向前迈了一步。

Tad crawled silently out from under the bed. Then he tackled the person wearing the shoes! The man yelled and fell to the floor. Tad sat on top of him and beat him with his small fists. 塔德悄悄地从床底下爬了出来。 然后他冲向穿那双鞋的人！ 那个男人大喊着摔倒在地。 塔德坐在他身上，用小拳头打他。

From his hiding place, Jack could see a dark-haired man lying on his side, groaning and moaning. Tad was attacking the president of the United States! 从藏身的地方，杰克看到一个黑头发的男人躺在地上，呻吟着。 塔德正在攻击美国总统！
