CHAPTER FOUR

Willie!

Jack was horrified. Would the Secret Service arrest him along with Tad? Jack had to stop him! 杰克吓坏了。 特勤局会连塔德一起逮捕他吗？ 杰克必须阻止他！

Suddenly the president burst out laughing. He wrapped his hands around Tad’s fists. “You little tadpole,” he said. “You didn’t scare me one bit!” Then he started to tickle Tad. 突然，总统大笑起来。 他紧紧抓住塔德的手拳。 “你这个小蝌蚪，”他说。 “你一点都不吓到我！”，然后他开始挠塔德的痒痒。

“Pa, don’t! Don’t, Pa, don’t!” Tad screamed and giggled and kicked. “爸爸，别挠了！ 别挠了，爸爸，别挠了！”，塔德又哭又笑又踢腿。

Pa? Pa? Jack thought. Abraham Lincoln is Tad’s “pa”! 爸爸？爸爸？ 杰克想。 亚伯拉罕·林肯是塔德的“爸爸”！

The president laughed. He stopped tickling Tad and kissed the top of his head. “What are you doing in here, my boy?” he said. 总统笑了。 他停止挠塔德，亲了亲他的头顶。 “你在这儿干什么，我儿子？”他说。

“Pa, me and Willie found a tree house,” said Tad. “Did you know there was a tree house here? Two kids were in it. Jack and Annie. Jack said it belonged to them. I told him it was mine because it’s in our yard. Isn’t it mine, Pa?” “爸爸，我和威利发现了一个树屋，”塔德说。 “你知道这儿有个树屋吗？ 有两个孩子在里面。杰克和安妮。 杰克说那是他们的。 我告诉他那是我的，因为它在我们的院子里。 这不是我的吗，爸爸？

Jack wanted to shout, No, it’s ours! But he was afraid to be caught under the bed. 杰克想喊，不，那是我们的！ 但他害怕被床底下发现。

“Wait—what did you say their names were?” the president asked, sounding serious. “Jack and Annie?” “等一下——你说他们的名字是什么？”总统问，听起来很严肃。 “杰克和安妮？”

“Yes,” said Tad. “They came out of nowhere. Don’t you think the tree house is mine, Pa? Mine and Willie’s?” “是的，”塔德说。 “他们突然就出现了。 爸爸，你不觉得这树屋是我的吗？ 是我的和威利的吗？”，

“They came out of nowhere?” said the president. “And their names are Jack and Annie? Are you sure?” “他们突然就出现了？”总统说。 “他们的名字是杰克和安妮？你确定吗？”

Why doesn’t Tad tell him I’m right here—under the bed? Jack wondered. Should I just crawl out? 为什么塔德不告诉他，我就在这里——在床底下？杰克想。 我应该直接爬出来吗？

“Yes, Pa, Jack and Annie,” said Tad. “But I want to know about the tree house. Do—” “是的，爸爸，杰克和安妮，”塔德说。 “但是我想知道树屋的事情。我—”

The door opened. “Mr. President, you must come at once,” a man said briskly. “You are late for your first meeting.” 门打开了。 “先生。 总统，您必须马上过来，”一个人匆忙地说。 “您迟到了，第一次会议。”

“Sorry, Mr. Nicolay,” President Lincoln said. “I’ll be right there.” He stood up. “对不起，先生。 “尼古拉，”林肯总统说。 “我马上就来。”他站了起来。

“The crowd is growing restless, sir,” Mr. Nicolay said. “Before you know it, they’ll storm your bedroom.” “人群越来越不耐烦了，先生，”尼科莱先生说。 “用不了多久，他们就会冲进你的卧室。”

“Oh, they wouldn’t dare,” said the president, chuckling. “Not with my bodyguard here.” He ruffled Tad’s hair. “Come along, tadpole. Escort me to my new office down the hall.” “哦，他们可不敢，”总统笑着说。 “有我在保镖这儿，他们不敢。”他挠了挠塔德的头发。 “快点，小蝌蚪。 带我去走廊尽头的新办公室。”

“But when will you come and see the tree house, Pa?” Tad said as they started out of the door. “可是，爸爸，你什么时候来看看我们的树屋呀？”塔德一边说，一边开始往门口走。

“Perhaps when I take my horseback ride later,” the president said. “I’d like to meet this Jack and Annie.” The door closed. And the room was quiet. “也许等我待会儿骑马出去的时候吧，”总统说。 “我想见见这个杰克和安妮。”门关上了。 房间里一下子安静下来。

Tad forgot about me! Jack thought. He couldn’t believe it! Then he realized he’d better get out of the president’s bedroom. He started to crawl out from under the bed, but the door opened again, so he quickly crawled back. 塔德把我给忘了！杰克心想。 他简直不敢相信！ 然后他意识到自己最好赶紧离开总统的卧室。 他开始从床底下爬出来，但门又打开了，所以他赶紧又爬回去了。

“Dust first?” Jack heard a girl say. “先打扫灰尘？”杰克听到一个女孩说。

“Aye, then shake out the pillows and change the linens,” said another. “对，然后把枕头拍一拍，换上新的床单，”另一个女孩说。

Jack could only see the black stockings and shoes of the two maids as they bustled around the room. Now, he thought, before they make the bed! He scrambled out and ran to the door. 杰克只能看到两个女仆的黑色长袜和鞋子，她们在房间里忙碌地走来走去。 现在，他想，在她们铺床之前！ 他赶紧爬出来，跑向门口。

One of the girls screamed. Jack didn’t look back. He threw open the door and headed for the stairs. As he bounded down the steps, one of the maids shrieked, “There was a boy under the bed!” 其中一个女孩尖叫起来。 杰克没有回头看。 他猛地推开房门，朝楼梯冲了过去。 他蹦蹦跳跳地跑下台阶时，一个女仆尖叫起来：“床底下有个男孩！”

Jack reached the bottom of the stairs and squeezed through the crowd until he found a nook off the hallway. He scrunched against the wall, then peeked around the corner to see if anyone was coming after him. 杰克跑到楼梯底部，挤过人群，直到他在走廊尽头找到一个角落。 他缩着身子贴在墙上，然后绕过拐角，看看有没有人追上来。

Someone grabbed his arm. 有人抓住了他的手臂。

“Ahh!” Jack yelled. “啊！”杰克喊道。

“It’s me!” said Annie. “Where have you been? I’ve been looking all over for you!” “是我！”安妮说。 “你去哪儿了？ 我到处找你！”

“I was upstairs! In President Lincoln’s bedroom!” Jack said. “Tad made me go in there! Did you know that President Lincoln is Tad and Willie’s dad?” “我在楼上！ 在林肯总统的卧室里！”杰克说。 “塔德逼我进去的！” 你知道吗？林肯总统是塔德和威尔利的爸爸！

“That’s what I was going to tell you. Willie introduced me to their mom!” said Annie. “She was really nice. Those were all her relatives in the parlor.” “这就是我要告诉你的。” “威利把我介绍给他们妈妈了！”安妮说。 “她真的很友善。 “那些都是她在客厅里的亲戚。”

“Well, Tad tricked me into hiding under the bed, and I almost got caught!” Jack said. “And Tad didn’t even remember I was there. He kept talking about the tree house, saying it was his.” “嗯，泰德骗我躲到床底下，我差点被抓住了！”杰克说。 “而且泰德根本没记得我还在那儿。 他一直在谈论树屋，说那是他的。”

“Willie says Tad gets overly excited,” said Annie. “He can’t help it. Plus, it’s their very first week in the White House.” “威利说泰德总是太兴奋了，”安妮说。 “他没办法，” 而且，这是他们第一次住进白宫。

“Well, it was awful,” said Jack. “I was trapped under the president’s bed!” “嗯，那真的很糟糕，”杰克说。 “我被困在总统的床底下！”，

Annie giggled. “You know, that’s actually pretty funny,” she said. 安妮咯咯地笑了。 “你知道吗，其实还挺好笑的，”她说。

“Not really,” said Jack. “其实不怎么好笑，”杰克说。

“Don’t worry,” said Annie. “Willie would have saved you. He told me to find you and then come upstairs to his dad’s office and he’ll introduce us. It’s on the second floor at the end of the hall.” “别担心，”安妮说。 “威尔会救你的。 他让我去找你，然后上楼去他爸爸的办公室，他会给我们介绍。 它在走廊尽头的二楼。

“Okay,” said Jack, sighing. “I heard the president say he’d like to meet us.” “好吧，”杰克叹了口气说。 “我听到总统说他想见我们。”

“Really?” said Annie. “真的吗？”安妮说。

“Yep, when Tad told him about the tree house and you and me, the president kept saying, ‘Jack and Annie? Jack and Annie? Are you sure their names are Jack and Annie?’ ” “对，当塔德告诉他有关树屋和你还有我的事时，总统一直在说：‘杰克和安妮？杰克和安妮？’ 你确定他们的名字是杰克和安妮吗？“

“That’s weird,” said Annie. “But I’m glad he wants to meet us. Come on.” “那太奇怪了，”安妮说。 “但是我很高兴他想见我们。来吧。”

Jack and Annie headed down the hallway and up the stairs. On the second floor, Jack kept his head down, just in case the maids were looking for him. Jack followed Annie down the hall toward a group of people standing outside a door. 杰克和安妮沿着走廊走下楼，然后走上楼梯。 在二楼，杰克把头低下来，以防女仆在找他。 杰克跟着安妮沿着走廊走，朝一群站在门口外的人走去。

A skinny man with a small pointed beard was speaking to them. “Ladies and gentlemen, please! I’m sorry, but only the names on my list can meet with the president today!” 一个瘦瘦的男子，留着一把小尖胡子，正在跟他们说话。 “女士们、先生们，请！ 对不起，但今天只有名单上的人才能和总统见面！”，

Jack recognized the man’s voice. “That’s Mr. Nicolay,” he told Annie. 杰克认出了这个男人的声音。 “那是先生。 尼科莱伊，”他对安妮说。

“Who are you to tell us we can’t see the president?” a woman in a pink bonnet asked Mr. Nicolay. “你们凭什么不让我们见总统？”戴粉红色帽子的女士问尼科莱先生。

“I am President Lincoln’s secretary, ma’am,” Mr. Nicolay said sourly, “and if you do not have an appointment, you must leave.” “我是林肯总统的秘书，女士，”尼科莱先生 苦着脸说，“如果你没有预约，就必须离开。”

“Excuse me, Mr. Nicolay, but I believe that I am on your list,” said a dignified man. He showed the secretary a piece of paper. The secretary checked the paper against his list. “打扰一下，先生。 尼科莱，但我相信我应该在你的名单上，”一位庄重的男士说道。 他给秘书看了一张纸。 秘书把纸和他手里的名单核对了一下。

“Of course. This way, Mr. Bennet,” Mr. Nicolay said. He opened the door and nodded for Mr. Bennet to enter, then followed him inside. While the door was open, Jack and Annie peeked into the room. “当然可以。这边走，先生。 “本内特，”尼科莱先生说。 他打开门，朝先生点了点头，示意他进去，然后跟着他走了进去。 门开着的时候，杰克和安妮偷偷往房间里看了一眼。 亚伯拉罕·林肯正坐在一张长桌旁。

Abraham Lincoln was sitting at a long table. Tad was perched on his lap, fiddling with his dad’s tie. Willie was studying a map on the wall, while the president was listening to one of the men at the table. “本内特，”尼科莱先生说。 塔德正坐在他腿上，摆弄着他爸爸的领带。 威利正在研究墙上的地图，而总统则在听桌子边的一位男士说话。

“Willie!” Annie whispered loudly. “威利！”安妮大声地低声说。

Willie didn’t hear, but Mr. Nicolay did—he rushed back out of the president’s office. 威利没听见，但先生 尼科莱确实做到了——他冲回了总统办公室。

“Willie!” Annie shouted. “威利！”安妮喊道。

Willie turned around just as Mr. Nicolay closed the door. 威利刚转身，就看见先生。 尼科拉伊关上了门。
