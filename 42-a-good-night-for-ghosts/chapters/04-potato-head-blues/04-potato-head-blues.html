4. Potato Head Blues 4. 马铃薯头蓝调

“Wat now?” Jack said. “怎么了？”杰克说。

“We have to follow him,” said Annie. “We can’t let him out of our sight.” “我们必须跟着他，”安妮说。 “我们不能让他离开我们的视线。”

Jack and Annie walked quickly after the mule cart. The hot brick road burned their feet. “Ow, ow, ow,” they both whispered. 杰克和安妮快步跟在骡车后面。 滚烫的砖路把他们的脚烫得生疼。 “哎哟，哎哟，哎哟，”他们俩小声地说。

“So let’s figure out—ow—what we’re going to say to him,” said Jack. “那我们得想一想——哎哟——我们该对他说些什么，”杰克说。

“Simple,” said Annie. “We’ll tell him we’d like to work with him. And then while we’re working, we’ll start talking to him about music. And put him on the right path to becoming the King of Jazz.” “很简单，”安妮说。 “我们会告诉他，我们想和他一起工作。 然后在我们工作的时候，我们会开始和他聊音乐。 并把他引上成为爵士乐之王的正确道路。”

“Hmm,” said Jack. It wasn’t much of a plan, but he couldn’t think of anything better. “嗯，”杰克说。 这个计划不算太好，但他想不出更好的办法了。

Up ahead, Dipper’s mule cart stopped near the back of a candy shop. 前方，迪珀的骡子车停在一家糖果店的后面。

“Hey, Dipper!” yelled Annie. “嘿，迪珀！”安妮喊道。

Dipper looked over his shoulder. He smiled. “What’s going on? Y’all are sticking to me like glue,” he said. 迪普转过身看了看。他笑了。 “发生什么事了？ “你们都像胶水一样粘着我，”他说。

“Well, actually, we were wondering—” started Jack. “其实，我们是想问问——”杰克开口了。

“If we could work with you?” finished Annie. “能不能和你一起工作？”安妮接着说。

“Work with me?” said Dipper. “I’m just delivering coal.” “和我一起工作？”迪普问。 “我只是在送煤。”

“Yeah, we know. We think it might be fun,” said Annie. “是啊，我们知道。 我们认为这可能会很有趣，”安妮说。

Dipper laughed. “Y’all are crazy,” he said. 迪普笑了。 “你们疯了，”他说。

“No, we’re not. We just like to work,” said Annie. “不，我们没疯。 我们就是喜欢干活，”安妮说。

“Yeah, yeah, we really do,” said Jack. “对，对，我们真的很喜欢，”杰克说。

Dipper laughed again. “Okay. I reckon there’s enough work to go around today,” he said. “There’s extra shovels and buckets in the cart.” 迪普又笑了。 “好吧。” “我觉得今天大家都有足够的活儿干，”他说。 “车里还有额外的铲子和桶。”

“Cool. Just tell us what to do, Dipper,” said Annie. “太棒了。 听我们的指挥，迪普尔，”安妮说。

“Fill your buckets with coal and toss each bucket load into the bin,” said Dipper. He pointed to a large wooden box at the back of the small candy shop. “Twelve bucket loads should do it.” “把桶装满煤，然后把每桶煤倒进箱子里，”迪普说。 他指着小糖果店后面的一个大木箱。 “十二桶水应该够了。”

“Got it,” said Annie. “明白了，”安妮说。

Annie carefully set the magic trumpet on the ground near Dipper’s cart. Jack left his cloth bag beside it. Dipper handed each of them a heavy shovel and a tin bucket from the back of the cart. Then all three of them started shoveling coal. 安妮小心地把魔法号角放在迪珀的推车旁边。 杰克把他的布袋子放在旁边。 迪普从手推车后面递给他们每人一把沉重的铲子和一个铁桶。 然后他们三个人开始铲煤。

Dipper whistled and worked quickly. But Jack and Annie had a hard time handling their heavy shovels. Whenever the shovels tipped to the side, all the coal fell off. Finally they both just grabbed pieces of coal with their hands and tossed them into their buckets. 迪普吹着口哨，干活很快。 但杰克和安妮很难用那些沉重的铲子。 每当铲子倾斜时，所有的煤都会掉下来。 最后，他们俩只好用手抓起煤块，扔进自己的桶里。

The afternoon sun beat down on Jack’s back as he worked. He was sweaty and short of breath. His hands were black from picking up the coal, and his clothes were covered with coal dust. This is a terrible job, he thought. He wondered how Dipper could be so cheerful. 下午的阳光照在杰克的背上，他正在干活。 他满身是汗，喘不过气来。 他的手因为捡煤变得黑乎乎的，衣服上也全是煤灰。 这真是个糟糕的工作，他想。 他想知道迪普怎么能这么开心。

“So, Dipper,” said Annie, “do you like music?” “那么，迪普，”安妮说，“你喜欢音乐吗？”，

Dipper’s answer was drowned out as he dumped a load of coal into the bin. 迪普的回答被他把一铲煤倒进煤桶的声音淹没了。

“What’d you say?” Jack called to Dipper. “你刚才说什么？”杰克朝迪普喊道。

Dipper answered again, but Jack didn’t hear him because a buggy rumbled by. This is a bad time for a serious discussion, Jack thought. He could hardly think in the burning sun. 迪普回答了，但杰克没听见，因为一辆马车从旁边驶过。 杰克想，这不是认真讨论的好时机。 在灼热的阳光下，他几乎没法思考。

As Dipper shoveled more coal, he started singing a song that seemed to give words to how Jack felt: 当迪普铲了更多煤时，他开始唱起一首歌，这首歌似乎道出了杰克的感受：

I’ve got those coal cart blues.

I’m really all confused—

I’m about to lose my very mind. 我有那种煤车忧郁，我真的好困惑——我快要疯掉了。

But Dipper didn’t seem confused at all, or about to lose his mind. He had a warm, raspy voice, and his song had a lively beat. 但迪普看起来一点也不困惑，也没有要发疯的样子。 他有一副温暖而沙哑的声音，他的歌还有一个欢快的节奏。

“Dipper! Dipper! Dipper!” some kids called. “迪普！迪普！ 迪普！”一些孩子喊道。

Dipper stopped singing. Three boys were running toward the coal cart. They were the singing trio Jack and Annie had seen on Jackson Square. 迪普停下了唱歌。 三个男孩正朝煤车跑来。 他们就是杰克和安妮在杰克逊广场上看到的那支会唱歌的三人组。

“I know you’re working,” said one of the kids. “But quit early today, Dipper! We just got a gig to sing in the parade.” “我知道你们在工作，”其中一个孩子说。 “但今天提早下班吧，迪珀！ 我们刚刚接到一个在游行中唱歌的演出机会。”

“Sorry, can’t do it, Little Mack,” said Dipper. “对不起，不行啊，小马克，”迪珀说。

Little Mack wasn’t exactly little, Jack noticed. He looked like he weighed over two hundred pounds. 小马克其实一点都不小，杰克注意到。 他看起来体重超过两百磅。

“Come on, Dipper,” said another boy. “来吧，迪珀，”另一个男孩说。

“Gotta work, Happy,” said Dipper. “得去工作了，哈皮，”迪珀说。

“Aww, Dipper,” said Happy. He didn’t look happy at all. “唉，迪珀，”哈皮说。 他看起来一点都不开心。

“Come on, Dipper!” said the third boy. “快点，迪普！”第三个男孩说。

“Can’t do it, Big Nose,” said Dipper. “别想得逞，大鼻子，”迪普说。

Jack looked at Big Nose’s nose. It was really quite small. 杰克看了看大鼻子的鼻子。 那真的非常小。

“Aww,” said Happy again. “啊，”快乐又说了一遍。

“Go on now. Y’all sound just fine by yourselves,” said Dipper. “Go on to the parade and have fun.” “现在走吧。 你们自己听起来就很好，”迪普说。 “去参加游行，玩得开心点。”

“But—” Big Nose began. “但是——”大鼻子开始说。

“Listen,” said Dipper. “Since sunup, I’ve delivered five cartloads of coal. I get paid fifteen cents a load. That makes seventy-five cents I’m going to take home to my family tonight. How much did you fellas make on the square today? How much you going to make in the parade?” “听着，”迪普说。 “从天亮开始，我已经送了五车煤。 每车我挣十五美分。 今晚我就能带回家七十五美分给我的家人了。 你们今天在广场上挣了多少钱？ 你们在游行中能挣多少钱？

The three boys were silent. 三个男孩沉默了。

“I’ve got a steady job now,” said Dipper. “You don’t need me. Go on to the parade and have a good time.” “我现在有一份稳定的工作，”迪普说。 “你们不需要我了。 去参加游行，玩得开心点。”

The three boys stared at Dipper for a long moment. “Come on, fellas,” Little Mack said finally. “Let him be. Ever since he got back from the Waif’s Home, he’s turned into a mama’s boy.” 三个男孩盯着迪普，愣了好一会儿。 “快走吧，伙计们，”小麦克终于说道。 “让他去吧。 自从他从孤儿院回来后，他就变成了一个妈宝。”

Jack wondered what the Waif’s Home was. 杰克想知道孤儿院是什么地方。

Dipper watched the three boys walk off. Then he looked at Jack and Annie. “Little Mack, Happy, and Big Nose Sidney are old buddies of mine,” he explained with a sigh. “We used to have a quartet. We sang everywhere together.” 迪普看着三个男孩走开。 然后他看了看杰克和安妮。 “小麦克、快乐和大鼻子西迪是我的老朋友，”他叹了口气解释道。 “我们以前是一个四人组。 我们到处一起唱歌。”

“Dipper, can’t you take just a little time off and go with your buddies to sing in the parade?” asked Annie. “迪普，你不能休息一会儿，和你的朋友们一起去游行唱歌吗？”安妮问。

“Nope,” said Dipper. “That’s just the way it has to be.” “不行，”迪普说。 “事情就是这样，必须如此。”

Dipper went back to shoveling coal. Jack wanted to ask him about the Waif’s Home, but Dipper didn’t look like he wanted to talk. He didn’t sing anymore as he filled up his coal bucket. 迪普又回到铲煤的工作中。 杰克想问他关于孤儿院的事情，但迪普看起来不想说话。 他一边往煤桶里装煤，一边不再唱歌了。

If Dipper doesn’t perform, he’ll never grow up to be the King of Jazz, Jack thought. He’ll never give his gifts to the world. 杰克想，如果迪普不演出，他就永远不可能成为爵士乐之王。 他永远都不会把他的天赋送给世界。

Finally Annie broke the silence. “Dipper, do you have to support your family all by yourself? You’re pretty young for that, aren’t you?” 最后，安妮打破了沉默。 “迪普，你必须一个人养活整个家吗？” 你太小了，不能做那种事，对吧？

“I’m not young. I’m fourteen,” said Dipper. “Mama Lucy, Mayann, and baby Clarence are all depending on me.” “我不小。 我十四岁了，”迪普说。 “露西妈妈、梅安和小克拉伦斯都指望我。”

“Is that your family?” asked Jack. “那是你的家人吗？”杰克问。

“Yep, and I love them a lot,” said Dipper. “是啊，我非常喜欢它们，”迪普说。

“I understand,” said Annie. “我明白，”安妮说。

“Me too,” said Jack. “But what about giving your gifts to the world?” “我也是，”杰克说。 “但是，你有没有想过把你的礼物送给世界呢？”，

Dipper laughed. “I can’t afford gifts for Lady the mule. How am I going to afford gifts for the world?” 迪普笑了。 “我没法给老杰克的骡子送礼物。” 我怎么才能给全世界送礼物呢？

“What about your musical gifts?” asked Jack. “那你的音乐天赋呢？”杰克问。

“Okay, good idea. I’ll sing a song to Lady on her birthday,” he said. “Let’s go now.” “好主意，好主意。 我生日那天要给老杰克唱首歌，”他说。 “我们现在就出发吧。”

Dipper tossed his shovel into the bucket. Then he reached into his pocket and took out some change. “Tomorrow I’ll be getting paid fifteen cents for this load,” he said. “In case I don’t see you again, here’s your share: five cents for you and five for you.” 迪普把铲子扔进了桶里。 然后他把手伸进口袋，拿出了一些硬币。 “明天我就能因为这趟活赚到十五美分了，”他说。 “万一我再也见不到你了，这是你的那份：五美分给你，五美分给你。”

“No, no, keep it,” said Annie. “You should keep it all for your family, Dipper.” “不，不，留着吧，”安妮说。 “你应该把所有东西都留给你的家人，迪普。”

“What? That’s not right,” said Dipper. “什么？ “那不对，”迪普说。

“It is right,” said Jack. “是对的，”杰克说。

“Then what did y’all do that work for?” asked Dipper. “那你们做这么多事是为了什么？”迪普问。

“We didn’t do that much,” Jack said. “Not nearly as much as you did. It was a lot harder job than I thought it would be.” “我们没做那么多，”杰克说。 “远没有你们做得多。 这比我想的要难得多。”

“Then why did y’all keep working?” asked Dipper. “那你们为什么还继续工作呢？”迪普问。

“It was fun hanging out with you,” said Annie. “和你一起玩真开心，”安妮说。

Dipper laughed. “Well, you two sure are a couple of potato heads,” he said. 迪普笑了。 “你们俩真是两个土豆脑袋，”他说。

“What’s that mean?” asked Annie. “这是什么意思？”安妮问。

“It means you don’t have any more brains than a pair of potatoes,” said Dipper. “意思是你们的脑子和一对土豆一样没用，”迪普说。

Jack and Annie just laughed. 杰克和安妮只是笑了笑。

“Sure you won’t take any money?” said Dipper. “你们真的不收钱吗？”迪普问。

“Absolutely!” said Annie. “当然不收！”安妮说。

“Well, thanks a million for your help,” said Dipper. He climbed back into the driver’s seat of the cart. “Hey want me and Lady to give y’all a ride back to the coal yard?” “那真是太感谢你们的帮助了，”迪普说。 他爬回了马车的驾驶座。 “嘿，要我和莉迪带你们回煤场吗？”

“Yes!” said Jack and Annie together. “好啊！”杰克和安妮异口同声地说。

“Good. Climb on!” said Dipper. “很好。 上来吧！”迪普说。

“Thanks, man!” said Jack. “谢谢你，老兄！”杰克说。

Annie grabbed their trumpet. Jack grabbed his bag, and they sat on the cart bench next to Dipper. Jack was still hot and sweaty. He was tired and his arms were sore. But for some reason, he felt great. 安妮抓起了他们的喇叭。 杰克拿起了他的包，他们坐在迪普旁边的推车座位上。 杰克还是又热又出汗。 他很累，手臂也酸痛。 但不知为什么，他感觉特别棒。

“Let’s go, Lady,” said Dipper. “走吧，莉迪，”迪普说。

Lady the mule began pulling the creaky cart along the Mississippi River. And Dipper began singing again: 骡子莉迪开始拉着吱呀作响的马车沿着密西西比河前进。 迪普又开始唱歌了：

I’ve got those coal cart blues.

I’m really all confused… 我得了那种煤车忧郁症，我真的好困惑……
