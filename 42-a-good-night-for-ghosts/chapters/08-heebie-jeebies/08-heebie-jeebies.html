8. Heebie Jeebies 8. 焦虑不安

Tunder shook the night. The wind howled. The pirate ghost floated down the stairs. 雷声把夜晚都震动了。风在嚎叫。 海盗幽灵飘着走下楼梯。

“The ghost of Jean Lafitte!” whispered Annie. “这是让·拉菲特的幽灵！”安妮小声说。

The ghost pointed a bony finger at Happy. “NOT AFRAID OF GHOSTS?” his voice boomed again. He pointed at Big Nose Sidney. “NOT EVEN A LITTLE BIT? HAH-HAH-HAH!” 幽灵用他那骨头般的手指指着哈皮。 “不怕幽灵吗？”他的声音再次轰隆隆地响起。 他指着大鼻子西德尼。 “连一点都不敢吗？哈哈哈！”

“AHHHHHHH!” everyone shrieked again. They all scrambled out of the back room to the front room. They pushed on the front door together. But the door still wouldn’t open. “啊啊啊啊啊！”大家又尖叫起来。 他们都从后房间慌慌张张地跑到了前房间。 他们一起推了推前门。 但门还是打不开。

“SCURVY DOGS! YOU CANNOT ESCAPE ME!” the ghost of Jean Lafitte shouted. “卑鄙的狗！ 你们逃不掉的！”，让·拉菲特的鬼魂大喊道。

The pirate ghost floated to the center of the room and stopped. He rested his hands on his hips, threw back his head, and laughed again. “YOU’RE TRAPPED NOW!” Jean Lafitte roared. “TRAPPED HERE FOREVER!” 海盗鬼魂飘到房间中央停了下来。 他双手叉腰，仰起头，又笑了起来。 “现在你们被困住了！”，让·拉菲特怒吼道。 “永远被困在这里！”，

To Jack’s horror, more ghost pirates began gliding through the walls into the room. One at a time they came: a pirate with a gold earring, another with a pistol, one with a head scarf, another with an eye patch, one with a saber, another with a bushy beard, one with a thin mustache, another with a sack, one with a striped shirt, another with a peg leg. 杰克惊恐地发现，更多的海盗鬼魂开始从墙壁中滑进来，进入房间。 一个接一个地出现：一个戴着金耳环的海盗，另一个拿着手枪，一个戴着头巾，另一个戴着眼罩，一个拿着剑，另一个留着浓密的胡须，一个留着细小的胡子，另一个拿着袋子，一个穿着条纹衬衫，另一个装着假腿。

Finally ten ghost pirates circled the room! 终于，十个幽灵海盗围住了房间！

From the center of the circle, Jean Lafitte let out another peal of mean laughter. “HAH-HAH-HAH!” 从圈的中心，让·拉菲特发出一阵邪恶的笑声。 “哈哈哈——！”

The pirate crew snorted, snarled, and growled, “YARRR! ARGHH! ARRL!” 海盗们哼哼唧唧，龇牙咧嘴，喊道：“呀——！啊——！ 啊——！”

Suddenly Jack started singing: 突然，杰克开始唱歌：

Skiddle-diddle dog!

Hey, hey, hey!

Ghost, go away,

Go away, go away! 跳跳狗！嘿，嘿，嘿！鬼魂，走开，走开，走开！

“Jack!” said Annie. “What are you doing?” “杰克！”安妮说。 “你在干什么？”

“Play Annie! Play!” squeaked Jack. “陪我玩，安妮！ “玩吧！”杰克咯咯地叫道。

“I can’t!” said Annie. “We used up the magic! It’s just an ordinary trumpet now!” “我做不到！”安妮说。 “我们用完了魔法！ 现在它只是一个普通的喇叭了！

“Here, give it to me!” said Dipper. “这里，给我！”杰克说。

Annie handed Dipper the trumpet. 安妮把喇叭递给了杰克。

Dipper put the trumpet to his lips. He closed his eyes. He took a deep breath, and then he blew. The air vibrated with a single warm note. Then Dipper’s fingers danced over the trumpet’s valves. A lively, swinging tune filled the blacksmith shop. 迪普把喇叭放在嘴唇上。 他闭上了眼睛。 他深吸一口气，然后吹了出去。 空气里传来一个温暖的音符。 接着，迪普的手指在喇叭的活塞上跳动起来。 一个欢快的、摇摆的曲子充满了铁匠铺。

Jean Lafitte stopped laughing his mean laugh. He held up his hands for his crew to be silent. As Dipper played, crooked smiles crossed the pirates’ faces. 让·拉菲特停止了他那令人讨厌的笑声。 他举高双手，让他的船员们安静下来。 当迪普演奏时，海盗们的脸上露出了歪歪的笑容。

Dipper’s joyful music drowned out the noise of the storm outside. Annie snatched two rungs from a broken chair. She used them as drumsticks, tapping them against a wooden bucket. Jack grabbed two more rungs and did the same. 迪普快乐的音乐盖过了外面风暴的嘈杂声。 安妮从一把坏掉的椅子上抓起了两根横木。 她用它们当鼓棒，敲打着一个木桶。 杰克抓了另外两根横木，也做了同样的事情。

Dipper paused long enough to yell to his trio, “Hey, boys! Sing the Heebie-Jeebie song!” 迪普暂停了一下，大声对他的三人组喊道：“嘿，伙计们！ 唱《希比-吉比》歌！”

“We don’t know it!” said Little Mack. “我们不知道！”小马克说。

“Make somethin’ up!” called Dipper. “编个故事吧！”迪普喊道。

As Dipper played the trumpet, Little Mack sang: 迪普吹着小号，小马克开始唱歌：

I got the heebies!

You got the jeebies! 我吓坏了！你也被吓到了！

Then Happy sang: 然后快乐也唱了：

The heebies make you hop!

And the jeebies make you quake! Heebies 让你跳起来！Jeebies 让你发抖！

Then Big Nose Sidney sang: 接着大鼻子西德尼也唱了：

Do a little dance, Mama!

Stomp and shake! 跳个舞吧，妈妈！跺脚，摇晃！

As the trio sang, the ghost of Jean Lafitte started to dance. He shook his head and clapped his hands. He waved his arms through the air. He turned in a circle. “Go, Mama, go!” he shouted. 当三人唱起来时，让·拉菲特的鬼魂开始跳舞了。 他摇着头，拍着手。 他挥舞着双臂。 他转了个圈。 “走吧，妈妈，走吧！”他喊道。

Lafitte’s crew began dancing like their captain. All the ghost pirates moved in a circle, shaking their heads and waving their hands. Some floated off the floor, turning this way and that. 拉菲特的船员们开始像他们的船长一样跳舞。 所有幽灵海盗都围成一圈，摇着头，挥舞着手臂。 有些海盗从地板上飘起来，来回转圈。

Little Mack sang: 小马克唱道：

Hey, Papa! Hey, Mama!

Hey! Hey! Hey! 嘿，爸爸！嘿，妈妈！嘿！嘿！嘿！

“Hey, hey, hey!” all the pirates shouted. “Hey, hey, hey!” “嘿，嘿，嘿！”所有海盗都喊道。 “嘿，嘿，嘿！”

Dipper played the trumpet. Jack and Annie drummed on the bucket. The trio sang. The floor shook. The windows rattled. And all the pirates stomped and shook, doing the Heebie-Jeebie dance. 迪普吹起了小号。 杰克和安妮在桶上敲鼓。三人一起唱歌。 地板开始震动。 窗户发出哗啦哗啦的响声。 所有海盗都踩着脚步，跳起了希比吉比舞。

“SWING THAT MUSIC!” shouted Jean Lafitte’s ghost. “把音乐摇起来！”让·拉菲特的鬼魂喊道。

“YARR!” the pirates all shouted. “YARR! YARR!” “呀！”海盗们齐声喊道。 “呀！呀！”，

The front door suddenly banged open. 前门突然砰地一声打开了。

“GO, MAMA! GO, PAPA! GO! GO! GO!” shouted the ghost of Jean Lafitte. He danced out of the shop, and his pirate crew followed, one by one. “快走，妈妈！快走，爸爸！ 走！走！ “走！”让·拉菲特的鬼魂喊道。 他从店里跳了出来，他的海盗队伍一个接一个地跟了上去。

As the ghosts all danced out of the shop, Dipper kept playing. The trio kept singing, and Jack and Annie kept drumming. 当所有的鬼魂都从店里跳出来时，迪普继续弹奏着。 三个人继续唱歌，杰克和安妮继续敲鼓。

“MY CREW AND I SURE ENJOYED YOUR VISIT!” the pirate ghost captain shouted back to Dipper and the others. “BE SURE TO COME BACK! SAME TIME NEXT YEAR!” “我和我的船员们真的很喜欢你们的来访！”海盗幽灵船长对着迪普和其他人喊道。 “一定要再来！ 明年同一时间见！”

The ghost of Jean Lafitte turned and waved his arms in the air again. “COME ON, BOYS! PAPA’ S DOIN’ THE HEEBIE-JEEBIES DANCE!” Then, doing the Heebie-Jeebies dance, all the pirate ghosts danced away into the dark New Orleans night. 让·拉菲特的幽灵转过身，再次在空中挥舞着双臂。 “来吧，伙计们！ 爸爸正在跳希比吉比舞！”然后，他们跳起了希比吉比舞，所有的海盗幽灵都跳着舞消失在新奥尔良的黑夜中。
