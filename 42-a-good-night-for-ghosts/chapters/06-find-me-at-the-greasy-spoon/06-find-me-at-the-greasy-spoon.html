6. Find Me at the Greasy Spoon 6. 在油腻的勺子餐厅见我

Streetlamps were coming on as Jack, Annie, and Dipper walked away from Jackson Square. When they came to Bourbon Street, vendors walked the sidewalks, calling out, “Ice cream!” “Lemon pie!” “Ham biscuits!” 杰克、安妮和迪普走开时，街灯亮了起来。 当他们来到波旁街时，商贩们在人行道上走来走去，喊着：“冰淇淋！”“柠檬派！”“火腿面包！”

“Hmm. Sounds good,” said Annie. “There seems to be lots of good food in New Orleans.” “嗯。 听起来不错，”安妮说。 “看起来新奥尔良有很多好吃的东西。”

“Best in the world!” said Dipper. “世上最好听的！”迪普说。

People were sitting outside dance halls and restaurants, laughing and talking, eating and drinking. Outside and inside, musicians were blaring away on their instruments. 人们坐在舞厅和餐厅外面，笑着、聊天着，吃着、喝着。 里面和外面，音乐家们都在乐器上大声演奏着。

“Hey, there’s Dipper! Sing something for us, Dipper!” the ice cream lady yelled. “嘿，是迪普！ 迪普，给我们唱一首歌吧！”冰淇淋小贩喊道。

Dipper waved and kept going. 迪普挥了挥手，继续往前走。

“Hey, girl, can you play that trumpet?” a man called to Annie. “嘿，姑娘，你能吹那个小号吗？”一个男人对安妮喊道。

“Not till the time’s right!” Annie shouted. “等时机到了再说！”安妮喊道。

“When’s that?” the man said. “什么时候？”那个男人说。

“She’ll know it when she feels it!” Dipper shouted. “她会感觉到的时候就知道了！”迪普喊道。

At the end of the block, under a streetlamp, a trio was singing in harmony. It was Little Mac, Happy, and Big Nose Sidney. 在街区的尽头，路灯下，三个人正在一起唱歌。 他们是小麦克、快乐和大鼻子西德尼。

“Look, Dipper, it’s your friends again,” said Annie. “看，迪普，是你的好朋友又来了，”安妮说。

“I see ’em,” said Dipper. But he ignored the three boys and crossed the street. “我看到了，”迪普说。 但他没理会这三个男孩，穿过马路走了。

Dipper led Jack and Annie down a narrow alley to the back of a shabby, run-down building. Good cooking smells came from inside. “Y’all wait for me outside this greasy spoon,” he said, and he slipped through the back door. 迪普带着杰克和安妮走进一条狭窄的小巷，来到一栋破旧不堪的建筑物后面。 从里面飘出了美味的饭菜香味。 “你们在这儿等着，我进去看看这家油腻的餐厅，”他说，然后从后门溜了进去。

“What’s a greasy spoon? ” asked Annie. “什么是油腻的餐厅？”安妮问。

“Smells like it must be a restaurant,” said Jack. “闻起来应该是一家餐馆，”杰克说。

Annie put down the trumpet. Jack put down his bag. They sat on the back steps of the greasy spoon. While they waited in the muggy twilight for Dipper, Jack wiped his forehead. He was starving and ached all over. 安妮放下喇叭。 杰克放下他的包。 他们坐在油腻汤勺店的后台阶上。 他们在闷热的黄昏里等着迪珀时，杰克擦了擦额头。 他饿得前胸贴后背，全身都疼。

Soon Dipper pushed open the back door with his foot. He was carrying a big bowl and a tall glass. “I got us some gumbo stew and some lemonade to share!” he said. “Talk about good!” 很快，迪珀用脚踢开了后门。 他拿着一个大碗和一个高高的玻璃杯。 “我给大家带了点 gumbo 炖菜和柠檬水来分享！”他说。 “太棒了！”

“Oh, man, thanks,” breathed Jack. “哦，天啊，谢谢你，”杰克喘着气说。

Dipper sat between Jack and Annie on the steps. He pulled spoons out of his pocket for each of them. “Dig in, y’all!” he said. 迪普坐在杰克和安妮之间，坐在台阶上。 他从口袋里拿出勺子，分给每个人。 “开始吃吧，你们俩！”，他说。

Together the three of them tackled the gumbo stew. They all ate their fill of spicy chicken, ham, tomatoes, okra, onions, and rice. When the bowl was empty, they shared the tall glass of lemonade. Then they sat back on the steps and heaved big sighs. Jack felt stuffed and happy. 他们三个人一起吃起了浓汤。 他们尽情地吃着香辣的鸡肉、火腿、番茄、秋葵、洋葱和米饭。 当碗空了的时候，他们一起分享了一杯冰柠檬水。 然后他们又坐回台阶上，深深地叹了口气。 杰克感觉饱饱的，非常开心。

“Mighty fine,” breathed Dipper. “非常美味，”迪普说道。

“Mighty fine,” echoed Jack. “非常美味，”杰克附和道。

“Mighty fine,” said Annie. “非常棒，”安妮说。

“Nothing tastes as good as gumbo after a hard day’s work,” said Dipper. He stood up. “Well, I have to leave y’all now. Thanks a million for your help today. And don’t forget to thank Teddy and Kathleen for sending y’all to find me.” Before Jack and Annie could stop him, Dipper climbed down the steps and vanished into the dark. “辛苦工作一天后，没有什么比吃一口 gumbo 更美味了，”迪普说。他站了起来。 “好吧，我现在得离开你们了。 今天非常感谢你们的帮助。 别忘了感谢泰迪和凯瑟琳，是他们让你们来找我的。”就在杰克和安妮还没来得及阻止他时，迪普爬下台阶，消失在黑暗中。

“Dipper?” called Jack. “迪普？”杰克喊道。

There was no answer. 没有回应。

“He’s gone again!” said Annie. “他又不见了！”安妮说。

“And we totally failed in our mission for Merlin,” said Jack. “We didn’t help Dipper get on the right path to give his gifts to the world.” “我们完全没能完成梅林交给我们的任务，”杰克说。 “我们没能帮助迪普走上正确的道路，把他的礼物送给世界。”

“I know. We have to find him. Come on,” said Annie. She hopped up and headed after Dipper. “我知道。” 我们必须找到他。 快点，”安妮说。 她跳起来，跟在迪普后面。

“Wait, the trumpet!” said Jack. He grabbed the magic trumpet and his bag and followed her. “等等，号角！”杰克说。 他抓起魔法号角和自己的包，然后跟了上去。

When Jack left the steps, it was too dark for him to see where Annie had gone. Thunder rumbled in the distance. The air felt heavy and thick as if the storm were about to break at any moment. 当杰克离开台阶时，天太黑了，他看不清安妮去了哪里。 远处传来雷声。 空气感觉又重又闷，仿佛暴风雨随时都会来临。

“Annie!” Jack called. “安妮！”杰克喊道。

“Here!” Annie called back from the front of the restaurant. Jack joined her. Together they peeked through a window that looked into a large kitchen. Dipper was alone, washing a mountain of dishes. “在这里！”安妮从餐厅前面喊道。杰克走到她身边。 他们一起透过一扇窗户，望进一个大厨房里。 迪普一个人在那儿，洗着一大堆盘子。

“Why’s he doing that?” whispered Jack. “他为什么在做这个？”杰克小声问。

“Hey Dipper!” said Annie. “嘿，迪普！”安妮说道。

Dipper turned and smiled. “You caught me,” he said. He looked embarrassed. 迪普转过身，微笑着。 “被你们发现了，”他说。 他看起来有点不好意思。

“Why are you washing dishes?” asked Annie. “你为什么在洗盘子？”安妮问。

Dipper shrugged. “Got to pay for our dinner somehow,” he said. 迪普耸了耸肩。 “得想办法付晚餐的钱，”他说。

“We’ll help you,” said Annie. “我们会帮你的，”安妮说。

“We love washing dishes!” said Jack. “我们喜欢洗碗！”，杰克说。

Dipper laughed. “Then come on in, potato heads,” he said. “I could use some help.” 迪普笑了。 “那进来吧，土豆脑袋们，”他说。 “我需要一些帮助。”

Jack and Annie slipped through a side door into the hot, steamy kitchen. 杰克和安妮从侧门溜进又热又潮湿的厨房。

Jack put down his bag and the trumpet. He and Annie picked up dirty plates from the counter. They began scraping leftovers into a garbage pail. They scraped fish heads, oyster shells, crab legs, shrimp tails, chicken bones, grease, and gravy off dozens of plates. 杰克放下他的包和喇叭。 他和安妮从柜台上的脏盘子捡起来。 他们开始把剩菜刮进垃圾桶里。 他们把鱼头、牡蛎壳、螃蟹腿、虾尾、鸡肉骨头、油渍和肉汁从几十个盘子上刮下来。

The work was messy and smelly. But Jack and Annie worked hard to keep up with Dipper. Every time he took a plate from them, he smiled and said, “Thank you.” He never frowned or complained or even seemed tired. 这项工作又脏又臭。 但杰克和安妮努力工作，跟上迪普的节奏。 每次他从他们手里拿走一个盘子，他都会笑着说：‘谢谢。’他从不皱眉，也不抱怨，甚至看起来一点都不累。

“Dipper,” said Annie while they worked, “your life seems really hard. How do you stay so cheerful?” “迪普，”安妮一边工作一边说，“你的生活看起来真的很艰难。 你怎么能一直这么开心呢？

“Why not? It’s more fun to be cheerful than sad,” he said. “为什么不呢？ 开心比难过更有趣，”他说。

“Don’t you ever feel like getting mad or complaining about stuff?” said Jack. “你从来没觉得生气或者想抱怨吗？”杰克问。

“Sure I do, I’m human,” said Dipper. “I feel all kinds of things. All day long I feel things. You could say I have a rich life. I might not get to have everything. But I get to feel everything!” He laughed. “当然有啊，我也是人，”迪普说。 “我感觉各种各样的情绪。 我整天都在感受各种事情。 你可以说我过着丰富多彩的生活。 我可能得不到所有东西。 但我能感受到一切！”他笑了。

“I’m the same way,” said Annie. “我也是这样，”安妮说。

“I thought so,” said Dipper. “That’s why I sure would like to hear you play that trumpet sometime.” “我猜是这样，”迪普说。 “这就是为什么我真的很想听你 someday 吹喇叭。”

“Maybe you will,” said Annie. “也许你会的，”安妮说。

By the time they finished washing dishes, rain was falling outside. 等他们洗完碗时，外面开始下雨了。

“Where to now?” said Jack. “现在去哪儿？”杰克说。

“One more treat for y’all,” said Dipper. “Let’s leave this greasy spoon and head back down to the river. To the River Café for dessert! Come on.” “再给你们一个惊喜，”迪普说。 “我们还是离开这家油腻的餐厅，回河边去吧。 去河边咖啡馆吃甜点！快点。”

“We better not,” said Jack. “The waiters there don’t like us.” “我们最好别去，”杰克说。 “那里的服务员不喜欢我们。”

“Don’t worry about them,” said Dipper. “You’re with me now.” “别担心他们，”迪普说。 “现在你和我是一伙的。”

Jack and Annie followed Dipper outside into the rain. The wind was blowing harder now. “Uh-oh,” said Dipper. “Here comes the storm! Let’s hurry!” 杰克和安妮跟着迪普走出门，走进雨中。 现在风刮得更猛了。 “糟了，”迪普说。 “暴风雨来了！快点！”

Thunder cracked and rain began to pour down. The three of them got soaked as they hurried through the alley back to Bourbon Street. 雷声轰响，雨开始倾盆而下。 他们三人匆忙穿过小巷，回到波本街时全身都湿透了。

The street was empty now. Partygoers and street musicians had fled from the storm. The restaurants and cafés had taken their chairs and tables inside. Lightning lit the sky, and thunder shook the ground. The wind was blowing hard, picking up sticks and leaves and trash. 现在街上空无一人。 参加派对的人和街头音乐家都逃走了。 餐馆和咖啡馆把椅子和桌子都搬进了店里。 闪电照亮了天空，雷声震动了地面。 风刮得很大，卷起了树枝、树叶和垃圾。

“We have to find cover!” said Dipper. “Run!” He and Jack and Annie bowed their heads against the downpour and ran up Bourbon Street. “我们必须找个地方躲起来！”迪普说。 “跑！”他和杰克、安妮低下头，顶着倾盆大雨，沿着波旁街往上游跑。

“Dipper! Over here, man!” someone yelled. It was Little Mack. “迪普！ “这边，伙计！”有人喊道。 是小马克。

Little Mack, Happy, and Big Nose Sidney were waving to Dipper from the doorway of a dark building on a corner. Dipper, Jack, and Annie ran across the street through the pounding rain. 小马克、哈皮和大鼻子西迪从街角一栋黑暗建筑的门口朝迪普波挥手。 迪普、杰克和安妮在倾盆大雨中跑过街道。

“Get over here, out of the street!” shouted Little Mack. “Before you get hit by lightning!” “快过来，别站在街上！”小马克喊道。 “不然会被闪电击中！”

“Thanks, fellas!” said Dipper. “谢谢你们，伙计们！”迪普说。

Soaking wet, Jack, Annie, Dipper, Little Mack, Happy, and Big Nose Sidney all crowded together just inside the dark building, looking out at the storm. 浑身湿透的迪普、杰克、安妮、小马克、哈皮和大鼻子西德尼全都挤在黑暗建筑里，看着外面的暴风雨。

“Who lives here?” asked Dipper. “这里住着谁？”迪普问。

“Nobody. It’s been empty for years,” said Happy. “没人住。 这里好几年都没人住了，”哈皮说。

“Used to be a blacksmith shop,” said Little Mack. “以前是家铁匠铺，”小麦克说。

“Lafitte’s Blacksmith Shop?” said Jack. He immediately stepped out of the shop and stood under the eaves. “是拉菲特的铁匠铺？”杰克问。 他立刻从店里走出来，站在屋檐下。

“Yeah, what’s wrong with that?” asked Little Mack. “怎么了？这有什么不对吗？”小麦克问。

“We read this place is haunted!” said Annie. “我们听说这里闹鬼！”安妮说。

“Y’all believe in ghosts?” asked Little Mack. “你们相信鬼吗？”小麦克问。

“No, not really,” said Jack. “不，其实我们不信，”杰克说。

“But I thought you said they gave you the heebie—” started Dipper. “但是我说你刚才说他们让你吓出一身冷汗——”迪普开始说。

“No, no, I was kidding,” Jack said quickly. He didn’t want Dipper’s friends to know he was afraid of ghosts. “不，不，我是在开玩笑，”杰克赶紧说道。 他不想让迪普的朋友知道他害怕鬼魂。

Lightning split the sky again. Another crash of thunder shattered the night. The wind blew so hard that shingles blew off the roof across the street and crashed to the sidewalk. 闪电再次劈开了天空。 又一声雷声震碎了夜晚的宁静。 风刮得如此猛烈，街对面屋顶上的瓦片被吹了下来，摔在了人行道上。

“Whoa! Come inside, man, we gotta close the door,” Dipper said to Jack. “哇！ 进来吧，伙计，我们必须关上门，”迪普对杰克说。

“Hold on,” said Little Mack. “We have to go.” “等等，”小马克说。 “我们必须走。”

“We do?” asked Happy. “我们真的要走吗？”哈皮问。

Little Mack whispered something to Happy and Big Nose Sidney. 小麦克悄悄对哈皮和大鼻子西德尼说了些什么。

“Oh, yeah, he’s right,” said Happy. “We have to leave. We’ll see y’all later.” “哦，对，他说得对，”哈皮说。 “我们必须离开了。 我们待会儿再见面。”

“Y’all are scared to stay here, aren’t you?” said Dipper. “你们是不是害怕待在这里啊？”迪普说。

“No, man. We forgot we have an important gig to play,” said Big Nose Sidney. “没有，伙计。 我们忘了我们还有一个重要的演出要参加，”大鼻子西德尼说。

“Oh. Suddenly y’all have got an important gig. I see…,” said Dipper. 突然间，你们就有了一个重要的演出。 我明白了……”迪普说。

“Yeah, we’ll have to try to make it through the storm. See y’all! Come on, fellas!” said Little Mack. “对，我们必须想办法穿过这场风暴。待会儿见！” “快点，伙计们！”小马克说。

The three boys hurried out of the blacksmith shop and turned the corner. 三个男孩匆匆从铁匠铺里跑出来，转过街角。

“They left ’cause they’re scaredy-cats,” said Dipper, chuckling. “他们离开了，因为他们是胆小鬼，”迪普笑着说。

“Yeah,” said Jack, “scaredy-cats.” “对，”杰克说，“胆小鬼。”

Thunder cracked the sky again, the loudest crack so far. It seemed to shake the whole block. Roof shingles flew through the air. 雷声再次撕裂天空，这是迄今为止最响的一声。 听起来好像整个街区都在震动。 屋顶的瓦片在空中飞舞。

“Come back inside!” said Dipper. “It’s dangerous out there.” “快回去里面！”迪普喊道。 “外面很危险。”

Jack took a deep breath and stepped back into Lafitte’s Blacksmith Shop. 杰克深吸一口气，走进了拉菲特铁匠铺。
