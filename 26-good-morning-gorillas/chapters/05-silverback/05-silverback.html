5. Silverback 5. 银背

Jack felt something tugging on his sleeve. He opened his eyes. 杰克觉得有什么东西在拽他的袖子。 他睁开眼睛。

Bu-bu. The small gorilla was staring at him in the dawn light. 一只大猩猩抱住布布，好像在安慰她。 小猩猩在黎明的光线里盯着他看。

Jack stood up. His arms and legs felt stiff and achy. His wet clothes stuck to his skin. 杰克站起来。 他的胳膊和腿又僵又疼。 湿衣服贴在皮肤上。

He looked around the cloud forest. Misty sunlight shined through the tree branches. 他环顾云雾林。 雾蒙蒙的阳光透过树枝照进来。

“Where’s Annie?” he asked the small gorilla. “安妮在哪儿？”他问小猩猩。

Bu-bu waved her arms. Then she bounded off between the trees. Jack pulled on his pack and followed. 布布挥舞着手臂。 然后她蹦跳着跑进树林里。 杰克背上背包跟上去。

As the small gorilla led him through the cloud forest, her head bobbed above the leafy plants. Finally, she stopped before a row of shrubs. 小猩猩带着他穿过云雾林时，她的脑袋在茂密的植物上方一颠一颠的。 最后，她在一排灌木前停下。

Jack took a few steps forward and peered over the shrubs. 杰克往前走了几步，从灌木上方往外看。

“Oh, man,” he whispered. “哦，天哪，”他小声说。

Large dark figures were sleeping in an open, grassy area— gorillas! There were at least ten of them. Some slept on their backs. Some slept on their bellies. 一些又大又黑的身影正睡在一片开阔的草地上——大猩猩！ 至少有十只。 有的仰面睡着。 有的趴着睡觉。

The gorillas were all sizes. The smallest was a baby sleeping in its mother’s arms. The biggest was a giant with black and silver fur. 大猩猩有大有小。 最小的是一只睡在妈妈怀里的宝宝。 最大的是一只黑银色皮毛的巨猩猩。

Jack pulled the book out of his pack. He found a chapter on gorillas and read: 杰克从背包里抽出书。 他找到关于大猩猩的一章，读道：

Mountain gorillas live together in families. The leader of the family is a large male called a “silverback” because he has silver fur on his back and shoulders. Gorillas do not hunt other animals. They mainly eat the plant growth of the forest. They are known to be shy and gentle giants. 山地大猩猩一起生活在一个家庭里。这个家庭的首领是一只体型较大的雄性，叫做“银背”，因为它的背部和肩膀上有银色的毛发。大猩猩不捕猎其他动物，它们主要吃森林里的植物。它们以害羞和温顺的巨人而闻名。

“Shy and gentle giants,” Jack repeated. That sounded good. “害羞又温柔的巨人，”杰克重复道。 听起来不错。

He peered over the shrubs again. Bu-bu waved at him. She was standing at the far edge of the clearing. She pointed to something in the tall grass. 他又从灌木丛上方探头张望。 布布朝他挥手。 她站在空地远远的那一边。 她指着高草里的什么东西。

Annie was fast asleep in the grass! 安妮在草地上睡得正香！

Jack didn’t know what to do. If he called her name, the gorillas would wake up. He had only one choice. He had to sneak over to her. 杰克不知道该怎么办。 要是喊她的名字，大猩猩们就会醒来。 他只有一个选择。 他得偷偷溜到她身边。

Jack put his book in his pack. He pushed past the shrubs and stepped into the clearing. His heart was pounding. He thought of the words from the book— shy and gentle giants. 杰克把书放进背包。 他拨开灌木，走进空地。 他的心怦怦直跳。 他想起书上的话——害羞而温和的巨人。

As he started toward Annie, he heard a grunt. The giant gorilla with silver fur opened his eyes. When the gorilla saw Jack, he sat up. 他朝安妮走去时，听见一声咕哝。 那只有着银色皮毛的巨猩猩睁开了眼睛。 大猩猩看见杰克时，坐了起来。

Jack stopped in his tracks. 杰克突然停住了脚步。

The gorilla just glared. This giant did not seem shy or gentle at all. 大猩猩只是瞪着他。 这个巨人一点也不像害羞或温和。

Jack saw a stick lying on the ground. He picked it up—just in case. 杰克看见地上有根棍子。 他把它捡起来——以防万一。

Jack’s stick made the gorilla growl. He stood up. He was very tall and very wide. 杰克手里的棍子让大猩猩咆哮起来。他站了起来。 他又高又壮。

Jack dropped his stick. 杰克扔掉棍子。

Bu-bu ran and hid behind a tree. 布布跑去躲到一棵树后面。

The silverback growled again. His long, shaggy arms touched the ground. His fingers curled under. Walking on his knuckles, he stepped toward Jack. 那只银背大猩猩又吼了一声。 他长长的、毛茸茸的胳膊碰到了地面。 他的手指蜷了起来。 他用指关节撑地走着，朝杰克走来。

Jack stepped back. 杰克往后退。

The gorilla stepped forward. 大猩猩往前走。

Jack stepped back again. 杰克又往后退。

The gorilla kept stepping forward. Jack kept stepping back until he had stepped out of the clearing. 大猩猩继续往前走。 杰克一直退到空地外面。

But the silverback kept coming. Jack stumbled back through the brush until he came to a thick wall of plants. 可银背还在跟过来。 杰克跌跌撞撞地往灌木丛后退，一直退到一道密密的植物墙前。

The gorilla kept coming. Jack couldn’t move back anymore. 大猩猩继续跟来。 杰克再也退不回去了。

“Uh … hi,” he said nervously. He held up his hand. “I come in—” “呃……你好，”他紧张地说。 他举起手。 “我是来——”

Before Jack could say “peace,” the giant gorilla went crazy. He hooted and leaped to his feet. 杰克还没说完“和平”，巨猩猩就发狂了。 他吼叫着跳起来。

Jack crouched down in a panic. 杰克惊慌地蹲了下去。

The gorilla kept hooting. He grabbed a tree limb. He shook it wildly. He ripped leaves from branches. 那只大猩猩不停地吼叫。 他抓住一根树枝。 他猛烈地摇晃它。 他把叶子从枝上撕下来。

He gnashed his teeth. He cupped his hands. He beat his chest. 他咬牙切齿。 他把手掌收成杯状。 他捶打自己的胸口。

WRAAGH! he roared. WRAAGH! 哇啊！他吼道。 哇啊！

The gorilla dropped on all fours. He charged back and forth past Jack. Then he threw himself on his belly. He began bashing the ground with his palms. He bashed and bashed and bashed. 那只大猩猩四肢着地。 他在杰克身边来回冲撞。 接着他扑通趴在地上。 他开始用掌心拍打地面。 他一下又一下地猛砸着。

Jack scrambled on his hands and knees over to a tree. He hid behind the trunk, hugging his head. 杰克手脚并用地爬到一棵树旁。 他躲到树干后面，抱着头。

He waited for the maniac gorilla to find him and tear him to pieces. 他等着那只发狂的大猩猩找到他，把他撕成碎片。
