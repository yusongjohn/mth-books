6. Good Morning, Gorillas 6. 早上好，大猩猩

The pounding ended. There was silence … a long silence. 捶打声停了。 一片寂静……很长的寂静。

Jack opened his eyes. He peeked around the tree. The silverback was sitting on the ground. His lips were curved in a smile. He looked pleased with himself. 杰克睁开眼睛。 他从树后偷看。 银背正坐在地上。 他的嘴唇弯成了微笑。 他看上去对自己很满意。

Was his whole act a fake? Jack wondered. 他那一整套举动都是装的吗？杰克心想。

Jack didn’t know whether to be scared or to laugh. The only thing he did know was he still had to get to Annie! 杰克不知道该害怕还是该笑。 他唯一知道的是，他还得去找安妮！

Jack pulled out the research book. He found the gorilla chapter again. He read: 杰克抽出资料书。 他又找到大猩猩那一章。他读道：

To safely get close to gorillas in the wild, it’s wise to act like a gorilla yourself. Crouch down and rest on your knuckles like a gorilla. Keep your head down and act friendly! 要想安全地接近野生大猩猩，最好自己也像大猩猩一样行动。蹲下来，像大猩猩一样用手掌支撑身体。低下头，表现得友好一些！

Jack packed up his research book. He put his pack on his back. Then he went down on his knees. 杰克收起资料书。 他背上背包。 接着他跪了下去。

Jack took a deep breath. He smiled a friendly smile. Pressing down on his knuckles, he moved out from behind the tree. His fingers hurt as he walked on them. 杰克深深吸了一口气。 他露出一个友好的微笑。 他用指关节向下撑着，从树后走了出来。 他用手走路时，手指很疼。

The silverback grunted. 银背咕哝了一声。

Jack didn’t look up. He kept smiling a friendly smile as he crawled through the brush toward the clearing. 杰克没有抬头。 他一边保持友好的微笑，一边爬过灌木朝空地爬去。

When he got to the edge of the clearing, he glanced back. The giant gorilla was following him. He was frowning, but he didn’t seem about to attack. 到了空地边缘，他回头看了一眼。 巨猩猩正跟着他。 他皱着眉，可看起来不像马上要进攻。

Jack kept going. He moved into the clearing. Then he stopped. 杰克继续往前。 他爬进空地。然后停下来。

More gorillas were waking up. A large gorilla hugged Bu-bu as if to comfort her. 更多大猩猩正在醒来。 一只大猩猩抱住布布，好像在安慰她。

When Bu-bu saw Jack, she screeched joyfully. 布布看见杰克时，高兴地尖叫起来。

All the other gorillas turned to look at him. They made nervous sounds. 其他大猩猩都转过来看他。 它们发出紧张的声音。

Jack’s heart pounded. But he just smiled his friendly smile and kept crawling. He crawled around the gorillas and over to Annie. 杰克的心怦怦跳。 可他只是保持着友好的微笑，继续爬。 他绕过大猩猩们，爬到安妮身边。

“Wake up!” he said, shaking her. “醒醒！”他一边摇她一边说。

Annie yawned, then opened her eyes. 安妮打了个哈欠，然后睁开眼睛。

“Oh, hi!” she said. “哦，嗨！”她说。

“Are you okay?” asked Jack. “你没事吧？”杰克问。

“Sure,” she said. She sat up and looked around. She gasped. “当然，”她说。 她坐起来四处看。她倒抽一口气。

The gorillas were staring at Jack and Annie with bright, darting eyes. The silverback stared the hardest. 大猩猩们正用亮晶晶、滴溜溜转的眼睛盯着杰克和安妮。 银背大猩猩盯得最紧。

“Oh, wow!” said Annie. A joyful smile crossed her face. “Good morning, gorillas!” “哦，哇！”安妮说。 她脸上浮现出开心的笑容。 “早上好，大猩猩们！”
