2. Cloud Forest 2. 云雾林

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开眼睛。

“I can’t tell what kind of rain forest this is,” said Annie. She stared out the window. “我看不出这是哪种雨林，”安妮说。 她盯着窗外。

Jack looked out, too. It seemed to be daytime, but he couldn’t see much of anything. The quiet forest was covered with fog. 杰克也向外望去。 好像已经是白天了，可他几乎什么也看不见。 安静的森林笼罩在雾里。

Jack opened their research book and read: 杰克打开资料书，读道：

The misty rain forest in the mountains of central Africa is called a “cloud forest.” 中非山区的雾雨森林叫做“云森林”。

“Oh, I get it,” said Annie. “We’re up so high, it’s like we’re in a cloud.” “哦，我懂了，”安妮说。 “我们在这么高的地方，好像置身云中。”

“Cool,” said Jack. He pulled out his notebook and wrote:



 “酷，”杰克说。 他拿出笔记本写道：

图中文字：cloud forest—rain forest high

up in mountains 图中文字：云林——高高山上长满树的雨林

Then he read more: 然后他又往下读：

The African cloud forest is home to many animals, including elephants, water buffaloes, black leopards …  非洲云林是许多动物的家园，包括大象、水牛、黑豹等等。

Jack looked up. 杰克抬起头。

“Black leopards?” he said. “黑豹？”他说。

“Don’t worry,” said Annie. “别担心，”安妮说。

Jack cleared his throat and kept reading: 杰克清了清嗓子，继续念道：……羚羊、野猪和大猩猩。

 … antelopes, wild hogs, and gorillas. ……羚羊、野猪和大猩猩。

“Gorillas?” said Annie. “大猩猩？”安妮说。

“Don’t worry,” said Jack. “别担心，”杰克说。

“I’m not worried. I love gorillas,” said Annie. “They’re totally great!” “我没担心。 我爱大猩猩，”安妮说。 “它们太棒了！”

“I don’t know about that,” said Jack. He pictured huge apes pounding their chests. “I’d like to study them, though. Write down their habits and behavior, just like a real scientist.” “这可说不准，”杰克说。 他想象着巨大的猿猴在捶打胸口。 “不过我想研究它们。 把它们的习惯和行为写下来，就像真正的科学家一样。”

“Whatever,” said Annie. “Let’s just go. This’ll be a fun adventure!” She took off down the ladder. “随便吧，”安妮说。 “我们走吧。 这次冒险会很好玩！”她顺着梯子爬下去。

Jack threw his notebook, the research book, and the flashlight into his pack. He hooked the umbrella over his arm. Then he followed Annie. 杰克把笔记本、资料书和手电筒扔进背包。 他把伞挂在胳膊上。 然后跟着安妮下去。

When they stepped onto the ground, Jack could see better. The fog had turned into a fine mist. 脚一着地，杰克就看得更清楚了。 雾变成了细细的水汽。

Jack and Annie started through the cloud forest. They walked around huge trees draped with moss. They pushed past tall shrubs and leafy plants. 杰克和安妮开始穿过云雾林。 他们绕过挂满苔藓的大树。 他们拨开高大的灌木和多叶的植物往前走。

“Wow, look at that tree,” said Annie. “哇，看那棵树，”安妮说。

She pointed to a fat tree. It had wide lower limbs padded with thick cushions of moss. 她指着一棵粗壮的树。 它宽阔的下层枝干上垫着厚厚的苔藓。

“It looks like a piece of furniture,” said Annie, “like an armchair.” “它看起来像一件家具，”安妮说，“像一把扶手椅。”

“Yeah,” said Jack. “I better draw it.” “嗯，”杰克说。 “我最好把它画下来。”

He put the umbrella on the ground. He pulled the flashlight out of his pack and put it next to the umbrella. Then he took out his notebook and pencil. 他把伞放在地上。 他从背包里抽出手电筒，放在伞旁边。 然后拿出笔记本和铅笔。

As Annie walked ahead, Jack started to draw a simple picture of the fat tree. 安妮往前走时，杰克开始简单地画那棵粗树。

“Hey, Jack,” Annie called in a whispery voice. “Come here. Quick!” “嘿，杰克，”安妮用耳语般的声音喊道。 “过来。快点！”

Jack grabbed his pack. He moved around the tree and caught up with Annie. 杰克抓起背包。 他绕过树，追上安妮。

“Listen,” she said. “听，”她说。

Jack heard branches snap. 杰克听见树枝折断的声音。

Crack! 杰克哈哈大笑起来。

A leopard? he wondered. 豹子？他想。

Crack! Crack! 咔嚓！咔嚓！

Jack nervously looked around the forest. 杰克紧张地朝森林四处张望。

“Maybe we should go back up to the tree house,” he said. “We could read a little more and learn a little more.” “也许我们该回树屋去，”他说。 “我们可以再多读一点，再多了解一点。”

Annie didn’t answer. Jack turned to her. She was grinning from ear to ear as she stared into the bushes. Jack followed her gaze. 安妮没回答。 杰克转向她。 她盯着灌木丛，咧着嘴笑得合不拢嘴。 杰克顺着她的目光看去。

A dark, shaggy little head was peeking out from a cluster of leaves. 一个黑黑的、毛茸茸的小脑袋正从一丛叶子里探出来。

“Bu, bu?” a small gorilla asked. “布，布？”一只小猩猩问。
