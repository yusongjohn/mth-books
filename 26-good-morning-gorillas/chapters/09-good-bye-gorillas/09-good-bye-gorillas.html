9. Good-bye, Gorillas 9. 再见，大猩猩

Jack couldn’t stop smiling. He had scared off a leopard! 杰克忍不住一直笑。 他把一只豹子吓跑了！

Bu-bu and Ho-ho stared at Jack with awe. Annie looked at him with awe, too. 布布和嚯嚯敬畏地盯着杰克。 安妮也敬畏地看着他。

“When did you learn to do that?” she asked. “你什么时候学会那样的？”她问。

Before Jack could answer, he heard a rustling in the brush. Then Big Guy stepped out from the shrubs. 杰克还没来得及回答，就听见灌木丛里传来沙沙声。 接着大家伙从灌木里走出来。

The giant gorilla walked silently over to Annie. He took Little Guy from her and put the baby on his back. Then he touched Annie’s cheek gently. 巨猩猩静静地走向安妮。 他从小伙儿怀里接过宝宝，放在自己背上。 接着他轻轻碰了碰安妮的脸颊。

Annie grinned at him. 安妮朝他咧嘴笑。

Bu-bu and Ho-ho ran to Big Guy and clung to his legs. 布布和嚯嚯跑到大家伙身边，抱住他的腿。

The giant gorilla barked at the small ones, directing them to come with him. 巨猩猩冲小猩猩们吠了一声，示意他们跟他走。

As he walked past Jack, Big Guy stopped. 从杰克身边走过时，大家伙停了下来。

Huh-huh-huh, he said in a low voice. He reached out toward Jack. 呵——呵——呵，他低声说。 他朝杰克伸出手。

Jack ducked. 杰克躲开了。

But the silverback patted him on the head. Then he and the small gorillas moved out of sight. 可银背拍了拍他的头。 然后他和小猩猩们走出了视线。

Jack felt as if the top of his head were glowing. 杰克觉得自己头顶好像在发光。

“Wow,” he whispered. “Did you see what he just did?” “哇，”他小声说。 “你看见他刚才做了什么吗？”

“Yeah,” said Annie. “He must have watched the show you put on. He was proud of you.” “看见了，”安妮说。 “他一定看了你上演的那一出。 他以你为傲。”

“Well, he was proud of you, too,” Jack said modestly. “嗯，他也以你为傲，”杰克谦虚地说。

Annie nodded, smiling. “I guess it’s time to leave now,” she said. 安妮笑着点了点头。 “我想现在该走了，”她说。

“Leave?” said Jack. “走？”杰克说。

“We have to say good-bye now,” said Annie. “我们现在得说再见了，”安妮说。

“Good-bye?” said Jack. He didn’t want to say good-bye to the gorillas. He loved them. They were totally great. “再见？”杰克说。 他不想和大猩猩们说再见。他爱他们。 他们太棒了。

“Yeah,” Annie said softly. “Come on.” “是的，”安妮轻声说。 “走吧。”

She led the way back through the shrubs, around the trees, to the clearing. 她领着路，穿过灌木，绕过树木，回到空地。

They found all the gorillas awake. Some were stretching and yawning. Others were munching on grass or leaves. 他们发现所有大猩猩都醒了。 有的在伸懒腰，打着哈欠。 其他的在大口吃草或树叶。

The baby was back in his mother’s arms. Bu-bu and Ho-ho were chattering away to their moms. 宝宝又回到了妈妈怀里。 布布和呵呵正跟各自的妈妈不停地叽叽喳喳。

They’re probably telling them what I did, Jack thought. 他们大概在讲我干的事，杰克想。

He and Annie walked over to Big Guy and stood in front of him. The other gorillas gathered around. 他和安妮走到大家伙面前，站在他前面。 其他大猩猩围了过来。

“We have to go now,” Annie said to all of them. “我们现在得走了，”安妮对大家说。

“We have to say good-bye,” said Jack. “我们得说再见了，”杰克说。

“Thanks for letting us be a part of your family,” said Annie. “谢谢你们让我们成为你们家的一员，”安妮说。

She and Jack held up their hands and waved. The gorillas looked sad. They murmured soft sounds. 她和杰克举起手挥了挥。 大猩猩们看起来很伤心。 他们发出轻柔的声音。

Big Guy lifted his hand in the air as if he were about to wave. But instead, the silverback raised his thumb, his index finger, and his little finger. 大家伙把手举到空中，好像要挥手。 可银背却竖起拇指、食指和小指。

I love you, the giant gorilla signed. 我爱你，巨猩猩打着手势。

Jack couldn’t believe his eyes. 杰克简直不敢相信自己的眼睛。

Annie signed back, I love you. 安妮也打着手势回应：我爱你。

Jack signed, too. 杰克也打了手语。

The silverback stared at them for a long moment with a gentle, shy look. Then he turned away and gave a short bark to his family. 银背用温和、害羞的眼神盯着他们看了很久。 然后他转过身，朝家人短促地吠了一声。

All the gorillas lined up behind him. The baby’s mother held her baby close. Ho-ho rode piggyback on his mom. Bu-bu held her mom’s hand. 所有大猩猩在他身后排起队来。 宝宝的妈妈把宝宝抱得紧紧的。 呵呵趴在妈妈背上。 布布拉着妈妈的手。

The silverback started away from the clearing. The others followed. 银背开始离开空地。 其他大猩猩跟在后面。

Bu-bu was the only one who looked back. She screeched and waved at Jack and Annie. Then she walked away, out of sight. 只有布布回头看了看。 她尖叫着朝杰克和安妮挥手。 然后她走开了，消失在视线里。

Jack couldn’t talk. His heart was too full. He took a few steps in the direction of the gorillas. 杰克说不出话来。 他心里太满了。 他朝大猩猩们走的方向迈了几步。

“Hey—” Annie said softly. “You’re going the wrong way.” “嘿——”安妮轻声说。 “你走反方向了。”

Jack looked back at her. 杰克回头看她。

“The tree house is over there,” she said. She pointed in the opposite direction—at the tree house peeking out from the fog. “树屋在那边，”她说。 她指着相反的方向——雾中隐约露出的树屋。

Jack sighed. Then he turned and started to follow her out of the clearing. 杰克叹了口气。 然后他转身，跟着她走出空地。

“Oh, don’t forget this,” said Annie. “哦，别忘了这个，”安妮说。

She leaned over and picked up Jack’s backpack from the grass. She handed it to him. 她俯下身，从草地上捡起杰克的背包。 她把它递给他。

“Thanks,” he said. “谢谢，”他说。

They kept walking. 他们继续走。

“And don’t forget this,” said Annie. She picked up Jack’s jacket from under a tree. She handed it to him. “还有这个，”安妮说。 她从树下捡起杰克的外套。 她把它递给他。

“Thanks,” said Jack. He tied his jacket around his waist. They kept walking. “谢谢，”杰克说。 他把外套系在腰上。 他们继续走。

“And don’t forget these,” said Annie. She pointed to the flashlight and umbrella. They lay on the grass under the wide, mossy limbs of a fat tree. “还有这些，”安妮说。 她指着手电筒和伞。 它们躺在一棵粗壮、枝干宽阔、长满苔藓的树下的草地上。

Annie picked them up and carried them herself. 安妮自己捡起来拿着。

It started to drizzle again just as she and Jack got to the rope ladder. They climbed up into the tree house. 她和杰克刚到绳梯旁，又下起了毛毛雨。 他们爬上树屋。

When they got inside, they looked out the window. Jack hoped to catch one last glimpse of the gorilla family. 进到里面后，他们朝窗外望去。 杰克希望能再看大猩猩一家最后一眼。

But there was nothing to see. A white mist covered the cloud forest. 可什么也看不见。 一层白雾笼罩着云雾林。

Annie picked up the Pennsylvania book. She pointed to a picture of Frog Creek woods. 安妮拿起宾夕法尼亚的书。 她指着蛙溪树林的图片。

“I wish we could go home,” she said. “我希望我们能回家，”她说。

Suddenly, a joyous screech rang out. The happy, wild sound shot through the white mist, through the cool rain, straight into Jack’s heart. 突然，一声欢快的尖叫响起。 那快乐又狂野的声音穿过白雾，穿过凉凉的雨，直直钻进杰克的心里。

He opened his mouth to answer the call of the gorillas. But it was too late. The wind began to blow. 他张开嘴，想回应大猩猩的叫声。 可已经太晚了。 风开始吹起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
