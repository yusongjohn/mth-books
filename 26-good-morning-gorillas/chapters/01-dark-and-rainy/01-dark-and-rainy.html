1. Dark and Rainy 1. 又黑又下雨

Tap-tap-tap. 嗒——嗒——嗒。

Jack sat up in bed. Rain tapped against his window. His clock said 5 A.M. It was still dark outside. 杰克从床上坐起来。 雨点敲打着他的窗户。 他的钟显示凌晨五点。 外面还是黑的。

Annie peeked into his room. 安妮朝他房间里偷看。

“Are you awake?” she whispered. “你醒着吗？”她小声问。

“Yep,” said Jack. “嗯，”杰克说。

“Ready to find some special magic?” she asked. “准备好去找特别的魔法了吗？”她问。

“Maybe we should wait,” said Jack. “It’s so dark and rainy.” “也许该等等，”杰克说。 “外面又黑又下着雨。”

“No waiting,” said Annie. “I’ll get an umbrella. You bring a flashlight. Meet you downstairs.” “不能等，”安妮说。 “我去拿把伞。 你带上手电筒。 楼下见。”

“Okay, okay,” said Jack. “好吧，好吧，”杰克说。

He jumped out of bed. He pulled on his clothes and put on a jacket. Then he grabbed his backpack and flashlight. 他跳下床。 他穿上衣服，又披上一件外套。 然后抓起背包和手电筒。

Jack slipped downstairs and out the front door. Annie stood on the porch in jeans and a T-shirt. The air was chilly and breezy. 杰克悄悄下楼，走出前门。 安妮穿着牛仔裤和T恤站在门廊上。 空气又阴冷，还有微风。

“Don’t you need a sweater or something?” said Jack. “你不需要穿件毛衣什么的吗？”杰克说。

“I’m okay,” she said. “Let’s go.” “我没事，”她说。 “走吧。”

Annie raised the umbrella. Jack turned on the flashlight. They followed a circle of rainy light down their street into the woods. 安妮撑起伞。 杰克打开手电筒。 他们跟着雨夜里的一圈光，沿着街道走进树林。

They headed through the Frog Creek woods. The flashlight lit up the trees—the wet leaves and dark branches. Then it shined on a dangling rope ladder. 他们穿过蛙溪树林。 手电光照亮了树木——湿漉漉的叶子和黑黝黝的树枝。 接着，光亮照到了一架垂下来的绳梯上。

Jack raised the flashlight beam. 杰克把手电筒光束抬高。

“There it is,” he said. “在那儿，”他说。

A circle of light lit the magic tree house. 一圈光照亮了魔法树屋。

“Morgan’s not there,” said Annie. “I can tell.” “摩根不在，”安妮说。 “我能感觉到。”

“Maybe she left us a message,” said Jack. “也许她给我们留了口信，”杰克说。

Jack grabbed the rope ladder and started up. Annie put the umbrella down and followed. When they climbed inside, Jack shined the flashlight around the tree house. 杰克抓住绳梯往上爬。 安妮放下伞，跟了上去。 爬进去后，杰克用手电在树屋里照来照去。

Morgan le Fay wasn’t there. But the scrolls from their trip to old England were. 摩根·勒费不在。 可他们去古老英格兰带回的卷轴还在。

“Here’s proof we found a special magic yesterday,” she said. “这就是我们昨天找到一种特别魔法的证据，”她说。

“Yeah,” said Jack, smiling. “Theater magic.” He had great memories of acting in a play by their friend William Shakespeare. “对，”杰克笑着说。 “戏剧的魔法。”他清楚地记得在朋友威廉·莎士比亚的戏里演戏的事。

“Did Morgan leave us a new secret rhyme?” asked Jack. “摩根给我们留了新的秘密韵诗吗？”杰克问。

He shined the flashlight on a book lying under the window. A piece of paper was sticking out of the book. 他用手电照着窗下一本书。 一张纸从书里露了出来。

“Yes!” said Annie. She picked up the book and pulled out the paper. “有！”安妮说。 她拿起书，抽出那张纸。

Jack shined his light on the paper while Annie read aloud:



 杰克用手电照着纸，安妮大声读道：

Dear Annie and Jack,

 亲爱的安妮和杰克。

Good luck on your second journey to find a special magic. This secret rhyme will guide you:



 祝你们第二次寻找特别魔法的旅程好运。 这首秘密韵诗会指引你们：

To find a special kind of magic 要找到一种特别的魔法

in worlds so far apart, 在相隔那么远的世界里。

speak a special language, 说一种特别的语言。

talk with your hands and heart.



 用手和心去交谈。

Thank you, Morgan 谢谢你们，摩根

“What kind of language does she mean?” Jack asked. “她说的是哪种语言？”杰克问。

“I guess we’ll find out,” said Annie. “Where are we going?” “我想我们会弄明白的，”安妮说。 “我们要去哪儿？”

Jack shined the flashlight on the cover of the book. It showed huge trees partly hidden by mist. The title was: 杰克用手电照着书的封面。 封面上是被薄雾遮住一部分的大树。书名是：

AN AFRICAN RAIN FOREST

“Rain forest?” said Jack. “Good thing we brought our umbrella and flashlight. Remember the rain in the Amazon rain forest? Remember how dark it was under the treetops?” “幸好我们带了雨伞和手电筒。 还记得亚马孙雨林里的雨吗？ 还记得树冠下有多黑吗？”

“Yeah,” said Annie. “Remember the spiders and scary ants?” “记得，”安妮说。 “还记得那些蜘蛛和可怕的蚂蚁吗？”

“Well … ,” Jack said, “not all rain forests have the same bugs.” “嗯……”杰克说，“不是所有雨林的虫子都一样。”

“Remember the river snakes?” said Annie. “And the crocodiles?” “还记得河里的蛇吗？”安妮说。 “还有鳄鱼？”

“Well … ,” said Jack, “not all rain forests have big rivers. There are different kinds of rain forests, you know.” “嗯……”杰克说，“不是所有雨林都有大河。 你知道，雨林也有不同种类。”

“Right,” said Annie. She pointed to the cover of the book. “I wish we could go there.” “对，”安妮说。 她指着书的封面。 “我希望我们能去那儿。”

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来。

“Oh, remember the jaguar?” said Annie. “And the vampire bats?” “哦，还记得那只美洲虎吗？”安妮说。 “还有那些吸血蝙蝠？”

“Wait!” said Jack. “等等！”杰克说。

But it was too late. The wind was blowing harder. The tree house started to spin. 可已经太晚了。 风刮得更猛了。 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
