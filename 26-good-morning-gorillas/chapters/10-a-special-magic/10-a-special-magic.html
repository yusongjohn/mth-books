10. A Special Magic 10. 一种特别的魔法

Tap-tap-tap. 嗒——嗒——嗒。

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开眼睛。

The Frog Creek woods were still dark and rainy. 蛙溪树林还是又黑又下着雨。

“We’re home,” Annie said. “我们到家了，”安妮说。

Jack sighed. 杰克叹了口气。

“I miss them already,” he said. “我已经开始想它们了，”他说。

“Me too,” said Annie. “Did you take a lot of notes on their habits and behavior?” “我也是，”安妮说。 “你有没有记下很多关于它们习惯和行为的笔记？”

Jack shrugged. 杰克耸了耸肩。

“I listed a few things about them,” he said. “But sometimes lists don’t tell you much. You have to love gorillas to really know them.” “我列了几条关于它们的东西，”他说。 “可有时候，清单并不能告诉你很多。 你得爱大猩猩，才能真正了解它们。”

“Yeah. That’s right,” said Annie. 没错，”安妮说。

Jack opened his backpack. He pulled out their research book and put it in the corner. 杰克打开背包。 他抽出资料书，放在角落里。

Then he pulled out the twig that Ho-ho had given him. He smiled as he showed it to Annie. 然后他抽出嚯嚯给他的那根小枝。 他一边拿给安妮看，一边笑了。

“I promised Ho-ho I’d eat this later,” he said. “But I think we should save it for Morgan instead.” “我答应过呵呵，晚点再吃这个，”他说。 “可我觉得我们应该把它留给摩根。”

“Good idea,” said Annie. “It’ll prove to her that we found a special magic.” “好主意，”安妮说。 “这能向她证明，我们找到了一种特别的魔法。”

“Yeah, gorilla magic,” said Jack. “对，大猩猩的魔法，”杰克说。

“The magic of all animals,” said Annie. “所有动物的魔法，”安妮说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “对，”杰克说。

He placed the twig next to the scrolls they’d brought back from old England. 他把小枝放在他们从古老英格兰带回的卷轴旁边。

“Let’s go,” said Annie. She started down. “走吧，”安妮说。她开始往下爬。

Jack pulled on his backpack. He put the flashlight in his pack. Then he grabbed the umbrella and followed Annie. 杰克背上背包。 他把手电筒放进背包。 然后抓起伞，跟着安妮。

They started through the Frog Creek woods. It was still cool and dark and rainy. 他们开始穿过蛙溪树林。 天气还是又凉又黑，下着雨。

Jack didn’t mind, though. He didn’t put on his jacket. He didn’t take out the flashlight. He didn’t put up the umbrella. 不过，杰克并不介意。 他没有穿外套。 他没有拿出手电筒。 他没有撑开伞。

Jack felt as if he weren’t completely human yet. There was still a bit of gorilla left in him. 杰克觉得自己好像还没有完全变回人类。 他身上还留着一点点大猩猩的感觉。

“Ho, ho, ho,” he said in a low voice. “嚯，嚯，嚯，”他低声说。

“Bu, bu,” Annie said back. “布，布，”安妮回应道。

“Huh, huh, huh,” they said together. “呵，呵，呵，”他们一起说。
