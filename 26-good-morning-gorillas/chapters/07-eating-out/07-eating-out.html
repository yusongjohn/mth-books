7. Eating Out 7. 在外面吃饭

Annie kept smiling at the gorillas. “Wow!” she said. “Wow, wow, wow.” 安妮继续对着大猩猩们微笑。 “哇！”她说。 “哇，哇，哇。”

“Didn’t you know you were sleeping next to them?” Jack asked. “你不知道自己就睡在它们旁边吗？”杰克问。

“No!” she said. “When you didn’t come back, Bu-bu led me here. But I couldn’t see anything. It was too dark.” “没有！”她说。 “你没回来时，布布带我来这儿的。 可我什么也看不见。太黑了。”

Just then, Bu-bu left her mother’s arms and bounded over to Annie. She climbed into Annie’s lap and hugged her. 就在这时，布布离开妈妈的怀抱，蹦跳着跑向安妮。 她爬到安妮腿上，抱住了她。

Another small gorilla left his mother and ran over to Annie, too. He was about the size of a two-year-old kid. 另一只小猩猩也离开妈妈，跑向安妮。 他大约有两岁小孩那么大。

“Ho, ho!” he said. He gave Annie a playful poke. “嚯，嚯！”他说。 他顽皮地戳了安妮一下。

“Ho, ho yourself!” said Annie. “Is Ho-ho your name?” “呵呵你自己！”安妮说。 “嚯嚯是你的名字吗？”

She tickled Ho-ho. She tickled Bu-bu, too. The two small gorillas made laughing sounds and fell onto their backs. 她胳肢起呵呵来。 她也挠了挠布布。 两只小猩猩发出笑声，仰面倒下。

The two mother gorillas laughed, too. Huh-huh-huh, they said. 两只母猩猩也笑了。 呵——呵——呵，她们说。

Jack felt a little jealous. He wanted the gorillas to like him as well. But he didn’t know how to join in the fun. So he just sighed and pulled out his notebook. He added to his “gorilla behavior” list:



 杰克有点嫉妒。 他也想让大猩猩们喜欢他。 可他不知道怎么一起玩。 于是他只是叹了口气，抽出笔记本。 他在“大猩猩行为”清单上补充道：

图中文字：gorillas like to tickle and laugh 图中文字：大猩猩喜欢挠痒痒和笑



Suddenly, he heard a low growl. 突然，他听见一声低沉的咆哮。

He looked up. The silverback had moved closer to him. He was glaring. 他抬起头。 银背靠近了些。他正瞪着杰克。

“That big guy doesn’t understand what you’re doing!” Annie called to Jack. “He’s never seen anyone take notes before.” “那个大家伙不明白你在干什么！”安妮对杰克喊道。 “他从没见过有人记笔记。”

Jack quickly put his notebook away. 杰克赶快收起笔记本。

The giant gorilla huffed. Then he turned to his family and gave a short bark. 巨猩猩哼了一声。 然后他转向家人，短促地吠了一声。

The gorillas began lining up behind the silverback. The baby traveled in his mother’s arms. Ho-ho traveled on his mother’s back. Bu-bu and Annie held hands. They all followed the silverback out of the clearing. 大猩猩们开始在银背大猩猩身后排起队来。 宝宝坐在妈妈怀里走。 嚯嚯趴在妈妈背上走。 布布和安妮手牵着手。 他们都跟着银背走出空地。

“Come on!” Annie called to Jack. “Let’s go with Big Guy and the gang!” “来呀！”安妮对杰克喊道。 “我们跟大块头和他那一伙一起走吧！”

Jack shook his head. 杰克摇了摇头。

“I don’t think they want me to come along,” he said. “我觉得它们不想让我跟着来，”他说。

Bu-bu screeched at Jack. She held out her free hand to him. 布布冲杰克尖叫。 她向他伸出空着的那只手。

“Bu- bu wants you!” said Annie. “布布想要你！”安妮说。

Jack smiled shyly. He took Bu-bu’s small, warm hand. Then he walked with Annie and the gorillas out of the clearing. 杰克不好意思地笑了笑。 他握住布布又小又暖的手。 然后他和安妮、大猩猩们一起走出空地。

On their ramble through the cloud forest, the gorillas found food everywhere. They munched flowers and ferns and leaves. They swallowed and burped. 大猩猩们在云雾林里漫步时，到处都能找到食物。 他们大口吃着花、蕨类和树叶。 它们吞下去，又打了嗝。

They munched twigs and branches and pieces of bark and bamboo. They swallowed and burped. 他们大口吃着小枝、树枝、树皮和竹子。 它们吞下去，又打了嗝。

As the gorillas ate breakfast, it started to rain again. But they didn’t seem to mind. 大猩猩们吃早饭时，又下起雨来。 可他们好像并不在意。

Annie didn’t seem to mind, either. She and Bu-bu played tag in the drizzly woods. They ran around the trees, laughing and screeching. 安妮好像也不在意。 她和布布在细雨蒙蒙的林子里玩捉人。 他们绕着树跑，又笑又叫。

Jack tried to follow them, but he gave up. He was tired and cold. Shivering, he stood under a mossy tree to keep dry. 杰克想跟上他们，可还是放弃了。 他又累又冷。 他发抖着站在一棵长满苔藓的树下躲雨。

While he was alone, Jack sneaked his notebook out of his pack. He made a new list:



 独自一人时，杰克偷偷从背包里拿出笔记本。 他列了个新清单：

图中文字：gorilla foods

flowers

ferns

leaves

twigs

branches

bamboo 图中文字：大猩猩吃食物、花、蕨类、叶子、树枝、竹子



As he wrote, he heard a low growl. He looked up. 他写着写着，听见一声低沉的咆哮。他抬起头。

Big Guy had spotted him. The silverback was standing close by. He was frowning at Jack, his lips tucked in a tight line. 大块头发现了他。 银背大猩猩就站在附近。 他正皱着眉瞪杰克，嘴唇抿成一条紧绷的线。

“Sorry, sorry!” said Jack. He quickly put away his notebook. “对不起，对不起！”杰克说。 他赶快收起笔记本。

Big Guy kept frowning. 大块头一直皱着眉。

Jack quickly tried to act like a gorilla. He went down on all fours. He tore off the leaf of a plant. He took a bite. It tasted bitter, like vinegar. Jack pretended to munch and swallow and burp. 杰克赶紧试着表现得像一只大猩猩。 他四肢着地趴下。 他撕下一片植物的叶子。咬了一口。 味道发苦，像醋一样。 杰克假装咀嚼、吞咽，还打了个嗝。

Big Guy huffed, then moved on. As soon as he was gone, Jack spat out the leaf. 大家伙哼了一声，然后走开了。 他一走开，杰克就把叶子吐了出来。

“Yuck, yuck, yuck!” he said, wiping his tongue. “呸，呸，呸！”他一边擦舌头一边说。

Jack felt a tap on his back. He jumped. But it was just Ho-ho. The small gorilla offered him a twig to eat. 杰克觉得背上被人拍了一下。他跳了起来。 原来只是嚯嚯。 那只小猩猩递给他一根小树枝吃。

“Oh, no thanks, Ho-ho,” said Jack. “哦，不用了，谢谢，嚯嚯，”杰克说。

Ho-ho kept holding out the twig. 嚯嚯继续把小枝递过来。

“Oh, okay,” said Jack, politely taking it. “I’ll eat it later.” He put the twig into his backpack. “哦，好吧，”杰克礼貌地接过来说。 “我等会儿再吃。”他把小枝放进背包。

Ho-ho’s mom came over to Jack. She held some berries to his lips. 嚯嚯的妈妈走到杰克身边。 她把一些浆果送到他嘴边。

“Uh, no thanks,” Jack said. “呃，不用了，谢谢，”杰克说。

The gorilla stared at him with a sad look. 大猩猩用悲伤的眼神看着他。

“Oh, okay,” said Jack. He opened his mouth. And Ho-ho’s mom fed him the berries. “哦，好吧，”杰克说。 他张开嘴。 嚯嚯的妈妈把浆果喂进他嘴里。

Jack munched the berries. To his surprise, they tasted good. He swallowed, then burped just like a gorilla. This time, he wasn’t pretending. 杰克大声地嚼着浆果。 让他吃惊的是，浆果很好吃。 他吞下去，然后像大猩猩一样打了个嗝。 这一次，他不是在假装。

Bu-bu’s mom then came over to Jack. She offered him some rainwater from a cupped plant. Jack was very thirsty. He sipped the water. It tasted fresh and cold. 布布的妈妈也走到杰克身边。 她用杯状的植物叶给他端来一些雨水。 杰克渴极了。 他喝了几口。 水又新鲜又凉。

Bu-bu’s mom took Jack’s hand in her wide hand. She led him through the forest to Annie and Bu-bu. 布布的妈妈用宽大的手握住杰克的手。 她领着他穿过树林，来到安妮和布布身边。

Bu-bu screeched happily when she saw Jack. She threw her furry arms around him. 布布看见杰克时高兴地尖叫起来。 她用毛茸茸的胳膊抱住他。

“Hi! We missed you!” Annie said to Jack. “Are you having fun?” “嗨，小家伙，”安妮温柔地说。 我们想你了！”安妮对杰克说。 “你玩得开心吗？”

Jack smiled and nodded. 杰克笑着点了点头。

Actually, he was having fun now. He didn’t mind the rain so much anymore. He didn’t feel so left out. Some of the gorillas really seemed to like him, he thought. They seemed to like him a lot. 其实，他现在真的很开心。 他不再那么介意下雨了。 他不觉得那么被冷落了。 有些大猩猩好像真的喜欢他，他想。 他们好像很喜欢他。
