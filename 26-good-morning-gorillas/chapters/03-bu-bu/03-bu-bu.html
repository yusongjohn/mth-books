3. Bu-bu 3. 布布

The gorilla’s fur was very black against the green leaves. She had large nostrils and small ears. Her bright brown eyes were full of mischief. 小猩猩的毛在绿叶衬托下显得特别黑。 她有大大的鼻孔和小小的耳朵。 她亮晶晶的棕色眼睛里满是淘气。

“Bu, bu, bu,” she said. “ Bu, bu.” “布，布，布，”她说。 “布，布。”

“Bu, bu yourself,” said Annie. “布布你自己，”安妮说。

The gorilla hid behind the leaves again. Then she poked her head out. 小猩猩又躲到叶子后面。 然后她探出头来。

“Peekaboo!” said Annie. “躲猫猫！”安妮说。

The gorilla clapped her hands together. She stuck out her tongue. 小猩猩拍起手来。 她吐了吐舌头。

Jack and Annie both laughed. 杰克和安妮都笑了。

“Bu, bu, bu!” the gorilla said. Then she bounded away through the misty forest. “布，布，布！”小猩猩说。 接着她跳跃着跑开，穿过雾蒙蒙的森林。

“Hey, Bu-bu! Don’t leave us!” Annie called. “嘿，布布！ 别丢下我们！”安妮喊道。

Jack rolled his eyes. “Don’t name her Bu-bu,” he said to Annie. “You don’t have to—” 杰克翻了翻白眼。 “别叫她布布，”他对安妮说。 “你不用——”

“Wait, Bu-bu!” Annie shouted. She took off after the small gorilla. “等等，布布！”安妮喊道。 她追着小猩猩跑了起来。

“—turn every animal into your best friend,” Jack finished. He shook his head. Then he made a list in his notebook.



 “——把每一种动物都变成你最好的朋友，”杰克接完了。 他摇了摇头。 然后他在笔记本上列了个清单。

图中文字：gorilla behavior

plays peekaboo

claps hands

sticks out tongue 图中文字：大猩猩的行为玩捉迷藏拍手伸出舌头

As he wrote, Jack heard Annie laughing. But then he heard high shrieks. 他写着写着，听见安妮在笑。 可接着他听见了尖厉的叫声。

He caught his breath. A leopard? he wondered. 他屏住呼吸。豹子？ 他想。

Carrying his notebook, Jack hurried in the direction of the noise. He found Annie and the small gorilla perched in two trees. 杰克拿着笔记本，朝声音的方向赶过去。 他发现安妮和小猩猩分别蹲在两棵树上。

“What’s wrong?” said Jack, standing beneath the trees. “怎么了？”杰克站在树下说。

“Nothing!” called Annie. “We’re just playing.” “没什么！”安妮喊道。 “我们只是在玩。”

The small gorilla screeched again. Then she scratched her head and hiccuped. 小猩猩又尖叫起来。 她挠了挠头，打了个嗝。

Annie screeched, too. She scratched her head and hiccuped. 安妮也尖叫起来。 她挠了挠头，打了个嗝。

While they played, Jack studied the gorilla a bit more. 他们玩耍的时候，杰克又多观察了一会儿那只大猩猩。

He noticed she was about the size of a three-year-old kid. Her fingers looked like human fingers. They even had fingernails! He made a new list:



 他注意到她大概有一个三岁小孩那么大。 她的手指看起来像人的手指。 它们甚至还有手指甲！ 他又列了个新清单：

图中文字：young gorilla

size of 3-year-old

fingers like humans'

film ails 图中文字：小 gorilla 的体型像3岁小孩，手指像人类的，拍电影时生病了



Jack heard the tree leaves shaking. He looked up. Annie and the gorilla had both climbed higher. 杰克听见树叶沙沙响。他抬起头。 安妮和大猩猩都爬得更高了。

“Hey, come down, Annie!” Jack called. “You might fall. Plus, it’s getting dark.” “嘿，下来，安妮！”杰克喊道。 “你会摔下来的。 而且，天越来越黑了。”

Jack looked around. Light was fading quickly from the forest. Is night falling? he wondered. Or is a storm coming? 杰克四下看了看。 林子里的光线很快暗了下去。夜幕降临了吗？ 他想。 还是暴风雨要来了？

The small gorilla screeched again and climbed even higher. 小猩猩又尖叫一声，爬得更高了。

“Hey, Bu-bu! Where you going?” said Annie. She climbed even higher, too. “嘿，布布！ 你去哪儿？”安妮说。 她也爬得更高了。

“That’s enough, Annie. Come down now!” said Jack. “I’m serious.” “够了，安妮。 现在下来！”杰克说。 “我是认真的。”

To his relief, the gorilla settled on a branch. Annie did the same. 让他松了口气的是，那只大猩猩在一根树枝上安顿下来。 安妮也照做了。

The gorilla broke off a piece of tree bark. She nibbled it like a candy bar. 大猩猩掰下一块树皮。 她像啃糖块一样啃着它。

Annie broke off a piece of bark. She nibbled it like a candy bar, too. 安妮也掰下一块树皮。 她像啃糖块一样啃着它。

The gorilla threw down her bark. She grabbed a tree branch and swung to another tree. 大猩猩扔掉树皮。 她抓住一根树枝，荡到另一棵树上。

“Don’t try it, Annie!” shouted Jack. “别试，安妮！”杰克喊道。

But his warning came too late. 可他的警告来得太晚了。

Annie threw down her bark. She grabbed a tree branch and tried to swing to another tree. 安妮把树皮扔了下去。 她抓住一根树枝，想荡到另一棵树上。

Annie didn’t swing like a gorilla. She fell from the tree—and crashed down to the ground near Jack. 安妮荡得不像大猩猩。 她从树上掉下来——坠落在杰克附近的地上。

“Annie!” he cried. “安妮！”他喊道。
