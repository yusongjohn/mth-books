4. Nightmare 4. 噩梦

Jack knelt beside Annie. She was gasping for breath. 杰克跪在安妮身边。 她喘着粗气。

The gorilla bounded down the tree and over to Annie. She bit her lower lip as if she were worried. 小猩猩从树上跳下来，跑到安妮身边。 她咬着下嘴唇，好像在担心什么。

“Are you okay?” Jack asked Annie. “你没事吧？”杰克问安妮。

“Yes—” Annie panted, “just—got the breath—knocked out of me—” “是——”安妮喘着气说，“只是——被摔——喘不过气来——”

“Wiggle your arms and your legs,” said Jack. “动动胳膊和腿，”杰克说。

Annie wiggled her arms and her legs. 安妮扭动着胳膊和腿。

“Good, nothing’s broken,” said Jack. “很好，没有骨折，”杰克说。

Just then, he felt a drop of water hit his arm. The mist had turned to rain. 就在这时，他觉得一滴水打在胳膊上。 雾气变成了雨。

“Uh-oh,” said Jack. He threw his notebook into his pack. “糟了，”杰克说。 他把笔记本扔进背包。

“I better get our umbrella and flashlight,” he said. “I left them near that tree that looked like a chair.” “我最好去拿伞和手电筒，”他说。 “我把它们落在那棵像椅子的树旁边了。”

“I’ll come, too,” said Annie. She started to sit up. “我也去，”安妮说。 她开始坐起来。

“No, no, catch your breath,” said Jack. “It’s not far. I’ll be right back.” “别，别，先喘口气，”杰克说。 “不远。 我马上回来。”

He took off his jacket and draped it over her. “This’ll help you stay dry,” he said. He pulled on his pack and stood up. 他脱下夹克，松松地披在她身上。 “这样你能少淋点雨，”他说。 他背上背包，站了起来。

The gorilla screeched. 那只大猩猩尖叫起来。

“Stay with Annie!” said Jack. “陪着安妮！”杰克说。

Then he dashed back through the cloud forest. He looked for the fat tree with the wide limbs padded with moss. 然后他冲回云雾林。 他寻找那棵枝干宽阔、垫着苔藓的粗树。

As he peered through the growing darkness, Jack saw many fat trees. He saw many limbs padded with moss. 透过越来越暗的光线，杰克看见许多粗树。 他看见许多大树枝上铺着苔藓。

Soon he could hardly see trees at all. He realized that both a storm and night had come to the forest. 很快他几乎看不见树了。 他意识到，暴风雨和黑夜一起来到了林子里。

Forget the umbrella and flashlight, he thought. It was more important to get back to Annie before it was too dark. They could wait together for daylight. 别管伞和手电筒了，他想。 更重要的是，在天太黑之前回到安妮身边。 他们可以一起等到天亮。

As Jack started back to Annie, he could hardly see. He didn’t know which way to go. 杰克往回找安妮时，几乎什么也看不见。 他不知道该往哪边走。

“Annie! Bu-bu!” he shouted. He felt silly shouting, “Bu-bu.” But he didn’t know what else to call the small gorilla. “安妮！ 布布！”他喊道。 喊“布布”让他觉得有点傻。可他不知道还能怎么叫那只小猩猩。

Jack put out his hands. He moved slowly through the dark, rainy forest. He kept calling for Annie and Bu-bu. He listened for them. But he couldn’t hear anything above the loud patter of the rain. 杰克伸出双手。 他慢慢在又黑又下雨的林子里挪动。 他不停地喊着安妮和布布。 他侧耳听着。 可除了嘈杂的雨声，他什么也听不见。

“Ahh!” he shouted. He had run into something that felt like a ball of spiderwebs! “啊！”他喊道。 他撞上了什么东西，摸起来像一团蜘蛛网！

As he jumped back, he slipped and fell in the mud. He crawled over to a tree and huddled between two of its giant roots. 他往后一跳，滑了一跤，摔进了泥里。 他爬到一棵树旁，缩在两根巨大的树根中间。

I’ll just wait here until morning, he thought. Then I’ll find Annie. Or she’ll find me. 我就在这儿等到早上吧，他想。 然后我再找安妮。 或者她会找到我。

As rain dripped all around him, Jack wondered if leopards came out at night. He quickly pushed the thought away. He tried to think about morning and finding Annie and going home. 雨在四周滴答落下时，杰克想知道豹子会不会在夜里出来。 他赶紧把那个念头推开。 他试着去想早晨，想找到安妮，想回家。

He was really ready to go home. 他真的很想回家了。

Why did Morgan even send us to the cloud forest? he wondered. He tried to remember the secret rhyme. 摩根为什么要把我们送到云雾林来呢？他想。 他试着记起那首秘密韵诗。

“To find a special magic … ,” he whispered. He couldn’t remember the rest. He felt tired and miserable. He took his backpack off and rested his head on it. He closed his eyes. “要找到一种特别的魔法……”他低声说。 后面的他记不起来了。 他觉得又累又难受。 他脱下背包，把头靠在上面。 他闭上眼睛。

“To find a special magic … ,” he mumbled. “要找到一种特别的魔法……”他咕哝着。

But he couldn’t find the magic. He couldn’t even find the words that finished the rhyme. Worst of all, he couldn’t find Annie. 可他找不到魔法。 他连韵诗剩下的词也想不起来。 最糟糕的是，他找不到安妮了。

Their fun adventure in the cloud forest had turned into a nightmare. 他们在云雾林里的快乐冒险变成了一场噩梦。
