8. A Special Language 8. 一种特别的语言

The rain ended. Slowly the feeding came to a stop. 雨停了。 慢慢地，进食停了下来。

Big Guy led his family into a clearing. The tall grass sparkled with misty sunlight. 大家伙领着家人走进一片空地。 高高的草在雾蒙蒙的阳光里闪着光。

The silverback lay down and tucked his arms under his head. 银背躺下，把胳膊垫在头下。

The other gorillas gathered around him. Some beat the grasses until they were flat. 其他大猩猩围在他身边。 有的拍打着草，直到草变得平平的。

Ho-ho’s mom made a bed of weed stalks for Ho-ho. Bu-bu’s mom made a bed of leaves for Bu-bu. Then she made two extra beds for Jack and Annie. 嚯嚯的妈妈用草茎给嚯嚯铺了张床。 布布的妈妈用树叶给布布铺了张床。 然后她又额外给杰克和安妮铺了两张床。

They lay down with all the gorillas to take their naps. Jack used his backpack for a pillow. 他们和大猩猩们一起躺下打盹。 杰克把背包当枕头。

Lying on his leafy bed, Jack watched the mother of the baby gorilla groom her small baby. The mother parted his hair and searched through it, picking at his skin now and then. 躺在叶子床上，杰克看着小猩猩的妈妈给宝宝梳理毛发。 妈妈拨开他的毛，仔细搜寻着，时不时轻轻抠一下他的皮肤。

The baby soon wiggled free and crawled around in the grass. His mother’s gaze then rested on Annie. She moved over to Annie and gently grabbed one of her pigtails. She studied it carefully. 宝宝很快挣脱出来，在草地上爬来爬去。 接着，他妈妈的目光落在了安妮身上。 她挪到安妮身边，轻轻抓住她的一条辫子。 她仔细研究着辫子。

“What are you doing?” Annie asked. “你在干什么？”安妮问。

“She’s looking for bugs, I think,” said Jack. “我想她在找虫子，”杰克说。

“Oh, yuck!” said Annie, sitting up. “哦，恶心！”安妮说着坐起来。

Jack laughed. Just then, the baby’s mother reached for him. 杰克笑了。 就在这时，宝宝的妈妈朝他伸过手来。

“Oops! No thanks! No bugs on me!” he said, and he sat up, too. “哎呀！不用了！ 我身上没虫子！”他说着也坐了起来。

The mother gorilla lay back in the grass and closed her eyes. Her baby crawled over to Annie. 母猩猩躺回草地上，闭上眼睛。 她的宝宝爬到安妮身边。

“Hi, Little Guy,” Annie said tenderly. She picked up the baby and stroked his head. The baby smiled at her and closed his eyes. “嗨，小家伙，”安妮温柔地说。 她抱起宝宝，轻轻抚摸他的头。 宝宝朝她笑了笑，闭上眼睛。

While all the gorillas napped, Jack sneaked the book out of his pack. He found the gorilla chapter. He read softly to Annie: 大猩猩们都打盹时，杰克偷偷从背包里拿出书。 他找到大猩猩那一章。 他轻声读给安妮听：

Gorillas are very smart. A captive gorilla named Koko has even learned sign language. Sign language is a special language used by people who cannot hear. Koko can say— 大猩猩非常聪明。一只名叫Koko的圈养大猩猩甚至学会了手语。手语是听不见的人使用的特殊语言。Koko可以说——

“What?” Annie said loudly. “ Sign language? A special language?” “什么？”安妮大声说。 “手语？ 一种特别的语言？”

Her voice woke Bu-bu and Ho-ho. They sat up and rubbed their eyes. 她的声音吵醒了布布和嚯嚯。 他们坐起来，揉了揉眼睛。

“So?” said Jack. “怎么了？”杰克说。

“Morgan’s secret rhyme! ” said Annie. “Don’t you remember?” She repeated the rhyme: “摩根的秘密韵诗！”安妮说。 “你不记得了吗？”她把韵诗又念了一遍：

To find a special kind of magic

in worlds so far apart,

speak a special language,

talk with your hands and heart. 要在相隔如此遥远的世界中找到一种特殊的魔法，要说一种特殊的语言，用手和心交谈。

“Oh … yeah!” said Jack. “哦……对了！”杰克说。

“I even know a little sign language,” said Annie. “In school, we learned how to sign I love you.” “我还会一点手语呢，”安妮说。 “在学校里，我们学过怎么比划‘我爱你’。”

Annie held up a closed hand. Slowly she lifted her thumb, index finger, and little finger. She showed the sign to Bu-bu and Ho-ho. 安妮举起一只握紧的手。 她慢慢地举起大拇指、食指和小拇指。 她把这个手势比给布布和嚯嚯看。

“I—love—you,” she said slowly. “我——爱——你，”她慢慢地说。

The small gorillas looked curious. 小猩猩们看起来很好奇。

Jack made the sign, too. 杰克也做了同样的手势。

“I—love—you,” he said to Bu-bu and Ho-ho. “我——爱——你，”他对布布和嚯嚯说。

The two little gorillas stared at Annie and Jack. Then both of them held up their hands. They tried to make the same sign. 两只小猩猩盯着杰克和安妮。 然后他们都举起手。 他们试着做同样的手势。

“They love us, too!” said Annie. “他们也爱我们！”安妮说。

“Wow,” said Jack. He glanced over at Big Guy. “哇，”杰克说。 他朝大家伙瞥了一眼。

The silverback’s eyes were open! He was watching them. Jack quickly closed the book. To his relief, the giant gorilla turned over. 银背的眼睛睁着！ 他正在看着他们。 杰克赶紧合上了书。 让他松了口气的是，巨猩猩翻了个身。

“Well,” Annie said with a sigh, “I guess that does it.” “好了，”安妮叹了口气说，“我想这就完成了。”

“We spoke a special language,” said Jack. “We talked with our—” Before he could finish his sentence, Bu-bu pushed him. “我们说了一种特别的语言，”杰克说。 “我们用——”他还没说完，布布就推了他一把。

“Whoa!” said Jack. “哇！”杰克说。

Ho-ho held his little arms above his head. He reared back and charged at Jack. With a flying tackle, he knocked Jack over. 嚯嚯把小胳膊举过头顶。 他往后仰，朝杰克冲过来。 他飞扑过来一抱，把杰克撞倒了。

“What’s going on?” said Jack. “怎么回事？”杰克说。

“They want to play with you!” said Annie. “他们想跟你玩！”安妮说。

Bu-bu jumped on Jack and put him in a headlock. Jack broke free from the two small gorillas. He jumped up and dashed into the forest. 布布跳到杰克身上，把他的头锁住。 杰克从两只小猩猩手里挣脱出来。 他跳起来冲进树林。

Bu-bu and Ho-ho charged after him. 布布和嚯嚯追在后面。

Annie carried Little Guy and followed. She laughed as the small gorillas looked for Jack. 安妮抱着小家伙跟上去。 小猩猩们找杰克时，她笑了。

Jack hid behind a tree. He pushed his glasses into place. He waited for Bu-bu. 杰克躲在一棵树后面。 他把眼镜推正。 他等着布布。

In a moment, she walked by. 过了一会儿，她走了过来。

“BOO!” Jack shouted, jumping out. “哇！”杰克跳出来大喊。

Bu-bu screeched and leaped straight up in the air. Jack cracked up laughing. 布布尖叫着直直蹦到空中。 杰克哈哈大笑起来。

Bu-bu didn’t laugh, though. She bit her lip. She hid her face behind her hands. 可布布没有笑。她咬着嘴唇。 她用手捂住脸。

“Ohhh, Bu-bu,” said Annie. “Don’t be scared.” “噢——噢，布布，”安妮说。 “别怕。”

She gently put the baby on the ground. She reached out to comfort Bu-bu. 她轻轻把宝宝放在地上。 她伸出手去安慰布布。

Bu-bu wrapped her arms around Annie’s neck. She buried her furry head in Annie’s shoulder. 布布用胳膊搂住安妮的脖子。 她把毛茸茸的头埋进安妮的肩膀。

“Jack was just playing,” said Annie. “杰克只是在玩，”安妮说。

Bu-bu raised her head. She looked at Jack over Annie’s shoulder. 布布抬起头。 她从安妮肩头上看着杰克。

“Friends?” he asked softly. “还是朋友吗？”他轻声问。

Bu-bu stuck her tongue out at him. 布布朝他吐了吐舌头。

Jack laughed. Bu-bu showed her teeth in a big smile. 杰克笑了。 布布露出牙齿，灿烂地笑了。

“Friends!” said Jack. “朋友！”杰克说。

Just then, Ho-ho started screeching. Jack and Annie looked around. Ho-ho was pointing into the bushes. 就在这时，嚯嚯开始尖叫。 杰克和安妮四处张望。 嚯嚯正指着灌木丛。

“Where’s Little Guy?” said Annie. She and Jack dashed around the shrubs. “小家伙呢？”安妮说。 她和杰克冲过灌木。

The baby had crawled to a tree. He was looking up at a branch. 宝宝已经爬到一棵树下。 他正抬头看着一根树枝。

A huge, sleek cat with black fur was sitting on the branch. His pale green eyes stared down at the baby gorilla. He looked hungry. 一只毛色光滑的巨大黑猫正坐在树枝上。 它淡绿色的眼睛盯着小猩猩。它看起来很饿。

“A black leopard,” breathed Jack. “一只黑豹，”杰克屏住呼吸说。

The leopard leaped lightly down from his perch. He faced Little Guy. The baby looked scared. 豹子从栖息处轻轻跳下来。 它面对着小家伙。 宝宝看起来很害怕。

“No!” cried Annie. “不！”安妮喊道。

She ran over to the baby gorilla and scooped him into her arms. 她跑到小猩猩身边，敏捷地把他抱进怀里。

The leopard let out a snarl. He lowered his head and started slowly toward Annie and the baby. 豹子发出一声咆哮。 它低下头，慢慢朝安妮和宝宝走去。

Jack panicked. He didn’t know what to do at first. Then he remembered Big Guy’s act. Jack took a deep breath. When he let it out, he made a loud hooting sound. 杰克慌了。 一开始他不知道该怎么办。 接着他想起了大家伙的样子。 杰克深深吸了一口气。 呼气时，他发出大声的吼叫。

He tore out from the brush. Hooting like a silverback, he ran between Annie and the leopard. 他从灌木里冲出来。 他像银背一样吼着，跑到安妮和豹子中间。

Jack grabbed a tree limb and shook it. He ripped leaves from branches. 杰克抓住一根树枝，摇晃它。 他把叶子从枝上撕下来。

He cupped his hands. He beat his chest. 他把手掌收成杯状。 他捶打自己的胸口。

“WRAAGH!” he roared. “ WRAAGH!” “哇啊！”他吼道。 “哇啊！”

Then Jack leaned over and charged back and forth past the leopard. 然后杰克俯下身，在豹子身边来回冲撞。

Finally, he threw himself on his belly. He began bashing the ground with his palms. He bashed and bashed and bashed. 最后，他扑倒在肚子上。 他开始用掌心拍打地面。 他一下又一下地猛砸着。

“Jack!” Annie called. “Jack!” “杰克！”安妮喊道。 “杰克！”

Jack looked up. 杰克抬起头。

“He’s gone,” Annie said in a quiet voice. “The leopard’s gone. He left a while ago.” “它走了，”安妮轻声说。 “豹子走了。 他刚才已经走了。”

“Oh,” said Jack. “哦，”杰克说。

He sat up. 他坐起来。

He pushed his glasses into place. He looked around. Then he smiled. 他把眼镜推正。他四处看了看。 然后他笑了。
