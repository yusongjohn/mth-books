9. Wisdom of the Heart 9. 心的智慧

“Merlin! Morgan!” shouted Annie. “梅林！ 摩根！”安妮喊道。

Teddy and Kathleen cried out in amazement. 泰迪和凯瑟琳惊讶地叫了起来。

Annie rushed to Morgan and hugged her. 安妮冲到摩根身边，紧紧地拥抱了她。

Jack was filled with giddy relief. “Hi!” he said. “Hi!” 杰克感到一阵欣喜若狂的轻松。 “你好！”他说。 “你好！

“Welcome back, sir!” Teddy said to Merlin. “欢迎回来，先生！”泰迪对梅林说道。

“Thank you,” said Merlin. He looked at Jack and Annie. “And thank you for turning us back into ourselves.” “谢谢，”梅林说。 他看着杰克和安妮。 “谢谢你们让我们变回原来的样子。”

“We didn’t know you and Morgan were the wolves!” Annie said. “我们不知道你们和摩根是狼！”安妮说。

“We were following you so we could help you,” said Morgan. “我们跟着你们，是为了能帮你们，”摩根说。

“The wizard told us that if you caught up with us, you would eat us!” said Jack. “巫师告诉我们，如果你追上我们，你们就会吃掉我们！”杰克说。

“Really?” said Morgan. “真的吗？”摩根说。

They all looked at the Ice Wizard. Standing with his sister, he stared guiltily at Morgan and Merlin. 他们都看着冰巫师。 站在他姐姐身边，他愧疚地看着摩根和梅林。

“I feared that if they got close to you, they might discover who you were,” he said. “But I will do no more harm, I promise—for I can see clearly now.” The wizard looked back at his sister, and his blue eyes shined with joy. “我担心如果他们靠近你们，他们可能会发现你们是谁，”他说。 “但我不会再造成伤害了，我发誓——因为我现在看得清楚了。”这位巫师回头看着他的姐姐，蓝色的眼睛里充满了喜悦。

“You can see because you have your heart back,” said Morgan. “It was not only your eye that was missing—it was also your heart. We see with our hearts as well as our eyes.” “你之所以能看见，是因为你找回了你的心。”摩根说道。 “你失去的不只是眼睛，还有你的心。” “我们不仅用眼睛看，也用心看。”

“And now perhaps you can find the wisdom you were seeking from the Norns,” said Merlin, “for wisdom is knowledge learned with the heart as well as the head.” “现在也许你可以从诺恩那里找到你一直在寻找的智慧，”梅林说道，“因为智慧不仅是用头脑学到的，也是用心学到的。”

The Ice Wizard nodded. “Please find it in your hearts to forgive me,” he said. “Use my sleigh to take you safely home.” 冰巫师点了点头。 “请用你们的心原谅我，”他说。 “用我的雪橇带你们安全回家。”

“Yes, indeed, we must leave now,” said Morgan. “We have been gone from Camelot too long.” “是的，确实，我们必须现在离开了，”摩根说道。 “我们离开卡梅洛特已经太久了。”

“The next time you come to Camelot, my friend, you must come as a guest,” said Merlin, “not as a thief in the night.” “下次你再来卡梅洛特的时候，我的朋友，你必须作为客人来，”梅林说，“而不是像黑夜里的小偷一样。”

“And you must bring your sister also,” Morgan said to the wizard. “你还要带上你的妹妹，”摩根对这位巫师说。

“Indeed I will,” the wizard said. “我确实会带的，”巫师说。

Merlin looked at Jack, Annie, Teddy, and Kathleen. “Is everyone ready to leave now?” he asked. 梅林看着杰克、安妮、泰迪和凯瑟琳。 “现在大家都准备好离开了吗？”他问。

“Yes, sir,” they all answered together. “是的，先生，”他们一起回答。

Merlin looked at the Staff of Strength in Jack’s hands. 梅林看着杰克手中的力量之杖。

“Oh! Sorry, I almost forgot,” said Jack. He handed the heavy staff to Merlin. 抱歉，我差点忘了，”杰克说。 他把那根沉重的杖交给了梅林。

As soon as Merlin held the Staff of Strength, he seemed more powerful. “Let us be off!” he said briskly. 梅林一握住力量之杖，看起来就更有力量了。 “我们快走吧！”他 briskly 地说道。

Merlin and Morgan led the way out of the throne room, their red cloaks billowing behind them. Teddy and Kathleen followed, and Jack and Annie hurried after them. 梅林和摩根带头走出了王座厅，他们红色的斗篷在身后飘扬。 泰迪和凯瑟琳跟在后面，杰克和安妮也赶紧跟了上去。

Just before they left the room, Jack and Annie glanced back at the Ice Wizard and his swan sister. They were deep in conversation again. 就在他们离开房间之前，杰克和安妮回头看了看冰巫师和他的天鹅妹妹。 他们又开始深入交谈了。

“They haven’t seen each other for years,” said Annie. “They must have a lot to talk about.” “他们已经很多年没见面了，”安妮说。 “他们一定有很多话要说。”

“Yeah,” said Jack. He couldn’t imagine not seeing Annie for years. “Come on, let’s go.” He took her hand and pulled her out of the throne room, through the front hall, and into the cold dawn. “是啊，”杰克说。 他无法想象自己和安妮几年都不见面。 “快点，我们走吧。”他拉起她的手，把她从王座厅拉出来，穿过前厅，走进寒冷的黎明中。

Jack and Annie followed their four Camelot friends to the wizard’s sleigh. Everyone climbed in. 杰克和安妮跟着他们的四个卡梅洛特朋友来到巫师的雪橇旁。 大家全都爬了上去。

Annie sat at the rudder. Jack stood at the front. He pulled out the wind-string and untied a knot. The sleigh rocked forward. He untied another, and the sleigh started moving very slowly. 安妮坐在舵后面。 杰克站在前面。 他拿出风绳，解开一个结。 雪橇向前晃了一下。 他又解开一个，雪橇开始慢慢地动起来。

The sleigh was heavier than before, so Jack quickly untied two more knots. The sleigh bolted across the snow. 雪橇比之前更重了，所以杰克赶紧又解开了两个结。 雪橇猛地在雪地上飞驰起来。

“Stand fast!” said Teddy. “站稳了！”泰迪说道。

As the sleigh swished through the dawn, Annie turned to Morgan and Merlin. “I have a question,” she said. “Can you tell us what the giant looks like—the Frost Giant?” 雪橇在黎明中滑行时，安妮转向摩根和梅林。 “我有个问题，”她说。 “你们能告诉我们巨人长什么样吗——那个冰霜巨人？”

Merlin smiled. “There is no Frost Giant,” he said. 梅林笑了。 “根本没有冰霜巨人，”他说。

“What?” said Kathleen and Teddy. “什么？”凯瑟琳和泰迪说。

“Sure there is,” said Annie. “We heard his breathing!” “当然有，”安妮说。 “我们听到他的呼吸声了！”

“He nearly froze us to death!” said Jack. “他差点把我们冻死了！”杰克说。

“At night, the wind often swirls through the Hollow Hill like a cyclone,” said Merlin. “You experienced one of those storms.” “夜晚，风常常像龙卷风一样在空山丘上盘旋，”梅林说。 “你经历了一场那样的暴风雨。”

“But what about the Norns’ story of giving the wizard’s eye to the Frost Giant as a gift?” said Jack. “但是关于诺恩们说的，把巫师的眼睛作为礼物送给冰巨人这件事呢？”杰克说道。

“Many ancient peoples believe that the forces of nature are actual giants or monsters,” said Morgan. “The Norns are the last of their kind. They hold to the idea that the Frost Giant is a living creature who haunts the Hollow Hill. In truth, the Frost Giant never accepted their gift because there is no Frost Giant.” “许多古代人都相信，自然的力量其实就是巨人或怪物，”摩根说道。 “诺恩是他们一族最后的幸存者。 她们坚信冰巨人是一种会出没在空山丘的活物。 事实上，冰巨人从未接受过她们的礼物，因为根本就没有冰巨人。”

Jack shook his head. “We believed what the Norns believed. They told us we’d freeze to death if we looked directly at the Frost Giant.” 杰克摇了摇头。 “我们相信诺恩们说的。 她们告诉我们，如果我们直接看向冰巨人，就会被冻死。”

“And we believed what the wizard told us, too,” said Annie, “that the wolves would eat us if they caught up to us!” “我们也相信巫师告诉我们的，”安妮说道，“他说如果狼追上我们，就会把我们吃掉！”

“People often try to convince us that the world is scarier than it truly is,” said Morgan. “人们常常试图说服我们，世界比它真正的样子更可怕，”摩根说。

Right now the world didn’t seem at all scary to Jack. Everything was calm and bright. Soft, rose-colored light was breaking through the morning clouds. 此刻，对杰克来说，世界一点都不可怕。 一切都平静而明亮。 柔和的粉红色光线正穿透清晨的云朵。

“Today is the first day after the winter solstice,” said Morgan. “Today the light starts to return. The days will grow longer.” “今天是冬至后的第一天，”摩根说。 “今天，光明开始回归。 日子会越来越长。”

Jack turned to look at the sun. He caught sight of the tree house sitting on top of a snowdrift, not far away. 杰克转身看向太阳。 他看见树屋坐在不远处的雪堆上。

Jack tied a knot in the wind-string. He tied three more, and the sleigh came to a stop at the foot of the snowdrift. 杰克在风绳上打了个结。 他绑了三个，雪橇停在了雪堆的脚下。

Merlin looked at them. “On the winter solstice, you showed great courage,” he said. “You endured storms and terror and extraordinary cold. You reunited the Ice Wizard and the swan maiden. And perhaps most important, you retrieved my Staff of Strength. I thank you.” 梅林看着他们。 “冬至那天，你们表现出了很大的勇气，”他说。 “你们经历了风暴、恐怖和极寒的天气。 你们让冰巫师和天鹅少女重逢了。 也许最重要的是，你们找回了我的力量法杖。我感谢你们。”

“Sure,” Jack and Annie said modestly. “当然，”杰克和安妮谦虚地说。

“You have done much for the kingdom of Camelot on your last four missions,” said Merlin. “On your next adventure, you will have a mission back in your world—in real time, not in the time of myth and magicians.” “在你们最后一次的四次任务中，你们为卡梅洛特王国做了很多贡献，”梅林说。 “在你们下一次冒险中，你们将有一个任务回到你们的世界——在现实时间，而不是神话和巫师的时间里。”

“We will call for you again soon,” said Morgan. “我们很快就会再召唤你们，”摩根说。

“Great!” said Annie. “太棒了！”安妮说。

Jack and Annie climbed out of the sleigh. They looked back at Teddy and Kathleen. “I hope you will help us with our next journey, too,” said Annie. 杰克和安妮从雪橇上爬了下来。 他们回头看向泰迪和凯瑟琳。 “我希望你们也能帮助我们完成下一次的旅程，”安妮说。

Teddy smiled. “If we all work together, we can do anything, aye?” he said. 泰迪笑了。 “如果我们大家齐心协力，我们什么都能做到，对吗？”他说。

“Aye!” said Jack and Annie together. Then they turned and trudged up the snowdrift. At the top, they climbed into the window of the tree house. Once they were inside, they looked back. “对！”杰克和安妮一起说。 然后他们转身，艰难地爬上雪堆。 到了山顶，他们爬进了树屋的窗户。 一旦他们进到里面，他们又回头看了看。

The sleigh was gone. 雪橇不见了。

“Bye,” Annie said softly. “再见，”安妮轻声说。

Jack picked up the small gray stone from the floor. He pointed at the words Frog Creek in the wizard’s message. “I wish we could go there,” he said. 杰克从地上捡起了那块小灰石头。 他指着巫师留言里的‘蛙溪镇’几个字。 “我希望我们能去那里，”他说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹了。

It blew harder and harder. 风越吹越猛。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。

Jack opened his eyes. They were back in the Frog Creek woods. No time at all had passed while they’d been gone. It was almost twilight. Snowflakes fell like tiny feathers outside the tree house window. 杰克睁开了眼睛。 他们又回到了蛙溪镇的森林里。 他们离开的时间里，时间几乎没过去。 天快黑了。 雪花像小羽毛一样，从树屋的窗户外面飘落下来。

Annie shivered. “I’m cold,” she said. 安妮打了个哆嗦。 “我冷，”她说。

“Here—take my scarf,” said Jack. He pulled off his scarf. “来，披上我的围巾，”杰克说。 他摘下了自己的围巾。

“No, you need it,” said Annie. “不，你才需要它，”安妮说。

“No, take it. I’ll be okay.” Jack put his scarf around Annie’s neck. “What will you tell Mom when she asks about your scarf?” he asked. “不，你拿去吧。” “我会没事的。”杰克把他的围巾绕在安妮的脖子上。 “当你妈妈问起你的围巾时，你会怎么跟她说？”他问。

“I’ll just tell her the Sisters of Fate took it as payment for telling us how to find the eye of the Ice Wizard in a hole in the Hollow Hill,” said Annie. “我只会告诉她，命运姐妹拿走了它，作为我们告诉她们如何在空洞山的洞里找到冰巫师眼睛的报酬，”安妮说。

“Right,” said Jack, laughing. “对，”杰克笑着说。

“We’d better get home before dark,” said Annie. She started down the rope ladder. Jack followed her. “我们最好在天黑之前回家，”安妮说。 她开始顺着绳梯往下爬。 杰克跟着她。

As they stepped onto the ground, Jack remembered the wind-string. “We forgot to give this back,” he said. He reached into his pocket and pulled out the string. “I guess Merlin’s magic took the sleigh back to Camelot.” 当他们踏上地面时，杰克想起了风绳。 “我们忘了把这还回去，”他说。 他把手伸进口袋，拿出那根绳子。 “我想梅林的魔法把雪橇送回了卡梅洛特。”

Jack and Annie looked at the string for a moment. “Untie a knot,” Annie whispered. 杰克和安妮盯着那根绳子看了好一会儿。 “解开一个结，”安妮小声说。

Jack took off his gloves and untied a knot. He held his breath and waited. Nothing happened. He gave Annie a little smile. “I guess in our world, it’s just a piece of string,” he said. 杰克摘下手套，解开一个结。 他屏住呼吸，等着。什么也没发生。 他朝安妮笑了笑。 “我想在我们这个世界，它只是一根普通的绳子，”他说。

Jack put the string back in his pocket. He and Annie started walking over the snowy ground between the trees. As they walked, Jack looked for Teddy’s and Kathleen’s footprints. But they were completely gone. 杰克把绳子放回口袋里。 他和安妮开始在雪地上走路，穿过树之间。 他们走着走着，杰克在找泰迪和凯瑟琳的脚印。 但是他们完全不见了。

Jack and Annie moved out of the woods and down their street. They saw Christmas tree lights sparkling in people’s houses and candles shining in windows. 杰克和安妮走出了树林，沿着他们的街道走下去。 他们看到人们家里圣诞树上的灯光闪闪发亮，窗户里还有蜡烛的光亮。

Their boots squeaked as they crossed their snow-covered yard. When they got to the stairs of the porch, Jack stopped. He stared in astonishment. 当他们穿过铺满积雪的院子时，靴子发出吱吱的声音。 当他们走到门廊的台阶前，杰克停了下来。 他惊讶地盯着看。

Annie’s red woolen scarf was draped over the railing of the porch. 安妮那条红色的羊毛围巾挂在门廊的栏杆上。

“I don’t believe it!” said Jack. “我不相信！”杰克说。

“I do!” said Annie. “我相信！”安妮说。

They hurried up the stairs and Annie grabbed her scarf. “Look!” she said. 他们赶紧走上台阶，安妮抓起了她的围巾。 “看！”她说。

She held up the scarf to show Jack. There was a tiny picture woven into it: a picture of him and Annie and two white wolves. 她举起围巾给杰克看。 围巾上织着一幅小小的图案：是他、安妮和两只白狼的图案。

Jack was speechless. 杰克说不出话来。

“Cool, huh?” said Annie. She gave Jack back his scarf. Then she tied her scarf around her neck. She tucked the part with the picture under her jacket collar. “酷吧？”安妮说。 她把围巾还给了杰克。 然后她把自己的围巾系在了脖子上。 她把有图案的部分塞到了夹克领子下面。

The front door opened. A delicious smell wafted out from the house. 前门打开了。 一股美味的香味从房子里飘了出来。

“Hi!” said their mom. “The cookies are ready. Come inside and get warm!” “你好！”他们妈妈说。 “饼干准备好了。 快进来，暖和暖和！“
