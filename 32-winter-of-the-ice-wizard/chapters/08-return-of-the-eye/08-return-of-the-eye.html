8. Return of the Eye 8. 眼睛的回归

Jack and Annie walked through the front hall of the palace, past the ice columns, and into the wizard’s throne room. The walls and floor glittered with the brilliant, cold light of dawn. 杰克和安妮穿过宫殿的前厅，经过冰柱，走进了巫师的宝座厅。 墙壁和地板在黎明寒冷而耀眼的光线下闪闪发光。

“Uh-oh,” said Jack. “糟了，”杰克说。

The wizard was waiting for them—and the two white wolves were sleeping on either side of his throne. Jack was confused. Why are they here? he wondered. Do they belong to the wizard? 巫师正在等他们——而且两只白狼正睡在他王座的两边。 杰克感到很困惑。 他们为什么在这里？他想知道。 他们属于巫师吗？

The wolves lifted their heads and sniffed the air. Their ears pricked up. When they caught sight of Jack and Annie, they sprang to their feet. They stared at them with piercing yellow eyes. 狼们抬起头，闻了闻空气。 它们的耳朵竖了起来。 当它们看到杰克和安妮时，立刻跳了起来。 它们用锐利的黄眼睛盯着他们。

The Ice Wizard was staring intently at Jack and Annie, too. “Well?” he said. “Did you bring back my eye?” 冰巫师也在专注地盯着杰克和安妮。 “好吧？”他说。 “你把我的眼睛带回来了吗？”

“Yes,” said Jack. “是的，”杰克说。

Annie took the hailstone out of her pocket and held it up to the wizard. Jack watched the wolves nervously as the hailstone passed from Annie’s small hand into the wizard’s large, rough hand. 安妮从口袋里拿出冰雹，举到巫师面前。 杰克紧张地看着冰雹从安妮的小手里传到巫师的大而粗糙的手里。

The wizard stared down at the chunk of ice. Then he looked at Jack and Annie. “Indeed, you are heroes,” he said breathlessly. 巫师低头看着那块冰块。 然后他看了看杰克和安妮。 “确实，你们是英雄，”他喘着气说。

“Not really,” Jack murmured. “其实不是，”杰克小声说。

The wizard looked again at his eye inside the hailstone. Then, with a quick movement, he slammed the ice chunk against the arm of his throne. 巫师再次看了看他眼里的冰雹石。 然后，他迅速将冰块砸在王座的扶手上。

Jack and Annie gasped and stepped back. The wizard slammed the hailstone against his throne again. This time, the ice cracked. 杰克和安妮惊叫了一声，往后退了一步。 巫师又把冰雹石砸在王座上。 这次，冰块裂开了。

The wizard gently pried his eye out of the heart of the hailstone. He lifted the frozen eyeball into the air and studied it in the light. Then, with an eager cry, he ripped off his eye patch. 巫师轻轻地把他的眼睛从冰雹石的中心取了出来。 他把那颗冰冻的眼睛举到空中，在灯光下仔细观察。 然后，他兴奋地喊了一声，撕下了自己的眼罩。

Jack and Annie watched in amazement as the wizard fitted the eye into its dark, empty socket. Jack held his breath. He was horrified, yet fascinated. He couldn’t imagine someone just shoving an eye back into his head. 杰克和安妮惊讶地看着这位巫师把眼睛放进那深黑空洞的眼窝里。 杰克屏住了呼吸。 他既害怕，又着迷。 他无法想象有人会把眼睛直接塞回自己的脑袋里。

The wizard slowly lowered his hand. He seemed to be holding his breath. He had two eyes. But the new one didn’t move. It looked as if it was still frozen. 巫师慢慢地放下手。 他似乎也在屏住呼吸。他有两只眼睛。 但新装上的那只眼睛没有动。 看起来它还是冰冻着的。

Jack grew worried. If the eye didn’t work, the wizard might not help them. “We—we brought you your eye,” he said. “So can you tell us where Merlin and Morgan are now?” 杰克变得担心起来。 如果眼睛不能用，巫师可能不会帮他们。 “我——我们给你带来了你的眼睛，”他说。 “你能告诉我们梅林和摩根现在在哪里吗？”

The wizard jerked his head to look at Jack. He covered one eye with his hand. Then he covered the other. In a frenzy, he went back and forth, covering and uncovering each eye. 巫师猛地转过头看向杰克。 他用手遮住了一只眼睛。 然后他又遮住了另一只。 他慌乱地来回移动，一会儿遮住一只眼睛，一会儿又揭开。

Finally the wizard dropped his hand and roared, “NO!” The wizard’s howl shook the ice columns. “You have tricked me!” 最后，巫师放下手，怒吼道：“不！”巫师的吼声震得冰柱都在发抖。 “你们骗了我！”

“No we haven’t,” said Annie. “我们还没呢，”安妮说。

“This eye is useless!” cried the wizard. “It has no life! No sight!” “这颗眼睛没用！”巫师喊道。 “它没有生命！没有视力！”，

“But that’s the eye you gave to the Norns,” said Annie. “You promised if we brought it back, you’d give us Merlin and Morgan.” “但那是你给诺恩的那只眼睛，”安妮说。 “你答应过，如果我们把它带回来，你就给我们梅林和摩根。”

The two white wolves threw back their heads and howled. 两只白狼仰起头，嚎叫起来。

“NO!” cried the wizard. “You tricked me! You tricked me!” “不！”巫师喊道。 “你骗了我！你骗了我！”

“Let’s get out of here,” whispered Jack. He pulled Annie toward the ice columns. “我们快离开这儿，”杰克小声说。 他拉着安妮朝冰柱那边走。

“STOP!” shouted the wizard. “YOU CANNOT ESCAPE ME!” He grabbed Merlin’s Staff of Strength. The wolves growled and yelped. The wizard pointed the staff at Jack and Annie. He started to say a spell—“RO-EEE—” “停住！”巫师喊道。 “你逃不掉的！”他抓住了梅林的力量法杖。 狼们咆哮着，嚎叫着。 巫师把法杖指向杰克和安妮。 他开始念咒语——“罗——”

“WAIT!” someone yelled. Teddy burst into the throne room. “Wait! Wait!” “等一下！”有人喊道。 泰迪冲进了王座厅。 “等一下！等一下！”

The wizard held his staff in the air. He stared wildly at Teddy. His face was twisted with rage. 巫师把法杖举到空中。 他疯狂地盯着泰迪。 他的脸因为愤怒而扭曲了。

“We have something for you!” Teddy shouted at the wizard. “Kathleen!” he called. “我们有东西要给你！”泰迪对那个巫师大喊。 “凯瑟琳！”他喊道。

Kathleen stepped out from behind the ice columns. With her was a young woman with long braids. The woman wore a flowing dress. Around her shoulders was a white feathered cloak. Her eyes rested on the wizard, and a radiant smile spread over her face. She began walking slowly toward the throne. 凯瑟琳从冰柱后面走了出来。 她身边站着一个扎着长辫子的年轻女子。 那女子穿着一件飘逸的长裙。 她肩膀上披着一件白色的羽毛斗篷。 她的眼睛注视着巫师，脸上露出灿烂的笑容。 她开始慢慢地朝王座走去。

The wizard lowered Merlin’s Staff of Strength. He stared back at the young woman. All the color drained from his face. For a long moment, he was as still as a statue. Then an ice-blue tear leaked out of his frozen eye and ran down his white cheek. 巫师放下了梅林的力量权杖。 他盯着那个年轻女子。 他的脸上一下子失去了所有颜色。 好一会儿，他一动不动，就像一尊雕像。 接着，他冰蓝色的眼泪从冻结的眼睛里流了出来，顺着苍白的脸颊滑落。

Jack and Annie stood with Kathleen and Teddy. They all watched the young woman and the Ice Wizard gaze silently at each other. 杰克和安妮和凯瑟琳、泰迪站在一起。 他们全都看着那个年轻女子和冰巫师默默地对视着。

“Is she his sister, the swan maiden?” whispered Annie. “她是他的姐姐，天鹅少女吗？”安妮小声问。

“Yes,” whispered Kathleen. “是的，”凯瑟琳也小声回答。

The swan maiden spoke to the Ice Wizard in a strange language—“Val-ee-ven-o-wan.” 天鹅少女用一种奇怪的语言对冰巫师说话——‘瓦利文奥万。’

The wizard did not answer. Tears flowed gently now from both his eyes. 巫师没有回答。 泪水现在从他的两只眼睛里慢慢地流了下来。

“Val-ee-ven-o-wan,” the swan maiden said again. “瓦勒维诺万，”天鹅少女又说了一遍。

“What’s she saying?” Jack asked. “她在说什么？”杰克问。

“She is saying, I have come back to forgive you,” said Kathleen. “她说，我回来是为了原谅你，”凯瑟琳说。

The wizard stood up. He walked down the steps from his throne. He gently touched the swan maiden’s face, as if to make sure she was real. Then he answered her softly in the strange language. “ Fel-o-wan.” 巫师站了起来。 他走下王座的台阶。 他轻轻地碰了碰天鹅少女的脸，好像在确认她是不是真的。 然后，他用那种奇怪的语言轻声回答她。 “费洛万。”

“How did you find her?” Jack asked Teddy. “你是怎么找到她的？”杰克问泰迪。

“A seal took us under the ice to the Isle of the Swans,” said Teddy. “一只海豹带我们穿过冰层，来到了天鹅岛，”泰迪说。

“When we found her, I told her how much the wizard has missed her,” said Kathleen. “I also told her about the two of you and how you always help each other. I told her she should return to her brother and be his friend again.” “当我们找到她时，我告诉她，巫师有多想念她，”凯瑟琳说。 “我还告诉了她有关你们两个人的事，以及你们总是互相帮助。 我告诉她，她应该回到她哥哥身边，重新做他的朋友。”

The wizard and his sister kept speaking softly to each other in their strange language. Warm sunlight shimmered through the palace windows. 巫师和他的妹妹用他们奇怪的语言，继续低声交谈着。 温暖的阳光透过宫殿的窗户闪烁着。

Annie stepped forward. “Um—excuse me,” she said. 安妮向前走了一步。 “呃——请问一下，”她说。

The wizard looked at her. “My sister has returned home,” he said with wonder. “I can see with both eyes now. I can see perfectly.” 巫师看着她。 “我的妹妹已经回家了，”他惊奇地说。 “我现在能用两只眼睛看见了！” 我看得一清二楚。”

“I’m glad,” said Annie. “But now you must give Merlin and Morgan back to us.” “我很高兴，”安妮说。 “但现在你必须把梅林和摩根还给我们。”

The wizard looked at his sister. She nodded. The wizard held out Merlin’s Staff of Strength. “Use this to bring them back,” he said. “Hold it tightly and call out for them.” He gave the staff to Annie. 这位巫师看着他的妹妹。她点了点头。 巫师拿出了梅林的力量法杖。 “用它把他们带回来，”他说。 “紧紧握住它，然后大声呼唤他们。”他把法杖交给了安妮。

Annie could barely lift it by herself. “Hold it with me, Jack,” she said. 安妮一个人几乎抬不起来。 “和我一起握着它，杰克，”她说。

Jack stepped forward and grabbed the magic staff. The smooth, golden wood felt warm and vibrant in his hands. 杰克向前走，拿起那根魔法杖。 光滑的金色木头在他手中感觉温暖而有活力。

As they gripped the staff together, Annie threw back her head and called out: “Merlin and Morgan, come back!” 当他们一起握住魔杖时，安妮仰起头喊道：“梅林和摩根，回来吧！”

A long burst of blue light shot out of the end of the staff—and flashed toward the two white wolves. 魔杖末端突然射出一道蓝色的光，直冲向那两只白狼。

Suddenly wolf eyes changed into human eyes! Wolf noses changed into human noses! Wolf mouths changed into human mouths! Wolf ears into human ears! Wolf paws into human hands and feet! Wolf fur into long red cloaks! 突然，狼的眼睛变成了人类的眼睛！ 狼的鼻子变成了人类的鼻子！ 狼的嘴巴变成了人类的嘴巴！ 狼的耳朵变成了人类的耳朵！ 狼的爪子变成了人类的手和脚！ 狼的毛皮变成了长长的红色斗篷！

The two white wolves were gone, and a man and a woman stood in their places. 两只白狼不见了，取而代之的是一个男人和一个女人。
