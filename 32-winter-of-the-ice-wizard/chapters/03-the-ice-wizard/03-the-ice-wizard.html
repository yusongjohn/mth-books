3. The Ice Wizard 3. 冰巫师

The air inside the palace was even colder than the air outside. Moonlight flooded through tall arches in the walls. The floor shined like a skating rink. Thick columns of sparkling ice held up a domed ceiling. 宫殿里的空气比外面还要冷。 月光从墙上的高拱门中洒进来。 地板亮得像一个溜冰场。 闪闪发光的冰柱支撑着圆顶的天花板。

“WELCOME, JACK AND ANNIE,” boomed a voice from beyond the columns. “欢迎你，杰克和安妮，”一个声音从冰柱后面传来。

Jack gasped. “Is that Merlin?” he whispered. 杰克惊呼起来。 “那是梅林吗？”他小声说。

“It does not sound like Merlin,” whispered Teddy. “但那声音不像是梅林，”泰迪小声说。

“But how does he know our names?” whispered Annie. “可是他怎么知道我们的名字呢？”安妮小声说。

“COME, JACK AND ANNIE. I HAVE BEEN WAITING FOR YOU,” bellowed the voice. “来吧，杰克和安妮。” 我一直在等你们，”声音大声喊道。

“Maybe it is Merlin!” said Annie. “Maybe he’s just using a different voice! Come on!” “也许真的是梅林！”安妮说。 “也许他只是用了不同的声音！快过来！”

“Annie, wait!” Jack called. But Annie had already disappeared into the sparkly room. “We have to follow her,” he said to Teddy and Kathleen. “安妮，等等！”杰克喊道。 但是安妮已经消失在那个闪闪发光的房间里了。 “我们必须跟着她，”他对泰迪和凯瑟琳说。

The three of them hurried after Annie. Beyond the columns, steps made of carved ice led up to a platform. Sitting on a throne on the platform was a huge bearded man. 他们三个赶紧追了上去。 在柱子后面，由雕刻的冰制成的台阶通向一个平台。 坐在平台上的王座上的是一个满脸胡须的大男人。

The man on the throne was definitely not Merlin. He was dressed in a worn robe trimmed with dirty fur. He had a rugged, weather-beaten face, a bushy beard, and a black eye patch. He leaned forward and glared down at Annie with his one good eye. 坐在王座上的人肯定不是梅林。 他穿着一件破旧的长袍，边缘镶着脏兮兮的毛皮。 他有一张粗糙、被风吹得发黑的脸，浓密的胡须，还有一只黑色的眼罩。 他向前倾身，用那只好的眼睛瞪着安妮。

“Who are you?” he demanded. “I was expecting Jack and Annie of Frog Creek.” “你们是谁？”他厉声问道。 “我原本是等着蛙溪镇的杰克和安妮的。”

Annie stepped toward the throne. “I am Annie and he’s Jack,” she said. “And these are our friends Teddy and Kathleen. We come in peace.” 安妮朝王座走去。 “我是安妮，他是杰克，”她说。 “还有我们的朋友泰迪和凯瑟琳。 我们是来和平的。”

“Annie? Jack?” the man snorted. “You are not Annie and Jack! You are far too small!” “安妮？” “杰克？”那人哼了一声。 “你不是安妮和杰克！ “你太小了！”

“We’re not so small,” said Annie. “I’m nine. Jack’s ten.” “我们并不小，”安妮说。 “我九岁，杰克十岁。”

“But you are children,” the man said with scorn. “Jack and Annie are heroes!” “但你们只是孩子，”那人带着轻蔑说。 “杰克和安妮是英雄！”

“Well, I don’t know if I’d call us heroes,” said Annie. “But we sometimes help Merlin and Morgan le Fay.” “嗯，我不确定我们是不是英雄，”安妮说。 “但我们有时会帮助梅林和摩根·勒·法伊。”

“Annie, shhh!” said Jack. He didn’t trust the man on the throne and worried that Annie was saying too much. “安妮，嘘——！”杰克说。 他不信任宝座上的那个人，担心安妮说得太多了。

But Annie went on. “In fact, Merlin told us to come to the Land-Behind-the-Clouds today,” she said. “He sent us a message written on a stone.” 但安妮继续说下去。 “事实上，梅林告诉我们今天要来到云后之国，”她说。 “他给我们发了一块石头上写的讯息。”

“Ah … ,” said the man on the throne. “Perhaps you really are Jack and Annie.” He leaned forward and spoke in a low voice: “啊……”，宝座上的男人说。 “也许你们真的是杰克和安妮。”他向前倾身，低声说道：

To Jack and Annie of Frog Creek: 致蛙溪镇的杰克和安妮：

My Staff of Strength has been stolen. On the winter solstice, journey to the Land-Behind-the-Clouds. Travel toward the setting sun and retrieve my staff—or all will be lost. 我的力量之杖被偷走了。 在冬至那天，前往云后之国。 朝落日的方向前进，把我的法杖拿回来——否则一切都会失去。

Jack didn’t understand. “How … ?” 杰克不明白。 “我怎么知道梅林的信息里写了什么？”那人说。

“How do I know what was in Merlin’s message?” the man said. “I know because I wrote it myself! I hoped it would find its way to you somehow.” “我知道，因为我自己写的！ 我希望它能以某种方式传到你们手里。”

Jack stepped back. So Merlin hadn’t sent them on this mission at all. The weird man on the throne had tricked them! 杰克往后退了一步。 原来梅林根本没有派他们执行这次任务。 坐在王座上的奇怪男人骗了他们！

“Who are you?” demanded Teddy. “你是谁？”泰迪质问道。

“I am the Ice Wizard,” said the man. “The Wizard of Winter.” “我是冰巫师，”那人说。 “冬之巫师。”

Teddy gulped. 泰迪咽了口唾沫。

Oh, no! thought Jack. They had heard about this wizard on their past Merlin missions. It was the Wizard of Winter who had put a spell on the Raven King and who had stolen the Sword of Light! 哦，不！杰克心想。 他们以前在梅林的任务中听说过这位巫师。 正是冬之巫师给渡鸦王施了魔法，还偷走了光明之剑！

The wizard glanced coldly from Teddy to Kathleen. “And who are the two of you?” 巫师冷冷地从泰迪看向凯瑟琳。 “你们两个是谁？”

“I am Teddy of Camelot,” said Teddy. “I am an apprentice to Morgan le Fay, in training to be a sorcerer myself.” “我是来自卡梅洛特的泰迪，”泰迪说。 “我是摩根勒菲的学徒，正在接受训练，将来要成为一名法师。”

“A sorcerer?” said the wizard. “法师？”巫师说道。

“Yes,” said Teddy. “My father was a sorcerer. My mother was a wood sprite.” “是的，”泰迪说。 “我父亲是个巫师。 我母亲是个树精灵。”

“And I am a selkie,” said Kathleen, “one of the ancient seal people.” “而我是个海豹人，”凯瑟琳说，“我是古代海豹族的一员。”

“So you are both from my world,” said the Ice Wizard. “You are of no use to me.” He looked back at Jack and Annie. “I am interested only in the two mortals, Jack and Annie of Frog Creek.” “所以你们两个都来自我的世界，”冰巫师说。 “你们对我没用。”他回头看着杰克和安妮。 “我只对两个凡人感兴趣，蛙溪镇的杰克和安妮。”

“Why are you interested in us?” said Jack. “你为什么对我们感兴趣？”杰克说。

“Because of what you have done for Merlin!” bellowed the Ice Wizard. “For Merlin, you found the Water of Memory and Imagination! For Merlin, you found the Diamond of Destiny! For Merlin, you found the Sword of Light! Now I want you to find something for me.” “因为你们为梅林做过的事！”冰巫师大声喊道。 “为了梅林，你们找到了记忆与想象之水！” 为梅林，你们找到了命运之钻！ 为梅林，你们找到了光明之剑！ 现在，我想让你们为我找一样东西。

“What do you want us to find?” asked Annie. “你们要找什么？”安妮问道。

The Ice Wizard grabbed the black patch covering his left eye. He yanked it off, revealing a dark, empty socket underneath. 冰巫师抓起了遮住他左眼的黑色眼罩。 他猛地扯下来，露出下面一个漆黑的空眼窝。

“Yikes,” Annie said softly. “天啊，”安妮轻声说道。

“I want you to find my eye,” the Ice Wizard said. “我想让你们找到我的眼睛，”冰巫师说道。

“Oh, man,” said Jack. He was horrified. “哦，天哪，”杰克说。他吓坏了。

“Are—are you quite serious?” said Teddy. “You want them to find your eye?” “你、你真的要我们去找你的眼睛吗？”泰迪说。 “你想让他们找到你的眼睛？”

The wizard covered his empty eye socket with the patch again. “Yes,” he said. “I want Jack and Annie to find my eye—and bring it back to me.” 巫师又用眼罩遮住了他那只空荡荡的眼窝。 “是的，”他说。 “我想让杰克和安妮找到我的眼睛——然后把它带回来给我。”

“But—why?” said Jack. “Even if we found it, we couldn’t make it work. We’re not medical experts or anything.” “但是——为什么？”杰克说。 “就算我们找到了，我们也无法让它重新运作。 我们又不是什么医学专家之类的。”

“And anyway, why can’t you get your eye yourself?” said Annie. “You’re a wizard!” “再说，你为什么不能自己去找到你的眼睛呢？”安妮说。 “你是个巫师！”

“DO NOT QUESTION MY ORDERS!” the wizard roared at her. “不要质疑我的命令！”巫师对她吼道。

“Hey, don’t yell at my sister!” said Jack. “嘿，别对你姐姐大喊大叫！”杰克说。

The wizard raised a bushy eyebrow. “You are brother and sister?” he said. 巫师挑起了他浓密的眉毛。 “你们是兄妹吗？”他说。

“Yes,” said Jack. “是的，”杰克说。

The wizard nodded slowly. His voice grew softer. “And you protect your sister,” he said. 巫师慢慢地点了点头。 他的声音变得柔和了。 “你们保护你的姐姐，”他说。

“We protect each other,” said Jack. “我们互相保护，”杰克说。

“I see,” whispered the wizard. Then his voice turned gruff again. “Long ago, I traded my eye for something I wanted very much. But I never got what I wanted. So now I want my eye back.” “我明白了，”巫师低声说。 然后他的声音又变得粗声粗气了。 “很久以前，我用我的眼睛换到了我非常想要的东西。” 但我从来没有得到我想要的东西。 所以现在，我想要回我的眼睛。”

“Who did you trade with?” asked Annie. “你和谁交换的？”安妮问。

“The Fates!” said the wizard. “I traded with the Fates! But they cheated me! And that is why I sent for you and Jack. You must go to the Fates and find my eye, and you must go alone.” “是命运三女神！”巫师说。 “我跟命运三女神做了交易！ 但他们骗了我！ 这就是为什么我叫你和杰克来。 你必须去命运三女神那里找到我的眼睛，而且你必须独自去。

“Why alone?” asked Jack. “为什么必须独自去？”杰克问。

“Because only mortals can undo a bargain with the Fates,” said the Ice Wizard, “not wizards like me—nor seal girls, nor the sons of sorcerers, like your two friends.” “因为只有凡人才能和命运女神反悔协议，”冰巫师说，“像我这样的巫师，或者像你那两个朋友一样的女巫女儿或巫师的儿子，都不能反悔。”

“But Jack and I succeeded in our other missions because Teddy and Kathleen or Morgan and Merlin helped us,” said Annie. “但是杰克和我之前完成任务时，泰迪和凯瑟琳，或者摩根和梅林帮过我们，”安妮说。

“What kind of help did they give you?” said the wizard. “他们给了你们什么样的帮助？”巫师问。

“Well, mostly magic rhymes and riddles,” said Annie. “大部分是魔法的押韵诗和谜语，”安妮说。

“Ah. Then I shall do the same,” said the wizard. He thought for a moment, then leaned forward on his throne. In a growly voice, he said: 那么我也会这么做，”巫师说。 他想了一会儿，然后向前靠在了他的王座上。 他用低沉的声音说：

Take my sleigh 乘坐我的雪橇

And find your way 找到你的路

To the House of the Norns 去诺恩之家

In the curve of the bay. 在海湾的拐弯处。

Pay them whatever 付给他们任何东西

They tell you to pay. 他们告诉你该付什么。

And bring back my eye 然后把我的眼睛带回来

By break of day.

The wizard reached into the folds of his ragged robe and pulled out a thick string with a row of knots. “This wind-string will speed you on your journey,” he said. He tossed the string to Jack. 巫师把手伸进他破旧长袍的褶皱里，拿出了一根粗粗的绳子，上面系着一排绳结。 “这根风绳会加快你们的旅程，”他说。 他把绳子扔给了杰克。

What’s a wind-string? Jack wondered. And who are the Norns? 什么是风绳？杰克想知道。 那谁是诺恩？

Before Jack could ask any questions, the Ice Wizard pointed at him. “Now listen carefully to this warning,” he said. “Beware the white wolves of the night. They may follow you on your quest. Never let them catch up with you. If they catch you, they will eat you!” 杰克还没来得及问问题，冰巫师就指着他说。 “现在仔细听好了，这是警告，”他说。 “小心夜晚的白狼。 它们可能会在你的冒险中跟着你。 千万不要让它们追上你。 如果它们抓住你，它们就会吃掉你！”

Jack felt a chill run down his spine. 杰克感到一阵寒意顺着脊背流下。

The Ice Wizard picked up a carved wooden stick from the floor beside his throne. Its smooth, polished wood glowed in the moonlight. 冰巫师从他宝座旁边的地板上捡起一根雕刻过的木棍。 它光滑的木头在月光下闪闪发光。

Teddy gasped. “ ’ Tis Merlin’s Staff of Strength!” he said. 泰迪惊呼起来。 “这是梅林的力量之杖！”他说。

“Indeed,” said the wizard. He turned to Jack and Annie. “Go now and find my eye,” he said. “Or you will never see Merlin and Morgan le Fay again.” “确实如此，”巫师说道。 他转向杰克和安妮。 “现在去找到我的眼睛，”他说。 “否则你们再也见不到梅林和摩根勒法伊了。”

“What have you done with them?” cried Annie. “你对他们做了什么？”安妮喊道。

The wizard stared at her coldly. “I will not tell you,” he said. “You will see them again only if you return my eye before the break of day.” 巫师冷冷地盯着她。 “我不会告诉你们的，”他说。 “只有在天亮之前把我的眼睛找回来，你们才能再次见到他们。”

“But—” said Annie. “但是——”安妮说。

“No more questions!” said the wizard. “Be on your way!” Before any of them could speak, the Ice Wizard slashed the air with Merlin’s Staff of Strength and shouted a spell—“OW-NIGH!” “不许再问了！”巫师说道。 “快走吧！”还没等他们中任何人开口，冰巫师就用梅林的强力法杖划过空气，喊出一个咒语——“奥尼！”

A flash of blue fire shot from the end of the staff. In an instant, Jack, Annie, Teddy, and Kathleen found themselves outside the palace in the freezing night. 一道蓝色的火焰从法杖末端射出。 转眼间，杰克、安妮、泰迪和凯瑟琳发现自己站在宫殿外，寒冷的夜晚里。
