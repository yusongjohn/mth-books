2. Land-Behind-the-Clouds 2. 云后之地

Jack felt the sharp bite of an icy wind. He looked out the window with the others. “Oh, man,” he whispered. 杰克感觉到一阵刺骨的寒风。 他和其他人一起看向窗外。 “天啊，”他小声说。

The tree house was not in a tree—for there were no trees anywhere to be seen. Instead, it was sitting high on top of a steep snowdrift. Other drifts rose and fell across a vast snowy plain. Beyond the plain were hills and mountains. 树屋不在树上——因为周围根本没有树。 相反，它高高地坐在一个陡峭的雪堆上。 其他的雪堆在广阔的雪原上起伏着。 平原的另一边是山和山峰。

“The books were right,” said Teddy, his teeth chattering. “ ’ Tis bleak here indeed.” “书上说得对，”泰迪说，牙齿直打颤。 “这里确实很荒凉。”

“No, ’tis lovely,” said Kathleen. “ ’ Tis the land where the northern seal people live.” “不，这里很美，”凯瑟琳说。 “这是北方海豹人居住的地方。”

“Cool,” said Annie. “真凉快，”安妮说。

Jack dug his hands into his pockets. He agreed with Teddy. The land did seem bleak—and freezing! “I wonder where Merlin’s Staff of Strength is,” he said, shivering. 杰克把手插进了口袋里。 他同意泰迪的看法。 这片土地看起来确实荒凉——而且冷得要命！ “我想知道梅林的强大力量杖在哪里，”他说，瑟瑟发抖。

“Let us begin our search!” said Kathleen. “The message tells us we must travel toward the setting sun.” “让我们开始寻找吧！”凯瑟琳说。 “这条信息告诉我们，我们必须朝落日的方向前进。”

Kathleen climbed out of the tree house window. She gathered her cloak around her and sat down on top of the snowdrift. Then she pushed off and slid down the steep slope. 凯瑟琳从树屋的窗户爬了出来。 她把斗篷裹紧，坐在雪堆的顶部。 然后她推了一把，滑下了陡坡。

“Oh, wow. Wait for me!” called Annie. She climbed out the window and followed Kathleen. Whooping, she slid to the bottom of the snowdrift. “Come on, you guys! It’s fun!” she shouted. “哦，哇—— 等等我！”安妮喊道。 她从窗户爬出来，跟着凯瑟琳去了。 她一边欢呼一边滑到了雪堆的底部。 “快点，你们这些家伙！ “真好玩！”她喊道。

Jack and Teddy looked at each other. “Shall we?” said Teddy. Jack nodded. He pulled his scarf tighter around his neck and followed Teddy out of the window. 杰克和泰迪互相看了看。 “我们出发吧？”泰迪说。杰克点了点头。 他把围巾更紧地绕在脖子上，跟着泰迪从窗户爬了出去。

Jack and Teddy sat down side by side. They pushed off and slid down the icy snowdrift. Jack couldn’t help whooping, too. It was fun. 杰克和泰迪并排坐了下来。 他们一推，顺着冰冻的雪堆滑了下去。 杰克忍不住也大声欢呼起来。真好玩。

At the bottom of the drift, Jack and Teddy scrambled to their feet. Jack brushed the snow off his clothes. He could see his breath in the frigid air. 滑到雪堆底部时，杰克和泰迪赶紧站了起来。 杰克把衣服上的雪刷掉。 在寒冷的空气中，他能看到自己呼出的白气。

“It’s j-just a little chilly,” said Annie, hugging herself. “这、这只不过是有点冷而已，”安妮说着，抱紧了自己。

Only Kathleen seemed not to mind the cold. She was smiling as she lay on the ground, gazing up at the sky. Her seal nature probably keeps her warm, Jack thought with envy. 只有凯瑟琳似乎一点都不在意寒冷。 她躺在地上，微笑着仰望天空。 杰克羡慕地想，她的海豹特性大概让她保持温暖。

Teddy peered across the snowy plain. “I believe not one living creature is here but us,” he said. 泰迪眺望着雪地平原。 “我敢说这里除了我们之外，没有其他活着的生物，”他说。

“Not true at all,” said Kathleen. She pointed upward. “I see snow geese and whistling swans.” “一点都不对，”凯瑟琳说。 她指着上方。 “我看到雪雁和吹哨天鹅了。”

“I can almost see them, too,” said Annie. “我也差不多能看见它们，”安妮说。

Kathleen stood up. She shielded her eyes and gazed across the plain. The cold sun was low in the sky. It cast long blue shadows beneath the snowdrifts. She pointed into the distance. “And see? A white hare is leaping home before dark,” she said. 凯瑟琳站了起来。 她用手遮住眼睛，望向平原的另一边。 寒冷的太阳低低地挂在天空中。 它在雪堆下投下长长的蓝色影子。 她指着远处。 “看到了吗？”， “一只白色的野兔正在天黑前跳回家，”她说。

Jack looked where Kathleen pointed, but he couldn’t see anything moving at all. 杰克看向凯瑟琳指的地方，但他什么都没看到在动。

“I see a snowy owl, too,” said Kathleen, “and—oh, no!” “我也看到一只雪鸮，”凯瑟琳说，“啊——不好！”

“What?” said Annie. “什么？”安妮说。

“Wolves,” Kathleen said with a shudder. “They just disappeared behind a snowdrift. My people greatly fear the wolves.” “狼，”凯瑟琳颤抖着说。 “他们刚刚消失在雪堆后面。 我的族人非常害怕狼。”

“You need not be afraid. I shall protect you,” said Teddy. He took Kathleen’s hand. “Come! Let us make haste toward the sun!” “你不必害怕。 我会保护你，”泰迪说。 他握住了凯瑟琳的手。 “来吧！ 让我们赶快朝太阳的方向跑！”

Together, Teddy and Kathleen headed across the snow-covered plain. Their woolen cloaks waved behind them. Annie and Jack dug their hands in their pockets and quickly followed them toward the setting sun. 泰迪和凯瑟琳一起，穿过那片被雪覆盖的平原。 他们的羊毛斗篷在他们身后飘扬。 安妮和杰克把手伸进口袋，赶紧朝落日的方向跟了上去。

As Teddy, Kathleen, Jack, and Annie trudged across the frozen plain, the sun sank closer and closer to the horizon. Its last rays poured purple-pink light over the snow. 泰迪、凯瑟琳、杰克和安妮踏着结冰的平原艰难前行，太阳越来越接近地平线。 最后的几缕阳光把雪地染成了紫粉色。

The wind blew against Jack’s face. He looked down and kept walking. The cold felt like needles on his skin. Each icy breath was painful. He hoped they found Merlin’s Staff of Strength soon. He couldn’t imagine anyone surviving for long in this lonely, freezing land. 风吹在杰克的脸上。 他低头看了看，继续往前走。 寒冷的感觉就像针一样扎在皮肤上。 每一次呼吸都让人感到疼痛。 他希望他们能尽快找到梅林的力量之杖。 他无法想象有人能在这片孤独又寒冷的土地上待太久。

Jack’s thoughts were interrupted when he heard Annie calling. He looked up. The sun had completely slipped behind the horizon. In the cold twilight, the snow had faded from purple-pink to a dark shade of blue. 杰克正在思考时，突然听到安妮在喊他。他抬起头。 太阳已经完全溜到地平线后面了。 在寒冷的暮色中，雪地的颜色从紫粉色变成了深蓝色。

“Jack! Come look!” Annie called. She, Teddy, and Kathleen were standing on the slope of a huge snowdrift. “杰克！ 快来看！”安妮喊道。 她、泰迪和凯瑟琳正站在一个巨大的雪堆的斜坡上。

Jack hurried to join them. 杰克赶紧跑过去加入他们。

“Look!” said Annie. “看！”安妮说。

“Oh, man,” Jack said softly. “哦，天哪，”杰克轻声说。

On the other side of the snowdrift was a glimmering palace made from huge blocks of ice. 雪堆的另一边是一座闪闪发光的宫殿，由巨大的冰块建成。

Beneath the rising moon, its gleaming spires pierced the blue dusk. 在升起的月光下，它闪闪发光的尖塔刺破了蓝色的黄昏。

“I wonder who lives there … ,” said Jack. “我好奇那里住着谁……”杰克说。

“Let us go and find out!” said Teddy. “我们去瞧瞧吧！”泰迪说。

Teddy led the way down the slope to the ice palace. Long icicles hung like spears in front of the entrance. 泰迪带头沿着斜坡走向冰宫殿。 长长的冰锥像矛一样挂在入口前面。

“It seems no one has visited this place in quite a while,” said Kathleen. “看起来这里很久没人来过了，”凯瑟琳说。

“Indeed,” said Teddy. He broke off several icicles, and they clattered to the ground. “Onward?” he said. “确实如此，”泰迪说。 他折断了几根冰锥，它们“哐啷”一声掉在地上。 “继续前进？”他说。

The others nodded. 其他人点了点头。

Kicking aside the chunks of ice, Teddy led them all into the ice palace. 泰迪踢开冰块，领着大家走进了冰宫殿。
