6. In the Hollow Hill 6. 在空山丘

Standing in the cold, Jack felt afraid again. There were big paw prints in the moonlit snow all around the house. 站在寒冷中，杰克又感到害怕了。 房子周围月光下的雪地上有大大的爪子印。

“The wolves were here,” said Kathleen. “狼在这里待过，”凯瑟琳说。

“Perhaps we should go back inside,” said Teddy. “也许我们应该回屋里去，”泰迪说。

“No,” said Kathleen. “We must walk with Jack and Annie back to the sleigh and send them on their journey to the Hollow Hill.” “不，”凯瑟琳说。 “我们必须陪杰克和安妮走回雪橇，送他们去空山丘。”

“Yes, of course,” said Teddy, nodding. “是的，当然，”泰迪点头说。

As they all headed cautiously toward the rocks, Jack glanced back at the House of the Norns. He wished they could return to its cozy warmth. 当他们小心地朝岩石走去时，杰克回头看了看诺恩之屋。 他希望他们能回到那温暖舒适的屋子里。

Kathleen put her hand on his shoulder. “Come,” she said. “You must hurry.” 凯瑟琳把手放在他的肩膀上。 “来，”她说。 “你们必须快点。”

Jack trudged with the others through the passage in the rocks. When they got to the other side, there was no sign of the two white wolves. The silver sleigh was waiting in the moonlight. Jack and Annie climbed inside it. 杰克和大家一起踩着岩石中的通道往前走。 当他们走到另一边时，那两只白狼已经不见了。 那辆银色的雪橇在月光下等着他们。 杰克和安妮爬进了雪橇里。

“Can’t you come with us?” Jack asked Teddy and Kathleen. “Remember you said if we all work together, we can do anything?” “你们不能和我们一起去吗？”杰克问泰迪和凯瑟琳。 “还记得你说过，如果我们齐心协力，就能做到任何事吗？”

“Aye,” said Teddy. “But what the Ice Wizard said is true. Only mortals can undo a bargain with the Fates.” “对，”泰迪说。 “但冰巫师说的也是真的。 只有凡人能与命运女神解除契约。”

“Do not fear,” said Kathleen. “We will be with you in spirit. And we will meet you back at the wizard’s palace at dawn.” “别害怕，”凯瑟琳说。 “我们会以精神陪伴你。 天亮时，我们会回到巫师的宫殿与你汇合。”

“How will you get there?” asked Annie. “你们怎么去那里？”安妮问。

“I have a few rhymes I can try,” said Teddy, smiling. “我有一些咒语可以试试，”泰迪微笑着说。

“And I have a bit of selkie magic,” said Kathleen. “我有一些海豹女巫的魔法，”凯瑟琳说。

“And we have our wind-string!” said Annie. “And we have our wind-string!” said Annie.

“Hasten, then, to the Hollow Hill,” said Kathleen. “Hasten, then, to the Hollow Hill,” said Kathleen.

“And remember what the Norns told you,” said Teddy. “ Never look at the Frost Giant.” “And remember what the Norns told you,” said Teddy. “Never look at the Frost Giant.”

“I know,” said Jack. He pulled out the wind-string. He took off his gloves and untied a knot. A breeze began to blow. “I know,” said Jack. 他拉出了风绳。 他摘下手套，解开一个结。 一阵微风开始吹起来。

Jack untied a second knot. The breeze grew stronger, the sail unfurled, and the runners slid forward. 杰克解开了第二个结。 风变得更强了，帆张开了，滑行器向前滑动了。

Jack untied a third knot. The wind blew hard. The white sail snapped, and the sleigh took off through the night. 杰克解开第三个结。 风猛烈地吹着。 白色的帆突然绷紧，雪橇在夜里飞快地冲了出去。

“Stand fast!” Teddy called after them. “站稳！”泰迪在后面喊道。

Jack and Annie waved good-bye to Teddy and Kathleen as the sleigh slid swiftly over the sea ice. Soon the sleigh bumped onto the snow-covered plain and veered off sharply to the right. 杰克和安妮向泰迪和凯瑟琳挥手告别，雪橇迅速滑过海冰。 很快，雪橇撞上了覆盖着雪的平原，猛地向右转去。

“No, toward the North Star!” Jack called to Annie. “不对，要朝北极星的方向！”杰克对安妮喊道。

Annie turned the rudder, steering the sleigh back on course. They sailed toward the bright star in the distance. 安妮转动舵轮，把雪橇重新拉回正确的路线。 他们朝着远处那颗明亮的星星驶去。

As the silver runners swished across the windswept snow, Jack braced himself against the cold. He kept a lookout for the white wolves, but he didn’t see any sign of them as the sleigh sped across the moonlit plain. 当银色的滑板在被风吹过的雪地上滑行时，杰克紧紧地靠在寒冷中。 他一直在留意白色的狼，但雪橇飞快地穿过月光照亮的平原时，他什么也没看到。

Soon he could see a row of snow-covered hills in the distance. “Look!” he said. “There it is!” He pointed to one of the hills—the only one without a peak. 很快，他看见远处有一排被雪覆盖的山丘。 “看！”他说。 “那就是！”他指着其中一座山丘——那是唯一一座没有山顶的山丘。

“Tie her down!” Annie shouted. “把她绑住！”安妮喊道。

Jack tied a knot in the string, and the sleigh began to slow down. He tied a second, then a third. The wind died down completely, and the sleigh coasted to a stop at the foot of the Hollow Hill. Jack and Annie climbed out. 杰克在绳子上打了个结，雪橇开始慢下来。 他又打了一个结，然后又打了一个结。 风完全停了，雪橇滑行着停在了空心山丘的脚下。 杰克和安妮爬了下来。

Jack looked up at the steep white slope. “How do we get inside?” he said. 杰克抬头看着那陡峭的白色山坡。 “我们怎么进去呢？”他说。

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “How do you think the Frost Giant gets inside?” “我不知道，”安妮说。 “你觉得冰霜巨人是怎么进去的？”

“Oh … the Frost Giant,” said Jack. He really wished Teddy and Kathleen were with them. He felt as if part of their team was missing. “哦……冰霜巨人，”杰克说。 他真希望泰迪和凯瑟琳和他们在一起。 他感觉好像队伍里少了一员大将。

Annie seemed to read his thoughts. “We can do it,” she said. “We have to—for Morgan and Merlin.” 安妮似乎看穿了他的想法。 “我们能做到，”她说。 “我们必须做到——为了摩根和梅林。”

Jack nodded. “You’re right,” he said. They studied the hill in the moonlight. 杰克点了点头。 “你没错，”他说。 他们在月光下研究着那座小山。

“Up there—is that an opening?” said Annie. “那儿——是不是有个入口？”安妮说。

“Maybe,” said Jack. “Let’s climb up and check it out.” When they climbed a little way up the hill, Jack could clearly see a break in the snow-covered slope. “可能吧，”杰克说。 “我们上去看看吧。”当他们爬了一段路后，杰克清楚地看到雪覆盖的斜坡上有个缺口。

“Let’s see if it leads inside!” said Annie. “我们去看看它是不是通到里面！”安妮说。

“Wait, what about the Frost Giant?” said Jack. “等等，那冰霜巨人呢？”杰克说。

“I have a feeling he’s not here right now,” said Annie. “We’d better go in and find the wizard’s eye before he comes back.” “我有种感觉，他现在不在这里，”安妮说。 “我们最好进去，先找到巫师的眼睛，免得他回来。”

“Okay,” said Jack. “But be careful!” “好的，”杰克说。 “但是要小心！”

They scurried farther up the slope. When they came to the opening, they stepped through the huge crack into the hill. 他们赶紧爬得更高了一些。 当他们来到洞口时，他们从山丘上的巨大裂缝中走了进去。

Jack and Annie found themselves on a ledge above a deep, rounded hollow. Moonlight flooded down through the open hilltop. At the bottom of the hollow was a flat spot where it looked as if the snow had been blown in circles. 杰克和安妮发现自己站在一个深而圆的凹地边缘上。 月光从山顶的空隙中洒了下来。 在凹地的底部，有一个平坦的地方，看起来像是雪被吹成了一个个圆圈。

“That must be where the giant sleeps!” said Annie. “那一定是巨人睡觉的地方！”安妮说。

“Yeah, and it’s probably where he hides the eye,” said Jack. “We just have to find a hole. Remember?” He repeated what the Norns had said: “对，而且他可能还把眼睛藏在那里，”杰克说。 “我们只要找到一个洞就行。 还记得吗？”他重复了诺恩们说过的话：

In the Hollow Hill is a hole. 在空心山丘里有个洞。

In the hole is a hailstone. 洞里有一块冰雹石。

In the heart of the hailstone 在冰雹石的中心

Hides the wizard’s eye. 藏着巫师的眼睛。

“Right,” said Annie. “对，”安妮说。

Jack looked down at the snowy swirl. He looked back at Annie. “Onward?” 杰克低头看着那片雪白的漩涡。 他回头看向安妮。 “向前？”

“Onward,” she whispered. “向前，”她小声说。

Jack and Annie scrambled down into the hollow. Stepping carefully through the silver moonlight, they studied the ground, looking for the hole. 杰克和安妮赶紧爬进树洞里。 他们小心翼翼地踩着银色的月光，仔细观察地面，寻找那个洞。

Annie stumbled and fell. “Whoa!” she said. “I think I just found the hole! I stepped in it!” 安妮绊了一跤，摔倒了。 “哇！”她说。 “我想我找到那个洞了！我踩到它了！”，

“Really?” said Jack. He knelt down beside her. “真的吗？”杰克说。 他蹲下来，站在她旁边。

Annie reached down into a small hole in the floor of the hollow. “There’s something in here!” she said. She pulled out a chunk of ice the size of an egg. “The hailstone!” 安妮伸手到树洞地板上的一个小洞里。 “这里面有东西！”，她说。 她拿出一块像鸡蛋一样大小的冰块。 “这就是冰雹！”，

In the dim light, it was impossible to see if anything was inside the ice chunk. “We don’t know if this is the right hailstone,” said Jack. “We’ll have to wait till daylight to see if the eye’s in there.” 在昏暗的光线下，根本看不清冰块里有没有东西。 “我们不知道这颗冰雹是不是对的，”杰克说。 “我们得等到天亮才能看看眼睛是不是在里面。”

“It has to be the right one,” said Annie. “How many hailstones are hidden in a hole in a hollow hill?” “一定是对的那颗，”安妮说。 “空山洞里藏着多少冰雹石？”

“Good point,” said Jack. “好主意，”杰克说。

Annie turned the hailstone over in her hand. “Maybe the eye is looking at us now,” she said. 安妮在手里翻来覆去地看那颗冰雹。 “也许那只眼睛正在看着我们，”她说。

“That’s scientifically impossible,” said Jack. “An eye can’t see unless it’s connected to a brain.” “这在科学上是不可能的，”杰克说。 “除非眼睛和大脑连在一起，否则眼睛是看不见的。”

“Yeah, and a string can’t make the wind blow, either,” said Annie. “Forget science in this place. Wait—” She caught her breath. “Did you feel that?” “是啊，绳子也不能让风吹起来，”安妮说。 “在这个地方，忘掉科学吧。 等一下—”她屏住呼吸。 “你感觉到了吗？”

“Feel what?” said Jack. “感觉什么？”杰克说。

“The ground’s shaking,” said Annie. “地面在震动，”安妮说。

Jack did feel the ground trembling. He heard a strange sound, too—a loud huffing sound coming from outside the hill—HUFFFF, HUFFFF, HUFFFF.… It sounded like breathing! 杰克确实感觉到地面在颤抖。 他还听到了一个奇怪的声音——从山丘外面传来的一种响亮的呼气声——呼ffff，呼ffff，呼ffff……听起来像是在呼吸！

“The giant’s back!” said Annie. “巨人回来了！”安妮说。

“Oh, no!” cried Jack. “哦，不！”杰克喊道。

The ground kept rumbling. The breathing sounds got louder. 地面一直在震动。 呼吸声越来越响了。

“Hide the hailstone!” said Jack. “把冰雹藏好！”杰克说。

Annie shoved the ice chunk into her pocket. 安妮把冰块塞进了口袋里。

HUFFFF, HUFFFF, HUFFFF.… It sounded like the giant was entering the hollow! “呼——呼——呼——……听起来好像巨人在进入洞穴！

“He’s coming!” said Annie. “他来了！”安妮说。

“Hide!” whispered Jack. “藏好！”杰克低声说。

Jack pulled Annie into the shadows. He remembered the gray Norn’s warning: Anyone who looks directly at the Frost Giant will freeze to death at once. 杰克把安妮拉进了阴影里。 他想起了灰色诺恩的警告：任何直接看向冰霜巨人的人，都会立刻被冻死。

“Whatever you do, don’t look at him!” he whispered to Annie. “不管你做什么，都不要看他！”他对安妮低声说。

Crouching in the dark, they buried their faces in their hands and waited.… 躲在黑暗中，他们把脸埋在手里，等着……
