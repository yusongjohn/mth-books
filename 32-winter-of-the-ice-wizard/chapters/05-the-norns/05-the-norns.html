5. The Norns 5. 命运女神

Teddy slammed the door against the wolves. Jack caught his breath. 泰迪把门关上，挡住了狼群。 杰克喘了口气。

“Welcome!” the three Norns said in unison. They all looked exactly alike, except they wore gowns of different colors—blue, brown, and gray. “欢迎！”三个命运女神异口同声地说。 她们看起来一模一样，只是穿的衣服颜色不同——蓝色、棕色和灰色。

“How are you, Jack, Annie, Teddy, and Kathleen?” said the blue Norn. “你们好吗，杰克、安妮、泰迪和凯瑟琳？”蓝色的命运女神问道。

“We’re good now, ” said Annie. “我们现在很好，”安妮说。

Jack was amazed that the Norns knew all their names. Despite their strange appearance, their friendly smiles and twinkling eyes put him at ease. In their cozy house, he began to feel warm for the first time since they had left home. 杰克惊讶地发现命运女神竟然知道他们的名字。 尽管她们看起来很奇怪，但她们友好的微笑和闪亮的眼睛让他感到很放松。 在他们温暖的房子里，他第一次离开家后感到暖洋洋的。

“Was your journey pleasant?” asked the brown Norn. “你们的旅程愉快吗？”棕色的诺恩问道。

“Yes. We came in the Ice Wizard’s sleigh,” said Annie. “是的。 我们坐的是冰巫师的雪橇，”安妮说。

“With the help of a wind-string,” said Teddy. Jack held up the string to show them. “还有一根风绳的帮助，”泰迪说。 杰克举起那根绳子给他们看。

The gray Norn cackled. “Yes, we know! I like a string with knots,” she said. 灰色的诺恩咯咯地笑了起来。 “是的，我们知道！ 我喜欢有结的绳子，”她说。

“A string without knots would be a boring string indeed!” said the blue Norn. “一根没有结的绳子确实会很无聊！”蓝颜色的诺恩说。

“A life without knots would be a boring life indeed!” chimed in the brown Norn. “没有结的生活确实会很无聊！”棕色的诺恩说道。

As they spoke, the Norns kept weaving. Their bulging eyes never blinked. Jack sensed that they never closed their eyes—or stopped their work. 他们说话时，诺恩们一直在编织着。 他们鼓胀的眼睛从不眨一下。 杰克感觉到他们的眼睛从未闭上——或者停止过工作。

“Sorry to bother you,” said Annie. “But Jack and I need the eye of the Ice Wizard of Winter so we can save our friends Merlin and Morgan.” “抱歉打扰你们，”安妮说道。 “但杰克和我需要冬之冰巫师的眼睛，这样我们才能救我们的朋友梅林和摩根。”

“We know,” said the blue Norn. “We are weaving the story of the Ice Wizard now. Come look.” “我们知道，”蓝颜色的诺恩说道。 “我们现在正在编织冰巫师的故事。来，看看吧。”

Jack moved with the others to the loom. Dozens of tiny pictures were woven into the tapestry. The threads were all wintry colors—blues, grays, and browns. 杰克和其他人一起走到织布机前。 织锦上织进了数十个小小的画面。 这些线都是冬天的颜色——蓝色、灰色和棕色。

“The pictures tell the story of the wizard’s life,” explained the brown Norn. “这些画讲述的是巫师的一生，”棕色的诺恩解释道。

One picture showed two children playing together. Another showed a boy running after a swan. Another showed two white wolves—and another showed an eye in a circle. 有一幅画显示两个孩子在一起玩耍。 另一幅画显示一个男孩在追一只天鹅。 另一幅画显示两只白狼——还有一幅画显示一个圆圈里的眼睛。

“What’s the story of the eye?” Jack asked. “眼睛的故事是什么？”杰克问。

“Long ago, the Ice Wizard came to us seeking all the wisdom of the world,” said the gray Norn. “We said we would give him wisdom if he gave us one of his eyes. He agreed to the bargain.” “很久以前，冰巫师来到我们这里，想要获得全世界的智慧，”灰发诺恩说。 “我们说，如果他给我们他的一只眼睛，我们就给他智慧。 他同意了这个交易。”

“The wizard doesn’t seem very wise,” said Annie. “这个巫师看起来一点都不聪明，”安妮说。

“Indeed he is not,” said the brown Norn. “We planted the seeds of wisdom in his heart, but they never grew.” “他确实不是，”棕色的诺恩说道。 “我们在他心里种下了智慧的种子，但它们从未发芽。”

“Why did you want his eye?” asked Jack. “你们为什么要他的眼睛？”杰克问。

“We wished to give it to the Frost Giant,” said the blue Norn. “我们希望把它送给霜巨人，”蓝色的诺恩说道。

“The Frost Giant?” said Teddy. “Who is the Frost Giant?” “霜巨人？”泰迪说。 “霜巨人是谁？”

“He is neither magician nor mortal,” said the blue Norn. “He is a blind force of nature that spares nothing in his path.” “他既不是魔术师，也不是凡人，”蓝色的诺恩说道。 “他是一个盲目的自然力量，所到之处什么都不留。”

“We hoped the Frost Giant would use the wizard’s eye to see the beauty of the world, so he might choose to care for it rather than destroy it,” said the brown Norn. “But alas, the Frost Giant does not use our gift at all! Instead, he keeps it hidden away—right where we left it!” “我们希望霜巨人能用巫师的眼睛看到世界的美丽，这样他也许会选择保护它，而不是摧毁它，”棕色的诺恩说道。 “但唉，霜巨人根本没有使用我们的礼物！” 相反，他把它藏了起来——就在我们放它的地方！”

“Where’s that?” asked Annie. “那在哪儿？”安妮问。

“The Frost Giant sleeps inside the Hollow Hill,” said the gray Norn. “冰霜巨人睡在空山丘里，”灰色的诺恩说。

“In the Hollow Hill is a hole,” said the blue Norn. “在空山丘里有个洞，”蓝色的诺恩说。

“In the hole is a hailstone,” said the brown Norn. “在洞里有一块冰雹石，”棕色的诺恩说。

“And in the heart of the hailstone hides the wizard’s eye,” said the gray Norn. “在冰雹的中心藏着巫师的眼睛，”灰色的诺恩说道。

Jack closed his eyes and repeated: 杰克闭上眼睛，重复道：

In the Hollow Hill is a hole. 在空山丘里有一个洞。

In the hole is a hailstone. 在洞里有一块冰雹。

In the heart of the hailstone 在冰雹的中心

Hides the wizard’s eye. 藏起了巫师的眼睛。

“Yes!” said the gray Norn. “That is where you must go. But beware: You must never look directly at the Frost Giant. Anyone who looks directly at the Frost Giant will freeze to death at once.” “对！”灰发诺恩说道。 “你必须去那里。 但要小心：你绝不能直接盯着霜巨人看。 任何直接盯着霜巨人看的人都会立刻被冻死。”

Jack shivered and nodded. 杰克打了个哆嗦，点了点头。

“Well, we’d better get going,” said Annie. “Thanks for your help. The Ice Wizard’s rhyme tells us to pay you whatever you ask us to pay.” “好吧，我们最好赶紧出发，”安妮说。 “谢谢你帮我们。 冰巫师的咒语告诉我们，要付给你我们愿意付的任何东西。”

The Norns looked at each other. “I like that weaving around her neck,” the gray Norn said to her sisters. “ ’ Tis red like the fiery dawn.” The other two Norns nodded eagerly. 诺恩们互相看了看。 “我喜欢她脖子上那条围巾，”灰色的诺恩对她的姐妹们说。 “它是红色的，就像火红的黎明。”另外两个诺恩高兴地点头。

“My scarf?” said Annie. “Sure. Here.” She took off her red woolen scarf and placed it on the floor near the Norns’ loom. “我的围巾？”安妮说。 “当然可以。 给你。”她取下自己红色的羊毛围巾，放在诺恩们织布机旁边。

“Lovely!” said the blue Norn. “Perhaps we will stop weaving fates and start weaving scarves!” “真漂亮！”蓝色的诺恩说。 “也许我们该停止编织命运，开始编织围巾了！”

The other Norns cackled. “Well, go now,” said the gray Norn. “Travel toward the North Star. When you reach the snowy hills, look for the one whose peak is missing.” 其他诺恩咯咯地笑了起来。 “那么，快走吧，”灰色的诺恩说。 “朝着北极星的方向前进。” 当你到达白雪皑皑的山丘时，找那个山顶缺失的那座山。”

Jack, Annie, and Teddy started toward the door, but Kathleen stayed behind. “Forgive me, but I have one more question,” she said. She pointed to the picture of the swan and the boy on the tapestry. “What is this story?” 杰克、安妮和泰迪朝门口走去，但凯瑟琳留在了后面。 “请原谅我，但我还有一个问题，”她说。 她指着挂毯上天鹅和男孩的图案。 “这是什么故事？”

“ ’Tis a sad tale,” said the gray Norn. “The Ice Wizard had a younger sister who loved him more than anything in the world. One day they fought over something foolish. He lost his temper and told her to leave him alone forever. She ran down to the sea in tears. There she found a flock of swan maidens. They gave her a white feathered dress. She put on the dress and became a swan maiden herself. She flew away with the others and never returned.” “这是一个悲伤的故事，”灰发诺恩说。 “冰巫师有一个妹妹，她比世界上任何东西都更爱他。 有一天，他们为了一个愚蠢的事情争吵起来。 他暴跳如雷，让她永远离开自己。 她哭着跑到了海边。 她在那儿发现了一群天鹅少女。 她们给她一件白色羽毛的裙子。 她穿上裙子，自己也变成了天鹅少女。 她和其他人一起飞走了，再也没有回来。”

“After that the Ice Wizard was never the same,” said the blue Norn. “When his sister left, he grew cold and mean-spirited. ’Twas as if his sister took his heart with her when she flew away.” “从那以后，冰巫师就再也没有从前那样了，”蓝皮肤的诺恩说道。 “当他姐姐离开后，他变得冷酷又小气。” 就好像他姐姐飞走的时候，把他的心也带走了。”

“That is sad,” said Annie. “How will the Ice Wizard’s story end?” “那太糟糕了，”安妮说。 “冰巫师的故事会怎么结束呢？”

“You—not we—will determine the threads we weave next,” said the brown Norn. “你——而不是我们——将决定我们下一步编织的线，”棕色的诺恩说道。

“We will?” said Annie. “我们要吗？”安妮说。

“Yes,” said the gray Norn. “Our powers are fading. Our plans no longer work the way we expect them to. The Ice Wizard has no wisdom! The Frost Giant has no sight! You must go now and finish the story.” “是的，”灰发诺恩说。 “我们的力量正在减弱。 我们的计划不再像我们期望的那样起作用了。 冰巫师没有智慧！ 霜巨人没有视力！ 你们现在必须离开，去完成这个故事。”

The three sisters smiled at their visitors. Their skinny fingers fluttered over their weaving like butterflies over flowers. 三个姐妹对他们的访客微笑着。 她们瘦长的手指在编织上翻飞，就像蝴蝶在花丛中飞舞。

Jack couldn’t help smiling back at them. But then he thought about Merlin and Morgan. He thought about all the dangers waiting outside. “One last question,” he said. “What’s the story of the two white wolves?” 杰克忍不住朝她们笑了笑。 但后来他想到了梅林和摩根。 他想到了外面等待着的各种危险。 “最后一个问题，”他说。 “那两只白狼的故事是什么？”

“Oh, the wolves!” said the blue Norn. “Do not fear the wolves! A life without wolves would be a boring life indeed!” Her two sisters smiled in agreement. For the moment, their smiles made Jack feel unafraid of the white wolves—and the Ice Wizard and the Frost Giant, too. “哦，是狼啊！”蓝皮肤的诺恩说。 “不要害怕狼！ 没有狼的生活，确实会很无聊！”她的两个姐妹微笑着表示赞同。 那一刻，她们的笑容让杰克不再害怕白狼，甚至也不害怕冰巫师和霜巨人了。

“Good-bye! Good-bye! Good-bye!” said the three sisters. “再见！再见！ 再见！”三个姐妹说。

Jack and the others waved good-bye. Then they slipped out of the House of the Norns and into the icy night. 杰克和其他人挥手说再见。 然后他们溜出了命运之屋，走进了寒冷的夜晚。
