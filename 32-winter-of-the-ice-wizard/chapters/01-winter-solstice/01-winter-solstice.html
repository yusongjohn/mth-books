1. Winter Solstice 1. 冬至

A cold wind rattled the windowpanes. But inside the house, it was warm and cozy. Jack and Annie were making Christmas cookies with their mom. Jack pressed a star-shaped cookie cutter into the dough. 一阵冷风把窗户震得哗哗响。 但屋内却温暖又舒适。 杰克和安妮正在和妈妈一起做圣诞饼干。 杰克把星星形状的饼干模子按进面团里。

“Hey, it’s snowing outside,” said Annie. “嘿，外面在下雪，”安妮说。

Jack looked out the window. Huge snow-flakes were falling from the late-afternoon sky. 杰克看着窗外。 巨大的雪花从傍晚的天空中飘落下来。

“You want to go out?” asked Annie. “你想出去吗？”安妮问。

“Not really. It’ll be dark soon,” said Jack. “其实不想。 很快天就要黑了，”杰克说。

“That’s right,” said their mom. “Today’s the first day of winter. It’s the shortest day of the year.” “没错，”他们妈妈说。 “今天是冬至的第一天。 这是今年最短的一天。”

Jack’s heart skipped a beat. “You mean it’s the winter solstice?” he said. 杰克的心跳漏了一拍。 “你是说今天是冬至吗？”他说。

“Yes,” said their mom. “是的，”他们的妈妈说。

Annie gasped. “The winter solstice?” she said. 安妮惊呼起来。 “冬至？”，她说。

“Yes … ,” their mom said, puzzled. “是的……”，他们的妈妈说，一脸困惑。

Jack and Annie looked at each other. Last summer, Merlin the magician had called for their help on the summer solstice. Maybe he would need them again today! 杰克和安妮互相看了看。 去年夏天，梅林魔法师在夏至那天曾请求他们帮忙。 也许他今天又需要他们帮忙！

Jack put down the cookie cutter and wiped his hands on a towel. “Actually, Mom, it might be fun to play in the snow for just a few minutes,” he said. 杰克放下饼干模具，用毛巾擦了擦手。 “其实，妈妈，可能玩几分钟雪会很有趣，”他说。

“Whatever you want,” their mom said. “Just dress warmly. I’ll finish up with the cookies and put them in the oven.” “随你们的便，”他们的妈妈说。 “穿暖和点。” “我先把饼干做好，然后放进烤箱。”

“Thanks!” said Jack. He and Annie raced to the closet and pulled on their boots. They threw on jackets, scarves, gloves, and caps. “谢谢！”杰克说。 他和安妮冲向衣柜，穿上靴子。 他们赶紧穿上外套、围巾、手套和帽子。

“Be home before dark,” their mom said. “天黑前必须回家。”他们的妈妈说。

“We will!” called Jack. “我们会的！”杰克喊道。

“Bye, Mom!” Annie shouted. “再见，妈妈！”安妮大喊。

Jack and Annie slipped out of their house into the snowy cold. Their boots squeaked as they ran across their white yard and headed toward the Frog Creek woods. 杰克和安妮溜出房子，走进寒冷的雪地里。 他们跑过白色的院子，朝蛙溪镇的森林跑去，靴子在雪地上发出咯吱声。

At the edge of the woods, Jack stopped. He couldn’t believe how beautiful the trees looked. White powder covered the branches of the hemlocks and pines. 在森林的边缘，杰克停下了脚步。 他简直不敢相信这些树看起来这么漂亮。 白色的粉末覆盖了冷杉和松树的枝条。

“Look,” said Annie. She pointed to two pairs of footprints that led out to the road and then back into the woods. “Somebody else has been here.” “看，”安妮说。 她指着两组脚印，这些脚印从路一直延伸到森林里，然后再回到路上。 “有人已经来过这里了。”

“It looks like they were walking out of the woods—but turned back,” said Jack. “Let’s hurry!” If the magic tree house had come back today, he didn’t want anyone else finding it first! “看起来他们是从树林里走出来的——但又折返回来了，”杰克说。 “快点！”如果神奇的树屋今天回来了，他可不想让别人先找到它！

Jack and Annie walked quickly through the woods, following the two sets of footprints. 杰克和安妮快速穿过树林，跟着两组脚印走。

“Stop!” said Annie. She pulled Jack behind a tree. “Over there!” “停下！”安妮说。 她把杰克拉到一棵树后面。 “在那里！”

Through the falling snow, Jack saw two people in long, dark cloaks. They were hurrying toward a tall oak—and high in the oak was the magic tree house! 在飘落的雪花中，杰克看到两个穿着又长又黑斗篷的人。 他们正匆匆朝一棵高大的橡树跑去——而在那棵橡树的高处，就是那座神奇的树屋！

“Oh, no!” said Jack. “哦，不！”杰克说。

The tree house was back! And someone else had found it! 树屋又回来了！ 还有其他人也发现了它！

“Hey!” Jack yelled. “Stop!” The tree house had come for him and Annie—no one else! “嘿！”杰克喊道。 “停下！”树屋是来找杰克和安妮的——不是其他人！

Jack started running. Annie followed. Jack slipped and fell in the snow, but he scrambled up and kept going. By the time he and Annie got to the tree house, the two people had climbed up the rope ladder and disappeared inside. 杰克开始跑起来。安妮也跟了上去。 杰克在雪地里滑了一跤，摔了一跤，但他赶紧爬起来继续往前走。 等杰克和安妮跑到树屋时，那两个人已经爬上了绳梯，消失在树屋里了。

“Come out!” Jack yelled. “出来！”杰克喊道。

“This is our tree house!” shouted Annie. “这是我们的树屋！”安妮大声喊道。

Two kids poked their heads out of the tree house window. They both looked like they were about thirteen years old. The boy had tousled red hair and freckles. The girl had sea-blue eyes and long, curly black hair. Their cheeks were rosy from the cold. They laughed when they saw Jack and Annie. 两个孩子从树屋的窗户里探出头来。 他们看起来都快十三岁了。 男孩有一头乱糟糟的红头发，脸上还有雀斑。 女孩有一双海蓝色的眼睛，长长的卷发是黑色的。 他们的脸颊因为寒冷变得红扑扑的。 他们看到杰克和安妮时，笑了起来。

“Excellent!” said the boy. “We came to find you, but you have found us instead.” “太棒了！”男孩说。 “我们是来找你们的，但你们却找到了我们。”

“Teddy!” cried Annie. “Kathleen! Hi!” “泰迪！”安妮喊道。 “凯瑟琳！你好！”

Teddy was the young sorcerer who worked with Morgan in her library in Camelot. Kathleen was the enchanted selkie girl who’d helped Jack and Annie on the summer solstice by magically turning them all into seals. 泰迪是卡梅洛特里和摩根一起在图书馆工作的年轻法师。 凯瑟琳是被施了魔法的水獭女孩，她在夏至那天帮助杰克和安妮，用魔法把他们全都变成了水獭。

Jack was stunned. He had never imagined that their two friends from Camelot might someday visit Frog Creek! “What are you guys doing here?” he shouted. 杰克惊呆了。 他从未想过来自卡梅洛特的两位朋友有一天会来蛙溪镇！ “你们 guys 怎么会在这里？”他喊道。

“Climb up and we will tell you!” said Teddy. “爬上来，我们会告诉你们！”泰迪说。

Jack and Annie hurried up the rope ladder. When they climbed inside the tree house, Annie threw her arms around Teddy and Kathleen. “I can’t believe you came to visit us!” she said. 杰克和安妮赶紧爬上了绳梯。 当他们爬进树屋时，安妮张开双臂抱住了泰迪和凯瑟琳。 “我真不敢相信你们来拜访我们！”她说。

“It pleases me to see you, Annie,” said Kathleen. “And you also, Jack.” Her large blue eyes sparkled. “见到你让我很高兴，安妮，”凯瑟琳说。 “还有你，杰克。”她那双大大的蓝眼睛闪闪发亮。

“It pleases me, too,” Jack said shyly. He still thought Kathleen was the most beautiful girl he had ever seen. Even when she’d been a seal, she’d been lovely. “我也很高兴见到你，”杰克害羞地说。 他仍然觉得凯瑟琳是他见过最漂亮的女孩。 即使她变成海豹的时候，也是非常美丽。

“We went looking for you!” said Teddy. “We climbed down and walked through the woods to a road.” “我们是来找你的！”泰迪说。 “我们爬下来，穿过树林走到一条路上。”

“But the road was full of monsters!” said Kathleen. “A big red creature nearly ran over us! It made a honking sound!” “但是路上全是怪物！”凯瑟琳说。 “一只大红色的生物差点撞到我们！ 它发出嘎嘎的叫声！”，

“Then before we knew it, a giant black monster charged at us! It had a ferocious growl!” said Teddy. “We came back here to gather our wits.” “然后在我们还没反应过来的时候，一只巨大的黑色怪物朝我们冲了过来！ 它发出可怕的咆哮声！”，泰迪说。 “我们回来这里是想整理一下思路。”

“Those weren’t monsters!” said Annie, laughing. “They were just cars!” “那些根本不是怪物！”安妮笑着说。 “那是汽车！”

“Cars?” said Teddy. “汽车？”泰迪说。

“Yeah, they have motors and people drive them,” said Jack. “对，它们有发动机，人们会开车，”杰克说。

“Motors?” said Teddy. “马达？”泰迪说。

“It’s hard to explain,” said Annie. “Just remember—in our world, you have to watch out for cars every time you cross a road.” “很难解释，”安妮说。 “记住，在我们这个世界，每次过马路都要小心汽车。”

“Indeed we will,” said Teddy. “我们一定会的，”泰迪说。

“Why have you come here?” asked Jack. “你们为什么来到这里？”杰克问。

“We found a message for you in Merlin’s chambers,” said Teddy, “and decided to deliver it ourselves.” “我们在梅林的房间里发现了一条给你的信息，”泰迪说，“我们决定自己把它送过来。”

“So we climbed into the tree house outside Morgan’s library,” said Kathleen. “Teddy pointed to the words Frog Creek in the message and made a wish to come here. The next thing we knew, we were here in these woods.” “所以我们爬进了摩根图书馆外面的树屋，”凯瑟琳说。 “泰迪指着信息上写着的蛙溪镇，许愿想要来到这里。 接下来我们知道自己已经来到这片树林里了。”

Teddy pulled a small gray stone from his cloak. “And this is the message we brought you,” he said. 泰迪从他的斗篷里拿出了一块小灰色石头。 “这是我们给你们带来的信息，”他说。

Jack took the stone from Teddy. 杰克从泰迪那里拿起了那块石头。

The message was written in tiny handwriting. Jack read it aloud: 信息是用非常小的字写的。 杰克大声读了出来：

图中文字：To Jack and Annie of Frog Creek: My Staff of Strength has been stolen. On the winter solstice, journey to the Land-Behind-the-Clouds. Travel toward the setting sun and retrieve my staff—or all will be lost.

Merlin 图中文字：给弗罗格克里克的杰克和安妮：我的力量之杖被偷走了。冬至那天，去云后之地。朝着落日的方向前进，找回我的杖——否则一切都会失去。梅林

“Oh, wow,” said Annie. “That sounds serious.” “哦，哇，”安妮说。 “那听起来很严重。”

“Yeah,” said Jack. “But why didn’t Merlin send us the message himself?” “是啊，”杰克说。 “但是为什么梅林不亲自给我们送信息呢？”

“We do not know,” said Teddy. “Neither Merlin nor Morgan has been seen for days.” “我们不知道，”泰迪说。 “梅林和摩根已经好几天都没人见到了。”

“Where did they go?” asked Annie. “他们去哪儿了？”安妮问。

“ ’Tis a mystery,” said Teddy. “Last week I journeyed to the selkie cove to bring Kathleen to Camelot. She is going to be a helper in Morgan’s library. But when we returned, we could not find Merlin or Morgan.” “这真是个谜，”泰迪说。 “上周我去了海豹湾，带凯瑟琳去卡梅洛特。 她要成为摩根图书馆的助手。 但我们回来后，找不到梅林和摩根。”

“We only found this message for you,” said Kathleen. “我们只找到了这条给你们的信息，”凯瑟琳说。

“Aye, and I thought that when Merlin does return,” said Teddy, “he will be greatly pleased to have his staff back. Much of his power comes from its ancient and mysterious magic.” “是啊，我想当梅林回来时，”泰迪说，“他会非常高兴能拿回他的魔杖。 他的很多力量都来自这根古老而神秘的魔杖。”

“Wow,” said Annie. “哇，”安妮说。

“In his message, he tells us to go to the Land-Behind-the-Clouds,” said Jack. “Where’s that?” “在他的信息里，他说我们要去云后之地，”杰克说。 “那是在哪儿？”

“ ’Tis a land far north of my cove,” said Kathleen. “I have never journeyed there.” “那是在我海湾以北很远的地方，”凯瑟琳说。 “我从来没去过那儿。”

“Nor I,” said Teddy. “But I have read about it in Morgan’s books. ’Tis as bleak as a frozen white desert. I am eager to see it for myself.” “我也没去过，”泰迪说。 “但我曾在摩根的书里读到过它。” “那地方冷得像一片结冰的白色沙漠。” “我真想亲眼看看它。”

“So you and Kathleen are coming with us?” said Annie. “所以你和凯瑟琳要和我们一起去吗？”安妮问。

“Indeed!” said Kathleen. “当然！”凯瑟琳说。

“Great!” said Jack and Annie together. “太棒了！”杰克和安妮异口同声地说。

“If we all work together, we can do anything, aye?” said Teddy. “如果我们齐心协力，我们什么都能做到，对吧？”泰迪说。

“Aye!” said Annie. “对！”安妮说。

I hope so, thought Jack. 希望如此，杰克心想。

Annie pointed at the words Land-Behind-the- Clouds in Merlin’s message. “Okay, ready?” she said to the others. 安妮指着梅林信息中的“Land-Behind-the-Clouds”这几个字。 “准备好了吗？”她对其他人说。

“Yes!” said Kathleen. “准备好了！”凯瑟琳说。

“I guess so,” said Jack. “我想是吧，”杰克说。

“Onward!” said Teddy. “前进！”泰迪说。

“I wish we could all go there!” Annie said. “真希望我们都能去那里！”安妮说。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后，一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
