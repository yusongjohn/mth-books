7. The Frost Giant 7. 冰霜巨人

H UFFFF, HUFFFF, HUFFFFF.… With each breath from the Frost Giant, a blast of cold wind swept through the hollow. 呼——呼——呼——，每一次冰霜巨人的呼吸，都带来一阵寒冷的风扫过洞穴。

Jack trembled. He felt chilled to the bone. HUFFFF, HUFFFF, HUFFFFF.… 杰克颤抖起来。 他感觉冷得骨头都发抖了。 呼——呼——呼——，

The giant’s breathing grew louder and stronger. Jack squeezed his eyes shut as icy, wet wind rushed against his body. 巨人的呼吸声变得更大更响了。 杰克紧紧闭上眼睛，冰冷潮湿的风吹在身上。

HUFFFF, HUFFFF, HUFFFFF.… 呼——呼——呼——，

Jack crouched lower and held on tightly to Annie. 杰克蹲得更低了，紧紧抓住安妮的手。

HUFFFF, HUFFFF, HUFFFFF.… 呼——呼——呼——……

The giant’s breath howled like a hundred ghosts through the hollow. Jack thought of the blue Norn’s words: He is a blind force of nature that spares nothing in his path.… 巨人的呼吸像一百个鬼魂一样，在洞穴中呼啸而过。 杰克想起了蓝诺恩的话：他是一个盲目的自然力量，所到之处，什么都不留。

But then the giant’s breathing seemed to grow a bit softer. What’s happening? Jack wondered. 但接着，巨人的呼吸似乎变得柔和了一些。发生了什么？ 杰克想道。

The breathing grew softer and softer. “Maybe he’s going to sleep,” Annie whispered. 呼吸变得越来越轻柔了。 “也许他要睡觉了，”安妮小声说。

The breathing became calm and steady. The wind died to a light breeze. 呼吸变得平静而稳定。 风渐渐变成了微风。

“I think the Frost Giant is sleeping,” Annie whispered. “We should try to sneak out of here.” “我想冰霜巨人正在睡觉，”安妮小声说。 “我们应该偷偷从这里溜出去。”

“Okay, but keep your eyes down. Just look at the ground!” whispered Jack. “好吧，但把眼睛低下。 只看地面！”杰克小声说。

“Right,” whispered Annie. “对，”安妮也小声说。

Their heads bowed, Jack and Annie crept cautiously across the floor of the hollow and began climbing up toward the crack. Jack’s teeth chattered, but he couldn’t tell if it was from cold or fear. 他们低着头，杰克和安妮小心翼翼地穿过洞穴的地面，开始朝裂缝爬上去。 杰克的牙齿打颤，但他不知道这是冷还是害怕引起的。

Suddenly a deafening roar shook the night! The Frost Giant screamed with windy rage! He was awake! 突然，一声震耳欲聋的咆哮打破了夜晚的寂静！ 冰霜巨人狂怒地吼叫着！他醒来了！

Jack was blown to the ground. He tried to crawl across the snow, but he didn’t know which way to go, and he was afraid to look up. 杰克被掀翻在地。 他试图在雪地上爬行，但他不知道该往哪个方向走，而且他不敢抬头看。

“Jack! This way!” Annie’s voice called above the roar of the giant’s breath. She helped him up and they struggled together against the wind. Finally they came to the crack in the wall. “杰克！ “这边！”安妮的声音在巨人呼气的轰鸣中传来。 她扶他起来，他们一起努力对抗着风。 最后，他们来到了墙上的裂缝前。

Jack and Annie scrambled through the crack. Outside, the wild wind knocked them over, and they tumbled down the side of the hill. 杰克和安妮爬进了裂缝里。 外面，狂风把他们吹倒了，他们从山坡上滚了下去。

The wind swirled the snow across the plain. “Annie! Annie!” Jack called. Where was she? Where was the sleigh? He couldn’t see anything. He couldn’t stay on his feet. 风卷着雪在平原上飘荡。 “安妮！ 安妮！”杰克喊道。她在哪里？ 雪橇在哪里？ 他什么也看不见。 他站不稳了。

The wind roared even louder. An avalanche of snow came crashing down the hillside. When it hit the ground, the snow exploded into great clouds of white powder. 风声变得更大了。 一堆雪从山坡上轰隆隆地滚了下来。 雪砸到地上时，炸开了大片白色的雪粉。

“Jack! Jack!” “杰克！杰克！”

Jack heard Annie’s voice in the screaming wind. He tried to stand up. But snow kept falling on top of him, until he was completely covered. 杰克在呼啸的风声中听到了安妮的声音。 他试着站起来。 但雪不断地落在他身上，直到他完全被埋住了。

As Jack lay buried under the snow, all his strength left his body. He knew he should dig his way out, but he was too cold and too tired. He was too tired to look for Annie. He was too tired to fight the Frost Giant. Instead, he closed his eyes and drifted into an icy sleep.



 当杰克被雪埋住时，他全身的力量都消失了。 他知道他应该挖路出去，但他太冷了，也太累了。 他太累了，找不到安妮。 他太累了，没法和冰霜巨人战斗。 于是，他闭上眼睛，进入了冰冷的睡眠。





Jack dreamed that cold fur was brushing against his face. He dreamed that a wolf was digging around him, nudging him, pushing him, sniffing him … . 杰克梦到冰冷的毛发正蹭着他的脸。 他梦到一只狼在他周围挖着，推着他，嗅着他……

Jack opened his eyes. He felt dazed. At first he couldn’t see. But he could feel that he wasn’t buried in snow anymore. He wiped off his glasses. He saw a low moon and some stars in a clear sky. 杰克睁开了眼睛。他感觉晕晕乎乎的。 一开始他什么也看不见。 但他能感觉到自己不再被雪埋着了。 他擦了擦眼镜。 他看见清澈的夜空中有一轮低低的月亮和一些星星。

The Frost Giant must have left, Jack thought. But then he heard a panting noise. He sat up and looked around. One of the white wolves was crouching right behind him! 杰克想，那个冰霜巨人一定已经离开了。 但接着他听到了喘息的声音。 他坐起来，环顾四周。 有一只白狼正悄悄地蹲在他身后！

Jack scrambled to his feet. “Go away!” he shouted. 杰克赶紧跳了起来。 “走开！”他喊道。

The wolf stepped back and growled. 那只狼往后退了一步，发出低吼。

“Go! Go! Go!” shouted Jack. He picked up handfuls of snow and threw them at the wolf. “走！走！ 走！”杰克喊道。 他抓起一把雪，朝狼扔了过去。

The wolf backed away a few more feet. Jack looked around wildly. Annie was lying very still on top of the snow. The other white wolf was sniffing and pawing at her. 狼又往后退了几步。 杰克疯狂地四处张望。 安妮静静地躺在雪上。 另一只白狼正在嗅她，用爪子扒拉她。

Jack’s anger made him fearless. “Leave her alone!” he shouted. “Go away!” He scooped up more snow and threw it. 杰克的愤怒让他变得无所畏惧。 “别碰她！”，他喊道。 “走开！”，他抓起更多的雪扔了过去。

The wolf stepped back. 狼往后退了一步。

“GO! GO!” shouted Jack. “Get away! Leave us alone!” He glared angrily at the two white wolves. “走！” “快走开！” “快走开！ “别管我们！”他生气地盯着那两只白狼。

The wolves stared back at Jack. Their yellow eyes gleamed. 狼盯着杰克。 它们的黄眼睛闪着光。

“I’m not kidding—GO!” shouted Jack. “我可不是在开玩笑——快走！”杰克喊道。

Jack stared fiercely at the wolves. Finally the wolves looked away. They glanced at each other and then slowly backed off. They looked at Jack and Annie one last time. Then they turned and trotted away over the snow. 杰克凶狠地盯着那些狼。 最后，狼们望向了别处。 它们互相看了看，然后慢慢地退开了。 它们最后一次看了杰克和安妮一眼。 然后他们转过身，蹦蹦跳跳地穿过雪地走了。

Jack rushed to Annie. He knelt beside her and lifted her head. “Wake up! Wake up!” he said. 杰克冲到安妮身边。 他跪在她旁边，抬起了她的头。 “醒醒！ 醒醒！”他说。

Annie opened her eyes. 安妮睁开了眼睛。

“You okay?” Jack asked. “你没事吧？”杰克问。

“Yes … I dreamed about white wolves,” Annie murmured. “是的……我梦见了白色的狼，”安妮轻声说。

“Me too!” said Jack. “And then when I woke up, they were here! They were about to eat us!” “我也是！”杰克说。 “然后当我醒来的时候，它们就在这里！ “他们马上要吃掉我们了！”

“Really?” Annie sat up and looked around. “真的吗？”安妮坐起来，环顾四周。

“Yeah, but I scared them off,” said Jack. “是的，不过我把它们吓跑了，”杰克说。

“What about the Frost Giant?” Annie said. “那冰霜巨人呢？”安妮说。

“He’s gone, too,” said Jack. “Come on. Let’s get out of here!” Jack helped Annie up from the snow. “Do you still have the wizard’s eye?” “他也走了，”杰克说。 “快点。” 我们赶紧离开这里！“杰克帮安妮从雪里站起来。 “你还有巫师的眼睛吗？“

Annie felt in her pocket. “Got it,” she said. 安妮摸了摸她的口袋。 “找到了，”她说。

“Good.” Jack looked around. Beyond the heaps of fallen snow, the silver sleigh was waiting for them. Overhead, the sky had turned to a light shade of gray. “好。”杰克环顾四周。 在堆积如山的积雪后面，银色的雪橇正等着他们。 头顶上，天空变成了浅灰色。

“It’s almost dawn,” said Jack. “Remember what the wizard said? We have to bring back his eye by the break of day—or we’ll never see Merlin or Morgan again!” “天快亮了，”杰克说。 “还记得巫师说过的话吗？ 我们必须在天亮之前把他的眼睛带回来——否则我们就再也见不到梅林和摩根了！

Jack held Annie’s hand and they trudged together through the snow. When they got to the sleigh, they climbed inside. Annie took her place at the rudder. Jack pulled out the wind-string and untied a knot. 杰克牵着安妮的手，他们一起踩着雪艰难地走着。 当他们到达雪橇时，他们爬了进去。 安妮坐在舵的位置上。 杰克拉出风绳并解开一个结。

The breeze rocked the sleigh. Jack untied a second knot, and the sail began to fill. He untied a third, and the silver sleigh moved forward, gliding over the white ground. 微风吹动着雪橇。 杰克解开第二个结，帆开始鼓起来。 他又解开第三个结，银色的雪橇向前移动，滑过白色的地面。

Swish—swish—swish. The sleigh moved through the thick snow and away from the Hollow Hill. As they sailed over the white plain, the sky was turning from gray to pale pink. 唰——唰——唰。 雪橇穿过厚厚的积雪，离开了空山丘。 当他们驶过这片白茫茫的平原时，天空从灰色变成了淡粉色。

“We have to go faster!” said Annie. “我们必须更快一点！”安妮说。

Jack untied a fourth knot. The wind whistled in his ears. The sleigh picked up speed. Annie steered it past the rocks and over the sea ice. She steered it over the plain, south to the palace of the Ice Wizard. 杰克解开了第四个结。 风在耳边呼啸。 雪橇的速度加快了。 安妮操控着它绕过岩石，滑过海冰。 她操控着它越过平原，向南驶向冰巫师的宫殿。

When the sleigh drew close to the palace, Jack tied a knot, and they began to slow down. He tied three more, and the sleigh came to a stop. 当雪橇靠近宫殿时，杰克打了个结，他们开始减速。 他又打了三个结，雪橇终于停了下来。

Jack and Annie looked around in the faint, cold light. “I wonder where Teddy and Kathleen are,” said Annie. “They said they’d meet us here at dawn.” 杰克和安妮在微弱而寒冷的光线下四处张望。 “我想知道泰迪和凯瑟琳在哪里，”安妮说。 “他们说天亮时会在这里和我们会合。”

Jack studied the vast white plain, but he saw no sign of their friends. He wished he had Kathleen’s vision. “I hope they’re okay,” he said. “I hope they didn’t run into the white wolves.” 杰克仔细观察着这片广阔的白色平原，但他没看到他们的朋友的踪影。 他希望他有凯瑟琳那样的视力。 “我希望他们没事，”他说。 “希望他们没遇到白狼。”

“I have a feeling the wolves wouldn’t hurt them,” said Annie. “The wolf in my dream seemed nice.” “我觉得狼不会伤害他们，”安妮说。 “我梦里的狼看起来很友善。”

“Dream wolves are different from real wolves,” said Jack. “梦里的狼和真正的狼不一样，”杰克说。

“I don’t think we can wait for them,” said Annie. “The eye has to be back by the time the sun comes up.” “我觉得我们不能等他们了，”安妮说。 “眼睛必须在太阳升起前放回去。”

“The eye!” said Jack. “We never looked to see if it was inside the hailstone.” “眼睛！”杰克说。 “我们从来没看看它是不是在冰雹里。”

Annie reached in her pocket and pulled out the hailstone. She held it up. 安妮从口袋里拿出冰雹，举了起来。

Jack gasped. Staring out at him from inside the ice was an eyeball. It was about the size of a large marble. The eyeball was white with a sparkling blue center. 杰克惊呼。 从冰里面盯着他看的是一只眼睛。 它大约有大玻璃弹珠那么大。 这只眼睛是白色的，中间闪着蓝色的光。

“Oh, man,” whispered Jack. “天啊，”杰克小声说。

“It’s beautiful, isn’t it?” said Annie. “真漂亮，不是吗？”安妮说。

“I don’t know about that.” Jack felt a little queasy. Seeing an eye outside of a human head was too weird for him. “Put it away for now,” he said. “我不太这么觉得。”杰克感觉有点恶心。 对他来说，看到一个人头之外的眼睛太奇怪了。 “现在先把它收起来，”他说。

Annie put the hailstone back in her pocket. Jack looked around again. The sky had brightened from pale pink to red. A thin sliver of the sun was peeking over the horizon. 安妮把冰雹石又放回了口袋里。 杰克又环顾了一下四周。 天空从淡粉色变得亮红色了。 太阳的一小部分正从地平线探出头来。

“The sun!” cried Jack. “Hurry!” He and Annie jumped out of the sleigh and charged toward the palace. “太阳！”杰克喊道。 “快点！”他和安妮从雪橇上跳下来，朝宫殿冲去。

When they got to the entrance, Annie stopped. “Look!” she said, pointing to big paw prints in the snow. “Wolf tracks!” 当他们到达入口时，安妮停了下来。 “看！”她指着雪地里巨大的爪印说。 “狼的脚印！”

“Oh, no,” said Jack. “Do you think the white wolves are inside? That’s weird.” “哦，不，”杰克说。 “你觉得白狼在里面吗？那太奇怪了。”

“It doesn’t matter! We have to go in! Hurry!” said Annie. They rushed into the palace—just as the fiery ball of the sun rose over the horizon. “没关系！我们必须进去！” 快点！”安妮说。 他们冲进宫殿——就在这时，太阳像一个燃烧的火球从地平线上升起来。
