1. Old Friends 1. 老朋友

Jack poured milk over his cereal. His stomach felt fluttery. It was Monday—the first day of a new school year. 杰克把牛奶倒在了他的麦片上。 他的肚子感觉有点发慌。 今天是星期一，新学年的第一天。

Jack always felt nervous on the first day. What would his new teacher be like? Would his desk be close to a window? Would friends from last year be in his class again this year? 杰克在第一天总是感到紧张。 他的新老师会是什么样子呢？ 他的课桌会离窗户近吗？ 去年的朋友今年还会在同一个班吗？

“Annie, hurry!” Jack’s mom called upstairs. “It’s fifteen minutes till eight. School starts in half an hour.” “安妮，快点！”杰克的妈妈从楼上喊道。 “离八点还有十五分钟。” “半小时后就要上课了。”

Jack’s dad walked into the kitchen. “Are you sure you and Annie don’t want me to drive you?” he asked. 杰克的爸爸走进了厨房。 “你们确定不想要我开车送你们吗？”他问。

“No thanks, we don’t mind walking,” said Jack. Their school was only three blocks away. “不用了，我们不介意走路，”杰克说。 他们的学校只有三个街区远。

“Annie, hurry!” their mom called again. “You’re going to be late!” “安妮，快点！”他们的妈妈又喊道。 “你会迟到的！”

The back door banged open. Annie rushed into the kitchen. She was out of breath. 后门猛地被推开。 安妮冲进了厨房。 她喘不过气来了。

“Oh, I thought you were upstairs,” their mom said with surprise. “You were outside?” “哦，我还以为你在楼上呢，”他们的妈妈惊讶地说。 “你刚才在外面？”

“Yes!” said Annie, panting. “Just taking a quick walk.” She looked at Jack. Her eyes sparkled. “Hurry, Jack. We really should go now!” “是的！”安妮喘着气说。 “只是去散了个小步。”她看着杰克。 她的眼睛闪着光。 “快点，杰克。 我们现在真的该走了！”

“Okay, I’m coming!” said Jack. He leapt up from the table. He could tell Annie wasn’t talking about school. The tree house must be back! Finally! “好的，我来了！”杰克说。 他从桌子里跳了起来。 他能感觉到安妮说的不是学校的事。 树屋回来了！终于！

Jack grabbed his backpack. Annie held the door open for him. 杰克抓起他的背包。 安妮为他扶着门。

“No breakfast?” their mom asked. “不吃早饭吗？”他们的妈妈问。

“Too nervous to eat now, Mom,” said Jack. “现在太紧张了，吃不下，妈妈，”杰克说。

“Have fun,” their mom said. “玩得开心点，”他们的妈妈说。

“Learn a lot,” said their dad. “多学点东西，”他们的爸爸说。

“Don’t worry, we will!” said Annie. “别担心，我们会的！”安妮说。

Jack and Annie slipped out the door and walked quickly across their yard. 杰克和安妮溜出门，快步穿过他们的院子。

“It’s back!” said Annie. “它回来了！”安妮说。

“I figured it was!” said Jack. “我猜对了！”杰克说。

“Morgan must want us to look for another secret of happiness to help Merlin,” said Annie. “摩根一定是想让我们去找另一个幸福的秘密，来帮助梅林，”安妮说。

“Yep!” said Jack. “Let’s run!” “没错！”杰克说。 “快跑吧！”，

Jack and Annie dashed up the sidewalk. They crossed the street and headed into the Frog Creek woods. They ran between the trees, through shadows and light, until they came to the tallest oak. 杰克和安妮沿着人行道飞快地跑了起来。 他们穿过街道，朝蛙溪镇的森林跑去。 他们跑在树之间，穿过阴影和阳光，直到来到最高的橡树前。

High in the tree was the magic tree house. The rope ladder was swaying in the chilly morning wind. 高高的树上就是那座神奇的树屋。 绳梯在凉爽的晨风中摇晃着。

“How did you know it was here?” asked Jack, catching his breath. “你怎么知道它在这里的？”杰克一边喘着气，一边问道。

“I woke up thinking about Teddy and Kathleen,” said Annie, “and I had this strange feeling.” “我醒来时一直在想泰迪和凯瑟琳，”安妮说，“我有一种奇怪的感觉。”

“Really?” said Jack. “Teddy! Kathleen!” he shouted up at the tree house. “真的吗？”杰克说。 “泰迪！ 凯瑟琳！”他朝树屋大声喊道。

Two young teenagers looked out the tree house window: a curly-haired boy with freckles and a big grin and a smiling girl with sea-blue eyes and dark wavy hair. 两个年轻人从树屋的窗户探出头来：一个有着卷发、雀斑和灿烂笑容的男孩，还有一个眼睛是海蓝色、头发是深棕色波浪状的微笑女孩。

“Jack! Annie!” the girl said. “杰克！ 安妮！”女孩说。

“Come up! Come up!” said the boy. “上来！ 上来！”男孩说。

Jack and Annie hurried up the rope ladder. When they climbed inside the tree house, they threw their arms around their friends. 杰克和安妮赶紧爬上了绳梯。 当他们爬进树屋时，他们立刻张开双臂拥抱了他们的朋友。

“Are we going to look for another secret of happiness?” said Annie. “To help Merlin?” “我们要去找另一个幸福的秘密吗？”安妮说。 “是为了帮助梅林吗？”

“Yes, and this time you will travel back to Florence, Italy, five hundred years ago,” said Teddy. “是的，这次你们要回到五百年前的意大利佛罗伦萨，”泰迪说。

“Florence, Italy?” said Jack. “What’s there?” “意大利佛罗伦萨？”杰克说。 “那里有什么？”

“An amazing person who will help you,” said Kathleen. “有一位非常了不起的人会帮助你们，”凯瑟琳说。

“Who?” asked Annie. “Is this person magical?” “是谁？”安妮问。 “这个人有魔法吗？”

Teddy grinned. “Some people might say so,” he said. He reached into his cloak and pulled out a book. The cover showed a drawing of a man wearing a purple cloak and floppy blue cap. He had a long nose, bright, kind eyes with heavy eyebrows, and a flowing beard. The title said: 泰迪露出了笑容。 “有些人可能会这么说，”他说。 他伸手到他的斗篷里，拿出了一本书。 封面画着一个穿着紫色斗篷和蓝色软帽的男人。 他有长长的鼻子，明亮而善良的眼睛，浓密的眉毛，还有一把飘逸的胡须。标题写着：

图中文字：LEONARDO DAVINCI 图中文字：列奥纳多·达·芬奇

“Leonardo da Vinci!” said Jack. “Are you kidding?” “列奥纳多·达·芬奇！”杰克说。 “你是在开玩笑吗？”，

“I’ve heard of him,” said Annie. “我听说过他，”安妮说。

“Who hasn’t?” said Jack. “He was an incredible genius!” “谁没听说过呢？”杰克说。 “他是个不可思议的天才！”

“This biography of Leonardo will help you on your mission,” said Teddy. “这本关于达·芬奇的传记会帮助你们完成任务，”泰迪说。

“And so will this rhyme from Morgan,” said Kathleen. She pulled a small piece of parchment paper from her cloak and gave it to Annie. “摩根的这首诗也会帮上忙，”凯瑟琳说。 她从斗篷里拿出一张小羊皮纸，递给安妮。

Annie read the words on the paper aloud. 安妮大声地读出纸上的文字。

To Jack and Annie of Frog Creek:
Though the question is quite simple,

Simple answers might be wrong.

If you want to know the right one,

Help the genius all day long,

Morning, noon, and afternoon,

Till the night bird sings its song. 虽然这个问题很简单，但简单的答案可能不对。如果你想了解正确的答案，就整天帮这位天才，早上、中午、下午，直到夜鸟唱起歌来。

“So to find the secret of happiness, we need to spend the whole day helping Leonardo da Vinci,” said Jack. “所以，要想找到幸福的秘密，我们需要一整天都在帮助达·芬奇，”杰克说。

“Yes,” said Kathleen. Teddy nodded. “对，”凯瑟琳说。泰迪点了点头。

“I wish you could come, too,” said Annie. “我希望你也能来，”安妮说。

“And help us,” said Jack. “帮我们，”杰克说。

“Never fear,” said Kathleen. “You will have the help of the great genius and the Wand of Dianthus.” “别担心，”凯瑟琳说。 “你们会得到天才的帮助，还有迪安图斯的魔杖。”

“Oh!” Annie said to Jack. “Did you bring our wand?” “哦！”安妮对杰克说。 “你带我们的魔杖了吗？”，

“Of course,” said Jack. “I always carry it with me for safekeeping.” He reached into his backpack and pulled out a gleaming silver wand. “当然带了，”杰克说。 “我总是随身带着它，以防万一。”他伸手到背包里，拿出一根闪闪发光的银色魔杖。

“The Wand of Dianthus,” Teddy said in a hushed voice. “迪安瑟斯的魔杖，”泰迪压低声音说。

The wand looked like the horn of a unicorn. It burned in Jack’s hand—with cold or warmth, he couldn’t tell which. He carefully put the wand back into his pack. 这根魔杖看起来像独角兽的角。 它在杰克的手里燃烧着——是冷还是热，他分不清。 他小心地把魔杖放回背包里。

“Remember the three rules of the wand?” said Kathleen. “还记得魔杖的三条规则吗？”凯瑟琳说。

“Sure,” said Annie. “You can only use it for the good of others. You can only use it after you’ve tried your hardest. And you can only use it with a command of five words.” “当然记得，”安妮说。 “你只能用它来帮助别人。” 你只有在尽了最大努力之后才能使用它。 而且你只能用五个字的指令来使用它。”

“Excellent,” said Kathleen. “太棒了，”凯瑟琳说。

“Thanks,” said Annie. “Ready?” she asked Jack. “谢谢，”安妮说。 “准备好了吗？”她问杰克。

Jack nodded. “Bye, Teddy. Bye, Kathleen.” 杰克点了点头。 “再见，泰迪。再见，凯瑟琳。”

“Good-bye,” said Teddy. “再见，”泰迪说。

“And good luck,” said Kathleen. “祝你好运，”凯瑟琳说。

Jack pointed at the cover of the book. “I wish we could go to Leonardo da Vinci!” 杰克指着书的封面。 “我希望我们能去到列奥纳多·达·芬奇那里！”，

In the distance, the school bell started to ring, letting kids know that school would start in ten minutes. But in the Frog Creek woods, the wind started to blow. 远处，学校的钟声开始响起，告诉孩子们学校将在十分钟内开始。 但是在蛙溪镇的森林里，风开始吹起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 绝对静止。
