9. The Smile 9. 微笑

At first no one spoke on the trip back. It was as if they didn’t want to break the spell of joy that had settled over them. Even though Jack was bumping up and down in the cart, he could still remember the feeling of flying smoothly through the sky. He could feel the wind rustling his long feathers. 回去的路上，一开始没人说话。 就好像他们不想打破笼罩在他们身上的快乐魔咒。 尽管杰克在马车里上下颠簸，他还是能记得自己在天空中平稳飞翔的感觉。 他能感觉到风轻轻吹动他长长的羽毛。

The cart passed through a gate in the city walls. As they started through the streets, Annie broke the silence. “So if fame is not the secret of happiness,” she said to Leonardo, “then what is? Do you think it could be flying?” 马车穿过了一座城门。 当他们开始穿过街道时，安妮打破了沉默。 “如果名声不是幸福的秘密，”她对列奥纳多说，“那什么才是呢？ 你觉得会不会是飞翔呢？

Leonardo thought for a moment. “No, no. The secret of happiness cannot be flying,” he said. 列奥纳多想了一会儿。 “不，不。” “幸福的秘密不可能是飞翔，”他说。

“Why not?” asked Jack. “为什么不是呢？”杰克问。

“Because flying is a great dream that no one but us will ever realize,” said Leonardo. “Surely happiness cannot be only for us.” “因为飞翔是一个伟大的梦想，除了我们之外，没有人能实现它，”列奥纳多说。 “幸福一定不只是属于我们的。”

“True,” said Annie. “没错，”安妮说。

“So what do you think the secret is?” asked Jack. “那你觉得幸福的秘密是什么呢？”杰克问。

“Hmm …” Leonardo was silent. Then he sighed. “I must think about it,” he said. “嗯……”列奥纳多沉默了。然后他叹了口气。 “我得好好想想，”他说。

Jack looked worriedly at the sky. The sun would go down soon and night would come. According to their rhyme, they were supposed to leave when the night bird sang its song. “Um … how long do you think it’ll take you to think about it?” Jack asked. 杰克担忧地看着天空。 太阳很快就落山了，夜晚就要来了。 根据他们的童谣，当夜鸟唱起歌时，他们应该离开。 “嗯……你觉得你要想多久才能想清楚？”杰克问。

“I do not know,” said Leonardo. “Right now all I know is that I must hurry to meet with my model. She is already unhappy enough without my being late.” “我不知道，”列奥纳多说。 “现在我只知道我必须赶快去见我的模特。 她已经够不开心了，我不能再迟到。”

“Why is she unhappy?” asked Annie. “她为什么这么不开心？”安妮问。

“She will not say,” said Leonardo. “Perhaps she is tired of posing for me. For three years, she has been sitting for her portrait.” “她不说，”列奥纳多说。 “也许她对我让她一直摆姿势已经厌烦了。 三年来，她一直在为她的画像摆姿势。”

“Whoa, that’s a really long time,” said Annie, “especially if you’re just sitting.” “哇，那真的要等好久啊，”安妮说，“特别是如果你只是坐着的话。”

“Yes, yes, it is,” said Leonardo. “Lately she will not even smile. She only stares at me sadly. I have tried hiring singers, musicians, and joke-sters to amuse her, but nothing helps.” “是啊，是啊，”列奥纳多说。 “最近她连笑都不笑了。 她只是悲伤地看着我。 我试着请歌手、音乐家和说笑话的人来逗她开心，但都没用。”

“Maybe you shouldn’t meet with her today,” said Jack. He didn’t want Leonardo to lose his good feelings from flying. And he wanted him to spend time thinking about the secret of happiness. “也许你今天不该见她，”杰克说。 他不希望列奥纳多因为飞行而失去好心情。 他想让他花点时间想想幸福的秘密。

“No, I must,” said Leonardo. “The light is perfect today. Late afternoon is the best time for painting a portrait—in my courtyard, when the sunlight is golden and shadows are beginning to fall.” “不，我必须去，”列奥纳多说。 “今天的光线刚刚好。” 傍晚是画肖像的最佳时间——在我家的庭院里，阳光是金色的，影子也开始落下来了。”

Shadows were falling as the white horse pulled the cart into Leonardo’s courtyard. A young woman was standing by the studio door. 影子越来越长，白马拉着马车驶入了列奥纳多的庭院。 一位年轻女子正站在工作室的门前。

“Lisa!” called Leonardo. “丽莎！”列奥纳多喊道。

“Hello, Leonardo,” the woman said. She wore a dark gown with a silk cloth over her shoulder. A thin veil covered her long brown hair. She had a high forehead and large brown eyes. Oddly, she looked like someone Jack knew, but he couldn’t remember who. “你好，列奥纳多，”女子说道。 她穿着一件深色的长裙，肩上披着一块丝绸布料。 一张薄薄的面纱遮住了她长长的棕色头发。 她有着高高的额头和一双大大的棕色眼睛。 奇怪的是，她看起来像杰克认识的某个人，但他却想不起是谁。

“Forgive me, Lisa. I am late,” said Leonardo, leaping down from the cart. “Will you wait for me to set up my things?” “请原谅我，丽莎。 “我迟到了，”莱昂纳多跳下马车说道。 “你等我一下，让我收拾东西好吗？”

“Yes, I will wait,” said Lisa. “好，我等你，”丽莎说。

Leonardo hurried inside, and Jack and Annie climbed down from the cart. “Hi, we’re Annie and Jack,” said Annie. 莱昂纳多赶紧走进去，杰克和安妮从马车上跳了下来。 “你好，我们是安妮和杰克，”安妮说。

The woman smiled at them. “I am Lisa,” she said. 那位女士微笑着看着他们。 “我叫丽莎，”她说。

“You look familiar to me,” said Annie. “你看起来有点眼熟，”安妮说。

“Really?” said Lisa. “Are you from Florence?” “真的吗？”丽莎说。 “你来自佛罗伦萨吗？”

“No, we’re from Frog Creek, Pennsylvania,” said Annie. “It’s far away.” “不，我们来自宾夕法尼亚州的蛙溪镇，”安妮说。 “它离这儿很远。”

Lisa smiled again. “I like the name of your town,” she said. 丽莎又笑了。 “我喜欢你们镇的名字，”她说。

So Lisa did smile for other people, thought Jack. He wondered why she wouldn’t smile for Leonardo. 所以丽莎确实会为其他人微笑，杰克想。 他想知道为什么她不愿意为莱昂纳多微笑。

Leonardo came back outside, carrying a small canvas, an easel, and a paint box. He then brought out a stool for Lisa. She sat down and folded her hands. 莱昂纳多回到外面，拿着一幅小画布、一个画架和一个颜料盒。 然后他为丽莎搬来一把小凳子。 她坐下来，双手合十。

Leonardo placed the canvas on his easel. As he prepared his paints, Jack and Annie looked at his painting-in-progress. 莱昂纳多把画布放在画架上。 杰克准备颜料时，安妮和杰克看着他正在画的画。

“Nice,” breathed Annie. “真不错，”安妮轻声说。

The small canvas showed the model, Lisa. Except for her mouth, her whole face had been painted. In the background was a misty landscape with mountains and winding rivers. 小画布上画的是模特丽莎。 除了嘴巴，她的整张脸都被涂上了颜料。 背景是一片迷雾缭绕的风景，有高山和蜿蜒的河流。

Leonardo picked up his brush, dipped it into a paint jar, and began to work. Jack and Annie watched closely as the great genius brushed a thin coat of green paint over the scenery. 达·芬奇拿起画笔，把它浸入颜料罐里，开始作画。 杰克和安妮仔细看着这位天才画家用一层薄薄的绿色颜料涂在风景上。

“What are you doing now?” whispered Annie. “你现在在做什么？”安妮小声问。

“I paint many very thin coats over the background,” murmured Leonardo. “This casts a soft green light over everything. So it all blends together like smoke, and you cannot tell light from shadow.” “我在背景上涂很多层非常薄的颜料，”达·芬奇低声说。 “这会让一切笼罩在柔和的绿色光晕中。” 所以一切都会混在一起，像烟一样，你分不清哪里是光，哪里是影子。”

“How’d you figure that out?” asked Annie. “I mean, you’re always coming up with new ways to do things. How do you do that?” “你怎么想到的？”安妮问。 “我是说，你总是想出新办法来做事。 你是怎么做到的？”

“I ask questions,” said Leonardo. “All the time, I ask questions: How can I paint the light? How can I capture the shadows? How can I do this? How can I do that?” Leonardo stopped painting. He put down his brush and looked at Jack and Annie. His eyes were sparkling. “And now, my friends, I know the secret.” “我问问题，”列奥纳多说。 “我一直都在问问题：我怎么才能画出光？ 我怎么才能捕捉到影子？ 我怎么才能做到这个？ 我怎么才能做到那个？”列奥纳多停下了画画。 他放下画笔，看着杰克和安妮。 他的眼睛闪闪发亮。 “现在，我的朋友们，我知道秘密了！”

“You do?” said Jack. “你真的会吗？”杰克说。

“Yes,” said Leonardo. “The secret of happiness is available to all of us, every hour of every day. Young, old, rich, poor— everyone can choose to find happiness in this way.” “是的，”列奥纳多说。 “幸福的秘密对每个人来说都是触手可及的，每一天的每一小时都如此。 “年轻、年老、富有、贫穷——每个人都可以用这种方式找到幸福。”

“How?” asked Annie. “What’s the secret?” She and Jack leaned forward, eager to hear the answer. “怎么找？”安妮问。 “秘密是什么？”她和杰克向前倾身，迫不及待地想听到答案。

“Curiosity,” said Leonardo. “好奇心，”列奥纳多说。

“Curiosity?” repeated Jack. He had curiosity. He had lots of it. “好奇心？”杰克重复道。他确实有好奇心。 他有很多这种东西。

“Always ask questions,” said Leonardo. “Always try to learn something new. Ask: Why? When? Where? What? Say: ‘I wonder what this means.’ ‘I wonder how that works.’ ‘I wonder what this person is like. And that person. And that one.’ I am always searching for answers to things I do not understand.” “总是要问问题，”列奥纳多说。 “总是试着学习新东西。问：为什么？ 什么时候？ 哪里？ 说：“我好奇这代表什么。”“我好奇这怎么运作。”“我好奇这个人是什么样的。还有那个人。 还有那个。”我一直在寻找我不理解的事情的答案。

“Me too!” said Jack. “我也是！”杰克说。

“And so I look forward to each new day, each spring and summer and fall and winter, and all the months and years ahead, because there is so much to discover,” said Leonardo. “因此，我期待着每一天，每一个春天、夏天、秋天和冬天，以及未来所有的月份和年份，因为还有这么多东西等着去发现，”列奥纳多说。

“Me too!” said Annie. “我也是！”安妮说。

“Through my curiosity, I forget my failures and sorrows, and I feel great happiness,” said Leonardo. He looked up at the sky. “For instance, one might wonder how they built that eight-sided dome on top of the cathedral.” “因为我的好奇心，我忘记了失败和悲伤，感到非常快乐，”莱昂纳多说。 他抬头看向天空。 “比如，有人可能会想知道，他们是怎么在教堂顶部建造那个八边形的圆顶的。”

“I do wonder that!” said Jack. “我确实想知道这个！”杰克说。

“And I wonder—what exactly makes the clouds change shape?” said Annie. “还有——是什么让云朵改变形状呢？”安妮说。

“And—and what makes bread crackly on the outside and soft on the inside?” asked Jack. “还有——是什么让面包外面酥脆，里面柔软呢？”杰克问。

“Are there really just ten types of noses?” said Annie. “真的只有十种鼻子吗？”安妮说。

“How many kinds of ears are there?” said Jack. “How many kinds of feet?” “耳朵有多少种呢？”杰克说。 “脚呢？有多少种？”

“Hands!” said Annie. “手！”安妮说。

“Eyebrows!” said Jack. “眉毛！”杰克说。

Their two voices spilled over each other as they kept calling out questions: “And who rings the bells in the bell tower?” “Why is the sky blue?” “Where do city birds sleep?” 他们的声音互相重叠，一边喊一边问问题：‘那么，是谁在钟楼里敲钟呢？’‘天空为什么是蓝色的？’‘城市里的鸟儿睡在哪里？’

“AND WHY WON’T LISA SMILE FOR LEONARDO?” asked Annie. ‘那为什么莉莎不愿意对着莱昂纳多笑呢？’安妮问。

Jack and Leonardo looked at Annie. Then they all turned and looked at Lisa. Jack had actually forgotten she was sitting near them. 杰克和莱昂纳多看着安妮。 然后他们都转过身，看着莉莎。 杰克其实已经忘记她坐在他们附近了。

The quiet, lovely woman blinked. “What?” she said. “What did you say?” 那个安静、美丽的女人眨了眨眼。 ‘什么？’她说。 ‘你刚才说什么？’

“Why won’t you smile for Leonardo, Lisa?” asked Annie. “Are you mad at him because you’ve been posing for three years?” ‘莉莎，为什么你不愿意对着莱昂纳多笑呢？’安妮问。 “你是因为他让你摆了三年姿势而生他的气吗？”

Lisa’s face grew red. She seemed to be fighting tears. She shook her head. 丽莎的脸变红了。 她似乎在和泪水作斗争。 她摇了摇头。

“Is—is there another reason?” Annie asked softly. “是——是不是还有别的原因？”安妮轻声问道。

Lisa looked at Leonardo. He was staring back at her. “Yes,” she whispered. “There is.” 丽莎看向莱昂纳多。 他正盯着她看。 “是的，”她低声说。 “有。”

“What is it?” Annie asked. “是什么？”安妮问道。

“I am afraid to smile,” said Lisa. She kept staring at Leonardo, though she was talking to Annie. “If I smile, Leonardo will paint my smile, and he will be done with me. He will sell my portrait to my family and never think of me again.” “我害怕笑出来，”丽莎说。 她一直盯着莱昂纳多看，尽管她是在和安妮说话。 “如果我笑了，莱昂纳多就会画下我的笑容，然后他就完成我了。 他会把我画像卖给我的家人，再也不想起我了。”

There was silence for a moment. Jack and Annie looked at Leonardo. “Annie,” said Leonardo finally, staring at Lisa. “Tell Lisa that if she will smile, I will finish her portrait. But tell her that I will not sell it to her family. I will carry it with me wherever I go, for the rest of my life, and I will never forget her.” 有一会儿没有人说话。 杰克和安妮看着莱昂纳多。 “安妮，”莱昂纳多终于说，他盯着丽莎看。 “告诉丽莎，如果她愿意笑，我就完成她的画像。 但要告诉她，我不会把它卖给她的家人。 我会带着它，无论我走到哪里，直到我生命的尽头，我永远不会忘记她。”

“Lisa, Leonardo says that—” started Annie. “丽莎，列奥纳多说—”安妮开始说。

But Lisa stopped her. “I heard,” she said softly. Then she smiled. It was a faint smile, but a mysterious and beautiful one. Her face glowed in the golden light of late afternoon. 但丽莎阻止了她。 “我听到了，”她轻声说。然后她笑了。 那是一个淡淡的微笑，但神秘而美丽。 她的脸在傍晚金色的光线下闪闪发光。

“Ah!” gasped Leonardo. “Keep that smile,” he said. He kept staring at Lisa as he dipped his paintbrush into a jar. “Please! Keep that smile, Mona Lisa.” “啊！”列奥纳多惊呼道。 “保持那个微笑，”他说。 他一直盯着丽莎，一边把画笔浸入罐子里。 “请！ 保持那个微笑，蒙娜丽莎。”

Mona Lisa? Jack had heard the name Mona Lisa before. 蒙娜丽莎？ 杰克以前听说过蒙娜丽莎这个名字。

Lisa kept smiling. Leonardo painted. 丽莎一直微笑着。列奥纳多在画画。

“Hey, listen,” Annie said to Jack. “嘿，听我说，”安妮对杰克说。

Jack listened. He heard a bird whistling and trilling. A plain brown bird was singing from the rooftop above the courtyard. 杰克听了。 他听到一只鸟在吹口哨和唱歌。 一只普通的棕色鸟正在庭院上方的屋顶上唱歌。

“That looks like the bird you let out of the cage,” said Jack. “那看起来就像你从笼子里放出来的那只鸟，”杰克说。

“It is him!” whispered Annie. “是它！”安妮小声说。

“He is a nightingale,” said Leonardo, keeping his eyes on Lisa. “A beautiful singer, yes?” “那是一只夜莺，”列奥纳多说，眼睛一直看着丽莎。 “一位漂亮的歌手，对吗？”

Annie smiled at Jack. “Time to go,” she said. “Remember Morgan’s rhyme—help the genius ‘Till the night bird sings its song.’ ” 安妮对杰克笑了笑。 “该走了，”她说。 “记住摩根的童谣——帮助天才‘直到夜鸟唱完它的歌’。”

“Right,” said Jack, sighing. “Good-bye, Leonardo.” “对，”杰克叹了口气说。 “再见，列奥纳多。”

Leonardo didn’t seem to hear him. 列奥纳多似乎没听见他的话。

“Good-bye, Lisa,” said Annie. “再见，丽莎，”安妮说。

Lisa turned her eyes to look at Jack and Annie. “Bye,” she whispered. 丽莎转过眼睛看向杰克和安妮。 “再见，”她小声说。

Then Leonardo turned and looked at them, too. “Yes, good-bye, my friends!” he said. “Come again soon, please! You have been a great help to me today.” 然后，列奥纳多也转过身，看着他们。 “再见吧，我的朋友们！”他说。 “请尽快再来，好吗？ 今天你对我帮助很大。”

“You helped us, too,” said Annie. “你也是帮了我们的，”安妮说。

Leonardo bowed to them. Then he turned back to his work. He painted Lisa’s smile as the nightingale sang on. The bird’s song grew louder and louder, until it seemed to fill the Florence night. 列奥纳多向他们鞠了一躬。 然后他又转身回到自己的工作上。 夜莺继续歌唱，他画出了丽莎的微笑。 鸟儿的歌声越来越响亮，直到仿佛充满了佛罗伦萨的夜晚。
