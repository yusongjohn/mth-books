4. Battle Scene 4. 战斗场景

Jack and Annie hurried to keep up with Leonardo. “So where are we going?” Annie asked. 杰克和安妮赶紧跟上列奥纳多。 “所以我们去哪儿呀？”安妮问。

“To the palace of the great council,” said Leonardo. “I was hired to paint a fresco in the council hall. I have been working on it for months.” “去大议会的宫殿，”列奥纳多说。 “我被雇来在议会大厅画一幅壁画。 我已经画了好几个月了。”

“What’s a fresco?” asked Jack. “什么是壁画呀？”杰克问。

“It is a work of art painted onto a wall,” said Leonardo. “One must spread plaster on the wall and then paint very quickly before it dries.” “这是画在墙上的艺术品，”列奥纳多说。 “必须先把石膏抹在墙上，然后在它干之前快速地画出来。”

“Sounds like fun,” said Annie. “听起来很有趣，”安妮说。

“Not for me,” said Leonardo. “I believe great art requires much thought. I like to paint slowly, and I change things as I go along. So for this fresco, I have invented a special oil paint that dries very slowly.” “对我来说不有趣，”列奥纳多说。 “我认为伟大的艺术需要很多思考。 我喜欢慢慢画，而且在画的过程中我会不断修改。 所以对于这幅壁画，我发明了一种特殊的油画颜料，它干得非常慢。”

“Does it work?” said Jack. “它有效吗？”杰克问。

“Too well,” said Leonardo. “Now I have a new problem: Neither the plaster nor my oil paints have dried at all.” “太有效了，”列奥纳多说。 “现在我有了一个新问题：石膏和我的油画颜料一点都没干。”

“Oh, no,” said Annie. “哦，不，”安妮说。

“But today all will be well!” Leonardo said cheerfully. “I have a plan to speed up the drying process. This morning I will fix everything!” “但今天一切都会好起来的！”列奥纳多愉快地说。 “我有一个计划，可以加快干燥过程。 今天早上我会把一切都修好！”

Leonardo led Jack and Annie into a square with a large building. “There it is,” he said. “The palace of the great council.” 列奥纳多带杰克和安妮走进了一个有大型建筑的广场。 “那就是，”他说。 “伟大的议会宫殿。”

The palace looked like a fortress. It had rough-looking stone walls and a tower that rose high into the air. 宫殿看起来像一座堡垒。 它有粗糙的石墙，还有一座高高耸立在空中的塔。

“The palace is a very important place,” said Leonardo. “It is where the governing council of Florence meets. Come along.” He opened one of the grand doors and guided Jack and Annie into a courtyard with a fountain. “This way to the council hall,” he said, “and the latest work of Leonardo da Vinci.” “这座宫殿是一个非常重要的地方，”列奥纳多说。 “这就是佛罗伦萨的治理委员会开会的地方。” 快点来。”他打开了一扇宏伟的大门，把杰克和安妮带进了一个有喷泉的庭院。 “这边去市政厅，”他说，“还有达·芬奇的最新作品。”

Leonardo bounded up some steps and down a corridor. Jack and Annie hurried after him until he passed through another grand doorway and stopped. 达·芬奇蹦蹦跳跳地上了几个台阶，然后沿着走廊走过去。 杰克和安妮赶紧跟在他后面，直到他穿过另一扇宏伟的大门停下。

Leonardo put down his basket and raised his hands. “My fresco,” he said. 莱昂纳多放下他的篮子，举起了双手。 “我的壁画，”他说。

“Oh, man,” breathed Jack. “哦，天啊，”杰克叹道。

They were in an enormous room with tall, arched windows and vast white walls. Several young men stood on a wooden platform on the far side of the room. On the wall above them was a giant painting of a battle scene. It showed a tangle of men on horseback, fighting over a flag. 他们在一个巨大的房间里，那里有高大的拱形窗户和广阔的白色墙壁。 几个年轻人站在房间远端的一个木制平台上。 他们头顶的墙上挂着一幅巨大的画，画的是战斗的场景。 画中有很多骑马的人在争夺一面旗帜，场面混乱。

The men in the painting seemed to be in a fury as they slashed at each other with their swords. Their faces were twisted, their mouths snarling. Even their horses looked wild and angry. 画中的人似乎非常愤怒，他们用剑互相砍杀。 他们的脸扭曲着，嘴巴张着发出怒吼。 就连他们的马看起来也又野又生气。

“The city has paid me to paint a scene from a battle once fought to defend Florence,” said Leonardo. “They wanted me to paint a scene of glory. But I believe war is a beastly madness. I hope my painting shows that.” “这座城市曾请我画一幅描绘保卫佛罗伦萨的战斗场景的画，”列奥纳多说。 “他们希望我画出一场光荣的战斗场面。 但我认为战争是一种野蛮的疯狂。 我希望我的画能表现出这一点。”

“Oh, it does,” said Annie. “哦，你的画确实做到了，”安妮说。

Jack nodded. It was the scariest painting he’d ever seen. 杰克点了点头。 这是他见过最吓人的画。

“Zorro!” called Leonardo. “佐罗！”莱昂纳多喊道。

One of the young men on the platform climbed down a ladder and jumped to the floor. He was a sturdy-looking teenager with a red face and wavy black hair. 平台上的一位年轻人爬下梯子，跳到了地板上。 他是个看起来结实的少年，脸红红的，有一头波浪状的黑发。

“Are things any better this morning?” asked Leonardo. “今天早上情况有好转吗？”问了莱昂纳多。

“No, the paint is still very damp to the touch,” said Zorro. “没有，油漆还是摸起来很湿，”佐罗说。

“Then let us proceed with our plan,” said Leonardo. “Did the pots arrive from the blacksmith?” “那么让我们按照计划行动吧，”莱昂纳多说道。 “那些锅是从铁匠铺送来的吗？

“Yes, over there,” said Zorro. He pointed at two large iron pots beneath the platform. “是的，在那边，”佐罗说道。 他指着平台下方的两个大铁锅。

“And you brought the wood?” said Leonardo. “你把木头带来了吗？”莱昂纳多问。

“Yes,” said Zorro. He pointed to a pile of wood stacked against a wall. “带来了，”佐罗回答。 他指着墙边堆着的一堆木头。

Leonardo set down his basket and headed over to the platform. 莱昂纳多放下篮子，朝平台走去。

“What’s the plan, Leonardo?” asked Annie as she and Jack followed him. “你的计划是什么，莱昂纳多？”安妮问，她和杰克跟着他。

“My apprentices and I will fill the pots with wood and lift them onto the platform,” said Leonardo. “Then we will light fires in them. The heat of the fires will quickly dry the fresco.” “我和我的学徒们会把木头装进锅里，然后抬到平台上，”莱昂纳多说。 “然后我们在锅里生火。 火的热量会很快把壁画晾干。”

“How can we help?” asked Jack. “我们怎么帮忙？”杰克问。

“Bring us some kindling,” said Leonardo. “给我们拿些引火物来，”莱昂纳多说。

“No problem!” said Jack. He put down his bag, and he and Annie hurried to the wood stack. “没问题！”杰克说。 他放下背包，和安妮赶紧跑到木堆那里。

“Kindling?” she said. “引火物？”她问。

“Small pieces of wood,” said Jack. “They catch fire first and help get the big pieces started.” “就是小木块，”杰克说。 “它们最容易着火，能帮助大木块也燃起来。”

Jack and Annie picked sticks and twigs from the wood stack. They carried the kindling back to Leonardo, and he dumped it into the iron pots. Zorro brought over some logs. Then he and Leonardo hooked the handles of the pots to a system of ropes and pulleys. 杰克和安妮从木堆里捡了树枝和小棍子。 他们把引火物拿回给莱昂纳多，他把它们倒进铁锅里。 佐罗搬来了几根木头。 然后他和莱昂纳多把锅柄挂到一套绳子和滑轮系统上。

“Pull!” Leonardo shouted. “拉！”列奥纳多喊道。

The apprentices on the platform pulled on the ropes. The heavy pots swung into the air. 平台上的人们拉住了绳子。 沉重的锅被吊到了空中。

“Steady! Steady!” Leonardo shouted. “稳住！ 稳住！”列奥纳多喊道。

The apprentices slowly hauled up the pots. Then they pulled them onto the platform and placed them in front of the fresco. 学徒们慢慢地把锅拉了上来。 然后他们把锅拉到平台上，放在壁画前面。

“Light the fires!” shouted Leonardo. “点火！”列奥纳多喊道。

Zorro lit a candle from a torch burning at the entrance of the hall. He carried the candle up the ladder and used its fire to light the kindling. Soon the wood in the pots began to blaze. 佐罗从大厅入口燃烧的火把上点燃了一根蜡烛。 他拿着蜡烛爬上梯子，用它的火点燃了引火物。 很快，锅里的木头开始燃烧起来。

“Bring more wood!” Leonardo shouted. “Bring more wood!” “搬更多木头来！”列奥纳多喊道。 “搬更多木头来！”，

Jack and Annie hurried back to the woodpile. They gathered bigger pieces of wood and rushed back to the ladder. Apprentices lifted the wood up to the platform and added it to the fires in the pots. 杰克和安妮赶紧跑回木堆那儿。 他们捡了更大的木头，又匆匆跑回梯子那儿。 学徒们把木头抬到平台上，加到锅里的火里。

Soon flames were shooting high into the air, warming the fresco. Standing with Leonardo below the platform, Jack and Annie stared up at the battle scene. The room grew hotter and hotter. 很快，火焰冲得很高，把壁画照得暖暖的。 站在平台下面，和列奥纳多一起，杰克和安妮抬头看着那场战斗的场景。 房间变得越来越热。

With the fires blazing above and smoke curling through the air, Jack felt like he was in the middle of the battle himself. He could hear the clanging swords, neighing horses, and shouting men. He could feel the “beastly madness” of war that Leonardo had talked about. 火光熊熊，烟雾在空中盘旋，杰克感觉自己仿佛真的置身于战斗之中。 他能听到剑刃碰撞的声音、马儿嘶叫的声音，还有人们大声喊叫的声音。 他能感受到战争带来的“野蛮疯狂”，就像莱昂纳多说过的那样。

Suddenly Jack heard real shrieks—Leonardo’s apprentices were all yelling. 突然，杰克听到了真正的尖叫声——莱昂纳多的学徒们都在大喊大叫。

“It is dripping, Master!” one cried. “它在滴下来了，大师！”一个人喊道。

“The paint is running!” shouted another. “颜料在往下流！”另一个人喊道。

Jack looked back at the fresco. The helmets of the warriors were melting down over their furious faces. 杰克回头看向那幅壁画。 战士们的头盔正在他们愤怒的脸上融化。

“AHHH!” cried Leonardo with a look of horror. “Kill the fires! Kill the fires!” “啊——！”莱昂纳多惊恐地喊道。 “灭火！灭火！”
