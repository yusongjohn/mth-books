10. Questions 10. 问题

It was twilight when Annie and Jack stepped back out into the street. 天色渐暗时，安妮和杰克重新走回街上。

“Where’s the tree with the tree house?” asked Annie. “带树屋的那棵树在哪里？”安妮问。

“Somewhere over the bridge beyond the big dome,” said Jack. “在那座大圆顶后面过桥的地方，”杰克说。

They kept their eyes on the dome as they threaded their way through the streets of Florence. When they came to the cathedral, the square was quiet. The cathedral’s great doors were open. Jack and Annie could see candles burning inside. 他们一直盯着圆顶，穿过了佛罗伦萨的街道。 他们来到大教堂时，广场上很安静。 大教堂的大门敞开着。 杰克和安妮能看到里面点燃的蜡烛。

Jack and Annie kept walking and soon came to the market. The hundreds of tents and stalls were all closed for the night. The square was empty. 杰克和安妮继续往前走，很快就到了市场。 成百上千的帐篷和摊位都为夜晚关上了。 广场上空无一人。

Jack and Annie returned the way they had come that morning. Walking down the same narrow lanes, they saw that all the shops were closed now, too. They crossed the covered bridge and walked along the flowing river, past quiet houses where smoke curled from chimneys into the darkening sky. 杰克和安妮沿着早上来的路返回了。 他们沿着同一条狭窄的小巷走着，发现现在所有的商店也都关门了。 他们穿过有顶的桥，沿着流淌的河岸走，经过一些安静的房子，烟囱里冒出的烟飘向渐渐变暗的天空。

Finally Jack and Annie came to the hedge that hid the tree with the tree house. In the gray light of dusk, they climbed up the rope ladder. 最后，杰克和安妮来到了那道隐藏着树屋的树篱前。 在黄昏灰暗的光线下，他们爬上了绳梯。

“Before we go home, I want to look something up,” said Jack. He pulled their research book out of his bag and looked in the index for Mona Lisa. He found it and turned to the right page. “在我们回家之前，我想查点东西，”杰克说。 他从包里拿出他们的研究书，在目录里查找蒙娜丽莎。 他找到了，翻到正确的那一页。

“Look! It’s Lisa!” said Annie. “看！ 是丽莎！”安妮说。

Jack and Annie stared at a picture of Leonardo’s painting. It looked exactly the same, except now there was a smile on Lisa’s face, the same smile they’d just seen in real life. Jack read aloud: 杰克和安妮看着一幅列奥纳多画作的图片。 看起来一模一样，只是现在丽莎的脸上带着微笑，和他们刚刚在现实中看到的一模一样。杰克大声读道：

Leonardo da Vinci’s painting of Mona Lisa is perhaps the most famous painting in the world. It is believed to be a portrait of Lisa del Gioconda. (The Italian word mona means “my lady.”) Leonardo da Vinci never sold the portrait of Lisa. He took it with him everywhere he traveled until he died. 达芬奇画的蒙娜丽莎可能是世界上最著名的画。人们认为这幅画是丽莎·德尔·吉奥康达的肖像。（意大利语中mona的意思是“我的女士”）达芬奇从未卖掉丽莎的肖像。他带着这幅画到处旅行，直到去世。

Jack closed the book. “He kept his promise,” he said. 杰克合上了书。 “他信守了承诺，”他说。

“I knew he would,” said Annie. She sighed. “Good-bye, Leonardo,” she whispered. Then she picked up their note from Morgan and pointed at the words Frog Creek. “ I wish we could go there,” she said. “我知道他会的，”安妮说。她叹了口气。 “再见了，列奥纳多，”她轻声说。 然后她捡起摩根给他们的便条，指着上面写着蛙溪镇的字。 “真希望我们能去那里，”她说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。

Sunlight flooded through the tree house window. No time had passed in Frog Creek. The school bell was still ringing, announcing that class would start in ten minutes. Jack and Annie were wearing their school clothes. Jack’s cloth bag had changed back into his backpack. 阳光透过树屋的窗户照了进来。 在蛙溪镇，时间一分都没过去。 学校铃声还在响，宣布十分钟后就要上课了。 杰克和安妮穿着他们的校服。 杰克的布袋又变回了他的书包。

“We have to hurry,” said Annie. “我们必须快点，”安妮说。

“I know,” said Jack. He looked inside his pack. He was happy to see the Wand of Dianthus. As he pulled out their research book, a piece of paper fell out. It was the sketch of Leonardo’s angel. “我知道，”杰克说。 他看了看自己背包里的东西。 他看到迪安瑟斯的魔杖时很高兴。 当他拿出他们的研究书时，一张纸掉了出来。 那是列奥纳多天使的草图。

“Oh, I forgot all about this,” said Jack. He and Annie looked at the sketch. “哦，我完全把这忘了，”杰克说。 杰克和安妮看着那张草图。

“It shows he was really a good drawer,” said Annie. “这说明他真的画得很好，”安妮说。

“Yep,” said Jack. “And it will remind us about Leonardo’s secret of happiness.” “是啊，”杰克说。 “它会提醒我们列奥纳多的快乐秘密。”

“He was curious about everything,” said Annie. “Angels, noses, birds.” “他对一切都很好奇，”安妮说。 “天使、鼻子、鸟。”

“Feathers, flowers, wolves, and spiders,” added Jack. “羽毛、花朵、狼和蜘蛛，”杰克补充道。

“Shadows, light,” said Annie. “影子、光，”安妮说。

“Bells, clouds, the moon,” said Jack. “钟声、云朵、月亮，”杰克说。

“And every time he was unhappy about something, his curiosity seemed to make him happy again,” said Annie. “每当他对某件事感到不开心时，他的好奇心似乎又让他变得开心起来，”安妮说。

Jack took the angel drawing from Annie and carefully put it back into his pack. “Come on,” he said. “We don’t want to be late for school.” Jack started down the rope ladder, and Annie followed. 杰克从安妮那里拿起了天使的画，小心地把它放回了自己的背包里。 “快点，”他说。 “我们可不想上学迟到。”杰克开始沿着绳梯往下走，安妮也跟着下来了。

Jack and Annie walked together through the sun-bright woods. “I wonder where my new class will be,” said Annie. 杰克和安妮一起穿过阳光明媚的树林。 “我想知道我的新班级会在哪里，”安妮说。

“Yeah,” said Jack, “and I wonder where my desk will be. Close to the window? Or the door?” “是的，”杰克说，“我不知道我的书桌会在哪里。 靠近窗户？还是靠近门？“

“And will Randy and Jenny be in my class again this year?” said Annie. “And will Randy and Jenny be in my class again this year?” said 安妮。

“Will Joe be in mine?” said Jack. “Will Joe be in mine?” said 杰克。

“Whatever happened to Raymond Johnson?” asked Annie. “Is he coming back this year?” “Whatever happened to Raymond Johnson?” asked 安妮。 “他今年还会回来吗？”

“I hope so,” said Jack. “And who’s the new librarian? And the new music teacher?” “希望如此，”杰克说。 “新来的图书管理员是谁？ 还有新来的音乐老师？”

“Yeah, and what kind of noses do they have?” said Annie. “对了，他们长什么样子的鼻子？”安妮说。

Jack laughed. All the questions about school didn’t make him nervous anymore. Now he was eager to find out the answers. He quickened his steps. “And how long will it take us to get there, if we walk really, really fast?” he said. 杰克笑了。 关于学校的所有问题都不再让他紧张了。 现在他迫不及待地想找到答案。 他加快了脚步。 “如果我们非常、非常快地走，要多久才能到那里呢？”他说。

“What if we run?” said Annie. “那如果我们跑呢？”安妮说。

Jack and Annie started running, as the wind blew through the trees and the leaves fluttered through the air and the birds sang from the branches in the Monday-morning woods. 杰克和安妮开始奔跑，风穿过树木，树叶在空中飘动，鸟儿在周一早晨的树林里枝头歌唱。
