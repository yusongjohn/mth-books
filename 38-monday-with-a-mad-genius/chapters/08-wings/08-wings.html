8. Wings! 8. 翅膀！

Annie held up the wand. “Close your eyes, Leonardo,” she said. 安妮举起了魔杖。 “闭上眼睛，莱昂纳多，”她说。

Leonardo just shook his head. 莱昂纳多只是摇了摇头。

“Please?” said Annie. “Just for a second?” “求你了？”安妮说。 “就一会儿？”

Leonardo put his head in his hands. 达·芬奇把头放在手里。

“Listen,” said Annie. “This morning you said that a great artist has to combine observation with imagination.” “听着，”安妮说。 “今天早上你说，一位伟大的艺术家必须把观察和想象力结合起来。”

Leonardo barely nodded. 达·芬奇微微点了点头。

“Well, watch out—because this is the imagination part!” said Annie. She waved the wand at Leonardo, then at herself and Jack. Counting her words on her fingers, she said in a loud, clear voice: “Make. Us. Fly. Like. Birds.” “好了，小心点——因为这是想象的部分！”安妮说。 她挥动魔杖指向列奥纳多，然后指向自己和杰克。 她一边数着手指上的词，一边用响亮清晰的声音说道：“让。我们。 飞。像。 鸟。”

Leonardo’s arms whipped out to his sides. They sprouted long grayish feathers. He let out a yelp. The next thing Jack knew, his arms had turned into feathery wings, too! So had Annie’s! 列奥纳多的双臂猛地向两侧张开。 他的手臂上长出了长长的灰白色羽毛。 他发出一声尖叫。 接下来杰克知道的事情是，他的手臂也变成了羽毛翅膀！安妮的也变成了！

“What’s happening?” cried Leonardo. “这是怎么回事？”列奥纳多喊道。

“Wings!” said Annie. “翅膀！”安妮说。

Jack’s wings felt light and airy, yet strong and powerful. 杰克的翅膀感觉又轻又飘，却又强壮有力。

“Now we can fly!” said Annie. “现在我们可以飞了！”安妮说。

“Wings?” said Leonardo, looking stunned. Then he burst out laughing. “We have wings! We have wings! Run! Run into the wind!” “翅膀？”列奥纳多惊讶地看着，说。 然后他突然笑起来。 “我们有翅膀！我们有翅膀！” 跑！ “冲进风里！”

Jack, Annie, and Leonardo all stretched out their wings and took quick steps forward. The wind rushed under their feathers and lifted them off the ground. 杰克、安妮和莱昂纳多都伸展翅膀，快速向前迈步。 风从他们的羽毛下冲过去，把他们抬离了地面。

“WHOOOAH!” cried Leonardo. “哇哦！”莱昂纳多喊道。

Leonardo, Jack, and Annie flapped their wings and soared high into the sky. Then they caught a gentle wind and stopped flapping. Twisting this way and that, they glided in a big circle above the countryside. 莱昂纳多、杰克和安妮扇动翅膀，飞向高空。 接着，他们抓住了一阵轻柔的风，停止了扇动翅膀。 他们左右扭动身体，滑翔着在乡村上空画出一个大圆圈。

Jack felt as light as the wind. His heart beat wildly. 杰克感觉轻得像风一样。 他的心跳得飞快。

“Incredible, huh?” yelled Annie. “太不可思议了，对吧？”安妮大喊。

“Best flying ever!” shouted Jack. “这是我飞得最好的一次！”杰克喊道。

Jack and Annie had flown lots before. They’d flown on a dragon, on a bicycle, on a winged lion, on a magic carpet, and on the back of a white stag in Camelot. They’d even flown as ravens over a haunted castle. But this was the first time they’d ever flown on their own, just as themselves. 杰克和安妮以前飞过很多次了。 他们骑过龙，骑过自行车，骑过有翅膀的狮子，骑过魔法地毯，还在卡梅洛特骑过一只白色的鹿。 他们甚至曾化作渡鸦，飞越一座闹鬼的城堡。 但这是他们第一次独自飞行，以自己原来的样子飞行。

“Follow me!” cried Leonardo. He tilted his wings and flew out of the circle. Jack and Annie flew after him. They all swooped high up over the quiet hills and glided through low clouds. “跟我来！”莱昂纳多喊道。 他倾斜翅膀，飞出了圆圈。 杰克和安妮跟在他后面飞了出去。 他们一起俯冲到安静的山丘上空，又滑翔穿过低低的云层。

The cool, wet mist blew against Jack’s face. He felt as if he were swimming through the sky, as if the clouds were water keeping him afloat. 清凉潮湿的雾气吹到杰克脸上。 他感觉就像在天空中游泳，仿佛云朵是水，托着他漂浮。

Laughing and whooping with delight, Leonardo led Jack and Annie out of the clouds and down over the yellow meadows and the pale green olive groves. 欢笑着、大声喊着，莱昂纳多带着杰克和安妮飞出云层，向下飞过金黄的草地和淡绿色的橄榄树林。

“Helloooo!” Leonardo shouted to farmers plowing a field. But the farmers didn’t look up. “哈喽——！”莱昂纳多朝正在田里耕作的农夫大声喊道。 但是农夫们没有抬头看。

“Helloooo!” he called to grape pickers working in a vineyard, but they didn’t look up, either. “你好！”他朝在葡萄园里采摘葡萄的人喊道，但他们也没有抬头看。

No one on the ground looked up, but all the birds in the sky seemed to take notice. Birds cawed and swooped near them, as if welcoming them to their world. Birds flew alongside them and spread out in front of them, leading them over the city walls of Florence. 地面上的人没有抬头，但天空中的鸟儿似乎都注意到了他们。 鸟儿们在他们附近叫着、盘旋着，好像在欢迎他们来到它们的世界。 鸟儿们飞在他们旁边，还飞在他们前面，领着他们飞过了佛罗伦萨的城墙。

Jack, Annie, and Leonardo circled with the birds over the sea of red-tiled roofs, over the great dome of the cathedral, and over the bell tower of the palace of the great council. 杰克、安妮和列奥纳多随着鸟儿在红瓦屋顶的上方盘旋，飞过了大教堂的穹顶，飞过了大议会宫的钟楼。

“Florence looks so neat and orderly from up here!” Leonardo cried to Jack and Annie. “I wish I had my sketchbook!” “从这里看，佛罗伦萨看起来井井有条、整整齐齐！”列奥纳多对杰克和安妮喊道。 “真希望我带着我的素描本！”

The city did look orderly from the sky, thought Jack: 从天上往下看，这座城市确实看起来井井有条，杰克心想：

the busy market 热闹的市场

with its rows of stalls and tents, 有成排的摊位和帐篷。

the narrow lanes with the brightly colored 狭窄的小巷里五颜六色的

clothes waving from clotheslines, 衣服在晾衣绳上飘扬。

the long covered bridge, 那座长长的有顶棚的桥。

the winding, sparkling river. 那条蜿蜒流淌、闪闪发光的河。

Jack, Annie, and Leonardo soared with the birds back over the city walls out to the countryside. They glided over the olive groves and vineyards. Then they circled above the spot where Leonardo’s Great Bird lay broken in the grass. 杰克、安妮和莱昂纳多随着鸟儿一起飞过城墙，飞向乡下。 他们滑翔着飞过橄榄树林和葡萄园。 然后他们绕着莱昂纳多的“大鸟”坠落在草地上的地方盘旋。

The birds swooped up and vanished behind the clouds. Leonardo, Jack, and Annie glided down toward the ground. They opened their wings wide and then, gently and easily, their feet touched the grass. Their wings fluttered with tiny beats, and the three of them took quick hopping steps before coming to a full stop. 鸟儿俯冲而上，消失在云朵后面。 莱昂纳多、杰克和安妮滑翔着向地面飞去。 它们张开翅膀，然后轻而易举地，脚碰到了草地。 它们的翅膀轻轻拍打，三个人快速跳了几下，然后停了下来。

When Jack, Annie, and Leonardo were steady on their feet, their long feathers disappeared and their bird wings became arms again. Leonardo looked dazed. He stared up at the sky. Then he staggered a few steps and fell facedown into the grass. 当杰克、安妮和莱昂纳多站稳脚跟时，他们长长的羽毛消失了，鸟翅膀又变回了手臂。 莱昂纳多看起来晕晕乎乎的。 他抬头看着天空。 然后他踉跄了几步，扑通一下脸朝下倒进了草里。

“Leonardo?” said Annie. “莱昂纳多？”安妮说。

Oh, no, thought Jack. He’s had a heart attack. 哦，天哪，杰克心想。 他心脏病发作了。

“Leonardo?” said Annie. She knelt down near him. “莱昂纳多？”安妮说。 她在他旁边蹲了下来。

Leonardo rolled over and stared up at Jack and Annie. “What … what just happened?” he stammered. “Did we fly? Did we really fly? Or was it a dream?” 列奥纳多翻了个身，抬头看着杰克和安妮。 “那……刚刚发生了什么？”他结结巴巴地说。 “我们……我们飞了吗？ 我们真的飞了吗？ 还是说只是个梦？”

“Uh … well …” Jack didn’t know what to say. To explain the wand to Leonardo, they’d have to start way back at the very beginning—with the tree house, Morgan, Merlin, Teddy, Kathleen, Dianthus. It would take forever. “呃……嗯……”杰克不知道该说什么。 为了向列奥纳多解释魔杖，他们得从最开始讲起——从树屋、摩根、梅林、泰迪、凯瑟琳、迪安图斯开始。 这会花很长时间。

“Well,” said Annie. “One day a long time ago, we were playing in the woods and we saw—” “好吧，”安妮说。 “很久很久以前的一天，我们在森林里玩耍，然后我们看到了——”

“Annie—” Jack shook his head. “安妮——” 杰克摇了摇头。

Annie frowned. “I guess it can’t really be explained,” she said. 安妮皱起了眉头。 “我想这真的很难解释，”她说。

Leonardo looked up at the sky. “No, no,” he said. “I think you are right. Perhaps some things should remain mysteries and are better kept in our hearts. We should not try to explain them.” 列奥纳多抬头看向天空。 “不，不，”他说。 “我想你是对的。 也许有些事情应该保持神秘，最好保留在我们心里。 我们不应该试图去解释它们。”

That’s an amazing statement, Jack thought, from a person who always tries to explain everything. 这是杰克从一个总是试图解释一切的人口中听到的惊人的话。

“But if I had to explain it, I would explain it this way,” said Leonardo. He leapt to his feet. “For years, I wrote down all my observations of bird flight. I made hundreds of drawings. These things helped me build my flying machine. But in the end, something was missing—something very important.” “但如果我要解释的话，我会这样解释，”列奥纳多说。 他猛地站了起来。 “多年来，我记录了所有关于鸟类飞行的观察。 我画了数百张图。 这些帮助我制造了我的飞行器。 但最后，还缺少了一样东西——非常重要的东西。”

“What?” asked Annie. “是什么？”安妮问。

“The spirit of a bird!” said Leonardo. “A bird is not just a machine. A bird has a spirit. And with the two of you, I somehow gained that spirit. If only for a short time and if only in my imagination, we all became more bird than human!” “鸟的精神！”列奥纳多说。 “鸟不仅仅是一台机器。 鸟有精神。” 而且有你们两个人，我竟然找回了那种精神。 哪怕只是一小会儿，哪怕只是在我的想象中，我们大家都变得比人类更像鸟！

“And did the spirit of the bird mend your heart?” Annie asked. “鸟的精神治愈了你的心吗？”安妮问。

Leonardo smiled. “Yes, my heart is mended now. I am ready to leave this dream behind and move on to others. And it does not matter that the world will never know of my great triumph.” 列奥纳多笑了。 “是的，现在我的心已经痊愈了。” 我准备离开这个梦，去迎接其他的梦。 而且，即使全世界都不知道我取得了伟大的胜利，也没关系。

“So maybe fame isn’ t the secret of happiness?” said Jack. “所以也许名声不是幸福的秘密？”杰克说。

“Absolutely not,” said Leonardo. “I know that now. We must do what we do to satisfy our own hearts. For instance, I am working on a painting now. I love it. I do not care if others ever see it.… Oh! Oh, no! What time is it?” He jerked his head around to look at the sun. “I must go! I will be late!” “绝对不是，”列奥纳多说。 “我现在知道了。” 我们必须做让自己心里舒服的事。 比如，我现在正在画一幅画，我很喜欢它。 我不在乎别人有没有看到它。…哦！哦，不！ 几点了？”他猛地转过头去看太阳。 “我得走了！我要迟到了！”，

“Late for what?” said Annie. “迟到干什么？”安妮说。

“To meet my model at the studio!” said Leonardo. “The woman I am painting in the portrait I was just talking about! We must return!” “去和我的模特在画室见面！”列奥纳多说。 “就是我刚刚提到的那幅肖像画里正在画的那位女士！我们必须回去！”，

Jack, Annie, and Leonardo hurried back to the cart and climbed in. Leonardo snapped the reins, and the white horse started clopping back toward Florence. 杰克、安妮和列奥纳多赶紧跑回马车，爬了进去。 列奥纳多猛地拉了拉缰绳，那匹白马开始往佛罗伦萨方向走起来。
