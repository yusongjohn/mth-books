10. ’Night, Peanut 10. 晚安，花生

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开眼睛。

Then he opened his fist. He stared at the moonstone in his hand. It was clear and smooth. It almost seemed to glow. 然后他松开拳头。 他盯着手里的月光石。 它又透明又光滑。 几乎在发光。

“We’re home,” said Annie. “我们到家了，”安妮说。

Squeak.

Annie and the mouse were looking out the window. 安妮和小老鼠正往窗外看。

Jack looked with them. 杰克也一起看。

The sun was setting in the distance. 远处，太阳正在落下。

No time at all had passed in Frog Creek. 蛙溪镇一点时间都没过。

They heard their neighbor’s dog, Henry, bark. They heard crickets chirping. 他们听见邻居的狗亨利在叫。 他们听见蟋蟀在叫。

In the distance, they could see their dad step out of their house. He stood on their porch. 远处，他们看见爸爸从家里走出来。 他站在门廊上。

“Ja-ack! An-nie!” he called. “杰——克！ 安——妮！”他喊。

Time for dinner. 该吃晚饭了。

“Com-ing!” Annie shouted. “来——了！”安妮喊。

Jack sat back on his heels. He looked at the moonstone again. 杰克蹲坐下来。 他又看了看月光石。

“I guess we have one of the four things,” he said. “看来我们有四样东西里的一样了，”他说。

“We’ll look for the other three tomorrow,” Annie said. “另外三样我们明天再找，”安妮说。

Jack nodded. They had a lot more work to do. 杰克点点头。 他们还有很多事要做。

He put the moonstone in his pocket. 他把月光石放进口袋。

He pulled on his pack. 他背上背包。

“Ready?” he said. “好了吗？”他说。

“Wait,” said Annie. She took off one of her sneakers. She pulled off her sock. Then she put her sneaker back on. “等一下，”安妮说。 她脱下一只运动鞋。 她脱掉袜子。 然后又把鞋穿上。

“What are you doing?” said Jack. “你在干什么？”杰克说。

“I’m making a bed,” she said. “我在做床，”她说。

“A what?” “做什么？”

“Bed! You know, for Peanut to sleep in.” Annie picked up the mouse. She tucked it inside her sock. “床！ 就是给花生睡觉的。”安妮捧起小老鼠。 她把它塞进袜子里。

“’Night, Peanut,” she said softly. “晚安，花生，”她轻声说。

Squeak.

“Oh brother,” said Jack. “天哪，”杰克说。

Annie held the mouse close to Jack. 安妮把小老鼠凑到杰克面前。

“Kiss it goodnight, Jack,” she said. “亲亲它，跟它说晚安，杰克，”她说。

“Don’t be silly,” he said. “Let’s go.” “别傻了，”他说。 “走吧。”

“Thanks for helping us,” Annie said to the mouse. “谢谢你帮我们，”安妮对小老鼠说。

She put Peanut gently down on the glowing M. She pulled Morgan’s message out of her pouch. And put it next to the mouse. 她轻轻把花生放在发光的M上。 她从口袋里拿出摩根的字条。 放在小老鼠旁边。

“See you tomorrow,” she said. Then she started down the ladder. “明天见，”她说。 然后她开始往梯子下爬。

Jack stared at the mouse. It looked back at him. 杰克盯着小老鼠。 它也看着他。

For a moment, its dark eyes looked old and wise. 有那么一会儿，它黑黑的眼睛看起来又老又聪明。

“Come on, Jack!” called Annie. “快点，杰克！”安妮喊。

Jack kissed its tiny head. 杰克亲了亲它的小脑袋。

“Night-night, Peanut,” he whispered. “晚安，花生，”他小声说。

Then Jack headed down the rope ladder. 然后杰克顺着绳梯往下爬。

It got darker and darker as he went down. 他越往下，天越来越黑。

By the time he stepped onto the ground, it was almost completely black. 等他踩到地上，几乎全黑了。

“Where are you?” said Jack. “你在哪儿？”杰克说。

“Here,” said Annie. Her hand bumped his. He took it. “这儿，”安妮说。 她的手碰到了他的手。他握住了。

“Careful,” he said. “小心，”他说。

“Careful yourself,” she said. “你自己小心，”她说。

Together they took off through the cool, dark woods. 他们一起穿过又凉又黑的树林。

They moved silently and swiftly—two shadow warriors returning home. 他们又快又静地走着——两个影子武士回家了。
