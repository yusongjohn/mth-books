1. Back into the Woods 1. 再进树林

“Let’s look again, Jack,” said Annie. “咱们再去看看吧，杰克。”安妮说。

Jack and Annie were walking home from the library. The path went right by the Frog Creek woods. 杰克和安妮正从图书馆走回家。 小路正好经过蛙溪镇的树林。

Jack sighed. “We looked this morning,” he said. “We looked the day before. And the day before that.” 杰克叹了口气。 “咱们早上已经看过了。”他说。 “前天也看过了。 大前天也看过了。”

“Then you don’t have to come,” said Annie. “I’ll go look by myself.” “那你就不用来了。”安妮说。 “我自己去看。”

She took off into the woods. 她一溜烟跑进了树林。

“Annie, wait!” Jack called. “It’s almost dark! We have to get home!” “安妮，等等！”杰克喊道。 “天都快黑了！ 咱们得回家了！”

But Annie had disappeared among the trees. 可安妮已经消失在树丛里了。

Jack stared at the woods. He was starting to lose hope. Maybe he would never see Morgan again. 杰克盯着树林。 他开始有点灰心了。 也许他再也见不到摩根了。

Weeks had passed. And there had not been one sign of Morgan le Fay. Nor had there been one sign of her magic tree house. 好几个星期过去了。 摩根·勒费伊连影子都没出现过。 她的神奇树屋也一点踪影都没有。

“Jack!” Annie called from the woods. “It’s back!” “杰克！”安妮在树林里喊。 “它回来了！”

Oh, she’s just pretending as usual, Jack thought. But his heart started to race. 哦，她又在装了，跟平时一样，杰克想。 可他的心还是怦怦跳了起来。

“Hurry!” called Annie. “快点！”安妮喊。

“She better not be kidding,” said Jack. “她最好别是在开玩笑。”杰克说。

He took off into the woods to find Annie. 他赶紧跑进树林去找安妮。

Night was falling fast. Crickets chirped loudly. It was hard to see through the shadows. 夜色很快落了下来。 蟋蟀叫得可响了。 阴影里什么都看不清。

“Annie!” Jack shouted. “安妮！”杰克大声喊。

“Here!” she called. “这边！”她喊。

Jack kept walking. “Here where?” he called back. 杰克继续往前走。 “这边是哪边？”他喊回去。

“Here here!”

Annie’s voice came from above. 安妮的声音是从上面传来的。

Jack looked up. 杰克抬头一看。

“Oh man,” he breathed. “天哪。”他轻轻吸了口气。

Annie waved from the window of a tree house. It was in the tallest oak in the woods. A long rope ladder hung down from it. 安妮在树屋的窗口朝他挥手。 树屋就在树林里最高的那棵橡树上。 一条长长的绳梯从上面垂下来。

The magic tree house was back. 神奇树屋回来了。

“Come on up!” Annie shouted. “快上来！”安妮喊。

Jack ran to the rope ladder. He started climbing. 杰克跑到绳梯跟前。 他开始往上爬。

He climbed and climbed and climbed. 他爬啊爬，爬啊爬。

As he climbed, he looked out over the woods. High above the treetops it was still light. 他一边爬，一边朝树林外面看。 树梢上面还亮着。

At last, Jack pulled himself into the tree house. 终于，杰克爬进了树屋。

Annie sat in the shadows. Books were scattered everywhere. 安妮坐在阴影里。 书扔得到处都是。

On the floor the letter M glowed in the dim light. The M stood for Morgan le Fay. 地板上，字母M在昏暗的光线里发着光。 这个M代表摩根·勒费伊。

But there was no sign of Morgan herself. 可摩根本人连影子都没有。

“I wonder where Morgan is,” said Jack. “不知道摩根去哪儿了。”杰克说。

“Maybe she went to the library to get some more books,” said Annie. “也许她去图书馆再借几本书了。”安妮说。

“We were just at the library. We would have seen her,” said Jack. “Besides, the library’s closed now.” “咱们刚从图书馆回来。 要是她在，咱们肯定能看见她。”杰克说。 “再说了，图书馆现在已经关门了。”

Squeak! 吱！

A little mouse ran out from behind a stack of books. It ran to the M shining in the floor. 一只小老鼠从一堆书后面跑了出来。 它跑到地板上发亮的M那儿。

“Yikes,” said Annie. “哎呀。”安妮说。

The mouse sat on the middle of the M. It looked up at Jack and Annie. 老鼠坐在M的正中间。 它抬头看着杰克和安妮。

“Oh, it’s so cute,” Annie said. “哇，好可爱啊。”安妮说。

Jack had to admit the mouse was cute. It had brown-and-white fur and big dark eyes. 杰克也不得不承认，这只老鼠挺可爱的。 它有棕白相间的毛，还有一双又大又黑的眼睛。

Annie slowly reached out her hand. The mouse didn’t move. Annie patted its tiny head. 安妮慢慢伸出手。 老鼠一动不动。 安妮轻轻拍了拍它小小的脑袋。

“Hi, Peanut,” she said. “Can I call you Peanut?” “嗨，花生。”她说。 “我能叫你花生吗？”

“Oh brother,” said Jack. “真受不了。”杰克说。

“Do you know where Morgan is?” Annie asked the mouse. “你知道摩根在哪儿吗？”安妮问老鼠。

Squeak.

“You’re nuts, Annie,” said Jack. “Just because the mouse is in the tree house doesn’t mean it’s magic. It’s a plain old mouse that crawled in, that’s all.” “你傻了吧，安妮。”杰克说。 “老鼠在树屋里，可不代表它有魔法。 它就是一只普普通通的老鼠爬进来了，仅此而已。”

Jack looked around again. He saw a piece of paper on the floor. 杰克又朝四周看了看。 他看见地板上有一张纸。

“What’s that?” he said. “那是什么？”他说。

“What’s what?” asked Annie. “什么什么？”安妮问。

Jack went over and picked up the paper. There was writing on it. 杰克走过去把纸捡起来。 上面写着字。

“Oh man,” whispered Jack, after he read the words. “天哪。”杰克看完那些字，小声说。

“What is it?” said Annie. “那是什么？”安妮说。

“A note,” said Jack. “It must be from Morgan. I think she’s in big trouble!” “一张纸条。”杰克说。 “一定是摩根留下的。 我觉得她遇上大麻烦了！”
