5. Flames in the Mist 5. 雾中的火光

The water grew shallow again. Then they were on dry land. The ninjas lowered Jack and Annie to the ground. 水又变浅了。 然后他们上了岸。 忍者把杰克和安妮放到地上。

“Thanks,” said Annie. “谢谢。”安妮说。

“Thanks,” said Jack. “谢谢。”杰克说。

Squeak, said the mouse. 吱，老鼠叫了一声。

The ninjas said nothing, but they looked around. 忍者什么也没说，可他们朝四周看了看。

Jack looked around, too. A full moon was rising in the sky. Dark rocks dotted the side of the mountain. 杰克也朝四周看了看。 一轮满月正在升起来。 山坡上散布着一块块黑石头。

Then the ninjas started moving. They went silently up the slope, between the rocks. 然后忍者开始往前走。 他们悄悄地从石头中间往坡上走。

Jack and Annie followed them. Jack wasn’t afraid of the ninjas now. In fact, he was starting to like them. Maybe they really could help find Morgan. 杰克和安妮跟着他们。 杰克现在不怕这些忍者了。 其实，他开始有点喜欢他们了。 也许他们真的能帮忙找到摩根。

The ninjas moved silently. But Jack and Annie made plenty of noise. 忍者走得很安静。 可杰克和安妮发出了好多声音。

They panted as they climbed the rocky hillside. Their wet sneakers made squishy sounds. 他们爬石坡的时候直喘气。 湿漉漉的运动鞋发出嘎吱嘎吱的声音。

Suddenly the ninjas froze. Jack could see their eyes darting around. Voices were coming from the valley below. Jack saw torches flaming in the mist. 突然，忍者停住不动了。 杰克看见他们的眼睛在四处扫。 下面山谷里传来说话声。 杰克看见雾里有火把在燃烧。

The ninjas started moving faster. Jack and Annie hurried after them. 忍者走得更快了。 杰克和安妮赶紧跟上。

“Who’s carrying the torches?” Annie asked. “谁拿着那些火把？”安妮问。

Jack was too out of breath to speak. He also didn’t have an answer. 杰克喘得说不出话来。 而且他也答不上来。

They came to a pine forest. Night birds called out. Wind rattled the branches. 他们来到一片松林。 夜鸟在叫。 风把树枝刮得沙沙响。

The ninjas moved like ghosts through the forest. They appeared and disappeared, through moonlight and shadows. 忍者像鬼影一样穿过树林。 他们在月光和阴影里出现，又消失。

Jack and Annie struggled to keep up. 杰克和安妮拼命跟着。

Finally the ninjas came to a stop. 终于，忍者停了下来。

One ninja held out his hand, as if to say, Wait. Then both ninjas stepped away into the shadows of the trees. And were gone. 一个忍者伸出手，好像在说：等一等。 然后两个忍者都走进树影里。不见了。

“Where did they go?” said Annie. “他们去哪儿了？”安妮说。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “Maybe the book can tell us.” “我不知道。”杰克说。 “也许书上能告诉咱们。”

He pulled the ninja book out of his pack. 他从背包里抽出那本忍者书。

He turned the pages until he came to a picture of a cave. 他一页页翻，直到翻到一张山洞的画。

By the light of the full moon, he read:

Sometimes ninjas held meetings in hidden mountain caves to plan secret missions. 有时，忍者会在隐蔽的山洞里开会，计划秘密任务。

“Oh man,” said Jack, “I bet they went inside a hidden cave.” “天哪。”杰克说，“我敢说他们进了一个隐藏的山洞。”

He pulled out his notebook and pencil. He wrote: 他拿出笔记本和铅笔。他写道：

图中文字：meetings in hidden caves 图中文字：在隐蔽的洞穴里开会

Jack turned the page. He stared at a picture of a ninja sitting on a mat. He read: 杰克翻过一页。 他盯着一张忍者坐在垫子上的画。他读道：

Ninjas took orders from a ninja master. The master was a mysterious wise person who knew many secrets of nature. 忍者听从忍者大师的命令。大师是个神秘的智者，知道很多自然的秘密。

“Wow,” whispered Jack. “哇。”杰克小声说。

Just then the two ninjas returned. Jack quickly put his books away. 就在这时，两个忍者回来了。 杰克赶紧把书收起来。

The short ninja motioned for Jack and Annie to follow. In the shadows was the entrance of a dark cave. 矮个忍者示意杰克和安妮跟上。 阴影里有一个黑洞洞的洞口。

“What’s in there?” Annie whispered. “里面有什么？”安妮小声问。

“The ninja master,” Jack whispered back. “忍者大师。”杰克小声回答。
