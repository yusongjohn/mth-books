3. E-hy! 3. 诶嗨！

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开眼睛。

Annie was already looking out the window. The mouse peeked out of her pouch. 安妮已经在往窗外看了。 老鼠从她口袋里探出头来。

Jack looked out the window, too. The air was fresh and cool. 杰克也往窗外看。 空气又清新又凉快。

The tree house was in a tree with white flowers. The tree was in a grove of trees on the side of a mountain. Nearby a wild stream rushed downhill. 树屋停在一棵开着白花的树上。 这棵树在山坡上的一片小树林里。 附近有一条湍急的溪流冲下山去。

Two ninjas were standing on rocks near the water. They were staring at the valley below. 两个忍者站在水边的石头上。 他们正盯着下面的山谷。

One ninja was tall. The other was short. They wore black pants and shirts. They had black scarves around their heads. And swords strapped to their backs. 一个忍者很高。 另一个很矮。 他们穿着黑裤子和黑上衣。 头上裹着黑布。 背上绑着剑。

It was exactly like the picture in the book. 跟书上的画一模一样。

Jack crouched below the window. 杰克蹲到窗台下面。

“Be careful,” he whispered. “Don’t let them see you.”

“Why not?” Annie whispered back. “为什么？”安妮小声问回去。

“They might think we’re some kind of enemy,” said Jack quietly. “他们说不定会把咱们当成敌人。”杰克轻轻说。

Annie crouched beside him. 安妮蹲到他旁边。

Jack pushed his glasses into place. Now he was going to look at the ninja book. 杰克把眼镜往上推了推。 这下他可要好好看看这本忍者书了。

He picked up the book. He turned to the beginning. He read: 他拿起书。 他翻到开头。他读道：

Very little is known about the shadowy warriors called ninjas. Historians believe that ninjas lived in Japan between the 14th and 17th centuries. Both men and women were ninjas. Sometimes they fought to protect their families. Sometimes warlords hired them to be spies. 人们对被称为忍者的一群神秘战士了解很少。历史学家认为，忍者生活在14世纪到17世纪之间的日本。男的和女的都有忍者。有时候他们为了保护自己的家人而战斗。有时候，军阀会雇佣他们当间谍。

“Wow,” whispered Jack. “We’re in Japan, hundreds of years ago.” “哇。”杰克小声说。 “咱们在日本，几百年前。”

Jack opened his backpack. He pulled out his notebook and pencil. He liked to take notes. He wrote: 杰克打开背包。 他拿出笔记本和铅笔。 他喜欢记笔记。他写道：

图中文字：ninsas were warriors in old Japan 图中文字：Ninsas是日本古代的战士

“Jack,” whispered Annie. “They’re looking up. I think they know we’re here.” “杰克。”安妮小声说。 “他们在往上看。 我觉得他们知道咱们在这儿了。”

Jack peeked over the windowsill. His eyes met the dark eyes of the tall ninja. 杰克从窗台探出头去。 他的眼睛对上了高个忍者那双黑眼睛。

“E-hy!” the ninja cried. He dashed toward the tree. The other ninja followed. “诶嗨！”那个忍者喊道。 他朝那棵树冲过来。 另一个忍者也跟了上来。

“Oh no!” said Annie. “糟了！”安妮说。

“We’ve got to go!” Jack said. “Where’s the Pennsylvania book?” “咱们得走了！”杰克说。 “宾夕法尼亚那本书呢？”

He and Annie looked around wildly. 他和安妮慌忙四处找。

But where was the book about Pennsylvania? It had the picture of the Frog Creek woods in it. Jack and Annie couldn’t get home without it. 可是那本讲宾夕法尼亚的书呢？ 上面有蛙溪镇树林的图画。 没有它，杰克和安妮就回不了家。

“It’s not anywhere!” cried Annie. “哪儿都找不到！”安妮叫道。

“We’ve got to do something. Fast!” said Jack. “Pull up the ladder!” “咱们得想办法。快！”杰克说。 “把梯子拉上来！”

He and Annie grabbed the top of the rope ladder. They pulled the ladder into the tree house. 他和安妮抓住绳梯的最上头。 他们把梯子拉进了树屋。

But the tall ninja leaped at the tree trunk. Then he started climbing up the tree! The short ninja followed. They climbed just like cats! 可高个忍者已经跳到了树干上。 然后他开始往树上爬！ 矮个忍者也跟了上来。 他们爬得跟猫一样！

Jack and Annie huddled in a corner. 杰克和安妮缩在一个角落里。

The ninjas climbed into the tree house. Neither one made a sound. 忍者爬进了树屋。 两个人都没发出一点声音。
