6. Shadow Warrior 6. 影子武士

Jack and Annie went into the cave. They followed the ninja through the darkness. 杰克和安妮走进山洞。 他们跟着忍者穿过黑暗。

The back of the cave was lit with dozens of candles. Shadows danced on the walls. 山洞深处点着好多蜡烛。 影子在墙上晃来晃去。

In the flickering light, Jack saw a dark figure sitting on a woven mat. 在闪烁的灯光里，杰克看见一个黑影坐在一张编织垫上。

The ninja master. 忍者大师。

The ninja bowed to the master. Then he stepped to one side. 那个忍者向大师鞠躬。 然后退到一边。

The master stared at Jack and Annie. 大师盯着杰克和安妮。

“Sit,” he said. “坐下。”他说。

Jack and Annie sat on the cold, hard floor. 杰克和安妮坐在又冷又硬的地上。

Squeak.

The mouse poked its head out of Annie’s pouch. 老鼠从安妮口袋里探出头来。

“It’s okay, Peanut,” said Annie. “没事的，花生。”安妮说。

The master stared at the mouse for a moment. Then he looked at Jack. “Who are you?” he asked. 大师盯着老鼠看了一会儿。 然后他看向杰克。 “你们是谁？”他问。

“I’m Jack and that’s my sister, Annie,” Jack answered. “我是杰克，那是我妹妹安妮。”杰克回答。

“Where do you come from?” the master asked. “你们从哪儿来？”大师问。

“Frog Creek, Pennsylvania,” Annie answered. “蛙溪镇，宾夕法尼亚。”安妮回答。

“Why are you here?” he asked. “你们来这儿做什么？”他问。

“We’re trying to help our friend Morgan le Fay,” said Jack. “She left us a message.” “我们想帮我们的朋友摩根·勒费伊。”杰克说。 “她给我们留了一张字条。”

Annie pointed to the short ninja. “We gave the message to him.” 安妮指着矮个忍者。 “我们把字条给了他。”

“You mean, you gave the message to her, ” said the ninja master. “And she has given it to me.” “你是说，你们把字条给了她。”忍者大师说。 “然后她交给了我。”

“She?” said Jack and Annie together. “她？”杰克和安妮一起说。

The woman ninja’s eyes sparkled. Jack thought she might be smiling. 女忍者的眼睛亮了亮。 杰克觉得她也许在笑。

The master held up Morgan’s note. 大师举起摩根的纸条。

“Perhaps I can help you,” he said. “But first you must prove yourselves worthy of my help.” “也许我能帮你们。”他说。 “可你们得先证明自己配得上我的帮助。”

Just then the tall ninja appeared. He made a sign to the master. 就在这时，高个忍者出现了。 他向大师打了个手势。

The master stood up. He handed Morgan’s note to Annie. 大师站了起来。 他把摩根的纸条交给安妮。

“We must go now,” he said. “The samurai are close.” “我们现在得走了。”他说。 “武士已经近了。”

“Samurai?” said Jack. He knew that the samurai were fierce Japanese fighters. “武士？”杰克说。 他知道武士是很厉害的日本战士。

“Were they the ones in the valley?” Jack asked. “The ones with the torches?” “山谷里的就是他们吗？”杰克问。 “拿火把的那些？”

“Yes, our family is at war with them,” said the master. “We must leave before they find us.” “对，我们家族正在跟他们打仗。”大师说。 “我们必须在他们找到我们之前离开。”

“But what about helping Morgan?” said Annie. “可是帮摩根的事怎么办？”安妮说。

The master strapped on his sword. 大师把剑绑在身上。

“I have no time now,” he said. “I must go.” “我现在没时间，”他说。 “我必须走了。”

“Can’t we go with you?” said Annie. “我们能不能跟你一起走？”安妮说。

“No, there is no place for you where we are going. You must find your way back to your house in the trees.” “不行，我们要去的地方，没有你们的位置。 你们得自己找到回树上房子的路。”

“Alone?” said Jack. “就我们自己？”杰克说。

“Yes. You must go alone. And beware of the samurai.” “对。 你们必须自己走。 还要小心武士。”

“Why?” said Jack. “为什么？”杰克说。

“They will think you are one of us,” said the master. “They will ask you no questions. They will show you no mercy.” “他们会以为你们是我们的人，”大师说。 “他们不会问问题。 也不会放过你们。”

“Yikes,” whispered Annie. “哎呀，”安妮小声说。

“But you have seen the way of the ninja. You can practice it yourselves now,” said the master. “不过你们已经见过忍者之道。 现在可以自己练习了，”大师说。

“H-how?” said Jack. “怎、怎么练？”杰克说。

“Remember three things,” said the master. “记住三件事，”大师说。

“What?” said Jack. “什么？”杰克说。

“Use nature. Be nature. Follow nature.” “利用自然。成为自然。 跟随自然。”

“I can do that!” Annie said. “我能做到！”安妮说。

Jack looked at her. “You can?” he said. 杰克看着她。 “你能？”他说。

The master turned to Jack. “Your tree house lies to the east. That is the way you must go,” he said. 大师转向杰克。 “你们的树屋在东边。 你们必须往那边走，”他说。

How? wondered Jack. How do we find the east? 怎么走？杰克心想。 我们怎么找到东边？

Before he could ask, the master bowed. Then he disappeared into the shadows. 他还没来得及问，大师就鞠躬了。 然后他消失在阴影里。

The two ninjas led Jack and Annie out of the cave, into the moonlight. 两个忍者带着杰克和安妮走出山洞，来到月光下。

The tall one pointed at the pine forest. Then they too disappeared into the darkness. 高个子指向松树林。 然后他们也消失在黑暗里。

Jack and Annie were all alone. 杰克和安妮只剩自己了。
