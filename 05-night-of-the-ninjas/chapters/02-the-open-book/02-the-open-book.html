2. The Open Book 2. 摊开的书

Jack showed Annie the piece of paper. It said: 杰克把那张纸给安妮看。上面写着：

图中文字：Help me - Under a your

Final 4 Shin 图中文字：帮帮我——在你的最终4 Shin下

“Oh no,” said Annie. “We have to help her. But what’s a thin?” “糟了。”安妮说。 “咱们得帮她。 可是，thin是什么意思？”

“Maybe she was trying to write things, ” said Jack. “See how the n sort of runs off the page?” “也许她想写的是things，东西。”杰克说。 “你看这个n好像写到纸外面去了？”

“Maybe the spell was starting to make her disappear or something,” said Annie. “也许咒语开始让她消失了，或者怎么的。”安妮说。

“Right,” said Jack. “I wonder if she left any other clues.” He glanced around the tree house. “对。”杰克说。 “不知道她有没有留下别的线索。”他朝树屋里扫了一眼。

“Look!” Annie pointed at a book in the corner. “That’s the only open book,” she said. “快看！”安妮指着角落里的一本书。 “那是唯一一本摊开的书。”她说。

Jack looked around again. Annie was right. He felt a shiver go down his spine. 杰克又看了看四周。安妮说得对。 他觉得脊背一阵发凉。

Jack went over to the book and picked it up. He held it near the window. Light from the setting sun was golden on the page. 杰克走过去把书拿起来。 他拿到窗边。 夕阳把书页照得金黄金黄的。

Jack stared at the picture on it. In the picture were trees with white flowers. The trees were on the side of a mountain. Near a wide, rushing stream. 杰克盯着上面的图画。 画里有开着白花的树。 那些树长在山坡上。 旁边是一条又宽又急的溪流。

Two people were also in the picture. They wore dark clothes. They had black scarves over their faces. And long swords strapped to their backs. 画里还有两个人。 他们穿着深色的衣服。 脸上蒙着黑布。 背上还绑着长剑。

“Oh man,” whispered Jack. “天哪。”杰克小声说。

“Who are they?” Annie asked. “他们是谁？”安妮问。

“Ninjas, I think,” said Jack. “我觉得是忍者。”杰克说。

“Ninjas? Really?” said Annie. 真的？”安妮说。

“Morgan must have left the book open to this page for a reason,” said Jack. “摩根把书摊开在这一页，一定有原因。”杰克说。

“Maybe that’s where she was when the spell got her,” Annie said. “也许咒语抓住她的时候，她就在那儿。”安妮说。

“Or maybe that’s where the four things are,” said Jack. “或者四样东西就在那儿。”杰克说。

“Let’s go!” said Annie. “咱们走吧！”安妮说。

“Now?” said Jack. “现在？”杰克说。

“Yes, Morgan’s in trouble! She needs us now!” said Annie. “对啊，摩根有麻烦了！ 她现在就需要咱们！”安妮说。

“But we should read this book first,” said Jack. “So we’ll be prepared.” “可咱们应该先看看这本书。”杰克说。 “这样咱们才有准备。”

“Forget it!” said Annie. “Every minute counts!” She grabbed the book from Jack. “别想了！”安妮说。 “每一分钟都很重要！”她从杰克手里把书抢了过去。

“Give it back,” he said. “We have to find out about this place.” “还给我。”他说。 “咱们得先搞清楚这是什么地方。”

Annie held the book out of reach. “We’ll find out when we get there,” she said. 安妮把书举得他够不着。 “到了那儿就知道了。”她说。

“We don’t even know where there is!” Jack said. “咱们连那儿是哪儿都不知道！”杰克说。

But Annie pointed at the picture. “I wish we could go here,” she said. 可安妮指着那张画。 “我希望我们能去这儿。”她说。

The leaves of the oak tree began to shake. 橡树的叶子开始晃起来。

Squeak! 吱！

“Don’t be scared, Peanut,” said Annie. She scooped up the mouse. Then she put it in the pouch of her sweatshirt. “别怕，花生。”安妮说。 她一把把老鼠捧起来。 然后塞进自己卫衣的口袋里。

The wind began to blow. 风开始刮起来。

It blew harder and harder. 风越刮越大。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始转起来。

Faster and faster! 越转越快！

Jack squeezed his eyes shut. 杰克紧紧闭上眼睛。

Then everything was still. Absolutely still. 然后一切都静了。完全静了。

Except for the sound of rushing water. 只剩下急流的水声。
