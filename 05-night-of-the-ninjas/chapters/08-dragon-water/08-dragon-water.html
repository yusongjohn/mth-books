8. Dragon Water 8. 龙水

Jack and Annie crouched together. Samurai were on both sides of them now. They were trapped! 杰克和安妮蹲在一起。 现在两边都有武士。 他们被困住了！

Jack pressed against the rock. 杰克紧紧贴着石头。

The warrior stepped closer and closer. He looked to the right. He looked to the left. 那个武士一步步走近。 他往右边看。 他往左边看。

Jack held his breath. 杰克屏住呼吸。

“Be nature, ” whispered Annie. “成为自然，”安妮小声说。

“What?” Jack whispered back. “什么？”杰克也小声问。

“Be nature. Be a rock.” “成为自然。变成石头。”

Oh brother, thought Jack. This was nuts. But he squeezed his eyes shut. Then he tried to be part of the rock. 天哪，杰克心想。这太离谱了。 可他还是把眼睛紧紧闭上。 然后他试着变成石头的一部分。

Jack tried to be as still as the rock. As solid as the rock. As quiet as the rock. 杰克试着像石头一样一动不动。 像石头一样结实。 像石头一样安静。

Soon he started feeling as strong as the rock. As safe as the rock. He wanted to be the rock forever. 很快他开始觉得自己像石头一样强壮。 像石头一样安全。 他想永远当这块石头。

Squeak.

“He’s gone,” said Annie. “They’re all gone.” “他走了，”安妮说。 “他们全走了。”

Jack opened his eyes. The samurai warrior was gone. Jack stood up and looked over the rock. The torches were gone, too. 杰克睁开眼睛。 那个武士不见了。 杰克站起来，从石头上面往外看。 火把也不见了。

“Let’s go,” Annie said. “走吧，”安妮说。

Jack took a deep breath. He felt great—he was getting more and more like a ninja every minute. Maybe even like a ninja master. 杰克深吸一口气。 他感觉棒极了——他越来越像忍者了。 说不定都像忍者大师了。

“East!” he said. “往东！”他说。

And they went east. Down the mountain, between the rocks. Until they came to the wide, icy stream. 于是他们往东走。 下山，从石头中间穿过。 一直走到那条又宽又冰的小溪。

The water seemed even wilder than before. 水看起来比刚才更猛了。

“I don’t see the tree house,” said Annie. “我没看见树屋，”安妮说。

Jack looked across the stream to the dark grove of trees. Moonlight shone on their pale flowers. But where was the tree house? 杰克隔着小溪，看着对面那片黑黑的树丛。 月光照在那些淡色的花上。 可树屋在哪儿？

“I don’t see it either,” said Jack. “We have to cross the water first. Then we’ll try and find it.” “我也没看见，”杰克说。 “我们得先过河。 然后再找树屋。”

The water was crashing and rushing over the rocks. 水哗啦啦地冲过石头。

Squeak. The mouse peeked out from its pouch. 小老鼠从口袋里探出头来。

“Don’t be afraid,” said Annie. She patted the mouse’s little head. “Be like us. Be like a ninja, too.” “别怕，”安妮说。 她拍了拍小老鼠的小脑袋。 “像我们一样。 也当个忍者。”

“Let’s go,” Jack said. “走吧，”杰克说。

He took a deep breath and stepped into the stream. The icy water swirled up to his knees. The current knocked him over. 他深吸一口气，踏进小溪。 冰凉的水打着旋，没到他的膝盖。 水流把他冲倒了。

Jack grabbed some weeds. He held on tight as water swirled around him. 杰克抓住一些水草。 他紧紧抓着，水在他身边打转。

He was freezing to death! 他快冻死了！

“Jack!” Annie grabbed Jack’s arms. She helped him back onto the bank. “杰克！”安妮抓住杰克的胳膊。 她把他拉回岸上。

“That was close!” said Annie. “好险！”安妮说。

Jack wiped his glasses. Luckily, they hadn’t fallen off in the water. 杰克擦了擦眼镜。 幸好眼镜没掉进水里。

“Are you okay?” said Annie. “你还好吗？”安妮说。

“N-not really,” said Jack, his teeth chattering. He was chilled to the bone. “不、不太好，”杰克说，牙齿直打颤。 他冻到骨头里了。

“We’ll never get across,” said Annie. “我们过不去了，”安妮说。

“We’ll drown if we try.” “再试就会淹死。”

“Or fr-freeze to death,” said Jack. “或者冻、冻死，”杰克说。

He pulled off the hood of his sweatshirt. He didn’t feel much like a ninja anymore. 他拉下卫衣的帽子。 他觉得自己一点也不像忍者了。

Annie pulled off her hood, too. She sighed. “What can we do?” she said. 安妮也拉下帽子。她叹了口气。 “我们怎么办？”她说。

Squeak.

Peanut climbed out of Annie’s sweatshirt pouch and leaped onto the ground. 花生从安妮卫衣口袋里爬出来，跳到地上。

The mouse scampered away. 小老鼠跑开了。

“Peanut, come back!” Annie called. “花生，回来！”安妮喊。

“No,” said Jack. “We have to follow Peanut.” “别，”杰克说。 “我们得跟着花生。”

“Why?” asked Annie. “为什么？”安妮问。

“We have to do what the master said!” said Jack. “Follow nature!” “我们得按大师说的做！”杰克说。 “跟随自然！”

“Oh. Right!” said Annie. “Follow Peanut! But where is Peanut?” “哦。 对！”安妮说。 “跟着花生！ 可花生在哪儿？”

In the moonlight Jack saw the little mouse. It was running through the grass along the stream. 月光下，杰克看见了那只小老鼠。 它正顺着小溪，在草里跑。

“There!” he cried. “Come on!” “在那儿！”他喊。 “快来！”

Annie hurried after Jack. Jack hurried after Peanut. They ran beside the rushing waters. 安妮赶紧跟上杰克。 杰克赶紧跟上花生。 他们沿着湍急的水流跑。

A moonlit branch had fallen across a narrow part of the stream. It touched both shores. 月光下，一根树枝横在小溪最窄的地方。 它连着两边的岸。

The mouse was running over the branch. 小老鼠正从树枝上跑过去。

“Peanut’s going over a bridge!” said Annie. She started to follow. “花生在过桥！”安妮说。 她开始跟着走。

“Wait!” cried Jack. “We can’t go on that branch. It’s too small! It’ll break!” “等一下！”杰克喊。 “我们不能走那根树枝。太细了！ 会断的！”
