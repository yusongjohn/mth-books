4. Captured 4. 被抓住了

The ninjas pulled iron bands off their hands. The bands had spikes like claws on them. 忍者把手上的铁箍摘下来。 铁箍上有尖刺，像爪子一样。

“That’s how they climbed the tree,” Annie whispered to Jack. “他们就是靠这个爬树的。”安妮小声对杰克说。

The ninjas stared at Jack and Annie with dark, piercing eyes. The rest of their faces were covered by their scarves. 忍者用又黑又锐利的眼睛盯着杰克和安妮。 脸的其他部分都被布蒙住了。

Jack felt frozen under their stares. 被他们这么盯着，杰克觉得自己冻住了。

Annie wasn’t frozen, though. She stepped right up to them. 可安妮没有冻住。 她直接走到他们跟前。

“Hi,” she said. “嗨。”她说。

The ninjas didn’t say “hi” back. They didn’t move at all. They were as still as Jack. 忍者没有回她“嗨”。 他们一动也不动。 他们跟杰克一样，完全不动。

“We’re trying to help our friend, Morgan,” said Annie. “我们想帮我们的朋友摩根。”安妮说。

She held up Morgan’s note. 她举起摩根的纸条。

The tall ninja took the note from her. He looked at it. Then he gave it to the short ninja. 高个忍者从她手里接过纸条。他看了看。 然后递给了矮个忍者。

The two ninjas stared at each other. Then they looked back at Jack and Annie. 两个忍者互相看了看。 然后又看向杰克和安妮。

Finally the short ninja nodded once. He put the note into the pocket of his shirt. 最后，矮个忍者点了一下头。 他把纸条放进衬衫口袋里。

“You can help us?” Annie asked. “你们能帮我们吗？”安妮问。

Neither ninja spoke. Jack wished he could see their faces. He couldn’t tell what they were thinking. 两个忍者都不说话。 杰克真希望能看见他们的脸。 他看不出他们在想什么。

The short ninja tossed the rope ladder back out of the tree house. The tall one pointed down the ladder. Then he pointed at Jack and Annie. 矮个忍者把绳梯又扔出了树屋。 高个忍者朝下面的梯子一指。 然后又指了指杰克和安妮。

Uh- oh, thought Jack. Were they being captured? 糟了，杰克想。 他们这是被抓住了吗？

“Us? Go with you?” said Annie. “我们？ 跟你们走？”安妮说。

The ninja nodded. 那个忍者点了点头。

“Oh boy!” said Annie. “太好了！”安妮说。

Oh boy? Is she nuts? wondered Jack. 太好了？她傻了吧？ 杰克心想。

The short ninja darted down the ladder. He went hand over hand. His feet didn’t touch the rungs of the ladder. 矮个忍者飞快地顺着梯子下去。 他两只手交替往下抓。 脚根本没碰到梯子的横档。

The tall one did the same. 高个忍者也一样。

Jack gasped. The ninjas moved very fast. They were like spiders dropping from webs. 杰克倒抽了一口气。 忍者动作非常快。 就像蜘蛛从网上掉下来一样。

“Wow!” said Annie. “哇！”安妮说。

“Now’s our chance to leave,” said Jack. “Quick!” He looked around the tree house again. Where was that Pennsylvania book? “现在是咱们逃走的机会。”杰克说。 “快！”他又在树屋里四处看了看。 那本宾夕法尼亚的书到底在哪儿？

“Let’s go with them, Jack,” said Annie. “咱们跟他们走吧，杰克。”安妮说。

“No! This isn’t a game!” Jack said. “不行！ 这可不是玩游戏！”杰克说。

“But I think they know something about Morgan!” said Annie. “可我觉得他们知道摩根的事！”安妮说。

She started down the ladder. 她开始顺着梯子往下爬。

“Come back!” said Jack.

But it was too late. 可是已经太晚了。

Jack sighed. “Why does this always happen?” he asked himself. 杰克叹了口气。 “怎么总是这样？”他问自己。

“Come on, Jack!” came Annie’s voice from below. “快点，杰克！”下面传来安妮的声音。

Jack put his notebook and the ninja book into his pack. He pushed his glasses into place. And he started down the ladder. 杰克把笔记本和忍者书放进背包。 他把眼镜往上推了推。 然后开始往下爬。

Jack joined Annie and the ninjas on the ground. 杰克在地上跟安妮和忍者会合了。

The sun had fallen behind the hills. The sky was streaked with red and gold. 太阳已经落到山后面去了。 天上划着一道道红色和金色。

The mouse peeked out from Annie’s sweatshirt pouch. 老鼠从安妮卫衣口袋里探出头来。

“Don’t be scared, Peanut,” Annie whispered. “We’ll take care of you.” “别怕，花生。”安妮小声说。 “我们会照顾你的。”

Great, thought Jack. But who is going to take care of us? 好极了，杰克想。 可谁来照顾我们呢？

The short ninja held Jack’s arm in one hand and Annie’s arm in the other. He led them through the twilight. The tall ninja walked behind them. 矮个忍者一只手抓住杰克的胳膊，另一只手抓住安妮的胳膊。 他带着他们走在暮色里。 高个忍者走在他们后面。

“Where are we going?” Jack asked. “我们去哪儿？”杰克问。

The ninjas stopped near the rushing water of the wide stream. The water roared as it raced downhill. 忍者在那条又宽又急的溪流边停了下来。 水轰隆隆地冲下山去。

The short ninja looked at Jack and Annie. He let go of their arms. Then he pushed them toward the stream. 矮个忍者看着杰克和安妮。 他松开了他们的胳膊。 然后把他们往溪流那边推。

“You want us to cross it?” shouted Annie. “你要我们过河？”安妮喊道。

The ninja nodded. Then he and the short ninja stepped into the wild stream. They started wading across. 那个忍者点了点头。 然后他和矮个忍者踏进了湍急的溪水。 他们开始蹚水过去。

“Let’s run back to the tree house!” said Jack. “咱们跑回树屋去！”杰克说。

“No, we have to follow them!” said Annie. “For Morgan’s sake!” “不行，咱们得跟着他们！”安妮说。 “为了摩根！”

Jack took a deep breath. She was right. 杰克深吸了一口气。她说得对。

Annie grabbed Jack’s hand. Together they stepped into the water. 安妮抓住杰克的手。 他们一起踏进水里。

“YIKES!” They both screamed and jumped out. “哎呀！”他们俩一起尖叫着跳了出来。

It was the coldest water Jack had ever felt! It was colder than ice. It was so cold it felt like fire. 这是杰克碰过的最冷的水！ 比冰还冷。 冷得像火烧一样。

“I can’t go back in,” said Annie, shivering. “我没法再下去了。”安妮哆嗦着说。

“Me neither,” said Jack. “I’ll have a heart attack.” “我也是。”杰克说。 “我会犯心脏病的。”

The ninjas looked at Jack and Annie. Then they turned around and came back. 忍者看着杰克和安妮。 然后他们转过身，又走了回来。

The tall ninja grabbed Jack. 高个忍者一把抓住杰克。

“Help!” Jack cried. “救命！”杰克喊。

But the ninja lifted Jack high into the air. And put him on his shoulder. 可忍者把杰克高高举了起来。 放到自己肩膀上。

The short ninja put Annie on his shoulder. 矮个忍者把安妮放到自己肩膀上。

Then the two ninjas stepped into the stream again. The icy wild waters swirled around them. It went up to the short ninja’s waist. 然后两个忍者又踏进了溪流。 冰冷湍急的水在他们身边打着转。 水没到了矮个忍者的腰。

But the ninjas moved through the stream as calmly as two sailing ships. 可忍者在溪水里走得稳稳的，像两艘帆船。
