7. To the East 7. 向东走

Jack and Annie stood still for a long moment. 杰克和安妮站着，好一会儿都没动。

Annie spoke first. “Well, I guess the tall ninja was pointing to the east,” she said. “I guess that’s the way we go.” 安妮先开口了。 “嗯，我猜高个子忍者指的是东边，”她说。 “那我们就往那边走吧。”

“Wait,” said Jack. “I need to write some stuff down.” “等一下，”杰克说。 “我得先记点东西。”

He took out his notebook. In the moonlight, he wrote: 他拿出笔记本。 借着月光，他写道：

图中文字：1. use nature 2. be nature 3. follow nature 图中文字：1. 利用自然 2. 成为自然的一部分 3. 遵循自然

Look, Jack,” whispered Annie. “Do I look like a ninja?” “看，杰克，”安妮小声说。 “我看起来像忍者吗？”

He looked at her. She had pulled her sweatshirt hood over her head and tied the strings tightly. 他看着她。 她把卫衣的帽子拉到头上，把绳子系得很紧。

She did look like a ninja—a very small one. 她还真像忍者——一个很小的忍者。

“Good idea,” Jack whispered. He pulled his hood up, too. “好主意，”杰克小声说。 他也把帽子拉了起来。

“Okay, let’s go,” said Annie. “好了，走吧，”安妮说。

Jack put his notebook away. Then he and Annie headed east into the woods. 杰克把笔记本收好。 然后他和安妮向东走进树林。

They slipped between trees. And more trees. And more trees. 他们从树和树中间溜过去。然后又是树。 还是树。

All the trees looked the same. Jack got confused. Were they still going in the right direction? 所有的树看起来都一样。 杰克糊涂了。 他们还在往正确的方向走吗？

“Wait,” he said. “等一下，”他说。

Annie stopped. They both stared at the woods around them. 安妮停下来。 他们俩盯着四周的树林。

“Do you think we’re still going east?” asked Jack. “你觉得我们还在往东走吗？”杰克问。

“I guess so,” said Annie. “我猜是吧，”安妮说。

“We can’t just guess,” said Jack. “We have to know for sure.” “不能光靠猜，”杰克说。 “我们得确定才行。”

“How do we do that?” said Annie. “We don’t have a compass.” “那怎么确定？”安妮说。 “我们没有指南针。”

Just then the master’s words came back to Jack. 就在这时，大师的话回到杰克脑子里。

“The ninja master said to use nature, ” he said. “忍者大师说要利用自然，”他说。

“How do we do that?” said Annie. “那怎么利用？”安妮说。

“Wait, I remember something—” Jack closed his eyes. “等一下，我想起来了——”杰克闭上眼睛。

He remembered something in a camping book. Now what was it? 他想起野营书里的内容。是什么来着？

He opened his eyes. “I’ve got it! First we need a stick,” he said. 他睁开眼睛。 “我想起来了！ 首先我们需要一根树枝，”他说。

Annie picked up a stick. “Here—” she said. 安妮捡起一根树枝。 “给——”她说。

“Great, now we just need a space with moonlight,” said Jack. “太好了，现在我们只要一块有月光的空地，”杰克说。

“There—” said Annie. “那儿——”安妮说。

They moved into a moonlit space between the shadows. 他们走进阴影之间一块有月光的空地。

“Now push the stick into the ground,” Jack said. “现在把树枝插进地里，”杰克说。

Annie pushed the stick into the ground. 安妮把树枝插进地里。

“The stick’s shadow looks like it’s more than six inches,” said Jack. “What do you think?” “树枝的影子好像超过六英寸了，”杰克说。 “你觉得呢？”

“It looks like it,” said Annie. “看起来是，”安妮说。

“Okay. Then that means the shadow’s pointing east,” said Jack. “好。 那就说明影子指向东边，”杰克说。

“Neat,” said Annie. “真棒，”安妮说。

“So that way is east!” Jack pointed to a new direction. “At least I hope it is.” “所以那边是东边！”杰克指向一个新方向。 “至少我希望是。”

“We’re real ninjas now!” said Annie. “我们现在是真正的忍者了！”安妮说。

“Yep,” said Jack. “Maybe we are. Come on!” “嗯，”杰克说。 “也许是吧。走！”

They took off—heading east, they hoped. 他们出发了——希望是往东走。

Soon they were out of the pine woods and walking down the rocky mountainside. They moved slowly from rock to rock. Finally they rested against a giant rock. 很快他们走出松树林，顺着乱石山坡往下走。 他们慢慢地从一块石头挪到另一块石头。 最后他们靠着一块大石头休息。

“Let’s check our direction again,” said Jack. “我们再检查一下方向，”杰克说。

Annie stuck another stick into the dirt. 安妮又把一根树枝插进土里。

“There,” he said. He pointed to the shadow on the ground. “That way—” “那边，”他说。 他指着地上的影子。 “往那边——”

Annie peeked over the rock, down the mountain. 安妮从石头后面往山下偷看。

“Yikes,” she said softly. “哎呀，”她轻声说。

Jack looked, too. His heart nearly stopped. 杰克也看了。 他的心差点停了。

There were flames of fire coming up the mountain. The samurai! 山上有火光往上爬。是武士！

Jack and Annie ducked behind the rock. 杰克和安妮躲到石头后面。

Squeak, said the mouse. 吱吱，小老鼠叫。

“Quiet, Peanut,” said Annie. “安静，花生，”安妮说。

Jack reached into his pack. He pulled out the ninja book. 杰克把手伸进背包。 他拿出那本忍者书。

“I hope something in here can help us,” he said. “希望这里面有能帮我们的东西，”他说。

Jack flipped through page after page until he found what he was looking for. It was a picture of warriors wearing bamboo armor. They were holding swords. He read: 杰克一页一页地翻，直到找到他要找的内容。 那是一幅画，上面的武士穿着竹甲。 他们拿着刀。他读道：

The samurai were fierce Japanese fighters. They carried two swords to cut down their enemies. 武士是勇猛的日本战士。他们带着两把剑，用来打败敌人。

Annie tapped Jack on the shoulder. 安妮拍了拍杰克的肩膀。

Jack looked at her. 杰克看着她。

She pointed up the mountain. 她指向山上。

A figure was coming down toward them. He was very near. 一个人影正朝他们走下来。已经很近了。

In the moonlight, his bamboo armor was shining. His two swords were gleaming. 月光下，他的竹甲在发亮。 他的两把刀闪着光。

It was a samurai warrior! 那是一个武士！
