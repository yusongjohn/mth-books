1. I Know Her! 1. I Know Her!

Jack sat in a sunny spot on the front porch, studying a book of magic tricks. He was planning to put on a magic show for his parents and grandparents. He took a sip of lemonade, then started making a list in his notebook: 杰克坐在房子前廊一个阳光明媚的地方，正在看一本魔法书。 他打算给爸爸妈妈和爷爷奶奶表演一场魔术秀。 他喝了一口柠檬水，然后开始在笔记本上列清单：

flying paper clips 会飞的回形针

“Hey.” Annie tapped on the screen door. “Let’s do something.” “嘿。” 安妮敲了敲屏幕门。 “我们去做点什么吧。”

“I’m already doing something,” said Jack. He took another sip of lemonade and added more tricks to his list: “我正在做事呢，”杰克说。 他喝了一口柠檬水，又在清单上加了更多的把戏：

magical clinging pen 神奇的粘笔

great pepper trick 厉害的胡椒把戏

Annie gasped. “Did you hear that?” she asked. She opened the door and came out onto the porch. 安妮惊呼起来。 “你听到声音了吗？”她问。 她打开门，走了出去，来到门廊上。

“Hear what?” said Jack. He added another trick: “听到什么？”杰克说。 他又加了一个把戏：

steal-the-strength trick 偷力量的把戏

“That whooshing sound,” said Annie. “就是那种呼呼的声音，”安妮说。

“Whooshing sound?” Jack read his list and decided he still needed two or three more tricks. “呼啸声？”杰克念着他的清单，决定他还需要再加两三个把戏。

“Like the tree house just whooshed into the woods!” said Annie. “就像树屋刚刚嗖的一声飞进树林里了！”安妮说。

“Yeah, yeah,” said Jack. He flipped through the pages of his book. “是啊，是啊，”杰克说。 他翻着书的页面。

“Come on, go with me,” Annie pleaded. “Let’s check the woods again. Please.” “求你了，跟我一起去吧，”安妮恳求道。 “我们再去树林里看看吧。求你了。”

“We’ve already checked five times since Tuesday,” said Jack. “自从星期二以来，我们已经检查了五次了，”杰克说。

“Once more won’t kill you,” said Annie. “I have this feeling … I’m serious.” “再检查一次也不会要你的命，”安妮说。 “我有种感觉……我是认真的。”

Jack sighed. “Okay. You win,” he said. “One more time.” He put his notebook and pencil into his backpack. Leaving his book of magic tricks on the porch, he stood up and followed Annie down the steps and across their yard. 杰克叹了口气。 “好吧。” “你赢了，”他说。 “再试一次。”他把笔记本和铅笔放进背包里。 他把那本魔术书留在门廊上，站起来，跟着安妮走下台阶，穿过他们的院子。

“Aren’t you dying to find another secret of greatness for Merlin?” Annie asked as they headed up the sidewalk. “And what about the magic mist? Don’t you want to have another great talent for an hour?” “你不是一直很想为梅林找到另一个伟大的秘密吗？”安妮边走边问。 “那魔法雾气又怎么办呢？ 你不想再拥有一个很棒的才能一小时吗？

“Of course,” said Jack. “But I’m also tired of looking for the tree house and not finding it! For two weeks you’ve had these hunches.” “当然，”杰克说。 “但是我也厌烦了找树屋却找不到！ 这两周你一直都有这种直觉。

Jack and Annie crossed the street and headed into the Frog Creek woods. Winding through the shadows of trees, Jack took a deep breath, inhaling the scent of warm earth and summer leaves. Hidden birds sang from the tree branches. As Jack and Annie drew closer to the tallest oak, Jack’s heart started to pound. This time, something was there, high in the branches of the tree. 杰克和安妮穿过街道，走进了蛙溪镇的树林里。 他们绕着树影穿行，杰克深吸一口气，闻到了温暖泥土和夏天树叶的香味。 隐藏在树上的鸟儿唱起了歌。 当杰克和安妮走近最高的橡树时，杰克的心开始怦怦跳。 这一次，树的高处有什么东西，藏在树枝之间。

“Whoosh,” Annie said softly. “呼——”，安妮轻声说道。

Jack grinned as he looked up at the small wooden house nestled high in the branches. “Okay,” he said. “I’m glad we checked.” 杰克抬头看着高高挂在树枝上的小木屋，露出了笑容。 “好吧，”他说。 “我很高兴我们来检查一下。”

Annie ran to the rope ladder and started up. Jack followed. Inside the magic tree house, the shadows of branches danced on the wooden walls. A piece of paper, a gold ring, a tiny bottle, a small book, and a scroll were waiting on the floor. 安妮跑到绳梯旁，开始往上爬。杰克跟在后面。 在神奇的树屋里，树枝的影子在木墙上跳来跳去。 一张纸、一个金戒指、一个小瓶子、一本小书和一卷羊皮纸躺在地板上。

“A new message from Merlin!” said Annie. She picked up the scroll, unrolled it, and read aloud: “这是梅林发来的新消息！”安妮说。 她拿起羊皮纸，展开它，然后大声读起来：

图中文字：Dear Jack and Annie,

I hope you enjoyed your time with the Houdinis. I ask you now to find the third secret of greatness from a woman named Florence Nightingale.

—M. 图中文字：亲爱的杰克和安妮，希望你们和胡迪尼一家在一起玩得开心。现在我请你们从一位名叫弗洛伦斯·南丁格尔的女士那里，找到伟大第三个秘密。——M。

“Florence Nightingale?” said Annie. “I know her! I gave a report on her!” “弗洛伦斯·南丁格尔？”安妮说。 “我知道她！ 我做过关于她的报告！”

“I just know her name. Who is she?” said Jack. “我只是知道她的名字。 她是谁？”杰克说。

“She’s amazing! She’s one of my heroes!” said Annie. “她非常了不起！ “她是我的英雄之一！”安妮说。

“Yeah, okay, but what did she do? ” asked Jack. “嗯，好吧，那她做了什么？”杰克问。

“Florence Nightingale lived in the 1800s, in England,” said Annie. “The English army was fighting in this place called the Crimea, on the Black Sea, and Florence Nightingale was a nurse there. The soldiers called her the Lady with the Lamp, because after the hospital was quiet and dark at night, she went alone from bed to bed with a lantern. She gave light and comfort and took care of wounds. She was so brave and amazing, she became famous everywhere! Later she changed the world of nursing by organizing—” “弗洛伦斯·南丁格尔生活在19世纪的英国，”安妮说。 “当时英国军队正在黑海附近的一个地方叫克里米亚作战，而弗洛伦斯·南丁格尔在那里当护士。 士兵们称她为提灯女士，因为每当医院晚上安静又黑暗时，她就独自提着灯笼，从一张床走到另一张床。 她带来了光明和安慰，还照顾伤员。 她如此勇敢又了不起，很快就出名了！ 后来，她通过组织——

“Okay, you don’t have to give your whole report,” Jack interrupted. “I get the picture. She sounds cool. Let’s go meet her.” “好了，你不用把整个报告都讲完，”杰克打断了她。 “我明白了。她听起来很酷。” 让我们去见见她。”

“I feel like I already know her!” said Annie with a laugh. “Oh, wow! I can’t wait!” “我觉得我好像已经认识她了！”安妮笑着说。 “哦，哇！我等不及了！”

“So let’s see where we’re going,” said Jack. He picked up the small book from the floor. Its faded leather cover had an old-fashioned look. “那我们来看看我们要去哪儿，”杰克说。 他从地上捡起了那本小书。 它褪色的皮革封面看起来非常复古。

“I’ll bet it’s about England,” said Annie, “or the Crimea.” “我打赌这本书是关于英国的，”安妮说，“或者克里米亚。”

“Neither,” said Jack. He showed the cover to Annie: “都不是，”杰克说。 他把封面展示给安妮看：

图中文字：TRAVELERS' HANDBOOK 图中文字：旅行者手册

“Egypt?” said Annie. “I never read that Florence Nightingale was a nurse in Egypt.” “埃及？”安妮说。 “我从来没读过弗洛伦斯·南丁格尔曾在埃及当护士。”

“We’ve been to Egypt before,” said Jack. “Remember the mummy in the pyramid?” “我们以前去过埃及，”杰克说。 “你还记得金字塔里的木乃伊吗？”

“The ghost-queen,” said Annie. “She was on her way to the Next Life.” “幽灵女王，”安妮说。 “她正前往来世。”

Jack shivered. “That was weird,” he said. 杰克打了个寒战。 “那太奇怪了，”他说。

“Don’t worry. The ghost-queen was thousands of years in the past,” said Annie. “Now we’re just going to 1850.” “别担心。 幽灵女王的故事发生在几千年前，”安妮说。 “我们现在只是要去1850年。”

“Ohhh … right,” said Jack drily. “I guess all the ancient ghosts were gone by then.” “哦……对，”杰克干巴巴地说。 “我想那时候所有古老的幽灵都消失了。”

“Whatever. Ready?” said Annie. “随你便吧。” 准备好了吗？”安妮问。

“Hold on,” said Jack. He picked up the gold ring and gave it to Annie. “It’s your turn to wear this,” he said. “等一下，”杰克说。 他捡起那枚金戒指，把它给了安妮。 “该你戴这个了，”他说。

Annie slipped the ring onto her finger. They both stared at it for a moment. The ring was magic. When Florence Nightingale shared a secret of greatness with them, it would glow like a burning ember. 安妮把戒指戴在了手指上。 他们俩盯着它看了片刻。 这枚戒指是魔法的。 当弗洛伦斯·南丁格尔告诉他们一个关于伟大的秘密时，它会像燃烧的余烬一样发光。

“The Ring of Truth,” said Annie. “真相之戒，”安妮说。

“Yep. Just remember to keep checking it when we’re talking to Florence,” said Jack. “对的。 “记住，当我们和弗洛伦斯说话时，要一直检查它，”杰克说。

“Don’t worry, I will,” said Annie. “Here, you carry this.” She picked up the tiny glass bottle and handed it to Jack. “别担心，我会的，”安妮说。 “来，你拿着这个。”她捡起那个小小的玻璃瓶，递给杰克。

Jack held the bottle up to the dappled sunlight and looked at the silver vapor swirling inside. “Mist gathered at first light on the first day of the new moon on the Isle of Avalon,” he said. 杰克把瓶子举到斑驳的阳光下，看着里面银色的蒸汽在旋转。 “在新月第一天的黎明时分，阿瓦隆岛上的雾气第一次聚集，”他说。

“Yep. Good for one hour of great talent,” said Annie. “对的。 “可以持续一小时的伟大才能，”安妮说。

Jack smiled, remembering their hour as horse trainers and their hour as stage magicians. “I wonder what we’ll be great at this time,” he said. 杰克笑了，想起了他们当马术教练的那一个小时，还有他们当舞台魔术师的那一个小时。 “我不知道这次我们会擅长什么，”他说。

“Maybe great nurses?” said Annie. “也许会是伟大的护士？”安妮说。

“We’ll see,” said Jack. He put the tiny bottle in his backpack; then he picked up the piece of paper from the floor. On the paper he had written the two secrets of greatness they’d already learned: “我们走着瞧，”杰克说。 他把那个小瓶子放进背包里，然后从地上捡起那张纸。 纸上写着他们已经学到的两个成功的秘密：

HUMILITY 谦虚

HARD WORK 努力工作

“Ready to find the third secret from Florence Nightingale?” he asked. “准备好从弗洛伦斯·南丁格尔那里找到第三个秘密了吗？”他说。

“A thousand times yes!” said Annie. “一千次的愿意！”安妮说。

Jack pointed to the picture on the cover of the Travelers’ Handbook to Egypt. “I wish we could go there,” he said. 杰克指着《埃及旅行手册》封面的那张图片。 “真希望我们能去那里，”他说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来了。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
