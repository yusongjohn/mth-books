Uhh-woh?

9. Heroes of the Dawn 9. 黎明的英雄

Jack felt Koku’s little hand on his ear. 杰克感觉到库库的小手放在了他的耳朵上。

He opened his eyes. The mosquito netting had been pulled back. The baby baboon was perched on the headboard of the bed. She grabbed Jack’s ear and tugged. He started to push her away, but to his surprise, his right arm was pinned across his chest. It was tucked tightly into a cotton sling. 他睁开了眼睛。 蚊帐被拉回去了。 小狒狒宝宝正坐在床头的床头板上。 她抓住了杰克的耳朵，使劲一拉。 他开始把她推开，但让他惊讶的是，他的右臂被牢牢地固定在胸前。 它被紧紧地裹在一条棉布带子里。

“No, no, Koku!” said Annie. She pushed the baby baboon away from Jack. Florence Nightingale was sitting on the edge of the bed, wrapping a long piece of cloth around Annie’s ankle. “不，不，库库！”安妮说道。 她把小狒狒宝宝从杰克身边推开。 弗洛伦斯·南丁格尔正坐在床边，用一条长长的布料裹住安妮的脚踝。

“Hello, Jack!” said Florence with a lovely smile. “I hope you don’t mind your sling. I maneuvered your arm into it while you were in a deep sleep.” “你好，杰克！”弗洛伦斯微笑着说道。 “希望你不会介意你的吊带。 “我趁你睡得很沉的时候，把你手臂放进去的。”

“Thanks,” he said. “谢谢，”他说。

“I’m afraid you dislocated your shoulder,” said Florence. “It will be less painful if you wear the sling. As soon as you get to a city, you must see a doctor.” “恐怕你脱臼了，”弗洛伦斯说。 “如果你戴上吊带，会没那么疼的。 你一到城里，就必须去看医生。”

“I will, thanks,” said Jack. “我会的，谢谢，”杰克说。

Florence finished pinning the cloth bandage snugly around Annie’s ankle. “There, m’lady! Your ankle will heal as well. But you must go very slowly for a while,” she said. She looked at Jack. “Your sister received a frightful sprain, but I’m happy to say she has broken no bones.” 弗洛伦斯把布绷带紧紧地缠在安妮的脚踝上。 “好了，女士！ 你的脚踝也会好起来的。 但你得有一段时间走路要非常慢，”她说。 她看着杰克。 “你姐姐受了很严重的扭伤，但让我高兴的是，她没有骨折。”

“Cool,” said Jack. “真酷，”杰克说。

Florence tucked Jack’s blanket tighter around him. “There, soldier. Warmer now?” 弗洛伦斯把杰克的毯子裹得更紧了。 “好了，士兵，现在暖和些了吗？”

Jack was confused at first; then he realized that Florence had thought he meant that he was cold when he said cool. He started to laugh, but it hurt his arm too much. 起初杰克感到很困惑；然后他意识到弗洛伦斯以为他刚才说“酷”是说他觉得冷。 他开始笑，但手臂疼得厉害。

“Miss Nightingale, you really are a great nurse,” said Annie. “What’s your secret?” “南丁格尔小姐，你真的是一位很棒的护士，”安妮说。 “你的秘诀是什么？”

“I have no secret,” said Florence. “I have only taken care of friends and relatives. As you heard from Lord Bickerson, my family does not allow me to work. But I would love to be a nurse. That is my greatest dream in life. It is why I found it so strange that you two thought I was a nurse. And now, through entirely unpredictable circumstances, I have been a nurse to you both! Is that not a remarkable coincidence?” Florence shook her head with wonder. “Well. Please excuse me, and I will pour your tea.” Florence stepped over to a small kerosene stove and poured water from a teakettle into two cups. “我没有秘诀，”弗洛伦斯说。 “我只照顾过朋友和亲戚。” 正如比克森伯爵大人所说，我的家人不允许我工作。 但我真的很想当一名护士。 这是我一生中最大的梦想。 这就是为什么我觉得你们俩以为我是护士这件事很奇怪。 而现在，通过完全无法预料的情况，我竟然成了你们两人的护士！ 这不是一个令人惊讶的巧合吗？”弗洛伦斯惊讶地摇了摇头。 “好吧。” 请原谅我，我要给你们倒茶了。”弗洛伦斯走到一个小型煤油炉旁，把水壶里的水倒入两个杯中。

“Good try,” Jack whispered to Annie. “She still doesn’t have a clue that she’s great. So how can she share her secret of greatness with us?” “干得不错，”杰克低声对安妮说。 “她还不知道她有多棒。” 那她怎么能把她的秘密告诉我们呢？

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “This is tricky.” “我不知道，”安妮说。 “这有点难。”

“Here you are,” said Florence, returning with two cups of tea. “你在这儿，”弗洛伦斯说，她拿着两杯茶回来了。

She handed Annie one cup, and then helped Jack take the other in his left hand. Jack carefully brought it to his lips. The hot tea and honey tasted wonderful. 她递给安妮一杯，然后帮杰克用左手拿着另一杯。 杰克小心地把茶杯送到嘴边。 热茶和蜂蜜的味道真好。

“So allow me to understand what you told me last night,” said Florence. “You both risked your lives to save Koku?” “让我先弄清楚你昨晚对我说的话，”弗洛伦斯说。 “你们俩冒着生命危险救了科库？”

Jack and Annie nodded. 杰克和安妮点了点头。

Florence smiled and turned to the baby baboon. “Little one, I believe your two friends are great heroes,” she said. 弗洛伦斯微笑着转向小狒狒。 “小家伙，我相信你的两个朋友是伟大的英雄，”她说。

“Not really,” said Annie. “We’re not like you. You are the great hero.” “其实不是，”安妮说。 “我们不像你。 “你是伟大的英雄。”

“What? How silly. Surely no one would call me a hero,” said Florence. “什么？多傻啊。” “肯定没人会叫我英雄，”弗洛伦斯说。

“But they will,” said Annie. “I know you’re not a real nurse yet. But someday you’re going to be the greatest nurse of all time.” “但他们将会这么叫你，”安妮说。 “我知道你还没成为一名真正的护士。” 但总有一天，你会成为最棒的护士。”

Florence looked at Jack. “Did you actually see your sister fall from the cliff?” she said under her breath. 弗洛伦斯看着杰克。 “你真的看到你姐姐从悬崖上掉下去了吗？”她小声说。

“No. Why?” Jack whispered. “没有。 为什么？”杰克小声问。

“I worry she might have hit her head,” said Florence. “She does not seem in her right mind.” “我担心她可能撞到了头，”弗洛伦斯说。 “她看起来不太正常。”

Jack laughed. “Don’t worry, she’s fine,” he said. “What she says about you is true. We know it for a fact.” 杰克笑了。 “别担心，她没事的，”他说。 “她说的关于你的话是真的。 我们有确凿的证据。”

“Oh, dear, perhaps you both fell on your heads,” said Florence. “I appreciate your gratitude, children, but an ankle bandage and a simple arm sling should not convince you I will be a great nurse.” “哦，亲爱的，也许你们俩都摔到头了，”弗洛伦斯说道。 “孩子们，我感谢你们的感激之情，但一条脚踝绷带和一个简单的手臂吊带不应该让你们相信我会成为一名伟大的护士。”

“Well, what do you think makes a great nurse?” asked Annie. “那么，你认为什么才能让一个人成为一名伟大的护士呢？”安妮问道。

“I—I have thought about that, actually,” said Florence. “I would have to say: discipline … quickness … and kindness.” “我……其实我思考过这个问题，”弗洛伦斯说道。 “我不得不说：纪律……敏捷……还有善良。”

Annie slipped her hand out from under the blanket. She and Jack glanced at the Ring of Truth. It wasn’t glowing. 安妮从毯子下面抽出了她的手。 她和杰克看了看真相之环。 它没有发光。

“Good try,” Jack whispered again. “不错的一次尝试，”杰克又小声说了一遍。

“It seems like you definitely have those qualities,” Annie said to Florence. “So why won’t your family let you be a nurse?” “看起来你确实有这些品质，”安妮对弗洛伦斯说。 “那为什么你家人不让你当护士呢？

“They mean no harm,” Florence said with a sigh. “They only want me to be a proper lady. They will allow me to care for sick relatives and even some of the villagers. But they would never permit me to work in a hospital.” “他们没有恶意，”弗洛伦斯叹了口气说。 “他们只想让我成为一个得体的女士。 他们允许我照顾生病的亲戚，甚至一些村民。 但他们永远不会允许我去医院工作。”

“That’s so unfair!” said Annie. “The world needs you!” “这太不公平了！”安妮说。 “世界需要你！”

“I don’t believe the world needs me,” Florence said, laughing. But then her expression grew more serious. “I do believe, though, that I need the world.” “我不认为世界需要我，”弗洛伦斯说着，笑了起来。 但随后，她的表情变得严肃起来。 “我确实觉得，我需要为世界做点事。”

“What do you mean?” asked Jack. “什么意思？”杰克问。

“I just feel that I must do something good for the world, for the poor and the sick.” Florence’s voice became strong and passionate. “I must lift the load of pain from the suffering. I must do more than walk around the garden, write meaningless letters, sit in drawing rooms, gossip about the neighbors, arrange flowers, and go to dances! I must! One’s work must be for the greater good of all, not just for oneself! I feel I must do something useful for the world, or I will go stark, raving mad.” Florence’s eyes shone. “我只是觉得，我必须为世界、为穷人和病人做些好事。”弗洛伦斯的声音变得坚定而充满激情。 “我必须减轻那些受苦者的痛苦。 我必须做点比在花园里散步、写毫无意义的信、坐在客厅里和邻居闲聊、插花、去参加舞会更有意义的事！我必须！ 一个人的工作必须是为了所有人的更大利益，而不是仅仅为了自己！ 我觉得我必须为世界做些有用的事，否则我会疯掉的。”弗洛伦斯的眼睛闪着光。

Annie and Jack glanced down at the ring. It wasn’t glowing brightly yet, but it was glimmering a little. 安妮和杰克低头看了看戒指。 它还没有亮得耀眼，但微微闪着光。

“Uh … could you explain all that again in just a few simple words?” he said. “呃……你能用几句话再简单解释一下刚才那些话吗？”他说。

“Let’s see …,” said Florence. “How to say it simply?” She stared out the window at the Nile. Then she spoke very clearly. “I truly believe, deep in my heart, that one’s life must have meaning and purpose.” “让我们看看……，”弗洛伦斯说。 “怎么简单地说呢？”她望着窗外的尼罗河。 然后她非常清楚地说。 “我真心相信，在我内心深处，一个人的生活必须有意义和目标。”

Jack and Annie looked at the Ring of Truth. It glimmered more and more … then glowed as brightly as a burning ember. 杰克和安妮看着真理之环。 它闪闪发光……然后像燃烧的炭一样明亮地发光。

Koku shrieked and pointed at the ring. Annie and Jack both laughed. Annie quickly hid her hand under the blanket. 科库尖叫着，指着那个戒指。 安妮和杰克都笑了。 安妮迅速把手藏在毯子下面。

Florence turned back to them. “Did that sound funny?” she said. She seemed embarrassed. 弗洛伦斯又转身面对他们。 “那听起来奇怪吗？”她说。 她看起来有点不好意思。

“No, no!” said Annie. “It sounded …” She paused. “It sounded true.” “不，不！”安妮说。 “听起来……”她停顿了一下。 “听起来是真的。”

“Meaning and purpose,” Jack repeated. “Do you think that’s a secret of greatness?” “意义和目的，”杰克重复道。 “你觉得这是伟大的秘密吗？”

“Yes, I do,” said Florence. “Do you agree?” “是的，我认为是的，”弗洛伦斯说。 “你同意吗？”

“Yes. Totally,” said Jack. “是的。” “完全正确，”杰克说。

“And we know you’ll find a way to do it,” said Annie. “Trust us.” “我们知道你一定能找到办法，”安妮说。 “相信我们。”

A rooster crowed, then another, and another. The first light of dawn crept through the window. 一只公鸡打鸣了，接着又一只，又一只。 黎明的第一缕光透过窗户照了进来。

“Listen to them all,” said Florence. “It’s a brand-new day.” “听它们全部的叫声，”弗洛伦斯说。 “这是全新的一天。”

“Yes!” agreed Annie. “对！”安妮同意道。

The door to the cabin swung open. Countess von Kensky stood at the threshold, wrapped in her red shawl, her dark hair falling in curls around her shoulders. “Koku! My baby!” she cried. 小屋的门猛地打开了。 肯斯基伯爵夫人站在门口，披着她的红色披肩，乌黑的头发卷曲地垂在肩膀两侧。 “库库！” 我的宝宝！”她哭喊道。

The baboon screeched, bounded off the bed, and leapt into the countess’s arms. The two of them waltzed around the room, the countess’s long curls swirling through the air. Jack, Annie, and Florence all laughed at the sight. 狒狒尖叫着，从床上跳起来，扑进了伯爵夫人的怀里。 两人在房间里跳着华尔兹，伯爵夫人的长发在空中飘舞。 杰克、安妮和弗洛伦斯看到这一幕都笑了起来。

Finally the countess put Koku on her shoulder and looked at Jack and Annie. “Oh, my!” she cried. “What happened to you?” 终于，伯爵夫人把科库放在肩膀上，看着杰克和安妮。 “哦，天哪！”，她喊道。 “你们怎么了？”，

“Koku ran away from jackals,” said Florence. “And they rescued her from the top of a cliff.” “科库从豺狼那里逃走了，”弗洛伦斯说。 “他们把她从悬崖上救了下来。”

“Oh, my dear children, thank you!” said the countess. “Koku, you little rascal, causing these nice children to suffer so!” “哦，我亲爱的孩子们，谢谢你们！”伯爵夫人说道。 “库库，你这个小调皮鬼，让这些可爱的孩子受苦了！”

“We’ll be fine,” said Annie. “Miss Nightingale has taken really good care of us.” “我们会没事的，”安妮说道。 “南丁格尔小姐真的好好照顾了我们。”

“Well, you are the best baby baboon caretakers in the world!” said the countess. “I would love to stay and admire you all day. But we must hurry away. Our boat is leaving for Cairo! Say good-bye, Koku. Say thank you to your wonderful friends!” “你们可是世界上最好的小狒狒照顾者！”伯爵夫人说道。 “我很想留下来，整天欣赏你们。 但我们必须赶快离开。 我们的船要开往开罗了！ 和库库说再见。 向你们这些 wonderful 的朋友说谢谢！”

Koku looked at Jack and Annie. Her dark eyes were soft and grateful. Uhh- woh, she said. 科库看着杰克和安妮。 她深色的眼睛温柔而感激。 呜哇，她说。

“Uhh-woh,” Annie answered. “呜哇，”安妮回答。

“Good-bye, Koku von Kensky,” said Jack, waving with his left hand. “再见了，科库·冯·肯斯基，”杰克用左手挥手说道。

“Farewell!” said the countess. Then she and the baby baboon headed out the door. “再见！”伯爵夫人说道。 然后她和小狒狒宝宝朝门口走去。
