4. Baboon Babysitters 4. 猩猩保育员

Everyone was silent for a moment after Florence left. The Bickersons were the first to speak. 弗洛伦斯离开后，大家沉默了一会儿。 比克森一家是第一个说话的。

“Well!” said Lady Bickerson. “好吧！”比克森夫人说。

“How utterly rude!” said Lord Bickerson. “她实在太无礼了！”比克森先生说。

“Excuse me for a moment while I check on Flo,” said Selina, a worried look on her face. “Perhaps the heat was too much for her today.” And with that, Selina left the sitting room, too. “请稍等一下，我要去看看弗洛，”塞莉娜说，脸上露出担忧的表情。 “也许今天对她来说，天气太热了。”说完，塞莉娜也离开了客厅。

“Well!” said Lady Bickerson again. “好吧！”比克森夫人又说道。

“I hope I did not offend Miss Nightingale by complaining about the rat,” said the countess. “I like her very much. Miss Nightingale, I mean, not the rat.” “我希望我抱怨那只老鼠时，没有冒犯到南丁格尔小姐，”伯爵夫人说道。 “我非常喜欢她。 我是说南丁格尔小姐，不是那只老鼠。”

“You do not know Florence as we do,” said Lord Bickerson. “You have no idea of the trouble she has caused her family.” “你们不了解弗洛伦斯，就像我们了解的一样，”比克森男爵说道。 “你们根本不知道她给家人带来了多少麻烦。”

“It’s shocking,” said Lady Bickerson. “She refuses to behave like a lady. She is full of her own thoughts and opinions. She tells her poor mother she has dreams to leave home so she can work!” “这太令人震惊了，”比克森夫人说道。 “她不愿意像一位女士那样行事。 她脑子里全是她自己的想法和意见。 她甚至告诉她的可怜母亲，她有梦想要离开家去工作！”

“What’s wrong with that?” asked Annie. “那有什么不对吗？”安妮问。

Uh- oh, thought Jack. Annie sounded angry. 糟了，杰克心想。 安妮听起来很生气。

“No proper lady works out in the world!” Lord Bickerson snapped. “没有一位体面的女士会去外面干活！”比克森勋爵厉声说道。

“Why not?” asked Annie. “I mean, if you wanted to follow your dreams and people wouldn’t let you, wouldn’t you feel bad, too? And what’s wrong with having thoughts and opinions? I have thoughts and opinions all the time!” “为什么不呢？”安妮问。 “我的意思是，如果你想要追随自己的梦想，而别人却不让你，你难道不会感到难过吗？ 而且，有什么不对的，拥有自己的想法和观点呢？ 我经常都有自己的想法和观点！”

“What a rude child you are!” said Lady Bickerson. “Is that how your parents taught you to speak to your superiors?” “你真是个粗鲁的孩子！”比克森夫人说道。 “这就是你父母教你如何对长辈说话的方式吗？”

Jack was starting to get angry, too. “We don’t have superiors at home,” he said. 杰克也开始生气了。 “我们家里没有上级，”他说。

Lord Bickerson glared at Jack and Annie. “It is high time you children left this boat,” he said. “Miss Nightingale does not even know who you are. None of us know who you are, or who your parents are!” 比克森勋爵瞪了杰克和安妮一眼。 “你们这些孩子该离开这艘船了，”他说。 “夜莺小姐甚至不知道你们是谁。 我们谁都不知道你们是谁，也不知道你们的父母是谁！“

“Excuse me, sir, but this is not your boat, is it?” Annie said. “I thought it belonged to Selina and Charles.” “对不起，先生，但这艘船不是您的吧？”安妮说。 “我以为这艘船是塞琳娜和查尔斯的。”

“Oooh!” the aristocrats both gasped. “哦！”两位贵族都惊呼道。

Lord Bickerson strode to the door. “Be gone!” he said. “You children are the very definition of the word impolite. Leave before I send for the local governor! He’d know what to do with you!” 比克森勋爵走到门边。 “走开！”他说。 “你们这些孩子就是不礼貌的代名词。 再不离开，我就叫当地总督来！ 他会让你们知道该怎么处置你们！”

“Seriously? What does that mean?” said Jack. “真的吗？ 这是什么意思？”杰克说。

“It means go,” said Annie, “which I’m happy to do.” “这是让你们走开，”安妮说，“我很乐意这么做。”

“Me too,” said Jack. “我也是，”杰克说。

Jack and Annie stood up. As they headed for the door, Annie looked back at the countess and her baby baboon. “Good-bye, Countess. Good-bye, Koku. It was great meeting you.” 杰克和安妮站了起来。 当他们朝门口走的时候，安妮回头看了一眼伯爵夫人和她的小狒狒宝宝。 “再见，伯爵夫人。再见，科库。” 很高兴认识你。

Jack nodded in agreement. 杰克点头表示同意。

“Must you leave?” said the countess. “你必须离开吗？”伯爵夫人说道。

“I fear we must,” said Annie, echoing Florence. She curtsied, and Jack bowed. Then they both headed out the door. “我担心我们必须离开，”安妮说道，模仿着弗洛伦斯的语气。 她行了个屈膝礼，杰克鞠了一躬。 然后他们一起走出了门。

Outside, the sun was brighter and hotter than ever. Flies buzzed everywhere. Jack put his hat back on. “Come on, let’s get away from these beasts,” he said. 外面，太阳比以往任何时候都更明亮、更热。 到处都是嗡嗡叫的苍蝇。 杰克把帽子又戴了回去。 “快点，我们离开这些野兽吧，”他说。

“The flies or the aristocrats?” asked Annie. “是苍蝇还是贵族？”安妮问。

“Both!” said Jack. “两者都有！”杰克说。

Jack and Annie headed over the gangplank to the riverbank, then walked toward some shade trees at the edge of the Nile. “Send for the local governor? Seriously?” said Jack. 杰克和安妮走过跳板来到河岸，然后朝尼罗河边缘的一些遮阳树走去。 “去请当地的总督？ “真的吗？”杰克说。

“Their behavior was unthinkable!” said Annie. “The very definition of impolite!” “他们的行为简直无法想象！”安妮说。 “这简直是不礼貌的定义！”

Jack laughed, but then he sighed. “We blew it,” he said. “We got sidetracked. How can we complete our mission now?” 杰克笑了，但随后他叹了口气。 “我们搞砸了，”他说。 “我们走偏了。” 我们现在怎么完成任务呢？

“Don’t worry, we’ll wait for the Bickersons to leave,” said Annie. “Then we’ll try to get back on board and visit with Florence.” “别担心，我们等比克森一家离开，”安妮说。 “然后我们再设法回到船上，去拜访弗洛伦斯。”

“Okay, sounds like a plan. Sort of,” said Jack. “好啊，听起来像个计划。 差不多吧，”杰克说。

“C’mon. Let’s wait on that big rock under the trees,” said Annie. “来吧。 我们去树下那块大石头那儿等着，”安妮说。

Jack and Annie headed toward the shady rock. Not far away, Mustafa was sitting next to his rowboat, reading a book. The guide looked up, gave them a nod, then went back to his reading. Across the river, Ali was resting with his donkeys. 杰克和安妮朝那块阴凉的石头走去。 不远处，穆斯塔法正坐在他的划艇旁边，读着一本书。 向导抬起头，朝他们点了点头，然后又继续看书去了。 在河对岸，阿里正和他的驴子一起休息。

“Could you believe the way those two talked about Florence?” said Annie. “She can’t be a nurse. She can’t work outside her house. She can’t even have her own opinions! How do you become great if people make you feel so bad about yourself? And did you notice how they tried to make us afraid of everything? That was crazy.” “你能相信那两个人谈论佛罗伦萨的方式吗？”安妮说。 “她不能当护士。 她不能出门工作。 她连自己的想法都没有呢！ 如果别人让你觉得自己这么糟糕，你怎么可能变得伟大呢？ 你有没有注意到他们试图让我们对一切事情都感到害怕？那太疯狂了。”

“Yeah … crazy,” said Jack. But the aristocrats’ warnings had gotten to him. Now he was on alert for all the beasts of the Nile. What do black mambas look like? he wondered. Do jackals ever come out during the day? When he heard the whine of a mosquito, he shook his head as if his hair were on fire. “是啊……疯狂，”杰克说。 但贵族们的警告已经影响到他了。 现在他时刻警惕着尼罗河的所有野兽。 黑曼巴蛇长什么样子呢？他想知道。 豺狼会在白天出来吗？ 当他听到蚊子的嗡嗡声时，他摇头，好像头发着了火一样。

“What’s wrong with you?” said Annie. “你怎么了？”安妮说。

Before Jack could answer, he heard a loud call. WAHOO! 杰克还没来得及回答，他听到了一声响亮的呼喊。WAHOO！

Jack and Annie jumped up and whirled around. 杰克和安妮跳起来，转过身去。

“Koku!” cried Annie. “库库！”安妮喊道。

The baby baboon was scrambling toward them on all fours. Countess von Kensky hurried after her. “Koku! Wait!” she called. 那只小狒狒正四肢着地朝他们爬来。 肯斯基伯爵夫人赶紧跟了上去。 “库库！” “等一下！”她喊道。

Koku leapt into Annie’s arms. “Well, hello!” cried Annie, hugging the baby baboon. 科库跳进安妮的怀里。 “你好呀！”安妮一边抱住小狒狒一边喊道。

“She wanted to find you!” the countess said, out of breath. “After you left, she screeched at the Bickersons. She even shook her little fists. So they ordered her off the boat, too! Now the poor couple is sitting there all alone!” “她想找到你！”伯爵夫人气喘吁吁地说。 “你离开后，她对着比克森一家大喊大叫。” 她甚至还挥舞着小拳头。” 所以他们也把她赶下了船！ 现在这对可怜的夫妻只能孤零零地坐在那儿！”

“Oh, thank you, Koku, for sticking up for us!” said Annie. “哦，谢谢你，科库，你为我们说话了！”安妮说道。

“I can see you like Koku and that she likes you,” said the countess. “Therefore I have a favor to ask you. Could you care for her this afternoon? I must take a ferry downriver to see a sick friend, but I cannot take Koku. My friend is afraid of creatures from the wild.” “我看得出来你很喜欢库库，而且她也喜欢你，”伯爵夫人说。 “因此我想请你帮个忙。 今天下午你能照顾她吗？ 我必须坐船顺流而下去看一个生病的朋友，但不能带库库去。 我的朋友害怕野外的动物。”

“Of course we’ll take care of her!” said Annie. “当然我们会照顾她的！”安妮说。

“Uh, Annie? You know we have a mission, right?” Jack said under his breath. Their job was to find a secret of greatness—not to babysit a baboon. “呃，安妮？ 你知道我们有一个任务，对吧？”杰克小声说。 他们的任务是寻找一个伟大的秘密——而不是照顾一只狒狒。

“Don’t worry, Koku won’t get in the way,” Annie said to Jack. She looked at the countess. “We’d love to!” “别担心，库库不会碍手碍脚的，”安妮对杰克说。 她看着伯爵夫人。 “我们非常乐意！”

“Thank you! Thank you very much! I trust you to be wonderful caretakers!” said the countess. She reached into her purse, brought out a chunk of brown bread, and gave it to Jack. “Please give her bits of this when she is hungry or sad. I won’t be back until nightfall. Where can we rendezvous then?” “谢谢你！ 非常感谢！ “我相信你们会是出色的监护人！”伯爵夫人说。 她把手伸进包里，拿出一块棕色的面包，给了杰克。 “当她饿了或者伤心的时候，请给她一点这块面包。 我直到天黑才会回来。 那我们在哪里碰头呢？

“Rendezvous?” said Annie. “碰头？”安妮说。

“Yes, meet up with each other,” said the countess. “是的，去和彼此会合，”伯爵夫人说道。

“Oh—how about Selina’s boat?” said Jack. “哦——用塞琳娜的船怎么样？”杰克说道。

“Good, good! You two are angels who dropped from heaven! Thank you so very much!” said the countess. “Be good, Koku!” And she hurried down the riverbank to catch her ferry. “好，好！ 你们两个是从天上掉下来的天使！ “非常非常感谢你们！”伯爵夫人说道。 “要乖，库库！”她赶紧沿着河岸跑过去，去赶她的渡船。

Uhh-woh? said Koku, looking after the countess. “唔——哇？”，库库看着伯爵夫人说道。

“Don’t be sad, baby,” said Annie, sitting back on the rock. “Your mom won’t be gone long.” “别难过，宝贝，”安妮坐回石头上说道。 “你妈妈不会离开很久。”

Uhh- woh, the baboon said. She settled into Annie’s lap, and Annie kissed her head. “唔——哇，”狒狒说道。 她坐在安妮的腿上，安妮亲了亲她的头。

“Would Koku like a snack?” Jack asked. He broke off a piece of bread and gave it to her. He put the rest in his pouch. “库库想吃点心吗？”杰克问。 他掰下一小块面包，递给了她。 他把剩下的面包放进自己的口袋里。

Koku held the bread with her two tiny hands. She chewed thoughtfully, looking from Jack to Annie with her bright eyes. She uttered funny little noises between bites. Suddenly she screeched and pointed upriver. 库库用她那双小小的手拿着面包。 她一边认真地嚼着，一边用明亮的眼睛看着杰克和安妮。 她咬着面包时发出一些有趣的小声音。 突然，她尖叫起来，指着上游。

Jack and Annie turned to look. Florence had left Charles and Selina’s boat! Carrying a small bag, she was walking quickly down the riverbank toward Mustafa. 杰克和安妮转过身去看。 弗洛伦斯离开了查尔斯和塞莉娜的船！ 她拿着一个小包，快步沿着河岸朝穆斯塔法走去。

“Now’s our chance!” Jack said. “现在是我们机会了！”，杰克说。

“Let’s go!” said Annie. Holding Koku, she hurried with Jack to Florence and the guide. “我们走吧！”，安妮说。 她抱着科库，和杰克一起快步走向弗洛伦斯和导游。

“I’d like to explore the Valley of the Queens,” Florence was saying to Mustafa as Jack and Annie approached. “我想去探索王后谷，”弗洛伦斯正对穆斯塔法说着，杰克和安妮走近了。

“Hi there!” said Annie. “你好啊！”，安妮说。

Florence turned around. She looked startled. “Oh. Hello,” she said. 弗洛伦斯转过身来。 她看起来很惊讶。 “哦，你好，”她说。

Koku let out another screech. 科库又发出一声尖叫声。

“We’re babysitting Koku,” Annie explained. “我们在照看科库,” 安妮解释道。

Florence nodded. Jack could tell she didn’t really want to talk to them. 弗洛伦斯点了点头。 杰克看得出来，她其实并不想和他们说话。

“Are you going to take more notes this afternoon?” Annie asked. “今天下午你还要做更多笔记吗?” 安妮问道。

“Yes … I am,” Florence answered. “是的……我要,” 弗洛伦斯回答。

“You’re just like Jack,” said Annie. “He loves to write in his notebook, too.” “你就像杰克，”安妮说。 “他也喜欢在笔记本上写字，”安妮说。

“Oh. How nice,” said Florence. Jack was embarrassed. Florence’s mind was clearly on other things. She nodded politely, and then turned back to the guide. “Shall we be off, Mustafa?” 真好，”弗洛伦斯说。 杰克感到很尴尬。 弗洛伦斯显然在想别的事情。 她礼貌地点了点头，然后转身回到导游身边。 “我们该出发了，穆斯塔法？”

Mustafa helped Florence into the rowboat and took his position. As the guide picked up his oars, Florence glanced back at Jack, Annie, and Koku. They were all just standing on the bank, staring at her. 穆斯塔法帮弗洛伦斯上了划艇，然后坐到了自己的位置上。 当导游拿起船桨时，弗洛伦斯回头看了看杰克、安妮和科库。 他们全都站在岸边，盯着她看。

Florence sighed. “Do you need a ride to the western shore?” she said. 弗洛伦斯叹了口气。 “你需要我送你去西岸吗？”她说。

“Yes, please,” said Annie. “是的，请送我们过去，”安妮说。

“Then come,” Florence said. “You are welcome to join us.” “那快上来吧，”弗洛伦斯说。 “欢迎你们和我们一起去。”

“Thanks!” said Annie. “谢谢！”安妮说。

Jack didn’t say anything. He didn’t feel welcome at all. 杰克什么也没说。 他一点都不觉得受欢迎。

Jack and Annie climbed in with Koku and sat behind Florence. Mustafa used his oars to push the boat off the riverbank. As he began rowing across the Nile, the white-bearded guide sang the same peaceful song he’d sung before. 杰克和安妮和科库一起爬进船里，坐在弗洛伦斯后面。 穆斯塔法用船桨把船从河岸推开。 当他开始划过尼罗河时，那位白胡子导游唱起了和之前一样的平静歌曲。

Annie gave Jack a thumbs-up sign. 安妮给杰克比了个大拇指。

Not really, Jack thought. Florence was sitting with her back to them now, writing in her notebook. How could they ever get her to pay attention to them? 其实不是，杰克心想。 弗洛伦斯现在背对着他们坐着，正在笔记本上写字。 他们怎么能让她注意到他们呢？

“Look up Valley of the Queens,” Annie whispered to him. Jack took the handbook out of the pouch on his belt. Then he and Annie silently read: “看看女王谷，”安妮小声对他说。 杰克从腰带上袋子中拿出手册。 然后他和安妮默默地读起来：

About 3,000 years ago, the wives of the pharaohs were buried in the Valley of the Queens, near the Valley of the Kings. The tombs were cut out of limestone cliffs. 大约3000年前，法老们的妻子被埋葬在女王谷，靠近国王谷。 这些墓穴是从石灰岩悬崖中凿出来的。

“Excuse me, Miss Nightingale?” Annie said. “Have you ever been to the Valley of the Queens?” “请问，夜莺小姐？”安妮说。 “你去过女王谷吗？”

Without looking back, Florence shook her head. 弗洛伦斯没有回头，摇了摇头。

“We find it fascinating,” said Annie. She nudged Jack, letting him know it was his turn to speak. “我们觉得它非常有趣，”安妮说。 她推了推杰克，告诉他轮到他说话了。

“Did you know that the wives of the pharaohs were buried there about three thousand years ago?” Jack said. “I believe their tombs were cut out of the limestone cliffs.” “你知道吗，法老王后的墓穴大约在三千年前就被埋在那里了？”杰克说。 “我认为她们的墓穴是从石灰岩悬崖中凿出来的。”

Florence barely nodded. She didn’t say a word or look back at them. Before Jack or Annie could think of another way to get her attention, the rowboat arrived on the western shore. 弗洛伦斯只是微微地点了点头。 她一句话也没说，也没有回头看向他们。 在杰克或安妮想出另一个办法引起她注意之前，划艇已经到达了西岸。
