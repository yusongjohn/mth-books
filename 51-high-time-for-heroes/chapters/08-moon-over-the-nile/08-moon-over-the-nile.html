8. Moon over the Nile 8. 尼罗河上的月亮

Jack took halting steps, supporting much of Annie’s weight as she hopped on her left foot. His shoulder hurt terribly, but he tried to hide his pain from Annie. The Valley of the Queens felt spookier than ever. The jackals must be close by, Jack thought. And what about those tomb raiders that Ali had mentioned? 杰克小心翼翼地走着，大部分的重量都由他支撑着，安妮用左脚蹦跳着。 他的肩膀疼得厉害，但他努力不让安妮看出他的痛苦。 女王谷比以往任何时候都更让人感到害怕。 杰克心想，那些豺狼一定就在附近。 至于阿里提到的那些盗墓贼呢？

“Do we know the way back?” Annie asked. “我们知道自己怎么回去吗？”安妮问。

“From the top of the cliff, we could see the moon rising over the Nile,” said Jack. “So, if we walk toward the moon now, we should come to the river.” “如果我们站在悬崖顶部，就能看到月亮从尼罗河上升起，”杰克说。 “所以，如果我们现在朝着月亮的方向走，应该就能走到河边。”

“Good thinking,” said Annie. “好主意，”安妮说。

Heading toward the moon and holding on to Annie, Jack kept walking slowly. “Oww!” Annie said softly with each hop she took. 朝着月亮前进，紧紧抓着安妮，杰克慢慢地继续走着。 “啊！”安妮每跳一下，就轻声说一次。

Annie’s hopping jostled Jack’s body, sending pain through his shoulder and arm. But he gritted his teeth and tried not to make a sound. He was afraid Annie wouldn’t let him help her if she found out how much he was hurting. 安妮的跳跃让杰克的身体晃动了一下，疼痛感传到了他的肩膀和手臂上。 但他咬紧牙关，努力不发出声音。 他担心如果安妮发现他有多疼，就不会让他帮忙了。

After only about fifty yards, Jack knew he couldn’t support Annie’s weight much longer. “Okay—let’s stop for a minute and rest—” he said. 走了大约五十码后，杰克知道自己不能再支撑安妮的体重了。 “好吧——我们停下来休息一会儿——”他说。

Koku screeched. She let go of Annie’s hand and bounded away. “Koku!” cried Annie. “Come back!” 库库尖叫起来。 她松开了安妮的手，飞快地跑开了。 “库库！”安妮喊道。 “回来！”

Oh, no, not again! thought Jack. Did she hear jackals? 哦，不，又来了！杰克心想。 她听到的是豺狼的声音吗？

“Look, there’s a light!” said Annie. She pointed at a flickering light moving toward them. “看，有光！”安妮说道。 她指着朝他们移动的闪烁灯光。

“We have to be quiet,” Jack whispered. “It could be tomb raiders.” “我们必须安静，”杰克低声说。 “可能是盗墓贼。”

“Tomb raiders?” said Annie, peering into the dark. “I don’t think so.” She called out, “We’re here! We’re over here! Help us!” “盗墓贼？”安妮朝黑暗中张望，说道。 “我不这么认为。”她喊道，“我们在那儿！我们在这儿！” 帮帮我们！

“Children?” a woman called. “孩子们？”一个女人喊道。

“We’re here!” Annie cried again. “我们到了！”安妮又喊道。

Koku screeched and came running back toward Annie and Jack. She chattered and jumped up and down. 科库尖声叫着，朝安妮和杰克跑回来。 她不停地说话，跳上跳下。

Out of the dark, Florence Nightingale appeared on her donkey. She was wearing a shawl and holding a lantern. Mustafa followed behind her on his packhorse. 从黑暗中，弗洛伦斯·南丁格尔骑着她的驴子出现了。 她披着一条披肩，手里拿着一盏灯。 穆斯塔法骑着他的驮马跟在她后面。

“It’s us, Jack and Annie!” cried Annie. “是我们，杰克和安妮！”，安妮喊道。

“Thank goodness!” said Florence. “We’ve been looking for you! When we arrived at the river, Ali reported that your donkeys had returned without you!” “太好了！”，弗洛伦斯说道。 “我们一直在找你！ 当我们到达河边时，阿里报告说你的驴子已经带着你回来了！

“We were trying to save Koku from jackals,” said Annie. “She escaped from them and then got stuck on top of a cliff. We got up there okay, but we fell coming down!” “我们正在努力救科库免受豺狼的伤害，”安妮说。 “她从豺狼那里逃了出来，然后被困在了一座悬崖的顶部。 我们爬上去的时候没问题，但下山的时候我们摔了下来！”

“You poor things!” cried Florence, climbing off her donkey. “Please bring water for them, Mustafa!” “你们太可怜了！”弗洛伦斯说着，从她的驴背上跳了下来。 “请给它们带点水来，穆斯塔法！”

Mustafa dismounted and untied two canteens from his saddle. Florence carried her lamp over to Jack and Annie. “Are you hurt, my dears?” she asked in a soft, comforting voice. 穆斯塔法下了马，从鞍子上解下两个水壶。 弗洛伦斯拿着她的灯走到杰克和安妮身边。 “你们受伤了吗，我亲爱的？”她用温柔安慰的声音问道。

“Yes, I hurt my ankle,” said Annie. “But I think Jack’s in worse shape, even though he says it’s no big deal.” “是的，我扭伤了脚踝，”安妮说。 “但是我觉得杰克的情况更糟，尽管他说这没什么大不了的。”

“It’s just my shoulder and my arm,” Jack said to Florence. “我只是扭伤了肩膀和手臂，”杰克对弗洛伦斯说。

“Oh, my goodness, you are lucky—both of you—to be alive!” said Florence. She gently felt Jack’s shoulder and arm. He winced with pain and groaned. “Can you move it, dear?” she asked him. “哦，天哪，你们俩能活下来真是太幸运了！”弗洛伦斯说。 她轻轻地摸了摸杰克的肩膀和手臂。 他疼得皱起眉头，发出痛苦的呻吟。 “亲爱的，你能动一下吗？”她问他。

“No,” he said. “不能，”他说。

Mustafa handed Jack a canteen and gave one to Annie, too. While Jack and Annie gulped down water, Florence spoke softly to Mustafa. “Let’s take them straight to the boat,” she said. “Jack can ride my donkey, and Annie and the little baboon can ride on your horse.” Then she turned back to Jack and Annie. “Mustafa and I will walk alongside you.” 穆斯塔法递给杰克一个水壶，也给了安妮一个。 当杰克和安妮大口喝水时，弗洛伦斯轻声对穆斯塔法说话。 “我们直接带他们去船那里，”她说。 “杰克可以骑我的驴，安妮和小狒狒可以骑你的马。”然后她转身面对杰克和安妮。 “穆斯塔法和我将和你们一起走。”

“Thank you,” said Jack. He was relieved to have someone else in charge. “谢谢，”杰克说。 有人来负责让他感到松了一口气。

Mustafa brought the packhorse over to them and carefully lifted Annie and Koku onto the saddle. Then he helped Jack climb onto the back of the donkey. 穆斯塔法牵着驮马走到他们面前，小心地把安妮和科库扶上马鞍。 然后他帮助杰克爬上驴的背。

“Good. Now let us go,” said Florence. She blew out the flame in her lamp. Mustafa tied it to the saddle of the packhorse, along with the canteens. “很好。 现在我们出发吧，”弗洛伦斯说。 她吹灭了她灯里的火焰。 穆斯塔法把水壶绑在驮马的鞍子上。

By the light of the moon, Mustafa led the animals through the chilly night. Florence walked beside the donkey, staying close to Jack. 在月光下，穆斯塔法带领动物们穿过寒冷的夜晚。 弗洛伦斯走在驴子旁边，一直靠近杰克。

They all traveled silently over the barren, stony ground until they came to the statues of the ancient pharaoh. The giants looked like faceless gray ghosts in the moonlight. 他们默默地走过荒凉的石头地，直到来到古代法老的雕像前。 巨人们在月光下看起来就像没有脸的灰色幽灵。

As the group headed down the dirt road toward the Nile, Jack did his best to sit up straight on the donkey’s back. But every step the animal took brought Jack more pain. Once or twice he couldn’t help crying out. 当这群人沿着泥路朝尼罗河走去时，杰克努力地坐在驴背上，坐得笔直。 但驴子每走一步，杰克就感到更多的疼痛。 有时候他忍不住大声哭了出来。

“Jack,” Annie called to him in a soft voice. “Remember what it felt like to be rock climbers?” “杰克，”安妮用温柔的声音对他说。 “还记得当攀岩者时的感觉吗？”

Though the words might have sounded strange to Mustafa and Florence, Jack knew exactly what Annie meant. “Yes,” he breathed. He worked hard to remember what it had felt like to be a great rock climber, to feel calm and centered. He tried to block out the pain in his arm and shoulder by closing his eyes and concentrating on the sounds of the night. 虽然这些话对穆斯塔法和弗洛伦斯来说听起来很奇怪，但杰克知道安妮是什么意思。 “是的，”他轻声说。 他努力回忆自己曾经是个出色的攀岩者时的感觉，那时他感到平静而专注。 他闭上眼睛，集中注意力听夜晚的声音，试图忽略手臂和肩膀的疼痛。

Jack heard Koku making her baby baboon noises. He heard the cornstalks whispering in the wind. He heard the baaing of sheep and bleating of goats. 杰克听到库库在发出她小狒狒的叫声。 他听到玉米秆在风中沙沙作响。 他听到绵羊的咩咩声和山羊的叫声。

As they came closer to the Nile, he heard the rustling of reeds and croaking of frogs. He heard the HEE- HAW of donkeys. 当他们越来越接近尼罗河时，他听到芦苇丛的沙沙声和青蛙的呱呱声。 他听到驴子的‘嘿—哈’声。

Jack’s eyes shot open. “Honey?” he said. 杰克的眼睛猛地睁开了。 “亲爱的？”他说道。

“Bunny?” Annie called out. “Is that you?” “小兔子？”安妮喊道。 “是你吗？”

“Hello! I have your two donkeys here!” Ali called, rushing toward the travelers. “They are safe! I am so glad that you are safe, too!” “你好！ 我这儿有你们的两头驴子！”阿里喊着，朝旅行者跑过去。 “他们安全了！” 我也非常高兴你平安无事！

“Can you please help us, Ali?” said Florence. “We must get Jack and Annie back to the Bracebridges’ boat.” “你能帮帮我们吗，阿里？”弗洛伦斯说。 “我们必须把杰克和安妮送回布拉塞布里奇的船上。”

Ali quickly took the reins of the packhorse and donkey. Mustafa and Florence helped Jack, Annie, and Koku dismount. With Florence’s help, Jack climbed into the rowboat. Mustafa picked up Annie and Koku and set them down next to Jack. 阿里迅速抓住了驮马和驴子的缰绳。 穆斯塔法和弗洛伦斯帮助杰克、安妮和科库下船。 在弗洛伦斯的帮助下，杰克爬进了划艇。 穆斯塔法抱起安妮和科库，把他们放在杰克旁边。

“Good night, Ali,” said Florence as she and Mustafa finally climbed into the rowboat, too. “Your grandfather will be back soon to take you home.” “晚安，阿里，”弗洛伦斯说，她和穆斯塔法终于也爬进了划艇。 “你的爷爷很快就会回来接你回家。”

“Good night, Miss Nightingale,” said the small boy. “晚安，南丁格尔小姐，”小男孩说。

Mustafa’s oars dipped into the water, and the boat started gliding across the river. As he rowed, the guide sang his rowing song in time to the splashing of the oars. The Nile waters sparkled, reflecting the moonlight. Jack loved the peaceful, mysterious feel of the ancient river. 穆斯塔法的船桨划入水中，船开始在河面上滑行。 他划船时，导游随着船桨拍打水面的声音唱起了划船歌。 尼罗河的水闪闪发光，映照着月光。 杰克喜欢这条古老河流宁静而神秘的感觉。

When they reached the opposite shore, Mustafa tied up the rowboat at the water’s edge. “Please help the children out of the boat,” said Florence. “Then you must hurry back to Ali. It is surely past his bedtime.” 他们到达对岸时，穆斯塔法把划艇绑在了水边。 “请帮孩子们从船上下来，”弗洛伦斯说。 “那你必须赶快回去找阿里。 肯定已经过了他的睡觉时间了。”

Mustafa nodded. 穆斯塔法点了点头。

Florence turned to Jack and Annie. “I will be back in a moment with Charles and Selina,” she said. She climbed out of the rowboat and hurried away. 弗洛伦斯转向杰克和安妮。 “我马上会和查尔斯、塞莉娜一起回来，”她说。 她爬出划艇，赶紧离开了。

Mustafa helped Jack, Annie, and Koku onto the grass. “Thank you,” said Annie. 穆斯塔法帮杰克、安妮和科库上了岸。 “谢谢，”安妮说。

The old Egyptian nodded again. Then he got into his boat and began rowing across the glimmering Nile, heading back to his grandson. 那位年迈的埃及人又点了点头。 然后他上了船，开始划着船穿过闪闪发光的尼罗河，回到他孙子身边。

“Are you okay?” Annie asked Jack. “你还好吗？”安妮问杰克。

“Fine,” he breathed. He was in too much pain to say anything else. “没事，”他喘着气说。 他疼得厉害，说不出其他话。

Jack heard voices coming toward them. Florence was hurrying down the riverbank with Selina and Charles. 杰克听到有声音朝他们这边来了。 弗洛伦斯正和塞莉娜、查尔斯一起匆匆沿着河岸跑来。

“Oh, my poor dears!” cried Selina. “And Koku! There you are!” “哦，我可怜的孩子们！”塞莉娜喊道。 “还有库库！你在这儿呢！”

“Did the countess come looking for her?” said Annie. “伯爵夫人是来找她的吗？”安妮说。

“No, she sent word asking us to keep Koku for the night,” said Selina. “不，她派人来告诉我们，今晚要留库库过夜，”塞琳娜说。

“Oh, good. And—and can we stay, too?” Annie asked shyly. “We’ll meet up with our parents tomorrow morning.” “哦，太好了。 那——那我们也可以留下吗？”安妮害羞地问。 “明天早上我们会和父母碰面。”

“Of course! Here, let me help you,” said Charles, leaning down. “Put your arm around my shoulder.” “当然可以！ 来，让我帮你，”查尔斯说着，弯下腰。 “把你的手臂搭在我肩膀上。”

Annie did as Charles said. He lifted her and carried her back toward the moored sailboat. 安妮照着查尔斯说的做了。 他把她抱起来，朝停泊的小船走去。

“We’ll follow them, Jack,” said Florence kindly. Florence and Selina walked on either side of Jack up the riverbank, and Koku clamored behind, shrieking and making noises. Jack imagined she was talking about their scary adventure in the dark hills. “我们会跟上他们，杰克，”弗洛伦斯亲切地说。 弗洛伦斯和塞尔ina走在杰克的两边，沿着河岸往上走，库库在后面蹦蹦跳跳，又喊又叫。 杰克想象着她是在说他们那次在黑暗山丘里的可怕冒险。

Finally the group arrived at the large sailboat. “Home, sweet home!” said Selina. 最后，这支队伍来到了那艘大帆船前。 “家，甜蜜的家！”塞尔ina说道。

“Into my cabin,” said Florence. “进我的船舱来，”弗洛伦斯说道。

Charles and Selina helped Jack and Annie into the middle door of the long cabin. Koku followed and leapt onto a tall bed. 查尔斯和塞尔ina帮助杰克和安妮走进长船舱的中间那扇门。 库库跟着跳了进去，然后跳到了一张高高的床上。

Florence helped Jack take off his heavy long-sleeved shirt, his belt, and his hat. “Now, make room for the children, Koku,” Florence said to the baby baboon. 弗洛伦斯帮杰克脱下他那件又重又长的袖子衬衫、腰带和帽子。 “现在，库库，给孩子们腾出地方来，”弗洛伦斯对那只小狒狒宝宝说道。

Jack and Annie climbed onto the large bed, and Florence gently covered them with blankets. “It gets quite cold at night,” she explained softly. She draped mosquito netting around the bedposts as Selina lit kerosene lamps. Jack could smell the kerosene as the flames flickered and shadows danced on the green paneled walls. 杰克和安妮爬上了那张大床，弗洛伦斯轻轻地用毯子盖住了他们。 “晚上这里会变得很冷，”她轻声解释道。 她把蚊帐披在床柱上，而塞尔ina点起了煤油灯。 杰克闻到了煤油的味道，火苗闪烁，影子在绿色的墙板上跳起了舞。

The boat rocked on the Nile, and a soft breeze came through an open porthole. Through the gauze of the delicate mosquito netting, Florence, Charles, and Selina looked like dream figures—or angels—to Jack. He closed his eyes, and finally, he felt safe. 小船在尼罗河上摇晃，一缕微风从敞开的舷窗吹进来。 透过轻柔的蚊帐，弗洛伦斯、查尔斯和塞尔ina看起来就像梦中的人物——或者天使——在杰克眼中。 他闭上眼睛，终于感到安全了。
