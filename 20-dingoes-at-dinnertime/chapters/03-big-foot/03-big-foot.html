3. Big Foot 3. 大脚

The loud cry rang again through the dry air. 那声大叫再次穿过干燥的空气。

Teddy barked. 泰迪叫了起来。

Jack and Annie turned around in the clearing. It was hard to tell where the sound was coming from. 杰克和安妮在空地里转过身。 很难判断声音从哪儿来。

The terrible cackle came again. 那可怕的咯咯笑声又来了。

“There!” said Annie. “那儿！”安妮说。

She pointed at a bird in a gum tree. The bird had brown feathers and a large head with a long beak. 她指着桉树上的一只鸟。 那只鸟有棕色的羽毛，大大的脑袋上长着一张长喙。

It stared down at Jack and Annie. Then it let out another cackle. 它低头盯着杰克和安妮。 然后又发出一声咯咯笑。

“Weird,” said Jack. “好奇怪，”杰克说。

He found the bird in his book and read: 他在书里找到这只鸟，读道：

The kookaburra (say KOOK-uh-burr-uh) is the best-known bird of Australia. There is even a popular song about it. The kookaburra is also called the “laughing donkey.” This is because the strange sound it makes reminds people of a braying donkey. 笑翠鸟（说 KOOK-uh-burr-uh）是澳大利亚最著名的鸟。甚至还有关于它的流行歌曲。笑翠鸟也被称为“笑猴子”。这是因为它发出的奇怪声音让人联想到驴子的叫声。

“I know that song!” said Annie. She began singing: “我知道那首歌！”安妮说。 她开始唱：

“Kookaburra sits on the old gum tree-ee. “笑翠鸟坐在老桉树上。”

Merry, merry king of the bush is he-ee … ” 灌木丛里快乐的国王是他——”

Jack wrote in his notebook:



 杰克在笔记本上写道：

图中文字：kookaburra—a big kook 图中文字：笑翠鸟——一个爱笑的家伙

Annie stopped singing. “Hey,” she said. “There’s another weird thing.” 安妮停下不唱了。 “嘿，”她说。 “还有个奇怪的东西。”

“Where?” said Jack. “在哪儿？”杰克说。

Annie pointed to a big bluish tan lump lying in a shallow, dusty hole. 安妮指着一个蓝灰色的大鼓包，它躺在一个浅浅的、满是尘土的坑里。

“Is it alive?” said Jack. “它还活着吗？”杰克说。

They stepped closer to the big lump. 他们走近那个大鼓包。

“It looks like it’s breathing,” said Annie. “它好像在呼吸，”安妮说。

The lump was an animal lying on its back. Its paws were crossed over its chest. 那鼓包是一只仰面躺着的动物。 它的爪子交叉放在胸前。

It had huge feet, large ears, a face like a deer’s, and a very long tail. It also had a very fat stomach. 它有巨大的脚、大耳朵、鹿一样的脸，还有一条很长的尾巴。 它还有一个非常肥大的肚子。

Just then, a small head peered out of its stomach. 就在这时，一个小脑袋从它肚子那儿探出来。

“Whoa!” said Jack. “哇！”杰克说。

“Oh, wow! It’s a kangaroo with her baby in her pouch!” said Annie. “哦，哇！ 是袋鼠，育儿袋里还有宝宝！”安妮说。

“Great!” said Jack. “Remember, we have to get a gift from a kangaroo!” “太好了！”杰克说。 “记住，我们得从袋鼠那儿拿到礼物！”

Their voices woke the kangaroo. The animal jumped up from her shallow bed. 他们的声音吵醒了袋鼠。 那动物从浅浅的窝里跳起来。

She glared at Jack and Annie. Her baby peeked out of her pouch. 她瞪着杰克和安妮。 她的宝宝从育儿袋里往外偷看。

The mother kangaroo gave an angry stamp. 袋鼠妈妈愤怒地跺了一下脚。

“Oh, we’re sorry!” Annie said. “We didn’t mean to wake you up.” “哦，对不起！”安妮说。 “我们不是有意要弄醒你的。”

The kangaroo eyed Annie curiously. Then she took a giant hop toward her. 袋鼠好奇地打量着安妮。 然后她朝安妮大跳了一步。

Copying the kangaroo, Annie hopped toward the big animal. 安妮学着袋鼠，朝那大动物跳去。

The kangaroo hopped again. 袋鼠又跳了一步。

Annie hopped. 安妮也跳。

The kangaroo and Annie began hopping around each other. They looked as if they were dancing. 袋鼠和安妮开始互相跳着转圈。 看起来像在跳舞。

Jack couldn’t believe how graceful the kangaroo was. She seemed to fly through the air, then land as softly as a butterfly. 杰克简直不敢相信袋鼠竟如此优雅。 它像是在空中飞，落地时却轻柔得像蝴蝶。

He looked up “kangaroo” in his book and read: 他在书里查“袋鼠”并读道：

The kangaroo is the most famous of the marsupials. The female carries her baby, known as a “joey,” in her pouch. Scientists call the kangaroo a “macropod,” which means “big foot.” And big feet help a kangaroo hop higher than any other animal in the world. With a running leap, a large kangaroo can jump over a school bus. 袋鼠是袋鼠科动物中最著名的。雌性袋鼠会把宝宝，叫做“小袋鼠”的，放在育儿袋里。科学家把袋鼠叫做“大脚兽”，意思是“大脚”。而大脚帮助袋鼠跳得比世界上任何动物都高。一个大袋鼠用一次跳跃，可以跳过一辆校车。

“Forget the hopping contest, Annie,” Jack called. “She can out-hop you by a mile.” “别比跳高了，安妮，”杰克喊道。 “它跳远能把你甩出一英里。”

He pulled out his notebook and wrote: 他拿出笔记本写道：

图中文字：kangaroo

“big foot”

Can jump over a school bus! 图中文字：袋鼠“大脚”能跳过校车！

The kangaroo began stamping her feet again. 袋鼠又开始跺脚。

“What’s wrong?” said Annie. “怎么了？”安妮说。

The kangaroo froze. 袋鼠僵住了。

Grrrr! Teddy growled from Jack’s backpack. 咕噜！ 泰迪从杰克的背包里低吼。

Some nearby bushes moved. 附近的灌木丛动了动。

A moment later, three dogs crept silently into the clearing. They were sand-colored and mean-looking. 片刻之后，三只狗静静地爬进空地。 它们是沙色的，样子很凶。

Teddy growled once more. 泰迪又低吼了一声。

But the dogs crept toward the kangaroo. 可那些狗朝袋鼠慢慢逼近。

Suddenly, the mother kangaroo sprang into the air, away from the dogs. 突然，袋鼠妈妈跃到空中，躲开了那些狗。

The dogs chased after her. 那些狗追着她跑。

“Stop!” cried Annie. “Stop! Leave her alone!” “停下！”安妮喊道。 “停下！别欺负她！”

As the kangaroo jumped, she turned in midair and landed facing a different direction. She then zigzagged over rocks and bushes. 袋鼠跳跃时，在空中转身，落到另一个方向。 接着它在岩石和灌木间曲折跳跃。

Howling, the wild dogs raced after the kangaroo and her baby. 野狗嚎叫着，追赶袋鼠和她的宝宝。
