7. Rain, Rain, Rain 7. 雨啊，雨啊，雨

Rain fell on Jack’s head and on Joey’s head. Rain fell on Annie’s head and on Teddy’s head and on the koala’s head. 雨落在杰克头上，也落在乔伊头上。 雨落在安妮头上、泰迪头上，还有考拉头上。

Annie opened her mouth and drank the rainwater. 安妮张开嘴喝雨水。

Jack did the same. The water tasted better than any water he’d ever drunk. 杰克也一样。 这水比他喝过的任何水都好喝。

When Jack looked back at the woods, misty steam was rising from the charred ground and burning bushes. 杰克回头看向树林，雾蒙蒙的蒸汽正从烧焦的地面和燃烧的灌木上升起。

The heavy rain was putting out the wildfire. 大雨正在把野火扑灭。

“You’ll be safe now,” Annie said to the koala. “I’ll put you back in a nice gum tree. Then you can finish your nap.” “你现在安全了，”安妮对考拉说。 “我把你放回一棵好桉树上。 然后你就可以把觉睡完。”

“I see a tree that’s not burned,” said Jack. “我看见一棵没被烧掉的树，”杰克说。

They walked over to the unburned gum tree. Annie placed the koala in the fork of two branches. 他们走到那棵没被烧到的桉树旁。 安妮把考拉放在两根树枝的分岔处。

“Go back to sleep now,” she said softly. “Pretend the fire was all a dream.” “现在回去睡觉吧，”她轻声说。 “假装这场火全是一场梦。”

“Good night,” said Jack. “晚安，”杰克说。

The koala seemed to smile at them. Then he closed his eyes and went to sleep, as if he’d never been disturbed at all. 考拉好像在对他们微笑。 然后它闭上眼睛睡着了，好像从来没有被打扰过。

Jack sighed and looked around. 杰克叹了口气，环顾四周。

“Man,” he said, “we were lucky that a storm came.” “天哪，”他说，“幸好来了一场暴风雨。”

Annie smiled. 安妮笑了。

“It wasn’t just luck,” she said. “It was magic.” “不只是运气，”她说。 “是魔法。”

“Magic?” said Jack. “魔法？”杰克说。

“Yeah … the glowing hands and the snake,” said Annie. “Somehow they brought the storm.” “对……发光的手和那条蛇，”安妮说。 “不知怎么的，它们带来了这场暴风雨。”

“That doesn’t make sense,” said Jack. “那说不通，”杰克说。

Joey stirred in his pack. Suddenly, Jack remembered something. 乔伊在他背包里动了动。 突然，杰克想起一件事。

“Hey, we have to get Joey back to the place where his mom left him,” he said. “Or she won’t be able to find him.” “嘿，我们得把乔伊送回他妈妈丢下他的地方，”他说。 “不然她就找不到他了。”

“Where was that place?” said Annie. “那地方在哪儿？”安妮说。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “我不知道。”杰克说。

He looked around at the rainy gray forest. Everything looked the same. 他环顾雨中灰蒙蒙的森林。 到处看起来都一样。

“Teddy can find the spot!” said Annie. “泰迪能找到那个地方！”安妮说。

Without even a bark, the little dog took off across the wet, muddy ground. 小狗连叫都没叫一声，就冲过湿漉漉、泥泞的地面。

Once again, Jack and Annie followed him. Jack’s back was beginning to hurt from carrying Joey. 杰克和安妮再一次跟着他。 杰克的背因为背着乔伊开始疼了。

Arf! Arf!

Jack and Annie caught up with Teddy. He stood over the Australia book! It was wet, but not burned. 杰克和安妮赶上了泰迪。 他站在澳大利亚书上方！ 书湿了，但没被烧掉。

“Hurray, we found it!” said Annie. “太好了，我们找到了！”安妮说。

“That’s right!” said Jack. “I left our book in the spot where we found Joey!” “没错！”杰克说。 “我把书落在我们发现乔伊的地方了！”

“Once again, Teddy helped us out,” said Annie. “泰迪又一次帮我们解决了困难，”安妮说。

She patted the little dog’s head. 她拍了拍小狗的头。

“Thanks, Teddy,” Jack said. “谢谢，泰迪，”杰克说。

He picked up the Australia book. The cover was wet, but the pages looked okay. The little kangaroo peeked out of his pack as Jack tucked the book under his arm. 他捡起澳大利亚书。 封面湿了，但里面的页看起来还好。 小袋鼠从他背包里往外偷看，杰克把书夹在胳膊下。

“Don’t worry, Joey,” Annie said. “We’ll stay right here till your mom comes back for you.” “别担心，乔伊，”安妮说。 “我们会一直待在这儿，直到你妈妈回来找你。”

If she hasn’t already come … Jack thought worriedly. 要是她已经来过了……杰克担心地想。

Jack and Annie stood in the rain with Teddy and Joey and waited. 杰克和安妮带着泰迪和乔伊，站在雨里等着。

They waited and waited. 他们等啊等。

The rain turned to a drizzle. Then the drizzle turned to a light sprinkle. 雨变成了蒙蒙细雨。 接着细雨又变成了疏疏落落的小雨。

Still, they waited … 还是等……

Jack grew sadder and sadder. 杰克越来越难过。

Maybe the mother kangaroo had come and left. Or maybe she had been caught by the dingoes. Or maybe she had been killed by the wildfire. 也许袋鼠妈妈来过又走了。 或者她被野狗捉住了。 或者她死在野火里了。

Jack was afraid to look at Annie, afraid to say anything. 杰克不敢看安妮，也不敢说什么。

“I know what you’re thinking,” she said finally. “我知道你在想什么，”她终于开口了。

Jack patted Joey’s head and sighed. 杰克拍了拍乔伊的头，叹了口气。

“Let’s wait a little longer,” he said. “If she doesn’t come back soon, we’ll take him home with—” “咱们再等一会儿，”他说。 “要是她不快点回来，我们就带他回家——”

Arf! Teddy barked softly. 汪！ 泰迪轻声叫了一下。

“Listen, ” said Annie. “听，”安妮说。

Jack listened. 杰克听了听。

The sound was very faint at first. But then it grew louder. 声音起初很轻。 但随后越来越响。

It was a squishy sound. It was a squashy sound. It was the sound of big feet slapping through mud! 那是一种湿软的声音。 那是一种软乎乎的声音。 那是大脚拍打泥地的声音！
