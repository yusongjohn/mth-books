9. What Boy? 9. 哪个男孩？

“Welcome back,” came a soft, lovely voice. “欢迎回来，”一个温柔动听的声音传来。

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开眼睛。

It was Morgan! They hadn’t seen Morgan in a long time. 是摩根！ 他们好久没见到摩根了。

“Morgan!” cried Annie. “摩根！”安妮喊道。

She threw her arms around the enchantress. Jack jumped up and hugged Morgan, too. 她张开双臂拥抱了女魔法师。 杰克也跳起来拥抱摩根。

“It’s good to see you both,” said Morgan. “见到你们俩真好，”摩根说。

Arf! Arf!

“And it’s good to see you, too,” Morgan said, smiling at the little dog. “见到你也真好，”摩根对着小狗微笑着说。

“Look,” said Annie. She reached into Jack’s pack and pulled out the piece of painted bark. “A gift from a kangaroo.” “看，”安妮说。 她把手伸进杰克的背包，取出那块画着画的树皮。 “一样来自袋鼠的礼物。”

“We have all four gifts now,” said Jack. “我们现在四样礼物都齐了，”杰克说。

“Good work,” said Morgan. “做得好，”摩根说。

She picked up their first gift. It was the pocket watch from the Titanic. 她拿起他们的第一样礼物。 那是泰坦尼克号上的怀表。

“Once upon a time, there was a boy who wasted time,” Morgan said. “This watch teaches him that time is very precious. It must be used wisely.” “从前，有个男孩浪费时间，”摩根说。 “这块表教会他，时间非常宝贵。 必须被明智地使用。”

Morgan picked up their second gift, the eagle’s feather from the Lakota Indians. 摩根拿起他们的第二样礼物，拉科塔印第安人的鹰羽。

“Sometimes the boy was afraid to stand up for himself,” she said. “The eagle’s feather teaches him that a small creature can be one of the bravest.” “有时候这个男孩不敢为自己挺身而出，”她说。 “鹰羽教他：小小的生物也可以是最勇敢的。”

Morgan picked up the lotus flower from the forest in India. 摩根拿起印度森林里的莲花。

“Sometimes the boy did not respect nature,” she said. “This flower teaches him that nature holds many wonders.” “有时候这个男孩不尊重大自然，”她说。 “这朵花教会他，大自然蕴藏着许多奇迹。”

Morgan picked up the piece of bark with the painting of the Rainbow Serpent. 摩根拿起那块画着彩虹蛇的树皮。

“Sometimes the boy didn’t want to study other times and places,” she said. “This painting teaches him there is mystery, magic, and wisdom in the traditions of ancient peoples.” “有时那男孩不想学习其他时代和地方，”她说。 “这幅画教会他，古代民族的传统里有神秘、魔法和智慧。”

“What boy?” Jack asked. “什么男孩？”杰克问。

“Who are you talking about?” asked Annie. “你在说谁？”安妮问。

Morgan didn’t answer right away. She placed her hands on Jack’s and Annie’s shoulders. 摩根没有立刻回答。 她把手放在杰克和安妮的肩上。

“Thank you,” she said, “for helping this boy learn his lessons. Thank you for breaking the spell.” “谢谢你们，”她说，“帮助这个男孩吸取了教训。 谢谢你们解除了咒语。”

“What boy?” Jack asked again. “什么男孩？”杰克又问。

Arf! Arf! Arrrrrrrf! 汪！汪！ 汪——！

Jack and Annie looked over at Teddy. 杰克和安妮看向泰迪。

Then something magical happened. 然后神奇的事发生了。

In a flutter of time … 在时间的一阵颤动里……

in the spin of a whirlwind … 在一阵旋风的旋转中……

Teddy was changed. 泰迪变了。

He was no longer a dog. 他不再是一只狗了。

He was a boy. 他变成了一个男孩。
