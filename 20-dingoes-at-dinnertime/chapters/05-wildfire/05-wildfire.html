5. Wildfire! 5. 野火！

“Wildfire?” said Annie. “野火？”安妮说。

“The woods are so dry, everything’s starting to burn!” said Jack. “We have to get out of here.” “树林太干了，什么都开始着火了！”杰克说。 “我们得离开这儿。”

“We can’t leave Joey,” said Annie. “我们不能丢下乔伊，”安妮说。

“We’ll take him with us!” said Jack. “我们带上他！”杰克说。

“But what if his mom comes back for him and he’s not here?” said Annie. “可要是他妈妈回来找他，他不在怎么办？”安妮说。

“We don’t have a choice,” said Jack. “我们没有选择，”杰克说。

Just then, the kookaburra flew through the sky, cackling. 就在这时，笑翠鸟飞过天空，咯咯叫着。

The emus raced by at top speed. 鸸鹋以最高速度飞奔而过。

The air was getting smokier and smokier. The fire was spreading quickly! 空气越来越烟熏火燎。 火势正在迅速蔓延！

“Come on!” said Jack. “We have to get back to the tree house before it burns down!” “快走！”杰克说。 “我们必须在树屋烧毁之前赶回去！”

“Which way’s the tree house?” said Annie. “树屋在哪边？”安妮说。

“I’m not sure,” said Jack. “我不确定，”杰克说。

Smoke hid the treetops. Jack’s eyes stung. 烟雾遮住了树顶。 杰克的眼睛一阵刺痛。

“Forget it,” he said. “Let’s just get away from this smoke. Come on!” “算了，”他说。 “咱们先躲开这烟。走！”

Jack and Teddy turned to go. The baby kangaroo hid his head inside Jack’s pack. 杰克和泰迪转身要走。 小袋鼠把头缩进杰克的背包里。

“I’ll catch up!” said Annie. “I have to get something!” “我随后赶上！” “我得去拿样东西！”

“What? ” cried Jack. “什么？”杰克喊道。

But Annie had dashed off in the other direction. 可安妮已经朝另一个方向冲了出去。

“Come back!” Jack shouted. “Annie!” “回来！”杰克喊道。 “安妮！”

Branches cracked and fell from the trees. Smoke billowed everywhere. 树枝噼啪断裂，从树上掉下来。 烟雾四处涌出。

Arf! Arf! 汪！汪！

“Annie!” Jack cried. “安妮！”杰克喊道。

Jack choked on the smoke. He coughed and rubbed his eyes. The air was getting hotter. 杰克被烟呛住了。 他咳嗽着，揉了揉眼睛。 空气越来越热。

He had no choice. He had to run. 他没有选择，只能跑。

Arf! Arf! Teddy barked from somewhere ahead. 汪！汪！ 泰迪在前面某处叫着。

“Hurry, Annie!” Jack called helplessly. Then he took off after Teddy. “快点，安妮！”杰克无助地喊道。 然后他追着泰迪跑了出去。

He stumbled blindly through the brush. All he could do was follow the sound of Teddy’s barking. His pack felt heavier and heavier. He held it up with his arms and kept going. 他摸黑跌跌撞撞地穿过灌木丛。 他只能跟着泰迪的叫声走。 他的背包越来越沉。 他用胳膊托着背包，继续往前。

Suddenly, Jack heard Annie calling him. 突然，杰克听见安妮在叫他。

Jack stopped. 杰克停下来。

“Here! Here! Here! We’re here!” he shouted. “Come on! Follow us!” “在这儿！在这儿！ 突然传来一声又大又刺耳的笑声。 我们在这儿！”他喊道。 “快来！跟着我们！”

Annie appeared through the haze of the hot smoke. She was coughing. Tears streamed from her eyes. 安妮从灼热的烟雾中出现了。 眼泪从她眼里涌了出来。

She was carrying the koala! 她抱着考拉！

“Come on!” Jack cried. “Follow Teddy!” “快走！”杰克喊道。 “跟着泰迪！”

Arf! Arf! 汪！汪！

Jack and Annie carried Joey and the koala. They followed Teddy’s barking through the smoky, fire-filled forest. 杰克和安妮抱着乔伊和考拉。 他们跟着泰迪的叫声，穿过烟雾弥漫、到处是火的森林。

Finally, they came to a giant rock. 终于，他们来到一块巨石旁。

Arf! Arf! 汪！汪！

Teddy was standing on a ledge. Behind him was the mouth of a cave. 泰迪站在一块突出的岩石上。 他身后是一个洞穴的入口。

Through the smoke, Jack could barely see the little dog. 透过烟雾，杰克勉强能看见那只小狗。

Teddy barked again, then vanished inside the cave. 泰迪又叫了一声，然后钻进了洞穴。

“Follow him!” said Annie. “跟着他！”安妮说。
