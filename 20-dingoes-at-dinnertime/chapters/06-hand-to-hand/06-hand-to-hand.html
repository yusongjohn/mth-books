6. Hand to Hand 6. 手把手

Jack and Annie climbed onto the rock ledge and stepped into the cave. The air inside was cleaner and cooler than the air outside. 杰克和安妮爬上岩架，走进洞穴。 里面的空气比外面干净凉爽。

“I can’t see anything,” said Jack. “什么也看不见，”杰克说。

He patted the head of the baby kangaroo. 他拍了拍小袋鼠的头。

“Me neither,” said Annie. “我也看不见，”安妮说。

Arf! Arf! 汪！汪！

“I guess we’ll have to follow Teddy’s bark,” said Annie. “Let’s hold hands.” “我想我们只能跟着泰迪的叫声，”安妮说。 “我们只好跟着泰迪的叫声走了，”安妮说，“牵着手吧。”

She held out her free hand to Jack. Jack took it. Then he put his other hand out and touched the wall. The joey moved in his pack. 她把空着的那只手伸向杰克。 然后他伸出另一只手，摸到了墙壁。 小袋鼠在他背包里动了动。

Jack and Annie walked into the darkness. 杰克和安妮走进黑暗。

Arf! 汪！

Teddy kept barking, leading them on. 泰迪不停地叫，给他们带路。

Arf! 汪！

Arf! 汪！

Arf! 汪！

Arf! 汪！

Suddenly, Jack felt something thump against his leg. He stopped and gasped. 突然，杰克觉得有什么东西撞到了他的腿。 他停下，倒吸一口气。

“What is it?” said Annie. “怎么了？”安妮说。

Arf! 汪！

It was Teddy! His tail was wagging and hitting Jack’s leg. 是泰迪！ 他的尾巴摇着，打在杰克腿上。

“What is it, boy?” Jack asked him. “怎么了，伙计？”杰克问他。

Teddy let out a howl. 泰迪发出一声嚎叫。

As he howled, an amazing thing happened. 就在它嚎叫的时候，一件令人惊奇的事发生了。

A white line began to glow in the air. The glowing line grew until it looked like a giant snake. Then glowing handprints appeared below the snake. 一条白线开始在空气中发光。 那条发光的线越变越长，直到看起来像一条巨蛇。 接着，蛇的下方出现了发光的手印。

Jack felt Annie squeeze his hand. 杰克感觉安妮捏紧了他的手。

“I think it’s painted on the wall,” she said. “我想是画在墙上的，”她说。

“But what is it?” whispered Jack. “可那是什么？”杰克低声说。

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “我不知道。”安妮说。

She let go of Jack and put her hand inside one of the painted handprints. 她松开杰克，把手放进其中一个画着的手印里。

Jack did the same. 杰克也一样做了。

Despite the glowing painting, the rock felt smooth and cool. It almost seemed to breathe. 尽管那幅画在发光，岩石摸起来却光滑而凉爽。 它几乎像在呼吸。

A ghost-like whistling sound came through the darkness. Then a loud boom! 然后是一声巨响！

“What’s that? ” Jack quickly took his hand off the wall. “那是什么？ ”杰克赶紧把手从墙上拿开。

The boom came again. 轰鸣声又来了。

“It sounded like thunder,” said Annie. “听起来像打雷，”安妮说。

Arf! Arf! 汪！汪！

“Teddy’s leaving!” said Annie. “泰迪要走了！”安妮说。

She grabbed Jack’s hand. They turned back the way they had come and followed Teddy’s barking again. 她抓住杰克的手。 他们顺着来时的路往回走，又跟着泰迪的叫声。

Arf! 汪！

They followed the little dog until they saw a flash of light. 他们跟着小狗，直到看见一道闪光。

“Lightning,” said Annie. “Lightning and thunder! We’re at the front of the cave! Yay!” “闪电，”安妮说。 “闪电和雷！ “闪电和雷声！我们到洞口了！太好了！”

Annie pulled Jack toward the mouth of the cave and out, into a pouring rain. 安妮拉着杰克走向洞口，冲进了倾盆大雨里。
