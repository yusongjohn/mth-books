1. The Last Gift 1. 最后一样礼物

Annie sat on the porch steps. She stared down the street at the Frog Creek woods. 安妮坐在门廊台阶上。 她盯着街尽头蛙溪镇的树林。

“Hey, Jack,” she said. “Do you hear it?” “嘿，杰克，”她说。 “你听见了吗？”

Jack sat next to her. He was reading a book. 杰克坐在她旁边。 他正在看书。

“Hear what?” he said. “听见什么？”他说。

“Teddy’s calling us,” said Annie. “泰迪在叫我们，”安妮说。

“You’re kidding,” said Jack. But he looked down the street and listened, too. “你在开玩笑吧，”杰克说。 可他也朝街尽头望去，听着。

A faint bark came from the distance. 远处传来一声微弱的吠叫。

Arf! Arf! 汪！汪！

A big smile crossed Jack’s face. 杰克脸上绽开一个大大的笑容。

“You hear it!” Annie said. “你听见了！”安妮说。

“Yep,” said Jack. “You’re right. Time to go.” “嗯，”杰克说。 “你说得对。该走了。”

He stood up and grabbed his backpack. 他站起来，抓起背包。

“Be back soon!” Annie shouted through the screen door. “很快回来！”安妮对着纱门喊道。

“Don’t be late for dinner!” their dad called. “晚饭别晚了！”爸爸喊道。

“We won’t!” said Jack. “不会的！”杰克说。

He and Annie ran down the street and into the Frog Creek woods. 他和安妮跑下街道，进了蛙溪镇的树林。

Soon they came to the tallest oak. 很快他们来到最高的那棵橡树下。

There was the magic tree house. A little black nose stuck out the window. 魔法树屋就在那儿。 一个小小的黑鼻子从窗口伸了出来。

“Hi, silly!” Annie called. “We’re coming!” “嗨，傻瓜！”安妮喊道。 “我们来了！”

Arf! came a happy bark. 汪！ 传来一声开心的吠叫。

Annie grabbed the rope ladder and started climbing. 安妮抓住绳梯开始往上爬。

Jack followed her up into the tree house. 杰克跟着她爬进树屋。

A small dog sat in a circle of afternoon sunshine. His tail wagged. 一只小狗坐在一圈午后的阳光里。它摇着尾巴。

“Hey, Teddy!” said Jack. “嘿，泰迪！”杰克说。

Jack and Annie hugged Teddy. And the dog licked both of them. 杰克和安妮拥抱了泰迪。 小狗舔了舔他们俩。

“Morgan’s note is still here,” said Annie. “摩根的字条还在这儿，”安妮说。

“Yep,” said Jack. He knew the note by heart now. “嗯，”杰克说。 这张字条他现在已经会背了。

This little dog is under a spell and needs your help. To free him, you must be given four special things: 这只小狗中了咒语，需要你们的帮助。 要解救它，你们必须得到四样特别的东西：

A gift from a ship lost at sea, 一样来自沉没船只的礼物。

A gift from the prairie blue, 一样来自蓝色大草原的礼物。

A gift from a forest far away, 一样来自遥远森林的礼物。

A gift from a kangaroo. 一份来自袋鼠的礼物。

Be wise. Be brave. Be careful. 要聪明。要勇敢。 要小心。

Morgan

Beside the note were the gifts from their first three trips: 字条旁边是他们前三次旅行得到的礼物：

1. a pocket watch from the Titanic 1. 泰坦尼克号上的怀表

2. an eagle’s feather from the prairie skies 2. 大草原天空中的鹰羽

3. a lotus flower from a forest in India 3. 印度森林里的莲花

“We just need to get a gift from a kangaroo,” said Annie, “and Teddy will be free from his spell.” “我们只要再拿到一样来自袋鼠的礼物，”安妮说，“泰迪就能从咒语中解脱了。”

“We must be going to Australia,” said Jack. “That’s where kangaroos live.” “我们一定是要去澳大利亚，”杰克说。 “袋鼠就住在那儿。”

“Cool,” said Annie. “酷，”安妮说。

Teddy whined and scratched at a book lying in the corner. 泰迪呜咽着，用爪子去挠角落里的一本书。

Jack picked it up. 杰克把它捡起来。

“What’d I tell you?” he said. “我说什么来着？”他说。

He showed the cover to Annie. The title was Adventure in Australia. 他把封面给安妮看。 书名是《澳大利亚历险》。

“Great,” said Annie. She looked at Teddy. “Ready to meet a kangaroo?” “太好了，”安妮说。 她看着泰迪。 “准备好去见袋鼠了吗？”

Arf! Arf!

Jack opened the book. He found a page with small pictures of different animals and a big picture of a forest. Jack pointed at the forest. 杰克打开书。 他找到一页，上面有各种小动物的图画，还有一幅大森林图。 杰克指着森林。

“I wish we could go there,” he said. “我希望我们能去那儿。”他说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 绝对静止。
