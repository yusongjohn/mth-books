4. Joey 4. 乔伊

“Oh, no!” cried Annie. “We have to save her!” “哦，不！”安妮喊道。 “我们得救她！”

She took off after the dogs. 她追着那些狗跑了出去。

Arf! Arf! Arf! Teddy barked over Jack’s shoulder. 汪！汪！ 汪！ 泰迪在杰克肩头叫着。

Jack ran after Annie with the book under his arm. He ran over the dry, cracked ground, past scrubby bushes and scattered gum trees. 杰克夹着书追着安妮跑。 他跑过干燥开裂的地面，穿过灌木丛和稀疏的桉树。

Jack kept his eye on Annie, running ahead of him. He saw her stop suddenly. She turned and dropped to her knees. 杰克盯着跑在前面的安妮。 他看见她突然停下。 她停下来，转过身，突然跪了下去。

“What happened?” he shouted. “怎么了？”他喊道。

“Come look!” she said. “过来看！”她说。

Jack reached Annie. Beside her in the grass was the baby kangaroo. It was trembling. 杰克赶到安妮身边。 她身边的草丛里有只小袋鼠。它正在发抖。

“Don’t be scared,” Annie was saying. Then she looked at Jack. “Where’s his mom? Why did she drop him?” “别怕，”安妮正在说。 然后她看着杰克。 “他妈妈呢？ 她为什么把他丢掉？”

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “我不知道。”杰克说。

He put his pack on the ground and opened the Australia book. Teddy jumped out of the pack. 他把背包放到地上，打开澳大利亚书。 泰迪跳出背包。

The little dog tried to sniff the baby kangaroo. 小狗想去嗅小袋鼠。

“Don’t scare him, Teddy,” Annie said. “别吓着他，泰迪，”安妮说。

Teddy sat back and watched politely. 泰迪坐回去，有礼貌地看着。

Jack opened the Australia book and found a picture of a baby kangaroo. He read: 杰克打开澳大利亚书，找到一张小袋鼠的图片。他读道：

The biggest enemy of the kangaroo is the dingo, the wild dog of Australia. When a mother kangaroo is chased by dingoes, she may throw her joey out of her pouch. Without the extra weight in her pouch, she can leap faster and farther. She then leads the dingoes away from her baby. If she escapes the dingoes, she returns to the joey. 袋鼠最大的敌人是澳洲野狗——dingo。当母袋鼠被野狗追赶时，她可能会把小袋鼠从育儿袋里扔出去。这样育儿袋就不再有额外的重量，她就能跳得更快更远。然后她会把野狗引开，离开她的宝宝。如果她成功逃脱了野狗，她就会回到小袋鼠身边。

“Oh, Jack,” Annie said sadly. “I hope his mother escapes from the dingoes.” “哦，杰克，”安妮伤心地说。 “希望它妈妈能逃过那些澳洲野犬。”

“Me, too,” said Jack. “我也是，”杰克说。

“Hi, Joey,” said Annie. She gently patted the baby kangaroo. “He’s so soft, Jack.” “嗨，乔伊，”安妮说。 她轻轻拍了拍小袋鼠。 “他好软啊，杰克。”

Jack knelt down and touched the brown fur. It was soft, the softest fur he had ever felt. 杰克跪下来，摸了摸那棕色的皮毛。 确实很软，是他摸过的最软的毛。

The shy little kangaroo stared at Jack with big brown eyes and trembled. 害羞的小袋鼠用大大的棕色眼睛盯着杰克，微微发抖。

“Don’t be scared, Joey,” Annie said. “Your mom’s going to come back for you.” “别怕，乔伊，”安妮说。 “你妈妈会回来找你的。”

Joey jumped away from Jack and Annie. He hopped toward Jack’s pack, which was sitting on the ground. 乔伊跳开，离开杰克和安妮。 他朝地上的杰克背包跳去。

The baby kangaroo took a giant leap and dived headfirst into the pack! His whole body went inside, but his big feet stuck out. Then he turned himself over and peeked out at Jack and Annie. 小袋鼠猛地一跳，一头扎进了背包里！ 整个身子都钻进去了，可大脚还露在外面。 然后他翻过身，从里面偷看杰克和安妮。

They both laughed. 他们俩都笑了。

“He thinks your pack is a pouch!” said Annie. “I know. Put it on backward. It will feel like when his mom carries him.” “他把你的背包当成育儿袋了！”安妮说。 “我知道你在想什么，”她终于开口了。 把它反过来背。 感觉会像妈妈背着他一样。”

Jack put his Australia book on the ground. Then Annie helped him put the pack on his chest instead of on his back. The joey was heavy! 杰克把澳大利亚书放在地上。 然后安妮帮他把背包背在胸前，而不是背上。 小袋鼠好重！

“There,” Annie said. “You look just like a mother kangaroo.” “好了，”安妮说。 “你看起来正好像一只袋鼠妈妈。”

“Oh, brother,” said Jack. “哎呀，真是的。”杰克说。

But he patted the baby’s soft fur. 可他还是拍了拍宝宝软软的毛。

“Don’t worry,” he said to Joey. “You can stay in there till your mom gets back.” “别担心，”他对乔伊说。 “你可以待在里面，直到你妈妈回来。”

“Here, Joey, would you like some grass to eat?” asked Annie. “来，乔伊，要不要吃点草？”安妮问。

Annie scooped up a handful of grass and gave it to the kangaroo. 安妮抓起一把草，递给小袋鼠。

He munched the grass, keeping his big eyes on Annie. 它大声嚼着草，大眼睛一直盯着安妮。

“I hope his mom comes back for him soon,” she said worriedly. “希望他妈妈快点回来找他，”她担心地说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “是啊，”杰克说。

He looked around the dry forest. There was no sign of the mother kangaroo. 他环顾干燥的森林。 没有袋鼠妈妈的踪影。

But Jack saw something else. 可杰克看见了别的东西。

“Look,” he said to Annie. “看，”他对安妮说。

The wisp of smoke in the sky had turned into a big black cloud. Jack noticed the smell of burning wood was much stronger. 天上那缕烟已经变成一大团黑云。 杰克注意到烧木头的气味浓多了。

“What are those campers doing?” said Annie. “Are they making a bonfire now or what?” “那些露营的人在干什么？”安妮说。 “那些露营的人在干什么？”安妮说，“现在是在生篝火吗，还是怎么了？”

A feeling of dread came over Jack. 一阵恐惧袭上杰克的心头。

“What if … ” he said. “What if … ” “要是……”他说。 “要是……”

In the distance, a tree suddenly burst into flames. 远处，一棵树突然燃起了火。

“We’re looking at a wildfire! ” he said. “我们看见的是野火！”他说。
