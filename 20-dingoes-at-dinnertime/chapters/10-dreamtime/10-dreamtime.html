10. Dreamtime 10. 梦时

The boy was on the ground on his hands and knees. 那男孩趴在地上，手脚着地。

“Meet my young helper from Camelot,” said Morgan. “来见见我来自卡米洛特的小助手，”摩根说。

The boy glanced up. He had a friendly freckled face and twinkly dark eyes. His hair was the same color that Teddy’s fur had been. He looked a bit older than Jack, about ten or so. 男孩抬头看了一眼。 他有一张友好的、长着雀斑的脸，还有一双亮晶晶的黑眼睛。 他的头发和泰迪原来的毛色一样。 他看起来比杰克稍大一点，大约十岁左右。

“Am I back?” he asked. “我回来了吗？”他问。

“You’re back,” said Morgan. “你回来了，”摩根说。

The boy leaped up and hugged her. 男孩跳起来拥抱她。

“Thank you!” he cried. “谢谢你！”他喊道。

“And I hope next time you’ll ask before trying the spells in my spell book,” said Morgan. “我希望下次你在用法术书之前会先问我，”摩根说。

The boy grinned sheepishly. 男孩难为情地笑了笑。

“I promise.” Then he looked at Jack and Annie. “I accidentally changed myself into a dog,” he said. “我保证。”然后他看着杰克和安妮。 “我不小心把自己变成了一只狗，”他说。

Annie laughed. 安妮也跟着它笑。

“But at least I got to have exciting adventures as a dog!” he said. “但至少我当狗的时候经历了刺激的冒险！”他说。

“You were a great dog,” said Annie. “We liked you as Teddy. What’s your real name?” “你以前是只好狗，”安妮说。 “我们喜欢你当泰迪的样子。 你真正的名字是什么？”

“If you like, you can keep calling me Teddy,” the boy said. “Or how about Ted?” “如果你们愿意，还可以叫我泰迪，”男孩说。 “或者叫特德怎么样？”

“Okay, Ted,” said Annie. “好的，特德，”安妮说。

Jack just nodded. He was still in shock. 杰克只是点点头。 他还处在震惊之中。

“Ted is training to work in my library at Camelot,” said Morgan. “He has a rare gift for magic.” “特德正在训练，准备在我卡米洛特的图书馆工作，”摩根说。 “他有罕见的魔法天赋。”

“Cool,” said Annie. “酷，”安妮说。

“You—you helped us a lot, Ted,” said Jack, finally finding his voice. “你——你帮了我们很多，特德，”杰克终于找回了自己的声音。

“Oh, no, it was both of you who helped me,” said Ted. “You helped break the spell. And I found new stories to take home.” “哦，不，是你们俩帮了我，”特德说。 “你们帮忙解除了咒语。 我还找到了新故事可以带回家。”

“You did?” said Annie. “是吗？”安妮说。

Ted nodded. 特德点点头。

“The story of the Titanic, the story of White Buffalo Woman, the story of the wounded tiger, and the story of the Rainbow Serpent,” he said. “I’ll write them down as soon as I get home. So people can read them in Morgan’s library.” “泰坦尼克号的故事，白水牛女人的故事，受伤老虎的故事，还有彩虹蛇的故事，”他说。 “我一回家就会把它们写下来。 这样人们就能在摩根的图书馆里读到它们。”

“And home is where we must go now, I’m afraid,” said Morgan. “恐怕我们现在也该回家了，”摩根说。

“Oh,” said Annie sadly. “That’s too bad.” “哦，”安妮难过地说。 “真可惜。”

“Yeah,” said Jack. He was sad, too. “是啊，”杰克说。他也很难过。

“I know we will meet again someday,” said Ted. “我知道我们总有一天会再见面，”特德说。

“I hope so,” said Jack. “希望如此，”杰克说。

“Me, too,” said Annie. “Bye!” “我也是，”安妮说。 “再见！”

She started down the ladder. 她开始顺着梯子往下爬。

Jack pulled on his pack. With a heavy heart, he followed. 杰克背上背包。 他心情沉重地跟了下去。

When they got to the ground, they looked up. 他们到了地上，抬头望去。

Morgan and Ted were at the window. They both seemed to glow in the late afternoon light. 摩根和特德在窗口。 在傍晚的光线里，他们俩好像都在发光。

“The magic tree house will return for you soon,” said Morgan. “I promise.” “魔法树屋很快会再来接你们，”摩根说。 “我答应。”

She waved, and they waved back. 她挥手，他们也挥手回应。

“Good-bye, Jack and Annie,” she said. “再见，杰克和安妮，”她说。

“Arf!” said Ted. “汪！”特德说。

In a flutter of time … 在时间的一阵颤动里……

in the spin of a whirlwind … 在一阵旋风的旋转中……

the magic tree house was gone. 魔法树屋消失了。

For a long moment, Jack and Annie stared at the empty tree. 有那么好一会儿，杰克和安妮盯着那棵空空的树。

“Ready for dinner?” Annie asked softly. “准备好吃晚饭了吗？”安妮轻声问。

Jack nodded. 杰克点了点头。

He felt dazed as they walked silently through the Frog Creek woods. 他们默默走过蛙溪树林时，他感到一阵茫然。

When they came to their street, the sun was setting. A flock of black birds flew through the silvery pink sky. 他们来到自己的街道时，太阳正西沉。 一群黑鸟飞过银粉色的天空。

Annie broke their silence as they headed for their house. 他们朝家走去时，安妮打破了沉默。

“We had great adventures with Teddy—I mean Ted—didn’t we?” she said. “我们和泰迪——我是说特德——有过很棒的冒险，对吧？”她说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “It was like … ” He searched for the right words. “Like … ” “是啊，”杰克说。 他搜寻着合适的字眼。 “比如……”

“Like living in Dreamtime,” said Annie. “比如生活在梦时，”安妮说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. He smiled. “是啊，”杰克说。他笑了。

That was exactly what it was like. 那正是那种感觉。
