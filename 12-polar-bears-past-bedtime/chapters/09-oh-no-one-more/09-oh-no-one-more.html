9. Oh, No, One More! 9. 哦，不，还有一道！

By the time the dogsled arrived at the tree house, the snowstorm had become a blizzard. 等狗拉雪橇到达树屋时，暴风雪已经变成了狂风暴雪。

“Can you wait just a minute?” Jack asked the seal hunter. “So we can check something?” “你能再等一分钟吗？”杰克问海豹猎人。 “好让我们去查点东西？”

The hunter nodded. His dogs whined as Jack and Annie climbed through the tree house window. 猎人点了点头。 杰克和安妮爬进树屋窗户时，他的狗发出哀叫声。

Jack grabbed the scroll that held the riddle. He unrolled it. The riddle was gone. In its place was one shimmering word: 杰克抓起写着谜语的卷轴。他展开它。 谜语不见了。 原来的位置上只剩一个闪闪发亮的词：

图中文字：MASK 图中文字：面具

“We did it!” said Annie. “The tree house will take us home now.” “我们做到了！”安妮说。 “树屋现在会带我们回家了。”

“Great!” said Jack. “Let’s say good-bye to the seal hunter and give him back his clothes.” “太好了！”杰克说。 “我们去跟海豹猎人道别，把衣服还给他。”

They quickly pulled off their sealskin clothing and their boots. 他们赶紧脱掉海豹皮衣服和靴子。

“Thanks for letting us borrow these!” Jack called through the window. “谢谢你借给我们这些！”杰克朝窗外喊。

The seal hunter walked to the tree house and took the clothes from Jack and Annie. They stood shivering in their pajamas and bare feet. 海豹猎人走到树屋前，从杰克和安妮手里接过衣服。 他们穿着睡衣，光着脚，冻得发抖。

“Th-th-thanks for everything!” said Annie, her teeth chattering. “谢——谢谢你做的一切！”安妮牙齿打着战说。

The seal hunter gave them a wave. Then he walked through the swirling snow to his sled. 海豹猎人向他们挥了挥手。 然后他穿过飞旋的雪花，走向他的雪橇。

“Mush!” he shouted. “驾！”他喊道。

The dogs took off through the stormy night. 那些狗冲进了风雪交加的夜色里。

“Let’s get out of here!” said Jack. He hugged himself. “Before we freeze to death!” “我们快走吧！”杰克说。 他抱紧自己。 “免得冻死！”

Annie grabbed the Pennsylvania book that always took them home. She pointed to a picture of the Frog Creek woods. 安妮抓起那本总能带他们回家的宾夕法尼亚书。 她指着蛙溪树林的图片。

“I wish we could go there!” she said. “我希望我们能去那儿！”她说。

They waited for the tree house to start spinning. 他们等着树屋开始旋转。

Nothing happened. 什么也没发生。

Jack shivered. 杰克打了个寒战。

“I wish we could go there!” Annie said again. “我希望我们能去那儿！”安妮又说了一遍。

Again nothing happened. 还是什么也没发生。

“Wh-wh-what’s going on?” said Jack. “怎——怎么回事？”杰克说。

He looked around the tree house. The four scrolls with the solved riddle answers were in the corner. 他环顾树屋。 写着已解谜语答案的四卷轴在角落里。

Then he saw it—a fifth scroll. 接着他看见了——第五卷轴。

“Where did th-th- that come from?” he said. “那——那是从哪儿来的？”他说。

Jack grabbed it and unrolled it. On it were the words: 杰克抓起它，展开。 上面写着：

Look at the letters:

the first, not the rest.

Discover the place

that you love the best. 看看这些字母：第一个，而不是其他的。发现你最喜欢的地方。

“Oh, no!” said Annie. “Another riddle!” “哦，不！”安妮说。 “又一道谜语！”

“Okay, okay. Let’s stay c-c-calm,” Jack said, shivering. “Look at the letters: the first, not the rest. Okay, the first letters in this riddle are L-A-T-L-T—” “好，好。 我们保——保冷静，”杰克冻得发抖地说。 “看这些字母：第一个，不是其余的。 好，这道谜语的首字母是 L-A-T-L-T——”

“That doesn’t make any sense,” Annie broke in. “那完全说不通。”安妮打断道。

Icy winds battered the tree house. Snow blew inside. 冰冷的风猛打着树屋。雪吹了进来。

“We have to hurry!” said Annie. “我们得赶快！”安妮说。

Jack was freezing. He looked around wildly. 杰克快冻僵了。 他慌乱地四处张望。

“Letters, letters, letters. What letters?” he said. “字母，字母，字母。 什么字母？”他说。

His gaze rested on the scrolls in the corner. 他的目光停在角落里的那些卷轴上。

“M-m-maybe we should look at the letters of the answers, ” he said. “也——也许我们应该看看答案的字母，”他说。

“Right,” said Annie. “对，”安妮说。

They began unrolling the scrolls. 他们开始展开卷轴。

The scroll from their adventure under the ocean said: 他们海底冒险得来的那卷轴上写着：

图中文字：OYSTER 图中文字：牡蛎

The scroll from their trip to the Wild West said: 他们西部荒野之旅的卷轴上写着：

图中文字：EGHO 图中文字：EGHO

The scroll from their journey to Africa said: 他们非洲之旅的卷轴上写着：

图中文字：HONEY 图中文字：蜂蜜

Their scroll from the Arctic said: 他们北极之旅的卷轴上写着：

图中文字：MASK 图中文字：面具

“Oyster, echo, honey, mask,” said Jack. “Their first letters are O-E-H-M.” “牡蛎、回声、蜂蜜、面具，”杰克说。 “它们的首字母是 O-E-H-M。”

“That doesn’t make any sense, either,” said Annie. “那样也完全说不通。”安妮说。

“Yeah, but maybe we have to unscramble those letters,” said Jack. “O-E-H-M … They could spell hemo.” “对，但也许我们得把这些字母重新排列。”杰克说。 “O-E-H-M……可以拼成 hemo。”

“Or meho, ” said Annie. “或者 meho，”安妮说。

“Or home!” said Jack. “或者 home！”杰克说。

“HOME!” cried Annie. “That’s the place we love the best!” “HOME（家）！”安妮叫道。 “那就是我们最爱的地方！”

Jack unrolled the fifth scroll again. The riddle was gone. In its place was one shimmering word: 杰克又一次展开第五卷轴。 谜语不见了。 原来的位置上只剩一个闪闪发亮的词：

图中文字：HOME 图中文字：首页

“Yay!” cried Annie. She grabbed the Pennsylvania book. “I wish we could go home! HOME! HOME! HOME!” “耶！”安妮叫道。 她抓起宾夕法尼亚书。 “我希望我们能回家！家！ 家！家！”

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster and faster. 它转得越来越快，越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
