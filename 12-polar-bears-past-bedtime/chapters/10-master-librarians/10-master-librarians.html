10. Master Librarians 10. 图书大师

Warm air washed over Jack. It felt wonderful. 暖空气涌向杰克。 感觉好极了。

“You have succeeded in your quest,” said a soft, soothing voice. “Are you glad to be home?” “你们已经完成了自己的追寻。”一个轻柔舒缓的声音说。 “回家高兴吗？”

Jack opened his eyes. Morgan le Fay stood in the moonlight. 杰克睁开眼睛。 摩根·拉菲站在月光里。

“Yes,” he said. “高兴，”他说。

“We solved all our riddles,” said Annie. “我们解开了所有谜语，”安妮说。

“Indeed,” said Morgan. “You have proved that you can find answers to very hard questions.” “确实如此，”摩根说。 “你们已经证明，自己能找出很难的问题的答案。”

She reached into the folds of her robe and took out two thin pieces of wood. 她伸手到袍子褶层里，拿出两片薄薄的木头。

“A magic library card for each of you,” she said. “给你们每人一张魔法借书卡，”她说。

She gave one to Annie and one to Jack. 她给了安妮一张，杰克一张。

“Oh, man,” said Jack, feeling the card. “哦，天哪。”杰克说着，摸了摸那张卡片。

The wooden card was as thin and smooth as an ordinary library card. On its surface shimmered the letters M and L. 那张木卡又薄又滑，跟普通的借书卡一样。 卡面上闪着字母 M 和 L。

“These are your Master Librarian cards,” said Morgan. “You are the newest members of the ancient Society of Master Librarians.” “这些是你们的图书大师卡，”摩根说。 “你们是古老的图书大师协会最新的成员。”

“What do we do with them?” asked Jack. “我们拿它们做什么？”杰克问。

“Take them on your future journeys,” said Morgan. “Only a very wise person or another Master Librarian will be able to see the letters. These will be the people who can help you.” “以后旅行时把它们带上。”摩根说。 “只有非常智慧的人，或另一位图书大师，才能看见这些字母。 这些人会帮助你们。”

“Wow,” said Annie. “Can we go on a mission right now?” “哇，”安妮说。 “我们现在就能去执行任务吗？”

“Now you must go home and rest,” said Morgan. “I will come back for you soon.” “现在你们必须回家休息，”摩根说。 “我很快会再来找你们。”

Jack and Annie put their secret cards in their pockets. Then Jack took out the Arctic book and put it with the other books. 杰克和安妮把秘密卡片放进口袋。 然后杰克拿出北极书，把它和其他书放在一起。

“Good-bye,” he said. “再见，”他说。

“See you soon,” Annie said to Morgan. “回头见，”安妮对摩根说。

Morgan gave them a little wave. 摩根向他们轻轻挥了挥手。

Jack and Annie climbed down the rope ladder. 杰克和安妮爬下绳梯。

As soon as they stepped onto the dark ground, they heard a roar. They looked up. They saw a blur of wind and light high in the oak tree. 他们一踏上黑暗的地面，就听见一阵呼啸。他们抬起头。 他们看见橡树高处一团风和光的模糊影子。

Then all was silent. 然后一切都静了。

Morgan and her magic tree house were gone. 摩根和她的魔法树屋都不见了。

Jack reached for his magic library card. When he felt its tingly warmth, he knew that amazing adventures lay ahead. 杰克伸手去摸他的魔法借书卡。 当他感到那股让人兴奋的温热时，他知道奇妙的冒险还在前面。

“Let’s go,” he said. “走吧，”他说。

He turned on his flashlight. 他打开手电筒。

“The woods don’t feel scary like they did before,” said Annie as they walked through the trees. “I’m not afraid anymore.” “这片树林不像以前那么吓人了。”安妮一边穿过树林一边说。 “我再也不怕了。”

“Me neither,” said Jack. “我也不怕了。”杰克说。

“Hey, the darkness is like a mask,” said Annie. “嘿，黑暗就像一副面具，”安妮说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “It hides the day, but it brings out your courage.” “是啊，”杰克说。 “它藏起了白天，却唤出了你的勇气。”

They came out of the woods. 他们走出了树林。

Jack saw their house in the distance. It looked warm and cozy. 杰克看见远处他们的家。 它看起来温暖又舒适。

The porch light glowed. The moon shone overhead. 门廊的灯亮着。 月亮在头顶照着。

“Home,” he whispered. “家，”他轻声说。

“Home,” said Annie. “家，”安妮说。

She started running. Jack took off after her, running to the place that they both loved the best. 她跑了起来。 杰克跟在后面跑，跑向他们俩最爱的那个地方。
