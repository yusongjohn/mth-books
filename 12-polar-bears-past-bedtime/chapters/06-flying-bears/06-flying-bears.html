6. Flying Bears 6. 会飞的熊

The cubs whimpered louder. 幼崽们呜咽得更响了。

“They want to go to her, but they’re afraid of the ice,” whispered Jack. “它们想去找妈妈，可又怕冰，”杰克小声说。

Annie petted the cubs. 安妮抚摸着幼崽。

“Don’t be afraid,” she told them. “You’ll get back to your mother.” “别害怕，”她对它们说。 “你们会回到妈妈身边的。”

The big polar bear growled. She paced back and forth, sniffing the air. 大北极熊咆哮了一声。 她来回踱步，嗅着空气。

Annie kept patting the two cubs and whispering to them. 安妮继续拍着两只幼崽，轻声跟它们说话。

Jack looked in the book for anything that might help. Finally he found something: 杰克在书里找能帮上忙的内容。 终于，他找到了一段说明：

Even though a female polar bear can weigh as much as 750 pounds, she can walk on ice too thin to hold a person by balancing her weight and sliding her paws over the ice. 虽然一只雌性北极熊的体重可以达到750磅，但她可以通过平衡体重和在冰面上滑动爪子，走过连人都站不住的薄冰。

“Oh, man, that’s incredible,” whispered Jack. “哦，天哪，太不可思议了，”杰克小声说。

He watched the mother polar bear walk down the snowbank. 他看着北极熊妈妈走下雪堆。

On large silent feet, she crept about at the edge of the frozen sea. 她迈着又大又静的脚，在结冰的海边悄悄走动。

She tried to step onto the ice. But each time she did, it cracked, and she had to retreat. At last, she found a firm spot. 她试着踏上冰面。 可每次她一踩上去，冰就裂了，她只得后退。 终于，她找到了一块结实的地方。

Then the polar bear stretched out her four legs and lay on the ice. Slowly she moved forward, pushing herself with her claws. 然后北极熊伸出四条腿，趴在冰上。 她慢慢向前挪动，用爪子推动自己。

“Is she coming for her babies?” said Jack. “Or is she coming to get us?” “她是来接宝宝的吗？”杰克说。 “还是来抓我们的？”

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “Hey, let’s put on the masks.” “我不知道，”安妮说。 “嘿，我们戴上面具吧。”

“What for?” said Jack. “干什么？”杰克说。

“Maybe they’ll protect us,” said Annie. “Maybe she’ll think we’re polar bears, too.” “也许它们能保护我们。”安妮说。 “也许她会以为我们也是北极熊。”

“Oh, brother,” said Jack. “真受不了，”杰克说。

But Annie gave him a bear mask. He took off his glasses and slipped it on. 可安妮还是给了他一副熊面具。 他摘下眼镜，戴上面具。

Jack peered through the mask holes. It was hard to see the huge white bear sliding over the frozen sea. He squinted. That helped. 杰克透过面具的眼洞往外看。 很难看清那只巨大的白熊在结冰的海面上滑行。他眯起眼睛。 这样好多了。

The polar bear looked at her cubs and let out a deep moan. 北极熊看着幼崽，发出一声低沉的呻吟。

The two little bears carefully went to their mother. She licked the cubs and touched her nose against each of theirs. Then they crawled onto her back. 两只小熊小心地走向妈妈。 她舔了舔幼崽，用鼻子碰了碰它们各自的鼻子。 然后它们爬到她背上。

“They’re safe now,” said Jack. “Even if the mother breaks through the ice, she can swim with them to the shore.” “它们现在安全了，”杰克说。 “就算妈妈踩破冰面，她也能带着它们游回岸边。”

“Yeah, I just wish she wouldn’t leave us behind,” said Annie. “是啊，我只是希望她别把我们扔下，”安妮说。

The mother bear slowly turned her body around. Then she pushed off with her hind legs. With her cubs on her back, she began sliding away. 熊妈妈慢慢转过身子。 接着她用后腿一蹬。 背上驮着幼崽，她开始滑开了。

“Let’s try moving like her,” Annie said. “我们也学她那样移动吧，”安妮说。

“But we could break through and freeze to death,” said Jack. “可我们可能会踩破冰，冻死的，”杰克说。

“If we just stay here, we’ll freeze, too,” said Annie. “Remember, the seal hunter said his people had learned from the polar bears.” “要是就待在这儿，我们也会冻死，”安妮说。 “记得吗，海豹猎人说过，他的族人是向北极熊学的。”

Jack took a deep breath. 杰克深吸一口气。

“Okay,” he said. “Let’s try it.” “好吧，”他说。 “我们试试。”

He lay on his stomach. He spread out his arms and legs. 他趴了下去。 他张开胳膊和腿。

Then he copied the bear. He pressed his mittens against the ice and pushed off, sliding his feet. 接着他模仿那只熊的动作。 他把连指手套按在冰上，一蹬，滑开双脚。

Amazingly, there was no cracking sound. 奇怪的是，没有破裂声。

“Grrr, ” he growled. And he pushed off again. “嗷，”他低吼一声。 他又蹬了一下。

Jack heard Annie sliding behind him. He kept going. He pushed and slid. He pushed again and slid again. 杰克听见安妮在他后面滑着。他继续往前。 他蹬一下，滑一下。 再蹬一下，再滑一下。

He made the movements over and over, until something happened: He didn’t feel like a boy anymore. He felt like a polar bear. 他一遍又一遍地做着这些动作，直到发生了奇怪的事：他觉得自己不再像个男孩。 他觉得自己像一只北极熊。

Then Jack felt something even stranger. He felt like a flying polar bear. 接着杰克感到更奇怪的事发生了。 他觉得自己像一只会飞的北极熊。

Jack swirled along as if his arms and legs were giant wings—and the moonlit sea ice were a glassy sky. 杰克一路滑去，双臂和双腿像巨大的翅膀——月光下的海冰像一片玻璃般的天空。

He remembered what the seal hunter had said: Polar bears can fly. 他想起海豹猎人说过的话：北极熊会飞。
