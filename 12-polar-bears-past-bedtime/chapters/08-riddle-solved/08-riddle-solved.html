8. Riddle Solved 8. 谜语解开了

Now only the moon shone on the snow. 现在只有月亮照在雪地上。

Jack looked around for the polar bear. 杰克四处寻找北极熊。

She was gone. 她不见了。

“Where’d she go?” asked Annie. “她去哪儿了？”安妮问。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. He looked over the tundra. There was no sign of the giant bear or her cubs. “我不知道，”杰克说。 他朝苔原望去。 那只巨大的熊和她的幼崽已经踪影全无。

“Maybe she’s not interested in scientific reasons,” Jack said. “也许她对科学解释不感兴趣。”杰克说。

Annie sighed. She took off her bear mask and handed it to Jack. He put both their masks in his pack. 安妮叹了口气。 她摘下熊面具，递给杰克。 他把他们俩的面具都放进背包。

“Now what?” asked Annie. “现在怎么办？”安妮问。

They looked around. The vast fields of snow ended in darkness. Jack had no idea where they were. 他们环顾四周。 辽阔的雪原消失在黑暗里。 杰克完全不知道他们在哪儿。

He shrugged. “I guess we just have to walk and hope for the best.” 他耸了耸肩。 “我想我们只好往前走，尽量往好处想。”

“Wait—listen,” said Annie. “等等——听，”安妮说。

From the distance came howling sounds. They grew louder and louder. 远处传来了嚎叫声。 声音越来越大。

“Yay! We don’t have to wait long!” said Annie. “The huskies are coming!” “耶！ 我们不用等太久了！”安妮说。 “雪橇犬来了！”

Howling filled the night as the dogsled came into view. 嚎叫声充满了夜空，狗拉雪橇出现在眼前。

The seal hunter was running beside it. 海豹猎人在雪橇旁边跑着。

“We’re here! Over here!” called Jack. He ran toward the sled. Annie followed. “我们在这儿！ 这边！”杰克喊道。 他朝雪橇跑去。安妮跟着。

“I was afraid you were lost,” said the seal hunter. “我还担心你们迷路了。”海豹猎人说。

“We were!” said Annie. “And we got stuck on thin ice, too! But a polar bear helped us.” “我们确实迷路了！”安妮说。 “我们还被困在薄冰上了！ 可是一只北极熊帮了我们。”

“Yeah,” said Jack. “And we wore your masks and they made us feel like bears—” “是啊，”杰克说。 “我们还戴了你的面具，感觉自己就像熊一样——”

“Yeah, the masks made us brave,” said Annie. She caught her breath. “对，面具让我们变得勇敢了。”安妮说。 她喘了口气。

“Oh, man, wait—” said Jack. Annie’s words sounded familiar. “哦，天哪，等等——”杰克说。 安妮的话听起来很耳熟。

He took out his notebook and read Morgan’s riddle aloud: 他拿出笔记本，大声读出摩根的谜语：

I cover what’s real

and hide what’s true.

But sometimes I bring out

the courage in you.

What am I? 我掩盖真实，隐藏真相。但有时候，我会激发你的勇气。我是什么？

“A mask!” Jack and Annie said together. “面具！”杰克和安妮一起说。

The seal hunter smiled. 海豹猎人笑了。

“You knew!” said Annie. “你早就知道了！”安妮说。

“It was for you to discover,” said the seal hunter. “Not me.” “这得由你们自己去发现。”海豹猎人说。 “不是我。”

Jack pulled the bear masks out of his backpack. 杰克从背包里拿出熊面具。

“Here,” he said. “Thanks a lot.” “给，”他说。 “多谢。”

The hunter took the masks and put them inside his parka. 猎人接过面具，放进自己的风雪外套里。

“We can go home now,” said Jack. “我们现在可以回家了，”杰克说。

“Do you mind taking us back to the tree house?” said Annie. “你介意送我们回树屋吗？”安妮说。

The seal hunter shook his head. 海豹猎人摇了摇头。

“Climb on,” he said. “爬上来吧，”他说。

Jack and Annie climbed onto the dogsled. 杰克和安妮爬上狗拉雪橇。

“Mush!” said the seal hunter. “驾！”海豹猎人说。

“Mush!” said Annie. “驾！”安妮说。

“Mush!” said Jack. “驾！”杰克说。

Snow began to fall as they took off across the dark ice. 他们冲过黑暗的冰面时，雪开始落下来。
