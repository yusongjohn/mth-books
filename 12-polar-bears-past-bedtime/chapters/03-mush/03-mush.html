3. Mush! 3. 驾！

“Did you come with the wolves?” asked Annie. “你是跟那些狼一起来的吗？”安妮问。

The seal hunter looked puzzled. 海豹猎人一脸困惑。

“Did Morgan send you to us?” said Jack. “是摩根派你来找我们的吗？”杰克说。

“I had a dream,” the man said. “You were in it. You needed help.” “我做了一个梦，”那人说。 “你们在梦里。你们需要帮助。”

Annie smiled. 安妮笑了。

“Morgan sends dreams sometimes,” she said. “We came in Morgan’s tree house. It flies through time.” “摩根有时会送梦来，”她说。 “我们是坐摩根的树屋来的。 它能飞越时间。”

Oh, brother, thought Jack. Who will believe that? 真受不了，杰克想。 谁会相信这个？

The seal hunter smiled as if he was not surprised at all. 海豹猎人笑了笑，好像一点也不吃惊。

“We do need help,” said Jack. “W-w-we’re fr-fr-freezing.” “我们确实需要帮助。”杰克说。 “我——我们快——快冻僵了。”

The seal hunter nodded. Then he left the window. He returned a moment later with two small parkas like his own. They were made of heavy dark skins with fur-trimmed hoods. 海豹猎人点了点头。 然后他离开了窗口。 过了一会儿，他拿着两件跟自己差不多的小风雪外套回来了。 它们是用厚实的深色兽皮做成的，帽子上镶着毛皮边。

He passed one to Jack and one to Annie. 他递给杰克一件，安妮一件。

“Thanks!” said Jack and Annie. “谢谢！”杰克和安妮说。

They put the parkas on. 他们穿上了风雪外套。

“Hooray!” said Annie. “It’s warm!” “太好了！”安妮说。 “好暖和！”

“Yeah,” said Jack. “They’re made of sealskin.” “是啊，”杰克说。 “它们是用海豹皮做的。”

“Poor seals,” said Annie. “可怜的海豹，”安妮说。

“Don’t think about it,” said Jack. He pulled his hood up. His head and upper body were very snug now. Only his legs, hands, and feet were still freezing. “别去想这个，”杰克说。 他拉起了帽子。 现在他的头和上半身都暖和极了。 只有腿、手和脚还冻得慌。

“Oh, thanks!” said Annie. “哦，谢谢！”安妮说。

Jack looked up. The seal hunter was giving Annie a pair of fur pants. Then he handed a pair to Jack. 杰克抬起头。 海豹猎人正递给安妮一条毛皮裤子。 然后他又递给杰克一条。

“Thanks,” said Jack. He quickly pulled the pants on over his pajamas. “谢谢，”杰克说。 他赶紧把裤子套在睡衣外面。

Next the seal hunter gave each of them a pair of fur boots and mittens. 接着海豹猎人又给他们每人一双毛皮靴子和连指手套。

Jack took off his sneakers and pulled on the boots. He wiggled his frozen fingers into the warm mittens. 杰克脱掉运动鞋，穿上靴子。 他把冻僵的手指扭进暖和的连指手套里。

“I have a quick question,” Jack said to the seal hunter. “Do you know the answer to this riddle?” “我有个小问题。”杰克对海豹猎人说。 “你知道这道谜语的答案吗？”

He opened his notebook and read: 他打开笔记本读道：

I cover what’s real

and hide what’s true.

But sometimes I bring out

the courage in you.

What am I? 我掩盖真实，隐藏真相。但有时我会激发你的勇气。我是什么？

The seal hunter shook his head. 海豹猎人摇了摇头。

“Come,” he said to Jack and Annie. Then he disappeared from the window. “来吧，”他对杰克和安妮说。 然后他从窗口消失了。

“What about those wolves out there?” Jack called. “外面那些狼怎么办？”杰克喊道。

But the seal hunter didn’t answer. 可海豹猎人没有回答。

Jack grabbed the Arctic book and looked for a picture of the seal hunter. 杰克抓起北极书，找海豹猎人的图片。

When Jack found the picture, he smiled. The seal hunter was standing beside a dogsled. 找到那张图时，杰克笑了。 海豹猎人正站在一架狗拉雪橇旁。

Jack read: 杰克读道：

In cold weather, the seal hunter travels by dogsled. Siberian Huskies often howl like wolves. A lead dog controls the others. The sled’s runners are sometimes made of frozen fish rolled up in sealskin. 在寒冷的天气里，海豹猎人乘坐狗拉雪橇出行。西伯利亚哈士奇常常像狼一样嚎叫。领头的狗会控制其他的狗。雪橇的滑板有时是用冻鱼裹上海豹皮卷成的。

“Hey, Annie, they’re not wolves,” said Jack. “They’re—” He looked up. “嘿，安妮，那些不是狼，”杰克说。 “它们是——”他抬起头。

Annie was gone. 安妮不见了。

Jack threw the book and notebook into his pack. But he was so fat in his furry clothes that the backpack wouldn’t fit. Jack loosened the shoulder straps and tried to put the backpack on again. It fit. 杰克把书和笔记本扔进背包。 可他穿着毛皮衣服太臃肿了，背包背不上去。 杰克把肩带放松，再试着背上背包。这次合身了。

Jack looked at the small window. That would be a tight fit, too. He went out headfirst and barely squeezed through. 杰克看了看那扇小窗。 那扇小窗也会很挤。 他头朝前钻出去，勉强挤了过去。

Jack fell onto the snowy ground. The snow was still drifting down. The air was misty white. 杰克摔在积雪的地上。 雪还在纷纷扬扬地往下落。 空气白茫茫的，一片雾气。

Jack heard barking and howling. He moved carefully toward the noise. 杰克听见狗叫声和嚎叫声。 他小心地朝声音走去。

At first, he couldn’t see the dogsled. But when he got closer, he counted nine Siberian Huskies. They had thick fur, big heads, and pointy ears. 起先，他看不见狗拉雪橇。 可等他走近些，他数出了九只西伯利亚雪橇犬。 它们毛皮厚实，脑袋大大的，耳朵尖尖的。

The lead dog barked at him. 领头的狗朝他吠了一声。

Jack stopped. 杰克停住了。

“He’s telling you to climb on!” said Annie. “它是在叫你爬上来！”安妮说。

She was standing on the back of the sled. The seal hunter stood next to her in the snow. 她正站在雪橇后面。 海豹猎人站在雪地里，在她旁边。

Jack jumped onto the sled next to Annie. 杰克跳上雪橇，站到安妮旁边。

The seal hunter cracked a long whip. “Mush!” he shouted. 海豹猎人甩响了长鞭。 “驾！”他喊道。

The huskies dashed off in a whirl of snow. 雪橇犬在一片雪雾中冲了出去。

Above them flew the snowy owl. 雪白的猫头鹰在他们上方飞着。
