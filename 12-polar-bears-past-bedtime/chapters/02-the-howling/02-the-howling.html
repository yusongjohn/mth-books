2. The Howling 2. 嚎叫声

The air was crisp and cold. 空气清爽而寒冷。

Jack and Annie shivered. They looked out the window at a dark gray sky. 杰克和安妮直发抖。 他们朝窗外望去，天空是深灰色的。

The tree house was on the ground. There were no trees and no houses—only an endless field of ice and snow. Morgan and the owl were gone. 树屋停在地面上。 没有树，也没有房子——只有望不到边的冰雪原野。 摩根和猫头鹰都不见了。

“R-r-read the riddle,” said Annie, her teeth chattering. “读——读谜语，”安妮说，牙齿打着战。

Jack unrolled the scroll. He read: 杰克展开卷轴。他读道：

I cover what’s real

and hide what’s true.

But sometimes I bring out

the courage in you.

What am I? 我掩盖真实，隐藏真相。但有时候，我会激发你的勇气。我是什么？

“I’ d better write it down,” said Jack, shivering.

He pulled out his notebook and copied the riddle. Then he opened the book. He found a picture of a barren white field. He read aloud: 他拿出笔记本，把谜语抄了下来。 然后他打开了那本书。 他找到一张荒芜白色原野的图片。他大声读道：

The Arctic tundra is a treeless plain. During the dark winter, it is covered with snow and ice. In early spring, snow falls, but the sky begins to get lighter. During the summer season, the snow and ice melt and the sun shines 24 hours a day. 北极苔原是一片没有树木的平原。在黑暗的冬天，它被雪和冰覆盖。早春时节，虽然还会下雪，但天空开始变亮。到了夏天，雪和冰融化，太阳一天24小时都在照耀。

“It must be early spring now,” said Jack. “There’s snow, but the sky is a little light.” “现在一定是初春，”杰克说。 “有雪，但天空已经有点亮了。”

He turned the page. There was a picture of a man wearing a hooded coat with fur trim. 他翻过一页。 有一张图，上面的人穿着带毛皮镶边的连帽外套。

“Look at this guy,” said Jack. He showed Annie the picture. “看看这家伙。”杰克说。 他把图片给安妮看。

“We need his coat,” said Annie. “我们需要他的外套，”安妮说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “Listen to this … ” “是啊，”杰克说。 “听听这个……”

He read aloud: 他大声读道：

This seal hunter wears sealskin

clothing to protect him from icy winds.

Before modern times, native people

of the Arctic lived by hunting seals,

caribou, polar bears, and whales. 这位海豹猎人穿着海豹皮衣服，用来抵御寒冷的风。在现代之前，北极的原住民靠猎杀海豹、驯鹿、北极熊和鲸鱼为生。

Jack took out his notebook. He wrote: 杰克拿出笔记本。他写道：

图中文字：Seal hunters wear sealskin 图中文字：捕海豹的人穿着海豹皮

He was too cold to write any more. 他冷得再也写不下去了。

He clutched his pack against his chest and blew on his fingers. He wished he were back home in bed. 他把背包紧紧抱在胸前，对着手指哈气。 他真希望自己还在家里的床上。

“Morgan said someone was coming to meet us,” said Annie. “摩根说会有人来接我们，”安妮说。

“If they don’t come soon, we’ll freeze to death,” said Jack. “It’s getting darker and colder.” “要是他们不快点来，我们会冻死的，”杰克说。 “天越来越黑，也越来越冷了。”

“Shh. Listen,” said Annie. “嘘。 听，”安妮说。

A howling sound came from the distance … then more howling sounds … and more. 远处传来一阵嚎叫……接着又是更多的嚎叫……还有更多。

“What’s that?” said Jack. “那是什么？”杰克说。

They looked out the window. Snow was falling now. It was hard to see. 他们朝窗外看。 这时开始下雪了。 很难看清。

The howling grew louder. It was mixed with yipping and yelping noises. Jack and Annie saw dark shapes coming through the snow. They seemed to be running toward the tree house. 嚎叫声越来越大。 里面夹杂着又尖又急的吠叫声。 杰克和安妮看见暗影从雪中奔来。 它们好像正朝树屋跑过来。

“Wolves?” said Annie. “狼？”安妮说。

“Great. That’s all we need,” said Jack. “We’re freezing, and now a pack of wolves is coming for us.” “太好了。 就差这个了，”杰克说。 “我们都快冻僵了，现在又有一群狼朝我们过来。”

Jack pulled Annie into the corner of the tree house. They huddled close together. 杰克把安妮拉到树屋角落。 他们紧紧挤在一起。

The howling got louder and louder. It sounded as if the wolves were circling the tree house. They whined and yelped. 嚎叫声越来越大。 听起来那些狼正在围着树屋转圈。 它们又是哀叫，又是吠叫。

Jack couldn’t stand it any longer. He grabbed the Arctic book. 杰克再也受不了了。 他抓起那本北极书。

“Maybe this can help us,” he said. “也许这本书能帮我们，”他说。

He searched for a picture of wolves. 他翻找狼的图片。

“Oh—hi!” Annie said. “哦——嗨！”安妮说。

Jack looked up. He caught his breath. 杰克抬起头。 他倒吸一口气。

A man was looking through the tree house window. His face was surrounded by fur. 有个人正从树屋窗外往里看。 他的脸被毛皮围着。

It was the seal hunter from the Arctic book. 正是北极书里的那个海豹猎人。
