7. Spirit Lights 7. 灵魂之光

“Jack, you can get up now,” said Annie. “杰克，你现在可以起来了。”安妮说。

Jack opened his eyes. Annie was standing over him. She still had her mask on. 杰克睁开眼睛。 安妮站在他上方。 她还戴着面具。

“We’re on safe ground,” she said. “我们到安全的地上了。”她说。

Jack felt as if he’d been dreaming. He looked around. They had reached the tundra at the edge of the frozen sea. 杰克觉得自己好像刚做了一场梦。他环顾四周。 他们已经到达结冰海边的苔原上。

The cubs were romping in the distance. But their mother was sitting nearby, gazing at Jack and Annie. 幼崽们正在远处嬉戏打闹。 可它们的妈妈正坐在附近，盯着杰克和安妮。

“She waited to make sure we were safe,” said Annie. “她等着，想确认我们安全，”安妮说。

Jack stared at the polar bear in awe. The words of the seal hunter came back to him: Always thank the animal spirits. 杰克敬畏地盯着那只北极熊。 海豹猎人的话又回到他脑海里：永远要感谢动物的灵魂。

“We should thank the polar bear spirit now,” he said. “我们现在应该感谢北极熊的灵魂，”他说。

“Of course,” said Annie. “当然，”安妮说。

Jack scrambled to his feet. Still wearing his bear mask, he stood before the polar bear and pressed his hands together. 杰克连忙爬了起来。 他仍戴着熊面具，站在北极熊面前，双手合十。

“We thank you,” he said, bowing. “我们感谢你。”他鞠着躬说。

“Yes, we thank you forever,” said Annie, also bowing. “是的，我们永远感谢你，”安妮也鞠着躬说。

“We thank you beyond the moon and the stars,” said Jack. “我们感谢你，超越月亮和星星，”杰克说。

“And beyond the deepest sea,” Annie added. “也超越最深的海，”安妮补了一句。

Then she threw out her arms and twirled around. Jack did the same. They both danced around in the snow, honoring the bear. Finally they stopped and bowed one last time. 接着她张开双臂，原地转了起来。 杰克也一样。 他们俩在雪地里转着跳舞，向熊致敬。 最后他们停下来，又鞠了一次躬。

When they looked up, the polar bear rose up on her hind legs. She was twice as tall as Jack. She lowered her huge head, as if she were bowing back to them. 他们抬起头时，北极熊用后腿站了起来。 她有杰克两倍那么高。 她低下巨大的头，好像也在向他们鞠躬。

At that moment, the sky exploded. The night became a giant swirl of red, green, and purple lights. It looked like a genie coming out of a magic lamp. 就在那一刻，天空炸开了。 夜空变成一大片红、绿、紫交织的光。 那景象像精灵从神灯里钻出来。

The sight took Jack’s breath away. He stared in wonder as the dazzling lights lit the tundra. 那景象让杰克屏住了呼吸。 他惊奇地盯着，那耀眼的光照亮了苔原。

“Is it the polar bear spirit?” Annie asked in a hushed voice. “那是北极熊的灵魂吗？”安妮压低声音问。

As far as Jack could see, the sky and snow shimmered. Even the bear’s fur shimmered in the strange light. 杰克目力所及之处，天空和雪地都在闪光。 连熊的毛皮也在这奇异的光里闪着亮。

“No, it’s not a spirit,” said Jack. “There’s got to be a scientific reason. I’ll find out.” “不，那不是灵魂，”杰克说。 “一定有科学上的原因。我来查查看。”

Shaking, he reached into his backpack and pulled out the Arctic book. He took off his bear mask and put on his glasses. 他颤抖着把手伸进背包，拿出北极书。 他摘下熊面具，戴上眼镜。

By the greenish glow, Jack found a picture of the sky lights. The picture didn’t come close to the real thing. He read aloud: 借着淡绿色的光，杰克找到了天空之光的图片。 那张图远比不上真实的景象。他大声读道：

One of the most amazing sights in the Arctic is the northern lights. The swirl of light is caused by electrically charged particles from the sun striking atoms and molecules in the earth’s atmosphere. 北极最令人惊叹的景象之一是极光。光的漩涡是由太阳释放的带电粒子撞击地球大气中的原子和分子造成的。

“See, there is a scientific reason!” said Jack. “It’s not the spirits.” “看，确实有科学原因！”杰克说。 “不是灵魂。”

Then suddenly all the dancing lights were gone, as if someone had blown out a candle. 接着所有跳动的光突然消失了，像有人吹灭了一支蜡烛。

The magic had ended. 奇迹结束了。
