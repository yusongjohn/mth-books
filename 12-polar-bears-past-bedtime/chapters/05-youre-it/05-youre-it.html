5. You’re It! 5. 抓到你了！

The dogs were barking wildly at two small creatures playing in the moonlight. 那些狗正对着月光下玩耍的两个小东西狂叫。

“Polar bear babies!” cried Annie. “北极熊宝宝！”安妮叫道。

One roly-poly cub leaped onto the other. Then they both rolled through the snow. 一只圆滚滚的幼崽扑到另一只身上。 然后两只一起在雪地里滚来滚去。

“Hi, little bears!” Annie called. “嗨，小熊！”安妮喊道。

The cubs jumped up and shook themselves like wet puppies. Then they scampered toward Annie, who rushed to greet them. 幼崽们跳起来，像湿漉漉的小狗一样抖了抖身子。 接着它们欢快地奔向安妮，安妮也跑上去迎接它们。

“Hi, hi, hi!” she called. “嗨，嗨，嗨！”她喊道。

“Wait—” shouted Jack. “Where’s their mother?” “等等——”杰克喊道。 “它们妈妈呢？”

He looked around for the mother bear, but she was nowhere in sight. Maybe they’re orphans, he thought. 他四处寻找熊妈妈，可她踪影全无。 也许它们是孤儿，他想。

Jack looked back at Annie. She was wrestling with the little bears in the snow. She was laughing so hard that she couldn’t stand. 杰克回头看安妮。 她正和小熊在雪地里摔跤。 她笑得站都站不稳。

Jack started laughing, too. He carefully put the bear masks into his pack. Then he ran to join Annie. 杰克也笑了起来。 他小心地把熊面具放进背包。 然后跑去和安妮会合。

She was running with the cubs across the snowy tundra. One of them raced to her, tagged her, then raced away. Annie ran after the bear and tagged him back. 她正和小熊一起跑过积雪的苔原。 其中一只冲向她，拍了她一下，又跑开了。 安妮追上去，又拍了那只熊一下。

“You’re it!” she said. “抓到你了！”她说。

Jack and the other cub joined in. Soon Jack and Annie and the two cubs were all chasing each other over the moonlit snow. 杰克和另一只幼崽也加入了。 很快，杰克、安妮和两只幼崽就在月光下的雪地里互相追来追去。

They ran until the two cubs fell down ahead of them. The cubs lay perfectly still. 他们一直跑到两只幼崽倒在前面。 两只幼崽躺着一动不动。

Panting, Jack and Annie stared at them. 杰克和安妮喘着气，盯着它们看。

“Are they hurt?” Annie wondered out loud. “它们受伤了吗？”安妮大声问。

Jack and Annie ran to the cubs. 杰克和安妮跑向幼崽。

Then, just as they leaned down to see if they were all right, the cubs jumped up. They pushed Jack and Annie over and scampered away. 可就在他们弯下腰想看看它们是不是没事时，幼崽们跳了起来。 它们把杰克和安妮推倒，然后一溜烟跑开了。

“They were pretending!” said Jack. He laughed. “它们在装的！”杰克说。他笑了。

Jack and Annie charged after the cubs. They ran over the white tundra until they came to the frozen sea. 杰克和安妮冲去追幼崽。 他们跑过白色苔原，一直跑到结冰的海边。

Jack looked around. 杰克环顾四周。

“We’re pretty far from the igloo. I don’t hear the huskies anymore,” he said. “Maybe we should go back.” “我们离冰屋已经很远了。 我已经听不见那些雪橇犬了，”他说。 “也许我们该回去了。”

“In a minute,” said Annie. “Look!” “再等一分钟，”安妮说。 “看！”

The bear cubs had scooted up a snowbank. They were on their backs, sliding down the bank onto the ice-covered sea. 小熊已经窜上了一座雪堆。 它们仰面躺着，从雪堆上滑向冰封的海面。

Jack and Annie laughed. 杰克和安妮大笑起来。

“It’s like sledding!” said Annie. “Let’s try it!” “跟滑雪橇一样！”安妮说。 “我们也试试！”

“Okay,” said Jack, “but then we have to go back.” “好，”杰克说，“不过滑完就得回去。”

Jack followed Annie up the snowbank. He clutched his pack in his arms. 杰克跟着安妮爬上雪堆。 他把背包紧紧抱在怀里。

Annie lay on her back. She whooped as she slid down the ice. 安妮仰面躺下。 她“哇”地一声滑下冰面。

Jack followed her. 杰克跟着她。

“Watch out below!” he shouted. “下面当心！”他喊道。

The little bears were sitting at the bottom of the snowbank. One gently whapped Jack in the face with her furry paw. Then she lay down. 小熊坐在雪堆底下。 一只幼崽用毛茸茸的爪子轻轻拍了一下杰克的脸。 然后她躺下了。

“I’m tired, too,” said Annie. “我也累了，”安妮说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “Let’s rest for just a minute.” “是啊，”杰克说。 “我们只休息一分钟。”

Jack and Annie looked up at the orange moon as they lay beside the cubs. All they could hear was the wind and the soft breathing of the cubs. 杰克和安妮躺在幼崽旁边，抬头看着橙红色的月亮。 他们只能听见风声，还有幼崽轻轻的呼吸声。

“That was fun,” said Annie. “真好玩，”安妮说。

“It was,” said Jack. “But we’d better head back to the igloo. The seal hunter’s probably looking for us. Plus we have to solve the riddle.” “是啊，”杰克说。 “不过我们最好回冰屋去。 海豹猎人可能在找我们。 而且我们还得解开谜语。”

Jack rolled onto his side and tried to stand. 杰克翻到一侧，想站起来。

Crack. 咔嚓。

“Uh-oh,” he said. He went back down onto his knees. “I think we’re on thin ice.” “不好，”他说。 他又跪了回去。 “我觉得我们正站在薄冰上。”

“What do you mean?” said Annie. She started to stand. “什么意思？”安妮说。 她开始站起来。

Another crack rang out. 又是一声破裂声响起。

“Uh-oh,” she said. “不好，”她说。

She carefully lay back down. 她小心地重新躺下。

The polar bear cubs moved closer to Jack and Annie. They made little crying sounds. 北极熊幼崽朝杰克和安妮靠得更近了。 它们发出小小的哭叫声。

Jack wanted to cry, too. But he took a deep breath. 杰克也想哭。 可他还是深吸了一口气。

“Let’s see what our book says,” he said. “看看书上怎么说，”他说。

He reached into his pack for the Arctic book. He took the masks out first and handed them to Annie. 他伸手进背包去拿北极书。 他先把面具拿出来，递给安妮。

“I took these from the igloo by mistake,” he said. “我不小心把这些从冰屋里带出来了。”他说。

As he started to reach for his Arctic book, he heard the loudest crack of all. 他正要去拿北极书时，听见了最大的一声破裂。

CRACK! 咔嚓！

“We’re not even moving and the ice is cracking,” said Annie. “我们动都没动，冰就在裂，”安妮说。

Just then, there was a new sound—a low, snorting sound. It came from the top of the snowbank, about fifty feet away. 就在这时，又传来一种新的声音——低沉的鼻息声。 声音来自大约五十英尺外的雪堆顶上。

Jack looked up. 杰克抬起头。

Staring down at them was a giant polar bear. 一只巨大的北极熊正低头盯着他们。

“The polar bear mother,” whispered Annie. “北极熊妈妈，”安妮小声说。
