1. Are You Serious? 1. 你是认真的吗？

Whoo. The strange sound came from outside the open window. 奇怪的声音从开着的窗外传来。

Jack opened his eyes in the dark. 杰克在黑暗中睁开了眼睛。

The sound came again. Whoo. 声音又来了。呜。

Jack sat up and turned on his light. He put on his glasses. Then he grabbed the flashlight from his table and shone it out the window. 杰克坐起来，打开了灯。 他戴上眼镜。 接着他从桌上抓起手电筒，朝窗外照去。

A white snowy owl was sitting on a tree branch. 一只雪白的猫头鹰正停在树枝上。

“Whoo,” the owl said again. Its large yellow eyes looked right into Jack’s. “呜，”猫头鹰又叫了一声。 它那双又大又黄的眼睛直直地看着杰克的眼睛。

What does he want? Jack wondered. Is he a sign, like the rabbit and the gazelle? 它想干什么？杰克心里想。 它是不是一个信号，跟那只兔子和那只瞪羚一样？

A long-legged rabbit and a gazelle had led Jack and Annie to the magic tree house for their last two adventures. 前两次冒险中，一只长腿兔子和一只瞪羚把杰克和安妮带到了魔法树屋。

“Whoo.”

“Wait a second,” Jack said to the owl. “I’ll get Annie.” “等一下，”杰克对猫头鹰说。 “我去把安妮叫来。”

Jack’s sister, Annie, always seemed to know what birds and animals were saying. 杰克的妹妹安妮，好像总是能听懂鸟和动物在说什么。

Jack jumped out of bed and hurried to Annie’s room. She was sound asleep. 杰克跳下床，赶紧跑到安妮的房间。 她睡得很沉。

Jack shook her and she stirred. 杰克摇了摇她，她动了动。

“What?” she said. “什么事？”她说。

“Come to my room,” whispered Jack. “I think Morgan’s sent another sign.” “到我房间来，”杰克小声说。 “我觉得摩根又送来了一个信号。”

In a split second, Annie was out of bed. She hurried with Jack to his room. 一瞬间，安妮就下了床。 她跟着杰克赶紧跑到他的房间。

Jack led her to the window. The snowy owl was still there. 杰克把她领到窗边。 那只雪白的猫头鹰还在那儿。

“Whoo,” said the owl. Then he raised his white wings and took off into the night. “呜，”猫头鹰说。 接着它张开白翅膀，飞进了夜色里。

“He wants us to go to the woods,” said Annie. “它想让我们去树林，”安妮说。

“That’s what I thought,” said Jack. “Meet you downstairs after we get dressed.” “我也这么想，”杰克说。 “我们穿好衣服后楼下见。”

“No, no. He says go now. Right now,” said Annie. “We’ll have to wear our pajamas.” “不，不。它说现在就去。 马上，”安妮说。 “那我们只好穿着睡衣了。”

“I have to put on my sneakers,” said Jack. “我得穿上运动鞋，”杰克说。

“Okay, I’ll put on mine, too. Meet you downstairs,” said Annie. “好吧，我也穿上我的。 楼下见，”安妮说。

Jack pulled on his sneakers. He threw his notebook into his backpack. Then he grabbed his flashlight and tiptoed downstairs. 杰克穿上运动鞋。 他把笔记本扔进背包。 然后他抓起手电筒，踮着脚尖下了楼。

Annie was waiting at the front door. They silently slipped outside together. 安妮已经在前门等着了。 他们一起悄悄溜了出去。

The night air was warm. Moths danced around the porch light. 夜气暖暖的。 飞蛾围着门廊的灯飞舞。

“I feel weird,” said Jack. “I’m going back to put on some real clothes.” “我觉得怪怪的，”杰克说。 “我回去穿件正经衣服。”

“You can’t,” said Annie. “The owl said right now.” “不行，”安妮说。 “猫头鹰说马上就去。”

She jumped off the porch and headed across their dark yard. 她跳下门廊，穿过他们黑乎乎的院子往前走。

Jack groaned. How did Annie know exactly what the owl said? he wondered. 杰克哼了一声。 安妮怎么能确切知道猫头鹰说了什么？他心里想。

Still, he didn’t want to be left behind. So he took off after her. 不过，他不想被落下。 于是他跟了上去。

The moon lit their way as they ran down their street. When they entered the Frog Creek woods, Jack turned on his flashlight. 他们沿街跑着，月亮照亮了他们的路。 走进蛙溪树林时，杰克打开了手电筒。

The beam of light showed shadows and swaying branches. 那道光束照出了阴影和摇晃的树枝。

Jack and Annie stepped between the trees. They stayed close together. 杰克和安妮在树间走着。 他们紧紧挨在一起。

“Whoo.”

Jack jumped in fear. 杰克吓得跳了起来。

“It’s just the white owl,” said Annie. “He’s somewhere nearby.” “只是那只白猫头鹰，”安妮说。 “它就在附近。”

“The woods are creepy,” said Jack. “树林好吓人，”杰克说。

“Yeah,” said Annie. “In the dark, it doesn’t even feel like our woods.” “是啊，”安妮说。 “天黑的时候，这儿都不像是我们平时的树林了。”

Suddenly the owl flapped near them. 忽然，猫头鹰在他们身边拍打着翅膀飞过。

“Yikes!” said Annie. “呀！”安妮说。

Jack shone his flashlight on the white bird as it rose into the sky. The owl landed on a tree branch—right next to the magic tree house. 杰克把手电筒照向那只白鸟，看着它飞上天空。 猫头鹰落在一根树枝上——正好在魔法树屋旁边。

And there was Morgan le Fay, the enchantress librarian. Her long white hair gleamed in the beam of Jack’s flashlight. 摩根·拉菲也在那儿，那位女魔法师图书管理员。 她长长的白发在杰克手电筒的光里闪着亮。

“Hello,” Morgan called softly in a soothing voice. “Climb up.” “你好。”摩根用柔和的声音轻轻说。 “爬上来吧。”

Jack used his flashlight to find the rope ladder. Then he and Annie climbed up into the tree house. 杰克用手电光照着，找到了绳梯。 然后他和安妮爬进了树屋。

Morgan was holding three scrolls. Each one held the answer to an ancient riddle that Jack and Annie had already solved. 摩根手里拿着三卷轴。 每一卷都写着杰克和安妮已经解开的一道古老谜语的答案。

“You have journeyed to the ocean, the Wild West, and Africa to find the answers to these three riddles,” said Morgan. “Ready for another journey?” “你们已经去过大海、西部荒野和非洲，找出了这三道谜语的答案。”摩根说。 “准备好再来一次旅程了吗？”

“Yes!” said Jack and Annie together. “准备好了！”杰克和安妮一起说。

Morgan pulled a fourth scroll from the folds of her robe. She handed it to Annie. 摩根从袍子的褶层里抽出第四卷轴。 她把它递给安妮。

“After we solve this riddle, will we become Master Librarians?” asked Annie. “等我们解开这道谜语，就能成为图书大师了吗？”安妮问。

“And help you gather books through time and space?” said Jack. “然后帮你穿越时空收集书籍？”杰克说。

“Almost … ” said Morgan. “差不多……”摩根说。

Before Jack could ask what she meant, Morgan pulled out a book and gave it to him. “For your research,” she said. 杰克还没来得及问她什么意思，摩根就拿出一本书递给他。 “给你们做研究用的，”她说。

Jack and Annie looked at the book’s title: ADVENTURE IN THE ARCTIC. 杰克和安妮看着书名：《北极探险》。

“Oh, wow, the Arctic!” said Annie. “哦，哇，北极！”安妮说。

“The Arctic?” said Jack. He turned to Morgan. “Are you serious?” “北极？”杰克说。 他转向摩根。 “你是认真的吗？”

“Indeed I am,” she said. “And you must hurry.” “我当然是认真的。”她说。 “而且你们得赶快。”

“I wish we could go there,” said Annie, pointing at the cover. “我希望我们能去那儿，”安妮指着封面说。

“Wait—wait a minute—we’ll freeze to death!” said Jack. “等——等一下——我们会冻死的！”杰克说。

“Fear not,” said Morgan. “I am sending someone to meet you.” “别怕。”摩根说。 “我会派人去接你们。”

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

“Meet us? Who?” said Jack. “来接我们？谁？”杰克说。

“Whoo?” said the snowy owl. “谁？”雪白的猫头鹰说。

Before Morgan could answer, the tree house started to spin. 摩根还没来得及回答，树屋就开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
