7. Dianthus 7. 石竹花

T he unicorn had a broad milk-white chest and graceful neck. A tuft of hair curled under his chin. A long, spiraled horn rose from the middle of his forehead. He stood very still and very tall. His whole body seemed to be glowing. 独角兽有着宽阔的乳白色胸部和优雅的脖子。 他的下巴下有一簇卷曲的毛发。 一根又长又螺旋的角从他额头的正中间向上生长。 他站得非常安静，也非常挺拔。 他的全身似乎都在发光。

Balor and Grinda just stared at him. They both looked frightened. But Annie stepped forward. “Hi, Dianthus,” she whispered. She reached out and gently touched the unicorn’s white chest. “Feel, Jack, feel his heart pounding.” 巴洛和格里达只是盯着他看。 他们俩看起来很害怕。 但安妮向前走了一步。 “你好，迪安瑟，”她小声说。 她伸出手，轻轻碰了碰独角兽白色的胸口。 “杰克，感觉一下，感觉他的心跳。”

“Move away!” ordered Grinda. The girl stepped between Jack and Annie. “He is ours and he is coming with us.” “走开！”格里达命令道。 那个女孩站在杰克和安妮中间。 “他是我们的，他要和我们一起去。”

“He’s not yours!” said Annie. “他不是你们的！”安妮说。

“Who are you, anyway?” said Jack. “Where did you come from?” “你到底是谁？”杰克说道。 “你从哪儿来的？”

Grinda glared at Jack. “We come from the same magic world he comes from,” she said. “He belongs with us, not you. Balor!” 格里达瞪着杰克。 “我们来自他来自的同一个魔法世界，”她说。 “他应该和我们在一起，而不是和你在一起，巴洛尔！”

The boy shoved Annie aside and tried to loop the black rope around the unicorn’s neck. But Dianthus reared up and wheeled around, forcing Balor and Grinda out of his way. 那个男孩把安妮推开，试图把黑色的绳子绕在独角兽的脖子上。 但迪安瑟斯猛地扬起前蹄，转过身去，把巴洛和格里达逼到了一边。

Dianthus leapt across the tapestry room toward the garden. Jack and Annie followed him through the open doorway into the snowy cloister. Balor and Grinda charged after them. They pushed past Jack and Annie to Dianthus. Balor grabbed the unicorn’s blue collar. “You are coming with us, stupid, like it or not!” 迪安瑟斯跳过挂毯房间，朝花园奔去。 杰克和安妮跟着他穿过敞开的门，走进了雪中的回廊。 巴洛和格里达也冲了上去。 他们挤过杰克和安妮，朝迪安瑟斯冲去。 巴洛抓住独角兽的蓝色项圈。 “你必须跟我们走，不管你喜欢不喜欢！”

“Don’t pull on him!” yelled Annie. “And don’t call him stupid!” “别拉他！”安妮喊道。 “别叫他傻瓜！”

Dianthus tried to back up. 迪安瑟斯试图后退。

“Let go!” screamed Annie. “He doesn’t want to go with you!” “松手！”安妮尖叫道。 “他不想跟你走！”

“He has no choice,” said Grinda. “As soon as that rope goes around his neck, all three of us will be instantly transported back to the Castle of the Dark Wizard.” “他别无选择，”格里达说道。 “只要那根绳子绕上他的脖子，我们三个人就会立刻被传送到黑巫师的城堡。”

“No!” Jack shouted. He charged at Balor and tried to grab the rope from his hand. Balor let go of the unicorn’s collar and shoved Jack into the snow. Then he turned back to the unicorn and began to swing the magic rope like a lasso. “不！”杰克喊道。 他冲向巴洛，试图从他手中抢过绳子。 巴洛松开了独角兽的项圈，把杰克推到了雪里。 然后他转身面对独角兽，开始像套索一样挥舞那根魔法绳子。

Dianthus reared up again and kicked his legs. Breath from his nostrils billowed into the icy air. 迪安瑟再次扬起前蹄，踢起了腿。 他鼻子里呼出的气在寒冷的空气中飘散开来。

“A rhyme, Jack!” yelled Annie. “A rhyme!” “一个押韵，杰克！”安妮喊道。 “一个押韵！”

Kneeling in the snow, Jack yanked the book from his briefcase. He frantically turned the pages, looking for a rhyme they hadn’t used. “ Call a Cloud from the Sky?” he shouted to Annie. 跪在雪地里，杰克从公文包里拽出了那本书。 他慌乱地翻着书页，寻找他们还没用过的押韵。 “从天空中召唤一朵云？”他对着安妮喊道。

“Anything!” she called. “Try it!” “任何东西都可以！”她喊道。 “试试看！”

Jack read the rhyme aloud: 杰克大声地念出这首童谣：

Down from high, out of the sky, 从高处，从天空中落下。

Ee-no-fain-ee-ro-lie! 埃诺弗莱！

A thick white fog instantly filled the cloister. The fog was so dense that Jack couldn’t even see his own hand. 一团厚厚的白雾立刻充满了回廊。 雾气如此浓密，杰克甚至看不见自己的手。

“Balor, where are you?” shrieked Grinda. “巴洛，你在哪里？”格里达尖叫道。

“Here!” the boy yelled. “在这儿！”男孩喊道。

Someone grabbed Jack. He struggled to break free. “Let me go!” he shouted. 有人抓住了杰克。 他努力想挣脱开。 “放开我！”，他喊道。

“It’s me!” whispered Annie. “Come on!” “是我！”，安妮小声说。 “快点！”，

Jack stood up, clinging to Annie. They stumbled together through the garden. Feeling their way along the walkway, they came to the door that led to the main hall. 杰克站了起来，紧紧抓着安妮。 他们一起跌跌撞撞地穿过花园。 他们沿着小路摸索着，来到了通往大厅的门前。

“Dianthus, here!” Annie whispered. “迪安瑟，过来！”，安妮小声说。

Jack heard the soft crunch of the unicorn’s hooves in the frozen snow. 杰克听见独角兽的蹄子在结冰的雪地上发出轻微的踩踏声。

“Where did he go?” Balor shouted. “他去哪儿了？”巴洛尔喊道。

“Find him!” yelled Grinda. “找到他！”格里达大叫。

Jack reached out and felt the unicorn’s soft mane. Annie pushed open the door. Then she, Dianthus, and Jack slipped inside the main hall. 杰克伸出手，摸到了独角兽柔软的鬃毛。 安妮推开了门。 然后她、迪安瑟和杰克溜进了大厅。

Jack closed the door before Balor and Grinda could escape the garden. There wasn’t a wisp of fog in the main hall. The museum lady was still behind her desk. She stared at Jack, Annie, and the glowing unicorn that had just come in from the garden. Her mouth opened, and then closed. 杰克在巴洛尔和格里达逃出花园之前关上了门。 大厅里连一丝雾气都没有。 博物馆的女士仍然坐在她的桌子后面。 她盯着杰克、安妮和刚刚从花园里走进来的发光独角兽。 她的嘴张开了，然后又闭上了。

Dianthus stepped lightly over the wooden floor. Jack and Annie followed him. 迪安瑟轻手轻脚地走过了木地板。 杰克和安妮跟在他后面。

The museum lady never blinked. As Dianthus walked gracefully past her desk, she reached out and touched him. She gasped and quickly drew back her hand. 博物馆的女士眼睛都不眨一下。 当迪安瑟优雅地走过她的桌子时，她伸出手碰了碰他。 她惊呼了一声，赶紧收回了手。

“Thanks for keeping the museum open today,” said Annie. “And thank Mr. Rockefeller for his gifts.” “谢谢你们今天帮博物馆开门，”安妮说。 “还要感谢先生。 洛克菲勒因为他的礼物。

The museum lady opened and closed her mouth again, still unable to speak. 博物馆的女士又张了张嘴，还是说不出话来。

Jack opened the door that led to the street. Jack and Annie followed Dianthus out of the museum and down the stone steps. The blizzard had gotten much worse. Snow was coming down harder than ever. The wind was wailing. The unicorn shook his head and knelt down. 杰克打开了通向街道的那扇门。 杰克和安妮跟着迪安图斯走出博物馆，走下石阶。 暴风雪变得越来越严重了。 雪下得比以往任何时候都大。 风在呜咽。 独角兽摇了摇头，然后跪了下来。

“He wants us to get on his back!” said Annie. “Quick! Climb on behind me!” Annie climbed onto the unicorn’s back. Jack climbed on behind her. “他想让我们骑到他背上！”安妮说。 “快！ 爬到我后面来！”安妮爬上了独角兽的背。 杰克爬到她后面。

Dianthus stood up to his full height. 迪安图斯站到了他全部的高度。

Grinda and Balor burst out the door of the museum. “Stop!” screamed Grinda. 格兰达和巴洛尔从博物馆的门冲了出来。 “住手！”格兰达尖叫道。

Dianthus looked back at them. Then he leapt nimbly over a snowdrift and headed into the darkening storm. 迪安瑟斯回头看着他们。 然后他灵巧地跳过了一堆雪堆，朝着越来越暗的风暴跑去。
