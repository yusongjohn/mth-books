8. Back to Life 8. 重返生命

Jack clung to Annie. Dianthus cleared the driveway of the Cloisters and began to run with ease down the street. His long strides were so light and graceful, Jack could hardly feel the movement. 杰克紧紧抓住安妮。 迪安瑟斯清理了克洛伊斯特的车道，轻松地沿着街道跑了起来。 他修长的步子轻盈而优雅，杰克几乎感觉不到他的移动。

As the unicorn ran, he held his head high. His long, spiraled horn pierced the raging storm, seeming to calm the winds and snow. Jack realized he could feel his fingers and toes again. His whole body began to feel warm. 独角兽奔跑时，昂着头。 他那长长的螺旋角刺破了狂暴的风暴，似乎让风雪都平静了下来。 杰克意识到他又能感觉到手指和脚趾了。 他的全身开始变得暖和起来。

Dianthus headed down a deserted parkway along the waterfront, past an empty bridge. The bridge’s towers and cables formed a silver arc over a wide river. Whitecaps swept across the water’s surface. As the unicorn trotted past the river, the water became still. 迪安瑟沿着 waterfront 附近的一条空荡荡的公园大道前进，经过一座空无一人的桥。 桥上的塔和缆索在宽阔的河面上形成了一道银色的弧线。 白色的浪花在水面上翻滚。 当独角兽 trotted 走过河边时，河水变得平静了。

Dianthus left the riverside and headed down a city street. The wind whistled between tall buildings, blowing the snow into great drifts. But the unicorn’s horn turned the howling wind into a soft breeze. The wild blowing snow became floating, star-shaped flakes, like the snowflakes on Christmas cards. 迪安瑟离开了河边，沿着一条城市街道前进。 风在高楼之间呼啸，把雪吹成了大堆。 但独角兽的角把呼啸的风变成了柔和的微风。 狂吹的雪变成了像圣诞贺卡上那样，呈星形飘浮的雪花。

As the unicorn trotted down the street, snow-bound taxis and streetcars started moving again. Lights came on in cafés and jazz clubs. Jack heard happy music coming from inside. 独角兽沿着街道小跑时，被积雪覆盖的出租车和有轨电车又开始动了。 咖啡馆和爵士俱乐部的灯亮了起来。 杰克听到从里面传来的快乐音乐。

With a calm, steady trot, the unicorn pranced on through the city. As he moved past old mansions, broken-down tenements, and shabby hotels, people peeked out of doorways and windows to see why the sounds of the storm had died away. When they caught sight of Dianthus, with his horn shining in the windless air, they broke into joyful smiles. Church bells started ringing through the white silence. 独角兽平静而稳当地小跑着穿过城市。 当他经过老旧的豪宅、破旧的公寓楼和破烂的旅馆时，人们从门廊和窗户里探出头来，想知道为什么风暴的声音消失了。 当他们看到迪安瑟斯，他的角在无风的空气中闪闪发光时，他们露出了开心的笑容。 教堂的钟声开始在白色的寂静中响起。

Finally Dianthus came to a stone wall bordering Central Park. He jumped over the wall and landed knee-deep in a heap of snow. The unicorn leapt effortlessly out of the drift and cantered over a field and down a slope. 最后，迪安瑟斯来到了中央公园边的一堵石墙前。 他跳过墙，落在一堆雪里，膝盖都埋在雪里了。 独角兽轻松地从雪堆里跃出，然后小跑着穿过一片田野，沿着斜坡向下跑。

Slowly the clouds parted, and light from the setting sun poured down. 乌云慢慢散开了，落日的光洒了下来。

“Hot corn!” a peddler shouted, plowing his pushcart through the snow. “热玉米！”一个卖东西的人喊着，推着他的手推车在雪地里穿行。

“Roasted chestnuts!” shouted another. “烤栗子！”另一个人喊道。

The good smells of the corn and nuts filled the crisp golden air of the park. The unicorn trotted past Belvedere Castle. Bill Perkins stood outside, staring up at the clear blue sky. 玉米和栗子的香味充满了公园里清爽的金色空气。 独角兽慢步经过贝尔维尤城堡。 比尔·珀金斯站在外面，抬头看着清澈的蓝天。

“Hey, Mr. Perkins, the monster storm’s not coming!” shouted Annie. “嘿，先生。 珀金斯，那场可怕的风暴不会来了！”安妮喊道。

The weatherman saw Jack and Annie on the back of the white unicorn. His jaw dropped. Then he smiled and waved. 气象员看见杰克和安妮骑在白色的独角兽背上。他惊讶得张大了嘴。 然后他笑了，挥了挥手。

Jack and Annie rode on. When they passed the statue of the winged angel, Jack thought he saw her move her great wings. “Did you see that?” he cried. 杰克和安妮继续骑马前进。 当他们经过有翅膀天使的雕像时，杰克觉得他看到她动了动她巨大的翅膀。 “你看到那了吗？”他喊道。

“Yes!” said Annie. “看到了！”安妮说。

When they passed the statue of Balto, Jack heard the sled dog bark. 当他们经过巴尔托的雕像时，杰克听到雪橇犬汪汪地叫。

When they rode past the merry-go-round, Jack heard a chorus of neighs and whinnies accompanied by lively carousel music. 当他们骑马经过旋转木马时，杰克听到一连串的嘶鸣声，伴随着欢快的旋转木马音乐。

Dianthus pranced down a shimmering pathway. He leapt over a stone wall and cantered over another field. 迪安瑟沿着一条闪闪发光的小路蹦蹦跳跳地跑下来。 他跳过了一堵石墙，又在另一片田野上小跑起来。

The unicorn’s horn glistened, and the snowy field reflected gold and copper light. Not until the unicorn came to the tree that held the tree house did he stop. 独角兽的角闪闪发光，雪白的田野反射出金黄色和铜色的光芒。 直到独角兽来到那棵挂着树屋的树前，他才停下来。

Annie hugged the unicorn’s long, graceful neck. “Thank you, thank you!” she whispered, and kissed him. 安妮抱住了独角兽修长优雅的脖子。 “谢谢，谢谢！”她小声说，然后亲了他一下。

“Yeah, wow,” breathed Jack. “是啊，真厉害，”杰克感叹道。

Annie looked at Jack over her shoulder. “What now?” she asked. 安妮转过身看着杰克。 “接下来怎么办？”她问。

“I guess we climb off,” said Jack. “我想我们得下去了，”杰克说。

“But then what?” Annie asked sadly. “Where does he go?” “那之后呢？”安妮难过地问。 “他去哪儿呢？”

“That’s a good question,” said Jack. “这是个好问题，”杰克说。

“He comes with us, of course,” someone said. “他当然和我们一起去，”有人说道。

Jack and Annie gasped. 杰克和安妮惊呼起来。

Balor and Grinda slunk out from behind the tree. Balor carried the black rope. 巴洛和格里达从树后溜了出来。 巴洛拿着一条黑绳子。

Jack was stunned. “How—how did you get here so fast?” he asked. 杰克惊呆了。 “怎么——你怎么这么快就到这里了？”，他问。

“We took the A train,” said Balor. “’Tis actually a bit faster than riding a unicorn.” The boy laughed meanly. “我们坐了A线地铁，”，巴洛说。 “其实这比骑独角兽还要快一点。”那个男孩恶狠狠地笑了。

“Shut up, Balor,” said Grinda. Then she turned to Jack and Annie. “I am glad you had a happy little ride. But you can say good-bye to Dianthus now. We will take him from here.” “闭嘴，巴洛，”，格里达说。 然后她转向杰克和安妮。 “我很高兴你们度过了一个愉快的小旅程。” 但是你现在可以和狄安图斯说再见了。 我们会把他带走了。

“No, you won’t!” said Annie. “不，你们不会的！”安妮说。

“Stay away from us!” said Jack. “离我们远点！”杰克说。

“Come on, Dianthus, let’s go!” said Annie. “来吧，狄安图斯，我们走吧！”安妮说。

Before Dianthus could move, Grinda rushed forward and grabbed his collar with both hands. The unicorn snorted and shook his head. Grinda clung fiercely to the collar. “Get the rope over his head, Balor!” she yelled. “Now!” 狄安图斯还没来得及动，格里达就冲了上来，用两只手抓住了他的领子。 独角兽喷出鼻息，摇了摇头。 格里达紧紧抓着领子不放。 “把绳子套在他头上，巴洛！”她喊道。 “现在！”

Balor looped the black rope into a noose again. The unicorn twisted and turned his head. 巴洛又把黑绳子打成了一个套索。 独角兽扭动着头，左躲右闪。

Jack kicked at Balor. “Stop!” he yelled. “Get away from us!” Jack felt stupid just yelling and kicking, but he didn’t know what else to do. 杰克朝巴洛踢了一脚。 “住手！”他大喊道。 “离我们远点！”杰克觉得光是喊叫和踢人有点傻，但他不知道还能做什么。

As Balor and Grinda struggled to get the noose over the unicorn’s head, Annie pointed at them. In a loud voice, she shouted: 当巴洛和格里达努力把套索套在独角兽头上时，安妮指着他们喊道。 她用响亮的声音喊道：

Fowl of air, appear now here! 空气之鸟，现在出现吧！

Aka-aka-aka-mere! 阿卡-阿卡-阿卡-默！

The black rope fell to the snow. Balor and Grinda started to spin around. They spun like two spinning tops. As they spun, they grew smaller and smaller. Jack saw a blur of colors: gray and brown, green and white, a dash of orange, a dash of yellow. 那条黑绳子掉落在了雪地上。 巴洛和格里达开始转圈。 他们转得像两个陀螺。 他们转着转着，变得越来越小。 杰克看到一片模糊的颜色：灰色和棕色，绿色和白色，一点橙色，一点黄色。

The spinning slowly came to a stop. The two scary teenagers were gone. In their place were two small mallard ducks. 旋转慢慢地停了下来。 那两个可怕的少年不见了。 他们原来的位置上，是两只小绿头鸭。
