7. Hi, There 7. 你好啊

The forest was cooler than the sunny plains. It was filled with shadows and bird calls. 森林里比阳光下的平原凉快。 树林里满是阴影和鸟叫声。

“Where are you?” Jack shouted. “你在哪儿？”杰克喊道。

“Here!” said Annie. “这儿！”安妮说。

He found her in a thick glade. 他在一片茂密的林间空地里找到了她。

Bright rays streamed between the trees. Green leaves and vines swayed in the dappled light. 明亮的光线从树缝间洒下来。 绿叶和藤蔓在斑驳的光线里摇晃。

The little gray bird sat in a tree, twittering at them. 那只小灰鸟停在树上，朝他们叽叽叫。

“Yuck, what’s that?” said Annie. She pointed at a round brown thing hanging from a low branch. Bees buzzed around it. “恶心，那是什么？”安妮说。 她指着低矮树枝上挂着的一个圆圆的棕色东西。 蜜蜂围着它嗡嗡飞。

“If that’s her nest, it’s a pretty weird nest,” said Annie. “如果那是她的窝，也太奇怪了，”安妮说。

“That’s not a nest,” said Jack. “It’s a beehive. Don’t you see the bees?” “那不是窝，”杰克说。 “那是蜂巢。 你没看见那些蜜蜂吗？”

“Yikes,” said Annie. She stepped back from the tree. “呀，”安妮说。 她从树旁往后退。

But the little bird darted at the beehive and pecked at it. 可那只小鸟冲向蜂巢，用喙啄了起来。

“What’s she doing?” said Annie. “她在干什么？”安妮说。

The bird kept pecking at the hive. 那只鸟不停地啄着蜂巢。

“I don’t know. Maybe she’s as nuts as you,” said Jack. “我不知道。 也许她跟你一样疯，”杰克说。

“Look her up in the book,” said Annie. “See if it says she’s nuts.” “在书里查查她，”安妮说。 “看看上面写没写她疯了。”

“Are you kidding?” said Jack. “That nutty bird isn’t going to be in this book.” “你在开玩笑吗？”杰克说。 “那只古怪的鸟不会出现在这本书里的。”

“Just look.” “查查嘛。”

Jack opened his Africa book. He kept turning the pages. Just as he thought—there was no gray bird in the book. 杰克打开非洲的书。 他不停地翻页。 正如他所想——书里没有小灰鸟。

“Forget it,” he said. “算了，”他说。

“Keep looking,” said Annie. “继续找，”安妮说。

Jack turned one more page. And there it was: a little gray bird, a beehive, and a tall, painted warrior with a spear. 杰克又翻了一页。 就在那儿：一只小灰鸟、一个蜂巢，还有一个高高的、身上有彩绘、拿着长矛的战士。

“I don’t believe this,” said Jack. “真不敢相信，”杰克说。

Then he read aloud: 然后他大声念道：

This bird is called a honey guide. It’s both a friend and a helper to the Masai (muh-SI) people, an African tribe known for their fierce fighting skills and bravery. 这种鸟叫做蜜导鸟。它既是马赛人（muh-SI）的朋友，也是他们的帮手。马赛人是非洲一个以战斗技巧高超和勇敢著称的部落。

“Hi, honey guide,” Annie called to the bird. “I knew you were important.” “你好，向蜜鸟。”安妮对那只鸟喊道。 “我就知道你很重要。”

Jack kept reading: 杰克继续读：

The honey guide leads a Masai tribesperson to a beehive. The bird waits for him or her to scatter the bees and take the honey. Then the bird feasts on the honeycomb. 导蜜鸟带一名马赛族人去蜂巢。小鸟等他或她把蜜蜂赶开并取走蜂蜜后，就去吃蜂巢里的蜂蜡。

“That’s cool,” said Jack. “They work together, like the zebras and wildebeests and gazelles.” “真酷，”杰克说。 “他们一起合作，就像斑马、角马和瞪羚一样。”

“Yeah,” said Annie. “And she wants us to be her helpers. We have to scatter the bees and leave her the honeycomb.” “是啊，”安妮说。 “她想让我们当她的帮手。 我们得把蜜蜂赶散，把蜂巢留给它。”

“How do we do that?” said Jack. He looked back at the book. It didn’t say how. “我们怎么帮？”杰克说。 他回头看书。 上面没写怎么做。

“Well, maybe we could wave those weeds at them,” said Annie. She pointed to some bright green plants that looked like giant fans. “也许我们可以朝它们挥那些杂草。”安妮说。 她指着一些鲜绿色的植物，样子像巨大的扇子。

Jack put his book and backpack down. He and Annie pulled up the weeds. They waved them near the tree, and the bees scattered. 杰克把书和背包放下。 他和安妮把那些杂草拔了起来。 他们在树旁挥动，蜜蜂四散飞开。

Next, Jack grabbed the tree branch and jiggled it. The hive fell to the ground and broke open. 接着，杰克抓住树枝快速摇晃起来。 蜂巢掉到地上，裂开了。

Annie stooped and stuck her finger into the golden honeycomb. 安妮弯下腰，把手指伸进金色的蜂巢里。

“Yummy,” she said when she tasted the honey. “Try it.” “真好吃。”她尝了尝蜂蜜后说。 “尝尝。”

Jack stuck his finger in the honeycomb, too. He licked off the golden honey. It was the sweetest honey he had ever tasted. 杰克也把手指伸进蜂巢。 他把那金色的蜂蜜舔掉了。 那是他吃过的最甜的蜂蜜。

“Now the honey guide can get to her honeycomb,” said Annie. “现在蜂蜜向导能吃到她的蜂巢了，”安妮说。

“Yeah, but she’d better hurry. Before the bees come back,” said Jack. “是啊，可她最好快点。 趁蜜蜂还没回来，”杰克说。

“It’s weird,” said Annie. “Honey’s so sweet and good. But to get it, you have to go past a lot of dangerous bees.” “真奇怪，”安妮说。 “蜂蜜又甜又好吃。 “可要得到它，你得经过许多危险的蜜蜂。”

“Oh, man,” whispered Jack. “That’s it.” “哦，天哪，”杰克轻声说。 “就是这个。”

“That’s what?” asked Annie. “那是什么？”安妮问。

Jack said Morgan’s riddle: 杰克说出摩根的谜语：

I’m the color of gold and as sweet as can be. But beware of the danger that’s all around me. What am I? 我是金色的，而且非常甜。但要小心我周围可能存在的危险。我是什么？

“I get it,” Annie whispered. “Honey … ” “我明白了，”安妮小声说。 “蜂蜜……”

“Honey,” said Jack, nodding and smiling. “That’s it. We’ve answered Morgan’s riddle. Let’s go home.” “蜂蜜，”杰克点头微笑着说。 “就是这个。” 我们已经解开摩根的谜语了。我们回家吧。”

He stood up to leave. He gasped. 他站起来要走，却倒抽一口气。

Standing in the shadows was a tall man with a spear and a curved sword hanging from his belt. His face was painted in fierce, bright colors. 阴影里站着一个高个子男人，腰带上挂着长矛和一把弯刀。 他的脸上涂着鲜艳而凶猛的颜色。

Jack knew at once what he was. 杰克马上就知道他是谁了。

AMasai warrior.

“Hi, there,” Annie said in a small voice. “你好呀。”安妮小声说。
