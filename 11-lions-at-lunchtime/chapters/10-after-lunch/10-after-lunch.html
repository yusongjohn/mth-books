10. After Lunch 10. 午饭后

Jack opened his eyes. His heart was still racing. Hyena laughter still rang in his ears. 杰克睁开眼睛。 他的心还在怦怦直跳。 鬣狗的笑声还在他耳朵里回响。

“We made it,” said Annie. “我们成功了。”安妮说。

“Yes,” said Jack. “But it was very close.” “是的，”杰克说。 “不过真的很悬。”

Jack took another moment to calm down. Then he pulled the Africa book out of his pack and put it with the other books. 杰克又花了一点时间让自己平静下来。 然后他从背包里拿出非洲的书，和其他书放在一起。

Annie put the scroll with the other two scrolls. 安妮把卷轴和其他两卷放在一起。

“The giraffe was the true honey on that trip,” she said, “sweet and golden, with danger all around it.” “那头长颈鹿才是这次旅行真正的‘蜂蜜’，”她说，“又甜又金黄，周围却全是危险。”

“Yep,” Jack said. “And now we have just one riddle to go.” “嗯，”杰克说。 “现在我们只剩一个谜语了。”

“Yep,” said Annie. “Ready?” “嗯，”安妮说。 “准备好了吗？”

“Ready.” “准备好了。”

She started down the ladder. Jack followed. When they hit the ground, they walked through the sunlit woods. 她开始下梯子。杰克跟着。 他们落到地上后，便走过洒满阳光的树林。

“It’s time for lunch,” said Jack. “该吃午饭了，”杰克说。

“I’m full from our picnic,” said Annie. “野餐吃饱了。”安妮说。

“Same here,” said Jack. “我也是。”杰克说。

“What do we tell Mom?” said Annie. “我们跟妈妈说什么？”安妮说。

“We say we ate our sandwiches coming back from the store,” said Jack. “就说我们从店里回来的路上把三明治吃了，”杰克说。

“What if she asks why?” said Annie. “要是她问为什么呢？”安妮说。

“Oh … just say we had a picnic with a Masai warrior in Africa,” said Jack. “哦……就说我们和一个马赛战士在非洲野餐了，”杰克说。

Annie laughed. “Right,” she said, “because we didn’t want him to be mad at us for taking his honey.” 安妮笑了。 “对，”她说，“因为我们不想让他为了我们拿走蜂蜜而生气。”

“Right,” said Jack, “the honey from a beehive that a honey guide led us to.” “对，”杰克说，“还有蜂蜜向导带我们找到的蜂巢里的蜂蜜。”

“Right,” said Annie, “and that happened after an elephant gave me a shower. And we scared off two hyenas.” “对，”安妮说，“那是在大象给我洗了个澡之后。 我们还吓跑了两只鬣狗。”

“Right,” said Jack, “and after you fell into a mudhole because you were helping a million wildebeests migrate across a river.” “对，”杰克说，“还有你掉进泥坑，因为你在帮一百万头角马迁徙过河。”

“Right,” said Annie. “And all that was before a giraffe saved us from a lion.” “对，”安妮说。 “而这一切，都发生在长颈鹿把我们从狮子那儿救出来之前。”

“Right,” said Jack. “对，”杰克说。

Jack and Annie left the Frog Creek woods and started up their sunny street. 杰克和安妮离开了蛙溪树林，走上洒满阳光的街道。

They were silent for a moment. 他们沉默了一会儿。

Then Jack pushed his glasses into place. “We better just say we ate our sandwiches on the way home from the store,” he said. 然后杰克推了推眼镜。 “我们最好就说，从店里回家的路上把三明治吃了，”他说。

“Right,” said Annie. “对，”安妮说。

“And if Mom asks why—” started Jack. “要是妈妈问为什么——”杰克开口说。

“We’ll just say it’s a really long story,” said Annie. “我们就说说来话长。”安妮说。

“Right,” said Jack, “with, like—ten chapters.” “对，”杰克说，“大概——有十个章节那么长。”

Annie laughed. “Good plan,” she said. 安妮笑了。 “好计划，”她说。

“Very good plan,” said Jack. “非常好的计划，”杰克说。

They crossed their yard. They went up their steps and through their front door. 他们穿过院子。 他们走上台阶，进了前门。

“We’re back!” Annie shouted. “我们回来了！”安妮喊道。

“Great!” called their mom. “Ready for lunch?” “太好了！”妈妈喊道。 “准备好吃午饭了吗？”
