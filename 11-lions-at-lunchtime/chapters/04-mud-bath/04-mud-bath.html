4. Mud Bath 4. 泥巴浴

Jack looked over the edge of the riverbank. 杰克从河岸边向下看去。

Annie had fallen into a pool of mud near the water. The thick black mud was up to her chest. 安妮掉进了靠近水面的一潭淤泥里。 厚厚的黑泥已经没到她胸口了。

“I slipped,” she said. “It feels like quicksand.” “我滑倒了，”她说。 “感觉像流沙一样。”

Jack threw down his pack and got on his knees. 杰克扔下背包，跪了下来。

“Be careful,” said Annie. “Don’t slip, too.” “小心，”安妮说。 “别也滑倒了。”

Jack pointed to a tangle of old tree roots sticking out of the bank. “Grab those!” he said. 杰克指着岸边伸出的一团老树根。 “抓住那些！”他说。

Annie reached for the roots. “Too far,” she said, breathing hard. “I’m sinking.” 安妮伸手去够树根。 “太远了，”她喘着粗气说。 “我在往下沉。”

She was sinking. The mud was up to her neck. 她正在往下沉。 泥巴已经到了她的脖子。

“Hold on!” Jack looked around wildly. He saw a fallen tree branch near the bank. “坚持住！”杰克慌乱地四处张望。 他看见岸边有一根倒下的树枝。

He raced to it, picked it up, and carried it back to Annie. Only her head and arms stuck out of the mud now. 他跑过去捡起来，带回安妮身边。 现在只有她的头和胳膊还露在泥外。

Jack held out the branch. Annie grabbed it. 杰克把树枝递过去。安妮抓住了。

“Hold tight,” said Jack. “I’ll drag you over to the roots!” “抓紧。”杰克说。 “我把你拖到那些树根那儿去！”

He started pulling on the branch. 他开始拉树枝。

“I’m still sinking!” Annie wailed. The mud was up to her chin. “我还在往下沉！”安妮哭喊着。 泥巴已经到了她的下巴。

“Come on!” said Jack. “You can do it! I know you can! Try! Try!” “加油！”杰克说。 “你能行的！我知道你能！ 试试！再试试！”

Just then, Jack heard a splash! He looked up. 就在这时，杰克听见扑通一声！他抬起头。

On the other side of the wide river, a wildebeest had jumped into the water. Another jumped … then another. They were headed right toward Jack and Annie. 在宽阔的河对岸，一头角马跳进了水里。 又一头跳了……然后又一头。 它们正径直朝杰克和安妮游来。

“Hold on tight!” said Jack. He pulled on the stick again. “抓紧！”杰克说。 他又拉了一下棍子。

Annie moved a tiny bit. 安妮稍微动了一点。

“Hey, Jack, on the moon it felt like I weighed ten pounds,” said Annie. “And in this mud it feels like I weigh a ton.” “嘿，杰克，在月球上我觉得自己只有十磅重，”安妮说。 “可在这泥里，我觉得自己有一吨重。”

“Concentrate, Annie,” said Jack, trying not to slip down the bank. “集中注意力，安妮。”杰克说，一边尽量不让自己滑下河岸。

“I am.”

The lead wildebeests were halfway across, swimming toward them. Many more wildebeests were jumping into the water. 领头的角马已经游到河中央，正朝他们游来。 更多角马正跳进水里。

“It’s now or never!” said Jack. He took a deep breath. He pulled really hard. “机不可失，时不再来！”杰克说。 他深吸一口气。 他使出很大力气拉。

Just then, a shadow passed over them. Jack looked up. 就在这时，一个影子掠过他们。杰克抬头看。

“Uh-oh,” he said. “呃哦，”他说。

A huge vulture circled overhead. 一只巨大的秃鹰在头顶盘旋。

“It thinks you’re near the end,” said Jack. “它以为你快不行了。”杰克说。

“Oh, get out of here!” Annie shouted at the vulture. “I’m fine!” “哦，走开！”安妮朝那只秃鹫喊道。 “我很好！”

In a burst of fury, she let go of the branch. She lunged for the roots. She grabbed them! 盛怒之下，她松开了树枝。 她朝那些树根扑过去。抓住了！

“Yes!” cried Jack. “Pull! Pull!” “好了！”杰克叫道。 “拉！拉！”

Slowly, Annie pulled herself out. She was covered with the black mud from head to toe. 慢慢地，安妮把自己拉了出来。 她从头到脚都糊满了黑泥。

Jack helped her onto the bank, getting mud all over himself. 杰克把她扶上岸，自己也弄得满身泥。

“See!” Annie shook her fist at the vulture. “I’m fine! Now beat it!” “看见没！”安妮朝秃鹰挥了挥拳头。 “我很好！现在快走开！”

But the giant, ugly bird still circled. 可那只又大又丑的鸟还在盘旋。

“Come on. Let’s get away from him,” said Jack. He pushed his glasses into place. “来吧。 我们离它远点，”杰克说。 他推了推眼镜。

“Rats,” he said. Now his glasses were muddy. “糟糕。”他说。 现在他的眼镜也沾上泥了。

He tried to clean his hands in the grass. 他试着在草地上擦干净手。

“Oh, no!” shouted Annie. “哦，不！”安妮喊道。

Jack turned to her. 杰克转向她。

“The wildebeests will get stuck in the mudhole!” she cried. She waved her arms at the wildebeests struggling to swim across the river. “角马会陷进泥坑里的！”她喊道。 她朝那些奋力游过河的角马挥着手臂。

“Not here,” she shouted. “Not here!” “别在这儿，”她喊道。 “别在这儿！”

But the frantic swimmers kept coming. 可那些拼命游泳的角马还是不断地游过来。
