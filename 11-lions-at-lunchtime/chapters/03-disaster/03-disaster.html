3. Disaster 3. 大麻烦

“Come on, Jack!” Annie called. She was almost to the river. “快点，杰克！”安妮喊道。 她差不多快到河边了。

“Just a minute!” he shouted. He wanted to study the giraffes and zebras. “等一下！”他喊道。 他想研究一下长颈鹿和斑马。

He pulled out the Africa book and found a picture of giraffes. He read: 他拿出非洲的书，找到一幅长颈鹿的图画，读道：

The giraffe is the tallest animal in the world. Its legs alone can be six feet tall, and its hooves can be as big as dinner plates. The giraffe has a very powerful kick, which makes it dangerous to attack. For this reason, lions tend to avoid giraffes. 长颈鹿是世界上最高的动物。它的腿就有六英尺高，脚掌可以大得像盘子一样。长颈鹿的踢腿非常有力，这让它变得很危险，所以狮子通常会避开长颈鹿。

Jack pulled out his notebook and wrote: 杰克拿出笔记本，写道：

图中文字：lions avoid giraffes 图中文字：狮子避开长颈鹿

He turned the page and read more: 他翻过一页，继续读：

Zebras live in family groups. As no two zebras have exactly the same pattern of stripes, every baby zebra must learn its own mother’s pattern. 斑马生活在家族群体中。因为没有两匹斑马的条纹图案是完全相同的，所以每只小斑马都必须学会自己妈妈的条纹图案。

Jack studied the zebras, trying to see their different patterns. But in the hazy afternoon light, all the stripes made him dizzy. 杰克仔细观察斑马，想看出它们不同的花纹。 可在朦胧的午后光线里，那些条纹看得他头晕。

He blinked to clear his head, then read more: 他眨了眨眼让脑子清醒过来，接着往下读：

Zebras are the first to cross the river because they eat the coarsest grass. After they’ve thinned down the top layer, the wildebeests arrive and eat the next layer. They prepare the grass for the gazelles, who come last. 斑马第一个过河，因为它们吃最粗糙的草。它们吃掉最上面一层后，角马就来了，吃下一层。它们为羚羊准备了草地，羚羊最后才来。

Wow, thought Jack. Each animal depends on the one that goes before. 哇，杰克想。 每一种动物都要依靠走在前面的那一种。

He wrote: 他写道：

图中文字：animals all connected 图中文字：所有动物都连在一起

Jack heard Annie shouting from the riverbank. “Jump, beasts! Jump! You can do it! Don’t be afraid! Come on!” 杰克听见安妮从河岸边喊叫。 “跳啊，野兽们！跳啊！ 你们能行的！别害怕！ 来吧！”

He looked up. Annie herself was jumping as she called to the wildebeests. 他抬起头。 安妮自己一边跳一边向角马们喊着。

Jack sighed. I’d better stop her before there’s trouble, he thought. 杰克叹了口气。 我最好在出麻烦之前拦住她，他想。

He put away the Africa book and his notebook. Then he jogged toward the river. His pack was heavy and lumpy, bumping against his back. He’d forgotten to take out the jar of peanut butter and the loaf of bread. 他把非洲的书和笔记本收起来。 接着他朝河边跑去。 他的背包又重又不平整，一下下撞着他的背。 他忘了把那罐花生酱和那条面包拿出来。

Jack decided to leave them at the tree house. He turned to go back. 杰克决定把它们留在树屋里。 他转身往回走。

But just then, Annie’s shouting stopped. 可就在这时，安妮的喊声停了。

Jack looked at the river. 杰克看向河。

She had vanished. 她不见了。

“Annie?” he called. “安妮？”他喊道。

No answer. 没有回答。

Where was she? 她在哪儿？

“Annie!” Jack shouted. “安妮！”杰克喊道。

She had completely disappeared. 她已经彻底消失了。

“Oh, man,” said Jack. “哦，天哪，”杰克说。

Their trip had barely begun, and already disaster had struck! 他们的旅程才刚刚开始，灾难就已经降临了！

He forgot about the stuff in his pack. He just ran as fast as he could. 他顾不上背包里的东西。 他只是拼命往前跑。

He wove his way between the grazing zebras and giraffes as he raced to the river. 他在吃草的斑马和长颈鹿中间穿来穿去，朝河边跑去。

“Help!” called Annie. “救命！”安妮喊道。
