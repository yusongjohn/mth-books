1. Before Lunch 1. 午饭前

Jack and Annie were walking home from the grocery store. Jack’s pack was heavy. It held a big jar of peanut butter and a loaf of bread. 杰克和安妮正从杂货店往家走。 杰克的背包很重。 里面装着一大罐花生酱和一条面包。

“Are you going to have a peanut butter and jelly sandwich?” said Annie. “Or a peanut butter and honey sandwich?” “你要吃花生酱果酱三明治吗？”安妮说。 “还是花生酱蜂蜜三明治？”

Jack started to answer, but stopped. 杰克正要回答，却停住了。

“Oh, man,” he whispered. “哦，天哪，”他轻声说。

“What is it?” said Annie. “那是什么？”安妮说。

“Look at that!” said Jack. “快看那个！”杰克说。

He pointed to the edge of the Frog Creek woods. In the shadows stood a small, delicate animal. It looked like a tiny deer. 他指着蛙溪树林的边缘。 阴影里站着一只小巧玲珑的动物。 它看起来像一只很小的鹿。

“It’s a sign,” whispered Annie. “Remember when we saw the rabbit? He was a sign of the Wild West.” “这是个征兆。”安妮低声说。 “还记得我们看见那只兔子吗？ 它是狂野西部的征兆。”

The deerlike creature leaped into the woods. 那只像鹿一样的小动物跳进了树林。

Jack and Annie didn’t stop to think. They followed as fast as they could. Jack’s heavy pack thumped against his back as he ran. 杰克和安妮想都没想。 他们拼命跟着往前跑。 杰克跑步时，沉重的背包一下下撞在他背上。

Finally, they stopped and looked around. 终于，他们停下来四处张望。

“Where did she go?” he said. “她去哪儿了？”他说。

“I don’t see her,” said Annie. “我看不见她，”安妮说。

“Oh, wow,” said Annie. She pointed up. “哦，哇，”安妮说。她向上指着。

There was the magic tree house. It was shining in the noon sun, at the top of the tallest tree in the woods. Its rope ladder swayed in the shadows below. 魔法树屋就在那儿。 它在正午的阳光下闪闪发亮，就在树林里最高的那棵树顶上。 绳梯在下面的阴影里轻轻摇晃。

“Where’s Morgan?” said Annie. “摩根在哪儿？”安妮说。

Morgan le Fay wasn’t waving at them from the window. She wasn’t even at the window. 摩根·勒菲没有在窗口朝他们挥手。 她甚至不在窗口。

“I don’t know. Let’s go up,” said Jack. “我不知道。 我们上去吧，”杰克说。

They climbed the ladder and went into the tree house. 他们爬上梯子，走进树屋。

Sunlight streamed through the window. It lit a stack of books and two scrolls in the corner. The ancient scrolls held the answers to riddles Jack and Annie had solved earlier. 阳光从窗口泻了进来。 阳光照亮了角落里的一摞书和两卷卷轴。 那些古老的卷轴里，藏着杰克和安妮先前解开的谜底。

Jack took off his heavy pack. 杰克卸下沉重的背包。

“Did Morgan leave us a third riddle?” said Annie. “摩根有没有给我们留下第三道谜题？”安妮说。

“Looking for someone?” said a soft voice. “在找谁吗？”一个轻柔的声音说。

Jack and Annie whirled around. 杰克和安妮猛地转过身。

“Morgan!” said Annie. “摩根！”安妮说。

Morgan le Fay had appeared out of nowhere. She looked ancient and lovely in the bright light. 摩根·勒菲突然出现了。 在明亮的光线里，她显得既古老又和蔼。

“Do you still want to become Master Librarians?” she asked Jack and Annie. “So you can help me in my work?” “你们还想成为大师级图书管理员吗？”她问杰克和安妮。 “这样你们就能帮我工作了？”

“Yes!” they said together. “想！”他们一起说。

“Wonderful,” Morgan said. Then she reached into her robe and pulled out a scroll. “太好了，”摩根说。 然后她把手伸进长袍，抽出一卷卷轴。

“You’ve solved two riddles so far,” she said. “Here is your third.” She handed the scroll to Annie. “And for your research—” “到目前为止，你们已经解开了两道谜题。”她说。 “这是你们的第三个。”她把卷轴递给安妮。 “还有给你们做研究用的——”

She pulled a book out from her robe and handed it to Jack. The book’s cover said THE PLAINS OF AFRICA. 她从长袍里抽出一本书，递给杰克。 书的封面上写着《非洲草原》。

“Africa?” said Jack. “Oh, man, I’ve always wanted to go there.” “非洲？”杰克说。 “哦，天哪，我一直想去那儿。”

He opened the book. He and Annie stared at a picture. 他打开书。 他和安妮盯着一幅图画。

It showed hordes of zebras, tall giraffes, big animals with horns, and tiny, deerlike creatures. 上面画着成群的斑马、高高的长颈鹿、长着角的大动物，还有小小的、像鹿一样的动物。

“Hey, that’s the animal that led us here!” said Annie. “嘿，那就是带我们来这儿的那只动物！”安妮说。

“A Thomson’s gazelle, I believe,” Morgan said. “我想是汤姆森瞪羚。”摩根说。

“Where are the lions?” said Jack. “狮子在哪儿？”杰克说。

“You’ll find out,” said Morgan. “你们会知道的。”摩根说。

“Um … maybe we need to plan this trip,” said Jack. “嗯……也许我们得先计划一下这次旅行，”杰克说。

Morgan smiled. “No. Go ahead. Make your wish now.” 摩根笑了。 “不用。去吧。 现在许愿吧。”

Annie pointed at the picture. “I wish we could go there,” she said. 安妮指着那幅画。 “我希望我们能去那儿，”她说。

“Be careful,” said Morgan. “Just keep an eye out.” “小心点，”摩根说。 “只要留心看着就行。”

“For what?” said Jack. “小心什么？”杰克说。

“The lions, of course,” she said. “当然是狮子。”她说。

“Wait!” said Jack. “等等！”杰克说。

Too late. 太晚了。

The wind had started to blow. 风已经开始刮起来。

The tree house had started to spin. 树屋已经开始旋转。

Jack squeezed his eyes shut. 杰克紧紧闭上眼睛。

The tree house spun faster and faster. 树屋转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 绝对静止。
