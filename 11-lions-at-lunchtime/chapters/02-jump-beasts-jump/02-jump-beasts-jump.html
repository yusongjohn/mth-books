2. Jump, Beasts! Jump! 2. 跳啊，野兽们！跳啊！

Bright light flooded the tree house again. A rustling sound came from outside the window. 明亮的光线再次洒满了树屋。 窗外传来一阵沙沙的声音。

Annie peeked out and laughed. “Hey, there,” she said. 安妮向外偷看，笑了。 “嘿，你好，”她说。

Jack looked out, too. A giraffe was eating leaves off the tree. It had a sweet, goofy face. 杰克也向外看。 一只长颈鹿正在树上吃叶子。 它有一张可爱又傻乎乎的脸。

Jack peered at the world beyond the giraffe. He couldn’t believe his eyes. 杰克朝长颈鹿身后的世界望去。 他简直不敢相信自己的眼睛。

He saw a huge grassy plain, a wide river, and tons of birds and animals—more than he had ever imagined in one place. 他看见一片辽阔的草原、一条宽阔的河，还有好多好多鸟和动物——比他想象过的还要多。

Giraffes and zebras were on the side of the river where Jack and Annie were. Thomson’s gazelles and the big horned animals were on the other side. 长颈鹿和斑马就在杰克和安妮这边的河岸上。 汤姆森瞪羚和那些长着角的大动物在河对岸。

“Where are the lions?” said Jack. “狮子在哪儿？”杰克说。

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “Do you think it’s always this crowded?” “我不知道，”安妮说。 “你觉得这儿总是这么挤吗？”

“Let’s find out,” said Jack. “我们去弄清楚，”杰克说。

He picked up the book on Africa and looked at the picture of the animals. He read aloud: 他拿起那本关于非洲的书，看着动物的图画，大声念道：

Every year, in late spring, thousands of zebras and gazelles and millions of wildebeests (WILL-duh-beests) migrate from the dry plains of Tanzania to Kenya. 每年晚春，成千上万的斑马、羚羊和数百万的角马（WILL-duh-beests）从坦桑尼亚的干草原迁徙到肯尼亚。

“What’s ‘migrate’ mean?” said Annie. “‘迁徙’是什么意思？”安妮说。

Jack pushed his glasses into place. “It means they go someplace else for part of the year—like birds going south for the winter.” 杰克推了推眼镜。 “意思是它们一年中有一段时间会去别的地方——就像鸟儿冬天飞到南方一样。”

“Oh, right,” said Annie. “哦，对，”安妮说。

Jack turned the page to read more. 杰克翻过一页，继续读。

Before they are safe in Kenya, the animals must first cross the Mara River. Zebras go first, then the wildebeests. The tiny gazelles swim last. 在它们安全到达肯尼亚之前，动物们必须先穿越马拉河。斑马先过河，接着是角马，最后是小羚羊游泳过河。

“Ohh,” said Annie in a sad voice. “哦，”安妮难过地说。

“What’s wrong?” said Jack. “怎么了？”杰克说。

“Poor beasts.” She looked out the window. “They seem afraid.” “可怜的野兽。”她向窗外看。 “它们好像很害怕。”

On the far side of the river, the horned animals were standing at the edge of the steep bank of the river. They stared down nervously at the rushing water. 在河的远端，那些长角的动物正站在陡峭的河岸边上。 它们紧张地低头盯着湍急的河水。

“Jump, beasts! Jump!” Annie shouted. “跳啊，野兽们！ 跳啊！”安妮喊道。

“Don’t be silly. They can’t hear you,” said Jack. “别傻了。 它们听不见你，”杰克说。

He studied the broad plain. “I wonder where the lions are,” he said. 他仔细打量着那片广阔的平原。 “我想知道狮子在哪儿，”他说。

“I don’t know. But I have to go,” said Annie. “我不知道。 可我得走了，”安妮说。

“Go where?” said Jack. “去哪儿？”杰克说。

“To the river to help them,” she said. “去河边帮它们，”她说。

“Help who?” said Jack. “帮谁？”杰克说。

“Those wild beasts on the other side!” said Annie. “I have to help them migrate.” “对岸那些野兽啊！”安妮说。 “我得帮它们迁徙。”

“Are you nuts?” said Jack. “你疯了吗？”杰克说。

Annie handed Jack the scroll and started out of the tree house. 安妮把卷轴递给杰克，开始往树屋外走。

“Wait a second!” said Jack. “We haven’t even read Morgan’s riddle yet!” “等一下！”杰克说。 “我们连摩根的谜语都还没读呢！”

Annie stopped on the ladder. 安妮在梯子上停住了。

“Read it now,” she said. “现在读，”她说。

Jack unrolled the ancient scroll and read aloud: 杰克展开古老的卷轴，大声念道：

I’m the color of gold and as sweet as can be. But beware of the danger that’s all around me. What am I? 我是金色的，而且非常甜。但要小心我周围可能存在的危险。我是什么？

Annie started down again. 安妮又开始往下爬。

“Annie!” “安妮！”

“We’ll look for the answer in a minute,” said Annie. “我们一会儿再去找答案。”安妮说。

“What are you doing?” Jack called. “你在干什么？”杰克喊道。

But there was no stopping her. Jack watched as she hopped off the ladder. Then she started to walk through the tall grass, between the zebras and giraffes. 可已经拦不住她了。 杰克看着她跳下梯子。 接着她开始穿过高高的草丛，走在斑马和长颈鹿中间。

“I don’t believe her,” he said to himself. He quickly put the Africa book into his pack. “真不敢相信她，”他自言自语。 他赶紧把非洲的书放进背包。

He started down the ladder. 他开始往下爬梯子。

When he stepped onto the ground, he looked around carefully. 脚一落地，他就小心地四处看了看。

The giraffes were eating the tree leaves. 长颈鹿正在吃树叶。

The zebras were grazing in the grass. 斑马正在草地上吃草。

Tons of birds flapped overhead. 无数鸟儿在头顶上扑打着翅膀。

This is okay, he thought. He just had one little question: 还好，他想。 他只剩一个小问题：

Where are the lions? 狮子在哪儿？
