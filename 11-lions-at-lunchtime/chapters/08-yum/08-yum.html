8. Yum 8. 好吃

The warrior stared back at Jack and Annie. 战士也盯着杰克和安妮。

“We were helping one of your honey guides,” said Annie. “我们在帮你们的一只蜂蜜向导，”安妮说。

The warrior was as still as a statue. 那位勇士像雕像一样一动不动。

“We didn’t mean to steal anything,” Jack said. “In fact, it’s all yours. We’ve had enough.” “我们不是故意要偷东西的。”杰克说。 “其实，都归你了。 我们已经吃够了。”

“Lots of good honey still there,” Annie said, smiling. “那儿还有好多好吃的蜂蜜。”安妮笑着说。

The warrior narrowed his eyes. 勇士眯起了眼睛。

Is he angry? Jack wondered. 他生气了吗？杰克想。

“I’m sorry we were trespassing,” said Jack. “We come in peace. In fact, we bring gifts.” He picked up his backpack and held it out to the warrior. “对不起，我们擅自闯进来了。”杰克说。 “我们是来和平相处的。 “其实，我们还带了礼物。”他拿起背包，递给那位勇士。

The warrior still didn’t move. 战士还是一动不动。

“This?” Jack held up his book. “这个？”杰克举起他的书。

Nothing. 没有反应。

“Uh—” Jack reached into his pack. He pulled out the big jar of peanut butter. “呃——”杰克把手伸进背包。 他拿出那一大罐花生酱。

“Peanut butter!” He pulled out the loaf of bread. “Bread! Hey. Hey! How about a peanut butter and honey sandwich?” “花生酱！”他又拿出那条面包。 “面包！嘿。 嘿！ “来个花生酱蜂蜜三明治怎么样？”

“Yum!” Annie said, watching the warrior. “好吃！”安妮说，看着战士。

The warrior stared at the food. 战士盯着食物。

“We’ll show you,” said Jack. “我们演示给你看，”杰克说。

As Jack unwrapped the bread, his hands shook. 杰克拆开面包包装时，手在发抖。

Annie opened the jar. 安妮打开罐子。

“We don’t have anything to spread it with,” she said. “我们没有东西可以用来涂。”她说。

“Use your fingers,” said Jack. “用手指，”杰克说。

“Excuse me,” Annie said to the warrior. “I have to use my fingers. But they’re pretty clean. An elephant just—” “对不起。”安妮对勇士说。 “我得用手指。 不过挺干净的。 一头大象刚——”

“Just do it, Annie!” said Jack. “快弄吧，安妮！”杰克说。

“Okay, okay.” “好好好。”

She spread the peanut butter onto a slice of bread with her fingers. At the same time, Jack spread the honey from the beehive on another slice. 她用手指把花生酱涂在一片面包上。 同时，杰克把蜂巢里的蜂蜜涂在另一片面包上。

Jack and Annie put their pieces of bread together. 杰克和安妮把两片面包合在一起。

“Ta-da!” said Annie, handing the sandwich to the warrior. “铛铛！”安妮说着，把三明治递给勇士。

The warrior took the sandwich, but he didn’t eat it. He just looked at it. 战士接过三明治，可没有吃。 他只是看着它。

“Let’s make sandwiches for us, too,” Jack said. “So he doesn’t have to eat alone.” “我们也给自己做三明治吧，”杰克说。 “这样他就不用一个人吃了。”

They quickly made two more sandwiches. 他们很快又做了两个三明治。

“See, like this,” said Annie. She bit into her sandwich. “Mmm … yum.” “看，像这样，”安妮说。 她咬了一口自己的三明治。 “嗯……好吃。”

Jack took a bite, too. “Mmm … ” he said. It was really good. 杰克也咬了一口。 “嗯……”他说。 真的很好吃。

Finally, the warrior bit into his sandwich. He chewed slowly. 终于，勇士咬了一口三明治。他慢慢地咀嚼着。

“This is called a picnic,” said Annie. “这叫野餐。”安妮说。

They ate their sandwiches in silence. 他们静静地吃着三明治。

When they finished, Jack screwed the lid back on the peanut butter jar. 吃完后，杰克把花生酱罐子的盖子拧了回去。

“Not bad, huh?” he said. “不错吧？”他说。

The warrior smiled. He had a kind, dignified smile. 战士笑了。 他露出和善而有尊严的微笑。

Jack and Annie smiled back at him. 杰克和安妮也朝他笑。

Then the warrior turned gracefully and vanished into the trees. 接着勇士优雅地转过身，消失在树林里。

“Oh, man,” said Jack. Part of him wanted to follow the silent warrior through the shadowy forest. “哦，天哪，”杰克说。 他心里有一部分想跟着这位沉默的勇士走进阴暗的树林。

“Ready?” Annie asked softly. “准备好了吗？”安妮轻声问。

Jack nodded. 杰克点点头。

Annie started to go. 安妮开始走。

“Wait,” said Jack. He put away the peanut butter and bread. “We’re going back to the tree house, right? We’re not going to do anything silly, like rescue anything or chase birds. Right?” “等等，”杰克说。 他把花生酱和面包收起来。 “我们要回树屋了，对吧？ “我们不会再去做傻事了，比如救人或者追鸟。对吧？”

“Those things aren’t silly,” said Annie. “Don’t forget that the bird gave us the answer to the riddle.” “那些东西一点也不傻，”安妮说。 “别忘了，是那只鸟给了我们谜语的答案。”

“Oh. Right,” said Jack. 对，”杰克说。

He looked at the little honey guide. She was on the ground, pecking at the honeycomb. 他看着那只小蜂蜜向导。 她在地上啄着蜂巢。

“Thanks,” Jack said to the bird. “谢谢，”杰克对鸟说。

“Have a good feast,” said Annie. “好好享用大餐吧，”安妮说。

Jack put on his pack. Then he and Annie started out of the forest. 杰克背上背包。 然后他和安妮开始走出森林。

When they passed the pond, they saw the elephant still splashing in the water. He lifted his trunk. He seemed to be waving at them. 经过池塘时，他们看见大象还在水里溅水玩。 它举起了鼻子。 它好像在向他们挥手。

“See ya!” Annie shouted, waving back. “再见！”安妮喊着，也朝它挥手。

They rounded the bend in the river, then started through the tall grass. 他们绕过河流的弯道，接着走进高高的草丛。

As they walked back toward the tree house, they saw the wildebeests in the distance. There were still some crossing the river. 他们往树屋走回去时，看见远处的角马群。 还有一些正在过河。

They saw a family of zebras grazing together. 他们看见一家斑马在一起吃草。

They saw lone giraffes walking from tree to tree, eating the leaves. 他们看见孤独的长颈鹿从一棵树走到另一棵树，吃着叶子。

And they saw a bunch of lions sleeping in the shade of a tree—the same tree that the tree house was in. 他们还看见一群狮子在一棵树荫下睡觉——正是树屋所在的那棵树。

“Whoops,” said Annie. “哎呀，”安妮说。

Jack’s heart gave a jump. 杰克的心猛地跳了一下。

“So there they are,” he said. “原来它们在那儿，”他说。
