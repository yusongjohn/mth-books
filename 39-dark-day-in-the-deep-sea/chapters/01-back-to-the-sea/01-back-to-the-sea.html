1. Back to the Sea 1. 返回大海

Jack felt raindrops. He looked up and saw a summer storm cloud. 他抬头一看，看到一片夏天的暴风雨云。

“Hurry!” Jack called to Annie. They were riding their bikes home from the library. Jack’s backpack was filled with library books. He didn’t want them to get wet. “快点！”杰克对安妮喊道。 他们正骑着自行车从图书馆回家。 杰克的背包里装满了图书馆的书。 他不想让他们淋湿。

As Jack and Annie pedaled faster, a large white bird swooped over them and flew into the Frog Creek woods. 杰克和安妮骑得更快时，一只大白鸟俯冲下来，飞进了蛙溪镇的树林里。

“Did you see that?” cried Jack. “你看到那鸟了吗？”杰克喊道。

“A seagull!” called Annie. “It’s a sign!” “是海鸥！”安妮喊道。 “这是个信号！”

“You’re right!” said Jack. The last time they’d seen a seagull in Frog Creek, the magic tree house was waiting for them! “你说得对！”杰克说。 上次他们在蛙溪镇看到海鸥时，神奇的树屋正等着他们！

“The woods!” said Annie. “是树林！”安妮说。

Jack and Annie bumped their bikes over the curb. The rain fell harder as they headed into the wet woods. Their bike tires bounced over the rough ground, crushing leaves and snapping twigs. 杰克和安妮骑着自行车冲过了路沿。 他们往湿漉漉的树林里骑去时，雨下得更大了。 他们的自行车轮胎在粗糙的地面上弹跳着，压碎了树叶，折断了树枝。

“It must be time to look for another secret of happiness for Merlin!” Jack called. “现在该为梅林寻找另一个幸福的秘密了！”杰克喊道。

“I hope Merlin’s feeling better!” shouted Annie. “希望梅林感觉好些了！”安妮喊道。

“I hope Teddy and Kathleen came with the tree house!” shouted Jack. “希望泰迪和凯瑟琳带着树屋一起来了！”杰克喊道。

“Me too!” shouted Annie. “我也是！”安妮喊道。

Jack and Annie steered their bikes under canopies of wet leaves. By the time they came to the tallest oak in the woods, the seagull had disappeared. But the magic tree house was back! It was high in the tallest oak, its rope ladder swaying in the wind and rain. 杰克和安妮骑着自行车，从湿漉漉的树叶形成的树冠下穿过。 等他们来到森林里最高的橡树时，海鸥已经不见了。 但是神奇的树屋又回来了！ 它高高挂在最高的橡树上，绳梯在风和雨中摇晃着。

Jack and Annie climbed off their bikes and propped them against the trunk of the tree. 杰克和安妮从自行车上跳下来，把自行车靠在树干旁边。

“Teddy! Kathleen!” Annie shouted. “泰迪!” “凯瑟琳！”安妮喊道。

There was no answer. 没有人回答。

“I guess they didn’t come this time,” Jack said. “我想他们这次没来，”杰克说。

“Darn!” said Annie. “I really wanted to see them.” “真糟糕！”安妮说。 “我真的好想看看他们。”

“Boo!” Two older kids looked down out of the tree house window: a curly-haired boy with a big grin and a girl with sea-blue eyes and a beautiful smile. Both were wearing long green cloaks. “嘘——！” 两个年纪较大的孩子从树屋的窗户探出头来：一个有着卷发、咧着嘴笑的男孩，还有一个眼睛是海蓝色、笑容美丽的女孩。 他们两个人都穿着长长的绿色斗篷。

“Yay!” cried Annie and Jack. “太棒了！”安妮和杰克喊道。

The rain fell harder as they started up the rope ladder. When they climbed into the tree house, they yanked off their bike helmets and hugged Teddy and Kathleen. 他们开始爬绳梯时，雨下得更大了。 他们爬进树屋时，摘下了自行车头盔，然后拥抱了泰迪和凯瑟琳。

“Morgan sent us to tell you about your next mission for Merlin,” said Teddy. “摩根派我们来告诉你们，梅林给你们的下一个任务，”泰迪说。

“How is Merlin?” asked Annie. “梅林怎么样了？”安妮问。

Teddy stopped smiling. He shook his head. 泰迪停下了微笑。 他摇了摇头。

“Merlin still suffers from an unspoken sorrow,” Kathleen said sadly. “梅林仍然承受着一种说不出的悲伤，”凯瑟琳难过地说。

“When can we see him?” asked Annie. “我们什么时候能见他？”安妮问。

“We’ve learned two secrets of happiness to share with him,” said Jack. “我们已经学会了两个关于幸福的秘密，可以和他分享，”杰克说。

“You may visit him after you have learned two more secrets,” said Kathleen. “Morgan believes four is the magic number that will ensure success.” “你们学会了两个更多的秘密之后，才可以去看他，”凯瑟琳说。 “摩根相信，数字四是个能确保成功的魔法数字。”

“We have come to send you on your search for a third secret,” said Teddy. “我们是来帮你寻找第三个秘密的，”泰迪说。

Kathleen took a book from under her cloak and handed it to Jack and Annie. “From Morgan’s hands to our hands to yours,” she said. 凯瑟琳从斗篷下拿出一本书，递给杰克和安妮。 “从摩根的手中，到我们的手中，再到你的手中，”她说。

Jack took the book from her. The cover showed waves crashing on a beach. 杰克从她手中接过书。 封面显示着海浪在海滩上冲刷。

图中文字：The Deep Sea 图中文字：深海

“Wow,” said Jack. “We’re going to the ocean?” “哇，”杰克说。 “我们要去海洋吗？”

“Yes,” said Teddy. “That is where you will next search for a secret of happiness.” “是的，”泰迪说。 “你接下来要在那儿寻找幸福的秘密。”

“The ocean always makes me happy,” said Annie. “Once Jack and I traveled to a coral reef and swam with dolphins. And we ran into an octopus. But he was nice and shy and—” “大海总是让我感到快乐，”安妮说。 “杰克和我曾经去过一个珊瑚礁，还和海豚一起游泳了。” 我们还遇到了一只章鱼。 但他很友善，也很害羞，——”

“But the shark we saw wasn’ t shy,” Jack broke in. “It was a big hammerhead.” “但我们看到的鲨鱼可一点都不害羞，”杰克插话道。 “那是一只大头鲨。”

“Oh, my,” said Kathleen. “哦，天哪，”凯瑟琳说道。

“We took a ride in a mini-sub,” said Annie. “It was so cool!” “我们还坐了一艘迷你潜水艇，”安妮说道。 “太酷了！”

“Until it started to leak and—” said Jack. “直到它开始漏水，——”杰克说道。

“We had to escape!” said Annie. “我们不得不逃出去！”安妮说道。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “We tried not to splash—so the shark wouldn’t notice us.” “是的，”杰克说。 “我们尽量不溅水——这样鲨鱼就不会注意到我们了。”

“We had so much fun!” said Annie. “我们玩得可开心了！”安妮说。

Kathleen smiled. “Well, I hope you will not find the same ‘fun’ on this journey,” she said. 凯瑟琳微笑了。 “好吧，我希望你们在这次旅程中不会遇到同样的‘乐趣’，”她说。

“But in case you do, you have your wand to help you, do you not?” asked Teddy. “The Wand of Dianthus?” “但万一你们遇到了，你们还有魔杖可以帮忙，对吧？”泰迪问。 “迪安瑟斯的魔杖？”

“Of course,” said Jack. “I always carry it, just in case.” Jack reached into his backpack and pulled out the silvery wand. It was shaped like the spiraled horn of a unicorn. “当然，”杰克说。 “我总是随身带着它，以防万一。”杰克伸手到他的背包里，拿出那根银色的魔杖。 它形状像独角兽螺旋状的角。

“You remember the three rules?” asked Kathleen. “你还记得那三条规则吗？”凯瑟琳问。

“Sure,” said Jack. “To make magic, we use a wish with only five words.” “当然记得，”杰克说。 “要创造魔法，我们要用只有五个字的愿望。”

“And before we use the wand, we have to try our hardest,” said Annie. “在使用魔杖之前，我们必须尽自己最大的努力，”安妮说。

“And the wand can only be used for the good of others,” said Jack, “not just ourselves.” “而且魔杖只能用来帮助别人，”杰克说，“而不是只为了我们自己。”

“Exactly,” said Teddy. “没错，”泰迪说。

“I wonder who the ‘others’ will be on this mission,” said Annie. She looked at Teddy and Kathleen. “Maybe you guys?” “我好奇这次任务中的‘其他人’会是谁，”安妮说。 她看着泰迪和凯瑟琳。 “也许是你们俩？”

“I fear not,” said Kathleen. “You must find the third secret on your own.” “我担心不是，”凯瑟琳说。 “你们必须自己找到第三个秘密。”

“Just remember to keep your wits about you,” said Teddy. “记住，要保持头脑清醒，”泰迪说。

“And listen to your hearts,” said Kathleen. “还要倾听你们的心，”凯瑟琳说。

“Okay,” said Annie. “We’ll tell you all about it when we see you again.” “好的，”安妮说。 “等我们再见到你们时，我们会告诉你们一切。”

Lightning flashed through the woods as Jack pointed to the cover of the ocean book. “I wish we could go there!” he said. 闪电划过森林，杰克指着海洋书的封面。 “真希望我们能去那里！”他说。

Thunder cracked in the dark sky. The wind blew harder. 雷声在漆黑的天空中炸响。 风刮得更猛了。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后，一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
