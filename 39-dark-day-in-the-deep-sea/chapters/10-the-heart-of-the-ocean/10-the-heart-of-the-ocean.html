10. The Heart of the Ocean 10. 海洋之心

Jack put the wand back in his pack. Then he and Annie charged out of the lab, with Henry behind them. 杰克把魔杖放回了他的背包里。 然后他和安妮冲出了实验室，亨利跟在他们后面。

The three of them climbed back up the stairs. When they stepped onto the top deck, they heard the sailors still shouting and yelling. Jack and Annie raced across the deck. They pushed through the crowd until they got to the railing. 他们三个爬上了楼梯。 当他们踏上顶层甲板时，他们听到水手们还在大声喊叫。 杰克和安妮在甲板上飞快地跑着。 他们穿过人群，直到到达栏杆边。

The octopus was still in the net, bright red and hiding his head in fear. The sailors had gathered weapons. Some were aiming harpoons. Others stood by with knives. Some pointed pistols. 章鱼还在网里，亮红色的，吓得把头藏了起来。 水手们已经拿起了武器。 有些人正在瞄准鱼叉。 其他人拿着刀站在一旁。 有些人举起了手枪。

“Stop!” cried Annie. “停住！”安妮喊道。

“Stand back!” the captain yelled at her. “退后！”船长朝她大喊。

“You must bring it up alive!” begged the professor. “I beg you to let me examine it before you slay it, Captain!” “你必须把它活着带上来！”教授恳求道。 “我恳求你，在你杀死它之前让我先检查它，船长！”

But before the captain could answer, someone hurled a harpoon at the creature. It missed and landed in the water with a splash. 但船长还没来得及回答，有人朝那个生物投掷了一支鱼叉。 鱼叉没命中，掉进水里溅起了一朵水花。

“No!” yelled Annie. “不！”安妮喊道。

“Listen to us!” Jack yelled in his loudest voice. “You have to hear the truth!” He stood up on the bottom rung of the ship’s railing so everyone could hear him. “Don’t touch him!” he shouted. “He’s not going to hurt you! He deserves to live peacefully in the sea! He’s a miracle of nature!” “听我们说！”杰克用他最大的声音喊道。 “你们必须听我说实话！”他站到船舷底部的横栏上，让所有人都能听见他的话。 “别碰他！”他大喊。 “他不会伤害你的！” 他应该在海里平静地生活！ 他真是自然的奇迹！“

But no one was listening to Jack. No one even turned to look at him. 但没有人听杰克说话。 甚至没有人转过头看他。

“Stop! Hear us!” said Annie. “We speak the truth! Leave him alone!” “停！“ 听我们吧！”安妮说道。 “我们说的是实话！别碰他！”

“Remove the children!” the captain roared again. “Now!” “把孩子们带走！”船长再次吼道。 “现在！”

Tommy and Joe grabbed Jack and Annie. 汤米和乔抓住了杰克和安妮。

“Let us go! We have to talk to them!” yelled Annie, kicking and flailing. She and Jack broke loose from the sailors. “我们走吧！ 我们必须和他们谈谈！”安妮一边踢腿一边挣扎着喊道。 她和杰克从水手们手里挣脱了出来。

“She’s right! They have to hear the truth!” yelled Jack, running toward the railing. “He’s okay where he is! He’s different from people, but he’s a miracle!” “她说得对！ 他们必须听到真相！”杰克一边朝栏杆跑去一边喊道。 “他现在待的地方很好！ 他和普通人不一样，但他是个奇迹！”，

Again, no one seemed to hear him. 可是，似乎没有人听得到他的话。

“The magic’s not working!” Annie cried to Jack. “魔法不管用了！”安妮对杰克喊道。

“I know!” said Jack. He was desperate. No one was hearing the truth! What was wrong? He and Annie had followed the rules: They were trying to help others, not themselves. They had tried their hardest first. And Jack had used only five words. “我知道了！”杰克说。他很着急。 没有人听到真相！怎么了？ 他和安妮已经遵守了规则：他们是在帮助别人，而不是自己。 他们已经尽了最大的努力。 而且杰克只用了五个词。

“Look!” a sailor shrieked. “Look at the monster!” “看！”水手尖叫道。 “看那只怪物！”

Jack and Annie looked back at the water. 杰克和安妮回头看向水里。

The giant octopus had uncovered his head and raised it out of the water. “LEHHHHHHHH!” The sound was breathy and whispery, as if it came from the wind. 那只巨大的章鱼露出了头，并把它从水里抬了起来。 “LEHHHHHHHH！”声音又喘又轻，好像来自风中。

The members of the crew lowered their weapons and stepped back in awe. “It—it speaks!” shouted a sailor. 船员们放下武器，惊讶地后退一步。 “它——它会说话！”一名水手喊道。

“MEEEEEEEEEEE!” The sound was wild and shrill, as if it came from a deep forest. “MEEEEEEEEEEE！”声音又野又尖，好像来自一片深林。

“GOOOOOOOOO!” The sound was hollow and haunted, as if it came from an ancient gong or bell or drum. “GOOOOOOOOO！”声音空洞而阴森，好像来自一个古老的锣、钟或鼓。

“HOOOOOOOOME!” The sound was unbearably sad, as if it came from the heart of the ocean. “HOOOOOOOOME！”声音令人难以忍受地悲伤，好像来自海洋的深处。

“IMMMMMNOOOOOMONNNSTERRR!” The octopus fell back into the water. All the seamen and scientists stared in wonder and terror. “IMMMMMNOOOOOMONNNSTERRR!” 章鱼掉回了水里。 所有的水手和科学家都惊讶又害怕地盯着看。

Then Annie’s voice rang through the silence. “Did you hear him? He said, ‘Let me go home. I’m no monster.’” 然后，安妮的声音在寂静中响起。 “你听到他说什么了吗？ 他说：‘让我回家吧。我不是怪物。’”

The crew looked at each other in shock. 船员们惊讶地互相看着。

“That’s what ’e said. I heard it,” one man said. “Heard it with my own ears.” “他说的就是这个。 “我听到了，”一个人说。 “我用我自己的耳朵听到了。”

“I heard it, too!” said another. “我也听到了！”另一个人说。

“Me too!” said another. “我也是！”另一个人说。

“It’s a sign,” said a sailor. “We can’t hurt ’im—” “这是一个信号，”水手说。 “我们不能伤害他——”

“Or any other like ’im!” said another. “或者伤害任何像他一样的人！”另一个人说。

All eyes turned to the captain. He stood still for a moment, staring down at the giant octopus in the net. The captain looked at the professor. The professor seemed stunned. He opened his mouth, but no words came out. 所有人的目光都转向了船长。 他站了一会儿，盯着网里那只巨大的章鱼。 船长看了看教授。 教授看起来很震惊。 他张开嘴，但没有说出话来。

The captain looked at Jack and Annie. “Please hear him,” Annie said. “He told you the truth.” 船长看了看杰克和安妮。 “请听他说，”安妮说。 “他告诉了你们真相。”

The captain stared at the octopus for a long moment. Then he raised his hand. “Free him,” he said softly. 船长盯着那只章鱼看了很久。 然后他举起了手。 “放了他，”他轻声说。

The sailors slowly put down their harpoons, axes, pistols, and ropes. 水手们慢慢地放下了他们的鱼叉、斧头、手枪和绳子。

“Sir, I request permission to cut the creature from the net,” Henry said to the captain. “先生，我请求允许我把这个生物从网里解救出来，”亨利对船长说。

The captain nodded. “Permission granted, Mr. Moseley,” said the captain. 船长点了点头。 “允许了，莫塞利先生。 ”船长说。

“Help me,” Henry said to Jack and Annie. “帮帮我，”亨利对杰克和安妮说。

“Sure!” said Jack. “当然可以！”杰克说。

“We’ll come, too, mate,” said Joe. “我们也会去，伙计，”乔说。

Joe and Tommy hurried after Henry, Jack, and Annie to a row of small boats on the deck. The sailors lowered one of the boats down to the surface of the water. Then Jack, Annie, Joe, Tommy, and Henry all climbed down the ladder and stepped inside. 乔和汤米赶紧跟在亨利、杰克和安妮后面，来到甲板上一排小船上。 水手们把其中一条小船降到水面上。 然后杰克、安妮、乔、汤米和亨利都顺着梯子爬下来，走进了船里。

Everyone else watched from the deck. Joe and Tommy rowed along the side of the ship until they came to the spot where the giant octopus was trapped. The two sailors held the boat steady with their oars. “Grab the net,” Henry said to Jack and Annie. 其他人都从甲板上看热闹。 乔和汤米沿着船边划船，直到他们来到那只巨型章鱼被困的地方。 两名水手用桨稳住小船。 “抓住网子，”亨利对杰克和安妮说。

Jack reached out and grabbed a corner of the net. Annie helped him pull it out of the water. Henry used a big knife to slice through the thick rope strands. 杰克伸出手，抓住了网子的一个角。 安妮帮他把网子从水里拉出来。 亨利用一把大刀切开了粗壮的绳子。

Even though the rowboat rocked violently, Jack didn’t feel sick at all. 即使小船剧烈摇晃，杰克一点也不觉得恶心。

Jack, Annie, and Henry pulled the torn net into the small boat. Jack kept staring at the octopus. He looked back at all of them with his great eyes, his tentacles spread about him like a giant flower. His fierce red color had changed back to gray. 杰克、安妮和亨利把破掉的网子拉进了小船里。 杰克一直盯着那只章鱼。 他用大大的眼睛看着他们，触手张开，就像一朵巨大的花。 他凶猛的红色已经变回了灰色。

“Good-bye, you,” Annie said softly. “Go home now.” “再见了，你，”安妮轻声说。 “现在回家吧。”

The octopus lifted two of his tentacles as if to wave good-bye. Then he slipped under the surface of the water and vanished. 章鱼抬起两条触手，好像在挥手告别。 然后他滑入水下，消失不见了。
