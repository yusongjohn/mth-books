8. Monster of the Deep 8. 深海怪物

Sailors were on the top deck. They were yelling and pointing. 水手们在顶层甲板上。 他们在大声喊叫，还指着什么。

The giant octopus loosened his hold on Jack and Annie. 那只巨大的章鱼松开了抓住杰克和安妮的手。

“The ship!” Jack gurgled before slipping under the water. His head popped back up. “Swim to the ship!” he shouted to Annie. “船！”杰克咕哝着，然后沉入了水下。 他的头又冒了出来。 “游向船！”他对安妮大喊。

Jack and Annie started swimming. The sky was still covered with clouds and the sea was still rough. But arm over arm, they swam until they got close to the ladder on the side of the ship. 杰克和安妮开始游泳。 天空仍然被乌云覆盖，海面依然波涛汹涌。 但是他们手拉着手，一直游到靠近船边的梯子旁边。

“Ahoy!” someone shouted from above. Jack looked up and saw Henry and the professor standing at the top of the ladder. “喂！”上面有人喊道。 杰克抬头一看，发现亨利和教授正站在梯子的顶端。

“Climb up!” yelled Henry. “爬上来！”亨利喊道。

“Hurry, children! Hurry!” cried the professor. “Before it comes back!” “快点，孩子们！ 快点！”教授喊道。 “在它回来之前！”

Jack swam as fast as he could to the ladder. He and Annie got there at the same time. They pulled themselves onto the ladder and climbed. When they reached the top, Henry and the professor helped them onto the deck. Henry wrapped wool blankets around them. 杰克拼命地游向梯子。 他和安妮同时到达了那里。 他们爬上了梯子，开始往上爬。 他们到达顶部时，亨利和教授帮他们上了甲板。 亨利用羊毛毯子裹住他们。

“Thank goodness you are saved!” said the professor. “谢天谢地，你们得救了！”，教授说。

“I thought you were below in the hold!” said Henry. “How did you end up in the water?” “我以为你们在下面的货舱里！”，亨利说。 “你们怎么会掉进水里？”

“The storm … the waves …,” gasped Jack, shivering. “暴风雨……海浪……”，杰克颤抖着说。

“The waves threw us overboard,” said Annie. “海浪把我们掀翻了，”安妮说。

“Why were you up on deck?” said Henry. “你们为什么会在甲板上？”亨利问。

“I was—I was seasick!” said Jack. “我……我晕船了！”，杰克说。

“I followed Jack, and big waves came crashing down and washed us into the water!” said Annie. “我跟着杰克，然后大浪扑过来，把我们冲进了水里！”，安妮说。

“Our life vests saved us at first,” said Jack. “But then they came off!” “我们的救生衣一开始救了我们，”杰克说。 “但是后来它们脱落了！”

“Then the octopus saved us,” said Annie. “然后章鱼救了我们，”安妮说。

“That monster saved you?” asked the professor. “那只怪物救了你们？”教授问。

“No, no, not a monster,” said Jack, “a giant octopus!” “不，不，不是怪物，”杰克说，“是一只巨大的章鱼！”

“Yes, the monster of the deep! I do not think it saved you, boy,” said the professor. “It is a miracle it did not eat you and your sister alive!” “没错，深海的怪物！ 我觉得它并没有救你们，孩子，”教授说。 “我觉得它没把你们和你妹妹吃掉已经是奇迹了！”

“No, no, he’s—he’s not a monster. He didn’t try to do any of that—” said Jack. “不，不，他——他不是怪物。 他根本就没想做那些事——”杰克说。

“He did save us!” said Annie. “He held us up above the water.” “他真的救了我们！”安妮说。 “他把我们举到了水面上。”

“Really!” said Jack. “He kept us from drowning. Then he got scared away by the ship.” “真的吗？”杰克说。 “他救了我们不让我们溺水， 然后他被那艘船吓跑了。”

“We didn’t even thank him, Jack,” said Annie. “We didn’t say good-bye.” “我们连谢谢都没对他说，杰克，”安妮说。 “我们也没跟他说再见。”

Jack and Annie looked back at the water. “Hey, what’s going on over there?” said Annie. She pointed to the rear of the ship. Sailors were facing the sea, yelling and shouting. 杰克和安妮回头看向水面。 “嘿，那边怎么回事？”安妮说。 她指着船的后面。 水手们正面对着大海，大声喊叫着。

“What are they yelling at?” Annie asked. “他们在喊什么呀？”安妮问。

Jack and Annie threw off their blankets and took off across the deck. They saw Joe and Tommy standing at the edge of the crowd. 杰克和安妮掀开毯子，跑过甲板。 他们看到乔和汤米站在人群的边缘。

“What are they doing?” Annie yelled. “What’s going on?” “他们在干什么？”安妮喊道。 “发生了什么事？”

“They snared it in the net!” shouted Tommy. “他们用网子抓住它了！”汤米喊道。

“We’ve caught the beast at last!” shouted Joe. “我们终于抓住那只野兽了！”乔喊道。

“Caught it?” Annie looked at Jack. “抓住了？”安妮看着杰克。

“Come on!” Jack cried. He and Annie pushed their way through the crowd. “快点！”杰克喊道。 他和安妮挤开人群往前走。

“Children, stop!” the professor shouted, hurrying after them. “Don’t get in the way!” “孩子们，停下！”教授喊着，赶紧追了上去。 “别挡道！”

But Jack and Annie squeezed through the crowd of sailors and scientists until they got to the railing of the ship. They looked down. 但杰克和安妮挤过一群水手和科学家，直到到了船边的栏杆旁。他们往下看。

The giant octopus was tangled in a net. His arms were churning the seawater into foam. 那只巨大的章鱼被困在了网里。 它的触手正搅动海水，溅起泡沫。

His body had turned bright red. A cloud of dark ink billowed around him. 它的身体变成了亮红色。 一团黑墨水在它周围飘散开来。

“You’re hurting him!” yelled Annie. “Leave him alone!” “你伤害了它！”安妮喊道。 “别伤害它！”

“Move away!” yelled a sailor. “让开！”水手喊道。

Some of the sailors were yelling mean things at the octopus. Others seemed terrified. Even the captain was caught up in the panic. “Stand back!” he shouted. “It could be powerful enough to sink the ship!” 有些水手正在对章鱼喊一些难听的话。 其他人看起来非常害怕。 就连船长也被恐慌感染了。 “退后！”他喊道。 “它可能强大到能把船弄沉！”

“That’s crazy!” yelled Jack. “Leave him alone!” “这太疯狂了！”杰克喊道。 “别打扰它！”

“Its tentacles are as hard as steel!” shouted one of the sailors. “它的触手硬得像钢铁！”一名水手喊道。

“No, they’re not!” cried Annie. “They’re soft! They saved us from drowning!” “不对，它们不是！”安妮喊道。 “它们很软！” 他们救了我们，让我们免于溺水！

But everyone kept shouting: 但是每个人都一直在喊叫：

“Hungry for human flesh!” “饿得想吃人肉！”，

“Devour a man in one bite!” “一口就能吃掉一个人！”，

“Thirsty for blood!” “渴求鲜血！”

“He’s not any of those things!” yelled Annie. “他可不是那些人之一！”安妮喊道。

“Throw the harpoons!” yelled a sailor. “把鱼叉扔出去！”水手喊道。

“Get your axes, swords, and knives!” yelled another. “拿好斧头、剑和小刀！”另一个人喊道。

“Ready the guns!” said the captain. “准备枪支！”船长说道。

“No!” screamed Annie. “不！”安妮尖叫道。

“No!” roared Jack. “不！”杰克吼道。

“The children are right!” shouted the professor. He and Henry had squeezed through the crowd, too. They were standing behind Jack and Annie. “孩子们说得对！”教授喊道。 他和亨利也挤过了人群。 他们站在杰克和安妮身后。

Jack felt a rush of relief. “The professor says we’re right!” yelled Annie. 杰克感到一阵如释重负。 “教授说我们是对的！”安妮大喊。

“Do not destroy it, Captain!” roared the professor. “Haul it on board!” “不要摧毁它，船长！”教授吼道。 “把它带上船！”

“On board?” said Jack. “带上船？”杰克说道。

“Capture it alive and whole!” said the professor. His eyes gleamed. “So we can study it!” “抓住它，活的，完整的！”教授说。他的眼睛闪着光。 “这样我们就能研究它了！”，

“No, no, let him go,” said Jack. “Just let him swim away and be free!” “不，不，放他走，”杰克说。 “让他游走，获得自由吧！”

“Please!” screamed Annie. “Let him go!” “求求你们！”安妮尖叫道。 “放了他！”

But neither the professor nor the captain seemed to hear Jack and Annie. They were too busy arguing over what to do with the giant octopus. 但教授和船长似乎都没听到杰克和安妮的话。 他们正忙着争论该如何处理那只巨大的章鱼。

“We must kill it, Professor!” said the captain. “It could grab the hull and drag the ship down to the bottom!” “我们必须杀了它，教授！”船长说。 “它可能会抓住船身，把船拖到海底！”

“But science needs it alive!” said the professor. “At least till we have examined it!” “但是科学需要它活着！”教授说。 “至少在我们检查完之前！”

By now the octopus had gathered his long arms around himself, as if for protection. 这时，章鱼已经用他长长的触手把自己围住，好像在保护自己。

Annie burst into tears. “Henry, help him!” she sobbed. “Don’t let them kill him! Or capture him!” 安妮突然哭了起来。 “亨利，帮帮他！”她抽泣着说。 “别让他们杀了他！或者抓住他！”

Henry looked upset, too. “Excuse me, sirs!” he shouted. “Excuse me! The girl is right! We should let him go!” 亨利看起来也很难过。 “打扰一下，先生们！”他喊道。 “打扰一下！ 女孩说得对！ “我们得让他走！”

But no one listened to Henry, either. 但没人听亨利的话。

“Jack!” cried Annie. She grabbed Jack. Tears were streaming down her cheeks. “Save him! We have to save him!” “杰克！”安妮喊道。她抓住了杰克。 泪水从她脸上流下来。 “救他！ 我们必须救他！”

“What can we do? We’ve tried everything!” said Jack. “我们能做什么？ 我们已经试过一切了！”杰克说。

Then he remembered. His own words helped him remember. And Jack knew exactly what to do. 然后他想起了。 他自己的话帮助他想起了。 杰克知道该怎么做。
