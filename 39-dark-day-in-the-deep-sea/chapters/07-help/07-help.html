7. Help! 7. 帮助！

Jack plunged down into the sea. The water was freezing cold. He bobbed back up to the surface, choking and coughing. The life vest kept Jack afloat, but the waves kept hammering him. 杰克沉入了海中。 海水冷得刺骨。 他浮出水面，呛着水，咳嗽着。 救生衣让杰克浮在水面上，但海浪不停地拍打着他的身体。

“Help!” Jack shouted. Thoughts of the twenty-foot tiger shark and the mysterious monster shot through his mind. “Help! Help!” he screamed. “帮助！”杰克喊道。 他脑海里闪过二十英尺长的虎鲨和那个神秘怪物的影子。 “救命！ 救命！”他大喊。

Jack heard someone else screaming, “Help!” It was Annie! She’d been washed off the ship, too! 杰克听到有人在喊：“救命！”是安妮！ 她也被冲下船了！

Jack flailed his arms and kicked his legs, trying to get to Annie. A wave lifted her up and tossed her toward him. But just as Jack reached for her hand, another wave pushed her past him, out of reach. 杰克挥舞着双臂，拼命踢腿，努力游向安妮。 一个浪头把她托起来，又朝他这边扔了过来。 但就在杰克伸手去抓她的时候，另一个浪头把她推到了他身后，再也够不到了。

“The wand, Jack!” Annie shouted. “Use the wand!” “用魔杖，杰克！”安妮喊道。 “用魔杖！”

The wand! Jack thought. The Wand of Dianthus! Where was it? In his backpack! He didn’t have his backpack! Where was his backpack? 那根魔杖！杰克想。 迪安瑟斯的魔杖！它在哪里？ 在他的背包里！ 他没有带他的背包！ 他的背包在哪里？

“Jack! The wand!” Annie yelled. “杰克！ 那根魔杖！”安妮喊道。

“I don’t have it!” Jack shouted. His voice was lost in the roar of the storm. Jack kept flailing and kicking, trying to reach Annie again. “我没有它！”杰克大喊。 他的声音被风暴的咆哮吞没了。 杰克继续挥舞和踢腿，试图再次抓住安妮。

Jack felt his cork life vest coming loose. Another wave crashed over him, and one side of the vest slipped off his arm! 杰克感觉他的救生背心开始松动了。 又一个浪头扑上来，背心的一边从他手臂上滑了下去！

Jack clutched the vest, trying to keep it on. But the waters kept pulling and tugging. The sea itself now seemed like a monster! 杰克紧紧抓住背心，努力不让它掉下去。 但海水一直拉扯着他。 现在，大海看起来就像一个怪物！

As Jack desperately fought the water, he saw another life vest floating free on the waves. It was Annie’s! 杰克拼命和海水搏斗时，他看见另一个救生背心正在海浪上漂浮。那是安妮的！

Where was she? 她在哪里？

“Annie!” Jack screamed. As he looked around, he lost his fight with the sea—a wave washed his vest right off him! Then another wave crashed down on his head and pushed him down into the deep. “安妮！”杰克大喊。 当他四处张望时，他没能继续和大海抗争——一个浪头把他身上的背心直接冲走了！ 接着，另一个浪头扑在他的头上，把他推到了深水里。

Jack held his breath as he plunged through the black water. He thrashed his arms and kicked his legs, trying to get back to the surface. His head popped above the waves, and he took one deep gulp of air before another wave washed over him and he went under again. 杰克屏住呼吸，跳进了漆黑的水里。 他挥舞着双臂，踢动着双腿，努力游回水面。 他的头浮出了水面，他深吸了一口气，但又一个浪头打来，把他再次淹没。

Jack kept kicking and trying to swim, but the fight to get back to the surface was too great. Just when he was about to run out of air, he felt something curl around his waist. Then he felt himself being lifted toward the surface! 杰克继续踢腿，努力游泳，但回到水面的挣扎实在太艰难了。 就在他快要没气的时候，他感觉有什么东西缠住了他的腰。 然后，他感觉有人把他往上托，朝水面拉！

Jack’s head popped above the water again. He opened his mouth and gasped for air. The waves were swirling and foamy, but Jack kept his head above them. Something around his waist was holding him up. 杰克的头再次浮出了水面。 他张开嘴，大口地喘着气。 海浪翻滚着，泡沫飞溅，但杰克一直把头保持在水面上。 缠着他腰的东西还在托着他。

Jack couldn’t think at first. He was trying too hard to breathe. But then he saw something spreading across the water near him. It looked like a giant gray umbrella with dark spots all over it. 杰克一开始想不出办法。 他太用力地想呼吸了。 但然后他看到有什么东西正在他附近的水面上蔓延开。 看起来像一把巨大的灰色雨伞，上面布满了黑色的斑点。

Rising from the center of the umbrella was a huge, rounded head! It had yellow eyes with black pupils. Stretching out from the umbrella were tentacles— lots of tentacles, all connected by a thick web, with double rows of suckers on each one. 从雨伞的中心升起了一颗巨大的、圆圆的脑袋！ 它有黄色的眼睛，瞳孔是黑色的。 从雨伞上伸出来的是触手——很多很多触手，全部由一张厚厚的网连接在一起，每条触手上都有两排吸盘。

A giant octopus! Jack thought with horror. It was much bigger than the octopus they’d seen a long time ago. And this time, a tentacle of the huge creature was curling around his waist! 是一只巨大的章鱼！ 杰克吓得想。 它比他们很久以前看到的那只章鱼大多了。 这次，那只巨大生物的一条触手正缠绕在他的腰上！

“Help!” Jack shrieked. He tried to pull himself free from the thick, rubbery arm. “救命！”杰克尖叫道。 他试图从那条又厚又像橡胶一样的手臂中挣脱出来。

The giant octopus held him tightly. Jack looked around wildly. Where was Annie? 那只巨大的章鱼紧紧抓住了他。 杰克疯狂地四处张望。安妮在哪里？

“Jack!” Annie shouted. “杰克！”安妮喊道。

Jack saw her. One of the octopus tentacles had lifted Annie above the surface of the water, too. 杰克看见了她。 章鱼的一条触手也把安妮抬到了水面上。

Jack’s relief at seeing Annie was matched by his terror. “We’re caught!” he cried. “Try to escape!” 看到安妮时，杰克的安心感和恐惧感一样强烈。 “我们被困住了！”他喊道。 “快逃！”

Jack kept trying to break free from the huge tentacle. But the more he fought, the tighter the grip of the octopus became. 杰克一直在努力挣脱那条巨大的触手。 但他越挣扎，章鱼的抓握就越紧。

“Don’t fight him, Jack!” Annie yelled. “He’s rescuing us!” “别跟他斗，杰克！”安妮喊道。 “他在救我们！”

Jack couldn’t think straight. Was she crazy? “He’ll strangle us and pull us back under!” he shouted. “We have to fight him!” 杰克一时想不清楚。她疯了吗？ “他会勒死我们，把我们拉回水下！”他喊道。 “我们必须跟他斗！”

“No!” shouted Annie. “You’re wrong! Can’t you see? He’s saving us from drowning!” “不！”安妮喊道。 “你错了！难道你看不见吗？ 他正救我们脱离溺水！”

He is? thought Jack. 他真的在救我们？杰克心想。

Jack tried not to panic. The octopus arm around his middle felt firm, but he could tell that it wasn’t trying to strangle him. It actually felt like it was hugging him, circling him like an inner tube, holding him above the water. 杰克努力让自己不慌张。 章鱼的触手缠绕着他的身体，感觉很结实，但他能感觉到它并没有想勒死他。 它实际上感觉像是在拥抱他，像救生圈一样环绕着他，把他托在水面上。

Jack saw the creature’s huge yellow eyes fixed on him. As Jack stared back into his eyes, he knew what Annie was saying was true. The creature wasn’t trying to hurt him. Instead, the octopus looked concerned. He looked curious, too, and even a little shy. 杰克看见那生物巨大的黄色眼睛正盯着他。 当杰克凝视着它的眼睛时，他明白安妮说的没错。 这生物并没有想伤害他。 相反，章鱼看起来很担心。 它还看起来很好奇，甚至有点害羞。

Staring back into the eyes of the octopus, Jack found himself smiling. As he and Annie kept bobbing up and down in the arms of the octopus, all of Jack’s fear vanished. This was the weirdest rescue he’d ever experienced. 杰克盯着章鱼的眼睛，自己竟然笑了出来。 杰克和安妮在章鱼的臂膀里上下起伏，杰克所有的恐惧都消失了。 这是一次他经历过的最奇怪的营救。

“Hi, you,” Annie said to the octopus. “We come in peace.” “你好，”安妮对章鱼说。 “我们是来和平的。”

Jack felt so dazed he started laughing. Annie laughed, too. Even the octopus looked amused. 杰克感到晕晕乎乎的，竟然开始笑了起来。 安妮也笑了。 就连章鱼看起来也觉得有趣。

Their laughter was interrupted by the blare of a horn. Jack heard men shrieking and shouting. He looked up and saw the HMS Challenger heading toward them. 他们的笑声被一声号角打断了。 杰克听到有人在尖叫和大喊。 他抬头一看，发现HMS挑战者号正朝他们驶来。
