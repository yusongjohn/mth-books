9. Think! Think! 9. 动脑筋！动脑筋！

“W here’s my backpack?” Jack yelled to Annie. “我的背包在哪里？”杰克对安妮喊道。

“What?” Annie said. “什么？”安妮说。

“My backpack!” said Jack. “我的背包！”杰克说。

“Don’t worry about that now,” said Annie. “We have to—” “别担心这个现在，”安妮说。 “我们必须—”

“The wand! The wand!” Jack interrupted her. “魔杖！ “魔杖！”杰克打断了她。

“Oh!” said Annie. “The wand!” “哦！”安妮说。 “魔杖！”

“We have to find my pack! Fast!” said Jack. “我们必须找到我的背包！快！”杰克说。

Jack and Annie pushed past the sailors crowding the railing. They ran across the deck. 杰克和安妮挤过挤在栏杆边的水手们。 他们跑过甲板。

“Did you bring it in the rowboat?” yelled Annie. “你把它带进划艇里了吗？”安妮喊道。

“Yes! No! I don’t know!” said Jack. “I can’t remember!” “是！不！ 我不知道！”杰克说。 “我记不起来了！”

“Think! Think!” said Annie. “想想！ 想想！”安妮说。

Jack tried to think as they ran around the upper deck of the ship. Did I have it in the row-boat? What happened in the rowboat? I felt sick in the rowboat! I hugged my backpack in the row-boat! 杰克一边跑一边在船的上层甲板上努力思考。 我是不是把它放在划艇里了？ 划艇里发生了什么？ 我在划艇里感觉很难受！ 我在划艇里紧紧抱着我的背包！

“I remember!” Jack shouted. “I was holding on to it when we first came aboard!” “我想起来了！”杰克喊道。 “我们刚上船的时候，我正抓着它呢！”

“Then it’s here somewhere!” said Annie. “那它应该就在这儿的某个地方！”安妮说。

“The wardroom!” said Jack. “医务室！”杰克说。

“Hurry!” cried Annie. “快点！”安妮喊道。

Jack and Annie raced down two flights of steps. They looked in the wardroom. Jack’s pack wasn’t there! 杰克和安妮一路跑下两段楼梯。 他们看了看医务室。 杰克的背包不在那儿！

“Henry’s lab!” said Annie. “是亨利的实验室！”，安妮说。

“Yes! Yes! It’s in there!” Jack cried. “I remember now! I left it on the table. I put it down when I looked through the microscope!” “对！对！ 就在里面！”，杰克喊道。 “我想起来了！ 我把它放在桌子上了。 我用显微镜看东西的时候把它放下的！”，

Jack and Annie ran up one flight of steps to Henry’s lab. They tried to open the door, but it was locked! 杰克和安妮一路跑上一段楼梯，来到亨利的实验室。 他们试着开门，但门被锁住了！

“We need Henry!” said Jack. “我们需要亨利！”杰克说。

“We don’t have time!” said Annie. “我们没时间了！”安妮说。

“We don’t have a choice!” said Jack. “我们别无选择！”杰克说。

Jack and Annie charged back up the stairs to the upper deck. 杰克和安妮冲回楼上甲板。

The crew was still gathered at the end of the deck, yelling and shrieking about the octopus. 船员们仍然聚集在甲板尽头，大声喊叫着关于章鱼的事情。

Henry was coming toward them! 亨利正朝他们走来！

“Henry, help us!” said Annie. She raced to Henry and grabbed him by the arm. She pulled him toward the stairs. “You have to unlock your lab! We have to get Jack’s pack! It’s life or death! Hurry, hurry!” “亨利，帮帮我们！”安妮说。 她跑向亨利，抓住了他的手臂。 她把他拉向楼梯。 “你得去解锁你的实验室！ 我们必须拿到杰克的背包！ 这是生还是死！快点，快点！“

“Why? What?” sputtered Henry. “为什么？” “什么？”亨利结结巴巴地说。

“We’ll explain later!” said Jack. “我们之后再解释！”杰克说。

“Just hurry!” said Annie. “快点！”安妮说。

Henry looked baffled, but he walked quickly and led Jack and Annie down the stairs. They nearly pushed him across the hall toward his lab. The scientist pulled out his key and unlocked the door. 亨利看起来很困惑，但他很快地走着，带领杰克和安妮走下楼梯。 他们几乎把他推过走廊，朝他的实验室走去。 科学家拿出钥匙，打开了门。

Jack and Annie burst into the lab. “Yay!” said Annie. Jack’s pack was on the table! 杰克和安妮冲进了实验室。 “太好了！”安妮说。 杰克的背包在桌子上！

Jack grabbed it and tore it open. He pulled out the Wand of Dianthus. The silver spiraled wand gleamed in the dim light of the room. 杰克抓起它，撕开了包装。 他拿出了一根迪安瑟斯魔杖。 那根银色的螺旋魔杖在房间昏暗的灯光下闪闪发光。

“What is that?” asked Henry. “这是什么？”亨利问。

“We’ll explain later!” said Annie. “我们稍后再解释！”安妮说。

“Come on, let’s go back up!” said Jack. “快点，我们得回去！”杰克说。

“No, we don’t have time!” cried Annie. “Use it now, Jack! Say something now! Before they hurt him!” “不行，我们没时间了！”安妮喊道。 “现在用它，杰克！ 现在说点什么！ 在他们伤害他之前！

Jack held up the wand. 杰克举起了魔杖。

“Five words!” said Annie. “五个字！”安妮说。

“I know,” said Jack. But what five words? This was their only chance! “我知道，”杰克说。 但是哪五个词呢？ 这是他们唯一的机会！

“Hurry!” said Annie. “快点！”安妮说。

“I am!” said Jack. “But we can’t just save him this one time. We have to make sure the ship never, ever tries to hurt him or others like him again.” “我正在快点！”杰克说。 “但我们不能只这一次救他。” 我们必须确保这艘船永远、永远不会再试图伤害他或像他这样的人。

Jack closed his eyes. He pictured the giant octopus … his umbrella-like body and long tentacles, the gaze of his yellow eyes—a gaze that had curiosity and shyness. 杰克闭上了眼睛。 他想象着那只巨大的章鱼……它像雨伞一样的身体和长长的触手，还有它那双黄色眼睛的目光——一种充满好奇和害羞的目光。

Jack thought of the sheer wonder of the octopus’s existence. The giant sea animal was incredibly amazing. He was like a miracle. 杰克想到了章鱼存在的纯粹奇迹。 那只巨大的海洋动物非常非常神奇。 他就像一个奇迹。

Jack wished he and Annie could explain the miracle of the octopus to everyone. But no one would listen to them. Unless— 杰克希望他和安妮能向所有人解释章鱼的奇迹。 但没有人愿意听他们说。除非——

“Hurry, Jack! Say the words!” said Annie. “快点，杰克！ 说这些话！”安妮说。

“Make them hear the truth!” The words had burst out of Jack. “让他们听到真相！”这些话突然从杰克嘴里冒了出来。

“What?” said Annie. “什么？”安妮说。

“Make—them—hear—the—truth!” said Jack, his eyes still shut tight. “That’s my wish. Make all the guys upstairs hear the truth about the octopus.” “让—他—们—听—到—真—相！”杰克说，眼睛仍然紧紧闭着。 “那是我的愿望。 让楼上所有男人都听到关于章鱼的真相。”

“What are you talking about?” said Henry. “What is that stick you’re waving?” “你在说什么？”亨利说。 “你手里拿的那根棍子是什么？”

Jack opened his eyes. He shook his head at Annie. But she didn’t see him. 杰克睁开了眼睛。 他摇了摇头，看着安妮。 但是她没看见他。

“It’s a magic wand!” Annie said to Henry. “这是一根魔法棒！”安妮对亨利说。

“Actually—” said Jack, trying to think of something else to say. “其实——”杰克说，努力想找个别的说法。

“Oh, oh, yes, I see,” said Henry. He smiled sadly. “You’re pretending, like children.” “哦，哦，是的，我明白了，”亨利说。他难过地笑了笑。 “你们是在假装，像小孩子一样。”

“We are children!” said Annie. “Come on, Jack. Let’s go tell them the truth!” “我们就是小孩子！”安妮说。 “杰克，走吧。 我们去告诉他们真相吧！”
