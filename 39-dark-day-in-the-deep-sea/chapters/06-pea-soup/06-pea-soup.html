6. Pea Soup 6. 豌豆汤

H enry led Jack and Annie out of the sea lab, locking the door behind them. Then he led the way through the hall and down another flight of steep steps. In the faint light of the lower deck, Jack saw naval officers and scientists streaming through a door ahead. He, Annie, and Henry lined up behind them and filed into the wardroom. 亨利带着杰克和安妮走出了海洋实验室，锁上了身后的门。 然后他带领他们穿过大厅，沿着另一段陡峭的台阶走下去。 在下层甲板微弱的灯光下，杰克看到海军军官和科学家们正从前方的门里鱼贯而入。 杰克、安妮和亨利排在他们后面，依次走进了军官餐厅。

Still wearing their bulky life vests, Jack and Annie sat awkwardly on a long bench in front of a table. Many of the men stared at them with curiosity. The professor smiled, but the captain did not look happy. 仍然穿着厚重救生衣的杰克和安妮，尴尬地坐在一张长凳上，长凳前面是一张桌子。 许多男人都好奇地盯着他们看。 教授微笑着，但船长看起来并不高兴。

“I thought our friends might join us for lunch, sir,” Henry said to the captain. “I promise they will return to shore after the storm.” “我想我们的朋友可能会和我们一起吃午饭，先生，”亨利对船长说道。 “我保证风暴过后他们会立刻返回岸上。”

“Very well,” said the captain. “Immediately after the storm.” “好吧，”船长说道。 “风暴一结束就立刻返回。”

“Aye, aye, sir,” said Henry. He turned to the other officers at the table. “Gentlemen, may I introduce Annie and Jack from America. They are adventurous travelers.” “遵命，长官，”亨利说道。 他转向桌边的其他军官。 “各位先生，让我介绍一下来自美国的安妮和杰克。 他们是勇敢的旅行者。”

“Hi,” murmured Jack and Annie. “你好，”杰克和安妮小声说道。

The officers and scientists nodded politely. 军官和科学家们礼貌地点了点头。

Sailors in white uniforms acted as waiters, bringing plates and cups to the table. “What are we having for lunch?” Annie whispered to Henry. 穿着白色制服的水手们当服务员，把盘子和杯子端到桌边。 “我们午餐吃什么？”安妮低声问亨利。

“The usual,” said Henry, sighing. “Salted meat, pickles, dried biscuits.” “照常，”亨利叹了口气说道。 “咸肉、腌黄瓜、干饼干。”

This is not good, thought Jack. He was right. When he was served, he could hardly look at his food, much less eat it. He was thirsty, though. So he reached for his cup and took a long sip of water. 这可不好，杰克心想。他说得对。 他被端上食物时，几乎连看都不敢看，更别说吃了。 不过，他确实口渴了。 所以他拿起杯子，大口喝了一口水。

Jack immediately spit the liquid back into his cup. It was unbelievably sour! He gagged and coughed. When he looked up, everyone was staring at him, including the captain. “Excuse me,” Jack said, wiping his mouth. His face felt hot from embarrassment. 杰克立刻把嘴里的水又吐回杯子里。 这水简直酸得要命！ 他呛得咳嗽起来。 他抬头一看，所有人都在盯着他看，连船长也在看。 “对不起，”杰克说，擦了擦嘴。 他因为尴尬，脸都红了。

“You just guzzled your lime juice, lad,” said the professor. “Most of us take it in small sips.” “你刚刚一口气喝完了你的青柠汁，小伙子，”教授说。 “我们大多数人都是小口喝的。”

“Lime juice?” asked Annie. “Why?” “青柠汁？”安妮问。 “为什么？”

“It prevents scurvy,” said the Professor. “Every day we all drink a cup of lime juice for vitamin C. Otherwise, we’d get sores and rotten teeth.” “它可以预防坏血病，”教授说。 “我们每天都喝一杯青柠汁来补充维生素C。 否则的话，我们就会得口腔溃疡和烂牙。”

“Yuck,” said Annie. “真恶心，”安妮说。

Henry smiled. “You won’t get scurvy if you eat fruits and vegetables,” he said. “But it is hard to keep vegetables and fruits fresh on board a ship.” 亨利笑了。 “如果你吃水果和蔬菜，你就不会得坏血病，”他说。 “但是，在船上保持蔬菜和水果的新鲜可不容易。”

Jack couldn’t think about eating or drinking anything at the moment. All of it—especially the pickles—made him feel even queasier. The rolling of the ship didn’t help, either. “The waves seem to be getting worse,” he said to Henry. 杰克此刻根本没法想到吃或喝任何东西。 所有的东西——尤其是腌黄瓜——让他感觉更恶心了。 船的颠簸也没有帮助。 “浪似乎变得更大了，”他对亨利说。

“They do indeed,” said Henry. He turned and looked out a small, round window. “Can’t see a thing. It’s as thick as pea soup out there.” “确实如此，”亨利说。 他转过身，透过一个小圆窗户往外看。 “什么都看不见。 外面的天气浓得像豌豆汤一样。”

Oh, no, Jack groaned to himself. Please don’t talk about pea soup. 哦，不，杰克自己呻吟道。 请不要谈论豌豆汤。

The ship lurched. Cups and plates slid across the table and crashed to the floor. Annie grabbed Jack as the ship rocked wildly. 船猛地一晃。 杯子和盘子从桌子上滑了出去，摔在了地上。 船剧烈摇晃时，安妮抓住了杰克。

“Steady as she goes!” said the captain. “保持稳定前行！”船长说道。

“It seems the storm is fully upon us,” said the professor. “看来暴风雨已经完全来临了，”教授说道。

The ship lurched again. More cups and plates slid off the table. Jack and Annie almost slid off their chairs. But without any sign of nervousness, the officers and scientists stood up and hurried out of the room, as if they knew just what to do in bad weather. 船又猛地一晃。 更多的杯子和盘子从桌子上滑了下去。 杰克和安妮差点从椅子上滑下来。 但军官们和科学家们却一点也不紧张，他们站起身来，迅速离开了房间，好像他们知道在恶劣天气下该怎么做。

“I’ll take you down to the hold,” Henry said to Jack and Annie. “We’ll wait there until the waves calm down.” “我带你们去下层舱，”亨利对杰克和安妮说。 “我们在那儿等到海浪平静下来。”

The ship pitched violently as they stood up from the table. Holding on to the back of their life vests, Henry steered Jack and Annie out of the wardroom. Jack nearly slipped on the wet floor. His bare feet crushed some soggy biscuits and pickles. He tried not to think about it. 船剧烈摇晃，他们从桌边站起来时差点摔倒。 亨利抓住他们救生衣的后背，把杰克和安妮从休息室领了出来。 杰克差点在湿滑的地板上滑倒。 他光着的脚踩碎了一些湿掉的饼干和腌黄瓜。 他努力不去想这件事。

Out in the dark hall, the wind howled down the stairwell from the top deck, spraying them all with ocean foam. 在黑暗的走廊里，风从顶层甲板的楼梯井呼啸而下，把他们全都淋上了海浪的泡沫。

“I must go secure my lab,” Henry shouted to Jack and Annie. He pointed to the stairs. “Go down one more flight! I’ll join you when I can!” “我必须去锁好我的实验室，”亨利对杰克和安妮喊道。 他指着楼梯。 “再下一层楼！ 我能在的时候就来加入你们！”

“We’ll come with you!” said Annie. “我们和你一起去！”安妮说。

“’Tis better to be safe!” said Henry. “Follow the others quickly! I’ll come soon!” Henry unlocked the door to his lab and disappeared inside. “安全最重要！”亨利说。 “快跟上其他人！ “我很快就来！”亨利打开他实验室的门，然后消失在里面了。

“Come on, let’s go down!” said Jack. He grabbed Annie’s hand and pulled her toward the stairwell. Officers and sailors were all heading down the steps ahead of them. “快点，我们下去吧！”杰克说。 他抓住安妮的手，把她拉向楼梯口。 警察和水手们都在他们前面往楼下走。

Just as Jack and Annie started down, the ship lurched again. Jack’s stomach lurched with it. He covered his mouth. I am definitely going to be sick! he thought. He really didn’t want to throw up on the stairs—or in front of the captain and the others! 就在杰克和安妮开始往下走的时候，船又猛地晃了一下。 杰克的胃一下子翻了上来。 他赶紧捂住了嘴巴。 我肯定要吐了！他心想。 他真的不想在楼梯上吐，也不想在船长和其他人面前吐！

“You go down! I’ll be right there!” Jack shouted to Annie. “你下去！ 我马上就来！”杰克对安妮喊道。

“Why? Where are you going?” Annie yelled. “为什么？ 你要去哪儿？”安妮大喊。

“Just go down!” said Jack. Then he whirled around and charged up two flights of steep steps to the top deck. “你先下去！”杰克说。 然后他猛地转身，冲着陡峭的楼梯一口气跑上了顶层甲板。

When Jack stepped onto the deck, the rain was pounding. The wind was howling. The waves looked like dark mountains. Sea foam was blowing everywhere. 杰克踏上甲板时，雨点正哗啦哗啦地砸下来。 风在呜呜地吼。 海浪看起来像黑色的山。 海浪的泡沫到处飞溅。

Jack knew at once he’d made a mistake. Better to be embarrassed than drown! he thought. Just as he turned to go back down the steps, Annie burst onto the deck. 杰克立刻知道自己犯了错误。 宁可被人笑话，也不要被淹死！他心想。 就在他转身要走下台阶时，安妮突然冲上了甲板。

“Jack!” she cried. “What are you doing?” “杰克！”她喊道。 “你在这儿干什么？”

Jack forgot all about throwing up. “I made a mistake!” he shouted. “We shouldn’t be up here! Go down! I’m coming with you! We have to go back down!” He pushed Annie toward the stairwell. 杰克一下子把想吐的事全忘了。 “我犯了一个错误！”他喊道。 “我们不应该在这里！快下去！ 我要和你一起去！ 我们必须下去！“他把安妮推向楼梯井。

A giant wave crashed over the side of the ship. Jack fell and slid across the foam-covered deck. It was impossible to see anything. The wind was screaming. Jack tried to stand, but wearing his clumsy life vest, he couldn’t get back on his feet. 一个巨大的海浪从船边冲了过去。 杰克摔倒了，滑过铺满泡沫的甲板。 什么也看不见。 风在尖叫。 杰克试图站起来，但穿着笨拙的救生衣，他无法重新站稳。

Jack finally hauled himself up. But just as he did, another giant wave broke over the side of the ship! The ship rolled again, and Jack fell to his knees. Another wave crashed over the deck. Jack was swept up by the foamy water—and tossed overboard into the churning sea! 杰克终于把自己拉了起来。 但就在他这么做时，另一股巨大的海浪冲上了船边！ 船又晃了一下，杰克跪倒在地。 又一个海浪冲上了甲板。 杰克被泡沫般的海水卷起，然后被抛入了翻腾的海中！
