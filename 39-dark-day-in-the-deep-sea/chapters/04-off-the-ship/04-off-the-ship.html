4. Off the Ship! 4. 下船！

“T he wind’s against us!” Joe said as he and Tommy rowed. “风对着我们吹来！”乔一边划船一边说。

Even after the boat moved past the breakers, it kept bouncing up and down on the swell of the waves. Water splashed over the sides and soaked Jack’s clothes. But Jack wasn’t worried about the wind or water. He was worried about being seasick, because waves of nausea had started to come over him. 即使船过了浪花区，它还是在海浪的颠簸中上下颠簸。 水从船边溅上来，把杰克的衣服都弄湿了。 但杰克并不担心风和水。 他担心的是晕船，因为恶心的感觉已经开始涌上来了。

“Sorry it’s a bit of tough going!” Henry said. “抱歉，路有点难走！”亨利说。

“We can take it!” Annie said. “我们可以带走它！”安妮说。

We hope, thought Jack. The last thing he wanted to do was throw up, especially in front of Joe and Tommy! 希望如此，杰克心想。 他最不想做的事就是呕吐，尤其是在乔和汤米面前！

“We should get back just in time to see the men haul up this morning’s catch!” said Henry. “我们应该刚好及时回去，看到那些人把今早的渔获拉上来！”亨利说。

“This is so much fun!” said Annie, her eyes shining as the little rowboat bobbed up and down. “这太好玩了！”安妮说，她的眼睛闪闪发亮，小划艇上下颠簸着。

Jack wasn’t having any fun at all. To keep from being sick, he gripped his backpack, closed his eyes, and gritted his teeth. 杰克一点也没玩得开心。 为了不吐出来，他紧紧抓住背包，闭上眼睛，咬紧牙关。

“Every day we make new discoveries,” said Henry. “Off the coast of Argentina, we found over one hundred new species! Giant worms several feet long! Shrimp the size of lobsters! Caught them in our nets, didn’t we, Joe?” “我们每天都发现新东西，”亨利说。 “在阿根廷海岸，我们发现了超过一百种新物种！ 几英尺长的巨大蠕虫！ 像龙虾一样大的虾！ 我们用网子抓住他们了，乔，对吧？

“Aye,” said Joe as he pulled on the oars. “But the creature that’s never been caught is the one these mates should be worried about!” “啊，”乔一边划着桨一边说道。 “但那些从未被抓住的生物，才是这些朋友们应该担心的！”

“What creature is that?” asked Annie. “是什么生物？”安妮问道。

“The great monster,” answered Tommy. “那只大怪物，”汤米回答道。

Jack opened his eyes. “What? You mean like a shark?” he asked. 杰克睁开了眼睛。 “什么？ 你是指像鲨鱼那样吗？”他问。

“No, no, lad, ’tis much worse than any shark, even the twenty-foot tiger shark that’s been following us,” said Tommy. The sailor blinked nervously. “不，不，孩子，这比任何鲨鱼都糟糕得多，甚至比一直跟踪我们的二十英尺长的虎鲨还要糟糕，”汤米说。 水手紧张地眨了眨眼。

Whoa, twenty-foot tiger shark?! thought Jack. He looked at the dark water for a shark fin. 哇，二十英尺长的虎鲨？！杰克心想。 他盯着漆黑的水面，寻找鲨鱼的鳍。

“Aye! This monster’s much bigger than any shark!” shouted Joe. “They say it looks like a cross between a dragon and a gigantic starfish.” “啊！ “这怪物比任何鲨鱼都大得多！”乔喊道。 “人们说它看起来像是龙和巨大的海星的混合体。”

“Nay, more like a floating nest of snakes, mate,” said Tommy with a shudder. “They say it’ll curl around your body and strangle you to death!” “不，更像是漂浮的蛇窝，伙计，”汤米皱着眉头说。 “人们说它会缠住你的身体，把你勒死！”

“A floating nest of snakes?” Annie asked. “漂浮的蛇窝？”安妮问。

Jack gulped. He turned to Henry. “Have you seen the monster?” he asked the scientist. 杰克咽了口唾沫。 他转向亨利。 “你看到怪物了吗？”他问科学家。

Henry shook his head. “I’ve never seen it,” he said. “But a few of our crewmen claim to have glimpsed something monstrous in these waters just yesterday.” 亨利摇了摇头。 “我从来没看到过，”他说。 “不过，我们的一些船员昨天声称在这片水域里看到过一些可怕的怪物。”

“Don’t be scared, mates!” said Joe. “If we see a monster, we’ll hurl our harpoons at ’im!” “别害怕，伙计们！”乔说。 “如果我们看到怪物，我们就用鱼叉朝它扔过去！”

“We’ll shoot ’im with our cannons!” said Tommy. He and Joe laughed loudly. “我们会用大炮把它打跑！”汤米说。 他和乔大声笑了起来。

Maybe the sailors on the ship are just trying to scare the scientists, Jack thought hopefully. Why else would they be laughing? 也许船上的水手只是想吓唬科学家们，杰克满怀希望地想。 他们为什么还会笑呢？

When they reached the HMS Challenger, the rowboat drew alongside the big ship, rocking on the water. Jack gripped his pack tighter as more waves of seasickness washed over him. 当他们到达HMS挑战者号时，小船靠上了大船，在水面上轻轻摇晃。 更多的晕船感袭来，杰克紧紧抓住他的背包。

“You go first, mate!” Joe said to Jack. “You’re looking a bit green.” “你先上吧，伙计！”乔对杰克说。 “你看起来有点儿脸色发青。”

Clutching his pack under one arm, Jack grabbed the sides of the ladder. He held on tightly and climbed from the wooden hull of the ship up to the top deck. Annie came after him, then Henry, Joe, and Tommy. When they were all on the deck, the two seamen hauled up the row-boat. 杰克用一只手夹着背包，抓住了梯子的两侧。 他紧紧抓住梯子，从船的木制船身上爬到了甲板上。 安妮跟了上来，接着是亨利、乔和汤米。 当他们都上了甲板后，那两个水手把小船拉了上来。

Jack took a long, deep breath. Though the large ship rocked in the wind, it wasn’t nearly as bad as the small rowboat’s movement on the waves. Looking around the deck, Jack saw teams of sailors working. Some were coiling thick ropes. Others were hauling up strange-looking buckets. 杰克深深吸了一口气。 虽然大船在风中摇晃，但和小划艇在海浪中颠簸比起来，要好得多。 杰克环顾甲板，看到许多水手在忙碌地工作。 一些人在缠绕粗壮的绳子。 另一些人正在把一些奇怪的桶拉上来。

Jack turned to ask Henry what the sailors were doing. But Henry was staring at a tall man in a white uniform and a heavyset older man in a dark suit who were walking briskly toward them. The two men were frowning. 杰克转身想问问亨利水手们在做什么。 但亨利正盯着一个穿着白色制服的高个子男人和一个穿着深色西装的胖些的年长男人，他们正快步朝他们走来。 那两个人皱着眉头。

“Oh, no,” murmured Henry. “Prepare to meet thy doom.” “哦，不，”亨利低声说。 “准备好接受你的命运吧。”

“Who are they?” asked Annie. “他们是谁？”安妮问。

Before Henry could answer, the man in white shouted, “What have you done this time, Mr. Moseley?” 还没等亨利回答，穿白衣服的男人就喊道，“亨利·莫泽利先生，这次你又干了什么？”

Jack moved closer to Annie. He clutched his pack to his life vest. 杰克走近了安妮。 他紧紧抓住背包，贴在救生衣上。

“Well, Captain, I—” started Henry. “好吧，船长，我——”亨利开口说道。

“Goodness, what have you brought up from the sea now, Henry?” the portly man asked. “A four-legged, four-armed creature of the deep?” “天哪，亨利，这次你又从海里打捞了什么奇怪的东西？”那个身材魁梧的男人问道。 “是一种四条腿、四条胳膊的深海生物吗？”

“Yes, Professor. It’s a Jack-and-Annie from America,” said Henry. “I found the creature vacationing on the island.” “是的，教授。 它是一个来自美国的杰克和安妮，”亨利说道。 “我在岛上发现这个生物正在度假。”

The portly man smiled. “Oh, I see. I thought perhaps it was the monster that was sighted by some of the men yesterday.” 那个身材魁梧的男人笑了。 “哦，我明白了。” 我以为也许那是昨天一些人看到的怪物。

The monster again! thought Jack. 又来了那个怪物！杰克心想。

“This ship is not a place for children, Mr. Moseley,” the captain said gruffly. “这艘船不适合孩子，莫塞利先生， ”船长粗声粗气地说。

“Yes, I know, sir,” said Henry. “But these two are extraordinary. They hardly seem like children at all. They’re quite independent and have great knowledge of the sea. I thought it might be permissible to bring them aboard for the afternoon and then return them to shore.” “是的，我知道，先生，”亨利说道。 “但这两个孩子非常特别。 他们看起来几乎不像小孩子。 他们非常独立，而且对大海了解很多。 我想或许可以允许他们在下午上船，然后送他们回岸。”

“I’m afraid it goes against all the ship’s rules,” said the captain. “我担心这违反了船上的所有规则，”船长说道。

“It’s not Henry’s fault, Captain,” Annie piped up. “We begged him to let us visit your ship.” “这不是亨利的错，船长，”安妮插话道。 “我们恳求他让我们参观你的船。”

“Ah, did you now?” the portly man asked, his eyes twinkling. “Why is that?” “啊，是吗？”那个身材魁梧的人问道，眼睛闪烁着光芒。 “为什么呢？”

“We love the ocean!” said Annie. “因为我们喜欢海洋！”安妮说道。

“And we’d really like to learn more about sea exploration, sir,” said Jack.
“Well, you’ve come to the right place!” said the man. “Allow me to introduce myself. I am Professor Thomson, the scientific director of the Challenger.”
“The professor is one of the world’s most renowned experts on the ocean,” said Henry.
“Wow,” said Annie.
“Well, I don’t know about that,” the professor said modestly. But he put his thumbs in his vest and began speaking as if he were giving a lecture. “Since the beginning of time, the secrets of the deep have been hidden from us. But now, with our expedition, we have learned many things.”
“Like what?” asked Jack.
“We have used miles and miles of steel wire to measure the depth of the sea,” said the professor. “We have lowered thermometers to measure temperatures in the deep. But perhaps most important of all, we are learning about the amazing creatures who live in the dark regions far beneath the ocean waves—”
“That is all well and good, Professor,” the captain broke in. “But I want these children off this ship immediately! Before the weather gets worse. Do you hear, Mr. Moseley? Off the ship!”
