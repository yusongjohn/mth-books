9. Message for the King 9. 给国王的讯息

“You are a genius,” Jack said to Annie. “你真聪明，”杰克对安妮说。

She smiled. “Thanks. You probably would have come up with the same plan sooner or later.” 她笑了。 “谢谢。 你可能迟早会想到同样的计划。”

“I don’t think so,” said Jack. “This might be the coolest idea you’ve ever had.” “我不这么认为，”杰克说。 “这可能是你这辈子最酷的主意。”

Annie turned to Heart-of-the-Wind. “This is for you,” she said, holding out the cell phone. “Watch.” She pressed the “on/off” button and the phone lit up. 安妮转向心之风。 “这是给你的，”她说着，把手机递了出去。 “看。”她按下“开/关”按钮，手机亮了起来。

“Oh!” Heart-of-the-Wind jumped back. “哦！”心之风往后跳了一步。

Jack laughed. “Don’t worry, it won’t hurt you. We’ll use it to send a message from me to your father.” 杰克笑了。 “别担心，它不会伤害你的。” 我们将用它从我这里给你爸爸发一条信息。”

Heart-of-the-Wind looked puzzled and curious at the same time. Keeping her distance from the phone, she said, “But what is it? Where did it come from?” Heart-of-the-Wind看起来又困惑又好奇。 她保持和电话的距离，说：“但是这是什么？ 它从哪儿来的？

“It’s a device that…well…someone named Alexander Graham Bell invented the telephone,” said Jack. “Telephones used poles and wires. But then people invented digital phones and wireless communications and satellites and—” “这是一个装置……嗯……一个叫亚历山大·格雷厄姆·贝尔的人发明了电话，”杰克说。 “电话以前用电线杆和电线。 但后来人们发明了数字电话、无线通信、卫星和——”

“Stop, Jack,” said Annie, interrupting. “We can’t go into all that now.” “停一下，杰克，”安妮打断了他。 “我们现在还不能进去。”

“Right, just watch,” Jack said to Heart-of-the-Wind. “对，你看着吧，”杰克对心之风说。

“And listen carefully,” Annie said. “而且要仔细听，”安妮说。

The Mayan girl nodded, still looking puzzled. 玛雅女孩点了点头，仍然一脸困惑。

“Ready?” Annie asked Jack. “准备好了吗？”安妮问杰克。

“Is it charged enough?” he said. “电量够吗？”他说。

“It should be good for today,” said Annie. “今天应该够用，”安妮说。

“Great,” said Jack. “太好了，”杰克说。

“Think about what you want to say to the Great Sun of Palenque,” said Annie. She tapped the “photos” icon on the screen. Then she held the phone in front of Jack. She tapped the “video camera” icon. “Whenever you’re ready,” she said. “想想你想要对帕伦克的大太阳说的话，”安妮说。 她点击了屏幕上的“照片”图标。 然后她把手机举到杰克面前。 她点击了“视频相机”的图标。 “你准备好了吗？”，她说。

Jack put down the swim bag, sat on a rock, and thought for a moment. Then he cleared his throat. “Roll camera,” he said. 杰克放下游泳包，坐在一块石头上，想了一会儿。 然后他清了清嗓子。 “开始录像吧。”，他说。

“Right,” said Annie. “Action!” Then she pressed “record.” “好的。”，安妮说。 “开始！”然后她按下了“录制”。

Looking straight at the phone, Jack spoke in a deep, serious voice: 她直视着手机，用低沉严肃的声音说道：

“Greetings, Great Sun of Palenque. I have a message for you. My sister and I have come from Frog Creek, a land far away, to tell you this: women can lead just as well as men. Many women are leaders in our world. They are presidents, queens, senators, school principals, and military leaders. They are just as smart and brave and responsible as our men. That is why you already have the perfect heir to your throne: your daughter Heart-of-the-Wind. She has wisdom and courage far beyond most people, and she will be a great leader in times of danger and times of peace. Trust this message from a distant time and place. Trust your daughter, Heart-of-the-Wind.” “问候吧，帕伦克的伟大太阳。” 我有一条消息要告诉你。 我和我的姐姐来自遥远的蛙溪镇，我们想告诉你：女人和男人一样，也能当领导。 在我们的世界里，有很多女性领导者。 她们是总统、女王、参议员、校长和军队的领导者。 她们和我们的男人一样聪明、勇敢、有责任心。 这就是为什么你已经有了一个完美的王位继承人：你的女儿心之风。 她拥有的智慧和勇气远远超过大多数人，她会在危险和和平的时期都成为一位伟大的领袖。 相信这条来自遥远时间和地方的信息。 相信你的女儿，心之风。

Jack stopped speaking and stared intently at the phone. 杰克停止了说话，专注地盯着电话。

“Brilliant!” exclaimed Annie after she clicked off the camera. Then she turned to Heart-of-the-Wind. “How did that sound to you?” “太棒了！”安妮关掉相机后喊道。 然后她转向Heart-of-the-Wind。 “听起来怎么样？”

“It…it was wonderful,” said Heart-of-the-Wind. “I only wish my father could have been here to hear it.” “它……它太棒了，”Heart-of-the-Wind说。 “我只希望我的父亲能在这里听到它。”

“Don’t worry,” said Annie, smiling. “Watch.” “别担心，”安妮微笑着说。 “看。”

She held up the cell phone so Heart-of-the-Wind could see the screen, and then she pressed “play.” Jack’s face appeared. “ Greetings, Great Sun of Palenque….” 她举起手机，让Heart-of-the-Wind能看到屏幕，然后她按下了“播放”。杰克的脸出现了。 “问候，帕伦克的伟大太阳……”

Heart-of-the-Wind gasped. “Ohhh! How—how does that happen?” Heart-of-the-Wind惊呼道。 “哦！“ “怎么——怎么会发生这样的事？”

“It’s too hard to explain,” said Jack. “这太难解释了，”杰克说。

“But here’s all you have to do,” Annie said. “After we go, show this to your father and his warriors. You must do this today. Once the sun goes down, the message can never be seen again. Do you understand?” “但你只要这么做就行，”安妮说。 “我们走了之后，把它拿给你父亲和他手下的战士看。 你今天必须这么做。 一旦太阳下山，这条信息就再也看不到了。 你明白了吗？

“Yes…yes,” the girl said, nodding. “是……是的，”女孩点点头说。

“Good,” said Annie. “So you press this button…then tap this… then tap that…. Easy, right? Try it.” “很好，”安妮说。 “所以你按下这个按钮……然后轻点这个……再轻点那个……很简单，对吧？ 试试看。”

Heart-of-the-Wind cautiously pushed the button and tapped the icons, and Jack’s image appeared again. “ Greetings, Great Sun of Palenque….” Heart-of-the-Wind watched Jack deliver his whole message. When the message was over, she kept looking at the screen in speechless wonder. 心之风小心地按下按钮，点击了图标，杰克的影像又出现了。 “问候吧，帕伦克的伟大太阳……”心之风看着杰克完整地传达了他的信息。 当信息结束时，她仍然盯着屏幕，惊讶得说不出话来。

“Do you think your father will believe me?” said Jack. “你觉得你爸爸会相信我吗？”杰克说。

The girl nodded slowly. “Yes…yes.” She took a deep breath. “He will think this is a great miracle.” 女孩慢慢地点了点头。 “是……是。”她深吸了一口气。 “他会认为这是一个伟大的奇迹。”

“Good! Then we leave it with you,” said Annie. “Try not to drop it or get it wet.” “很好！ 然后我们就把它交给你了，”安妮说。 “尽量别把它弄掉或者弄湿。”

“Thank you,” Heart-of-the-Wind said, bowing her head. “谢谢，”Heart-of-the-Wind说，低头行了个礼。

“We’d better go now,” said Jack. “Before anyone discovers we’re not in the House of Columns.” “我们最好现在就走，”杰克说。 “在有人发现我们不在柱子房之前。”

“Yes. The sun is rising,” Heart-of-the-Wind said. She looked up at the cliff. “The watchmen have left.” “对。 太阳正在升起，”Heart-of-the-Wind说。 她抬头看向悬崖。 “守卫已经离开了。”

“Come on, we have to find our raft,” said Jack. As they walked over the cool sand, the mist slowly lifted. In the rosy dawn, a flock of seagulls cried out. They hovered and glided above the waves. The water was sparkling with early light. “快点，我们必须找到我们的木筏，”杰克说。 他们踩着凉凉的沙子走过去，雾气慢慢散开了。 在玫瑰色的黎明中，一群海鸥大声叫着。 它们在海浪上空盘旋着，滑翔着。 海水在清晨的阳光下闪闪发亮。

“There’s the raft!” said Annie. She broke into a run. Heart-of-the-Wind and Jack followed close behind her. When she reached the raft, Annie fell to her knees and felt around in the sand. “It’s here, Jack!” she cried, holding up the coin purse. “I found it!” “那是木筏！”安妮说。 她立刻跑了起来。 心之风和杰克紧跟着她。 当她跑到木筏边时，安妮跪下来，在沙子里翻找。 “找到了，杰克！”她举起钱袋喊道。 “我找到了！”

“Great!” said Jack. “Is the last gold coin still there?” “太棒了！”杰克说。 “最后一枚金币还在吗？”

Annie opened the little purse and looked inside. “Yes! Everything is still here—the last coin and all our money! We can go home!” She jumped to her feet. 安妮打开小钱包，往里面看了看。 “在的！ 一切都在这里——最后一枚金币，还有我们所有的钱！ 我们可以回家了！”她跳了起来。

Jack sighed with relief, and then turned to Heart-of-the-Wind. “Good luck with the message for your father,” he said. 杰克松了口气，然后转向心之风。 “祝你给父亲带去消息好运，”他说。

The girl pointed to their raft. “Is that how you came here from across the sea?” she asked, looking surprised. “Where is your paddle?” 女孩指着他们的木筏。 “你们就是坐这个从海对岸过来的吗？”她惊讶地看着，问道。 “你的桨在哪里？”

Annie laughed. “We don’t have a paddle,” she said. “A shark came after us and—” 安妮笑了。 “我们没有桨，”她说。 “有一条鲨鱼追我们过来，然后——”

“A shark?” interrupted Heart-of-the-Wind, her eyes wide. “A friendly shark?” “鲨鱼？”Heart-of-the-Wind打断了她，眼睛睁得大大的。 “是友好的鲨鱼吗？”

“I didn’t know there were friendly sharks,” said Jack. “我不知道还有友好的鲨鱼，”杰克说。

“Oh, yes. There are many,” said Heart-of-the-Wind. “哦，是的。 有很多，”Heart-of-the-Wind说。

Boy, she’s not afraid of anything, thought Jack. 天啊，她什么都不怕，杰克心想。

“Well, this one had big teeth,” said Annie. “嗯，这个牙齿很大，”安妮说。

“And it wasn’t smiling,” said Jack. “而且它没有在笑，”杰克说。

“Too bad…,” said Heart-of-the-Wind. “太糟糕了……”Heart-of-the-Wind说道。

“Anyway, the shark attacked our paddle,” said Annie. “But we used a special gold coin that our friends gave us, and we made a wish with it, and the raft magically brought us here.” “不过，鲨鱼袭击了我们的桨，”安妮说道。 “但我们用了一枚朋友给我们的特殊金币，我们用它许了一个愿，小船就神奇地把我们带到了这里。”

The Mayan girl just nodded. 那个玛雅女孩只是点了点头。

I guess it takes a lot to shock a girl who talks to jaguars and walking trees, thought Jack. 杰克心想：也许要让一个会和美洲豹以及会走路的树说话的女孩感到震惊，需要很多很多东西。

“So you will use your last gold coin to help you across the sea?” asked Heart-of-the-Wind. “所以你会用最后一枚金币来帮助你们渡过大海吗？”Heart-of-the-Wind问道。

“We will,” said Annie. “我们会的，”安妮说。

“And what will you wish for?” asked the girl. “那你们会许什么愿望呢？”女孩问道。

“We’ll wish for a safe trip back to Cozumel,” said Jack. “我们希望回去的时候能安全到达科苏梅尔,”杰克说。

“A safe and fun trip,” said Annie. “The magic only works if we wish to have a wonderful time—that’s what our friends wanted.” “安全又好玩的旅程,”安妮说。 “魔法只有在我们希望度过美好时光时才会生效——这就是我们的朋友想要的。”

Annie reached into the purse, took out the gold coin, and put the purse back in their bag. “You want to watch us leave?” she asked. 安妮把手伸进钱包，拿出金币，然后把钱包放回他们的包里。 “你想看着我们离开吗？”她问。

“Oh, yes,” said Heart-of-the-Wind. “哦，对，”Heart-of-the-Wind说。

“Let’s get this in the water first,” said Jack. He set their bag on top of the raft. Then he and Annie pushed the raft across the sand into the shallow water. As Heart-of-the-Wind watched from the shore, Jack and Annie climbed aboard. “我们先把这放进水里，”杰克说。 他把他们的袋子放在筏子上。 然后他和安妮把筏子推过沙滩，放进浅水里。 Heart-of-the-Wind站在岸边看着，杰克和安妮爬上了筏子。

By now, the sky was orange and red. The sun was rising in the east. Annie held up the gold coin. It glittered in the dawn light. 这时候，天空变成了橙色和红色。 太阳正在东方升起。 安妮举起了那枚金币。 在晨光中，金币闪闪发光。

“Wait!” said Heart-of-the-Wind. “May I make the wish for you?” “等一下！”风之心说。 “我可以替你们许愿吗？”

“I think we’d better do it ourselves,” said Jack. “我想我们最好自己来许愿，”杰克说。

“Oh, she can do it,” said Annie. “哦，她可以许愿的，”安妮说。

“But—” said Jack. “但—”杰克说。

“Oh, come on, let her try,” said Annie. “哦，来吧，让她试试看，”安妮说。

“Okay,” said Jack, sighing. “好吧，”杰克叹了口气说。

Heart-of-the-Wind waded into the water, and Annie handed her the gold coin. “All you have to do is wish for us to have a safe, fun trip to Cozumel. Then toss the coin into the air. Got it?” 心之风走进了水里，安妮把金币递给了她。 “你只要许愿，希望我们去科苏梅尔岛的旅行既安全又有趣。” 然后把硬币抛向空中。明白了吗？

“Yes,” said the Mayan girl. “I will give you a miracle, like you gave to me.” She tightened her fist around the coin. She whispered something, and then tossed the coin high into the air. “明白了，”玛雅女孩说。 “我会给你一个奇迹，就像你曾经给我的那样。”她紧握着硬币，握得更紧了。 她小声说了些什么，然后把硬币高高抛向空中。

The gold coin burst into the most radiant display yet—tiny purple, green, blue, and yellow sparks flew into the sky, vanishing into the bright dawn. 那枚金币迸发出前所未有的灿烂光芒——紫色、绿色、蓝色和黄色的小火花飞向天空，消失在明亮的黎明中。

“Thanks! Good—” Before Jack could say bye, the raft shot across the water into the blinding orange-red sunrise. “谢谢！ “很好——”杰克还没来得及说再见，木筏就冲着水面，驶向那耀眼的橙红色日出。

“Bye! Bye!” Jack and Annie called. “再见！” “再见！”杰克和安妮喊道。

Heart-of-the-Wind was laughing and waving. 心之风在笑，还在挥手呢。

“Good luck with your future!” Jack called. “祝你未来好运！”杰克喊道。

The girl at the edge of the sea grew smaller as the raft headed east, gliding over the calm water. When they couldn’t see Heart-of-the-Wind anymore, Jack sat back and relaxed. He looked up at the sky. Gulls were circling lazily overhead. The sky was deep blue now. 海边的女孩随着木筏向东漂去，木筏在平静的水面上滑行，她越来越小了。 当他们再也看不到心之风的时候，杰克坐回原处，放松下来。 他抬头看向天空。 海鸥在头顶懒洋洋地盘旋。 天空现在变成了深蓝色。

“I hope you don’t mind that we had to leave our cell phone behind,” Annie said. “我希望你不会介意我们不得不把手机留在后面，”安妮说。

“Not at all,” said Jack. “I’m not a big cell phone user. We can tell Mom and Dad we’re sorry we left our phone…” “一点都不,” 杰克说。 “我不怎么用手机。 我们可以告诉爸爸妈妈，我们为离开手机感到抱歉……

“By the water,” Annie finished. “在水边，”安妮说完了。

“And that’s the truth,” said Jack, smiling. As he felt sea spray and the warmth of the rising sun on his face, the seagulls began to screech overhead. Jack looked up again: all the gulls were dipping up and down in the sky, shrieking and flapping. “What’s wrong with them?” he asked. “这是真的，”杰克笑着说。 当他感受到海风扑面而来，还有晨光洒在脸上时，海鸥开始在头顶上尖叫起来。 杰克再次抬头看：所有的海鸥都在天空中上下翻飞，尖叫着，拍打着翅膀。 “它们怎么了？”他问。

Annie gasped. “Oh, no! Look!” she cried, pointing behind them. 安妮惊叫起来。 “哦，天哪！ “看！”她喊道，指着他们身后。

“What?” said Jack. “什么？”杰克说。

“That! That!” shouted Annie, waving her finger. “那！ “那！”安妮喊道，挥了挥手指。

Jack couldn’t believe his eyes. 杰克简直不敢相信自己的眼睛。

A huge shark fin was moving across the water, heading for their raft. 一条巨大的鲨鱼鳍正在水面上移动，朝他们的木筏游来。
