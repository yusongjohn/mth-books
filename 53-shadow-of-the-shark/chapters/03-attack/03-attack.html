3. Attack! 3. 攻击！

The underwater coral reef was huge, and the world around it was teeming with life. Seaweed waved in the gentle current. Pink and orange coral branched out in all directions. And hundreds of fish, as colorful as jelly beans, swam among the plants and the lacy coral. 水下的珊瑚礁非常大，周围的水域里充满了生命。 海藻在轻柔的水流中摇摆。 粉红色和橙色的珊瑚向四面八方延伸出去。 成百上千条鱼，颜色鲜艳得就像果冻豆一样，在植物和珊瑚之间游来游去。

Jack saw sleek, skinny fish, spiny fish, and flat fish. He saw fish that looked like butterflies, porcupines, and spindly stars. 杰克看见了光滑细长的鱼、带刺的鱼，还有扁平的鱼。 他看见了看起来像蝴蝶、刺猬和细长星星的鱼。

As he kept swimming and looking through his mask, Jack saw green turtles feeding on algae. He saw huge crabs, jellyfish, and even a stingray among the wavy grasses. 当他继续戴着面罩游泳，四处张望时，杰克看见了绿色的海龟在吃海藻。 他看见了巨大的螃蟹、水母，甚至在波浪般的草丛中还有一条魔鬼鱼。

But nothing Jack saw scared him, and none of the creatures seemed afraid of him, either. With his mask and flippers, Jack figured he looked like just another strange sea animal. A yellow-striped fish darted directly up to his mask, looked him in the eyes, then darted off again. A school of tiny white fish swam over his body, tickling him like feathers. 但杰克看到的任何东西都没吓到他，而且这些生物看起来也不怕他。 戴着面罩和脚蹼，杰克觉得自己看起来就像另一只奇怪的海洋动物。 一条有黄色条纹的小鱼直接游到他的面罩前，盯着他的眼睛看了几秒，然后又迅速游开了。 一群白色的小鱼游过他的身体，像羽毛一样挠得他发痒。

As he drifted around the reef, Jack saw orange starfish clustered over the coral ridges and tiny yellow sea horses bobbing near the coral branches. Annie would love those little horses, he thought. 当他漂浮在珊瑚礁周围时，杰克看见橙色的海星聚集在珊瑚的山脊上，还有小黄海马在珊瑚枝附近上下摆动。 安妮一定会喜欢那些小马，他心想。

Annie! Jack had been having such a great time, he’d forgotten all about Annie! Treading water, he pulled the snorkel out of his mouth, lifted his mask, and looked around for her. The raft was far away. The offshore breeze had pushed it farther out to sea. Jack waved and shouted, “Annie!” 杰克玩得这么开心，竟然把安妮给忘了！ 他踩着水，把呼吸管从嘴里拿出来，摘下面罩，四处张望寻找她。 小船离得很远。 海风把小船吹得更远了。 杰克挥手大喊：‘安妮！’

Annie waved back. She paddled toward him. 安妮也挥手回应。 她划着船朝他游过来。

“Your turn!” Jack said, gasping, when he reached the raft. ‘轮到你了！’杰克气喘吁吁地说，他到了小筏子旁边。

“How was it?” said Annie, pulling off her tunic. ‘怎么样？’安妮一边脱下上衣一边问。

“Unbelievable!” said Jack, climbing aboard. “You’re going to love it!” He put his glasses back on and pulled off his flippers. “It’s like a beautiful garden! Turtles! Starfish. And look for the tiny yellow sea horses around the coral!” ‘太棒了！’杰克爬上了小筏子。 ‘你会喜欢的！’他重新戴上眼镜，脱下脚蹼。 ‘就像一个美丽的花园！海龟！’ ‘海星！’ 看看珊瑚周围有没有小黄海马！

“I can’t wait!” said Annie, cramming her feet into her flippers. “Here.” She handed Jack the paddle. “You have to paddle pretty hard—the wind’s pushing the raft.” “我等不及了！”，安妮把脚塞进脚蹼里。 “给你。”她把桨递给了杰克。 “你得用力划桨——风在推着小船。”

“I noticed,” said Jack. “Swim toward the beach, and you’ll see the reef. I’ll paddle after you. Then we’ll head back to shore.” “我注意到了，”杰克说。 “游向海滩，你就会看到珊瑚礁。” 我会划船追上你。 然后我们回岸边去。”

“Good plan!” Annie pulled on her mask and stuck the snorkel in her mouth. Jack balanced the raft as Annie sat on the edge. “好主意！”安妮戴上她的面罩，把呼吸管放进嘴里。 杰克稳住皮划艇，安妮坐在边缘。

“Have fun!” said Jack. “玩得开心点！”杰克说。

The raft rocked as Annie slid into the water. She started swimming back toward the reef. 安妮滑进水里时，木筏晃了一下。 她开始朝珊瑚礁游回去。

Jack tried to follow close behind her, but the current kept pushing the raft farther out to sea. For a long time, he paddled hard just to stay in place. At one point, the current was so strong the raft turned sideways. Jack struggled to turn it back toward the beach. 杰克试图紧跟在她后面，但洋流把木筏越推越远。 好长时间，他拼命划桨，只为保持原地不动。 有一会儿，洋流非常强，木筏都侧过来了。 杰克努力把木筏转回朝向海滩的方向。

When he got the raft under control again, he looked back toward the reef area. He couldn’t see Annie. The waves had gotten higher. The sea seemed vast, and not nearly as safe and friendly. 当他再次控制住木筏时，他回头看向珊瑚礁那边。 他看不到安妮了。 海浪变得更高了。 海水看起来非常广阔，一点都不安全和友好。

Jack looked in every direction. Finally he saw something farther out to sea. Can that be Annie’s snorkel? Why would she swim so far out? he wondered. 杰克向四周看了看。 最后，他看见海面上更远处有什么东西。 那是安妮的 snorkel 吗？ 她为什么游得这么远呢？他想。

“Jack!” “杰克！”

He turned around. 他转过身。

Annie was bobbing on a wave not far from the raft. “It is incredible!” she shouted, holding up her mask. “Totally incredible!” 安妮正在小筏子不远的地方，随着海浪上下起伏。 “太神奇了！”她举着面罩大声喊道。 “简直太神奇了！”

“Come on! Get aboard!” Jack yelled. “We have to go back! The current’s too strong! Or maybe the wind! Whatever, we have to get back!” “来吧！” 上车！“杰克喊道。 “我们得回去！” 水流太强了！ 或者可能是风！ “不管怎样，我们必须回去！”

Something else was bothering Jack—really bothering him. What was that thing I saw? He wondered. He turned and looked out to sea again. 还有别的事情让杰克烦恼——真的非常烦. 我刚才看到的是什么东西？他想知道. 他转过身，再次望向大海.

Jack froze in horror. That thing was still sticking up out of the water. Now it was closer, and he could see what it was. It was a fin, a large fin. The fin was slicing swiftly through the water, heading straight for them. 杰克吓得僵在原地。 那个东西还在水里露着呢。 现在它更近了，他能看清那是什么了。 那是一片鳍，一片很大的鳍。 那鳍正快速地在水里划动，直直地朝他们游来。

“GET ON THE RAFT!” Jack screamed to Annie. “HURRY!” He began frantically paddling toward her, struggling against the current. When he looked over his shoulder, he saw the huge fin still zigzagging toward them. “上筏子！”杰克朝安妮大喊。 “快点！”他拼命地划着桨朝她划去，努力地对抗着水流。 当他回头一看，他看见那片巨大的鳍还在朝他们来回游动。

“HURRY!” Jack shrieked. “快点！”杰克尖叫起来。

Annie flung herself toward the raft. Grabbing the side, she gasped for air. “What’s wrong?” she sputtered. “Why are you freaking out?” 安妮猛地朝筏子扑去。 抓住筏子的边，她喘着气。 “你怎么了？”她结结巴巴地说。 “你怎么这么紧张？”

“Just get on board!” he cried. “快上船！”他喊道。

“Why?” “为什么？”

“Don’t ask! Get on! Or die!” he yelled. “别问！快上船！ 不然就死！”他大喊。

Annie clambered aboard. “What is it?” she asked, pulling off her mask and snorkel. “What did you see?” 安妮爬上了船。 “怎么了？”她问，摘下自己的面罩和呼吸管。 “你看到了什么？”

“Look behind us!” Jack shouted. He was still struggling with the paddle, trying to point the raft toward shore. “看看我们后面！”杰克大喊。 他还在用桨挣扎，试图把筏子往岸边划。

“Ahhhh! Shark!” Annie cried. “Jack, a shark!” “啊——！ “有鲨鱼！”，安妮喊道。 “杰克，有鲨鱼！”，

“I know!” said Jack. “我知道！”，杰克说。

“Let’s go! Go!” cried Annie. “快走！快走！”，安妮喊道。

“I’m trying!” said Jack. He looked over his shoulder. The shark fin moved toward them. Then it started to circle the raft. “我正在努力！”，杰克说。 他回头看了看。 鲨鱼的鳍朝他们游了过来。 然后它开始绕着筏子转圈。

Sharks circle their prey before they attack! Jack remembered. Using all his strength, he tried to turn the raft and head back to shore. But the wind and waves were too strong. 鲨鱼在攻击前会先包围猎物！杰克记得。 他用尽全力，试图把小船转回去，朝岸边驶去。 但风和浪太大了。

“Where is it?” cried Annie. “Where did it go?” “它在哪里？”安妮喊道。 “它去哪儿了？”

With the waves rising, Jack couldn’t see the shark fin, either. “Maybe it’s gone!” he said. 随着海浪越来越高，杰克也看不到鲨鱼的鳍了。 “也许它已经走了！”他说。

“AHHHH!” screamed Annie. “啊——！”安妮尖叫起来。

The shark’s huge head came over the side of the raft, its mouth wide open. 鲨鱼巨大的头从船边露了出来，嘴巴张得大大的。

“AHHHH!” yelled Jack. Without thinking, he raised his paddle and whacked the giant shark on the nose. The shark reared up and dug its jagged teeth into the wooden paddle, ripping it out of Jack’s hand. Then the creature sank back down into the sea, and its fin glided away through the water. “啊——！”杰克大叫。 他没想那么多，举起木桨，狠狠打在巨鲨的鼻子上。 巨鲨猛地抬起头，用尖利的牙齿咬住木桨，把它从杰克手里扯了下来。 接着，那怪物又沉回海里，它的鳍在水中滑动着渐渐远去。

“It’s gone!” said Annie. “它不见了！”安妮说。

“Not gone!” cried Jack. “Look!” “没不见！”杰克喊道。 “看！”

The shark had moved away from the raft, but it was circling them again. 鲨鱼已经离开了小筏子，但它又开始绕着他们转圈。

“We need magic, Jack!” shouted Annie. “We have to use a gold coin!” “我们需要魔法，杰克！”安妮喊道。 “我们必须用金币！”

“We can only use them to have fun!” said Jack. “我们只能用金币来玩乐！”杰克说。

“You don’t think it would be fun to escape that shark?” said Annie. She pulled out the green velvet purse with the gold coins. She took out one of the three coins and held it up. The magic coin glittered in the Caribbean sunshine. “We wish to have fun escaping that shark!” she shouted. She tossed the coin high into the air. Like a tiny fireworks display, it burst into a shower of fiery red and blue sparks. “你不会觉得逃开那条鲨鱼很有趣吗？”安妮说。 她拿出装着金币的绿色天鹅绒钱包。 她拿出其中一枚金币举了起来。 这枚魔法金币在加勒比海的阳光下闪闪发光。 “我们希望可以开心地逃开那条鲨鱼！”她喊道。 她把金币高高抛向空中。 就像一场小小的烟花表演，它迸发出一片红色和蓝色的火花。

The raft suddenly spun like a top. Then it shot up the face of a large wave. 小船突然像陀螺一样旋转起来。 接着，它猛地冲上了一个大浪的浪面。

“AHHHH!” Jack and Annie screamed. “啊——！”杰克和安妮尖叫起来。

The raft crested the wave, slid down the other side, and zoomed farther out to sea. Another swell lifted the raft, then brought it down smoothly. And again, the raft shot across the surface of the water. 木筏冲上浪头，滑到另一边，然后飞快地向海的更远处冲去。 又一个浪头把木筏托起，然后平稳地落下来。 接着，木筏再次飞快地在水面上滑行。

Jack and Annie held on to each other as the raft rolled over one swell after another, rising and dipping in rhythm with the waves. Sea spray blasted them. Salt water sloshed over the sides, but magically Jack and Annie stayed aboard. 杰克和安妮紧紧抓着彼此，木筏在一波又一波的浪头上翻滚，随着海浪的节奏上下起伏。 海水飞溅到他们身上。 咸咸的海水从木筏两边泼洒过来，但神奇的是，杰克和安妮仍然留在木筏上。

“It’s like the Bullet ride at Wade City Water Park!” Annie cried. “这就像瓦德城水上乐园的子弹滑道！”，安妮喊道。

It is like a water park ride, Jack thought. But he was still in shock. Where’d the shark go? he wondered. Are there other sharks? How will we get back without a paddle? 这确实像水上乐园的游乐设施，杰克心想。 但他仍然感到震惊。 鲨鱼去哪儿了？他心想。 还有其他鲨鱼吗？ 没有桨的话，我们怎么回去呢？

“I love this!” shouted Annie, laughing. “It’s so much fun!” “我太喜欢了！”安妮一边喊着，一边笑着。 “这太好玩了！”，

Finally the exhilaration of the ride overcame Jack’s worries. As if harnessing the wind and current, the raft kept sliding up and down the swells, heading west. Seagulls followed it, shrieking overhead. 终于，这次刺激的旅程让杰克忘记了担忧。 仿佛驾驭着风和海流，木筏不断在波浪上滑上滑下，朝着西方前进。 海鸥在空中尖叫着，紧随其后。

The raft kept roller-coastering across the sea, sliding up and down over more waves. Moving with great speed, the raft seemed to know exactly where to go. 木筏像过山车一样在海上穿梭，不断在更大的浪上滑上滑下。 以惊人的速度前进，木筏似乎清楚地知道该往哪里去。

“Hey, land! Is that land?” Annie said, pointing across the water. “嘿，陆地！ “那是陆地吗？”安妮指着水的另一边说。

Jack saw a jagged rocky coastline with a strip of gleaming white sand. “Yes! Yes!” he shouted. “It’s land!” 杰克看到一片崎岖的岩石海岸，还有一条闪闪发光的白色沙滩。 “是的！是的！”他喊道。 “是陆地！”
