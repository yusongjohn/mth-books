4. Where Are We? 4. 我们在哪里？

The raft slowed down. As it floated toward the distant shore, the sea changed, becoming calm and smooth. The seagulls were silent. 木筏慢了下来。 当它漂向远处的岸边时，海水变得平静而光滑。 海鸥安静了下来。

“Where are we?” Annie asked. “我们在哪里？”安妮问。

“I’ll check,” said Jack. He reached into their waterproof bag and pulled out the travel guide. He quickly found a map inside. He pointed to the tip of Cozumel. Then he moved his finger across the Caribbean, landing on the eastern shore of the Yucatán Peninsula. Dotting the shoreline were symbols for hotels and resorts. “我去看看，”杰克说。 他把手伸进他们的防水袋，拿出了一本旅行指南。 他很快在里面找到了一张地图。 他指着科苏梅尔岛的尖端。 然后他用手指沿着加勒比海滑动，停在尤卡坦半岛的东岸。 海岸线上有许多代表酒店和度假村的符号。

Jack showed the map to Annie. “I think we’ve come to the shore of the Yucatán Peninsula,” he said. 杰克把地图展示给安妮看。 “我想我们已经来到尤卡坦半岛的海岸了，”他说。

“Cool,” said Annie. “太酷了，”安妮说。

Jack read aloud from the guide: 杰克大声读着指南上的内容：

Vacationers flock to the eastern coast of the Yucatán of Mexico. Along the coast are comfortable luxury spa hotels and four-star Mexican restaurants. Tour buses visit Mayan ruins and hidden sinkholes filled with water. 度假的人们纷纷涌向墨西哥尤卡坦半岛的东海岸。沿岸有舒适的豪华水疗酒店和四星级的墨西哥餐厅。旅游巴士会参观玛雅遗址和隐藏在水中的洞穴。

“Hidden sinkholes filled with water?” said Jack. “That sounds weird.” He kept reading: “隐藏的水坑里装满了水？”杰克说。 “那听起来很奇怪。”他继续读着：

Cruise ships dock at ports along the coast while ferries take tourists over the Caribbean to Cozumel. 游轮停靠沿岸的港口，而渡轮则载着游客前往加勒比海的科苏梅尔岛。

“Good news,” said Jack. “We can take a ferry back to Cozumel. Then we won’t have to worry about sharks anymore.” He looked around. “I wonder why we haven’t seen any ferries or cruise ships.” “好消息，”杰克说。 “我们可以乘渡轮回科苏梅尔岛。 这样我们就不用再担心鲨鱼了。”他环顾四周。 “我想知道为什么我们还没看到渡轮或游轮。”

“Maybe they’re docked in their ports now,” said Annie. “It’s late afternoon. It’ll be dark soon.” The sun was low in the sky. Soon it would sink behind the jagged cliff. “也许它们现在停泊在港口里，”安妮说。 “现在是傍晚。 很快天就要黑了。”太阳低低地挂在天空中。 很快它就会沉落在那陡峭的悬崖后面。

“Makes sense. I guess we’ll have to catch the ferry first thing tomorrow,” said Jack. “这说得通。” “我想明天第一件事就是先坐渡轮，”杰克说。

“Cool, we can stay in one of those comfortable luxury spa hotels the guide talks about,” said Annie. “Doesn’t that sound good?” “太棒了，我们可以住在导游提到的那些舒适的豪华水疗酒店之一，”安妮说。 “听起来不错吗？”

“Yeah, really good,” said Jack. He was ready for a little comfort and luxury. “是啊，真的不错，”杰克说。 他准备好享受一点舒适和奢华了。

“And get dinner, too,” said Annie, “like at a four-star Mexican restaurant.” “还能吃晚饭，”安妮说，“比如在一家四星级的墨西哥餐厅。”

“Great,” said Jack. He loved Mexican food, and he was starving. “太好了，”杰克说。 他喜欢墨西哥食物，而且他饿坏了。

“We’d just be doing our mission,” said Annie. “我们只是完成我们的任务而已，”安妮说。

“Yep,” said Jack. “Having a good time.” “对，”杰克说。 “玩得正开心。”

“Oh, look!” said Annie, pointing toward shore. “What’s that? Up there!” “哦，快看！”安妮说，指着岸边。 “那是什么？在那儿！”

Jack saw stone buildings high on top of the cliff. “I’ll bet those are Mayan ruins,” he said, “like that pyramid we saw in Cozumel.” 杰克看到悬崖顶部有石头建筑。 “我敢打赌那是玛雅遗迹，”他说，“就像我们在科苏梅尔看到的那座金字塔。”

“Look it up,” said Annie. “查一下，”安妮说。

Jack flipped through their travel guide until he found a photo that looked like the same rocky cliff with the same stone buildings. “I found it,” he said. He read aloud: 杰克翻阅他们的旅行指南，直到找到一张看起来和同样岩石悬崖以及同样石头建筑的照片。 “我找到了，”他说。他大声读出来：

Yucatán Park is a tourist attraction on the east coast of the Yucatán. In ancient times it was a port city for Mayans sailing around the Caribbean. Today, it’s a theme park that welcomes vacationers from all over the world. 尤卡坦公园是尤卡坦东海岸的一个旅游景点。古代时，这里是玛雅人环加勒比海航行时的港口城市。如今，它是一个主题公园，欢迎来自世界各地的游客。

“A theme park!” said Annie. “What’s the theme?” “主题是什么？”

Jack turned the page and saw Mayan dancers in tall feather headdresses. “I think the theme is ancient Mayan life.” 杰克翻过一页，看到穿着高大羽毛头饰的玛雅舞者。 “我觉得这个主题是古代玛雅人的生活。”

“Oh, that’s cool,” said Annie. “哦，这太酷了，”安妮说。

Jack read more: 杰克继续读下去：

Tourists flock to song-and-dance spectaculars that take them through Mayan history. A walking path connects the park to a hotel zone. 游客们纷纷前往观赏结合歌舞表演的精彩节目，这些节目带他们了解玛雅的历史。一条步行小路将公园和酒店区连接起来。

“Wow, song-and-dance spectaculars!” said Annie. “I’d love to see one of those!” “哇，歌舞表演真精彩！”安妮说。 “我真想亲眼看看那样的表演！”

“I’d love to find a luxury hotel first,” said Jack. “我真想先找到一家豪华酒店，”杰克说。

“Watch out!” said Annie. The raft was heading for a pile of rocks at the edge of the water. “小心！”安妮喊道。 木筏正朝着水边的一堆石头冲去。

“Turn!” Jack shouted. “转方向！”杰克大喊。

Amazingly, the raft swerved to the right and missed the rocks. A wave then carried it into a sheltered cove below the cliff. The raft slid over bubbly foam at the edge of the sea and stopped on pebbly wet sand. 神奇的是，木筏向右一转，避开了那些岩石。 接着，海浪把木筏带到了悬崖下方一个隐蔽的海湾里。 木筏滑过海浪边缘的泡沫，停在了湿漉漉的鹅卵石沙滩上。

Their magic ride had come to an end. 他们的神奇旅程到此结束了。

“Safe!” said Annie. “Our wish came true. We had a fun ride.” “安全了！”安妮说。 “我们的愿望实现了。 我们玩得真开心。”

“A lot of fun,” said Jack. Even the terrifying shark attack didn’t seem like such a big deal anymore, especially now that they could catch a ferry back to Cozumel in the morning. “玩得非常开心，”杰克说。 就连刚才那可怕的鲨鱼攻击，现在看起来也不那么严重了，特别是因为他们明天早上可以乘渡轮返回科苏梅尔岛。

As Jack and Annie packed up their gear, the sun was sinking behind the rocky bluff, turning the sky pink, then purple. Jack’s face was warm with sunburn, but his body felt chilled in the sudden cool air. He was looking forward to settling into a nice hotel room. 当杰克和安妮收拾好装备时，太阳正渐渐沉落在岩石峭壁后面，天空变成了粉色，然后是紫色。 杰克的脸被晒伤了，红红的，但突然变凉的空气让他感觉身体发冷。 他正期待着住进一间舒适的酒店房间。

“Okay!” he said. “Let’s find that hotel zone.” “好啊！”他说。 “我们去找找那家酒店区域吧。”

“Let’s check out the Mayan theme park first,” said Annie. “我们先去参观一下玛雅主题公园吧，”安妮说。

“It must be closed by now,” said Jack. “It’s almost nighttime.” “现在应该已经关门了吧，”杰克说。 “都快到晚上了。”

“Okay,” said Annie, sighing. “But we have to visit it first thing tomorrow before we go back.” “好吧，”安妮叹了口气说。 “不过明天我们回去之前，一定要先去那里参观。”

“Sure,” said Jack. “I’d love to know more about Mayan history.” He and Annie put on their flip-flops and stepped off the raft. “Uh-oh, I just thought of something.”

“What?” said Annie.

“We need money,” said Jack.

“Oh, right, for a hotel and dinner and the theme park,” said Annie.

“And ferry tickets,” said Jack.

Annie grinned. “No problem!” she said. “We use one of our gold coins to wish for money so we can have a good time.”

Jack laughed. “Okay,” he said. “That should work.”

He reached in their bag, found the velvet purse, and took out a second gold coin. “We’ll just have one left after we use this one.”

“I know,” said Annie. “But I think we can have lots of fun here even without magic.”

“So how much should we wish for?” asked Jack.

“How about five hundred?” said Annie.

“What? Are you serious?” said Jack. “No kid carries around five hundred dollars.”

“Well, then maybe we should wish for a credit card instead?” said Annie.

“Yeah, and how do you expect Morgan to pay a credit card bill?” said Jack.

“Okay. Back to five hundred dollars. We need enough for the luxury spa hotel, a four-star dinner, breakfast, and tickets for the theme park and ferry.”

“Okay, okay,” said Jack. “Five hundred. I still think it’s too much.” He held up the coin. “Get ready for dollar bills to rain down on us.”

“Ready,” said Annie. She held out both hands.

“We wish for five hundred dollars—to have a good time!” said Jack. He tossed the gold coin into the air. Again, like tiny fireworks, it burst into a shower of fiery red and blue sparks. When the sparks cleared, nothing rained down from the air.

“Oh, no,” said Jack. “It didn’t work.”

“Yes it did! Look!” said Annie. Sitting in the middle of the raft was a neat stack of crisp green bills. She picked them up and counted. “Hurray! We have ten fifty-dollar bills.”

“Great!” said Jack. “We can probably change them into Mexican money—pesos—when we get to the hotel.”

“No problem,” said Annie. “Give me the purse. We’ll put the money in with our last gold coin.”

Jack handed Annie the purse, and she stuffed the wad of bills inside.

“Before we go, we should pull the raft higher up on the sand so it doesn’t wash out to sea,” said Jack. “Maybe someone else can use it—if they can find a paddle.”

Jack and Annie leaned over and gripped the raft.

“Heave ho!” said Annie. And together they pulled it higher up on the beach.

“Wait. Hear that?” said Annie. “Listen! It’s coming from up there!”

The sounds of musical instruments were coming from the top of the cliff. “The theme park is open!” said Annie. “Maybe they’re doing one of those song-and-dance spectaculars we read about! Let’s go see!”

“I’d rather just get to a hotel,” said Jack. “We’ve had a full day.”

“Oh, please,” begged Annie. “Let’s check out the show really fast, and then we’ll go straight to the hotel, I promise.”

Jack listened to the music for a moment. It was filled with the haunting sound of flutes and thumping drums. It sounded mysterious and joyful. “Okay,” he said.

“Come on!” Annie started across the sand.

Jack grabbed their bag. “How do we get up the cliff?” he said, hurrying after her.

“I saw some steps when we were coming in!” said Annie. “They’re just around those rocks!”

As Jack followed Annie around a pile of boulders, a cool wind blew across the sand. The full moon had turned the sea to silver.
