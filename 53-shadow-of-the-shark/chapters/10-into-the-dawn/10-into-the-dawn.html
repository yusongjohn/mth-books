10. Into the Dawn 10. 进入黎明

“Not again!” Jack cried. “Didn’t Heart-of-the-Wind wish for a safe ride?” “又来了！”杰克喊道。 “心之风不是希望有一次安全的旅程吗？”

“I don’t know!” said Annie. “I thought I made it clear!” “我不知道！”安妮说。 “我以为我说得很清楚了！”

The shark fin sliced through the sea, following the swiftly moving raft. 鲨鱼的鳍在海中划过，紧跟着快速移动的木筏。

Jack didn’t know what to do. They’d used their last coin! He stared in shock at the huge fin. Though the shark seemed to be keeping its distance, it swam steadily after the raft. 杰克不知道该怎么办。 他们用掉了最后一枚硬币！ 他惊愕地盯着那巨大的鳍。 虽然鲨鱼似乎保持了一定距离，但它稳步地跟在木筏后面游动。

“Maybe he won’t attack!” said Annie. “也许它不会攻击！”安妮说。

Annie had barely said these words before the shark shot forward, its humongous body rising above the surface of the sea. It was as big as a whale! Its brown skin was speckled with white spots. It had a flattened head with a blunt snout and a huge mouth at least five feet wide! 安妮话音刚落，鲨鱼就猛地向前冲去，它庞大的身躯从海面升起。 它和鲸鱼一样大！ 它棕色的皮肤上布满了白色斑点。 它的头扁扁的，鼻子又圆又钝，嘴巴大得至少有五英尺宽！

The shark dipped under the raft! Then the raft was lifted into the air and held aloft. The raft was sitting on the back of the shark! 鲨鱼从木筏下面钻了过去！ 接着，木筏被举到空中，稳稳地悬着。 木筏正坐在鲨鱼的背上！

“AHHHH!” screamed Jack and Annie. They grabbed each other and held on tightly. As the raft teetered back and forth on the shark’s back, Jack expected to be hurled into the waves. He closed his eyes, waiting for the worst. “啊——！”杰克和安妮尖叫起来。 他们紧紧抓住彼此，牢牢地抱住。 木筏在鲨鱼的背上左右摇晃，杰克以为自己会被抛进海浪里。 他闭上眼睛，等着最糟糕的事情发生。

But nothing bad happened. The raft kept going, and the wind kept blowing. 但什么坏事情都没发生。 木筏继续向前，风也一直吹着。

“Open your eyes, Jack!” Annie said. “睁开眼睛，杰克！”安妮说。

Jack opened his eyes. Swimming close to the surface of the water, the shark perfectly balanced the raft on its back. 杰克睁开了眼睛。 鲨鱼在水面上游动，它把筏子稳稳地平衡在背上。

“What is he doing?” said Jack. “它在干什么？”杰克说。

“I don’t know!” shouted Annie. “Maybe he’s taking us to Cozumel! Maybe that’s what Heart-of-the-Wind wished for!” “我不知道！”安妮喊道。 “也许它要把我们带到科苏梅尔！ 也许这就是心之风的愿望！”

“What?” cried Jack. “什么？”杰克喊道。

“For a shark to help us have a fun, safe ride!” shouted Annie. “让鲨鱼帮我们来一次又好玩又安全的旅程！”安妮喊道。

“That’s crazy!” cried Jack. “这太疯狂了！”杰克喊道。

“It’s wonderful!” said Annie. “太棒了！”安妮说。

Wonderful? thought Jack. Riding on the back of a monster shark? 很棒？杰克心想。 骑在一条巨鲨的背上？

“I’ll bet he’s a friendly shark!” cried Annie. “Look up sharks in the travel guide!” “我敢打赌他是一条友好的鲨鱼！”安妮喊道。 “去旅行指南里查查鲨鱼吧！

“Oh, brother,” said Jack. But as the shark gently carried them across the waves, he reached into the bag and pulled out their travel guide. With the wind whipping the pages, he looked in the index for shark. His eyes caught two words: shark, whale. “哦，天哪，”杰克说。 但当鲨鱼温柔地带着他们穿过海浪时，他伸手到包里，拿出他们的旅行指南。 在风的吹拂下，书页哗哗作响，他翻到目录里查找鲨鱼。 他的眼睛看到了两个词：鲨鱼，鲸鱼。

“Whale shark! That’s it!” Jack shouted. He found the right page and read aloud: “鲸鲨！ “就是这里！”杰克喊道。 他找到了正确的那一页，大声读起来：

The whale shark is the biggest fish in the world. Every year, hundreds of these gigantic creatures migrate to waters off the Yucatán. Whale sharks eat only sea plants and fish and do not harm people. In fact, sometimes these gentle giants allow divers to hitch a ride. 鲸鲨是世界上最大的鱼。每年，成百上千条这种巨大的生物会迁徙到尤卡坦海域。鲸鲨只吃海藻和鱼，不会伤害人。事实上，有时这些温和的庞然大物会允许潜水员搭便车。

“See! I told you!” cried Annie. “Whale sharks don’t harm people. This is the miracle Heart-of-the-Wind wanted to give us!” “看！ 我告诉过你！”安妮喊道。 “鲸鲨不会伤害人。 这就是心之风想要送给我们的奇迹！”

Holding the raft on his back, the giant shark kept swimming across the Caribbean, heading into the light of the sunrise. 巨鲨背着木筏，继续在加勒比海中游动，朝着日出的光芒前进。

Overhead, seagulls, terns, and pelicans careened and cawed raucously. They seemed to be laughing at the shark swimming with the raft on his back. Jack began to relax and enjoy himself. The nutty seabirds, the salty air, and the giant shark’s speedy rolling motion through the water, rising and falling with the waves, made the ride extra spectacular. Jack wanted it to last forever. 头顶上，海鸥、 terns 和鹈鹕飞来飞去，大声地叫着。 它们似乎在嘲笑那只背着木筏游泳的鲨鱼。 杰克开始放松，享受起来。 那些古怪的海鸟、咸咸的空气，还有那条大鲨鱼在水里快速滚动着，随着海浪上下起伏，让这次旅程格外精彩。 杰克希望这一刻能永远持续下去。

“Land ho!” Annie shouted. “陆地来了！”安妮喊道。

Jack shaded his eyes and squinted at the horizon. In the distance he could see Cozumel—the sugary-white sand, the stone pyramid, and the palm trees. 杰克用手遮住眼睛，努力向地平线望去。 在远处，他看到了科苏梅尔岛——那白色的沙滩、石头金字塔和棕榈树。

When they drew close to shore, the shark dipped lower in the water, leaving the raft floating on the surface for a moment. Then the raft began skimming across the sea on its own, heading for the beach, until finally it came to rest on the hard, wet sand. 当他们靠近岸边时，鲨鱼在水里游得更低了，让小船在水面上漂浮了一小会儿。 接着，小船自己开始在海面上滑行，朝着海滩前进，直到最后停在了坚硬潮湿的沙滩上。

Jack and Annie jumped off and looked back at the water. They saw the huge fin moving out to sea. 杰克和安妮跳下船，回头看向水面。 他们看到那巨大的鱼鳍正慢慢游向大海。

“Bye! Thanks!” Annie shouted, waving. “再见！” 谢谢！”安妮喊道，挥了挥手。

The whale shark’s fin vanished into the sparkling horizon. 鲸鲨的鳍消失在闪闪发光的天际线上。

Neither Jack nor Annie spoke for a moment. Then Jack heaved a sigh. Exhausted, sunburned, and salty from sea spray, he put the damp travel guide into the bag. He handed Annie her flip-flops, and he put his on, too. Then he slung the bag over his shoulder. “Ready?” 杰克和安妮一时都没有说话。 然后杰克叹了一口气。 又累又晒伤，还被海浪打得满身盐水，他把湿漉漉的旅行指南放进了袋子里。 他给安妮递上她的人字拖，然后他也穿上自己的。 接着，他把背包甩到肩膀上。 “准备好了吗？”，

“Ready,” said Annie. “准备好了，”安妮说。

They walked through the warm sand to the palm tree with the rope ladder. Annie climbed up first, and Jack followed. 他们穿过温暖的沙子，走向挂着绳梯的棕榈树。 安妮先爬了上去，杰克跟在后面。

Inside the tree house, a breeze rustled the palm leaves. Jack picked up the Pennsylvania book. “Ready?” he said again. 树屋里，一阵微风吹动着棕榈叶。 杰克捡起了那本宾夕法尼亚的书。 “准备好了吗？”，他又问了一遍。

Annie stood at the window, looking out at the turquoise-blue sea. She turned to Jack with a big grin. “That was some vacation,” she said. 安妮站在窗边，望着外面碧蓝的海水。 她转过身对杰克露出灿烂的笑容。 “那真是一次难忘的假期，”她说。

“Yep. Some vacation,” Jack agreed. He shook his head in wonder and then pointed at a picture of the Frog Creek woods. “I wish we could go there.” “是啊， 真是一次难忘的假期，”杰克同意道。 他惊讶地摇了摇头，然后指着一张蛙溪镇森林的图片。 “我希望我们能去那里。”

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来了。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转起来。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。

A breeze blew into the tree house. Jack smelled summer leaves and pine needles. “Ah, home,” he said. 一阵微风吹进了树屋。 杰克闻到了夏天的树叶和松针的味道。 “啊，家，”他说。

“Home,” said Annie, smiling. “It’s always nice to come home after a vacation.” “家，”安妮微笑着说。 “假期结束后回家总是让人很开心。”

“But did you have a good time?” said a voice from nearby. “但你们玩得开心吗？”附近传来一个声音。

“Teddy!” Jack and Annie said together. “泰迪！”杰克和安妮异口同声地说。

“I thought I’d come back to welcome you home.” The young sorcerer was sitting on a limb of the oak tree. Holding on to the branch, he climbed nimbly through the tree house window. “So. Did you? Have a good time?” he said. “我以为我该回来欢迎你们回家。”年轻的法师正坐在橡树的一根树枝上。 他抓着树枝，灵巧地从树屋的窗户爬了进去。 “所以。你们玩得—— 开心吗？”他说道。

“Yes!” said Annie. “The best!” “是的！”安妮说。 “超级开心！”

“Even though it wasn’t exactly what we expected,” said Jack. “即使这并不是我们预期的那样，”杰克说。

“What happened? Did your snorkel equipment not work properly?” said Teddy. “发生了什么？ “你的潜水呼吸管设备没有正常工作吗？”泰迪说道。

“No, our gear was fine. What surprised us was the shark attack,” said Jack. “不，我们的装备很好。” 让我们惊讶的是鲨鱼攻击，”杰克说。

“And being captured by ancient Mayan warriors,” said Annie. “还有被古代玛雅战士抓住，”安妮说。

“And escaping through a swampy forest,” said Jack. “而且从一片沼泽森林里逃了出来，”杰克说。

“And then traveling through the Underworld, where the ancestor spirits live,” said Annie. “然后又穿越了冥界，那里住着祖先的精灵，”安妮说。

“And almost becoming the next Great Sun of Palenque,” said Jack. “差点成了帕伦克的下一任大太阳，”杰克说。

“I see…,” said Teddy. “But other than that, you had fun?” “我明白了……，”泰迪说。 “不过除此之外，你玩得开心吗？”

Jack and Annie laughed. 杰克和安妮笑了。

“Seriously, what happened?” asked Teddy. “真的吗？发生了什么？”泰迪问。

“Seriously, what we just said is exactly what happened,” said Annie. “真的，我们刚才说的，就是刚刚发生的事，”安妮说。

“Let me explain,” said Jack. He unzipped their swim bag, pulled out their travel guide, and pointed to the illustration of the pyramid on the cover. “By pointing to this picture, we went back to ancient Mexico—like Mexico more than a thousand years ago.” “让我来解释，”杰克说。 他拉开他们的游泳包，拿出旅行指南，指着封面金字塔的插图。 “通过指着这张图片，我们回到了古代墨西哥——就像一千多年前的墨西哥。”

“Ah, I see…,” said Teddy. “So no luxury spa hotels?” “啊，我明白了……”，泰迪说。 “那没有豪华水疗酒店吗？”

“Nope,” said Jack. “没有，”杰克说。

“But with help from your magic coins, everything worked out fine,” said Annie. “We actually had a really great time, and we think we might have helped a Mayan girl become the ruler of her people.” “但有了你们的魔法硬币帮忙，一切都很顺利，”安妮说。 “我们其实玩得非常开心，我们觉得我们可能帮助了一位玛雅女孩成为她人民的统治者。”

“Really?” said Teddy. “真的吗？”泰迪说。

“Really. And we hitched a ride on the back of the biggest shark on the planet,” said Jack. “真的。 我们还搭了地球上最大的鲨鱼的背去旅行，”杰克说。

“Literally,” said Annie. “We used all your coins to have fun.” “字面意思，”安妮说。 “我们用你所有的硬币去玩了。”

“Speaking of that, you can have your coin purse back,” said Jack. He reached again into the swim bag and took out the small velvet purse. Before he handed it to Teddy, he opened it. All the fifty-dollar bills were gone. “It’s empty!” he said. “说到这个，你可以拿回你的钱袋了，”杰克说。 他又一次伸手到游泳袋里，拿出那个小天鹅绒钱袋。 在把它递给泰迪之前，他打开了它。 里面所有的五十美元纸币都不见了。 “里面空空的！”他说。

Annie shrugged. “I guess the money was only meant for our vacation, and it turns out we didn’t need money at all.” 安妮耸了耸肩。 “我想这钱只是打算用来度假的，结果我们根本不需要钱。”

Jack laughed. “Easy come, easy go,” he said. He gave the coin purse to Teddy, and Annie handed over the travel guide. 杰克笑了。 “来得容易，去得快，”他说。 他把钱袋给了泰迪，安妮递上了旅行指南。

“You must tell me more of the story sometime,” said Teddy. “你得 sometime 告诉我更多这个故事，”泰迪说。

“We will. But right now we should head home,” said Jack. He felt a little homesick. It was probably because they had talked to Heart-of-the-Wind about missing their parents. “我们会的。 但现在我们应该回家了，”杰克说。 他感到有点想家。 可能是因为他们跟心之风聊过，说很想念爸爸妈妈。

“Please thank Morgan and Merlin for our dream vacation,” said Annie. “请代我们感谢摩根和梅林，让我们度过了这么棒的假期，”安妮说。

“Indeed,” said Teddy. “Perhaps they will send you on another one soon.” “确实如此，”泰迪说。 “也许他们很快就会再送你们一次旅行。”

“Not too soon, I hope,” said Annie. “We need to recover from this one first.” “我希望不要太快，”安妮说。 “我们得先从这次假期中恢复过来。”

“Yeah, we need a vacation from our vacation,” said Jack. “是啊，我们需要从这次假期中休息一下，”杰克说。

Teddy laughed. 泰迪笑了。

Jack picked up their swim bag and started down the rope ladder. Annie followed. Teddy was looking out the window when they reached the ground. “Good-bye!” he called. “I hope to see you again in these woods!” 杰克拿起他们的游泳包，开始顺着绳梯往下走。安妮跟在后面。 当他们到达地面时，泰迪正望着窗外。 “再见！”他喊道。 “希望你再次在这片森林里见到我们！”

A blinding light swirled upward around the trunk of the oak—and in a flash, the magic tree house was gone. 一道刺眼的光围绕着橡树的树干向上旋转——转眼间，神奇的树屋就消失了。

“Good-bye,” Annie said softly. “再见，”安妮轻声说道。

“Home,” said Jack. He strapped the bag onto his bike rack. Then he and Annie climbed on their bicycles and headed down the rough path between the trees. They bumped over roots and fallen bark and pine needles, until they came out of the Frog Creek woods and onto their street. Then they pedaled to their house. “回家，”杰克说道。 他把包绑在自行车的车架上。 然后他和安妮爬上自行车，沿着树间的小路往下滑去。 他们颠簸着越过树根、掉落的树皮和松针，直到他们走出蛙溪镇的森林，来到自己的街道上。 然后他们骑车回家。

Jack and Annie parked their bikes in the garage and headed up to their porch. 杰克和安妮把自行车停在车库里，然后朝家里的门廊走去。

“Mom! Dad!” Annie shouted as they went through the front door. “We’re back from snorkeling!” “妈妈！ “爸爸！”安妮一边往门口走一边喊道。 “我们从浮潜回来了！”

“Come tell us all about it!” their dad called from the kitchen. “来给我们讲讲吧！”他们的爸爸从厨房喊道。

“And have some pizza!” said their mom. “It just came out of the oven.” “再吃点披萨吧！”他们的妈妈说道。 “它刚从烤箱里出来。”

“Thanks! Be right there!” said Jack. “We have to check something real fast.” “谢谢！ 我马上来！“杰克说。 “我们必须马上检查一件事。

“What are we checking?” Annie asked as she followed him into the living room. “我们在检查什么？”安妮一边跟着他走进客厅一边问。

“Something online,” said Jack. He sat down at the computer and typed the search words Mayan ruler, Heart-of-the- Wind. “网上有东西，”杰克说。 他坐在电脑前，输入了搜索词：玛雅统治者，Heart-of-the-Wind。

An entry popped up immediately. Jack clicked on it, then read aloud: 立刻出现了一条信息。 杰克点开了它，然后大声读出来：

Yohl Ik’nal was the ruler of the Mayan city of Palenque from 583 to 604. Her name means “Heart of the Wind.” Yohl Ik’nal是583年到604年玛雅城市帕伦克的统治者。 她的名字意思是“风的心”。

“Ohhh! YAY!” said Annie. “哦！太棒了！”，安妮说。

“Wait,” said Jack. He read on: “等一下，”杰克说。他继续读：

She became queen after the death of her father, the Great Sun of Palenque. She was the first female ruler in recorded Mayan history. She must have come to the throne due to extremely unusual circumstances, the details of which have not survived. 她在父亲——帕伦克的大太阳去世后成为了女王。 她是历史上第一位被记录的玛雅女性统治者。 她一定是遇到了非常特别的情况，才来到王位上的，但具体细节已经没人知道了。

Jack and Annie looked at each other. “That is so cool,” Annie whispered with awe. 杰克和安妮互相看了看。 “这也太酷了，”安妮惊叹着小声说。

“I guess a cell phone message from a kid more than a thousand years in the future could be considered an unusual circumstance,” said Jack. “我想，一个来自一千多年前的未来孩子的手机信息，可以算是一种特别的情况，”杰克说。

“Yep. Extremely,” said Annie. “是啊。 特别得不得了，”安妮说。

Jack smiled and shut down the computer. “I’m starving,” he said. “Let’s get some pizza.” 杰克笑了笑，关掉了电脑。 “我饿了，”他说。 “我们去吃披萨吧。”
