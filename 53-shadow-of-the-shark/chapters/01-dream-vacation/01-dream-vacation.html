1. Dream Vacation 1. 梦想假期

“Did you see anything amazing?” Annie asked Jack. They were standing on the warm, sunny shore of Frog Creek Lake, packing up their new swimming gear—flippers, masks, snorkels, and red life vests. “你看到什么神奇的东西了吗？”安妮问杰克。 他们正站在蛙溪镇湖温暖、阳光明媚的岸边，收拾他们的新游泳装备——脚蹼、面罩、呼吸管和红色救生衣。

“Not really,” said Jack. “Weeds and rocks.” “没有，”杰克说。 “只是水草和石头。”

“Same here,” said Annie. “How was your new mask?” “我也是，”安妮说。 “你的新面罩怎么样？”

“Cool,” said Jack as he put his glasses back on. “It didn’t leak or fog up at all.” “真酷，”杰克一边把眼镜重新戴上，一边说道。 “一点都没漏水，也没起雾。”

“Great,” said Annie, pulling a tunic over her bathing suit. “太好了，”安妮一边说，一边把一件长袍套在泳衣上。

Jack and Annie stepped into their flip-flops. Jack picked up the waterproof swim bag with all their gear, and they headed to the bike rack. “I’d love to snorkel around a coral reef someday,” he said. 杰克和安妮穿上了人字拖。 杰克拿起防水的游泳包，里面装着他们的所有装备，然后他们朝自行车架走去。 “我真希望有一天能潜入珊瑚礁里看看，”他说。

“Me too!” said Annie. “我也是！”安妮说。

Jack strapped the bulky bag to the rack over his back wheel. He and Annie pulled on their helmets and pedaled out of the lake parking lot. 杰克把那个又大又重的包绑在自行车后轮的架子上。 他和安妮戴上头盔，骑车离开了湖边的停车场。

“Remember when Randy and Jenny snorkeled around a coral reef last spring?” Jack said. “还记得去年春天兰迪和珍妮在珊瑚礁周围浮潜的情景吗？”杰克说。

“Oh, right, they went to a place called Cozumel with their parents,” Annie said. “They did all kinds of cool stuff. Hey, should we call them? They said they’d be back from their aunt’s house by two.” “哦，对了，他们和父母一起去过一个叫科苏梅尔的地方，”安妮说。 “他们做了很多很酷的事情。” 嘿，我们应该叫他们吗？ 他们说会在两点前从姑姑家回来。

“Sure,” said Jack. “当然，”杰克说。

“Hold on, stop,” said Annie. “抓紧，停下，”安妮说。

Jack and Annie pulled their bikes over to the side of the quiet road. Annie reached into her handlebar bag and took out the old cell phone their dad had given them when he had gotten a newer model. “Hey, we have a text from Randy.” She read the message. “Darn. He says they’re not coming back until tomorrow.” 杰克和安妮把自行车停在安静的路边。 安妮伸手到她车把上的包里，拿出他们爸爸在换新手机时给他们的旧手机。 “嘿，我们收到了兰迪的短信。”她读出信息。 “该死。” 他说他们要到明天才会回来。”

“That’s okay. Let’s just go home, then,” said Jack. He started pedaling again and turned at the corner. He started up their street, heading for their house. “没关系。 “那我们回家吧，”杰克说。 他又开始踩踏板，然后在拐角处转了方向。 他沿着他们的街道骑了上去，朝他们的家驶去。

“Jack, stop!” Annie called from behind him. “杰克，停下！”安妮从他身后喊道。

Jack stopped his bike and looked back. Annie was speeding after him. “What’s wrong?” he called. 杰克停下了自行车，回头看了看。 安妮正飞快地朝他追来。 “怎么了？”他喊道。

She screeched to a halt. “We have a message from Teddy!” she cried, waving a yellow piece of paper. “When I put the phone away, I noticed this in my handlebar bag!” 她猛地刹住了车。 “我们收到泰迪的消息！”她喊道，挥舞着一张黄色的纸片。 “我把电话收起来的时候，发现这个在我的车把包里！”

“How did it get there?” said Jack. “它是怎么到那里的？”杰克问。

“I don’t know!” said Annie. “Teddy must have put it there when we were snorkeling! Or maybe when my bike was in our yard or parked at the library! Or maybe—” “我不知道！”安妮说。 “泰迪一定是在我们浮潜的时候把它放进去的！ 或者在我自行车停在我们院子里或者停在图书馆的时候！或者可能——”

“Okay, okay, just read it to me,” said Jack. “好吧，好吧，你读给我听吧，”杰克说。

Annie read breathlessly: 安妮急促地读道：

Jack and Annie, 杰克和安妮。

Please meet me at the tree house as soon as possible. 请尽快到树屋来见我。

Teddy 泰迪

“Let’s go!” said Jack. “我们走吧！”杰克说。

Annie put the message back in the bag, and she and Jack sped up the street. When they reached the edge of the Frog Creek woods, they turned and rode their bikes between the trees, bouncing over roots, pine needles, and leaves. 安妮把信息放回袋子里，她和杰克加快速度沿着街道骑了上去。 当他们到达蛙溪镇森林的边缘时，他们调转方向，骑着自行车在树间穿行，跳过树根、松针和树叶。

“Teddy hasn’t come to Frog Creek in a long time!” said Annie. “泰迪已经很久没有来蛙溪镇了！”安妮说。

“I know!” said Jack. “我知道！”杰克说。

“I hope nothing’s wrong back in Camelot!” said Annie. “I hope Morgan and Merlin are all right.” “我希望卡梅洛特那边一切安好！”安妮说。 “我希望摩根和梅林都还好。”

“Yeah, and Kathleen!” said Jack. “对了，还有凯瑟琳！”杰克说。

“And Penny!” said Annie. “And Arthur—and, well, all of Camelot!” “还有彭妮！”安妮说。 “还有亚瑟，还有……整个卡梅洛特！”

Jack and Annie soon came to the tallest oak. 杰克和安妮很快就来到了最高的橡树前。

The magic tree house was back. A freckle-faced teenager with tousled red hair was leaning out of the window. 神奇的树屋又回来了。 一个脸上有雀斑、红头发凌乱的少年正从窗户里探出头来。

“Teddy!” Annie cried, waving. “泰迪！”安妮喊道，挥了挥手。

“Hello!” he called. “Climb on up!” “你好！”他喊道。 “快上来！”

Annie and Jack pulled off their helmets, left their bikes leaning against the tree, and climbed up the rope ladder. Inside the tree house, they both hugged Teddy. 安妮和杰克摘下头盔，把自行车靠在树边，然后爬上了绳梯。 他们走进树屋，两个人都拥抱了泰迪。

“What’s happening?” said Jack. “Is everyone okay?” “发生什么事了？”杰克说道。 “大家都还好吗？”

“Oh, yes! Camelot is doing quite well,” said Teddy. “哦，很好！ 卡梅洛特现在过得非常好，”泰迪说。

“Good,” said Annie. “太好了，”安妮说。

“Do Merlin and Morgan have a new mission for us?” said Jack. “梅林和摩根给我们有新的任务吗？”杰克问。

“Indeed they do!” said Teddy. “确实有！”泰迪说。

“What do they want us to do?” asked Annie. “他们想让我们做什么？”安妮问。

“They want you to have a fantastic time,” said Teddy. “他们想让你们玩得非常开心，”泰迪说。

“What does that mean?” said Jack. “这是什么意思？”杰克问。

“Merlin and Morgan believe they have never properly thanked you for all your hard and dangerous work,” said Teddy. “梅林和摩根认为他们从来没有好好感谢过你们所做的一切辛苦又危险的工作，”泰迪说。

“Oh, sure they have,” said Annie. “They always thank us.” “哦，当然有，”安妮说。 “他们总是感谢我们的。”

Teddy smiled. “Well, today they want to thank you in a very special way: they want to send you on a holiday.” 泰迪笑了。 “嗯，今天他们想用一种特别的方式感谢你：他们想送你去度假。”

“A holiday? You mean like a vacation?” said Jack. “度假？ 你是说像去度假吗？”杰克说。

“Yes, a vacation. I believe that’s what you call it,” said Teddy. “是的，去度假。 我想这就是你们说的。”泰迪说。

“Oh, man, we were just talking about that,” said Jack. “哦，天哪，我们刚刚还在谈论这个呢，”杰克说。

“Wonderful!” said Teddy. “Then their timing is perfect. The question is: where would you most like to go?” “太棒了！”泰迪说。 “那他们的时机就刚刚好。” 问题是：你最想去哪里？

“Well, we were just talking about a place called Cozumel,” said Annie. “嗯，我们刚谈到一个叫科苏梅尔的地方，”安妮说。

“Cozumel?” said Teddy. “I do not believe I have heard of that land.” “科苏梅尔？”泰迪说。 “我不认为我听说过那个地方。”

“It’s an island in the Caribbean Sea, next to the Yucatán Peninsula,” said Jack. “It’s part of Mexico.” “它是加勒比海中的一座岛屿，位于尤卡坦半岛旁边，”杰克说。 “它属于墨西哥。”

“We want to go there because it’s a great place for snorkeling,” said Annie. “我们想去那里，因为那里是浮潜的好地方，”安妮说。

“Snorkeling?” said Teddy, wrinkling his nose. “What is that?” “浮潜？”泰迪皱着鼻子说。 “那是什么？”

Jack laughed. “You swim in a lake or ocean and look underwater through a mask.” 杰克笑了。 “你在湖里或海里游泳，用面罩看水下。”

“And breathe through a plastic tube,” said Annie. “然后通过塑料管呼吸，”安妮说。

“Our lake isn’t great for snorkeling,” said Jack. “But our friends Randy and Jenny went to a beach in Cozumel last spring, and they said the snorkeling was incredible.” “不过我们的湖不太适合浮潜，”杰克说。 “但我们的朋友兰迪和珍妮去年春天去了科苏梅尔的海滩，他们说那里的浮潜非常棒。”

“Ah, I see. Then Cozumel is where you shall go,” said Teddy. He reached into his cloak and pulled out a wand. He waved it in a circle over the tree house floor, whispering words that Jack couldn’t understand, except for Cozumel. Slowly, magically, a small paperback book took shape on the floor. “啊，我明白了。 那么你就要去科苏梅尔，”泰迪说。 他伸手到斗篷里，拿出一根魔杖。 他用魔杖在树屋地板上画了个圈，低声念着杰克听不懂的咒语，除了科苏梅尔这个词。 慢慢地，神奇地，一本小平装书在地板上成形了。

Teddy picked up the book. “Your travel guide,” he said proudly. “Cozumel and the Yucatán Peninsula.” 泰迪捡起了那本书。 “这是你的旅行指南，”他骄傲地说。 “科苏梅尔和尤卡坦半岛。”

图中文字：Cozumel and the

Yucatán Peninsula 图中文字：科苏梅尔岛和尤卡坦半岛

He showed the book to Jack and Annie. The cover was a collage of several small paintings and photos. One showed a large, fancy hotel, another showed a stone pyramid, and a third showed a person snorkeling. 他把这本书拿给杰克和安妮看。 封面是一些小画和照片的拼贴画。 一个显示了一座又大又豪华的酒店，另一个显示了一座石制金字塔，第三个显示的是一位人在水下潜水。

Teddy turned the book over and read from the back cover: 泰迪把书翻过来，从书的背面读道：

Visit Cozumel and the Yucatán Peninsula! Stay in a luxury spa hotel! See ancient Mayan pyramids and ruins! Snorkel in the clear blue water of the Caribbean Sea! 去科苏梅尔和尤卡坦半岛吧！住在豪华水疗酒店！看看古代玛雅金字塔和遗迹！在加勒比海清澈的蓝色水中浮潜！

“Oh, wow! My dream vacation!” said Annie. “哦，哇！”，安妮说。 “我的梦想假期！”，安妮说。

“I’ll get our snorkeling gear!” said Jack. “我要去拿我们的浮潜装备！”，杰克说。

“I’ll grab our phone to take photos!” said Annie. “我来拿我们的手机拍照！”安妮说。

Jack and Annie hurried down the rope ladder. Annie took their cell phone from her handlebar bag and put it in one of the pockets of her tunic. Jack grabbed their swim bag, and they climbed up to Teddy. 杰克和安妮赶紧顺着绳梯往下爬。 安妮从她的把手包里拿出他们的手机，放进她长袍的一个口袋里。 杰克拿起了他们的游泳包，他们爬到了泰迪那里。

“We’re ready!” said Annie. “我们准备好了！”安妮说。

Teddy handed her the travel book. “Take this to guide you,” he said. “And take this, too.” He reached into his cloak and pulled out a green velvet coin purse. He handed the purse to Jack. 泰迪把旅行手册递给了她。 “拿这个来指引你们，”他说。 “还有这个也要拿。”他伸手到他的斗篷里，拿出一个绿色的天鹅绒钱袋。 他把钱袋递给了杰克。

Jack opened the purse and took out three golden coins. “What are these?” 杰克打开钱袋，拿出三枚金色的硬币。 “这是什么？”

“We want you to have as much fun as possible on your holiday,” said Teddy. “Each of these coins contains a bit of magic. Throw one into the air, make a wish, and your wish shall come true.” “我们希望你们在假期里玩得尽兴，”泰迪说。 “每枚硬币里都有一小点魔法。 把它抛向空中，许个愿，你的愿望就会实现。”

“Seriously? That’s great!” said Annie. “真的吗？ 太棒了！”安妮说。

“Yes,” said Teddy. “You may wish for anything! The only requirement is that the magic be used to help you have fun.” “是的，”泰迪说。 “你可以许任何愿望！ 唯一的要求是，魔法必须用来帮助你玩得开心。”

“This is going to be a great trip,” said Jack. He put the purse with the magic coins into his bag. “这次旅行一定会很棒，”杰克说。 他把装着魔法硬币的钱包放进袋子里。

“To Cozumel now you must go!” said Teddy. “现在你们必须去科苏梅尔！”泰迪说。

“But, wait—are you sure we don’t have a mission of some kind?” said Jack. They’d never been on a journey without an assignment from Morgan or Merlin. “但是，等等——你确定我们没有某种任务吗？”杰克说。 他们以前从未在没有摩根或梅林布置任务的情况下出发旅行。

“Yes, indeed, you do have a mission,” said Teddy. “As I told you, your mission is to have a wonderful time.” “是的，确实，你们有一个任务，”泰迪说。 “正如我告诉你的，你们的任务就是玩得开心。”

“Nothing else?” said Jack. “没有别的任务吗？”杰克说。

“Nothing else,” Teddy said with a smile. “没有别的，”泰迪微笑着说。

“Wow,” said Annie. “Can you come, too, and have a wonderful time at the beach with us?” “哇，”安妮说。 “你能一起来，和我们在海滩上玩得开心吗？”

“I wish I could,” said Teddy. “But I have to do more errands for Morgan and Merlin today—none quite as delightful as this one, however. You must tell me all about your journey when we meet again.” “我希望我能去，”泰迪说。 “但今天我得帮摩根和梅林跑更多的差事——不过没有哪一件会像这次这样让人开心。 等我们再见面时，你一定要告诉我你这次旅程的全部经历。”

“Of course we will!” said Annie. “Come back to Frog Creek soon.” “当然我们会再见面的！”安妮说。 “很快回来蛙溪镇吧。”

“I plan to,” said Teddy. “Do you have all you need?” “我打算去，”泰迪说。 “你带齐所有需要的东西了吗？”

“Yes!” said Jack. “And you don’t need to magically change our clothes. We’re already dressed for the beach.” “是！”杰克说。 “而且你们不需要用魔法把我们的衣服变掉。 我们已经穿好去海滩的衣服了。”

“Excellent. Go now,” said Teddy. “太棒了。”，泰迪说。 “现在出发吧，”泰迪说。

Annie pointed at the cover of the travel guide. “I wish we could go there,” she said. 安妮指着旅行指南的封面。 “我希望我们能去那里，”她说。

“To have a wonderful time,” added Jack. “去玩得开心，”杰克补充道。

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
