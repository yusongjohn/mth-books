7. Fogbound 7. 大雾笼罩

At the rocks, the sea gulls still screeched. Jack could barely see the steep path leading down into the fog. 在岩石边，海鸥还在尖声叫着。 杰克几乎看不见通向雾中的陡峭小路。

“Go slowly,” Jack whispered to Annie as they started down. “慢点走。”杰克对安妮小声说，他们开始往下走。

“Whoops!” said Annie. She slipped and fell forward, bumping into Jack. “My foot got caught in my stupid dress—” “哎哟！”安妮说。 她滑倒了，向前摔去，撞上了杰克。 “我的脚被这讨厌的裙子绊住了——”

“Shh!” said Jack. “嘘！”杰克说。

He held on to Annie. They listened as pebbles and rocks rolled down the cliff. 他紧紧抓住安妮。 他们听着卵石和岩石滚下悬崖。

Jack took a deep breath. 杰克深吸了一口气。

“We’ve got to watch out for Vikings, too,” he whispered. “我们还得留神维京人。”他低声说。

They started down the steep path again. They went one step at a time. The sound of the waves against the rocks grew louder. 他们又开始沿着陡峭的小路往下走。 他们一次只走一步。 海浪拍打岩石的声音越来越大。

Finally, they stepped down onto a flat strip of pebbles. 终于，他们走下一片平坦的卵石地带。

“Where are we?” whispered Annie. “我们在哪儿？”安妮小声说。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “我不知道。”杰克说。

“Oh, look!” said Annie. She pointed to the shoreline. “哦，看！”安妮说。 她指向海岸线。

Through the fog rose the serpent prows of the Viking ships! 雾中升起维京船的蛇形船首！

Jack and Annie crept closer to the ships. Their sails were down. Each ship had been tied to a tall jagged rock. They seemed deserted as they bobbed in the shallow waves. 杰克和安妮悄悄靠近那些船。 它们的帆都放下了。 每艘船都拴在一块高高的、参差不齐的岩石上。 它们在浅浪里轻轻晃着，看上去空无一人。

Jack really wanted to check out the ships. But he was afraid of wasting time. 杰克很想去看看那些船。 可他又怕浪费时间。

“We’d better find the tree house,” he said to Annie. “我们最好找到树屋。”他对安妮说。

They crept away from the three Viking ships. 他们悄悄离开那三艘维京船。

Suddenly, they both froze. 突然，他们都愣住了。

Through the mist, they saw a group of Viking warriors. The Vikings were looking at the top of the cliff. 透过雾气，他们看见一群维京武士。 维京人正看着悬崖顶。

Their long yellow hair hung down from beneath their iron helmets. They carried round wooden shields and swords and axes. 他们长长的黄头发从铁头盔下面垂下来。 他们拿着圆形木盾、刀剑和斧头。

“It looks like they’re planning to climb the cliff,” whispered Annie. “看起来他们打算爬悬崖。”安妮小声说。

“We need to hide until they go,” whispered Jack. “Then we can look for the tree house.” “我们得先藏起来，等他们走。”杰克小声说。 “然后我们再找树屋。”

“Let’s hide in a ship!” said Annie. “咱们藏到船里！”安妮说。

“Good idea,” said Jack. “好主意。”杰克说。

They crept back to where the ships were tied. 他们悄悄回到系船的地方。

Jack was happy to see that the sides of the smallest ship were very low. They would easily be able to climb over them. 杰克很高兴看到最小那艘船的船舷很低。 他们会很容易翻过去。

“You go first,” said Annie. “你先上。”安妮说。

Jack waded through the shallow water. It was cold! 杰克涉过浅水。水很冷！

He reached the ship and grabbed one side. He pushed himself up and onto the ship’s deck. 他走到船边，抓住一侧。 他撑起身子，爬上了船的甲板。

The ship jerked forward. Jack looked at the shore. It was now thirty feet away. The ship’s anchoring rope was pulled tight. The serpent prow bobbed up and down with the waves. 船猛地往前一冲。 杰克看着岸边。 现在离岸已经有三十英尺远了。 船上的锚绳被拉得很紧。 蛇形船首随着波浪上下晃动。

The fog and the movement of the ship made Jack feel as if he was in a dream. For a moment, he forgot to be scared of the Vikings. 雾气和船的晃动让杰克觉得自己像在做梦。 有那么一会儿，他忘了要害怕维京人。

“This is so cool,” he called. “Come on, Annie!” “这太酷了。”他喊道。 “快点，安妮！”

Annie started to wade out to the ship. Suddenly, she disappeared. 安妮开始蹚水走向船。 突然，她消失了。

“Annie?” called Jack. “安妮？”杰克喊道。

Her head popped out of the water. She splashed with her arms. 她的头突然从水里冒了出来。 她用胳膊拍打着水。

“It—it’s deep!” she gasped. “My dress—too heavy!” “好——好深！”她喘着气说。 “我的裙子——太沉了！”

“Use the rope!” called Jack. “Like when we climbed the stairs!” “用绳子！”杰克喊道。 “就像我们爬台阶时那样！”

Annie grabbed the rope that stretched from ship to shore. It held her weight as she began inching along it. 安妮抓住了从船拉伸到岸边的绳子。 绳子承受住了她的重量，她开始沿着绳子一点点挪过去。

“Hold on tight!” cried Jack. “抓紧！”杰克喊道。

“I—I am!” gasped Annie. “我——我抓紧了！”安妮喘着气说。

She kept going, hand over hand, along the rope, toward the ship. 她双手交替，沿着绳子继续朝船的方向挪。

When Annie got close to the ship, Jack reached out to help her. As he pulled her in, the side of the ship dipped down. 安妮靠近船时，杰克伸出手去帮她。 他把她拉上船时，船舷向下沉去。

Then the rope went slack, and the Viking ship slid out to sea. 接着绳子松了，那艘维京船滑向了大海。
