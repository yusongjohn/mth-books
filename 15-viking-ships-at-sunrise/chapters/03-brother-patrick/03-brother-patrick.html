3. Brother Patrick 3. 帕特里克修士

Jack’s glasses were wet with mist. He quickly wiped them, then looked up. 杰克的眼镜被雾水弄湿了。 他赶紧把眼镜擦了擦，然后抬起头。

A man in a brown robe stood before him. The man had a round red face. He was bald, except for a fringe of hair around his head. 一个穿棕色袍子的男人站在他面前。 那人有一张又圆又红的脸。 他秃着头，只在脑袋周围留着一圈头发。

Nearby the rope was tied around a tree. 附近，绳子拴在一棵树上。

“I—I’m not an invader,” said Jack. “我——我不是入侵者。”杰克说。

“He’s Jack!” said Annie. She was standing behind the man. “I’m Annie. We’re from Frog Creek, Pennsylvania.” “他是杰克！”安妮说。 她站在那人后面。 “我是安妮。 我们来自宾夕法尼亚的蛙溪镇。”

“We—we come in peace,” stammered Jack. “我——我们是和平来的。”杰克结巴着说。

The man’s blue eyes twinkled. 那人的蓝眼睛闪着光。

“Oh, do you now?” he said. “I wondered what was going on. I had dropped the rope so I could climb down the steps. But you two grabbed it instead. How in the world did you get on this island?” “哦，是吗？”他说。 “我还在纳闷发生了什么事。 我放下绳子，好顺着台阶往下爬。 可你们俩反而抓住了它。 你们究竟是怎么上这座岛的？”

Jack stared at the man. He didn’t know how to explain the magic tree house. 杰克盯着那人。 他不知道该怎么解释魔法树屋。

“In our boat,” Annie said quickly. “在我们的船上。”安妮赶紧说。

The man looked puzzled. “Not many boats can come ashore at this dark, early hour.” 那人一脸困惑。 “这么黑、这么早，能靠岸的船可不多。”

“Well, we’re very good sailors,” said Annie. “嗯，我们可是很棒的水手。”安妮说。

Oh, brother, thought Jack. He hoped their sailing skills wouldn’t be tested. 哎呀，真是的，杰克想。 他希望他们的航海本领可别真被考到。

“Where exactly are we?” asked Annie. “And who exactly are you?” “我们到底在哪儿？”安妮问。 “你到底是谁？”

“You’re on an island off the coast of Ireland,” the man said. “And I am Brother Patrick.” “你们在爱尔兰海岸外的一座岛上。”那人说。 “我是帕特里克修士。”

“Whose brother are you?” said Annie. “你是谁的兄弟？”安妮说。

The monk smiled. “The ‘brother’ means I’m a Christian monk.” 修道士笑了。 “‘修士’的意思是我是一名基督教修道士。”

“Oh, you’re one of the monks who saved civilization!” said Annie. “哦，你是那些拯救了文明的修道士之一！”安妮说。

The man smiled again. 那人又笑了。

Annie turned to Jack and whispered, “Let’s show him our cards. I trust him.” 安妮转向杰克，小声说：“咱们把卡给他看。我信任他。”

“Okay,” said Jack. He trusted the monk, too. “好。”杰克说。 他也信任这位修道士。

They both pulled out their secret library cards and showed them to Brother Patrick. 他们都抽出秘密图书卡，给帕特里克修士看。

The M’ s and L’ s for Master Librarian shined in the gray light. 代表图书大师的 M 和 L 在灰光里闪闪发亮。

The monk looked at them and bowed his head. 修士看着他们，低下了头。

“Welcome, my friends,” he said. “欢迎，我的朋友们。”他说。

“Thank you,” said Jack and Annie. “谢谢。”杰克和安妮说。

“I did not truly think you were invaders,” said Brother Patrick. “But on our small island, we are careful of strangers.” “我并不是真的以为你们是入侵者。”帕特里克修士说。 “但在我们这座小岛上，我们对陌生人很小心。”

“Why?” said Annie. “为什么？”安妮说。

“There are terrible stories about Viking raiders,” he said. “When we see their serpent ships, we must hide or be taken as slaves.” “关于维京劫掠者，有些很可怕的故事。”他说。 “一看见他们的蛇船，我们就必须躲起来，否则就会被抓去当奴隶。”

“Serpent ships?” said Jack. “蛇船？”杰克说。

“The prows of their ships are often carved in the shape of a serpent’s head,” said Brother Patrick. “I am afraid it stands for their fierce, cold-blooded ways.” “他们的船头常常雕成蛇头的形状。”帕特里克修士说。 “恐怕它象征着他们凶猛、冷血的行事方式。”

Jack looked at the misty gray sea. 杰克望着雾蒙蒙的灰色大海。

“Do not worry,” said Brother Patrick. “They cannot land safely on this island before daylight. They’re not as good sailors as some people.” He winked at Annie. “别担心。”帕特里克修士说。 “天亮之前他们没法安全登上这座岛。 他们不像有些人那么会航海。”他对安妮眨了眨眼。

“Too bad for them,” she said cheerfully. “那他们可就倒霉了。”她高兴地说。

“But tell me, why have you come here?” asked Brother Patrick. “可告诉我，你们为什么来这儿？”帕特里克修士问。

“Oh,” said Jack. “I almost forgot.” “哦。”杰克说。 “我差点忘了。”

He pulled Morgan’s paper from his leather bag. He showed the Latin words to the monk. 他从皮袋里抽出摩根的纸。 他把拉丁文给修道士看。

erpens Magna 图中文字：Serpens Magna

“This is the title of a story that we have to take back to our friend and teacher, Morgan le Fay,” said Annie. “这是一个故事的标题，我们得把它带回去给我们的朋友兼老师摩根·勒菲。”安妮说。

“I see … ” said Brother Patrick. He gave Jack and Annie a mysterious look. “我明白了……”帕特里克修士说。 他神秘地看了杰克和安妮一眼。

What’s he thinking? Jack wondered. 他在想什么？杰克想。

But the monk changed the subject. 可修士把话题岔开了。

“I think you would like to visit our monastery,” he said. “我想你们会愿意去看看我们的修道院。”他说。

“What’s that?” said Annie. “那是什么？”安妮说。

“The place where we monks live and work,” said Brother Patrick. “Come.” “我们修道士生活和工作的地方。”帕特里克修士说。 “来吧。”

“But the sun’s not up yet,” said Jack. “Won’t the others be asleep?” “可太阳还没升起来。”杰克说。 “别人不是还在睡觉吗？”

“Oh, no,” said Brother Patrick. “In the summer, we rise long before daylight. We have much to do. You’ll see.” “哦，不。”帕特里克修士说。 “夏天，我们天不亮很久就起床了。 我们有很多事要做。你们会看到的。”

The monk led them up a dirt path. Jack hoped their book was at the monastery. He wanted to leave the gloomy island with its threat of Viking invaders as soon as possible. 修道士领着他们走上一条土路。 杰克希望他们要找的书在修道院里。 他想尽快离开这座阴沉的岛，以及维京入侵者的威胁。

A low bell started to ring. Jack saw a lonely church steeple against the gray sky ahead. 一口低沉的钟开始敲响。 杰克看见前方灰色天空下，一座孤零零的教堂尖塔。
