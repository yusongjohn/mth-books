1. Before Dawn 1. 黎明之前

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开眼睛。

A thin gray light came through his window. His clock read 5 A.M. All was quiet. 一道灰蒙蒙的微光从窗户透进来。 他的钟显示凌晨五点。一切都很安静。

Today we’re going to ancient Ireland, he thought, back more than a thousand years. 今天我们要去古代爱尔兰，他想，回到一千多年前。

Morgan le Fay had told him that it was a very dangerous time, with Vikings raiding the coasts. 摩根·勒菲告诉过他，那是个非常危险的时代，维京人会突袭海岸。

“You awake?” came a whisper. “你醒了吗？”传来一声耳语。

Annie stood in his doorway. She was dressed and ready to go. 安妮站在他的门口。 她已经穿好衣服，准备出发了。

“Yeah, meet you outside,” said Jack as he climbed out of bed. “嗯，外面见。”杰克一边下床一边说。

He pulled on his jeans, T-shirt, and sneakers. He put his secret library card into his backpack with his notebook and pencil. Then he hurried downstairs. 他穿上牛仔裤、T恤和运动鞋。 他把秘密图书卡连同笔记本和铅笔放进背包。 然后他匆匆下了楼。

Annie was waiting for him in their yard. 安妮在院子里等他。

The air was damp and misty. 空气潮湿，还起了雾。

“Ready?” she asked. “准备好了吗？”她问。

Jack took a deep breath. 杰克深吸了一口气。

“I guess,” he said. He was a little worried about the Vikings. “应该吧。”他说。 他对维京人有点担心。

They walked silently over the dewy grass. Then they ran up their street and into the Frog Creek woods. 他们默默走过带露水的草地。 然后他们跑上街道，进了蛙溪树林。

Mist clung to the trees as they walked through the dark woods. 他们穿过黑暗的树林时，雾气附着在树上。

“It’s hard to see,” said Jack. “很难看清。”杰克说。

“Where’s the tree house?” asked Annie. “树屋在哪儿？”安妮问。

“I have no idea,” said Jack. “我不知道。”杰克说。

Just then something fell in front of them. 就在这时，一样东西落在他们面前。

“Watch out!” shouted Jack. He covered his head. “小心！”杰克喊道。 他护住头。

“The ladder!” cried Annie. “梯子！”安妮喊道。

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开眼睛。

The rope ladder from the magic tree house dangled in front of them. 魔法树屋里的绳梯在他们面前晃荡着。

Jack looked up. The tree house was hidden in the mist. 杰克抬起头。 树屋隐藏在雾里。

“Come on, let’s go,” said Annie. “快点，咱们走。”安妮说。

She grabbed the ladder and started up. Jack followed. 她抓住梯子往上爬。杰克跟着。

They climbed through the wet air and into the tree house. 他们爬过潮湿的空气，进了树屋。

“Hello,” said Morgan. “I’m glad to see you.” “你好。”摩根说。 “见到你们真高兴。”

She was sitting in the corner. At her feet were the scroll they’d brought back from Roman times and the bamboo book from ancient China. 她坐在角落里。 她脚边放着他们从罗马时代带回来的卷轴，还有来自古代中国的竹书。

“I’m so glad to see you,” said Jack. “真高兴见到你。”杰克说。

“Me too,” said Annie. “我也是。”安妮说。

“It’s good that you both came early,” said Morgan. “幸好你们俩都来得早。”摩根说。

She reached into the folds of her robe and pulled out a piece of paper. 她把手伸进袍子的褶里，抽出一张纸。

“Here’s the ancient story you must find today,” she said. “这是你们今天必须找到的古代故事。”她说。

Morgan handed the paper to Jack. On it were the words: 摩根把纸递给杰克。 上面写着：

图中文字：S

erpens Magna 图中文字：S erpens Magna

The mysterious writing reminded Jack of the writing from their trip to the Roman town of Pompeii. 那神秘的文字让杰克想起他们去罗马城镇庞贝时见过的文字。

“That looks like Latin,” he said. “看起来像拉丁文。”他说。

“Very good,” said Morgan. “It is Latin.” “很好。”摩根说。 “是拉丁文。”

“But I thought they spoke Latin in ancient Rome,” said Annie. “Aren’t we going to Ireland?” “可我以为只有古罗马才说拉丁文。”安妮说。 “我们不是要去爱尔兰吗？”

“You are,” said Morgan. “But during the Dark Ages in Europe, educated people wrote in Latin.” “是的。”摩根说。 “不过在欧洲的黑暗时代，受过教育的人用拉丁文写作。”

“The Dark Ages?” said Jack. “黑暗时代？”杰克说。

“Yes,” said Morgan. “The time after the fall of the Roman Empire.” “是的。”摩根说。 “罗马帝国灭亡之后的时代。”

“Why is it called dark?” said Jack. “为什么叫黑暗？”杰克说。

“It was a difficult time,” said Morgan. “People had to work very hard just to feed and clothe themselves. There was not a lot of time for playing, learning, or making art and music.” “那是一个艰难的时代。”摩根说。 “人们得非常辛苦地干活，才刚够填饱肚子、穿上衣服。 没有多少时间玩耍、学习，或创作艺术和音乐。”

Morgan pulled a book from her robe. 摩根从袍子里抽出一本书。

“Your research,” she said, handing it to Annie. The title read: Ireland Long Ago. “你们的研究资料。”她说着，把书递给安妮。 书名是：《很久以前的爱尔兰》。

“Remember,” said Morgan. “Your research book will guide you. But in your darkest hour—” “记住，”摩根说。 “你们的研究书会指引你们。 但在你们最危急的时刻——”

“Only the ancient story can save us,” Jack and Annie said together. “只有古代故事能救我们。”杰克和安妮一起说。

“And remember this,” said Morgan. “It must be your darkest hour, when there is no hope left. If you ask for help too soon, it will not come.” “还有，记住这个。”摩根说。 “必须是你们最黑暗的时刻，当希望都没了。 如果你们太早求助，它不会来。”

“And we have to find the story first,” said Annie. “而且我们得先找到那个故事。”安妮说。

“That is true,” said Morgan. “Do you have your secret library cards?” “确实如此。”摩根说。 “你们带着秘密图书卡了吗？”

Jack and Annie nodded. 杰克和安妮点了点头。

“Show them to the wisest person you meet,” said Morgan. “把它们拿给你们遇见的最有智慧的人看。”摩根说。

“Don’t worry,” said Annie. “I think we’re ready now.” “别担心。”安妮说。 “我想我们现在准备好了。”

Annie pointed at the cover of the Ireland book. 安妮指着爱尔兰书的封面。

“I wish we could go there,” she said. She gave Morgan a little wave. “See you soon.” “我希望我们能去那儿。”她说。 她向摩根轻轻挥了挥手。 “再见。”

“Good luck!” said Morgan. “祝你们好运！”摩根说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后，一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止。
