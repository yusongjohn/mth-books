4. Books of Wonder 4. 奇妙之书

The monastery had a stone wall all around it. 修道院四周环绕着石墙。

Brother Patrick took Jack and Annie through the gate. Beyond the gate was a small church with a hanging bell. 帕特里克修士带着杰克和安妮进了大门。 大门更远处是一座小教堂，上面挂着一口钟。

There was also a vegetable garden and six stone huts shaped like giant beehives. 还有一座菜园，以及六座形状像巨大蜂巢的石屋。

“We grow all our own food,” said Brother Patrick. “Carrots, turnips, spinach, wheat, and beans.” “我们所有食物都自己种。”帕特里克修士说。 “胡萝卜、芜菁、菠菜、小麦和豆子。”

He led them to the entrance of the first hut. Jack and Annie peeked inside. A monk was pulling flat bread from a low stone oven. 他领他们到第一座小屋的入口。 杰克和安妮往里偷看。 一位修士正从低矮的石炉里取出薄面饼。

“This is our bakery,” Brother Patrick said. “这是我们的面包房。”帕特里克修士说。

“It smells good!” said Annie. “好香啊！”安妮说。

“Come along,” Brother Patrick said. “跟我来。”帕特里克修士说。

He pointed to each hut as they passed. 他们经过时，他指着每一座小屋。

“There are our sleeping quarters,” he said. “And that’s where we spin our cloth. In here, we cobble our sandals. There we carve our wooden tools.” “那边是我们睡觉的宿舍。”他说。 “那边是我们纺布的地方。 这里，我们做凉鞋。 我们在那儿雕刻木制工具。”

In each of the huts, Jack and Annie could see monks. They were busy spinning or cobbling or carving. 在每一座小屋里，杰克和安妮都能看见修道士。 他们正忙着纺线、做鞋或雕刻。

Finally, Brother Patrick came to the largest beehive-like hut. 最后，帕特里克修士来到最大的蜂巢状小屋。

“I have saved the best for last,” he said. “This is where we do our most important work.” “我把最好的留在了最后。”他说。 “这是我们做最重要工作的地方。”

He stepped inside. 他走了进去。

Jack and Annie followed. 杰克和安妮跟着。

The hut was warm and peaceful, yet very alive. It glowed with the golden light of many candles. 小屋温暖而宁静，却又充满生气。 它因许多蜡烛的金光而发亮。

Monks sat at wooden tables. Some were reading. Others played chess. Best of all, some were writing and painting in books. 修道士们坐在木桌旁。 有的在读书。 有的在下棋。 最棒的是，有些人正在书上书写和绘画。

“This is our library,” said Brother Patrick. “Here we study math, history, and poetry. We play chess. And we make books.” “这是我们的图书馆。”帕特里克修士说。 “我们在这里学习数学、历史和诗歌。我们下象棋。 我们还制作图书。”

“Jack,” said Annie. “I think this is it.” “杰克，”安妮说。 “我想就是这儿了。”

“What?” said Jack. “什么？”杰克说。

“Civilization!” said Annie. “文明！”安妮说。

Brother Patrick laughed. 帕特里克修士笑了。

“Yes, this is where civilization hides,” he said. “On top of our lonely island in the sea.” “是的，这就是文明藏身的地方。”他说。 “就在我们这座海上孤岛的顶端。”

“Oh, man,” said Jack. “I love this place.” “天哪。”杰克说。 “我爱这个地方。”

“What kind of books do you make here?” asked Annie. “你们在这儿做什么样的书？”安妮问。

“Books of wonder,” said Brother Patrick. “We record Christian stories as well as the old myths of Ireland.” “奇迹之书。”帕特里克修士说。 “我们记载基督教故事，也记载爱尔兰的古老神话。”

“Myths?” said Jack. “神话？”杰克说。

“Yes,” said Brother Patrick. “They were gathered from our storytellers—the old women who sing the tales of long ago, when people believed in magic.” “是的。”帕特里克修士说。 “它们是从我们的说书人那里搜集来的——那些老妇人唱着很久以前的故事，那时人们还相信魔法。”

“Wow,” said Annie. “哇。”安妮说。

“Come,” said Brother Patrick, “look at the book of Brother Michael. He has worked on it his whole life.” “来，”帕特里克修士说，“看看迈克尔修士的书。 他为此倾注了整整一生。”

Brother Patrick led Jack and Annie over to an old monk. The monk was painting a blue border around one of the pages in a book. 帕特里克修士把杰克和安妮带到一位老修士那里。 那位修士正在给书中的一页画蓝色的装饰边。

“Michael, these two Master Librarians from faraway would like to see your work,” said Brother Patrick. “迈克尔，这两位远道而来的图书管理员大师想看看你的作品。”帕特里克修士说。

The old monk looked up at Jack and Annie. His wrinkled face broke into a smile. 老修道士抬头看着杰克和安妮。 他布满皱纹的脸上突然露出了微笑。

“Welcome,” said Brother Michael in a thin, shaky voice. “欢迎。”迈克尔修士用细弱、颤抖的声音说。

“Hi,” said Annie. “你好。”安妮说。

Brother Michael showed them the cover of his book. It was decorated with gleaming red and blue jewels. 迈克尔修士给他们看他的书的封面。 它装饰着闪亮的红宝石和蓝宝石。

Then he turned the pages. Each was covered with fancy writing and delicate paintings in green, gold, and blue. 然后他翻开书页。 每一页都满是花哨的文字，还有绿色、金色和蓝色的精美绘画。

“I wish I could paint like that,” said Annie. “真希望我也能画成那样。”安妮说。

“It’s beautiful,” whispered Jack. “真美。”杰克小声说。

“Thank you,” said Brother Michael. “谢谢。”迈克尔修士说。

“How do you make a book like this?” asked Annie. “这样的书是怎么做的？”安妮问。

“I write on sheepskin and use goose quill pens,” said Brother Michael. “My paints are made of earth and plants.” “我在羊皮上写字，用的是鹅毛笔。”迈克尔修士说。 “我的颜料是用泥土和植物做的。”

“Wow,” said Annie. “哇。”安妮说。

“Show Michael what you are seeking,” Brother Patrick said. “把你们在找的东西给迈克尔看看。”帕特里克修士说。

“Oh, right!” said Jack. He pulled out the paper Morgan had given them. He showed the Latin writing to the old monk. “哦，对！”杰克说。 他抽出摩根给他们的那张纸。 他把拉丁文给老修道士看。

Brother Michael nodded. 迈克尔修士点了点头。

“Yes,” he said with a smile. “I know that one quite well.” “是的。”他笑着说。 “那一篇我非常熟悉。”

Brother Michael turned to the page he had been painting with a blue border. He pointed to the writing at the top of the page. 迈克尔修士翻到他正在画蓝色边框的那一页。 他指着页面顶部的文字。

“Oh, man,” whispered Jack. “天哪。”杰克小声说。

The words were: 文字是：
