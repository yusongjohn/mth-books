6. The Vikings Are Coming! 6. 维京人来了！

“Annie!” Jack cried. “Vikings!” “安妮！”杰克喊道。 “维京人！”

Annie looked out the tree house window. “Vikings?” 安妮从树屋窗户往外看。 “维京人？”

“They’re headed straight for the island!” said Jack. “他们正朝这座岛直冲过来！”杰克说。

He turned back to the stone steps. 他转回石阶。

“Where are you going?” cried Annie. “你去哪儿？”安妮喊道。

“To warn the monks!” Jack said. “去提醒修道士们！”杰克说。

“I’ll come, too!” cried Annie. She scrambled out of the tree house. “我也去！”安妮喊道。 她急忙从树屋里爬出来。

“Hurry!” said Jack. “快点！”杰克说。

Jack didn’t even use the rope. He pulled himself up the steep steps with his hands. 杰克甚至没用绳子。 他用手把自己拉上陡峭的台阶。

As Jack and Annie climbed up the face of the cliff, clouds began to cover the sun. When they reached the top, a fog had almost hidden the serpent ships. 杰克和安妮顺着悬崖正面往上爬时，云开始遮住了太阳。 他们到达山顶时，雾几乎已经把那些蛇船遮住了。

“Run!” cried Annie. “跑！”安妮喊道。

The fog blanketed the whole island. Jack and Annie could barely see the path to the monastery. 雾覆盖了整座岛。 杰克和安妮几乎看不见通向修道院的小路。

When they arrived at the gate, the misty white world was silent. 他们到达大门时，雾蒙蒙的白色世界一片寂静。

“Vikings!” Jack cried. “Vikings!” “维京人！”杰克喊道。 “维京人！”

“The monks are still in church!” said Annie. She yanked the bell rope. “修道士们还在教堂里！”安妮说。 她猛拽了一下钟绳。

Dong! Dong! 咚！咚！

Jack and Annie watched as Brother Patrick and the other monks ran out of the church. 杰克和安妮看着帕特里克修士和其他修道士跑出教堂。

“The Vikings are coming!” Jack shouted. “维京人来了！”杰克喊道。

Brother Patrick’s rosy face turned white. 帕特里克修士红润的脸变得煞白。

“Make haste!” he said to the other monks. “Gather the books and hide.” “赶快！”他对其他修道士说。 “把书收起来藏好。”

The monks ran into the library. Brother Patrick turned to Jack and Annie. 修道士们跑进图书馆。 帕特里克修士转向杰克和安妮。

“We have a secret hiding place, a cave on the other side of the island,” he said. “You can come with us. But I am not certain you will be safe.” “我们有一个秘密藏身处，是岛另一边的一个洞穴。”他说。 “你们可以跟我们走。 但我不能确定你们会安全。”

“Don’t worry,” said Jack. “We’re going to try to go home.” “别担心。”杰克说。 “我们要试着回家。”

“Do not use the steps,” Brother Patrick said. “The Vikings will climb them.” “别走台阶。”帕特里克修士说。 “维京人会爬那些台阶。”

“Then how do we get down?” said Jack. “那我们怎么下去？”杰克说。

“Go that way,” said Brother Patrick, pointing. “At the cliff’s edge are two large rocks. A path between those rocks will take you down to the shore. Then you can walk around to your boat.” “走那边。”帕特里克修士指着说。 “悬崖边有两块大岩石。 那些岩石之间的一条小路会带你们下到岸边。 然后你们可以绕到船那儿。”

“Thanks!” said Annie. “谢谢！”安妮说。

“Be careful!” said Brother Patrick. He hurried inside the library. “小心！”帕特里克修士说。 他匆匆进了图书馆。

“Wait!” came a thin voice as Jack and Annie turned to run. “等等！”杰克和安妮刚要跑时，一个细细的声音传来。

It was Brother Michael. He hobbled over to them, holding out his book of Irish tales. 是迈克尔修士。 他一瘸一拐地走到他们跟前，拿出他那本爱尔兰故事书。

“Take it,” he said. “拿着。”他说。

“Are you sure?” Jack asked. He knew it was Brother Michael’s life’s work. “你确定吗？”杰克问。 他知道那是迈克尔修士倾注毕生心血的作品。

“Please,” said Brother Michael. “It is better that the world should have some of it than none at all. Just in case … ” “请拿着。”迈克尔修士说。 “这个世界有一部分总比一点都没有好。以防万一……”

“We’ll take good care of it,” said Jack. He gently placed the jeweled book in his leather bag. “我们会好好保管它的。”杰克说。 他轻轻把那本镶着宝石的书放进皮袋里。

“Good luck!” said Annie. “祝你们好运！”安妮说。

Annie and Jack waved good-bye to the old man. Then they raced to the rocks that Brother Patrick had told them about. 安妮和杰克向老人挥手告别。 然后他们朝帕特里克修士告诉他们的那些岩石跑去。
