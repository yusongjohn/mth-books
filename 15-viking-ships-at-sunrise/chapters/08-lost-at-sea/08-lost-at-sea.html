8. Lost at Sea 8. 海上迷航

Annie fell to the deck of the ship. 安妮摔倒在船的甲板上。

Jack pulled the rope out of the water. The end of it was still tied in a loop. 杰克把绳子从水里拉出来。 绳子的末端还挽成一个圈。

“What happened?” asked Annie. “发生了什么？”安妮问。

“We’re heading out to sea,” said Jack. “I guess all the pulling lifted the rope off the rock.” “我们正向出海的方向漂。”杰克说。 “我猜是我们一拉，把绳子从岩石上提了起来。”

Annie sat up and looked at the misty whiteness. 安妮坐起来，看着雾蒙蒙的白茫茫一片。

“I can’t see the island,” she said. “我看不见岛了。”她说。

“I can’t see anything, ” said Jack. “我什么也看不见。”杰克说。

Annie looked at Jack. 安妮看着杰克。

“Do you think this is our darkest hour?” she asked. “你觉得这是我们最黑暗的时刻吗？”她问。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “Maybe the book will help.” “我不知道。”杰克说。 “也许书能帮忙。”

He pulled out their research book. He found a picture of a Viking ship. He read the caption aloud: 他抽出研究书。 他找到一幅维京船的图画。 他大声读出说明文字：

Viking warships were the best ships of their times. When there was no wind, the crew would take down the sails and row with oars. The smallest ships had four rowers, and the largest had as many as thirty-two. Rowers sat on boxes that stored their belongings. 维京战船是当时最好的船。没有风的时候，船员会降下帆，用桨划船。最小的船有四个划桨的人，最大的船多达三十二个。划桨的人坐在装他们物品的箱子上。

“Great,” said Annie, jumping up. “This isn’ t our darkest hour!” “太好了。”安妮跳起来说。 “这不是我们最黑暗的时刻！”

“Why do you say that?” asked Jack. “你为什么这么说？”杰克问。

“There’s still hope,” said Annie. “We can row to the other side of the island and find the tree house.” “还有希望。”安妮说。 “我们可以划到岛的另一边，找到树屋。”

“Are you nuts?” said Jack. “你疯了吗？”杰克说。

“Please, Jack,” said Annie. “Can we just try?” “求你了，杰克。”安妮说。 “我们能不能试试？”

She grabbed one of the oars. She could barely lift it. 她抓起其中一支桨。 她几乎举不动它。

“Forget it, Annie,” said Jack. “It takes four big Viking guys to row this thing. You’re too little. I’ m too little.” “算了吧，安妮。”杰克说。 “划这艘船得四个高大的维京人才行。 你太小了。我也太小了。”

“Come on, Jack. Just try,” said Annie. “You get an oar, too. We’ll sit on boxes across from each other.” “来吧，杰克。 就试试。”安妮说。 “你也拿一支桨。 我们坐在面对面的箱子上。”

“Oh, brother,” said Jack. “哎呀，真是的。”杰克说。

Annie dragged her long oar over to a storage box. 安妮把她那支长桨拖到一个储物箱旁。

“I’m not doing this alone,” she said. “我才不要一个人划。”她说。

Jack groaned. Then he dragged an oar to the box across from Annie. 杰克哼了一声。 然后他把一支桨拖到安妮对面的箱子旁。

“Cool!” said Annie. She peered into a storage box. “Look, one for each of us!” “酷！”安妮说。 她朝一个储物箱里看。 “看，我们每人一个！”

She lifted out two small Viking helmets. 她拿出两顶小维京头盔。

“Maybe these were made for Viking kids who sometimes ride in this ship,” said Annie. “也许这些是给偶尔坐这艘船的维京小孩做的。”安妮说。

“Maybe,” said Jack. “也许吧。”杰克说。

He hadn’t thought of Vikings as real people before—people with families and little kids. 他以前从没想过维京人也是真正的人——有家人和小孩的人。

Annie pulled off her scarf and put a helmet on her head. 安妮扯下围巾，把一顶头盔戴到头上。

“Now I feel like a Viking, too,” she said. “I bet it will help me row.” “现在我觉得自己也像个维京人了。”她说。 “我敢打赌它会帮我划桨。”

She handed Jack a helmet. He put it on. It made him feel a little different. 她递给杰克一顶头盔。他戴上了。 它让他感觉有点不一样。

“I don’t know about this,” he said. The helmet wasn’t as heavy as the one he had worn in the time of castles. But it was still pretty heavy. “我不太确定这个。”他说。 这顶头盔没有他在城堡时代戴过的那顶那么重。 可它还是挺沉的。

“Well, I’ m braver with mine on,” said Annie. “嗯，戴上之后我更勇敢了。”安妮说。

Jack smiled. He didn’t know how Annie could be braver than she already was. 杰克羞怯地笑了。 他不知道安妮怎么还能比现在更勇敢。

“Ready to row?” she asked. “准备好划了吗？”她问。

“Yup,” said Jack. He was feeling braver himself. “嗯。”杰克说。 他自己也感觉更勇敢了。

The wind picked up as Jack lifted his heavy oar over the side of the ship. 风大了起来，杰克把沉重的桨抬过船舷。

He lowered it into the water. But the current was so strong that the oar was ripped from his hands. 他把它放进水里。 可水流太急，桨从他手里被猛地扯走了。

Jack fell over backward as his oar slipped into the sea. 杰克的桨滑进海里，他向后摔倒。

“I lost my oar!” Annie yelled. “我的桨丢了！”安妮喊道。

Jack looked up as rain started to fall. The sky was black. A gush of seawater poured over the side of the ship! 杰克抬头时，雨开始下了。 天空一片漆黑。 一股海水从船舷涌了进来！

“Brrr!” said Annie as she tried to stand. “嘶——！”安妮一边想站起来一边发出颤抖声。

The black sky shook with thunder and flashed with lightning. 黑沉沉的天空随着雷声震动，还闪着闪电。

Jack crawled to the side of the ship and pulled himself up. 杰克爬到船边，把自己拉起来。

Another huge wave was coming right toward them! 又一道巨浪正朝他们扑来！

“It’s our darkest hour now!” cried Annie. “Get Brother Michael’s book!” “现在是我们最黑暗的时刻了！”安妮喊道。 “拿出迈克尔修士的书！”

Jack reached into his leather bag. He pulled out the jeweled book and held it up. 杰克把手伸进皮袋。 他抽出那本镶着宝石的书，举了起来。

“Save us, story!” he cried. “救救我们，故事！”他喊道。

He looked at the sea again. What he saw made him scream. 他又看向大海。 他看见的景象让他尖叫起来。

Rising from the oncoming wave was a giant sea serpent! 从迎面而来的海浪中升起一条巨大的海蛇！
