9. Sea Monster! 9. 海怪！

The serpent’s head rose higher and higher above the water. 蛇头在水面上方越升越高。

Jack couldn’t move. 杰克动不了了。

“He’s beautiful!” said Annie. “它好美！”安妮说。

“Beautiful?” cried Jack. “美？”杰克喊道。

The serpent’s neck was as tall as a two-story building. Its green scales were covered with sea slime. 蛇的脖子有两层楼那么高。 它绿色的鳞片上覆盖着海中的黏液。

“Go away!” shouted Jack. “走开！”杰克喊道。

“No—stay! Help us!” shouted Annie. “别——留下来！ 帮帮我们！”安妮喊道。

The great serpent glided closer to the ship. 巨蛇向船滑得更近了。

Jack ducked. 杰克猛地低下了头。

“Come on!” said Annie. “You can do it! Get us to shore before the ship sinks!” “快点！”安妮说。 “你能行的！ 在船沉之前把我们送到岸边！”

Jack closed his eyes. He felt the ship jerk, then move forward. 杰克闭上眼睛。 他感到船猛地一震，然后向前移动。

He looked up. They were gliding over the giant waves. 他抬起头。 他们正滑过巨浪。

Jack turned. The great serpent was pressing its long neck against the back of the ship, pushing it toward the shore. 杰克转过身。 巨蛇用长长的脖子抵住船尾，把它推向岸边。

As the serpent pushed them, the wind grew calmer. The clouds lifted and the water glittered with sunlight. 巨蛇推着他们时，风渐渐平静下来。 云散开了，水面在阳光下闪闪发亮。

The rocky shore grew closer. Jack could see the tree house on the ledge above it. 岩石海岸越来越近。 杰克能看见岩台上的树屋。

“Hurry!” Annie called to the serpent monster. “快点！”安妮对蛇怪喊道。

The great serpent gave the ship one last push. The ship swooshed onto a sand bar near the shore. 巨蛇给了船最后一推。 船嗖的一声冲上了近岸的沙洲。

Jack put the jeweled book carefully back into his bag. Then he and Annie climbed out of the ship onto the wet sand. They looked back at the sea. 杰克小心地把那本镶宝石的书放回袋子里。 然后他和安妮从船上爬出来，踏上潮湿的沙子。 他们回头望向大海。

The great serpent was rearing its long neck into the air. Its scales glittered pink and green in the sunlight. 巨蛇把长长的脖子竖向空中。 它的鳞片在阳光下闪着粉红和绿色的光。

“Bye!” shouted Annie. “Thanks a lot!” “再见！”安妮喊道。 “非常感谢！”

The monster seemed to nod at her. Then he dove into the sea and was gone. 那怪物好像朝她点了点头。 然后它潜入海里，消失了。

Jack and Annie headed toward the rocks. All of a sudden, Annie gasped. 杰克和安妮朝岩石走去。 突然，安妮倒抽了一口气。

“Uh-oh,” she said, pointing to the top of the cliff. “呃噢。”她说着，指向悬崖顶。

Two Vikings were staring down at them! 两个维京人正朝下盯着他们！

“To the tree house!” cried Jack. “去树屋！”杰克喊道。

The Vikings shouted and started down the steep stairs. 维京人喊着，开始冲下陡峭的台阶。

Jack and Annie began scrambling up the rocks. 杰克和安妮开始急速往岩石上爬。

They reached the tree house and climbed inside. 他们到达树屋，爬了进去。

Jack grabbed the Pennsylvania book. 杰克抓起宾夕法尼亚的书。

Annie stuck her head out the window. 安妮把头探出窗外。

“Go home! Stop causing trouble!” she yelled to the Vikings, who were almost to the ledge. “回家！ 别再惹麻烦了！”她朝那些快到岩台的维京人喊道。

Jack pointed at the picture of the Frog Creek woods. 杰克指着蛙溪树林的图画。

“IWISHWECOULDGOTHERE!” Jack shouted. “我希望我们能去那儿！”杰克喊道。

Just as the Vikings reached the ledge, the wind began to blow. 就在维京人到达岩台时，风开始刮起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后，一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止。
