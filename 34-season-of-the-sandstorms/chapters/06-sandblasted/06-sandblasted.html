6. Sandblasted 6. 沙暴来袭

In the distance, a haze of sand was spreading over the desert. As the wind picked up, the sky turned red and the haze thickened into a brown cloud. The cloud began gliding toward Jack, Annie, and Mamoon like a moving wall. 在远处，一片沙尘正在沙漠上蔓延开来。 随着风势增强，天空变成了红色，沙尘也变得越来越浓，形成了一片棕色的云。 那片云像一堵移动的墙一样，朝杰克、安妮和马穆恩缓缓靠近。

“Get down! Lie on your bellies!” ordered Mamoon. “Quickly! Cover your faces with your head cloths!” “趴下！ 趴下！”马穆恩命令道。 “快点！ 用头巾捂住脸！

Jack clucked his tongue. Beauty knelt to the ground. Jack, Annie, and Mamoon jumped off their camels and lay down on the sand beside them. 杰克吐了吐舌头。 贝蒂跪在地上。 杰克、安妮和马穆恩从骆驼上跳下来，躺在他们旁边的沙地上。

Jack tried desperately to cover his face with his head cloth, but the raging wind kept whipping the cloth from his hands. The sky turned from red to black. The moaning sound turned into a loud rumbling. 杰克拼命用头巾捂住脸，但狂风一直把头巾从他手里吹走。 天空从红色变成了黑色。 呜咽声变成了轰隆隆的响声。

Jack looked up and saw the wind rip the saddlebag off Mamoon’s kneeling camel! The bag hit the ground and fell open. The box bounced out and tumbled away in the driving wind. 杰克抬头一看，发现狂风把马穆恩跪着的骆驼上的鞍袋吹了下来！ 袋子掉在地上，然后张开了。 盒子弹了出来，被狂风吹着滚走了。

“The treasure!” cried Jack, but his voice was drowned out by the wind. He jumped up and bolted after the box. “宝藏！”杰克喊道，但他的声音被风吹得听不见了。 他跳起来，赶紧追着盒子跑。

Jack raced across the desert as the sand battered his body. The wind tried to push him down. But Jack ran with all his might, until finally he caught up with the box and threw himself down on top of it. He grabbed the ends of his head scarf and covered his face. 杰克在沙漠中奔跑，沙子不停地打在他的身上。 风试图把他吹倒。 但杰克用尽全力奔跑，直到终于追上盒子，扑在盒子上。 他抓住头巾的两端，遮住了自己的脸。

The sandstorm blasted over Jack, sounding like the hoofbeats of a hundred galloping camels. Jack’s eyes burned. He felt as if he were suffocating. 沙暴席卷着杰克，声音就像一百匹奔跑的骆驼的蹄声。 杰克的眼睛被灼烧得生疼。 他感觉好像要窒息了。

Slowly the thundering softened to a low rumbling. The rumbling faded to a moaning. The wind died down. The hot desert grew still and silent. 雷声渐渐变小，变成了低沉的轰鸣。 轰鸣声又变成了呜咽声。 风停了。 炎热的沙漠变得安静无声。

Coughing, Jack rolled over and sat up. He had sand in his mouth, sand in his ears, and sand in his nose. He pulled off his glasses and rubbed his stinging eyes. But rubbing only made them worse. 杰克咳嗽着翻了个身，坐了起来。 他的嘴里、耳朵里、鼻子里都灌满了沙子。 他摘下眼镜，揉了揉刺痛的眼睛。 但揉眼睛反而让眼睛更不舒服了。

Blinking, Jack clutched the box and looked around for the others. The air was thick with dust. He had completely lost his sense of direction. 杰克眨了眨眼睛，紧紧抓住盒子，四处张望寻找其他人。 空气里充满了灰尘。 他完全失去了方向感。

“Jack! Jack!” He heard Annie shouting. “杰克！ “杰克！”他听到安妮在喊。

Jack clutched the small wooden box and stood up. His legs were so wobbly, he fell over. “Annie!” he croaked. 杰克紧紧抓住那个小木盒，站了起来。 他的腿摇摇晃晃，一下子摔倒了。 “安妮！”他沙哑地喊道。

“Jack!” she called through the dust. “Where are you?” “杰克！”她穿过灰尘喊道。 “你在哪儿？”

“Here!” he said. “在这儿！”他说。

“Where?” “哪儿？”

“Here!” “在这儿！”

“There you are!” said Annie, stumbling out of the haze. “Are you okay?” “找到你了！”安妮一边说着，一边从雾中跌跌撞撞地走出来。 “你没事吧？”

“I’m okay,” croaked Jack. “Are you?” “我没事，”杰克沙哑地说。 “你真的没事吗？”

“Yes! I ran after you,” said Annie. Her voice was hoarse, too. “是的！ 我追着你来了，”安妮说。 她的声音也沙哑了。

“I had to save the box,” said Jack. “Where’s Mamoon?” “我必须救出那个盒子，”杰克说。 “玛穆恩在哪里？”

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “I don’t think he saw us chase after the box.” “我不知道，”安妮说。 “我想他没看到我们追那个盒子。”

“Mamoon!” they shouted together. “Mamoon!” “玛穆恩！”他们一起喊道。 “玛穆恩！”

There was no answer. 没有回应。

Peering through the sandy haze, Jack heard thumping sounds. He and Annie turned around. Their two camels were trotting toward them. 杰克透过沙尘眯着眼看，听到了咚咚的脚步声。 他和安妮转过身。 他们的两峰骆驼正朝他们走来。

“Cutie!” cried Annie. “Beauty!” “小可爱！”安妮喊道。 “美！”

Jack and Annie stumbled to their camels and grabbed their reins. 杰克和安妮跌跌撞撞地跑到骆驼旁边，抓住了缰绳。

“Thank you for finding us!” said Annie. “谢谢你们找到我们！”安妮说。

“Yeah, thanks,” said Jack, patting Beauty. “对，谢谢你们，”杰克说，一边拍了拍美。

“Mamoon!” Annie called. “Mamoon!” “玛穆恩！”安妮喊道。 “玛穆恩！”

“He must have gone in the wrong direction to look for us,” said Jack. “他一定是朝错误的方向去找我们了，”杰克说。

“If we don’t find him, how will we get to Baghdad? And what will we do with his treasure?” asked Annie. “如果我们找不到他，怎么去巴格达呢？ 我们又该怎么处理他的宝藏呢？”安妮问。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. He held up the wooden box. “我不知道，”杰克说。 他举起了那个木盒子。

“Look, the top’s broken,” said Annie. She pointed to a long crack running down the lid of the box. “看，盖子坏了，”安妮说。 她指着盒子盖子上一道长长的裂缝。

“I hope the treasure wasn’t hurt,” said Jack. “我希望宝藏没受伤，”杰克说。

“Maybe we should check and see,” said Annie. “也许我们应该看看里面，”安妮说。

Jack took a deep breath. He still thought Mamoon might not want them to look inside the box. But his curiosity got the best of him. “Okay,” he said. “I guess it wouldn’t hurt to make sure it’s all right.” 杰克深吸了一口气。 他还是觉得摩蒙可能不希望他们打开盒子。 但他的好奇心战胜了自己。 “好吧，”他说。 “我想确认一下里面没事应该没问题。”

Jack pried apart the two pieces of wood and lifted them off. Inside the box was a book. 杰克把两块木头掰开，把它们拿了起来。 盒子里有一本书。

“A book?” said Jack, surprised. He’d expected gold or jewels. He carefully lifted the book out of the box. It had a plain leather cover with no title on it. “一本书？”杰克惊讶地说。 他原本以为里面是金子或者珠宝。 他小心地把书从盒子里拿出来。 书的封面是普通的皮革，上面没有标题。

“It doesn’t look like a precious treasure,” said Annie. “看起来不像是珍贵的宝藏，”安妮说。

“Maybe the writing inside is the treasure,” said Jack. “也许书里的文字才是宝藏，”杰克说。

Jack gently opened the book. Inside were pages of thick yellow paper. The pages were bound with stitching and covered with writing on both sides. The first page said: 杰克轻轻地翻开书。 里面是一页页厚厚的黄色纸。 书页是用线缝起来的，两面都写满了字。 第一页上写着：

THE WRITINGS OF ARISTOTLE 亚里士多德的著作

“Who is Ar-is-totle?” said Annie, sounding out the name. “亚里士多德是谁？”安妮一边拼读这个名字一边说。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “I’ll look him up in our research book.” He unhooked his bag from the saddle horn. Sand covered the books inside. He pulled out their research book and brushed it off. Then he looked up Aristotle in the index. “Good. He’s here,” Jack said. He turned to the right page and read: “我不知道，”杰克说。 “我得去我们的研究书里查一查。”他从马鞍角上取下背包。 沙子把书里的书都盖住了。 他拿出他们的研究书，把它擦干净。 然后他在目录里查了亚里士多德。 “太好了。 他在这里，”杰克说。 他翻到右边的那一页开始读：

Aristotle (say AIR-is-TAH-tul) lived in ancient Greece over 2,300 years ago. He is known as one of the greatest philosophers of all time. The wordphilosophermeans “lover of wisdom.” Aristotle’s works were introduced to the Western world by the Arabs in the Middle Ages. 亚里士多德（发音为AIR-is-TAH-tul）生活在2300多年前的古代希腊。他被公认为有史以来最伟大的哲学家之一。哲学家这个词的意思是“热爱智慧的人”。中世纪时，阿拉伯人将亚里士多德的作品介绍给了西方世界。

“So Aristotle was a great lover of wisdom,” said Annie. “所以亚里士多德非常热爱智慧，”安妮说。

“I guess so,” said Jack. “But I wonder why this book is such a treasure.” “我想是吧，”杰克说。 “但我不明白为什么这本书这么珍贵。”

“Wait a minute,” said Annie. “Doesn’t Merlin’s note tell us that we have to help the caliph of Baghdad spread wisdom to the world?” “等一下，”安妮说。 “梅林的笔记不是告诉我们，我们必须帮助巴格达的哈里发把智慧传播到世界各地吗？”

Jack caught his breath. “Yeah,” he said. “And if this is a book of Aristotle’s writings, it must be full of wisdom…. We have to get this book to the caliph—that’s our mission for Merlin!” 杰克屏住了呼吸。 “对，”他说。 “如果这是一本亚里士多德的著作，那里面一定充满了智慧…… 我们必须把这本书交给哈里发——这是梅林交给我们的任务！

“We better get going!” said Annie. “我们最好赶紧出发！”安妮说。

Jack and Annie pulled on the reins of their camels and clucked their tongues. Beauty and Cutie knelt in the sand, and Jack and Annie climbed on. Leaving the broken box behind, Jack carefully packed the ancient book of Aristotle’s wisdom and the research book into his bag. Then he hung the bag from his saddle horn. 杰克和安妮拉住骆驼的缰绳，轻轻吐了吐舌头。 美 Beauty 和酷酷 Cutie 跪在沙子里，杰克和安妮爬了上去。 留下那个破箱子，杰克小心地把亚里士多德智慧的古老书籍和研究书装进了他的包里。 然后他把包挂在了马鞍的角上。

“Which way?” said Annie. “往哪走？”安妮说。

“Toward the rising sun in the east,” said Jack. “That’s what Mamoon said.” “朝东边升起的太阳方向走，”杰克说。 “这就是玛穆恩说的方向。”

“It’s that way, then,” said Annie. She pointed toward a bright blur in the hazy sky. “那我们就朝那个方向走，”安妮说。 她指着天空中模糊的亮光。

Jack and Annie’s camels headed into the dusty, glaring sun. “Hey, we’re following Merlin’s second instruction now,” said Annie. “ Ride through the dust and hot morning light.” 杰克和安妮的骆驼走进了炎热刺眼的阳光中。 “嘿，我们现在要执行梅林的第二个指示了，”安妮说。 “穿过尘土和炎热的晨光。”

“You’re right,” said Jack. “你说得对，”杰克说。

As Jack and Annie kept traveling toward the east, the desert sands shimmered and sparkled with heat. The air cleared, but still they saw no sign of Mamoon. 杰克和安妮继续向东前进，沙漠的沙子在热浪中闪烁着光芒。 空气变得清晰了，但他们还是没有看到玛穆恩的踪影。

Jack looked down to shield his eyes from the burning sunlight. Exhausted, he closed his eyes. As Beauty swayed gently from side to side, Jack’s head dropped forward. Suddenly he heard Annie shout, “Jack! Look!” 杰克低头遮住眼睛，以免被刺眼的阳光灼伤。 筋疲力尽的他闭上了眼睛。 当贝蒂轻轻左右摇晃时，杰克的头向前低了下去。 突然，他听到安妮喊道：“杰克！看！”

“What? Mamoon?” said Jack, jerking awake. “什么？ “妈妈？”杰克说着，猛地惊醒过来。

“No! Look!” said Annie. “不！” 看！”安妮说。

In the distance, Jack saw sunlit towers and domes shining against the clear blue sky. “Oh, man,” he whispered. “ Baghdad.” 在远处，杰克看见阳光照耀的塔楼和圆顶在清澈的蓝天背景下闪闪发光。 “哦，天哪，”他小声说。 “巴格达。”
