7. Behind the Third Wall 7. 第三面墙后面

“Let’ s hurry!” said Annie. “快点！”安妮说。

As Beauty and Cutie walked toward Baghdad, the loose sand of the desert changed to sunbaked earth. Then the hard earth changed to scrubby grass. Goats and sheep grazed in the grass, and farms dotted the countryside. 当贝蒂和库蒂朝巴格达走去时，沙漠松软的沙子变成了被太阳晒硬的泥土。 然后坚硬的土地变成了杂草丛生的草地。 山羊和绵羊在草地上吃草，农田点缀在乡村的各个地方。

It wasn’t long before Jack and Annie came to a red clay path. The path led to a crossroads where many travelers were coming from different directions. They were all heading toward the shining city. Jack and Annie rode alongside boys driving sheep and farmers in donkey carts. They passed women with veils over their faces, carrying pots on their shoulders. 不久之后，杰克和安妮来到了一条红色黏土的小路上。 小路通向一个岔路口，许多旅行者从不同的方向走来。 他们都朝着那座闪亮的城市前进。 杰克和安妮骑着马，和赶着羊群的男孩以及坐着驴车的农民并肩而行。 他们还经过了一些蒙着面纱的妇女，她们肩上扛着水罐。

Jack kept looking for Mamoon. But he never saw him. Moving with the crowd, Beauty and Cutie clomped across a bridge. Rowboats and barges glided along the yellowish brown river. 杰克一直在寻找马穆恩。 但他从来都没见到他。 随着人群一起前进，贝蒂和库蒂踩着脚步走过一座桥。 小船和货船在黄褐色的河面上滑行。

On the other side of the river was a huge outdoor market with a maze of tents. The market seemed to be filled with people from many countries. The sharp smell of incense filled the air. Stalls were jammed with burlap sacks, baskets, and carpets. Shoemakers sat cross-legged, stitching shoes. Potters were bent over ovens, baking earth-colored pots. Weavers sat at small looms, spinning silk brocade. 河对岸有一个巨大的露天市场，帐篷像迷宫一样排列着。 市场里似乎挤满了来自许多国家的人。 空气里弥漫着香料的浓烈气味。 摊位上堆满了麻袋、篮子和地毯。 鞋匠盘腿坐着，正在缝制鞋子。 陶匠弯着腰，在炉子前烤制土黄色的陶罐。 织布工坐在小织布机前，编织着丝绸锦缎。

“Paper? Pearls?” a merchant shouted. “纸？ 珍珠？”一个商人喊道。

“No thanks!” said Annie. “不用谢！”安妮说。

“Pigeons, parrots?” another shouted at Jack. “鸽子、鹦鹉？”另一个人对杰克喊道。

“No thanks!” he said. “不用谢！”他说。

“I love this place!” said Annie. “What is it?” “我太喜欢这个地方了！”安妮说。 “这是什么？”

“I’ll look it up,” said Jack. He pulled out their research book and read: “我去查一下，”杰克说。 他拿出他们的研究书读起来：

In the ninth century, traders from all over the world brought their goods to Baghdad to sell. Baghdad merchantstraded paper, fine cloth, and jewelry for goods from Spain, India, Africa, China, Greece, and other lands. These goods were sold in a huge open-air market called a bazaar. 在第九世纪，来自世界各地的商人都把货物带到巴格达去卖。巴格达的商人用纸张、精美的布料和珠宝，去交换来自西班牙、印度、非洲、中国、希腊和其他地方的商品。这些商品在一座巨大的露天市场里出售，这个市场叫做巴扎尔。

“Oh, so a bazaar is sort of like a mall,” said Jack. “哦，原来集市有点像商场，”杰克说。

“It’s a lot better than a mall!” said Annie. “Let’s get off and look around.” “这比商场好太多了！”安妮说。 “我们下去看看吧。”

“We don’t have time to shop,” said Jack. “We have to finish our mission.” He pulled out Merlin’s letter and read: “我们没时间逛街，”杰克说。 “我们必须完成任务。”他拿出梅林的信，读道：

Find a horse on a dome,

the one who sees all,

in the heart of the city

behind the third wall. 在圆顶上找到一匹马，那匹马能看见一切，在城市中心，第三面墙后面。

“It sounds like we pass three walls and then we come to a horse,” said Jack. “We’d better keep going.” “听起来我们得穿过三面墙，然后才能找到一匹马，”杰克说。 “我们最好继续前进。”

Jack put the book and letter away. Beauty and Cutie ambled through the noisy bazaar. When they left the bazaar, they came to a curved brick wall. Running along the base of the wall was a muddy moat. 杰克把书和信收了起来。 美丽和小可爱慢悠悠地穿过喧闹的集市。 当他们离开集市时，来到了一座弯曲的砖墙前。 沿着墙基流淌着一条泥泞的护城河。

“Look—that must be the first wall!” said Annie. “看——那一定是第一面墙！”安妮说。

“Cool,” said Jack. “酷，”杰克说。

Jack and Annie rode the two camels over an arched bridge that crossed the moat. They passed through a gate with double iron doors. On the other side of the wall was a busy avenue lined with buildings. 杰克和安妮骑着两只骆驼，走过一座拱形桥，这座桥横跨护城河。 他们穿过一扇有双层铁门的门。 墙的另一边是一条热闹的街道，街道两旁都是建筑物。

As their camels wove slowly in and out of the crowd, Jack pulled out their research book. Swaying from side to side, he read to Annie: 他们的骆驼在人群中慢慢穿行时，杰克拿出他们的研究书。 他左右摇晃着，给安妮读起来：

During its golden age, Baghdad had good hospitals. The city was also known for its excellent police system and public schools, along with its many libraries and stores and a zoo with a hundred lions. 在巴格达的黄金时代，那里有很好的医院。这座城市还以出色的警察系统和公共学校、众多的图书馆和商店以及一个有一百只狮子的动物园而闻名。

“I’d like to visit those lions,” said Annie. “我想去看看那些狮子，”安妮说。

“We don’t have time now,” said Jack. “我们现在没时间，”杰克说。

Their camels clopped down the busy avenue until they came to a green field. 他们的骆驼沿着热闹的街道走着，直到来到一片绿油油的田野。

“Look, there’s the second wall!” said Annie. “看，那是第二道墙！”安妮说。

Bordering the field was another wall. It was curved, too, but much taller than the first. It looked like it was at least a hundred feet high. There were guards at the gate, but they didn’t seem to be checking any of the people passing through. 场边还有另一堵墙。 这堵墙也是弯的，但比第一堵高得多。 看起来至少有一百英尺高。 大门那里有守卫，但他们似乎没有检查通过的人。

“Act normal,” Jack advised Annie. “Don’t attract their attention.” “表现正常，”杰克建议安妮。 “不要引起他们的注意。”

Jack and Annie joined a stream of people passing through the gate. Beyond the second wall was another grand avenue. And at the end of the wide cobbled road was another green field. “Look! The third wall!” said Annie. 杰克和安妮加入了一群通过大门的人流。 第二堵墙的另一边是一条宽阔的大道。 在那条铺着鹅卵石的宽路上，尽头是一片绿色的田野。 “看！ “第三面墙！”安妮说。

The third wall was even taller than the second. Again, people were passing freely through its gate. 第三面墙比第二面还要高。 人们再次自由地穿过它的大门。

“This part of the mission is easy!” said Annie. “这部分任务很简单！”安妮说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “But we still have to find that horse on the dome, and then find the caliph and get him to meet with us.” “是啊，”杰克说。 “但我们还得找到穹顶上的那匹马，然后找到哈里发，让他和我们见面。”

Beauty and Cutie ambled through the gate of the third wall, carrying Jack and Annie into the heart of Baghdad. A palace loomed before them. Its roof was a glittering green dome. At the top of the dome stood the statue of a horse. “美丽”和“可爱”悠闲地穿过第三面墙的大门，把杰克和安妮带到了巴格达的中心。 一座宫殿出现在他们面前。 它的屋顶是一个闪闪发光的绿色穹顶。 穹顶的顶部站着一匹马的雕像。

“Yay, the horse who sees all!” said Annie. “I’ll bet the caliph lives in that palace. Let’s follow those people inside.” She pointed to a stream of people entering an arched passageway outside the palace. “太棒了，这是能看见一切的马！”安妮说道。 “我敢打赌，那位哈里发就住在那座宫殿里。 我们跟着这些人进去吧。”她指着一队正走进宫殿外拱形通道的人。

Jack and Annie rode under the arch and into a beautiful garden. The warm breeze smelled of flowers. They rode down a path bordered by date palms until they came to a courtyard where boys were playing ball. Near the courtyard was a stable with camels in it. 杰克和安妮骑马穿过拱门，进入了一个美丽的花园。 温暖的微风中飘着花香。 他们沿着两旁种着枣椰树的小路骑下去，直到来到一个庭院，那里有男孩在玩球。 庭院附近有一座马厩，里面养着骆驼。

“Looks like we’ll have to go the rest of the way on foot,” said Jack. “看起来我们必须步行走完剩下的路了，”杰克说道。

“Yeah, I guess Beauty and Cutie can stay there,” said Annie, pointing to the stable. “对，我想贝蒂和小可爱可以留在那儿，”安妮指着马厩说道。

Jack and Annie rode into the stable. They clucked, and the camels knelt. As Jack reached for his shoulder bag, a ball rolled into the stable. Annie picked it up and stepped outside. Jack followed her. 杰克和安妮骑马走进了马厩。 它们咕哝着，骆驼们跪了下来。 杰克伸手去拿他的肩包时，一个球滚进了马厩。 安妮捡起球，走出了马厩。 杰克跟着她走了出来。

“Here!” shouted a young boy with curly black hair. He was holding out his hands. Annie threw the ball to him, and the boy caught it. He grinned at Annie’s good throw. “Who are you?” he called. “Where are you from?” “在这儿！”一个有着卷曲黑发的小男孩喊道。 他张开双手，准备接球。 安妮把球扔给了他，男孩接住了球。 他对着安妮漂亮的投掷露出了笑容。 “你们是谁？”他喊道。 “你们是从哪儿来的？”

Before Jack could say anything, Annie crossed to the boys. Jack hurried after her. 杰克还没来得及说话，安妮就走到男孩们面前。 杰克赶紧跟了上去。

“I’m Annie and this is my brother, Jack,” Annie said. “We’re from Frog Creek, Pennsylvania.” “我是安妮，这是我哥哥杰克，”安妮说。 “我们来自宾夕法尼亚州的蛙溪镇。”

“Why have you come to Baghdad?” the boy asked. “你们为什么来到巴格达？”那个男孩问。

“We have to see the caliph,” said Annie. “我们必须去见哈里发，”安妮说。

The boy and his friends laughed. 那个男孩和他的朋友们笑了。

“What’s so funny?” said Annie. “你们觉得什么好笑？”安妮说。

“Our caliph is the most powerful man in the world,” said another boy. “He does not have time to visit with children.” “我们的哈里发是世界上最强大的人，”另一个男孩说。 “他没有时间陪孩子们。”

“That’s what everyone keeps saying,” said Annie. “But we’re here on a very important mission. We—” “这就是大家一直说的，”安妮说。 “但我们这次来是执行一项非常重要的任务。我们—”

“Annie,” Jack interrupted, “come on. I left my bag back in the stable.” He waved to the boys. “See you guys later. Let’s go, Annie.” “安妮，”杰克打断了她，“快点吧。 我把包落在马厩里了。”他朝男孩们挥手示意。 “待会儿再和你们玩吧。安妮，快走。”

Annie started to leave with Jack. 安妮开始和杰克一起离开。

“When you discover that the caliph will not meet with you, come back and play with us,” called the curly-haired boy. “当你发现哈里发不愿意见你们时，回来和我们一起玩，”那个卷发男孩喊道。

Annie stopped. “Don’t worry,” she called back. “He’ll meet with us! We’re special!” 安妮停下了脚步。 “别担心，”她回头喊道。 “他会见我们的！我们很特别！”

“Annie, come on,” said Jack. “安妮，快点，”杰克说。

“Why are you special?” shouted the boy. “你为什么这么特别？”那个男孩喊道。

“For one thing, we just saved a precious treasure from bandits and from a sandstorm in the desert,” said Annie. “For another—” “首先，我们刚刚从强盗和沙漠里的沙暴中救出了一件珍贵的宝藏，”安妮说。 “其次——”

“Annie!” Jack said sternly. He grabbed her arm. “Come on! ” He pulled Annie away. “安妮！”杰克严厉地说。 他抓住了她的手臂。 “快点！”他拉着安妮离开了。

As they headed back into the stable, Jack shook his head. “Don’t say stuff like that,” he said. 当他们重新走进马厩时，杰克摇了摇头。 “不要说这种话，”他说。

“Why not?” said Annie. “为什么不能说？”安妮说。

“Because it’s bragging,” said Jack. “It makes other kids feel bad. Even if you are special, you don’t have to—” “因为这是在炫耀，”杰克说。 “这会让其他孩子感觉不好。 即使你很特别，你也不必——”

“Oh, no!” cried Annie. She covered her mouth with her hands. “哦，不！”安妮喊道。 她用手捂住了嘴。

“What?” said Jack, following her shocked gaze. “什么？”杰克顺着她震惊的目光看去。

Jack’s leather bag lay at Beauty’s feet. It was open. Beside it was the torn leather cover of Aristotle’s book. Wet pages hung in shreds from Beauty’s mouth as she happily chewed the precious treasure. 杰克的皮质背包躺在贝蒂的脚下，它敞开着。 它旁边是亚里士多德的书的破旧皮质封面。 湿漉漉的书页从贝蒂的嘴里垂下来，她开心地咀嚼着这珍贵的宝藏。
