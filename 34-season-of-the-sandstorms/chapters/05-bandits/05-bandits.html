5. Bandits! 5. 强盗！

Jack looked around wildly. Dark figures on horses were galloping across the sand toward them. They were yelling and shouting. 杰克慌乱地四处张望。 沙丘上出现了骑着马的黑影，正朝他们疾驰而来。 他们大声喊叫着。

“Oh, no!” cried Jack. “What should we do?” “天啊，不好！”杰克喊道。 “我们该怎么办？”

“We will fight them off!” said Mamoon. “You and Annie take this box and ride to the dunes!” Mamoon pulled a flat wooden box out of one of his saddlebags. He thrust the box into Jack’s hands. “Hurry! Ride as fast as you can! Protect it with your lives!” “我们会赶走他们！”梅蒙说道。 “杰克，你和安妮拿着这个盒子，快去沙丘那边！”梅蒙从他马鞍袋里拿出一个扁平的木盒子。 他把盒子塞进杰克的手里。 “快点！ 骑得越快越好！ 用你们的命保护好它！”

Jack frantically tried to stuff the box into his shoulder bag. But Mamoon slapped the back of Jack’s camel, and she bolted forward. The reins slipped from Jack’s hands. He grabbed the saddle horn with one hand and clutched the wooden box to his chest with the other. He held on for his life as Beauty galloped across the dark desert. 杰克慌慌张张地把盒子往他的肩包里塞。 但玛穆恩抽了杰克骆驼的屁股，骆驼猛地向前冲去。 缰绳从杰克手里滑了出去。 他一只手抓住马鞍的把手，另一只手紧紧抱着那个木盒子。 贝蒂在黑暗的沙漠中飞快奔跑，杰克紧紧抓住不放。

Annie’s camel ran beside Jack’s. Like two racehorses, Beauty and Cutie thundered across the sand toward the distant dunes. Rocking crazily from side to side, Jack clung to the box. “Slow down!” he yelled. “Please!” 安妮的骆驼跑在杰克的旁边。 就像两匹赛马，贝蒂和小可爱在沙丘上飞奔着朝远处的沙丘冲去。 杰克左右摇晃，紧紧抓着那个盒子。 “慢点！”他喊道。 “求求你了！”

It was no use. Beauty ran like the wind. She and her sister practically flew over the desert under the starry sky. Jack wanted the camels to stop. But at the same time, he wanted to get far away from the bandits. 没用的。 美美像风一样跑起来。 她和她的姐姐在星光下几乎飞过了沙漠。 杰克想让骆驼停下来。 但与此同时，他又想离那些强盗远远的。

Finally the camels began to slow their pace. Jack looked back. He couldn’t see the caravan at all, and no one seemed to be following them. 最后骆驼开始放慢了脚步。杰克回头看了看。 他完全看不到那支商队了，好像也没有人跟着他们。

When the two camels reached the dunes, they began plodding around the steep hills. Once they were nestled safely between tall sand drifts, they stopped to rest. Beauty grunted. Cutie snorted. 当两只骆驼到达沙丘时，它们开始在陡峭的山丘间缓慢地走动。 一旦它们安全地藏在高高的沙丘之间，它们就停下来休息了。美美咕哝了一声。 小可爱喷出了一口气。

“Thanks … thanks, girls,” said Annie, panting. “谢谢……谢谢你们，女孩们，”安妮喘着气说。

“I hope Mamoon and the others are safe from the bandits,” said Jack. “我希望马穆恩和其他人都能安全地避开强盗，”杰克说。

“Me too,” said Annie. “What’s in the box he gave us?” “我也是，”安妮说。 “他给我们的盒子里装着什么？”

Jack held up the flat wooden box. “I don’t know,” he said. “But Mamoon said we should protect it with our lives.” 杰克举起了那个扁平的木盒子。 “我不知道，”他说。 “但马穆恩说我们必须用生命来保护它。”

“Maybe it’s a precious spice,” said Annie. “也许它是一种珍贵的香料，”安妮说。

“I hope it’s more than that, ” said Jack. “I’d hate to risk my life for cinnamon or pepper.” “我希望它不仅仅是那样，”杰克说。 “我不想为了肉桂或胡椒而冒生命危险。”

“Should we look?” said Annie. “我们该看看吗？”安妮说。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “Mamoon might not want us to.” “我不知道，”杰克说。 “摩蒙可能不希望我们这么做。”

“But don’t you think we could protect it better if we knew what it was?” said Annie. “但是你不觉得如果我们知道它是什么，就能更好地保护它吗？”安妮说。

“Maybe …,” said Jack. He could see Annie’s point. “Okay.” “也许……”杰克说。 他能明白安妮的观点。 “好吧。”

Jack tried to open the lid of the box, but he couldn’t. In the dark, his finger pressed against a keyhole. “Forget it,” he said. “It’s locked.” 杰克试着打开盒子的盖子，但打不开。 在黑暗中，他的手指按到了一个钥匙孔上。 “算了吧，”他说。 “它被锁住了。”

“Shh! Listen!” said Annie. “嘘——！ 听！“安妮说。

Jack listened. He heard a high-pitched moaning sound. It sounded like music from a violin. Wafting through the dry sand dunes, the haunting music grew louder. 杰克听了。 他听到了一个尖锐的呜咽声。 那声音听起来像小提琴演奏的音乐。 幽幽的音乐飘荡在干燥的沙丘之间，声音越来越响。

“What is that?” said Jack. “那是什么？”杰克说。

“Uh-oh,” said Annie. “Now I hear something else.” “糟了，”安妮说。 “现在我听到别的声音了。”

Jack held his breath. He heard hooves galloping over the desert. “The bandits!” he said. 杰克屏住了呼吸。 他听到马蹄声在沙漠上飞快地奔跑。 “强盗们！”他说。

“We have to hide the box!” said Annie. “我们必须把盒子藏起来！”安妮说。

“Where?” said Jack. “哪儿？”杰克说。

“In the sand!” said Annie. She clucked her tongue, and Cutie knelt down to the ground. Beauty knelt, too. Jack and Annie jumped off their saddle cushions and started digging in the sand. “在沙子里！”安妮说。 她吐了吐舌头，库蒂立刻蹲下身来。 贝蒂也蹲了下来。 杰克和安妮跳下马鞍垫，开始在沙子里挖起来。

The sound of hoofbeats grew louder and louder. Jack and Annie dug frantically. They threw sand behind them like puppies digging in the dirt. 马蹄声越来越响了。 杰克和安妮拼命地挖着。 他们把沙子往身后扔，就像小狗在泥土里刨洞一样。

“That’s deep enough!” said Jack. He placed the box in the hole they had dug. Then he and Annie pushed piles of sand back on top of it. “挖得够深了！”杰克说。 他把盒子放进他们挖好的洞里。 然后他和安妮把一堆堆沙子盖在盒子上。

When they stood up, Annie gasped. “Look!” 当他们站起来时，安妮惊叫起来。 “看！”

A dark figure on a camel was silhouetted against the starlit sky. The rider was winding his way through the dunes toward them. Jack’s heart nearly pounded out of his chest. 一头骆驼上有个黑影，在星光照耀的天空中显得格外清晰。 骑骆驼的人正穿过沙丘朝他们走来。 杰克的心几乎要从胸口跳出来了。

“Should we use a magic rhyme?” Annie asked. “我们用魔法咒语吗？”安妮问。

“We don’t have time!” said Jack. “我们没时间了！”杰克说。

The rider drew closer, until he stopped in front of Jack and Annie. “You are safe, no?” he said. 骑马的人越来越近，直到他停在杰克和安妮面前。 “你们安全了吗？”他说。

“Mamoon!” said Annie. “马穆恩！”安妮说。

Relief flooded through Jack. He laughed. “Yes, we’re safe!” he said. “And you’ re safe, too!” 杰克感到一阵轻松，他笑了。 “是的，我们安全了！”他说。 “你也很安全！”

“My men fought well,” said Mamoon. “The thieves fled with only a few bags of pepper and painted beads.” “我的手下战斗得很英勇，”马穆恩说。 “那些贼只带着几袋胡椒和彩珠逃走了。”

“And we kept your box safe, too!” said Annie proudly. She knelt and dug in the sand until she uncovered the wooden box. She handed it to Mamoon. “我们还帮你保管好了那个盒子！”安妮骄傲地说。 她蹲下身，在沙子里挖呀挖，直到挖出了那个木盒子。 她把盒子递给了马穆恩。

“Ahh, very good,” the caravan leader said. “啊啊，非常好，”商队首领说。

“What’s in the box?” asked Annie. “盒子里有什么？”安妮问。

“A priceless treasure,” said Mamoon. “I have brought it all the way from Greece. And I am taking it to Baghdad. Thank you both for guarding it with your lives. You are very special.” “一件无价的宝藏，”马穆恩说。 “我把它从希腊一路带到这里。 现在我要把它带到巴格达去。 谢谢你们用生命守护它。 你们真的很特别。”

“Sure, no problem,” said Jack. He still wondered what was in the box. Gold? Silver? Precious jewels? “当然，没问题，”杰克说。 他仍然想知道盒子里有什么。金子？ 银子？珍贵的宝石？

But Mamoon did not say. He put the box back into his camel’s saddlebag. “Let us be on our way now,” he said. 但马穆恩没有说。 他把盒子放回骆驼的鞍袋里。 “我们现在出发吧，”他说。

Jack climbed on top of his kneeling camel. He clucked his tongue. He was surprised and pleased when Beauty rose up on her tall legs. 杰克爬上了跪着的骆驼。 他用舌头发出咔哒声。 当贝蒂用她长长的腿站起来时，他感到又惊讶又高兴。

“We will catch up with the others in Baghdad,” said Mamoon. “If all goes well, we will arrive in the city in the afternoon. We must head east toward the morning sun.” “我们会在巴格达追上其他人，”马穆恩说。 “如果一切顺利，我们下午就能到达那个城市。” 我们必须向东，朝向早晨的太阳。”

Mamoon rode out of the dunes. Jack and Annie followed him. As their camels rocked through the chilly dawn, daylight shimmered over the sand. 摩根从沙丘上骑了出来。 杰克和安妮跟了上去。 当他们的骆驼在寒冷的黎明中摇晃前行时，阳光在沙子上闪烁。

“Mamoon, last night we heard strange sounds in the dunes,” said Annie. “Like music playing.” “妈妈，昨晚我们在沙丘里听到了奇怪的声音，”安妮说。 “像是有人在放音乐。”

“Ah, yes,” said Mamoon, “the whistling sands.” “啊，是的，”妈妈说，“那是会唱歌的沙子。”

“What are the whistling sands?” asked Jack. “会唱歌的沙子是什么？”杰克问。

“Some say it is magic,” said Mamoon. “But I believe that all things in nature have their reasons. That is why I like the study of science. Science says we must observe our world. We must make experiments and try to find out why things happen. We have learned the whistling is made by sands settling in the drifts.” “有人说那是魔法，”妈妈说。 “但是我相信，大自然中的一切都有它的原因。” 这就是为什么我喜欢研究科学。 科学告诉我们必须观察我们的世界。 我们必须做实验，试着找出事情发生的原因。 我们已经了解到，呼啸声是沙子在沙丘中堆积时发出的。”

“Oh,” said Annie. “I’d hoped it was magic.” “哦，”安妮说。 “我希望那是魔法。”

“Learning the reasons for things is magic,” said Mamoon. “True knowledge brings light to the world. And that is a magical thing, no?” “了解事物的原因就是魔法，”玛穆恩说。 “真正的知识能为世界带来光明。 那不是一件很神奇的事吗？”

“Yes,” said Jack. “是的，”杰克说。

Annie nodded thoughtfully. “I guess, when you put it that way,” she said. 安妮若有所思地点点头。 “我想，你这么说的话，”她说。

Swaying from side to side on their camels, the three riders traveled toward the dawn. As the sun rose higher in the sky, the desert grew blazing hot. A dry wind whipped through the air, making snaky patterns in the sand. 三名骑手在骆驼上左右摇晃，朝黎明的方向前进。 随着太阳升得更高，沙漠变得热得发烫。 干燥的风吹过空气，在沙地上吹出蛇形的图案。

Mamoon halted his camel. He looked around and frowned. 马穆恩让他的骆驼停下来。 他环顾四周，皱起眉头。

“What’s wrong?” said Jack. “Are there signs of bandits?” “怎么了？”杰克说。 “有强盗的踪迹吗？”

Mamoon shook his head. “No, it is the desert itself that worries me now,” he said. “It is restless.” He clucked his tongue, and his camel began walking again. 马蒙摇了摇头。 “不，现在让我担心的是沙漠本身，”他说。 “它很不安分。”他吐了吐舌头，他的骆驼又开始走了。

As they rode over the restless desert, the wind picked up loose sand and tossed it into the air. Jack and Annie lowered their heads to keep the sand from blowing into their eyes. Their head cloths flapped in the wind. More and more sand started blowing. The desert seemed alive as the sand shifted and swirled. 当他们骑着骆驼穿越这片不安分的沙漠时，风卷起了松散的沙子，把它们抛向空中。 杰克和安妮低下头，防止沙子吹进他们的眼睛。 他们的头巾在风中飘荡。 越来越多的沙子开始吹起来。 沙漠似乎活了过来，沙子不断移动和旋转。

Mamoon stopped again and looked about. The snaky patterns in the sand were blowing into round, curly patterns. Jack heard a weird moaning sound. “Is that the whistling sands again?” he asked hopefully. 马蒙又停了下来，四处张望。 沙子中蛇形的图案被风吹成了圆形、卷曲的图案。 杰克听到一个奇怪的呜咽声。 “这是沙丘的呜咽声吗？”他满怀希望地问。

“No,” said Mamoon. “That is the cry of a terrible sandstorm. And it will soon be upon us.” “不是，”马穆恩说。 “那是可怕的沙暴的呼啸声。 很快它就会来到我们身边。”
