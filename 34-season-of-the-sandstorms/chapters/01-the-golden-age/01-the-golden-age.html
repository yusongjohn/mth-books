1. The Golden Age 1. 黄金时代

Jack put his math homework aside. He opened the drawer beside his bed and pulled out a small, handmade book. For the hundredth time, he stared at the title on the cover: 他打开床边的抽屉，拿出一本小巧的手工制作的书。 他第一百次盯着书封上的标题：

10 MAGIC RHYMES FOR ANNIE AND JACK

FROM TEDDY AND KATHLEEN 安妮和杰克从泰迪和凯瑟琳那里收到的10个魔法押韵

For weeks, Jack had kept the book hidden in his drawer, wondering when he and Annie would be able to use its magic again. The book’s ten rhymes were to be used on four missions, and each rhyme could be used only once. Jack and Annie had already used two rhymes on a mission in Venice, Italy. 几周以来，杰克一直把这本书藏在抽屉里，想知道他和安妮什么时候才能再次使用它的魔法。 这本书里的十首押韵诗是用来完成四次任务的，而且每首押韵诗只能使用一次。 杰克和安妮已经在意大利威尼斯的任务中用过两个押韵了。

“Jack!” Annie rushed into Jack’s room. Her eyes were shining. “Bring the book! Let’s go!” “杰克！”安妮冲进杰克的房间。 她的眼睛亮晶晶的。 “带上书！我们走吧！”

“Where?” said Jack. “去哪儿？”杰克说。

“You know where! Come on!” Annie called as she ran back downstairs. “你知道在哪里！“ “快点！”安妮一边往楼下跑，一边喊道。

Jack quickly put Teddy and Kathleen’s book into his backpack. He pulled on his jacket and took off down the stairs. 杰克赶紧把泰迪和凯瑟琳的书放进他的背包里。 他穿上外套，冲下楼梯跑开了。

Annie was waiting on the front porch. “Hurry!” she cried. 安妮正在前门廊上等着。 “快点！”她喊道。

“Wait! How do you know it’s there?” Jack said. “等一下！ 你怎么知道它在那里？”杰克说。

“Because I just saw it!” Annie shouted. She hurried down the porch steps and crossed the yard. “因为我刚刚看到了它！”安妮喊道。 她急忙跑下门廊的台阶，穿过院子。

“You saw it? Actually saw it?” yelled Jack as he followed Annie through the chilly afternoon air. “你看到了它？ 真的看到了？”杰克一边跟着安妮穿过寒冷的下午空气，一边大喊。

“Yes! Yes!” Annie yelled. “是的！ 是的！”安妮大喊。

“When?” shouted Jack. “什么时候？”杰克大喊。

“Just now!” said Annie. “I was walking home from the library and I had this feeling— so I went and looked! It’s waiting for us!” “刚才！”安妮说。 “我从图书馆走路回家的时候，有一种感觉——所以我去看了！ 它在等我们！

Jack and Annie raced into the Frog Creek woods. They ran between the budding trees, over the fresh green moss of early spring, until they came to the tallest oak. 杰克和安妮冲进了蛙溪镇的森林里。 他们跑过正在发芽的树，跑过早春新鲜的青苔，直到来到最高的橡树前。

“See?” said Annie. “看到了吗？”安妮说。

“Yes,” breathed Jack. He stared up at the magic tree house. Its rope ladder dangled above the mossy ground. Annie started climbing up. Jack followed. When they got inside, Jack pulled off his backpack. “是的，”杰克轻声说。 他抬头望着那座神奇的树屋。 它的绳梯悬挂在长满青苔的地上方。 安妮开始往上爬。杰克跟在后面。 他们走进去后，杰克取下了他的背包。

“Look, a book and a letter!” Annie said. She picked up a folded letter from the floor, and Jack picked up a book with a gold cover. “看，有一本书和一封信！”，安妮说。 她从地上捡起一封折好的信，杰克捡起了一本有金色封面的书。

“Baghdad,” Jack said. He showed the book to Annie. Its title was: “巴格达，”，杰克说。 他把书展示给安妮看。书的标题是：

THE GOLDEN AGE OF BAGHDAD 巴格达的黄金时代

“A golden age?” said Annie. “That sounds cool. Let’s go!” “黄金时代？”，安妮说。 “那听起来很酷。我们走吧！”，

图中文字：TheGolden Ageof Blackbush 图中文字：黑柏树的黄金时代

“Wait, we should read our letter first,” said Jack. “等等，我们应该先读我们的信，”，杰克说。

“Right,” said Annie. She unfolded the paper. “Merlin’s handwriting,” she said. She read aloud: “对，”，安妮说。 她把纸张展开。 “这是梅林的笔迹，”她说。她大声读出来：

Dear Jack and Annie of Frog Creek,

Your mission is to journey to Baghdad of long

ago and help the caliph spread wisdom to

the world. To succeed, you must be humble

and use your magic wisely. Follow these— 亲爱的弗罗格克里克的杰克和安妮，你们的使命是前往很久以前的巴格达，帮助哈里发把智慧传播到世界各地。要成功，你们必须谦虚，并明智地使用你们的魔法。请遵循这些—

“Wait, what’s a caliph? ” said Jack. “And what’s Merlin mean—‘spread wisdom to the world’? That’s a big responsibility.” “等等，什么是哈里发？”杰克说。 “那梅林的意思是——‘把智慧传播到全世界’？ 那是个很大的责任。”

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “Let me finish.” She kept reading: “我不知道，”安妮说。 “让我读完。”她继续读着：

Follow these instructions:

   Ride a ship of the desert

   on a cold starry night.

   Ride through the dust

   and hot morning light. 按照这些指示做：在寒冷的星光夜晚，骑上沙漠中的船。穿过尘土和炎热的晨光。
Find a horse on a dome,

   the one who sees all,

   in the heart of the city

   behind the third wall. 在城市中心第三堵墙后面，找到一匹站在圆顶上、能看见一切的马。
Beneath birds who sing

   in the Room of the Tree,

   greet a friend you once knew

   and a new friend to be. 在树屋里，鸟儿们唱着歌，向你曾经认识的朋友问好，也向即将成为你朋友的新朋友问好。
Remember that life

   is full of surprises.

   Return to the tree house

   before the moon rises. 记住，生活中充满惊喜。月亮升起之前，回到树屋。
—M. —先生。

“This sounds pretty easy,” said Annie. “这听起来好像很容易，”安妮说。

“No, it doesn’t,” said Jack. “All these instructions are so mysterious. We don’t know what any of them mean.” “不，一点都不容易，”杰克说。 “所有这些说明都太神秘了。 我们不知道它们是什么意思。”

“We’ll find out when we get there,” said Annie. “But first we have to get there. Make the wish.” “我们到了就知道了，”安妮说。 “但首先我们必须到那里。许个愿吧。”

“Okay,” said Jack. He pointed to the cover of the book. “I wish we could go to the golden age of Baghdad,” he said. “好的，”杰克说。 他指着书的封面。 “我希望我们能去巴格达的黄金时代，”他说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
