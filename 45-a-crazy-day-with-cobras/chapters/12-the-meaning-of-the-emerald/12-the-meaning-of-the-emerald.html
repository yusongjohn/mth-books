12. The Meaning of the Emerald 12. 绿宝石的意义

Jack, Annie, and the Great Mogul were silent for a long moment. The marble of the Taj Mahal seemed to change color as the sun went down. Against the darkening sky, it went from pale pink to orange to purple. The dome sparkled as if it were covered with a thousand tiny lights. 杰克、安妮和大莫卧儿沉默了很长一段时间。 随着太阳下山，泰姬陵的大理石似乎改变了颜色。 在天色渐暗的天空下，它从淡粉色变成了橙色，又变成了紫色。 穹顶闪闪发亮，好像覆盖着成千上万颗小灯泡。

“How did you make it sparkle like that?” Annie asked finally. “你是怎么让它闪闪发光的？”安妮终于问。

“Stonecutters set precious stones in the marble,” said the Great Mogul. “Many were cut in the shapes of flowers and leaves. The emerald rose you chose today is like the carved flowers set in the Taj Mahal.” “石匠们在大理石上镶嵌了珍贵的宝石，”大莫卧儿说。 “许多宝石都被切割成花朵和叶子的形状。 你今天选的翡翠玫瑰，就像泰姬陵里雕刻的花朵。”

“It’s really beautiful,” Jack said. “真的很美，”杰克说。

“Yes. Many say it is the most beautiful building in all the world,” said the Great Mogul. “But hidden beneath the sparkle and magnificence of the Taj Mahal is simply the lonely heart of one person who loved another.” The Great Mogul had tears in his eyes. He cleared his throat and looked away from them. “And the elephant I gave you—where is she now?” “是的。 很多人说，它是世界上最美妙的建筑，”大莫卧儿说。 “但是，在泰姬陵闪耀和壮丽的外表之下，只有一颗孤独的心，那是一个人对另一个人的爱。”大莫卧儿眼里含着泪水。 他清了清嗓子，然后转过头不去看他们。 “至于我送你的那头大象——她现在在哪里？”

“Uh … actually …,” Jack started. He didn’t know what to say. How could he protect Morning Breeze? “呃……其实……，”杰克开始说。 他不知道该说什么。 他怎么能保护晨风呢？

“We left her in the forest,” Annie finished. “She has a baby she wanted to take care of. You probably didn’t know this, but Morning Breeze was a new mom when she was captured. She’s a good mother. She should stay with her baby.” “我们把她留在森林里了，”安妮说完了。 “她有一个宝宝，她想照顾它。 你可能不知道这件事，但晨风在被抓住的时候是一个新妈妈。 她是个好妈妈。 她应该和她的宝宝在一起。”

The Great Mogul nodded. “I understand,” he said. “My wife was a good mother, too. The last thing she said to me was ‘Please take care of our children.’ Do not worry, the elephant will remain in the forest with her child.” 大莫卧儿点了点头。 “我明白了，”他说。 “我的妻子也是个好妈妈。 她对我说的最后一句话是‘请照顾好我们的孩子。’不要担心，大象会和她的孩子留在森林里。”

“Thank you,” said Annie. “谢谢你，”安妮说。

The Great Mogul stared at them for a long moment. When he spoke again, his voice was kind. “You are ambassadors from far away,” he said, “but you are children, too. You should go back to your home, to those who care for you.” 大莫卧儿盯着他们看了好一会儿。 当他再次说话时，声音很温柔。 “你们是从很远的地方来的使者，”他说，“但你们也是孩子。” 你们应该回到你们的家，回到那些关心你们的人那里。

“A splendid idea,” said Jack, trying to sound like an ambassador. “一个绝妙的主意，”杰克说，努力让自己听起来像个大使。

“Come.” The Great Mogul walked with Jack and Annie back to the arched entrance and through the tall gate. He waved at the driver in the cart. Then he turned to Jack and Annie. “来。”大莫卧儿陪着杰克和安妮走回拱形入口，穿过高大的大门。 他向马车里的司机挥手。 然后他转向杰克和安妮。

“Farewell,” he said. “Travel safely.” “再见，”他说。 “一路平安。”

“Thank you,” said Annie. “谢谢，”安妮说。

“Bow,” Jack whispered to her. “鞠躬，”杰克小声对她说。

Jack and Annie performed their bow one last time. When they straightened up, the Great Mogul nodded. 杰克和安妮最后一次鞠了一躬。 当他们站直时，莫卧儿皇帝点了点头。

“Oh, and thank you for the emerald rose,” said Annie. “It is a priceless treasure.” “哦，还有谢谢你们送的翡翠玫瑰，”安妮说。 “这是一个无价的宝藏。”

“You are most welcome,” said the Great Mogul. “The rose was my wife’s favorite flower. And, as you know, emeralds stand for love.” For the first time, a warm smile crossed his face. “非常感谢你，”大莫卧儿说道。 “这朵玫瑰是我妻子最喜欢的花。 正如你们所知，翡翠象征着爱情。” 第一次，他脸上浮现出温暖的笑容。

Jack and Annie smiled back at him. 杰克和安妮朝他微笑着。

But the Great Mogul was suddenly serious again, as if he wasn’t allowed to smile. He looked at Jack and Annie for a moment. Then, without another word, he turned and walked through the tall gate, back toward the shimmering tomb of his wife. 但大莫卧儿突然又变得严肃起来，好像他根本不能笑。 他看了杰克和安妮一会儿。 然后，他没有再说一句话，转身走进高大的门，朝他妻子闪闪发光的墓地走去。

Jack looked at Annie. “Let’s go home now,” he said. 杰克看着安妮。 “我们现在回家吧，”他说。

Jack and Annie walked to the guard and his horse-drawn carriage. “Please take us to the trees outside the walls of the Red Fort,” Jack said. 杰克和安妮走到守卫和他那辆马车旁。 “请带我们到红堡城墙外的树那里，”杰克说。

The guard flicked his reins. The black horses trotted along the river in the pink twilight. They passed veiled women on the backs of elephants, white oxen pulling a cart, and small boys driving a herd of sheep. They stopped near the row of dark trees outside the Red Fort. 守卫抽了一下缰绳。 黑色的马在粉红色的暮色中沿着河岸小跑。 他们经过了骑在大象背上的蒙着面纱的妇女、拉着车的白色牛，还有赶着一群羊的小男孩。 他们停在红堡外面一排黑树旁边。

“This is fine. We’ll get out here, please,” said Jack. “这地方不错。 请在这里下车，”杰克说。

The guard gave them a silent nod. 卫兵默默地点头。

Jack and Annie jumped out of the cart. They ran under the trees to the rope ladder and climbed into the tree house. Jack grabbed the Pennsylvania book. Before he made the wish, he and Annie looked out the window. 杰克和安妮从马车里跳了下来。 他们跑到树下，抓住绳梯爬进了树屋。 杰克拿起了宾夕法尼亚的书。 在许愿之前，他和安妮望向窗外。

In the distance, they could just see the Taj Mahal. It glowed faintly in the hot night, like a shimmering cloud. 在远处，他们刚好能看到泰姬陵。 它在炎热的夜晚微微发光，就像一朵闪烁的云彩。

“Oh, wait …,” said Jack. “I just remembered something.” He reached into his bag and pulled out the note from Kathleen and Teddy. He read aloud: “哦，等等……，”杰克说。 “我刚想起来一件事。”他把手伸进袋子里，拿出凯瑟琳和泰迪的纸条。他大声读道：

Ye say that ye wish

your spell be reversed?

Four things ye must find.

Here is the first: 你说你想解除你的魔法？你必须找到四样东西。这是第一样：
In the shape of a rose

is an emerald stone

that uncovers the heart

of one who’s alone. 一朵玫瑰形状的宝石，能揭示孤独之人的内心。

Jack put the note away. “Our mission wasn’t really done until now,” he said. “We were so worried about the emerald rose, we forgot the part about uncovering the heart of one who’s alone.” 杰克把纸条收了起来。 “我们的任务其实直到现在才算完成，”他说。 “我们一直担心那朵翡翠玫瑰，却忘了关于揭示一个孤独者内心的部分。”

“The Great Mogul,” said Annie. “Even though he has millions of followers, he’s really lonely.” “伟大的莫卧儿皇帝，”安妮说。 “尽管他有成千上万的追随者，但他其实很孤独。”

Jack and Annie looked out the window again. “Good-bye, Great Mogul,” Annie said. “I hope your heart feels better someday.” 杰克和安妮又看向窗外。 “再见了，伟大的莫卧儿皇帝，”安妮说。 “希望有一天你的心能感觉好一些。”

Jack took a deep breath. He pointed at a picture of the Frog Creek woods. “I wish we could go home,” he said. 杰克深吸了一口气。 他指着一张蛙溪镇森林的图片。 “我希望我们能回家，”他说。

The wind began to blow. 风开始吹起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。

Jack and Annie were wearing their own clothes again. Jack’s bag was a backpack. A warm wind was blowing the trees in the Frog Creek woods. 杰克和安妮又穿上了自己的衣服。 杰克的包是一个背包。 暖暖的风吹动着蛙溪镇森林里的树木。

“I’m glad it’s not boiling hot here,” said Jack. “这儿不那么热，我真高兴，”杰克说。

“It feels good,” said Annie. “You’ve still got the emerald rose, right?” “感觉真舒服，”安妮说。 “你还有那朵翡翠玫瑰吧？”

Jack looked in his backpack. “Got it,” he said. He pulled out their note, their research book, and the blue bottle, and he left them in the corner of the tree house. 杰克看了看他的背包。 “有，”他说。 他拿出他们的便条、研究书和蓝色瓶子，把它们放在树屋的角落里。

Then Jack took out the sparkling stone and held it up to the afternoon light. “This is for you, Penny,” Jack said. “We’ll take this home and keep it safe until we see Teddy and Kathleen again.” 接着，杰克拿出那块闪闪发光的石头，对着下午的阳光举起来。 “这是给佩妮的，”杰克说。 “我们会把它带回家，好好保管，直到再次见到泰迪和凯瑟琳。”

“Good,” said Annie. “好，”安妮说。

Jack carefully put the emerald rose back into his backpack. Then he looked at Annie. “Home,” he said. 杰克小心地把翡翠玫瑰放回他的背包里。 然后他看了看安妮。 “回家，”他说。

Jack and Annie climbed down the rope ladder. As they walked through the Frog Creek woods, Annie was unusually quiet. 杰克和安妮顺着绳梯爬了下来。 他们穿过蛙溪镇的森林时，安妮异常安静。

“What’s on your mind?” Jack asked. “你在想什么？”杰克问。

“Well, I was just thinking that it’s really cool that emeralds stand for love,” said Annie. “Love was the reason for every big thing that happened today.” “嗯，我只是在想，祖母绿代表爱真的很酷，”安妮说。 “爱就是今天发生所有大事的原因。”

“How do you mean?” said Jack. He thought their day had been crazy. “你是怎么想的？”杰克说。 他觉得他们这一天过得疯狂极了。

“Well, we went on our mission because Merlin—and everyone else in Camelot—loves Penny,” said Annie, “and we love her, too.” “嗯，我们执行任务是因为梅林——还有卡梅洛特的每个人——都爱彭妮，”安妮说，“我们也一样爱她。”

“Right …,” said Jack. “对……，”杰克说。

“And the Great Mogul gave us Morning Breeze because I loved her,” said Annie. “而且大莫卧儿给了我们晨风，因为我爱她，”安妮说。

“Right,” said Jack. “对，”杰克说。

“And Morning Breeze took us into the wild because she loved her baby,” said Annie. “然后晨风带我们进入荒野，因为她爱她的宝宝，”安妮说。

“Right,” said Jack. “对，”杰克说。

“And we ran into trouble with the cobras because they loved their babies-to-be,” said Annie. “我们还遇到了蛇，因为它们爱它们的宝宝，”安妮说。

“Yeah …,” said Jack. “Okay.” “是啊……，”杰克说。 “好吧。”

“And finally, the Great Mogul showed us the Taj Mahal, which is the most amazing building we’ve ever seen, and he built it because he loved his wife,” said Annie. “最后，大莫卧儿给我们看了泰姬陵，那是我们见过最令人惊叹的建筑，他建它是因为他爱他的妻子，”安妮说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “You’re right.” Annie made it all sound so simple. “对，”杰克说。 “你说得对。”安妮说得好像一切都很简单。

“And now I would love to go home,” said Annie. “现在，我想回家了，”安妮说。

“Me too,” said Jack. “我也是，”杰克说。

“Hey, we can tell Mom and Dad you got an A plus on your story,” said Annie. “We’ll print it out again.” “嘿，我们可以告诉爸爸妈妈你写的故事得了A加，”安妮说。 “我们会再打印一次。”

“Yeah, and I made copies of your artwork,” said Jack. “Except the sparkles don’t really show.” “对，我还复印了你的画作，”杰克说。 “不过那些闪光的东西其实印不出来。”

“That’s okay,” said Annie. “The sparkles aren’t that important.” “没关系，”安妮说。 “这些闪光的东西并不那么重要。”

“Right,” said Jack. “It’s the heart that counts.” And the two of them left the Frog Creek woods and headed home under the cloudless May sky. “对，”杰克说。 “重要的是心。”然后他们两个人离开了蛙溪镇的森林，踏着五月晴朗的天空回家了。
