5. Not Funny 5. 不好笑

The tall guard escorted Jack and Annie out the back door of the hall and into a walled courtyard. The other ambassadors were already entering the palace quarters on the far side. 高大的卫兵护送杰克和安妮从大厅的后门出去，走进了一个有围墙的庭院。 其他大使们已经进入宫殿远端的房间了。

Jack was so frustrated he barely noticed the crimson and purple flowers, the bubbling fountains, and the dazzling water pools in the courtyard. All he wanted to do was get back to the tree house. Their mission was done. He tried to think of an excuse to get away. 杰克非常沮丧，几乎没注意到庭院里那些红色和紫色的花朵、喷涌的喷泉和闪闪发光的水池。 他只想回到树屋里。 他们的任务已经完成了。 他试着想找个借口离开。

“Annie!” he whispered. “安妮！”他小声说。

By now Annie was walking ahead and chatting with the guard. “Where do the royal women and children live?” she asked. 此时安妮已经走在前面，和卫兵聊天。 “王后和孩子们住在哪儿？”她问。

The guard pointed to the palace quarters on the left side of the courtyard. Jack noticed veiled women peeping out from behind the arched windows. Annie waved at them, but the women moved out of sight. 卫兵指着庭院左边的宫殿区域。 杰克注意到一些戴面纱的妇女从拱形窗户后偷偷往外看。 安妮朝她们挥手，但那些妇女立刻躲到了视线之外。

The guard escorted Jack and Annie out of the courtyard and through a quiet passageway. The passageway led to a chamber lit with brass lanterns hanging from the wall. The guard handed them each a silk coat, a sash, a pair of pointy shoes, and a jeweled turban. 卫兵带杰克和安妮离开了庭院，穿过了一条安静的小通道。 通道通向一个房间，墙上挂着黄铜灯笼，照亮了整个房间。 卫兵给他们每人一件丝绸外套、一条腰带、一双尖头鞋和一顶镶满宝石的头巾。

“Um … what’s this?” asked Jack. “呃……这是什么？”杰克问。

“Gifts from the Great Mogul. You may change in here,” the guard said. “这是大莫卧儿送的礼物。 你们可以在这里换衣服，”卫兵说。

“Oh. Thanks,” said Jack. 谢谢，”杰克说。

The guard pulled aside a curtain to reveal a small space like a dressing room. Jack and Annie stepped inside, and the guard pulled the curtain shut. 卫兵拉开了一块窗帘，露出了一个像更衣室一样的小空间。 杰克和安妮走了进去，卫兵把窗帘拉上了。

Jack whirled around to Annie. “This is awful!” he whispered. “We have to get out of here!” 杰克转身面对安妮。 “这太糟糕了！”他小声说。 “我们必须从这里出去！”

“Calm down,” said Annie. “We don’t have a choice.” She rubbed her hand over her purple silk coat. “I love these clothes.” “冷静点，”安妮说。 “我们别无选择。”她用手抚摸着她紫色的丝绸外套。 “我太喜欢这些衣服了。”

“Oh, brother,” said Jack, sighing. But he took off his hot jacket and pulled on his orange coat and tied the sash. He definitely felt cooler in the soft silk coat, but the puffy pants made the coat balloon out around his waist. “I really hate this,” he groaned. “哦，天哪，”杰克叹了口气说。 但他脱下热乎乎的夹克，穿上橙色的外套，系上腰带。 他穿着柔软的丝绸外套感觉凉快多了，但蓬松的裤子让外套在腰间鼓鼓囊囊的。 “我真的讨厌这个，”他抱怨道。

“Don’t worry, we’ll leave right after the parade,” said Annie. She tucked her braids under her orange turban. “Do I still look like a boy?” “别担心，游行一结束我们就离开，”安妮说。 她把编好的辫子塞进了橙色的头巾里。 “我看起来还是个男孩吗？”

“You look ridiculous,” said Jack. “We both look ridiculous. We have to make some excuse so we can leave.” “你看起来很滑稽，”杰克说。 “我们俩看起来都很滑稽。 我们必须找个借口，这样我们才能离开。”

“Don’t you want to see the parade?” asked Annie. “你不想看看游行吗？”安妮问。

“Not with a person who kills you if you speak to him or bow the wrong way,” said Jack. “不能和一个说话或鞠躬不对就会杀了你的人一起走，”杰克说。

“Maybe we won’t have to bow again,” said Annie. “也许我们不用再鞠躬了，”安妮说。

“You’re missing my point,” said Jack. He put on his blue turban. Then he took off his buckled shoes and pulled on the pointy slippers. “你没明白我的意思，”杰克说。 他戴上了蓝色的头巾。 然后他脱下系带的鞋子，穿上尖尖的拖鞋。

Annie looked at him and laughed. 安妮看着他，笑了起来。

“Not funny,” said Jack. “这不好笑，”杰克说。

“Well, don’t forget, if we run into trouble, we can always drink the potion and make ourselves small,” said Annie. “好吧，别忘了，如果我们遇到麻烦，我们总是可以喝下药水，把自己变小，”安妮说。

“And get squashed by the Mogul’s guards? No thank you,” said Jack. “然后被莫卧儿的卫兵压扁？ 谢谢，我不干，”杰克说。

Annie laughed again. 安妮又笑了。

“Not funny,” said Jack. “不 funny（有趣）,” 杰克说。

The imperial guard pushed open the curtain to the dressing room. “Come with me now,” he said. 帝国卫兵推开更衣室的帘子。 “现在跟我走，”他说。

Jack and Annie followed the guard out of the chamber and down a hallway of the palace. “Remember, don’t talk to the other ambassadors,” whispered Jack. “And never, ever say anything to the Great Mogul. Promise?” 杰克和安妮跟着卫兵走出房间，沿着宫殿的走廊走。 “记住，不要和其他大使说话，”杰克小声说。 “而且，永远、永远不要对大莫卧儿说任何话。答应我吗？”，

“Don’t worry, I know what to do,” whispered Annie. “别担心，我知道该怎么做，”安妮小声说。

Jack and Annie followed the guard through another chamber and out to a wide, shaded balcony. The balcony overlooked a river and a sandy shore. 杰克和安妮跟着卫兵穿过另一个房间，来到一个宽敞的、有遮阳的阳台。 阳台俯瞰着一条河流和一片沙质的海岸。

The Great Mogul stood near the railing of the balcony, with imperial guards surrounding him. The ambassadors stood in a row behind them. Jack was relieved to see that they, too, were wearing pointy shoes and silk coats that stuck out. 大莫卧儿站在阳台的栏杆旁，周围是帝国的卫兵。 大使们排成一行站在他们后面。 杰克松了一口气，看到他们也穿着尖尖的鞋子和鼓鼓的丝绸外套。

Jack gestured to Annie to stand behind the ambassadors. He hoped none of them would try to talk to him. He was relieved to hear trumpets suddenly blaring and drums booming. All the men stepped forward to watch the parade. 杰克向安妮比划，让她站在大使们后面。 他希望他们中没有人会试着和他说话。 他听到号角突然吹响，鼓声隆隆，感到很欣慰。 所有男人都向前走了几步，观看游行。

Musicians came first. Hundreds of them marched in formation down the flat sandy shore between the palace and the river. Some blew long golden trumpets. Others beat drums tied around their bodies. 音乐家们第一个出场。 数百人排着队，沿着宫殿和河流之间的平坦沙滩行进。 有些人吹着长长的金色号角。 其他人把鼓绑在身上，敲打着鼓。

Dozens of flag bearers followed the musicians, their red and yellow banners waving in the hot, dry wind. After them came row after row of tall, elegant horses with golden bridles. Some were dark gray. Others were copper colored, milk white, or midnight black. 几十个举着旗帜的人跟在音乐家后面，他们红色和黄色的旗帜在炎热干燥的风中飘扬。 接着是一排排高大优雅的马，马嚼子都是金色的。 有些是深灰色的。 有些是铜色的、乳白色的，还有漆黑的。

“One hundred Arabian horses!” an imperial guard announced to the ambassadors. “The most beautiful animals in the world!” “有一百匹阿拉伯马！”一名帝国卫兵向大使们宣布。 “世界上最美丽的动物！”

Jack glanced at the Great Mogul. The ruler was coldly watching the parade. 杰克看了看大莫卧儿。 这位统治者冷冷地注视着游行。

Next came carts pulled by white oxen. Riding on the carts were cheetahs with tan fur and black spots. They wore gold collars that glittered in the sunlight. 接下来是被白色牛拉着的马车。 骑在推车上的是身披黄褐色毛发和黑色斑点的猎豹。 它们戴着闪闪发光的金色项圈。

“One hundred cheetahs!” announced the guard. “The fastest land animals in the world!” “有一百只猎豹！”，守卫宣布道。 “世界上最快的陆地动物！”，

Then came rows and rows of elephants kicking up a sea of dust. They looked like a fleet of gray ships. 接着，一排排大象来了，扬起一片尘土海洋。 它们看起来像一艘艘灰色的船。

“One hundred elephants caught in the wild!” said the guard. “The largest land animals in the world!” “有一百只野生大象！”，守卫说道。 “世界上最大的陆地动物！”，

The elephants wore gold chains and silver bells around their necks. The tops of their heads were covered with fancy fringed cloth. On their backs were straw carriages with riders inside. 大象们脖子上戴着金链子和银铃铛。 它们的头顶覆盖着华丽的镶边布料。 它们的背上都有装着骑手的稻草马车。

“Oh, wow, elephants,” said Annie, her eyes shining. “哦，哇，是大象，”安妮说，眼睛亮晶晶的。

“Shhh! We can’t talk,” whispered Jack. 我们不能说话，”杰克小声说。

Elephants, oxen, and horses all stepped in time to the beat of the drums and the blare of the trumpets. 大象、牛和马都随着鼓声和号角声有节奏地迈步。

Suddenly the music was interrupted by wild shrieks and bellows. 突然，音乐被一阵狂野的尖叫和吼声打断了。

The Great Mogul, his guards, and the ambassadors all moved to get a better view. Jack and Annie stood on their tiptoes to see what was happening. 大莫卧儿、他的卫兵和大使们都向前移动，想看得更清楚。 杰克和安妮踮起脚尖，想看看发生了什么。

Jack could see that an elephant had broken out of line and left the parade. Guards were struggling to capture it. The elephant had reared up on its hind legs, and its rider had tumbled from the carriage on its back. The elephant was making a terrible noise, like a screaming trumpet. 杰克看到一头大象从队列中冲了出来，离开了游行队伍。 卫兵们正在努力抓住它。 大象用后腿站了起来，骑手从马车上摔了下来，背朝下躺在地上。 大象发出可怕的响声，就像一个尖叫的喇叭。

“What’s happening? What’s making that noise?” said Annie. “发生什么事了？ 是什么在发出那个声音？”安妮说。

“An elephant,” whispered Jack. “是一头大象，”杰克小声说。

“Oh, no!” said Annie. “哦，不！”安妮说。

Jack grabbed her by the arm. “Don’t say anything. Don’t do anything,” he whispered. “Think of Penny.” 杰克抓住她的手臂。 “什么都别说。 什么都别做，”他小声说。 “想想佩妮。”

“I am! I hate it when animals are hurt!” said Annie. She pulled away from Jack and slipped through the row of ambassadors. “我在想！” “动物受伤的时候我最讨厌了！”安妮说。 她挣脱了杰克，滑进了大使们的行列中。

Jack moved after her. “Annie! Come back!” he whispered. 杰克跟在她后面。 “安妮！ 回来！”他小声说。

One of the guards saw Annie heading toward the railing where the Great Mogul stood. He quickly stepped in front of her. 守卫中的一位看到安妮朝大莫卧儿站立的栏杆走去。 他迅速走到她前面。

“Excuse me, sir, I’m worried about that elephant,” Annie said in a low voice. “What’s wrong with it?” “对不起，先生，我担心那只大象，”安妮低声说。 “怎么了？”

“She tried to escape,” the guard said. “They are capturing her now.” “她试图逃跑，”守卫说。 “他们正在抓住她。”

“What’s going to happen to her?” asked Annie, trying to look around the guard. “她会怎么样？”安妮问，试图绕过守卫看一眼。

“She will be punished,” said the guard. “她会被惩罚。”守卫说。

“What?” said Annie. “Why?” “什么？”安妮说。 “为什么？”

“She has failed to show respect for the Great Mogul,” said the guard. “因为她没有对大莫卧儿表示尊重。”守卫说。

“What?” Annie said. “什么？”安妮说。

“Annie!” Jack warned. “安妮！”杰克警告道。

But Annie didn’t seem to hear him. “That’s crazy! She’s just an animal!” she said to the guard. Her voice was fierce. 但安妮似乎没听到他的话。 “这太疯狂了！” 她只是一只动物！“她对守卫说。 她的声音很凶。

“Annie, quiet! Please!” said Jack. “安妮，安静！ 请！“杰克说。

The imperial guards and the other ambassadors were looking at Annie with alarm. Worst of all, the Great Mogul was staring at her, too. 帝国卫兵和其他大使都惊慌地看着安妮。 最糟糕的是，大莫卧儿也在盯着她。
