8. SHHHHHH-WISSSSSST! 8. 咦——嘘——嘘！

The king cobra circled the leafy mound. Its skin was olive brown, the color of the dead leaves. It had speckled yellow bands that ran around its scaly body. 眼镜蛇绕着那堆叶子转了一圈。 它的皮肤是橄榄棕色，和枯叶的颜色一样。 它身上有斑点状的黄色条纹，沿着鳞片覆盖的身体延伸。

“Back up, back up. Go slow,” Jack whispered to Annie. Jack picked up his bag, and they quietly stepped backward, until they got to the edge of the clearing. “Now run!” “往后退，往后退。 “慢慢来，”杰克小声对安妮说。 杰克拿起他的包，他们悄悄地往后退，直到退到空地的边缘。 “现在跑！”，

Jack and Annie took off. Clutching his bag, Jack ran with little steps, trying not to lose his pointy slippers. After about a hundred yards, Annie came to a halt. “Stop, stop!” She grabbed Jack’s arm. “We shouldn’t get too far away!” 杰克和安妮立刻跑了起来。 紧紧抓着他的包，杰克小步跑着，努力不把他的尖尖拖鞋弄丢。 跑了大约一百码后，安妮突然停了下来。 “停住，停住！”，她抓住了杰克的手臂。 “我们不应该跑得太远！”，

“Why not?” said Jack. “为什么不能呢？”，杰克说。

“Penny!” said Annie. “We have to get the emerald for Penny!” “佩妮！”，安妮说。 “我们必须为彭妮拿到绿宝石！”

“Right, right,” said Jack. “Okay!” He took a deep breath. “First, we have to get calm.” Jack took another deep breath. Then he pulled out their research book. “Okay. Let’s read about cobras.” “对，对，”杰克说。 “好的！”他深吸了一口气。 “首先，我们必须冷静下来。”杰克又深吸了一口气。 然后他拿出了他们的研究书。 “好的。 让我们读一读关于眼镜蛇的内容。”

Jack opened the book and found a section about Indian wildlife. He read: 杰克翻开书，找到了关于印度野生动物的一节。他读道：

The king cobra is the only snake known to make a nest for its eggs. The nest is made of a mound of dead leaves. The cobra’s scaly skin is the same color as the leaves, a good example of natural camouflage. 眼镜蛇是唯一一种会为蛋筑巢的蛇。它们用一堆枯叶做巢。眼镜蛇的鳞片颜色和叶子一样，这是自然伪装的一个好例子。

“So that mound of leaves was its nest,” said Jack. “And the cobra was probably a mother snake guarding her eggs.” “所以那堆树叶就是它的巢，”杰克说。 “而眼镜蛇很可能是一条守护自己蛋的母蛇。”

“How does that information help us?” said Annie. “这些信息怎么帮我们呢？”安妮说。

“It doesn’t,” said Jack. He went on: “帮不上。”杰克说。他继续读下去：

King cobras cannot hear, but they have excellent vision and can feel vibrations. They will attack anything they see that gets too close to their nests. 眼镜蛇听不见，但它们的视力很好，还能感觉到震动。它们会攻击任何靠近它们巢穴的靠近的东西。

“That’s bad news,” said Annie. “这真是坏消息，”安妮说。

“Yep, really bad news,” said Jack. He kept reading: “是啊，真的很坏，”杰克说。他继续读下去：

When threatened, the king cobra flattens its neck into a hood. A single bite from the snake contains enough venom to kill twenty men or a large elephant. 受到威胁时，眼镜蛇会把脖子扁平成一个兜帽。这条蛇咬一下的毒液，足够杀死二十个人或一头大象。

“Whoa,” said Annie. “That must be why Morning Breeze panicked and ran away.” “哇，”安妮说。 “那一定就是为什么晨风惊慌失措地逃走了。”

Jack closed the book. He shook his head. “I don’t see how we can possibly get close to that nest,” he said. 杰克合上了书。 他摇了摇头。 “我看我们根本不可能靠近那个鸟巢，”他说。

“Then there’s no way we can get an emerald rose for Penny,” said Annie, “unless we go back to the Great Mogul and ask for another one.” “那我们就没有办法为彭妮拿到翡翠玫瑰了，”安妮说，“除非我们回到大莫卧儿那里，再请求一朵。”

“And that’s not going to happen,” said Jack. “这不会发生的，”杰克说。

“Then how can we save Penny?” asked Annie. “Think of Penny.” “那我们怎么救彭妮呢？”安妮问。 “想想彭妮。”

Jack thought about Penny. He would do anything for her. “Okay,” he said. “There might be a way we could get the emerald rose. What if … what if we were really small?” 杰克想起了彭妮。 他愿意为她做任何事。 “好吧，”他说。 “也许我们有办法得到那朵翡翠玫瑰。 如果我们变得非常小呢？”

“Yes!” said Annie. “对！”安妮说。

“The cobra can’t hear us, right?” said Jack. “She can only see us and feel vibrations. So if we make ourselves small, maybe we can sneak back and get the emerald without being seen.” “眼镜蛇听不见我们，对吧？”杰克说。 “她只能看到我们，感受到震动。” “所以如果我们把自己变小，也许可以偷偷回去拿到翡翠，而不会被看到。”

“Yes!” said Annie. “对！”安妮说。

Jack reached into his bag and pulled out the blue bottle. He and Annie both stared at it. “So how many sips should we take?” Jack asked. “One for ten minutes?” 杰克把手伸进包里，拿出蓝色的瓶子。 他和安妮都盯着它看。 “那我们应该喝几口呢？”杰克问。 “一口，持续十分钟？”

Annie shook her head. “Two for twenty.” 安妮摇了摇头。 “两口，持续二十分钟。”

“Okay …” Jack took a deep breath. “But just so you know, when we get small, everything else will be huge, like flies and spiders and—” “好吧……”杰克深吸了一口气。 “不过你得知道，当我们变小的时候，其他所有东西都会变得非常大，比如苍蝇、蜘蛛和——”

“Spiders?” Annie said in a small voice. “蜘蛛？”安妮用很小的声音说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “Hey, you know what? You don’t have to do this. I can do it by myself. It only takes one person to get the emerald.” “对，”杰克说。 “嘿，你知道吗？ 你不用做这件事。 我可以自己来。 只要一个人就能拿到翡翠。

“No! I’ ll go. You stay,” said Annie. “不！我要去。” “你留下，”安妮说。

“No way,” said Jack. “We’ll both go.” “没门，”杰克说。 “我们两个都去。”

“Good,” said Annie. “好，”安妮说。

Jack held the bottle up to his lips. “Okay,” he said, “two sips.” He took two quick sips, then handed the bottle to Annie. 杰克把瓶子举到嘴边。 “好吧，”他说，“喝两口。”他快速喝了两口，然后把瓶子递给安妮。

Jack felt dizzy. He closed his eyes and hugged himself. He felt as if he were falling through a hole. 杰克感觉头晕。 他闭上眼睛，抱住了自己。 他感觉好像掉进了一个洞里。

SHHHHHH-WISSSSSST!

Suddenly the forest was filled with chirping, whirring, crunching, and squeaking. 突然，森林里充满了叽叽喳喳、嗡嗡声、咔咔声和吱吱声。

“Oh, wow!” whispered Annie. “Open your eyes.” “哦，哇！”安妮小声说。 “睁开眼睛。”

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开眼睛。

He and Annie were both small— very small. Their clothes and shoes and Jack’s bag were all small, too. 他和安妮都变得很小——非常小。 他们的衣服、鞋子，还有杰克的包也都变得很小。

“Oh, man, we got really small,” said Jack. He looked around at the grass and the weeds and the mushrooms—they were all taller than he was! “I think we shrunk to about six or eight inches.” “天啊，我们真的变小了，”杰克说。 他环顾四周，看着草、杂草和蘑菇——它们都比他还要高！ “我觉得我们缩小到大约六到八英寸高。”

Close to the ground, the scrubby forest was awake and alive, filled with the ripe smells of earth, noisy insect sounds, and the rustling and whispering of grasses and weeds. The dirt glittered as if sprinkled with flecks of silver. 靠近地面时，灌木丛般的森林苏醒了，充满了泥土的成熟气息、嘈杂的虫鸣声，还有草和杂草沙沙作响和低语的声音。 泥土闪闪发光，就像撒上了银色的碎屑。

“It’s really beautiful,” said Annie. “这里真的很美，”安妮说。

“Yeah …,” said Jack. “是啊……，”杰克说。

Wildflowers looked like elegant and luminous umbrellas with pale pink petals and silvery leaves. Berries were the size of apples. 野花看起来像优雅又闪亮的伞，有淡粉色的花瓣和银色的叶子。 浆果有苹果那么大。

“Look up!” said Annie. “抬头看！”安妮说。

“Wow!” said Jack. “哇！”杰克说。

The tall trees of the forest were like skyscrapers. It was hard to see where they ended. 森林里高大的树就像摩天大楼一样。 很难看出它们在哪里结束。

WHUP! Something plopped down in the dirt beside Jack. WHUP! 有什么东西啪嗒一声掉在杰克旁边的泥土上。

“AHHH!” Jack and Annie grabbed each other in terror. “啊！”杰克和安妮吓得紧紧抓住对方。

It was a giant insect—as long as Jack’s arm! It had a flat brown body, six legs, and two sets of wings. It looked at them with huge, goggle-like eyes. It waved its antennae and crept forward. 那是一只巨大的昆虫，有杰克的手臂那么长！ 它有一个扁平的棕色身体，六条腿，还有两对翅膀。 它用巨大、像护目镜一样的眼睛看着他们。 它挥动着触角，慢慢向前爬。

“AHHH!” Jack and Annie stumbled backward. “啊——！”杰克和安妮往后退了一步。

The giant bug stopped. Then it rubbed its front wings together. The forest erupted with shrill chirping—Creekle! CREEKLE! Creekle! CREEKLE! 那只巨大的虫子停了下来。 然后它摩擦着前翅。 森林里突然响起尖锐的鸣叫——克里克！克里克！ 克里克！克里克！

Jack covered his ears and laughed. The giant insect was a cricket! He knew a cricket wouldn’t hurt them. The cricket pushed off the ground with its long hind legs and leapt into the brush. 杰克捂住耳朵，笑了起来。 那只巨大的昆虫原来是一只蟋蟀！ 他知道蟋蟀不会伤害他们。 蟋蟀用它长长的后腿蹬地，跳进了灌木丛里。

“Look!” said Annie, glancing up. “看！”安妮抬头说。

A giant golden yellow butterfly hovered above her. As if Annie were a flower, the butterfly touched down lightly on her head and opened its wings. Annie held her breath. She didn’t move a muscle. She looked like she was wearing a wide yellow hat. 一只巨大的金黄色蝴蝶在她头顶盘旋。 就像安妮是一朵花一样，蝴蝶轻轻地落在她的头上，展开翅膀。 安妮屏住呼吸。 她一动也不动。 她看起来像是戴了一顶又宽又黄的帽子。

The butterfly closed its wings, then opened them, and with a whispery sound, fluttered deeper into the forest. 蝴蝶合上翅膀，然后又张开，发出沙沙的声音，飞得更深进了森林。

Bzzzzzzzzzzz-zzzzzzzzzzz.

A gigantic bee circled above Jack. “Whoa. Keep moving, buddy!” Jack said, ducking and waving his hands. “We’re not flowers!” 一只巨大的蜜蜂在杰克头顶盘旋。 “哇哦。 继续跑，伙计！”杰克说着，低头躲开，挥舞着双手。 “我们可不是花朵！”，

The bee buzzed lazily away. 那只蜜蜂懒洋洋地飞走了。

“Hey, we’d better hurry and get our emerald before we become our real size again,” said Annie. “嘿，我们最好赶紧拿到翡翠，不然等我们恢复原大小就来不及了，”安妮说。

“Oh, right!” said Jack. “哦，对啊！”杰克说。

“Which way?” asked Annie. “往哪走？”安妮问。

Jack looked around. It was hard to tell where they were. “I remember the emerald was shining in the sunlight.” 杰克环顾四周。 他们很难分辨自己现在在哪儿。 “我记得翡翠在阳光下闪闪发光。”

“It looks sunny over there,” said Annie. She pointed to a clearing. “那边看起来阳光很好，”安妮说。 她指着一片空地。

“So let’s creep through the shade toward the light,” said Jack. “Remember, the cobra can’t hear us, but she can see us and feel our vibrations. We have to stay hidden and step lightly.” “那我们悄悄穿过树荫，朝光亮的地方走过去，”杰克说。 “记住，眼镜蛇听不见我们，但她能看见我们，还能感受到我们的震动。 “我们必须躲起来，走路要轻。”

“Hey, I wonder where the dad is,” said Annie. “嘿，我想知道爸爸去哪儿了，”安妮说。

“Don’t ask,” said Jack. “Don’t think about it. We have enough to worry about. Come on. Let’s go.” “别问，”杰克说。 “别去想它。 我们有太多事情要担心了。来吧。 让我们走吧。”

He and Annie crouched down and started creeping toward the sunlight. 他和安妮蹲下身子，悄悄地朝阳光的方向爬去。
