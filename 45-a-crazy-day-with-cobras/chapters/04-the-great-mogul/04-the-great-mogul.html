4. The Great Mogul 4. 伟大的莫卧儿皇帝

Jack and Annie froze. The hall was completely still and silent. The Great Mogul climbed the steps to the Peacock Throne. He wore an orange turban and a silver coat trimmed with diamonds and pearls. He wore ropes of jewels around his neck and sparkling rings on his fingers. 杰克和安妮顿时僵在了那里。 大厅里完全安静了下来，没有任何声音。 伟大的莫卧儿皇帝走上台阶，坐到了孔雀宝座上。 他戴着橙色的头巾，穿着镶满钻石和珍珠的银色外套。 他的脖子上戴着一串串珠宝，手指上戴着闪闪发光的戒指。

The Great Mogul sat cross-legged on a cushion and looked coldly at his audience. He had a handsome face with a sharp nose and dark, distant eyes. A servant stood near him, waving a large peacock-feather fan. Another waved a broom to shoo away flies. 伟大的莫卧儿皇帝盘腿坐在垫子上，冷冷地望着他的臣民。 他有一张英俊的脸，鼻子尖尖的，眼睛又黑又远。 一个仆人站在他身边，挥动着一把大孔雀羽毛扇子。 另一个仆人挥舞着扫帚，赶走飞虫。

An imperial guard escorted a pair of European ambassadors up the wide steps that led to the throne. 一名帝国卫兵护送着一对欧洲大使，沿着通往王座的宽大台阶走上来。

Jack and Annie watched carefully as the ambassadors stood before the Great Mogul. The two men bowed from their waists and brushed the back of their right hands over the carpet. They straightened up, raised their right hands into the air, and placed their palms on top of their heads. Then they lowered their hands and stood very still. 杰克和安妮仔细看着大使们站在大莫卧儿面前。 这两个人从腰部鞠躬，然后用右手的背面轻轻拂过地毯。 他们站直身体，举起右手，手掌放在头顶。 然后他们放下手，一动不动地站着。

So that’s the way to bow before a Great Mogul, thought Jack. He hoped he and Annie could remember how to do it. 原来向大莫卧儿鞠躬是这个样子，杰克心想。 他希望他和安妮能记住怎么鞠躬。

One of the ambassadors then opened a bag and pulled out a jewel-studded crown and a golden goblet. He gave them to the Great Mogul. The ruler looked at the glittering gifts. Without smiling, he handed them to a servant who had appeared beside him. 然后，其中一位大使打开一个袋子，拿出一顶镶嵌宝石的王冠和一个金色的酒杯。 他把它们交给了大莫卧儿。 统治者看着闪闪发光的礼物。 他没有微笑，而是把它们递给了一位出现在他身旁的仆人。

Another servant stepped forward with a silver tray. On the tray was a red satin pillow. Spread across the pillow were dozens of glittering jewels. The ambassadors selected several precious gems. Then they turned and walked slowly down the steps of the throne. 另一位仆人拿着一个银盘走了过来。 盘子里有一个红色的丝绸枕头。 枕头上铺着数十颗闪闪发光的宝石。 大使们挑选了几颗珍贵的宝石。 然后他们转身，慢慢地走下王座的台阶。

Annie nudged Jack. He looked at her, and she smiled. He knew just what she was thinking: Those guys got jewels without saying a word! 安妮推了推杰克。 他看着她，她笑了。 他知道她在想什么：那些人不用说一句话就得到了珠宝！

But Jack was still worried. An emerald rose wasn’t an ordinary jewel. Plus, his story seemed like such a teeny gift compared to a crown and a gold goblet. 但杰克还是担心。 一颗翡翠玫瑰可不是普通的珠宝。 再说，他的礼物和皇冠、金杯相比，好像太小了。

The other ambassadors climbed the Peacock Throne in pairs. They all bowed in the same way. They all opened their bags and gave gifts to the Great Mogul—a carved wooden clock, a crystal necklace, a gleaming silver sword. As the ruler received each exquisite gift, he never changed his expression. 其他大使都是两人一组爬上孔雀宝座的。 他们都用同样的方式鞠躬。 他们都打开自己的袋子，向莫卧儿大帝献上礼物——一个雕刻的木钟、一条水晶项链、一把闪闪发光的银剑。 当统治者收到每一件精美的礼物时，他的表情从未改变。

Jack grew more and more anxious. If the Great Mogul seemed bored with these treasures, what would he think of a kid’s story? 杰克越来越紧张了。 如果莫卧儿大帝对这些珍宝都感到无聊，那他会怎么看待一个孩子的故事呢？

All the ambassadors from Europe were offered jewels from the silver tray. Then they resumed their place in line. Finally an imperial guard beckoned to Jack and Annie. It was their turn! 来自欧洲的所有大使都被邀请从银盘上挑选珠宝。 然后他们重新排好队。 终于，一名帝国卫兵朝杰克和安妮招了招手。 轮到他们了！

Jack’s legs felt wobbly as he walked with Annie up the steps of the throne. The Great Mogul looked at them with his distant, cold stare. 杰克和安妮一起走上王座的台阶时，杰克的腿感觉有点发抖。 莫卧儿大帝用他遥远而冰冷的目光看着他们。

Together, Jack and Annie bowed from their waists. They brushed the back of their right hands over the carpet. They stood up and raised their right hands into the air. They placed their palms on top of their heads. Jack held his breath the whole time. A fly landed on his nose, but he didn’t flinch. 杰克和安妮一起弯下腰鞠躬。 他们用右手的后背轻轻拂过地毯。 他们站直身子，把右手举到空中。 他们把手掌放在头上。 杰克一直屏住呼吸。 一只苍蝇停在他鼻子上，但他没有动一下。

Jack and Annie lowered their arms. Jack’s hands were shaky as he unbuckled his bag and pulled out his story. Without a word, he handed it to the Great Mogul. 杰克和安妮放下了手臂。 杰克的手在解开背包、拿出故事书时一直在发抖。 他没有说一句话，就把书交给了大莫卧儿。

The ruler stared at the first page. Slowly he ran his fingers over the fancy type. He turned the page and stared at Annie’s sparkly drawing of the tree house in the Frog Creek woods. Again he touched the page. 统治者盯着书的第一页。 他慢慢地用手指抚摸着精美的字体。 他翻过一页，盯着安妮在蛙溪镇树林里画的闪闪发光的树屋。 他又一次摸了摸那一页。

Then the Great Mogul raised his eyes and looked straight at Jack and Annie. The next thing Jack knew, the servant was standing in front of them with the tray of jewels. In a panic, Jack stared at the pile of diamonds, pearls, and red, green, and yellow gems. The jewels were all cut in different shapes. 然后，大莫卧儿抬起头，直直地看向杰克和安妮。 接下来发生的事情，杰克知道的下一件事就是，仆人拿着装满珠宝的托盘站在他们面前。 杰克惊慌失措地盯着那堆钻石、珍珠，还有红的、绿的、黄的宝石。 这些珠宝都被切割成了不同的形状。

Emerald rose! Jack thought wildly. He tried to focus, but the glittering gems swirled in front of his eyes—flowers, fish, stars, birds, butterflies, pears, and apples. But no emerald rose! 翡翠玫瑰！ 杰克疯狂地想着。 他试图集中注意力，但闪闪发光的宝石在他眼前旋转着，变成了花朵、鱼儿、星星、鸟儿、蝴蝶、梨子和苹果。 但是没有翡翠玫瑰！

When Jack glanced up, he saw the Great Mogul watching him. Jack was ready to grab anything! 当杰克抬头一看，他看见大莫卧儿正盯着他。 杰克准备抓起任何东西！

Annie reached forward and carefully picked up a green stone. She solemnly showed it to Jack. It was a small emerald shaped like a rose! 安妮伸出手，小心地捡起一块绿色的石头。 她郑重其事地把它展示给杰克。 这是一块形状像玫瑰的小翡翠！

Jack nodded, trying to appear calm. His heart was pounding. He glanced at the Great Mogul. The ruler was looking at them with interest. He seemed about to say something. But then a guard beckoned for Jack and Annie to leave the throne platform. 杰克点点头，努力装作很冷静。 他的心跳得很快。 他看了看大莫卧儿。 统治者饶有兴趣地看着他们。 他似乎想要说些什么。 但就在这时，一名卫兵示意杰克和安妮离开王座平台。

Jack and Annie climbed down the steps and returned to their places behind the other ambassadors. 杰克和安妮走下台阶，回到其他大使后面的位置。

Wow, wow, wow! Jack thought. We did it! 哇，哇，哇！杰克想。 我们成功了！

The Great Mogul stood up from his cushion. Quickly and quietly, he walked down the steps of the Peacock Throne. No one moved as the all-powerful ruler slipped out of the hall. 大莫卧儿从他的垫子上站了起来。 他迅速而安静地走下孔雀宝座的台阶。 所有人都一动不动，因为这位至高无上的统治者悄悄溜出了大厅。

The whole audience seemed to breathe a sigh of relief. Everyone began talking and moving about. The guards stepped forward to escort the ambassadors through the door at the back of the hall. 整个观众似乎都松了一口气。 大家开始说话，四处走动。 卫兵们走上前，护送大使们从大厅后门走出去。

“Come on, let’s get out of here!” Jack whispered to Annie. He pulled her away from the other ambassadors, and they joined the stream of court nobles heading toward the doors at the front of the hall. “快点，我们得赶快离开这里！”杰克对安妮小声说。 他把她从其他大使身边拉走，然后他们加入了朝大厅前门走去的贵族队伍。

“Mission accomplished!” Annie exclaimed. She handed Jack the emerald rose. “任务完成啦！”安妮兴奋地说。 她把翡翠玫瑰递给了杰克。

Jack stared at the delicately cut stone. It had five tiny rose petals that curled back at the edges. “Great work,” he said to Annie. He carefully put the jewel in his bag and buckled it. “I was wrong. You were right.” 杰克盯着那块精致切割的石头。 石头上有五片小小的玫瑰花瓣，边缘微微卷起。 “干得真棒，”他对安妮说。 他小心地把宝石放进包里，系好扣子。 “我错了，你才是对的。”

“I won’t rub it in,” said Annie with a grin. “我不会得瑟的，”安妮笑着说。

“That’s okay, I deserve it,” Jack said. “I shouldn’t have worried so much. Now we just have to get back to the tree house. Keep your head down. Move quickly.” “没关系，我确实应该得到表扬，”杰克说。 “我不该那么担心的。” 现在我们只要回到树屋就可以了。 低下头，快点走。

Jack and Annie wound their way through the crowd, heading for the front doors. Just as they were about to step into the sunlight, another guard blocked their way. 杰克和安妮在人群中穿行，朝大门走去。 就在他们准备走进阳光下时，另一个卫兵挡住了他们的路。

“Excuse us,” said Jack. He tried to step around the guard who towered over them. “打扰一下，”杰克说。 他试图绕过那个站在他们面前的高大卫兵。

But the guard put out his large hand. “I will escort you now to the palace,” he said, “to the private balcony of the Great Mogul.” 但卫兵伸出他那双大手。 “我现在要带你们去宫殿，”他说，“去大莫卧儿的私人阳台。”

“Uh … why?” Jack asked. “呃……为什么？”杰克问。

“All the ambassadors are to view a parade in their honor,” said the guard. “所有大使都要为他们观看一场荣誉游行，”卫兵说。

“Parade?” said Annie. “I love parades.” “游行？”安妮说。 “我喜欢游行。”

“Uh … but we’re kind of in a hurry. Do we have a choice?” Jack asked. “呃……但我们有点赶时间。” “我们有选择吗？”杰克问。

“No,” said the guard. “没有，”守卫说。

“Oh,” said Jack. “Okay.” “哦，”杰克说。 “好的。”

“This way,” said the guard. “这边走，”守卫说。

Jack and Annie walked after him back through the empty hall. 杰克和安妮跟着他穿过空荡荡的走廊往回走。

“Don’t worry,” Annie whispered to Jack. “What could possibly go wrong at a parade?” “别担心，”安妮低声对杰克说。 “游行的时候怎么可能出错呢？”
