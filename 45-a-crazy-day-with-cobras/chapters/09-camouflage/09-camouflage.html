9. Camouflage 9. 隐身术

Jack and Annie stepped lightly through dead leaves and pushed aside feathery weeds that towered above them. They carefully climbed over twigs, pebbles, and tangled roots. They skirted around a deserted anthill as high as Jack’s knees. 杰克和安妮轻手轻脚地踩着枯叶，拨开那些高过他们头顶的羽毛状的杂草。 他们小心地爬过树枝、鹅卵石和缠绕的树根。 他们绕过了一个废弃的蚁丘，那个蚁丘高到杰克的膝盖。

When they got to the edge of the cobra’s clearing, Jack stopped. He held up his hand and Annie nodded. They peered between tall blades of grass. 当他们来到眼镜蛇活动区域的边缘时，杰克停了下来。 他举起了手，安妮点了点头。 他们透过高高的草叶缝隙朝外张望。

The emerald rose glittered in the sunlight. It looked as big as a softball. The cobra was still coiled around her nest. But now she looked as big as a monster in a fairy tale. 那朵翡翠色的玫瑰在阳光下闪闪发光。 看起来它有棒球那么大。 眼镜蛇仍然盘绕在她的巢穴周围。 但现在她看起来像童话故事里的怪物一样大。

“Whoa!” Jack breathed. “哇！”杰克喘着气说。

“What do you think we should do?” Annie whispered. “Should I just run as fast as I can, grab it, and run back?” “你觉得我们该怎么办？”安妮小声问。 “我是不是应该跑得尽可能快，拿到它，然后马上跑回来？”

“No, no!” said Jack. He was shocked by how big the cobra looked. “不，不行！”杰克说。 他被眼镜蛇看起来那么大的样子吓了一跳。

“I think she’s asleep,” said Annie. “我觉得她睡着了，”安妮说。

“We can’t really tell,” said Jack. “Her eyes are hard to see because of her camouflage.” “我们真的没法确定，”杰克说。 “她因为有伪装色，所以很难被看见。”

“So how—” started Annie. “那怎么—”安妮开始说。

“That’s it!” said Jack. “Camouflage!” “就是它！”杰克说。 “伪装色！”

“What about it?” said Annie. “那又怎样？”安妮说。

“That’s how we’ll get the emerald!” said Jack. “We’ll use our own camouflage.” “这就是我们得到翡翠的方法！”杰克说。 “我们会用我们自己的伪装色。”

“You mean like leaves?” said Annie. “你是说像树叶那样？”安妮说。

“Yeah, leaves would be good,” said Jack. They looked around. “There.” He pointed to a vine with large light green leaves. He and Annie gripped one of the leaves and pulled until it tore off the vine. “对，树叶会很好，”杰克说。 他们环顾四周。 “那儿。”他指着一根长着大而鲜绿色叶子的藤蔓。 他和安妮抓住其中一片叶子，用力一拉，直到叶子从藤蔓上撕下来。

Annie held it up in front of her like a shield. “How’s this?” she said. 安妮把它举到面前，像一面盾牌。 “这样怎么样？”她说。

“Great,” said Jack. “Now one for me.” He and Annie gripped another leaf and pulled until they ripped it from the vine, too. “很好，”杰克说。 “现在我来一片。”他和安妮又抓住另一片叶子，用力一拉，也把叶子从藤蔓上撕了下来。

“Poke holes in them, so we can see,” said Annie. “在叶子上戳几个洞，这样我们就能看见了，”安妮说。

“Good idea,” said Jack. They poked holes in their leaves and then held them up, covering their faces. Jack moved his leaf around until he could see the emerald through the eyeholes. “好主意，”杰克说。 他们戳了叶子上的洞，然后举起来，遮住自己的脸。 杰克把叶子来回移动，直到他能透过眼洞看到翡翠。

“Go slowly and stick with me,” Jack said. “Two steps, then stop. Two steps, then stop.” “慢慢来，跟着我，”杰克说。 “走两步，然后停下。 走两步，然后停下。”

“Got it,” said Annie. “明白了，”安妮说。

Holding their leaves in front of them, Jack and Annie carefully stepped into the clearing. They took two steps toward the emerald, then stopped … two steps, then stopped. 杰克和安妮把树叶举在前面，小心翼翼地走进了空地。 他们朝翡翠走了两步，然后停下了……走两步，然后停下。

The cobra didn’t move. Jack desperately hoped she was asleep. He and Annie took two more steps, then stopped. The emerald glittered in the hot sunlight. They took two more steps. Then Annie gasped. 眼镜蛇没有动。 杰克拼命希望她已经睡着了。 他和安妮又走了两步，然后停下了。 翡翠在炎热的阳光下闪闪发光。 他们又走了两步。 然后安妮惊叫起来。

The cobra was lifting her body above her nest! Her head was broad and flat. She swayed back and forth and looked around with staring eyes. 眼镜蛇正把身体抬离她的巢穴！ 她的头又宽又扁。 她左右摇晃，用呆滞的眼神四处张望。

Jack and Annie froze. 杰克和安妮僵住了。

Then slowly the cobra lowered her head. Jack let out his breath. Their camouflage had worked! He motioned to Annie, and they both took another two steps, then stopped. 接着，眼镜蛇慢慢地把头低了下来。 杰克松了口气。 他们的伪装成功了！ 他向安妮做了个手势，他们又走了两步，然后停住了。

Jack was only a step away from the jewel now. Holding up his leaf camouflage with one hand, he leaned over and scooped up the emerald. It felt as heavy as a big rock. Holding the leaf with one hand, Jack used his other hand to shove the emerald into his bag. With Annie behind him, he stepped backward—two steps, then stopped. 杰克现在离那颗宝石只差一步了。 他用一只手举着树叶伪装，弯下腰，捡起了那颗翡翠。 它感觉重得像一块大石头。 杰克用一只手拿着树叶，另一只手把翡翠塞进了背包里。 安妮在他身后，他往后退了两步，然后停了下来。

Jack kept his eye on the cobra coiled around her nest. He and Annie kept backing up. 杰克一直盯着盘绕在她巢穴周围的蛇。 他和安妮继续往后退。

Suddenly a sound came from behind them—a strange hissing, growling sound. A chill went through Jack. He turned around. 突然，他们身后传来一声奇怪的嘶嘶声和低吼声。 杰克感到一阵寒意，他转过身。

A monster-size cobra towered over them. 一条怪物般巨大的蛇高高耸立在他们面前。

“The dad!” yelled Annie. “爸爸！”安妮喊道。

The male cobra had the same coloring as the female. His hood was spread out as he raised himself high above the grass. He swayed from side to side, staring down at Jack and Annie with cold, unblinking eyes. He opened his giant mouth, showing two deadly fangs. 这条雄蛇的颜色和雌蛇一样。 他的头罩张开，他高高抬起了自己，站在草地上。 他左右摇晃着，用冰冷、没有眨眼的眼睛盯着杰克和安妮。 他张开巨大的嘴巴，露出两颗致命的毒牙。

“AHHH!” said Jack and Annie. “啊——！”杰克和安妮喊道。

They dropped their leaves and crouched down, hiding themselves in the grass. “Go—go!” sputtered Jack. They took off, scrambling through the undergrowth of the forest. 他们扔下树叶，蹲下来，躲进了草丛里。 “快——快！”杰克结结巴巴地说。 他们跑了起来，穿过森林的灌木丛。

Jack led the way as they both half crawled, half ran over the dirt and through the grass. Jack lost his slippers and ripped his coat. Like a mouse, he clambered over sticks and twigs, nuts, seeds, bark, feathers, mosses, and mushrooms. All kinds of ants and beetles scuttled out of the way. The air was filled with screeching insect sounds. 杰克带头，他们一边爬一边跑，穿过泥土和草地。 杰克弄丢了拖鞋，还把外套撕破了。 像一只老鼠一样，他爬过了树枝、小棍、坚果、种子、树皮、羽毛、青苔和蘑菇。 各种各样的蚂蚁和甲虫都赶紧躲开了。 空气中充满了昆虫尖叫声。

Jack had no idea where he was running, but he knew he couldn’t stop. He was sure the cobra was slithering after them, thrashing through the undergrowth. Maybe both king cobras were searching for them now, he thought, and all the forest was screaming about it. 杰克不知道自己跑到了哪里，但他知道不能停下。 他确信眼镜蛇正在他们后面滑行，穿过灌木丛追赶他们。 他想，也许两条眼镜蛇王现在都在找他们，整个森林都在为这件事尖叫。

Jack stumbled over a stick. He scrambled up from the dirt and looked around wildly. Where was Annie? He didn’t see her! Had she gone off in another direction? Where were the cobras? Had they chased after Annie instead of him? 杰克被一根棍子绊了一下。 他从泥土里爬起来，疯狂地四处张望。安妮在哪里？ 他没看到她！ 她是不是朝另一个方向去了？ 眼镜蛇在哪里？ 他们是不是追着安妮而不是他？

Jack panicked. “Annie!” he shouted. 杰克慌了神。 “安妮！”他喊道。

The ground trembled. Jack heard thumping, thrashing, and bells. He crouched down in the grass and covered his head. He heard Annie shout, “Jack! Where are you, Jack?” She sounded as if she was up in a tree! What was going on? 地面在颤抖。 杰克听到咚咚的脚步声、乱七八糟的声音和铃铛声。 他蹲下来，藏在草丛里，用手护住头。 他听到安妮喊道：“杰克！ 杰克，你在哪里？“她听起来好像在树上！ 怎么回事？

Jack looked up. Suddenly a gigantic snake dropped down in front of him. 杰克抬头看了看。 突然，一条巨大的蛇从他面前掉了下来。

“AHHH!” Jack yelled. “啊！”杰克大叫起来。

“Yay!” Annie yelled from overhead. “太棒了！”安妮从上面喊道。

The snake didn’t have the olive brown skin of a cobra. It was gray and wrinkled. It wasn’t a snake at all! It was the trunk of an elephant! 这条蛇的皮肤不是眼镜蛇那种橄榄色的。 它是灰色的，还有皱纹。 这根本不是蛇！ 这是大象的鼻子！

Before Jack could think, the tip of the elephant’s trunk curled around him. Clutching him like a banana, Morning Breeze lifted Jack up in the air. Jack clung to his bag so he wouldn’t lose the emerald again. The trunk curled back, and Jack could see the sky whirling above him. 杰克还没来得及反应，大象的鼻子尖已经缠住了他。 紧紧抓着杰克，就像抓着一根香蕉，晨风把他举到了空中。 杰克紧紧抓着他的包，以免再次弄丢那块翡翠。 大象的鼻子卷了回来，杰克看见天空在他头顶上旋转。

The elephant uncoiled her trunk and let go of Jack. He plopped down into the straw carriage. Annie was already there! 大象松开了鼻子，放开了杰克。 他扑通一声掉进了稻草做的马车里。 安妮已经在那儿了！

“Morning Breeze!” Annie cried. “She came back for us!” “晨风！”安妮喊道。 “她回来救我们了！”

Jack was so small, he couldn’t see over the sides of the carriage. He tried to stand up to look for the cobras. But just then Morning Breeze bellowed and bolted through the forest. Jack lost his balance and fell backward. 杰克太小了，他够不到马车的边沿。 他试着站起来，想找眼镜蛇。 但是就在这时，晨风号突然大声嘶鸣，冲进了森林。 杰克失去了平衡，向后摔倒了。

Jack and Annie both laughed as they bounced around in the basket on the back of the elephant. They were safe from the cobras! They had their emerald rose! And Morning Breeze had risked her life to save them! 杰克和安妮都笑起来，他们坐在大象背上的篮子里蹦蹦跳跳。 他们安全了，躲过了眼镜蛇！ 他们得到了翡翠玫瑰！ 晨风号为了救他们，差点丢了性命！
