11. Heart to Heart 11. 心与心

Jack and Annie silently followed the guard to his carriage and climbed in the back. The guard flicked the reins, and the pair of black horses took off, trotting down the dirt path. 杰克和安妮默默地跟着卫兵走到他的马车旁，然后爬进车后。 卫兵挥动了缰绳，两匹黑马立刻奔跑起来，沿着泥路小跑。

Annie looked at Jack. “I wonder where we’re going,” she whispered. 安妮看着杰克。 “我不知道我们要去哪儿，”她小声说。

Jack just shook his head. His worst thought was that the Great Mogul had changed his mind and wanted to punish them. 杰克只是摇了摇头。 他最害怕的想法是，莫卧儿大帝改变了主意，想要惩罚他们。

The horses passed the mud huts, the men and boys hauling hay, the women and girls cooking outdoors. They passed goats and sheep and chickens. 马儿经过泥巴小屋，男人们和男孩们在搬运干草，女人们和女孩们在户外做饭。 他们经过山羊、绵羊和鸡。

The horses clopped down the stone road. They moved easily through the bazaar. Jack saw that the silks and carpets were hanging on the lines again. Oranges, lemons, and pineapples were back in the baskets. Tent poles were all standing, and the bearded merchants looked happy and busy. 马儿踏着石头路走了下去。 他们轻松地穿过了集市。 杰克看到丝绸和地毯又挂回了线上。 橙子、柠檬和菠萝又回到了篮子里。 帐篷的杆子都竖起来了，长着胡子的商人们看起来又开心又忙碌。

The horses trotted down the street toward the Red Fort. When they came to the drawbridge, Jack expected them to cross the moat and head back to the palace, but they didn’t. The horses trotted past the fort and down a road along the river. 马儿沿着街道小跑，朝红堡方向前进。 他们来到吊桥时，杰克以为他们会穿过护城河，然后回到宫殿，但他们没有。 马沿着河岸的道路，经过堡垒继续向前走。

The sun was low in the sky when the horse-drawn carriage came to a square. The horses stopped before a massive red gateway with an arched entrance. 当马车到达广场时，太阳已经快落山了。 马在一座巨大的红色大门前停下，大门有一个拱形入口。

The guard turned to Jack and Annie. “You are to go inside and wait,” he said. 卫兵转向杰克和安妮。 “你们要进去等着，”他说。

“Okay. Thanks for the ride,” said Annie. “好的。 谢谢你的车程，”安妮说。

The guard stayed in the carriage while Jack and Annie climbed out. They walked up to the looming gateway. 卫兵留在马车里，而杰克和安妮下了车。 他们走到那座高耸的大门前。

“Where are we?” Annie whispered to Jack. “And why are we here?” “我们在哪里？”安妮小声对杰克说。 “我们为什么在这里？”

“If I knew, I’d tell you,” whispered Jack. “如果我知道，我就会告诉你，”杰克小声说。

Jack and Annie stepped under the high, arched entrance. Leading from the entrance was a narrow canal stretching to the horizon. 杰克和安妮走进了高高的拱形入口下。 入口后面是一条狭窄的运河，一直延伸到地平线。

In the sunset, Jack thought he saw a cloud of mist hovering above the ground. Then he realized it was a shimmering, milky white dome about twenty stories high. It seemed to float between the earth and the sky. There were four tall towers at the corners of a terrace beneath the dome. 在夕阳下，杰克觉得他看到地上有一团雾气漂浮着。 然后他意识到，那其实是大约二十层楼高的闪闪发光的乳白色圆顶。 它似乎漂浮在大地和天空之间。 圆顶下方的露台上，四个高高的塔楼位于角落。

Jack and Annie just stared at the dreamlike vision. Then they saw a man walking along the narrow canal toward them. As the man got closer, Jack caught his breath. It was the Great Mogul! No guards were with him. He was all alone. 杰克和安妮只是呆呆地望着这梦幻般的景象。 然后他们看到一个男人沿着狭窄的运河朝他们走来。 当那个人走近时，杰克屏住了呼吸。 那是大莫卧儿！ 他身边没有卫兵，他一个人。

The Great Mogul stopped and stared at them. His gaze took in their ragged, torn coats and bare feet. 大莫卧儿停下了，盯着他们看。 他的目光扫过他们破旧、撕裂的外套和光着的脚。

Jack panicked. They looked terrible. Would the ruler think they weren’t showing proper respect for him? 杰克慌了。 他们看起来糟透了。 这位统治者会不会觉得他们没有对他表示应有的尊重？

“Bow,” Annie whispered. “鞠躬，”安妮小声说。

Together, they bowed from their waists and brushed their right hands on the ground. They straightened up, raised their right hands into the air, and placed their palms on their heads. Then they lowered their hands and stood perfectly still. Jack cast his eyes down, afraid to look directly at the Great Mogul. 他们一起弯下腰，用右手轻轻触碰地面。 他们站直身体，把右手举过头顶，手掌贴在头上。 然后他们放下手，一动不动地站着。 杰克低着头，不敢直视大莫卧儿。

There was a moment of silence. Then the all-powerful ruler spoke. “I have never read such writing before. Or seen such artwork,” he said. 有一小会儿，房间里一片寂静。 然后，这位至高无上的统治者开口说话了。 “我从来没有见过这样的文字。 也没有见过这样的艺术品，”他说。

Jack looked up at the Great Mogul. But he didn’t know what to do. Were they allowed to talk? 杰克抬头看向大莫卧儿。 但他不知道该怎么做。 他们可以说话吗？

“Excuse me, Your Majesty,” said Annie. “Is it all right to speak?” “打扰了，陛下，”安妮说。 “可以说话吗？”

“Yes,” said the Great Mogul. “可以，”大莫卧儿说。

“Thank you. Well, Jack wrote the words,” said Annie. “And I drew the pictures.” “谢谢。 “杰克写了这些字，”安妮说。 “我画了这些画。”

The Great Mogul nodded. “Your story and pictures tell of riding through the world in a tree house. You tell of saving a huge octopus, a baby gorilla, and a baby kangaroo. You tell of helping brilliant men named Leonardo da Vinci and William Shakespeare. You tell of rescuing children from a giant ocean wave and from a sinking ship. You are a great storyteller,” he said to Jack. 大莫卧儿点了点头。 “你的故事和画讲的是乘坐树屋穿越世界的事。 你讲述了拯救一只巨大的章鱼、一只小山 gorilla 和一只小袋鼠的事。 你讲述了帮助名叫列奥纳多·达·芬奇和威廉·莎士比亚的聪明人。 你讲述了从巨大的海浪和即将沉没的船上救出孩子们的故事。 “你是个很棒的故事讲述者，”他对杰克说。

“Oh, not really,” said Jack. He felt embarrassed. “I just told about stuff that happened to us.” “哦，其实不是真的，”杰克说。 他感到很尴尬。 “我只是讲了一些发生在我们身上的事情。”

The Great Mogul turned to Annie. “Your pictures are filled with life and joy. They are rich and stirring. You are a great artist,” he said. 伟大的莫卧儿皇帝转向安妮。 “你的画充满了生机和快乐。 它们丰富而激动人心。 你是个很棒的艺术家，”他说。

“Not really,” Annie said. “Lots of people draw better than me. What you probably loved was the perfect printing of Jack’s story and the sparkly colors of my pens.” “其实不是，”安妮说。 “有很多人画得比我好。 你可能喜欢的是杰克故事里完美的排版，还有我笔画出的闪亮颜色。”

“Yeah, we can’t take credit for those things,” said Jack. “是啊，我们不能为这些事情负责，”杰克说。

The Great Mogul almost smiled. “The perfect letters and the sparkles were not what I liked best,” he said. “I liked the heart in the story. I liked the heart in the drawings.” 大莫卧儿几乎笑了。 “完美的字母和闪亮的装饰并不是我最喜欢的地方，”他说。 “我喜欢故事里的真情实感。 我喜欢画中的真情实感。”

Jack couldn’t explain why, but he thought he understood what the Great Mogul meant. 杰克无法解释原因，但他觉得他明白了大莫卧儿的意思。

“And now I want to answer the question you asked me today,” the Great Mogul said to Annie. “You asked if I loved anything. I could not speak freely in front of my guards or my foreign guests. But the answer is yes. I once did love something— some one— very much. My wife. She was my best friend and the mother of many children. I wept an ocean of tears when she died.” “现在我想回答你今天问我的问题，”大莫卧儿对安妮说。 “你问过我有没有什么最喜爱的东西。” 我不能在卫兵和外国客人面前自由说话。 但答案是肯定的。 我曾经非常爱过某样东西——某个人——我的妻子。 她是我最好的朋友，也是许多孩子的母亲。 “她去世时，我流了整整一片海洋的泪水。”

“Oh. I’m so sorry,” said Annie. “真 sorry（对不起）,” 安妮说。

“Me too,” said Jack. “我也是,” 杰克说。

“You have shown me your hearts,” said the Great Mogul. “Now I show you mine.” He turned and looked at the shimmering dome. “This is the tomb of marble I built for my wife. It is called the Taj Mahal.” “你向我展示了你们的心,” 大莫卧儿说。 “现在，我向你展示我的。” 他转过身，看着闪闪发光的圆顶。 “这是用大理石建造的我妻子的墓。” 它叫做泰姬陵。”
