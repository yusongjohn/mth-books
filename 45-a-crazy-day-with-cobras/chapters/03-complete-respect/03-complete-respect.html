3. Complete Respect 3. 完全的尊重

“Very good!” the guard said. “The Great Mogul eagerly awaits your arrival—and your treasure!” “很好！”卫兵说。 “伟大的莫卧儿皇帝迫不及待地等待你们的到来——还有你们的宝藏！”

What treasure? thought Jack. What is Annie talking about? 什么宝藏？杰克想。 安妮在说什么？

“You may pass through the gate,” said the guard. “Follow the other ambassadors to the Hall of Public Audience, where the Great Mogul appears each morning before his court nobles.” “你可以穿过大门，”守卫说。 “跟着其他大使去公众听政厅，大莫卧儿每天早上在贵族们到来之前都会在那里出现。”

“Thanks!” said Annie. She pulled Jack along toward the drawbridge. “谢谢！”安妮说。 她拉着杰克朝吊桥走去。

“And do not worry,” the guard called after them. “The Great Mogul will not mind your youth. His grandfather became emperor when he was thirteen.” “别担心，”卫兵朝他们喊道。 “大莫卧儿不会介意你们还年轻。 他爷爷十三岁就当上了皇帝。”

“Super! Thanks!” Annie called back. “太棒了！ 谢谢！”安妮喊道。

“Hey, what were you talking about back there?” Jack whispered as he and Annie started across the bridge. “We didn’t bring any treasure for the Great Mogul! We didn’t bring anything!” “嘿，刚才你们在说什么？”杰克小声问，他和安妮开始过桥。 “我们没给大莫卧儿带来任何宝藏！ 我们什么都没带！”

“Yes, we did,” said Annie. “是的，我们带了，”安妮说。

“What? Tell me what,” said Jack. “什么？ “告诉我是什么，”杰克说。

“Your story,” said Annie. “It’s in your bag, right?” “你的故事，”安妮说。 “它在你的包里，对吧？”

“You’ve got to be kidding!” said Jack. “That’s not treasure!” “你该不会是开玩笑吧！”杰克说。 “那不是宝藏！”

“Yes, it is! Think about it! You printed your story in fancy curly type from the computer, right?” said Annie. “I did my drawings with sparkle pens. There were no computers or sparkle pens four hundred years ago! The Great Mogul has never seen writing and artwork like that. Our ordinary stuff would be treasure in his time!” “是的，是的！想一想吧！” 你是不是用电脑把故事打印出来，用的是那种花花的卷曲字体呀？”安妮说。 “我用闪亮笔画了我的画。” 四百年前还没有电脑和闪亮的钢笔！ 大莫卧儿从没见过这样的文字和艺术品。 “我们的普通东西在他那个时代会是宝藏！”

“I—I don’t know.…” Jack didn’t know what to think. He just shook his head. “我……我不知道。……”杰克不知道该怎么想。 他只是摇了摇头。

“It would be! Trust me,” said Annie. “And hurry! We don’t want to lose the other ambassadors!” She ran to the entrance of the Red Fort. “那会是的！ 相信我，”安妮说。 “快点！” 我们不能失去其他大使！”她跑到红堡的入口处。

Jack followed her. They passed through a gateway that opened onto a stone road. The road led to a large square. At the opposite side of the square was a long, low building with columns and arches. 杰克跟在她后面。 他们穿过一扇门，门后是一条石子路。 这条路通向一个大广场。 广场对面有一座长长的低矮建筑，上面有柱子和拱门。

“There they are,” said Annie. She pointed to the ambassadors’ oxcarts rolling toward the building. “他们在那儿，”安妮说。 她指着使节的牛车正朝那座建筑驶来。

“That must be the Hall of Public Audience,” said Jack. “那一定是公众听政厅，”杰克说。

Jack and Annie walked quickly toward the building. The air felt as if it were coming from a hot furnace. By the time they arrived at the hall, Jack felt faint from the heat. 杰克和安妮快步朝那座建筑走去。 空气热得就像从一个大火炉里吹出来的一样。 等他们到达大厅时，杰克因为热得快要晕过去了。

The ambassadors from Europe had already gone inside. Fierce-looking guards stood at all the entrances. Daggers and curved swords hung from their belts. 来自欧洲的使节们已经进去了。 看起来很凶的卫兵站在所有入口处。 他们的腰带上挂着短剑和弯刀。

Annie walked up to one of the burly guards. “Excuse me,” she said in a low voice, “but we are ambassadors from Frog Creek, and we are supposed to be with those other ambassadors.” 安妮走到一个身材魁梧的卫兵面前。 “打扰一下，”她低声说，“但我们是从蛙溪镇来的使节，我们应该和那些其他的使节在一起。”

To Jack’s surprise, the guard asked no questions. He nodded and gestured for them to follow him. 让杰克感到惊讶的是，卫兵没有问任何问题。 他点了点头，然后做了个手势让他们跟着他走。

Annie and Jack followed the guard into the shadowy hall. The air inside was much cooler. It smelled of roses and mint. Hidden musicians played soft music. 安妮和杰克跟着卫兵走进了昏暗的大厅。 里面空气凉凉的。 这里闻起来有玫瑰和薄荷的味道。 隐藏的乐手们演奏着轻柔的音乐。

The burly guard escorted Jack and Annie past the audience of court nobles. All the men had beards or mustaches and wore beautiful coats of many colors and patterns—bright orange, purple, turquoise, green striped, and pale pink with flowers. 那个强壮的卫兵护送杰克和安妮，走过一群贵族观众。 所有男人都留着胡子或小胡子，穿着漂亮的衣服，颜色和图案五彩缤纷——鲜亮的橙色、紫色、蓝绿色、绿色条纹，还有淡粉色带花朵的。

Next to the nobles, the eight ambassadors from Europe stood along a silver railing. They were lined up in pairs. The burly guard ushered Jack and Annie to the end of the line. 在贵族旁边，来自欧洲的八位大使站在一条银色的栏杆旁。 他们排成一对一对的。 那个强壮的卫兵把杰克和安妮带到队伍的最后面。

Everyone in the hall was facing a high platform with wide steps. The platform was made of sparkling diamonds, emeralds, and pearls. Gold columns supported a golden canopy above the platform. Statues of two jeweled peacocks with rubies in their beaks were perched above the canopy. Over the golden canopy was another canopy of rich cloth. 大厅里的每个人都面对着一个高高的平台，平台上有一级级宽宽的台阶。 这个平台是由闪闪发光的钻石、祖母绿和珍珠做成的。 金色的柱子支撑着平台上方的金色穹顶。 两只镶嵌珠宝的孔雀雕像，嘴里叼着红宝石，停在穹顶上方。 金色穹顶上方还有另一层华丽的布料穹顶。

Tall, thin candlesticks held flickering candles, and servants sprayed rose-scented water into the air. As Annie looked around at everything, Jack slipped their book out of his bag. He huddled over it and read to himself. 高高的细长烛台托着摇曳的蜡烛，仆人们往空中喷洒玫瑰香味的水。 安妮环顾四周时，杰克悄悄从包里拿出他们的书。 他蹲下身，自己读了起来。

Every morning, the Great Mogul sat on his Peacock Throne in the Hall of Public Audience. He demanded complete respect. Everyone had to be perfectly still and silent in his presence. No one was allowed to sit or leave as long as he was in the hall. If his guests spoke to him without permission or did not bow the proper way, he could have them killed. 每天早上，大莫卧儿都坐在公众听政厅的孔雀宝座上。他要求得到完全的尊重。在他就坐时，每个人都必须保持完全的安静和不动。只要他在大厅里，没有人可以坐下或离开。如果他的客人未经允许对他说话，或者没有以正确的方式鞠躬，他可能会把他们杀死。

Oh, man! thought Jack. He shoved the book back into his bag and turned to Annie. “Don’t speak!” he whispered. “And we have to bow the proper way!” 天啊！杰克心想。 他把书塞回包里，转向安妮。 “别说话！”他小声说。 “而且我们必须用正确的方式行礼！”

“Don’t worry, we learned how to bow in the palace in Vienna,” Annie said. “别担心，我们在维也纳的皇宫里学会了怎么鞠躬，”安妮说。

“No, this is different!” whispered Jack. “When I messed up there, people just laughed. Here, they kill you!” “不，这不一样！”杰克小声说。 “我上次搞砸了，那里的人只是笑了。 这里，他们会杀了你！”

“Oh. Well, don’t forget we’ve got our potion from Kathleen,” Annie whispered. “If worse comes to worst, we can make ourselves small and escape.” “别忘了，我们还有凯瑟琳给的魔药，”安妮小声说。 “如果情况变得更糟，我们可以把自己变小然后逃跑。”

“Forget it,” Jack whispered. “I don’t want to be a tiny little person! The guards will just chase us down and squash us like bugs!” “别想了，”杰克小声说。 “我不想变成一个小小的矮人！ 卫兵会追着我们，像踩死虫子一样把我们压扁！”

Annie laughed. 安妮笑了。

“This isn’t funny,” said Jack. “Let’s go back outside and make another plan. We don’t know what we’re doing here.” “这不 funny，”杰克说。 “我们回到外面去，再想个新计划吧。 我们不知道在这里该做什么。”

“Yes, we do,” Annie said. “We’re going to give your story to the Great Mogul, and then we’re going to ask him for an emerald rose. Simple.” “是的，我们知道，”安妮说。 “我们要把你的故事告诉大莫卧儿，然后我们再向他要一朵翡翠玫瑰。很简单。”

“No, no!” said Jack. “I told you we can’t say a word to him! Not a word! Come on, we have to leave while we’re still alive.” “不，不！”杰克说。 “我告诉你，我们不能对他说一句话！一句话也不能说！ 快点，我们必须在还活着的时候离开。”

Annie sighed. “Okay, fine,” she said. 安妮叹了口气。 “好吧，行，”她说。

But as Jack and Annie turned to go, trumpet blasts shattered the air. The trumpet sounds were followed by deafening drumrolls. 但杰克和安妮转身要走时，号角声突然划破了空气。 号角声过后，紧接着是震耳欲聋的鼓声。

Shah Jahãn, the Great Mogul of India, stepped through a door at the back of the hall. 沙贾汗，印度伟大的莫卧儿皇帝，从大厅后门走了出来。
