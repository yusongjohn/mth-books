6. Morning Breeze 6. 清晨微风

“Your Majesty, sir!” Annie called to the Great Mogul. “She’s just an animal! Please don’t let anyone hurt her!” “陛下，大人！”安妮对大莫卧儿喊道。 “她只是一只动物！ 请别让任何人伤害她！

“Shhh! You can’t talk to him!” Jack whispered furiously. “嘘！ “你不能和他说话！”杰克气愤地低声说。

“Please help her!” Annie said to the Great Mogul. “She doesn’t know how great you are. She doesn’t know she should respect you.” “请帮帮她！”安妮对大莫卧儿说。 “她不知道你有多伟大。 她不知道她应该尊重你。”

The Great Mogul kept staring at Annie but said nothing. 大莫卧儿一直盯着安妮看，但什么也没说。

“Don’t you love animals?” Annie asked. “你不喜欢动物吗？”安妮问。

The Great Mogul didn’t answer. 大莫卧儿没有回答。

“Don’t you love anything? ” asked Annie. “你什么都不喜欢吗？”安妮问。

The ambassadors gasped. Two of the guards grabbed Annie by her arms. 大使们惊呼起来。 两个卫兵抓住了安妮的手臂。

“No, please, she— he can’t help it!” Jack explained to the guards. “That’s just the way my brother is. He can’t stop himself from talking. Please, tell your ruler that!” “不，求你们了，她——他控制不了自己！”杰克对卫兵解释道。 “这就是我哥哥的个性。 他控制不住自己说话。 求你们，告诉你们的统治者吧！”

The Great Mogul murmured something to a bodyguard. Then he turned and left the balcony. 大莫卧儿低声对一名卫兵说了些什么。 然后他转身离开了阳台。

The guard looked startled. He said something in a low voice to the men holding Annie. Jack held his breath. Were they going to drag her away? To his surprise, the guards let Annie go. 卫兵看起来很惊讶。 他低声对拿着安妮的那些人说了些什么。 杰克屏住了呼吸。 他们要把她带走吗？ 让安妮惊讶的是，守卫们放过了她。

“Is the Great Mogul going to punish the elephant?” Annie asked. “大莫卧儿会惩罚这头大象吗？”安妮问。

“No, he is not,” a guard said. “He has given her to you as a gift.” “不，他不会，”一个守卫说。 “他已经把她送给你作为礼物了。”

“What?” said Jack. “什么？”杰克说。

“Really?” said Annie. “真的吗？”安妮说。

“Given her to us as a gift? What does that mean, exactly?” Jack asked. “把她作为礼物送给我们？” 这到底是什么意思？”杰克问。

“It means you must take her home,” said the guard. “Back to your own country.” “这意味着你们必须带她回家，”卫兵说。 “回你们自己的国家去。”

“Take the elephant home? ” said Jack. “把大象带回家？”杰克说。

“Come with me,” the guard said. “跟我来，”守卫说。

“Come on, Jack!” Annie called as she hurried after the guard, leaving the balcony. “快点，杰克！”安妮一边喊着，一边赶紧跟在守卫后面，离开了阳台。

“Take the elephant home? ” Jack repeated. “把大象带回家？”杰克又重复了一遍。

The ambassadors were still gaping at him. “Who are you?” one of them asked. “Where are you from?” 大使们还在惊讶地看着他。 “你是谁？”其中一个人问。 “你来自哪里？”

“Jack, ambassador from Frog Creek,” said Jack. “Excuse me.” In a daze, he hurried after Annie and the guard. When he caught up to them, he whispered to Annie, “Have you lost your mind? We can’t take an elephant home!” “杰克，来自蛙溪镇的大使，”杰克说。 “对不起。”他恍惚着，赶紧跟在安妮和守卫后面。 当他追上他们时，他低声对安妮说：“你是不是疯了？” “我们不能把大象带回家！”

“We can’t let her stay here, either,” said Annie. “我们也不能让她待在这里，”安妮说。

“They’ll punish her!” “他们会惩罚她！”

“Okay. Then you can be the one who carries her up the rope ladder,” said Jack. “好的。 “那你可以负责把她沿着绳梯抬上去，”杰克说。

“Ha-ha,” said Annie. “哈哈哈，”安妮说。

“It’s not ha-ha,” said Jack, “it’s insane.” “这不是哈哈哈，”杰克说，“这是疯狂。”

“Look, let’s just get her out of the Red Fort first,” said Annie. “Then we’ll figure out what to do.” “听着，我们先把她的救出来，”安妮说。 “然后我们再想办法。”

Jack and Annie followed the guard until they emerged at the edge of the square in front of the Hall of Public Audience. Several guards were scuffling with the elephant. They had a rope around her neck. Her ears were flapping. She was snorting and stamping her feet. 杰克和安妮跟着卫兵走，直到他们来到大厅前面广场的边缘。 几个卫兵正和大象扭打在一起。 他们用一根绳子套住了她的脖子。 她的耳朵不停地拍打。 她一边喷气，一边用脚踩地。

The guards pulled on the rope, forcing the elephant down on her knees. Her eyes looked desperate and furious. 卫兵们拉紧绳子，把大象按倒在地。 她的眼睛看起来又绝望又愤怒。

“Hey, don’t hurt her!” Jack blurted out. “别伤害她！”杰克突然喊了出来。

A guard pointed his saber at Jack. “Climb on. Now. Both of you,” he said. “You have caused enough trouble this day. It is a miracle you are still alive!” 一个卫兵用剑指着杰克。 “上来，现在。 “你们两个，”他说。 “今天你们已经惹够麻烦了。 你们还能活下来真是个奇迹！”，

Jack clutched his bag and scrambled awkwardly into the straw carriage on the elephant’s back. Annie climbed in behind him. Crammed together, they gripped the sides of the carriage. 杰克抓住他的袋子，笨拙地爬上了大象背上的稻草车。 安妮爬到他后面。 他们挤在一起，紧紧抓住马车的两边。

“Her name is Morning Breeze!” the guard shouted. “Take her back to your own land! Go now! Before the Great Mogul changes his mind!” “她的名字叫晨风！”守卫喊道。 “把她送回你自己的国家！快走！ 在大莫卧儿改变主意之前！

As if obeying the guard, Morning Breeze rose to her full height. She moved quickly across the square and headed down the stone road that led to the gate. 仿佛听从了守卫的命令，晨风站直了身子。 她迅速穿过广场，沿着通往大门的石路跑下去。

Jack and Annie bounced up and down. They clutched the sides of the straw carriage, trying not to fall out. Jack could hear the guards roaring with laughter behind them. “Slow down!” Jack shouted to the elephant. 杰克和安妮上下跳动。 他们紧紧抓住稻草马车的两边，努力不掉出去。 杰克听到身后的卫兵们大声笑着。 “慢点！”杰克对大象喊道。

But Morning Breeze ran even faster, her big leathery ears flapping and her bells jangling. 但晨风跑得更快了，她那大而粗糙的耳朵扇来扇去，铃铛叮当作响。

“She’s more like a wind than a breeze!” shouted Annie. “她更像是风，而不是微风！”安妮喊道。

“More like a tornado!” Jack yelled. “更像是龙卷风！”杰克大叫。

Morning Breeze thundered through the gate and over the wooden drawbridge. Jack and Annie held on to the sides of the carriage for dear life. After she’d crossed the moat, the elephant finally slowed to a walk. 晨风轰隆隆地穿过大门，冲过木制吊桥。 杰克和安妮紧紧抓住马车的两边，拼命不让自己掉下去。 她跨过护城河后，大象终于慢下来，开始走路了。

Morning Breeze held her trunk high. She seemed to be sniffing the hot wind. Her ears spread out as if she were listening for a distant sound. 晨风高高举起了她的象鼻。 她似乎在嗅着热风。 她的耳朵张开，好像在听远处的声音。

“We’re near the tree house! It’s right there in that row of trees!” said Jack. “We have the emerald rose. If we can just make her stop, we can jump off and go home. We can get out of this heat and away from this place—” “我们快到树屋了！ 就在那排树那里！”杰克说。 “我们有翡翠玫瑰。 只要能让它停下来，我们就能跳下去回家。 我们可以离开这炎热的地方，离开这里——”

“I know, I know,” said Annie. “But—” “我知道，我知道，”安妮说。 “但是—”

“Stop! Stop, Morning Breeze!” said Jack. “Let us off!” “住手！ “住手，晨风！”杰克说。 “让我们下去！”

“But what about her? ” said Annie. “We can’t just leave her here by herself!” “那她怎么办？”安妮说。 “我们不能就这样把她一个人丢在这里！”

Jack looked at Morning Breeze’s trunk waving in the air. He heard what sounded like crying coming from the wild elephant. She seemed terribly lost and sad. 杰克看着晨风的树干在空中摇晃。 他听到好像有声音在哭泣，是从那只野生大象那里传来的。 她看起来非常迷路，也非常伤心。

Suddenly Jack wanted desperately to help the elephant get back to the wild, wherever that was. “All right,” he said. “Keep going. Go fast!” His voice rose to a shout. “Hurry! Go, Morning Breeze! Go!” 突然，杰克非常想帮助这只大象回到野外，不管那是在哪里。 “好吧，”他说。 “继续往前走。 快点！他的声音提高成了喊声。 “快点！ 走吧，晨风！快点！”，
