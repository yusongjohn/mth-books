4. Put on Your Life Belts! 4. 穿上救生衣！

Jack and Annie slipped through a door off the boat deck. Teddy was at their heels. 杰克和安妮从船甲板的一扇门溜了进去。 泰迪紧跟在他们脚后。

“Wow,” breathed Annie. “哇，”安妮屏息说。

“Arf! Arf!” Teddy barked. “汪！ 汪！”泰迪叫道。

They were at the top of the empty grand stairway. It was beautiful. It was made of dark, glossy wood. A huge dome with lights hung above it. 他们站在空荡荡的大楼梯顶端。真漂亮。 它是用深色、光滑发亮的木头做成的。 上方悬着一个巨大的带灯穹顶。

At the top of the stairs was a fancy clock. 楼梯顶上有一座精致的钟。

The hands of the clock were at 12:20. 钟的指针指在12点20分。

“Oh, man, it’s twenty minutes after midnight!” said Jack. “The ship’s going down in two hours!” “天哪，已经午夜过二十分钟了！”杰克说。 “这艘船再过两小时就要沉了！”

They hurried down the carpeted steps and into the first-class hallway. Teddy followed along. 他们赶紧走下铺着地毯的台阶，进入一等舱走廊。 泰迪跟在后面。

Jack looked at the map in the book. 杰克看着书里的地图。

“These are the staterooms,” he said. “This hall will take us to the third-class open deck.” “这些是舱房，”他说。 “这条走廊会带我们到三等舱露天甲板。”

“Hey, look,” said Annie. “The floor’s slanting down.” “嘿，看，”安妮说。 “嘿，看，”安妮说。“地板在往下倾斜。”

Jack caught his breath. She was right. 杰克倒吸一口气。她说得对。

“That means the front of the ship is already sinking,” he said. “这说明船头已经在下沉了，”他说。

Just then, a man in a white uniform came down the hall. 就在这时，一个穿白制服的男人沿走廊走来。

He knocked on the doors. 他敲着门。

“Put on your life belts at once and come up to the boat deck!” he called. “立刻穿上救生衣，到船甲板上来！”他喊道。

Men and women stumbled out of their rooms. They wore elegant robes made of shiny cloth and velvet. 男男女女蹒跚着走出房间。 他们穿着闪亮布料和天鹅绒做成的高雅长袍。

“What’s going on?” a woman asked. “怎么回事？”一个女人问。

“There’s been a little accident,” the man in the uniform said cheerfully. “出了点小事故，”那个穿制服的人兴高采烈地说。

“Oh, how silly,” said the woman. “哦，真荒唐，”那女人说。

“It’s not silly!” said Annie. “Do what he says!” “这不是胡闹！”安妮说。 “照他说的做！”

“Arf! Arf!” Teddy barked, as if he agreed with Annie.

“Shhh, Teddy!” said Jack. “嘘，泰迪！”杰克说。

He picked up the little dog. Then he and Annie hurried down the hall. They crossed the third-class open deck, where more people were standing. 他抱起小狗。 然后他和安妮急忙沿走廊走去。 他们穿过三等舱露天甲板，那儿站着更多人。

These people were not dressed in fancy clothes. They mostly wore plain, dark coats. They didn’t seem worried either. They were all joking and laughing. 这些人没穿华丽的衣服。 他们多半穿着朴素的深色外套。 他们看起来也不担心。 他们都在开玩笑、大笑。

Jack and Annie slipped through the crowd. They went into a big smoky room. 杰克和安妮从人群中挤过去。 他们走进一间烟雾弥漫的大房间。

Four men were playing cards. 四个男人在打牌。

A woman played the piano. A young couple danced to the music. 一个女人在弹钢琴。 一对年轻夫妇随着音乐跳舞。

“Put on your life belts and go up to the boat deck!” Annie shouted. “穿上救生衣，到船甲板上去！”安妮喊道。

The people looked at Annie in surprise. The card players smiled at her. 人们惊讶地看着安妮。 打牌的人对她笑了笑。

She opened her mouth to yell again, but Jack pulled her out the door. 她又要张嘴喊，杰克却把她拉出了门。

“Come on,” he said. “We have to get down to the third-class cabins before it’s too late.” “走吧，”他说。 “我们必须在太晚之前下到三等舱客舱。”

They hurried down another hall. Then they climbed down another stairway. Jack carried Teddy the whole way. 他们又匆匆走过另一条走廊。 然后又爬下一道楼梯。 杰克一路上都抱着泰迪。

At the bottom of the stairs, they rounded a corner, and they both gasped. 到了楼梯底下，他们转过一个拐角，两人都倒抽了一口气。

The floor was really slanting down here, and water sloshed at the end of a hallway. 这里的地板真的在往下倾斜，走廊尽头有水在溅动。

“The Titanic is sinking,” said Jack. “泰坦尼克号在下沉，”杰克说。

“But no one understands!” said Annie. “可没人明白！”安妮说。

“I know,” said Jack. It made him feel terribly sad. “我知道，”杰克说。 这让他觉得非常难过。

Teddy whined. Jack buried his face in the little dog’s fur. 泰迪呜咽起来。 杰克把脸埋进小狗的毛里。

“Come on!” said Annie. “快点！”安妮说。

She began banging on the cabin doors. 她开始用力敲舱房的门。

The doors swung open. The cabins were all empty. 门晃开了。 客舱都是空的。

“The people from this hallway must be the ones up near the lounge,” said Jack. “Maybe we should go down to a lower deck.” “这条走廊里的人一定就是上面休息厅附近的那些人，”杰克说。 “也许我们该去更下面的一层甲板。”

He started to walk back to the stairs. But Teddy began barking furiously. 他开始往楼梯走回去。 可泰迪开始拼命地叫起来。

“What’s wrong with him?” said Jack. “它怎么了？”杰克说。

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “我不知道，”安妮说。

Suddenly, the dog leaped out of Jack’s arms. He charged down the hall. 突然，小狗从杰克怀里跳了出去。 它冲过走廊。

He was running straight toward the water! 它径直朝水跑去！

“Watch out!” cried Jack. “小心！”杰克喊道。

He and Annie ran after Teddy. 他和安妮追着泰迪跑。

The dog began barking at a closed door. 狗开始对着一扇关着的门叫。

The door opened. 门开了。

A very small boy peeked out. 一个很小的男孩探出头来。
