10. A Touch of Magic 10. 一点魔法

“Oh, man,” whispered Jack. “天哪，”杰克低声说。

He was lying on the floor of the tree house. He was wearing his pajamas and rain poncho again. 他躺在树屋地板上。 他又穿着睡衣和雨披了。

“You okay?” Annie asked. “你还好吗？”安妮问。

“Yeah. You?” said Jack. “嗯。你呢？”杰克说。

“My heart’s beating really fast,” said Annie. “我的心跳得很快，”安妮说。

“Mine, too,” said Jack. “我也是，”杰克说。

He thought of the Titanic sinking into the cold black sea. His eyes filled with tears. 他想起泰坦尼克号沉入冰冷漆黑的海里。 他眼里满是泪水。

“It was terrible,” he said. “太可怕了，”他说。

Annie nodded. Jack could see tears on her cheeks. 安妮点点头。 杰克看得见她脸颊上的泪水。

Teddy licked Jack’s face. 泰迪舔了舔杰克的脸。

“Hey,” said Jack. “How did you get out of my knapsack?” “嘿，”杰克说。 “你怎么从我背包里出来的？”

Teddy whined. Jack and Annie stroked his furry head and ears. 泰迪呜咽着。 杰克和安妮抚摸着他毛茸茸的脑袋和耳朵。

“I think he has a touch of magic,” said Annie. “我觉得他身上有一点儿魔法，”安妮说。

Slowly, Jack sat up. 慢慢地，杰克坐起来。

“He saved our lives,” he said. “它救了我们的命，”他说。

“And now we have the first gift to break the spell he’s under,” said Annie. “现在我们有了解开他咒语的第一样礼物，”安妮说。

She turned on her flashlight. She shined it on the silver pocket watch that hung from her neck. 她打开手电筒。 她把手电照在挂在脖子上的银色怀表上。

“Lucy’s gift,” she said. “露西的礼物，”她说。

The watch had stopped. The hands were at 2:20. 表停了。 指针停在2点20分。

Jack was silent. Then he sighed. 杰克沉默着。然后他叹了口气。

“That’s exactly when the ship went down,” he said. “那正好是船沉下去的时候，”他说。

Annie looked at him. 安妮看着他。

“I guess that’s when time stopped for the Titanic,” she said. “我想那就是泰坦尼克号时间停止的时候，”她说。

Jack nodded. 杰克点点头。

Annie put the pocket watch on top of Morgan’s note. 安妮把怀表放在摩根的便条上面。

“A gift from a ship lost at sea,” she said softly. “来自一艘沉没海船的礼物，”她轻声说。

They were both quiet. 他们都安静了。

Then Jack took off his glasses and wiped the tears from his eyes. 然后杰克摘下眼镜，擦去眼里的泪水。

Annie stood up. She took a deep breath. 安妮站起来。 她深吸一口气。

“I’m ready to go,” she said. “Let’s put Teddy in your pack and take him home with us.” “我准备好走了，”她说。 “我们把泰迪放进你的背包，带它回家吧。”

She shined her flashlight around the tree house. 她把手电在树屋里四处照。

“Teddy?” she said. “泰迪？”她说。

There was no sign of the little dog. 小狗已经不见踪影。

“He’s not here,” said Annie. “它不在这儿，”安妮说。

“What are you talking about?” said Jack. “We were just petting him.” “你在说什么？”杰克说。 “我们刚刚还在摸他呢。”

“He’s up to his magic tricks again,” said Annie. She sighed. “We’ll have to go home without him.” “他又在玩他的魔法把戏了，”安妮说。 “我们得不带它回家了。”

“But what happened to him?” said Jack. “可他怎么了？”杰克说。

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “But I have a feeling we’ll see him again soon.” “我不知道，”安妮说。 “可我有种感觉，我们很快会再见到它。”

She started down the rope ladder. Jack looked around the tree house one last time. 她开始顺着绳梯往下爬。 杰克最后一次环顾树屋。

“Teddy?” he said. “泰迪？”他说。

But the tree house was quiet. 可树屋静悄悄的。

Jack pulled on his backpack and climbed down the rope ladder. 杰克背上背包，爬下绳梯。

Annie was waiting for him. 安妮在等他。

Without a word, Jack took her hand. 杰克一言不发，握住了她的手。

The rain had stopped. But water still dripped from the trees. 雨停了。 可树上还在滴水。

Stars glittered overhead in a clear sky. 星星在晴朗的天空中闪烁。

Silently, Jack and Annie left the Frog Creek woods. They walked up the dark street to their house and climbed the steps to their porch. 杰克和安妮默默离开蛙溪树林。 他们沿黑暗的街道走向家，爬上台阶到门廊。

Before they went inside, they looked out again at the night. 进屋前，他们又朝夜空望去。

“Time might have stopped for the Titanic,” Jack said. “But books and memories keep the Titanic alive, don’t they? It’s a true story, but it’s also like a myth now.” “对泰坦尼克号来说，时间也许已经停止了，”杰克说。 “可书本和记忆让泰坦尼克号继续存在着，不是吗？ 这是一个真实的故事，但现在也像一个神话了。”

“Yeah,” said Annie. “And every time the story’s told, we wish it had a different ending.” “是啊，”安妮说。 “而且每次这个故事被讲起，我们都希望它有一个不同的结局。”

Jack nodded. That was exactly how he felt. 杰克点点头。 那正是他的感受。

Staring at the starry sky, he shuddered at the memory of the sinking ship. 他盯着星空，一想起那艘正在下沉的船就浑身发抖。

He knew that he and Annie were lucky. They had come home. 他知道他和安妮很幸运。 他们回家了。

“Good night, Titanic,” he said softly. “Good-bye.” “晚安，泰坦尼克号，”他轻声说。 “再见。”

Then he and Annie slipped quietly into their house, where it was cozy and dry and very safe. 然后他和安妮悄悄溜进家里，那里温暖、干燥，非常安全。
