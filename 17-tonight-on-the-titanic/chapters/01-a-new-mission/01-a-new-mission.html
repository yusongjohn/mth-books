1. A New Mission 1. 新任务

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开眼睛。

It was a stormy night. The rain tapped hard on his window pane. 那是个暴风雨的夜晚。 雨点重重地敲打着他的窗玻璃。

“Do you hear what the rain is saying?” said a voice. “你听见雨在说什么了吗？”一个声音说。

Jack turned on his lamp. 杰克打开了台灯。

Annie stood in his doorway. She wore her rain poncho over her pajamas and held a flashlight. 安妮站在他的门口。 她把雨披套在睡衣外面，手里拿着手电筒。

“It’s saying, Come now!” she said. “它在说：现在就来！”她说。

“You’re nuts,” said Jack. “你疯了，”杰克说。

“Listen, just listen,” said Annie. “听，你听就行，”安妮说。

Jack listened. 杰克听了听。

The rain did seem to be tapping out Come now! Come now! 雨点也确实像是在敲出“快来！快来！”的节奏。

“We have to go to the tree house,” said Annie. “Something important is about to happen.” “我们得去树屋，”安妮说。 “有件重要的事即将发生。”

“Now?” said Jack. “现在？”杰克说。

Jack didn’t want to leave his warm, cozy room. But he had a feeling Annie was right. Something important was about to happen. 杰克不想离开他温暖舒适的房间。 可他觉得安妮是对的。 有重要的事即将发生。

“You coming?” she said. “你来吗？”她说。

“Yeah, yeah,” he said. “好吧，好吧，”他说。

He climbed out of bed. 他爬下床。

“Just put on your rain poncho,” said Annie. “穿上你的雨披就行，”安妮说。

Jack threw his rain poncho over his pajamas. He pulled on his sneakers and grabbed his backpack. 杰克把雨披套在睡衣外面。 他穿上运动鞋，抓起背包。

“Don’t forget your Master Librarian card,” said Annie. “I have mine with me.” “别忘了你的首席图书管理员卡，”安妮说。 “我带着我的。”

Jack put the card with the glowing letters ML on it into his backpack. 杰克把那张印着发光字母ML的卡片放进背包。

“I’m ready,” he said. “我准备好了，”他说。

Jack and Annie went quietly down the stairs. Then they slipped out the door into the chilly, damp night. 杰克和安妮悄悄走下楼梯。 然后他们溜出门，走进寒冷潮湿的夜里。

The storm had eased up. Rain fell gently as they ran up the street. The wet ground glistened in the beam of the flashlight. 风暴已经减弱了。 他们沿街跑时，雨轻轻落下。 潮湿的地面在手电筒的光束里闪闪发亮。

They headed into the Frog Creek woods. Wind blew through the trees, shaking water to the ground. 他们走进蛙溪树林。 风吹过树丛，把水珠抖落到地上。

Jack shivered. He wiped raindrops off his glasses. 杰克打了个寒颤。 他擦掉眼镜上的雨滴。

“Brrr,” said Annie. “I’m cold.” “嘶，好冷，”安妮说。 “我好冷。”

“Me, too,” said Jack. “我也是，”杰克说。

As they kept walking, Annie pointed the flashlight up into the trees. 他们继续走时，安妮把手电朝树上照。

“There it is,” she said. Her light had found the tree house. “在那儿，”她说。 她的光照到了树屋。

“Morgan!” Jack called. “摩根！”杰克喊道。

There was no answer. 没有应答。

“I wonder what’s wrong,” said Annie. “I was sure she’d be here.” “不知道出了什么事，”安妮说。 “我还以为她一定在这儿。”

“Let’s go up and see,” said Jack. “我们上去看看，”杰克说。

Annie grabbed the rope ladder and started climbing. 安妮抓住绳梯开始往上爬。

Jack followed. Rain dripped from the trees onto his poncho. 杰克跟着她走到下面一层。 雨水从树上滴到他的雨披上。

They climbed into the tree house. Annie shined her light into each corner. The first three corners were empty. 他们爬进树屋。 安妮把手电照向每个角落。 前三个角落都是空的。

Then the flashlight beam hit the fourth. Jack and Annie gasped with surprise. 接着，手电筒的光束照到了第四个角落。 杰克和安妮惊讶得倒吸一口气。

Sitting in the corner was a small dog. He looked like a terrier puppy. He had scruffy light brown fur. 角落里坐着一只小狗。 他看起来像一只梗犬幼犬。 他有着一头乱蓬蓬的浅棕色毛。

He stared sadly at Jack and Annie. 它伤心地盯着杰克和安妮。

“Oh!” whispered Annie. “哦！”安妮低声说。

“Where did you come from?” said Jack. “你从哪儿来的？”杰克说。

Annie patted the dog’s head. He wagged his tail. 安妮拍了拍狗的头。 他摇了摇尾巴。

“He’s so cute,” she said. “He looks like a little teddy bear. Hi, Teddy.” “它好可爱，”她说。 “他看起来像一只小泰迪熊。嗨，泰迪。”

Teddy was actually a good name for him, Jack thought. 泰迪其实挺适合它的，杰克想。

“Where did you come from, Teddy?” he asked. “你从哪儿来的，泰迪？”他问。

The little dog whimpered. 小狗呜咽了一声。

“Don’t be sad,” said Annie. “Everything’s okay.”

“How did he get up here?” Jack asked. “它是怎么上来的？”杰克问。

“I don’t know. But I bet Morgan had something to do with it,” said Annie. “我不知道。 “但我敢肯定摩根和这件事有关，”安妮说。

Jack’s gaze fell on a piece of paper lying on the floor. 杰克的目光落在地板上的一张纸上。

“I think you’re right,” he said. “我觉得你说得对，”他说。

He picked up the paper. On it was fancy writing that said: 他捡起那张纸。 上面是花体字，写着：

This little dog is under a spell and needs your help. To free him, you must be given four special things: 这只小狗中了咒语，需要你们的帮助。 要解救它，你们必须得到四样特别的东西：

A gift from a ship lost at sea, 来自一艘沉没海船的礼物。

A gift from the prairie blue, 来自草原蓝天的礼物。

A gift from a forest far away, 来自远方森林的礼物。

A gift from a kangaroo. 来自一只袋鼠的礼物。

Be wise. Be brave. Be careful. 要智慧。要勇敢。 要小心。

Morgan

P.S. Your Master Librarian cards won’t help on this mission. Just be yourselves and all will be well. 附言： 你们的图书大师卡在这次任务中帮不上忙。 做你们自己，一切都会顺利。

“What kind of spell do you think Teddy is under?” said Annie. “你觉得泰迪中了什么咒语？”安妮说。

“Who knows?” said Jack. “谁知道呢？”杰克说。

“Poor thing,” said Annie. She patted the dog’s head. He licked her hand. “真可怜，”安妮说。 她拍了拍狗的头。 它舔了舔她的手。

“It looks like we have to take four trips,” said Jack. “看来我们得去四趟，”杰克说。

Teddy trotted over to a book. He pushed it with his nose. 泰迪小跑到一本书旁边。 它用鼻子推了推那本书。

“Look!” said Annie. “I bet that will take us on the first trip.” “看！”安妮说。 “我敢打赌那会带我们去第一趟。”

She picked up the book that the little dog had chosen. 她捡起小狗选中的那本书。

“Good job, Teddy,” she said. “干得好，泰迪，”她说。

The title of the book was The Unsinkable Ship. 书名是《不沉之船》。

“Well, at least that’s good,” said Jack. “The ship won’t sink, even if it is lost.” “嗯，至少这还不错，”杰克说。 “这艘船不会沉，就算它失事了也不会。”

“Ready, Teddy?” asked Annie. “准备好了吗，泰迪？”安妮问。

“Arf! Arf!” he answered. “汪！ 汪！”它回答。

Jack pointed at the cover of the book. 杰克指着书的封面。

“I wish we could go there,” he said. “我希望我们能去那里，”他说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 绝对静止。
