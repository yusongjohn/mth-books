5. William and Lucy 5. 威廉和露西

The boy wore a nightshirt. He had red hair and freckles. He looked about four years old. 男孩穿着长款睡衣。 他有红头发和雀斑。 他看起来大约四岁。

He rubbed his eyes sleepily. Then he saw Teddy. 他睡眼惺忪地揉了揉眼睛。 然后他看见了泰迪。

“Puppy!” he said with a huge smile. “小狗！”他露出大大的笑容说。

He threw his arms around the little dog’s neck. Teddy licked his face. 他伸开双臂搂住小狗的脖子。 他伸开双臂搂住小狗的脖子。泰迪舔了舔他的脸。

“Come back to bed, William,” a voice called from inside the room. “回床上去，威廉，”房间里一个声音喊道。

“Come out!” Annie shouted. “It’s an emergency!” “出来！”安妮喊道。 “出来！”安妮喊道。“出事了！”

A moment later, the door opened wider. A girl in a long white nightgown peeked out. 片刻后，门开得更大了。 一个穿白色长睡袍的女孩探出头来。

She had red hair and freckles, too. She was tall and thin. She looked to be about twelve or thirteen years old. 他有红头发和雀斑。 她又高又瘦。 她看上去大约十二三岁。

“Hello,” she said. She put her arm around the little boy. “I’m Lucy O’Malley. This is my brother, William.” “你好，”她说。 她搂住小男孩。 “我是露西·奥马利。 这是我弟弟威廉。”

“I’m Annie,” said Annie. “And this is my brother, Jack.” “我是安妮，”安妮说。 “这是我哥哥杰克。”

“Get your parents and tell them to come with us,” said Jack. “去叫你们的父母，让他们跟我们走，”杰克说。

Lucy looked confused. 露西看上去很困惑。

“Our parents aren’t here. They’re in New York,” she said. “We’re on our way to them.” “我们父母不在这儿。 他们在纽约，”她说。 “我们正在去他们那儿的路上。”

“Listen, the Titanic has hit an iceberg,” said Annie. “We’ll take you to a lifeboat.” “听着，泰坦尼克号撞上冰山了，”安妮说。 “我们带你们去救生艇。”

“What do you mean?” said Lucy. “你什么意思？”露西说。

“The ship’s sinking,” said Annie. “Look.” “船在下沉，”安妮说。 “看。”

She pointed to the water at the end of the hall. 她指着走廊尽头的水。

“Oh, no!” cried Lucy. “哦，不！”露西喊道。

“Don’t be afraid,” said Jack. “Just get your coats and your life belts. We don’t have much time.” “别怕，”杰克说。 “快拿上外套和救生衣。 我们没多少时间了。”

Lucy nodded. Then she went back inside the room and came out with their things. 露西点点头。 然后她回房间拿了东西出来。

Lucy pulled on her coat and her life belt. Annie helped William put his on. 露西穿上外套和救生衣。 安妮帮威廉穿上。

“Let’s go,” said Jack. “走吧，”杰克说。

“Wait—can Teddy fit in your knapsack?” said Annie. “等等——泰迪能塞进你的背包吗？”安妮说。

“Try,” said Jack. “试试，”杰克说。

Annie slipped the little dog into the leather knapsack on Jack’s back. Only Teddy’s front paws and head stuck out. 安妮把小狗塞进杰克背上的皮背包。 只有泰迪的前爪和脑袋露在外面。

“Stay there, honey,” said Annie. She kissed Teddy on the nose. “待在那儿，宝贝，”安妮说。 她亲了亲泰迪的鼻子。

Jack didn’t feel any extra weight in his knapsack. The little dog felt as light as air. 杰克没有觉得背包里多了什么分量。 这只小狗轻得像空气一样。

“Wait—I forgot something,” said Lucy. “等等——我忘了东西，”露西说。

“We don’t have time—” started Jack. “我们没时间——”杰克开口说。

But Lucy rushed back into the room. 可露西冲回了房间。

“Hurry!” shouted Annie. “快点！”安妮喊道。

When Lucy came out, Jack saw her slip something into her coat pocket. Then she grabbed William’s hand. 露西出来时，杰克看见她把什么东西塞进大衣口袋。 然后她抓住威廉的手。

“Ready?” said Jack. “好了吗？”杰克说。

Suddenly, he felt freezing water brush against his shoes. 突然，他感到冰冷的水擦过他的鞋子。

He looked down. The green sea water was slowly moving down the hall. 他低头看。 绿色的海水正慢慢沿走廊漫过来。

“Arf! Arf!” barked Teddy from Jack’s pack. “汪！ 汪！”泰迪从杰克背包里叫道。

“Run!” cried Annie. “跑！”安妮喊道。
