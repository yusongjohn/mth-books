7. The Gift 7. 礼物

The lifeboat was waiting to go down. It looked tiny as it swung on cables at the side of the big ship. The water below looked black. 救生艇正等着下放。 它用缆绳吊在大船一侧，看上去小小的。 下面的海水看上去黑漆漆的。

“Get in! Get in!” a uniformed man shouted. “上去！ 上去！”一个穿制服的男人喊道。

“No, no,” said William. He hid his face against Lucy’s coat. “不，不，”威廉说。 他把脸埋进露西的外套里。

Lucy was shaking her head. 露西摇着头。

“I’d rather stay here,” she said to Jack and Annie. “我宁愿留在这儿，”她对杰克和安妮说。

Jack understood. The brightly lit Titanic seemed so solid and safe compared to the little lifeboat. 杰克明白了。 灯火通明的泰坦尼克号看上去那么结实安全，相比之下那艘小救生艇就不一样了。

“You can’ t stay here,” said Annie. “The Titanic is going to sink soon.” “你们不能待在这儿，”安妮说。 “泰坦尼克号很快就要沉了。”

“Very soon,” said Jack. “很快，”杰克说。

Lucy kept shaking her head. Jack saw tears in her eyes. 露西继续摇头。 杰克看见她眼里含着泪。

“Lucy, we’re telling the truth,” said Jack. “You and William are in great danger.” “露西，我们说的是真的，”杰克说。 “你和威廉正处于极大的危险中。”

“You have to be brave now,” said Annie. “For your brother’s sake.” “你现在必须勇敢，”安妮说。 “为了你弟弟。”

Lucy straightened up and tried to smile. 露西直起身，努力挤出笑容。

“All right,” she said. “I will.” “好吧，”她说。 “我会的。”

“This way!” a man called. “Women and children! This way, you four!” “这边！”一个男人喊道。 “女人和孩子！ 这边，你们四个！”

He pointed at them. 他指着他们。

“Get in,” said Jack. He gently pushed Lucy and William forward. “上去，”杰克说。 他轻轻把露西和威廉往前推。

“Good-bye, Lucy,” said Annie. “Good-bye, William.” “再见，露西，”安妮说。 “再见，威廉。”

Lucy looked surprised. 露西看上去很惊讶。

“You’re not coming?” she asked. “你们不来？”她问。

“No, we’re going home another way,” said Annie. “不，我们另有回家的路，”安妮说。

“Oh, dear,” said Lucy. “I hope you’ll be safe.” “哦，天哪，”露西说。 “希望你们平安。”

“We will. Don’t worry!” said Jack.

“Wait,” said Lucy. “等等，”露西说。

She reached into her coat pocket and pulled out a silver watch on a chain. 她把手伸进外套口袋，掏出一块带链子的银表。

“This is a gift for both of you,” she said. “It’s our father’s watch. We carried it on the voyage for good luck. I have a feeling that the two of you were our good luck tonight.” “这是给你们俩的礼物，”她说。 “这是我们父亲的表。 我们带着它航海，图个好运。 我有种感觉，今晚你们俩是我们的好运。”

Jack looked at the watch as Lucy put it around Annie’s neck. 露西把表戴到安妮脖子上时，杰克看着那块表。

The time on the watch was 1:50. 表上的时间是1点50分。

There were only thirty minutes left! 只剩下三十分钟了！

“Hurry, hurry! ” he said. “快点，快点！”他说。

Jack and Annie watched as a big man picked Lucy up and swung her into the little boat. Then he picked William up and put him in Lucy’s lap. 杰克和安妮看着一个大块头男人把露西抱起来，把她荡进小船。 然后他把威廉抱起来，放在露西腿上。

“Bye!” cried Annie. She stepped forward to blow a kiss. “再见！”安妮喊道。 她走上前去飞了一个吻。

Just then, the man picked Annie up. 就在这时，那人抱起了安妮。

“No!” shouted Annie. “不！”安妮喊道。

“Into the lifeboat, my dear!” the man said. And he tossed Annie into the lifeboat. “进救生艇，亲爱的！”那人说。 接着他把安妮扔进了救生艇。

“No! No!” cried Jack. “不！不！”杰克喊道。

Then the man reached for him, too. Jack jumped away just in time. 然后那人也朝他伸手。 杰克刚好及时跳开。

“Annie!” he yelled. “Get out!” “安妮！”他喊道。 “出来！”

Annie tried to climb out of the lifeboat. 安妮想爬出救生艇。

“Let me out!” she cried. “放我出去！”她喊道。

“Arf! Arf!” Teddy barked from over Jack’s shoulder. “汪！ 汪！”泰迪从杰克肩头叫道。

The lifeboat jerked. It started creaking down toward the dark, cold sea. 救生艇猛地一晃。 它开始嘎吱作响地朝黑暗冰冷的海水降下去。

“Come back!” Jack called. “回来！”杰克喊道。

But there was nothing he could do as Annie disappeared from sight. 可安妮从视线中消失时，他什么也做不了。
