6. Women and Children First 6. 妇孺优先

Annie led everyone down the hall to the stairs, away from the cold sea water. 安妮带着大家沿走廊走向楼梯，远离冰冷的海水。

As she and Lucy helped William up the stairs, Jack and Teddy followed. 她和露西扶威廉上楼梯时，杰克和泰迪跟在后面。

Halfway up the staircase, Teddy let out a yelp. 走到楼梯一半时，泰迪突然叫了一声。

Jack looked back. 杰克回头看。

The water was creeping up the stairs, step by step. 水正一步一步地顺着楼梯往上漫。

“Come on, Jack!” Annie shouted. “快点，杰克！”安妮喊道。

Jack ran up the rest of the stairs. 杰克跑完剩下的楼梯。

He and Annie led William and Lucy through the smoky room. The men were still playing cards. 他和安妮领着威廉和露西穿过烟雾弥漫的房间。 那些男人还在打牌。

“To the lifeboats!” Annie yelled at the card players. “Right now! Hurry!” “去救生艇！”安妮对打牌的人喊道。 “马上！快点！”

The men smiled at her again. 那些男人又对她笑了笑。

“Little girl,” one said with a laugh, “even if this ship does sink, it will take all night. There’s plenty of time for everyone on board to be rescued.” “小姑娘，”一个人笑着说，“就算这艘船真的沉了，也得一整夜。 “船上每个人都有足够的时间获救。”

“Indeed. Many ships are on their way right now,” another man said in a soothing voice. “There’s nothing to worry about.” “的确。 许多船现在正在赶来，”另一个人用抚慰的口气说。 “没什么好担心的。”

“Not true!” said Annie. “不是真的吗！”安妮说。

Lucy turned to Jack. 露西转向杰克。

“This doesn’t sound so bad, after all,” she said. “听起来终究没那么糟，”她说。

“It is bad. Please trust me,” said Jack. “We have to keep going.” “情况很糟。 请相信我，”杰克说。 “我们必须继续走。”

They went outside. The crowd on the third-class deck had grown. 他们走到外面。 三等舱甲板上的人群变多了。

Many wore life belts. But no one here seemed very worried yet. 许多人穿着救生衣。 可这里还没有人显得很担心。

Jack and Annie pulled Lucy and William along. They hurried through the crowd and down the first-class hall. They came to the end and trooped up the grand staircase. 杰克和安妮拉着露西和威廉一起往前走。 他们匆匆穿过人群，走下一等舱走廊。 他们走到尽头，成群结队地登上大楼梯。

On the top deck, the Titanic was as bright as a Christmas tree. 在顶层甲板上，泰坦尼克号亮得像一棵圣诞树。

A band played lively music. 一支乐队演奏着欢快的音乐。

With a hiss and rush of light, a rocket streaked into the sky. It made a loud boom! Then it burst into many colored balls. 伴随着嘶嘶声和一道闪光，一枚火箭划过天空。 它发出一声巨响！ 然后爆开，变成许多彩色的光球。

Shivering in the cold, William laughed and clapped. 威廉在寒冷中发抖，却笑着鼓起掌来。

“Fireworks!” he said. “烟花！”他说。

Lucy smiled at Jack and Annie. 露西对杰克和安妮笑了笑。

“This is a trick, isn’t it?” she said. “You’ve brought us to a party.” “这是恶作剧，对吧？”她说。 “你们带我们来参加派对了。”

“No, it isn’t,” said Jack. “Don’t you remember the water downstairs?” “不，不是，”杰克说。 “你不记得楼下的水了吗？”

Lucy’s smile faded. 露西的笑容渐渐消失了。

“Women and children first!” someone shouted. “女人和孩子优先！”有人喊道。

“That’s you!” said Annie. “Come on!” “那就是你们！”安妮说。 “来！”

She pulled Lucy and William toward a lifeboat. 她拉着露西和威廉朝一艘救生艇走去。
