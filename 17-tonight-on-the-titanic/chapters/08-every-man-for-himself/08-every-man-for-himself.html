8. Every Man for Himself 8. 各自逃生

“Annie!” cried Jack. “安妮！”杰克喊道。

“Let me out!” he heard Annie shout. “放我出去！”他听见安妮喊道。

But the lifeboat kept going down. 可救生艇继续往下放。

“Wait for me!” came a loud voice. “Wait for me!” “等等我！”一个响亮的声音传来。 “等等我！”

A woman in a fur coat appeared at the railing. She nearly threw herself over the side of the ship. 一个穿着毛皮大衣的女人出现在栏杆边。 她差点把自己从船舷边扔下去。

“Stop!” the uniformed man called. “Bring the boat back up for Lady Blackwell!” “停下！”那个穿制服的人喊道。 “把船升回来，让布莱克威尔夫人上来！”

Slowly, the lifeboat was brought back up. 慢慢地，救生艇被升了回来。

Jack pushed his way forward. The lifeboat came even with the ship. Jack reached out to Annie. 杰克挤上前去。 救生艇升到了和轮船齐平的位置。 杰克向安妮伸出手。

She grabbed his hands. He pulled her back onto the sinking ship. 她抓住他的手。 他把她拉回正在下沉的船上。

“Room for one more!” Annie shouted to Lady Blackwell. “还能再上一个人！”安妮对布莱克威尔夫人喊道。

Then she and Jack took off before anyone could catch them. 然后她和杰克在被人抓住前跑开了。

They ran up the slanting deck. Annie stopped and peered over the railing. 他们沿着倾斜的甲板往上跑。 安妮停下来，从栏杆上往外看。

Jack looked, too. 杰克也看了。

They saw Lucy and William’s little lifeboat creaking down toward the Atlantic Ocean. It reached the glassy black water. Then it floated off into the darkness. 他们看见露西和威廉的小救生艇正嘎吱作响地降向大西洋。 它落到了光亮漆黑的水面上。 然后它漂进了黑暗里。

Annie waved. 安妮挥了挥手。

“Bye, William! Bye, Lucy!” she shouted. “Thank you for your gift!” “再见，威廉！ 再见，露西！”她喊道。 “谢谢你们的礼物！”

She held up the watch that hung from her neck. Then she and Jack looked at it. 她举起挂在脖子上的表。 然后她和杰克看着它。

The time was 2:05. 时间是2点05分。

“Only fifteen minutes left!” said Annie. “只剩十五分钟了！”安妮说。

“We have to get back to the tree house now! ” said Jack. “Let’s climb the stairs to the smokestacks!” “我们现在必须回树屋！”杰克说。 “我们爬楼梯去烟囱那儿！”

Suddenly, the front of the ship dipped down into the sea. Deck chairs started to slide past Jack and Annie. 突然，船头向海里沉下去。 甲板椅开始从杰克和安妮身边滑过。

The band played a slow, calm song. It sounded like a church hymn. 乐队演奏着一首缓慢平静的曲子。 听起来像教堂里的赞美诗。

But the crowd started to panic. People pushed and shouted, trying to get to a safer part of the ship. 可人群开始惊慌起来。 人们推挤喊叫，想赶到船上更安全的地方。

“It’s every man for himself!” the captain shouted to all of his crew. “各求生路！”船长向全体船员喊道。

The men all stopped what they were doing and ran up the deck. 那些男人都停下手里的事，往甲板上方跑。

Jack and Annie ran, too. 杰克和安妮也跑。

They dodged sliding tables and chairs. 他们躲开滑动的桌子和椅子。

They reached the stairs that led to the smokestacks. 他们到达通向烟囱的楼梯。

They grabbed the railing and pulled themselves up the steps. 他们抓住栏杆，把自己拉上台阶。

The ship tilted further. 船倾斜得更厉害了。

“Get to those smokestacks!” cried Jack. “赶到那些烟囱那儿！”杰克喊道。

They slipped and crawled down the deck. 他们滑倒了，沿着甲板爬下去。

But when they got to the smokestacks, Jack and Annie looked around wildly. 可等他们到了大烟囱那儿，杰克和安妮狂乱地四处张望。

The magic tree house was gone! 魔法树屋不见了！
