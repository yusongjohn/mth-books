9. Time Stops 9. 时间停止

“Where is it?” shouted Annie. “它在哪儿？”安妮喊道。

The front of the Titanic sank deeper into the sea. Jack and Annie fell forward. 泰坦尼克号的船头更深地沉入海中。 杰克和安妮向前栽去。

They grabbed the railing and held on for their lives. 他们抓住栏杆，拼命抓着不放。

“Maybe the tree house fell into the ocean!” shouted Jack. “也许树屋掉进海里了！”杰克喊道。

A great roar started coming from the ship. 船上开始传来巨大的轰鸣。

Jack imagined everything crashing forward—all the furniture, dishes, bicycles, the grand clock on the staircase. 杰克想象着一切都在向前猛撞——所有家具、碗碟、自行车，还有楼梯上那座大钟。

He looked down. A giant wave of water rolled over a lower deck. 他低头看。 一大片水浪淹没了下层甲板。

Jack imagined water flooding the third-class corridor, the card room, and the grand staircase. 他想象着水淹没三等舱走廊、打牌室和大楼梯。

He closed his eyes, waiting to be washed away. 他闭上眼睛，等着被冲走。

“Arf! Arf!”

The barking came from a distance. 吠叫声从远处传来。

“Teddy!” cried Annie. “泰迪！”安妮喊道。

Jack had forgotten all about the little dog. 杰克完全忘了那只小狗。

Holding on to the railing, he used one hand to pull off his knapsack. 他抓住栏杆，用一只手拉开小背包。

Teddy wasn’t there! 泰迪不在！

There was more frantic barking. 又传来更发狂的吠叫声。

“Where’s Teddy?” cried Jack. “泰迪在哪儿？”杰克喊道。

“He’s calling us!” shouted Annie. “它在叫我们！”安妮喊道。

“We can’t look for him!” shouted Jack. “We’ll fall off the ship!” “我们不能去找它！”杰克喊道。 “我们会掉下船的！”

Teddy barked and barked. 泰迪叫了又叫。

“He’s close by!” said Annie. “他就在附近！”安妮说。

She held on to the railing and moved slowly down the steep deck. 她抓住栏杆，沿着陡峭的甲板慢慢移动。

“Annie!” cried Jack. “安妮！”杰克喊道。

Suddenly, the lights on the Titanic went out. The world was pitch-dark. 突然，泰坦尼克号上的灯灭了。 世界变得一片漆黑。

Jack couldn’t see Annie at all. 杰克根本看不见安妮。

“Annie!” he shouted. “安妮！”他喊道。

He tried to move down the deck, too. 他也想往甲板下方移动。

But the ship tilted again. Jack slipped and fell. 可船又倾斜了。 杰克滑倒摔了下去。

He rolled until he crashed into a smokestack. 他一直滚，直到撞上一根大烟囱。

“Jack!” cried Annie. “Here! Here!” “杰克！”安妮喊道。 “这儿！这儿！”

Teddy kept barking. 泰迪不停地叫。

The back of the Titanic was rising out of the ocean. The front of the ship was going down. 泰坦尼克号的船尾正从海里升起。 船头正在下沉。

Jack tried to go around the smokestack without falling. 杰克试着绕过烟囱而不摔倒。

In the dark, he could barely see the tree house. It was stuck between a smokestack and the railing. It was lying on its side. 黑暗中，他几乎看不见树屋。 它卡在一根大烟囱和栏杆之间。 它侧躺着。

Annie and Teddy were looking out the window. 安妮和泰迪正从窗口往外看。

“Teddy’s barking led me here!” cried Annie. “Hurry, Jack!” “泰迪的叫声把我引到这儿的！”安妮喊道。 “快点，杰克！”

Jack crawled around the smokestack. 杰克爬着绕过烟囱。

He held out his hand. Annie grabbed it. She pulled him into the tree house. 他伸出手。安妮抓住了。 她把他拉进树屋。

Teddy licked his face. 他伸开双臂搂住小狗的脖子。泰迪舔了舔他的脸。

“I wish we could go home!” shouted Annie, pointing at the Pennsylvania book. “我希望我们能回家！”安妮指着宾夕法尼亚的书喊道。

Jack heard a loud CRA-A-A-ACK! 杰克听见一声巨大的咔——咔——咔！

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 绝对静止。
