5. Angel of the Battlefield 5. 战场天使

Jack still didn’t remember who Clara Barton was. He pulled out the Civil War book and read: 杰克还是不记得克拉拉·巴顿是谁。 他抽出内战书读道：

Clara Barton was a famous Civil War nurse. When she began nursing, she used her own money for her supplies. She drove a horse-drawn “ambulance” right onto the battlefield to help save wounded soldiers. For this reason she became known as the “Angel of the Battlefield.” 克拉拉·巴顿是一位著名的内战护士。她开始护理工作时，用自己的钱购买物资。她驾驶着一辆马车“救护车”，直接开到战场上去救助受伤的士兵。因此，她被称为“战场上的天使。”

Jack put the book away. Then he hurried to Annie. 杰克把书收好。 然后他赶紧走向安妮。

He looked at the woman sitting in the driver’s seat of the wagon. 他看着坐在马车驾驶座上的女人。

She doesn’t look like an angel, Jack thought. 她看起来不像天使，杰克想。

The woman was very small. She had a plain, serious face and dark hair pulled back in a bun. She wore a long black skirt and a black jacket. 那女人个子很小。 她有一张朴素而严肃的脸，黑发向后挽成一个发髻。 她穿着黑色长裙和黑色外套。

In the back of her wagon were more wounded soldiers in torn, bloody uniforms. They moaned and cried out. 马车后面还有更多穿着破烂血衣的伤兵。 他们呻吟着，哭喊着。

Nurses, both men and women, were putting the wounded men on stretchers. 男女护士们正把伤兵抬上担架。

Clara Barton wiped her forehead. She looked hot and tired. 克拉拉·巴顿擦了擦额头。 她看起来又热又累。

“Can we help you, Miss Barton?” Annie asked. “我们能帮您吗，巴顿小姐？”安妮问。

“Who are you?” said Clara Barton. “你们是谁？”克拉拉·巴顿说。

“Jack and Annie,” said Annie. “We’re volunteer nurses. What can we do, Miss Barton?” “杰克和安妮，”安妮说。 “我们是志愿护士。 我们能做什么，巴顿小姐？”

Clara Barton smiled. 克拉拉·巴顿笑了。

“First, you can call me Clara,” she said. “Second, would you ride with me back toward the battlefield? There are more wounded waiting to be picked up.” “第一，你们可以叫我克拉拉。”她说。 “第二，你们愿意和我一起坐车回战场方向吗？ 还有更多伤员等着被接走。”

“Sure!” said Annie. “当然！”安妮说。

Jack didn’t answer. After seeing all the suffering men in the wagon, he was afraid of getting closer to the battlefield. 杰克没有回答。 看到马车上那些受苦的人之后，他害怕离战场更近。

“And you?” Clara asked Jack. Her dark, serious eyes looked right into his. “你呢？”克拉拉问杰克。 她深色、认真的眼睛直视着他。

Jack didn’t want to admit he was afraid. “Sure, no problem,” he said. 杰克不想承认自己害怕。 “当然，没问题，”他说。

“Very good,” said Clara Barton. “Let’s go.” “很好，”克拉拉·巴顿说。 “走吧。”

Jack and Annie climbed up into the driver’s seat next to her. 杰克和安妮爬上驾驶座，坐在她旁边。

By now, all the soldiers had been taken out of the wagon. 到现在为止，所有士兵都已经被抬下了马车。

“Take care of my new family members,” Clara called to the nurses. “照顾好我的新家人，”克拉拉对护士们喊道。

She snapped the reins. The horse-drawn ambulance rolled off, sending up clouds of dust. 她猛地一拉缰绳。 马拉救护车开走了，扬起一团团尘土。
