6. Keep Low! 6. 伏低！

The wagon jerked and swayed as it bumped over the dry ground. 马车在干燥的地面上颠簸着，颠得东摇西晃。

Jack felt as though he were frying in the hot sunlight. Dust from the road filled his throat and eyes. 杰克觉得自己仿佛在炎热的阳光里被煎烤。 路上的尘土灌进他的喉咙和眼睛。

The boom of cannons grew louder and louder. Jack heard popping sounds, too, like the noise of firecrackers. 大炮的轰鸣声越来越响。 杰克也听见了噼啪声，像鞭炮的响声。

“What’s that popping noise?” he shouted, blinded by the dust and sunlight. “那噼啪声是什么？”他喊道，被尘土和阳光弄得睁不开眼。

“Musket fire!” said Clara. “火枪声！”克拉拉说。

Jack remembered that muskets were long, old-fashioned guns. 杰克记得，火枪是又长又老式的枪。

“What are those flashes?” asked Annie. “那些闪光是什么？”安妮问。

Jack tried to open his eyes and see what she was talking about. 杰克试着睁开眼睛，看她说的是什么。

He saw bright flashes of light in the distance and puffs of smoke in the sky. 他看见远处有明亮的闪光，天空中还有一团团烟雾。

“Cannon shells exploding,” said Clara Barton. “Shells are like small bombs. They have ruined much farmland.” “炮弹在爆炸。”克拉拉·巴顿说。 “炮弹就像小炸弹。 它们毁掉了许多农田。”

Jack squinted at the passing countryside. The ground was filled with ugly holes. Long ditches were also cut through the fields. 杰克眯起眼睛看向路过的乡野。 地面上满是难看的坑洞。 田野里还挖着长长的沟渠。

“Did the shells make those ditches, too?” he asked. “那些壕沟也是炮弹炸的吗？”他问。

“No. Those are trenches the soldiers dug for a battle,” Clara said. “Each side digs their own. Day after day, they sit in the trenches, firing their muskets at one another.” “不是，那是魔法！一团旋转的光！”安妮说。 “那是士兵们为打仗挖的战壕。”克拉拉说。 “双方各自挖自己的。 日复一日，他们坐在战壕里，用火枪彼此射击。”

Jack tried to imagine how terrible it would be to sit in a trench all day, waiting to be shot—or waiting to shoot someone else. 杰克试着想象，整天坐在战壕里等着被打中——或者等着去打别人，那会有多么可怕。

“We have to get some water,” said Clara. “我们得弄点水，”克拉拉说。

She drove the wagon to a narrow creek. A stream of water flowed downhill, running over rocks. 她把马车赶到一条窄溪旁。 一股水流从山上冲下来，流过岩石。

The wagon came to a halt. Jack heard a whistling sound, then another. 马车停住了。 杰克听见一声呼啸，接着又是一声。

“Keep low!” cried Clara. “伏低！”克拉拉喊道。

“What’s that?” Jack asked. “那是什么？”杰克问。

“Cannon fire!” she said. “炮火！”她说。

Jack and Annie crouched down in the driver’s seat. 杰克和安妮在赶车的座位上蹲了下来。

Jack felt a rush of panic. He pulled out their list. His hands were shaking as he read: 杰克感到一阵恐慌涌上来。 他抽出他们的清单。 他读的时候手在发抖：

Be brave. 要勇敢。

Oh, great, he thought. 哦，太好了，他想。

Another cannon shell whizzed overhead, then another. 又一发炮弹呼啸着从头顶掠过，接着又是一发。

The ground exploded over and over in flashes of light. Dirt clods flew everywhere. Clouds of smoke and more dust filled the air. 地面一次又一次地爆炸，闪着光。 土块飞得到处都是。 空气里充满烟云和更多尘土。

The horses neighed and whinnied. 马匹嘶叫着，发出嘶鸣。

Be brave! Jack thought. Be brave! 要勇敢！杰克想。 要勇敢！
