10. Home, Sweet Home 10. 甜蜜的家

There was a booming in the distance. 远处传来隆隆声。

Jack opened his eyes and caught his breath. Is that cannon fire? he wondered. Are we still back in the Civil War? 杰克睁开眼睛，倒抽了一口气。 那是炮火吗？他想。 我们还在内战里吗？

“We’re home,” said Annie. “Home, sweet home.” “我们到家了。”安妮说。 “甜蜜的家。”

“Oh, man,” Jack whispered. “哦，天哪，”杰克小声说。

They were home. They were back in the Frog Creek woods. They were wearing their own comfortable clothes again. 他们到家了。 他们回到了蛙溪的树林。 他们又穿上了自己舒服的衣服。

The cannon fire was really only thunder. At that moment, Jack loved the thunder. 那炮火其实只是雷声。 就在那一刻，杰克爱上了这雷声。

Raindrops tapped against the tree house. 雨点轻轻敲打着树屋。

“We better hurry,” said Jack. “我们得快点，”杰克说。

“Wait. Leave the list in the tree house,” said Annie. “It’s the first special writing for Morgan’s library. Something to follow.” “等等。 把清单留在树屋里，”安妮说。 “这是给摩根图书馆的第一份特别文字。 可以遵循的东西

Jack took the list of Clara Barton’s rules out of his pack. He put it on the floor next to Morgan’s letter. 杰克从背包里取出克拉拉·巴顿的规则清单。 他把它放在地板上，摩根的信旁边。

“I wonder how that list will help save Camelot?” he said. “我不知道这份清单怎么帮得上拯救卡米洛特？”他说。

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “But you know what’s weird about getting that list? I think we couldn’t just have taken it home when we first got it. We had to live it first.” “我不知道。”安妮说。 “可你知道拿到那张清单有什么奇怪的吗？ 我想我们不能刚拿到就带回家。 我们得先亲身经历它。”

Jack nodded. Annie was completely right, he thought. He picked up his backpack. 杰克点了点头。 安妮完全是对的，他想。 他拿起了背包。

“Wait! I see another note!” said Annie. “等等——我们本该离开的。”杰克有气无力地说。 我又看见一张纸条！”安妮说。

She picked a piece of paper off the tree house floor. On it was written: Come back on Wednesday. 她从树屋地板上捡起一张纸。 上面写着：星期三再来。

“I guess that’s when Morgan wants us to look for the next special writing,” said Annie. “我猜摩根想让我们那天去找下一份特别文字，”安妮说。

“That’s in three days,” said Jack. “Let’s go home and rest.” “那是三天后。”杰克说。 “我们回家休息吧。”

He started down the rope ladder. Annie followed. 他开始顺着绳梯往下爬。安妮跟着。

When they stepped onto the ground, the rain began to pour down. 他们一踩到地上，雨就开始倾盆而下。

“Run!” said Jack. “快跑！”杰克说。

They ran through the Frog Creek woods, then down their street. They ran to their porch and dashed into their dry, cozy house. 他们跑过蛙溪的树林，然后跑上自家的街道。 他们跑到门廊，冲进干燥、温暖舒适的家里。

They found their parents reading in the living room. 他们发现父母正在客厅里看书。

“Dad! Mom!” Annie cried. “We’re so glad to see you!” “见到你们真高兴！”

“Well, we’re—we’re glad to see you, too,” their dad said, sounding a little puzzled. “嗯，我们——我们也很高兴见到你们。”他们的爸爸说，听上去有点困惑。

“Go put on dry clothes,” said their mom. “去换上干衣服，”妈妈说。

Jack and Annie started up to their rooms. Halfway up the stairs, Jack stopped. 杰克和安妮往楼上自己的房间走。 走到楼梯半途，杰克停了下来。

“Oh, I have a question,” he called to his parents. “Did anyone in our family fight in the Civil War?” “哦，我有个问题，”他对爸妈喊道。 “我们家有人打过内战吗？”

Their dad looked surprised. “Yes,” he said. “One of your great-great-great-grandfathers was a drummer boy.” 爸爸看上去很吃惊。 “有，”他说。 “你们的一位高曾祖父曾是鼓手男孩。”

“Oh, man,” whispered Jack. “哦，天哪，”杰克轻声说。

“What was his name, Dad?” asked Annie. “他叫什么名字，爸爸？”安妮问。

“John,” their dad said. “约翰，”爸爸说。

Jack and Annie gasped. 杰克和安妮倒吸了一口气。

“And—and what happened to John?” Jack asked. “Was he hurt in the war?” “那——那约翰后来怎么样了？”杰克问。 “他在战争中受伤了吗？”

“No, he grew up to be a schoolteacher,” their mom said. “He had five children.” “没有，他长大后成了一名教师。”他们的妈妈说。 “他有五个孩子。”

Jack and Annie whooped with joy. 杰克和安妮高兴得欢呼起来。

“That’s great news!” said Annie. “那真是好消息！”安妮说。

“Really great news!” said Jack. “Thanks for telling us!” “真是太好了！”杰克说。 “谢谢你们告诉我们！”

“Sure.” Their dad smiled, though he looked puzzled again. “当然。”他们的爸爸笑了，尽管看上去又有些困惑。

As Jack hurried up to his room, some words from the Civil War song ran through his mind:



 杰克赶紧往自己房间走时，内战歌里的几句歌词掠过他的脑海：

Give us a song to cheer 给我们一首歌来鼓劲

Our weary hearts, a song of home …



安慰我们疲惫的心，一首关于家的歌……
