1. A Light in the Woods 1. 树林里的光

Jack looked out his window. 杰克望着窗外。

It was a dreary Sunday afternoon. There were dark clouds in the sky. 那是一个沉闷的星期天下午。 天上有乌云。

Thunder rumbled in the distance. 远处传来隆隆的雷声。

Jack stared down the street at the Frog Creek woods. 杰克盯着街尽头蛙溪镇的树林。

When is the magic tree house coming back? he wondered. 魔法树屋什么时候回来？他想。

“Hey, guess what!” Annie said. She charged into Jack’s room. “I saw a light flash in the woods!” “嘿，你猜怎么着！”安妮说。 她冲进杰克的房间。 “我看见树林里闪过一道光！”

“It was just lightning,” said Jack. “那只是闪电，”杰克说。

“No, it was magic! A swirl of light!” said Annie. “I think the tree house just came back!” “不是，那是魔法！ 一团旋转的光！”安妮说。 “我觉得树屋刚回来了！”

“I’m sure it was just lightning,” Jack said. “Didn’t you hear the thunder?” “我肯定那只是闪电，”杰克说。 “你没听见雷声吗？”

“Yeah,” said Annie. “But let’s go check anyway.” “听见了，”安妮说。 “不过我们还是去看看吧。”

She started out of Jack’s room. Then she peeked back in. 她往杰克房间外走。 然后又探头回来。

“Bring your backpack, just in case!” she said. “带上你的背包，以防万一！”她说。

Jack was always glad for a chance to look for the magic tree house. He grabbed his backpack and followed Annie down the stairs. 杰克总是很高兴能有机会去找魔法树屋。 他抓起背包，跟着安妮下楼。

“Where are you two going?” their mom called. “你们俩去哪儿？”妈妈喊道。

“Out to play,” said Annie. “出去玩，”安妮说。

“Don’t go far,” said their mom. “And come in if it starts to rain.” “别走远，”妈妈说。 “要是下雨就进来。”

“We will,” said Jack. “Don’t worry.” “好的，”杰克说。 “别担心。”

They slipped out the front door. Then they ran up the street and into the Frog Creek woods. 他们溜出前门。 然后跑上街道，进了蛙溪镇的树林。

The woods were dark under the storm clouds. A cool wind shook the leaves. 在暴风云下，树林里很暗。 一阵凉风摇动着树叶。

Soon Jack and Annie came to the tallest oak tree. 很快杰克和安妮来到最高的那棵橡树下。

“Oh, man,” said Jack. “You were right!” “天哪。”杰克说。 “你说对了！”

The magic tree house stood out against the gray sky. 魔法树屋在灰色天空的衬托下十分醒目。

“Morgan!” called Annie. “摩根！”安妮喊道。

There was no sign of the enchantress. 没有女巫的踪迹。

“Let’s go up!” said Jack. “咱们上去！”杰克说。

He grabbed the rope ladder and started up. Annie followed. 他抓住绳梯开始往上爬。安妮跟着。

They climbed into the tree house. It was hard to see in the dim light. 他们爬进了树屋。 在昏暗的光线里很难看清。

“Look,” said Annie. “看，”安妮说。

She pointed to a piece of paper and a book lying on the floor. 她指着地板上的一张纸和一本书。

Jack picked up the paper. Annie picked up the book. 杰克捡起那张纸。 安妮拿起那本书。

“Listen,” said Jack. He held the paper close to the window and read aloud: “听，”杰克说。 他把纸凑近窗口，大声读道：

Dear Jack and Annie, 亲爱的杰克和安妮。

Camelot is in trouble. To save the kingdom, please find these four special kinds of writing for my library:



 卡米洛特有麻烦了。 为了拯救王国，请为我的图书馆找到这四种特别的文字：

Something to follow 可以遵循的东西

Something to send 可以寄出的东西

Something to learn 可以学习的东西

Something to lend 可以借出的东西

Thank you, 谢谢。

              Morgan

图中文字：CIVIL WAR 图中文字：美国内战

“Camelot is in trouble?” said Jack. “What’s that mean?” “卡米洛特有麻烦了？”杰克说。 “那是什么意思？”

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “But we better hurry and find these writings. Let’s go look for the first: Something to follow.” “我不知道。”安妮说。 “可我们最好赶紧找到这些文字。 我们去找第一样吧：可以遵循的东西。”

“I wonder where we should look for it,” said Jack. “What’s the title of the book you’re holding?” “不知道该去哪儿找，”杰克说。 “你拿的那本书叫什么名字？”

Annie held the book close to the window to read the title. 安妮把书凑近窗口读书名。

“Yikes,” she said softly. She showed the book to Jack. “哎呀，”她轻声说。 她把书给杰克看。

On the cover was a painting of a peaceful-looking field and a blue sky. The title said The Civil War. 封面上画着一片看上去很宁静的田野和蓝色的天空。 书名是《内战》。

“The Civil War?” said Jack. “Cool.” “内战？”杰克说。 “酷。”

Annie frowned. 安妮皱起眉头。

“Cool?” she said. “War’s not cool.” “酷？”她说。 “战争可不酷。”

“It sort of is,” Jack said uncomfortably. He knew war was bad. But some parts of it seemed fun, like a game. “某种程度上是吧。”杰克不太自在地说。 他知道战争是坏事。 可有些部分好像挺好玩，像玩游戏。

“I guess we’ll find out,” said Annie. She pointed at the cover. “I wish we could go there.” “我想我们会弄明白的，”安妮说。 她指着封面。 “我希望我们能去那儿。”

Thunder boomed through the woods. 雷声轰隆隆地穿过树林。

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 绝对静止。
