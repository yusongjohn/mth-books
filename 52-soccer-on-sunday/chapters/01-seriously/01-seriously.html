1. Seriously? 1. 真的吗？

S lowly and carefully, Jack ran his hands over the face of the stony cliff. His fingers gripped ridges and bumps. He lodged his bare toes into cracks in the rock. He knew he had to get to the top. He pushed with his legs and pulled with his hands. He was almost there. He reached up and grabbed the overhang of the cliff. 杰克慢慢地、小心地用手抚摸着石头悬崖的表面。 他的手指紧紧抓住岩石上的凸起和凹陷。 他把光着的脚趾塞进岩石的裂缝里。 他知道他必须到达山顶。 他用腿发力，用手拉拽。 他快要到了。 他伸出手，抓住了悬崖上方的突出部分。

The wind screamed and blew against Jack, knocking him off balance. His feet slipped! He was dangling by one hand! “Jack!” 风尖叫着，吹向杰克，把他吹得失去平衡。他的脚滑了一下！ 他一只手抓着，悬在半空中！ “杰克！”

Jack opened his eyes. It was dark. He could faintly see Annie in the moonlight. “Annie!” he whispered. 杰克睁开眼睛。天很黑。 他能隐约在月光下看到安妮。 “安妮！”他小声说。

“We have to go,” she said. “我们必须走，”她说。

“Have to get down,” said Jack. He closed his eyes. He was still hanging from the cliff. “得下去，”杰克说。 他闭上了眼睛。 他仍然挂在悬崖边。

Annie shook his shoulder. “Jack! Wake up!” 安妮摇了他的肩膀。 “杰克！醒醒！”

“Oh! Whoa …” Jack sat straight up. “Oh, man, I was hanging from a cliff in Egypt—you—” 哇……”杰克一下子坐了起来。 “哦，天哪，我正在埃及的悬崖上悬着——你——”

“You were dreaming about our last mission,” interrupted Annie. “Remember—you were hanging from a cliff, but everything turned out okay. We saved Koku. Remember? The little baboon?” “你是在梦到我们上次的任务，”安妮打断了他。 “还记得吗——你当时正挂在悬崖上，但最后一切都好起来了。我们救了库库。 还记得吗？ 那只小狒狒？

“Right … right,” breathed Jack. He rubbed his eyes and looked at Annie. “What’s going on? What time is it? Why are you awake?” “对……对，”杰克低声说。 他揉了揉眼睛，看着安妮。 “发生什么事了？现在几点了？ 你为什么醒着？“

“We have to go to the woods,” said Annie. “It’s back.” “我们必须去森林里，”安妮说。 “它回来了。”

“How do you know?” said Jack. “你怎么知道的？”杰克说。

“I couldn’t sleep,” said Annie. “I was looking out my window and saw the streak of light—” “我睡不着，”安妮说。 “我正从窗户往外看，然后看到了一道光——”

“That’s it,” said Jack, jumping out of bed. “Let’s go. Are Mom and Dad awake?” “就是它，”杰克说着，从床上跳了起来。 “我们走吧。 妈妈和爸爸醒着吗？

“No. If we leave now, we’ll be back before they get up,” said Annie. “Meet you outside.” She slipped out of Jack’s room. “不。” “如果我们现在离开，我们会在他们起床前回来，”安妮说。 “在屋外见。”她悄悄溜出了杰克的房间。

Jack didn’t even turn on his light. He changed into a pair of shorts and a T-shirt. He pulled on his socks and sneakers. He threw his notebook and pencil into his backpack. Then he tiptoed down the hall, down the stairs, and out the front door. 杰克甚至没有开灯。 他换上了一条短裤和一件T恤。 他穿上袜子和运动鞋。 他把笔记本和铅笔塞进了背包里。 然后他轻手轻脚地沿着走廊走，下楼，从正门走出去。

Annie was waiting on the porch. The sun was about to rise, and birds had started to chirp in the warm summer dawn. 安妮正在门廊上等着。 太阳即将升起，鸟儿们在温暖的夏日清晨开始鸣叫。

“Ready,” Jack whispered. “准备好了，”杰克小声说。

Jack and Annie left the front porch and stepped lightly over the wet grass of their front yard. They hurried up the sidewalk and crossed the street into the Frog Creek woods. As they walked between the dark trees, the purple-blue sky overhead was growing lighter. Birdsong filled the woods. 杰克和安妮离开了前廊，轻手轻脚地跨过门前湿漉漉的草地。 他们快步走上人行道，穿过街道，走进了蛙溪镇的树林里。 当他们走在黑暗的树之间时，头顶那片紫蓝色的天空正在变得越来越亮。 鸟儿的歌声充满了树林。

“I wonder where we’ll go today,” said Jack. “我想知道我们今天会去哪里，”杰克说。

“And what will be the secret of greatness that makes the Ring of Truth glow?” Annie said. Then she began to speak in a dramatic voice, like a TV announcer. “What amazing person will Jack and Annie meet today?” “还有，是什么让真理之环发光的伟大的秘密呢？”安妮说。 然后她用一种戏剧性的声音说话，就像电视上的主持人一样。 “今天杰克和安妮会遇到什么样的了不起的人呢？”

Jack laughed, then spoke dramatically, too. “And when will they sniff the mist-gathered-at-first-light-on-the-first-day-of-the-new-moon-on-the-Isle-of-Avalon?” 杰克笑了，然后也用戏剧性的语气说话。 “那他们什么时候才会闻到黎明初现时，在新月第一天的阿瓦隆岛上聚集的雾气呢？”

“And when they do, what great talent will they choose for themselves?” said Annie. “当他们闻到时，他们会为自己选择什么样的伟大才能呢？”安妮说道。

Jack laughed again and shook his head. 杰克又笑了，摇了摇头。

“Our lives are kind of crazy,” said Annie. “我们的生活有点疯狂，”安妮说道。

“You think?” said Jack. “你这么觉得吗？”杰克说道。

The chorus of birds grew louder as the pale gold of dawn filtered down through the trees. When Jack and Annie came to the tallest oak in the woods, they looked up into the branches. 随着黎明的淡金色透过树木洒下，鸟儿的合唱声变得越来越响亮。 当杰克和安妮来到森林里最高的橡树时，他们抬头望向枝叶间。

The magic tree house was outlined against the sky. Without a word, Annie grabbed the rope ladder and started up. Jack followed. 魔法树屋在天空的映衬下清晰可见。 安妮没有说话，直接抓住了绳梯开始往上爬。杰克跟在后面。

Jack and Annie climbed into the shadowy tree house. A ray of sunlight slanted through a window. It shone directly on a sheet of paper lying on the wooden floor. On the paper, Jack had written the secrets of greatness they had learned on their last three missions: 杰克和安妮爬进了阴暗的树屋里。 一束阳光斜斜地照进窗户里。 它正好照在地板上的一张纸上。 纸上，杰克写下了他们在上次三次任务中学到的伟大秘密：

HUMILITY 谦虚

HARD WORK 努力工作

MEANING AND PURPOSE 意义与目标

A gold ring, a tiny bottle, and a yellowed scroll lay next to the paper. Jack picked up the scroll, unrolled it, and read aloud: 一张金戒指、一个小瓶子和一张泛黄的卷轴躺在纸旁边。 杰克拿起卷轴，展开后大声读起来：

图中文字：Dear Jack and Annie,

I hope you enjoyed traveling to Egypt and learning the third secret of greatness from Florence Nightingale. Now you must learn the fourth from one of the finest athletes of all time, Pelé the Great— 图中文字：亲爱的杰克和安妮，希望你们喜欢去埃及旅行，并从弗洛伦斯·南丁格尔那里学到了伟大第三个秘密。现在你们必须从有史以来最伟大的运动员之一，贝利（Pelé）那里学习第四个秘密——

“Pelé the Great?” said Jack. “He was a brilliant soccer player from Brazil.” “伟大的贝利？”杰克说。 “他是一位来自巴西的出色足球运动员。”

“I know. Coach Cooney told us all about him,” said Annie. “我知道。” “科尼教练告诉我们他的一切，”安妮说。

“This is so cool!” said Jack. “这也太酷了！”杰克说。

“Wait,” said Annie. “There’s more.” “等等，”安妮说。 “还有更多。”

When you complete your mission, please come to Camelot before you return home. 当你完成任务后，请在回家之前先去卡梅洛特。

—M.

“So, we’re going to Camelot!” said Jack. “那么，我们要去卡梅洛特了！”杰克说。

“Yep,” said Annie. “And I wonder if we’re going to Brazil, too.” She and Jack looked around the tree house for a book that would help them on their journey. “是的，”安妮说。 “而且我想知道我们是不是也要去巴西。”她和杰克在树屋里四处寻找一本能帮助他们完成旅程的书。

“There!” said Annie. She picked up a pamphlet lying in a shadowy corner. “I don’t believe it!” “看到了！”安妮说。 她捡起一份躺在阴暗角落里的小册子。 “我不相信！”

“What? What?” said Jack. “什么？什么？”杰克说。

“Look.” Annie handed him the pamphlet. “你看。”安妮把小册子递给他。

“The World Cup!” said Jack. “That’s the biggest sports event on the planet!” “世界杯！”杰克说。 “那是地球上最大的体育赛事！”

“I know,” said Annie. “Look, it’s in Mexico! I’ve always wanted to go to Mexico.” When she opened the pamphlet, she gasped. “And look what Merlin gave us.” She held up two purple tickets. “我知道，”安妮说。 “看，它在墨西哥！ 我一直想去墨西哥。”当她打开小册子时，她惊呼起来。 “快看，梅林给我们带来了什么。”她举起两张紫色的票。

“Mexico 70 Final?” said Jack, reading a ticket. “There are lots of games during the World Cup, but there’s only one final.” “墨西哥70决赛？”杰克一边读票一边说。 “世界杯期间有很多比赛，但只有一场决赛。”

“Thank you, Merlin!” said Annie with a laugh. “谢谢你，梅林！”安妮笑着说道。

“So Pelé must be playing in the final!” Jack said. “所以贝利一定在决赛中出场！”杰克说。

“Yep.” Annie shoved their tickets in her back pocket. “没错。”安妮把两张票塞进她背后的口袋里。

“Don’t forget this,” said Jack. He grabbed the small bottle lying on the floor and held it up to the sunlight. Sparkling mist swirled inside. “别忘了这个，”杰克说。 他捡起地上那个小瓶子，举到阳光下。 瓶子里闪着晶莹的雾气。

“Mist gathered at first light on the first day of the new moon on the Isle of Avalon,” Jack said. Whenever he and Annie breathed in the mist and made a wish, they magically had a great talent for one hour. Jack tucked the bottle into his backpack. “在新月第一天的黎明时分，阿瓦隆岛上的雾气开始聚集，”杰克说。 每当他和安妮吸进雾气并许愿时，他们就会神奇地拥有一小时的特殊才能。 杰克把瓶子塞进他的背包里。

Annie picked up the gold ring from the floor. “And here’s the Ring of Truth,” she said. “You get to wear it this time. Just make sure to keep your eye on it when we’re with Pelé.” 安妮从地上捡起了金戒指。 “这就是真理之戒，”她说。 “这次你来戴吧。 只是要确保当我们和贝利在一起的时候，你一直看着它。

“Don’t worry,” said Jack as he slipped the ring onto his finger. The Ring of Truth would shine with a fiery light when they discovered the fourth secret of greatness. “别担心，”杰克说着，把戒指戴在了手指上。 当他们发现伟大第四秘密时，真理之戒会发出火一般的光芒。

“Let’s go,” said Annie. “我们走吧，”安妮说。

“I still can’t believe we’re actually headed to Mexico, to a final World Cup soccer game, to meet Pelé the Great,” Jack said. “I mean, seriously?” “我仍然不敢相信我们真的要前往墨西哥，去参加一场世界杯足球决赛，去见伟大的贝利，”杰克说。 “我是说，真的吗？”

“Seriously,” said Annie. “真的，”安妮说。

Jack pointed at the cover of their program. “I seriously wish we could go to Mexico City, 1970!” 杰克指着他们节目封面。 “我真希望我们能去1970年的墨西哥城！”

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
