4. Follow Me! 4. 跟我来！

“W ait till we stop again!” Annie yelled at Jack. “等我们再次停下来再说！”安妮对杰克喊道。

“But what if we just missed our stop?” said Jack. “What if that was Pino Suaréz?” “但是如果我们错过了站怎么办？”杰克说。 “那会不会是皮诺·苏亚雷兹？”

“We’re stopping again! We have to check that map!” said Annie. “我们要再次停车！ 我们必须看看那张地图！”安妮说。

As the train slowed for the next stop, Jack and Annie both got ready to jump out of their seats. But when the metro doors jerked open, hardly anyone got off. Instead, new travelers pushed their way into the crowded car. Jack and Annie hardly had room to breathe, much less stand up and check the map. 当火车为下一站减速时，杰克和安妮都准备从座位上跳下来。 但是当地铁门猛地打开时，几乎没人下车。 相反，一些新乘客挤着上了这辆拥挤的车厢。 杰克和安妮几乎没有呼吸的空间，更不用说站起来查看地图了。

“No way!” Jack shouted to Annie. “不可能！”杰克对安妮喊道。

“Can someone help us?” Annie called. “We need to get to the World Cup game! Where is the stop for Pino Suaréz?” Nobody seemed to hear her. “有人能帮帮我们吗？”安妮喊道。 “我们需要去观看世界杯比赛！ 皮诺·苏亚雷斯站的停靠点在哪里？”似乎没人听到她的话。

“This is hopeless!” said Jack. “这太难了！”杰克说道。

Then he heard a small voice, shouting above the noise of the train. “Excuse me! Pardon me, please!” 然后他听到一个微弱的声音，在火车的嘈杂声中喊着。 “对不起！ 请原谅我！”

A Mexican boy squeezed through the crowd to get to Jack and Annie. “Did you say you are going to the World Cup game?” he shouted. 一个墨西哥男孩挤过人群，来到杰克和安妮身边。 “你刚刚说你要去参加世界杯比赛吗？”他喊道。

“Yes!” said Annie. “是的！”安妮说。

“Me too!” said the boy. “Can I help you?” “我也是！”那个男孩说。 “我能帮您吗？”

“Yes!” said Jack, relieved. “We don’t know where to get off!” “是的！”杰克松了口气说。 “我们不知道在哪里下车！”

“The next stop! Pino Suaréz!” said the boy. “You can follow me!” “下一站！ 皮诺·苏亚雷斯！”男孩说。 “你可以跟着我！”

As soon as the train stopped, Jack and Annie followed the boy, squeezing past people to get to the door. “Excuse me! Excuse me!” they said. Then all three of them popped off the hot, airless train. Half the people on the train got off with them. 火车一停下，杰克和安妮就跟着那个男孩，挤过人群朝门口走去。 “对不起！ 对不起！”他们说。 然后三个人一下子从闷热的火车上跳了下来。 火车上一半的人都和他们一起下了车。

“Man, that was crazy,” said Jack. He was covered in sweat. “天啊，那太疯狂了，”杰克说。 他全身都出汗了。

“Thank you!” Annie said to the boy. “谢谢你！”安妮对那个男孩说。

“It’s not over yet,” the boy said, smiling. He had a cheerful, open face. “We have to catch the Blue Line to Taxqueña.” “还没完呢，”男孩微笑着说。 他有一张开朗、开朗的面孔。 “我们必须乘坐蓝线前往塔克斯克纳。”

“Can we come with you?” asked Annie. “We lost our directions.” “我们可以和你一起去吗？”安妮问道。 “我们迷路了。”

“Of course!” said the boy. “This way! Pronto!” “当然可以！”男孩说道。 “这边来！快点！”

Jack and Annie followed the boy down a tunnel and around a corner to the Blue Line. Hordes of people were waiting for that metro line, too. 杰克和安妮跟着男孩走进一条隧道，绕过拐角，来到蓝线车站。 也有很多人等着乘坐那条地铁线。

“My father told me to go to the end of the platform,” said the boy. “The last train cars are never as crowded.” “我父亲告诉我，要走到月台的尽头，”男孩说。 “最后一节车厢从来都不那么拥挤。”

As the train rolled into the station, Jack and Annie quickly followed the boy down the platform. They sped up when the doors opened. Just before the doors closed again, the three of them jumped into the last car of the train. 火车驶入车站时，杰克和安妮赶紧跟着那个男孩走下月台。 门一打开，他们就加快了脚步。 就在门再次关闭之前，他们三个跳进了火车最后一节车厢。

The boy was right. The car wasn’t crowded at all. They got good seats together. Jack felt hugely relieved. 那个男孩说得对。 这节车厢一点也不拥挤。 他们找到了一起坐的好位置。 杰克感到非常轻松。

“Thanks for helping us,” he said. “谢谢你们帮我们，”他说。

“You’re welcome,” said the boy. “My name is Roberto.” He held out his hand. “不用谢，”男孩说。 “我叫罗伯托。”他伸出了手。

Jack shook Roberto’s hand. “I’m Jack,” he said. 杰克握了握罗伯托的手。 “我是杰克，”他说。

“And I’m Annie,” said Annie, shaking his hand, too. “Are you going to the game alone, Roberto?” “我是安妮，”安妮说，也握了握他的手。 “罗伯托，你要一个人去比赛吗？

“Yes. My parents gave me a ticket for my birthday present,” said Roberto. “It’s the best present I’ve ever gotten.” “是的。 我的父母给我一张票，作为我生日的礼物，”罗伯托说。 “这是我收到的最好的礼物。”

“Is today your birthday?” asked Jack. “今天是你的生日吗？”杰克问。

Roberto nodded. “I turn ten years old today.” 罗伯托点了点头。 “我今天满十岁了。”

“Happy birthday!” Jack and Annie said together. “生日快乐！”杰克和安妮一起说。

“I’m ten, too,” said Annie. “Jack’s eleven.” “我也是十岁，”安妮说。 “杰克十一岁。”

“Why didn’t your mom and dad come with you?” Jack said. “你爸爸妈妈怎么没和你一起来？”杰克说。

“They could only afford one ticket,” said Roberto. “I have eight brothers and sisters. It will be my job to tell them everything I see. They will all be waiting at home for my stories about the great game.” He smiled his big smile again. “他们只能买一张票，”罗伯托说。 “我有八个兄弟姐妹。 我要负责告诉他们我看到的一切。 他们都会在家里等着听我讲关于那场精彩比赛的故事。”他又一次露出了他那大大的微笑。

The train began to slow down. “We get off here,” said Roberto, standing up. 火车开始减速了。 “我们在这里下车，”罗伯托站起来说。

Jack and Annie followed Roberto off the metro, down an underground passageway, then out of the stifling station into the breezy, wet air. Jack felt a thousand times better. 杰克和安妮跟着罗伯托走下地铁，穿过一条地下通道，然后走出闷热的车站，来到凉爽潮湿的空气里。 杰克感觉好太多了。

“Now we take tramline 53,” said Roberto. “现在我们要坐53路电车，”罗伯托说。

“That’s like a subway above ground, right?” said Jack. “那就是在地上跑的地铁，对吗？”杰克问。

“Yes. Come with me.” Roberto led Jack and Annie across a walkway to the trolley tracks. Hundreds of people stood in lines, waiting to board the packed trams. As they joined one of the lines, Jack pulled out some pesos. “对。 跟我来。”罗伯托带杰克和安妮走过一座人行天桥，来到电车轨道旁。 数百人排着队，等着登上拥挤的电车。 当他们加入其中一队时，杰克掏出了一些比索。

“Everyone’s going to the World Cup championship game,” said Roberto. “People have come to Mexico from all over South America and Europe to see this game.” “大家都去观看世界杯决赛，”罗伯托说。 “人们从南美洲和欧洲各地来到墨西哥，就是为了看这场比赛。”

“Did you hear that it would be the game of a lifetime?” asked Jack. “你听说了吗？这会是人生中最棒的一场比赛！”杰克问。

“Absolutely,” said Roberto. “当然听到了，”罗伯托说。

“Did you know Pelé was playing?” Annie asked. “你知道贝利要参加比赛吗？”安妮问。

“Of course! Pelé the Great!” said Roberto. “He has made over a thousand goals. And his whole team is fantastic. They are called the Beautiful Team!” “当然知道！ 伟大的贝利！”罗伯托说。 “他已经踢进了一千多个球。 他的整个队伍都非常棒。 他们被称为‘美丽球队’！”

“That’s cool,” said Jack. “这太酷了，”杰克说。

“Three pesos,” said the driver at the door of the tram. “三比索，”门口的有轨电车司机说。

Roberto reached into his pocket, but Jack stopped him. “I got it,” he said. Jack handed over three pesos. 罗伯托伸手去口袋里拿东西，但杰克拦住了他。 “我来拿，”他说。 杰克给了他三个比索。

“Thank you, Jack,” said Roberto. Then they all hopped aboard the tram bound for the World Cup championship game. There were no empty seats, so they stood in the aisle and held on to a pole. As the tram began to move, Jack, Annie, and Roberto huddled together and talked excitedly. “谢谢你，杰克，”罗伯托说。 然后他们全都上了开往世界杯决赛的电车。 没有空座位，所以他们站在过道里，抓着一根柱子。 电车开始移动时，杰克、安妮和罗伯托挤在一起，兴奋地聊着天。

“Brazil has the best team in history,” said Roberto. “But Italy has a great team, too. Did you hear the story of Italy playing West Germany in the semifinal match?” “巴西拥有历史上最强的球队，”罗伯托说。 “但意大利的球队也很厉害。 你听说过意大利在半决赛中对阵西德的故事吗？”

“No. What happened?” said Annie. “不。” “发生了什么？”安妮说。

“Even though Italy was behind, they never gave up,” said Roberto. “Italy scored five goals in extra time!” He heaved a big sigh. “I can’t believe it. I never thought in my life I would be here today to see this game.” “尽管意大利队落后了，但他们从未放弃，”罗伯托说。 “意大利队在加时赛进了五个球！”他叹了口气。 “我简直不敢相信。 我这辈子做梦都没想到，今天会在这里看到这场比赛。”

“Me neither!” said Annie. “我也没想到！”安妮说。

Jack laughed. “Not in a million years.” 杰克笑了。 “我做梦都不敢想。”

“We’ll arrive at Aztec Stadium soon,” said Roberto, looking out the window. “我们很快就到阿兹特克体育场了，”罗伯托看着窗外说。

“Thanks for your help!” said Jack. He really liked this kid. He was also amazed that a ten-year-old could get around the city so easily. “Do you go to the stadium a lot?” “谢谢你的帮助！”杰克说。 他真的很喜欢这个孩子。 他也很惊讶，一个十岁的孩子竟然能这么轻松地在城里到处走动。 “你经常去体育场吗？”，

“No. This is my first time,” he said. “But I have practiced this trip in my mind many times. My father told me exactly what to do.” “不。” “这是我第一次来，”他说。 “但我已经在心里练习过很多次这次旅行。 我爸爸告诉我该怎么做。”

“Do you play soccer?” Annie asked. “你踢足球吗？”安妮问。

“I try. But I am not that great,” Roberto said. “我试过。” 但我不那么厉害，”罗伯托说。

“Me neither,” said Annie. “I love it, but I’m not great.” “我也不厉害，”安妮说。 “我超喜欢，但我不是特别厉害。”

“Same with me,” said Jack. “Love it. Not great.” And they all laughed. “我也是，”杰克说。 “我也超喜欢。 不太妙。”他们全都笑了。

By the time the tram arrived at the station near the stadium, the clouds had disappeared. Bright sunshine had replaced the drizzling rain. Steam was rising from a gigantic parking lot. 等到缆车到达体育场附近的车站时，云朵已经消失了。 阳光明媚，取代了淅淅沥沥的雨。 蒸汽从一个巨大的停车场升腾而起。

“That’s Aztec Stadium,” Roberto said, pointing out the window to an enormous concrete building. “那是阿兹特克体育场，”罗伯托指着窗外一座巨大的混凝土建筑说。

“Cool,” said Jack. He looked at his watch. “It’s eleven-thirty-five.” “酷毙了，”杰克说。 他看了看手表。 “是十一点三十五分。”

“Twenty-five minutes until kickoff,” said Roberto. “We made it!” “开球还有二十五分钟，”罗伯托说。 “我们赶到了！”

“All because of you, Roberto!” said Annie. “全靠你，罗伯托！”安妮说。

“Stay with me,” the Mexican boy said with a smile, “and I’ll take good care of you.” “跟我待在一起，”那个墨西哥男孩微笑着说，“我会好好照顾你的。”
