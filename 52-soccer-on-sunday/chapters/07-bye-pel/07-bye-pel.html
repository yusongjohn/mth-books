7. Bye, Pelé 7. 再见，贝利

“I’ m so glad!” shouted Annie. “Come and squeeze in here with us to watch! Everyone has gone crazy!” “我太高兴了！”安妮喊道。 “快来这儿和我们一起看吧！ 大家都疯了！

Jack climbed up onto the bench and stood between Annie and Roberto as they watched the happy celebration on the field. The president of Mexico presented a giant trophy to Brazil, and Carlos Alberto, the captain of the team, lifted it high above his head. 杰克爬上了长椅，站在安妮和罗伯托之间，看着他们在场地上开心地庆祝。 墨西哥的总统给巴西颁发了一个巨大的奖杯，巴西队的队长卡洛斯·阿尔贝托高高举起了奖杯。

“Yay, Brazil!” shouted Roberto. “Yay, Pelé!” “耶，巴西！”罗伯托喊道。 “耶，贝利！”

“Did the Ring of Truth ever glow?” Jack asked Annie. “When you were watching Pelé up close, did you learn a secret of greatness from him?” “真相之环曾经发光过吗？”杰克问安妮。 “当你近距离观看贝利的时候，他有没有教你伟大的秘密？”

“No, not yet,” Annie said. “Yay, Pelé!” “还没有，”安妮说。 “耶，贝利！”

“Not yet? ” said Jack. “Then when?” “还没有？”杰克说。 “那什么时候呢？”

“Don’t worry,” said Annie. “We still have time.” She pulled the ring off her finger. “Here, you can wear it again.” “别担心，”安妮说。 “我们还有时间。”她把戒指从手指上取了下来。 “给你，你可以再戴上。”

Jack slipped the ring back onto his finger. But what’s the use? he thought. We lost our big chance. How can we ever get close to Pelé now? 杰克把戒指重新戴回手指上。 但这有什么用呢？他心想。 我们失去了大好机会。 现在我们怎么才能接近贝利呢？

Followed by hundreds of photographers and TV cameras, the Beautiful Team was marching around the field. Confetti and streamers floated down from the stands. Firecrackers were whistling and popping over the stadium. Flag bearers waved huge green, yellow, and blue Brazilian flags. A band was playing. 数百名摄影师和电视摄像机紧随其后，美丽的球队正在场地上绕场而行。 彩纸和彩带从看台上飘落下来。 体育场上方响起了鞭炮声，噼里啪啦地炸开。 旗手们挥舞着巨大的绿色、黄色和蓝色的巴西国旗。 有一支乐队在演奏。

Annie and Roberto clapped in rhythm to the music. Trying to hide both his worry and his disappointment, Jack clapped with them. 安妮和罗伯托随着音乐有节奏地鼓掌。 杰克努力掩饰自己的担心和失望，也跟着他们鼓掌。

“They’re leaving now!” said Roberto. “他们现在要离开了！”罗伯托说。

Police were leading Pelé and all the other Brazilian players off the crazy, crowded field. TV cameras, reporters, and a mob of fans followed the World Cup champions. 警察正带领着贝利和其他所有巴西球员离开那疯狂拥挤的球场。 电视摄像机、记者和一群粉丝跟着世界杯冠军们走。

“We should follow the team, too,” Annie said to Jack. “Want to?” “我们也应该跟着队伍走，”安妮对杰克说。 “想跟吗？”，

“We have to,” Jack said sharply. “We still have a mission to accomplish, remember?” “我们必须跟，”杰克语气坚定地说。 “我们还有任务要完成，还记得吗？”

“Come with us, Roberto!” said Annie. “Let’s all follow the team! Pronto!” “和我们一起去吧，罗伯托！”安妮说。 “大家快跟上队伍！快点！”

They climbed down from the bench and started running across the huge green soccer field. The parade of fans and players was leaving by the farthest exit. 他们从长椅上跳下来，开始跑过那片巨大的绿色足球场。 球迷和球员的队伍正从最远的出口离开。

“We’ll catch up to them outside the stadium,” said Roberto, running alongside Jack and Annie. “If I could get even twenty feet from Pelé, I would be happy forever!” “我们会在体育场外追上他们，”罗伯托一边跑一边对杰克和安妮说。 “如果我能离贝利近二十英尺，我就会永远开心！”

“Me too!” said Annie. “Jack and I have a question for him!” “我也是！”安妮说。 “杰克和我有个问题要问他！”

No kidding, thought Jack. 杰克心想，真有你的。

Jack, Annie, and Roberto hurried through the exit, leaving Aztec Stadium and heading into the parking lot. 杰克、安妮和罗伯托赶紧从出口跑出去，离开了阿兹特克体育场，朝停车场跑去。

“Over there! They’re getting on a bus!” said Roberto. “那边！ “他们正在上公交车！”罗伯托说。

Not far away, fans and media were swarming around a bus. Photographers were snapping pictures, one after another, flashbulbs blazing. TV reporters were shouting questions at the team. Supporters were cheering and waving flags. 不远处，粉丝和媒体记者们围在一辆公交车周围。 摄影师们一个接一个地拍照，闪光灯不停地闪烁。 电视记者们对着队员们大声提问。 支持者们在欢呼，挥舞着旗帜。

“Hurry!” cried Jack. “快点！”杰克喊道。

They all ran faster, but before they could get to the bus, it pulled away from the crowd. Police pushed fans out of its path so it could leave. 他们都跑得更快了，但就在他们能赶到公交车之前，公交车已经从人群中开走了。 警察把粉丝们推到公交车的路线上，好让公交车离开。

“Good-bye!” said Annie. “再见！”安妮说道。

“Good-bye, Pelé!” Roberto yelled. “再见，佩莱！”罗伯托喊道。

Jack, Annie, and Roberto watched the bus move out of the giant parking lot of Aztec Stadium. 杰克、安妮和罗伯托看着公交车驶出阿兹特克体育场巨大的停车场。

Well, there goes our chance to accomplish our mission, Jack thought dismally. There will be no reason to journey to Camelot now. It’ll be the first time we’ve ever failed. 好吧，我们的机会就这样没了，杰克沮丧地想着。 现在我们再也没有理由去卡梅洛特了。 这将是我们第一次失败。

“Don’t worry,” Annie said to him, as if she’d read his mind. “I’ll bet the team’s headed to the city. Let’s go back there and look for them.” “别担心，”安妮对他说，仿佛看穿了他的想法。 “我打赌球队正往城里去了。 我们回去找他们吧。”

“Okay. I guess that might work,” said Jack, trying not to give up hope. “I heard on the radio they were all going to a banquet in Mexico City tonight.” “好吧。 我想这可能有用，”杰克说，努力不让自己失去希望。 “我刚刚在收音机里听到，他们今晚都要去墨西哥城参加宴会。”

“Cool!” said Annie. “Someone’s bound to know where that banquet is. Then maybe we can use our magic mist to get into the party.” “太酷了！”，安妮说。 “肯定有人知道那个宴会的地点。 然后我们也许可以用我们的魔法雾气混进派对里。”

“What? Like, make a wish to be the greatest party crashers in the world?” said Jack. “什么？ 比如，许愿成为世界上最棒的闯入派对的人？”杰克说。

Annie laughed. “Whatever it takes,” she said. She turned to Roberto and asked, “Roberto, can you help us get back into the city?” 安妮笑了。 “不管用什么办法，”她说。 她转向罗伯托，问：“罗伯托，你能帮我们回到城里吗？”

“Of course! I owe you everything!” he said. “当然可以！” “我一切都得感谢你！”他说。

“Not really,” said Jack. “Everyone deserves a good birthday.” “其实不用，”杰克说。 “每个人都应该过个好生日。”

“Thank you, Jack!” said Roberto with a big grin. “Come with me!” “谢谢你，杰克！”罗伯托咧着嘴笑着说。 “跟我来！”

Jack and Annie followed Roberto away from the giant concrete building, through the crowded parking lot, and toward the tramline. 杰克和安妮跟着罗伯托离开那栋巨大的混凝土建筑，穿过拥挤的停车场，朝电车轨道走去。

“Whoa!” said Annie. “哇！”安妮说。

Thousands of people were waiting to board the trams. “We will have to wait many hours to get one,” said Roberto. 成千上万的人都在等着上电车。 “我们必须等好几个小时才能坐上一辆电车，”罗伯托说。

“Is there another station nearby?” Jack asked. “附近还有别的车站吗？”杰克问。

“Yes, but it will be just as crowded,” Roberto answered. “是的，但那里也会很拥挤，”罗伯托回答道。

“What do you think we should do?” Annie asked. “你觉得我们应该怎么做？”安妮问。

“I have an idea,” said Roberto. “My great-aunt lives in a neighborhood north of here. Near her house, there is a bus stop. Want to give it a try?” “我有个主意，”罗伯托说。 “我姑姑住在北边的一个社区里。 她家附近有一个公交站。 要试试看吗？”

“Sure,” said Jack. “We don’t have another choice.” He and Annie followed Roberto away from the tram station. “当然，”杰克说。 “我们没有其他选择。”他和安妮跟着罗伯托离开了电车站。

Clouds had moved back in. A soft rain fell as they walked along a road crowded with cars leaving the stadium. Horns were blaring and drivers were shouting at each other. 云又回来了。 他们沿着一条挤满了离开体育场的汽车的道路走着，下着细雨。 喇叭响个不停，司机们互相大声喊叫。

“This way,” said Roberto, and he led Jack and Annie away from the busy road into a run-down neighborhood. They passed St. Anthony’s Church, a junk store, a Laundromat, and a beauty shop. Then Roberto led them down a dirt path between rows of stucco houses. “这边走，”罗伯托说，他带着杰克和安妮离开繁忙的马路，走进了一个破旧的社区。他们经过了斯特的家。 安东尼教堂、一家旧货店、一家自助洗衣店和一家美容美发店。 然后罗伯托带他们沿着一条泥路，穿过一排灰泥房子。

In the light rain, families sat on front porches. Several were listening to portable radios. The announcer was repeating the news about the World Cup final. 在细雨中，一些家庭坐在房子的前廊上。 有几个人正在听便携式收音机。 播音员正在重复播放关于世界杯决赛的新闻。

At the end of the street was a vacant lot filled with kids. When Jack, Annie, and Roberto drew closer, they saw about twenty boys and girls of all ages running around barefoot and covered with mud. The kids were playing soccer. The goal lines had been marked off with tattered nets. 街道尽头是一块空地，孩子们在那儿玩耍。 当杰克、安妮和罗伯托走近时，他们看到大约二十个不同年龄的男孩女孩光着脚，浑身沾满泥巴，在那儿跑来跑去。 孩子们在踢足球。 比赛线用破破烂烂的网标了出来。

A grown-up on the sideline was acting as a referee. He blew a whistle and yelled, “Halftime!” All the kids stopped running. Several players walked away from the field as the others gathered in small groups to talk. sidelines 上有一个大人当裁判。 他吹了哨子，大喊：“中场休息！”所有孩子都停下了奔跑。 几个球员走开了，其他孩子聚成小团体聊天。

“Hey, Roberto!” An older boy waved. “嘿，罗伯托！”一个年纪较大的男孩挥手打招呼。

“Hey, Miguel!” Roberto waved back. “嘿，米格尔！”罗伯托也挥手回应。

“What are you doing here?” shouted Miguel. “你在这里干什么？”米格尔喊道。

“I’m taking my friends back to the city,” Roberto answered. “We’re going to catch the bus.” “我带朋友们回城里去，”罗伯托回答。 “我们要去坐公交车。”

“Hey, Roberto!” another kid yelled. “嘿，罗伯托！”另一个孩子大喊。

“Hey, Diego!” “嘿，迪埃戈！”

“Did you hear Brazil just won?” called Diego. “你听到巴西刚刚赢了吗？”迪埃戈喊道。

“Yay!” Roberto pumped the air with his fist. “Pelé the Great!” Then he turned to Jack and Annie. “I played ball with these kids once when I was visiting my aunt. If they come over, please do not tell them we just saw the World Cup final. It would make them sad that they could not afford to go.” “太棒了！”罗伯托挥舞着拳头，兴奋地拍手。 “伟大的贝利！”然后他转向杰克和安妮。 “我以前去我姑姑家的时候，和这些孩子一起踢过球。 如果他们来了，请不要告诉他们我们刚刚看了世界杯决赛。 那样会让他们难过，因为他们买不起票去。”

“Okay, we won’t,” said Annie. “好的，我们不会说的，”安妮说道。

Jack nodded. Again he was impressed by Roberto’s kindness. The boy seemed to care about everyone’s feelings. 杰克点了点头。 再一次，他被罗伯托的善良所感动。 这个男孩似乎很在意每个人的感受。

“Roberto, can your friends play soccer?” Miguel yelled. “Our team just lost some players for the second half!” “罗伯托，你的朋友能踢足球吗？”米格尔喊道。 “我们的队伍在下半场刚刚失去了几个队员！”

Before Roberto could answer, another boy jumped in. “No, Miguel! Not Roberto! Remember that time he played? He was the worst player ever!” 罗伯托还没来得及回答，另一个男孩就插话了。 “不，米格尔！不是罗伯托！” 还记得他那次打球吗？ 他当时是史上最差的球员！

“Yeah, Miguel, remember?” yelled a tall girl. “He made us lose big-time!” “没错，米格尔，记得吗？”一个高个子女孩喊道。 “他让我们输得惨兮兮的！”

“I didn’t ask Roberto to play, Carla!” Miguel said. “I asked if his two friends could play! We lost our three forwards, but we can play with two.” “我没有让罗伯托来玩，卡洛拉！”米格尔说道。 “我问他的两个朋友能不能一起玩！ 我们失去了三名前锋，但我们可以用两名来打。

Jack glanced at Roberto. The boy’s smile was gone and he stared at the ground. 杰克看了看罗伯托。 男孩的笑容消失了，他盯着地面。

“No way,” Annie said to Jack, gritting her teeth. “Not in a million years.” “没门，”安妮对杰克说，咬紧了牙关。 “一千万年都不行。”

“Right. Not even if we didn’t have a mission and were the best players in the world,” said Jack. “Come on, Roberto. Let’s get out of here.” “对。 就算我们没有任务，而且还是世界上最棒的球员，也不行，”杰克说。 “快点，罗伯托。 我们快点离开这儿。”

“Wait … wait a minute,” said Annie. Then she turned back to the kids and yelled, “Yes, Miguel! We can play!” “等……等一下，”安妮说。 然后她转身对着孩子们大喊：“是的，米格尔！我们可以玩！”

“Are you crazy?” Jack asked her. “你疯了吗？”杰克问她。

“We’ll play on your team!” Annie yelled again to Miguel. “But only if you’ll let Roberto play, too! All three of us want to be forwards!” “你们队会加入我们！”安妮又对米格尔大喊。 “但前提是你们也让罗伯托加入！” 我们三个人都想当前锋！

Roberto shook his head. “No, not me. They don’t want me.” 罗伯托摇了摇头。 “不，不是我。” 他们不要我。”

“I’m not that great, either,” said Jack. “Annie, what’s wrong with you?” “我也不太好，”杰克说。 “安妮，你怎么了？”

“What’s wrong with me? Nothing!” Annie reached into Jack’s backpack and took out the small bottle of Merlin’s magic mist. “In fact, everything’s about to be great—for all three of us!” “我怎么了？” “什么也没有！”安妮伸手到杰克的背包里，拿出了一小瓶梅林的魔法喷雾。 “事实上，一切马上就会变得很棒——对我们三个人来说都是如此！”
