10. Candles in the Forest 10. 森林里的蜡烛

G olden light flooded the tree house. Jack and Annie looked out the window. It was early evening. The magic tree house had not landed in a tree. It had landed in a small clearing in a Camelot forest. All around, thousands of lanterns hung from oak trees. Their candle flames shone on knights in gleaming armor and ladies in cone-shaped hats with flowing veils. 金色的光芒照进了树屋。 杰克和安妮望向窗外。 天色还早，是傍晚时分。 神奇的树屋没有落在一棵树上。 它落在了卡梅洛特森林里的一片小空地上。 四周，成千上万的灯笼挂在橡树上。 蜡烛的火光照亮了身披闪亮盔甲的骑士和戴着锥形帽子、披着长纱的女士们。

Jack couldn’t speak. Even Annie was speechless. 杰克说不出话来。 就连安妮也惊讶得说不出话。

Morgan le Fay stepped out from the crowd. The enchantress librarian was dressed in a long red velvet gown. Her white hair shimmered in the candlelight. 摩根·勒·法伊从人群中走了出来。 这位女巫图书管理员穿着一件长长的红色天鹅绒长裙。 她的白发在烛光下闪闪发亮。

“Welcome back, Jack and Annie,” said Morgan. “欢迎回来，杰克和安妮，”摩根说道。

“Thank you,” breathed Jack. “谢谢，”杰克低声说。

“I’m sorry we’re so dirty,” said Annie. “We just played a tough game of soccer.” “对不起，我们太脏了，”安妮说。 “我们刚玩了一场激烈的足球比赛。”

“You look wonderful to us,” said Morgan, smiling. “All of Camelot celebrates your return. Fifty-two times you have traveled to faraway places in my magic tree house. Fifty-two times you have proven your courage and kindness under the most difficult of circumstances. It is a magical number. The same number as there are weeks in a year.” “你们看起来太棒了，”摩根微笑着说。 “整个卡梅洛特都在庆祝你们的归来。 你们已经乘坐我的魔法树屋，去了52个遥远的地方。 你已经证明了五十两次，在最困难的情况下，你都表现出了勇气和善良。 这是一个神奇的数字。 和一年中周数一样多。

Carrying his staff, Merlin the magician stepped forward and stood beside Morgan. “Jack and Annie, I heard stories of your adventures long before I met you,” he said. “I could not believe two children would venture into the lands of dinosaurs, castles, ancient Egypt, and pirates and return unharmed. You even outsmarted me when you saved Morgan from a spell I had playfully put her under.” 梅林法师拿着他的拐杖走上前，站在摩根身旁。 “杰克和安妮，我很久以前就听说过你们的冒险故事，”他说。 “我简直不敢相信，两个孩子竟然敢去恐龙、城堡、古埃及和海盗的地盘冒险，还能毫发无损地回来。 你们甚至在我开玩笑地给摩根施了魔法后，还成功地救了她。”

Morgan gave Merlin a look. Then she turned back to Jack and Annie. “And we had fun, didn’t we?” she said. “When we journeyed to the time of the ninjas, the rain forest, the Ice Age, and the moon?” 摩根看了梅林一眼。 然后她又转向杰克和安妮。 “我们玩得可开心了，对吧？”她说。 “当我们去到了忍者时代、雨林、冰河时期和月球的时候？”

“Yes, and Jack and I had fun when we solved your four riddles,” Annie said breathlessly, “on the coral reef, in the Wild West, on the Serengeti plain of Africa, and in the Arctic.” “是的，杰克和我解开了你的四个谜题，”安妮喘着气说，“在珊瑚礁，在西部荒野，在非洲的塞伦盖蒂大草原，还有在北极。”

“And when we became Master Librarians,” said Jack, “and rescued the four ancient stories from Rome, China, Ireland, and Greece.” “还有当我们成为图书管理员的时候，”杰克说，“我们从罗马、中国、爱尔兰和希腊救出了四个古老的故事。”

“And when you freed me from a spell!” said a voice from the crowd. “ Woof, woof.” “还有当你把我从咒语中解救出来的时候！”人群中有人喊道。 “汪，汪。”

“Teddy!” said Annie. “泰迪！”安妮说。

The firelight shone on the young sorcerer’s tousled red hair as he stepped forward. “Remember when I was a dog?” Teddy said, grinning. 火光映照着年轻法师凌乱的红发，他向前走了一步。 “还记得我曾经是一只狗吗？”泰迪微笑着说道。

Jack and Annie laughed and nodded. 杰克和安妮笑了，并点了点头。

“And we escaped the Titanic,” said Teddy, “and the buffalo stampede, the tiger in India, and the forest fire in the Australian outback?” “还有我们逃过了泰坦尼克号，”泰迪说，“还有野牛群的踩踏，印度的老虎，以及澳大利亚内陆的森林大火？”

“Yes!” said Jack and Annie. “是的！”杰克和安妮说。

“And do not forget how you saved His Majesty from despair,” said a woman in a snow-white gown. A veil hung from a wreath of blue flowers around her hair. Standing next to her was a man in a purple tunic and a red cape. On his head was a simple golden crown. “还有不要忘记你如何拯救了国王免于绝望，”一位穿着雪白礼服的女士说。 她的头发周围缠着一条蓝色花朵的花环，上面挂着一条面纱。 她旁边站着一位穿着紫色长袍、红色斗篷的男士。 他的头上戴着一顶简单的金色王冠。

“Queen Guinevere! King Arthur!” said Annie. “桂妮薇尔女王！ 亚瑟国王！”安妮说。

“You brought me wisdom and courage from the American Civil War,” said King Arthur. “And the Revolutionary War, a schoolhouse on the American prairie, and an earthquake in San Francisco.” “你从美国南北战争中给我带来了智慧和勇气，”亚瑟国王说。 “还有独立战争、美国大草原上的一所学校房子，以及旧金山的一次地震。”

“And then you discovered the secrets of everyday magic,” said Morgan, “from the people of the first Thanksgiving, the gorillas of the cloud forest, William Shakespeare, and friends in Hawaii.” “然后你发现了日常魔法的秘密，”摩根说，“来自第一次感恩节的人们、云林的猩猩、威廉·莎士比亚，以及夏威夷的朋友。”

“And you saved the four treasures of Camelot for Merlin,” said King Arthur. “你为梅林保存了卡梅洛特的四件宝物，”亚瑟王说道。

Merlin laughed. “Yes, you outwitted the Dragons of the Otherworld, the Raven King, the Sea Serpent, and the Ice Wizard,” he said. 梅林笑了。 “是的，你骗过了异世界里的龙、渡鸦王、海蛇和冰巫师，”他说。

“And do not forget when Teddy and I journeyed with you to the four beautiful cities,” said a young girl. Her dark hair curled down over her shoulders. Her sea-blue eyes shone in the firelight. “Ancient Baghdad, Venice, Paris, and New York?” she said. “还有别忘了，泰迪和我曾经和你们一起去过那四个美丽的城市，”一个年轻女孩说道。 她的深色头发垂落在肩膀上。 火光映照着她海蓝色的眼睛。 “古代的巴格达、威尼斯、巴黎和纽约？”她说道。

Jack nodded. “Hi, Kathleen,” he said shyly. 杰克点了点头。 “你好，凯瑟琳，”他害羞地说。

“And thank you, Jack and Annie, for saving another from a spell, too,” said Kathleen, “on our mission in New York City.” “谢谢你们，杰克和安妮，也救了我们纽约市任务中的另一个人，”凯瑟琳说，“从一个咒语中。”

A white unicorn gracefully stepped forward and stood beside Kathleen. He lowered his head. 一匹白色的独角兽优雅地走上前，站在凯瑟琳身旁。 他低下头。

“Dianthus!” said Annie. “迪安瑟！”安妮说道。

“The Wand of Dianthus helped you learn four secrets of happiness,” said Merlin, “from Leonardo da Vinci, Basho the poet, the deep sea—and, best of all, from this little one you brought me from Antarctica.” The magician picked up a baby penguin that had been hiding behind his cloak. “迪安瑟的魔杖帮助你学会了幸福的四个秘密，”梅林说道，“来自达·芬奇、诗人芭蕉、深海——还有，最棒的是，来自你从南极洲带给我这只小家伙。” 魔法师捡起了藏在他斗篷后面的一只小企鹅。

“Penny!” said Jack. “彭妮！”杰克说道。

“And four creative geniuses of history thank you, too,” said Morgan, “for helping them begin the path to giving their gifts to the world.” “还有，历史上的四位创意天才也感谢你，”摩根说道，“感谢你帮助他们开始将他们的天赋献给世界的旅程。”

“Mozart and Louis Armstrong,” said Jack, remembering their friends, “and Lady Gregory and Charles Dickens.” “莫扎特和路易斯·阿姆斯特朗，”杰克说道，想起了他们的朋友，“还有格雷戈里夫人和查尔斯·狄更斯。”

“And thank you for helping Teddy to undo the spell he accidentally cast on Penny,” said Merlin. “He confessed all to us later.” “还要感谢你帮助泰迪解除了他不小心施加在彭妮身上的咒语，”梅林说道。 “他后来向我们坦白了一切。”

“We went to the Taj Mahal in India for that,” said Annie, “and met Saint Bernard dogs in Switzerland and Abraham Lincoln—” “我们为了这件事去了印度的泰姬陵，”安妮说道，“还遇到了瑞士的圣伯纳犬和亚伯拉罕·林肯——”

“And pandas in China,” said Jack. “还有中国的熊猫，”杰克说。

“And now you have returned from four more missions,” said Merlin. “And you were successful, I presume?” “现在你已经完成了四次更多的任务，”梅林说。 “你们成功了，对吧？”

“Yes,” said Annie. “We learned secrets of greatness by spending time with Alexander the Great and his black stallion, and with the Great Houdini and his wife, Bess; Florence Nightingale—” “是的，”安妮说。 “我们通过与亚历山大大帝和他的黑马，还有伟大的霍迪尼和他的妻子贝丝，以及弗洛伦斯·南丁格尔——”

“And finally,” said Jack, “from Pelé the Great.” He held out the notebook paper on which he had written the four secrets of greatness. “最后，”杰克说，“还有伟大的贝利。”他拿出写有四项伟大秘密的笔记本纸。

Merlin took the paper from Jack and read in a deep, resonant voice: 梅林从杰克手中接过纸，用低沉而洪亮的声音读道：

HUMILITY “谦逊”

HARD WORK “努力工作”

MEANING AND PURPOSE “意义与目标”

ENTHUSIASM 热情

The magician was silent for a moment. He seemed deep in thought. Then he looked back at Jack and Annie. “Yes,” he said. “Perfect. Thank you, Jack and Annie, for your greatness.” 魔术师沉默了一会儿。 他似乎在深思。 然后他看向杰克和安妮。 “是的，”他说。 “完美。 谢谢你们，杰克和安妮，感谢你们的伟大。”

Jack and Annie both smiled and shrugged. “We’re not that great,” said Jack. 杰克和安妮都笑了笑，耸了耸肩。 “我们并没有那么厉害，”杰克说。

“We’re just two regular kids,” said Annie. “Really lucky regular kids.” “我们只是两个普通的孩子，”安妮说。 “真的很幸运的普通孩子。”

“On your journeys you have learned many things,” said Morgan. “You have seen many parts of the world and met many remarkable people. Jack, you have become kinder and more courageous. Annie, you have become more thoughtful.” “在你们的旅程中，你们学到了很多东西，”摩根说。 “你们看到了世界上的很多地方，也遇到了很多了不起的人。 杰克，你变得更有爱心、更勇敢了。 安妮，你变得更有想法了。”

“Your next missions may be even more challenging,” said Merlin. “你们接下来的任务可能会更加挑战，”梅林说。

“Really?” breathed Jack. “How—how do we prepare for that?” “真的吗？”杰克屏住呼吸说。 “我们——我们怎么为那做准备呢？”

“Do not worry,” said Morgan. “We want you to do what others your age do—learn new things in school, have fun with your family and friends, read books—” “别担心，”摩根说。 “我们希望你们做和你们这个年纪的孩子一样的事情——在学校里学习新知识，和家人朋友一起玩，读书——”

“Play games outside,” said Teddy. “Play music.” “在外面玩游戏，”泰迪说。 “播放音乐。”

“Play with animals,” added Kathleen. “和动物玩玩吧，”凯瑟琳补充道。

Jack and Annie smiled. 杰克和安妮笑了。

“Good-bye, Jack and Annie,” said Merlin. “再见，杰克和安妮，”梅林说道。

“For now,” said Morgan. “现在先这样，”摩根说。

The candle flames danced wildly in the gentle breeze. In the golden light, bells rang through the air. Sweet music filled the forest with the sounds of flutes and horns. 蜡烛的火焰在微风中跳动着。 在金色的光线下，钟声在空中回荡。 甜美的音乐充满了森林，有笛子和号角的声音。

The wind began to blow. 风开始吹起来了。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转了。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。

The pale gold light of dawn shone through the window of the tree house. Jack and Annie were back in the woods of Frog Creek, and their clothes were clean. No time at all had passed in Frog Creek. 黎明时分淡淡的金色光线透过树屋的窗户照了进来。 杰克和安妮又回到了蛙溪镇的森林里，他们的衣服变得干净了。 在蛙溪镇，一点时间都没有过去。

“Did—did all that just happen?” said Jack, dazed. “这—这刚刚都发生了吗？”杰克迷迷糊糊地说。

“All what?” said Annie. “什么？”安妮说。

“Thousands of candles in the woods, King Arthur and Queen Guinevere, Merlin, Morgan, Teddy, Kathleen, all that?” said Jack. “森林里成千上万的蜡烛，亚瑟王和桂妮薇尔王后，梅林，摩根，泰迪，凯瑟琳，所有这些？”杰克说。

“I think,” said Annie. “我觉得，”安妮说。

“Amazing,” said Jack. “I wonder what our next mission will be.” “太棒了，”杰克说。 “我想知道我们接下来的任务会是什么。”

“I hope we find out soon,” said Annie. “我希望我们能尽快知道，”安妮说。

“Yep,” said Jack. “Let’s go home.” He pulled on his backpack and started down the rope ladder. Annie followed him. They stepped onto the ground and started through the summer woods. Neither spoke as they walked under the trees, through patches of shadow and light. “对，”杰克说。 “我们回家吧。”他拉上背包，开始顺着绳梯往下爬。 安妮跟着他。 他们踏上地面，穿过夏日的森林。 他们一边走，一边在树下穿行，时而走在阴影里，时而走在阳光下，谁也没有说话。

Finally Jack broke the silence. “I think I’ll buy a soccer goal with my birthday money,” he said. “And set it up in the backyard.” 最后，杰克打破了沉默。 “我想用我的生日钱买一个足球门，”他说。 “然后在后院把它搭起来。”

“Good idea,” said Annie. “And let’s email Jenny and Randy and Quinn and see if they want to practice with us.” “好主意，”安妮说。 “我们还应该给珍妮、兰迪和奎因发电子邮件，问问他们是否想和我们一起练习。”

“Yeah, and Will and Lallie and Griffin,” said Jack. “And I want to pick up some books at the library, too. Check out some exciting ones, with big adventures and interesting information.” “对，还有威尔、拉莉和格里芬，”杰克说。 “我还想在图书馆买几本书。 看看有没有一些有趣的书，有精彩的冒险故事和有趣的知识。”

“Cool,” said Annie. “太酷了，”安妮说。

“Okay, so I guess this is what we’ll do today,” said Jack. “Breakfast, bike to the library and the sports shop, home for lunch, practice soccer, eat dinner, and read before bed.” “好吧，我想这就是我们今天要做的事情，”杰克说。 “吃早饭，骑车去图书馆和体育用品店，回家吃午饭，练习足球，吃晚饭，然后睡前读书。”

“Sounds like a good plan,” said Annie. “听起来是个好主意，”安妮说。

“A perfect one,” said Jack. “完美无缺，”杰克说。

And they quickened their steps as they left the Frog Creek woods and headed for home. 他们加快了脚步，离开了蛙溪镇的森林，朝家的方向走去。
