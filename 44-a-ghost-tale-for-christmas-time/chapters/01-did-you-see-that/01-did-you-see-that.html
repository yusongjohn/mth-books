1. Did You See That? 1. 你看到那东西了吗？

Jack and Annie were walking home from soccer practice. It was four-thirty in the afternoon. Sunlight was fading quickly. 杰克和安妮正从足球练习回家。 下午四点半了。 阳光正在迅速消失。

“November has really short days,” said Jack. “十一月的日子真的非常短，”杰克说。

“Yeah, but it has really beautiful skies,” said Annie. “是啊，但它有非常美丽的天空，”安妮说。

The horizon glowed with the orange light of sunset. Suddenly a streak of light blazed over the Frog Creek woods. 地平线被夕阳的橙色光芒照得闪闪发光。 突然，一道光束划过蛙溪镇的森林。

“Whoa!” said Jack. “Did you see that?” “哇！”杰克说。 “你看到了吗？”

“See what?” said Annie. “看到什么了？”安妮说。

“A streak of light!” said Jack. “Like a shooting star!” “一道光！”杰克说。 “像一颗流星！”

“Over the woods?” asked Annie. “在树林上空？”安妮问。

“Over the woods!” said Jack. He started to run. “在树林上空！”杰克说。 他开始跑起来。

“Run!” said Annie. “跑！”安妮说。

“I’m running!” said Jack. “我正在跑！”杰克说。

Jack and Annie ran down the sidewalk and into the Frog Creek woods. Their feet crunched through fallen leaves as they raced among the shadows. Finally they came to the tallest oak in the woods. The magic tree house sat high in the branches. 杰克和安妮沿着人行道跑进蛙溪镇的树林里。 他们的脚踩着落叶，跑在树影之间。 最后他们来到了树林里最高的橡树下。 神奇的树屋高高地挂在树枝上。

Teddy and Kathleen were looking out of the tree house window. The two young enchanters seemed to glow in the fading daylight. Teddy was grinning and waving. Kathleen’s long dark hair blew in the breeze. 泰迪和凯瑟琳正从树屋的窗户往外看。 这两个年轻的魔法师在渐渐消失的 daylight 中闪闪发光。 泰迪正咧着嘴笑，挥手打招呼。 凯瑟琳长长的黑发在风中飘动。

“Hello!” Teddy called. “你好！”泰迪喊道。

“Hello yourself!” Annie shouted back. “你也一样！”安妮大声回应。

“Come up!” called Kathleen. “上来吧！”凯瑟琳喊道。

Jack and Annie hurried up the rope ladder. They climbed inside the tree house and hugged Teddy and Kathleen. 杰克和安妮赶紧爬上了绳梯。 他们爬进树屋，拥抱了泰迪和凯瑟琳。

“We haven’t seen you guys in so long!” said Jack. “What have you been doing?” “我们好久没见到你们了！”杰克说道。 “你们最近在干什么？”

“Oh, learning more magic—and practicing it,” said Teddy. “Turning frogs into boys.” “哦，学习更多魔法——还有练习它，”泰迪说。 “把青蛙变成男孩。”

“And boys into frogs,” said Kathleen, smiling at Teddy. “还有把男孩变成青蛙，”凯瑟琳微笑着对泰迪说。

“Yes, I rather enjoyed being a frog for a day,” said Teddy. “是的，我那天还挺喜欢当青蛙的，”泰迪说。

Jack and Annie laughed. 杰克和安妮笑了。

“We missed you!” said Annie. “我们想你了！”安妮说。

“We missed you, too,” said Kathleen. “We were both delighted when Merlin told us it was time to send you on a new mission.” “我们也想你，”凯瑟琳说。 “当梅林告诉我们该送你们去执行新的任务时，我们俩都非常高兴。”

“Does he want us to help another great artist give their gifts to the world?” asked Jack. “他想让我们帮助另一位伟大的艺术家把他们的才华献给世界吗？”杰克问。

“Yes,” said Teddy. “So far you have helped Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, Louis Armstrong, and Lady Augusta Gregory. Now your mission is to travel to Victorian England and help Charles Dickens.” “是的，”泰迪说。 “到目前为止，你已经帮助了沃尔夫冈·阿玛多伊斯·莫扎特、路易斯·阿姆斯特朗和奥古斯塔·格雷戈里夫人。 现在你的任务是去维多利亚时代的英格兰，帮助查尔斯·狄更斯。

“His name sounds familiar,” said Jack. “But I can’t remember why.” “他的名字听起来很耳熟，”杰克说。 “但我就是想不起为什么。”

“Me neither,” said Annie. “我也是，”安妮说。

“I am sure you will know a great deal about him by the end of your mission,” said Kathleen. “In the meantime, here is something to help you.” She reached into the folds of her robe and pulled out a book. “我确信你在完成任务之前，会对他了解很多，”凯瑟琳说。 “与此同时，这里有一样东西能帮到你们。”她伸手到长袍的褶皱里，拿出了一本书。

图中文字：Victorian London 图中文字：维多利亚时代的伦敦

The cover showed two girls wearing long skirts and big bonnets. The girls were walking along a path lined with trees. Behind the trees were buildings with towers and tall chimneys. 书的封面显示着两个女孩，穿着长裙，戴着大帽子。 女孩们沿着一条两旁种满树的小路走着。 树的后面是带有塔楼和高烟囱的建筑物。

“London?” said Jack. “That’s the city in England where we met William Shakespeare!” “伦敦？”杰克说。 “那是我们在英国遇到威廉·莎士比亚的城市！”

“Yes, but Charles Dickens lived over two hundred years after Shakespeare’s time,” said Kathleen. “He lived in the 1800s, during Victorian times.” “是的，但查尔斯·狄更斯生活在莎士比亚时代之后两百多年，”凯瑟琳说。 “他生活在19世纪，维多利亚时代。”

“What’s that mean? ‘Victorian times’?” asked Annie. “那是什么意思？ “维多利亚时代？”安妮问。

“It means the years during which a queen named Victoria ruled the British Empire,” said Teddy. “这意味着维多利亚女王统治大英帝国的那个时期，”泰迪说。

“Cool, a queen,” said Annie. “But can I ask you something? This cover shows girls in long hoop-skirts like I wore when we went to the time of Mozart. It was really hard to run and do stuff in those clothes. Can I please wear something else?” “真酷，是个女王，”安妮说。 “不过，我可以问你一个问题吗？ 这封面显示的是穿着长长的圆裙的女孩子，就像我们去莫扎特时代时我穿的那样。 穿着那些衣服真的很难跑，也很难做事。 我可不可以换一身别的衣服？”

Teddy laughed. “Yes, I think we can arrange for you to wear more comfortable clothing,” he said. “But you may have to pretend to be a boy.” 泰迪笑了。 “是的，我想我们可以安排你穿得更舒服一点，”他说。 “但你可能得假装自己是个男孩。”

“That’s okay,” said Annie. “没关系，”安妮说。

“So do we have a magic instrument for this mission?” asked Jack. “我们这次任务有魔法乐器吗？”杰克问。

“Indeed,” said Kathleen. “We gave you a magic flute to help Mozart, a magic trumpet to help Louis Armstrong, and a magic Irish whistle to help Lady Augusta Gregory. Do you still have the whistle?” “确实有，”凯瑟琳说。 “我们给了你一支魔法笛子帮助莫扎特，一支魔法号角帮助路易斯·阿姆斯特朗，还给了你一个魔法爱尔兰口哨帮助奥古斯塔·格雷戈里女士。 你们还留着那个口哨吗？

“Sure, we left it here,” said Annie. She grabbed the whistle from the corner of the tree house and gave it to Kathleen. “当然，我们把它放在这里了，”安妮说。 她从树屋角落拿起口哨，递给凯瑟琳。

“Thank you,” said Kathleen. She tossed the whistle into the air. It spun around and around. There was a flash of blue. The whistle was gone. Floating in its place were a small violin and bow. Kathleen reached up and took them from the air. “谢谢，”凯瑟琳说。 她把口哨抛向空中。 口哨在空中转了个又一个圈。 突然闪过一道蓝光。 哨子不见了。 浮在它原来的位置上的是一个小提琴和琴弓。 凯瑟琳伸手向上，从空中接住了它们。

“Here is a magic violin to help you on your journey,” she said. “这是一把魔法小提琴，能帮助你们完成旅程，”她说。

“I really like violin music,” said Jack. “我真的很喜欢小提琴音乐，”杰克说。

“Good,” said Kathleen. She handed the violin and bow to Jack. “Then on this mission, perhaps you should play while Annie makes up a song.” “很好，”凯瑟琳说。 她把小提琴和琴弓交给了杰克。 “那么在这次任务中，也许你应该在安妮创作歌曲时演奏一下。”

“No problem,” said Annie. “And whatever I sing will come true, right?” “没问题，”安妮说。 “我唱的任何歌都会成真，对吧？”

“Exactly,” said Teddy. “没错，”泰迪说。

“Any more questions?” asked Kathleen. “还有问题吗？”凯瑟琳问。

“Nope,” said Annie. “没有，”安妮说。

Jack was sure he had more questions. But before he had time to think of any, Annie pointed to the cover of the book. 杰克确信自己还有更多问题。 但还没等他想出问题，安妮就指着书的封面。

“I wish we could go there!” she said. “我希望我们能去那里！”她说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
