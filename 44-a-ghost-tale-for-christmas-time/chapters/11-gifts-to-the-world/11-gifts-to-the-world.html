11. Gifts to the World 11. 送给世界的礼物

No time at all had passed in Frog Creek. The sky was lit by the orange afterglow of the sunset. Jack and Annie were wearing their jeans and jackets and sneakers again. The green velvet bag had changed back into Jack’s backpack. 在蛙溪镇，一点时间都没有过去。 天空被夕阳的橙色余晖照亮了。 杰克和安妮又穿上了他们的牛仔裤、夹克和运动鞋。 那条绿色的天鹅绒袋子又变回了杰克的背包。

“Jack! Annie!” two voices called from the woods below. “杰克！ “安妮！”下面树林里传来两个声音喊道。

Jack and Annie looked out the window. Teddy and Kathleen stood in the shadows beneath them! 杰克和安妮看向窗外。 泰迪和凯瑟琳站在他们下方的阴影里！

“Hi! Hi!” Annie and Jack called. They quickly climbed down the rope ladder and hopped to the ground. “你好！ 嗨！”安妮和杰克喊道。 他们迅速爬下绳梯，跳到了地上。

“We’re glad to see you!” said Annie. “Why are you here?” “我们很高兴见到你！”安妮说道。 “你们为什么来这里？”

Before Teddy or Kathleen could answer, the leaves rustled, and out from the dark trees stepped Morgan le Fay and Merlin the magician. 在泰迪或凯瑟琳能回答之前，树叶沙沙作响，接着从黑暗的树丛中走出了摩根·勒·法伊和梅林这位魔术师。

“Morgan! Merlin!” said Annie. “摩根！” “梅林！”安妮说道。

Peep. 啾啾。

“Penny!” said Jack. “佩妮！”杰克说道。

Waddling behind Merlin was the baby penguin Jack and Annie had given to Merlin after their trip to Antarctica. 在梅林后面蹒跚而行的是杰克和安妮在去南极洲旅行后送给梅林的小企鹅。

Peep. 咕噜。

Kathleen picked up Penny and held the little penguin in her arms. 凯瑟琳抱起彭妮，把小企鹅抱在怀里。

Peep. Peep. 咕噜。咕噜。

Jack and Annie laughed. “How are you, Penny?” asked Jack. 杰克和安妮笑了。 “你好吗，彭妮？”杰克问。

“She is wonderful,” said Morgan. “She is dearly loved in Camelot. I would say Penny has become the very heart of our kingdom.” “她非常棒，”摩根说。 “她在卡梅洛特深受爱戴。 我觉得宾妮已经成为了我们王国的心脏。

“She hasn’t gotten any bigger,” said Jack. “她并没有变大，”杰克说。

Merlin smiled. “Time passes very slowly in Camelot,” he said. 梅林笑了。 “在卡梅洛特，时间过得非常慢，”他说。

“Indeed,” said Morgan. “Camelot has hardly aged at all since we last saw you.” “确实如此，”摩根说。 “卡梅洛特自上次见到你们以来几乎一点都没变。”

“But you have accomplished much in that time,” said Merlin. “You have completed your missions to help four artists give their gifts to the world.” “但你们在这段时间里完成了很多事情，”梅林说。 “你们已经完成了帮助四位艺术家将他们的礼物献给世界的任务。”

“And from all we hear,” said Morgan, “you were as successful with Charles Dickens as you were with Lady Augusta Gregory, Louis Armstrong, and Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart.” “据我们所知，”摩根说，“你和查尔斯·狄更斯相处得和与奥古斯塔·格雷戈里夫人、路易斯·阿姆斯特朗以及沃尔夫冈·阿玛多伊斯·莫扎特相处得一样成功。”

“I guess we were,” Jack said modestly. “我想是吧，”杰克谦虚地说。

“I loved Charles,” said Annie. “I loved all of them. I felt like we became good friends with them.” “我超喜欢查尔斯，”安妮说。 “我超喜欢他们所有人。 我觉得我们和他们成为了好朋友。”

“Yeah, and I’m really sad that I won’t see them again,” said Jack. “是啊，我很伤心以后再也见不到他们了，”杰克说。

“We thought you might feel that way,” said Kathleen. “我们认为你可能会有这种感觉，”凯瑟琳说。

“Merlin and Morgan have something to show you,” said Teddy. “梅林和摩根有东西要给你们看，”泰迪说。

“But first, may we have the magic violin and bow back?” asked Kathleen. “但首先，我们可以把魔法小提琴和琴弓还给我们吗？”凯瑟琳问。

“Oh, sure,” said Jack. He reached into his backpack and pulled out the violin and bow. “哦，当然可以，”杰克说。 他从背包里拿出小提琴和琴弓。

Teddy took the violin from him, and Kathleen took the bow. “On your last four missions, you played a magic violin, a magic Irish whistle, a magic trumpet, and a magic flute,” said Kathleen. 泰迪从小提琴手里拿过小提琴，凯瑟琳拿走了琴弓。 “在你上次的四次任务中，你用过一把魔法小提琴、一个魔法爱尔兰口哨、一个魔法小号和一个魔法长笛，”凯瑟琳说。

Jack and Annie nodded. 杰克和安妮点了点头。

“Do you remember where the magic came from?” said Kathleen. She tossed the violin bow into the air. It was still for a moment. Then it began to twirl around and around. There was a flash of blue light. The violin and bow disappeared. Floating in the air was an object shaped like the spiraled horn of a unicorn. “你还记得魔法是从哪里来的吗？”凯瑟琳说。 她把小提琴的琴弓抛向空中。 它停在空中一会儿。 然后开始一圈又一圈地旋转。 一道蓝色的闪光出现了。 小提琴和琴弓消失了。 空中漂浮着一个物体，形状像独角兽螺旋状的角。

“The Wand of Dianthus!” Jack and Annie said together. “迪安瑟斯的魔杖！”杰克和安妮异口同声地说。

“Yes,” said Kathleen. She plucked the wand from the air and handed it to Merlin. “是的，”凯瑟琳说。 她从空中摘下魔杖，递给梅林。

Merlin closed his eyes. He waved the Wand of Dianthus in a circle and whispered words Jack couldn’t understand. 梅林闭上眼睛。 他挥舞着迪安瑟斯魔杖画了个圈，嘴里念着杰克听不懂的话。

There was a whoosh of wind, and they were all standing at the corner of Jack and Annie’s street. Some people were walking by in the dusk. Jack looked worriedly at Merlin, Morgan, Teddy, Kathleen, and Penny. What would people say when they saw them? 一阵风吹过，他们全都站在杰克和安妮家的街角。 天色渐暗，有些人正从旁边走过。 杰克担心地看着梅林、摩根、泰迪、凯瑟琳和彭妮。 当人们看到他们时，会说什么呢？

“Do not worry,” Morgan said, as if she could read Jack’s mind. “They see and hear only you and Annie.” “别担心，”摩根说，好像她能读出杰克的心思。 “他们只能看到你和安妮，也能听到你们说话。”

“Listen carefully,” said Merlin. “仔细听，”梅林说。

Jack listened. Beautiful music was coming from a house on the corner. 杰克听了。 美妙的音乐正从街角的一栋房子中传来。

“A string quartet is rehearsing for a Mozart concert at a church this Saturday,” said Merlin. “一支弦乐四重奏乐队正在为周六在教堂举行的莫扎特音乐会排练，”梅林说。

“Oh, wow,” said Annie. “哦，哇，”安妮说。

Merlin waved the wand in a circle and whispered more magic words. 梅林挥舞着魔杖画了个圈，又低声念出更多的魔法咒语。

Whoosh! They were all standing outside a window of a large brick building. Inside, kids were playing trumpets, saxophones, and drums. “Hey, it’s the band room at the middle school,” Jack said. 呼——！ 他们全都站在一座大砖楼的窗户外面。 里面，孩子们正在吹小号、萨克斯和打鼓。 “嘿，这是中学的乐队室，”杰克说。

“Yes, the band is rehearsing a Louis Armstrong song for the jazz festival next week,” said Morgan. “是的，乐队正在为下周的爵士音乐节排练一首路易斯·阿姆斯特朗的歌曲，”摩根说。

Again Merlin waved the wand in a circle and whispered magic words. Whoosh! They all stood at a window of a white wooden building. “It’s the Frog Creek library!” said Annie. Inside the library, a woman sat in an armchair, talking and waving her hands. Children sat at her feet, listening. 梅林再次挥舞魔杖画了个圈，轻声念出魔法咒语。呼——！ 他们全都站在一座白色木制建筑的窗户前。 “这是蛙溪镇的图书馆！”，安妮说。 在图书馆里，一位女士坐在扶手椅上，一边说话一边挥手。 孩子们坐在她的脚下，认真地听着。

“A storyteller is telling Irish folktales,” said Kathleen. “Stories collected by Augusta Gregory.” “有一位讲故事的人正在讲爱尔兰民间故事，”凯瑟琳说。 “这些故事是由奥古斯塔·格雷戈里收集的。”

Merlin waved the wand in a circle and whispered magic words again. Whoosh! They were all standing at the back of a dark auditorium. Actors were rehearsing on the stage. 梅林挥舞着魔杖画了个圈，又小声念了魔法咒语。呼——！ 他们全都站在一个黑暗礼堂的后面。 演员们正在舞台上排练。

“Back by popular demand,” Teddy whispered, “the Frog Creek Little Theater presents Charles Dickens’s A Christmas Carol.” “应观众强烈要求，”泰迪小声说，“蛙溪镇小剧院再次上演查尔斯·狄更斯的《圣诞奇遇》。”

“Yay! We can go see it again,” whispered Annie. “太棒了！ 我们又能再看一遍了，”安妮小声说。

“Yes. And that is how you will visit with Charles Dickens again,” said Merlin. “是的。 这就是你们再次与查尔斯·狄更斯见面的方式，”梅林说。

“Charles Dickens, Lady Augusta Gregory, Louis Armstrong, Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, and all other great artists live on through their work,” said Morgan. “查尔斯·狄更斯、奥古斯塔·格雷戈里女士、路易斯·阿姆斯特朗、沃尔夫冈·阿玛多伊斯·莫扎特，以及所有其他伟大的艺术家，都会通过他们的作品继续活在世上，”摩根说。

“You put your four friends on the path to giving their gifts to the world,” said Kathleen. “你们把四位朋友送上了向世界传递礼物的道路上，”凯瑟琳说。

“And the world still receives their gifts,” said Teddy. “而且世界仍然接受他们的礼物，”泰迪说道。

“You accomplished your mission,” Merlin said to Jack and Annie. “Thank you for helping bring happiness to millions.” “你完成了你的任务，”梅林对杰克和安妮说道。 “感谢你们帮助带来了数百万人的快乐。”

“You’re welcome,” said Jack. “不用谢，”杰克说道。

“No problem,” said Annie. “没问题，”安妮说道。

Jack turned back to watch the rehearsal of A Christmas Carol. 杰克转身回去观看《圣诞奇缘》的彩排。

The Ghost of Christmas Future had just left the stage, and Scrooge was alone in his bedroom. The actor playing Scrooge started hopping about, laughing and crying in the same breath. “I am as light as a feather!” he shouted. “I am as happy as an angel!” 圣诞未来之灵刚刚离开舞台，史克鲁奇独自一人在卧室里。 扮演史克鲁奇的演员开始蹦跳起来，一边笑一边哭。 “我轻得像一根羽毛！”他喊道。 “我快乐得像天使！”

Jack and Annie laughed at Scrooge’s wild joy. Jack turned around to laugh with the others. But they were gone. Merlin, Morgan, Teddy, Kathleen, and Penny had all vanished. 杰克和安妮为斯克鲁奇狂野的快乐笑了起来。 杰克转身想和其他人一起笑。 但他们已经不见了。 梅林、摩根、泰迪、凯瑟琳和彭妮全都消失了。

“Where …?” Jack said. “……去哪儿了？”杰克说。

“They must’ve gone home to Camelot,” said Annie. “But I’ll bet we’ll see them again soon.” “他们一定回卡梅洛特家去了，”安妮说。 “不过我敢打赌，我们很快又会见到他们。”

Jack nodded. “We should go home now, too,” he said. 杰克点了点头。 “我们也该回家了，”他说。

As Scrooge was shouting “Merry Christmas!” to the world, Jack and Annie slipped out of the Frog Creek Little Theater. Then they ran through the cool autumn evening, heading for home. 当斯克鲁奇向全世界喊出“圣诞快乐！”时，杰克和安妮悄悄离开了蛙溪镇小剧院。 然后他们跑过凉爽的秋夜，朝家跑去。
