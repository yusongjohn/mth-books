10. Good Night, Magicians! 10. 晚安，魔术师们！

I t was much easier walking down 1,652 steps than walking up. Jack and Annie walked down and down and down and down and down and down and down and down and down and down and down—until finally they stepped onto the ground. 走下1,652级台阶比走上去容易多了。 杰克和安妮一直往下，一直往下，一直往下，一直往下，一直往下，一直往下，一直往下，一直往下，一直往下，一直往下，一直往下——直到最后他们终于踏上了地面。

Jack noticed that the two-seater bicycle was gone. “I guess those two people came and got their bike,” he said. 杰克注意到那辆双人自行车不见了。 “我想那两个人已经来把他们的自行车拿走了，”他说。

Jack and Annie looked around. Exhibits were covered and gates were locked. All the motion and noise of the World’s Fair had ended for the day. The living encyclopedia had gone to sleep. Suddenly Jack felt very exhausted. 杰克和安妮环顾四周。 展品都被盖上了，大门也被锁上了。 世界博览会的所有热闹和声音都结束了。 那本活百科全书已经睡着了。 突然，杰克感到非常疲惫。

“Home?” said Annie. “家？”安妮说。

Jack nodded. “Frog Creek,” he said, sighing. 杰克点了点头。 “蛙溪镇，”他说，叹了口气。

Jack and Annie hurried over the bridge and across the avenue. “Those guys were really nice,” said Jack as they walked through the dark, rose-scented park. 杰克和安妮赶紧走过桥，穿过大道。 “那些人真的很友好，”杰克边走边说，他们穿过黑暗的、充满玫瑰香味的公园。

“I know,” said Annie. “They acted like regular people. But they’ve done all those amazing things.” “我知道，”安妮说。 “他们表现得就像普通人一样。 但他们却完成了所有那些了不起的事情。”

“Yeah,” said Jack. “They’re like magicians in disguise.” “是啊，”杰克说。 “他们就像是穿着隐身衣的魔术师。”

Jack and Annie came to the magic tree house. They climbed up the rope ladder and looked one last time out the window. The Eiffel Tower seemed to stand watch over Paris, its spotlights sweeping over the city. 杰克和安妮来到神奇的树屋前。 他们爬上了绳梯，最后一次从窗户往外看。 埃菲尔铁塔似乎在守护着巴黎，它的探照灯扫过整座城市。

Jack pulled Merlin’s letter out of his satchel. He opened it and pointed to the words Frog Creek. “ I wish we could go—” 杰克从他的帆布包里拿出梅林的信。 他打开信，指着上面写着‘蛙溪镇’的字。 “我希望我们能去——”

Before Jack could finish making the wish, he and Annie were bathed in brilliant white light. Jack looked up. One of the beams of the tower’s spotlights had come to rest on the tree house. It shined on them for a long moment. 杰克还没说完愿望，他和安妮就被耀眼的白光包围了。杰克抬头看去。 塔上的探照灯其中一束正好照在树屋上。 它照在他们身上好一会儿。

With both hands, Annie waved wildly into the blinding light. Jack waved, too. 安妮用双手朝刺眼的灯光疯狂挥手。杰克也跟着挥手。

“Good night, magicians!” Annie shouted. “晚安，魔术师们！”安妮喊道。

Jack laughed. Then he pointed at Merlin’s letter again and finished his wish: “… home to Frog Creek,” he said. 杰克笑了。 然后他又指着梅林的信，完成了他的愿望：“……回到蛙溪镇，”他说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。

Jack opened his eyes. He and Annie were dressed in their regular clothes again. Dusky light filtered into the tree house. No time at all had passed in Frog Creek. 杰克睁开了眼睛。 他和安妮又穿上了平常的衣服。 昏暗的光线透进树屋。 蛙溪镇一点时间都没有过去。

“That was a great trip,” Jack said softly. “那是一次很棒的旅行，”杰克轻声说道。

“Really great,” said Annie. “真的很棒，”安妮说。

Jack pulled the guide book to the 1889 Paris World’s Fair out of his backpack. He left it on the tree house floor along with Merlin’s letter. But he kept Teddy and Kathleen’s book of magic rhymes. 杰克从背包里拿出一本关于1889年巴黎世界博览会的指南书。 他把它和梅林的信一起放在树屋的地板上。 但他留住了泰迪和凯瑟琳的魔法韵文书。

“So. We have three rhymes left for our fourth adventure,” he said. “所以， 我们还剩下三句韵文，用于我们的第四次冒险，”他说。

“Quack, quack,” said Annie. “嘎嘎，”安妮说。

“Very funny,” said Jack. “Ready?” “真好笑，”杰克说。 “准备好了吗？

“Yep,” said Annie. She climbed down the rope ladder, and Jack followed. “准备好了，”安妮说。 她顺着绳梯爬下来，杰克跟在后面。

As they started walking through the darkening woods, the world felt familiar and ordinary again. “I can’t believe we just met all those guys,” said Jack. “I can’t believe I actually shook hands with Thomas Edison.” 当他们开始穿过逐渐变暗的树林时，世界又变得熟悉而普通了。 “我简直不敢相信我们刚刚遇见了那些人，”杰克说。 “我简直不敢相信我真的和托马斯·爱迪生握手了。

“You mean with Alva,” said Annie. “你是说和阿尔瓦，”安妮说。

“Yeah. Alva…. Wow,” Jack said softly. “对，阿尔瓦…… 哇，”杰克轻声说。

“What do you think Merlin meant when he said that we had lived all their secrets, as well as learned them?” said Annie. “你认为梅林说我们不仅学会了他们的秘密，还活过了他们的秘密是什么意思？”安妮说道。

“Well, think about it,” said Jack. “We wouldn’t have gone on our mission in the first place if we didn’t have a love for adventure and responsibility—like Mr. Eiffel.” “嗯，想一想吧，”杰克说道。 “如果我们没有对冒险和责任的热爱，就像埃菲尔先生那样，我们根本就不会开始这次任务。”

“Right,” said Annie. “And we sure put a lot of sweat into our mission—when we climbed the stairs.” “对，”安妮说。 “而且我们这次任务真的付出了很多努力——比如我们爬楼梯的时候。”

“And we didn’t lose hope when the door of the institute was locked,” said Jack. “We stuck around until another door opened.” “门被锁住了，但我们没有失去希望，”杰克说。 “我们一直等到另一扇门打开，”

“And you prepared us by reading from the research book,” said Annie, “so chance favored us when we heard someone call Thomas Edison ‘the Wizard of Menlo Park.’” “你还记得吗？你读了那本研究书，给我们做了准备，”安妮说，“所以当我们听到有人叫托马斯·爱迪生‘门洛帕克的魔术师’时，机会就站在我们这边了。”

“And chance favored us when those two people lent us their bike,” said Jack. “还有那次，那两个人借给我们自行车，机会也站在我们这边了，”杰克说。

“Actually, I don’t think that was chance,” Annie said. “其实，我觉得那不是机会，”安妮说。

“What do you mean?” said Jack. “你是什么意思？”杰克说。

“Did you notice that man looked more like a kid in disguise?” said Annie. “His beard and mustache looked kind of fakey.” “你注意到那个男人看起来更像是个戴了假面具的孩子吗？”安妮说。 “他的胡子和小胡子看起来有点假。”

“I did notice that!” said Jack. “But there was so much going on, I didn’t have time to think about it.” “我注意到了！”杰克说。 “但是当时事情太多，我没时间去想。”

“And the woman talked in that funny, squeaky voice, and the veil of her hat covered her face,” said Annie. “And the guy told us to spin like a whirlwind. That was a weird thing to say, but it reminded us of the rhyme in Teddy and Kathleen’s book.” “而且那个女人用那种奇怪的、尖尖的声音说话，她帽子上的面纱遮住了她的脸，”安妮说。 “那个男人让我们像龙卷风一样转圈。 那句话听起来很奇怪，但它让我们想起了泰迪和凯瑟琳书里的那首童谣。”

Jack nodded slowly. Then he smiled. “You think those two were actually Teddy and Kathleen?” he said. 杰克慢慢点了点头。然后他笑了。 “你觉得那两个人真的是泰迪和凯瑟琳吗？”他说。

“Maybe,” said Annie. “On our last three missions, I felt like they were with us, helping us get to the right place at the right time.” “也许吧，”安妮说。 “在我们上次的三次任务中，我感觉他们一直在我们身边，帮助我们在正确的时间到达正确的地方。”

“Next time, maybe we can catch them when they help us,” said Jack. “下次，也许我们能抓住他们，让他们帮助我们，”杰克说。

Annie laughed. “Yeah, we’ll try to surprise them for a change!” 安妮笑了。 “对，这次我们试着去吓他们一跳吧！”

“Good plan,” said Jack. “好主意，”杰克说。

A bell jingled in the distance. 远处传来一阵铃声。

“Ice cream!” said Annie. “冰淇淋！”，安妮说。

“Yep, that’s our mission now!” said Jack. “没错，这就是我们现在要完成的任务！”，杰克说。

The ice cream bell jingled again. Jack and Annie ran out of the woods into the soft summer twilight. 冰淇淋的铃声再次响起。 杰克和安妮从树林里跑出来，进入了温暖的夏日黄昏。
