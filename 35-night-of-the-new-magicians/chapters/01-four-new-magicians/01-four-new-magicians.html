1. Four New Magicians 1. 四位新魔法师

J ack sat on the porch, reading in the summer twilight. Crickets chirped in the Frog Creek woods. The bell of an ice cream truck jingled down the street. 蟋蟀在蛙溪镇的树林里鸣叫。 冰淇淋车的铃铛沿着街道叮叮当当地响着。

Annie stepped out the front door. “Let’s go,” she said. 安妮从门前走了出来。 “我们走吧，”她说。

“Where?” said Jack. “哪儿？”杰克说。

“Mom gave us money for ice cream,” said Annie. “妈妈给了我们钱买冰淇淋，”安妮说。

“Cool,” said Jack. He pulled on his backpack. Then he followed Annie down the porch steps. As they headed up the sidewalk, the smell of damp leaves and moss wafted from the woods. “太棒了，”杰克说。 他拉了拉背包。 然后他跟着安妮走下门廊的台阶。 当他们沿着人行道走时，树林里潮湿的树叶和青苔的气味飘了过来。

Annie stopped walking. “Listen,” she said. 安妮停下了脚步。 “听，”她说。

Jack listened. “What?” he said. “I don’t hear anything.” 杰克听了。 “什么？”他说。 “我什么也没听到。”

“That’s the point,” said Annie. “A minute ago, the crickets were blaring away. Now everything’s super quiet.” “这正是重点，”安妮说。 “一分钟前，蟋蟀们还在大声叫着。 现在却安静得要命。”

Jack listened again. Annie was right. All the Frog Creek woods seemed to be holding its breath. 杰克又听了听。安妮说得对。 整个蛙溪镇的森林似乎都屏住了呼吸。

“Do you think …?” said Jack. “你觉得……？”杰克说。

“Maybe,” said Annie, grinning. “Let’s go check!” “也许吧，”安妮微笑着说。 “我们去看看吧！”

Jack and Annie hurried across the street and into the dimly lit woods. They walked quickly between the leafy trees, until they came to the tallest oak. A rope ladder dangled from the tree-top. The magic tree house sat high in the branches, catching the last light of day. 杰克和安妮赶紧穿过街道，走进昏暗的森林里。 他们快速地穿过了长满叶子的树，直到来到最高的橡树前。 一根绳梯从树顶垂下来。 神奇的树屋高高地坐在树枝上，接住了白天最后的光线。

Jack smiled. “I guess ice cream will have to wait,” he said. 杰克笑了。 “我想冰淇淋只能等一下了，”他说。

“Yep,” said Annie. She grabbed the ladder and climbed up. Jack climbed up after her. “对，”安妮说。 她抓住梯子爬了上去。 杰克也爬了上去。

Inside the tree house, dusky light filtered through the window. Lying on the wooden floor was a folded piece of paper and a slim book with a red cover. 在树屋里，昏暗的光线从窗户透进来。 木制地板上躺着一张折叠的纸和一本红色封面的薄书。

Annie grabbed the paper. Jack picked up the book. “This must be a research book from Morgan,” he said. 安妮拿起了那张纸。 杰克捡起了那本书。 “这一定是摩根的研究书，”他说。

The book’s title was written in gold letters: 书的标题是用金色字母写的：

图中文字：Guide Book: Paris World's Fair

~1889~ 图中文字：指南书：巴黎世界博览会～1889～

“Paris World’s Fair?” said Jack. “巴黎世界博览会？”杰克说。

“That sounds like fun!” said Annie. “听起来很有趣！”安妮说。

“Yeah, but I wonder why we’re going there,” said Jack. “是啊，但我想知道我们为什么要去那里，”杰克说。

“This should tell us,” said Annie. She unfolded the paper. “It’s Merlin’s handwriting.” She read aloud: “这本书应该会告诉我们，”安妮说。 她展开那张纸。 “这是梅林的笔迹。”她大声读道：

To Jack and Annie of Frog Creek:I have discovered that an evil sorcerer isplotting to steal the secrets of four newmagicians at the Paris World’s Fair.
Your mission is to find the magicians, warn them, and learn their secrets for me.The four new magicians are:

The Magician of Sound—his voice can be heard for a thousand miles. 声音魔法师——他的声音可以传到一千里之外。

The Magician of Light—his fires glow, but they do not burn. 光魔法师——他的火光闪闪发亮，但不会燃烧。

The Magician of the Invisible—he battles deadly enemies no one can see. 隐形魔法师——他与看不见的敌人战斗。

The Magician of Iron—he bends the metals of earth and triumphs over the wind. 铁魔法师——他能弯曲大地上的金属，战胜风。

Good luck, M. 祝你好运，M。

“Our mission sounds more like a fairy tale than real life,” said Jack. “An evil sorcerer. Magicians of the Invisible, Light, Sound, and Iron. They sound like they belong in a magical place like Camelot, not a real place like Paris, France.” “我们的任务听起来更像是童话故事，而不是现实生活，”杰克说。 “一个邪恶的巫师。 隐形、光、声音和铁的魔法师。 他们听起来像是属于像卡梅洛特这样的魔法地方，而不是像法国巴黎这样的真实地方。”

“But we’re going to a World’s Fair,” said Annie. “That sounds kind of magical, doesn’t it?” “但我们要去的是世界博览会，”安妮说。 “那听起来有点像魔法，对不对？”

“Maybe,” said Jack. “But why do such powerful magicians need our help in the first place? Why can’t they defeat the evil sorcerer with their own powers?” “也许吧，”杰克说。 “但是为什么这么强大的魔法师一开始就需要我们的帮助呢？ 他们不能用自己的力量打败邪恶的巫师吗？”

“Maybe the sorcerer’s power is stronger than theirs,” said Annie. “也许巫师的力量比他们更强，”安妮说。

“So maybe we can help them with Teddy and Kathleen’s rhymes,” said Jack. “所以也许我们可以用泰迪和凯瑟琳的押韵诗来帮他们，”杰克说。

Annie gasped. “Oh, no! We need the rhyme book! We have to go back home and get it!” 安妮惊呼起来。 “哦，天哪！ 我们需要韵律书！ 我们必须回家去拿它！”

“Don’t worry, I have it,” said Jack. “Ever since we got back from Baghdad, I’ve been taking it with me everywhere I go—-just in case Merlin sends for us.” “别担心，我有它，”杰克说。 “自从我们从巴格达回来后，我就一直把它带在身边——以防梅林召唤我们。”

“Whew,” said Annie. “Let’s take a look.” “哇，”安妮说。 “我们来看看。”

Jack reached into his backpack. He pulled out the small book written by their two young sorcerer friends from Camelot: 杰克伸手进了他的背包。 他拿出了一本小书，那是他们来自卡梅洛特的两位年轻法师朋友泰迪和凯瑟琳写的：

10 MAGIC RHYMES FOR ANNIE AND JACK FROM TEDDY AND KATHLEEN 10 魔法押韵诗送给安妮和杰克——泰迪和凯瑟琳

Jack turned to the table of contents. “Okay, we’ve used five rhymes on our last two missions,” he said. “So we have five left for the next two. We haven’t used Spin into the Air, or Make Something Disappear, or Pull a Cloud from the Sky, or Find a Treasure You Must Never Lose, or Turn into Ducks.” 杰克翻到了目录页。 “好吧，我们在上两次任务中已经用了五个押韵诗，”他说。 “所以我们还有五个留给接下来的两次任务。” 我们还没用过“飞上天空”、“让东西消失”、“从天上拽下一片云”、“找到你永远不能丢失的宝藏”或者“变成鸭子”这些咒语。

“Quack! Quack!” “嘎嘎！嘎嘎！”，

Jack looked up. 杰克抬头看了看。

“Just kidding,” said Annie. “开个玩笑啦，”安妮说。

“You’d better not make jokes about these rhymes,” Jack said. “You might end up saying the wrong one at the wrong time and really get us in trouble.” He closed the rhyme book. “Ready to go?” “你最好别拿这些咒语开玩笑，”杰克说。 “你可能会在错误的时间说错的那句咒语，这样真的会让我们惹上麻烦。”他合上了押韵书。 “准备好了吗？

“Ready,” said Annie. “准备好了，”安妮说。

Jack took a deep breath and picked up the guide book to the 1889 Paris World’s Fair. He pointed to the title. “I wish we could go there,” he said. 杰克深吸了一口气，拿起了一本关于1889年巴黎世界博览会的指南书。 他指着标题。 “我希望我们能去那里，”他说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来了。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
