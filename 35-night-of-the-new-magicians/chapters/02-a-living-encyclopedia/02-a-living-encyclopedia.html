2. A Living Encyclopedia 2. 活的百科全书

Jack opened his eyes. The scent of roses floated through the warm twilight. Jack was wearing an old-fashioned cap, a rust-colored jacket, and knee-length pants. His backpack had turned into a leather satchel. 杰克睁开了眼睛。 玫瑰的香气在温暖的暮色中飘散。 杰克戴着一顶老式的帽子，穿着一件锈红色的夹克和及膝的裤子。 他的背包变成了一只皮质小包。

Annie was wearing a puffy white blouse and a long purple skirt with a ruffle. “Look, there’s the Eiffel Tower,” she said. 安妮穿着一件蓬松的白色衬衫和一条长长的紫色裙子，裙边有褶皱。 “看，那是埃菲尔铁塔，”她说。

Jack looked. They had landed in a park filled with trees. Beyond the park, rising into the sky, was a tall tower with lights beaming from the top. 杰克看了看。 他们降落在一个树木茂密的公园里。 公园的另一边，一座高高的塔楼耸立在天空中，塔顶有灯光闪烁。

“That’s the Eiffel Tower, all right,” said Jack. “But where’s the World’s Fair?” He opened their guide book and found a map. “Oh, great, it looks like the fair is directly below the tower. That should make it easy to find.” “那确实是埃菲尔铁塔，”杰克说。 “但是世界博览会在哪里呢？”他打开他们的指南书，找到了一张地图。 “哦，太好了，看起来博览会就在塔的正下方。 这样应该很容易找到。”

“Let’s get going,” said Annie. “我们出发吧，”安妮说。

“Wait, we need to go over what we have to do on our mission,” said Jack. “等等，我们得先回顾一下这次任务要做什么，”杰克说。

“It’s simple,” said Annie. “We have to find the Magician of Sound, the Magician of Light, the Magician of the Invisible, and the Magician of Iron. We have to warn them about the evil sorcerer and then learn their secrets for Merlin.” “很简单，”安妮说。 “我们要找到声音魔术师、光的魔术师、隐形的魔术师和铁的魔术师。 我们要警告他们那个邪恶的巫师，然后为梅林学到他们的秘密。”

“That doesn’t sound simple to me,” said Jack. “It sounds like a huge responsibility.” “听起来一点都不简单，”杰克说。 “这听起来像是一个巨大的责任。”

“So we’d better start now,” said Annie. “Come on.” Annie led the way down the tree house ladder. “所以我们最好现在就开始，”安妮说。 “来吧。”安妮带头走下树屋的梯子。

Jack put their World’s Fair guide into his satchel, along with their letter from Merlin and the book of rhymes from Teddy and Kathleen. Then he followed Annie down the ladder. 杰克把世界博览会指南、梅林的信，还有泰迪和凯瑟琳写的押韵书一起放进他的帆布袋里。 然后他跟着安妮顺着梯子往下爬。

As Jack and Annie started across the park, a clinking sound came from Annie’s skirt pocket. She reached into the pocket and pulled out a handful of coins. “Hey, our ice cream money got changed into French coins!” she said. 杰克和安妮开始穿过公园时，安妮的裙子口袋里传来叮叮当当的声音。 她把手伸进口袋，掏出一把硬币。 “嘿，我们的冰淇淋钱变成法国硬币了！”，她说。

“Good,” said Jack. “We might need them at the fair.” “很好，”杰克说。 “我们在游乐园可能用得上它们。”

Jack and Annie followed a gravel path that led out of the park and onto an avenue lit by gas streetlamps. Horse-drawn carriages and old-fashioned bicycles clattered over the cobblestones. They all seemed to be headed toward a crowded bridge that crossed a wide river. 杰克和安妮沿着一条碎石小路走，这条路从公园里延伸出去，通向一条被煤气路灯照亮的大道。 马拉的马车和老式的自行车在鹅卵石路上发出咔哒咔哒的声音。 看起来他们都朝着一座拥挤的桥走去，那座桥横跨一条宽阔的河流。

Boats were gliding down the river, their lights reflecting in the water. On the far side of the river, thousands of tiny lamps twinkled along the bank. The Eiffel Tower glowed in the silver twilight. 船在河上滑行，船上的灯光在水面上反射。 在河的对岸，成千上万盏小灯沿着河岸闪烁着微光。 埃菲尔铁塔在银色的暮色中闪闪发光。

“Paris is so beautiful,” said Annie. “巴黎真美，”安妮说。

“No kidding,” said Jack. “Let’s cross that bridge to the fair.” They hurried to join the people streaming across the bridge. “可不是嘛，”杰克说。 “我们过那座桥去参加博览会吧。”他们赶紧跑过去，加入正在过桥的人群中。

Jack and Annie blended in easily with the happy crowd. The kids in the crowd were all wearing clothes like theirs. Most of the men wore black top hats and black coats and pants. The women wore hats as big as flower baskets. Their long, colorful dresses puffed out in back. 杰克和安妮很容易地融入了欢乐的人群中。 人群中孩子们都穿着和他们一样的衣服。 大多数男人都戴着黑色的高礼帽，穿着黑色的外套和裤子。 女人们戴着像花篮一样大的帽子。 她们长长的、色彩鲜艳的裙子后面蓬蓬地鼓起来。

There seemed to be visitors from many countries. Jack saw Chinese straw hats, Dutch caps, several Indian turbans, and a Mexican sombrero. 看起来有很多来自不同国家的游客。 杰克看到了中国草帽、荷兰帽子、几顶印度头巾，还有一顶墨西哥宽边帽。

“This reminds me of the carnival we went to in Venice,” said Annie. “这让我想起我们在威尼斯参加的嘉年华，”安妮说。

“Me too,” said Jack. “Except in Venice, people were wearing costumes. Here, they’re wearing their real clothes. Remember, this is a world’ s fair.” “我也是，”杰克说。 “不过在威尼斯，人们都穿着服装。 这里，他们穿的是真正的衣服。 记住，这是世界博览会。”

“Cool,” said Annie. “太酷了，”安妮说。

Jack looked around. How would they recognize the four new magicians? he wondered. Would they be dressed like people from Paris? Or people from another country? Or would they look like Merlin or Morgan in flowing medieval robes? And what about the evil sorcerer? 杰克环顾四周。 他们怎么才能认出这四个新来的魔术师呢？他想。 他们会穿得像来自巴黎的人吗？ 还是来自其他国家的人？ 还是看起来像穿着中世纪长袍的梅林或摩根？ 那邪恶的巫师又会是什么样子呢？

“Looks like we buy our tickets over there,” said Annie as they reached the end of the bridge. “看起来我们得在那里买票了，”安妮说道，当他们走到桥的尽头时。

Jack and Annie headed for a ticket booth near an entrance gate. Above the gate, a giant banner read: 杰克和安妮朝入口处附近的一个售票亭走去。 在大门上方，一块巨大的横幅上写着：

Welcome to the 1889 Paris World’s Fair 欢迎来到1889年巴黎世界博览会

As they stood in line waiting to buy tickets, Jack pulled out their guide book. “We need to prepare for our mission,” he said. He turned to the first page and read aloud: 当他们在排队买票时，杰克拿出了他们的指南书。 “我们需要为我们的任务做好准备，”他说。 他翻到第一页，大声读起来：

Welcome to the World’s Fair—a living encyclopedia with over 60,000 exhibits from all over the globe! 欢迎来到世界博览会——这是一个活生生的百科全书，有来自世界各地的6万多个展品！

“Maybe some of the exhibits are magic shows,” said Annie. “And that’s where we’ll find the new magicians.” “也许有些展品是魔术表演，”安妮说。 “那就是我们找到新魔术师的地方。”

“Maybe,” said Jack. He kept reading: “也许吧，”杰克说。他继续读下去：

This World’s Fair is a showcase of progress! Discover the genius of man! Learn all about science and technology! See wondrous new machines and inventions! 这次世界博览会展示了进步！发现人类的天才！了解科学和技术！看看奇妙的新机器和发明！

Jack looked up. “Hmm,” he said. “It sounds like this fair is mainly about inventions and scientific stuff. I don’t see anything here about magic or magicians.” 杰克抬起头。 “嗯，”他说。 “听起来这个博览会主要是关于发明和科学的东西。 我这里没看到任何关于魔术或魔术师的内容。”

“How many?” the ticket seller asked gruffly. They had reached the front of the line. “多少张票？”售票员粗声粗气地问。 他们已经到了队伍的最前面。

Annie held out a handful of French coins. “Two, please,” she said. 安妮拿出一把法国硬币。 “两张，请。”她说。

The ticket seller took two coins. Annie put the remaining change back into her pocket. Then she and Jack walked through the gate into the 1889 Paris World’s Fair. 售票员拿了两张硬币。 安妮把剩下的零钱放回口袋里。 然后她和杰克穿过大门，走进了1889年的巴黎世界博览会。
