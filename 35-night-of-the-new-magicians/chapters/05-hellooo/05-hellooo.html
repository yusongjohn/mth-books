5. Hellooo? 5. 喂？

J ack and Annie pressed through the crowd of people swarming about the Hall of Machines. Finally they reached the exit and slipped back out into the warm Paris night. The fair was just as crowded outside the hall as inside. Musicians played guitars, singers sang, food sellers shouted, “Chocolate milk! Cheese! Bread! Wine!” 杰克和安妮穿过人群，人群正围绕着机器大厅忙碌着。 终于他们到达了出口，溜回到温暖的巴黎夜晚中。 博览会外面的人群和里面一样拥挤。 音乐家们弹着吉他，歌手们唱歌，卖食物的人喊着：‘巧克力牛奶！奶酪！’ 面包！葡萄酒！

“We have to get to the Pasteur Institute fast!” Annie shouted to Jack. “我们必须赶快赶到巴斯德研究所！”安妮对杰克喊道。

Jack pulled out their guide book and scanned the index, looking for the Pasteur Institute. “It’s not in here,” he said. He closed the guide book. “It must not be part of the fair.” 杰克拿出他们的指南书，翻看目录，寻找巴斯德研究所。 “这里没有，”他说。 他合上了指南书。 “它不能成为博览会的一部分。”

“Maybe one of those horse-and-buggies can take us there,” said Annie. She pointed to a row of carriages along a street. There was a line of people waiting to get into them. “也许有马车能带我们去那里，”安妮说。 她指着街上一排马车。 有一排人在排队等着上车。

“Come on!” said Jack. “快点！”杰克说。

Annie and Jack made their way through the crowd and stood in the carriage line. 安妮和杰克穿过人群，站到了马车队伍里。

“Thomas Alva Edison and Alexander Graham Bell!” said Jack. “The sorcerer must think they’re new magicians with secret powers!” “托马斯·阿尔瓦·爱迪生和亚历山大·格雷厄姆·贝尔！”杰克说。 “那个巫师一定以为他们是拥有秘密力量的新魔法师！”

“And now he’s invited them to some kind of party,” said Annie, “so he can steal their secrets.” “现在他邀请他们去参加某种聚会，”安妮说，“这样他就能偷走他们的秘密了。”

“I’ll bet he’s inviting the other two,” said Jack, “the Magician of Iron and the Magician of the Invisible.” “我打赌他也会邀请另外两位，”杰克说，“铁之魔术师和隐形魔术师。”

“I wonder if they’re inventors, too,” said Annie. “我想他们可能也是发明家，”安妮说。

“Come on, it’s our turn,” said Jack. “快点，轮到我们了，”杰克说。

They had reached the front of the carriage line. “We need to go to a place called the Pasteur Institute,” Jack called to the coachman. “Can you take us?” 他们已经到达了马车队伍的最前面。 “我们需要去一个叫巴斯德研究所的地方，”杰克对车夫喊道。 “你能带我们去吗？”

“But of course,” said the man. “当然啦，”那人说。

“Thanks!” said Annie. She and Jack climbed into the back of the open carriage. The coachman shook the reins, and his white horse clopped down the cobblestone street. “谢谢！”安妮说。 她和杰克爬进了敞开的马车后部。 车夫抖了抖缰绳，他的白马在鹅卵石街道上踏着蹄声前行。

“Excuse me,” Annie said, leaning forward. “But what exactly is the Pasteur Institute?” “请问，”安妮向前倾身说。 “但是，巴斯德研究所到底是什么地方？”

“It is a laboratory for finding the cures for diseases,” said the coachman. “那是一个寻找疾病治疗方法的实验室，”车夫说。

“Oh …,” said Annie. “Interesting.” She turned back to Jack. “Why would an evil sorcerer look for magicians in a place like that?” “哦……”，安妮说。 “真有趣。”她转身对杰克说。 “为什么一个邪恶的巫师会在那样的地方寻找魔法师呢？”

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “我不知道，”杰克说。

“Maybe the sorcerer got sick,” said Annie. “也许那个巫师生病了，”安妮说。

“I don’t think so,” said Jack. “But now we really need a plan. What if we run into him at the institute? Remember, he has magic powers.” “我不这么认为，”杰克说。 “但现在我们真的需要一个计划。 如果我们在他去研究所的时候遇到他怎么办？ 记住，他有魔法力量。”

“But we have magic powers, too,” said Annie. “但我们也有魔法力量，”安妮说。

“Right,” breathed Jack. He reached into his satchel and pulled out their book: “对，”杰克低声说。 他伸手到他的帆布袋里，拿出他们的书：

10 MAGIC RHYMES FOR ANNIE AND JACK FROM TEDDY AND KATHLEEN 10个魔法韵文，献给安妮和杰克——泰迪和凯瑟琳

By the light of the carriage lantern, Jack and Annie looked at the table of contents. 在马车灯的光线下，杰克和安妮看着目录表。

“Remember, we can only use a rhyme once,” Jack said to Annie. “ Make a Stone Come Alive— we’ve done that. Make Helpers Appear out of Nowhere—done that. Mend What Cannot Be Mended—done that.” “记住，我们只能用一次押韵，”杰克对安妮说。 “让石头活过来——我们做过那件事。 让助手凭空出现——我们也做过那件事。 修补无法修补的东西——我们也做过那件事。”

“But we haven’t used Spin into the Air.” said Annie, “or Make Something Disappear, or Find a Treasure You Must Never Lose, or Pull a Cloud from the Sky, or Turn into Ducks.” “但我们还没用过‘飞上天空’。”安妮说，“也没用过‘让某物消失’，‘找到你永远不能丢失的宝藏’，‘从天空中拉下一朵云’，或者‘变成鸭子’。”

“Go back, go back,” said Jack. “ Make Something Disappear. What about that?” “回去，回去，”杰克说。 “让某物消失。这个怎么样？”

“Is a person a ‘something’?” asked Annie. “一个人算不算‘某物’？”安妮问。

“Why not?” said Jack. “This one rhyme could solve our whole problem. We’ll just make the sorcerer disappear.” “为什么不呢？”杰克说。 “这个押韵的句子也许能解决我们所有的问题。” 我们让巫师消失就行了。”

“Yes,” breathed Annie. “是的，”安妮轻声说。

“Okay, here’s the plan,” said Jack. “Let’s memorize the rhyme now. Then as soon as we see the sorcerer, we can say it without having to look in the book.” “好，这是计划，”杰克说。 “我们现在先记住这首童谣。 然后我们一看到那个巫师，就可以直接说出来，不用再看书了。

“Great,” said Annie. “太好了，”安妮说。

Jack turned to a page in the rhyme book. “I’ll memorize the line that Teddy wrote. You memorize the line in Kathleen’s language,” he said. 杰克翻开了韵律书的一页。 “我会记住泰迪写的那句话。 你记住凯瑟琳那句话的版本，”他说。

“Got it,” said Annie. She looked at the rhyme and started to say her line, “Thee-be-wan—” “明白了，”安妮说。 她看了看咒语，开始念自己的那句，“ thee-be-wan—”

“No, don’t!” yelped Jack, putting his hand over her mouth. “Don’t say it out loud until we need it! You might accidentally make one of us or something really important disappear!” “别念！”，杰克喊道，把手放在她的嘴上。 “别在需要之前大声念出来！ 万一不小心让其中一个人或者某个很重要的东西消失掉！“

“Sorry,” said Annie. “对不起，”安妮说。

“We’ll practice silently,” said Jack. “And we’ll each only learn our own line. So neither of us can say the whole rhyme at the wrong time.” “我们会安静地练习，”杰克说。 “而且我们每个人只学自己的台词。 这样我们都不会在错误的时间把整首诗念出来。”

“Good plan,” said Annie. “好主意，”安妮说。

Annie studied her line silently while Jack studied his. As Jack repeated his line in his head, the carriage rolled down a busy street. The street was filled with more carriages and many bicycles. Some of the bikes were built for two people. Couples dressed in fancy evening clothes pedaled together. 安妮默默地背诵着自己的台词，杰克也在默默地背诵自己的台词。 杰克在心里反复念着自己的台词时，马车沿着一条繁忙的街道缓缓驶下。 这条街上挤满了马车，还有许多自行车。 有些自行车是两个人一起骑的。 穿着华丽晚礼服的情侣们一起骑着自行车。

Other Parisians ate by candlelight in outdoor cafés. Waiters in white aprons carried trays high in the air. Everyone seemed relaxed and cheerful. As the carriage turned onto a quiet tree-lined street, Jack wished that he and Annie could just have fun in Paris like everyone else and not be worrying about an evil sorcerer. 其他巴黎人则在户外咖啡馆里，点着蜡烛吃东西。 穿着白色围裙的服务员高高举着托盘。 每个人看起来都很放松，也很开心。 当马车转进一条安静、两旁种满树的街道时，杰克希望他和安妮也能像其他人一样，在巴黎尽情玩耍，而不用担心那个邪恶的巫师。

“Here we are!” said the driver, interrupting Jack’s thoughts. He brought the carriage to a stop. “The Pasteur Institute.” “到了！”司机说道，打断了杰克的思绪。 他让马车停了下来。 “巴斯特研究所。”

“This is it?” said Jack. The Pasteur Institute looked like a spooky mansion. Its huge front doors were closed. Its tall windows were dark. “这就是吗？”杰克说。 巴斯特研究所看起来像一座阴森的豪宅。 它巨大的正门紧闭着。 它的高窗户都是黑的。

“Are you sure we’ve come to the right place?” Annie asked in a small voice. “你确定我们来对地方了吗？”安妮小声地问。

“But of course I am sure,” said the coachman. “The institute appears to be closed. Would you like me to take you somewhere else?” “当然确定，”马车夫说。 “这所学院看起来是关着的。 你想让我带你去别的地方吗？”

“No thanks,” said Jack. “We’ll get out here.” “不用谢，”杰克说。 “我们会从这儿出去的。”

Annie gave the coachman a few coins. Then she and Jack climbed out of the carriage. 安妮给了马车夫几枚硬币。 然后她和杰克爬出马车。

“Thanks,” said Annie. “谢谢，”安妮说。

The coachman flicked his reins, and the white horse trotted away down the street. 马车夫抽了抽缰绳，白色马匹沿着街道小跑着离开了。

Jack and Annie stared at the dark, silent building. 杰克和安妮盯着那栋黑暗、寂静的建筑。

“I guess we should go up and knock,” said Annie. She and Jack climbed the stone steps to the gigantic front doors. “我想我们应该上去敲门，”安妮说。 她和杰克爬上石阶，来到巨大的正门前。

“We’ve come to the right place,” said Jack. A gas lamp lit a small metal sign that said: “我们到地方了，”杰克说。 一盏煤气灯照亮了一个小金属牌子，牌子上写着：

Louis Pasteur Institute 路易·巴斯德研究所

Jack knocked on the door three times. 杰克敲了三次门。

No one answered. 没有人回答。

Annie turned the huge handle and pushed. The door was locked. 安妮转动着巨大的门把手，然后推了推。 门是锁着的。

“Maybe there’s another door somewhere,” said Annie. “也许还有另一扇门在别的地方，”安妮说。

Jack and Annie walked around the institute. They knocked at a back door and a side door, but no one answered. 杰克和安妮绕着研究所走了一圈。 他们敲了后门和侧门，但没有人回答。

When they got back to the front of the building, Jack heaved a sigh. “It’s no use,” he said. “We’ve come to a complete dead end.” 当他们回到大楼前面时，杰克叹了口气。 “没用的，”他说。 “我们来到死胡同了。”

“We can’t give up,” said Annie. “我们不能放弃，”安妮说。

“I know,” said Jack. They both stood looking at the street. All was quiet, except for a few bikes rattling by. “我知道，”杰克说。 他们俩站在那里，看着街道。 一切都很安静，除了几辆自行车叮叮当当地经过。

Suddenly a whispery voice came from behind them. “ Hellooo?” 突然，一个沙哑的声音从他们身后传来。 “你好吗？”
