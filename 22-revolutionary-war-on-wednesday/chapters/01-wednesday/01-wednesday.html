1. Wednesday! 1. 星期三！

“Wake up, Jack!” Annie whispered. “醒醒，杰克！”安妮小声说。

Jack opened his eyes. He looked at his clock. It was six A.M. 杰克睁开了眼睛。 他看了看时钟。早上六点。

“Come on!” Annie whispered. She was standing at his bedroom door, already dressed. “快点！”安妮小声说。 她已经站在他卧室门口，衣服也穿好了。

“Now?” said Jack. “现在？”杰克说。

“Yeah, it’s Wednesday! We have to go to the tree house!” she said. “对，今天是星期三！ 我们得去树屋！”她说。

“Oh, man, Wednesday!” said Jack. Suddenly, he was wide awake. “哦，天哪，星期三！”杰克说。 他一下子完全清醒了。

“We have to help save Camelot,” said Annie. “我们得去帮助拯救卡米洛特，”安妮说。

“I know, I know,” said Jack, scrambling out of bed. “我知道，我知道，”杰克说着，从床上爬了起来。

“Meet you out front,” said Annie. “门口见，”安妮说。

Jack quickly changed into his jeans and T-shirt. He threw his notebook and pencil into his backpack. 杰克很快换上牛仔裤和T恤。 他把笔记本和铅笔塞进背包。

Then he slipped down the stairs and out the front door. 然后他悄悄下楼，走出前门。

Annie was waiting in the gray early light. 安妮正在灰蒙蒙的晨光里等着。

“All set?” she whispered. “都准备好了？”她小声问。

“Yep,” said Jack. “嗯，”杰克说。

They took off running across their yard. They ran down their quiet street and into the Frog Creek woods. 他们拔腿跑过院子。 他们沿着安静的街道跑进了蛙溪树林。

Soon they came to the tree house. They climbed up the rope ladder. 很快他们来到了树屋。 他们爬上了绳梯。

In the early light, they saw Morgan’s note, the same note they’d found on Sunday. Jack picked it up and read aloud:

 在晨光中，他们看见了摩根的纸条，就是星期天找到的那张。 杰克把它捡起来，大声读道：

Dear Jack and Annie,

 亲爱的杰克和安妮：

Camelot is in trouble. To save the kingdom, please find these four special kinds of writing for my library:

 卡米洛特有难了。 为了拯救王国，请为我的图书馆找到这四种特别的文字：

Something to follow 用来遵循的东西

Something to send 用来送出的东西

Something to learn 用来学习的东西

Something to lend

 用来出借的东西

Thank you, 谢谢。

        Morgan

Annie picked up a piece of paper lying on the floor. It was a list of rules from the famous nurse Clara Barton. They had met her on their journey to the Civil War. 安妮捡起地板上的一张纸。 那是著名护士克拉拉·巴顿的一份规则清单。 他们在南北战争之旅中遇见过她。

“On Sunday we found the first special writing,” said Annie, “something to follow.” “星期天我们找到了第一种特别的文字，”安妮说，“用来遵循的东西。”

“Yeah,” said Jack, “and now we need to find the second thing, something to send.” “对，”杰克说，“现在我们得去找第二种，用来送出的东西。”

He picked up a book lying near the note. The cover showed soldiers on a snowy riverbank. 他捡起纸条旁边的一本书。 封面上是雪地河岸上的士兵。

The title was The Revolutionary War. 书名是《独立战争》。

Jack frowned. 杰克皱起了眉头。

“Uh-oh,” said Annie. “哎呀，”安妮说。

“Another war,” said Jack, sighing. “又是一场战争，”杰克叹了口气说。

“You still want to go?” said Annie. “你还想去吗？”安妮说。

“We have to,” said Jack. He hated the suffering he’d seen in the Civil War. But they had no choice. They had to help Morgan and save Camelot. “我们得去，”杰克说。 他讨厌自己在南北战争里见过的那些苦难。 可他们别无选择。 他们必须帮助摩根，拯救卡米洛特。

He pointed at the cover of the book. 他指着书的封面。

“I wish we could go there,” Jack said. “我希望我们能去那里，”杰克说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
