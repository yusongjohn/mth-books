5. The Letter 5. 信

“George Washington? Really?” said Annie. “乔治·华盛顿？ 真的？”安妮说。

“Yeah, I think he is!” said Jack. “对，我想是他！”杰克说。

“Wow! Where’d he go?” said Annie. “I want to see him again! Come on!” “哇！ 他去哪儿了？”安妮说。 “我还想再看看他！快点！”

She started toward the river. 她朝河边跑去。

“Wait—don’t go far,” said Jack. “I just want to make sure it’s him.” “等等——别跑远，”杰克说。 “我只是想确认一下是不是他。”

He opened the Revolutionary War book. He found a picture of the boats on the riverbank. He read: 他打开独立战争的书。 他找到一幅河岸上船的画。他读道：

When General George Washington gathered his troops by the Delaware River, he was commander-in-chief of the whole American army. The general led the army for six years, until America became a free and independent nation. In 1789, he was elected the first president of the new United States. 当乔治·华盛顿将军在特拉华河畔集结军队时，他是整个美军的总司令。这位将军带领军队六年，直到美国成为一个自由独立的国家。1789年，他被选为新成立的美国的第一任总统。

“Oh, man, it is him,” said Jack. “哦，天哪，真是他，”杰克说。

He pulled out his notebook and wrote:



 他掏出笔记本写道：

图中文字：General George Washington helped

America become independent 图中文字：乔治·华盛顿将军帮助美国获得独立

“Hey, what are you writing?” someone asked. “嘿，你在写什么？”有人问。

Jack looked up. 杰克抬起头。

A bearded soldier was pointing at him. 一个留着胡子的士兵正指着他。

Jack shoved the Revolutionary War book and his notebook into his bag. 杰克把独立战争的书和笔记本塞进袋子。

“Nothing, sir,” he said. He started walking away. “没什么，长官，”他说。 他开始走开。

The man shouted after Jack. But Jack ran down toward the river and lost himself in a crowd of soldiers. 那人在杰克身后喊。 可杰克朝河边跑去，消失在一群士兵中间。

When he looked over his shoulder, he was relieved. The bearded man was nowhere in sight. 他回头看时，松了口气。 那个留胡子的人已经不见了。

“Stop, young man!” Someone shone a lantern right in Jack’s face. “站住，年轻人！”有人把灯笼正照在杰克脸上。

Jack gasped. 杰克倒吸一口气。

It was the captain. 是上尉。

“I told you to go home, Jack,” the captain said sternly. “Where’s your sister?” “我告诉过你回家去，杰克，”上尉严厉地说。 “你妹妹呢？”

Jack looked around. Where was Annie? 杰克四处看了看。安妮在哪儿？

“I don’t know,” he said. “我不知道，”他说。

“Find her at once and go back to your family!” the captain ordered. “Our secret mission is very important. Children will only get in the way.” “马上找到她，回家去！”上尉命令道。 “我们的秘密任务非常重要。 小孩子只会碍事。”

“Yes, sir!” said Jack. “是，长官！”杰克说。

The captain started to leave. But he stopped. 上尉正要离开，又停住了。

“I wonder if you could do me a favor, Jack?” he asked. “杰克，能不能帮我一个忙？”他问。

“Sure,” said Jack. “当然，”杰克说。

The captain pulled out his letter. 上尉掏出他的信。

“This is my letter to my children,” he said. “It’s a farewell letter. Would you please take it back with you to Frog Creek?” “这是写给我孩子的信，”他说。 “这是一封告别信。 请你把它带回蛙溪镇，好吗？”

“Yes, sir,” said Jack. “是，长官，”杰克说。

“You must only send it if you hear that we have failed in our mission and many patriots were lost,” said the captain. “只有当你听说我们任务失败、许多爱国者牺牲了，才可以寄出这封信，”上尉说。

“Yes, sir,” said Jack. “是，长官，”杰克说。

The captain handed his letter to Jack. 上尉把信交给杰克。

“I copied the general’s speech for my children,” the captain said. “If anything bad happens to me, I hope those words will give them courage.” “我把将军的讲话抄给孩子们了，”上尉说。 “要是我出了什么事，希望这些话能给他们勇气。”

The captain then turned and disappeared into the crowd. 然后上尉转身消失在人群中。

“Good luck, Captain!” Jack called. He hoped he would never have to send the letter to the man’s children. “好运，上尉！”杰克喊道。 他希望自己永远不用把这封信寄给那人的孩子。

Suddenly, Jack clutched the letter to his chest. 忽然，杰克把信紧紧捂在胸口。

“Send!” he whispered. “送出！”他小声说。

This letter was the writing they’d been looking for—something to send! He and Annie could go home now! Their mission was over! 这封信就是他们一直在找的文字——用来送出的东西！ 他和安妮现在可以回家了！ 他们的任务结束了！

Jack shoved the captain’s letter into his bag. Now he just had to find Annie. 杰克把上尉的信塞进袋子。 现在他只要找到安妮就行了。

As he looked around, he shivered. 他四处张望，打了个寒战。

“Where is she?” he muttered. “她在哪儿？”他嘀咕道。

Jack started moving through the crowd, looking for Annie. 杰克开始在人群中穿行，寻找安妮。

It was hard to see. The wind was blowing harder. The snow fell faster. 很难看清。 风刮得更猛了。 雪下得更快了。

Jack started to panic. 杰克开始慌了。

“Annie!” he called. “安妮！”他喊道。

As he wove quickly in and out of the crowd, he kept calling for her. None of the soldiers noticed him. They were all too busy. 他在人群中迅速迂回穿行，一边不停地喊她。 那些士兵一个也没注意到他。 他们都太忙了。

Finally, Jack came to the river. 终于，杰克来到了河边。

Through the lamplit mist, he saw soldiers waiting to get into the boats. Some had already climbed aboard. 透过灯笼照亮的雾气，他看见士兵们正等着上船。 有些人已经爬上船了。

“Jack!” came a cry. “杰克！”传来一声喊叫。

Jack saw the figure of a small girl. She was sitting in the back of the biggest boat. 杰克看见一个小女孩的身影。 她坐在最大那艘船的后部。

“No way,” he whispered. “不可能吧，”他小声说。

Jack charged down to the boat. He stood at the edge of the water. 杰克冲向那艘船。 他站在水边。

“What are you doing?” Jack shouted. “你在干什么？”杰克喊道。

“This is George Washington’s boat,” Annie said. “It’s our big chance to spend time with him! We might not get another one.” “这是乔治·华盛顿的船，”安妮说。 “这是我们和他待在一起的大好机会！ 也许不会再有下一次了。”

Jack looked at the other end of the huge boat. Through the mist and falling snow, he saw the commander-in-chief talking to his crew. 杰克朝大船的另一头望去。 透过雾和落雪，他看见总司令在跟船员说话。

“We can’t go with him,” said Jack. “We’ll get in the way of his secret mission. Besides, we have something to send now!” “我们不能跟他走，”杰克说。 “我们会妨碍他的秘密任务。 而且，我们现在已经有了要送出的东西！”

“What? How?” said Annie. “什么？怎么回事？”安妮说。

“A letter! The captain gave me his letter to take back to Frog Creek!” said Jack. “We’re only supposed to send it if something bad happens to the captain. We can go home now!” “一封信！ 上尉给了我一封信，让我带回蛙溪镇！”杰克说。 “只有上尉出了事，我们才能寄出它。 我们现在可以回家了！”

“Oh, can’t we go a little later?” Annie asked. “哦，就不能再晚一点吗？”安妮问。

Jack climbed into the boat to pull her out. 杰克爬上船去拉她出来。

“No, come on,” he said, taking her hand. “不行，快走，”他说着，抓住她的手。

Suddenly, the crew moved to the back of the boat, near Jack and Annie. The men grabbed their oars and started pushing the boat away from the shore. 忽然，船员们移到船尾，靠近杰克和安妮。 那些人抓起船桨，开始把船推离岸边。

“We’re taking off,” said Annie. “我们要出发了，”安妮说。

“No! We have to get out!” Jack said to the rowers. “不！ 我们得下去！”杰克对桨手们说。

But the men were working too hard to pay attention. They were using their oars to hack up the ice at the edge of the river. 可那些人忙得顾不上注意。 他们正用船桨劈开河边的冰。

“Excuse me,” Jack said in a loud voice. “不好意思，”杰克大声说。

Just then, the boat jolted forward. Jack nearly lost his balance. 就在这时，船猛地向前一颠。 杰克差点失去平衡。

The boat broke through more ice. Rough waves sloshed against its sides. 船又破开更多的冰。 汹涌的浪拍打着船舷。

“We have to go back!” said Jack. “我们得回去！”杰克说。

“Too late,” said Annie. “太晚了，”安妮说。

They were headed across the Delaware River! 他们正驶过特拉华河！
