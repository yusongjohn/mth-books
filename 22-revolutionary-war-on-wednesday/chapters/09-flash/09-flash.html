9. Flash! 9. 闪光！

“All aboard!” one of the rowers called. “都上船！”一名桨手喊道。

Jack and Annie hurried down the steep riverbank. They climbed back into the boat. 杰克和安妮急忙跑下陡峭的河岸。 他们爬回船上。

The crew pushed away from the shore. The boat started back through the icy, churning waters of the Delaware River. 船员把船推离岸边。 船开始驶回特拉华河冰冷湍急的水面。

Jack was freezing in the sleet and snow. But he didn’t worry about that now. He was thinking instead about how they had helped George Washington. He was thinking about how they had helped keep history on its course. 杰克在雨夹雪里冻僵了。 可他现在顾不上担心这个。 他反而在想，他们是怎样帮助了乔治·华盛顿。 他在想，他们是怎样帮助历史按预定路线走下去的。

Jack felt great. 杰克感觉棒极了。

When they got to the riverbank, Jack and Annie jumped out of the boat. 到了河岸，杰克和安妮跳下船。

“Thanks!” Jack yelled to the rowers. “谢谢！”杰克朝桨手们喊道。

With the wind at their backs, Jack and Annie took off through the blizzard. They ran down the icy bank of the Delaware River. 借着背后的风，杰克和安妮冲进暴风雪。 他们沿着特拉华河结冰的河岸跑。

Thunder rumbled in the snowy sky. 雪空中响起隆隆的雷声。

Lightning zigzagged over the woods. 闪电在树林上空曲折划过。

“How will we find the tree house?” cried Annie. “我们怎么才能找到树屋？”安妮喊道。

“I don’t know!” said Jack. “But don’t worry! We’ll find it!” “我不知道！”杰克说。 “不过别担心！我们会找到的！”

He felt very confident now, after meeting George Washington. After being thanked by George Washington! 见过乔治·华盛顿之后，他现在非常自信。 还被乔治·华盛顿道过谢呢！

He and Annie ran on through the rain and the snow and the sleet. 他和安妮在雨、雪和雨夹雪中继续跑。

They ran along the riverbank—until a bright flash of lightning lit up the sky. Then Jack saw it! 他们沿着河岸跑着——直到一道明亮的闪电照亮了天空。然后杰克看见了！

The tree house was directly to their left, high in a tall tree covered with snow. 树屋正好在他们左侧，高高地架在一棵积雪的大树上。

“Over there!” he shouted. “在那边！”他喊道。

Jack and Annie ran toward the edge of the woods. 杰克和安妮朝树林边跑去。

In the dark, Jack looked up, searching for the tree house. 黑暗中，杰克抬头寻找树屋。

Lightning lit the woods again. Jack saw the rope ladder flapping wildly in the wind. 闪电又照亮了树林。 杰克看见绳梯在风中狂乱地甩动。

He grabbed it. 他抓住了它。

“Annie!” he called. “安妮！”他喊道。

“Here!” she said. “在这儿！”她说。

“Let’s go!” he said. “走吧！”他说。

They climbed up the swaying ladder and scrambled into the tree house. They were soaking wet and covered with slush and snow. 他们爬上摇晃的梯子，钻进了树屋。 他们浑身湿透，身上满是雪泥和雪。

Annie grabbed the Pennsylvania book. 安妮抓起宾夕法尼亚的书。

“I wish we could go there!” she shouted. “我希望我们能去那里！”她喊道。

The wind blew even harder. 风刮得更猛了。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
