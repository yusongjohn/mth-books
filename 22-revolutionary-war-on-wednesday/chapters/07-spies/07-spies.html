7. Spies! 7. 间谍！

“What are you doing here?” George Washington asked. The commander-in-chief sounded furious. “你们在这儿干什么？”乔治·华盛顿问。 总司令听上去怒不可遏。

“We made a mistake,” Jack said. “We—we didn’t mean to come.” “我们犯了个错，”杰克说。 “我们——我们不是故意上来的。”

George Washington turned to the rowers. 乔治·华盛顿转向桨手们。

“Who let these children sneak aboard?” he asked in a stern voice. “谁让这两个孩子偷偷上船的？”他严厉地问。

The men looked at Jack and Annie with surprise. 那些人惊讶地看着杰克和安妮。

“It’s not their fault,” Annie said quickly. “They were working too hard to notice us.” “这不是他们的错，”安妮连忙说。 “他们忙得根本没注意到我们。”

Just then, the boat banged against the ice. The ice cracked. The boat moved on, then bumped against the shore. 就在这时，船猛地撞上了冰。冰裂开了。 船继续前进，然后撞上了岸。

Two soldiers jumped out and pulled the boat up on the shore. 两名士兵跳下去，把船拉上岸。

George Washington looked at Jack and Annie. 乔治·华盛顿看着杰克和安妮。

“This boat is returning to get more men,” he said. “When it does, you two will get out and stay on the other shore.” “这艘船要回去接更多的人，”他说。 “到时候，你们两个就下去，待在对岸。”

“Yes, sir,” said Jack. He felt very embarrassed. “是，长官，”杰克说。 他觉得非常尴尬。

George Washington then gave orders to the rowers. 接着乔治·华盛顿向桨手下了命令。

“Make sure these children do not board any other boats when you return,” he said. “你们回去时，千万别让这两个孩子再上别的船，”他说。

The general stepped onto the riverbank. 将军踏上河岸。

The wind started to pick up. The snow fell harder. As the crew unloaded the boat, neither Jack nor Annie spoke. 风开始更大了。 雪下得更猛了。 船员卸船时，杰克和安妮谁都没说话。

Jack was miserable. They had caused trouble for George Washington—just when the general was trying to make America an independent nation. 杰克心里难受极了。 他们给乔治·华盛顿惹了麻烦——正值将军努力让美国成为独立国家的时候。

Jack desperately wished he and Annie had gone home earlier. 杰克真希望自己和安妮早点回家。

They watched more and more boats land on the riverbank. As the soldiers unloaded their weapons and horses, a freezing rain began. Now rain, snow, and sleet fell together. 他们看着越来越多的船停靠在河岸。 士兵们卸下武器和马匹时，开始下起冰冷的雨。 现在雨、雪和雨夹雪一起落下来。

Jack heard George Washington call to one of his men. 杰克听见乔治·华盛顿在叫他的一个部下。

“This storm is getting worse, Major!” the general said. “这场风暴越来越厉害了，少校！”将军说。

“Yes, sir!” the major said. “是，长官！”少校说。

“I think we’re in for a blizzard,” said Washington. “我想我们马上要遇上暴风雪了，”华盛顿说。

“Yes, sir! Our mission may be hopeless, sir,” said the major. “Should we call it off?” “是，长官！ 我们的任务可能没希望了，长官，”少校说。 “我们是不是该取消行动？”

“No, you shouldn’t,” Jack whispered. “You’re going to win.” “不，不该，”杰克小声说。 “你们会赢的。”

“Should we turn back, sir?” the major said. “我们该撤退吗，长官？”少校说。

“No, no!” said Annie. “不，不！”安妮说。

She stood up. The boat rocked. 她站了起来。船摇晃起来。

“Don’t turn back, George Washington, sir!” she shouted. “You have to march on, sir! You have to attack the Hessians, sir!” “别撤退，乔治·华盛顿长官！”她喊道。 “您必须继续前进，长官！ 您必须去袭击黑森士兵，长官！”

“Shh!” Jack tried to pull Annie back down. “We’re not supposed to know about their secret mission!” “嘘！”杰克想把安妮拉坐下。 “我们不该知道他们的秘密任务！”

“How does she know our plans, Major?” George Washington asked. “她怎么知道我们的计划，少校？”乔治·华盛顿问。

“Listen to us, sir!” said Annie. “You’re going to win!” She pulled away from Jack and jumped out of the boat. “听我们说，长官！”安妮说。 “你们会赢的！”她挣脱杰克，跳下了船。

“Annie!” Jack leaped onto the riverbank. He scrambled after Annie up the steep, icy slope. “安妮！”杰克跳上河岸。 他跟着安妮，吃力地爬上陡峭结冰的斜坡。

“You have to lead your men, General Washington, sir!” Annie said. “The Hessians will be surprised! They think no army will be marching on a night like this!” “您必须带领您的部下，华盛顿将军长官！”安妮说。 “黑森士兵会大吃一惊！ 他们以为这样的夜里不会有军队行军！”

“How do you know all this?” the major shouted above the storm. “How do you know what the Hessians are doing and thinking?” “你怎么知道这一切？”少校的喊声盖过了风暴。 “你怎么知道黑森士兵在做什么、在想什么？”

“I—I—” For once, Annie seemed at a loss for words. “我——我——”这一次，安妮似乎无言以对。

“She just guessed!” said Jack. “她只是猜的！”杰克说。

Just then, the bearded soldier who had yelled at Jack earlier stepped forward. 就在这时，先前对杰克喊过的那个留胡子的士兵走上前来。

“I saw this boy earlier,” he said. “He was writing things down.” “我早些时候见过这个男孩，”他说。 “他在写东西。”

“No, I was just … ” Now Jack was at a loss for words. “不，我只是……”现在杰克也说不出话了。

“Seize them!” the major shouted. “They’re spies!” “抓住他们！”少校喊道。 “他们是间谍！”
