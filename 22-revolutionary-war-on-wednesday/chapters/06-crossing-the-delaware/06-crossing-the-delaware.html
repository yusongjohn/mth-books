6. Crossing the Delaware 6. 渡过特拉华河

The huge boat rocked in the water. Giant chunks of ice smashed against its sides. 大船在水里摇晃。 大块的冰撞上了船舷。

“Thanks a lot, Annie,” Jack whispered. He shivered in the snowy cold. “We’re not supposed to go on their secret mission with them.” “真是太谢谢你了，安妮，”杰克小声说。 他在下雪的寒冷里发抖。 “我们不该跟他们一起去执行秘密任务。”

“It’s okay,” she whispered. “Maybe we can help George Washington.” “没关系，”她小声说。 “也许我们能帮上乔治·华盛顿。”

“Are you nuts?” Jack whispered. “We should be on our way home now.” “你疯了吗？”杰克小声说。 “我们现在该在回家的路上了。”

The boat hit a piece of ice. The boat bounced, then dipped into the river. 船撞上了一块冰。 船弹了一下，随即栽进河里。

Jack clung to the wooden side. He hoped they wouldn’t turn over. Nobody could survive in this icy water, he thought. It would be like sinking on the TITANIC. 杰克紧紧抓住木制船舷。 他希望船不要翻过去。 谁也没法在这冰水里活下来，他想。 那会像泰坦尼克号沉没一样。

The crew fought hard to keep the boat moving forward. They rowed past chunks of ice into a smoother part of the river. 船员们拼命划着，好让船继续向前。 他们划过冰块，进入河面较平静的一段。

Light from oil lamps shone on the water, making the ice chunks behind them glow like huge, glittering jewels. 油灯光照在水面上，让身后的冰块像巨大的宝石一样闪闪发亮。

Jack looked back. Other boats were following them. They were filled with soldiers, horses, and cannons. 杰克回头看。 其他船跟在他们后面。 船上满是士兵、马和大炮。

“Where exactly are we going?” Annie whispered. “我们到底要去哪儿？”安妮小声问。

Jack shrugged. He reached into his bag and pulled out their Revolutionary War book. 杰克耸了耸肩。 他伸手进袋子，掏出独立战争的书。

By the dim lantern light in the boat, he searched through the book. He found a painting of General Washington crossing the Delaware River. 借着船上昏暗的灯笼光，他翻找着书。 他找到一幅华盛顿将军渡过特拉华河的画。

He showed the painting to Annie. They each read the caption silently: 他把画给安妮看。 他们各自默默读着说明文字：

After George Washington crossed the Delaware, he led his men on a nine-mile march to a British post. The post was filled with Hessians, German soldiers hired by the British to fight for them. The American patriots caught them off guard. The Hessians never thought the patriots would attack on a stormy Christmas night. It was a great victory for the patriots. They captured almost 1,000 Hessians. Hardly any of Washington’s men were lost. 乔治·华盛顿渡过特拉华河后，带领他的士兵进行了九英里的行军，前往一个英国驻军的据点。这个据点里驻扎着黑森士兵，他们是英国雇佣的德国士兵，为英国作战。美国爱国者们趁他们不备发动了攻击。黑森士兵们没想到爱国者会在暴风雪的圣诞节之夜发起攻击。这是爱国者们的一次重大胜利。他们几乎俘虏了1000名黑森士兵，华盛顿的士兵几乎没有伤亡。

“Yay! We won’t have to send the captain’s letter!” Annie exclaimed. “耶！ 我们就不用寄上尉的信了！”安妮喊道。

“Shh!” said Jack. “嘘！”杰克说。

But George Washington turned around and looked back at Jack and Annie. 可乔治·华盛顿转过身，回头看着杰克和安妮。

Oh, no, Jack thought, caught again. 哦，不，杰克想，又被发现了。

He closed his eyes, as if that would make him invisible. 他闭上眼睛，好像这样别人就看不见他了。

“He’s coming,” said Annie. “他过来了，”安妮说。

Jack looked up. 杰克抬起头。

George Washington was making his way back toward them. 乔治·华盛顿正朝他们走回来。

In the next moment, the commander-in-chief loomed above them like a giant shadow. 下一刻，总司令像巨大的影子一样耸立在他们上方。

“Children?” he asked in a quiet, angry voice. “孩子们？”他用平静却愤怒的声音问。

“Sorry,” Jack squeaked. “对不起，”杰克小声吱唔道。

“Merry Christmas!” said Annie. “圣诞快乐！”安妮说。

But George Washington did not say “Merry Christmas” back. 可乔治·华盛顿没有回一句“圣诞快乐”。
