2. Day or Night? 2. 白天还是夜里？

Jack shivered. The wind blew hard. 杰克打了个寒战。 风刮得很猛。

“It’s cold,” he said. He could see his breath in the air. “好冷，”他说。 他能看见自己呼出的气。

“Wrap your scarf tighter,” said Annie. “把围巾裹紧点，”安妮说。

Jack looked down. He had a wool scarf around his neck. 杰克低头看了看。 他脖子上围着一条羊毛围巾。

He was also wearing woolen pants that buttoned at the knees, a coat, and a hat with three corners. On his feet were funny-looking shoes with buckles. In place of his backpack was a leather bag. 他还穿着膝盖处扣纽扣的羊毛裤子、一件外套，以及一顶三角帽。 他脚上是一双样子滑稽、带搭扣的鞋子。 他的背包换成了一个皮袋子。

Annie was wearing a long coat over a long dress. 安妮穿着一件长外套，里面是长裙。

Jack pulled his scarf tighter. Then he looked out the window with Annie. 杰克把围巾裹得更紧了。 然后他和安妮一起望向窗外。

They’d landed in trees near an icy river. The sky was gray and heavy with clouds. 他们落在结冰河边的树林附近。 天空灰蒙蒙的，乌云很沉。

“It’s almost day—or almost night. I can’t tell,” said Annie. “差不多是白天了——或者差不多是夜里。 我分不清，”安妮说。

“Yeah. I hope it’s almost day,” said Jack. “对。 我希望快天亮了，”杰克说。

“I wonder where we’ll find something to send?” said Annie. “不知道我们会在哪儿找到要送出的东西？”安妮说。

Jack shrugged. 杰克耸了耸肩。

“First we have to find the Revolutionary War,” he said. “首先我们得找到独立战争，”他说。

He opened their research book. By the gray light, he read: 他打开了研究书。 借着灰蒙蒙的光线，他读道：

Over 200 years ago, the United States was made up of thirteen colonies ruled by Britain. From 1775 until 1782, American “patriots” fought for independence from Britain. This was called the Revolutionary War. 200多年前，美国由十三个英国殖民地组成。从1775年到1782年，美国的‘爱国者’为了从英国独立而战斗。这被称为独立战争。

Jack took out his notebook. He wrote:



 杰克拿出笔记本，写下：

图中文字：American patriots fight for

independence from Britain 图中文字：美国爱国者为从英国独立而战

Jack turned the page. There was a picture of soldiers in red uniforms. He read aloud: 杰克翻过一页。 有一幅画，上面是穿着红色制服的士兵。他大声读道：

During the Revolutionary War, British soldiers wore red uniforms. For this reason, they were called “redcoats.” 在独立战争期间，英国士兵穿着红色的军装。因此，他们被称为“红衣军。”

Jack wrote in his notebook:



 杰克在笔记本上写道：

图中文字：British = red coats 图中文字：英国人=红衣

“Wow, snow,” said Annie. “哇，下雪了，”安妮说。

Jack looked up from his writing. Annie was holding her hand out the window. A few snowflakes blew into the tree house. 杰克从书写中抬起头。 安妮正把手伸到窗外。 几片雪花吹进了树屋。

“Not much yet,” said Jack. “But we better find the special writing soon.” “还不多，”杰克说。 “不过我们最好快点找到那份特别的文字。”

“Well, stop reading and come on,” said Annie. She buttoned up her coat and started down the ladder. “好了，别读了，快走，”安妮说。 她扣好外套，开始下梯子。

“Okay, okay,” said Jack. He packed the research book and his notebook into his bag. Then he followed Annie. “好，好，”杰克说。 他把研究书和笔记本装进袋子。 然后跟着安妮下去。

When they stepped onto the ground, there were more snow flurries. The sky was growing darker. 他们踩到地上时，小阵雪下得更密了。 天色越来越暗。

“I’m afraid it’s almost night instead of day,” said Jack. “恐怕现在快到夜里了，而不是白天，”杰克说。

“Yeah,” said Annie, looking around. “Hey, look—people.” “是啊，”安妮四处张望着说。 “嘿，看——有人。”

She pointed up the river. In the misty distance was a campfire. Several men sat around the fire. They all held muskets. 她指着河的上游。 在雾蒙蒙的远处有一堆营火。 几个男人围坐在火堆旁。 他们都握着火枪。

“Maybe they can help us,” said Annie. She started away. But Jack grabbed her. “也许他们能帮我们，”安妮说。她拔腿就走。 可杰克一把抓住了她。

“Wait, I think they’re soldiers,” he said. “They have muskets. Remember the muskets from the Civil War? The guns that the soldiers carried?” “等等，我觉得他们是士兵，”他说。 “他们有火枪。 还记得南北战争里的火枪吗？ 就是士兵们背的那种枪？”

“Oh, yeah,” said Annie. “哦，对，”安妮说。

“They might be redcoats,” said Jack. “Let’s sneak closer and get a better look at their uniforms.” “他们可能是红衣军，”杰克说。 “我们偷偷靠近点，好好看看他们的制服。”

“Quick,” said Annie, “before it’s too dark to see.” “快，”安妮说，“趁天还没黑得看不清。”
