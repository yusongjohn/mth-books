10. Our Will? 10. 我们的威尔？

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开眼睛。

They were wearing their own clothes again. A lightning bug blinked inside the growing darkness of the tree house. 他们又穿上了自己的衣服。 一只萤火虫在渐渐变暗的树屋里闪了一下。

Annie picked up Morgan’s note. She repeated the rhyme: 安妮拿起摩根的纸条。 她把那首押韵短诗又念了一遍：

To find a special magic,

You must step into the light

And without wand, spell, or charm,

Turn daytime into night. 要找到特别的魔法，你必须走进光里，不用魔杖、咒语或魔法，把白天变成黑夜。

“We found the special magic,” said Annie. “Theater magic!” “我们找到了特别的魔法，”安妮说。 “戏剧的魔法！”

“Yep,” said Jack. “嗯，”杰克说。

He opened his backpack. He and Annie took out the two scrolls Will had given them. When they unrolled them, Jack saw that Will had written something. He read aloud: 他打开背包。 他和安妮拿出威尔给他们的两卷卷轴。 他们展开卷轴时，杰克看见威尔写了些东西。他大声读道：

图中文字：Thank you both for helping me.

Your friend,

William Shakespeare 图中文字：谢谢你们两个帮我。你们的朋友，威廉·莎士比亚

“William Shakespeare? ” said Annie. “I’ve heard that name before.” “威廉·莎士比亚？”安妮说。 “我以前听说过这个名字。”

“Me too,” said Jack. “我也是，”杰克说。

He took out their research book. He looked up Shakespeare in the index. He turned to a page and read aloud: 他拿出资料书。 他在索引里查找莎士比亚。 他翻到一页，大声读道：

William Shakespeare lived from 1564 to 1616. He wrote thirty-seven plays and many sonnets and other poems. Many people think he was the greatest writer who ever lived. 威廉·莎士比亚生于1564年，死于1616年。他写了三十七部戏剧，还有很多十四行诗和其他诗歌。很多人认为他是有史以来最伟大的作家。

“The greatest?” said Annie. “ Our Will?” “最伟大的？”安妮说。 “我们的威尔？”

“Oh, man,” whispered Jack. He stared in amazement at William Shakespeare’s autograph. “哦，天哪，”杰克小声说。 他惊讶地盯着威廉·莎士比亚的亲笔签名。

“Hey, we can leave our scrolls with Morgan’s note,” said Annie. “It’ll prove to her we found a special magic.” “嘿，我们可以把卷轴和摩根的纸条放在一起，”安妮说。 “这能向她证明，我们找到了一种特别的魔法。”

They put their two scrolls next to the note on the floor. Then they climbed down the rope ladder. 他们把两卷卷轴放在地板上纸条旁边。 然后他们爬下绳梯。

When they started walking through the woods, a breeze blew, shaking the tree leaves. Shadows shifted. Birds called from hidden places. 他们开始穿过树林时，一阵风吹过，树叶沙沙作响。影子晃动着。 鸟儿从隐蔽的地方叫着。

“Remember the enchanted woods?” Annie said in a hushed voice. “The fairy queen and the fairy king?” “还记得那片被施了魔法的树林吗？”安妮低声说。 “仙后和仙王？”

Jack smiled and nodded. 杰克笑着点了点头。

“And Puck, the merry wanderer of the night?” said Annie. “And Will, our Will.” “还有帕克，那个快乐的夜游者？”安妮说。 “还有威尔，我们的威尔。”

Jack nodded again. 杰克又点了点头。

“I had a great time,” said Annie. “Didn’t you?” “我玩得很开心，”安妮说。 “你呢？”

Jack sighed. 杰克叹了口气。

“Yes,” he said. Then he took a deep breath and spoke very clearly and with feeling: “开心，”他说。 然后他深深吸了一口气，说得非常清楚，也很有感情：

“I have had a most rare vision. “我做了一个极其罕见的梦。

I have had a dream … .” 我做了一个梦……。”
