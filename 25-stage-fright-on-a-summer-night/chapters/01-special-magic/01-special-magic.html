1. Special Magic 1. 特别的魔法

Jack and Annie sat on their porch. Lightning bugs blinked in the warm summer twilight. 杰克和安妮坐在自家门廊上。 萤火虫在温暖的夏日暮色中闪烁。

“Wow, a shooting star!” said Annie, pointing at the sky. “哇，一颗流星！”安妮指着天空说。

Jack looked up, just in time to see a streak of light flash through the sky. The light hovered above the Frog Creek woods. Then it disappeared into the treetops. 杰克抬起头，刚好看见一道光从天空划过。 那道光在蛙溪树林上空徘徊。 接着，那道光消失在树梢里。

Jack caught his breath. He turned to Annie. 杰克屏住了呼吸。 他转向安妮。

“That was no shooting star,” he said. “那不是流星，”他说。

“Right,” she said. “对，”她说。

They jumped up. Jack grabbed his backpack from inside the front hall. 他们跳了起来。 杰克从前厅里抓起他的背包。

“Dad, Mom! Can we go out?” he called. “We’ll be back soon!” “爸爸，妈妈！ 我们能出去吗？”他喊道。 “我们很快就回来！”

“Ten minutes, no more!” their mom said. “十分钟，不能再多了！”妈妈说。

“Okay!” said Jack. He closed the door. “Let’s go! Hurry!” “好！”杰克说。 他关上门。 “走吧！快点！”

He and Annie ran across their yard. They ran down their street. They ran into the woods. They ran until they came to the tallest oak. They looked up. 他和安妮跑过院子。 他们沿着街道跑下去。 他们跑进了树林。 他们一直跑到最高的那棵橡树下。他们抬头看。

“Yep,” said Annie. “没错，”安妮说。

Jack just smiled. He was too happy for words. 杰克只是笑了笑。 他高兴得说不出话来。

“That’s our shooting star,” said Annie. “The magic tree house.” “那就是我们的流星，”安妮说。 “魔法树屋。”

She grabbed the rope ladder and started up. Jack followed. 她抓住绳梯往上爬。杰克跟在后面。

When they climbed inside the tree house, they both gasped. A beautiful woman with long white hair stood in the shadowy corner. 他们爬进树屋时，两人都倒抽了一口气。 一位留着长长白发的美丽女子站在阴暗的角落里。

“Hello, Jack and Annie,” said Morgan le Fay. “你好，杰克，安妮，”摩根·勒费说。

“Morgan!” Jack and Annie cried. “摩根！”杰克和安妮喊道。

They threw their arms around her. 他们张开双臂抱住了她。

“Why are you here?” said Annie. “What do you want us to do for you?” “你怎么来了？”安妮说。 “你想让我们为你做什么？”

“You have already done many good things for me,” said Morgan, “and for King Arthur and Camelot. Now I want you to do something good for yourselves. You are going to learn magic.” “你们已经为我做了很多好事，”摩根说，“也为亚瑟王和卡米洛特做了很多。 现在我想让你们为自己做点好事。 你们要去学魔法。”

“Oh, wow,” said Annie. “Are we going to become magicians? Will you teach us charms and spells?” “哦，哇，”安妮说。 “我们要变成魔术师吗？ 你会教我们魔法和咒语吗？”

Morgan laughed. “There is magic that does not need charms or spells,” she said. “You’ll find a special magic on each of your next four adventures.” 摩根笑了。 “有一种魔法不需要魔咒或咒语，”她说。 “在接下来的四次冒险里，你们每次都会找到一种特别的魔法。”

“How?” asked Jack. “怎么找？”杰克问。

“A secret rhyme will guide you on each journey,” Morgan said. “Here, this is the first.” She held up a slip of paper. “每次旅程都会有一首秘密韵诗指引你们，”摩根说。 “给，这是第一张。”她举起一张小纸条。

Annie took the paper from Morgan and read the rhyme aloud: 安妮从摩根手里接过纸条，大声读出那首押韵短诗：

To find a special magic,
You must step into the light
And without wand, spell, or charm,
Turn daytime into night.

“Turn daytime into night?” said Jack. “How can we do that?” “把白天变成黑夜？”杰克说。 “我们怎么才能做到呢？”

Morgan smiled. 摩根笑了笑。

“That’s what you have to find out,” she said. “那正是你们要去弄清楚的，”她说。

Jack had lots of questions. But before he could ask any of them, a flash lit up the tree house. He closed his eyes against the light. When he opened them, Morgan le Fay was gone. On the floor where she had stood was a book. 杰克有好多问题。 可他还没来得及问，一道闪光就照亮了树屋。 他闭上眼睛，躲开那道强光。 等他睁开眼，摩根·勒费已经不见了。 她刚才站的地方，地板上放着一本书。

“Morgan didn’t tell us enough,” said Jack. “摩根告诉我们的不够，”杰克说。

“But she left this research book,” said Annie. She picked up the book. “And it’ll tell us the first place to go.” She held the book up to the gray light at the window. “可她留下了这本资料书，”安妮说。 她拿起那本书。 “它会告诉我们第一站去哪儿。”她把书举到窗边灰色的光线下。

The cover showed a busy river with boats and a bridge. The title was 封面画着一条热闹的河，河上有船，还有一座桥。书名是

Merry Olde England

“What’s o-l-d-e mean?” asked Annie. “o-l-d-e是什么意思？”安妮问。

“I think that’s the old way of spelling old,” said Jack. “You say it the same.” “我想那是old的老式拼法，”杰克说。 “读音是一样的。”

“So we’re going to merry olde England to find magic?” said Annie. “That sounds fun. Ready?” “这么说我们要去快乐的古老英格兰找魔法？”安妮说。 “听起来好玩。准备好了吗？”

“I guess,” said Jack. He still wished they’d gotten more information from Morgan. But he pointed at the cover of their research book. “大概吧，”杰克说。 他还是希望摩根能多告诉他们一些信息。 可他还是指着资料书的封面。

“I wish we could go there,” he said. “我希望我们能去那儿，”他说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

The wind blew harder and harder. 风越刮越猛。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
