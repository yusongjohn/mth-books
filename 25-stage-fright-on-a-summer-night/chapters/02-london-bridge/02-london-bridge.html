2. London Bridge 2. 伦敦桥

Warm daylight flooded into the tree house. Jack opened his eyes. 温暖的日光涌进树屋。 杰克睁开眼睛。

Annie was wearing a long dress with an apron. Jack was wearing a shirt with puffy sleeves, knee-length pants, and tights. Their shoes were leather slippers. Jack’s backpack was now a leather bag. 安妮穿着一件带围裙的长裙。 杰克穿着一件袖子蓬松的衬衫、及膝短裤和连裤袜。 他们的鞋是皮拖鞋。 杰克的背包变成了一个皮袋子。

“These clothes are weird,” said Jack. His voice was nearly drowned out by the sound of thundering wagon wheels coming from below. “这些衣服好奇怪，”杰克说。 他的声音几乎被下面轰隆隆的马车轮声盖过了。

“What’s going on?” said Annie. “怎么回事？”安妮说。

She and Jack looked out. 她和杰克向外望去。

The tree house had landed in a patch of trees near a wide brown river. Wagons, carts, and people were heading toward the river. 树屋落在一片树林里，旁边是一条宽阔的棕色河流。 马车、手推车和人们都朝河边走去。

Ferryboats, sailing ships, and white swans glided across the water. 渡船、帆船和白天鹅在水面上滑行。

“Wow, it’s so busy,” said Annie. “哇，好热闹啊，”安妮说。

Jack opened their research book and read: 杰克打开资料书，读道：

In 1600, over 100,000 people lived in London, England. At that time, England was ruled by Queen Elizabeth the First. She was much loved by her people. 1600年时，有超过10万人住在英国伦敦。当时，英国由伊丽莎白一世女王统治。她深受人民爱戴。

“A queen? Cool,” said Annie. “一位女王？ 太酷了，”安妮说。

Jack took out his notebook and wrote: 杰克拿出笔记本写道：

图中文字：London-1600

Queen Elizabeth the First 图中文字：伦敦-1600年伊丽莎白一世

“I’ve never seen a bridge like that,” said Annie, looking to the left. “我从没见过那样的桥，”安妮看着左边说。

Jack looked with her. A giant stone bridge crossed the river. The bridge looked like a small town. It was crowded with houses, shops, and even a church. 杰克和她一起看。 一座巨大的石桥横跨在河上。 那座桥看起来像一座小镇。 桥上挤满了房屋、店铺，甚至还有一座教堂。

Jack found a picture of the bridge in their research book. He read aloud: 杰克在资料书里找到了这座桥的图。他大声读道：

At the heart of London was London Bridge. The bridge crossed the Thames (say TEMZ) River. At different times in history, the bridge fell down. But it was always built again. 伦敦市中心有伦敦桥。这座桥横跨泰晤士河（说TEMZ）。在历史上不同时期，这座桥倒塌过。但之后总会被重新建造。

“Oh, wow,” said Annie. “That must be where the song comes from.” She sang, “London Bridge is falling down, falling down … .” “哦，哇，”安妮说。 “歌一定就是从这儿来的。”她唱道，“伦敦桥要塌了，要塌了……。”

While Annie sang, Jack took out his notebook and wrote: 安妮唱歌时，杰克拿出笔记本写道：

图中文字：London Bridge—crosses Thames (TEMZ)

River, crowded with stores and houses 图中文字：伦敦桥—横跨泰晤士河（TEMZ），桥上商店和房屋很多

“Let’s go look for the magic,” said Annie. She read Morgan’s note again: “我们去找魔法吧，”安妮说。 她又读了一遍摩根的纸条：

To find a special magic,
You must step into the light
And without wand, spell, or charm,
Turn daytime into night.

Jack squinted up at the sky. It was very blue, without even a cloud. 杰克眯起眼睛望着天空。 天很蓝，连一朵云都没有。

“It’s just not possible,” he said, shaking his head. “这根本不可能，”他摇着头说。

But he threw the research book and his notebook into his leather bag. Then he followed Annie down the rope ladder. When they reached the ground, they started walking toward the river. 可他还是把资料书和笔记本扔进皮袋子。 然后他跟着安妮爬下绳梯。 到了地面，他们开始朝河边走去。

“P-U!” Annie said, holding her nose. “好臭啊！”安妮捂着鼻子说。

The river smelled terrible. 河水气味难闻极了。

No one else seemed to mind the smell, though. People were cheerfully piling into ferryboats or heading for the bridge. They all seemed happy, as if they were going someplace fun. 不过，好像别人都不在乎那股气味。 人们兴高采烈地挤上渡船，或朝桥走去。 他们看起来都很开心，好像要去什么好玩的地方。

A group of ragged boys brushed past Jack and Annie. They were about twelve or thirteen years old. They were laughing and out of breath. 一群衣衫褴褛的男孩从杰克和安妮身边擦身而过。 他们大约十二三岁。 他们一边笑一边上气不接下气。

“Hurry! We’ll be late!” one shouted. “快点！ 我们要迟到了！”有人喊道。

The boys ran toward the stone gateway leading onto the bridge. 那些男孩朝通往桥上的石门跑去。

“Late for what?” said Annie. “What’s on the other side of that bridge? Why are they in such a hurry to get there?” “迟到什么？”安妮说。 “桥那边有什么？ 他们干嘛这么急着赶过去？”

“I don’t know,” said Jack. He pulled out their research book. “I’ll see what the book says.” “我不知道，”杰克说。 他抽出资料书。 “我看看书上怎么说。”

“No, let’s just go—or we’ ll be late!” said Annie. She took off running. “不，我们直接去——不然就真迟到了！”安妮说。 她拔腿就跑。

“Okay, okay,” said Jack. “好吧，好吧，”杰克说。

He put away the book and ran after her toward London Bridge. 他把书收好，追着她朝伦敦桥跑去。
