7. In the Forest, in the Night 7. 夜里，在森林里

Jack stood onstage in the bright sunlight. He felt three thousand pairs of eyes staring at him. 杰克站在舞台上，阳光很刺眼。 他觉得有三千双眼睛在盯着他。

“How now, spirit!” Puck said in a loud voice. “Whither wander you?” “喂，精灵！”帕克大声说。 “你往哪儿游荡？”

Jack looked down at his scroll. He pushed his glasses into place. He opened his mouth. No sound came out. 杰克低头看自己的卷轴。 他把眼镜推正。 他张开嘴。 却没有声音出来。

One of the groundlings hissed. 一个站票观众发出嘘声。

“How now, spirit!” Puck shouted even louder this time. “Whither wander you?” “喂，精灵！”帕克这次喊得更响了。 “你往哪儿游荡？”

Jack closed his eyes. He felt the summer night. He took a deep breath. He cleared his throat. He looked at his speech. 杰克闭上眼睛。 他感觉到了夏夜。 他深深吸了一口气。 他清了清嗓子。 他看着自己的台词。

Then he began to read: 接着他开始读：

Over hill, over dale,

Through bush, through briar,

Over park, over pale,

Through flood, through fire,

I do wander everywhere,

Swifter than the moon’s sphere;

And I serve the Fairy Queen … . 翻过山丘，越过山谷，穿过灌木，绕过荆棘，跨过公园，越过围墙，渡过洪水，穿过烈火，我到处游荡，比月亮的轨迹还要快；我为仙后效劳……。

As Jack read, the audience grew quiet. Jack forgot he was Jack. He was in the forest, in the night, talking to Puck. 杰克读的时候，观众渐渐安静下来。 杰克忘了自己是杰克。 他在森林里，在夜里，正和帕克说话。

When he finished, not a single groundling hissed or threw things. 等他念完，站票观众一个也没嘘，也没扔东西。

Jack took a deep breath as Puck started his lines. Jack knew he had one more speech. His heart pounded. But it was more from excitement now than fear. 帕克开始念自己的台词时，杰克深深吸了口气。 杰克知道自己还有一段台词。 他的心怦怦直跳。 不过现在更多是因为兴奋，而不是害怕。

When it was time to start his second speech, he was ready. This time, he spoke very clearly and with feeling. He tried to be as natural as possible. When he finished his speech, the audience clapped and clapped. 到第二段台词时，他已经准备好了。 这一次，他说得非常清楚，也很有感情。 他尽量表现得自然。 念完后，观众鼓起掌来，鼓了又鼓。

Jack hardly remembered leaving the stage. Will was waiting for him. 杰克几乎不记得自己是怎么离开舞台的。 威尔在等他。

“Hurrah!” said Will, slapping Jack on the back. “You were brilliant!” “好哇！”威尔拍着杰克的背说。 “你太出色了！”

Jack blushed as he gave Will his scroll back. He couldn’t believe he’d just performed, acted, in front of all those people! And he’d actually had fun—just like Annie had said. 杰克把卷轴还给威尔时，脸红了。 他简直不敢相信，自己刚才居然在那么多人面前表演、演戏了！ 而且他还真玩得很开心——就像安妮说的那样。

Jack waited in the shadows for Annie to do her part. He watched her go onstage with the fairy queen and the other fairies. 杰克在阴影里等着安妮上场。 他看着她和仙后以及其他小仙子一起上台。

When the queen asked the fairies to sing her to sleep, Annie stepped forward. Reading from her scroll, she sang out clearly—and with lots of feeling: 当仙后请小仙子们唱歌哄她睡觉时，安妮走上前。 她照着卷轴，唱得又清楚——又特别有感情：

You spotted snakes, with double tongue,

Thorny hedgehogs, be not seen; 你看到蛇，舌头分叉，带刺的刺猬，不要靠近；

Annie waved her hand as if shooing away the snakes and hedgehogs. 安妮挥着手，好像在赶走蛇和刺猬。

Newts and blind worms, do no wrong;

Come not near our Fairy Queen … . 蝾螈和盲蚯蚓，不要做错事；别靠近我们的仙女女王……。

Annie shook her finger at the newts and blind worms. The audience howled with laughter. 安妮朝蝾螈和盲虫晃了晃手指。 观众哄堂大笑。

Annie kept singing. She made funny movements and silly faces to go with the words. She even added a little dance to her song. 安妮继续唱着。 她一边念词，一边做出滑稽的动作和鬼脸。 她甚至还给歌曲加了一点小舞蹈。

By the time she finished, the audience clapped and cheered and stamped their feet. 等她唱完，观众鼓掌、欢呼，还跺起脚来。

“Wonderful job, Andy!” Will said when Annie left the stage. “干得漂亮，安迪！”安妮下台时威尔说。

“You were brilliant!” Jack told her. “你太出色了！”杰克对她说。

“Thanks!” said Annie. She gave her scroll back to Will. “Do I go on again?” “谢谢！”安妮说。 她把卷轴还给威尔。 “我还要再上场吗？”

“Not until the end, when we all bow,” said Will. “要等到最后大家一起鞠躬，”威尔说。

Just then Jack heard the audience laughing again. He really wanted to see the play. So he found a shadowy spot at the back of the theater and watched from there. 就在这时，杰克又听见观众在笑。 他真的很想看这出戏。 于是他在剧院后面找了个阴暗的地方，从那儿看。

Jack couldn’t understand everything people said, but he could understand the story. It was about people in love. But none were able to marry the people they loved. 杰克听不懂人们说的每一句话，可他能听懂故事。 戏讲的是相爱的人。 可谁也没能和自己爱的人结婚。

The funniest part was about the fairy king and fairy queen. The king was mad at the queen. So he put magic juice on her eyelids to make her fall in love with the first person she saw. 最滑稽的部分是关于仙王和仙后的。 国王在生王后的气。 于是他在她眼皮上涂了魔法汁，让她爱上睁眼看见的第一个人。

Puck worked for the king. He wanted to make the king’s trick even funnier. So he put the head of a donkey on a funny man. When the queen woke up, she saw the donkey-man. The magic made her fall madly in love with him! 帕克给国王干活。 他想让国王的把戏更好笑。 于是他给一个滑稽的人安上了驴头。 王后醒来时，看见了那个驴头人。 魔法让她疯狂地爱上了他！

The fairy king finally broke the spell. Puck turned the donkey-man back into a human while he slept. When the man woke up, he looked about in wonder. 仙王终于解除了咒语。 帕克趁驴头人睡着时，把他变回了人。 那人醒来时，惊奇地四处张望。

“I have had a most rare vision,” he said. “I have had a dream … .” “我做了一个极其罕见的梦，”他说。 “我做了一个梦……。”

Jack whispered the words to himself. “ I have had a most rare vision. I have had a dream.” 杰克对自己小声念着这些话。 “我做了一个极其罕见的梦。 我做了一个梦。”

Beside him, a group of actors gathered for the last scene of the play. 在他旁边，一群演员聚在一起准备演最后一场。

“My lion mask is missing!” one of them wailed. “I cannot be a lion without my mask!” “我的狮面具不见了！”其中一个哭丧着脸说。 “没有面具我怎么演狮子！”

“Hush, of course you can,” said Will. “Just roar! And roar again!” “嘘，你当然可以，”威尔说。 “只管吼！再吼！”

Will pushed the actor onstage. He wiped his brow. Then he caught sight of Jack. 威尔把那个演员推上了舞台。 他擦了擦额头。 接着他突然看见了杰克。

“Get Andy!” he said. “It’s almost time for our bow.” “把安迪找来！”他说。 “快到我们鞠躬了。”

Annie? Where is Annie? Jack wondered. He hadn’t seen her in a while. He peeked into the costume room. She wasn’t there. 安妮？安妮在哪儿？ 杰克心里想。 他有一阵子没看见她了。 他往戏服间偷看。她不在。

Jack’s heart started to pound. He had a scary thought … . 杰克的心开始怦怦跳。 他闪过一个可怕的念头……。

“Oh, no,” he whispered. “哦，不，”他低声说。

Jack ran down the stairs. He opened the door. He was happy to see Annie running out from some trees behind the theater. 杰克跑下楼梯。 他打开门。 他高兴地看见安妮正从剧院后面的几棵树里跑出来。

“It’s time to bow!” he said, grabbing her hand. “Where have you been? What did you do?” “该鞠躬了！”他抓住她的手说。 “你去哪儿了？你干了什么？”

“I’ll show you later!” said Annie. “等会儿给你看！”安妮说。

Jack and Annie charged upstairs together. They found Will and some of the actors waiting. 杰克和安妮一起冲上楼。 他们看见威尔和几个演员在等着。

Puck was finishing his last speech onstage: 帕克正在台上念最后的台词：

So good night unto you all.

Give me your hands if we be friends … . 晚安，大家。如果我们是朋友，就给我你们的手……

“Andy! Jack!” said Will. He grabbed Annie and Jack. “安迪！杰克！”威尔说。 他抓住安妮和杰克。

Puck ended his speech. The audience gave a big hand. They clapped and whistled and shouted. 帕克念完了台词。 观众热烈鼓掌。 他们鼓掌、吹口哨、大声叫好。

Jack and Annie ran onstage with Will and the other actors. While the crowd cheered, they all bowed once … twice … and once again. 杰克和安妮跟着威尔和其他演员跑上舞台。 人群欢呼时，他们鞠了一躬……两躬……又再鞠了一躬。
