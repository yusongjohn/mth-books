6. Onstage! 6. 上台！

Will led Jack and Annie through a door into the back of the Globe Theater. Then he led them up a dark stairway. 威尔领着杰克和安妮穿过一扇门，进了环球剧院的后台。 然后他领着他们走上一段黑暗的楼梯。

As they headed upstairs, Jack heard laughter coming from the audience. His legs felt like jelly. 往上走时，杰克听见观众席传来笑声。 他的双腿软得像果冻。

“This way,” said Will. “这边走，”威尔说。

He led Jack and Annie into a crowded, dimly lit room. Actors were rushing about everywhere. Each seemed to be in his own world. One was pulling on a cape. Another was tying a rope around his waist. A third was whispering words to himself. 他领着杰克和安妮走进一间又挤又暗的房间。 演员们到处忙着跑来跑去。 每个人似乎都沉浸在自己的世界里。 一个人正在披上斗篷。 另一个人正往腰上系绳子。 第三个人正在自言自语。

“I’ll find your costumes,” said Will. “我去给你们找戏服，”威尔说。

As Will dug through a large basket of clothes, Jack and Annie looked around the costume room. It was crammed with fancy gowns, purple and blue capes, gold and silver wigs, stacks of hats, and masks. 威尔在大衣服筐里翻找时，杰克和安妮朝戏服间四处打量。 里面塞满了华丽的长裙、紫色和蓝色的斗篷、金银假发、成堆的帽子和面具。

“Cool,” whispered Annie. She touched a donkey mask and a lion mask. “These would make good disguises, huh?” “酷，”安妮小声说。 她摸了摸驴面具和狮面具。 “这些当伪装挺不错吧，哈？”

Jack was amazed she was acting so calm. Didn’t she know they were about to stand in front of three thousand people? The thought made him break into a sweat. His stomach felt fluttery. 杰克很吃惊她居然这么冷静。 难道她不知道他们马上要站在三千人面前吗？ 这个念头让他突然冒出一身汗。 他的胃里一阵翻腾。

“Here!” said Will. He handed them green tunics, hats, and slippers. “Put these on! No time to dally! Your parts come up soon!” “给！”威尔说。 他递给他们绿色短袍、帽子和鞋子。 “快穿上！没时间磨蹭了！ 你们很快就要上场了！”

Jack and Annie slipped behind a curtain and changed into their costumes. As they put on their hats, Annie hid her pigtails. 杰克和安妮溜到窗帘后面换上戏服。 戴帽子时，安妮把辫子藏了起来。

When they came out, Will handed them each a small scroll. “Here are your rolls,” he said. “They have only your lines on them, no one else’s.” 他们出来后，威尔给他们每人一个小卷轴。 “这是你们的台词卷，”他说。 “上面只有你们自己的台词，没有别人的。”

Jack unrolled his scroll. He had two long speeches to read. 杰克展开卷轴。 他有两段很长的台词要读。

“Wait a second,” he said. “I thought I just had a few lines. I didn’t know I had a ton.” “等一下，”他说。 “我以为我只有几句台词。 我都不知道自己有这么多。”

“Don’t worry,” said Will. “Just remember—speak very clearly and with feeling. And above all, act natural.” “别担心，”威尔说。 “只要记住——说得清楚，还要有感情。 最重要的是，要表现自然。”

Act natural? thought Jack. How do you act natural when you’re about to have a heart attack? 表现自然？杰克想。 眼看要心脏病发作了，还怎么表现自然？

Just then a short, chubby man burst into the costume room. He had curly hair and bright red cheeks. He was dressed all in green, too. 就在这时，一个矮胖的男人冲进了戏服间。 他有着卷曲的头发和红通通的脸颊。 他也一身绿色。

“For goodness’ sakes, Will!” he said in a frantic whisper. “What will we do?” “看在老天的份上，威尔！”他慌张地低声说。 “我们怎么办？”

“Don’t worry! Look who I’ve found! They both can read!” said Will. He pushed Jack and Annie forward. “Jack and Andy, meet Puck, ‘merry wanderer of the night.’ He’ll take you to the stage. Good luck!” “别担心！ 看看我找到了谁！ 他们俩都会读！”威尔说。 他把杰克和安妮往前推。 “杰克和安迪，来认识帕克，‘快乐的夜游者’。他会带你们上台。祝你们好运！”

Annie smiled. Jack groaned. 安妮笑了。杰克唉了一声。

“Come, boys!” said Puck. “Follow me!” “来吧，孩子们！”帕克说。 “跟我来！”

Puck led Jack and Annie out of the costume room into the hallway. Then he guided them to a dark area at the back of the stage. 帕克领着杰克和安妮走出戏服间，来到走廊。 然后他带他们到舞台后方一块黑暗的地方。

Actors stood silently nearby, waiting to go on. One wore a beautiful white gown. Others wore tattered brown rags. 演员们静静站在附近，等着上场。 一个人穿着漂亮的白袍。 其他人穿着破烂的棕色破布衣服。

Through an arch, Jack saw the roof of the stage. It was blue with stars and a moon. A huge crowd stood directly in front of the stage. More people watched from the galleries above. 透过一道拱门，杰克看见舞台的顶棚。 顶棚是蓝色的，上面有星星和月亮。 一大群人就站在舞台正前方。 更多人从上面的楼座观看。

Every single person in England is out there! Jack thought with horror. How did I let Annie talk me into this? 英格兰所有人都在外面！ 杰克惊恐地想。 我怎么会被安妮说服来干这个？

“I’ll take you onstage first,” Puck whispered to Jack. “When I say, ‘How now, spirit! Whither wander you?’, start reading your lines. Understand?” “我先带你上台，”帕克对杰克低声说。 “等我说‘喂，精灵！ 你往哪儿游荡？’，就开始读你的台词。明白了吗？”

Jack barely nodded. His mouth felt dry. He tried to swallow, but he couldn’t. 杰克几乎没点头。 他的嘴巴发干。 他想咽口水，可咽不下去。

Puck turned to Annie. 帕克转向安妮。

“You go onstage with the fairy queen,” he whispered. He pointed to the actor dressed in the beautiful white gown. “When she tells you to sing her to sleep, you start your song.” “你跟着仙后一起上台，”他低声说。 他指着那个穿着漂亮白袍的演员。 “等她让你唱歌哄她睡觉时，你就开始唱。”

“What’s the tune?” asked Annie. “是什么调子？”安妮问。

“Just make it up,” said Puck. “Now, if they yell rude things, do not stop. Just—” “随便编就行，”帕克说。 “还有，要是他们喊粗鲁的话，不要停。只管——”

“If who yells rude things?” Jack broke in. “要是谁喊粗鲁的话？”杰克插嘴道。

“The groundlings get a bit wild,” said Puck. “站票观众有点野，”帕克说。

“Groundlings?” said Jack. “站票观众？”杰克说。

“The rowdy folk who don’t have seats,” said Puck. “They’re standing close to the stage. If they throw rotten fruit, don’t stop, either. Just keep going.” “那些没有座位、爱起哄的人，”帕克说。 “他们站得离舞台很近。 要是他们扔烂水果，也不要停。继续演下去。”

That does it, thought Jack. He couldn’t go onstage—not with groundlings throwing things, not with three thousand people watching, not with a million lines to read—and not when he was about to throw up! 这下够了，杰克想。 他不能上台——不能面对扔东西的站票观众，不能面对三千观众，不能面对那么多要读的台词——更不能在快要吐出来的时候上台！

While Puck and Annie watched the show, Jack slipped away. He looked for the exit. Just as he found the stairs, he bumped into Will. 帕克和安妮看戏的时候，杰克溜走了。 他找起了出口。 刚找到楼梯，他就撞上了威尔。

“Where are you going?” Will whispered. “你要去哪儿？”威尔低声问。

“I can’t stay,” said Jack. “I’m sick!” “我待不下去了，”杰克说。 “我难受！”

Will sucked in his breath. But then he put his hands on Jack’s shoulders and spoke calmly. 威尔倒吸了一口气。 可接着他把双手放在杰克肩上，平静地说。

“Close your eyes for a moment, Jack,” he said. “闭上眼睛一会儿，杰克，”他说。

Jack closed his eyes. He could hear his heart pounding in his ears. 杰克闭上眼睛。 他听见自己的心跳在耳边怦怦响。

“There is nothing to fear,” Will whispered. “Imagine you are a fairy. You’re in the forest, on a summer night. See the silver moon overhead? Hear the owls? Hooo-hooo.” “没什么好怕的，”威尔低声说。 “想象你是一个小仙子。 你在森林里，在夏夜里。 看见头顶的银色月亮了吗？听见猫头鹰了吗？ 咕——咕。”

Will’s deep whisper seemed to cast a spell over Jack. He felt calmer. He could picture the silver moon. He could hear the hooting of the owls. 威尔低沉的耳语仿佛给杰克施了魔法。他平静了些。 他能想象出那轮银色的月亮。 他能听见猫头鹰的叫声。

“Are you in the forest, on a summer night?” asked Will. “你是在森林里、在夏夜里吗？”威尔问。

Jack nodded. 杰克点了点头。

“If you believe that, the audience will believe it, too,” whispered Will. “如果你相信，观众也会相信，”威尔低声说。

“We’re on!” whispered Puck. The chubby actor ran to Jack. He grabbed his hand and pulled him along. “该我们了！”帕克低声说。 那个胖乎乎的演员朝杰克跑来。 他抓住杰克的手，拉着他往前走。

Before he knew it, Jack was onstage! 他还没反应过来，就已经站在舞台上了！
