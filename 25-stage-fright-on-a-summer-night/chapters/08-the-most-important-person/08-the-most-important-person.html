8. The Most Important Person 8. 最重要的人

Will stepped forward and held up his hands. Slowly the crowd quieted down. 威尔走上前，举起双手。 人群渐渐安静下来。

“Thank you all,” he said. “And thank you to the most important person in all the world. She has graced us with her presence today.” “谢谢大家，”他说。 “也感谢全世界最重要的人。 她今天赏光光临了。”

Will made a sweeping bow to a woman in a gallery above the stage. The woman wore a white dress with pearls. A veil covered her face. 威尔向舞台上方楼座里的一位女士深深鞠躬。 那位女士穿着一件镶着珍珠的白裙子。 面纱遮住了她的脸。

The woman stood up and slowly lifted the veil. She had pale, wrinkled skin and small dark eyes. She wore a red wig. 那位女士站起来，慢慢掀开面纱。 她有着苍白起皱的皮肤和小小的黑眼睛。 她戴着红色假发。

The audience members let out a gasp. They all fell to their knees. 观众们倒抽了一口气。 他们全都跪了下去。

“Long live Queen Elizabeth!” said Will. “伊丽莎白女王万岁！”威尔说。

“Long live Queen Elizabeth!” the crowd shouted. “伊丽莎白女王万岁！”人群喊道。

“Long live Queen Elizabeth!” shouted Jack and Annie. “伊丽莎白女王万岁！”杰克和安妮也喊道。

The queen smiled. Her teeth were all black! The audience didn’t seem to mind. They cheered even louder. 女王笑了。 她的牙全是黑的！ 观众好像并不在意。 他们欢呼得更响了。

The queen raised one hand and the crowd instantly hushed. 女王举起一只手，人群立刻安静了。

“I thank you, my good people,” she said. “And I thank all these good players, every one. Today, they gave us a special kind of magic—the magic of theater. They turned the very daytime into night.” “谢谢你们，我的好子民，”她说。 “也谢谢所有这些优秀的演员，每一位。 今天，他们给了我们一种特别的魔法——戏剧的魔法。 他们把白昼变成了黑夜。”

“Oh, man,” whispered Jack. That was it—the special magic. Their search was over. “哦，天哪，”杰克小声说。 就是它——那种特别的魔法。 他们的寻找结束了。

The audience cheered again. When the actors left the stage, they gathered around Will to congratulate him on his success. 观众又欢呼起来。 演员们离开舞台后，围着威尔祝贺他的成功。

Annie pulled Jack aside. 安妮把杰克拉到一边。

“We found it!” she said. “The magic!” “我们找到了！”她说。 “魔法！”

“I know!” Jack said. “Will helped us. Let’s thank him!” “我知道！”杰克说。 “威尔帮了我们。我们去谢谢他！”

“Later,” said Annie. “First I have to show you something. I need your help! Quick!” “等会儿再说，”安妮说。 “我得先给你们看样东西。我需要你们帮忙！ 快点！”

Annie led Jack downstairs and outside. As people streamed away from the Globe, the late afternoon sun was going down. 安妮领着杰克下楼，走到外面。 人们从环球剧院散去时，傍晚的太阳正在落下。

“This way,” said Annie. She headed for the patch of trees behind the theater. “这边走，”安妮说。 她朝剧院后面那片树林走去。

When she and Jack stepped into the gloomy shade, Jack saw an odd figure near a tree. A purple cape barely covered his furry back. A golden wig and a lion mask barely hid his furry head. 她和杰克走进阴暗的树荫时，杰克看见树旁有个奇怪的身影。 一件紫色斗篷几乎遮不住他毛茸茸的背。 一顶金色假发和一个狮面具几乎遮不住它毛茸茸的头。

Jack gasped. “The bear! You stole him!” 杰克倒抽一口气。 “那只熊！你偷了他！”

“I had to,” said Annie. “I went to the cart when no one was looking. I put a costume on him. So if we passed people on the way here, they’d think he was an actor.” “我不得不这么做，”安妮说。 “趁没人注意时，我去了马车那儿。 我给他穿上戏服。 这样路上要是碰到人，他们会以为他是演员。”

“But you can’t just steal him!” said Jack. “可你不能就这么偷走他！”杰克说。

“I wasn’t stealing. I was saving,” said Annie. “I’m not sure what to do with him now. What do you think?” “我不是在偷。 我是在救他，”安妮说。 “我现在不知道拿他怎么办。 你觉得呢？”

Just then the bear’s owner charged into the woods. “Where’s my bear?” he shouted. His face was red. He was scowling. 就在这时，熊的主人冲进了树林。 “我的熊呢？”他喊道。他的脸涨得通红。 他怒气冲冲。

“Thieves!” he yelled. “Give him back! I’m selling him to the fights!” “小偷！”他大叫。 “还给我！ 我要把他卖去斗熊！”

“NO!” said Annie, standing between the bear and the man. “He’s a tame bear! Not a fighter!” “不行！”安妮站在熊和那人中间说。 “他是只温顺的熊！不是打架的！”

“She’s right!” said Jack, jumping in. “And besides, bear fighting is stupid! Really stupid!” “她说得对！”杰克突然插话道。 “再说，斗熊太蠢了！真的很蠢！”

“ ’Tis, indeed,” said a deep voice. “的确如此，”一个低沉的声音说。

Jack, Annie, and the bear’s owner whirled around. Will and Puck were standing at the edge of the woods. 杰克、安妮和熊的主人猛地转过身。 威尔和帕克正站在树林边。
