7. Stallion at Starlight 7. 星光下的骏马

Jack stared at Annie for a moment. “So … what do you have in mind?” he said. 杰克盯着安妮看了片刻。 “那……你有什么想法？”他说。

“We could try to train Bucephalus for Alexander,” said Annie. “Then maybe his dad would let him keep the horse.” “我们可以试着训练布塞法勒斯给亚历山大，”安妮说。 “那也许他爸爸会让他留下这匹马。”

“Are you serious?” said Jack. “You and me train him?” “你是认真的吗？”杰克说。 “你和我一起训练他吗？”

“We’ve done it before,” said Annie. “Remember when we went to the Wild West and helped save the mustangs? We learned some rules on how to treat a horse.” “我们以前做过一次，”安妮说。 “还记得我们去西部，帮忙救下野马那次吗？ 我们学会了怎么对待马的一些规则。”

“That journey was a long time ago,” said Jack. “那次旅程已经很久以前了，”杰克说。

“But you took notes,” said Annie. “I saw them in your notebook today.” “但你做了笔记，”安妮说。 “我今天在你的笔记本里看到了。”

“Yeah, well, we might have notes, but we don’t have much experience,” said Jack. “We weren’t there long enough.” “是啊，我们可能有笔记，但我们没什么经验，”杰克说。 “我们没在那里待太久。”

“Let’s just look at your notes,” said Annie. “我们来看看你的笔记吧，”安妮说。

Jack pulled out his notebook and found the notes he’d taken during their adventure in the Wild West. “Horse rules,” he read aloud. “Soft hand, firm voice, sunny attitude, praise, reward.” 杰克拿出他的笔记本，找到了他在西部冒险时做的笔记。 “马的规则，”他大声读出来。 “轻柔的手，坚定的声音，阳光般的态度，表扬，奖励。”

“Simple. We just have to be nice and kind and positive,” said Annie. “Let’s try it.” She started toward the gate. “很简单。 我们只要友善、善良、积极就好，”安妮说。 “我们试试看吧。”她朝大门走去。

“Are you crazy? There’s lots more to it than that,” said Jack, following her. “Those guys in the ring have tons of experience! And they couldn’t do anything with that horse!” “你疯了吗？ 这不仅仅是这些，”杰克跟着她说道。 “那些在环子里的人有超多经验！ 他们根本没办法对付那匹马！

“Forget those guys,” said Annie. “We’re a million times nicer to animals than they are. And I have a sunny attitude.” She opened the gate and started across the long stretch of grass to the stable. “忘了那些人吧，”安妮说。 “我们对动物比他们友好一百万倍。 而且我态度很阳光。”她打开栅栏，开始穿过长长的草地走向马厩。

“But that horse is dangerous, Annie,” Jack said as he followed her to the stable entrance. “Don’t just think you can tame him!” “但是那匹马很危险，安妮，”杰克一边跟着她走到马厩入口，一边说道。 “别以为你能驯服他！”

“Now you sound like King Philip,” said Annie. “Let’s find the horse first, then we’ll figure out what to do.” “你现在听起来像菲利普国王，”安妮说道。 “我们先找到那匹马，然后再想办法。”

Jack and Annie peeked into the shadowy stable. It smelled of wood, barley, and hay. Contented sounds of snuffling, snorting, and munching came from the stalls. From the other end of the barn, though, came neighing and stamping noises. 杰克和安妮偷偷 peeked 进入昏暗的马厩。 这里有一股木头、大麦和干草的味道。 畜栏里传来满足的哼哼、喷鼻和咀嚼声。 不过，从谷仓的另一头却传来嘶鸣和踏步的声音。

“I think he’s at the far end,” said Annie. She looked around. “I don’t think anyone’s in here now. Let’s look.” “我觉得他在最远的那头，”安妮说。 她环顾四周。 “我想现在这里没人了。我们去看看吧。”

“Move fast!” said Jack. They stepped into the dark stable. Jack grabbed a lamp from a wooden table. The flickering light cast strange shadows as he and Annie moved past the stalls toward the end of the barn. “动作快点！”，杰克说。 他们走进了黑暗的马厩。 杰克从木桌上的拿了一盏灯。 摇曳的灯光在他们和安妮走过畜栏，朝谷仓尽头移动时投下奇怪的影子。

Jack held the lamp high as they passed a white horse, several brown horses, and a brown-and-white spotted horse. Bucephalus was in the very last stall. 杰克高高举着灯，他们经过了一匹白马、几匹棕色的马，还有一匹棕白相间的斑马。 布塞法鲁斯就在最后一个马厩里。

His coat shone in the lamplight. He was completely black, except for a white star on his forehead. His eyes still had a wild look, and his ears were flattened against the sides of his head. Jack thought he looked angry. 他的皮毛在灯光下闪闪发亮。 他全身都是黑色的，除了额头上的一个白色星星。 他的眼睛仍然带着野性，耳朵紧贴着头的两侧。 杰克觉得他看起来很生气。

“Hi, Bucephalus,” Annie said in a warm, friendly voice. “Boy, are we happy to see you. And I know you’re happy to see us, too!” “你好，布塞法鲁斯，”安妮用温暖友好的声音说道。 “天啊，我们见到你真是太高兴了。 我知道你见到我们也很开心！”

“No he isn’t,” Jack said. “This isn’t going to work.” “他才不开心呢，”杰克说。 “这不行。”

But Annie lifted the latch on the door and calmly stepped inside, closing the door behind her. 但安妮抬起门闩，平静地走进去，然后关上了门。

“Don’t worry, Jack. I have a way with animals, remember?” said Annie. She jumped onto a bale of hay and put her leg over the horse’s back. “别担心，杰克。 我跟动物有办法，还记得吗？”安妮说。 她跳上一堆干草，把腿跨到马背上。

“No! No! You don’t just climb on—” said Jack. “不！不！ 你不能就这样爬上去—”杰克说。

The stallion gave a loud snort, then kicked his back hooves and dropped his head down, trying to buck Annie off. 那匹骏马发出一声响亮的喷鼻声，然后用后蹄踢了一下，低下头，试图把安妮甩下去。

“Whoa!” cried Annie, clutching the horse’s mane. “哇！”安妮喊道，紧紧抓住马鬃。

The horse hurled himself against the door. Jack barely got out of the way before the animal shot out of the stall. With Annie clinging to his mane, the horse ran through the long barn and out the open doorway! 马猛地冲向门。 杰克刚躲开，那只动物就从马厩里冲了出来。 安妮紧紧抓着马鬃，马冲过长长的马厩，跑出了敞开的门！

“Stop!” Jack shouted. He blew out the lamp and ran after the stallion and Annie. “停下！”杰克喊道。 他吹灭了灯，追着那匹马和安妮跑过去。

Outside in the riding ring, the horse reared. Annie was holding on with all her might. The stallion loomed above Jack, raking the air with his front hooves. Jack could see the whites of his eyes. 在骑马场外面，那匹马突然扬起了前蹄。 安妮用尽全力紧紧抓着不放。 那匹马高高立起，前蹄在空中划来划去。 杰克能看到它眼睛里的白眼球。

Jack stumbled backward and fell. At the same time, Annie tumbled off the horse. The stallion galloped across the grassy ring. 杰克往后退了一步，摔倒了。 与此同时，安妮从马上摔了下来。 骏马在草地上飞快地奔跑着。

Jack scrambled over to Annie. “You okay?” he cried. 杰克赶紧跑向安妮。 “你没事吧？”他喊道。

“Yes!” She sat up. “You were right … being nice wasn’t enough…,” she said, trying to catch her breath. “I made a fool of myself.… I guess I don’t have enough experience.” “没事！”她坐了起来。 “你刚才说得对……光是友善是不够的……，”她说，努力喘着气。 “我让自己出丑了……我想我缺乏经验。”

The stallion stopped near the fence. He stood against the purple-blue sky of early evening, snorting loudly. 骏马停在了围栏附近。 它站在傍晚紫色的天空下，大声地喷着鼻息。

“Don’t feel bad,” said Jack. “It would take tons of experience to train a horse like him.” “别难过了，”杰克说。 “要训练一匹像他这样的马，需要非常多的经验。”

“Well …” Annie was still struggling to breathe. “We … you know we could be great horse trainers if we really wanted to.” “嗯……”安妮还在努力呼吸。 “我们……你知道，如果我们真的想的话，可以成为很棒的马术训练师。”

“What do you mean?” asked Jack. “什么意思？”杰克问。

“The … the magic,” Annie said. “We sniff the magic mist and then … then make a wish to become great horse trainers.” “那……那魔法，”安妮说。 “我们闻到魔法雾，然后……然后许愿成为很棒的马术训练师。”

“But does this have anything to do with our mission?” Jack said. “If it doesn’t, it wouldn’t be practical to use our magic now.” “但这和我们的任务有关系吗？”杰克说。 “如果没关系，现在用魔法就不现实了。”

“It might not be practical,” said Annie, “but I feel like it’s the right thing to do.” “也许不现实，”安妮说，“但我感觉这是该做的事。”

Jack looked at the stallion. The animal made a soft blowing sound. He stared intensely back at Jack, as if trying to tell him something. “Okay,” said Jack, surprising himself. “I guess we could give it a try. Why not?” 杰克看着那匹骏马。 那动物发出了一声轻柔的喷气声。 他盯着杰克，好像想跟他说些什么。 “好吧，”杰克说，连他自己都感到惊讶。 “我想我们可以试试看。为什么不呢？”

“Great!” said Annie. “This is going to be so much fun!” She jumped to her feet. “太好了！”安妮说。 “这一定会非常有趣！”她一下子跳了起来。

“Slow down,” said Jack, standing up. He reached into his bag and pulled out the tiny glass bottle. The silver mist from the Isle of Avalon swirled inside. “So here’s what we do—we make a wish to be two great horse trainers.” “慢点，”杰克站起来说。 他把手伸进包里，拿出了一只小小的玻璃瓶。 来自阿瓦隆岛的银色雾气在里面旋转着。 “我们该这么做——许一个愿望，成为两个伟大的马术训练师。”

“Yes!” said Annie. “对！”安妮说。

“Okay,” said Jack. “I wonder what happens when you’re the greatest horse trainers ever.” “好，”杰克说。 “我想知道，当你成为世界上最棒的马术训练师时会发生什么。”

“We’ll soon find out,” said Annie. “我们很快就会知道的，”安妮说。

Jack lifted the bottle into the air. “We wish to be two great horse trainers!” Then he uncorked the bottle, closed his eyes, and deeply inhaled the magic mist. 杰克把瓶子举到空中。 “我们希望成为两位最棒的马术训练师！”然后他拔开瓶塞，闭上眼睛，深深吸了一口魔法雾气。

A mix of wonderful smells overcame him: sweet honeysuckle with damp summer grass and leaves in sunshine. 一股奇妙的气味扑面而来：甜美的金银花，混合着潮湿的夏日草地和阳光下的树叶的香味。

When Jack opened his eyes, he felt light-headed. He held the bottle out to Annie, and she inhaled the mist, too. “Ahhhh,” she breathed. 杰克睁开眼睛时，感觉晕晕乎乎的。 他把瓶子递给安妮，她也吸了一口雾气。 “啊——，”她轻声说道。

Jack corked the tiny bottle and put it away. He and Annie grinned at each other. “All set?” Annie asked. 杰克重新给小瓶子塞上瓶塞，把它收了起来。 他和安妮互相笑了笑。 “准备好了吗？”安妮问。

“You bet,” said Jack. He wasn’t worried now. He felt as calm and relaxed as if he had tamed a thousand wild stallions. “当然了，”杰克说。 现在他一点都不担心了。 他感觉平静又放松，就像他已经驯服了上千匹野马一样。

“Hello, Buddy,” Annie said to the stallion. “Do you mind if we just call you Buddy? It’s much easier than Bucephalus.” “你好， buddy，”安妮对那匹马说。 “我们能不能就叫你 buddy 呢？ 叫 bucephalus 可太麻烦了。”

Jack laughed quietly. Buddy was a better name. 杰克轻声笑了。 buddy 是个更好的名字。

Buddy pricked his ears as if he were listening to her. “Do you mind if we come closer, Buddy?” Annie asked. 布迪竖起耳朵，好像在听她说话。 “布迪，我们靠近一点你介意吗？”安妮问。

The horse didn’t move. 马儿没有动。

Annie started walking very slowly toward him. Jack walked with her. When they were close enough to touch him, Buddy backed up nervously. He swished his tail and arched his neck. 安妮慢慢地朝他走去。 杰克陪她一起走。 当他们靠近到能碰到他时，布迪紧张地往后退了一步。 他甩着尾巴，昂起头。

“It’s okay, Buddy,” Jack said in a soft voice. “It’s okay, it’s okay.” Jack concentrated on the stallion. He poured all his energy, all his compassion, and all his strength into the horse … until he felt that he and Buddy were one and the same creature. “没关系，布迪，”杰克用温柔的声音说。 “没关系，没关系。”杰克专注于这匹骏马。 他把所有的精力、所有的同情心和所有的力量都注入到这匹马上……直到他感觉到自己和布迪已经融为一体。

DO NOT COME CLOSE. 不要靠近。

“What?” Jack said, looking around. Was he hearing the horse’s thoughts? “什么？”杰克说道，环顾四周。 他是不是听到了马的想法？

“You don’t want us to come close to you, Buddy?” Annie asked. “你不想我们靠近你吗， buddy（伙伴）？”安妮问道。

Oh, man, thought Jack. Annie heard him, too. This must be what happens when you’re a really great horse trainer: you understand what horses are thinking! 天啊，杰克心想。 安妮也听到了他的想法。 这一定是当你成为一名非常出色的马术训练师时会发生的事：你能听懂马在想什么！

Jack looked deeply into the stallion’s eyes. He tried to hear the horse’s thoughts again. 杰克深深地注视着这匹骏马的眼睛。 他又试着去听马的想法。

DO NOT COME CLOSER. 不要靠近我！

“Okay, we won’t,” Jack said, keeping his voice calm and soothing. “We won’t come closer until you are ready.” “好吧，我们不会的，”杰克说，保持声音平静而温柔。 “在你准备好之前，我们不会靠近。”

“That’s right,” said Annie. “But tell us, why can’t we come close?” “没错，”安妮说。 “但是告诉我们，为什么我们不能靠近？”

NO RIDERS. NO RIDERS.

“Why no riders?” Jack asked the horse. “You once had riders, didn’t you?” “为什么没有骑手？”杰克问那匹马。 “你们以前有骑手，对不对？”

“We won’t hurt you, Buddy,” said Annie. “We promise.” “我们不会伤害你的， buddy，”安妮说。 “我们承诺。”

Buddy lowered his head. buddy 低下头。

LOST. 迷路了。

“You’re lost?” said Annie. “你迷路了吗？”安妮说。

LOST. 迷路了。

Jack remembered what Aristotle had told them. “He used to belong to the Royal Horse Master of Thessaly,” he said to Annie. He looked into the horse’s eyes again. “Is it your master?” he asked the stallion. “Is your master lost?” 杰克想起了亚里士多德告诉他们的事情。 “他以前是色萨利皇家马术师的马。”他对安妮说。 他又一次看着马的眼睛。 “这是你的主人吗？”他问那匹公马。 “你的主人迷路了吗？”

The horse threw back his head and sniffed the air. 那匹马扬起头，闻了闻空气。

Jack and Annie were quiet for a moment. Then Annie said softly, “I’m afraid your master is not coming back. Not ever.” 杰克和安妮安静了一会儿。 然后安妮轻声说：“我担心你的主人不会回来了。永远都不会。”

The horse was still. 那匹马一动不动。

“Do you miss him?” Annie asked. “你很想他吗？”安妮问。

The stallion tossed his head. Jack thought he heard a word. 那匹骏马甩了甩头。 杰克觉得自己听到了一个词。

SAD. 悲伤。

The stallion’s sadness washed over Jack, filling him with sorrow, too. “I’m sorry,” he said. 骏马的悲伤笼罩了杰克，他也感到很伤心。 “对不起，”他说。

“We’re really sorry,” said Annie. She sounded like she might cry. “我们真的很对不起，”安妮说。 她的声音听起来像是要哭了。

Jack took another step toward the horse. Annie stepped with him. This time, the stallion didn’t back away. 杰克又朝马儿迈了一步。 安妮也跟着他一起走。 这次，这匹骏马没有后退。

Annie breathed on the horse’s muzzle so he could smell her breath. Jack did the same. Then Annie touched the stallion’s forehead, and Jack touched him, too. Annie moved her hand down the horse’s nose, while Jack stroked the horse’s long neck. The animal’s velvety coat smelled of grass and wind. 安妮对着马儿的鼻子吹了口气，好让马儿闻到她的气息。 杰克也做了同样的事。 然后，安妮摸了摸骏马的额头，杰克也摸了摸它。 安妮的手沿着马儿的鼻子慢慢滑下，而杰克则抚摸着马儿长长的脖子。 这匹动物柔软的毛皮散发着青草和风的味道。

“Just know this,” Jack said to the stallion. He spoke tenderly and with all his heart. “Whatever happened to your master was not your fault. Not at all.” “你只要记住这一点，”杰克对骏马说。 他温柔地说，而且是发自内心的。 “你主人发生的事跟你一点关系都没有。一点都不。”

The stallion lowered his head and nuzzled Jack and Annie. His whole body shuddered, as if he were sighing with relief. 那匹骏马低下头，蹭了蹭杰克和安妮。 他的全身都在颤抖，好像在长长地叹了一口气。
