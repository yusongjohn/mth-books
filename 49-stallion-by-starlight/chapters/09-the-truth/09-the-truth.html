9. The Truth 9. 真相

The stallion stopped and neighed at the prince. Alexander stood with his legs apart and his arms crossed. He was not smiling. 骏马停了下来，对着王子嘶鸣。 亚历山大双腿分开，双臂交叉站在那里。 他没有笑。

“Hi there!” Annie said. “你好啊！”安妮说。

Alexander didn’t answer. He glared at Jack and Annie. “I shall have you punished for stealing him,” he said. “I have been looking for him everywhere.” 亚历山大没有回答。 他瞪着杰克和安妮。 “我要惩罚你们偷了他的事，”他说。 “我到处都找过他。”

“We didn’t steal him. We just took him out for a ride,” said Jack. “我们没偷他。 我们只是带他出去兜了兜风，”杰克说。

“Why did he let you two ride him?” Alexander asked. “Of all people?” “他为什么让你们两个骑他？”亚历山大问。 “所有的人？”

Jack started to make a comment about Alexander’s attitude, but then he made a different decision—he decided to be kind. “Maybe he let us ride him because he knew that you are a friend of ours,” he said. 杰克本来想评论亚历山大态度不好，但他改变了主意——他决定要友善一些。 “也许他让我们骑他，是因为他知道你是我们的朋友，”他说。

The prince looked confused by Jack’s answer. “Really?” he asked. 王子对杰克的回答感到困惑。 “真的吗？”他问。

Jack nodded. 杰克点了点头。

“Well, I suppose that could be possible,” Alexander said. He cleared his throat and took a deep breath. “So where did he take you?” he asked. The anger had left his voice. Now it was filled with curiosity. “好吧，这也许是真的，”亚历山大说。 他清了清嗓子，深吸了一口气。 “那他带你去了哪里？”他问。 他的声音里已经没有了愤怒。 现在，他们充满了好奇。

“We just rode through town and down the main road and around the swamps and fields,” said Annie. “I wish you’d been with us!” She swung her leg over the horse’s back and slid to the ground. “我们刚刚骑着马穿过镇子，沿着主路，绕过沼泽和田野，”安妮说。 “真希望你当时和我们在一起！”她把腿跨过马背，滑到地上。

Jack did the same. The stallion neighed and brushed his head against Annie, then Jack. Annie giggled. “That tickles,” she said. 杰克也做了同样的事。 那匹骏马嘶叫着，把头靠向安妮，然后是杰克。安妮咯咯地笑了起来。 “这有点痒，”她说。

“How did you tame him?” Alexander asked. “你们是怎么驯服他的？”亚历山大问。

“We listened to him,” said Jack. “我们听他说话，”杰克说。

“Listened to him?” the prince asked. “What do you mean?” “听他说话？”王子问。 “你是说什么意思？”

“It was sort of like … we forgot about ourselves and paid total attention to him instead,” said Jack. “有点像……我们忘了自己，全都注意着他了，”杰克说。

Alexander watched as Annie and Jack stroked the stallion’s mane. “My father vows he will not waste his gold on this horse,” he said. “But I … I see something very special in him. I believe he is extraordinary.” 亚历山大看着安妮和杰克抚摸那匹骏马的鬃毛。 “我父亲发誓他不会浪费金币买这匹马，”他说。 “但……但我看到他身上有非常特别的东西。 我相信他很不寻常。”

“He is,” said Jack. “He’s incredibly loyal.” “他确实很特别，”杰克说。 “他非常忠诚。”

“And he has a great heart,” said Annie. “他有一颗伟大的心，”安妮说。

“I know. I can tell,” said Alexander. “我知道。 我能看出来，”亚历山大说。

“Maybe you could tell your father that you will pay for him yourself,” suggested Annie. “Do you get an allowance?” “也许你可以告诉你的父亲，你会自己付钱给他，”安妮建议道。 “你有零花钱吗？

“I do not know what that means,” said Alexander. “But no matter. I doubt that my father would agree to anything I propose. He has no respect for my opinions—or my accomplishments.” “我不知道这是什么意思，”亚历山大说道。 “但没关系。 我怀疑我的父亲会同意我提出的任何建议。 他一点都不尊重我的意见——或者我的成就。”

“Your father is wrong not to give you respect,” said Jack, and he meant it. “One day all the history books will call you Alexander the Great.” “你爸爸不给你尊重是错的，”杰克说，他确实是这么想的。 “有一天，所有的历史书都会称你为亚历山大大帝。”

“Are you mocking me?” Alexander said, scowling. “你是在嘲笑我吗？”亚历山大皱着眉头说。

“No! It’s true, I promise,” said Jack. “没有！ “是真的，我发誓，”杰克说。

“Believe us,” said Annie. “We know.” “相信我们，”安妮说。 “我们知道。”

“But listen,” said Jack. “Being great doesn’t mean you go around bragging about yourself all the time.” “但是听着，”杰克说。 “成为伟大的人并不意味着你总是到处炫耀自己。”

“Don’t tell everyone that you are a living Greek god,” said Annie, “or that you’re going to be master of the universe.” “别告诉别人你是个活着的希腊神，”安妮说，“也不要告诉别人你将成为宇宙的主宰。”

“Or that you are the best athlete in the known world,” said Jack. “Just accept that no matter what you can do, you’re still a regular human being, a person like everyone else.” “或者告诉别人你是世界上最好的运动员，”杰克说。 “只是接受无论你能做什么，你仍然是个普通人，和大家一样。”

“Are you insulting me?” said Alexander. “你是在侮辱我吗？”亚历山大说。

“No!” said Jack with a smile. “Stop that! We’re all regular human beings. Why don’t you just let yourself be a real person for a change and laugh at yourself? Be okay with making mistakes and looking like a fool.” “没有！”杰克微笑着说。 “住手！” 我们都是普通的人类。 为什么你不试着改变一下，当个真正的人，然后笑一笑自己呢？ 接受犯错，接受看起来像个傻瓜。

“Jack’s right,” said Annie. “Know that sometimes you’re great and sometimes you’re terrible. You’re strong and you’re weak.” “杰克说得对，”安妮说。 “要知道，有时候你很棒，有时候你很糟糕。 你很强，你也可能很弱。”

The prince frowned. “A fool? Terrible? Weak?” he said. 王子皱起了眉头。 “一个傻瓜？很糟糕？ 很弱？”他说。

“The truth is this: nobody’s perfect. Even you,” said Jack. “So just accept it.” “真相是这样的：没有人是完美的。” “就连你，”杰克说。 “那你就接受吧。”

“You speak nonsense,” said Alexander. “你胡说八道，”亚历山大说。

“It’s called humility,” said Jack. “这叫谦虚，”杰克说。

“Humility?” the prince said slowly. “谦虚？”王子慢慢地说。

“Jack!” said Annie. “Look!” She held her hand close to Jack’s face. The Ring of Truth was glowing as if it were made of fire. “杰克！”安妮说。 “看！”她把她的手靠近杰克的脸。 真相之环正在发光，就像它是由火制成的一样。

Jack smiled again. “Yes. Humility,” he said to the prince. That was the secret of greatness that Merlin wanted them to discover on this mission. 杰克又笑了。 “是的。” “谦虚，”他对王子说。 这就是梅林希望他们在此次任务中发现的伟大秘密。

“But you cannot expect me to show humility to the world,” said Alexander, shaking his head. “No mighty king would ever do that.” “但是你不能指望我要向全世界表现谦虚，”亚历山大摇头说。 “没有哪个强大的国王会这么做。”

“Well, no matter what you have to do to be a mighty king, you can at least have humility inside yourself,” said Annie. “And if you do, I promise this horse will trust you.” “好吧，不管你要做些什么才能成为一位强大的国王，你至少可以在自己内心拥有谦虚，”安妮说。 “如果你这么做，我保证这匹马会信任你。”

“There he is!” a gruff voice said. “在那里！”一个粗哑的声音说。

Jack, Annie, and Alexander turned to see two grooms coming out of the barn. One of the grooms held a whip. 杰克、安妮和亚历山大转过身，看见两个马夫从谷仓里走出来。 其中一个马夫手里拿着一根鞭子。

“You were looking for this horse?” Prince Alexander asked sternly. He stepped between the stallion and the grooms. “你是在找这匹马吗？”亚历山大王子严肃地问。 他走到马和马夫之间。

“The king wants to see him in the ring again, with the others,” said the first groom. He moved around Alexander toward the stallion. As the man’s shadow fell over Buddy, the stallion snorted loudly and shied away. “国王想让他和其他马一起再次进入赛场，”第一个马夫说。 他绕过亚历山大朝那匹马走去。 当那个人的影子落在布迪身上时，那匹马大声嘶叫，猛地躲开。

Alexander jumped in front of the groom. “Get away from him!” he ordered the man. “Leave him alone!” 亚历山大跳到马夫前面。 “离他远点！”他对那个人下令。 “别打扰他！”

The groom stepped back. 马夫退后一步。

“Leave us!” roared Alexander. “滚开！”亚历山大怒吼道。

The two grooms turned and disappeared back into the barn. 两个 groom 转身，又消失回了谷仓里。

Buddy neighed. 布迪嘶叫了一声。

“Shh, my friend, shh,” Alexander said softly, stepping toward the stallion. “You mustn’t be afraid.” “嘘，我的朋友，嘘，”亚历山大轻声说，朝那匹骏马走去。 “你不能害怕。”

The horse shook out his mane and backed up. 那匹马抖了抖鬃毛，往后退了一步。

“Alexander, listen to him,” Jack whispered. “亚历山大，听他说话，”杰克小声说。

“Remember … humility,” whispered Annie. “记住……谦卑，”安妮低声说。

Alexander stopped and stared into the horse’s eyes. The horse stared back at him. The prince slowly held out his hand and stroked the horse’s head. Then he spoke softly. “Something frightened you just now,” he said, “when the man stepped in front of you. What was it?” 亚历山大停下了，盯着马的眼睛看。 那匹马也回望着他。 王子慢慢地伸出一只手，抚摸着马的头。 然后他轻声说道。 “刚才有人挡在你面前时，一定是有什么东西吓到了你，”他说，“是什么？”

Buddy blinked and lowered his head. 布迪眨了眨眼，低下头。

“The … shadows?” asked Alexander. The prince held still. Buddy raised his head. He and Alexander stared at each other for a moment. “是……影子？”亚历山大问。 王子一动不动。 布迪抬起了头。 他和亚历山大彼此盯着看了片刻。

Then Alexander turned to Jack and Annie. “I listened, and I heard him!” he said in a voice filled with wonder. “He is frightened by shadows. I must remember to always mount him with both of us facing the sun.” 然后亚历山大转向杰克和安妮。 “我听了，我听到了他！”他用充满惊奇的声音说。 “他被影子吓到了。” 我必须记住，每次骑他时，我们都要面朝太阳。”

Jack and Annie grinned at each other. Jack didn’t know if Alexander had really heard the horse’s thoughts, but he knew that Alexander and the stallion would be fine together. 杰克和安妮互相笑了笑。 杰克不知道亚历山大是否真的听到了马的想法，但他知道亚历山大和这匹马在一起会很安全。

“Now you should prove to your father that you can ride him,” Annie said to the prince. “现在你应该向你父亲证明，你能骑着他，”安妮对王子说。

Alexander nodded. “Yes,” he said, his eyes sparkling. He looked like a different person. “Yes, I will.” 亚历山大点了点头。 “是的，”他说，眼里闪着光。 他看起来像另一个人。 “是的，我会的。”

“Good,” said Jack. “Go stand with the king. We’ll take Bucephalus through the barn and release him into the ring.” “很好，”杰克说。 “去站在国王身边。” 我们要带布塞法鲁斯穿过谷仓，然后放他进竞技场。“

“Thank you!” said Alexander. He left them and disappeared around the corner of the barn. “谢谢！”亚历山大说。 他离开了他们，消失在谷仓的角落。

“Come on, Buddy,” Jack said. “来吧，布迪，”杰克说。

Jack and Annie led the horse through the barn. Before they reached the entrance to the ring, they stopped. The stallion snorted and dipped his head, and they both rubbed his neck and his nose. 杰克和安妮牵着马穿过谷仓。 在他们到达竞技场入口之前，他们停了下来。 这匹骏马喷着鼻息，低下头，他们两个都摸了摸它的脖子和鼻子。

“Please help Alexander, Buddy,” Jack said. “He needs a loyal friend. And you need one, too.” “请帮帮亚历山大，布迪，”杰克说。 “他需要一个忠诚的朋友。 你也需要一个。”

“We love you,” Annie said. “Don’t forget that.” “我们爱你们，”安妮说。 “别忘了这一点。”

A groom appeared at the stable doorway. “Bucephalus!” he shouted. 一个 groom 出现在马厩的门口。 “布塞法鲁斯！”，他喊道。

Jack and Annie reluctantly pulled away from the stallion. “Be great, Bucephalus!” said Jack, his voice cracking. 杰克和安妮不情愿地离开了那匹骏马。 “祝你一切顺利，布塞法鲁斯！”，杰克说，声音有些颤抖。

Bucephalus looked at them with soft eyes. Then he walked with the groom out of the barn, into the riding ring. 布塞法鲁斯用温柔的眼神看着他们。 然后他跟着 groom 走出了谷仓，走进了骑马场。

“Come on!” said Annie. “Let’s go watch!” “快点！”，安妮说。 “我们去看看吧！”

Jack and Annie hurried to the back door of the stable. They shot outside into the sunlight and ran around the building to the riding ring. 杰克和安妮赶紧跑到马厩的后门。 他们冲出去，来到阳光下，然后绕着建筑物跑到骑马场。

The king, his companions, and Aristotle were lined up at the fence. Prince Alexander stood with his father. All eyes were on the black stallion as he trotted around the ring, tossing his head and snorting. 国王、他的随从和亚里士多德排着队站在围栏边。 亚历山大王子和他的父亲站在一起。 所有人的眼睛都盯着那匹黑马，它在场地上慢跑，甩着头，还喷着鼻子。

“Good morning!” Aristotle called to Jack and Annie. They ran to join him. “早上好！”亚里士多德对杰克和安妮喊道。 他们跑过去加入他。

“The king asked about you last night and this morning,” he said in a soft voice. “What have you been doing?” “昨晚和今早，国王都问起过你们，”他用轻柔的声音说道。 “你们在做什么？”

“Training them,” Annie said breathlessly. “在训练它们，”安妮气喘吁吁地说。

“Training who?” the philosopher asked. “训练谁？”哲学家问道。

“The stallion,” said Annie. “那匹骏马，”安妮说。

“The prince,” said Jack. “那位王子，”杰克说。

Aristotle raised his eyebrows, but before he could speak, the king’s voice rang out. “Come back, you fool!” 亚里士多德挑了挑眉毛，但还没等他说话，国王的声音响起来了。 “回来吧，你这个傻瓜！”，

Jack, Annie, and Aristotle all looked at the ring. 杰克、安妮和亚里士多德都看向了戒指。

Prince Alexander had hopped over the fence. He was heading toward the black stallion. 亚历山大王子跳过了围栏。 他正朝那匹黑马走去。
