10. A Place of Honor 10. 一个荣誉之地

“Alexander! Do not try to ride him!” King Philip yelled at the prince. “Do not be a fool!” “亚历山大！” “别试图骑他！”菲利普国王对王子喊道。 “别犯傻！”

Alexander ran around the ring. Soon he was running alongside Bucephalus. When the stallion was facing the sun, Alexander vaulted onto his back. 亚历山大在马场周围跑了起来。 很快，他就在布塞法鲁斯身边奔跑。 当这匹骏马面向太阳时，亚历山大跳上了它的背。

Bucephalus broke into a canter. Horse and boy moved together perfectly around and around the riding ring. 布塞法鲁斯开始小跑起来。 马和男孩一起完美地在骑马场周围一圈又一圈地奔跑。

The King’s Companions clapped and cheered. Jack, Annie, and Aristotle joined them. King Philip stared in amazement. 国王的随从们鼓掌欢呼。 杰克、安妮和亚里士多德加入了他们。 菲利普国王惊讶地盯着看。

The prince raised his right arm in the air. He looked over at Jack and Annie and shouted with victory. 王子举起右手，高高举过头顶。 他朝杰克和安妮看了看，高兴地大声喊起来。

Laughing, they both gave him a thumbs-up. 他们笑着，都竖起了大拇指。

“You’re great, too, Buddy!” Annie shouted. “你也很棒， buddy（ buddy 是朋友的意思）！”安妮喊道。

The horse neighed. 马儿叫了一声。

The king himself then broke into applause. He was smiling with pride at Alexander. 国王本人随后鼓起掌来。 他看着亚历山大，满脸骄傲的笑容。

“How amazing!” said Aristotle. “That horse will have the highest place of honor as the prince’s horse now.” “太棒了！”亚里士多德说道。 “那匹马现在将成为王子的马，享有最高的荣誉。”

“Cool,” said Jack. He sighed, and then looked at Annie. “Are you ready to go home?” “真酷，”杰克说。 他叹了口气，然后看向安妮。 “你准备好回家了吗？”，

“Ready,” she said with a grin. “准备好了，”她笑着说。

“Must you leave now?” said Aristotle. “I know King Philip plans to invite you to stay in the Royal House. Will you not stay and help me educate Alexander?” “你必须现在就离开吗？”亚里士多德说。 “我知道菲利普国王计划邀请你住进王宫。 你不会留下来帮助我教育亚历山大吗？

“No. We have to go back to our parents,” said Annie. “Pretty soon we’ll start missing them.” “不。” “我们必须回去找我们的父母，”安妮说。 “很快我们就会开始想他们了。”

“Don’t worry,” Jack said. “You’ll do a good job with Alexander. All the world will say that someday.” “别担心，”杰克说。 “你会把亚历山大照顾得很好。” 全世界都会说，总有一天。”

“Just get him to work on the idea of humility,” said Annie. “先让他想想谦虚这个想法，”安妮说。

“Humility?” asked the philosopher. “谦虚？”哲学家问。

“Yes,” said Jack. “Tell him that one of the secrets of greatness is humility. He’s great enough that he can afford to have humility.” “对，”杰克说。 “告诉他，伟大之一的秘密是谦逊。” 他足够优秀，可以保持谦虚。”

“Even if only you and he and Bucephalus know it,” added Annie. “即使只有你、他和布塞法鲁斯知道，”安妮补充道。

“I will remember,” said the philosopher. “我会记住的，”哲学家说道。

“Thanks for all your help,” said Jack. “谢谢你们的帮助，”杰克说道。

“Tell Alexander good-bye for us, please,” said Annie. “替我们向亚历山大说再见好吗，”安妮说。

Jack and Annie stepped away from the fence and headed toward the hill path. They crunched over the pebbles, down to the square. Then they started down the main road, away from town. 杰克和安妮走开篱笆，朝山路上走去。 他们踩着鹅卵石，走到了广场。 然后他们沿着主路走，离开小镇。

They passed the field where the warriors were still marching. They passed the shepherd, the sheep, the goat herder, the goats, and the farmer plowing with the ox. They passed the rocky meadows dotted with cows, until they came to the olive grove. They were about to head between the trees when they heard galloping behind them. 他们经过了战士们仍在行进的那片田野。 他们经过了牧羊人、绵羊、山羊牧人、山羊，还有用牛耕地的农夫。 他们经过了布满奶牛的岩石草地，直到来到了橄榄树林。 他们正要穿过树林时，听到后面传来马蹄声。

They turned and saw Prince Alexander thundering toward them on the back of Bucephalus. Clouds of dust billowed behind the horse. 他们转过身，看到亚历山大王子骑着布西法勒斯朝他们疾驰而来。 马后面扬起了大片尘土。

“Jack and Annie!” Alexander cried. He brought Bucephalus to a halt. “杰克和安妮！”亚历山大喊道。 他让布塞法鲁斯停下来。

The stallion shook out his mane, then dipped his head and whinnied. Jack and Annie rubbed his muzzle. “Hey, you,” said Jack. “It’s good to see you again!” 这匹骏马抖了抖鬃毛，然后低下头，发出一声嘶鸣。 杰克和安妮摸了摸他的鼻子。 “嘿，你，”杰克说。 “很高兴再次见到你！”

The prince was out of breath. “You … you did not say good-bye!” he said. 王子气喘吁吁。 “你……你没有说再见！”他说。

“Sorry!” said Jack. “对不起！”杰克说。

“We have to get home now,” said Annie. “我们现在必须回家了，”安妮说。

“You did a great job!” said Jack. “你做得太棒了！”杰克说。

“You really did!” said Annie. “You and Bucephalus were made for each other!” “你真的做得很好！”安妮说。 “你和布塞法勒斯真是天生一对！”

“My father thinks so, too,” said Alexander, smiling broadly. “After he saw me ride today, he said, ‘My son, you had better find a larger kingdom, because mine will not be big enough for you!’ ” “我父亲也这么认为，”亚历山大微笑着说道。 “在他今天看到我骑马之后，他说：‘我儿，你最好找一个更大的王国，因为我的王国对你来说太小了！’”

“Oh, brother,” Jack said, “so much for humility.” “哦，兄弟，”杰克说，“这可真是谦虚啊。”

Surprisingly, Alexander laughed. “Thank you for everything you did for me,” he said. 出人意料的是，亚历山大笑了。 “谢谢你为我做的一切，”他说。

“You’re welcome,” said Annie. “Have a good life with Bucephalus.” “不用谢，”安妮说。 “祝你和布塞法勒斯过上好日子。”

“I will. Jack and Annie, you make me want to travel all over the world with him,” said Alexander. “You make me want to know about all things—like koala bears and kangaroos.” “我愿意。” “杰克和安妮，你们让我想和他一起环游世界，”亚历山大说。 “你们让我想了解所有的事物——比如考拉熊和袋鼠。”

“That’s great,” said Annie. “太棒了，”安妮说。

“You make me want to surround myself with thinkers and scientists and travelers,” said Alexander. “你们让我想让自己被思想家、科学家和旅行者包围，”亚历山大说。

“Cool,” said Jack. “酷毙了，”杰克说。

“You make me want to sing my song to the four quarters of the earth!” shouted Alexander. “你们让我想把我的歌唱给地球的四个角落听！”亚历山大喊道。

Jack smiled. “Do that. Go sing your song,” he said. 杰克笑了。 “去做吧。 去唱你的歌，”他说。

“I will! Farewell, my friends!” said the prince. “We are going back to inspect my army now!” He and Bucephalus turned around and took off down the dusty road. “我愿意！” 再见了，我的朋友们！”王子说道。 “我们现在要回去检查我的军队了！”他和布塞法鲁斯转过身，沿着那条尘土飞扬的小路飞奔而去。

“Give those army guys a rest, why don’t you?” yelled Annie. “让那些军队的人休息一下，怎么样？”安妮喊道。

Jack laughed. “Come on,” he said. 杰克笑了。 “快点，”他说。

He and Annie ran through the olive grove to the rope ladder. They climbed up into the tree house and looked out the window. In the distance the prince and his magnificent horse were dashing up the dirt road. 他和安妮跑过橄榄树林，来到绳梯前。 他们爬进树屋，从窗户往外看。 在远处，王子和他的骏马正沿着土路飞奔而来。

“I almost expect them to take off and soar into the sky,” said Annie. “At least that’s what riding Buddy felt like to me.” “我几乎以为他们会起飞，飞向天空，”安妮说。 “至少对我来说，骑布迪的感觉就是这样。”

Jack’s heart was heavy as they watched Alexander and Bucephalus disappear into a cloud of dust lit by the morning sun. “Good-bye, Buddy,” he said. 杰克看着亚历山大和布塞法鲁斯消失在晨光中扬起的尘土里，心里很难过。 “再见了，布迪，”他说。

“Bye, Buddy,” Annie echoed. “再见，布迪，”安妮重复道。

Jack sighed. “Well, the good news is Buddy will have a place of honor for life.” 杰克叹了口气。 “好消息是，布迪将永远享有荣誉的位置。”

“And Alexander will have a friend for life,” said Annie. “亚历山大也会永远有一个朋友，”安妮说。

Jack picked up their Pennsylvania book from the corner of the tree house. He pointed to a picture of the Frog Creek woods. “I wish we could go home!” 杰克从树屋角落拿起他们的宾夕法尼亚州书籍。 他指着一张蛙溪镇森林的图片。 “真希望我们能回家！”

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来了。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转起来。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。

A soft summer breeze blew through the tree house window. Jack and Annie were wearing their own clothes again. 一阵柔和的夏日微风从树屋的窗户吹进来。 杰克和安妮又穿上了自己的衣服。

“Mission accomplished,” said Jack. “任务完成了，”杰克说。

“I’d better leave the ring here,” said Annie. She pulled the Ring of Truth off her finger and carefully placed it on the floor in a patch of sunlight. Jack put the bottle of magic mist and the book about Macedonia beside the ring. “我最好把戒指留在这里，”安妮说。 她把真理之戒从手指上取下来，小心地放在地板上的一片阳光里。 杰克把装着魔法雾的瓶子和关于马其顿的书放在戒指旁边。

“Good. Let’s go,” said Annie. “好。 我们走吧，”安妮说。

“Wait—how can we give our secret of greatness a place of honor?” Jack asked. “等等——我们怎么才能给我们的伟大秘密一个荣誉的位置呢？”杰克问。

Annie shrugged. “We could just write it down,” she said. 安妮耸了耸肩。 “我们可以把它写下来，”她说。

“I guess that’ll work,” said Jack. He carefully tore one of the last pieces of paper out of his notebook and wrote in large letters:



 “我想这应该可以，”杰克说。 他小心地从笔记本里撕下最后的一张纸，用大写字母写上：

HUMILITY



 谦虚

Jack slipped the paper under the Ring of Truth. Maybe it was just the morning light, but the ring seemed to glow brighter as it lay on the paper. 杰克把这张纸 slips 在真理之戒下面。 也许只是早晨的光线，但戒指躺在纸上时似乎亮得更耀眼了。

Jack pulled on his backpack. “Ready?” he asked. 杰克背上了他的背包。 “准备好了吗？”他问。

“Yep,” said Annie. “Let’s go sing our song to the four quarters of the earth.” “准备好了。”安妮说。 “让我们唱首歌，送给地球的四个角落吧。”

“Why not?” said Jack. “为什么不呢？”杰克说。

Jack and Annie went down the ladder. Then they walked together through the dappled sunshine. The air smelled of summer. Crows called out to each other. 杰克和安妮顺着梯子走下去。 然后他们一起走在斑驳的阳光下。 空气里弥漫着夏天的味道。 乌鸦互相叫着。

“It feels good to be home,” said Annie. “回到家里感觉真好，”安妮说。

“No kidding,” said Jack. “可不是嘛，”杰克说。

They came to the edge of the Frog Creek woods and crossed the street and started up the sidewalk. 他们来到蛙溪镇森林的边缘，穿过街道，开始沿着人行道走起来。

“I’m so thirsty,” said Jack. “我渴死了，”杰克说。

“Me too,” said Annie. “Hey, we can have some of Dad’s lemonade.” “我也是，”安妮说。 “嘿，我们可以喝点爸爸做的柠檬水。”

“Cool,” said Jack. “太棒了，”杰克说。

“I’m really glad Dad’s our father instead of King Philip the Second of Macedonia,” said Annie. “我真的很高兴爸爸是我们的爸爸，而不是马其顿的菲利普二世国王，”安妮说。

“Tell Dad that,” said Jack. “告诉爸爸去吧，”杰克说。

“Yeah, I will,” said Annie. “It’ll totally confuse him.” They both laughed, and then took off running for home. “是的，我会的，”安妮说。 “这会让他完全搞糊涂。”他们俩都笑了，然后飞快地跑回家。
