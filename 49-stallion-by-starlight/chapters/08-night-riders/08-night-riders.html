8. Night Riders 8. 夜骑者

Stars twinkled in the night sky. Jack and Annie were quiet for a long time. Then Annie gently rubbed the horse’s neck and said, “Hey, Buddy, let’s go for a ride. Want to?” 夜空中星星闪闪发亮。 杰克和安妮安静了很久。 然后安妮轻轻摸了摸马的脖子，说：“嘿， buddy，我们去骑一骑吧。想不想？”

The stallion stood very still for a moment. Jack wondered if the horse understood. But then he raised his head. 那匹骏马站了一会儿，一动不动。 杰克想知道这匹马是不是听懂了。 但接着，他抬起头来。

YES. 好啊。

“Great. You go first,” Annie said to Jack. “太棒了。 你先来，”安妮对杰克说。

Jack grabbed a handful of the horse’s mane. Buddy didn’t move. He didn’t snort or balk. Jack took a couple of short steps and pushed off the ground. As if he had springs on his feet, he gracefully vaulted onto the horse’s back. 杰克抓了一把马鬃。 布迪一动不动。 他没有喷鼻息，也没有退缩。 杰克迈出几步，然后跳了起来。 就像他的脚下装了弹簧一样，他优雅地跳上了马背。

Jack reached out his hand. Annie took it. Then, as smoothly as a gymnast, she pushed off the ground and leapt onto the horse’s back behind Jack. 杰克伸出了手，安妮抓住了它。 接着，她像体操运动员一样流畅地跳起来，落在了杰克身后的马背上。

Jack and Annie settled comfortably into place, balancing themselves. Jack felt as if he’d lived on the back of a stallion all his life. He grabbed Buddy’s mane again and held on tightly. He gripped with his legs and leaned forward and whispered, “Let’s go.” 杰克和安妮舒服地坐好，努力保持平衡。 杰克感觉他这辈子好像一直都在一匹骏马背上生活。 他又抓住了布迪的鬃毛，紧紧地抓着不放。 他用腿紧紧夹住，身体前倾，小声说：“我们出发吧。”

The stallion stamped the ground. Then he started walking slowly forward. Jack and Annie moved in perfect rhythm with his step. When they came to the gate of the riding ring, Jack pushed it open with his foot. 骏马用蹄子重重地踩了下地面。 然后他慢慢地开始向前走。 杰克和安妮的节奏和他完全一致。 他们来到骑马场的门口时，杰克用脚把门踢开了。

The stallion pranced out of the ring. Soon his hooves were crunching lightly over the pebble path leading down to the square. The town was quiet under a blanket of stars. Market stalls had shut down for the night. Merchants, craftsmen, and shoppers had gone home. 骏马欢快地从骑马场里走了出来。 很快，他的蹄子轻快地踩着鹅卵石小路，朝广场走去。 小镇在星星的覆盖下安静了下来。 集市的摊位已经为夜晚关掉了。 商贩、工匠和购物者都回家了。

When the stallion reached the square, a warm wind urged him forward. He quickened his gait and began to trot. Jack and Annie moved in time with his steps: one-two, one-two, one-two. 当那匹骏马到达广场时，一股温暖的风吹着它向前。 它加快了步伐，开始小跑起来。 杰克和安妮随着它的步伐移动：一、二，一、二，一、二。

The stallion picked up speed, until he began to canter. Jack and Annie moved in time to a different, smoother rhythm: one-two-three, one-two-three. Jack felt as if they were all dancing together. 骏马加快了速度，直到它开始慢跑起来。 杰克和安妮随着一种不同的、更平稳的节奏移动：一、二、三，一、二、三。 杰克感觉好像他们都在一起跳舞。

When the stallion came to the wide road that led from the square, he began to gallop. He took long, swinging strides, until his hooves barely touched the ground. 当骏马来到从广场延伸出去的宽阔道路时，它开始奔跑起来。 他迈着长长的、摇晃的步子，直到蹄子几乎不再触地。

Jack had the feeling that his legs had become one with the horse’s legs, his breath had become one with the horse’s breath, his skin had become one with the horse’s skin. 杰克感觉自己的腿已经和马的腿融为一体，自己的呼吸也和马的呼吸融为一体，自己的皮肤也和马的皮肤融为一体。

Buddy ran on, unafraid of the dark. He galloped along the dirt road, passing the moonlit military field where the king’s army was still drilling for war. 布迪继续向前奔跑，一点也不害怕黑暗。 他沿着泥路奔跑，经过月光照耀的军营，那里的国王军队仍在为战争做训练。

The stallion kept galloping. He passed meadows where sheep and cows slept under the stars. He passed quiet farmhouses and barking dogs. 这匹骏马继续奔跑着。 他跑过了有绵羊和奶牛在星光下睡觉的草地。 他跑过了安静的农舍和吠叫的狗。

As Buddy galloped lightly over the countryside, Jack and Annie knew how to sit evenly and how to breathe deeply. They knew when to lean forward and when to shift their weight back. If they wanted Buddy to slow down or speed up or turn, they only had to shift their bodies slightly, and the horse seemed to understand. 当布迪轻快地在乡间奔跑时，杰克和安妮知道该怎么坐得平稳，该怎么深呼吸。 他们知道什么时候该向前倾身，什么时候该把重心往后移。 如果他们想让布迪慢下来、加速或转弯，他们只需要稍微调整一下身体，马似乎就能明白。

Buddy soon left the road and started over a grassy field. Jack couldn’t see what was ahead, but he trusted the horse’s instincts. He trusted his own, too. He felt completely safe. buddy 很快离开了道路，开始穿过一片草地。 杰克看不到前方有什么，但他相信马的直觉。 他也相信自己的直觉。 他感觉非常安全。

When Buddy jumped over narrow ravines, Jack knew how to relax his body and cling to the horse’s mane. When Buddy splashed through swamps and marshes, Jack knew how to stay centered on the horse’s back. 当 buddy 跳过狭窄的峡谷时，杰克知道该怎么放松身体，紧紧抓住马的鬃毛。 当 buddy 跳进沼泽和泥潭时，杰克知道该怎么保持在马背上。

All through the night, Jack, Annie, and Buddy traveled over the countryside. When the horse finally slowed down to a steady walk, Jack began to grow sleepy. He rested his head on Buddy’s neck. 整晚，杰克、安妮和 buddy 穿过乡村的田野旅行。 当马终于慢下来，以稳定的步伐行走时，杰克开始感到困倦。 他把头靠在 buddy 的脖子上。

Jack heard frogs croaking in the wet reeds and crickets chirping in the dry grass. His body rocked as Buddy’s hooves tapped over the hard, cracked earth, through olive groves and rocky fields. He closed his eyes …. 杰克听到湿漉漉的芦苇丛里青蛙在叫，干草丛里蟋蟀在鸣叫。 他的身体随着布迪的蹄子在坚硬、开裂的土地上踏过，穿过橄榄树林和岩石遍布的田野而摇晃。 他闭上了眼睛……

“Jack.” Annie nudged him from behind. “杰克。”安妮从后面推了他一下。

“What?” Jack asked in a daze. “怎么了？”杰克迷迷糊糊地问。

“Wake up.” “醒醒。”

“What?” Jack opened his eyes. “怎么了？”杰克睁开了眼睛。

The sun was up. In the rosy dawn, Buddy was ambling along the wide dirt road that led back to town. Sheep had risen to their feet and were grazing on dewy grass. A cool breeze blew by Jack’s face. It smelled of open fields and damp woolly lambs. 太阳已经升起来了。 在玫瑰色的黎明中，布迪正沿着那条通往镇上的宽阔土路慢慢走着。 羊群站了起来，在沾满露水的草地上吃草。 一阵凉爽的风吹过杰克的脸。 这里飘着开阔田野和湿漉漉小羊羔的味道。

“Guess what?” said Annie. “Our hour was up a long time ago.” “猜猜看？”安妮说。 “我们早就超过时间了。”

“What do you mean?” said Jack, sitting up straight. “什么意思？”杰克坐直了身子说。

“The magic hour, when we were great horse trainers,” said Annie. “就是那个魔法时间，我们当时是伟大的马术训练师。”安妮说。

“What about it?” said Jack. “怎么了？”杰克说。

“It ended,” said Annie. “For a long time, we’ve been riding Buddy just as ourselves.” “结束了，”安妮说。 “很长一段时间以来，我们一直只是以自己的样子骑着布迪。”

“Really?” Jack said. “The magic ended?” “真的吗？”杰克说。 “魔法结束了？”

“Don’t worry,” said Annie. “Buddy doesn’t mind riders now. We helped him understand that we’re his friends, and that it’s okay to let other people ride him. He’s not waiting for his master anymore.” “别担心，”安妮说。 “布迪现在不介意有人骑他了。 我们帮助他明白，我们是他的朋友，让别人骑他是可以的。 他不再等着他的主人了。”

“Oh, wow,” Jack said softly. He stroked Buddy’s damp neck. “Thank you for the great ride.” “哦，哇，”杰克轻声说。 他抚摸着布迪湿漉漉的脖子。 “谢谢你给我的精彩骑行。”

The stallion whinnied and kept trotting. Soon he trotted past the military field again, where the king’s soldiers were still marching. “Remind me never to join the Macedonian army,” said Jack. 这匹骏马嘶叫了一声，继续小跑着。 很快，他又小跑着经过了军营，那里的国王士兵们还在行军。 “提醒我以后千万别加入马其顿军队，”杰克说。

Annie laughed. “We’d better get Buddy back to the barn before everyone wakes up,” she said. 安妮笑了。 “我们最好在大家醒来之前，把布迪送回谷仓，”她说。

“Okay. Then we’ll figure out what to do next,” said Jack, “about helping Alexander and stuff.” “好的。 然后我们再想想接下来该怎么做，”杰克说，“比如怎么帮助亚历山大，还有其他事情。”

“And we have to learn a truth about greatness from him, too,” said Annie. “Remember our mission?” She held out her hand, reminding Jack about the Ring of Truth. “而且我们还得从他那里学到关于伟大的真相，”安妮说。 “还记得我们的任务吗？”她伸出手，提醒杰克关于真理之环的事情。

“Right,” said Jack. He’d almost forgotten. “Keep checking that ring to see if it’s glowing.” “对，”杰克说。 他差点忘记了。 “继续检查那个戒指，看看它有没有发光。”

“I am,” said Annie. “Don’t worry.” “我在检查，”安妮说。 “别担心。”

The stallion gathered speed and cantered up the road toward the market square. By now, the sellers were setting out their fish and fruits and vegetables. 那匹骏马加快了速度，沿着道路小跑着朝市场广场奔去。 这时，商人们已经开始摆出他们的鱼、水果和蔬菜。

Jack looked up at the hilltop. King Philip’s white mansion caught the fiery light of the rising sun. “We need to take you back to your stall now, Buddy,” he said. 杰克抬头看向山顶。 菲利普国王的白色豪宅被升起的太阳的火红光芒照得闪闪发亮。 “我们需要把你送回你的摊位那儿去， buddy，”他说。

The stallion stepped carefully up the pebble path. When they reached the stable area, Jack spied the king and his companions walking toward the riding ring. “Oh, no! They’re here already,” he said. 骏马小心翼翼地沿着鹅卵石小路前进。 当他们到达马厩区域时，杰克看见国王和他的随从正朝骑马场走来。 “哦，不！ 他们已经来了，”他说。

“Then let’s take Buddy through the stable’s back entrance,” said Annie. “Hurry!” “那我们从马厩的后门带布迪进去，”安妮说。 “快点！”

“Good idea,” said Jack. He shifted his weight. “Turn, Buddy, go right.” “好主意，”杰克说。 他调整了一下重心。 “转，布迪，往右走。”

The stallion changed direction and headed for the back of the stable. But as they drew closer, Jack saw someone standing in the open doorway. 那匹骏马改变了方向，朝马厩的后方走去。 但当他们越来越近时，杰克看见有人站在敞开的门口。

It was Prince Alexander. 那是亚历山大王子。
