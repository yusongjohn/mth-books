1. The Ring of Truth 1. The Ring of Truth

“I love looking at your notebook,” Annie said. “It helps me remember all of our adventures.” She turned a few pages. “Aww, polar bears in the Arctic. The cubs were so cute … and Pompeii and the volcano! Oh my gosh, remember Hercules?” “我特别喜欢看你的笔记本，”安妮说。 “它能帮我记住我们所有的冒险。”她翻了几页。 “啊，北极的北极熊。 小熊宝宝太可爱了……还有庞贝和火山！ 天啊，还记得赫拉克勒斯吗？

“Yep,” said Jack without looking up. “记得，”杰克没有抬头地说。

It was a warm day in June. Jack and Annie were sitting on the front porch of their house. While Annie thumbed through his notebook, Jack was reading a book about giant pandas in China. 那是一个六月温暖的白天。 杰克和安妮正坐在他们家的前廊上。 安妮翻着他的笔记本时，杰克正在读一本关于中国大熊猫的书。

“Ooh,” said Annie as she flipped through more pages. “The ghost town in the Wild West! The invisible piano player, remember?” “哦，”安妮一边翻着更多的页面一边说道。 “西部的鬼城！ 还记得那个看不见的钢琴师吗？”

“Yep.” “记得。”

Annie turned another page. “Remember Australia? The baby kangaroo, the koala, the dingoes! The forest fire!” 安妮又翻了一页。 “还记得澳大利亚吗？ 那只小袋鼠、考拉、野犬！还有森林大火！”

Jack looked up. “Yep,” he said. “Look, that’s all cool, but I can’t talk now. I’m trying to read the last page of my book.” 杰克抬起头。 “对，”他说。 “听着，这都挺酷的，但我现在不能说话。 我正在读我书的最后一页。”

“You’re going to need a new notebook soon. This one is almost full.” Annie closed the notebook and put it into Jack’s backpack. She stretched. “I think I’ll go for a bike ride,” she said. “Maybe stop by the library … go to the pool.” “你很快就要用新笔记本了。 这本快写满了。”安妮合上笔记本，把它放进杰克的背包里。她伸了个懒腰。 “我想我去骑自行车，”她说。 “也许去图书馆……去游泳池。”

Jack closed his book. “Done!” he said. “Now I need something new to read. I’ll ride to the library with you.” As he pulled on his backpack, Jack caught sight of something out of the corner of his eye. He turned, and what he saw was incredible. 杰克合上他的书。 “完成了！”他说。 “现在我需要找点新的东西来读。 我跟你一起去图书馆。”他背上背包时，杰克突然从眼角看到了什么东西。 他转过身，看到的景象令人难以置信。

A small penguin was standing on the sidewalk in front of their house. 一只小企鹅正站在他们家房子前面的人行道上。

“Penny?” Jack said. “佩妮？”杰克说。

“Oh!” cried Annie. “ Oh! Oh! Oh!” She dashed off the porch. “哦！”安妮喊道。 “哦！”她从门廊上冲了出去。

Jack ran after Annie. They both knelt down beside the little penguin. “What are you doing here?” Annie asked. 杰克追着安妮跑。 他们俩蹲下来，站在小企鹅旁边。 “你在这里干什么？”安妮问。

Jack picked Penny up, and he and Annie stroked the penguin’s downy head. “Hey, what’s happening, Penny?” he said. “Why are you here?” 杰克把佩妮抱起来，他和安妮轻轻抚摸着企鹅柔软的头部。 “嘿，佩妮，怎么了？”他说。 “你为什么在这里？”

Peep.

“The tree house!” said Annie. “It must be back.” “树屋！”安妮说。 “它一定回来了。”

“Hey, guys!” Jack and Annie’s dad called from behind the screen door of their porch. “嘿，孩子们！”杰克和安妮的爸爸从门廊的屏风门后喊道。

Annie quickly jumped in front of Jack to hide the little penguin. “What is it, Dad?” she said. 安妮迅速跳到杰克前面，把那只小企鹅藏起来。 “怎么了，爸爸？”她说。

“I just made some fresh lemonade,” their dad said. “我刚做了些新鲜的柠檬水，”他们的爸爸说。

“Thanks!” Annie said. “We’ll have some as soon as we come back!” “谢谢！”安妮说。 “我们回来后就喝一些！”

“We’re heading into the woods for a few minutes!” said Jack. “我们要进树林待几分钟！”杰克说。

“Okay. It’ll be in the fridge,” their dad said. “好的。 它在冰箱里，”他们的爸爸说。

“Thanks,” said Jack. “See you later!” “谢谢，”杰克说。 “再见！”

“Let’s go!” said Annie. “我们走吧！”安妮说。

Holding Penny in his arms, Jack hurried with Annie down the sidewalk. “Did you come here with Teddy and Kathleen?” he asked the penguin. 抱着小宾尼，杰克和安妮赶紧沿着人行道跑下去。 “你是和泰迪、凯瑟琳一起来的吗？”他问企鹅。

Peep. 咕噜。

“Is that yes or no?” asked Jack. “这是答应还是不答应？”杰克问。

Peep.

“Yes,” Jack said. “是的，”杰克说。

“No,” said Annie at the same time. “不，”安妮同时说道。

“I guess we’ll soon find out,” said Jack. “我想我们很快就会知道了，”杰克说。

Jack and Annie crossed the quiet street and headed into the Frog Creek woods. As they hurried through the dappled light, the air smelled of summer. Squirrels scurried up trees. Crows cawed to one another. 杰克和安妮穿过安静的街道，走进了蛙溪镇的树林里。 当他们匆匆穿过斑驳的阳光时，空气里充满了夏天的味道。 松鼠们急忙爬上树。 乌鸦们互相叫着。

When Jack and Annie came to the tallest oak, they stopped. The tree house was nestled in the branches near the top. A long rope ladder dangled down to the ground. 当杰克和安妮来到最高的橡树前，他们停了下来。 树屋静静地藏在树枝间，靠近树顶的位置。 一条长长的绳梯从树上垂下来，一直挂到地面。

“Teddy! Kathleen!” shouted Annie. “泰迪!” 安妮喊道：“凯瑟琳！”

There was no answer. 没有人回答。

Peep. 咕咕。

“So you came here all by yourself?” Jack said to Penny. “Is something wrong in Camelot? Are Teddy and Kathleen okay? What about Morgan and Merlin?” “所以你是自己一个人来到这里的？”杰克对彭妮说。 “卡梅洛特出什么事了吗？“ 泰迪和凯瑟琳还好吗？ 那摩根和梅林呢？

“Let’s go!” said Annie. She started up the rope ladder. Still holding the little penguin, Jack awkwardly climbed after her. “我们走吧！”安妮说。 她开始爬绳梯。 杰克仍然抱着那只小企鹅，笨拙地爬了上去。

“Oh, wow!” said Annie when she reached the tree house. “哦，哇！”安妮到达树屋时说道。

“Oh, wow what? ” asked Jack. He hoisted Penny into the tree house and climbed in after her. Then he also whispered “Oh, wow.” “哦，哇什么？”杰克问。 他把小企鹅抱进树屋，然后爬了进去。 然后他也小声说：“哦，哇。”

An old man with a long white beard, a pointed hat covered in stars, and a red cloak stood in a shadowy corner. 一位留着长长的白胡子、戴着缀满星星的尖帽子、披着红色斗篷的老人站在阴暗的角落里。

“Merlin!” breathed Annie. “梅林！”安妮屏住呼吸说。

“I see Penny found you,” the master magician of Camelot said in his velvety voice. The little penguin waddled to Merlin and stood beside him. 卡梅洛特的魔法大师用他那柔和的声音说：‘我看到彭妮找到你了。’ 那只小企鹅摇摇晃晃地走到梅林身边，站在他旁边。

“Yes,” said Jack. “She came right up to our house.” “是的，”杰克说。 “她直接来到我们家。”

“Is something wrong in Camelot?” asked Annie. “卡梅洛特出什么事了吗？”安妮问。

“No, all is fine,” said Merlin. “Your friends are well. I just decided I wanted to visit you myself.” “没有，一切都很好，”梅林说。 “你的朋友们都很好。 我只是决定想亲自来看看你们。”

“Cool,” said Jack shyly. Merlin had never come to Frog Creek all by himself. “太酷了，”杰克害羞地说。 梅林以前从未一个人来到蛙溪镇。

“Let me tell you what is on my mind,” said Merlin. “I have been thinking very deep thoughts, pondering questions that wise men and women have pondered through the ages.” “让我告诉你我心里在想什么，”梅林说。 “我一直在深入思考，思考着那些智者们从古至今一直在思考的问题。”

“Pondering?” asked Annie. “思考？”安妮问。

“Ponder means to think carefully about a subject,” Jack said. “Right?” he asked Merlin. “Ponder means to think carefully about a subject,” 杰克说。 “对吗？”他问梅林。

“Exactly,” said Merlin. “I have been pondering questions about life. For instance, I have been wondering about the idea of greatness. What are the secrets of greatness? What makes a person truly great?” “没错，”梅林说。 “我一直在思考关于生命的问题。 比如，我一直在思考伟大的概念。 什么是伟大的秘密？ 什么让一个人真正伟大？”

“That is a good question,” said Jack. “这是一个好问题，”杰克说。

“I cannot answer it by myself, as I do not live in your world—the world of time and mortals,” said Merlin. “So, on each of your next four missions, you will meet someone who will help you learn a true secret of greatness.” “我不能自己回答，因为我并不生活在你们的世界——那个时间和凡人的世界，”梅林说。 “所以，在你们接下来的四次任务中，你们会遇到某个人，他会帮助你们学习一个真正的伟大秘密。”

“That sounds like fun,” said Annie. “那听起来很有趣，”安妮说。

“I hope it will be,” said Merlin. “To begin, how would you like to meet someone called Alexander the Great?” “我希望会是这样，”梅林说。 “首先，你想见一个叫亚历山大大帝的人吗？”，

“Oh, man, I’ve heard of him!” said Jack. “哦，天哪，我听说过他！”，杰克说。

“I haven’t,” said Annie, “but he sounds … well, great.” “我没听说过，”安妮说，“但他听起来……嗯，真的很厉害。”

Merlin smiled. 梅林笑了。

“Can I ask a question?” Jack said. “我能问一个问题吗？”杰克说。

“Of course,” said Merlin. “当然可以，”梅林说。

“To be practical, how will we know when we’ve found a secret of greatness?” Jack asked. “实际一点说，我们怎么知道我们找到了伟大的秘密呢？”杰克问。

“I pondered that as well,” said Merlin. “I have brought something magical to help you.” The magician reached into his cloak and pulled out a small gold ring. “I call this the Ring of Truth.” “我也思考过这个问题，”梅林说。 “我带来了一件神奇的东西来帮助你们。” 魔法师伸手到他的斗篷里，拿出一枚小小的金戒指。 “我称它为‘真理之戒’。”

“The Ring of Truth,” Annie repeated. “‘真理之戒’，”安妮重复道。

“I have cast a spell on the ring,” said Merlin. “Wear it on your journeys. When you discover a true secret of greatness, the ring will glow.” “我已经在戒指上施了魔法，”梅林说。 “在你们的旅程中戴上它。 当你发现一个真正的伟大秘密时，戒指会发光。”

“May I wear it?” Annie asked Merlin. He nodded. Annie held out her hand, and the magician slipped the gold band onto her finger. “我可以戴上它吗？”安妮问梅林。他点了点头。 安妮伸出她的手，魔法师把那枚金戒指戴在她的手指上。

“I have brought another bit of magic as well,” said Merlin. He reached into his cloak again, and this time, he pulled out a tiny glass bottle. Silver mist swirled inside of it. “我还带来了一点其他的魔法，”梅林说。 他再次伸手到他的斗篷里，这次他拿出了一只小小的玻璃瓶。 银色的雾气在里面盘旋。

“Mist gathered at first light on the first day of the new moon on the Isle of Avalon,” said Merlin. “新月第一天清晨的第一缕阳光照在阿瓦隆岛时，雾气就会出现，”梅林说。

“Wow,” said Annie. “哇，”安妮说。

“The magical mist will allow you to experience greatness in yourselves, ” said Merlin. “When you need help, make a wish to have an extraordinary talent. Then breathe in the scent of the mist, and for one hour that talent will be yours. The magic will work only once on each journey.” “这神奇的雾气能让你们发现自己内心的伟大，”梅林说。 “当你需要帮助时，许愿拥有非凡的才能。 然后吸入雾气的香味，一小时内，这个才能就是你的。 这种魔法在每次旅程中只能生效一次。”

“Thanks!” said Jack. He took the bottle from Merlin and put it into his backpack. “谢谢！”杰克说。 他从梅林那里接过瓶子，把它放进背包里。

“And finally, Morgan asked me to give you a research book.” The magician reached into his cloak for a third time and pulled out a book. “最后，摩根让我给你们一本研究书。”这位魔术师第三次伸手到斗篷里，拿出了一本书。

图中文字：ANCIENT MACEDONIA 图中文字：古代马其顿

“I’ve never heard of Macedonia,” said Jack. “我从来没听说过马其顿，”杰克说。

“It is the kingdom where Alexander was born,” said Merlin. “The ancient Macedonians are fierce and warlike, so travel cautiously.” “那是亚历山大大帝出生的王国，”梅林说。 “古代马其顿人很凶猛，喜欢打仗，所以要小心旅行。”

Fierce? Warlike? Jack wanted to hear more. 凶猛？好战？ 杰克想听更多。

Merlin picked Penny up and raised his hand in farewell. “Go now,” he said. “Good luck.” 梅林抱起彭妮，挥手告别。 “现在出发吧，”他说。 “祝你好运。”

“Bye, Merlin. Bye, Penny,” said Annie. “再见，梅林。 再见，彭妮，”安妮说。

Peep. 咦？

“Wait—” began Jack. “等等——”杰克说。

But before Jack could ask another question, Merlin and Penny vanished. 但就在杰克还没问出第二个问题时，梅林和彭妮消失了。

“Let’s go,” said Annie. She pointed at the cover of the book. “I wish we could go there!” she said. “我们走吧，”安妮说。 她指着书的封面。 “我希望我们能去那里！”她说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止不动。
