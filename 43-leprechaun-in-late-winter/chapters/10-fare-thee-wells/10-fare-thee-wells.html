10. Fare-thee-wells 10. 再见了

“Well, that was simple and direct,” said Annie. “嗯，这很简单直接，”安妮说。

“Yeah.…” Jack felt dazed. “是啊……”杰克感到晕晕的。

Augusta looked at Jack and Annie. “I saw them,” she said, her eyes wide. “I finally saw the Shee. I really saw them!” She burst out laughing. Her laughter was so full of joy that Jack and Annie started laughing, too. 奥古斯塔看着杰克和安妮。 “我看到了他们，”她说，眼睛睁得大大的。 “我终于看到谢伊了。 我真的看到了他们！”她突然笑出声来。 她的笑声充满了快乐，杰克和安妮也开始笑了。

“I saw them! I saw them!” Augusta kept repeating. “I saw the Shee! And now I have important things to do!” “我看到了他们！ 我看到了他们！”奥古斯塔不停地重复着。 “我看到了谢伊！ 现在我有重要的事情要做啦！”，

“Yes, you do,” said Annie. “是的，你有，”安妮说。

“I must learn the old language! And I must start gathering stories at once, that’s what Queen Aine told me!” said Augusta. “I can’t wait to visit the old storytellers! There’s Mary Sheridan and Biddy Early, too! I’ll start with Mary! Let’s go see her right now! Hurry! Will you come with me? Hurry!” “我必须学会古老的语言！ “我必须马上开始收集故事，这是艾恩女王告诉我的！”奥古斯塔说。 “我迫不及待想去拜访那些老讲故事的人！ 还有玛丽·谢里登和比迪·埃利！ 我先去拜访玛丽！ 我们现在就去见她！快点！ 你和我一起来吗？快点！

“Sure,” said Annie, “but just a second.” She turned to Jack. “What about Willy?” “当然可以，”安妮说，“不过等一下。”她转身对杰克说。 “那威尔呢？

“Who’s Willy?” asked Augusta. “谁是威尔？”，奥古斯塔问。

“A friend of ours,” said Jack, looking around. “He said he’d wait for us. But where was he going to wait? Willy!” “我们的一个朋友，”杰克说，一边环顾四周。 “他说他会等我们。” 但是他在哪里等呢？威利！

“Willy!” called Annie. “威利！”安妮喊道。

“I guess he’s still somewhere on the other side of the river …,” said Jack. “我想他可能还在河对岸的某个地方……”，杰克说道。

“That’s too bad,” said Annie. “那太糟糕了，”安妮说道。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “是的，”杰克说。

“Come, let us go see Mary now!” said Augusta, grabbing Annie’s hand and pulling her along. “来吧，我们现在去见玛丽！”奥古斯塔一边说，一边抓住安妮的手，把她拉了过去。

Jack looked around for Willy one last time. He was sad about not seeing the leprechaun again. But he was a little relieved, too. He knew they couldn’t keep their part of the deal: teaching Willy how to play the magic whistle. 杰克最后一次环顾四周，寻找威利。 他因为没能再见到那个精灵而感到难过。 但他也感到有点释然。 他知道他们不能履行协议：教威尔学会吹魔法口哨。

“Come on, Jack!” cried Augusta. “快点，杰克！”奥古斯塔喊道。

Jack followed the two girls through the wet meadow, over the stone wall, down the dirt lane, and across the muddy field to Mary Sheridan’s cottage. 杰克跟着两个女孩穿过湿漉漉的草地，翻过石墙，沿着泥路走，穿过泥泞的田野，来到玛丽·谢里登的小屋前。

“Mary! Mary!” Augusta called. She dashed ahead of Jack and Annie. She didn’t even stop to knock. She threw open the door to the cottage. “Oh!” she said, freezing in her tracks. “玛丽！ 玛丽！”奥古斯塔喊道。 她冲到杰克和安妮前面。 她甚至没有停下来敲门。 她猛地推开小屋的门。 “哦！”她说道，吓得站在原地不敢动。

Jack and Annie caught up with Augusta and looked inside. 杰克和安妮追上奥古斯塔，朝里面看了看。

Mary was sitting in front of her fire. Next to her was a small man wearing a green jacket and a three-cornered red cap with a white feather. 玛丽正坐在火炉前。 她旁边站着一个穿绿色夹克、戴着三尖红色帽子、帽上插着一根白羽毛的小矮人。

“Willy!” cried Annie. “威利！”安妮喊道。

Annie and Jack hurried past Augusta into the cottage. 安妮和杰克匆匆穿过奥古斯塔，跑进小屋。

“You’re here!” said Jack. “你在这里！”杰克说道。

“Of course! I said I’d wait for you!” said the leprechaun. “I see you found your dear friend and brought her home. Good work!” “当然！ 我说过我要等你们的！”小矮人说道。 “我看到你找到了你亲爱的朋友，并把她带回家了。干得好！”

Augusta stood in the doorway, gaping at Willy. “Who are you?” she asked. 奥古斯塔站在门口，惊讶地盯着威利。 “你是谁？”她问。

“Name’s Willy. Just plain Willy,” said the leprechaun. “I usually leave before you visit Mary. But now that you’ve seen the Shee, I suppose there’s no point hiding from you anymore.” “我叫威利。 就只是个叫威利的精灵。” “我通常在你们去拜访玛丽之前就离开了。 但既然你们已经看到了希伊，我想现在躲着你们也没什么意思了。”

“Are—are you real?” asked Augusta. “你——你是真的吗？”奥古斯塔问。

“Who knows?” said the leprechaun. “Maybe I’ m real and you’re not! Depends on which one of us is asking the question!” “谁知道呢？”精灵说。 “也许我是真的，而你不是！ 这取决于你们当中谁在问这个问题！”

Everyone laughed. “Good point,” said Annie. 大家都笑了。 “说得对，”安妮说。

“Well, we’d better be getting home,” said Jack. He wanted to leave before Willy asked Annie for his whistle lesson. “好吧，我们最好回家了，”杰克说。 他想在威尔利请安妮上口哨课之前离开。

Jack took Annie’s hand and started backing toward the door. “Thanks for helping us, Willy,” he said. 杰克牵起安妮的手，开始往门口退。 “谢谢你的帮助，威尔利，”他说。

“Yeah, and thanks, Mary, for everything!” said Annie, waving. “Good-bye, Augusta! Good-bye, everyone!” “是啊，还有谢谢你，玛丽，为我们做的一切！”安妮一边说，一边挥手。 “再见，奥古斯塔！ 再见，大家！”

“Wait just a minute, my friends,” said Willy. “等等，我的朋友们，”威尔利说。

Uh- oh, thought Jack. 糟了，杰克心想。

“Have you forgotten our deal?” said Willy. “My fingers are itching to make beautiful music for Mary.” “你们忘记我们的约定了吗？”威尔利说。 “我的手指都痒痒的，想为玛丽演奏美妙的音乐。”

“Well, you see … there’s a problem with that,” said Jack, squirming. “嗯，你瞧……这有个问题，”杰克说着，扭来扭去。

A frown crossed Willy’s face. “A problem?” he said. “How could there be a problem? Find your friend, teach Willy to play the whistle. Doesn’t get any simpler than that.” 威尔的脸皱了起来。 “有问题？”他说。 “怎么会有问题？ 找到你的朋友，教威尔吹口哨。 没有比这更简单的了。”

“Right,” said Jack. “对，”杰克说。

“So what’s the problem?” asked Willy. “那问题是什么？”威尔问。

“Well, the whistle …” Jack didn’t know how to finish. “嗯，这个口哨……”杰克不知道该怎么说下去。

“I’ll answer that question,” said Annie. “Willy, I’m going to be simple and direct and honest with you.” “我来回答这个问题，”安妮说。 “威利，我会简单、直接、诚实地告诉你。”

“Yes …?” said the leprechaun. “是……？”小精灵说。

“Merlin the magician gave us the whistle to help us on our missions,” said Annie. “The whistle is magic, and the magic only works once. Without the magic, I don’t really know how to play. So I can’t teach you. There you have it.” “梅林魔法师给了我们哨子，用来帮助我们完成任务，”安妮说。 “这个哨子有魔法，但魔法只能用一次。 没有魔法的话，我真的不知道怎么玩。 所以，我没法教你。 就是这样。”

“Ah,” said Willy. He looked at the floor and shook his head. “Then I’m afraid I’m going to have to turn you both into chipmunks.” “啊，”威利说。 他看着地板，摇了摇头。 “那我只好把你们两个变成花栗鼠了。”

“What?” said Jack. “什么？”杰克说。

Willy burst out laughing. “Joking! Only joking!” he said. “Merlin! You should have told me you were friends with Merlin in the first place!” 威利突然笑起来。 “开玩笑的！ 只是开玩笑而已！”他说。 “梅林！ 你早该告诉我你一开始就是梅林的朋友！”

“Do you know Merlin?” asked Jack. “你认识梅林吗？”杰克问。

“Oh, yes, we spent a great deal of time together. Must be about eight hundred years ago now,” said Willy. “How is he?” “哦，是的，我们以前花了很多时间在一起。 现在应该有八百年了，”威利说。 “他怎么样了？”

“He’s happy,” said Annie. “他很开心，”安妮说。

“Good!” Willy turned back to Mary and Augusta. “I first met the master magician on the Isle of Merlin in the Irish Sea. He—” “太好了！”威尔利转身面对玛丽和奥古斯塔。 “我第一次遇见这位伟大的魔法师是在爱尔兰海的梅林岛上。他—”

“Wait, wait, please, Willy,” said Augusta. “Mary, do you have pen and paper?” “等等，等等，请别说话，威尔利，”奥古斯塔说。 “玛丽，你有笔和纸吗？”

“No, my dear, I’m afraid I don’t,” said Mary. “抱歉，亲爱的，我没有，”玛丽说。

“I can help,” said Jack. He took his pencil and small notebook out of his pocket. He tore some clean pages out of the notebook. Then he gave the pencil and pages to Augusta. “There,” he said. “我可以帮忙，”杰克说。 他从口袋里拿出铅笔和小笔记本。 他从笔记本里撕下几页干净的纸。 然后他把铅笔和纸页给了奥古斯塔。 “好了，”他说。

“Oh, thank you, Jack!” said Augusta. She turned back to Willy. “Continue, please.” “哦，谢谢你，杰克！”奥古斯塔说。 她转身面对威尔利。 “请继续说吧。”

“Well,” said Willy, “Merlin was only a few centuries old then, and I was a mere lad.…” “嗯，”威尔利说，“那时候梅林才几百岁，而我只是一个小伙子……”

As Willy told his story, Augusta began to write. Jack put his notebook back into his pocket and nodded to Annie. She nodded back, and the two of them started toward the door. 当威尔利讲述他的故事时，奥古斯塔开始写了起来。 杰克把他的笔记本放回口袋里，朝安妮点了点头。 安妮也点了点头，两人一起朝门口走去。

Just as they were about to leave, Annie called out to the others, “Bye!” 就在他们准备离开时，安妮朝其他人喊道：“再见！”

“We have to go home now,” said Jack. “我们现在得回家了，”杰克说。

“Trip lightly!” said Willy. “轻点走！”，威尔利说。

“Thank you for everything!” said Augusta. “谢谢你们做的一切！”，奥古斯塔说。

“One and twenty fare-thee-wells!” said Mary. “一加二十，二十一个再见！”，玛丽说。

“Same to you guys,” said Jack. Then he and Annie slipped out of the cottage. “你们也一样，”杰克说。 然后他和安妮悄悄溜出了小屋。

The rain was pouring again, and the wind was blowing hard. 雨又下起来了，风也刮得很大。

“I think we inspired Augusta,” said Annie. “我想我们让奥古斯塔感动了，”安妮说。

“Yep, we accomplished our mission,” said Jack. “Now let’s get out of here.” He couldn’t wait to go home and get warm and dry. “是啊，我们完成了任务，”杰克说。 “现在我们快点离开这儿吧。”他迫不及待地想回家，去暖和和地待着。

Jack and Annie ran against the wind. They climbed over the stone wall, then hurried down the lane, slipping and sliding in the muck. They ran across the soaked field. By the time they arrived at the rope ladder, their clothes were caked with mud. 杰克和安妮逆着风跑了起来。 他们翻过石头墙，然后赶紧沿着小路跑下去，在泥浆里滑来滑去。 他们跑过了被雨水浸湿的田野。 等他们到达绳梯时，他们的衣服上已经沾满了泥巴。

Jack and Annie climbed into the tree house. Annie found the Pennsylvania book in the corner. As rain blew through the window, she pointed to a picture of the Frog Creek woods. “I wish we could go there!” she said. 杰克和安妮爬进了树屋。 安妮在角落里找到了一本宾夕法尼亚的书。 雨水从窗户吹进来时，她指着一张蛙溪镇森林的图片。 “我希望我们能去那里！”她说。

The wind blew harder. 风刮得更猛了。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转起来。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
