9. Skunks or Weasels? 9. 臭鼬还是貂？

“Augusta!” whispered Jack. “She’s—she’s tiny!” “她——她好小！”

“They must have shrunk her!” whispered Annie. “他们一定是把她变小了！”安妮小声说。

“Hide!” whispered Jack. He crawled away from the door. Annie crawled after him. They pressed their backs against the grassy mound. “躲起来！”杰克小声说。 他爬开了门。 安妮跟在他后面爬。 他们背靠着那堆草丛。

“Why are we hiding?” asked Annie. “我们为什么要躲起来？”安妮问。

“We can’t let them see us!” said Jack. “They might shrink us, too!” “我们不能让他们看到我们！”杰克说。 “他们也可能把我们变小！”

“But how are we going to save Augusta?” asked Annie. “但是我们怎么才能救奥古斯塔呢？”安妮问。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “我不知道，”杰克说。

“Hey, I just remembered something,” said Annie. “Didn’t Mary say the girl in her story would have become small if she’d gone inside the hollow hill?” “嘿，我想起来了，”安妮说。 “玛丽不是说过，她故事里的女孩如果走进了山洞里的空山丘，就会变小吗？”

“Yeah, she did,” said Jack. “So maybe that means you can only get shrunk if you go inside.” “对，她说过，”杰克说。 “所以也许只有走进去的人才会被变小。”

“Right,” said Annie. “So let’s go back to the doorway and call from outside. We’ll be simple, direct, honest, and very polite, like Willy said. We’ll tell the king and queen we’ve come to take our friend back to her family.” “对，”安妮说。 “那我们回到门口，从外面喊吧。 我们要像威尔说的那样，简单、直接、诚实，而且非常有礼貌。 我们会告诉国王和王后，我们是来把我们的朋友送回她家的。”

“But wait a minute,” said Jack. “How can she go back to normal life with her family if she’s only eight inches tall?” “但是等一下，”杰克说。 “如果她只有八英寸高，怎么才能和家人一起恢复正常的生活呢？”

“Good point,” said Annie. “好主意，”安妮说。

“This is so weird,” said Jack. “这太奇怪了，”杰克说。

“Let’s worry about her size later,” said Annie. “For now, we just have to help her escape.” “我们以后再担心她的体型吧，”安妮说。 “现在，我们只能先帮她逃出去。”

“Right,” said Jack. “Let’s try it.” “对，”杰克说。 “我们试试看。”

Jack and Annie crawled back to the entrance of the hollow hill and peeked inside. 杰克和安妮爬回空山洞的入口，往里面张望。

In the pale-green light, a very small Augusta stood watching the dancers. 在淡绿色的光线下，一个非常小的奥古斯塔站在那里，看着跳舞的人。

“Excuse us, please!” Annie called. “请原谅我们！”安妮喊道。

The drumming stopped. The dancers froze. All eyes turned to Jack and Annie. The High King and High Queen looked startled. 鼓声停了下来。 舞者们僵在了那里。 所有人的目光都转向了杰克和安妮。 高国王和高王后看起来很惊讶。

“Who are you?” the king called. “How did you find us?” “你们是谁？”国王喊道。 “你们是怎么找到我们的？”

“That’s not important!” said Annie. “We are very sorry to bother you! But we’ve come to get our very dear friend Augusta! We have to take her home to her very loving family!” “那不重要！”安妮说道。 “非常抱歉打扰你们！ 但我们是来接我们非常亲爱的朋友奥古斯塔的！ 我们必须把她带回家，回到她非常爱她的家人那里！”，

“Please! Thank you!” added Jack, trying to sound polite. “请！ 谢谢！”杰克补充道，努力让自己显得有礼貌。

Before the king or queen could speak, Augusta rushed forward. “No! I don’t want to go home!” she screamed in a high little voice. “I don’t want to leave the Shee!” 在国王或王后说话之前，奥古斯塔冲了上来。 “不！ 我不想回家！”她用高高的小声音尖叫道。 “我不想离开谢伊！”

“Whoa,” said Jack. That was a surprise. Maybe Augusta was under a spell! “哇，”杰克说。 这真是个惊喜。 也许奥古斯塔被施了魔法！

“Leave here at once!” the High King commanded Jack and Annie in a squeaky voice. “You were not invited! You have no business here!” “立刻离开这里！”高国王用吱吱的声音命令杰克和安妮。 “你们没有被邀请！ 你们在这里没有事情可做！”

“Yes, we are leaving right away. But Augusta has to come with us!” Annie called. “Thank you!” “是的，我们马上就离开。 但奥古斯塔必须和我们一起去！”安妮喊道。 “谢谢！”

“She’s right!” said Jack. “Augusta has to come with us, please. Thank you!” “她说得对！”杰克说。 “奥古斯塔必须和我们一起去，好吗？谢谢！”

“No! I want to stay here!” said Augusta. “I’m not good for anything at home! Mary was right, I’m not happy there!” “不！” “我想待在这里！”奥古斯塔说。 “在家里我什么都做不好！ 玛丽说得对，我在那里不开心！“

Jack was amazed—Augusta wasn’t under a spell. She really wanted to stay with the Shee! 杰克很惊讶——奥古斯塔没有被施了魔法。 她真的很想和希伊在一起！

“The girl will stay with us!” the High King shouted. “Go, now! Now!” “这个女孩会和我们在一起！”高国王喊道。 “快走！现在！现在！”

“No way!” Jack blurted out. “We won’t leave without Augusta!” “不可能！”杰克脱口而出。 “我们不离开，除非奥古斯塔来了！”

The crowd gasped. 人群惊呼起来。

“What?” roared the High King. “什么？”高王怒吼道。

“Sorry, sorry,” said Jack. “I meant—” “对不起，对不起，”杰克说。 “我是说——”

“You will pay for this rudeness!” the king yelled. “你们要为这种粗鲁付出代价！”国王大喊。

Before Jack and Annie could get away, the king thrust out his arm. Sparks flew from the tips of his tiny fingers. Suddenly Jack and Annie couldn’t move their arms or legs! 杰克和安妮还没来得及逃开，国王就伸出胳膊。 他手指尖上迸发出火星。 突然，杰克和安妮动不了胳膊和腿了！

The king kept pointing at them. “Skunks?” he shouted at the crowd. “Or weasels?” 国王一直指着他们。 “臭鼬？”他对着人群大喊。 “还是貂？”

“Neither, please! Thank you!” shouted Jack. He was glad he could still talk! “都不是，求你们了！ 谢谢！”杰克喊道。 他很高兴自己还能说话！

But the crowd began chanting in strange, high voices, “Skunks! Skunks! Skunks!” 但人群开始用奇怪的高声 chanting：“臭鼬！臭鼬！ 臭鼬！”，

The king nodded and raised both arms into the air. Jack was desperate. He didn’t want to live the rest of his life as a skunk! 国王点了点头，把两只手臂举到空中。 杰克感到绝望。 他不想这辈子都当一只臭鼬！

“Wait! Please!” Jack shouted. “I’m sorry I was rude! Before you change us, I really, really need to tell Augusta some things! Thank you!” “等一下！ “请！”杰克喊道。 “对不起，我刚才太粗鲁了！” 在你们改变我们之前，我真的、真的需要告诉奥古斯塔一些事情！谢谢！”

The king looked at Jack for a long moment. Then he lowered his arms, and the crowd grew silent. 国王盯着杰克看了好一会儿。 然后他放下了手臂，人群安静了下来。

“Thank you!” said Jack. “Augusta, listen to me! You should go back home. You’re good for lots of things back there! You’re very kind! And Mary said you have a brave heart and a fine mind! Those are really good things to have!” “谢谢！”杰克说。 “奥古斯塔，听我说！ 你应该回家去。 你在那儿有很多可以做的事情！你非常善良！ 安妮说你有一颗勇敢的心和聪明的头脑！ 这些真的都是非常棒的东西！

Tears rolled down Augusta’s cheeks. She shook her head. 奥古斯塔的眼泪顺着脸颊流了下来。 她摇了摇头。

“Listen to me, please!” Jack went on. “Mary says you’re not happy. But some things do make you happy, Augusta. You said you feel close to nature! You said you love simple folk like Mary. And I know you love stories, too! Mary said you remembered every story she told you! You used to tell them yourself, word for word! You have a great memory!” “听我说，好吗！”杰克继续说道。 “玛丽说你不开心。” 但有些事情确实会让你开心，奥古斯塔。 你刚才说你感觉离大自然很近！ 你刚才说你爱像玛丽这样朴实的人。 我知道你也爱听故事！ 玛丽说你记得她告诉你的每一个故事！ 你以前都是自己一字不差地讲这些故事！ 你的记忆力真好！

“He’s right, Augusta!” yelled Annie. “Those are your gifts! You need to give your gifts to the world!” “他说得对，奥古斯塔！”安妮喊道。 “那些都是你的礼物！ 你需要把你的礼物送给世界！”

Augusta was still for a moment. Then she shook her head. “I want to stay!” she said. 奥古斯塔停顿了一下。 然后她摇了摇头。 “我想留下！”她说。

“You have had your say!” the High King shouted at Jack and Annie. “Now prepare to become skunks!” He raised his arms again. “你已经说过了！”高王对杰克和安妮喊道。 “现在准备变成臭鼬吧！”他再次举起双手。

Oh, no! thought Jack. 哦，不！杰克心想。

“Wait, Finvara!” said the High Queen. “等等，芬瓦拉！”高皇后说道。

The High Queen stepped closer to Augusta. The queen wore a silver cloak that glittered with diamonds. She had jewels in her long red hair that shone like stars. Her high, clear voice rang like a bell. “I am Queen Aine of the Shee,” she said. “The boy said you love stories—and that you remember every story you hear. Is this true?” 高皇后走近奥古斯塔。 女王披着一件闪着钻石光芒的银色斗篷。 她的长红发里缀满了像星星一样闪耀的珠宝。 她高亢清晰的声音如同钟声一般响亮。 “我是希伊族的艾恩女王，”她说。 “那个男孩说你爱听故事——而且你听到的每个故事都能记住。这是真的吗？”

Augusta nodded. 奥古斯塔点了点头。

“Then listen now to our story,” said Queen Aine. “那现在听一听我们的故事，”艾恩女王说。

All the Shee were very still, watching Augusta and their queen. 所有的希伊人都非常安静，看着奥古斯塔和他们的女王。

“In the morning of time, out of a rosy sky and a windy light, we came,” said Queen Aine. “We were tribes of a supernatural people called the Tuatha Dé Danann. Strong, fearless, and noble we were. Five roads carried our armies through the wild, wooded lands of Ireland, and for eons we ruled the Irish world. “在时间的清晨，从玫瑰色的天空和微风中透出的光里，我们来了，”艾恩女王说。 “我们是被称为图阿达·德·达内恩的超自然民族的部落。 我们坚强、勇敢、高贵。 五条道路带领我们的军队穿越爱尔兰荒野的森林，我们统治爱尔兰世界已有无数个世纪。

“But when the humans came, the wild woods gave way to villages and pastures. Our tribes hid in the hollow hills, in ruined forts, and under the sea. Over time we made ourselves smaller and smaller so we could more easily hide from humans. “但当人类到来时，茂密的森林让位于村庄和牧场。 我们的部落躲进了空荡的山丘里，躲进了废墟中的堡垒里，还躲到了海底。 随着时间的推移，我们变得越来越小，这样更容易躲过人类。

“Eventually we became known as the Shee, and we were mocked as the Wee Folk of Faerie. But, in truth, we are a tribe of the great Tuatha Dé Danann, and we live in enchanted places like this one, protected by what is left of our magic. Do you understand?” “最终，我们被称为希伊人，还被嘲笑为仙境中的小精灵。 但是，实际上，我们是伟大的图阿塔·德·达纳的部落，我们住在像这样被魔法保护的神奇地方。 你明白了吗？

“I do, yes,” breathed Augusta. “我明白了，”奥古斯塔轻声说。

“Our stories were passed down for centuries in the old language of Ireland. But as the old language was replaced with English, the stories began to fade away,” Queen Aine said. “我们的故事在几个世纪里用爱尔兰古老的语言流传下来。 但随着古老的语言被英语取代，这些故事开始慢慢消失，”艾恩女王说。

“Go home now with your friends, human child. Go back, for our sake. Seek out the old storytellers, and ask them to tell you the tales of my people. Learn the old language. Read the old manuscripts. Write our stories down and share them before they are lost completely. Share them with all the people of Ireland and all the world. Will you do that for us? Will you use your gifts to tell our stories and restore our dignity?” “现在，孩子，和你的朋友一起回家吧。 为了我们的缘故，回去吧。 去找那些古老的讲故事人，让他们告诉你我族人的故事。 学习古老的语言。 阅读古老的手稿。 把我们的故事写下来，在它们完全消失之前分享出去。 把它们分享给爱尔兰的所有人，分享给全世界的人。 你会为我们这么做吗？ 你会用你的天赋来讲我们的故事，恢复我们的尊严吗？

Augusta’s eyes shone. “Yes,” she said, “yes, I will, yes.” 奥古斯塔的眼睛闪着光。 “好，”她说，“好，我会的，好。”

“Good. Then I will send you all swiftly home,” said Queen Aine. She beckoned to a small dancer, who stepped forward with a tiny silver chalice. “很好。 那么我会很快送你们所有人回家，”艾恩女王说。 她向一个小舞者招手，那个小舞者走上前，拿着一个小小的银杯。

“Sip the honey nectar of the Shee,” said the High Queen. She took the chalice and held it out to Augusta. Augusta took a sip. “喝一下希伊族的蜂蜜蜜露，”高王后说。 她拿起圣杯，递给了奥古斯塔。 奥古斯塔喝了一口。

A second later, Jack felt cold wind and rain. He and Annie were standing on the bank of the river. Augusta was standing beside them. She was her normal size again. 一秒钟后，杰克感到冷风和雨。 他和安妮站在河边。 奥古斯塔站在他们旁边。 她又恢复了原来的大小。
