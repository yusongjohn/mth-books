2. The Big House 2. 大房子

A cold wind blew rain into the tree house. Jack shivered. He was wearing an old overcoat and ragged trousers. Annie wore a scarf, a shawl, and a long red wool dress. They both wore scuffed, worn boots. 一阵冷风吹着雨进了树屋。杰克打了个寒战。 他穿着一件旧外套和破旧的裤子。 安妮戴着围巾，披着披肩，穿着一条长长的红色羊毛裙子。 他们俩都穿着磨破、旧了的靴子。

“So where are we?” said Jack. He and Annie looked out the window. “那我们这是在哪里？”杰克说。 杰克和安妮看向窗外。

The tree house had landed in a tree at the edge of a green meadow dotted with woolly white sheep. Next to the meadow was a narrow lane. It ran uphill between low stone walls. Through the drizzle, Jack could see mist-covered mountains in the distance and a flash of silver sea. 树屋降落在一棵树上，这棵树位于一片绿油油的草地上，草地上散落着许多毛茸茸的白色绵羊。 草地旁边是一条狭窄的小路。 小路沿着低矮的石墙向上延伸。 透过细雨，杰克能看到远处被薄雾笼罩的山脉，还有远处闪烁的银色大海。

“It looks like a scene in a fairy tale,” said Annie. “看起来像是童话里的场景，”安妮说。

“Yeah, a fairy tale with bad weather,” said Jack. “是啊，一个天气不好的童话，”杰克说。

“I wonder where the Big House is,” said Annie. “我想知道大房子在哪里，”安妮说。

“I don’t know, but I’d like to get inside it now,” said Jack. “我不知道，但我想现在进去，”杰克说。

“Me too,” said Annie, shivering. “Let’s go.” “我也是，”安妮说着，打了个哆嗦。 “我们走吧。”

Jack crammed his cold hands into the pockets of his torn coat. In one pocket, he felt his notebook and pencil. In the other, he felt the Irish whistle. “I’ve got the whistle,” he said. 杰克把冰冷的手塞进了他那件破旧外套的口袋里。 在一个口袋里，他摸到了他的笔记本和铅笔。 在另一边，他感觉到了爱尔兰口哨声。 “我拿到口哨了，”他说。

“Good,” said Annie. She held her red skirt and started down the rope ladder. Jack climbed down after her. “很好，”安妮说。 她抓住她的红裙子，开始顺着绳梯往下爬。 杰克跟着她爬了下来。

Annie pulled her shawl tightly around her shoulders. Jack turned up the collar of his coat. As they tramped through the wet meadow, he felt cold water seeping through holes in his boots. 安妮把她的披肩紧紧地裹在肩膀上。 杰克把外套的领子翻上来。 当他们踩着湿漉漉的草地走过时，他感觉到冷冰冰的水从靴子的破洞里渗进来。

Jack and Annie climbed over a stone wall onto the muddy lane. A horse-drawn wagon was rattling toward them down the hill. The wagon was filled with squealing pigs. 杰克和安妮爬过一道石墙，来到泥泞的小路上。 一辆马拉的货车正沿着山下朝他们驶来，发出咔哒咔哒的声音。 货车里装满了尖叫的猪。

“Excuse me!” Annie called to the driver. “Can you tell us where the Big House is?” “对不起！”安妮对司机喊道。 “你能告诉我们大房子在哪里吗？”

An old man with a tired, rugged face pointed back up the hill. 一个满脸疲惫、皮肤粗糙的老人指着山上的方向。

“Thanks!” said Jack. “谢谢！”杰克说道。

The large wooden wheels of the wagon rumbled past, splashing Jack and Annie with mud. 马车的大木轮滚滚驶过，溅起了泥巴，弄脏了杰克和安妮。

“Yuck!” said Annie. “真恶心！”安妮说。

“Now we’re cold and wet and dirty,” said Jack. “现在我们又冷又湿又脏，”杰克说。

“Yeah, we’re going to look great when we get to the Big House,” said Annie. “是啊，到了大房子的时候，我们看起来可真棒，”安妮说。

“So what do we do when we get there?” asked Jack. “那我们到了那儿之后该怎么办呢？”杰克问。

“When we find Augusta, maybe we tell her that Teddy and Kathleen sent us,” said Annie, “like we told Louis Armstrong in New Orleans.” “如果我们找到奥古斯塔，也许可以告诉她，是泰迪和凯瑟琳派我们来的，”安妮说，“就像我们在新奥尔良告诉路易斯·阿姆斯特朗那样。”

Just thinking about their adventure with Louis Armstrong made Jack smile. “I don’t know if that will work,” he said. “His world seemed so different from this world. There it was so noisy and busy. Here it feels lonely.” 只是想想他们和路易斯·阿姆斯特朗的那次冒险，就让杰克露出了微笑。 “我不知道这会不会有用，”他说。 “他的世界看起来和我们的世界完全不同。 那里非常吵闹，而且很忙碌。 这里感觉好孤单。”

“Well, we won’t know until we find Augusta,” said Annie. “Let’s go.” “好吧，除非我们找到奥古斯塔，否则我们不会知道的，”安妮说。 “我们走吧。”

Jack and Annie lowered their heads. They plodded up the lane, sloshing in and out of giant puddles. When they reached the top of the hill, they stopped. The muddy lane wound down, past more sheep meadows and some cottages, past a long stable and several barns. 杰克和安妮低下了头。 他们沿着小路慢慢走着，踩进又踩出巨大的水坑里。 当他们走到山顶时，停了下来。 泥泞的小路蜿蜒而下，经过更多的羊群草地和一些小屋，经过一个长长的马厩和几座谷仓。

At the end of the lane was a large open gate that led onto the grounds of a white mansion. Gray smoke rose from the mansion’s chimneys. 在小路的尽头，有一个巨大的敞开的门，通向一座白色豪宅的庭院。 灰色的烟雾从豪宅的烟囱里升起。

“The Big House!” said Annie. “大房子！”安妮说道。

“Maybe the people who live there will invite us inside to get warm and dry by a fire,” said Jack. “也许住在那儿的人会邀请我们进去，用火炉取暖，变得又暖和又干燥，”杰克说。

Jack and Annie started down the lane. As they passed the sheep meadows, black-and-white dogs barked at them. When they walked by several boys hauling wet hay, the workers looked up and eyed them suspiciously. 杰克和安妮开始沿着小路往下走。 当他们经过羊群的草地时，黑白相间的狗对着他们叫了起来。 当他们走过几个正在搬运湿干草的男孩时，工人们抬头看了看他们，带着怀疑的眼神。

Jack was relieved to get to the gate and head toward the Big House. When they reached the front door, Annie lifted the heavy knocker and let it drop. 杰克一到达大门，就松了口气，朝大房子走去。 当他们到达前门时，安妮拿起那个沉重的门环，然后让它掉下来。

A moment later, the door opened. A pale teenage girl looked out. “Who are you? Why are you here?” 片刻之后，门打开了。 一个脸色苍白的 teenage 女孩探出头来。 “你们是谁？ 你们为什么来这里？”

“Uh … well …,” started Jack. “呃……嗯……，”杰克开口了。

“Are you the ones the butler sent for?” the girl asked. “你们是管家叫来的吗？”女孩问道。

“The butler?” said Jack. “管家？”杰克说。

“Yes, we are!” said Annie. “是的，我们是！”安妮说。

“Then you should go round to the back!” the girl said. Before Jack or Annie could ask for Augusta, the girl slammed the door in their faces. “那你们应该去后面！”女孩说。 杰克和安妮还没来得及问奥古斯塔的事，女孩就砰地一声把门关上了。

“Nice,” said Jack. “真不错，”杰克说。

“I hope she wasn’t Augusta,” said Annie. “我希望她不是奥古斯塔，”安妮说。

“Why did you tell her ‘yes’?” asked Jack. “你为什么告诉她‘是’？”杰克问。

“It’s a way to get inside the Big House,” said Annie. “Come on.” “这是进大房子的一种方法，”安妮说。 “来吧。”

Jack and Annie tramped through the mud to the back of the mansion. They stopped at a door beneath a large smoking chimney. Annie knocked again. 杰克和安妮踩着泥水走到豪宅后面。 他们在一座大烟囱下方的一扇门前停了下来。 安妮又敲了敲门。

This time a young red-haired girl in a cap and apron opened the door. “Yes?” she said. 这次，一个戴着帽子和围裙、红头发的小女孩打开了门。 “是吗？”她说。

“Is your name—” started Annie. “你叫——”安妮开始问。

“Who is it, Molly?” someone called from inside. “是谁，莫莉？”屋里有人喊道。

Molly? So she’s not Augusta, thought Jack. 莫莉？ 所以她不是奥古斯塔，杰克心想。

“Who are you?” Molly asked them. “你们是谁？”莫莉问他们。

“We’re the ones the butler sent for,” said Annie. “我们是管家派来的人，”安妮说。

“You?” said Molly. She looked doubtful. “Well, come in and see him then.” “你们？”莫莉说。 她看起来不太相信。 “那进来见他吧。”

Jack and Annie stepped inside. 杰克和安妮走进了里面。

“He’s in the kitchen,” said Molly. She started down the hall. “他在厨房里，”莫莉说。 她开始沿着走廊走。

Jack and Annie followed Molly to the doorway of a dimly lit kitchen. The kitchen smelled of fish and onions. Pots and pans hung from a long rack over a big wooden table. A stout older woman was bent over the table, rolling out dough. 杰克和安妮跟着莫莉来到一个昏暗厨房的门口。 厨房里飘着鱼和洋葱的味道。 锅碗瓢盆挂在一张大木桌上方的长架上。 一位身材结实的中年妇女弯着腰，正在桌边揉面团。

“Cook, here are the ones the butler sent for,” said Molly. “厨师，这是管家要的那些，”莫莉说。

The cook looked up from her dough and squinted at Jack and Annie. “You’re the ones he sent for?” she said. 厨师从面团上抬起头，眯着眼看向杰克和安妮。 “你们就是他要找的那两个人？”她问。

“Uh, yes, ma’am, that’s us,” said Annie. “呃，是的，夫人，就是我们，”安妮说。

The cook turned toward the fireplace. Next to the fire an ancient-looking man with white whiskers sat slumped in a chair, snoring. “Mr. O’Leary!” 厨师转身朝壁炉走去。 壁炉旁边，一位满脸皱纹、白胡须的老人瘫坐在椅子上，打起了呼噜。 “奥莱里先生！”，

The old man jerked and opened his eyes. 老人猛地一惊，睁开了眼睛。

“The ones you sent for are here!” the cook shouted, as if the man were hard of hearing. “你叫的人来了！”厨师喊道，好像那个人听不清似的。

The groggy butler peered at Jack and Annie. “I sent for you?” he growled. “Not possible! I sent for a coach driver and a blacksmith.” 昏昏欲睡的管家盯着杰克和安妮。 “你叫的是我？”他咆哮道。 “不可能！ 我叫的是马车夫和铁匠。”

“Really?” said Annie. “I guess there was a mistake. But maybe there are some other jobs we could do around here.” “真的吗？”安妮说。 “我想可能是搞错了。 不过也许这里还有一些其他的工作我们可以做。”

“Well, what are you good for?” asked the butler. “那么，你们能做什么？”管家问。

“What do you mean?” said Jack. “你什么意思？”杰克说。

“Do you know how to sweep the inside of a chimney?” said the old man. “你知道怎么打扫烟囱里面吗？”老人说道。

“Um … no,” said Jack. “嗯……不知道，”杰克说道。

“Pluck a chicken?” the cook asked. “拔鸡毛吗？”厨师问道。

“No way,” said Annie. “才不要呢，”安妮说道。

“What about rats?” the butler said. “那老鼠呢？”管家说道。

“What about them?” asked Jack. “他们怎么办？”杰克问。

“They’re all over the cellar,” said the cook. “Can you catch ’em?” “它们全都跑进地窖了，”厨师说。 “你能抓住它们吗？”，

“I—I don’t think so,” said Jack. “我……我想不能，”杰克说。

“Then you’re no good to us here!” snorted the butler. “Be on your way!” “那你在这里对我们一点用处都没有！”管家哼了一声。 “快走吧！”

At that moment, Jack heard the back door open and shut. A girl about Jack’s age stepped into the kitchen. She wore a red cape and carried two large, empty baskets. Her wet hair was parted neatly down the middle and pulled into a tight bun in the back. 就在这时，杰克听到后门被打开了，又迅速关上。 一个和杰克年龄相仿的女孩走进了厨房。 她穿着一件红色斗篷，手里拿着两个又大又空的篮子。 她湿漉漉的头发中间分开，后面扎成了一个紧绷的发髻。

“Ah!” said the cook. “Welcome back, Miss Augusta!” “啊！”厨师说道。 “欢迎回来，奥古斯塔小姐！”
