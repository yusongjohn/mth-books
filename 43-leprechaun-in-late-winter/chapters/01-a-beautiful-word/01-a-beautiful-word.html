1. A Beautiful Word 1. 一个美丽的词

It was a chilly afternoon in late winter. Annie was doing her homework on the computer in the living room. Jack sat on the couch and stared at a blank page in his small notebook. He heaved a sigh. 那是冬末一个寒冷的下午。 安妮正在客厅里用电脑做作业。 杰克坐在沙发上，盯着他小笔记本上的一张空白页。他叹了口气。

“What’s wrong?” said Annie. “怎么了？”安妮说。

“I have to write a story for school,” said Jack, “and I’m stuck.” “我得为学校写一个故事，”杰克说，“可我卡住了。”

“Well, you’d better get unstuck,” said Annie. “Mom and Dad said we have to get our homework done before we go to the theater with them tonight.” “那你就得赶紧摆脱困境，”安妮说。 “爸爸妈妈说，我们必须在今晚和他们去剧院之前把作业做完。”

“I know,” said Jack. “But I can’t think of anything to write about.” “我知道，”杰克说。 “但是我想不出写什么。”

“Why don’t you do what you love to do?” said Annie. “Go outside and write down some facts about what you see. Then turn them into a story.” “你为什么不写你最喜欢做的事情呢？”安妮说。 “出去走走，把你看到的东西写下来。 然后把它们变成一个故事。”

“Hey, that’s a good idea,” said Jack. “Thanks.” He jumped up and grabbed his coat from the hall closet. Then, taking his pencil and notebook with him, he headed outdoors. “嘿，这真是个好主意，”杰克说。 “谢谢。”他跳起来，从门厅的储物柜里拿了他的外套。 然后，带着他的铅笔和笔记本，他走出了门。

The early March weather was sunny but cold and windy. Jack looked around. Then he wrote down some facts in his notebook: 三月早上的天气阳光明媚，但又冷又风大。 杰克环顾四周。 然后他在笔记本上写下了这些事实：

old snow in yard

sun sparkling on sidewalk 院子里的老雪，阳光在人行道上闪闪发光

Jack looked up again. Treetops swayed in the March winds. Jack started to write about them. But when he looked down at his notebook, he nearly dropped his pencil. On the page were two large, fancy letters: 杰克又抬头看了看。 三月的风吹得树顶摇晃。 杰克开始写关于它们的事情。 但当他低头看笔记本时，差点把铅笔掉下来了。 那页纸上写着两个大大的、漂亮的字母：

T K T K

“Oh, man!” whispered Jack. He dashed back in the house and into the living room. “Annie! Look!” Jack held up his notebook. “Look at this!” “天啊！”杰克小声说。 他冲回房子，跑进了客厅。 “安妮！ 看！”杰克举起了他的笔记本。 “快看这个！”

Annie stared at the page. “Old snow … sun sparkling … Nice.…” 安妮盯着那一页书。 “旧雪……阳光闪烁……真漂亮。……”

“No, not that!” said Jack. “The letters!” “不，不是那个！”杰克说。 “是字母！”

Annie looked at Jack like he was a little crazy. “Uh … what letters?” she said. 安妮用一种好像杰克有点疯了的眼神看着他。 “呃……什么字母？”她说。

Jack looked back at the page. “They’re gone!” he said. “A big, fancy T and K!” 杰克又看了看那一页书。 “它们不见了！”他说。 “一个又大又漂亮的T和K！”

“T and K?” said Annie. “T和K？”安妮说。

“Yes! For Teddy and Kathleen!” said Jack. “The letters just appeared on the page when I was outside! They were there! Really!” “是的！ “为了泰迪和凯瑟琳！”杰克说。 “这些字母刚出现在页面上，当我在外面的时候！它们真的在那里！ 真的！

“I believe you,” said Annie. She jumped up from the computer. “Let’s go.” “我相信你，”安妮说。 她从电脑前跳了起来。 “我们走吧。”

“Wait, I have to get my backpack from upstairs,” said Jack. “等等，我得从楼上拿我的书包，”杰克说。

“Forget it! Come on! The tree house must be waiting for us!” said Annie. “别管了！快点！ “树屋一定在等我们！”安妮说。

“Okay, okay,” said Jack. He quickly shoved his notebook and pencil into a pocket of his coat. “好吧，好吧，”杰克说。 他赶紧把笔记本和铅笔塞进外套的口袋里。

Annie grabbed her jacket. “Mom! Dad! We’re going to take a little break from our homework!” she called. 安妮抓起了她的夹克。 “妈妈！爸爸！ 我们要暂时休息一下，不写作业了！”她喊道。

“Okay, but make it short! We have to leave for the theater by seven!” their dad called from the kitchen. “好吧，但时间要短一点！ 我们七点得去剧院！”爸爸从厨房里喊道。

“We will!” said Jack. “我们会的！”杰克说。

Jack and Annie headed outside. They ran over the melting snow in their front yard and up the sun-sparkling sidewalk. They charged across the street and into the Frog Creek woods. They hurried between the windblown trees until they came to the tallest oak. 杰克和安妮走出了屋子。 他们跑过门前正在融化的雪，沿着阳光闪闪发亮的人行道跑了上去。 他们冲过街道，跑进了蛙溪镇的树林里。 他们赶紧在被风吹动的树之间穿行，直到来到最高的橡树前。

High in the branches was the magic tree house. Their friends from Camelot, Teddy and Kathleen, were looking out the window. 高高的树枝上就是那座神奇的树屋。 来自卡梅洛特的朋友泰迪和凯瑟琳正站在窗边往外看。

“Hello!” called Kathleen. “你好！”凯瑟琳喊道。

“Hi!” shouted Annie, waving. “嗨！”安妮挥着手喊道。

“Good trick with the magic letters!” Jack called. “你们用魔法字母的把戏真棒！”杰克喊道。

“We thought you’d like that!” said Teddy. “I just learned how to do it!” “我们想你们会喜欢这个！”泰迪说道。 “我刚刚才学会怎么做的！”，

Annie grabbed the rope ladder and started up. Jack followed her. They climbed into the tree house and hugged the young enchanters. 安妮抓住了绳梯，开始往上爬。 杰克跟了上去。 他们爬进树屋，拥抱了年轻的女巫们。

“So what’s up today?” asked Jack. “今天有什么计划？”杰克问。

“Where does Merlin want us to go now?” asked Annie. “梅林这次想让我们去哪儿？”安妮问。

“Merlin wants you to go to Galway, Ireland,” said Kathleen. “梅林想让你们去爱尔兰的加里韦，”凯瑟琳说。

“Ireland? Cool!” said Annie. “爱尔兰？ 太酷了！”安妮说。

“Morgan sent us to Ireland once before—to the ninth century,” said Jack. “摩根以前曾派我们去过爱尔兰——在第九世纪，”杰克说。

“Yes. Well, this time you will go to Ireland in the nineteenth century,” said Teddy. “To 1862, to be exact. Your mission is to find an imaginative and creative girl named Augusta.” “对。 不过这次你们将去爱尔兰的十九世纪，”泰迪说。 “去1862年，确切地说是那一年。” 你的任务是找到一个富有想象力和创造力的女孩，她的名字叫奥古斯塔。

“Augusta doesn’t know yet what her talents are,” said Kathleen. “She lives in a time when it is not easy for girls to explore their creativity. Your mission will be to inspire her, so she can give her gifts to the world.” “奥古斯塔现在还不知道自己有什么天赋，”凯瑟琳说。 “她生活的时代，女孩们很难去探索自己的创造力。” 你的任务就是激励她，让她能够把自己的才华献给世界。

“What does that word mean exactly?” asked Annie. “ Inspire?” “这个词到底是什么意思呢？”安妮问。 “激励？”

“’tis a beautiful word,” said Kathleen, her sea-blue eyes shining. “It means to breathe life into a person’s heart, to make her feel joyful to be alive.” “这是一个美丽的词，”凯瑟琳说，她海蓝色的眼睛闪闪发亮。 “它的意思是给一个人的心灵注入生命，让她感受到活着的快乐。”

“That is beautiful,” said Annie. “这真美，”安妮说。

“You may need some magic to help you,” said Teddy. From the corner of the tree house, he picked up the magic trumpet that had helped them on their last journey. “Only this time …” “你可能需要一些魔法来帮忙，”泰迪说。 他从树屋的角落里捡起了那支魔法号角，这支号角曾帮助他们完成上一次的旅程。 “但这一次……”

Teddy handed the trumpet to Kathleen. She held the shiny brass instrument for a moment. Then she tossed it into the air. The trumpet spun like a whirlwind. There was a flash of blue light—and the trumpet was gone! In its place was a thin silver pipe with six holes. 泰迪把号角递给了凯瑟琳。 她拿着那支闪亮的黄铜乐器，停留了一小会儿。 然后她把它抛向空中。 号角像龙卷风一样旋转起来。 一道蓝光闪过——号角不见了！ 取而代之的是一根细长的银色管子，上面有六个孔。

“What’s that?” breathed Jack. “这是什么？”杰克低声说道。

“An Irish whistle,” said Kathleen. She plucked the instrument from the air. “When you face great danger, one of you must play it. It will make magical music. And anything the other one sings will come true.” “这是一个爱尔兰口哨，”凯瑟琳说。 她从空中摘下了这个乐器。 “当你们面临巨大危险时，你们中的一人必须吹响它。 它会奏出神奇的音乐。 而另一个人唱的任何歌都会成真。”

“But remember,” said Teddy, “its magic will work only once.” “但记住，”泰迪说，“它的魔法只能起一次作用。”

“Right,” said Annie. “对，”安妮说。

“Thanks,” said Jack. He took the Irish whistle from Kathleen and put it into his pocket. “And did Morgan send a research book to give us information?” “谢谢，”杰克说。 他从凯瑟琳那里接过爱尔兰口哨，把它放进了口袋里。 “摩根是不是寄了一本研究书给我们提供信息？”

“Not this time,” said Teddy. “Morgan wants you to draw upon your own experiences in life to help you on this journey.” “这次不行，”泰迪说。 “摩根希望你能依靠自己生活中的经历来帮助你完成这次旅程。”

“No problem,” said Annie. “没问题，”安妮说。

Jack wasn’t sure about that. He liked having a book of facts to help them. 杰克对这个不太确定。 他喜欢有一本事实书籍来帮助他们。

“So how do we find Augusta?” said Annie. “那我们怎么找到奥古斯塔呢？”安妮说。

“It should be easy to find her,” said Teddy. “When you land in the county of Galway, Ireland, just ask anyone for directions to the Big House.” “找到她应该很容易，”泰迪说。 “当你降落在爱尔兰的加里郡时，只要问任何人去大房子的路就行了。”

“Hold on.” Jack pulled out his notebook and wrote: “等等。”杰克拿出他的笔记本，开始写下来：

County of Galway, Ireland

Augusta

Big House 爱尔兰加里郡奥古斯塔大房子

“Got it,” said Jack. “But how do we get to Ireland in the first place if we don’t have a research book?” “记住了，”杰克说。 “但是如果我们没有研究书，我们怎么才能先到爱尔兰呢？”

“Point to the notes you just made and make your wish,” said Kathleen. “指着你刚刚做的笔记，许个愿吧，”凯瑟琳说。

“And when you are ready to come home,” said Teddy, “use the Pennsylvania book as you usually do.” “当你准备回家的时候，”泰迪说，“就像平时那样使用宾夕法尼亚州的书。”

“Got it,” said Jack. “明白了，”杰克说。

“Go now, and help Augusta,” said Kathleen. “She needs you.” “现在去吧，去帮奥古斯塔，”凯瑟琳说。 “她需要你。”

Jack pointed to the words Galway, Ireland in his notebook. “I wish we could go there!” he said. 杰克指着他在笔记本上写的“爱尔兰加里威”。 “我希望我们能去那里！”他说。

“Bye!” Annie said to Teddy and Kathleen. “再见！”安妮对泰迪和凯瑟琳说。

“Farewell!” said Kathleen. “再见！”凯瑟琳说。

“Good luck!” said Teddy. “好运！”泰迪说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始吹起来了。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转起来。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
