8. The Great Parade 8. 盛大的游行

The huge mammoth walked across the open plain. 巨大的猛犸象走过开阔的平原。

“Look!” said Annie. She pointed to a herd of elk in the distance. They had great, wide antlers. “看！”安妮说。 她指着远处的一群驼鹿。 它们长着又大又宽的鹿角。

“There!” said Jack as a herd of reindeer came into view. They pranced gracefully across the snow. “在那儿！”杰克说，这时一群驯鹿进入了视野。 它们姿态优美地腾跃着穿过雪地。

Then a woolly rhino joined them on the open plain. Then a bison! 接着，一头长毛犀牛也加入了它们，来到开阔的平原上。然后又来了一头野牛！

The elk, reindeer, rhino, and bison moved along with them, at a distance. 驼鹿、驯鹿、犀牛和野牛隔着一段距离，跟着他们一起走。

They seemed to be escorting Jack and Annie back to the tree house. 它们好像在护送杰克和安妮回树屋。

The snow sparkled with sunlight. 雪在阳光下闪闪发光。

This is a great parade, Jack thought. Fantastic. 真是一场盛大的游行啊，杰克想。太棒了。

They were getting closer and closer to the grove of tall trees. 他们离那片高大的树林越来越近。

“I told you,” said Annie. “Lulu’s taking us home.” “我跟你说过，”安妮说。 “露露正带我们回家。”

But just then the mammoth let out a cry. All the other animals bounded off. 可就在那时，猛犸象发出了一声叫。 其余的动物都跳着跑开了。

Peanut started to squeak. 花生开始吱吱叫。

Jack looked around. 杰克环顾四周。

Behind them the sabertooth was slinking across the sunlit snow! 在他们身后，剑齿虎正悄悄穿过洒满阳光的雪地！

The woolly mammoth roared and plunged forward. 长毛猛犸象吼了一声，猛地向前冲去。

Jack and Annie nearly fell off. 杰克和安妮差点掉下去。

Jack clutched Annie. She and Peanut clutched the mammoth’s shaggy hair. 杰克紧紧抓住安妮。 她和花生紧紧抓住猛犸象蓬松的毛。

The mammoth thundered wildly over the ground. 猛犸象在地面上狂奔，蹄声轰隆隆的。

“Ahhh!” Jack and Annie yelled. “啊！”杰克和安妮大叫。

The mammoth charged to the grove of trees. 猛犸象冲向那片树林。

But the tiger had circled around the trees. He stood between the tallest tree and the mammoth. 可老虎已经绕到了树林另一边。 它站在最高的那棵树和猛犸象之间。

They were trapped. 他们被困住了。

The sabertooth began moving slowly toward the mammoth. 剑齿虎开始慢慢朝猛犸象走来。

The woolly mammoth roared fiercely. 长毛猛犸象发出凶猛的吼叫。

But Jack knew a sabertooth could kill any creature, including a mammoth. 可杰克知道，剑齿虎能杀死任何动物，包括猛犸象。

The huge tiger’s head was down. His burning eyes were fixed on the mammoth. His long white fangs glinted in the sunlight. 那头巨大的老虎低着头。 它燃烧的目光死死盯着猛犸象。 它长长的白色獠牙在阳光下闪着光。
