1. The “M” Things 1. 那些“M”开头的东西

“Let’s go to the tree house,” said Annie. “我们去树屋吧，”安妮说。

She and Jack were passing the Frog Creek woods on their way home from their swimming class at the Y. 她和杰克正路过蛙溪树林，那是他们上完游泳课回家的路上。

“No. I want to go home and change out of my bathing suit,” said Jack. “不。 我想先回家换下泳衣，”杰克说。

“Oh, that’ll take too long,” said Annie. “Don’t you want to save Morgan as soon as possible?” “哦，那太花时间了，”安妮说。 “你不想尽快救出摩根吗？”

“Of course,” said Jack. “当然想，”杰克说。

“Then come on! Before the sun sets!” said Annie. “那就走吧！ 趁太阳还没落山！”安妮说。

She darted into the woods. 她一下子冲进了树林。

Jack sighed. He gave up on the idea of changing out of his bathing suit. 杰克叹了口气。 他放弃了换下泳衣这个念头。

He pushed his glasses into place. He followed Annie into the Frog Creek woods. 他把眼镜往上推了推。 他跟着安妮走进了蛙溪树林。

The warm air smelled fresh and green. 温暖的空气闻起来又清新又带着绿意。

He moved through patches of sunlight and shadow. Soon he came to a small clearing. 他穿过一片片阳光和阴影。 很快，他来到了一小块空地。

He looked up. There it was. The magic tree house in the tallest tree in the woods. 他抬头一看。就在那儿。 魔法树屋就在树林里最高的那棵树上。

“Hurry!” called Annie. She was climbing the rope ladder up to the tree house. “快点！”安妮喊道。 她正顺着绳梯往树屋上爬。

Jack grabbed the ladder. He started up after her. 杰克抓住绳梯。 他跟着她往上爬。

Finally they reached the tree house. 他们终于到了树屋。

Squeak. A mouse sat on the window sill. 一只老鼠坐在窗台上。

“Hi, Peanut!” cried Annie. “嗨，花生！”安妮叫道。

Jack patted the tiny head. 杰克轻轻拍了拍那小小的脑袋。

“Sorry we didn’t come sooner,” Annie said. “But we had to go to our swimming lesson.” “抱歉我们没能早点来，”安妮说。 “可我们得去上游泳课。”

Squeak.

“What happened while we were gone?” asked Annie, looking around the tree house. “我们不在的时候发生了什么？”安妮问，一边环顾树屋。

Jack stared at the large M carved into the wooden floor. 杰克盯着木地板上刻着的那个大大的 M。

On the M were a moonstone and a mango, the special things they’d found on their last two journeys. M 上面放着一块月长石和一只芒果，那是他们前两次旅行找到的特别东西。

“Hey, guess what?” said Jack. “ Moonstone and mango start with the letter M. Just like Morgan.” “嘿，你猜怎么着？”杰克说。 “月光石和芒果都是字母M开头。 跟摩根一样。”

“You’re right,” said Annie. “你说得对，”安妮说。

“I bet all four things start with an M,” said Jack. “我敢打赌，四样东西都以 M 开头，”杰克说。

“Right,” said Annie. “I wonder where we’ll find the next one.” “对，”安妮说。 “真不知道下一样我们会在哪儿找到。”

She and Jack stared at the stacks of books in the tree house. Books on the Amazon rain forest, ninjas, pirates, mummies, knights, and dinosaurs. 她和杰克盯着树屋里一摞摞的书。 有亚马逊雨林的书、忍者的书、海盗的书、木乃伊的书、骑士的书，还有恐龙的书。

All of them were closed. Only one book lay open in the corner. 那些书都是合上的。 只有一本书摊开着，放在角落里。

“We’re just about to find out,” said Jack. “我们马上就要知道了，”杰克说。

They walked over to the open book. 他们走到那本翻开的书旁边。

They looked at the page the book was opened to. It showed a picture of rocks and snow. 他们看了看书翻开的那一页。 上面画着岩石和雪。

“Wow,” said Annie, running her finger over the picture. “I love snow. I wish we could go there right now.” “哇，”安妮说，手指在图画上滑过。 “我爱雪。 真希望我们现在就能去那儿。”

“Wait,” said Jack. “We’re not prepared.” Then he had another thought. “And we’re wearing our bathing suits! Stop!” “等等，”杰克说。 “我们还没准备好。”接着他又有了另一个想法。 “而且我们还穿着泳衣！停下！”

“Oops,” said Annie. “哎呀，”安妮说。

Too late. The wind started to blow. 太晚了。 风开始刮起来。

The leaves started to shake. 树叶开始摇晃。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster! 它转得越来越快！

Then everything was silent. 然后，一切都静了下来。

It was as silent as the falling snow. 四周安静得像正在飘落的雪。
