3. Brrr! 3. 好冷啊！

“Annie!” whispered Jack again. “安妮！”杰克又低声叫道。

No answer. 没有回答。

He put his book quietly into his pack. He stepped deeper into the cave. 他把书轻轻放进背包。 他往山洞更深处走去。

“Annie!” he said a little louder. “安妮！”他把声音稍稍抬高了一点。

Jack stepped on the bones. 杰克踩在骨头上。

The wet dog smell grew stronger. 那股湿狗味更浓了。

He kept going, deeper into the smelly blackness. 他继续往前走，深入那臭烘烘的黑暗里。

He ran into something. He gasped. 他撞到了什么东西。他倒抽一口气。

“Jack?” said Annie. “Is that you?” “杰克？”安妮说。 “是你吗？”

“Didn’t you hear me calling?” Jack whispered. “We have to get out of here!” “你没听见我叫你吗？”杰克低声说。 “我们得马上离开这儿！”

“Wait,” she said. “Someone’s sleeping back there. Hear him snoring?” “等等，”她说。 “后面有人在睡觉。 听见他打呼噜了吗？

Jack heard a low, deep moaning. It was loud, then soft. Loud, then soft. 杰克听见一阵低沉、深沉的呻吟声。 声音时而响，时而轻。响，然后又轻。

“That’s not a person,” he said. “It’s a great cave bear!” “那不是人，”他说。 “那是一头大洞熊！”

A booming snore shattered the air. 一声低沉洪亮的鼾声炸开了空气。

“Yikes!” said Annie. “哎呀！”安妮说。

“Go! Go!” said Jack. “走！快走！”杰克说。

He and Annie ran through the cave, over the bones, and out into the falling snow. 他和安妮跑过山洞，踩过骨头，冲进飘落的雪里。

They kept on going. They ran between fallen rocks and under jagged cliffs. 他们继续往前跑。 他们从倒下的岩石间跑过，又钻过参差不齐的悬崖。

Finally they stopped and turned around. 终于他们停下来，转过身。

All they could see was snow and rocks and their own footprints. 他们能看见的只有雪、岩石，还有自己的脚印。

No bear. 没有熊。

“Whew,” said Annie. “That was lucky.” “呼，”安妮说。 “真走运。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “He probably never even woke up. We just got in a panic.” “是啊，”杰克说。 “他大概根本没醒。 我们只是自己慌了神。

Annie huddled close to Jack. “Brrr! I’m f-freezing,” she said. 安妮紧紧挨着杰克。 “好冷啊！ 我……我快冻僵了，”她说。

“Me too,” he said. “我也是，”他说。

He took off his glasses to wipe off the snow. The cold wind blew against his bare legs. 他摘下眼镜，把上面的雪擦掉。 寒风吹打着他裸露的腿。

“Wow,” Annie said. “Look.” She pointed to something behind Jack. “哇，”安妮说。 “看。”她指着杰克身后的什么东西。

“What?” Jack put his glasses back on and turned around. “什么？”杰克重新戴上眼镜，转过身。

Under a cliff was a wide ledge. Under the ledge was another cave. 悬崖下面有一块宽宽的岩架。 岩架下面还有另一个山洞。

Only this cave seemed to have a golden glow. 不过这个山洞似乎透着金色的光。

This one looked cozy and safe and warm. 这个看起来又舒适、又安全、又暖和。
