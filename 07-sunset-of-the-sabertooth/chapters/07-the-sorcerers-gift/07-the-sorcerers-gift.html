7. The Sorcerer’s Gift 7. 巫师的礼物

The sorcerer didn’t speak. He stared through the eyeholes of the owl mask. 巫师没有说话。 他从猫头鹰面具的眼洞往外盯着看。

“Help us, please,” said Annie. “请帮帮我们，”安妮说。

The sorcerer threw a rope into the pit. Jack grabbed it. 巫师把一根绳子扔进坑里。杰克抓住了它。

“He wants to pull us up,” said Annie. “他想把我们拉上去，”安妮说。

Jack looked up. The sorcerer was gone. 杰克抬起头。 巫师不见了。

“Where did he go?” Jack said. “他去哪儿了？”杰克说。

“Tug on the rope,” said Annie. “拉拉绳子，”安妮说。

Jack tugged. The rope tightened. It began rising. 杰克拉了拉。 绳子绷紧了。它开始往上升。

“I’ll go first!” said Annie cheerfully. “我先上！”安妮高兴地说。

“Annie, this isn’t a game,” warned Jack. “安妮，这可不是游戏，”杰克警告说。

“Don’t worry, I’ll be careful.” “别担心，我会小心的。”

Jack gave her the rope. “Okay. But hold on tight,” he said. 杰克把绳子给了她。 “好。 但要抓紧，”他说。

Annie held the rope with both hands. She pushed her feet against the side of the pit. She rose into the air with the rope. 安妮两只手抓住绳子。 她用脚抵着坑壁。 她随着绳子升到空中。

She kept pressing against the side of the pit—until she reached the top. 她一直用脚抵着坑壁——直到爬到了顶上。

Jack saw the sorcerer reappear and help Annie up. Then they moved out of sight. 杰克看见巫师又出现了，把安妮拉了上去。 然后他们就从视线里消失了。

Jack was puzzled. The sorcerer had used both hands to help Annie. So who held the other end of the rope? 杰克感到很困惑。 巫师用两只手帮安妮上去了。 那绳子的另一头是谁在拉？

“Wow!” came Annie’s voice. “哇！”传来安妮的声音。

What’s going on? Jack wondered. 怎么回事？杰克想。

The sorcerer came back and threw the rope down again. 巫师回来了，又把绳子扔了下来。

Jack grabbed it. And the rope started to rise. 杰克抓住它。 绳子开始往上升。

Jack held on tight. He started up. His hands burned. His arms felt as if they were being pulled out of their sockets. 杰克紧紧抓住。他开始往上。 他的手火辣辣地疼。 他觉得胳膊像要从肩窝里被拽出来一样。

But he kept his hold on the rope and his feet against the side of the pit. 可他仍然抓紧绳子，脚抵着坑壁。

At the top the sorcerer pulled Jack onto the snowy ground. 到了顶部，巫师把杰克拉上了白雪覆盖的地面。

“Thanks,” said Jack, standing. “谢谢，”杰克站起来说。

The sorcerer was tall. He wore a long fur robe. Jack could see only his eyes through the owl mask. 巫师很高。 他穿着一件长长的毛皮袍。 透过猫头鹰面具，杰克只能看见他的眼睛。

“Hey!” Annie called. “嘿！”安妮喊道。

Jack turned. 杰克转过身。

Annie was sitting on a woolly mammoth. 安妮正坐在一头长毛猛犸象上。

Squeak. Peanut was sitting on the mammoth’s head. 花生正坐在猛犸象的头上。

The mammoth looked like a giant elephant with shaggy reddish hair and long, curved tusks. 这头猛犸象看起来像一头巨象，长着蓬松的淡红色毛和又长又弯的象牙。

The other end of the rope was around the mammoth’s huge neck. 绳子的另一头绕在猛犸象巨大的脖子上。

“Lulu pulled us up,” said Annie. “露露把我们拉上来的，”安妮说。

“Lulu?” said Jack. “露露？”杰克说。

“Don’t you think she looks like a Lulu?” said Annie. “你不觉得她看起来像个露露吗？”安妮说。

“Oh brother,” said Jack. He walked up to the mammoth. “唉，”杰克说。 他走到猛犸象跟前。

“Hey, mammoth starts with M,” said Annie. “Maybe Lulu’s the special thing!” “嘿，猛犸象是以 M 开头的，”安妮说。 “也许露露就是那件特别的东西！”

“I don’t think so,” said Jack. “我不这么觉得，”杰克说。

The great creature knelt down, just like a circus elephant. 这头巨兽跪了下来，就像马戏团的大象一样。

“Whoa!” said Annie. She clutched the mammoth’s ears to keep from falling off. “哇！”安妮说。 她抓住猛犸象的耳朵，免得自己掉下去。

The sorcerer helped Jack climb onto the mammoth. 巫师帮杰克爬上猛犸象。

“Thanks,” said Jack. “谢谢，”杰克说。

Then the sorcerer reached into a pouch. He pulled out a smooth white bone and handed it to Jack. 接着巫师把手伸进一个小袋子里。 他拿出一根光滑的白骨头，递给杰克。

The bone was hollow. It had four holes along one side. And two on the other. 骨头是空心的。 一边有四个孔。 另一边有两个。

“Oh man, I think it’s his flute,” said Jack. “The book said they make flutes from mammoth bones.” “天哪，我觉得这是他的笛子，”杰克说。 “书上说他们用猛犸象骨头做笛子。”

Jack tried to give the flute back to the sorcerer. 杰克想把笛子还给巫师。

“Nice,” he said politely. “很好，”他礼貌地说。

But the sorcerer held up his hand. 可巫师举起了手。

“He wants you to keep the mammoth bone,” said Annie. “他想让你留下这根猛犸象骨头，”安妮说。

“Mammoth bone, ” whispered Jack. ‘‘Hey, maybe this is the third thing.” “猛犸象骨头，”杰克低声说。 “嘿，也许这就是第三样东西。”

Jack looked at the sorcerer. “Do you know Morgan?” he asked.

The sorcerer did not answer. But his eyes sparkled with kindness. 巫师没有回答。 可他的眼睛闪着善意的光。

He turned away from Jack and untied the mammoth’s rope. Then he whispered in the ear of the giant woolly creature. 他转身离开杰克，解开猛犸象的绳子。 然后他对着这头巨大的长毛动物的耳朵低语。

When the mammoth stood up, Jack gripped Annie’s coat to keep from falling off. He felt miles above the ground. 猛犸象站起来时，杰克抓住安妮的外套，免得掉下去。 他觉得自己离地面有好几英里高。

He nestled behind Annie, in the dip between the mammoth’s head and huge curved back. 他靠在安妮身后，坐在猛犸象头部和巨大弧形背部之间的凹处。

The mammoth took slow, plodding steps across the snow, then picked up speed. 猛犸象迈着缓慢沉重的步子走过雪地，接着加快了速度。

“Where are we going?” said Jack as they bumped up and down. “我们要去哪儿？”杰克一边上下颠簸一边问。

“To the tree house!” said Annie. “去树屋！”安妮说。

“How does he know where it is?” said Jack. “他怎么知道树屋在哪儿？”杰克说。

“She just knows,” said Annie. “她就是知道，”安妮说。

Jack looked back. 杰克回头看。

The sorcerer was standing in the snow, watching them. 巫师站在雪地里，看着他们。

But at that moment the clouds parted, and the sun came out. 可就在那一刻，云分开了，太阳出来了。

Jack was blinded by sunlight on the snow. 雪地上的阳光刺得杰克睁不开眼。

He squinted to see—but the sorcerer had vanished. 他眯起眼睛去看——可巫师已经消失了。
