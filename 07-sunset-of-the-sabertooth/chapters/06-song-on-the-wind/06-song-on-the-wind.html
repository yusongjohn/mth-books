6. Song on the Wind 6. 风中的歌

“Peanut’s tracks,” said Annie. “We have to find her before she freezes.” “花生的脚印，”安妮说。 “我们得在她冻坏之前找到她。”

She wrapped her reindeer coat around her and headed across the snow. 她裹上驯鹿皮外套，朝雪地对面走去。

Jack pulled on his backpack and followed. 杰克背上背包跟了上去。

The mouse’s tracks led them between the fallen rocks and back onto the open plain. 老鼠的足迹领着他们穿过倒下的岩石，又回到开阔的平原上。

The wind blew harder. Snow swirled over the ground, covering the tiny footprints. 风刮得更猛了。 雪在地面上打着旋，盖住了那些小小的脚印。

“I can’t see them anymore!” wailed Annie. “我再也看不见那些脚印了！”安妮哭着说。

She and Jack now stood in the middle of the plain. They stared at the windswept snow. 她和杰克这会儿正站在平原中央。 他们盯着被大风刮过的雪地。

The mouse’s tracks had vanished. 老鼠的足迹消失了。

“Yikes,” whispered Annie, staring up. “哎呀，”安妮低声说，抬头望着。

Jack followed her gaze. On one of the cliffs was a tiger. A giant tiger with two long, sharp fangs. 杰克顺着她的目光看去。 其中一座悬崖上有一只老虎。 一只巨虎，长着两颗又长又尖的獠牙。

“A sabertooth, ” said Jack. “一只剑齿虎，”杰克说。

“I hope he doesn’t see us,” whispered Annie. “希望它没看见我们，”安妮低声说。

“Me too,” Jack whispered back. “We’d better head back to the tree house.” “我也是，”杰克低声答道。 “我们最好回树屋去。”

Jack and Annie stepped very softly across the snow. Then Jack glanced back at the cliff. 杰克和安妮非常轻地踩着雪走。 然后杰克回头看了看悬崖。

The sabertooth was gone. 剑齿虎不见了。

“Oh man,” he said. “Where is he?” “天哪，”他说。 “它去哪儿了？”

“Run to the trees!” said Annie. “跑向那些树！”安妮说。

He and Annie started running. They ran over the snowy plain, heading toward the tall, bare trees in the distance. 他和安妮开始跑。 他们跑过白雪皑皑的平原，朝远处光秃秃的高树奔去。

Suddenly Jack heard a crack. 突然，杰克听见咔嚓一声。

The ground caved in, and Jack went with it. 地面塌了下去，杰克也跟着掉了下去。

Annie fell beside him. 安妮倒在他旁边。

They crashed down onto a heap of branches, snow, and earth. 他们摔到一堆树枝、雪和泥土上。

They struggled to stand. Jack pushed his glasses into place. 他们挣扎着想站起来。 杰克把眼镜往上推了推。

“You all right?” he asked Annie. “你没事吧？”他问安妮。

“Yes,” she said. “没事，”她说。

They both looked up. They were in a deep hole. All Jack could see were gray clouds moving overhead. 他们俩都抬起头。 他们掉进了一个深深的坑里。 杰克能看见的只有头顶上飘过的灰云。

“This is a trap,” Jack said. “The Cro-Magnons must have put branches over this hole. Then the snow hid the branches. So we didn’t see them.” “这是个陷阱，”杰克说。 “克罗马农人一定在坑口铺了树枝。 然后雪把树枝盖住了。 所以我们没看见。”

“There’s no way out,” said Annie. “没有出路，”安妮说。

She was right. They were helpless. The pit was too deep to climb out of. 她说得对。 他们毫无办法。 坑太深了，爬不出去。

“I feel like a trapped animal,” Annie said. “我觉得自己像只被困住的动物，”安妮说。

“Me too,” said Jack. “我也是，”杰克说。

He heard a yowl in the distance. 他听见远处传来一声嚎叫。

“The sabertooth!” whispered Annie. “剑齿虎！”安妮低声说。

Jack pulled out the Ice Age book. He found a picture of the sabertooth. He read: 杰克拿出冰河时代书。 他找到一幅剑齿虎的图。他读道：

The sabertooth was the fiercest beast of the Ice Age. It attacked humans as well as woolly mammoths and other large animals. 剑齿虎是冰河时代最凶猛的野兽。 它会袭击人类，也会袭击长毛猛犸象和其他大动物。

“Oh brother,” said Jack. “唉，”杰克说。

“Listen!” Annie grabbed him. “听！”安妮抓住他。

“What?” Jack jumped. “什么？”杰克跳了起来。

“I hear music.” “我听见音乐了。”

Jack listened. But all he heard was the wind. 杰克听了听。 可他只听见风声。

“You hear it?” said Annie. “你听见了吗？”安妮说。

“No,” said Jack. “没有，”杰克说。

“Listen carefully.” “仔细听。”

He closed his eyes. He listened very carefully. 他闭上眼睛。 他非常仔细地听。

He heard the wind. But this time he heard another sound, too. 他听见风声。 可这一次，他还听见了另一种声音。

Strange, haunting music. 奇怪而萦绕不去的音乐。

“Ahhh!” cried Annie. “啊！”安妮叫道。

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开眼睛。

Staring down at them was a figure wearing reindeer antlers and an owl mask. 低头盯着他们的，是一个戴着驯鹿角和猫头鹰面具的身影。

“The sorcerer,” whispered Jack. “巫师，”杰克低声说。

Squeak.

Peanut peered down at them, too! 花生也在往下看着他们！
