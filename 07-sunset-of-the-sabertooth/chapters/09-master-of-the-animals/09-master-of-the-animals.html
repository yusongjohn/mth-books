9. Master of the Animals 9. 动物之主

The sabertooth crept forward. 剑齿虎悄悄向前爬。

Jack stared in horror. 杰克惊恐地盯着看。

“Play the flute,” whispered Annie. “吹笛子，”安妮小声说。

Is she nuts? Jack thought. 她疯了吗？杰克想。

“Try!” said Annie. “试试！”安妮说。

Jack held the mammoth-bone flute to his lips. He blew. 杰克把猛犸象骨笛子放到唇边。他吹了起来。

The flute made a strange sound. 笛子发出一种奇怪的声音。

The tiger froze. He glared at Jack. 老虎愣住了。 它瞪着杰克。

Jack’s hands shook. 杰克的手在发抖。

The tiger growled. He took another step. 老虎低吼着。 它又迈了一步。

The mammoth roared and stomped the ground. 猛犸象吼叫着，重重地跺着地面。

“Play it!” said Annie. “Keep playing!” “吹啊！”安妮说。 “继续吹！”

Jack blew again. 杰克又吹起来。

The sabertooth froze again. 剑齿虎又愣住了。

Jack kept blowing until he ran out of breath. 杰克不停地吹，直到喘不上气。

The tiger snarled. 老虎呲牙低吼了一声。

“He’s still here,” whispered Annie. “Keep it up.” “它还在这儿，”安妮低声说。 “继续吹。”

Jack closed his eyes. He took a deep breath. Then he blew as hard and as long as he could. He covered and uncovered the holes on the bone. 杰克闭上眼睛。 他深深吸了一口气。 然后他用尽全力，尽可能长地吹。 他捂上又放开骨头上的那些孔。

The music sounded strange—as if it were coming from another world. 那音乐听起来很奇异——仿佛来自另一个世界。

“He’s leaving!” Annie whispered.

Jack raised his eyes. The sabertooth was slinking off toward the cliffs. 杰克抬起眼睛。 剑齿虎正朝悬崖方向溜走。

“We did it!” said Annie. “我们成功了！”安妮说。

Jack lowered the flute. He felt very tired. 杰克放下笛子。 他觉得非常累。

The mammoth waved her trunk happily. 猛犸象高兴地甩着鼻子。

“To the tree house, Lulu,” said Annie.

The woolly mammoth snorted. Then she lumbered over to the tallest tree. 长毛猛犸象喷了喷鼻息。 接着她迈着沉重的步子走到那棵最高的树旁。

From the back of the mammoth, Jack grabbed the rope ladder. He held it for Annie. 杰克从猛犸象背上抓住绳梯。 他帮安妮扶着。

She stroked the mammoth’s giant ear. “Bye, Lulu. Thank you,” she said. 她抚摸着猛犸象巨大的耳朵。 “再见，露露。 谢谢你，”她说。

Annie grabbed the rope ladder. Then she started up. Peanut climbed up, too. 安妮抓住绳梯。 然后她开始往上爬。 花生也爬了上去。

After they disappeared into the tree house, Jack climbed onto the ladder. 他们消失在树屋里之后，杰克爬上了梯子。

He looked back at the woolly mammoth. “Bye, girl,” he said. “Go home now. And watch out for the sabertooth.” 他回头看着长毛猛犸象。 “再见，姑娘，”他说。 “现在回家吧。 还要小心剑齿虎。

The mammoth walked away into the sunset. 猛犸象走进了夕阳里。

When Jack couldn’t see her anymore, he started up the rope ladder. He pulled himself into the tree house. 当杰克再也看不见她时，他开始顺着绳梯往上爬。 他把自己拉进了树屋。

“Ta-da!” said Annie. She handed the Pennsylvania book to Jack. “嗒嗒！”安妮说。 她把宾夕法尼亚那本书递给杰克。

Jack smiled. Now he was positive they had found the third M thing. Their mission was complete. 杰克笑了。 这下他确信他们已经找到了第三件M宝物。 他们的任务完成了。

“Before we leave, we have to give our coats back,” said Annie. “走之前，我们得把外套还回去，”安妮说。

“Oh right,” said Jack. “哦对，”杰克说。

They took off their reindeer-skin coats and dropped them to the ground. 他们脱下驯鹿皮外套，扔到地上。

“Brrr!” said Annie. “I hope the Cro-Magnon people find them.” “好冷啊！”安妮说。 “希望克罗马农人能找到它们。”

Jack stared out the window. He wanted to take one last look at the prehistoric world. 杰克望着窗外。 他想再看最后一眼这个史前世界。

The sun was setting behind the hills. Four people were crossing the snowy plain. It was the Cro-Magnon family. 太阳正落在山丘后面。 四个人正穿过白雪皑皑的平原。 是克罗马农人一家。

“Hey!” shouted Annie. “嘿！”安妮喊道。

“Shhh!” said Jack. “嘘！”杰克说。

The Cro-Magnons stopped and peered in Annie and Jack’s direction. 克罗马农人停下来，朝安妮和杰克的方向张望。

“We left your reindeer skins! Down there!” Annie pointed to the ground. “我们把你们的驯鹿皮留在那儿了！ 就在下面！”安妮指着地面。

The tallest person stepped forward and raised a spear. 最高的那个人走上前，举起了一支长矛。

“Time to go,” said Jack. “该走了，”杰克说。

He grabbed the Pennsylvania book. He found the picture of Frog Creek and pointed at it. “I wish we could go home,” he said. 他抓起宾夕法尼亚那本书。 他找到蛙溪的图画，指着它。 “我希望我们能回家，”他说。

“Good- bye! Good luck!” Annie called, waving out the window. “再见！祝你们好运！”安妮探出窗口挥手喊道。

The wind started to blow. And the tree house started to spin. 风开始刮起来。 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静了下来。

Absolutely still. 完全静止。
