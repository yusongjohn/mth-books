5. Snow Tracks 5. 雪地足迹

“Wow, what is this place?” said Jack. “哇，这是什么地方？”杰克说。

“Maybe it’s an art gallery,” said Annie. “也许这是个画廊，”安妮说。

“I don’t think so,” said Jack. “It’s too hard to get to.” “我不这么觉得，”杰克说。 “太难进去了。”

He read about the cave paintings: 他读了读关于洞穴壁画的内容：

These Ice Age beasts were painted 25,000 years ago. Cro-Magnons painted pictures of animals they hunted. They may have believed the paintings would give them power over the animals. 这些冰河时代的野兽是两万五千年前画的。 克罗马农人画下他们猎捕的动物。 他们也许相信，这些画能让他们支配那些动物。

“Wow, look at this,” said Annie. “哇，看这个，”安妮说。

She pointed at a painting farther down the wall. 她指着墙上更远处的一幅画。

It showed a figure with human arms and legs, reindeer antlers, and an owl face. It seemed to be holding a flute. 那幅画画着一个有人手人腿、驯鹿角和猫头鹰脸的形象。 它好像拿着一支笛子。

Jack looked at the book again. He found a picture of the figure and read: 杰克又看了看书。 他找到那个人物的图，读道：

Cave men may have been led by a sorcerer, or “Master of the Animals.” He may have worn reindeer antlers so he could run like a reindeer—and an owl mask so he could see like an owl. 穴居人也许由一位巫师带领，也就是“动物之主”。他也许戴着驯鹿角，好能跑得像驯鹿一样——还戴着猫头鹰面具，好能看得像猫头鹰一样。

“What is it?” said Annie. “那是什么？”安妮说。

“The Master of the Animals,” said Jack. “He’s a sorcerer.” “动物之主，”杰克说。 “他是个巫师。”

“Oh wow,” breathed Annie. “That’s it.” “哦哇，”安妮喘着气说。 “就是它。”

“That’s what?” “什么就是它？”

“That’s who we have to find.” “就是我们要找的人。”

“Why?” “为什么？”

“Maybe he’s a friend of Morgan’s,” said Annie. “也许他是摩根的朋友，”安妮说。

Jack nodded slowly. “Maybe,” he said. 杰克慢慢点了点头。 “也许吧，”他说。

“Let’s go find him,” said Annie. “我们去找他吧，”安妮说。

They went back through the tunnel into the first cave. 他们穿过隧道，回到第一个山洞。

“We better put our lamps back,” said Jack. “我们最好把灯放回去，”杰克说。

He and Annie blew out their lamps. 他和安妮把灯吹灭了。

They placed them back by the fire. 他们把灯放回火堆旁边。

Jack’s backpack was on the floor next to the skins. He put the Ice Age book into it. 杰克的背包就在地上，紧挨着那些兽皮。 他把冰河时代书放了进去。

“How’s Peanut?” said Annie. “花生怎么样了？”安妮说。

Jack looked into his pack. “She’s not here,” he said. 杰克往背包里看。 “她不在这儿，”他说。

“Oh no!” cried Annie. “She must have crawled out when we were looking at the paintings.’’ “哦不！”安妮叫道。 “一定是我们看那些画的时候，她爬出去了。”

“Peanut!” Jack called. “花生！”杰克喊道。

“Peanut!” called Annie. “花生！”安妮喊道。

Annie walked slowly around the cave, looking into the shadows. 安妮在洞里慢慢走着，往阴影里看。

Jack peered around the fire and under each of the furry skins. 杰克往火堆四周看，又掀起每一张毛茸茸的兽皮查看。

“Jack! Come here!” said Annie. “杰克！ 到这儿来！”安妮说。

She was standing near the entrance to the cave. 她站在靠近洞口的地方。

The snow had stopped falling. 雪已经停了。

In the snow were tiny tracks. 雪地上有细小的足迹。
