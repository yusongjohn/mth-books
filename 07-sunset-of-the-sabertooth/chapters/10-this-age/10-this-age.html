10. This Age 10. 这个时代

Birds sang. The air was soft and warm. 鸟儿在歌唱。 空气柔和而温暖。

“I hope they find their coats,” said Annie. “希望他们能找到外套，”安妮说。

“Me too,” said Jack. He pushed his glasses into place. “我也是，”杰克说。 他把眼镜往上推了推。

Squeak.

“Hey, you—how did you find the sorcerer?” Annie asked Peanut. “嘿，你——你是怎么找到巫师的？”安妮问花生。

Squeak.

“It’s a secret, huh?” said Annie. She turned to Jack. “Where’s the flute?” “这是个秘密，对吧？”安妮说。 她转向杰克。 “笛子呢？”

He held up the mammoth bone. Then he placed it on the M carved into the floor. Next to the mango from the rain forest. Next to the moonstone from the time of ninjas. 他举起猛犸象骨头。 然后把它放在地板上刻着的 M 上面。 挨着雨林来的芒果。 挨着忍者时代来的月长石。

“Moonstone, mango, mammoth bone … ” Annie said. “We need just one more M thing. Then Morgan will be free from her spell.” “我们只再差一件M宝物。” 这样摩根就能摆脱咒语了。

“Tomorrow,” said Jack. “明天吧，”杰克说。

Annie patted Peanut on the head. “Bye, you,” she said. 安妮拍了拍花生的头。 “再见，你，”她说。

She started down the rope ladder. 她开始顺着绳梯往下爬。

Jack gathered his things. 杰克收拾好自己的东西。

He paused and glanced at the mouse. She stared at him with big brown eyes. 他停了一下，看了那只老鼠一眼。 她用大大的棕色眼睛盯着他。

“Thanks again for helping us,” he said. “再次谢谢你帮助我们，”他说。

Then he climbed down the rope ladder and jumped onto the ground. 然后他爬下绳梯，跳到地上。

Jack and Annie ran through the Frog Creek woods onto their street. 杰克和安妮跑过蛙溪树林，跑上他们的街道。

Their neighborhood looked rosy in the sunset. 他们的街区在夕阳里看起来一片玫瑰色。

It’s great to be back in this age, Jack thought. Warm and safe and almost home. 回到这个时代真好，杰克想。 温暖、安全，而且差不多到家了。

“I’m glad we don’t have to go hunting for dinner,” he said. “真高兴我们不用去打猎找晚饭，”他说。

“Yeah, Mom and Dad already did that,” said Annie, “at the supermarket.” “是啊，爸爸妈妈已经找好了，”安妮说，“在超市里。”

“I hope they trapped some spaghetti and meatballs,” said Jack. “希望他们捕获了一些意大利面和肉丸，”杰克说。

“I hope they trapped a pizza,” said Annie. “希望他们捕获了一个比萨，”安妮说。

“Hurry, I’m starving,” said Jack. “快点，我饿死了，”杰克说。

They ran up their sidewalk and through their front door. 他们沿着自家门前的人行道跑上去，冲进了前门。

“We’re home!” shouted Annie. “我们回来了！”安妮喊道。

“What’s for dinner?” shouted Jack. “晚饭吃什么？”杰克喊道。
