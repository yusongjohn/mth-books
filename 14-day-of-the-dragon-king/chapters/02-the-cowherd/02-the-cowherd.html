2. The Cowherd 2. 放牛郎

“Oh, wow,” Annie said. “These clothes feel so soft. And look, I have a pocket for my secret library card.” “哦，哇。”安妮说。 “这些衣服摸起来好柔软。 看，我还有口袋放秘密图书卡。”

Jack opened his eyes. Their clothes had magically changed. 杰克睁开眼睛。 他们的衣服神奇地变了。

They no longer wore jeans, T-shirts, and sneakers. Instead, they had on baggy pants, loose shirts, straw shoes, and round hats. Annie’s shirt had a pocket in it. 他们不再穿着牛仔裤、T恤和运动鞋。 取而代之的是肥大的裤子、宽松的衬衫、草鞋和圆帽子。 安妮的衣服上有个口袋。

Jack saw that his backpack had become a rough cloth sack. Inside were his research book, his notebook, his library card, and the bamboo strip. 杰克看见自己的背包变成了一个粗糙的布袋。 里面有他的研究书、笔记本、图书卡，还有那根竹简。

“Cows,” said Annie, looking out the window. “牛。”安妮看着窗外说。

Jack looked out, too. The tree house had landed in a lone tree in a sunny field. Cows grazed, and a young man stood watching over them. At the edge of the field was a farmhouse. Beyond the house was a walled city. 杰克也往外看。 树屋落在阳光照耀的田野里一棵孤零零的树上。 牛在吃草，一个年轻人站在那里照看它们。 田野边上有一座农舍。 房子那边是一座有围墙的城。

“It looks so peaceful,” said Annie. “看起来好平静。”安妮说。

“You can never be sure,” said Jack. “Remember, Pompeii looked peaceful before the volcano went off.” “你永远不能确定。”杰克说。 “记住，火山爆发之前，庞贝看起来也很平静。”

“Oh, yeah,” said Annie. “哦，对。”安妮说。

“Let’s see what the book says,” said Jack. “咱们看看书怎么说。”杰克说。

He reached in the sack and pulled out the China book. He opened it and read aloud: 他伸手进布袋，抽出那本中国书。 他打开书，大声读道：

Over 2,000 years ago, China was ruled by its first emperor. Because he chose the dragon to be his symbol, he was called the “Dragon King.” In China, dragons are seen as brave and powerful creatures. 2000多年前，中国由第一位皇帝统治。因为他选择了龙作为自己的象征，所以他被称为“龙王”。在中国，龙被视为勇敢而强大的生物。

“Dragon King? That sounds a little scary,” said Jack. “龙王？ 听起来有点吓人。”杰克说。

“I like his outfit,” said Annie. “我喜欢他那身衣服，”安妮说。

Next to the writing was a picture. It showed a man wearing a rich, flowing robe with wide sleeves. He also wore a tall hat with beads hanging from it. 文字旁边有一幅画。 图上显示一个男人穿着华丽飘动、袖子宽大的长袍。 他还戴着一顶高帽子，上面垂着珠子。

Jack pulled out his notebook and wrote: 杰克抽出笔记本，写道：

图中文字：first emperor called Dragon King 图中文字：第一位皇帝被称为龙王

“The book we need must be in the Dragon King’s library,” said Annie. “I bet his palace is in that city.” “我们需要的书一定在龙王的图书馆里。”安妮说。 “我敢肯定他的宫殿就在那座城里。”

Jack looked up. 杰克抬起头。

“Right,” he said. “And that’s how to get there.” He pointed across the field to a dirt road that led to the walled city. “对。”他说。 “那就是去那里的路。”他指着田野对面那条通向围墙城的泥土路。

“Good plan,” said Annie. “好计划。”安妮说。

She climbed out of the tree house and started down the rope ladder. 她爬出树屋，顺着绳梯往下走。

Jack threw the China book and his notebook into his sack. He slung the sack over his shoulder and followed Annie. 杰克把中国书和笔记本扔进布袋。 他把布袋挎在肩上，跟着安妮走。

When they reached the ground, they started through the field. 他们落到地面后，开始穿过田野。

“Look, that guy’s waving at us,” said Annie. “看，那个人在向我们招手。”安妮说。

The man tending the cows was shouting and waving. He started running toward them. 那个照看牛的人正一边喊一边挥手。 他朝他们跑过来。

“Uh-oh, what’s he want?” said Jack. “呃哦，他想干什么？”杰克说。

A moment later, the man stood in their path. He was young and handsome with a kind face. 过了一会儿，那人挡在了他们前面。 他年轻英俊，面容和善。

“Can you do me a great favor?” he asked. “I would be most grateful.” “你们能帮我一个大忙吗？”他问。 “我会非常感激。”

“Of course,” said Annie. “当然。”安妮说。

“Give a message to the silk weaver. You will see her at the farmhouse,” said the young man. “Tell her to meet me here at twilight.” “给织丝女带个口信。 你会在农舍见到她。”年轻人说。 “告诉她黄昏时分在这儿见我。”

“Sure, no problem,” said Annie. “好，没问题。”安妮说。

The young man smiled. 年轻人笑了。

“Thank you,” he said. Then he started to leave. “谢谢。”他说。 然后他开始离开。

“Wait, excuse me—” said Jack. “Do you know where we can find the Imperial Library?” “等等，打扰一下——”杰克说。 “你知道皇家图书馆在哪儿吗？”

A look of horror crossed the man’s kind face. 惊恐的神色从那人和善的脸上闪过。

“Why?” he whispered. “为什么？”他低声说。

“Oh, I—I just wondered,” said Jack. “哦，我——我只是想问问。”杰克说。

The young man shook his head. 年轻人摇了摇头。

“Beware of the Dragon King,” he said. “Whatever you do, beware.” “要提防龙王，”他说。 “不管你们做什么，都要小心。”

Then he turned and ran back to his cows. 然后他转身跑回牛群那里。

“Oh, man,” whispered Jack. “Now we know one thing for sure.” “天哪。”杰克小声说。 “现在有一件事我们确定了。”

“What?” asked Annie. “什么？”安妮问。

“This place is not as peaceful as it seems,” Jack said. “这个地方并不像看上去那么平静，”杰克说。
