5. The Scholar 5. 学者

“It’s a book from our country,” said Jack. “Your books are made of bamboo, but ours are made of paper. Actually, your country invented paper. But later, in the future.” “这是我们国家的书。”杰克说。 “你们的书是竹子做的，我们的则是纸做的。 其实，纸是你们国家发明的。 不过是以后，在未来。”

The man looked confused. 那人看上去很困惑。

“Never mind,” said Annie. “It’s for reading. It’s for learning about faraway places.” “没关系。”安妮说。 “是用来读的。 它是用来了解遥远地方的。”

The man stared at them. Tears filled his eyes. 那人盯着他们。 他眼里含着泪水。

“What’s wrong?” Annie asked softly. “怎么了？”安妮轻声问。

“I love reading and learning,” he said. “我热爱阅读和学习。”他说。

“So do I,” said Jack. “我也是。”杰克说。

The man smiled. “You don’t understand! I am dressed as a farmer,” he said. “But in truth, I am a scholar!” 那人笑了。 “你们不明白！ 我打扮成了农夫，”他说。 “可其实，我是个学者！”

“What’s a scholar?” said Annie. “什么是学者？”安妮说。

“We are great readers, learners, and writers,” he said. “We have long been the most honored citizens in China.” “我们是伟大的读者、学者和写作者。”他说。 “我们长久以来都是中国最受尊敬的公民。”

The scholar’s smile faded. 学者的笑容消失了。

“But now scholars are in danger,” he said. “And many of us have gone into hiding.” “可现在学者处于危险之中，”他说。 “我们当中许多人都藏起来了。”

“Why?” said Jack. “为什么？”杰克说。

“The Dragon King is afraid of the power of our books and learning,” said the scholar. “He wants people to think only what he wants them to think. Any day he may order the burning of the books!” “龙王害怕我们书籍和学问的力量，”学者说。 “他只想让人们想他想让他们想的事。 他随时都可能下令焚书！”

Annie gasped. 安妮倒吸一口气。

“Does that mean what I think it means?” said Jack. “那是不是我以为的那个意思？”杰克说。

The scholar nodded. 学者点了点头。

“All the books in the Imperial Library will be burned,” he said. “皇家图书馆里所有的书都将被烧掉。”他说。

“That’s rotten!” said Annie. “太可恶了！”安妮说。

“Indeed it is!” the scholar said quietly. “确实如此！”学者轻声说。

“Listen, we have a mission to get a book from that library,” said Jack. “听着，我们有个任务，要从那个图书馆取一本书。”杰克说。

“Who are you?” asked the scholar. “你们是谁？”学者问。

“Show him,” said Annie. “给他看。”安妮说。

She reached into her shirt pocket as Jack reached into his sack. They brought out the secret library cards. The letters shimmered in the sunlight. 她把手伸进衬衫口袋，杰克则把手伸进布袋。 他们拿出秘密图书卡。 字母在阳光下闪闪发光。

The scholar’s mouth dropped open again. 学者又张大了嘴。

“You are Master Librarians,” he said. “I have never met ones so honored who were so young.” “你们是图书大师。”他说。 “我从未见过如此受尊敬又这么年轻的人。”

He bowed to show his respect. 他鞠躬表示敬意。

“Thank you,” said Jack and Annie. “谢谢。”杰克和安妮说。

They bowed back to him. 他们也向他鞠躬。

“How can I help you?” asked the scholar. “我能怎样帮助你们？”学者问。

“We need to go to the Imperial Library and find this book,” said Jack. “我们需要去皇家图书馆，找到这本书。”杰克说。

He held out Morgan’s bamboo strip to the scholar. 他把摩根的竹简递给学者。

“We will go to the Imperial Library,” said the scholar. “As for the story, I know it well. It is a true one, written not long ago. But I warn you. We will be in great danger.” “我们去皇家图书馆。”学者说。 “至于那个故事，我很熟悉。 那是一个真实的传说，不久前才写成。但我要警告你们。 我们会有很大危险。”

“We know!” said Annie. “我们知道！”安妮说。

The scholar smiled. 学者笑了。

“I am happy to be doing something I believe in again,” said the scholar. “Let us go!” “我很高兴又能做自己相信的事了，”学者说。 “那我们走吧！”

They all climbed into the front of the cart. The long line of wall builders was marching in the distance. As the oxen started forward, the scholar turned to Jack and Annie. 他们都爬上了牛车前面。 那一长队筑墙的人正在远处行进。 牛车开动时，学者转向杰克和安妮。

“Where are you from?” he asked. “你们从哪儿来？”他问。

“Frog Creek, Pennsylvania,” said Annie. “宾夕法尼亚的蛙溪镇。”安妮说。

“I have never heard of it,” said the scholar. “Do they have a library there?” “我从没听说过。”学者说。 “那儿有图书馆吗？”

“Oh, sure, there’s a library in every town,” said Jack. “In fact, there are probably thousands of libraries in our country.” “哦，当然，每个城镇都有图书馆。”杰克说。 “事实上，我们国家大概有成千上万座图书馆。”

“And millions of books,” said Annie. “And no one burns them.” “还有数百万本书，”安妮说。 “而且没人烧它们。”

“Right,” said Jack. “Everyone gets to go to school to learn to read them.” “对。”杰克说。 “每个人都可以上学学习读这些书。”

The scholar stared at him and shook his head. 学者盯着他，摇了摇头。

“It sounds like paradise,” he said. “听起来像天堂，”他说。
