6. The Dragon King 6. 龙王

The oxcart bumped across the wooden bridge that crossed over a moat. Then it passed guards standing by giant wooden gates. 牛车颠簸着驶过护城河上的木桥。 然后车子经过站在巨大木门旁的卫兵。

“Are the gates ever closed?” Jack asked. “城门曾经关过吗？”杰克问。

“Oh, yes, every day at sunset,” said the scholar. “When the gong sounds, the gates close. The bridge comes up. And the city is sealed shut for the night.” “哦，是的，每天日落时，”学者说。 “锣一响，城门就会关上。 吊桥会升起。 整座城就会被封死，直到天亮。”

“I guess visitors have to leave before that happens,” Annie said. “Or they’ll be stuck here for the whole night. Right?” “我想访客得在那之前离开。”安妮说。 “否则他们就要在这儿困上一整夜。对吧？”

“Yes,” said the scholar. “是的。”学者说。

The cart bumped between the city gates. 牛车颠簸着驶进城门。

Rows of small houses were bunched together on either side of the street. They were made of mud with straw roofs. People cooked over outdoor fires. They washed their clothes in wooden tubs. 一排排小房子挤在街道两边。 它们是泥做的，屋顶铺着稻草。 人们在户外火堆上做饭。 他们在木盆里洗衣服。

As the oxcart bumped along, the houses got larger. These were made of painted wood and pottery tiles. They all had curved roofs. 牛车继续颠簸前行，房子变得越来越大。 这些房子是用彩绘木头和陶瓦建的。 它们都有弯曲的屋顶。

“Why are those roofs like that?” asked Jack. “那些屋顶为什么是那样的？”杰克问。

“To keep away the bad spirits,” said the scholar. “为了赶走邪灵，”学者说。

“How do they do that?” said Annie. “他们是怎么做的？”安妮说。

“The spirits can only travel in straight lines,” said the scholar. “鬼魂只能沿直线行进，”学者说。

“Wow,” whispered Annie. “哇。”安妮小声说。

The cart went by some open tea shops. Then it passed a large market square filled with stalls and shoppers. People were buying and selling fish, chickens, firewood, wagon wheels, silk cloth, furs, and jade jewelry. 车子经过几家敞着门的茶馆。 接着它经过一个摆满摊位、挤满顾客的大集市广场。 人们在买卖鱼、鸡、柴火、车轮、丝绸、毛皮和玉器。

Some people were lined up at a stall filled with tiny cages. 有些人在一个摆满小笼子的摊位前排队。

“What’s for sale there?” said Annie. “那儿卖什么？”安妮说。

“Crickets,” the scholar said. “They make good pets. You can feed them tea leaves and enjoy their delicate song.” “蟋蟀，”学者说。 “它们是很好的宠物。 你可以喂它们茶叶，欣赏它们轻柔美妙的叫声。”

The cart moved on toward the Dragon King’s walled palace. They stopped in front of the palace gates. 车子继续向前，驶向龙王有围墙的宫殿。 他们停在宫殿大门前。

“Grain delivery!” the scholar shouted up to the guard at the tower. “送粮！”学者朝塔楼上的卫兵喊道。

The guard waved them through. Inside were beautiful gardens and huge mounds of earth surrounded by a low brick wall. 卫兵挥手示意他们通过。 里面是美丽的花园，还有被矮砖墙围着的巨大土堆。

“That is the Imperial Burial Grounds,” said the scholar, pointing at the mounds. “那是皇家墓地，”学者指着那些土堆说。

“Who is buried there?” asked Jack. “那儿埋的是谁？”杰克问。

“The ancestors of the Dragon King,” said the scholar. “龙王的祖先，”学者说。

“What are ancestors?” Annie asked. “什么是祖先？”安妮问。

“They are the people in your family who lived before you,” said the scholar. “Someday the Dragon King himself will be buried there. Three hundred thousand workers have been building his burial tomb.” “就是你家族里在你之前生活过的人。”学者说。 “有一天龙王自己也会葬在那儿。 三十万工人一直在建造他的陵墓。”

“Oh, man,” said Jack. “天哪。”杰克说。

He looked over his shoulder at the burial grounds. He wondered why it took so many workers to build a tomb. 他回头看着那片墓地。 他想知道为什么建一座墓需要那么多工人。

“No!” said the scholar. “不！”学者说。

Jack whirled around. 杰克猛地转过身。

“What’s wrong?” he asked. “怎么了？”他问。

The scholar pointed at the palace courtyard. A dark cloud of smoke was rising into the sky. 学者指向宫殿院子。 一团黑烟正升向天空。

“Fire!” said the scholar. “火！”学者说。

“The books!” said Jack. “那些书！”杰克说。

“Hurry!” said Annie. “快点！”安妮说。

The scholar snapped the reins. The oxen trotted up the stone path. When the cart rolled into the courtyard, soldiers were everywhere. 学者猛地一拉缰绳。 牛沿着石路小跑上去。 牛车滚进院子时，到处都是士兵。

Some threw wood on a huge bonfire. Others were carrying bamboo strips down the steep stairs that led from the palace. 有人把木头扔进巨大的火堆。 另一些人正沿着宫殿延伸下来的陡峭台阶搬运竹简。

“Are those books?” asked Jack. “那些是书吗？”杰克问。

“Yes. The strips are tied together into different bundles,” moaned the scholar. “Each bundle is a book.” “是的。 竹简被绑成不同的捆，”学者呻吟着说。 “每一捆都是一本书。”

“Look!” said Annie, pointing to the palace entrance. “看！”安妮指着宫殿入口说。

Stepping outside was a man in a rich, flowing robe and a tall hat. Jack knew him at once—the Dragon King! 一个穿着华丽长袍、戴着高帽子的男人正走出来。 杰克立刻认出了他——龙王！
