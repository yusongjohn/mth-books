3. The Silk Weaver 3. 织丝女

Jack and Annie kept walking across the pasture toward the road. Annie stopped when they neared the farmhouse. 杰克和安妮继续穿过牧场，朝大路走去。 快到农舍时，安妮停了下来。

“We have to find the silk weaver and give her the message,” she said. “我们得找到织丝女，把口信给她。”她说。

“Let’s do that on our way back,” said Jack. “I’m worried about finding the Imperial Library.” “我们回来的路上再做吧，”杰克说。 “我担心找不到皇家图书馆。”

“What if we don’t have time?” said Annie. “We promised. And he was so nice.” “要是我们来不及怎么办？”安妮说。 “我们答应过了。 而且他人很好。”

Jack sighed. 杰克叹了口气。

“Okay,” he said. “But let’s find her fast. And remember to keep your head down so no one will notice us.” “好吧。”他说。 “不过咱们快点找到她。 还要记得低着头，这样没人会注意到我们。”

Jack and Annie bowed their heads as they headed toward the house. 杰克和安妮低着头朝那座房子走去。

As they got closer, Jack peeked out from under his hat. An ox pulled a cart filled with hay. Men hoed the ground. Women pushed wheelbarrows piled high with grain. 当他们走近时，杰克从帽子底下往外偷看。 一头公牛拉着一辆装满干草的车。 男人们在用锄头锄地。 女人们推着堆得高高的装满粮食的独轮车。

“There!” said Annie. She pointed to an open porch where a young woman was weaving cloth on a loom. “That must be her!” “那儿！”安妮说。 她指着一个敞开的门廊，那里有个年轻女子正在织布机上织布。 “一定是她！”

Annie ran to the silk weaver. Jack looked around to see if anyone was watching. Luckily, all the farmworkers seemed too busy to notice anything. Still looking around carefully, Jack walked toward the porch. 安妮跑向织丝女。 杰克四处看，看有没有人在盯着。 幸好，所有农工都忙得顾不上注意任何事。 杰克仍然小心地四处张望，朝廊子走去。

Annie was already talking to the silk weaver. 安妮已经在跟织丝女说话了。

“What did he say?” the young woman asked. Her voice was soft but strong. Her dark eyes glowed with happiness. “他说什么了？”年轻女人问。 她的声音轻柔却有力。 她乌黑的眼睛闪耀着幸福。

“He said you should meet him in the field at twilight,” said Annie. “He’s so handsome!” “他说让你黄昏时在田里见他。”安妮说。 “他好英俊！”

“Yes, he is.” The silk weaver gave Annie a shy smile. Then she reached down to a basket near her loom and picked up a ball of yellow thread. “是的，他是。”织丝女给了安妮一个羞怯的微笑。 然后她伸手去拿织布机旁篮子里的一团黄线。

“It was very brave of you to bring the message,” she said. “Please accept this silk thread as my thank-you.” “你们来带口信真勇敢。”她说。 “请收下这团丝线，当作我的感谢。”

She handed Annie the ball of silk. 她把那团丝递给安妮。

“It’s beautiful,” said Annie. “Feel.” “真漂亮。”安妮说。 “摸摸看。”

She handed it to Jack. The thread was smooth and soft. 她把它递给杰克。 丝线又滑又软。

“How do you make silk?” said Jack. “丝绸是怎么做的？”杰克说。

“It is made from the cocoons of silkworms,” said the weaver. “它是用蚕茧制成的，”织女说。

“Really? Worms? That’s neat,” said Jack. “Let me write that down.” “真的？虫子？ 真有意思，”杰克说。 “让我记下来。”

He reached into his sack. 他伸手进布袋。

“Please don’t!” said the silk weaver. “The making of silk is China’s most valuable secret. Anyone who steals the secret will be arrested. The Dragon King will have him put to death.” “请别记！”织丝女说。 “丝绸的制作是中国最宝贵的秘密。 任何偷窃这个秘密的人都会被逮捕。 龙王会让他被处死。”

“Oops,” said Jack. “哎呀。”杰克说。

He dropped the ball of silk into his sack. 他把那团丝丢进布袋。

“I think you must leave quickly,” whispered the silk weaver. “You have been seen.” “我觉得你们得马上离开。”织丝女小声说。 “你们被人看见了。”

Jack looked over his shoulder. A man was pointing at them. 杰克回头看了一眼。 一个男人正指着他们。

“Let’s go,” he said. “咱们走。”他说。

“Bye!” said Annie. “Good luck on your date!” “再见！”安妮说。 “祝你们约会好运！”

“Thank you,” the silk weaver said. “谢谢你们。”织丝女说。

“Come on,” said Jack. “快点。”杰克说。

They hurried away from the silk weaver. 他们匆匆离开织丝女。

“Stop!” someone shouted. “站住！”有人喊道。

“Run!” said Annie. “跑！”安妮说。
