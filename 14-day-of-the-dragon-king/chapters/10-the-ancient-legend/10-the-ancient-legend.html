10. The Ancient Legend 10. 古代传说

Jack opened his eyes. He was wearing his own clothes and his sneakers. The cloth sack had turned back into his backpack. 杰克睁开眼睛。 他穿着自己的衣服和运动鞋。 布袋已经变回了他的背包。

“Welcome home, Master Librarians,” said Morgan. “欢迎回家，图书大师。”摩根说。

She stood in the tree house, smiling at them. 她站在树屋里，对他们微笑。

“Hi!” said Annie. “嗨！”安妮说。

“We brought you the ancient legend,” said Jack. “我们给你带来了那个古代传说，”杰克说。

“Wonderful!” said Morgan. “太好了！”摩根说。

Jack reached into his pack. He took out the China book. Then he pulled out the bamboo book. He handed them to Morgan. 杰克把手伸进背包。 他取出中国书。 然后抽出那本竹简书。 他把它们递给摩根。

“What’s the legend about?” said Annie. “那个传说讲的是什么？”安妮说。

“It’s called The Silk Weaver and the Cowherd,” said Morgan. “It’s a very famous Chinese tale.” “它叫《织丝女与放牛郎》。”摩根说。 “这是一个非常有名的中国传说。”

“Guess what, Morgan?” said Annie. “We actually met them! We helped them get together!” “你猜怎么着，摩根？”安妮说。 “我们真的遇见他们了！ 我们帮他们相聚了！”

“Oh, did you?” said Morgan. “哦，是吗？”摩根说。

“Yes!” said Jack. “The silk weaver’s ball of silk saved us!” “是的！”杰克说。 “织丝女的那团丝救了我们！”

“What does the legend say about them?” asked Annie. “传说里怎么说他们？”安妮问。

“It says that long ago they were heavenly beings who lived in the sky,” said Morgan. “When they came to earth, they fell in love.” “书上说，很久以前他们是住在天上的神仙，”摩根说。 “他们来到人间后，坠入了爱河。”

“That’s when we met them!” said Annie. “我们就是那时候遇见他们的！”安妮说。

“Yes, I imagine so,” said Morgan. “The book you brought back tells about their happiness on earth. But I’m afraid a later legend tells us that when they returned to the sky, the king and queen of the skies separated them by a heavenly river called the Milky Way.” “是的，我想是这样。”摩根说。 “你们带回来的书讲述了他们在人间的幸福。 可恐怕后来的传说告诉我们，他们回到天上后，天帝和天后用一条叫银河的天河把他们分开了。”

“Oh, no,” said Annie. “哦，不。”安妮说。

“They get back together once a year,” said Morgan. “On that night, birds make a bridge in the sky over the Milky Way.” “他们一年团聚一次，”摩根说。 “在那个夜晚，鸟儿会在银河上搭起一座桥。”

Jack and Annie gazed up at the bright summer sky. 杰克和安妮抬头望着明亮的夏日天空。

“Go home now,” said Morgan. “Come back two weeks from today. Next you’re going to find a book in the country of Ireland, over a thousand years ago.” “现在回家吧。”摩根说。 “从今天起两周后再来。 下一次你们要去一千多年前的爱尔兰找一本书。”

“That sounds like fun,” said Annie. “听起来很好玩。”安妮说。

Morgan frowned. 摩根皱起眉头。

“I’m afraid it was a very dangerous time,” she said. “For Viking raiders often attacked the seacoasts.” “恐怕那是一个非常危险的时代。”她说。 “因为维京袭击者常常进攻海岸。”

“Vikings?” said Jack. He’d had enough danger to last a long time. “维京人？”杰克说。 他经历的危险已经够久了。

“Don’t worry about it now,” said Morgan. “Just go home and rest.” “现在先别担心。”摩根说。 “只管回家休息。”

Jack nodded. 杰克点了点头。

“I’ll try,” he said, pulling on his backpack. “我会试试。”他说着背上背包。

“Bye,” said Annie. “See you in two weeks.” “再见。”安妮说。 “两周后见。”

“Thank you for your help,” said Morgan. “谢谢你们的帮助。”摩根说。

“Anytime,” said Annie. “别客气，”安妮说。

They headed down the rope ladder. 他们顺着绳梯往下走。

From the ground, they waved to Morgan. Then they started for home. 在地面上，他们向摩根挥手。 然后他们开始往家走。

As they got to the edge of the woods, Annie stopped. 走到树林边缘时，安妮停了下来。

“Listen to the crickets,” she said. “听听蟋蟀叫。”她说。

Jack listened. The cricket chirps sounded louder than usual. 杰克听着。 蟋蟀的叫声听起来比平时更响。

“Their ancestors lived in the time of the Dragon King,” said Annie. “它们的祖先生活在龙王的时代，”安妮说。

“Oh, brother,” said Jack. “哎呀，真是的。”杰克说。

“Right now the grownups are telling the little crickets a legend,” said Annie. “现在大蟋蟀正在给小蟋蟀讲一个传说，”安妮说。

“Yeah, sure,” said Jack. “嗯，当然。”杰克说。

“A legend passed down from their ancestors,” said Annie. “一个从祖先那里传下来的传说，”安妮说。

Jack smiled. He didn’t want to admit it, but the cricket noise did sound like storytelling. He could almost hear them saying, Dragon King, Dragon King, Dragon King. 杰克笑了。 他不想承认，可那蟋蟀声听起来真像在讲故事。 他几乎能听见它们在说：龙王，龙王，龙王。

“Jack! Annie!” came a voice. “杰克！ 安妮！”一个声音传来。

It was their mother calling them. 是妈妈在喊他们。

The spell was broken. The cricket stories were just plain old cricket sounds again. 魔咒被打破了。 蟋蟀的故事又变回了普通的蟋蟀叫声。

“Coming!” Jack shouted. “来了！”杰克喊道。

Jack and Annie ran down their street and across their yard. 杰克和安妮跑下街道，穿过院子。

“Did you have a good time in China?” their mom asked. “你们在中国玩得开心吗？”妈妈问。

“It was pretty scary,” said Annie. “挺吓人的。”安妮说。

“We got lost in a tomb,” said Jack. “But we were saved by an ancient book.” “我们在一座陵墓里迷路了。”杰克说。 “不过我们被一本古书救了。”

Their mom smiled and shook her head. “My, books are wonderful, aren’t they?” she said. 妈妈笑着摇了摇头。 “哎呀，书真奇妙，对吧？”她说。

“Yep!” said Jack and Annie. “对！”杰克和安妮说。

And they followed her inside. 然后他们跟着她进了屋。
