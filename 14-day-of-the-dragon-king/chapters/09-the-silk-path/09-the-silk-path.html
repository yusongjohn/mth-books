9. The Silk Path 9. 丝线小路

“What’s going on?” said Jack. “这是怎么回事？”杰克说。

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “But we’d better follow it.” “我不知道。”安妮说。 “不过我们最好跟着它。”

She hurried after the ball of silk thread. 她追着那团丝线跑。

Jack put the bamboo book into his sack and took off after her. 杰克把竹书放进布袋，飞奔去追她。

They followed the trail of thread down the row, where it turned down another row. 他们顺着线的痕迹走过那一排，线又拐进了另一排。

“Hey, that’s impossible!” said Jack. “That’s scientifically impossible!” “嘿，那不可能！”杰克说。 “那在科学上是不可能的！”

“I told you, it’s magic!” cried Annie. “我告诉过你，这是魔法！”安妮喊道。

Jack couldn’t believe it. But he kept following the thread. 杰克简直不敢相信。 可他还是跟着那根线。

Suddenly the trail of thread was gone. The ball had completely unrolled. 突然，丝线的痕迹消失了。 线团已经完全展开了。

Jack and Annie stood still for a moment and caught their breath. 杰克和安妮静止地站了一会儿，喘了口气。

“What—what now?” said Jack. “现——现在怎么办？”杰克说。

“I guess we go up those stairs,” Annie said. “我想我们该上那些台阶。”安妮说。

“What stairs?” said Jack. “什么台阶？”杰克说。

“There.” “那儿。”

Jack looked through the dim light and saw the entrance to a stairway only a few feet away. 杰克透过昏暗的光线，看见几英尺外有个楼梯入口。

“Let’s get out of here!” he said. “咱们离开这儿！”他说。

They ran up the stairs. At the top, they saw that they were in the hall that led to the entrance of the mound. 他们跑上台阶。 到了顶部，他们发现自己在通向土堆入口的走廊里。

They walked and walked and walked down the lamp-lit passage. Finally, Jack stopped. 他们走啊走啊，沿着被灯点亮的通道一直走。 终于，杰克停了下来。

“I don’t remember this hall being so long,” he said. “我不记得这条走廊有这么长。”他说。

“Me neither,” said Annie. “I guess those weren’t the same stairs that we went down.” “我也不记得。”安妮说。 “我想那些不是我们下去时的同一段台阶。”

“What should we do now?” said Jack. “我们现在该怎么办？”杰克说。

“We’d better keep going,” said Annie. “我们最好继续走。”安妮说。

“Yeah, we don’t have much choice,” said Jack. “是啊，我们也没什么别的选择，”杰克说。

They started walking again. They rounded a corner and came to a door. 他们又开始走。 他们拐过一个弯，来到一扇门前。

“Oh, great!” said Annie. “哦，太好了！”安妮说。

“Wait. We don’t know what’s on the other side,” said Jack. “Go slow. Be careful.” “等等。 我们不知道另一边有什么。”杰克说。 “慢点。小心点。”

“Okay,” said Annie. “好。”安妮说。

Slowly and carefully, she opened the door. 她慢慢地、小心地打开门。

Then she peeked out. 然后向外偷看。

“Yippee,” she said softly. “耶。”她轻声说。

Annie stepped into the fading daylight. Jack stepped out after her. 安妮走进渐渐暗下去的日光里。 杰克跟着她走出去。

The sun had gone down. 太阳已经落山了。

They were standing outside the gate of the Dragon King’s palace. They could see the market not far away. The stalls were closing for the day. 他们正站在龙王宫殿的大门外。 他们能看见不远处的集市。 摊位正在收摊，今天营业结束了。

“We’re safe!” said Annie. “我们安全了！”安妮说。

Jack breathed a huge sigh of relief. 杰克大大地松了一口气。

Just then, a gong sounded. It was coming from the tower of the city walls! 就在那时，锣响了。 声音正从城墙的塔楼传来！

“Oh, man! They’re going to close the gates!” said Jack. “天哪！ 他们要关城门了！”杰克说。

He clutched his sack as they took off. They charged up the street. They ran past the market. They ran past the rich houses … past the poor houses. 他攥紧布袋，拔腿就跑。 他们冲上街道。 他们跑过集市。 他们跑过富人家的房子……又跑过穷人家的房子。

Their straw shoes fell off. But Jack and Annie kept running barefoot. 他们的草鞋掉了。 可杰克和安妮还是光着脚继续跑。

Just as the giant wooden gates started to close, they tore through them. 就在巨大的木门开始关上时，他们从门缝里冲了出去。

They charged across the bridge and kept running up the dirt road, past the farmhouse, and through the field. 他们冲过桥，继续沿着土路往上跑，经过农舍，穿过田野。

By the time they reached their tree, Jack’s lungs ached. His heart pounded. His feet burned. 等他们跑到那棵树时，杰克的肺都疼了。 他的心砰砰直跳。脚底火辣辣地疼。

He followed Annie up the rope ladder. When they got inside the tree house, Jack collapsed. 他跟着安妮爬上绳梯。 他们进了树屋后，杰克瘫倒了。

“Let’s—go—home,” he said, out of breath. “咱们——回——家。”他气喘吁吁地说。

He reached for the Pennsylvania book. 他伸手去拿宾夕法尼亚的书。

“Wait,” said Annie, looking out the window. “They found each other.” “等等。”安妮看着窗外说。 “他们找到对方了。”

“Who—found—who?” said Jack, panting. “谁——找到——谁？”杰克喘着气说。

He dragged himself to the window and looked out. 他拖着疲惫的身体走到窗边往外看。

Two figures embraced at the edge of the field. 两个人影在田野边上拥抱。

“The silk weaver and the man who takes care of the cows!” said Annie. “织丝女和那个放牛的人！”安妮说。

“Oh, yeah,” said Jack. “哦，对。”杰克说。

“Bye!” Annie called to them. “再见！”安妮朝他们喊道。

The couple waved back. 那对情侣也朝他们挥手。

Annie sighed happily. 安妮高兴地叹了口气。

“We can leave now,” she said. “我们现在可以走了。”她说。

Jack opened the Pennsylvania book and pointed at the picture of the Frog Creek woods. 杰克打开宾夕法尼亚的书，指着蛙溪树林的图画。

“I wish we could go there,” he said. “我希望我们能去那儿。”他说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

Jack looked out one last time at the Chinese couple. They seemed to be glowing like stars. 杰克最后一次朝那对中国情侣望去。 它们好像像星星一样发光。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后，一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止。
