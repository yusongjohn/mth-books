1. The Bamboo Book 1. 竹简书

Annie peeked into Jack’s room. 安妮把头探进杰克的房间。

“Ready to go to China?” she asked. “准备好去中国了吗？”她问。

Jack took a deep breath. 杰克深吸一口气。

“Sure,” he answered. “当然。”他回答。

“Bring your secret library card,” Annie said. “I have mine in my pocket.” “带上你的秘密图书卡。”安妮说。 “我的在口袋里。”

“Yep,” said Jack. “带了。”杰克说。

He opened his top dresser drawer and took out a thin wooden card. The letters M L on it shimmered in the light. Jack dropped the card into his backpack. Then he threw in his notebook and a pencil. 他打开最上面的抽屉，取出一张薄薄的木卡。 上面的字母 M L 在光线里闪闪发光。 杰克把卡放进背包。 然后他把笔记本和一支铅笔扔了进去。

“Let’s go,” said Annie. “咱们走吧。”安妮说。

Jack pulled on his pack and followed her. 杰克背上包，跟着她。

What are we in for today? he wondered. 今天我们会遇上什么？他心想。

“Bye, Mom!” said Annie as they passed their mom in the kitchen. “妈妈，再见！”他们经过厨房时安妮说。

“Where are you going?” she asked. “你们去哪儿？”她问。

“China!” said Annie. “中国！”安妮说。

“Great,” said their mom. She winked at them. “Have fun.” “真棒。”妈妈说。 她朝他们眨了眨眼。 “玩得开心。”

Fun? thought Jack. He was afraid that fun wasn’t quite the right word. 好玩？杰克想。 他担心“好玩”并不完全是个合适的词。

“Just wish us luck,” he said as he and Annie headed out the front door. “就祝我们好运吧，”他一边说，一边和安妮走出了前门。

“Good luck!” their mother called. “祝你们好运！”妈妈喊道。

“If only she knew we aren’t pretending,” Jack whispered to Annie. “要是她知道我们不是在假装就好了，”杰克对安妮低声说。

“Yeah,” said Annie, grinning. “嗯。”安妮笑着说。

Outside, the sun shone brightly. Birds sang. Crickets chirped. Jack and Annie walked up their street toward the Frog Creek woods. 屋外，阳光明亮地照着。 蟋蟀在唧唧叫。 杰克和安妮沿着街道走向蛙溪树林。

“I wonder if the weather will be this nice in China,” Annie said. “我想知道中国的天气会不会也这么好，”安妮说。

“I don’t know. Remember, Morgan said this would be a very scary adventure,” said Jack. “我不知道。 记住，摩根说过这次会是一次非常吓人的冒险。”杰克说。

“They’re always scary,” said Annie. “But we always meet animals who help us, or people.” “它们总是很吓人。”安妮说。 “可我们总会遇见帮忙的动物，或者人。”

“True,” said Jack. “没错。”杰克说。

“I bet we meet someone great today,” said Annie. “我敢肯定我们今天会碰到很棒的人，”安妮说。

Jack smiled. He was starting to feel excited now instead of scared. 杰克笑了。 他现在开始感到兴奋，而不是害怕。

“Let’s hurry!” he said. “咱们快点！”他说。

They ran into the Frog Creek woods. They slipped between the tall trees until they came to a huge oak. 他们跑进蛙溪树林。 他们从高大的树木间迅速穿过，一直走到一棵巨大的橡树前。

“Hello!” came a soft voice they knew well. “你好！”一个他们很熟悉的轻柔声音传来。

They looked up. Morgan was peering down from the magic tree house. 他们抬头看。 摩根正从魔法树屋往下看。

“Ready for your next mission as Master Librarians?” she asked. “准备好作为图书大师执行下一项任务了吗？”她问。

“Yes!” said Jack and Annie. “是的！”杰克和安妮说。

They grabbed the rope ladder and started up. 他们抓住绳梯往上爬。

“Are we still going to China?” asked Annie when they had climbed into the tree house. “我们还是要去中国吗？”爬进树屋后安妮问。

“Indeed,” said Morgan. “You’re going to ancient China. Here is the title of the story you must find.” “的确如此，”摩根说。 “你们要去古代中国。 这是你们必须找到的那个故事的标题。”

She held up a long, thin strip of wood. It looked like a ruler, except it had strange writing on it instead of numbers. 她举起一根又长又薄的木条。 它看起来像一把尺子，只不过上面是奇怪的文字，而不是数字。

“Long ago, the Chinese discovered how to make paper. It was one of the world’s most important discoveries,” said Morgan. “But you are going to a time earlier than that, to a time when books were written on bamboo strips like this one.” “很久以前，中国人发现了如何造纸。 那是世界上最重要的发现之一，”摩根说。 “但你们要去的时代比那更早，那时书是写在像这样的竹简上的。”

“Wow,” said Annie, pointing at the figures on the bamboo. “So this is Chinese writing?” “哇，”安妮指着竹子上的符号说。 “所以这就是中文文字？”

“Yes,” said Morgan. “Just as we have letters, Chinese writing is made up of many characters. Each one stands for a different thing or idea. These characters are the title of an ancient Chinese legend. You must find the first writing of the legend before the Imperial Library is destroyed.” “是的。”摩根说。 “就像我们有字母一样，中文是由许多文字组成的。 每一个都代表不同的事物或想法。 这些文字是一个中国古代传说的标题。 你们必须在皇家图书馆被毁掉之前，找到这个传说最早的文字。

“Hurry, let’s go,” said Annie. “快点，咱们走。”安妮说。

“Wait, we need our research book,” said Jack. “等等，我们需要研究书。”杰克说。

“Yes, you do,” said Morgan. “对，你们需要。”摩根说。

From the folds of her robe, she pulled out a book. On the cover was the title: The Time of the First Emperor. 她从长袍的褶皱里抽出一本书。 封面上的书名是：《第一位皇帝的时代》。

Morgan handed the book to Jack. 摩根把书递给杰克。

“This research book will guide you,” she said. “But remember, in your darkest hour, only the old legend can save you.” “这本资料书会指引你们，”她说。 “但记住，在你最黑暗的时刻，只有那个古老传说能救你。”

“But we have to find it first,” said Annie. “可我们得先找到它。”安妮说。

“Exactly,” said Morgan. “正是如此，”摩根说。

She handed Jack the bamboo strip, and he slipped it into his pack. 她把那根竹简递给杰克，杰克把它塞进了背包。

Jack pushed his glasses into place, then pointed at the cover of their research book. 杰克把眼镜推正，然后指着研究书的封面。

“I wish we could go there!” he said. “我希望我们能去那儿！”他说。

The wind started to blow. 风开始刮起来。

The tree house started to spin. It spun faster and faster. 树屋开始旋转。 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后，一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止。
