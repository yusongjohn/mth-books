7. The Burning of the Books 7. 焚书

The Dragon King watched the bonfire as it blazed up toward the sky. Around the fire the air was thick and wavy. Bamboo books were stacked beside the fire, waiting to be burned. 龙王看着火堆朝天空熊熊燃烧。 火堆周围的空气又浓又晃。 竹书堆放在火堆旁，等着被烧掉。

“Hurry!” said the scholar. “快点！”学者说。

They jumped down from the cart and joined the crowd by the bonfire. 他们跳下牛车，加入篝火旁的人群。

The Dragon King shouted to the soldiers. They began throwing the books into the fire. The bamboo crackled as it burned. 龙王对士兵们喊着。 他们开始把书扔进火里。 竹子燃烧时劈啪作响。

“Stop!” cried Annie. “住手！”安妮喊道。

Jack grabbed her. 杰克抓住她。

“Quiet!” he said. “别出声！”他说。

Annie pulled away. 安妮挣脱开。

“Stop!” she shouted again. But her voice was lost in the noise of the roaring fire. “住手！”她又喊道。 可她的声音被淹没在熊熊大火的喧嚣里。

“There’ s your story!” said the scholar. “你们要找的故事在那儿！”学者说。

He pointed to a bamboo book that had fallen off a waiting stack. 他指着一捆从等待堆上掉下来的竹简书。

“I’ll get it!” said Annie. “我去拿！”安妮说。

She dashed over to the book. 她冲向那本书。

“Annie!” cried Jack. “安妮！”杰克喊道。

But she had already snatched up the bundle of bamboo strips and was charging back to them. 可她已经抓起那捆竹简，正朝他们冲回来。

“Got it! Quick, put it in your sack!” she said. “拿到了！ 快，放进你的袋子里！”她说。

Jack put the bundle of bamboo strips in his sack. Then he looked around fearfully. He gasped. 杰克把那捆竹简放进布袋。 然后他害怕地环顾四周。

The Dragon King was glaring at them! Then he headed their way. 龙王正怒视着他们！ 然后他朝他们走过来。

“Seize them!” the Dragon King shouted. “抓住他们！”龙王喊道。

“Run through the burial grounds!” the scholar said to Jack and Annie. “The soldiers will be afraid to follow. They fear the spirits of the ancestors!” “穿过墓地跑！”学者对杰克和安妮说。 “士兵会害怕跟过来。 他们害怕祖先的鬼魂！”

“Thanks!” said Jack. “Thanks for everything!” “谢谢！”杰克说。 “谢谢你做的一切！”

“Good luck!” cried Annie. “祝你们好运！”安妮喊道。

Then she and Jack took off. Soldiers shouted after them. An arrow whizzed by. 然后她和杰克跑开了。 士兵在后面喊着。 一支箭嗖的一声飞过。

But Jack and Annie kept running. They ran down the path to the burial grounds. They jumped over the low brick wall and ran between the huge mounds of earth. 可杰克和安妮一直跑着。 他们沿着小路跑向墓地。 他们跳过低矮的砖墙，在巨大的土堆之间奔跑。

Suddenly arrows filled the air around them. The archers were shooting from the tower! 突然，箭矢在他们周围飞过。 弓箭手正从塔楼上射箭！

“Look!” cried Jack. “看！”杰克喊道。

There was a doorway in one of the mounds. Jack and Annie ducked inside. 其中一个土堆上有个门口。 杰克和安妮弯腰躲了进去。

They were in a long hall lit with oil lamps. 他们进了一条点着油灯的长廊。

“It’s so quiet,” said Annie. She walked down the passageway. “Hey, there are some steps here.” “好安静。”安妮说。 她沿着通道走下去。 “嘿，这儿有台阶。”

“Don’t go any farther!” said Jack. “别再往前走了！”杰克说。

“Why not?” said Annie. “为什么不行？”安妮说。

“We don’t know what’s down there,” said Jack. “This is a burial tomb, remember? It’s creepy.” “我们不知道下面有什么。”杰克说。 “这是一座陵墓，记得吗？怪吓人的。”

“Let’s just take a quick look,” said Annie. “Maybe it’s the way out of here.” “我们就快速看一眼，”安妮说。 “也许那是出去的路。”

Jack took a deep breath. 杰克深吸一口气。

“You might be right,” he said. “Okay, but go slow.” He didn’t want to stumble upon a dead body. “你也许说得对。”他说。 “好吧，但慢点走。”他不想突然撞见一具尸体。

Annie started down the steep steps. Jack followed. The lamps lit their way as they kept going down and down. Finally, they reached the bottom. 安妮顺着陡峭的台阶往下走。杰克跟着。 油灯照着路，他们一直往下走，往下走。 终于，他们走到了底部。

Jack blinked. Even though oil lamps glowed everywhere, it was hard to see at first. 杰克眨了眨眼。 尽管到处都有油灯柔和地发光，一开始还是很难看清。

When Jack’s eyes got used to the strange light, his heart nearly stopped. 等杰克的眼睛习惯了那奇怪的光线，他的心几乎停住了。

“Oh, man,” he breathed. “天哪。”他喘着气说。

They were in a room filled with soldiers—thousands of them. 他们进了一个满是士兵的房间——成千上万个。
