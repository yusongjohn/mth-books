4. The Great Wall 4. 长城

Jack and Annie ran around the farmhouse. At the back was an oxcart filled with bags of grain. There was no one in sight. 杰克和安妮绕着农舍跑。 后面有一辆装满粮袋的牛车。 一个人也看不见。

The shouting behind them got louder. 身后的喊声越来越大。

Jack and Annie looked at each other, then dived into the back of the wooden cart. They buried themselves in the middle of the bags of grain. 杰克和安妮对视一眼，然后跳进木车后部。 他们把自己藏在粮袋中间。

Jack’s heart pounded as the shouts came closer. He held his breath and waited for the people to leave. 喊声越来越近，杰克的心怦怦直跳。 他屏住呼吸，等着那些人离开。

Suddenly the cart lurched forward. Someone was driving them away! 突然，车子猛地向前一晃。 有人正赶着车离开！

Jack and Annie peeked over the bags. Jack saw the back of the driver. He was calmly steering the oxcart over the dirt road. They were on their way to the walled city! 杰克和安妮从袋子上方偷偷往外看。 杰克看见了赶车人的背影。 他正平静地赶着牛车沿泥土路前行。 他们正前往那座围墙城！

Jack and Annie ducked down again. 杰克和安妮又低下了身子。

“This is great!” whispered Annie. “All we have to do is jump out when we get into the city.” “太棒了！”安妮小声说。 “我们只要进城时跳下车就行了。”

“Yep,” Jack said softly. “Then we’ll find the Imperial Library, find the book, and get back to the magic tree house.” “嗯。”杰克轻声说。 “然后我们找到皇家图书馆，找到那本书，再回魔法树屋。”

“No problem,” whispered Annie. “没问题。”安妮小声说。

“Whoa!” The cart slowly came to a halt. “吁！”车子慢慢停了下来。

Jack held his breath. He heard voices and the heavy tramping of feet—lots of feet. He and Annie peeked out. 杰克屏住呼吸。 他听见说话声和沉重的脚步声——很多很多脚步。 他和安妮向外偷看。

“Oh, man,” he whispered. “天哪。”他小声说。

A long line of men was crossing the road in front of the cart. They carried axes, shovels, and hoes. Guards marched alongside them. 一大队人正从车前穿过马路。 他们拿着斧头、铁锹和锄头。 卫兵在他们旁边齐步走。

“Let’s find out what’s happening,” said Jack. “咱们看看发生了什么。”杰克说。

He reached into his sack and pulled out the China book. Pushing his glasses into place, he found a picture of the workers. He read: 他伸手进布袋，抽出中国书。 他把眼镜推正，找到工人的图画。他读道：

The Dragon King forced many of his subjects to start building a wall to protect China from invaders. Later emperors made the wall even longer. Finally, it stretched 3,700 miles along China’s border. The Great Wall of China is the longest structure ever built. 龙王强迫许多子民开始修建一道墙，用来保护中国免受入侵者侵害。后来的皇帝又把墙修得更长。最后，这道墙沿着中国的边境延伸了3700英里。中国的长城是有史以来最长的建筑。

“Wow, the Great Wall of China,” said Jack. “哇，中国的长城。”杰克说。

“I’ve heard of that,” said Annie. “我听说过。”安妮说。

“Who hasn’t?” said Jack. “Those guys are going to work on it right now.” “谁没听说过？”杰克说。 “那些人现在就要去修它了。”

Just then, someone grabbed Jack and Annie. They looked up. It was the driver of the cart. 就在这时，有人抓住了杰克和安妮。他们抬起头。 是赶车的人。

“Who are you?” he asked angrily. “你们是谁？”他生气地问。

“We—uh—” Jack didn’t know what to say. “我们——呃——”杰克不知道该说什么。

The man’s gaze fell on the open book in Jack’s hands. His mouth dropped open. He let go of Jack and Annie. Slowly he reached out and touched the book. He looked back at Jack and Annie with wide eyes. 那人的目光落在杰克手里翻开的书上。 他张大了嘴。 他松开了杰克和安妮。 他慢慢伸出手，摸了摸那本书。 他瞪大眼睛回头看着杰克和安妮。

“What is this?” he said. “这是什么？”他说。
