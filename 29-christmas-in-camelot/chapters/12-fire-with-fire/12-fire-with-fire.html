12. Fire with Fire 12. 以火攻火

The four real Keepers of the Cauldron crawled closer to Jack and Annie, hissing and snorting blue fire. 四位真正的坩埚守护者爬得更近了，朝杰克和安妮喷出蓝色的火，发出嘶嘶声和喷鼻息声。

“What do we do now?” whispered Annie. “我们现在该怎么办？”安妮小声说道。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “We’re trapped.” “我不知道，”杰克说。 “我们被困住了。”

“I have an idea,” whispered Annie. “Let’s drink the water.” “我有个主意，”安妮小声说。 “我们喝点水吧。”

“What?” said Jack. “什么？”杰克说。

“It’s the Water of Memory and Imagination, right?” said Annie. “So maybe if we drink it, we can imagine a way to escape!” “这是记忆与想象之水，对吧？”安妮说。 “所以如果我们喝了它，也许就能想象出一条逃脱的路！”

“That’s crazy,” said Jack. “这太疯狂了，”杰克说。

The Keepers crawled closer, snorting more blue flame and filling the air with their rotten stench. 守护者们爬得更近了，喷出更多的蓝色火焰，空气中充满了他们腐臭的气味。

“Okay, okay, let’s try it,” said Jack. “好吧，好吧，我们试试看，”杰克说。

Annie took a sip from the silver cup, then handed it to Jack. His hands trembled as he held the cup to his lips and took a sip. The water tasted sweet, bitter, and spicy, all at the same time. 安妮从银杯里喝了一口，然后把它递给杰克。 他颤抖着双手把杯子举到嘴边，喝了一口。 水的味道又甜又苦又辣，同时都有。

Jack gave the cup back to Annie. 杰克把杯子还给了安妮。

“Now imagine we’re saved!” she said. “现在想象我们得救了！”，她说。

Jack closed his eyes. He tried to imagine being saved. He pictured the four Keepers crawling back through their doorways. 杰克闭上了眼睛。 他试着想象自己被救了。 他想象着四个守护者爬回他们各自的门里。

“Okay. Ready to fight?” said Annie. “好吧。” “准备战斗了吗？”安妮说。

Jack opened his eyes. “What? Fight?” 杰克睁开了眼睛。 “什么？战斗？”

Annie set the silver cup on the floor. 安妮把银杯子放在地上。

“Now!” she said. “现在！”她说。

Suddenly, Jack felt like he’d been hit by a bolt of lightning. His fears slipped away. He was filled with strength and fury. 突然，杰克感觉就像被一道闪电击中一样。 他的恐惧消失了。 他充满了力量和愤怒。

Without thinking, he lunged with Annie toward the wood fire under the cauldron. They each grabbed two long, straight branches from the edge of the fire. They raised them high in the air. The branches blazed with purple fire like flaming swords. 不假思索地，他和安妮一起朝锅下面的木柴火冲了过去。 他们每人从火堆边抓了两根又长又直的树枝。 他们高高举起树枝。 树枝上燃起了紫色的火焰，就像燃烧的剑一样。

“AAAHHH!” Jack and Annie shouted. “AAAHHH!” 杰克和安妮喊道。

The four Keepers hissed louder than before. Great balls of blue fire exploded from their mouths and nostrils! 四个守护者发出的声音比之前更大了。 巨大的蓝色火球从他们的嘴巴和鼻孔里爆炸出来！

Jack and Annie slashed the air with their fiery weapons, jabbing at the Keepers. They fought fire with fire, blue flame with purple flame. 杰克和安妮用他们燃烧的武器在空中挥舞，刺向守护者。 他们用火对抗火，用蓝色的火焰对抗紫色的火焰。

“Back! Back!” they shouted. “后退！ 后退！”他们喊道。

With each jab and shout, Jack felt stronger and braver. Waving their burning branches, he and Annie drove the Keepers toward the walls. 每次戳一下、喊一声，杰克都觉得越来越强壮、越来越勇敢。 他们挥舞着燃烧的树枝，杰克和安妮把守门人赶向墙壁。

The Keepers’ blue flames grew weaker and weaker, as if they were running out of fuel. Finally, one by one, each Keeper slunk back into the doorway from which it had come. 守门人蓝色的火焰越来越弱，好像快没燃料了。 最后，一个接一个，每个守门人都缩着身子，退回它来的地方。

When all the Keepers had disappeared, Jack and Annie placed a burning branch in front of each of the four doorways to keep the monsters from coming back out. 当所有的守门人都消失后，杰克和安妮在四个门口前各放了一根燃烧的树枝，防止怪物再出来。

Then they brushed off their hands. 然后他们拍了拍手上的灰。

“Let’s go,” Annie said coolly. “我们走吧，”安妮冷静地说。

Jack nodded. 杰克点了点头。

Annie carefully picked up the silver cup of water from the cauldron. Then she and Jack squeezed through the narrow crack and strode through the bright crystal cave. 安妮小心地从大锅里捡起那个银杯子的水。 然后她和杰克挤过那条狭窄的裂缝，走进了明亮的水晶洞。

They stepped out into the daylight. 他们走进了阳光下。

The glass key was still in the keyhole. 玻璃钥匙还在锁孔里。

Jack calmly locked the door behind them. He handed the key to Annie. 杰克平静地在他们身后锁上了门。 他把钥匙交给了安妮。

Then Jack’s knees gave way, and he sank to the ground. 接着，杰克的膝盖一软，他跌坐在地上。
