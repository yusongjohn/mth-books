8. The Otherworld 8. 另一个世界

The Otherworld was completely different from the dark, cold world Jack and Annie had just left behind. 另一个世界和杰克与安妮刚刚离开的那片又黑又冷的世界完全不同。

They were standing at the edge of a pale green meadow. The meadow was bathed in warm, rosy sunlight. Three horses—one black, one brown, one gray—were grazing nearby. On a hillside beyond the meadow, red and purple flowers sparkled like bright buttons. 他们正站在一片淡绿色草地的边缘。 草地被温暖的粉红色阳光照得闪闪发亮。 有三匹马——一匹黑色，一匹棕色，一匹灰色——在附近吃草。 草地后面的山坡上，红色和紫色的花朵像亮闪闪的纽扣一样闪闪发光。

“It’s so nice here,” said Annie. “这里真漂亮，”安妮说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “Maybe we won’t need this anymore.” He pulled the hood of the cloak off their heads. He was relieved to see Annie’s face—and to see himself! “对，”杰克说。 “也许我们再也不需要这个了。”他摘下斗篷的兜帽，从他们头上拿开。 他松了一口气，看到安妮的脸——也看到了自己！

“What was the first rhyme again?” asked Annie. “第一个押韵诗是什么来着？”安妮问。

Jack took out his notebook. He found the first rhyme and read aloud: 杰克拿出他的笔记本。 他找到了第一个押韵诗，大声读出来：

“Beyond the iron gate “越过铁门

The Keepers of the Cauldron wait.” 守护者正在等待。”

He looked around warily. “I wonder where the Keepers of the Cauldron are?” he said. 他警惕地环顾四周。 “我想知道守护者在哪里？”他说。

“What do you mean?” asked Annie. “We just sneaked past them. Remember? WHOOOSH?” “你是什么意思？”安妮问。 “我们刚刚偷偷从他们身边溜过去啦。还记得吗？ WHOOOSH？

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “The rhyme says ‘ beyond the iron gate.’ Those guards were standing in front of the gate. They weren’t beyond it.” “我不知道，”杰克说。 “这句咒语说的是‘越过铁门’。那些卫兵正站在门前。 他们并没有越过那扇门。”

“Shh,” said Annie. “Listen.… ” “嘘——”安妮说。 “听……”

From over the hill came the faint sounds of sweet, joyful music. 从山丘那边传来了甜美、快乐音乐的微弱声音。

“Maybe the Keepers of the Cauldron are playing that music,” said Annie. “也许锅的守护者正在演奏那首音乐，”安妮说。

“Maybe … ,” said Jack. He listened for a moment and smiled. The music made him feel light and happy. “也许……，”杰克说。 他听了片刻，然后笑了。 音乐让他感觉轻快又开心。

“Let’s go meet the Keepers!” said Annie. “我们去见守护者吧！”安妮说。

“Not so fast,” said Jack. “Shouldn’t we be invisible again? Just in case?” “别急，”杰克说。 “我们不应该再次隐形吗？以防万一？”

“I guess so,” said Annie, sighing. “我想是吧，”安妮说，叹了口气。

Jack pulled the hood of the cloak over their heads. Together, they started walking invisibly across the soft meadow. They passed the three horses and climbed the flower-covered hill. At the top, they looked down. 杰克把斗篷的兜帽拉过他们的头。 他们一起开始在柔软的草地上隐形地走路。 他们经过三匹马，爬上了开满花朵的小山。 在山顶上，他们向下望去。

“Oh, man,” said Jack. “天啊，”杰克说。

The hill sloped gently down into a misty green glade. In the middle of the glade, a band of musicians played flutes and pipes, drums and violins. Around the band, hundreds of dancers danced in a huge circle. 小山缓缓向下延伸，进入一片雾气弥漫的绿色空地。 空地中央，一群音乐家正在演奏长笛、风笛、鼓和小提琴。 音乐家周围，数百名舞者围成一个大圆圈跳舞。

“The Keepers of the Cauldron!” said Annie. “坩埚的守护者！”，安妮说。

The dancers and musicians were smiling and laughing. They wore blue coats and green coats, white gowns and yellow gowns. They wore sparkling red slippers and hats with colored feathers. 舞者和音乐家们都在微笑和大笑。 他们穿着蓝色的外套和绿色的外套，白色的长裙和黄色的长裙。 他们穿着闪闪发光的红色拖鞋，戴着带有彩色羽毛的帽子。

The dancers looked like people—except they all had glittering gold skin and wings that shimmered in the mist like spun silver. 这些舞者看起来像普通人——不过他们全都拥有闪闪发光的金色皮肤，还有在雾气中像银丝一样闪烁的翅膀。

“They’re beautiful!” said Annie. “它们真漂亮！”安妮说。

“Yeah, they are,” said Jack. “是啊，它们确实漂亮，”杰克说。

“I don’t think we need to be invisible with them,” said Annie. “我觉得我们和它们在一起不需要隐身，”安妮说。

“I think you’re right,” said Jack. “我想你是对的，”杰克说。

He and Annie threw off the red cloak. They left it in the dewy grass and ran down the hillside to the winged dancers. The dancers paid no attention to them. They just kept going around and around in their joyous circle. 他和安妮脱下了红色斗篷。 他们把它留在露水草地上，然后跑下山坡，朝那些有翅膀的舞者跑去。 舞者们根本没有注意他们。 他们只是继续在欢乐的圈子里转来转去。

“I feel like dancing with them!” said Annie. “我想和他们一起跳舞！”安妮说。

“Me too!” said Jack. It was strange—he was usually shy about dancing. But he wanted to join this dance more than anything. “我也是！”杰克说。 这很奇怪——他平时跳舞时总是很害羞。 但他很想参加这个舞蹈，比任何事情都更想。

Jack pulled off his backpack. As he set it down, he saw three swords lying in the grass. But he didn’t stop to wonder about them. The music was calling. 杰克摘下他的背包。 当他把它放下时，他看到草地上有三把剑。 但他没有停下来去想它们。 音乐在召唤他。

The winged dancers broke their circle and welcomed Jack and Annie into their dance. Annie held Jack’s right hand as he grasped the slender golden hand of the dancer on his left. 长着翅膀的舞者们打破了他们的圆圈，欢迎杰克和安妮加入他们的舞蹈。 安妮握着杰克的右手，而他则握住了左边舞者那细长的金色手。

The dancer smiled down at him. Like the others, she was as tall as a grownup. But she didn’t have any lines or wrinkles on her face. All the dancers looked very young—yet they seemed ancient at the same time. 舞者微笑着看着他。 和其他人一样，她和一个成年人一样高。 但是她的脸上没有任何皱纹。 所有舞者看起来都很年轻——但同时又显得非常古老。

As Jack danced around in the circle, his heart leaped. His spirits soared. His glasses fell off, but he didn’t care. He kept dancing. As he danced, everything in his mind became a blur. He forgot about Morgan and Camelot. He forgot about the quest for the Water of Memory and Imagination. He forgot all his fears and worries. 杰克在圈子里跳舞时，心跳加快了。 他的心情变得非常愉快。 他的眼镜掉下来了，但他一点也不在意，继续跳舞。 他跳舞时，脑海里的一切都变得模糊起来。 他忘记了摩根和卡梅洛特。 他忘记了寻找记忆与想象之水的任务。 他忘记了所有的恐惧和烦恼。

“Jack, look!” Annie cried. “杰克，看！”安妮喊道。

Jack looked at her. “Hi!” he shouted, laughing. 杰克看着她。 “你好！”他喊道，还笑了起来。

“No! Don’t look at me!” she called. “Look there! Look across the circle!” “不！ 别看我！”她喊道。 “看那边！ 看看圆圈那边！”

“I can’t see!” he said. “我看不见！”他说。

“Three knights!” Annie shouted. “Three knights dancing!” “三个骑士！”安妮喊道。 “三个骑士在跳舞！”

“Great!” Jack shouted. “太棒了！”杰克喊道。

“No, Jack! They look awful! They look sick!” Annie yelled. She pulled away from the circle and tumbled back into the grass. “不，杰克！它们看起来糟糕透了！” “他们看起来生病了！”安妮喊道。 她从圈子里挣脱出来，滚回草地上。

“Jack!” she called. “Stop dancing!” “杰克！”她喊道。 “停下跳舞！”

But Jack didn’t want to stop. He wanted to dance to the wild music forever. Forever … and ever … and ever. 但是杰克不想停下来。 他想一直随着那狂野的音乐跳舞。 永远……一直……一直……
