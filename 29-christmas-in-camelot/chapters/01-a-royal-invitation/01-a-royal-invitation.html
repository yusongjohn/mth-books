1. A Royal Invitation 1. 皇家邀请

Sunlight had faded from the late-afternoon sky. Puffy snow clouds were moving in. 阳光已经从傍晚的天空中消失了。 蓬松的雪花云正在飘过来。

“Let’s hurry. I’m cold,” said Jack. “我们快点走吧。 我冷死了，”杰克说。

He and Annie were walking home from school. Their Christmas vacation was just beginning. 他和安妮正从学校走回家。 他们的圣诞假期刚刚开始。

Cooo—cooo. 咕——咕。

“Wait,” said Annie. “Look.” “等一下，”安妮说。 “看。”

She pointed to a white bird sitting on a bare tree branch at the edge of the woods. The bird was staring straight at them. 她指着树林边缘的一棵光秃秃的树枝上，坐着一只白鸟。 那只鸟正直直地盯着他们。

“It’s a dove,” said Jack. “这是一只鸽子，”杰克说。

“It’s a messenger,” said Annie, “from Morgan.” “它是一只信使，”安妮说，“来自摩根。”

“No,” Jack said, afraid to get his hopes up. They hadn’t seen Morgan le Fay in a long time. He really missed her. “不，”杰克说，他害怕希望落空了。 他们已经很久没有见到摩根·法伊了。 他真的很想念她。

“Yes,” said Annie. “She has a mission for us. I can feel it.” “是的，”安妮说。 “她给我们有一个任务，我能感觉到。”

In the hush of the cold twilight, the dove spread its wings and flew into the Frog Creek woods. 在寒冷的暮色中，鸽子展开翅膀，飞进了蛙溪镇的森林里。

“Come on!” said Annie. “The tree house is back!” “快点！“安妮说。 “树屋回来了！“

“You’re just hoping!” said Jack. “你只是在希望而已！“杰克说。

“I’m knowing!” said Annie. She ran into the woods, following the white dove. “我是确信！“安妮说。 她跑进森林，跟着白鸽走。

“Oh, brother,” said Jack. But he took off after Annie. “哦，天哪，”杰克说。 但他还是跟在安妮后面跑了起来。

Even in the growing darkness, they easily found their way. They zigzagged between the bare trees and ran over the frozen ground until they came to the tallest oak in the woods. 即使天色越来越暗，他们也能轻松找到路。 他们在光秃秃的树之间来回穿梭，跑过结冰的地面，直到来到森林里最高的橡树前。

“See?” said Annie, pointing to the top of the tree. “看到了吗？”安妮指着树顶说。

“Yeah,” whispered Jack. “嗯，”杰克小声说。

There it was: the magic tree house. 那就是：神奇的树屋。

“Morgan!” shouted Annie. “摩根！”安妮喊道。

Jack held his breath, waiting to see the enchantress at the tree house window. But Morgan did not appear. 杰克屏住呼吸，等着看树屋窗户里出现女巫。 但摩根没有出现。

Annie grabbed the rope ladder and started up. Jack followed. 安妮抓住绳梯开始往上爬。杰克跟在后面。

When they climbed inside the tree house, Jack saw something lying on the floor. It was a scroll, rolled up and tied with a red velvet ribbon. 当他们爬进树屋时，杰克看到地板上躺着一样东西。 这是一卷卷起来的羊皮纸，用一条红色天鹅绒丝带系着。

Jack picked up the scroll and unrolled it. The thick, yellowed paper shimmered with large gold writing. 杰克拿起卷轴把它展开。 厚厚的、泛黄的纸张上闪耀着大大的金色文字。

“Wow, Morgan sent us a really fancy note,” said Annie. “哇，摩根给我们寄了一张非常华丽的便条，”安妮说。

“It’s an invitation,” said Jack. “Listen.” “这是一张邀请函，”杰克说。 “听。”

图中文字：Dear Jack and Annie,

Please accept this Royal Inpitation to spend Christmas in the Kingdom of Camelot.

-N 图中文字：亲爱的杰克和安妮，请接受这份皇家邀请，到卡米洛特王国过圣诞节。-N

“Christmas in Camelot!” said Annie. “I don’t believe it!” “卡梅洛特的圣诞节！”，安妮说。 “我不敢相信！”，

“Cool,” whispered Jack. He pictured a beautiful, glowing castle lit with candles and filled with knights and ladies feasting and singing. “太酷了，”杰克小声说。 他想象着一座美丽的城堡，烛光闪烁，里面充满了骑士和贵妇人，他们正在享用美食和唱歌。

“We’re going to celebrate Christmas with Morgan and King Arthur!” said Annie. “And Queen Guinevere!” “我们要和摩根以及亚瑟王一起庆祝圣诞节！”安妮说。 “还有桂妮薇尔王后！”，

“Yeah,” said Jack. “And the Knights of the Round Table, like Sir Lancelot!” “对，”杰克说。 “还有圆桌骑士，比如兰斯洛特爵士！”，

“Let’s go!” said Annie. “Where’s the book?” “我们出发吧！”安妮说。 “书在哪里？”

She and Jack looked around the tree house for a book about Camelot. The only book they saw was the Pennsylvania book that always brought them home. 安妮和杰克在树屋里四处寻找一本关于卡梅洛特的书。 他们看到的唯一一本书是那本总是能把他们送回家的宾夕法尼亚书。

“That’s strange,” said Jack. “Morgan didn’t send a book about Camelot with the Royal Invitation. How does she expect us to get there?” “这很奇怪，”杰克说。 “摩根没有随皇家邀请函一起寄来一本关于卡梅洛特的书。” 她怎么指望我们能到那儿呢？

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “Maybe she forgot.” “我不知道，”安妮说。 “也许她忘了。”

Jack picked up the invitation. He read it again. He turned it over, hoping to find more information. The back of the scroll was blank. He handed the invitation to Annie. 杰克捡起了邀请函。他又读了一遍。 他翻过来看，希望能找到更多信息。 卷轴的背面是空白的。 他把邀请函递给安妮。

“She must have forgotten,” he said. “她一定是忘了，”他说。

“Darn,” said Annie, staring at the gold writing. “I really wish we could go to Camelot.” “真糟糕，”安妮盯着金色的文字说。 “我真的希望我们能去卡梅洛特。”

The tree branches rustled. 树枝轻轻摇晃着。

The wind began to blow. 风开始吹起来了。

“What’s happening?” said Jack. “发生什么事了？”杰克说。

“I don’t know—” said Annie. “我不知道——”安妮说。

“Wait a minute,” said Jack. “You were holding the invitation, and you made a wish.… ” “等一下，”杰克说。 “你拿着邀请函，还许了一个愿望……”

The wind blew harder. 风刮得更猛了。

“That must have made the magic work!” cried Annie. “这一定让魔法生效了！”安妮喊道。

Jack felt a surge of joy. 杰克感到一阵快乐涌上心头。

“We’re going to Camelot!” he said. “我们要去卡梅洛特了！”他说。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转了。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
