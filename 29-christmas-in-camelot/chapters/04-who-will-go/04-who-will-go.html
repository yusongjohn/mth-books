4. Who Will Go? 4. 谁会去？

“I have come to see Arthur the king!” the Christmas Knight said. His deep voice echoed from inside his helmet. His red armor gleamed in the firelight. “我来见亚瑟国王！”圣诞骑士说。 他低沉的声音从头盔里传出来。 他的红色盔甲在火光中闪闪发光。

King Arthur stood up. He stared fiercely at the knight, but he spoke in a calm, steady voice. “ I am Arthur the king,” he said. “Who are you?” 亚瑟国王站了起来。 他凶狠地盯着骑士，但说话的声音却平静而坚定。 “我是亚瑟国王，”他说。 “你是谁？”

The knight did not answer Arthur’s question. “So. You are the legendary King Arthur of Camelot,” he said in a mocking voice. “And these must be the famous Knights of the Round Table.” 骑士没有回答亚瑟的问题。 “哦， 你就是传说中的卡梅洛特的亚瑟国王，”他用嘲讽的语气说。 “这些一定是著名的圆桌骑士。”

“Yes,” said King Arthur, “and again, I ask: Who are you?” “是的，”亚瑟王说，“我再次问你：你是谁？”

The Christmas Knight still did not answer Arthur’s question. 圣诞骑士仍然没有回答亚瑟王的问题。

“The spell of the Dark Wizard has robbed Camelot of its joy,” said the Christmas Knight. “Has it robbed you and your men of your courage as well?” “黑暗巫师的咒语已经夺走了卡梅洛特的欢乐，”圣诞骑士说。 “它也夺走了你和你的士兵们的勇气吗？”

“You dare to question our courage?” King Arthur said in a low, angry voice. “你竟敢质疑我们的勇气？”亚瑟王用低沉而愤怒的声音说。

“CAMELOT IS DYING!” the Christmas Knight boomed. “Why has no one journeyed to the Otherworld to recapture its joy?” “卡梅洛特正在死去！”圣诞骑士大声喊道。 “为什么没有人去异世界夺回它的欢乐？”

“I have sent my best knights on such a quest,” said King Arthur. “They never returned.” “我派我最优秀的骑士去完成这个任务，”亚瑟王说。 “他们再也没有回来。”

“THEN SEND MORE!” thundered the Christmas Knight. “然后派更多人去！”圣诞骑士怒吼道。

“NO!” shouted King Arthur, pounding his fists on the table. “Never again will I feed good men to the magic and monsters of the Otherworld!” “不！”亚瑟王喊道，用力捶打着桌子。 “我再也不会让好人被异界里的魔法和怪物吃掉！”

Jack felt a chill of fear. What monsters? 杰克感到一阵恐惧。什么怪物？

“Then you choose your fate,” said the Christmas Knight. “If you will send no one else to the Otherworld, all that your kingdom has gained through time—all beauty, music, wonder, and light, all that Camelot has ever been or could ever be—will be lost and forgotten forever.” “那么你就要自己选择命运，”圣诞骑士说道。 “如果你不愿意再派其他人去异界，那么你王国通过时光所获得的一切——所有的美丽、音乐、奇迹和光明，卡梅洛特曾经或可能成为的一切——都将永远丢失和被遗忘。”

“No!” shouted Annie. “不！”安妮喊道。

“Shh, Annie!” said Jack. “嘘，安妮！”杰克说道。

The Christmas Knight turned to the knights at the table. “WHO WILL GO?” he boomed. 圣诞骑士转向桌边的骑士们。 “谁愿意去？”他大声问道。

“We will!” shouted Annie. “我们一定会的！”安妮喊道。

“We will?” said Jack. “我们一定会吗？”杰克说。

“Yes! We’ll go on the quest!” Annie yelled. She jumped up. “是的！ 我们要去完成任务！”安妮大喊。她跳了起来。

“No!” cried Morgan le Fay. “不！”摩根·法伊哭喊道。

“Never!” said King Arthur. “绝不！”亚瑟王说道。

“Annie!” said Jack. He leaped up from his chair and tried to grab her. “安妮！”杰克说。 他从椅子上跳起来，试图抓住她。

“YES!” thundered the Christmas Knight. He pointed his red-gloved hand at Annie and Jack. “The youngest of all—these two—they will go.” “是的！”圣诞骑士怒吼道。 他用戴着红色手套的手指着安妮和杰克。 “最年轻的一对——他们两个——他们会去。”

“You are mocking us!” King Arthur shouted. “你是在嘲笑我们！”亚瑟王喊道。

“THEY WILL GO!” boomed the knight. His words echoed throughout the hall. “他们会去！”骑士大声说道。 他的话在大厅里回荡。

Oh, no, thought Jack. 哦，不，杰克心想。

“Yes!” said Annie. She pulled Jack toward the Christmas Knight. “是的！”安妮说道。 她拉着杰克朝圣诞骑士走去。

King Arthur turned to his men. “Stop them!” 亚瑟王转向他的手下。 “拦住他们！”

Several knights started to rush toward Jack and Annie. The Christmas Knight raised his gloved hand high in the air. 几位骑士开始朝杰克和安妮冲去。 圣诞骑士高高举起戴着手套的手。

In an instant, the room fell deathly quiet. 瞬间，房间里变得死一般寂静。

Everyone around the table was as still as a statue. 桌子周围的所有人都像雕像一样一动不动。

King Arthur looked like the statue of a furious king. Queen Guinevere looked like the statue of a worried queen. The Knights of the Round Table looked like statues of fierce knights. 亚瑟王看起来就像一个暴怒国王的雕像。 桂妮薇尔王后看起来就像一个担忧王后的雕像。 圆桌骑士看起来就像一群凶猛骑士的雕像。

And Morgan le Fay looked like the statue of a caring friend. Her mouth was open, as if she were calling out to Jack and Annie. But no sound came from her lips—no sound at all. 而摩根勒菲看起来就像一个关心朋友的雕像。 她的嘴张开着，好像在向杰克和安妮呼喊。 但她的嘴唇没有发出任何声音——完全没有声音。
