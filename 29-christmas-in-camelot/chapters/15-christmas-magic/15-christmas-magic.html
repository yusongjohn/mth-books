15. Christmas Magic 15. 圣诞节的魔法

Jack and Annie watched in horror as the water from the cup splashed over the stone floor. It trickled into the cracks between the stones and disappeared. 杰克和安妮惊恐地看着杯子里的水泼洒在石板地上。 水顺着石板之间的缝隙流了进去，然后消失了。

Jack scrambled to the cup. He picked it up. It was completely empty. 杰克赶紧爬到杯子旁边，捡起了它。 杯子已经完全空了。

“Oh, no,” Jack moaned. He sat back and put his head in his hands. Camelot will never wake up now, he thought. The legend will end forever. “哦，不，”杰克呻吟道。 他往后一坐，把头放在手里。 卡梅洛特现在永远也醒不过来了，他心想。 这个传说将永远结束。

“Jack!” said Annie. “Look!” “杰克！”安妮说道。 “看！”

Jack raised his head. He pushed his glasses into place. A golden cloud was rising from the cracks between all the stones of the floor. 杰克抬起头。 他把眼镜推到正确的位置。 一团金色的云雾正从地板上所有石头的缝隙中升起。

The cloud spread quickly throughout the great hall, filling the room with wonderful smells—the scent of cedar smoke and evergreen, of roses and almonds. 云雾迅速蔓延到大厅的各个角落，房间里充满了美妙的气味——雪松烟和常青树的香味，还有玫瑰和杏仁的香气。

The cloud rose up and up, then wafted out through the upper windows of the hall. Suddenly, a white dove flew through one of the windows. It soared across the dark room like a bright light, then swooped back out into the night. 云朵越升越高，然后从大厅的上层窗户飘了出去。 突然，一只白鸽从其中一扇窗户飞了进来。 它像一束明亮的光一样飞过黑暗的房间，然后又俯冲回夜色中。

Soft, gentle laughter came from the end of the hall. The laughter grew louder. Jack saw King Arthur and Queen Guinevere looking at one another—they were laughing! The Knights of the Round Table were laughing, too! 柔和、轻柔的笑声从大厅的尽头传来。 笑声越来越响亮。 杰克看到亚瑟王和桂妮薇尔女王互相看着——他们在笑！ 圆桌骑士们也在笑！

Best of all, Jack saw Morgan le Fay smiling at him and Annie! 最棒的是，杰克看到摩根勒菲正朝他和安妮微笑！

“Jack! Annie! Come here!” she called. She held out her arms. “杰克！安妮！ 过来！”她喊道。 她张开双臂。

“Morgan!” cried Annie. She ran to Morgan and threw her arms around the enchantress. Jack stood up. Still holding the empty cup, he ran to Morgan and hugged her, too. “摩根！”安妮喊道。 她跑到摩根身边，张开双臂拥抱了这位女巫。杰克站了起来。 仍然拿着那个空杯子，他跑到摩根身边，也拥抱了她。

“We did what the Christmas Knight told us to do!” said Annie. “We brought back the Water of Memory and Imagination!” “我们按照圣诞骑士告诉我们的做了！”安妮说道。 “我们带回了记忆与想象之水！”

“But I dropped the cup,” said Jack, “and spilled all the water!” “但我把杯子掉了，”杰克说，“把所有的水都洒了！”

“But the water made a gold cloud,” said Annie. “And everyone came back to life!” “但水形成了一个金色的云朵，”安妮说。 “然后每个人都复活了！”

Morgan laughed with amazement. 摩根惊讶地笑了。

“You have just returned from the Otherworld?” she asked. “你刚从另一个世界回来吗？”她问。

“Yes!” said Annie. “是的！”安妮说。

“A white stag brought us back!” said Jack. He turned to King Arthur. “一只白色的鹿把我们带回去了！”杰克说。 他转向亚瑟王。

“Your Majesty,” he said, “we have good news. Your knights are safe. Sir Lancelot said to tell you they’ll be home before the first night of the New Year.” “陛下，”他说，“我们有好消息。” 你们的骑士们安全。 兰斯洛特爵士说，他要告诉你，他们会在新年的第一个夜晚之前回家。”

The king looked bewildered. “You found them—?” 国王看起来很困惑。 “你们找到他们了——？”，

“Yes, and they’re all fine,” said Annie. “是的，他们都很好，”安妮说。

“Here—” said Jack. He handed the silver cup to the king. “Please give this back to Sir Galahad.” “这里——”杰克说。 他把银杯子递给了国王。 “请把这个还给加拉哈德爵士。”

“And this to Sir Lancelot,” said Annie. She took the glass key from around her neck and gave it to King Arthur. “这个给兰斯洛特爵士，”安妮说。 她从脖子上取下玻璃钥匙，递给了亚瑟王。

“Oh, and this to Sir Percival,” said Jack. He pulled the wooden compass box from his pack and gave it to the king. “哦，这个给珀西瓦尔爵士，”杰克说。 他从背包里拿出木制指南针盒子，递给了国王。

At first, King Arthur was too stunned to speak. Then he clapped his hands and laughed joyfully. 起初，亚瑟王太震惊了，说不出话来。 然后他拍手笑了起来。

“Thank you!” he said to Jack and Annie. “谢谢！”他对杰克和安妮说。

The Knights of the Round Table all cheered. 圆桌骑士们都欢呼起来。

“Ring the bells!” King Arthur shouted. “Call the people of Camelot to the castle!” “敲响钟声！”亚瑟王喊道。 “召集卡梅洛特的人民到城堡来！”，

“They have already gathered outside the doors, Your Majesty,” said a page. “他们已经在城堡大门外集合好了，陛下，”一个侍从说道。

“Bring them in!” said King Arthur. “We must rejoice together!” “让他们进来！”亚瑟王说道。 “我们得一起庆祝！”

Queen Guinevere smiled at Jack and Annie. Her eyes sparkled now. “Once again, you have helped save Camelot,” she said. “Thank you very much.” 桂妮薇尔女王对杰克和安妮微笑。 她的眼睛现在闪闪发亮。 “再次感谢你们拯救了卡梅洛特，”她说。 “非常感谢你们。”

“You’re welcome,” said Jack and Annie together. “谢谢，”杰克和安妮一起说道。

Then Jack heard the sounds of children talking and laughing. He turned to see a crowd of people streaming through the arched doorway of the great hall. They carried candles, a giant fir tree, and boughs of holly and pine. Musicians followed them with stringed instruments. 然后杰克听到了孩子们说话和笑的声音。 他转过身，看到一群人正从大厅的拱形门廊里涌进来。 他们拿着蜡烛、一棵巨大的冷杉树，还有冬青和松枝。 音乐家们跟着他们，拿着弦乐器。

As everyone started to decorate the hall, the musicians began playing and singing a beautiful Christmas carol. 当大家开始装饰大厅时，音乐家们开始演奏并唱起了一首美丽的圣诞颂歌。

“Jack!” said Annie. “Look!” “杰克！”安妮说道。 “看！”

The white stag was standing in the arched doorway. 那只白色的鹿正站在拱形门廊里。

Jack turned excitedly to Morgan. “See that white stag?” he said. “He took us to the Otherworld! See him?” 杰克兴奋地转向摩根。 “你看到那只白鹿了吗？”他说。 “他带我们去了另一个世界！看到他了吗？”

Morgan smiled. 摩根笑了。

“Yes, I do see him,” she said. “And now I see everything.” “是的，我看到他了，”她说。 “现在我什么都能看到了。”

Jack looked back at the doorway. The stag was gone. In his place stood an old man with a long white beard. He held a staff and wore a flowing red cloak—the same cloak Jack and Annie had worn on their quest. 杰克回头看向门口。 那头鹿不见了。 站在那里的是一个留着长长白胡子的老人。 他拿着一根拐杖，穿着一件红色的长斗篷——就是杰克和安妮在任务中穿的那件斗篷。

“Who’s that?” Jack asked. “那是谁？”杰克问。

“That is Merlin the magician,” said Morgan. “It was Merlin who invited you here. I see that now.” “那是梅林，一位魔术师，”摩根说。 “是梅林邀请你们来的。我现在明白了。”

“Merlin?” said Jack. “He sent us the Royal Invitation?” “梅林？”杰克说。 “他给我们发了皇家邀请函？”

“Yes,” said Morgan. “Then he put the rest of us under a spell. And he carried you to the Otherworld.” “是的，”摩根说。 “然后他把我们其他人施了魔法。 他还把你带到了另一个世界。”

“No,” said Annie, “the Christmas Knight put you under a spell.” “不是，”安妮说，“是圣诞骑士给你们施了魔法。”

“And the white stag carried us to the Otherworld,” said Jack. “而且是白鹿带我们去了另一个世界，”杰克说。

Morgan smiled. 摩根笑了。

“Merlin was both the Christmas Knight and the white stag,” she said. “Remember, he’s a magician, not a mortal. He can change his shape whenever he wishes.” “梅林既是圣诞骑士，也是那只白鹿，”她说。 “记住，他是个魔术师，不是凡人。 他想变成什么样子，随时都能变。”

“Oh, wow,” whispered Annie. “哦，哇，”安妮小声说。

“Why did Merlin do these things?” asked Jack. “梅林为什么要这么做？”杰克问。

“Merlin was angry when King Arthur banished magic from Camelot,” said Morgan. “I see now he finally took matters into his own hands.” “当亚瑟王把魔法从卡梅洛特驱逐出去时，梅林非常生气，”摩根说。 “我明白了，他终于决定自己采取行动了。”

“How?” said Jack. “怎么采取行动？”杰克说。

“He knew King Arthur would send no more knights to the Otherworld for the Water of Memory and Imagination,” said Morgan. “So I assume he brought you to Camelot hoping that you would offer to go instead.” “他知道亚瑟王不会再派骑士去异世界取记忆与想象之水了，”摩根说。 “所以我猜，他把你带到卡梅洛特，是希望你能代替他们去。”

“Why did he want us to go?” asked Annie. “他为什么让我们去呢？”安妮问。

“Merlin has often heard my tales of your adventures in the tree house,” said Morgan. “He knows you both have a great desire to fight for the good. And he knows you use the gift of imagination very well. Those are two special qualities needed to succeed in any quest.” “梅林经常听我讲你们在树屋里冒险的故事，”摩根说。 “他知道你们俩都有强烈的愿望去为正义而战。 他也知道你们都非常擅长运用想象力。 这两点是完成任何任务都需要的特殊品质。”

Jack and Annie looked back at Merlin. From far across the room, the white-bearded magician smiled at them. He raised his staff. Then he slipped out the door. 杰克和安妮回头看向梅林。 从房间的另一头，这位白胡子的魔术师微笑着看着他们。 他举起自己的魔杖。 然后他悄悄地走了出去。

Jack looked around the great hall. All the candles and torches were lit now. A fire blazed in the hearth. The musicians were playing. Everyone was singing. The room glowed with warm firelight and rosy faces. 杰克环顾着这间大礼堂。 所有的蜡烛和火把现在都点着了。 壁炉里燃起了熊熊大火。 音乐家们正在演奏。 每个人都唱着歌。 房间里充满了温暖的火光和红润的脸庞。

At last, Christmas in Camelot was just as Jack had imagined it would be. The spell of the Dark Wizard had been broken. The great hall was filled with beauty and love and joy and light. 最后，卡梅洛特的圣诞节和杰克想象的一模一样。 黑暗巫师的咒语已经被打破了。 大厅里充满了美丽、爱、快乐和光明。
