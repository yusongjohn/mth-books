13. Your Horses Are Waiting 13. 你的马正在等你

“I don’t believe what just happened,” said Jack. “我简直不敢相信刚才发生的事，”杰克说。

“What part don’t you believe?” said Annie, holding the silver cup. “你哪一部分不相信？”安妮一边说，一边拿着银杯子。

Jack laughed and shook his head. 杰克笑了，摇了摇头。

“I don’t believe any of it,” he said. “我一点都不相信，”他说。

Annie laughed, too. “That was cool, huh?” 安妮也笑了。 “刚才好酷啊，对吧？”

Jack pushed his glasses into place and stared at her. “Seriously, what just happened in there?” he asked. 杰克把眼镜推到正确的位置，盯着她看。 “说实话，刚才里面发生了什么？”他问。

“I imagined us fighting the Keepers with flaming swords,” said Annie. “What did you imagine?” “我想象着我们用燃烧的剑和守护者战斗，”安妮说。 “你想象了什么？”

Jack shrugged. “I—I just imagined the Keepers going back in their holes,” he said. 杰克耸了耸肩。 “我……我只是想象守护者们又回到他们的洞里，”他说。

“Good,” said Annie. “We both got what we imagined.” “很好，”安妮说。 “我们都得到了自己想象的东西。”

“Yeah,” said Jack, smiling. “But what you imagined made a much better story.” “是的，”杰克笑着说。 “但你想象出来的故事要好得多。”

A shriek of fury came from inside the cave. 一阵愤怒的尖叫声从洞里传来。

“Yikes!” said Annie. “天哪！”安妮说道。

“Let’s get out of here!” said Jack. “我们快离开这儿吧！”杰克说。

He scrambled to his feet, and together they climbed back down over the big rocks to the thicket. Annie moved very carefully to keep from spilling the water in the cup. 他赶紧站起来，两人一起爬过那些大石头，回到了灌木丛边。 安妮非常小心地移动，以免打翻杯子里的水。

When they came to the thicket, Jack pulled out Sir Percival’s compass. “If we came west to get here, we have to go east to get back,” he said. “East is that way.… ” 当他们来到灌木丛时，杰克拿出了珀西瓦尔爵士的指南针。 “如果我们是往西来到这儿，那我们就得往东才能回去，”他说。 “东边是那个方向……”

As they started into the wild growth, Jack went first so he could clear the way for Annie. Without talking, they pushed steadily through the trees and bushes, moving farther and farther from the Keepers’ cave. 他们开始走进茂密的灌木丛时，杰克第一个走，这样他就能为安妮清理出一条路。 他们没有说话，一直稳步穿过树木和灌木，离守护者的洞穴越来越远。

Finally, they heard music in the distance. They walked closer and closer toward the sound, until they stepped back into the green glade. 最后，他们听到了远处传来的声音。 他们一步一步走近声音，直到再次走进那片绿色的草地。

The winged dancers were still there, dancing in their magic circle. Jack’s heart raced. He wanted to join them again. But he knew that if he did, he would never escape their dance. 那些长着翅膀的舞者还在那里，在他们的魔法圈里跳舞。 杰克的心跳加快了。 他想再次加入他们。 但他知道，如果他这么做，他就永远无法逃脱他们的舞蹈了。

“Look!” said Annie. “The knights are awake!” “看！”安妮说。 “骑士们醒来了！”

Sir Lancelot, Sir Galahad, and Sir Percival were standing just beyond the circle of dancers. They were buckling on their swords. 兰斯洛特爵士、加拉哈德爵士和珀西瓦尔爵士正站在舞者们围成的圈外。 他们正在系上自己的剑。

“Hi!” called Annie. “Guess what! We got it!” “你好！”安妮喊道。 “猜猜看！我们拿到了！”

The knights walked shakily toward Jack and Annie. They still looked thin and tired, but color had returned to their cheeks. 骑士们摇摇晃晃地朝杰克和安妮走来。 他们看起来仍然又瘦又累，但脸色已经恢复了血色。

“We have the Water of Memory and Imagination,” said Annie. She held up the silver cup. “我们拿到了记忆与想象之水，”安妮说道。 她举起了那个银杯子。

The knights smiled. 骑士们笑了。

“Now we just have to get it back to Camelot,” said Jack. “现在我们只需要把它送回卡梅洛特就行了，”杰克说道。

“We would like to help you,” said Sir Lancelot. “But it seems we have lost our three horses.” “我们想帮助你，”兰斯洛特爵士说道。 “但看起来我们失去了三匹马。”

“No, you haven’t!” said Annie. “Your horses are waiting for you!” “不，你们没有！”安妮说道。 “你们的马正在等你们呢！”

“They’re on the other side of the hill,” said Jack. “它们在山的另一边，”杰克说道。

Jack and Annie led the knights over the hill. On their way, Jack picked up the red velvet cloak. When they came to the meadow, they saw the three horses. 杰克和安妮带领骑士们翻过山丘。 在路上，杰克捡起了那件红色的天鹅绒斗篷。 当他们来到草甸时，看到了三匹马。

The horses neighed and cantered over to the knights. As Sir Lancelot stroked the mane of the black horse, he turned to Jack and Annie. 马儿嘶叫着，小跑着来到骑士们身边。 当兰斯洛特爵士抚摸着黑马的鬃毛时，他转向了杰克和安妮。

“You can both ride back to Camelot with me,” he said. “你们两个可以和我一起骑马回卡梅洛特，”他说。

“Thanks!” they said. “谢谢！”他们说。

Jack fastened the red cloak around his neck. Sir Lancelot helped them onto the back of his horse, then climbed on himself. 杰克把红色斗篷系在脖子上。 兰斯洛特爵士帮他们上了自己的马，然后自己也骑了上去。

Annie sat behind Lancelot. She held on to the knight with her right hand. She held the silver cup in her left hand. 安妮坐在兰斯洛特后面。 她用右手抓住骑士。 她用左手拿着银杯子。

“Can you carry the water without spilling it?” Jack asked her, worried. “你能不洒出水来把水带回去吗？”杰克担心地问她。

“I’ll try,” she said. “我会试试的，”她说。

Sir Galahad mounted the brown horse, and Sir Percival climbed on the gray horse. Then the three knights started through the pale green meadow. 加拉哈德爵士骑上了棕色的马，珀西瓦尔爵士骑上了灰色的马。 然后，三位骑士开始穿过那片淡绿色的草地。

“Careful, careful,” Jack whispered to Annie. “小心，小心，”杰克对安妮小声说。

“I got it, I got it,” she said. “我拿到了，我拿到了，”她说。

When they came to the iron gate, the knights drew their swords. 当他们来到铁门前时，骑士们拔出了剑。

“Open the gate! In the name of King Arthur of Camelot!” Sir Lancelot called. Though he still sounded hoarse, the knight’s deep voice carried an amazing strength. “打开城门！ 以卡梅洛特的亚瑟王之名！”兰斯洛特爵士喊道。 虽然他的声音仍然沙哑，但骑士低沉的声音却带着惊人的力量。

The iron gate slowly swung open. Sir Lancelot urged his horse onward. 铁门缓缓地打开了。 兰斯洛特爵士催促着他的马继续前进。

The guards watched silently as the knights passed by them and started across the bridge. 守卫们默默地注视着骑士们从他们身边经过，开始走上桥。

The three horses trod in single file over the wooden planks. Again, Jack was amazed by the difference between this world and the Otherworld. Here it was dark and freezing and foggy. The red cloak flapped in the bitter wind. 三匹马排成一行，踏着木板走过去。 杰克又一次惊叹于这个世界和另一个世界的不同。 这里又黑又冷，还满是雾气。 红色斗篷在刺骨的风中飘荡。

As the horses stepped off the bridge, they each neighed loudly. 当马匹踏下桥时，它们都大声嘶叫起来。

“Oh, wow!” whispered Annie. “哦，哇！”安妮小声说。

Standing high on a rock, in a swirl of fog, was the white stag. 在一块高高的岩石上，雾气中站着一只白色的鹿。
