7. A Good Trick 7. 一个好主意

As the wind blew away more fog, Jack and Annie saw a bridge. It was made of thick wooden planks held together with iron bands. It stretched all the way from the edge of the cliff where they were standing to the iron gate. 随着风把更多的雾吹散，杰克和安妮看到了一座桥。 桥是用厚厚的木板做的，木板之间用铁带紧紧绑在一起。 桥一直延伸到他们站着的悬崖边缘，一直到那扇铁门前。

“Come on, let’s go!” said Annie. “快点，我们走吧！”安妮说。

“Wait!” said Jack. “What about the guards?” “等等！”杰克说。 “那守卫怎么办？”

The two guards in gold armor stood perfectly still. Their huge spears gleamed in the torchlight. 两个身穿金色盔甲的守卫一动不动地站着。 他们巨大的长矛在火把的光线下闪闪发亮。

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “Read the second rhyme.” “我不知道，”安妮说。 “读第二首童谣。”

Jack looked in his notebook and read aloud. 杰克翻开他的笔记本，大声读了出来。

“Four gifts you will need— “你需要四件礼物——

The first from me. 第一件来自我。

Then a cup, a compass, 然后是一只杯子，一个指南针。

And, finally, a key.” 最后是一把钥匙。”

“The first gift is the Christmas Knight’s cloak,” said Annie. “第一件礼物是圣诞骑士的斗篷，”安妮说。

“Yeah, I guess it’s supposed to help us somehow,” said Jack. “嗯，我想它应该能帮上我们的忙，”杰克说。

He unbuttoned the cloak from around his neck. Then he held it out to get a good look at it. 他解开脖子上的斗篷。 然后他把它拿起来，好好看了看。

“Maybe it can make us invisible,” said Annie. “也许它能让我们隐身，”安妮说。

“That’s nuts,” said Jack. “这太荒谬了，”杰克说。

“Seriously,” she said, “cloaks sometimes do that in stories.” “真的，”她说，“在故事里斗篷有时会这样。”

“Well, it didn’t make me invisible, did it?” said Jack. “那它没让我隐身，对吧？”杰克说。

“Maybe you were wearing it wrong,” said Annie. “Give it to me.” “也许你穿错了，”安妮说。 “给我。”

“Oh, brother,” said Jack. But he handed the cloak to Annie. It flapped in the wind as she pulled it around her shoulders. “哦，天哪，”杰克说。 但他把斗篷交给了安妮。 她把它披在肩上时，斗篷在风中飘动。

“Can you see me?” she said. “你能看到我吗？”她说。

“Yes, Annie,” said Jack, rolling his eyes. “I can see you.” “是的，安妮，”杰克说着，翻了翻眼睛。 “我能看见你。”

Jack looked back at the gate. Even if we get past the guards, what then? he wondered. The Otherworld swallowed up Camelot’s best knights. King Arthur said it was filled with magic and monsters. 杰克回头看向大门。 即使我们能通过守卫，那又怎样？他想。 异世界吞没了卡梅洛特最优秀的骑士。 亚瑟王说那里充满了魔法和怪物。

“Jack! Look at me now.” “杰克！现在看我。”

Jack turned to Annie. She wasn’t there. 杰克转向安妮。她不在那里。

“Where are you?” he said, staring at the darkness. “你在哪里？”他说，盯着黑暗。

“Cool, it works!” “太棒了，它真的有用！”

“Where are you?” Jack said again, turning around. “你在哪里？”杰克又问了一遍，转过身来。

“Here.”

Jack felt a hand touch his face. 杰克感觉到有一只手碰了他的脸。

“Ahh!” he said, jumping back. “啊！”他喊道，跳了回去。

“It’s me! I’m invisible! I pulled the hood over my head. That’s the trick.” “是我！我隐身了！ 我把兜帽拉到了头上。 这就是诀窍。”

Jack felt a chill run down his spine. 杰克感到一阵寒意从脊背窜上来。

“Oh, man,” he whispered. “哦，天哪，”他小声说。

“Watch. I’m going to take the hood off.” “看好了。 我要把兜帽拿下来了。”

In a flash, Annie was back. 转眼间，安妮就回来了。

“It feels creepy to be invisible,” she said. “变得看不见真让人觉得毛骨悚然，”她说。

Jack was speechless. 杰克说不出话来。

“The magic only happens when you wear the hood,” said Annie. “Good trick, huh?” “只有戴上兜帽时，魔法才会生效，”安妮说。 “这招不错，对吧？”

“Uh—yeah,” said Jack. He shook his head. “This is just too weird.” “呃——是的，”杰克说。 他摇了摇头。 “这太奇怪了。”

“Don’t worry about it being weird. It’s a great way to get past the guards,” said Annie. “Plus it’s a way to hide in the Otherworld. We don’t know what we’ll find there, right?” “别担心奇怪的事。 这是绕过守卫的好办法，”安妮说。 “而且这还是在异世界藏身的好办法。 我们不知道在那里会发现什么，对吧？”

“Yeah, right,” said Jack. “Okay.” “对，就是这样，”杰克说。 “好的。”

“Good,” said Annie. “Now, stand beside me and don’t move.” “很好，”安妮说。 “现在，站到我旁边，别动。”

Jack put away his notebook. Annie threw the velvet cloak over his shoulders and backpack. 杰克收起了他的笔记本。 安妮把天鹅绒斗篷披在他的肩膀和背包上。

“Great. It’s big enough for both of us,” she said. She carefully arranged the folds around them. Then she pulled the huge hood over both their heads. “太好了。 它足够大，我们可以两个人一起躲进去，”她说。 她小心地把褶皱整理好，盖在他们身上。 然后，她把巨大的兜帽拉过他们的头。

Jack looked down. He couldn’t see his body at all! He felt like he couldn’t breathe. In a panic, he threw off the hood. 杰克低头看了看。 他完全看不到自己的身体了！ 他感觉好像喘不过气来。 他慌张地把兜帽扯了下来。

“I hate that!” he said. “我讨厌这个！”他说。

“I told you it’s creepy,” said Annie. “But if we don’t wear it, we won’t get past the guards.” “我告诉过你，这很吓人，”安妮说。 “但是如果我们不戴它，就没办法通过守卫。”

“Yeah, I know, and we won’t have protection in the Otherworld,” said Jack. He took a deep breath. “Okay. Let’s do it.” “是啊，我知道，而且如果我们不戴它，在异世界就没有保护了，”杰克说。 他深吸了一口气。 “好吧。我们开始吧。”

Annie pulled the hood up again. 安妮又把兜帽拉了起来。

“I’ll hold on to the hood so it won’t blow off,” she said. “You just think about getting across that bridge. Nothing else.” “我会抓住兜帽，这样它就不会被吹掉了，”她说。 “你只要想着过那座桥，别的什么也别想。”

“But I can’t see my feet,” said Jack. “但是我看不见我的脚，”杰克说。

“You don’t need to see your feet to walk!” said Annie. “Come on. Do it for Morgan!” “你走路不需要看脚！”安妮说。 “加油。 为了摩根，做到！”

“Right,” said Jack. “对，”杰克说。

He and Annie stepped onto the bridge. 他和安妮走上了桥。

“Whatever you do, don’t look down,” said Annie. “不管你做什么，都不要往下看，”安妮说。

As they started over the bridge, the wind whistled around them. Jack couldn’t help it—he looked down. 当他们开始过桥时，风在他们周围呼啸。 杰克忍不住——他往下看了。

Not only was his body missing, but the fog beneath the bridge was moving in a wild, spinning whirl. Jack felt dizzy and faint. He stopped. 不仅他的身体不见了，桥下的雾气还在疯狂地旋转着。 杰克感到头晕目眩，差点晕倒。他停了下来。

“Keep going,” whispered Annie. “继续走，”安妮小声说。

Jack took a deep breath and looked straight ahead. Then he started walking again. He went slowly—step by step—toward the pale light beyond the bars of the gate. 杰克深吸了一口气，直视前方。 然后他又开始走路了。 他慢慢地——一步一步地——朝铁门另一边那道微弱的光走去。

In the flickering torchlight, the guards looked like giants. As Jack and Annie slipped invisibly by them, Jack held his breath. 在忽明忽暗的火光中，守卫看起来像巨人。 杰克和安妮悄悄地从他们身边溜过去时，杰克屏住了呼吸。

How will we open the gate? he wondered. 我们怎么才能打开这扇门呢？他想。

“WHOOOSSSHHH!” said Annie loudly. “呼——！”安妮大声说道。

Jack’s heart nearly stopped. Had Annie lost her mind? “What are you doing?” he whispered. 杰克的心几乎停跳了。 安妮是不是疯了？ “你在干什么？”他小声问。

“I’m the wind!” Annie whispered back. “ WHOOOSSSHHH!” “我是风！”安妮小声回答。 “呼——！”

Annie gave the gate a shove. It swung open, as if pushed by the wind. 安妮用力推了推门。 它被风吹开了，就像有人推了一样。

Jack looked back and saw that the guards had turned in their direction. 杰克回头一看，发现守卫们朝他们的方向转过身来了。

“Quick!” whispered Annie. “快！”安妮小声说。

She and Jack moved silently through the gateway. 她和杰克悄悄地穿过大门。

“WHOOOSSSHHH!” said Annie. “WHOOOSSSHHH！”安妮说。

She pushed the gate back. It shut with a clang. Through the bars, Jack saw the guards face the bridge again. 她把门推了回去。 门砰的一声关上了。 透过栏杆，杰克看到守卫们又面向了桥。

“Good work,” he said to Annie. “干得好，”他对安妮说。

“Thanks,” she said. “谢谢，”她说。

Jack and Annie then turned away from the gate. 杰克和安妮然后转身离开了大门。

“Ohh!” whispered Annie. “哦！”安妮小声说。

“The Otherworld,” whispered Jack. “另一个世界，”杰克小声说。
