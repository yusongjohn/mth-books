3. The Knights of the Round Table 3. 圆桌骑士

A pair of torches dimly lit the drafty entrance hall of the castle. Shadows danced on the worn tapestries. 一对火把微弱地照亮了城堡阴冷的门厅。 阴影在破旧的挂毯上跳动。

“Wait here,” said Morgan. “I will tell the king of your arrival.” She headed through the huge stone archway that led to the great hall. “你们在这里等着，”摩根说。 “我要去告诉国王你们要来了。”她穿过通往大厅的巨大石拱门走了进去。

“Let’s peek in,” Annie said to Jack. “我们偷偷看看吧，”安妮对杰克说。

Jack pushed his glasses into place. He and Annie walked quietly over to the big arch and peered in. 杰克把眼镜推到正确的位置。 他和安妮悄悄走到大拱门旁，往里面张望。

The ceiling of the great hall towered high above a stone floor. At the far end of the room, King Arthur and his knights were sitting around a huge, round table. They all wore brown tunics. They had shaggy hair and beards. Their names were carved in gold letters on the backs of their chairs. 大厅的天花板高高耸立在石地板上方。 房间的尽头，亚瑟王和他的骑士们正围坐在一张巨大的圆桌旁。 他们全都穿着棕色的长袍。 他们留着蓬乱的头发和胡须。 他们的名字用金色的字母刻在椅子的背面。

“The Knights of the Round Table!” whispered Jack. “圆桌骑士们！”杰克小声说。

Morgan was talking to King Arthur. Beside the king sat a woman in a plain gray robe. She had pale skin and brown, curly hair. 摩根正在和亚瑟王说话。 国王身边坐着一位穿着普通灰色长袍的妇女。 她皮肤苍白，有一头棕色的卷发。

“Queen Guinevere,” whispered Annie. “吉尼弗蕾王后，”安妮小声说。

Morgan left the king, and Jack and Annie moved quickly back into the shadows. A moment later, Morgan appeared. 摩根离开了国王，杰克和安妮赶紧又躲进了阴影里。 一会儿后，摩根出现了。

“I told the king that two special friends of his have just arrived,” she said. “Come with me.” “我告诉国王，他的两位特别的朋友刚刚到了，”她说。 “跟我来。”

As they walked with Morgan through the great hall, Jack shivered. The huge room was drafty and damp. There was no fire in the fireplace. The stone floor was so cold that Jack could feel the chill through his sneakers. 当他们和摩根一起穿过大厅时，杰克打了个哆嗦。 这个大房间又冷又潮湿。 壁炉里没有火。 石头地板非常冷，杰克能透过他的运动鞋感觉到寒意。

They stopped near the Round Table. King Arthur stared at them with his piercing gray eyes. 他们停在圆桌旁边。 亚瑟王用他锐利的灰色眼睛盯着他们。

“Greetings from Frog Creek,” Annie said to the king and queen. Annie bowed, and Jack bowed, too. “来自蛙溪镇的问候，”安妮对国王和王后说道。 安妮鞠了一躬，杰克也鞠了一躬。

The queen smiled. But King Arthur did not. 王后笑了。 但亚瑟王没有笑。

“Your Majesty, you remember Jack and Annie?” said Morgan. “You met them last summer in my library?” “陛下，您还记得杰克和安妮吗？”摩根说道。 “你们去年夏天在我的图书馆里见过他们吗？”

“Indeed, I shall never forget them,” King Arthur said softly. “Greetings, Annie. Greetings, Jack. How do you come to be in Camelot on this bleak night?” “确实，我永远不会忘记他们，”亚瑟王轻声说道。 “你好，安妮。你好，杰克。 你们怎么会在这么寒冷的夜晚来到卡梅洛特？”

“We came in the magic tree house,” said Annie. “我们是乘着神奇的树屋来的，”安妮说。

A shadow crossed the king’s face. He looked at Morgan. 国王脸上闪过一道阴影。 他看向摩根。

“No, Your Majesty. I did not use my magic to bring them here,” she said. “Perhaps a bit of magic still lingers in the tree house, and it traveled on its own.” “不，陛下。 我没有用我的魔法把他们带来这里，”她说。 “也许树屋里还残留着一点魔法，它自己跑来了。”

What’s going on? Jack wondered. Why does King Arthur seem unhappy about the magic tree house? 到底发生了什么事？杰克心想。 为什么亚瑟国王看起来对这间有魔法的树屋不太开心呢？

King Arthur looked back at Jack and Annie. 亚瑟国王回头看了看杰克和安妮。

“However you have come, you are welcome in my kingdom,” he said. He turned to the queen. “Guinevere, these are the two friends who once gave me hope and courage in a time of need.” “不管你们是怎么来到这里的，你们都欢迎来到我的王国，”他说。 他转向了王后。 “桂妮薇尔，这两位是曾经在我最需要的时候给我带来希望和勇气的朋友。”

Queen Guinevere smiled again. But there was a sad look in her eyes. “I have heard much about you,” she said. 桂妮薇尔王后又笑了。 但她的眼睛里流露出一丝悲伤。 “我听说过很多关于你们的事情，”她说。

“I’ve heard about you, too!” said Annie. “我也听说过关于你们的事情！”安妮说。

“Allow me to present my knights,” said King Arthur. “Sir Bors, Sir Kay, Sir Tristram … ” “让我来介绍一下我的骑士们，”亚瑟国王说。 “伯斯爵士、凯爵士、特里斯坦爵士……”

As the king named each knight, Jack and Annie nodded shyly. The knights nodded at them in return. Jack waited to hear the name Sir Lancelot, the most famous of Camelot’s knights. But the king never said it. 国王每念出一个骑士的名字，杰克和安妮就害羞地点头。 骑士们也向他们点了点头。 杰克等着听最著名的卡梅洛特骑士兰斯洛特爵士的名字。 但国王一直没有说出来。

“And finally, Sir Bedivere and Sir Gawain,” King Arthur said. “最后是贝迪维尔爵士和高文爵士，”亚瑟国王说。

The king then turned to three empty chairs at the table. “And there once sat three who are lost to us now,” he said. 国王然后转向了桌边的三把空椅子。 “曾经有三个人坐在这里，如今却永远地离开了我们，”他说。

Lost how? wondered Jack. 他们是怎么离开的？杰克心想。

“You may sit at their places and join our dinner,” King Arthur said. “你们可以坐在他们的位置上，和我们一起共进晚餐，”亚瑟国王说。

“Thank you,” said Annie. “谢谢，”安妮说。

Following Morgan around the table, Jack read the names carved on the backs of the three empty chairs: SIR LANCELOT, SIR GALAHAD, SIR PERCIVAL. 杰克跟着摩根绕着桌子走，他读着刻在三把空椅子背上的名字：SIR LANCELOT，SIR GALAHAD，SIR PERCIVAL。

Jack took off his backpack and sat down in Sir Lancelot’s place. 杰克取下背包，坐在了兰斯洛特爵士的位置上。

As he sat tall and straight in the heavy wooden chair, Jack looked at the king and his knights. They were gnawing meat off bones and slurping wine from heavy goblets. They ate without manners or delight. 当他挺直腰板坐在那把沉重的木椅上时，杰克看着国王和他的骑士们。 他们正啃着骨头上的肉，从沉重的杯子里吸着葡萄酒。 他们吃饭时没有礼仪，也没有享受。

Jack really wanted to take notes. He reached into his pack under the table and pulled out his notebook and pencil. But before he could write a word, a serving boy brought more food. Jack quickly put his things away. The boy set a greasy slab of beef on a soggy piece of bread in front of him. The food looked terrible. 杰克真的很想做笔记。 他伸手到桌子下面的背包里，拿出他的笔记本和铅笔。 但他还没来得及写一个字，一个侍应生就端来了更多的食物。 杰克赶紧把东西收了起来。 男孩把一块油腻的牛肉放在一块湿漉漉的面包上，放在自己面前。 这食物看起来糟糕透了。

“Not much of a Christmas feast, huh?” Annie said in a low voice. “这算什么圣诞盛宴啊？”安妮低声说道。

Jack shook his head. 杰克摇了摇头。

Annie leaned close to Morgan and whispered so King Arthur wouldn’t hear. “What happened to the three lost knights?” she asked. 安妮靠近摩根，小声说话，以免亚瑟王听见。 “那三个失踪的骑士去哪儿了？”她问。

“After Mordred’s Dark Wizard cast his spell, the king sought help from the magicians of Camelot,” Morgan said quietly. “They told him he must send his knights on a quest to the Otherworld, to recapture our kingdom’s joy.” “在莫德雷德的黑巫师施了魔法之后，国王向卡梅洛特的魔法师们寻求帮助，”摩根轻声说。 “他们告诉他，他必须派他的骑士去另一个世界，夺回我们王国的快乐。”

“What’s the Otherworld?” said Jack. “另一个世界是什么地方？”杰克问。

“It is an ancient, enchanted land beyond the edge of the Earth,” said Morgan. “The place where all magic first began.” “那是地球边缘之外一个古老而充满魔法的地方，”摩根说。 “所有魔法最初开始的地方。”

“Wow,” whispered Annie. “哇，”安妮小声说。

“The king chose his three bravest knights to journey there,” said Morgan. “When they did not come back, Arthur turned against his magicians. He blamed magic for all of Camelot’s woes. Hence, he has banned magic of any kind from the kingdom forever.” “国王挑选了他最勇敢的三名骑士去那里，”摩根说。 “当他们没有回来时，亚瑟开始反对他的魔法师们。 他把卡梅洛特的所有烦恼都归咎于魔法。 因此，他永远禁止了王国中任何形式的魔法。”

“But you’re a magician,” whispered Annie. “Did the king turn against you, too?” “但你是个魔法师，”安妮小声说。 “国王也反对你吗？”

“Arthur and I have a long friendship,” said Morgan. “He has allowed me to stay in the castle as long as I promise not to practice the art of magic ever again.” “我和亚瑟有很长的友谊，”摩根说。 “他允许我留在城堡里，只要我承诺再也不练习魔法。”

A feeling of dread crept over Jack. “So … does that mean the magic tree house is … ?” 杰克感到一阵害怕涌上心头。 “那……这意味着神奇的树屋……？”

Morgan nodded. “Yes. Banished from Camelot,” she said. “I’m afraid this will be your last journey. And the last time we see each other.” Her eyes filled with tears. She looked away. 摩根点了点头。 “是的。 被赶出卡梅洛特，”她说。 “我担心这将是你们最后一次旅程。 也是我们最后一次见面。”她眼里充满了泪水。她转过头去。

“What? The last time we see each other? Forever?” said Annie. “什么？ 我们最后一次见面？ 永远吗？”安妮说。

Before Morgan could answer, the wooden doors swung open with a bang. A wind rushed through the great hall. The torches and candles flamed brighter, making the shadows leap wildly on the walls. 摩根还没来得及回答，木门“砰”地一声猛地打开了。 一阵风吹进了大厅。 火把和蜡烛的火光变得更强了，墙上的影子疯狂地跳来跳去。

The sound of hoofbeats filled the room. A knight on a huge horse rode through the arched doorway. 马蹄声充满了整个房间。 一个骑士骑着一匹大马从拱形门里骑了进来。

The knight was dressed all in red—from his shining helmet to the long cloak on his back. His horse was dressed all in green—from the armor that covered his head to the cloth that hung from his saddle. 这位骑士全身都穿着红色的衣服，从他闪闪发光的头盔到他背上的长斗篷都是红色的。 他的马全身都穿着绿色的衣服，从覆盖着马头的盔甲到挂在马鞍上的布料都是绿色的。

“Oh, wow!” breathed Annie. “A Christmas Knight!” “哦，哇！”安妮惊叹道。 “圣诞骑士！”
