2. This Is Camelot? 2. 这是卡梅洛特吗？

Jack shivered. He could see his breath in the dim light. 杰克打了个寒战。 在昏暗的光线下，他能看到自己呼出的白气。

Annie was staring out the window. “This is Camelot?” she said. 安妮正盯着窗外看。 “这是卡梅洛特吗？”她说。

Jack looked out with her. The tree house had landed in a grove of tall, bare trees. A huge, dark castle loomed against the gray sky. No light shone from its windows. No banners waved from its turrets. Wind whistled through its tall towers, sounding sad and lonely. 杰克也和她一起往外看。 树屋降落在一片高大、光秃秃的树丛中。 一座又大又黑的城堡在灰蒙蒙的天空下若隐若现。 它的窗户里没有一丝光亮。 它的塔楼上也没有旗帜飘扬。 风吹过它高高的塔楼，发出悲伤而孤独的声音。

“It looks deserted,” said Annie. “看起来没人住，”安妮说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “I hope we came to the right place.” “是啊，”杰克说。 “希望我们来对地方了。”

Jack pulled his notebook and pencil out of his pack. He wanted to write a description of the dark castle. 杰克从背包里拿出他的笔记本和铅笔。 他想写下这座黑暗城堡的描述。

“Hey, I see someone,” said Annie. “嘿，我看到有人，”安妮说。

Jack looked out the window again. 杰克又朝窗外看了看。

A woman was crossing the castle drawbridge. She wore a long cloak and carried a lantern. Her white hair blew in the wind. 一位女士正走过城堡的吊桥。 她穿着一件长斗篷，手里拿着一盏灯。 她的白发在风中飘动。

“Morgan!” said Annie and Jack together. They laughed with relief. “摩根！”安妮和杰克一起喊道。 他们松了口气，笑了起来。

Morgan hurried over the frost-covered ground toward the grove of trees. “Annie? Jack? Is that you?” she called. 摩根赶紧穿过结了霜的地面，朝树林那边跑来。 “安妮？杰克？ 真的是你们吗？”她喊道。

“Of course! Who’d you think?” shouted Annie. She started down from the tree house. “当然！ “你猜是谁？”安妮喊道。 她从树屋开始往下走。

Jack threw his notebook into his backpack. He followed Annie down the rope ladder. When they reached the icy ground, they ran to Morgan and both threw their arms around her. 杰克把他的笔记本扔进了背包里。 他跟着安妮沿着绳梯往下走。 他们到达冰地后，跑向摩根，两人立刻张开双臂抱住她。

“I was looking out a window in the castle and saw a bright flash in the orchard,” said Morgan. “What are you doing here?” “我正在城堡里透过窗户看，看到果园里有一道明亮的闪光，”摩根说。 “你们在这里干什么？”

“You didn’t send the tree house for us?” asked Jack. “你们没给我们寄树屋吗？”杰克问。

“With a Royal Invitation to spend Christmas in Camelot?” asked Annie. “带着一封皇家邀请函，邀请我们在卡梅洛特过圣诞节？”安妮问。

“No!” said Morgan. She sounded alarmed. “不！”摩根说。 她听起来很惊慌。

“But the invitation was signed with an M,” said Jack. “但是邀请函上签的是M，”杰克说。

“I don’t understand … ,” said Morgan. “We are not celebrating Christmas in Camelot this year.” “我不明白……”摩根说。 “我们今年不会在卡梅洛特庆祝圣诞节。”

“You aren’t?” said Jack. “你们不庆祝？”杰克说。

“Why not?” said Annie. “为什么？”安妮说。

A look of sadness crossed Morgan’s face. “Do you remember when you visited my library and gave King Arthur the hope and courage to challenge his enemy?” she asked. 摩根脸上露出悲伤的表情。 “你还记得你曾经来过我的图书馆，给了亚瑟王希望和勇气去挑战他的敌人吗？”她问。

“Sure,” said Jack. “当然记得，”杰克说。

“Well, Arthur’s enemy was a man named Mordred,” said Morgan. “After you left, Arthur defeated him, but not before Mordred’s Dark Wizard cast a spell over the whole kingdom. The spell robbed Camelot of all its joy.” “嗯，亚瑟的敌人是一个叫莫德雷德的人，”摩根说。 “你们离开后，亚瑟打败了他，但没等莫德雷德的黑巫师对整个王国施下咒语。 这个咒语让卡梅洛特失去了所有的快乐。”

“What? All its joy?” whispered Annie. “什么？ 所有的快乐？”安妮小声说。

“Yes,” said Morgan. “For months, Camelot has been without music, without celebration, and without laughter.” “是的，”摩根说。 “几个月来，卡梅洛特没有音乐，没有庆祝，也没有笑声。”

“Oh, no,” said Annie. “哦，天哪，”安妮说。

“What can we do to help?” said Jack. “我们能做些什么来帮忙呢？”杰克说。

Morgan smiled sadly. “This time, I don’t think you can do anything,” she said. “But perhaps it will lift Arthur’s spirits to see you both again. Come, let us go inside the castle.” 摩根难过地笑了笑。 “这次，我想你什么都做不了，”她说。 “但也许看到你们俩再次出现，会让亚瑟的心情好一些。 来吧，我们进去城堡。”

Morgan held up her lantern and started toward the drawbridge. 摩根举起她的提灯，朝吊桥走去。

Jack and Annie hurried after her. As they walked through the outer courtyard, the frozen grass cracked under their sneakers. 杰克和安妮赶紧跟了上去。 他们走过外院时，冻僵的草在他们的运动鞋下发出咔嚓声。

They followed Morgan over the bridge and through a tall gate. There were no signs of life in the castle’s inner courtyard. 他们跟着摩根走过桥，穿过一扇高高的门。 城堡的内院里看不到任何生命的迹象。

“Where is everyone?” Annie whispered to Jack. “大家都在哪儿？”安妮小声对杰克说。

“I don’t know,” he whispered back. Jack really wished they had a book about Camelot. It might help them understand what was going on. “我不知道，”他小声回答。 杰克真希望他们有一本关于卡梅洛特的书。 这可能会帮助他们理解到底发生了什么。

Morgan led them to a huge archway with two wooden doors. She stopped and looked at them. 摩根带他们来到一个巨大的拱门前，那里有两扇木门。 她停了下来，看着他们。

“I am afraid no book would help you tonight, Jack,” she said. “我担心今晚没有一本书能帮到你，杰克，”她说。

Jack was startled that Morgan had read his thoughts. 杰克惊讶地发现摩根竟然读透了他的想法。

“Why not?” asked Annie. “为什么不能呢？”安妮问。

“On all your other journeys, you visited real places and times in history,” said Morgan. “Camelot is different.” “在你们之前的旅程中，你们都去过真实的地方和历史时期，”摩根说。 “卡梅洛特是不一样的。”

“How?” said Jack. “怎么不一样？”杰克说。

“The story of Camelot is a legend,” said Morgan. “A legend is a story that begins in truth. But then imagination takes over. Different people in different times tell the story. They use their imaginations to add new parts. That is how a legend is kept alive.” “卡梅洛特的故事是一个传说，”摩根说。 “传说是一个从真实开始的故事。 但之后想象力就接管了。 不同时代的不同人都会讲述这个故事。 他们用想象力添加新的部分。 这就是传说如何一直流传下去的。”

“Tonight we’ll add our part,” said Annie. “今晚我们要添加我们的部分，”安妮说。

“Yes,” said Morgan. “And please, I beg you”—in the lantern light, she looked very serious—“do not let the story of Camelot end forever. Keep our kingdom alive.” “是的，”摩根说。 “请务必，我恳求你们——在灯笼的光线下，她看起来非常严肃——不要让卡梅洛特的故事永远结束。 让我们的王国继续活下去。”

“Of course we will!” said Annie. “当然会的！”安妮说。

“Good,” said Morgan. “Come, then. Let us go into the great hall and see the king.” “很好，”摩根说。 “来吧， 让我们去大厅里见国王。”

Morgan lifted an iron latch and pushed open the heavy doors. Jack and Annie followed her into the dark castle. 摩根抬起铁制的门闩，推开沉重的门。 杰克和安妮跟着她走进了黑暗的城堡。
