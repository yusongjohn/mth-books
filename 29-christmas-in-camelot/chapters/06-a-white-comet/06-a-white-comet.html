6. A White Comet 6. 一颗白色的彗星

Once the knight was gone, the candles and torches in the great hall grew dimmer. A bitter chill crept over the room. 骑士一走，大厅里的蜡烛和火把就变得暗淡了。 一股刺骨的寒意蔓延了整个房间。

“What do these three rhymes mean?” said Jack, looking at his notebook. “Who are the Keepers of the Cauldron? What secret door?” “这三个押韵的意思是什么？”杰克看着他的笔记本说。 “谁是坩埚的守护者？ “什么秘密门？”

“I don’t know,” said Annie. “I just know we have to save Morgan.” “我不知道，”安妮说。 “我只是知道我们必须救摩根，”

She gathered the red cloak up in her arms. “We’ve got our first gift,” she said. “Let’s go.” 她把红色斗篷抱在怀里。 “我们得到了第一个礼物，”她说。 “我们走吧。”

“Wait—we should figure this out first,” said Jack. “等等——我们应该先弄清楚，”杰克说。

“No. We should just go,” said Annie. She turned and headed for the archway. “不。 我们应该直接走，”安妮说。 她转身朝拱门走去。

Jack pushed his glasses into place and looked back at the Round Table, at the frozen king and queen, at the frozen knights, and at Morgan le Fay. 杰克把眼镜推到正确的位置，回头看向圆桌，看向被冻结的国王和王后，看向被冻结的骑士，还有摩根·勒·法伊。

He loved Morgan. She was their great friend and teacher. If he and Annie did not go on their quest, Morgan’s story and the stories of Camelot and all the stories about the magic tree house would end forever. 他喜欢摩根。 她是他们的朋友和老师。 如果他和安妮不去完成他们的任务，摩根的故事，还有卡梅洛特的故事，以及所有关于神奇树屋的魔法故事，都将永远结束。

Jack took a deep breath. He put his notebook into his backpack. Then he turned toward the archway. 杰克深吸了一口气。 他把笔记本放进背包里。 然后他朝拱门走去。

“Annie?” he said. “安妮？”他说道。

She was gone. 她不见了。

“Annie, wait!” he shouted. “Wait!” “安妮，等等！”他喊道。 “等一下！”

Jack ran out of the great hall. 杰克从大厅里跑了出来。

“Annie!” “安妮！”

“I’m here,” she said quietly. “I’m waiting.” She was standing at the end of the entrance hall peering outside. “我在这里，”她轻声说道。 “我在等你。”她站在入口大厅的尽头，朝外张望着。

“How do we get to the Otherworld?” she asked. “我们怎么才能去另一个世界呢？”她问。

“Maybe the tree house can take us there,” said Jack. “Come on.” “也许树屋能带我们去那里，”杰克说。 “快点。”

Together, Jack and Annie hurried through the inner courtyard of the castle and over the drawbridge. They ran over the frozen ground to the moonlit grove of trees. 杰克和安妮一起，匆匆穿过城堡的内院，走过吊桥。 他们跑过结冰的地面，来到月光下的树林里。

Clutching the red cloak, Annie started up the rope ladder. Jack followed. They climbed inside the tree house and sat on the floor. 安妮紧紧抓着红色斗篷，开始爬绳梯。杰克跟在后面。 他们爬进树屋，坐在地板上。

Annie picked up the Royal Invitation. “Close your eyes. I’ll make the wish,” she said. 安妮捡起了皇家邀请函。 “闭上眼睛。 我要许愿了，”她说。

Jack closed his eyes. He was shivering from the cold. 杰克闭上了眼睛。 他因为寒冷而发抖。

“I wish we could go to the Otherworld,” said Annie. “我希望我们能去另一个世界，”安妮说。

The bare branches of the trees rattled in the wind. 树木光秃秃的枝条在风中发出哗啦哗啦的声音。

“I think it’s working!” whispered Annie. “我觉得它起作用了！”安妮小声说。

The wind stopped blowing. 风停了。

Jack opened his eyes. He and Annie looked out the window. The dark castle loomed against the sky. They were still in Camelot. 杰克睁开了眼睛。 他和安妮看向窗外。 那座黑暗的城堡在天空下显得格外庞大。 他们还在卡梅洛特。

“It d-didn’t work,” said Jack, his teeth chattering. “它没——没用，”杰克说道，牙齿在打颤。

“Yes, it did!” whispered Annie. “Look down.” “是的，它有用！”安妮小声说。 “往下看。”

Standing below the tree house was the biggest deer Jack had ever seen. The deer was staring up at them with amber eyes. His huge antlers seemed to glow in the cold moonlight. 树屋下面站着一头杰克见过的最大的鹿。 那只鹿用琥珀色的眼睛盯着他们。 他的大鹿角在寒冷的月光下似乎闪闪发光。

Most amazing of all, the deer was completely white, as white as new-fallen snow. 最令人惊奇的是，这只鹿全身都是白色的，像新落下的雪一样白。

“A white stag!” said Jack. “一只白鹿！”，杰克说道。

Puffs of frosty air blew from the stag’s nostrils. He stepped toward the tree house and shook his giant head. 白鹿的鼻子里呼出一团团冰冷的雾气。 它朝树屋走来，然后摇晃着它巨大的头。

“He’s come to take us on our journey,” said Annie. “他来带我们开始旅程了，”安妮说道。

“People don’t ride deer,” said Jack. “人们不会骑鹿的，”杰克说道。

But Annie had already started down the rope ladder. Jack watched from the window as she walked to the stag and spoke softly. The stag knelt. Annie climbed on his back. 但安妮已经开始往下爬绳梯了。 杰克从窗户看着她走到鹿身边，轻声说话。鹿跪了下来。 安妮爬上了它的背。

“Come on!” she called to Jack. “Bring the cloak!” “快点！”她对杰克喊道。 “带上斗篷！”

“Okay, okay,” said Jack. He gathered up the heavy velvet cloak. Clutching it against his chest, he climbed down the rope ladder. He hurried over to Annie and the white stag. “好吧，好吧，”杰克说道。 他捡起了那件厚重的天鹅绒斗篷。 他把它紧紧地贴在胸前，顺着绳梯爬了下来。 他赶紧走到安妮和那头白鹿身边。

“Put on the cloak and climb on behind me,” said Annie. “穿上斗篷，然后从我后面爬上来，”安妮说道。

Jack put the cloak on over his backpack. He pulled it around his shoulders and buttoned it at the neck. As the cloak fell down around his body, the soft, smooth cloth made him feel warm and safe. 杰克把斗篷披在背包上。 他把斗篷披在肩上，并在脖子处系好扣子。 当斗篷披在身上时，那柔软光滑的布料让他感觉又暖和又安全。

“Ready?” said Annie. “准备好了吗？”安妮说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. He climbed on the stag’s back behind Annie. “准备好了，”杰克说。 他爬到鹿的背上，跟在安妮后面。

The white stag slowly stood up. Annie leaned forward, putting her arms around its neck. Jack leaned forward, too, and held on to Annie. The red velvet cloak draped over both of them, falling past their feet. 那只白鹿慢慢地站了起来。 安妮向前倾身，双臂环住它的脖子。 杰克也向前倾身，紧紧抓住安妮。 红色的天鹅绒斗篷披在他们两人身上，垂落到脚边。

The white stag stepped gracefully over the frozen grass. He walked through the outer gate of the castle. He blew out a puff of air, then broke into a leaping run. 那只白鹿优雅地踏过结冰的草地。 他走进城堡的大门。 他吹出一口气，然后开始飞快地奔跑。

Jack held on tightly to Annie as the stag dashed across a frost-covered field. He jumped over hedgerows and stone walls. He bounded across icy streams. 杰克紧紧抓住安妮，因为那头鹿正飞快地穿过结了冰霜的田野。 它跳过了篱笆和石头墙。 它在结冰的小溪上一跃而过。

Annie’s braids floated on the wind. The red cloak billowed behind them. Jack was amazed at how easy it was to ride on the stag’s back. He felt calm and safe as the stag sped like a white comet through the wintry countryside. 安妮的辫子在风中飘扬。 红色的斗篷在他们身后飘荡。 杰克惊讶地发现，骑在鹿背上竟然这么容易。 当鹿像一颗白色的彗星一样在冬日的乡野间飞驰时，杰克感到非常平静和安全。

The stag ran past flocks of sheep and herds of goats asleep in the meadows. He ran past thatched huts and quiet stables. 鹿跑过了在草地上睡觉的羊群和山羊群。 它跑过了茅草小屋和安静的马厩。

The stag ran on and on through the starry night. Jack saw a cloud-covered mountain range in the distance. When they came close to the craggy mountains, Jack was sure the stag would stop. But he galloped on—not even breaking his stride as he started up a rocky slope. 鹿一直奔跑着，穿过星光闪烁的夜晚。 杰克在远处看到一片被云雾笼罩的山脉。 当他们靠近那崎岖的山脉时，杰克确信那头鹿会停下来。 但那头鹿继续奔跑着——甚至在开始爬上陡峭的山坡时也没有停下脚步。

The stag finally came to a halt on the ledge of a steep cliff. In a windy swirl of fog and cloud, he knelt to the ground, and Jack and Annie slid off his back. 那头鹿终于在一个陡峭悬崖的边缘停了下来。 在一阵风吹起的雾气和云朵中，他跪在地上，杰克和安妮从他背上滑了下来。

The stag stood up. He stared down at them with his glowing amber eyes. 那头鹿站了起来。 他用闪闪发光的琥珀色眼睛低头看着他们。

“Thank you!” said Annie. “Do you have to leave now?” “谢谢！”安妮说。 “你现在就要离开了吗？”

The stag lowered his head and raised it again. He blew out a frosty puff of air, then leaped away, vanishing into the mist. 那头鹿低下头，又抬了起来。 他呼出一口白气，然后跳开，消失在雾中。

“Bye,” Annie said wistfully. She stared into the mist for a moment, then turned to Jack. “What do we do now?” “再见，”安妮依依不舍地说。 她盯着雾看了片刻，然后转向杰克。 “我们现在该怎么办？”

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “Let’s read the three rhymes again.” “我不知道，”杰克说。 “我们再读一遍那三首童谣吧。”

He reached under the red cloak and pulled off his pack. He took out his notebook and started to read the first rhyme: 他伸手到红色斗篷下面，取下背包。 他拿出笔记本，开始读第一首童谣：

“Beyond the iron gate—” “在铁门之外——”

“Jack!” interrupted Annie. “Look!” “杰克！”安妮打断了他。 “看！”

Jack looked up. The wind had blown away some of the fog. Beyond the cliff rose another mountain. A huge gate was built into its side. A pale light shone between the gate’s thick iron bars. Two knights in gold armor stood guard under flaming torches. 杰克抬头看了看。 风把一些雾吹走了。 在悬崖的另一边，另一座山升了起来。 一座巨大的门建在山的一侧。 一道淡白色的光从门厚重的铁栏杆之间照出来。 两名穿着金色盔甲的骑士站在燃烧的火把下守卫着。

“Oh, man,” whispered Jack. “天啊，”杰克小声说。

“That’s it—the iron gate!” said Annie. “If we pass through that gate, we’ll be in the Otherworld!” “那就是——铁门！”安妮说。 “如果我们穿过那扇门，我们就会进入另一个世界！”
