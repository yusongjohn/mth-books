14. Return 14. 返回

The three knights gazed in wonder at the white stag. 三位骑士惊奇地望着那只白鹿。

“Here, take this!” Annie said to Jack. She handed him the silver cup. Then she slipped off the back of Lancelot’s horse and ran to the stag. “来，拿着这个！”安妮对杰克说。 她把银杯子递给他。 然后她从兰斯洛特的马背上滑下来，跑向那头鹿。

“Thanks for coming for us!” she cried, throwing her arms around his neck. “谢谢你们来救我们！”她喊道，一把抱住他的脖子。

The three knights looked at Jack. 三个骑士看着杰克。

“That’s the white stag,” said Jack. “He brought us here.” “那是白鹿，”杰克说。 “他带我们来到这里。”

“Are you wizards?” Sir Percival asked in a hushed voice. “你们是巫师吗？”珀西瓦尔爵士压低声音问。

“No, just ordinary kids,” said Jack. “But I know he’ s magic. We got here from Camelot in no time at all. I guess he’s come to take us back.” “不，我们只是普通的孩子，”杰克说。 “但我知道他是有魔法的。 我们从卡梅洛特到这里，转眼就到了。 “我想他是来带我们回去的。”

“Then you must go with him,” said Sir Lancelot. “You will have a much faster journey, I can assure you.” “那么你必须跟他走，”兰斯洛特爵士说。 “你们的旅程会快得多，我可以向你们保证。”

Sir Lancelot held the silver cup as Jack slipped down from the black horse. Then Jack took the cup and carefully climbed onto the back of the stag behind Annie. He held the cup with both hands as the stag stood up. 兰斯洛特爵士拿着银杯，杰克从黑色的马上滑了下来。 然后杰克拿起杯子，小心地爬到安妮后面的鹿背上。 他用两只手拿着杯子，鹿站了起来。

“Tell King Arthur we will return to Camelot before the first night of the New Year,” said Sir Lancelot. “告诉亚瑟王，我们会在新年第一个夜晚之前返回卡梅洛特，”兰斯洛特爵士说。

“Farewell, Jack and Annie!” said Sir Galahad. “再见，杰克和安妮！”加拉哈德爵士说。

“Godspeed!” said Sir Percival. “祝你们好运！”珀西瓦尔爵士说。

“Same to you!” said Annie. “也祝你们好运！”安妮说。

“Have a safe trip!” said Jack. “旅途平安！”杰克说。

The knights solemnly bowed. 骑士们庄严地鞠了一躬。

The white stag blew out a puff of frosty air. Then he started down the mountainside. 白色的鹿吹出一团冰冷的气。 然后他开始沿着山下跑起来。

When the stag came to the base of the mountain, he took off again like a white comet. The red cloak billowed around Jack and Annie, keeping them warm and safe. 当鹿跑到山脚时，他又像一颗白色的彗星一样飞快地跑开了。 红色的斗篷在杰克和安妮周围飘动，让他们感到温暖又安全。

The stag dashed across the wintry fields. He ran past quiet stables and thatched huts. He ran past flocks of sheep and herds of goats asleep in meadows. He leaped over frozen streams and stone walls and hedgerows. 鹿在冬天的田野上飞快地奔跑。 他跑过了安静的马厩和茅草小屋。 他跑过了在草地上睡觉的羊群和山羊群。 他跳过了结冰的小溪、石头墙和树篱。

The stag ran on and on through the starry night, until he brought Jack and Annie back to the dark castle grounds of Camelot. 鹿在星光闪烁的夜晚不停地奔跑，直到它把杰克和安妮带回了卡梅洛特黑暗的城堡前庭。

He walked over the frozen grass of the outer courtyard. He came to a halt near the grove below the tree house. He knelt in the grass, and Jack and Annie climbed off his back. 它走过外院结冰的草地。 它在树屋下方的树林旁停了下来。 它跪在草地上，杰克和安妮从它背上跳了下来。

Miraculously, the silver cup still brimmed with water from the cauldron. Not a drop had spilled out. 奇迹般地，那个银杯子仍然装满了从大锅里取来的水。 一滴水都没有洒出来。

“We’d better leave the cloak here,” said Jack, “so I don’t trip on it.” “我们最好把斗篷留在这儿，”杰克说，“不然我会被它绊倒。”

Jack carefully set the cup on the ground. Annie helped him unbutton the red velvet cloak from around his neck. Then she draped it over the stag’s back. 杰克小心地把杯子放在地上。 安妮帮他解开脖子上的红色天鹅绒斗篷。 然后她把斗篷披在鹿的背上。

“To keep you warm and safe,” she whispered to him. “And thanks for everything.” “让你暖和又安全，”她轻声对他说。 “谢谢你做的一切。”

“Yeah, thanks,” said Jack. “Good-bye.” “是啊，谢谢你，”杰克说。 “再见。”

The white stag stared at them with his mysterious amber eyes. He nodded once. Then he turned and headed into the darkness. 那只白鹿用神秘的琥珀色眼睛盯着他们。他点了点头。 然后他转身走进了黑暗中。

Jack picked up the cup. “Come on!” he said. He started walking quickly through the outer courtyard. 杰克捡起了杯子。 “快点！”他说。 他开始快速穿过外院。

“Careful, careful!” said Annie. “小心点，小心点！”安妮说。

“I got it, I got it,” said Jack. “我找到了，我找到了，”杰克说。

They crossed the drawbridge to the inner courtyard of the castle. Then they pushed open the giant arched doors. 他们穿过吊桥，来到城堡的内院。 然后他们推开了巨大的拱形门。

The great hall was just as they had left it—dimly lit and freezing cold. King Arthur, Queen Guinevere, the Knights of the Round Table, and Morgan le Fay were all still frozen and silent. 大厅和他们离开时一模一样——昏暗而寒冷。 亚瑟王、桂妮薇尔王后、圆桌骑士以及摩根勒菲都仍然被冻结着，一动不动。

“What do we do now?” said Jack. “我们现在该怎么办呢？”杰克说。

“Let’s try putting a drop of water on each of them! Morgan first!” said Annie. “我们试试在每个上面滴一滴水吧！ “摩根先！”安妮说。

“Okay,” said Jack. “Come on.” “好吧，”杰克说。 “快点。”

Holding his breath and keeping his eyes on the cup, Jack walked carefully toward the Round Table. Suddenly, his left foot stepped on the shoelace of his right sneaker—and he stumbled. 杰克屏住呼吸，眼睛一直盯着杯子，小心翼翼地朝圆桌走去。 突然，他的左脚踩到了右脚鞋的鞋带，他一下子绊倒了。

“Jack!” yelled Annie. “杰克！”安妮喊道。

Jack tried to regain his balance, but it was too late! As he fell to the floor, the silver cup slipped from his hands. 杰克努力想稳住身体，但已经太晚了！ 他摔倒在地时，银杯子从他手里滑落了。
