16. Welcome Home 16. 欢迎回家

“Wake up, Jack,” said Annie. “杰克，醒醒，”安妮说。

Jack opened his eyes. 杰克睁开了眼睛。

He was lying in the dark on the wooden floor of the tree house. Through the window, he saw the cloudy sky above the Frog Creek woods. 他躺在树屋的木地板上，周围一片漆黑。 透过窗户，他看见蛙溪镇的森林上空布满了乌云。

“Time to go home,” said Annie. “该回家了，”安妮说。

“Oh, I must have fallen asleep,” said Jack. “I had the most incredible dream. I dreamed that we went to Camelot. It was Christmas, and Merlin—” “哦，我一定是睡着了，”杰克说。 “我做了一个最不可思议的梦。 我梦见我们去了卡梅洛特。 那是圣诞节，梅林——”

“That wasn’t a dream,” said Annie. “It was real. You fell asleep at the Round Table during the party. King Arthur carried you to the tree house. And I made the wish for us to come home.” “那不是梦，”安妮说。 “那是真的。 你在圆桌派对上睡着了。 亚瑟王把你带到了树屋。 我和我们一起许愿回家。”

Jack sat up. 杰克坐了起来。

“Seriously?” he whispered. “真的吗？”他小声说。

“Seriously,” she said. “真的吗，”她说。

“Ja-ack! An-nie!” their mom called in the distance. “杰-克！ 安-妮！”，他们的妈妈在远处喊道。

“Coming!” Annie shouted out the tree house window. She turned to Jack. “Let’s go!” “来了！”，安妮从树屋的窗户里喊道。 她转向杰克。 “我们走吧！”

“I mean—seriously? It really happened?” Jack asked Annie again. “我是说——真的吗？ “真的发生了？”杰克又问安妮。

“Yes! Really!” she said. She held up the Royal Invitation. “See? Proof.” “是的！ 真的！”她说。 她举起了皇家邀请函。 “看？证据。”

“Oh … yeah,” he whispered. “哦……对，”他小声说。

“This time, the letter M stood for Merlin, not Morgan,” said Annie. “这次，字母M代表梅林，而不是摩根，”安妮说。

Jack smiled. 杰克笑了。

“Thanks, Merlin,” he said softly. “谢谢，梅林，”他轻声说道。

Jack picked up his backpack. Then he and Annie started down the rope ladder and headed home. As they walked through the deep December twilight, snowflakes started to fall. 杰克捡起了他的背包。 然后他和安妮开始沿着绳梯往下爬，朝家走去。 当他们穿过深冬十二月的暮色时，雪花开始飘落。

By the time they left the woods and headed down their street, snow was swirling everywhere. Ahead they could see their house glowing with lamplight. Their mom was waiting on their front porch. 等他们离开树林，沿着街道走回家时，雪片已经在四处飞舞了。 前方，他们能看到自己的房子被灯光照得通亮。 他们的妈妈正等着他们，站在前门廊上。

“Hi, Mom!” said Annie. “妈妈好！”安妮说道。

“Hi, Mom!” said Jack. “妈妈好！”杰克说道。

“Hi, kids. Did you have a good day?” she asked. “孩子们好。” “你今天过得愉快吗？”她问。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “是的，”杰克说。

“Pretty good,” said Annie. “还不错，”安妮说。

“I’m glad,” said their mom. “Welcome home.” She held the door open, and Jack and Annie slipped inside. “我很高兴，”他们的妈妈说。 “欢迎回家。”她把门打开，杰克和安妮溜了进去。

Their house felt extra warm and cozy. Good smells came from the kitchen. Jack and Annie took off their snow-covered jackets, then headed up the stairs. 他们的家感觉特别温暖舒适。 厨房里飘来好闻的味道。 杰克和安妮脱下沾满雪的外套，然后走上楼梯。

In the hallway, Annie turned to Jack. “Merry Christmas,” she said simply. 在走廊里，安妮转向杰克。 “圣诞快乐，”她简单地说。

“Merry Christmas,” he said. “圣诞快乐，”他说。

Annie slipped into her room, and Jack into his. 安妮溜进了她的房间，杰克则进了他的房间。

Jack closed his door and sat on his bed. He took his notebook out of his pack and opened it. His spirits sank. Except for the three rhymes, he’d taken no notes on their journey—not one. 杰克关上门，坐在床上。 他从背包里拿出笔记本，打开它。他的心情一下子低落下来。 除了那三句押韵的诗，他一路上根本没有记下任何笔记，一条都没有。

Exhausted, Jack lay back on his bed. He squeezed his eyes shut. He tried to remember the details of their adventures in Camelot and the Otherworld. 筋疲力尽的杰克，倒在床上躺下。 他紧紧闭上眼睛。 他努力回忆他们在卡梅洛特和异世界冒险的细节。

He could feel the terrible chill in the great hall when Morgan was frozen. He could hear the joyful music as the winged dancers danced around in their circle. He could taste the sweet, bitter, spicy taste of the Water of Memory and Imagination. 他能感觉到大殿里那可怕的寒冷，当时摩根被冻住时的场景。 他能听到翼舞者们在圆圈里跳舞时那欢快的音乐。 他能尝到记忆与想象之水又甜又苦又辣的味道。

Jack sat up. All at once, he felt very awake. He turned to a clean page in his notebook. He grabbed his pencil and wrote:



 杰克坐了起来。 突然间，他感觉非常清醒。 他翻到笔记本上一页干净的纸。 他拿起铅笔，开始写：

图中文字：It all started when we saw the white

dove in the twilight.... 图中文字：这一切都始于我们在暮色中看到那只白鸽……

Using his memory and his imagination, Jack kept writing, doing his part to keep the legend of King Arthur, the Knights of the Round Table, Merlin, and Morgan le Fay alive. 杰克一边回忆，一边想象，继续写下去，努力让亚瑟王、圆桌骑士、梅林和摩根勒菲的传奇故事继续流传下去。

As the snow swirled outside his window, Jack wrote and wrote and wrote. He didn’t stop writing until he had written down the whole story— his story of their Christmas in Camelot. 窗外的雪花纷纷扬扬，杰克一直写啊写啊写啊。 直到他把整个故事都写下来——他和他们在卡梅洛特过圣诞节的故事。
