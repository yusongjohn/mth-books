9. The Lost Knights 9. 迷失的骑士

Annie chased Jack around the circle. 安妮在圆圈里追着杰克跑。

“Stop, Jack!” she cried. “Stop!” She grabbed his shirt and tried to pull him out of the dance. “停下，杰克！”她喊道。 “停下！”她抓住他的衬衫，试图把他从舞蹈中拉出来。

“Let go, Annie!” he said. “Leave me alone!” “放开我，安妮！”他说。 “别管我！”

But Annie wouldn’t let go. Finally, she pulled so hard that Jack broke hands with the dancers and tumbled backward into the grass. 但安妮不肯放开。 最后，她用力一拉，杰克和舞者们的手松开了，他向后跌倒在草地上。

The winged dancers didn’t seem to notice. They closed their circle and kept going around and around. 那些有翅膀的舞者似乎没有注意到。 他们围成一圈，继续转来转去。

“Why did you do that?” said Jack, sitting up. “I was having fun!” “你为什么这么做？”杰克坐起来问。 “我在玩得正开心呢！”

“Look at the knights!” said Annie. “See them?” “看那些骑士！”安妮说。 “看到了吗？”，

Jack still couldn’t see. The world was spinning before his eyes. He ached to get back into the dance. 杰克还是看不见。 世界在他眼前旋转着。 他很想回到舞会上。

“Here, I found your glasses!” said Annie. “Put them on!” “喂，我找到你的眼镜了！”，安妮说。 “戴上吧！”，

Jack put on his glasses. He peered at the circle of dancers. He caught sight of armor glinting in the sunlight. He saw three knights dancing in a row. Two of them looked very young. The third looked much older. 杰克戴上了眼镜。 他仔细看着一圈舞者。 他看见阳光下闪闪发光的盔甲。 他看到三个骑士排着队跳舞。 其中两个看起来非常年轻。 第三个看起来要年长得多。

As they came closer, Jack saw their faces. All the joy of the music drained out of him. The knights looked tired and sick. Their hair and beards were long and scraggly. Their faces were bony and pale. Their eyes stared wildly and their lips were frozen in ghostly smiles. 当他们走近时，杰克看到了他们的脸。 音乐带来的所有快乐都从他身上消失了。 骑士们看起来又累又病恹恹的。 他们的头发和胡须又长又乱糟糟的。 他们的脸又瘦又苍白。 他们的眼睛疯狂地盯着，嘴唇凝固在可怕的微笑上。

“What’s wrong with them?” asked Jack. “他们怎么了？”杰克问。

“They can’t stop dancing!” said Annie. “They’re dancing themselves to death!” “他们跳个不停！”安妮说。 “他们跳得连命都要没了！”

“They must be the lost knights from Camelot,” said Jack. “他们一定是来自卡梅洛特的迷路骑士，”杰克说。

“We have to save them!” said Annie. “我们必须救他们！”安妮说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. He tried to clear his mind and think. “What about this? We get back in the dance—and we take places between the dancers and the knights.” “对，”杰克说。 他努力让自己冷静下来，好好想想。 “那这个呢？ 我们再回到舞蹈中，然后在舞者和骑士之间站好位置。”

“Yes! Then we can pull the knights out of the circle!” said Annie. “对！ 这样我们就能把骑士从圈子里拉出来！”安妮说。

“Wait,” said Jack. “What if I can’t stop dancing again?” “等等，”杰克说。 “万一我再次跳不停了怎么办？”

“Just don’t let yourself get caught by the music,” said Annie. “You have to think about something else. Think about why we’re here. Think about Morgan.” “只要别让自己被音乐抓住就行，”安妮说。 “你得想想别的事情。 想想我们为什么来到这里。 想想摩根。”

“Okay,” said Jack. “I’ll try.” “好的，”杰克说。 “我会试试的。”

Jack and Annie crouched in the grass. They watched and waited as the knights danced closer … and closer … and closer.… 杰克和安妮蹲在草丛里。 他们看着骑士们越跳越近……越来越近……越来越近……。

“Now!” shouted Annie. “现在！”安妮喊道。

Jack and Annie rushed forward. They broke into the circle on either side of the knights. As Jack started dancing, his feet seemed to fly to the beat of the drum. He felt a wave of great joy. His worries left him. 杰克和安妮冲了上去。 他们从骑士的两边冲进圆圈里。 当杰克开始跳舞时，他的脚似乎随着鼓点飞快地跳动。 他感到一阵巨大的快乐。 他的烦恼都离开了他。

“Now, Jack!” cried Annie. “Pull away!” “杰克，现在！”安妮喊道。 “拉开距离！”

But Jack didn’t want to pull away. The music rang in his ears. Nothing mattered except the dancing. 但杰克不想拉开距离。 音乐在他耳边回响。 除了跳舞，其他什么都不重要了。

“Jack! Pull away NOW!” Annie shouted again. “杰克！ “快躲开！”安妮又喊了一遍。

Jack shook his head, trying to shake off Annie’s voice. 杰克摇了摇头，试图甩掉安妮的声音。

“Morgan! Morgan!” Annie yelled. “摩根！ “摩根！”安妮喊道。

The word Morgan made Jack stumble a bit in the dance. 听到“摩根”这个名字，杰克在跳舞时绊了一下。

“Morgan! Morgan!” Annie shouted. “摩根！ “摩根！”安妮大喊。

Jack stumbled again. Then he used all his might to stop himself from dancing. He let go of the hand of the dancer on his right and threw himself out of the dance—pulling the knight on his left with him. Annie and the other two knights tumbled back with them onto the grass. 杰克又绊了一下。 然后他用尽全力，才没跳起舞来。 他松开了右边舞者的手，把自己从舞圈里甩了出去——还把左边的骑士也带了出去。 安妮和其他两位骑士被他们拉回草地上，摔了个跟头。

Just as before, the dancers didn’t seem to notice. They closed their circle and kept going round and round in their joyous, timeless dance. 和之前一样，跳舞的人好像根本没有注意到。 他们又围成一个圈，继续跳着那欢乐又无尽的舞蹈。
