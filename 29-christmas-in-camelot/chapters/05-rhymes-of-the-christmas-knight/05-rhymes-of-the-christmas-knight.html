5. Rhymes of the Christmas Knight 5. 圣诞骑士的韵律

“Morgan?” said Annie. “摩根？”安妮说。

Annie ran to the table. She touched Morgan’s cheek, then quickly pulled back her hand. 安妮跑到桌边。 她碰了碰摩根的脸颊，然后迅速缩回了手。

“She’s cold. She’s as cold as ice!” said Annie. Tears filled her eyes. “她冷得像冰一样！”安妮说。 她的眼睛充满了泪水。

Annie turned to the Christmas Knight in a fury. “What did you do to Morgan?” she asked. “Bring her back!” 安妮怒气冲冲地转向圣诞骑士。 “你对摩根做了什么？”她问。 “把她带回来！”

“Do not fear,” said the Christmas Knight. His voice was softer and kinder. “She will come back to life after you complete your quest.” “不要害怕，”圣诞骑士说。 他的声音变得温柔而亲切。 “她会在你们完成任务后重新获得生命。”

“What—what exactly is our quest?” said Jack. “什——什么 exactly 是我们的任务？”杰克说道。

“You must journey to the Otherworld,” said the Christmas Knight. “There you will find a cauldron. The cauldron is filled with the Water of Memory and Imagination. You must bring a cup of the water back to Camelot. If you fail, Camelot will never come back to life. Never.” “你们必须前往异世界，”圣诞骑士说道。 “在那里你们会找到一口大锅。 这口锅里装满了记忆与想象之水。 你们必须带回一杯水回到卡梅洛特。 如果你们失败了，卡梅洛特将永远无法重获生命。永远。”

“How do we do all that?” asked Annie, wiping her eyes. “我们该怎么做到这些？”安妮一边擦着眼睛一边问道。

“Remember these three rhymes,” said the Christmas Knight. “记住这三句押韵的句子，”圣诞骑士说道。

“Wait, let me write them down,” said Jack. “等一下，让我把它们写下来，”杰克说道。

His hands trembled as he pulled out his notebook and pencil. He looked at the Christmas Knight. 他的手在颤抖，因为他拿出了笔记本和铅笔。 他看着圣诞骑士。

“Okay, I’m ready,” he said. Gripping his pencil made Jack feel stronger. “好吧，我准备好了，”他说。 握紧铅笔让杰克感觉更强壮了。

The knight’s voice rang out from inside his helmet. 骑士的声音从头盔里传了出来。

“Beyond the iron gate “穿过铁门

The Keepers of the Cauldron wait.” 守护者正在等待。”

Jack quickly wrote down the knight’s words. “Okay, what’s next?” he asked. 杰克迅速写下骑士的话。 “好吧，接下来是什么？”他问。

The Christmas Knight went on: 圣诞骑士继续说道：

“Four gifts you will need— “你需要四件礼物——

The first from me. 第一件来自我。

Then a cup, a compass, 然后是一只杯子、一个指南针。

And, finally, a key.” 最后是一把钥匙。”

“Cup … compass … key … . Got it,” said Jack. “杯子……指南针……钥匙…… 记住了，”杰克说。

The Christmas Knight’s voice boomed again: 圣诞骑士的声音再次响起：

“If you survive to complete your quest, “如果你能活下来完成你的任务。

The secret door lies to the west.” 秘密的门就在西边。”

Jack copied down the last rhyme, then looked up at the knight. 杰克记下了最后的那句押韵诗，然后抬头看向骑士。

“Anything else?” he asked. “还有别的吗？”他问。

Without a word, the knight pulled off his red cloak. He dropped it to the floor. It fell silently into a heap at Jack and Annie’s feet. 骑士没有说话，摘下了他的红色斗篷。 他把它扔在了地上。 它悄无声息地堆在杰克和安妮的脚边。

The Christmas Knight snapped his horse’s red reins, then galloped out of the great hall. 圣诞骑士抽了抽他马的红色缰绳，然后策马离开了大厅。
