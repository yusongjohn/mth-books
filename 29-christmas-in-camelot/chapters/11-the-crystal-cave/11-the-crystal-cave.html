11. The Crystal Cave 11. 水晶洞穴

“T he secret door!” whispered Jack. “秘密的门！”杰克小声说。

“Yes!” said Annie. “对！”安妮说。

Jack dropped the compass into his pack. Then he and Annie scrambled through the bushes and climbed up the rocks to the door. 杰克把指南针放进背包里。 然后他和安妮爬过灌木丛，沿着岩石爬到那扇门前。

Annie took Sir Lancelot’s glass key from around her neck. She slipped the key into the keyhole. She turned the key slowly. 安妮从脖子上取下兰斯洛特爵士的玻璃钥匙。 她把钥匙插进锁孔里。 她慢慢地转动钥匙。

Clink. 咔嗒。

“Yippee,” Annie said softly. She pushed open the door. “太棒了，”安妮轻声说。 她推开门。

Beyond the door was a huge, glittering cave. The floor, walls, and ceiling were made of clear crystal. 门后是一个巨大而闪闪发光的洞穴。 地板、墙壁和天花板都是由透明的水晶制成的。

Jack and Annie stepped inside. The cave was filled with dancing streams of purple light. 杰克和安妮走进了洞穴。 洞穴里充满了紫色光芒的溪流。

“It’s so bright!” whispered Jack. “Where’s all the purple light coming from?” “太亮了！”，杰克小声说。 “紫色的光是从哪儿来的？”

“There,” said Annie. She pointed to a crack on the far side of the cave. “Let’s look.” “那儿，”安妮说。 她指着洞穴远端的一条裂缝。 “我们去看看。”

They crossed the cave and peered through the crack into a room. Along the brilliant crystal walls of the room were four doorways. 他们穿过洞穴，透过裂缝望进了一间房间。 房间内闪亮的水晶墙上，有四个门。

In the far corner of the room was a fire. The fire blazed with leaping purple flames. Over the flames hung a gleaming golden cauldron. 房间远端的角落里，有一堆火。 火堆里跳动着紫色的火焰。 火焰上方挂着一个闪闪发光的金色大锅。

“There it is,” whispered Jack. “找到了，”杰克小声说。

“Wow,” whispered Annie. “哇，”安妮小声说。

“The cauldron with the Water of Memory and Imagination,” whispered Jack. “装有记忆与想象之水的坩埚，”杰克小声说。

“I know,” whispered Annie. “Let’s go!” “我知道，”安妮小声说。 “我们走吧！”

They squeezed through the crack, then walked toward the gleaming cauldron. Jack reached into his pack and pulled out Sir Galahad’s silver cup. 他们挤过裂缝，然后朝闪闪发光的坩埚走去。 杰克从背包里拿出圣加拉哈德的银杯子。

“The cauldron’s too high,” said Annie. “We can’t get to the water.” “锅太高了，”安妮说。 “我们够不到水。”

“Here, take this,” said Jack, handing her the cup. “Climb on my back.” “来，拿着这个，”杰克把杯子递给她。 “爬到我背上。”

He bent over, and Annie climbed on piggyback. Jack stood up shakily. “Hurry!” he said. “You’re heavy.” 他弯下腰，安妮爬到他背上。 杰克摇摇晃晃地站了起来。 “快点！”，他说。 “你太重了。”

“I can’t reach it,” said Annie. “Move closer.” “我够不到，”安妮说。 “靠近一点。”

Jack staggered forward a few steps. Stretching as far as she could, Annie reached again. She skimmed water from the top of the bubbling cauldron, filling the silver cup. 杰克向前踉跄走了几步。 尽力伸长手臂，安妮再次伸手去够。 她从咕嘟咕嘟冒泡的坩埚顶部舀了一些水，装进了银杯里。

“Got it!” she whispered. “Now set me down. Slowly!” “拿到了！”，她小声说。 “现在把我放下来。慢慢来！”

Annie held the cup with both hands. Jack slowly bent his knees and Annie climbed carefully off his back. They stared silently for a moment at the Water of Memory and Imagination in the cup. It was clear and shimmering. 安妮用双手捧着杯子。 杰克慢慢弯曲膝盖，安妮小心地从他背上爬下来。 他们默默地盯着杯子里的回忆与想象之水看了片刻。 水清澈而闪闪发光。

“Now we can save Morgan,” said Annie. “现在我们可以救摩根了，”安妮说。

Just then Jack smelled something strange—it was like the smell of rotten seaweed. He heard weird gurgling sounds behind them. 就在这时，杰克闻到了一种奇怪的气味——就像是腐烂海藻的味道。 他听到身后传来奇怪的咕噜声。

He and Annie turned around. 他和安妮转过身。

A giant, slimy, mud-colored creature crawled out through one of the doorways. The creature was long and scaly like a crocodile but much, MUCH bigger. It had wings that looked as if they’d been spun from a thousand spiderwebs. It had glowing red eyes and long, curled claws. 一只又大又滑、呈泥褐色的怪物从其中一个门洞里爬了出来。 这个生物又长又满是鳞片，像鳄鱼一样，但大得多，多得多。 它的翅膀看起来像是由一千张蜘蛛网编织而成的。 它有发着红光的眼睛和又长又弯的爪子。

The creature opened its huge jaws. Strands of drool dripped from its sharp, pointed teeth. The creature hissed and hot blue flame shot from its mouth. 这个生物张开了它巨大的嘴巴。 口水从它尖尖的牙齿上滴落下来。 这个生物嘶嘶作响，嘴里喷出了炽热的蓝色火焰。

Another monster crawled through a different doorway, quickly followed by a third, and then a fourth. 另一个怪物从另一个门洞爬进来，紧接着第三个、第四个也跟了进来。

“Yikes!” said Annie. “天哪！”安妮说道。

“The real Keepers of the Cauldron … ,” whispered Jack. “真正的坩埚守护者……”，杰克小声说。
