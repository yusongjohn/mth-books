7. Arm Exercises 7. 手臂操练

Priscilla led Jack and Annie away from the village toward a large field. The Pilgrims and Wampanoag men had already gathered there. 普丽西拉领着杰克和安妮离开村子，走向一片大田地。 清教徒和万帕诺亚格男人已经聚集在那儿。

Jack could hear the beat of a drum. But he couldn’t see what was going on. 杰克能听见鼓声。 可他看不见在发生什么。

“Make haste or we will miss it!” said Priscilla. “赶紧，不然我们就错过了！”普丽西拉说。

“Miss what?” asked Annie. “错过什么？”安妮问。

“Captain Standish is about to lead the men and boys,” said Priscilla. “They will exercise their arms.” “斯坦迪什船长就要带领男人和男孩们，”普丽西拉说。 “他们要操练手臂。”

Why do they exercise their arms? Jack wondered. Will they expect me to join in? 他们为什么要操练手臂？杰克想。 他们会指望我也参加吗？

As he hurried after Priscilla toward the crowd, Jack practiced. He stretched his arms out wide. He made circles in the air. Then he flapped his arms up and down. 杰克赶紧跟着普丽西拉朝人群走去，一边练习着。 他张开双臂。 他在空中画圈。 然后他上下扇动胳膊。

Priscilla caught sight of him. 普丽西拉看见了他。

“What art thou doing, Jack?” she asked. “你在做什么，杰克？”她问。

“Exercising my arms,” he said. “我在锻炼胳膊，”他说。

Priscilla smiled. Then she started to laugh. She laughed and laughed. 普丽西拉微笑了。 然后她开始笑。 她笑啊笑啊。

So did Jack, but he wasn’t sure why. 杰克也笑了，可他不确定为什么。

A loud BANG! came from the field. 一声巨大的砰！ 从田地里传来。

Jack jumped. He stopped laughing. 杰克跳了起来。 他不笑了。

A puff of smoke rose into the air. As the crowd parted, Jack saw the Pilgrim men and boys proudly holding up their long guns. 一缕烟升到空中。 人群分开时，杰克看见清教徒男人和男孩们骄傲地举着长枪。

“What just happened?” said Annie. “刚才发生了什么？”安妮说。

“The men fired their muskets,” said Priscilla. “On special occasions they like to show off their arms.” “男人们开火枪了，”普丽西拉说。 “在特殊场合，他们喜欢炫耀自己的武器。”

Oh! thought Jack. Now I get it! The long guns are muskets, which are also called arms. So “exercising arms” means firing muskets! 哦！杰克想。 现在我明白了！ 那些长枪是火枪，也叫兵器（arms）。 所以“操练手臂”其实是开火枪！

Jack blushed. Priscilla must think I’m an idiot, he thought. 杰克脸红了。 普丽西拉一定觉得我是个傻瓜，他想。

But she just smiled at him fondly. 可她只是亲切地对他笑了笑。

“I thank thee for making me laugh, Jack,” she said. “I have not laughed hard in a long time.” “谢谢你让我发笑，杰克，”她说。 “我好久没有这样大笑过了。”

Jack shrugged, as if he had meant to make her laugh. 杰克耸耸肩，好像他本来就是故意逗她笑的。

“It is time now to serve our feast,” said Priscilla. “I must help with the bread.” “现在该端上我们的盛宴了，”普丽西拉说。 “我得去帮忙做面包。”

“What can we do?” asked Jack. “我们能做什么？”杰克问。

“Return to my home,” said Priscilla, “take the turkey off the spit, put it on a platter, and bring it to a table.” “回我家去，”普丽西拉说，“把火鸡从烤叉上取下，放到盘子上，端到桌上。”

“Oh, great, we get to help with the turkey!” said Annie. “I always help with the turkey at home.” “太好了，我们能帮忙弄火鸡！”安妮说。 “我在家也总是帮忙弄火鸡。”

“Good,” said Priscilla. “May thou feel my home is thy home today.” “好，”普丽西拉说。 “愿你们今日把我家当作自己的家。”

Jack was excited, too. He and Annie were about to serve the first turkey at the first Thanksgiving! They ran back to the smoky house and rushed inside. 杰克也很兴奋。 他和安妮马上就要在第一个感恩节端上第一只火鸡了！ 他们跑回那间烟雾腾腾的屋子，冲了进去。

“Where’s the platter?” said Jack, looking around. He saw a flat wooden block. “That must be it.” “盘子在哪儿？”杰克四处看着说。 他看见一块扁平的木板。 “一定是那个。”

Annie picked up the wooden platter. “How do we get the turkey on it?” she asked. 安妮拿起了那个木盘子。 “我们怎么把火鸡弄上去？”她问。

They moved close to the fire and stared at the turkey roasting on the iron rod. 他们靠近火，盯着铁杆上烤着的火鸡。

“That must be the spit,” said Jack. The spit sat on iron legs. It had a handle. “那一定是烤叉，”杰克说。 烤叉架在铁腿上。它有个把手。

Jack pushed his glasses into place. “I’ll lift the spit,” he said. “Then we’ll push the turkey onto the platter.” 杰克把眼镜推正。 “我来抬烤肉叉，”他说。 “然后我们把火鸡推到盘子上。”

“Be careful,” said Annie. “小心点，”安妮说。

Jack reached out and wrapped his fingers around the handle of the iron spit. 杰克伸出手，抓住铁烤叉的把手。

“OWW!” he shouted. The handle was super hot! He yanked his hand away and knocked the spit off its legs. “哎哟！”他大叫。 把手烫极了！ 他猛地抽回手，把烤肉叉从支架上撞了下来。

The turkey fell into the fire. The grease from the turkey sputtered and popped. The turkey burst into flame! The fire roared! 火鸡掉进了火里。 火鸡上的油脂噼啪作响，爆出小火花。 火鸡突然着火了！火呼呼地吼起来！

“AHH!” yelped Jack and Annie together. They jumped back from the hearth. “啊！”杰克和安妮一起大叫。 他们从炉边往后跳开。

Jack grabbed the water pot on the floor. He threw the water into the fire. The fire sizzled and smoke billowed up. When the smoke cleared, the fire was out. 杰克抓起地上的水锅。 他把水泼进火里。 火滋滋作响，烟雾涌了起来。 烟散开后，火灭了。

But the turkey was completely black. 可火鸡完全烧黑了。
