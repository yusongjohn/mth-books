10. Thankful 10. 感恩

Jack opened his eyes. He sighed. They were wearing their own clothes again. His leather bag was a backpack. 杰克睁开眼睛。他叹了口气。 他们又穿上了自己的衣服。 他的皮袋子又变成了背包。

Sunlight slanted through the tree house window. As always, no time at all had passed in Frog Creek. 阳光斜斜地照进树屋的窗户。 和往常一样，蛙溪一点儿时间都没过去。

“Home,” said Annie. She held up the pouch of corn seeds. “Proof for Morgan we found a special magic.” “回家了，”安妮说。 她举起那袋玉米种子。 “给摩根的证据，证明我们找到了一种特别的魔法。”

“The magic of community,” said Jack. “共同体的魔法。

Annie placed the pouch on the floor—next to the scrolls from Shakespeare and the twig from the gorillas of the cloud forest. 安妮把小袋子放在地板上——旁边是莎士比亚的卷轴，还有云雾林大猩猩那儿带回的小树枝。

“Let’s go,” she said. “走吧，”她说。

Jack took the research book out of his pack. He left it under the window. Then they climbed down the rope ladder. 杰克从背包里取出研究书。 他把它留在窗下。 然后他们顺着绳梯爬下去。

As they started through the woods, a warm wind blew, rattling the leaves. Jack felt happy. He was looking forward to visiting their grandmother today and seeing their cousins and aunts and uncles. 他们走进树林时，一阵暖风吹来，树叶嘎嘎作响。杰克觉得很开心。 他期待着今天去看望奶奶，还想见见表兄弟姐妹和叔叔阿姨们。

“You know, Pilgrim kids had a really hard life,” said Annie. “你知道吗，清教徒小孩的生活真的好苦，”安妮说。

“Yeah. They did as much work as the grown-ups,” said Jack. “Maybe more.” “是啊。 他们干的活跟大人一样多，”杰克说。 “也许更苦。”

“Worst of all, lots of their friends and family members died,” said Annie. “最糟糕的是，他们许多朋友和家人都去世了，”安妮说。

“Yeah,” said Jack. “是啊，”杰克说。

Both were silent for a moment. 两人都沉默了一会儿。

“If they could be so thankful,” said Annie, “we should be really thankful.” “如果他们都能那么感恩，”安妮说，“我们就更该好好感恩了。”

“No kidding,” said Jack. “Really, really thankful.” “可不是嘛，”杰克说。 “真的、真的很感激。”

And they were. 给杰克、安妮和你的更多事实！ 他们是打猎、捕鱼和种植的专家。
