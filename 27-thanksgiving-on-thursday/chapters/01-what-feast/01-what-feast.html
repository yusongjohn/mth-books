1. What Feast? 1. 什么宴会？

“Come on,” said Annie. She stood in the doorway to Jack’s bedroom. “Let’s check the woods.” “快点，”安妮说。 她站在杰克卧室的门口。 “我们去林子里看看吧。”

“But it’s Thursday,” said Jack. “We’re going to Grandmother’s soon.” “可今天是星期四啊，”杰克说。 “我们很快就要去奶奶家了。”

“I know,” said Annie. “But I have a feeling the tree house might be back. I think Morgan might have sent us a new rhyme.” “我知道，”安妮说。 “可我有种感觉，树屋可能又回来了。 我想摩根也许给我们送来了一首新韵诗。”

Jack trusted Annie’s feelings. 杰克相信安妮的感觉。

“Okay, but we’ll have to be quick,” he said. He threw his notebook and pencil into his backpack. He followed her downstairs. “好吧，不过我们得快点，”他说。 他把笔记本和铅笔塞进背包。 他跟着她下了楼。

“Be back soon!” Jack called to their parents. “很快就回来！”杰克朝父母喊道。

“Very soon!” their dad said. “很快！”爸爸说。

“Don’t forget—it’s Thursday,” said their mom. “We’re leaving for Grandmother’s at nine!” “别忘了——今天是星期四，”妈妈说。 “我们九点出发去奶奶家！”

“I know!” said Jack. “我知道！”杰克说。

“We’ll be back in ten minutes!” said Annie. “我们十分钟就回来！”安妮说。

They hurried out of their house. They ran across their yard and up their street and into the Frog Creek woods. 他们匆忙从家里跑了出来。 他们跑过院子，沿着街道，跑进了蛙溪树林。

Jack and Annie ran through light and shadow, until they stopped under the tallest oak. 杰克和安妮在光影间奔跑，直到在最高的那棵橡树下停住。

“Yay!” said Annie. “耶！”安妮说。

“You were right!” said Jack. “你说对了！”杰克说。

High in the tree was the magic tree house. 高高的树上，魔法树屋就在那儿。

Jack grabbed the rope ladder and started up. Annie was right behind him. 杰克抓住绳梯往上爬。 安妮紧跟在他后面。

They climbed into the tree house. Sunlight slanted in through the window. 他们爬进了树屋。 阳光斜斜地从窗户照进来。

“Good, our gifts from our last trips are still here,” said Annie. “很好，我们上次旅行带回来的礼物还在这儿，”安妮说。

She pointed to the scrolls from Shakespeare’s theater and the twig from the gorillas. 她指着莎士比亚剧院带回的卷轴，还有大猩猩那儿带回的小树枝。

“Proof we found the magic of the theater and the magic of animals,” said Jack. “这证明我们发现了戏剧的魔法和动物的魔法，”杰克说。

“Look,” said Annie. She pointed to a book lying in a dark corner. A piece of paper was sticking out of it. “看，”安妮说。 她指着一本放在黑暗角落里的书。 一张纸从里面露了出来。

Jack pulled out the paper. 杰克把纸抽了出来。

“It’s from Morgan,” he said. “是摩根写的，”他说。

He read: 他读道：

Dear Jack and Annie, 亲爱的杰克和安妮。

Good luck on your third journey to find a special magic. This rhyme will guide you:



 祝你们第三次寻找特殊魔法的旅程好运。 这首韵诗会指引你们：

To find a special magic, 祝你们第三次寻找特殊魔法的旅程好运。

When work and toil are done, 当工作和辛劳都做完的时候。

Gather all together, 大家聚在一起。

Turn three worlds into one.



 把三个世界变成一个。

Thank you,

 Morgan

 谢谢你们，摩根

“So who do we gather with?” wondered Jack. “那我们要和谁聚在一起呢？”杰克想。

Annie held up the book. The painting on the cover showed a basket of corn on a wooden table. The title said A Feast to Remember. 安妮举起那本书。 封面上的画是木桌上的一篮玉米。 书名写着《难忘的盛宴》。

“We gather at a feast,” she said. She pointed to the cover. “I wish we could go there.” “我们聚在一起享用盛宴，”她说。 她指着封面。 “但愿我们能去那儿。”

“Hold on,” said Jack. “What kind of feast? Where and when?” “等一下，”杰克说。 “什么样的宴会？在哪儿？什么时候？”

But the wind had started to blow. 可风已经开始刮了起来。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 它转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely still. 完全静止了。
