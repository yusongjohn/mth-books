4. We Fish! 4. 我们捕鱼！

Annie grinned. “Good day, Squanto!” she said. 安妮咧嘴笑了。 “日安，斯匡托！”她说。

“Good day, Annie,” said Squanto. He smiled at her and Jack. “日安，安妮，”斯匡托说。 他对着她和杰克微笑。

Jack was too surprised to speak. Why did Squanto say he remembers us? he wondered. Is he mistaking us for two other kids? 杰克惊讶得说不出话。 斯匡托为什么说他记得我们？他想。 他把我们当成另外两个孩子了吗？

Captain Standish looked surprised, too. But Governor Bradford smiled warmly. 斯坦迪什船长看起来也很吃惊。 可总督布拉德福德温和地笑了。

“ ’Tis a wonder,” he said. “We welcome all the small folk sent to us. Children are a gift from God—no matter where they come from.” “真是奇事，”他说。 “我们欢迎所有派来给我们的小孩子。 孩子是上帝的礼物——无论他们来自何处。”

That’s a nice way of looking at things, Jack thought. 这真是看待事情的好方式，杰克想。

Just then, a boy ran up. “Chief Massasoit is here with ninety men!” he shouted. 就在这时，一个男孩跑了过来。 “马萨索伊特酋长带着九十个人来了！”他喊道。

The boy pointed to a long line of men walking down a path near a cornfield. 那个男孩指着一长队走在玉米地旁小路上的男人。

Chief Massasoit walked ahead of the others. His face was painted red. He wore a fur robe and white beads. 马萨索伊特酋长走在其他人前面。 他的脸涂成了红色。 他穿着毛皮袍，戴着白珠子。

Governor Bradford, Captain Standish, and Squanto went to meet the visitors. 总督布拉德福德、斯坦迪什船长和斯匡托前去迎接来客。

“Mercy!” a Pilgrim woman whispered. “天哪！”一位清教徒妇人低声说。

All the Pilgrims looked worried. 所有清教徒都露出担心的神色。

“Art thou afraid?” Annie asked. “你害怕吗？”安妮问。

“Oh, no,” said Priscilla. “We invited Chief Massasoit and his men to our harvest feast. But we did not expect so many. We have not prepared enough food.” “哦，不，”普丽西拉说。 “我们邀请了马萨索伊特酋长和他的人来参加我们的丰收盛宴。 可我们没想到会来这么多人。 我们准备的食物不够。”

Governor Bradford and Squanto spoke to the chief. Then Squanto led a number of men into the woods. And the governor walked back to the Pilgrims. 总督布拉德福德和斯匡托与酋长交谈。 然后斯匡托带着一些男人进了树林。 总督走回清教徒这边。

“The Wampanoag men will hunt more deer,” he said. “But we must also bring more food to the table. Priscilla, please tell the young folk what they must do.” “万帕诺亚格人会再去猎鹿，”他说。 “可我们也得把更多食物摆上桌。 普丽西拉，请告诉年轻人他们该做什么。”

The grown-ups went back to the village as the Pilgrim kids gathered around Priscilla. She told some to carry water or set up tables. She told others to gather vegetables or hunt small animals. 大人们回到村子里，清教徒孩子们围到了普丽西拉身边。 她叫有的人去打水，有的人去摆桌子。 她吩咐其他人去采蔬菜或猎小动物。

Once the kids were given their jobs, they rushed off to do them. Finally only Jack, Annie, and a small girl holding a big basket were left. 孩子们一领到任务，就赶快去做了。 最后只剩下杰克、安妮，还有一个抱着大篮子的小女孩。

“Jack, would thou like to go fowling with the boys?” Priscilla asked him. She pointed to a group of boys who had just headed off with the dog. “杰克，你可愿与男孩们一起去打水鸟？”普丽西拉问他。 她指着刚带着狗出发的一群男孩。

Jack stared at her in panic. What does she mean? he wondered. 杰克惊慌地盯着她。 她是什么意思？他想。

“What’s ‘fowling’?” Annie asked. “‘捕鸟’是什么意思？”安妮问。

“Thou does not know?” said the little girl. “ ’ Tis hunting water birds, of course.” “你不知道吗？”小女孩说。 “那当然是猎水鸟啦。”

“Jack doesn’t know how to do that,” said Annie. “杰克不会做那个，”安妮说。

“ ’Tis true? How does thou eat and live?” the little girl asked curiously. “当真？ 那你们怎么吃、怎么活？”小女孩好奇地问。

“We, uh … ” Jack froze. “我们，呃……”杰克呆住了。

“We catch—fish!” said Annie. “我们捕——鱼！”安妮说。

We do? thought Jack. 我们会吗？杰克想。

“Ah, good!” said Priscilla. “Then I bid thee bring back as many eels and clams as thou can. We have near one hundred fifty mouths to feed.” Priscilla took the basket from the small girl and gave it to Annie. “啊，好！”普丽西拉说。 “那我就吩咐你，尽量多带些鳗鱼和蛤蜊回来。 我们差不多有一百五十张嘴要养活。”普丽西拉从小女孩手里接过篮子，递给了安妮。

“We will see thee later!” Priscilla said, waving. “Mary and I must go help with the cooking.” “我们稍后再见！”普丽西拉挥着手说。 “玛丽和我得去帮忙做饭了。”

“Um … ?” said Jack. “嗯……？”杰克说。

But before he could ask any questions, Priscilla and the little girl started back to the village. 可他还没来得及问问题，普丽西拉和小女孩就朝村子走回去了。
