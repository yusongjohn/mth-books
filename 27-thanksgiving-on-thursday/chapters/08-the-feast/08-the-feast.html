8. The Feast 8. 宴会

Jack buried his face in his hands. 杰克把脸埋进双手里。

“I don’t believe it,” he said. “I just burned up the Pilgrims’ turkey!” “我不相信，”他说。 “我刚把清教徒的火鸡给烧了！”

“Stay calm,” said Annie. “I’ll get Priscilla.” “保持冷静，”安妮说。 “我去找普丽西拉。”

“No, don’t tell Priscilla,” moaned Jack. “不，别告诉普丽西拉，”杰克呻吟道。

“We have to tell Priscilla,” said Annie. “我们必须告诉普丽西拉，”安妮说。

She hurried out of the house. 她匆匆出了屋子。

Jack lifted his head and stared at the burned turkey. 杰克抬起头，盯着烧焦的火鸡。

“Oh, man,” he whispered unhappily. The Pilgrims had worked so hard to get their food. They had had such a terrible winter—especially Priscilla. And now he had ruined their first Thanksgiving! “天啊，”他难过地低声说。 清教徒们辛苦劳作才得到这些食物。 他们度过了一个那么糟糕的冬天——尤其是普丽西拉。 可现在他毁了他们的第一个感恩节！

The door opened. Annie pulled Priscilla over to the hearth. 门开了。 安妮把普丽西拉拉到炉边。

“See!” said Annie. “The turkey fell into the fire! It burned up!” “看！”安妮说。 “火鸡掉进火里了！烧焦了！”

“I did it,” Jack confessed. “是我干的，”杰克承认道。

Priscilla just stared at the burned turkey in the wet, messy hearth. Then she looked at Jack. He looked away from her. 普丽西拉只是盯着又湿又乱的炉灶里那只烧焦的火鸡。 然后她看着杰克。 他目光躲开了。

“Ah, Jack,” Priscilla said softly. “Thou looks sad.” “啊，杰克，”普丽西拉轻声说。 “你看起来很难过。”

He nodded. 他点点头。

“I ruined everything,” he mumbled. “我把一切都毁了，”他嘟囔着。

“No, thou did not,” said Priscilla. She reached out her hand. “Come.” “不，你没有，”普丽西拉说。 她伸出手。 “来。”

Priscilla led Jack and Annie out into the bright autumn light. 普丽西拉领着杰克和安妮走到明亮的秋光里。

“Look,” she said. “看，”她说。

Jack saw Pilgrim women and kids walking to the tables. They all carried wooden platters piled with food. 杰克看见清教徒妇人和孩子们朝桌子走去。 他们都端着堆满食物的木盘。

“In the other houses, there was cooking also,” said Priscilla. “别的屋子里也在做饭，”普丽西拉说。

Jack saw roasted ducks, turkeys, and deer meat. He saw baked fish, lobsters, eels, clams, and oysters. 杰克看见烤鸭、火鸡和鹿肉。 他看见烤鱼、龙虾、鳗鱼、蛤蜊和牡蛎。

He saw pumpkins, beans and corn, dried plums, berries and roasted nuts, steamy pots of soups and puddings, and loaves of baked breads. 他看见南瓜、豆子和玉米，干李子、浆果和烤坚果，热气腾腾的汤锅和布丁，还有一条条烤面包。

“We had a very good harvest this fall,” said Priscilla. “We stored many vegetables. We salted our fish and cured our meat. And today, our Wampanoag neighbors brought back five deer from the forest for our feast.” “今年秋天我们收成很好，”普丽西拉说。 “我们储存了许多蔬菜。 我们用盐腌鱼，还把肉熏制储存起来。 今天，我们的万帕诺亚格邻居又从森林带回五头鹿，供我们宴席享用。”

Jack was relieved to see all the food. 看见这么多食物，杰克松了口气。

Priscilla knelt down and looked him in the eye. 普丽西拉跪下来，看着他的眼睛。

“See, thou did not ruin anything, Jack,” she said. “Thou and Annie have helped me a lot this day. You have both made me laugh. And you have both acted with kind hearts.” “看，你什么都没有毁掉，杰克，”她说。 “你和安妮今天帮了我很多。 你们俩都让我笑了。 你们俩都怀着善良的心。”

Jack was amazed. He thought he’d been no help at all. 杰克很惊讶。 他觉得自己一点儿忙也没帮上。

“Come,” said Priscilla. “Let us join the others. Art thou hungry?” “来，”普丽西拉说。 “让我们加入其他人吧。你们饿了吗？”

Jack nodded. Seeing all the platters of food had made him really hungry. 杰克点点头。 看见那么多盘食物，他真的饿了。

He and Annie followed Priscilla. 他和安妮跟着普丽西拉。

In the golden glow of autumn light, Jack and Annie joined the Pilgrims and the Wampanoag at the long tables. 在秋日金色的光辉里，杰克和安妮与清教徒、万帕诺亚格人一起坐到了长桌旁。

Priscilla gave Jack and Annie wooden plates. She gave them big white cloth napkins. Then she served them plenty of food. 普丽西拉给了杰克和安妮木盘。 她递给他们大大的白色餐巾布。 接着她给他们端上许多食物。

Before they started to eat, Governor Bradford stood up to speak. 他们开始吃之前，总督布拉德福德站起来讲话。

“Those of us who came here on the Mayflower did not know how to live in this land,” he said. “But Squanto came to help us. And today, we give thanks for him, and for the peace we share with his people, and for all our great blessings.” “我们这些乘五月花号来到这里的人，并不知道如何在这片土地上生活，”他说。 “可斯匡托来帮助了我们。 今天，我们为他感恩，为与他的族人共享的和平感恩，也为我们所有伟大的恩惠感恩。”

Governor Bradford looked at Jack and Annie. 总督布拉德福德看着杰克和安妮。

“Welcome to our feast,” he said. “At this moment, three worlds— your world, our world, and the world of the Wampanoag—are not three. They are one. ’Tis the magic of community.” “欢迎来到我们的盛宴，”他说。 “此刻，三个世界——你们的世界、我们的世界，和万帕诺亚格人的世界——不再是三个。它们是一个。 这就是共同体的魔法。”

“Indeed!” said Annie. She clapped her hands and looked at Jack. “We did it,” she whispered. “当真！”安妮说。 她拍着手，看着杰克。 “我们做到了，”她小声说。

Did what? thought Jack. 做到了什么？杰克想。

Governor Bradford then put his napkin over his shoulder. 总督布拉德福德然后把餐巾搭在肩上。

“Now!” he said. “Let us feast till our bellies are filled!” “现在！”他说。 “让我们大吃一顿，直到肚子填满！”

As everyone started to eat, Annie leaned close to Jack. 大家开始吃时，安妮凑近杰克。

“We found the special magic,” she whispered. “The magic of community. Remember the rhyme?” She repeated Morgan’s words: “我们找到了特别的魔法，”她小声说。 “共同体的魔法。 记得那首韵诗吗？”她重复摩根的话：

“To find a special magic, “去寻找一种特别的魔法。

When work and toil are done, 当工作和辛劳都做完的时候。

Gather all together, 大家聚在一起。

Turn three worlds into one.” 把三个世界变成一个。”

“Oh, man,” said Jack. He’d forgotten all about it. “天啊，”杰克说。 他已经全忘了。

“We can go home now,” said Annie. “我们现在可以回家了，”安妮说。

“No way,” said Jack. “We have to eat first.” “不行，”杰克说。 “我们得先吃。”

Jack and Annie used their fingers to pick up their food. And they ate and ate and ate. Jack tried everything on his plate—except a little bit of eel and two clams. Everything he did eat, he liked—even the turnips. 杰克和安妮用手指抓起食物。 他们吃啊吃啊吃。 杰克尝了盘子里的每一样东西——除了一小块鳗鱼和两只蛤蜊。 凡是他吃的，他都喜欢——连芜菁也喜欢。

Food really tastes good, he thought as he chewed, when you eat it outside, on a beautiful day, with lots of nice people. 食物真好吃啊，他一边嚼一边想，当你在户外、在美好的日子里，和许多好人一起吃的时候。
