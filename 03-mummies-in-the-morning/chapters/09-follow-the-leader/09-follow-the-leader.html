9. Follow the Leader 9. 跟着领路的

It was pitch dark. 一片漆黑。

“What happened?” asked Annie. “怎么了？”安妮问。

“I don’t know. Something weird,” said Jack. “We have to get out of here fast. Push against the door.” “我不知道。 有点奇怪，”杰克说。 “我们得赶快离开这儿。 去推门。”

“Good idea,” said Annie in a small voice. “好主意，”安妮小声说。

They felt their way through the darkness. To the top of the stairs. 他们在黑暗里摸索着往前走。 走到楼梯顶上。

“Don’t worry. Everything’s going to be okay,” said Jack. He was trying to stay calm. “别担心。 一切都会没事的，”杰克说。 他努力让自己保持冷静。

“Of course,” said Annie. “当然了，”安妮说。

They leaned against the wooden door and pushed. 他们靠着木门用力推。

It wouldn’t budge. 门一动也不动。

They pushed harder. 他们推得更用力。

No use. 没用。

Jack took a deep breath. It was getting harder to breathe. And harder to stay calm. 杰克深深吸了一口气。 呼吸越来越困难。 也越来越难保持冷静。

“What can we do?” asked Annie. “我们怎么办？”安妮问。

“Just … just rest a moment,” said Jack, panting. “先……先歇一会儿，”杰克喘着气说。

His heart was pounding as he tried to see through the darkness. 他心跳得厉害，努力想在黑暗里看清东西。

“Maybe we should start down the hall,” he said. “Maybe we’ll eventually come to … to an exit.” “也许我们该沿着走廊往下走，”他说。 “也许最后会走到……走到一个出口。”

He wasn’t sure about that. But they had no choice. 他自己也不确定。 可他们没有别的办法。

“Come on,” he said. “Feel the wall.” “走吧，”他说。 “扶着墙走。”

Jack felt the stone wall as he climbed slowly down the stairs. Annie followed. 杰克摸着石墙，慢慢走下楼梯。安妮跟在后面。

Jack started down the dark hallway. It was impossible to see anything. 杰克沿着黑暗的走廊走下去。 什么也看不见。

But he kept going. Taking one step at a time. Moving his hands along the wall. 可他还是继续走。 一步一步地走。 手一直扶着墙。

He went around a corner. He went around another corner. He came to some stairs. He went up. 他拐过一个弯。 他又拐过一个弯。 他走到一段楼梯前。他走上去。

There was a door. He pushed against it. Annie pushed, too. This door wouldn’t budge either. 有一扇门。 他推了推门。 安妮也推。 这扇门也一动不动。

Was this the same door they had started at? 这是不是他们刚才出发的那扇门？

It was no use. They were trapped. 没用。 他们被困住了。

Annie took his hand in the dark. She squeezed it. 黑暗里，安妮握住他的手。她捏了捏。

They stood together at the top of the stairs. Listening to the silence. 他们一起站在楼梯顶上。 听着四周的安静。

“Meow.”

“Oh, man,” Jack whispered. “哦，天哪，”杰克小声说。

“He’s back!” said Annie. “它回来了！”安妮说。

“Meow.”

“Follow him!” cried Jack. “He’s going away from us.” “跟着它！”杰克喊道。 “它正离我们越来越远。”

They started down the dark hallway. Following the cat’s meow. 他们沿着黑暗的走廊走下去。 跟着猫咪的叫声。

Hands against the wall, Jack and Annie stumbled through the darkness. 手扶着墙，杰克和安妮在黑暗里跌跌撞撞地走。

“Meow.”

They followed the sound. All the way through the winding hallway. Down, down, down. 他们跟着那个声音。 一路穿过弯弯曲曲的走廊。往下，往下，再往下。

Around one corner, then another. And another … 拐过一个弯，又一个弯。再一个弯……

Finally they saw a light at the end of the tunnel. They rushed forward—out into the bright sunlight. 终于，他们看见通道尽头有光。 他们冲上前——冲进了明亮的阳光里。

“Yay!” Annie shouted. “耶！”安妮喊道。

But Jack was thinking. 可杰克在想事情。

“Annie,” he said. “How did we get out of the false passage?” “安妮，”他说。 “我们是怎么从假通道里出来的？”

“The cat,” said Annie. “那只猫，”安妮说。

“But how could the cat do it?” asked Jack. “可猫怎么能做到？”杰克问。

“Magic,” said Annie. “魔法，”安妮说。

Jack frowned. “But—” 杰克皱起眉。 “可是——”

“Look!” said Annie. She pointed. “快看！”安妮说。她用手指着。

The cat was bounding away. Over the sand. 那只猫正蹦蹦跳跳地跑开。跑过沙子。

“Thank you!” called Annie. “谢谢你！”安妮喊道。

“Thanks!” Jack shouted at the cat. “谢谢！”杰克也对猫喊。

His black tail waved. 它的黑尾巴晃了晃。

Then he disappeared in the shimmering waves of heat. 然后它消失在闪闪发亮的热浪里。

Jack looked toward the palm trees. At the top of one sat the tree house. Like a bird’s nest. 杰克朝棕榈树望去。 有一棵树顶上，坐着那座树屋。 像一个鸟窝。

“Time to go home,” Jack said. “该回家了，”杰克说。

He and Annie set off for the palm trees. It was a long hot walk back. 他和安妮朝棕榈树走去。 回去的路又长又热。

At last Annie grabbed onto the rope ladder. Then Jack. 终于，安妮抓住了绳梯。接着是杰克。

Once they were inside the tree house, Jack reached for the book about Pennsylvania. 他们一进树屋，杰克就去拿那本关于宾夕法尼亚的书。

Just then he heard a rumbling sound. The same sound they had heard in the pyramid. 就在这时，他听见一阵轰隆声。 跟他们在金字塔里听见的声音一样。

“Look!” Annie said, pointing out the window. “快看！”安妮说，她指向窗外。

Jack looked. 杰克看过去。

A boat was beside the pyramid. It was gliding over the sand. Like a boat sailing over the sea. 金字塔旁边有一条船。 它正从沙子上滑过去。 像船在海上航行。

Then it faded away. Into the distance. 然后它渐渐消失了。 消失在远方。

Was it just a mirage? 那只是海市蜃楼吗？

Or was the ghost-queen finally on her way to the Next Life? 还是幽灵女王终于踏上了去来世的路？

“Home, Jack,” whispered Annie. “回家吧，杰克，”安妮小声说。

Jack opened the Pennsylvania book. 杰克打开那本宾夕法尼亚的书。

He pointed to the picture of Frog Creek. 他指着蛙溪镇的图片。

“I wish we could go home,” he said. “我希望我们能回家，”他说。

The wind began to blow. 风开始刮起来。

The leaves began to shake. 叶子开始摇晃。

The wind blew harder. It whistled louder. 风刮得更猛了。 呼啸声更响了。

The tree house started to spin. 树屋开始旋转。

It spun faster and faster. 转得越来越快。

Then everything was still. 然后一切都静止了。

Absolutely silent. 绝对安静。
