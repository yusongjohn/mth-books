6. The Writing on the Wall 6. 墙上的字

Jack and Annie squinted at the pyramid wall. 杰克和安妮眯着眼看金字塔的墙壁。

A series of tiny pictures were carved into the stone. 石头上刻着一串小小的图画。

“There are four pictures here,” Jack told the ghost-queen. “这儿有四幅画，”杰克告诉幽灵女王。

“Describe them to me, Jack. One at a time, please,” she said. “把它们描述给我听，杰克。 请一幅一幅来，”她说。

Jack studied the first picture. 杰克仔细看第一幅画。

“Okay,” he said. “The first one is like this.” He made a zigzag in the air with his finger. “好，”他说。 “第一幅是这样的。”他用手指在空中画了一道锯齿线。

“Like stairs?” asked the ghost-queen. “像楼梯吗？”幽灵女王问。

“Yes, stairs!” said Jack. “Just like stairs.” “对，楼梯！”杰克说。 “就像楼梯。”

She nodded. 她点了点头。

Easy enough. 够简单。

Jack studied the second picture. 杰克仔细看第二幅画。

“The second one has a long box on the bottom,” he said. He drew it in the air. “第二幅下面有一个长盒子，”他说。 他在空中把它画出来。

The ghost-queen looked puzzled. 幽灵女王看起来很困惑。

“With three things on top. Like this,” said Annie. She drew squiggly lines in the air. “上面还有三样东西。 像这样，”安妮说。 她在空中画了几道弯弯曲曲的线。

The ghost-queen still seemed puzzled. 幽灵女王好像还是没明白。

“Like a hat,” said Jack. “像一顶帽子，”杰克说。

“Hat?” said the ghost-queen. “帽子？”幽灵女王说。

“No. More like a boat,” said Annie. “不对。 更像一条船，”安妮说。

“Boat?” said the ghost-queen. She got excited. “Boat?” “船？”幽灵女王说。她一下子激动起来。 “船？”

Jack took another look at the wall. 杰克又看了看墙壁。

“Yes. It could be a boat,” he said. “对。 可能是一条船，”他说。

The ghost-queen looked very happy. She smiled. 幽灵女王看上去很开心。她笑了。

“Yes. Of course,” she said. “对。 当然了。”她说。

Jack and Annie studied the next picture. 杰克和安妮仔细看下一幅图。

“The third one is like a thing that holds flowers,” said Annie. “第三个像是插花的东西，”安妮说。

“Or a thing that holds water,” said Jack. “或者是装水的东西，”杰克说。

“Like a jug?” asked the ghost-queen. “像罐子？”幽灵女王问。

“Exactly,” said Jack. “没错，”杰克说。

“Yes. A jug,” said Annie. “对。 就是罐子。”安妮说。

Jack and Annie studied the last picture. 杰克和安妮仔细看最后一幅图。

“And the last one looks like a pole that droops,” said Annie. “最后一个看起来像一根往下垂的杆子，”安妮说。

“Like a curved stick,” said Jack. “But one side is shorter than the other.” “像一根弯棍子，”杰克说。 “可是一边比另一边短。”

The ghost-queen looked puzzled. 幽灵女王一脸困惑。

“Wait,” said Jack. “I’ll draw it in my notebook. Big! So you can see it.” “等一下，”杰克说。 “我画在笔记本上。画大一点！ 这样你就能看清了。”

Jack put down the scepter and got out his pencil. He drew the hieroglyph. 杰克放下权杖，拿出铅笔。 他画出那个象形文字。

“A folded cloth,” said the ghost-queen. “一块叠好的布，”幽灵女王说。

“Well, not really,” said Jack. He studied his drawing. “嗯，不太像，”杰克说。 他仔细看自己的画。

“But that is the hieroglyph for a folded cloth,” said the ghost-queen. “可那就是‘叠好的布’的象形文字，”幽灵女王说。

“Well, okay,” said Jack. “好吧，行，”杰克说。

He looked at the fourth hieroglyph again. He still couldn’t see the folded cloth. Unless it was like a towel hanging over a bathroom rod. 他又看了看第四个象形文字。 他还是看不出那是叠好的布。 除非它像搭在卫生间毛巾杆上的毛巾。

“So that’s all of them,” said Annie. She pointed at each picture. “Stairs. Boat. Jug. Folded cloth.” “那这些就全了，”安妮说。 她一个一个指着那些图。 “楼梯。船。 罐子。叠好的布。”

Jack wrote the words in his notebook. 杰克把这些词写在笔记本上。

“So what does the message mean?” he asked the ghost-queen. “那这条信息是什么意思？”他问幽灵女王。

“Come,” she said. She held out her hand. “来，”她说。 她伸出手。

“Come to my burial chambers.” “到我的墓室来。”

And she floated away. 然后她飘走了。
