3. It’s Alive! 3. 它活了！

“Where did he go?” said Jack. “它去哪儿了？”杰克说。

He and Annie peeked through the hole. 他和安妮从洞口往里瞧。

They saw a long hallway. Burning torches lit the walls. Dark shadows loomed. 他们看见一条长长的走廊。 燃烧的火把照着墙壁。 黑影森森地压过来。

“Let’s go in,” said Annie. “我们进去吧，”安妮说。

“Wait,” said Jack. “等等，”杰克说。

He pulled out the Egypt book and turned to the section on pyramids. 他拿出埃及书，翻到讲金字塔的那一节。

He read the caption aloud: 他大声读出说明：

Pyramids were sometimes called Houses of the Dead. They were nearly all solid stone, except for the burial chambers deep inside. 金字塔有时被叫做死者之屋。 它们几乎全是实心的石头，只有里面深处才有墓室。

“Wow. Let’s go there. To the burial chambers,” said Annie. “I bet a mummy’s there.” “哇。我们去那儿吧。 去墓室，”安妮说。 “我敢说那儿有木乃伊。”

Jack took a deep breath. 杰克深深吸了一口气。

Then he stepped out of the hot, bright sunlight into the cool, dark pyramid. 然后他走出又热又亮的阳光，走进又凉又暗的金字塔。

The hallway was silent. 走廊里静悄悄的。

Floor, ceiling, walls—everything was stone. 地板、天花板、墙壁——全都是石头。

The floor slanted up from where they stood. 地板从他们站的地方往上斜。

“We have to go farther inside,” said Annie. “我们得再往里面走，”安妮说。

“Right,” said Jack. “But stay close behind me. Don’t talk. Don’t—” “对，”杰克说。 “不过要紧跟在我后面。别说话。 别——”

“Go! Just go!” said Annie. She gave him a little push. “走！ 赶紧走啊！”安妮说。 她轻轻推了他一把。

Jack started up the slanting floor of the hallway. 杰克沿着走廊倾斜的地板往上走。

Where was the cat? 那只猫在哪儿？

The hallway went on and on. 走廊一直往前延伸。

“Wait,” said Jack. “I want to look at the book.” “等等，”杰克说。 “我想看看这本书。”

He opened the Egypt book again. He held it below a torch on the wall. The book showed a picture of the inside of the pyramid. 他又打开埃及书。 他把书举到墙上的火把下面。 书上有一幅金字塔内部的图画。

“The burial chamber is in the middle of the pyramid. See?” Jack said. He pointed to the picture. “It seems to be straight ahead.” “墓室在金字塔的正中间。看见了吗？”杰克说。 他指着那幅画。 “好像就在正前方。”

Jack tucked the book under his arm. Then they headed deeper into the pyramid. 杰克把书夹在胳膊下。 然后他们往金字塔更深处走去。

Soon the floor became flat. The air felt different. Musty and stale. 很快，地板变得平坦了。 空气也不一样了。又霉又陈。

Jack opened the book again. “I think we’re almost at the burial chamber. See the picture? The hallway slants up. Then it gets flat. Then you come to the chamber. See, look—” 杰克又打开书。 “我觉得我们快到墓室了。看见这幅画了吗？ 走廊先往上斜。 然后变平。 然后就到墓室了。看，你看——”

“Eee- eee!” A strange cry shot through the pyramid. “咿——咿！”一声奇怪的叫声穿过金字塔。

Jack dropped the Egypt book. 杰克把埃及书掉在了地上。

Out of the shadows flew a white figure. 一个白色的身影从阴影里飞了出来。

It swooshed toward them! 它嗖地朝他们扑过来！

A mummy! 木乃伊！

“It’s alive!” Annie shouted. “它活了！”安妮大喊。
