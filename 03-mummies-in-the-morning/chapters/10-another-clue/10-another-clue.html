10. Another Clue 10. 又一个线索

Late-morning sunlight shone through the tree house window. Shadows danced on the walls and ceiling. 上午晚些时候的阳光从树屋窗户照进来。 影子在墙上和天花板上跳动。

Jack took a deep breath. He was lying on the floor of the tree house. 杰克深深吸了一口气。 他躺在树屋的地板上。

“I wonder what Mom’s making for lunch,” said Annie. She was looking out the window. “不知道妈妈午饭做什么，”安妮说。 她正朝窗外看。

Jack smiled. Lunch. Mom. Home. It all sounded so real. So calm and safe. 杰克笑了。午饭。 妈妈。家。 这一切听起来那么真实。那么安静，那么安全。

“I hope it’s peanut butter and jelly sandwiches,” he said. “希望是花生酱果酱三明治，”他说。

He closed his eyes. The wood floor felt cool. 他闭上眼睛。 木头地板凉凉的。

“Boy, this place is a mess,” said Annie. “We’d better make it neater. In case M comes back.” “哎呀，这儿真乱，”安妮说。 “我们最好收拾整齐一点。 万一M回来了。”

Jack had almost forgotten about M. 杰克差点忘了M。

Would they ever meet the mysterious M? The person who seemed to own all the books in the tree house? 他们会不会见到那个神秘的M？ 好像树屋里所有的书都是那个人的？

“Let’s put the Egypt book on the bottom of the pile,” said Annie. “我们把埃及书放在最下面吧，”安妮说。

“Good idea,” said Jack. He needed a rest before he visited any more ancient tombs. “好主意，”杰克说。 再去别的古代陵墓之前，他得先歇一歇。

“Let’s put the dinosaur book on top of the Egypt book,” said Annie. “恐龙书放在埃及书上面，”安妮说。

“Yeah, good,” said Jack. And a long rest before he visited another Tyrannosaurus rex. “嗯，好，”杰克说。 再见霸王龙之前，他还得好好休息很久。

“The castle book can go on the very top of the pile,” said Annie. “城堡书可以放在最上面，”安妮说。

Jack nodded and smiled. He liked thinking about the knight on the cover of the castle book. He felt as if the knight were his friend. 杰克点点头，笑了。 他喜欢想城堡书封面上的那个骑士。 他觉得那个骑士像是他的朋友。

“Jack,” said Annie. “ Look!” “杰克，”安妮说。

Jack opened his eyes. She was pointing at the wooden floor. 杰克睁开眼睛。 她正指着木头地板。

“What is it?” he asked. “那是什么？”他问。

“You have to see for yourself.” “你得自己看。”

Jack groaned as he got up. He stood next to Annie and looked at the floor. He didn’t see anything. 杰克哼了一声，站起来。 他站在安妮旁边，看着地板。 他什么也没看见。

“Turn your head a little,” said Annie. “You have to catch the light just right.” “头稍微转一下，”安妮说。 “得刚好对着光才看得见。”

Jack tipped his head to one side. Something was shining on the floor. 杰克把头往一边歪。 地板上有什么东西在发光。

He tipped his head a bit more. A letter came into focus. 他又歪了一点。 一个字母渐渐清楚了。

The letter M! It shimmered in the sunlight. 字母M！ 它在阳光里闪闪发亮。

This proved the tree house belonged to M. 这证明树屋是M的。

Absolutely for sure. No question. No doubt about it. 绝对没错。毫无疑问。 一点也不用怀疑。

Jack touched the M with his finger. His skin tingled. 杰克用手指摸了摸那个M。他的皮肤麻麻的。

Just then the leaves trembled. The wind picked up. 就在这时，叶子颤动起来。 风刮起来了。

“Let’s go down now,” he said. “我们现在下去吧，”他说。

Jack grabbed his backpack. Then he and Annie climbed down the ladder. 杰克抓起背包。 然后他和安妮爬下梯子。

As they stood on the ground below the tree house, Jack heard a sound in the bushes. 他们站在树屋下面的地上时，杰克听见灌木丛里有声音。

“Who’s there?” he called. “谁在那儿？”他喊道。

The woods grew still. 树林安静下来。

“I’m going to bring the medallion back soon,” Jack said loudly. “And the bookmark, too. Both of them. Tomorrow!” “我很快就会把勋章还回来，”杰克大声说。 “还有书签。两样都还。 明天！”

“Who are you talking to?” asked Annie. “你在跟谁说话？”安妮问。

“I feel like M is nearby,” Jack whispered. “我觉得M就在附近，”杰克小声说。

Annie’s eyes grew wide. “Should we look for him?” 安妮的眼睛睁得大大的。 “我们要不要去找他？”

But just then their mother’s voice came from the distance. “Ja-ack! An-nie!” 可就在这时，远处传来妈妈的声音。 “杰——克！安——妮！”

Jack and Annie looked around at the trees. Then they looked at each other. 杰克和安妮朝四周的树看了看。 然后他们对视一眼。

“Tomorrow,” they said together. “明天，”他们一起说。

And they took off, running out of the woods. 然后他们拔腿就跑，跑出了树林。

They ran down their street. 他们跑过自家那条街。

They ran across their yard. 他们跑过院子。

They ran into their house. 他们跑进家里。

They ran into their kitchen. 他们跑进厨房。

They ran right into their mom. 他们正好撞上了妈妈。

She was making peanut butter and jelly sandwiches. 她正在做花生酱果酱三明治。
