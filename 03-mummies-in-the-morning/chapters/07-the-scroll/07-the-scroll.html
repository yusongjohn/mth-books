7. The Scroll 7. 卷轴

Jack put the scepter and his notebook and pencil into his pack. 杰克把权杖、笔记本和铅笔放进背包。

He and Annie followed the ghost-queen. Deeper into the pyramid. Until they came to some stairs. 他和安妮跟着幽灵女王。 往金字塔更里面走。 一直走到一段楼梯前。

“The STAIRS!” said Jack and Annie. “楼梯！”杰克和安妮一起说。

The ghost-queen floated up the stairs. 幽灵女王飘上楼梯。

Jack and Annie followed. 杰克和安妮跟上去。

The ghost-queen floated right through a wooden door. 幽灵女王径直从一扇木门穿了过去。

Jack and Annie pushed on the door. It opened slowly. 杰克和安妮推了推门。门慢慢打开了。

They stepped into a cold, drafty room. 他们走进一间又冷又漏风的房间。

The ghost-queen was nowhere in sight. 幽灵女王不见了。

Dim torchlight lit the huge room. It had a very high ceiling. On one side was a pile of tables, chairs, and musical instruments. 昏暗的火把光照着这间大屋子。 天花板非常高。 一边堆着桌子、椅子，还有乐器。

On the other side of the room was a small wooden boat. 房间另一边有一条小木船。

“The BOAT!” said Jack. “船！”杰克说。

“What’s it doing inside Queen Hutepi’s pyramid?” asked Annie. “它怎么会在哈特皮女王的金字塔里？”安妮问。

“Maybe it’s supposed to carry her to the Next Life,” said Jack. “也许它是用来载她去来世的，”杰克说。

He and Annie went over to the boat. They looked inside it. 他和安妮走到船边。 他们往船里看。

The boat was filled with many things. Gold plates. Painted cups. Jeweled goblets. Woven baskets. Jewelry with blue stones. Small wooden statues. 船里装满了好多东西。金盘子。 彩绘杯子。镶着宝石的高脚杯。 编织的篮子。 镶着蓝石头的珠宝。 小小的木头雕像。

“Look!” said Jack. “快看！”杰克说。

He reached into the boat and lifted out a clay jug. 他伸手到船里，拿出一个陶罐子。

“The JUG!” said Annie. “罐子！”安妮说。

Jack looked inside the jug. 杰克往罐子里看。

“Something’s in here,” he said. “里面有东西，”他说。

“What is it?” asked Annie. “是什么？”安妮问。

Jack felt down inside the jug. 杰克把手伸进罐子里摸。

“It feels like a big napkin,” he said. “摸起来像一块大餐巾，”他说。

“The FOLDED CLOTH!” said Annie. “叠好的布！”安妮说。

Jack reached into the jug and pulled out the folded cloth. It was wrapped around an ancient-looking scroll. 杰克把手伸进罐子，把那块叠好的布拉出来。 布里裹着一卷看起来很古老的卷轴。

Jack slowly unrolled the scroll. 杰克慢慢展开卷轴。

It was covered with wonderful hieroglyphs. 上面写满了好看的象形文字。

“The Book of the Dead!” whispered Annie. “We found it. We found her book.” “亡灵之书！”安妮小声说。 “我们找到了。 我们找到她的书了。”

“Oh, man.” Jack traced his finger over the scroll. It felt like very old paper. “哦，天哪。”杰克用手指顺着卷轴摸过去。 摸起来像很旧的纸。

“Queen Hutepi!” called Annie. “We have it! We found your Book of the Dead!” “哈特皮女王！”安妮喊道。 “我们拿到了！ 我们找到你的亡灵之书了！”

Silence. 一片安静。

“Queen Hutepi!” “哈特皮女王！”

Then another door on the other side of the chamber creaked open. 接着，墓室另一边又有一扇门吱呀一声开了。

“In there,” said Annie. “Maybe she’s in there.” “在那儿，”安妮说。 “也许她在里面。”

Jack’s heart was pounding. Cold air was coming through the open doorway. 杰克的心跳得厉害。 冷风从开着的门口吹进来。

“Come on,” said Annie. “走吧，”安妮说。

“Wait—” “等一下——”

“No,” said Annie. “She’s waited a thousand years for her book. Don’t make her wait anymore.” “不行，”安妮说。 “她等这本书等了一千年。 别再让她等了。”

Jack put the ancient scroll into his backpack. Then he and Annie slowly started to cross the drafty room. 杰克把那卷古老的卷轴放进背包。 然后他和安妮慢慢穿过这间漏风的房间。

They came to the open door. Annie went through first. 他们走到那扇开着的门前。 安妮先走了进去。

“Hurry, Jack!” she said. “快点，杰克！”她说。

Jack stepped into the other room. 杰克走进另一间屋子。

It was nearly bare. Except for a long gold box. The box was open. The cover was on the floor. 里面几乎是空的。 只除了一只长长的金盒子。盒子是开着的。 盖子在地板上。

“Queen Hutepi?” called Annie.

Silence.

“We found it,” said Annie. “Your Book of the Dead.” “我们找到了，”安妮说。 “你的亡灵之书。”

There was still no sign of the ghost-queen. 还是看不见幽灵女王。

The gold box glowed. 金盒子发着光。

Jack could barely breathe. “Let’s leave the scroll on the floor. And go,” he said. 杰克几乎喘不过气来。 “我们把卷轴放在地上，然后走吧，”他说。

“No. I think we should leave it in there,” said Annie. She pointed to the gold box. “不。 我觉得应该放进去，”安妮说。 她指着那个金盒子。

“No,” said Jack. “不行，”杰克说。

“Don’t be afraid,” said Annie. “Come on.” “别害怕，”安妮说。 “走吧。”

Annie took Jack by the arm. They walked together. Across the room. To the glowing gold box. 安妮拉住杰克的胳膊。 他们一起走。穿过房间。 走到那只发着光的金盒子前。

They stopped in front of the box. And they peered inside. 他们在盒子前面停下来。 然后往里面看。
