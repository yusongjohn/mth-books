2. Oh, Man. Mummies! 2. 天哪。木乃伊！

Jack and Annie looked out the window. 杰克和安妮朝窗外看去。

The tree house was perched on the top of a palm tree. The tree stood with other palm trees. A patch of green surrounded by a sandy desert. 树屋停在一棵棕榈树的树顶上。 这棵树和其他棕榈树站在一起。 一小片绿地，四周全是沙沙的沙漠。

“Meow!”

Jack and Annie looked down. 杰克和安妮往下看。

The black cat was sitting at the base of the tree. His yellow eyes were staring up at Jack and Annie. 那只黑猫坐在树底下。 它那双黄眼睛正抬头盯着杰克和安妮。

“Hi!” Annie shouted. “你好！”安妮大声喊。

“Shhh,” said Jack. “Someone might hear you.” “嘘，”杰克说。 “说不定有人会听见。”

“In the middle of the desert?” said Annie. “在沙漠正中间？”安妮说。

The black cat stood and began walking around the tree. 黑猫站起来，开始绕着树走。

“Come back!” Annie called. She leaned out the window to see where the cat was going. “回来！”安妮喊道。 她把身子探出窗外，想看看猫要去哪儿。

“Oh, wow!” she said. “Look, Jack.” “哇！”她说。 “快看，杰克。”

Jack leaned forward and looked down. 杰克往前探身，朝下看。

The cat was running away from the palm trees. Toward a giant pyramid in the desert. 那只猫正从棕榈树这儿跑开。 朝着沙漠里一座巨大的金字塔跑去。

A parade was going toward the pyramid. The same parade as in the Egypt book. 一支队伍正朝着金字塔走去。 跟埃及书里的那支队伍一模一样。

“It’s the picture from the book!” said Jack. “就是书上的那幅画！”杰克说。

“What are those people doing?” asked Annie. “那些人在干什么？”安妮问。

Jack looked down at the Egypt book. He read the words under the picture: 杰克低头看着埃及书。 他读了图画下面的文字：

When a royal person died, a grand funeral procession took place. Family, servants, and mourners followed the coffin. The coffin was called a sarcophagus. It was pulled on a sled by four oxen. 一位王室的人去世时，会举行盛大的送葬队伍。 家人、仆人和送葬的人跟在棺材后面。 这种棺材叫做石棺。 它由四头牛拉着雪橇运送。

“It’s an Egyptian funeral,” said Jack. “The box is called a sar … sar … sar … oh, forget it.” “这是一场埃及葬礼，”杰克说。 “那个盒子叫石……石……石……唉，算了。”

He looked out the window again. 他又朝窗外看去。

Oxen, sled, Egyptians, black cat. All were moving in a slow, dreamy way. 牛，雪橇，埃及人，黑猫。 全都慢慢地、像做梦一样向前走。

“I’d better make some notes about this,” said Jack. “我最好把这些记下来，”杰克说。

He reached into his backpack and pulled out his notebook. Jack always kept notes. 他伸手进背包，拿出笔记本。 杰克总是做笔记。

“Wait,” said Jack. And he wrote: “等一下，”杰克说。接着他写道：

图中文字：Coffin called sarcophagus 图中文字：棺材叫石棺

“We’d better hurry,” said Annie, “if we want to see the mummy.” “我们得快点，”安妮说，“要是想看到木乃伊的话。”

She started down the rope ladder. 她开始顺着绳梯往下爬。

Jack looked up from his notebook. 杰克从笔记本上抬起头。

“Mummy?” he said. “木乃伊？”他说。

“There’s probably a mummy in that gold box,” Annie called up. “We’re in ancient Egypt. Remember?” “那个金盒子里大概有一具木乃伊，”安妮朝上喊。 “我们在古埃及。记得吗？”

Jack loved mummies. He put down his pencil. 杰克超喜欢木乃伊。 他放下铅笔。

“Good-bye, Jack!” called Annie. “再见，杰克！”安妮喊道。

“Wait!” Jack called. “等等！”杰克喊。

“Mummies!” Annie shouted. “木乃伊！”安妮大喊。

“Oh, man,” said Jack weakly. “Mummies!” She sure knew how to get to him. “天哪，”杰克有气无力地说。 “木乃伊！”她可太知道怎么说动他了。

Jack shoved his notebook and the Egypt book into his pack. Then he started down the ladder. 杰克把笔记本和埃及书塞进背包。 然后他开始往下爬梯子。

When he got to the ground, he and Annie took off across the sand. 一落到地上，他就和安妮穿过沙子跑了起来。

But as they ran a strange thing happened. 可他们跑着跑着，一件奇怪的事发生了。

The closer they got to the parade, the harder it was to see it. 离队伍越近，就越看不清它。

Then suddenly it was gone. The strange parade had disappeared. Vanished. 然后忽然间，它不见了。 那支奇怪的队伍消失了。没了。

But the great stone pyramid was still there. Towering above them. 可那座巨大的石头金字塔还在。 高高地耸立在他们头顶上。

Panting, Jack looked around. 杰克喘着气，四处张望。

What had happened? Where were the people? The oxen? The gold box? The cat? 发生了什么事？ 那些人呢？那些牛呢？ 金盒子呢？那只猫呢？

“They’re gone,” said Annie. “他们都没了，”安妮说。

“Where did they go?” said Jack. “他们去哪儿了？”杰克说。

“Maybe they were ghosts,” said Annie. “也许他们是幽灵，”安妮说。

“Don’t be silly. There’s no such thing as ghosts,” said Jack. “It must have been a mirage.” “别傻了。 根本没有幽灵这种东西，”杰克说。 “那一定是海市蜃楼。”

“A what?” “是什么？”

“Mirage. It happens in the desert all the time,” said Jack. “It looks like something’s there. But it just turns out to be the sunlight reflecting through heat.” “海市蜃楼。 沙漠里经常会这样，”杰克说。 “看起来好像有东西在那儿。 其实只是阳光穿过热气反射出来的。”

“How could sunlight look like people, a mummy box, and a bunch of cows?” said Annie. “阳光怎么会看起来像人、木乃伊盒子，还有一群牛呢？”安妮说。

Jack frowned. 杰克皱起眉头。

“Ghosts,” she said. “就是幽灵，”她说。

“No way,” said Jack. “才不是，”杰克说。

“Look!” Annie pointed at the pyramid. Near the base was the sleek black cat. “快看！”安妮指着金字塔。 靠近底部，那只油亮的黑猫在那儿。

He was standing alone. He was staring at Jack and Annie. 它独自站着。 正盯着杰克和安妮。

“He’ s no mirage,” said Annie. “它可不是海市蜃楼，”安妮说。

The cat started to slink away. He walked along the base of the pyramid and slid around a corner. 猫开始悄悄溜走。 它沿着金字塔底部走，从拐角滑了过去。

“Where’s he going?” said Jack. “它要去哪儿？”杰克说。

“Let’s find out,” said Annie. “我们去看看，”安妮说。

They dashed around the corner—just in time to see the cat disappear through a hole in the pyramid. 他们冲过拐角——正好看见那只猫钻进金字塔上的一个洞里，消失了。
