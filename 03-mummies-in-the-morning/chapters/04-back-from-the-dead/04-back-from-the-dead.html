4. Back from the Dead 4. 死而复生

Jack pulled Annie down. 杰克把安妮拉低。

The white figure moved swiftly past them. Then disappeared into the shadows. 那个白色身影飞快地从他们身边掠过。 然后消失在阴影里。

“A mummy,” said Annie. “Back from the dead!” “木乃伊，”安妮说。 “死而复生了！”

“F-forget it,” stammered Jack. “Mummies aren’t alive.” He picked up the Egypt book. “不、不可能，”杰克结结巴巴地说。 “木乃伊不会活过来。”他捡起埃及书。

“What’s this?” said Annie. She lifted something from the floor. “Look. The mummy dropped this thing.” “这是什么？”安妮说。 她从地上捡起一样东西。 “看。 木乃伊把这东西掉在这儿了。”

It was a gold stick. About a foot long. A dog’s head was carved on one end. 那是一根金棍子。 大约一英尺长。 一头刻着一个狗头。

“It looks like a scepter,” said Jack. “看起来像权杖，”杰克说。

“What’s that?” asked Annie. “那是什么？”安妮问。

“It’s a thing kings and queens carry,” said Jack. “It means they have power over the people.” “就是国王和女王拿着的东西，”杰克说。 “表示他们管着大家。”

“Come back, mummy!” Annie called. “We found your scepter. Come back! We want to help you!” “我们找到你的权杖了。回来吧！ 我们想帮你！”

“Shush!” said Jack. “Are you nuts?” “嘘！”杰克说。 “你疯了吗？”

“But the mummy—” “可是木乃伊——”

“That was no mummy,” said Jack. “It was a person. A real person.” “那不是木乃伊，”杰克说。 “那是人。一个真人。”

“What kind of person would be inside a pyramid?” asked Annie. “什么样的人会待在金字塔里面？”安妮问。

“I don’t know,” said Jack. “Maybe the book can help us.” “我不知道，”杰克说。 “也许这本书能帮我们。”

He flipped through the book. At last he found a picture of a person in a pyramid. He read: 他快速翻着书。 终于，他找到一幅金字塔里有人的画。他读道：

Tomb robbers often carried off the treasure buried with mummies. False passages were sometimes built to stop the robbers. 盗墓贼常常把跟木乃伊一起埋下的宝物抢走。 有时会修一些假通道，用来挡住盗贼。

Jack closed the book. 杰克合上书。

“No live mummy,” he said. “Just a tomb robber.” “没有活的木乃伊，”他说。 “只是个盗墓贼。”

“Yikes. A tomb robber?” said Annie. “呀。 盗墓贼？”安妮说。

“Yeah, a robber who steals stuff from tombs.” “对，就是从陵墓里偷东西的盗贼。”

“But what if the robber comes back,” said Annie. “We’d better leave.” “可要是那个盗贼回来了呢，”安妮说。 “我们最好离开。”

“Right,” said Jack. “But first I want to write something down.” “对，”杰克说。 “不过我先想写点东西。”

He put the Egypt book into his pack. He pulled out his notebook and pencil. 他把埃及书放进背包。 他拿出笔记本和铅笔。

He started writing in his notebook: 他开始在笔记本上写：

图中文字：tomb robber 图中文字：盗墓贼

“Jack—” said Annie. “杰克——”安妮说。

“Just a second,” said Jack. He kept writing: “等一下，”杰克说。他继续写：

图中文字：tomelai 图中文字：tomelai

“Jack! Look!” said Annie. “杰克！ 快看！”安妮说。

Jack felt a whoosh of cold air. He looked up. A wave of terror went through him. 杰克感到一阵冷风嗖地吹来。他抬起头。 一阵恐惧涌过全身。

Another figure was moving slowly toward them. 又一个身影正慢慢向他们走来。

It wasn’t a tomb robber. 那不是盗墓贼。

No. It was a lady. A beautiful Egyptian lady. 不是。那是一位女士。 一位美丽的埃及女士。

She wore flowers in her black hair. Her long white dress had many tiny pleats. Her gold jewelry glittered. 她的黑头发上戴着花。 她长长的白裙子有很多细细的褶。 她的金首饰闪闪发亮。

“Here, Jack,” Annie whispered. “Give her this.” She handed him the gold scepter. “给，杰克，”安妮小声说。 “把这个给她。”她把金权杖递给他。

The lady stopped in front of them. 那位女士在他们面前停住了。

Jack held out the scepter. His hand was trembling. 杰克把权杖递出去。 他的手在发抖。

He gasped. The scepter passed right through the lady’s hand. 他倒吸了一口气。 权杖从女士的手里直接穿了过去。

She was made of air. 她是空气做成的。
