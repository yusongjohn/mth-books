8. The Mummy 8. 木乃伊

A real mummy. 一具真正的木乃伊。

Bandages were still wrapped around the bald skull. But most of the bandages had come off the face. 光秃秃的头骨上还缠着绷带。 可脸上的绷带差不多都掉了。

It was Hutepi. Queen of the Nile. 那是哈特皮。 尼罗河女王。

Her broken teeth were showing. Her little wrinkled ears. Her squashed nose. Her withered flesh. Her hollow eye sockets. 她露出了残缺的牙齿。 她小小的、皱巴巴的耳朵。 她扁扁的鼻子。 她干枯的皮肉。 她空空的眼眶。

Plus the rotting bandages on her body were coming off. You could see bones. 身上腐烂的绷带也在往下掉。 能看见骨头。

“Oh, gross!” cried Annie. “Let’s go!” “好恶心！”安妮叫道。 “我们走吧！”

“No,” said Jack. “It’s interesting.” “不，”杰克说。 “这很有意思。”

“Forget it!” said Annie. She started out of the room. “才不要！”安妮说。 她往屋外走。

“Wait, Annie.” “等等，安妮。”

“Come on, Jack. Hurry!” cried Annie. She was standing by the door. “快走，杰克。 快点！”安妮喊道。 她站在门口。

Jack pulled out the Egypt book and flipped to a picture of a mummy. He read aloud: 杰克拿出埃及书，翻到一幅木乃伊的图。他大声读起来：

Ancient Egyptians tried to protect the body so it would last forever. First it was dried out with salt. 古埃及人想保护尸体，让它永远保存下去。 先用盐把身体弄干。

“Ugh, stop!” said Annie. “呃，别念了！”安妮说。

“Listen,” said Jack. He kept reading: “听着，”杰克说。他继续读：

Next it was covered with oil. Then it was wrapped tightly in bandages. The brain was removed by— 然后涂上油。 再紧紧缠上绷带。 大脑是这样取出来的——

“Yuck! Stop!” cried Annie. “Good-bye!” She dashed out of the room. “恶心！ 别念了！”安妮叫道。 “再见！”她冲出了房间。

“Annie!” called Jack. “We have to give her the Book of the Dead!” “安妮！”杰克喊道。 “我们得把亡灵之书给她！”

But Annie was gone. 可安妮已经走了。

Jack reached into his pack. He pulled out the scroll and the scepter. He put them next to the mummy’s skull. 杰克把手伸进背包。 他拿出卷轴和权杖。 他把它们放在木乃伊的头骨旁边。

Was it just his imagination? Or did a deep sigh seem to shudder throughout the room? Did the mummy’s face grow calmer? 只是他的想象吗？ 还是一声深深的叹息，好像让整间屋子都颤了一下？ 木乃伊的脸是不是变得更安详了？

Jack held his breath as he backed away. Out of the mummy room. Out of the boat room. Down the stairs. 杰克屏住呼吸，往后退。 退出木乃伊的房间。 退出放船的房间。走下楼梯。

At the bottom of the stairs, he heaved his own sigh. A sigh of relief. 到了楼梯底下，他自己也长长叹了一口气。松了一口气。

He looked down the hallway. It was empty. 他朝走廊望去。空荡荡的。

“Hey! Where are you?” he said. “嘿！ 你在哪儿？”他说。

No answer. 没有人回答。

Where in the world was Annie? 安妮到底去哪儿了？

Jack started down the hallway. 杰克沿着走廊走下去。

“Annie!” he called. “安妮！”他喊道。

Had she run out of the pyramid? Was she already outside? 她是不是跑出金字塔了？ 她是不是已经在外面了？

“Annie!” “安妮！”

“Help, Jack!” came a cry. The voice sounded far away. “救命，杰克！”传来一声喊。 声音听起来很远。

It was Annie! Where was she? 是安妮！她在哪儿？

“Help, Jack!” “救命，杰克！”

“Annie!” “安妮！”

Jack started to run. Along the shadowy hallway. 杰克跑了起来。 沿着阴暗的走廊。

“Help, Jack!” Her cry seemed fainter. “救命，杰克！”她的喊声好像更弱了。

Jack stopped. 杰克停下来。

He was running away from her voice. 他跑的方向跟她的声音相反。

“Annie!” he called. He went back toward the burial chambers. “安妮！”他喊道。 他往回走向墓室。

“Jack!” “杰克！”

There! Her voice was louder. 在那儿！ 她的声音更大了。

“Jack!” “杰克！”

Even louder! 更大了！

Jack climbed the stairs. He went back into the boat room. 杰克爬上楼梯。 他回到放船的房间。

He looked around the room. At the furniture. The musical instruments. The boat. 他环顾房间。看看家具。 看看乐器。看看那条船。

Then he saw it. Another door! Right next to the door he had just come through. 然后他看见了。还有一扇门！ 就在他刚进来的那扇门旁边。

The other door was open. 那扇门是开着的。

Jack dashed through it. He found himself at the top of some stairs. 杰克冲了进去。 他发现自己站在一段楼梯顶上。

They were just like the stairs in the other hallway. 跟另一条走廊的楼梯一模一样。

He went down into the hallway. It was lit by torches on the wall. 他走下楼梯，进了走廊。 墙上的火把照着走廊。

It was just like the other hallway. 跟另一条走廊一模一样。

“Annie!” he called. “安妮！”他喊道。

“Jack!” “杰克！”

“Annie!” “安妮！”

“Jack!” “杰克！”

She was running through the hallway toward him. 她正沿着走廊朝他跑来。

She crashed into him. 她撞到了他身上。

“I was lost!” she cried. “我迷路了！”她叫道。

“I think this is one of those false passages. Built to fool the tomb robbers,” said Jack. “我想这是那种假通道。 专门用来骗盗墓的人的，”杰克说。

“A false passage?” said Annie, panting. “假通道？”安妮喘着气说。

“Yeah, it looks just like the right hallway,” said Jack. “We have to go back into the boat room. And out the right door.” “对，看起来跟真正的走廊一模一样，”杰克说。 “我们得回到放船的房间。 再从正确的门出去。”

Just then they heard a creaking noise. 就在这时，他们听见吱呀一声。

Jack and Annie turned around. They looked up the stairs. 杰克和安妮转过身。 他们朝楼梯上面看。

Then they watched in horror as the door slowly creaked shut. 然后他们惊恐地看着那扇门慢慢吱呀一声关上了。

A deep sound rumbled in the distance. And all the torches went out. 远处传来一阵低沉的轰隆声。 所有的火把都灭了。
